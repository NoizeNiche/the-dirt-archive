## Evidence basis
- ECB x NAMM identifies R Diner Sounds as a Nagoya brand whose pedals connect sound and food as the central concept. SPICE CURRY is documented as an overdrive based on a transparent-style drive platform with 9V/18V and clipping options.
- ABiRu's current R Diner Sounds catalog identifies SPICE CURRY SC-2, MISOKATSU MK-2 and CHICKEN HAMMER CH-1, and preserves discontinued SC-1 and MK-1. CHICKEN HAMMER is explicitly introduced as a fuzz.
- Japanese retail coverage independently documents SPICE CURRY, MISOKATSU and the brand's product concept.
- RIPPERS historical Japanese coverage confirms a 2015 launch by a former major electronic-musical-instrument developer, but its most clearly documented KENTA/KENTALOW/KENBLE family is explicitly clone-oriented; no sufficiently broad original-design catalog was recovered in this pass.

## Duplicate / relationship gate
- R Diner Sounds WTKG is excluded from central dirt inventory because it is a clean/treble booster 2-in-1 utility product.
- SPICY KATSU CURRY remains a collaboration / 2-in-1 edition combining SPICE CURRY and MISOKATSU rather than a separate conceptual model.
- R Diner Sounds SC-1/SC-2 and MISOKATSU MK-1/MK-2 are retained as named product generations/states for historical traceability and should later be normalized into family relationships.
- RIPPERS KENTA, AKEMI, KENTA LOW, KENBLE and related clone families remain research relationships rather than canonical original-dirt inventory until stronger evidence of original conceptual products is recovered.
- No schematics, PCB layouts, gutshots, cloning instructions, or complete BOM/component inventories were added.

## Important boundary decisions
- Product photography references remain evidence trails for later credit/licensing review and are not automatic republication permission.
- Limited editions and collaboration enclosures should remain edition/specimen metadata unless a meaningful functional/product distinction warrants separate conceptual treatment.
