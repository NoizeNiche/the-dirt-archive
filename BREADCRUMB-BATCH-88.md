## Evidence basis
- One Control's first-party current catalog contains a broad overdrive/distortion/fuzz range, including BJF Series products and later 4K/BIYH families. Its history page documents the 2021 rollout of Anodized Brown Distortion 4K, Strawberry Red Overdrive variants, Dyna Red Distortion 4K, Rebel Red Distortion 4K and other named dirt models.
- One Control's current BJFE in your hand collection contains Dyna Red Distortion 4K, Honey Bee OD 4K and Shades Of Green O.D. Deluxe, providing a direct product-family bridge to the BJFE designer lineage.
- Noah'sark/Effects Database preserves 15 historical products, including Distortion M, Distortion B, Green Over Drive, Purple Fuzz and Yellow Over Drive; a 2011 Japanese product notice documents the compact line's return after an earlier discontinuation. However, current evidence supports Noah'sark primarily as an Ikebe house/private-label brand and does not yet establish a single in-house manufacturer for every model.

## Duplicate / relationship gate
- Do not create a separate BJFE builder record from One Control's catalog alone. Preserve Bjorn Juhl/BJFe as a designer lineage and One Control as the public brand/manufacturing presentation, with explicit relationship metadata where evidence supports it.
- One Control 4K, RC, DLX, Japonism and BIYH states should later be normalized into family/generation/edition relationships instead of multiplying conceptual models unnecessarily.
- Noah'sark remains a private-label/dealer-brand research relationship. Ikebe's ownership/house-brand relationship is useful genealogy evidence but is not treated as proof that Ikebe manufactured all historical pedals in-house.
- Clean utilities, buffers, switchers, pedal kits and non-dirt accessories remain outside the central dirt inventory.

## Important boundary decisions
- Product photography references are source trails only. No automatic republication rights are inferred from retailer or archive images.
- No schematics, PCB layouts, gutshots, cloning instructions, or complete BOM/component inventories were added.
- Model names are preserved where the manufacturer presents them as distinct products; cosmetic-only changes should remain edition/specimen metadata.
