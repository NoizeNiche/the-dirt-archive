Correction channel

Help keep the archive accurate.

The Dirt Department is maintained as a factual reference. Builders, collectors, repair techs, historians, and other visitors can report missing pedals, wrong model identities, version distinctions, inaccurate photographs, or better source material.

What to report

Model identity, builder attribution, version or edition relationship, missing record, exact-photo problem, documented control/power detail, or an authoritative source we should review.

What helps

Give the exact Builder + Pedal identity, explain what appears wrong, and include a manufacturer page, manual, dated catalog, exact-unit photo, or other reliable evidence when available.

What happens next

Submissions are reviewed before publication. A correction request does not directly change the canonical catalog, and an uncertain claim will remain unresolved rather than being guessed.
