## Evidence basis
- LEP International's company history dates the Animals Pedal launch to 2016 and records earlier Ninevolt Pedals releases beginning in 2015; its own history records Major Overdrive, Rover Fuzz, Rust Rod Fuzz, Surfing Bear Overdrive, I Was A Wolf In The Forest Distortion, Tioga Road Cycling Distortion, In Oct,3 Foxes talking of dreamy FUZZ and PUSH & PULL DISTORTION among the brand's releases.
- Animals Pedal's own brand page explicitly describes the line as working with globally known pedal designers and identifies named design/engineering relationships with BJFE, Skreddy Pedals and Wren and Cuff. Therefore collaborators remain designer/collaboration relationships, not duplicate builder entities.
- Animals Pedal's pedal-summary page documents FISHING IS AS FUN AS FUZZ as a Civil War-era Russian Big Muff-inspired fuzz and the current store preserves Angel Bear Face Fuzz and the wider dirt category.
- LEP International's history dates Effects Bakery to 2018 and identifies it as an original brand. Current Ninevolt product pages document Bagel OverDrive as a named overdrive with an explicitly stated low-cost bulk-production model and specialist sound-design input; current news/catalog material preserves Croissant Distortion, Sandwich Fuzz and KAMOME DISTORTION, while collaboration pages document Suguru Foods Big Katsu Distortion and Fukuda Pan An Butter Coppe Drive.

## Duplicate / relationship gate
- Animals Pedal and Effects Bakery are distinct public brands even though both originate in the LEP International/Ninevolt ecosystem.
- BJFE/BJF, Skreddy Pedals and Wren and Cuff remain designer/production collaborators associated with Animals Pedal products. Do not create duplicate Animals builder identities from those collaborations.
- Limited, charity, cosmetic-illustrated and food-brand collaboration editions remain product states or collaboration relationships unless a meaningful product-function change warrants a separate conceptual model.
- Effects Bakery's Cream Pan Booster and other clean utility products remain outside the central dirt inventory.
- No schematics, PCB layouts, gutshots, cloning instructions, or complete BOM/component inventories were added.

## Important boundary decisions
- Animals Pedal is treated as a canonical public brand lineage because its first-party history establishes it as an original LEP International/Ninevolt brand with a substantial named catalog, even though many individual designs involve external pedal designers.
- Effects Bakery is likewise treated as a canonical public brand lineage because LEP's own company history identifies it as an original 2018 brand with its own catalog and product development system.
- Product photography references remain evidence trails for later credit/licensing review and are not automatic republication permission.
