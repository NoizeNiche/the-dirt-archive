## Evidence basis
- Sound Project SIVA first-party About page identifies Ozawa Hiroshi in Kitakyushu, Fukuoka and explicitly states that he manages SIVA and makes all products. Its first-party products page separates distortion/overdrive and fuzz categories and documents Crispy Overdrive, Secretarial Distortion, Unexpected Overdrive, Mechanical Cute Partner Distortion and the low-gain fuzz "If you smiled,this summer would be forever." The Flammable distortion page documents its development as a collaboration/signature with Misaki Ohmura.
- RoShi Pedals' current first-party shop lists Blacklon, Blacklon GINZA, DEZZYNER, ELF FUZZ SUSTAINER, GRUFF, GRUFF V1, Plexition, Plexition Re:voice and Seji. Japanese retail pages identify Blacklon as Made in Japan and document its overdrive/amp-style function; Seji is presented as a further development of the Natural Drive concept.
- Phantom fx is consistently identified in current Japanese coverage as a handmade Japanese brand associated with Masafumi Todaka. Japanese retail and secondary-market documentation preserves MOTHER, Sabbath, Sabbath MK2, Sabbath Black Gaze, Sabbath Bliss, RUINS FUZZ and FLUID Distortion. MOTHER is documented as a fuzz/distortion model; Sabbath states are preserved as historical product states rather than unrelated builders.
- September Sound historical coverage identifies producer/guitarist Tate Mitsuo as the maker behind the brand and documents RAT2 DUAL CLIP MOD and an original Mini Fuzz; community/retail records also preserve the experimental Envelope Filter Wah Fuzz. BOSS SD-1 Spangle Mod remains a modification project rather than an original September Sound conceptual model.

## Duplicate / relationship gate
- SIVA signature/collaboration products stay attached to SIVA; they do not become separate builder records.
- RoShi GINZA, V1 and Re:voice products are retained as named states for historical traceability but should later be normalized into family/edition relationships where appropriate.
- Phantom EVR products involving Shun Nokina remain collaboration relationships with the Leqtique/Shun Nokina lineage rather than independent Phantom conceptual models.
- Phantom Sabbath, Black Gaze, MK2 and Bliss are treated as a connected product family; historical states are preserved without assuming every enclosure or limited run is a distinct conceptual circuit.
- September Sound's BOSS SD-1 modification is kept outside the original-dirt model count. Clean boosters remain utility products.
- No schematics, PCB layouts, gutshots, cloning instructions, or complete BOM/component inventories were added.

## Important boundary decisions
- Product photography references remain evidence trails for later credit/licensing review and are not treated as automatic republication permission.
- Retailer and enthusiast sources are used as corroboration for historical products where first-party pages are gone, but builder identity is anchored in stronger maker/retailer documentation where available.
- Experimental pedals remain central only when fuzz, distortion or overdrive is an explicit defining function; utility-only and modulation-only products remain outside the dirt count.
