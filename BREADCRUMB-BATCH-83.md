# The Dirt Archive — Phase One Breadcrumb — Batch 83

## Completed
- Continued Phase One breadth-first research after Batch 82.
- South Korea / Taiwan / Hong Kong yielded no additional builder identities strong enough to promote without weakening the archive's identity gate.
- Pivoted to a deep Japanese historical audit of ALBIT / Cranetortoise.
- Added ALBIT / Cranetortoise as a canonical builder/manufacturer lineage with a substantial documented dirt catalog.
- Added 23 dirt-focused inventory records spanning historical Cranetortoise and later/current ALBIT products.
- Added census addendum 174.

## Evidence basis
- ALBIT first-party site identifies Albit Corporation as a Japanese electronic-musical-instrument manufacturer in Soka, Saitama, and states products are made in Japan.
- ALBIT's first-party effector catalog separates booster/distortion, fuzz and other effect families and preserves both current and discontinued products.
- ALBIT's support archive explicitly lists discontinued Cranetortoise-era models including AD-1, DD-1/DD-1B, FB-1, FD-1, GS-1, HM-1, OD-1, SD-1 and SW-1/SW-1B.
- The historical HM-1 and OD-1 manuals carry ALBIT Corporation and Cranetortoise branding; the FB-1 manual does likewise.
- Effects Database groups 26 Cranetortoise products under "Cranetortoise by Albit," providing a useful secondary historical cross-check.

## Duplicate / relationship gate
- Do not create a separate canonical builder for Cranetortoise at this stage. Model it as an ALBIT product line / historical brand relationship unless stronger contrary evidence is recovered.
- Do not count pure clean boosters, compressors, switch boxes, loop utilities or preamps in the central dirt inventory merely because they appear in the same ALBIT effect archive.
- Bass variants are retained where the manufacturer presents them as named products with distinct intended voicing/function.

## Important boundary decisions
- FB-1 retained as Fuzz because the original manual describes it as a fuzz-voiced booster and the historical catalog classifies it within the Cranetortoise dirt family.
- OD-1 retained as Overdrive despite its booster wording because the original manual is explicitly titled overdrive booster and the product is a named historical dirt model.
- TB-1/TB-1B retained as Overdrive because ALBIT's first-party description explicitly calls them overdrive boosters and describes their drive function.
- GFD-1, SD-2, MG-1, MS-1, GWF-1, GWF-1B, FZ-2B and MF-1 are directly described by ALBIT as distortion/fuzz products.
- No schematics, PCB layouts, gutshots, cloning instructions, or complete BOM/component inventories are included.

## Next move
Continue Phase One through remaining Japanese long-tail / OEM / sub-brand history, while checking South Korea, Taiwan, Hong Kong and mainland China again only when a candidate surfaces with a clear builder identity plus a genuine dirt catalog. Prioritize historical manufacturer lineages, unusual regional production histories and connected brands that can improve the archive's genealogy rather than simply increasing model counts.
