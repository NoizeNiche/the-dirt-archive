# The Dirt Archive — Phase One Breadcrumb — Batch 85

## Completed
- Continued Phase One breadth-first research through the Japanese long-tail J-to-L range.
- Added K&R as a Japanese handmade pedal brand with a documented multi-model dirt catalog spanning Plus and Pro/Groove families.
- Added Lee Custom Amplifier as a current Japanese builder with a coherent tube-driven overdrive/distortion pedal family.
- Added 13 inventory records across the two lineages.
- Added census addendum 176.

## Evidence basis
- K&R's first-party site describes the brand as providing handmade effects, effect kits and audio kits, with a dedicated Groove series. Groove Drive is explicitly documented as an overdrive with clean-tone blending, while the site also preserves the Sleek Drive Pro line and related product history.
- Effects Database preserves K&R Plus Series products Brown Driver, Drive Booster, Driven Sustainer and White Driver, and K&R Pro Series products including Distortion Final, Naturalist, Sleek Drive Pro and The Bluesman. Japanese used-market sources independently document K&R completed pedals and identify the brand as Japanese handmade.
- Lee Custom Amplifier's first-party site identifies the builder as an engineer who worked 15 years at a major instrument manufacturer before going independent, with SHINOS&L design experience. Its current store lists VOD-1, V-808, VAC-30, VBM-1 and VJCM-800 among the builder's own products.
- Japanese retailer documentation identifies VAC-30 and VBM-1 as Made in Japan tube overdrive pedals, while current and retailer descriptions document VOD-1, V-808 and VJCM-800 as tube-driven overdrive/distortion products.

## Duplicate / relationship gate
- K&R utility-oriented products such as Groove Buffer and compressors remain outside central dirt inventory. Drive Booster is retained because historical indexing classifies it within the overdrive family; future normalization may reclassify it as drive-adjacent if stronger first-party functional evidence requires that distinction.
- Lee Custom Amplifier's Valve Breaker is treated as a collaboration relationship with idea sound product, not a separate Lee conceptual model. The collaboration can be represented later in genealogy data.
- No separate Japanese builder record was created for JAM Pedals despite Japanese retail search results because JAM identifies itself as Athens, Greece based and handmade there since 2005.

## Important boundary decisions
- No schematics, PCB layouts, gutshots, cloning instructions, or complete BOM/component inventories were added.
- Historical product states are preserved as named models where the available catalogs present them as distinct products; finish-only and cosmetic changes should remain specimen/variant metadata.
- K&R's kit products remain a separate documentation layer from completed pedals so DIY kit availability does not inflate the finished-product census.

## Deferred / research leads
- Lee Custom Amplifier's broader amplifier and preamp family should be audited later for pedal-adjacent products, but the current central dirt inventory remains limited to clearly documented stompbox overdrive/distortion units.
- Jujiya/Jeugia 1968 FB-1 remains a manufacturer-identity research lead rather than a canonical builder.

## Next move
Continue Phase One through remaining Japanese historical manufacturers, connected labels and overlooked boutique lineages around L-to-M, then return to East Asian long-tail candidates where builder identity plus genuine dirt catalogs remain under-documented. Prioritize provenance and genealogy over raw model-count expansion.
