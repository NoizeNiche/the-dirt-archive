# The Dirt Archive — Phase One Breadcrumb — Batch 87

## Completed
- Continued Phase One through the Japanese M-to-N historical/manufacturer corridor.
- Added Nisshin Onpa / Maxon as a major Japanese manufacturer and brand lineage with a documented 1970s-origin dirt catalog and extensive Ibanez OEM history.
- Added Noel as Sho Iwata's Japanese handmade artist/builder lineage, with Cornet as the early signature fuzz and subsequent overdrive products.

## Evidence basis
- Maxon/Nisshin Onpa sources document the company's mid-1960s electronics background, transition into effects manufacture, own-brand Maxon products, and long-running OEM design/manufacture for Ibanez. Maxon's D&S debuted in 1974; Susumu Tamura's history with Nisshin Onpa and the 808 overdrive design is independently documented by Premier Guitar. Maxon/industry sources preserve OD808, OD-9, OD820, SD-9, DS830 and multiple 9-Series and Fuzz Elements dirt families.
- Noel interview evidence identifies Sho Iwata as the designer and primary maker, with design, painting and production carried out in his Sapporo home workshop. The same interview ties Noel's origin to Cornet and documents its early development period; Effects Database and Japanese product coverage preserve Cornet, Cornet revu and the broader Noel overdrive lineage.
- Masatone's first-party site currently documents Blues Rock Machine tube overdrive and Hard Rock Machine high-gain distortion. Pedal Geeks Meeting identifies Masatone as a Tokyo effects brand established in 2016 with its own product line.

## Duplicate / relationship gate
- Nisshin Onpa / Maxon is one canonical manufacturing lineage. Ibanez-branded pedals manufactured by Nisshin Onpa should be represented later through manufacturer/brand/OEM relationships rather than duplicating the same builder identity. This batch does not attempt to enumerate every Ibanez OEM pedal.
- Maxon conceptual families such as D&S/D&S II and OD808/OD-9/OD-9 Pro+ are retained as named products because the surviving catalogs present them distinctly, while future normalization should add generation/family relationships.
- Noel's Cornet and Cornet revu remain separate named product states because the surviving historical sources present revu as a substantial rebuild of the Cornet concept. No internal construction or component inventory is included.
- Masatone's two currently documented dirt products are both retained because one is an overdrive and the other a distinct high-gain distortion.
- Noah'sark remains a relationship/dealer-brand research lead rather than a canonical builder record until the underlying manufacturing identity is established strongly enough. Ikebe's relationship to the brand is useful genealogy evidence but is not yet treated as proof of in-house manufacture for every historical model.

## Important boundary decisions
- No schematics, PCB layouts, gutshots, cloning instructions, or complete BOM/component inventories were added.
- Product photography references are evidence trails for later rights/credit review, not automatic republication permission.
- Retailer pages and enthusiast archives are used as corroboration where first-party historical material is incomplete.

## Deferred / research leads
