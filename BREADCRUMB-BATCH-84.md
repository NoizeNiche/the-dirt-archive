# The Dirt Archive — Phase One Breadcrumb — Batch 84

## Completed
- Continued Phase One breadth-first research from Batch 83 with a Japanese long-tail historical/boutique audit.
- Added Jersey Girl as a Japanese handmade pedal lineage with a documented 1990s start and a compact but historically useful dirt catalog.
- Added SEA SIDE SOUND as a Saga, Kyushu handmade builder with a multi-product overdrive/distortion/fuzz catalog.
- Added Lastgasp Art Laboratories as a Japanese-origin experimental builder with a substantial documented dirt history extending from the 2000s into current production.
- Added 24 inventory records across the three lineages.
- Added census addendum 175.

## Evidence basis
- Premier Guitar's interview with Jersey Girl documents pedal building beginning in 1996 and identifies Fulltender as the builder's best-selling overdrive released in 1998. Effects Database preserves five Jersey Girl products, including Fulltender, Keekruder, Middranger, Hustlemaker and Plusdriver.
- Effects Database documents Jersey Girl Plusdriver as a Fulltender-based boostable overdrive born around 2000 and Keekruder as a catalogued distortion product.
- ECB's 2023 Japanese boutique showcase identifies SEA SIDE SOUND as a handmade brand from Saga, Kyushu, founded by guitarist Issei Iwata; Japanese retailers document AVESTER, BRAVE, Iblis, NYANTAUR, NYAT and Nyan Bit Fuzz, while Saga's regional product program also lists BALOR.
- Effects Database preserves 27 Lastgasp Art Laboratories products, including multiple generations of fuzz/distortion instruments. The L.A.L. 87 and 88 pages provide dated historical documentation from 2005 and 2006; current LAL store pages updated in 2026 list the Hachikyu Shiki Dualoscillo Fuzz. Recent distributor coverage describes the brand as operated by a Japanese artist based in Australia.

## Duplicate / relationship gate
- Jersey Girl's Hustlemaker and Middranger are retained in the historical manufacturer context but not promoted to central dirt inventory because their primary documented roles are boosting/mid-boosting. Plusdriver is retained centrally because its defining unit combines the Fulltender overdrive with a booster.
- SEA SIDE SOUND cosmetic or enclosure-only variants, including Limited BoosNyaa configurations, remain specimen/finish states rather than separate conceptual models.
- Lastgasp's filter, noise, modulation and utility instruments are not counted as dirt merely because they belong to the same manufacturer catalog. Dirt is included where fuzz, distortion or overdrive is an explicit defining function.
- Jujiya FB-1 remains a research lead rather than a canonical builder record: current evidence identifies Jujiya/Jeugia as a Kyoto music retailer and the 1968 FB-1 as a rare historical product, but maker/manufacturer identity is not yet sufficiently established.

## Important boundary decisions
- No schematics, PCB layouts, gutshots, cloning instructions, or complete BOM/component inventories were added.
- Effects Database and retailer pages are being used as historical/index cross-checks and source trails, not as automatic permission to republish product photography.
- Lastgasp's 2005/2006 numbered fuzzes are retained as distinct named products because the available historical documentation presents them as separate catalog models; future revision work should distinguish conceptual models from specimen/finish changes.

## Next move
Continue Phase One through the remaining Japanese long-tail, especially historically obscure manufacturers and connected labels around the J-to-L range, then return to Taiwan/Hong Kong/mainland China only where genuine builder identity and a substantive dirt catalog can be demonstrated. Prioritize genealogy, provenance and hidden historical catalogs over raw model-count expansion.
