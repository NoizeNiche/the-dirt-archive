## Evidence notes
Effects Database documents PSK ODV-2 as a Korean-made vintage analog overdrive originating in the mid-to-late 1980s and explicitly ties it to later RAK and Music-Son branded versions. Its PSK family records also preserve ODV-3, ODV-5, SOD-series and related dirt models. Korean retail evidence currently lists ODV-5 and historical DST-5 examples under PSK / Kyung Nam branding.

The DST family has especially strong relationship evidence. Effects Database identifies PSK Kyung Nam Co as the Korean manufacturer behind DST-2 and DST-3 units that also appeared under Allsound, Music-Son, RAK, MSL and Samson labels. This is precisely the kind of manufacturer/brand split the archive's relationship model is intended to capture.

The PSK lineage is historically important even though many products were strongly derived from popular Japanese and American effect concepts. The archive records the genuine Korean manufacturer and named products while keeping circuit ancestry and rebrand relationships separate; no cloning instructions or schematic reconstruction is published.

## Boundary / scope notes
- PSK is treated as a historical manufacturer/builder lineage, not merely a single retail brand.
- RAK, Music-Son, Allsound, MSL, Samson, Venson and similar labels remain relationship/rebrand records when shared PSK manufacture is documented.
- Model numbers across ODV, DST and SOD families remain distinct named products, while cosmetic/rebrand differences are handled as relationship or specimen states.
- This batch intentionally does not infer that every Korean pedal carrying a related label was made by PSK; each relationship requires evidence.
- No schematics, PCB layouts, gutshots, cloning instructions, or complete BOM data are published.
