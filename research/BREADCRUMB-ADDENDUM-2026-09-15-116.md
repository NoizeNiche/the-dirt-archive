# YARP Breadcrumb 116

## Purpose
American early-fuzz manufacturer discovery focused on the Astro Tone / Sam Ash corridor and manufacturer-versus-retail identity separation.

## Cross-reference / dedupe decisions

- **Astro Amp / Universal Amplifier Corporation** is added as a manufacturer node for the mid-1960s New York Astro Tone Fuzz.
- **Sam Ash Fuzzz Boxx** is retained as a separate marketed/retail identity tied to the Astro manufacturer lineage.
- **Analog Man Astro Tone** is treated as a modern revival/reinterpretation and not merged into the original Astro Amp production record.
- No new builder record is created for Sam Ash solely because it marketed the product.

## Evidence notes

Analog Man identifies the original Astro Tone and Sam Ash Fuzzz Boxx as New York City-made products from Astro Amp in 1966-1968. Effects Database independently records the Astro Tone maker lineage and the Sam Ash marketed state. The surviving evidence is sufficient for a high-confidence manufacturer discovery, while the exact corporate relationship between the Astro Amp trade name and Universal Amplifier Corporation remains a naming detail for future primary-source work.
