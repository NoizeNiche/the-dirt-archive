# YARP 2026-09-15 Batch 38

## Focus
Mad Professor as a Finnish effects-builder/brand and its relationship to Björn Juhl/BJFE design, with emphasis on documented dirt products.

## Findings

### Mad Professor baseline
Effects Database dates the Mad Professor Fire Red Fuzz to NAMM 2008 and explicitly describes it as a BJF design, hand-made in Finland. This establishes a clear Finnish production identity in the 2000s.

### Fire Red Fuzz
The Fire Red Fuzz is retained as a canonical Mad Professor fuzz product with a 2008 public anchor. Its design relationship to Björn Juhl is explicit, but Mad Professor remains a separate marketed/manufacturing entity from BJFE because the brands and production arrangements are not identical.

### Sweet Honey Overdrive
Historical research on the Honey Bee family documents Björn Juhl's collaboration with Mad Professor and identifies the Sweet Honey Overdrive as a distinct design developed for the Mad Professor platform. It should not be treated as a clone of the BJFE Honey Bee, even though the two share design lineage and inspiration.

### Relationship graph
The archive now treats the relationship as: Björn Juhl designer -> BJFE original hand-built Swedish production -> Mad Professor authorized Finnish production/brand -> later BearFoot U.S. production for selected Juhl designs -> One Control Japan authorized relationship. These are relationship edges, not duplicate manufacturers of one continuous product line.

## Catalog signal
The next Mad Professor completion pass should enumerate the dirt catalog around Fire Red Fuzz, Sweet Honey Overdrive and other overdrive/distortion/fuzz products, while separating discontinued, PCB/manual, hand-wired and current production states where evidence supports them.
