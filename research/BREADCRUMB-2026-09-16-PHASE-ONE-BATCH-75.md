## Evidence notes
A3 Stompbox's first-party shop identifies a Seongnam, Gyeonggi-do address and lists ANGEL, AWESOME and GROOVIM as core stompbox products. The builder describes ANGEL as its first overdrive after years of field testing and development, based around a JCM800-type response. Current product listings also document AWESOME BASS Overdrive. Secondary product documentation identifies Groovim 808 as a Groovim derivative with 808-style midrange voicing, while the builder's own shop confirms the underlying GROOVIM Distortion product. This is sufficient to preserve A3 as a named Korean builder lineage.

MED Amplification is different in character. Its first-party history says the project began in 2022 as a musician/engineer effort focused on DIY guitar effects, with prototype development followed by first-batch pedals in January 2026. Current product pages provide named Overdrive and Twin Fuzz modules, each available as assembled or DIY-oriented systems. Preserve the products as research candidates but do not yet use MED as proof of a conventional boutique manufacturing lineage until a clearer builder/manufacturer identity emerges.

## Boundary / scope notes
- A3 volume and expression pedal products are outside the central dirt archive but remain part of the builder's broader product context.
- Groovim 808 remains a product-state relationship to Groovim rather than an unrelated builder record.
- MED assembled pedals and DIY kits are retained together under one candidate platform identity; kit/module packaging should not create artificial model counts.
