# The Dirt Archive · Current Priority

Effective immediately, the public archive has **one priority**:

**Builder → Pedals → Photos → Information on the pedal**

## What we are collecting

For each builder:

1. **Builder**
   - Canonical builder name
   - Enough identity information to place the builder correctly

2. **Pedals**
   - Documented dirt pedals made, marketed, or clearly associated with that builder
   - Exact model/product names
   - Important model variants only when they are meaningful to identifying the pedal

3. **Photos**
   - Exterior/reference photographs of the actual pedal
   - Multiple photographs when they help distinguish the model or an important version
   - Source, credit, and licensing information where available
   - No gutshots, PCB photographs, schematics, or internal reconstruction material

4. **Information on the pedal**
   - What the pedal is
   - When it was made, when known
   - Short history
   - Useful identification details
   - Documented production/model differences when they materially help identify the pedal
   - Reliable sources/evidence behind the information

## What is not the priority right now

Do not let the research queue drift into building elaborate public browse systems, advanced identification tools, exhaustive taxonomy, specimen databases, or other secondary features.

The underlying research data may remain richer when necessary for accuracy, but the work performed now should directly improve one of the four things above.

## Research rule

A builder is useful to the public archive when we can progressively fill its four layers. Prefer collecting a builder's pedals, photos, and pedal information over adding another layer of site machinery.

The guiding question for every task is:

> Does this directly help us build Builder → Pedals → Photos → Information?

If not, it is deferred.
