# Variant Backfill Queue 01

The archive should not treat a pedal record as finished merely because the builder and model have been identified. Each existing record should be evaluated for variant depth.

## Required pass for every existing pedal

1. Identify all manufacturer-named versions, generations, revisions, reissues and documented production transitions.
2. Separate genuine product/generation changes from colorways, artwork-only editions, retailer editions and other non-circuit variants.
3. Record exterior identification clues: enclosure size/shape, graphics, control count/layout, knob style, footswitches, LED placement, jack placement, power jack, labels and country markings.
4. Record functional differences: controls, clipping modes, gain structure, routing, switching, expression/MIDI/remote features and other player-facing changes.
5. Record production identity: manufacturer/OEM, geography, production period, announced date, first release date, discontinuation and successor relationships when supported.
6. Record electronics identity only at the level needed to distinguish a production identity. Do not publish cloning recipes, schematics, PCB layouts or complete component lists.
7. Attach each meaningful claim to its evidence and preserve unresolved contradictions.

### Cohort B: major historical family branches
Fuzz Face; Tone Bender; Big Muff Pi; Tube Screamer; DOD 250 / Distortion+; Fuzzrite; Harmonic Percolator; Rangemaster; Buzzaround.

### Cohort C: remaining public records
Evaluate every other pedal on the live catalog for hidden generations, editions, OEM transitions or visually distinct production identities before declaring its research stable.

## Completion rule
A pedal can be cataloged while its variant mapping is incomplete, but it should not be marked as fully identification-ready until this pass is complete.
