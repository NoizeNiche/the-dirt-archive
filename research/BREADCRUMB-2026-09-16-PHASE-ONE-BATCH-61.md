## Evidence notes
Audiolithe's first-party history identifies Gaëtan and Christophe as the founding team and states that the pedals are designed and built in France, with circuits assembled by hand in the Montreuil workshop. A 2022 French trade report and a 2024 interview place the brand's origin around 2020 and describe Doomer Fuzz as the first major design, followed by La Montagne and Extinction Drive. Current first-party catalogs add bass-focused The Empress Bass and Chaosculpt Bass.

## Scope discipline
