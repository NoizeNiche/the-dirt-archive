## Important research notes
- Benson Stonk Box is a Tone Bender MkI interpretation with expanded controls and automatic thermal-bias behavior. Treat thermal-bias status and the four-control surface as identification clues.
- EAE 0xEAE Fuzz is archived by the manufacturer as V1, a collaboration with Obstructures. Later Glaive is explicitly a reworking, not the same generation.
- Crazy Tube Circuits Motherload is a dual-dirt product combining distinct all-analog Muff- and Rat-inspired sections with separate EQ plus LINK EQ behavior.
- Tark SquidInk blends fuzz with an analog PLL multi-octave generator. Manual revisions must remain separate from product-generation claims.
- Farmer Factory Fresh Fuzz 3K is currently a strong secondary-source lead. Do not promote its 3K label to a formal generation until primary evidence is found.
- Hello Sailor Compact Anchor Drive is currently a discovery-level lead requiring further primary-source enrichment.

## Data rule reinforced
Every promoted record needs both a research-layer entry and a catalog-layer connection. Record what changed, when it changed, how a collector can recognize it, and which source proves the distinction. A product with no documented revision still gets a variant-state evaluation with uncertainty preserved.
