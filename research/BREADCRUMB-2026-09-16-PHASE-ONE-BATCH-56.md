## Evidence notes
Jinx Pedals' own history identifies the company as a Kumanovo, North Macedonia operation begun by musicians Predrag and Damjan after building their own pedals in a small TV-service shop. The site says they later developed their own prototypes and launched Jinx as a guitar-pedal and audio-equipment company. The indexed Jinx catalog contains Cheetah Drive, Dister, Dentist and Pump Preamp in addition to Bluefin Chorus and Botox Compressor.

Effects Database describes Cheetah Drive as a Jinx-designed overdrive with active Bass/Treble EQ and notes manufacturer video evidence dating back to 2023. One Thousand Pedals documents Dister as analog high-gain distortion and Dentist as analog fuzz/octave fuzz, both made in North Macedonia.

Volt Strike Effects is documented on Reverb from Martjanci, Slovenia. Ol' Fuzz is a 2024 Slovenian-made handwired Fuzz Face-style pedal using NOS germanium with external bias. Infinity Burst is a 2024 handwired overdrive/boost derived from the Lovepedal Eternity Burst and offered as a preorder/customizable build. These records are retained because the evidence directly identifies the builder and product. Custom reproductions are intentionally separated.

## Photo workflow
Every inventory row includes a product or catalog source usable as a later photo/specimen breadcrumb. Reverb listing pages provide multiple specimen photographs for the Volt Strike products; Effects Database and One Thousand Pedals provide manufacturer-linked catalog trails for Jinx. These are evidence trails for later rights review and specimen capture, not permission to republish images.

## Scope discipline
Pump Preamp is retained as gain-adjacent rather than counted as conventional overdrive/distortion/fuzz. No custom copy of a third-party pedal is treated as a new original family merely because a local builder assembled it. No schematics, PCB layouts, gutshots, cloning instructions, or complete BOM material is published.
