# YARP 2026-09-15 Batch 30

## Focus
Kay / Uecks chronology correction and Japanese OEM identity reconciliation.

## Findings

### Kay Sound Benders chronology correction
2026 research by Tone Machines Blog found the earliest documented announcement of the original three-model Kay Sound Benders series in the late-1973/January-1974 trade-publication corridor. The series included the FUZ-1 Fuzz Tone, WaWa-1 and Trem-1. This substantially weakens the older collector date of 1968-1969 for the later Kay Fuzz Tone product and should supersede that older date in the archive's chronology queue.

### Japanese manufacturing
The Kay pedal carries Made in Japan evidence and contemporary specimens show Japanese-origin components. However, the physical factory is not established. The archive therefore keeps Kay as a marketed/export brand entity and does **not** promote Uecks/Ueki Gakki to factory status merely from the shared branding trail.

### Uecks relationship
Uecks was the Japanese-market name associated with Ueki Gakki. Specialist Japanese retail/archive material documents a Uecks Fuzz Tone and Uecks Wah Wah in the 1970s. The relationship to Kay is therefore recorded as a marketed/export pairing, while the actual OEM builder remains unresolved.

### Explicit correction
Older sources that date Kay Fuzz Tone to the late 1960s are now flagged as superseded by stronger 1973-1974 documentary evidence. The archive should preserve the earlier claim in evidence history rather than deleting it, but the preferred date field should move to **late 1973 onward**, with specimen pot-code evidence extending into 1975-1977 and a tentative endpoint around 1978.
