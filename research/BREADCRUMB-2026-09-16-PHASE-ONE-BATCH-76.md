## Evidence notes
Gervana has one of the more useful surviving first-party historical footprints among the smaller Chinese pedal makers. Its current website gives a Liaoning, China address and retains a multi-generation product history. The 2016 Bingzi page identifies Bingzi as an overdrive designed by Mr. G and documents multiple overdrive/clipping combinations. The 2017 Wuwu page documents a British Plexi-style distortion with preamp functionality. The 2017 Wuyin page documents an expanded fuzz design using Big Muff-era reference vocabulary while explicitly describing additional original circuit behavior and controls. These three products establish a coherent dirt-oriented builder identity.

The current Gervana site also lists Yimao and Yiyou preamps plus other named effect families. Those are retained as drive-adjacent candidates because their primary role is preamplification rather than central stompbox dirt. A 2026 secondary report uses the name Ji Wei for a distortion model, but the available first-party Gervana material located during this batch did not yet reconcile that product cleanly with the historical catalog, so it remains a candidate rather than an indexed model.

## Boundary / scope notes
- Gervana's named product-family taxonomy is a brand/product system, not evidence of multiple builders. Preserve individual product names while maintaining one builder lineage.
- Bingzi, Wuwu and Wuyin are promoted because first-party pages explicitly document their dirt roles and design history.
- Yimao and Yiyou remain drive-adjacent preamp candidates until the archive can establish a stronger central-dirt role.
- Ji Wei remains a candidate pending first-party chronology reconciliation; do not use the 2026 secondary technical report as the sole identity anchor.
- Bananana Effects was audited but not added here because its current catalog is centered on glitch, synth, delay and reverb products rather than the archive's central dirt scope.
- Miraki Effects appeared primarily through a Hong Kong collaboration product trail and was not promoted as a new Hong Kong builder in this batch.
- No schematics, PCB layouts, gutshots, cloning instructions, or complete BOM data are published.
