# British Pedal Company / JMI, early-2000s lineage audit

## Scope
Historical builder, recreation, and brand-transition layer. This record is not a claim that modern British Pedal Company products are original 1960s production.

## Identity and chronology
British Pedal Company states that its team originally developed, designed and recreated pedals for the JMI brand in the early 2000s. It says the team ended its relationship with JMI in 2011 and launched its own British Pedal Company range in 2014.

## Dirt relevance
The early-2000s JMI work is important to the archive because it demonstrates a revival/recreation business operating before the British Pedal Company name existed. The archive should therefore model:

- JMI as the marketed historical brand served by the early-2000s recreation team.
- The early-2000s builder/development team as a separate manufacturing/development relationship pending fuller corporate attribution.
- British Pedal Company as the later 2014 successor retail identity.

## Catalog signal
British Pedal Company's current catalog contains vintage-family fuzz and overdrive products, including compact and special-edition lines. Those current products must not be automatically backdated into the early-2000s JMI period.

## Evidence disposition
- Early-2000s JMI recreation work: PRIMARY, current British Pedal Company history.
- 2011 separation from JMI: PRIMARY.
- 2014 British Pedal Company launch: PRIMARY.
- Complete early JMI recreation catalog: NOT ENUMERATED.
