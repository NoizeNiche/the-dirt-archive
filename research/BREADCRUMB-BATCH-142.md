# The Dirt Archive — Breadcrumb Batch 142

## Governing priority
**Builder → Pedals → Photos → Information on the pedal**

## Batch target
Audit one substantial Japanese builder catalog rather than chase a single famous pedal.

## Builder researched
**sobbat / Kinko Music Co.,Ltd.** (Kyoto, Japan)

## Why this batch matters
sobbat's surviving first-party material spans the original 1995-era Drive Breaker and Fuzz Breaker families, the 2006 limited DB-4, the later DB-4R reproduction/refinement, and the compact S-series. The official site also explicitly identifies the operation as handmade and gives Kinko Music Co.,Ltd. as the company behind the brand.

## Product-layer rule applied
Central records include documented distortion, overdrive, fuzz, octave-fuzz and bass-overdrive models. Clean booster BS-1S and the A/B routing utility SW-1 remain relationship-only. DB-3 is retained as central-pending because the surviving 2015 catalog gives the model identity but the product-level description is less complete than the better-documented Drive Breaker models.

## Photo rule
Use exterior/reference photography from first-party sobbat pages and historical catalogs as evidence trails. Do not publish gutshots, schematics, PCB views or complete internal construction material.

## Lineage rule
DB-4 and DB-4R are preserved as separate historical product states because the official DB-4R page explicitly describes DB-4 as the 2006 limited model being reproduced/refined. S-series models are retained as distinct named products when official pages describe changed form factor and sound behavior, but they are still linked to their parent Drive Breaker lineage.
