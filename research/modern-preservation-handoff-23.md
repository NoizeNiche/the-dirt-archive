## Important research notes
- Halcyon Gold has a dated August 2023 Origin Effects release announcement. Adaptive Circuitry, ADAPT behavior, KLN/MOD voice switching and DRY control are core identification markers. Later manual revisions remain documentation revisions, not automatic product generations.
- Fairfield Roger That is a highly distinctive 2023-era experimental distortion built around FM modulation/demodulation, JFET preamp, dry/wet mixing, Tune, Shift, Range, filter behavior and CV. Its unusual architecture makes it a high-value specimen-identification record.
- Kernom MOHO was publicly demonstrated by Kernom in 2023 and uses the company's Analog Morphing Core approach to span multiple fuzz/clipping characters. Preserve the analog-audio architecture and morphing interface as product identity clues.
- Browne Gritador remains a 2023 introduction anchored to Browne's own press-release material and should not gain an invented generation number without later evidence.
- Farmer Factory Fresh Fuzz 3K and Hello Sailor Compact Anchor Drive remain discovery-level because the strongest current evidence is secondary coverage. Do not overstate their lineage or formal generation status.

## Data rule reinforced
Every promoted record needs both a research-layer entry and a catalog-layer connection. Record what changed, when it changed, how a collector can recognize it, which source proves the distinction, and what remains unresolved. Product with no documented revision still gets a variant-state evaluation.
