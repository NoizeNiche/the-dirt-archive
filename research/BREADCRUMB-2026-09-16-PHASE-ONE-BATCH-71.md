## Evidence notes
Organic Sounds' current first-party catalog documents RAIN STORM as an original fuzz built from the builder's vintage Buzzaround reference using a silicon/silicon/germanium hybrid transistor arrangement. Its current THE TOWER listing is an original fuzz/distortion reconstructed after analysis of a specific vintage Big Muff Rams Head example. These should remain products of Organic Sounds, with reference-family ancestry stored separately.

Ovaltone's first-party catalog is active and unusually well dated. ECTOPLASM FUZZ MK I launched in February 2026 and spans fuzz through high-gain distortion territory. FOUNTAIN has a documented 2025 revision of the 2016 overdrive, and HEAVY BLOW BASS-OVERDRIVE is a dedicated bass overdrive introduced in December 2025 with an integrated clean-content control and internal voltage increase. Preserve release dates and revisions for later generation work.

Tanabe.TV's current first-party Kudo page documents SUNKUDO FUZZ as a controlled germanium fuzz positioned between fuzz and distortion, alongside DUMKUDO and ZENKUDO drive products and custom configurations. Tanabe.TV remains a builder identity with a coherent dirt family rather than being split by enclosure or premium configuration.

Free Fall Diver remains unusually elusive. Secondary Japanese boutique sources document a substantial dirt catalog, including the CSF Upper Octave Fuzz, C2W, Huge Muff and Twelve Multi Distortion. Because the surviving primary-source trail is thin, preserve the identity as RESEARCH_CANDIDATE until stronger first-party material can establish chronology and builder identity.

minimichine's first-party shop is a valuable historical object record. It documents まほろば as a one-knob fuzzy distortion and たまゆら as a bass overdrive created from the まほろば concept. The shop states business paused in December 2017 while repair support continued, making minimichine a historical builder record rather than a currently active manufacturer.

## Boundary / scope notes
- Organic Sounds' use of vintage reference pedals is recorded as design ancestry, not OEM identity or duplication.
- Ovaltone version years should remain attached to model-state records rather than inflate conceptual model counts.
- Tanabe.TV custom finishes and dual-case configurations remain specimen/configuration data unless a distinct repeatable series is established.
- Free Fall Diver is not promoted to a canonical builder until first-party identity evidence improves.
- minimichine is historical; stopped business activity in 2017 should not erase its catalog presence from the census.
- No schematics, PCB layouts, gutshots, cloning instructions, or complete BOM data are published.
