## Historical findings

- EKO Multitone is documented as an Italian EKO pedal appearing in the 1973 EKO catalogue; builder/assembly responsibility remains unresolved at model level.
- A secondary source proposes Japanese influence in the Multitone fuzz section, but this is not sufficient evidence for a Japanese supplier or physical subassembly origin. The claim remains LOW-confidence HOLD_FOR_REVIEW.
- Schaller's own fuzz products and the Hofner No. 539, Hofner Wha-Wha-Fuzz, and Schaller Fuzz-Sustain families have substantially stronger model-specific OEM evidence.
- Hofner No. 539 is retained as a Schaller OEM state rather than a separate physical factory.
- Schaller Fuzz-Sustain is retained as a builder-centered family with related Höfner and Allsound marketed states kept distinct.
- Schaller Double Pedal is retained as a late-1960s German physical-manufacturer record with a documented Hofner-branded equivalent.

## Validation posture

The EKO Multitone review reinforces the rule that design similarity, country of sale, and component origin cannot by themselves establish physical manufacture. Schaller/Höfner relationships can be retained at high confidence where the model-specific sources explicitly support them.
