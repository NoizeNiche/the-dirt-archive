## Information on the pedal

- Chinese Food Overdrive is a mini transparent-style overdrive.
- Jiangshi Face Fuzz is a mini silicon Fuzz Face-style fuzz.
- Red Brick Overdrive references Plexi-era Marshall sound.
- Yellow Brick Overdrive references classic Tweed Fender tones.
- Tower Bender Mk II is a British-style fuzz using rare germanium transistors.
- Black Brick Bass Overdrive references Ampeg SVT-series sound.
- Silver Brick Bass Overdrive references 1970s Fender Bassman sound.
- Midnight Ace Fuzz is a 2026 release reproducing the FM-3-style sound with heavy low-mid response.
- Newport Muff models are explicit vintage reproductions of distinct Big Muff-era references and remain relationship-only.
- Bashamichi Drive is explicitly a four-knob Centaur clone and remains relationship-only.

## Normalization decisions

- KAMINARI's original-design 2025-2026 dirt releases enter the central inventory.
- The Newport Muff family remains visible but does not inflate original-design counts because the builder describes each as a vintage reproduction.
- Bashamichi Drive remains relationship-only because it is documented as a Centaur clone.
- Finish and limited presentation changes do not create new conceptual models.
- No schematics, PCB layouts, gutshots or complete component inventories are collected.
