## Historical findings

- Clark SS-600 now has a stronger builder record for the Clark-branded state. The Clark enclosure statement identifies Clark Music Industries as manufacturer/distributor, while former employee Rich Excellente independently described wiring fuzz boxes at Clark Electronics in Clark, New Jersey.
- Manny's Fuzz has a firm January 18 1968 retail-distribution anchor in Cleveland, but the physical factory remains unresolved.
- Orpheum Fuzz has a September 13 1968 circulation anchor in St. Louis, but the physical factory remains unresolved.
- E.U. Wurlitzer Fuzzer Buzzer is confirmed as a recognized East Coast family member; a 1967 pot code supports early circulation, but a seller's Apollo/Orpheum production attribution is not treated as factory evidence.
- MGI, Andre Fuzzier, Gracin's Fubar, Mica-Tone Fuzz, AAT Fuzzier, and Tim's Own Fuzzer Buzzer remain family-level identities without a defensible physical-builder assignment.
- The East Coast family continues to show multiple enclosure, control-layout, and transistor-state variations. Shared family identity is therefore kept separate from factory identity.

## Validation posture

Clark can be treated as high-confidence for the Clark-marketed state, but the evidence does not justify projecting Clark manufacture onto every East Coast Fuzz variant. The public lineage layer remains unchanged.
