# 2013-2014 Microboutique Audit 11

## Purpose

This batch expands the builder-first census around the 2013-2014 boutique wave. The emphasis is on products that entered contemporary historical indexes in this period and on builders whose dirt catalogs can be expanded beyond a single headline pedal.

## Cog Effects

Cog Effects is owned and run by Tom George in Sheffield, UK. The Effects Database timeline distinguishes the builder's personal pedal-building start in summer 2008 from the later Cog Effects identity: first Cog prototypes in October 2012, formal launch in February 2013 with Tarkin, Darklighter and Knightfall, Grand Tarkin Bass Fuzz in August 2013, and a move into a purpose-built workshop in early 2014. The current indexed catalog contains 17 products. The Dirt Archive therefore preserves the distinction between pre-company experimentation and the Cog-branded production era.

## Bondi Effects

Bondi enters the contemporary record with Sick As and later Del Mar-related products. The 2013 weekly archive establishes the Sick As as a historical catalog anchor, while the current Effects Database index retains standard and limited-edition identities separately. The archive does not infer a 2013 founding date from first database appearance alone.

## Vick Audio

Vick Audio's 2013 catalog footprint is unusually clear through the weekly archive: 66 Triangle Big Muff, Black Russian Big Muff, Black Bird Overdrive, Red Fish OD and OD-TMY appear together in May 2013. The current index now contains 30 products, allowing the builder to move from discovery into a substantive catalog-completion target. Midnight Sun Fuzz is separately anchored in the 2014 weekly record. These products remain distinct retail models even where their historical families are obvious.

## GFI System

Eigendrive was published January 4, 2014 and is a useful early anchor for GFI's dirt catalog. The product combines multiple gain stages with independently switchable pre- and post-drive boosts. This batch records GFI as a discovered builder entity rather than treating the 2014 product date as proof of company founding.

## Big Ear NYC

The LOAF Fuzz was published January 4, 2014. Its description explicitly positions it as a silicon fuzz taking inspiration from 1960s/70s fuzzes while rejecting direct Big Muff/Fuzz Face cloning. The archive records that design-positioning evidence without inferring internal circuit lineage.

## Aural Dream

Aural Dream's 2014 dirt group is unusually concentrated: Bold Distortion, Compelling Drive, Intense Distortion, Purely Fuzz and Warm Drive. The three named distortion/drive products were all published March 28, 2014. Current indexed coverage contains 36 products across dirt and non-dirt categories. Because physical manufacturer and OEM identity are not established in this batch, Aural Dream remains a discovery/candidate builder rather than a canonical manufacturer attribution.

## Mantic Effects

The December 2, 2013 weekly archive introduces Mantic Effects with Density Hulk and Flex among the new effects. These provide a documentary foothold, but the builder's full dirt catalog and identity history remain unresolved.

## Union Tube & Transistor

The December 2, 2013 weekly archive introduces Beelzebuzz, More, Sub Buzz and Third Man Bumble Buzz together. The Third Man identity is retained as a collaboration relationship rather than merged with the core Union Tube & Transistor catalog.

## Evidence policy

Weekly database appearance is treated as documentary existence/visibility, not automatically as first-sale date or builder-founding date. Current catalog indexes are used for breadth mapping. Exact production-state claims, manufacturing attribution and generation boundaries remain separate research tasks unless supported by stronger primary or contemporary evidence.
