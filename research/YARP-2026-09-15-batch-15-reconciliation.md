# YARP 2026-09-15 Batch 15 — Japanese OEM + Coron + Public Lineage Review

## Scope
This batch stress-tests the Japanese OEM and Coron relationship layers and promotes only evidence-stable edges to the public-safe lineage candidate dataset.

## Findings

### Shin-Ei / Honey / Companion
The current Shin-Ei historical/storefront material supports treating Honey FY-6 and Shin-Ei FY-6 as sequentially related historical identities. The Companion catalog includes FY-2, FY-6, WF-8 and WF-24 as distinct product records. This batch does not infer that every later branded FY-6/WF unit came from the same exact production run.

### JEN / Vox / Thomas Organ
JEN is retained as a physical Italian manufacturer/OEM entity. Vox Showroom evidence supports EME and later JEN production for Vox wah products, while specialist historical data links JEN Fuzz with the Vox V828/V8281 family. The archive keeps US-made Thomas Organ states distinct from Italian JEN production states.

### Teisco / Standard Fuzz family
The Teisco Fuzz Machine, Ibanez Standard Fuzz No.59, Antoria, Bruno, Mica, Marlboro Wailer and Aria Diamond names are recorded as a broad OEM/market family. Factory attribution remains evidence-qualified because specialist sources disagree over the exact allocation of Teisco, Shin-Ei and related OEM roles. The archive therefore does not claim a single universal factory for every marketed state.

### Coron
The Coron Distortion 10/15 family is now substantially enumerated with Grant, HMI, Storm, Asama, Bias, J&J, KMD, Labo, Memphis, MX four and Nashville marketed states. Coron Over Drive is separately mapped with Storm and Bias overdrive relationships. Marketed brand names remain searchable records without becoming duplicate physical builders.

### Sobbat
DB-2 and DB-4 remain distinct products within the Drive Breaker family. DB-4 is not treated as a revision of DB-2; the relationship is product-family only.

## Public-lineage rule
Only high-confidence relationship edges are marked PROMOTE. Medium-confidence OEM-family edges remain HOLD_FOR_REVIEW until model-specific factory evidence is stronger.

## Editorial boundary
No schematics, PCB layouts, complete BOMs, gutshot libraries, cloning instructions or circuit-reconstruction material are published.
