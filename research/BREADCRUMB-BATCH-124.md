## Information on the pedals

- Ovaltone's current product index establishes an active 2026 dirt catalog including ECTOPLASM FUZZ MK I and its 2025-2026 drive line. Official pages document historical products and revision boundaries, including GD-013, GD-013 Version 2.0, 34-Xtreme and the discontinued OD-FIVE family.
- Ovaltone's official GD-013 page dates the original model to 2014 and explicitly distinguishes the simplified PROTO LOOK state from the full version. GD-013 Version 2.0 is a 2017 successor with changed gain/fat behavior and documented exterior photographs.
- Ovaltone's 34-Xtreme page dates the model to 2016 and classifies it as overdrive/distortion. The current Fat Plexi documentation explicitly traces its sound target to the older 34-Xtreme while describing Fat Plexi as a complete redesign.
- Ovaltone's current catalog documents Fountain 2025 ver. as a redesigned generation of the 2016 Fountain. Fountain HS-model is based on the 2025 version but adds another hard-clipping stage and is therefore retained as a signature/relationship state.
- Ovaltone's current 2026 ECTOPLASM FUZZ MK I is explicitly categorized as fuzz/distortion, with surviving official exterior imagery and a detailed control description. Its first release date is February 23, 2026.
- Limetone Audio's first-party site categorizes JACKAL, JACKAL MIDNIGHT, JACKAL MIDNIGHT ADD CBF and fuzzhog proto under OD/DS/FZ and states the brand is a Japanese handmade pedal brand. The site identifies Yujin Imanishi as sound designer/builder.
- Limetone's news archive dates JACKAL's launch to November 26, 2021 and records funther as production-ended January 4, 2021. Therefore funther remains a historical central dirt model even though it is not part of the current OD/DS/FZ storefront list.
- JACKAL MIDNIGHT ADD CBF is kept subordinate to JACKAL MIDNIGHT because the first-party page explicitly describes it as an added Clean Blend/Clean LPF version rather than a wholly separate product family.

## Normalization decisions

- Generation and reduced-function states are not counted as new conceptual families unless the builder documents them as distinct products.
- Color/event-only variants are retained only as relationships where they add useful identification evidence.
- Ovaltone's NUE DEVICE parts/accessory line is not counted as a dirt pedal because it is not an OD/distortion/fuzz pedal product.
- Limetone's compressor, booster/buffer, volume, routing and utility products remain outside the central dirt count.
- No schematics, PCB layouts, gutshots, complete BOMs or cloning instructions were collected.
