# Baldwin-Burns / Burns London: Dirt Catalog Audit 01

## Scope
This audit establishes a manufacturer-first baseline for Baldwin-Burns Ltd / Burns London effects relevant to the Dirt Archive. It separates original 1960s production from later licensed/reissue products and keeps uncertain attribution visible.

## Manufacturer / entity frame
- **Baldwin-Burns Ltd** was the UK corporate corridor joining the Burns London guitar operation with Baldwin Piano & Organ interests. Period evidence places the Buzzaround in this entity's 1960s product range.
- **Burns London** is retained as the broader marketed brand identity. The archive does not automatically treat every Burns-branded effects product as a Baldwin-Burns factory product without period evidence.

## Verified core dirt product
### Buzzaround
- Category: fuzz / distortion.
- Period: mid-to-late 1960s, with period sources supporting a 1966 introduction and production into roughly 1968-1969. Exact first/last production dates remain source-dependent.
- Controls: Sustain, Balance, Timbre.
- Enclosure: wedge-shaped steel case with sliding battery tray / drawer construction in surviving originals.
- Designer attribution: **Gary Hurst is widely credited/reported**, but the archive marks the attribution as reported rather than treating every secondary retelling as primary proof.
- Production: very limited by surviving-specimen evidence and historical accounts; avoid hard unit counts unless a primary production record is found.
- Evidence note: a Burns price list dated April 1966 is cited by Fuzzboxes as period evidence for the Buzzaround. British Pedal Company and multiple vintage-specimen sources place the pedal in the 1966-1968 corridor.

## Important historical relationships
### Buzzaround ↔ Sola Sound Tone Bender Mk III
- The historical record supports a **close relationship / derivative corridor**, but the Dirt Archive should not collapse the two products into one model.
- Fuzzboxes describes the Sola Sound Tone Bender Mk III as very likely based on the earlier Buzzaround design.
- This remains a relationship claim, not permission to publish circuit reconstruction or schematics.

### Buzzaround ↔ Elka Dizzy Tone
- Elka's later Dizzy Tone is frequently described as derivative of the Buzzaround family. Preserve as a separate marketed product / manufacturer node.
- Attribution should remain secondary unless stronger period documentation is located.

### Australian distribution
- The unusually high number of surviving original Buzzarounds in Australia has been used by historical researchers to infer that Baldwin-Burns may have had Australian distribution arrangements. This is recorded as an **inference**, not a verified exclusive distributor relationship.

## Variant / generation cautions
1. Do not infer generation solely from enclosure cosmetics.
2. Do not merge later JMI / Burns-branded reproductions into original Baldwin-Burns production.
3. Do not treat a single U.S.-found Baldwin-labeled fuzz as proof of a broad American production run. A specimen has been reported as a possible American Buzzaround prototype, but its production status remains unresolved.
4. Preserve the distinction between original 1960s product, later licensed reissue, and modern clone/reproduction.

## Later reissue / revival boundary
- **JMI Burns Buzzaround**: a limited later reproduction exists and must remain a separate edition/reissue record, not an original Baldwin-Burns generation.
- Sources describe a 2010 JMI reproduction run associated with the Burns name/licensing context. Exact authorized quantity varies in secondary reporting; do not freeze an unverified number into the canonical catalog.

## Dirt Archive catalog status
### Verified / bounded
- Buzzaround, original Baldwin-Burns / Burns London corridor.

### Separate relationship records
- Sola Sound Tone Bender Mk III relationship.
- Elka Dizzy Tone relationship.
- Possible Australian distribution corridor.
- Reported U.S. Baldwin prototype/specimen.
- Later JMI Burns Buzzaround reproduction.

### Still open
- Complete 1965-1969 Baldwin-Burns effects catalog from surviving period price lists/catalogs.
- Whether additional fuzz/overdrive models existed under Baldwin-Burns / Burns London beyond the Buzzaround.
- Exact transition dates among Baldwin-Burns, Burns London, and any later Baldwin-branded effects marketing.
- Primary documentation for designer, production quantities, distributors, and export markets.

## Evidence discipline
Primary period advertising and price-list evidence should outrank later dealer descriptions, auction copy, or collector recollection. Conflicting dates are retained rather than averaged. No schematics, PCB layouts, BOMs, or circuit reconstruction material are included in this archive.
