# Catalog Completeness Phase 22

## Scope

This phase adds complete lineup discovery records for five builders/labels:

- Empress Effects — 31 current/archived catalog entries in Effects Database.
- Guild — 15 entries covering Guild-branded effects and multiple identified OEM/manufacturing relationships.
- DenTone Electronics — 19 entries.
- Montgomery Appliances — 15 entries.
- Blue Skool Records — 10 entries identified through Effects Database catalog history.

## Research notes

### Empress Effects
Effects Database documents Empress as an Ottawa, Canada pedal company founded in 2005 by Steve Bragg. Its builder history includes an early one-person phase, later manufacturing staff, and design collaboration with Jay (Jason Fee). The catalog is broad rather than dirt-only, so the archive preserves compressors, buffers, EQs, delays, modulation, utility products and the dirt lineup rather than filtering only fuzz/drive/distortion.

Source: https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/empress

### Guild
Guild's effects catalog is historically important because the brand name sits across several production relationships. Effects Database lists Applied-built Fuzz Box, FW-3, SP-5, Stinger and VW-1; Aria-built MM-500; fOXX-built VW-400; Top Gear-built Grizzly Fuzz and Roadrunner; plus the Brian May boosters, Foxey Lady, RV-1 Rotoverb, Timbre Generator and Trioct.

The Foxey Lady requires relationship-level treatment rather than one generic product entry. Fuzzboxes documents a Mosrite-built Guild Foxey Lady using the Fuzzrite family and places surviving Mosrite-built examples no earlier than mid-1968. The same source documents later Electro-Harmonix production, with the Foxey Lady continuing in Fuzzrite-derived form before a later Big Muff-based redesign. Kit Rae similarly separates Mosrite, Aul Instruments and Electro-Harmonix production phases and explicitly treats version numbers as research taxonomy rather than manufacturer identifiers. EHX's own history confirms that Mike Matthews supplied Guild with Foxey Lady pedals and that this work helped lead into the founding of Electro-Harmonix.

Sources:
- https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/guild
- https://fuzzboxes.org/features/fuzzrite
- https://www.kitrae.net/music/History_of_OEM_Big_Muffs.html
- https://www.ehx.com/the-ehx-story/

Important future work: create separate Guild Foxey Lady generations/relationships for Mosrite, Aul/early Matthews, Electro-Harmonix Fuzzrite-derived production, and later Big Muff-derived production. Do not collapse these into a single undifferentiated record.

### DenTone Electronics
Effects Database identifies DenTone as a one-person operation owned by Dennis Menard in Barre, Vermont. Menard's own timeline describes a kitchen-table operation and emphasizes iterative component substitution and listening. The public archive should record the builder history and product lineage but should not publish a reconstructible circuit recipe.

Source: https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/dentone

### Montgomery Appliances
Effects Database identifies Montgomery Appliances as David Gill's one-man operation in Montgomery County, Maryland. Gill describes a small first production run in 2007, early one-offs, and later focus on fuzz boxes. The catalog currently contains 15 listed products, including the FSH, multiple Tone Bender/fuzz-derived products, and the earlier A/DA flanger replica lineage.

Source: https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/montgomery

### Blue Skool Records
Effects Database's historical update log documents the first Blue Skool Records additions in June 2014 and then a larger batch including Au Fuzz, Black Boost, Domino Effect, Fuzz 408, Phozz, Phuket Phozz, Pink Beaver, Red Box, Space Fuzz and Universal Drive. The Space Fuzz page describes it as a handmade silicon-transistor Tonebender-based fuzz with additional user controls, making it a useful candidate for future product-level variation research.

Sources:
- https://www.effectsdatabase.com/updates/weekly
- https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/blueskool/spacefuzz
