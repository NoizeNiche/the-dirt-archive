## Builder-first work

This batch extends the early Australian builder corridor with Claybridge Sound Systems and Fi-Sonic.

### Claybridge Sound Systems

Claybridge is recorded as an early Australian PA/amplifier builder with a documented dirt-product sequence around the Fuzz Box / Fuzzmaster family. Surviving research supports a pre-Fuzzmaster two-control fuzz state and later named Fuzzmaster, Wildcat and MKIV states. The archive keeps the chronology open where evidence is incomplete.

### Fi-Sonic

Fi-Sonic is retained as a medium-confidence research builder associated with Philip Dreoni. Period newspaper evidence establishes a Fuzz product by 1965, while surviving hardware includes an early amp-top Fuzz Blender and a later MKIV stompbox. The relationship between these states, and whether MKII/MKIII products existed, remains unresolved.

## Variation-flag layer

A new variation flag layer was added to prevent breadth-first cataloging from losing important revision evidence. The rule is now:

1. Enumerate the builder's dirt products.
2. Briefly scan each product for known or suspected variations.
3. Record a variation flag when multiple production states, revisions, formats, branding transitions, factory states or specimen differences appear.
4. Assign confidence and a future deep-research priority.
5. Do not split the public catalog into generations/editions until the deeper evidence review is completed.

Initial flags cover Claybridge Fuzzmaster states, Fi-Sonic Fuzz Blender states, Maestro FZ-1 production/factory states, Rosac Nu-Fuzz and Nu-Wa transition evidence, and Zonk Machine production-state questions.

## Evidence discipline

Variation reports based on surviving specimens or specialist secondary research are flags, not automatically verified chronology. The repository should use the flags to build a future archaeology queue without promoting uncertain differences into canonical public history.

## Promotion decision

No public lineage or generation edges promoted from the variation flags in this batch.
