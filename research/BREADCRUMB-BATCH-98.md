# The Dirt Archive · Breadcrumb · Batch 98

## Completed

Phase One Batch 98 added the AKG Sound Design dirt lineage after finding current official documentation for its pedal families.

## Builder

- AKG Sound Design is treated as a Japanese handmade amp-and-effects workshop / builder lineage.
- Current official material documents both TSpecial and Magnus as dedicated effects products.

## Pedals

- TSpecial is the core overdrive family.
- Magnus is the core fuzz family.
- White finishes and the limited JRC4558 / NTE103A / NTE103B presentations remain variants/relationship records rather than separate conceptual models.

## Photos

Official AKG Sound Design product pages provide exterior product imagery and are retained as primary photo-source leads for future rights/credit review.

## Information on the pedal

- TSpecial extracts the distortion-channel concept of an Overdrive Special and is documented with a thick, focused drive response.
- Magnus is documented as a transparent-style fuzz and is also usable as a clean booster.
- AKG currently lists multiple Magnus germanium variants and TSpecial JRC4558 editions, demonstrating why variant-level documentation is useful without inflating model counts.
