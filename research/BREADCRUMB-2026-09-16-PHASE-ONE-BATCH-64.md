Date: 2026-09-16

## Mission state
Continued Phase One builder-first discovery in Southeast Asia with FM Pedals, an Indonesian boutique builder associated with Sylvanus “Ivan” Hardiyanto.

## Evidence notes
The Jakarta Post reported in July 2021 that Sylvanus “Ivan” Hardiyanto is the founder of FM Pedals, lives in Bekasi, West Java, and had been making effects since 2016. The same report identifies Barong High-Gain Distortion, Arjuna Overdrive, Arwana Fuzz Distortion and Papua Distortion as FM Pedals releases. Effects Database independently catalogs Arjuna, Arwana and Barong. RockBoard’s current PedalPedia index additionally lists Atjeh, 1928 and Andra Ramadhan Signature under Fmpedals.

## Catalog discipline
The four models documented by the Jakarta Post and Effects Database form the highest-confidence core. Atjeh, 1928 and Andra Ramadhan Signature are included because they appear in a secondary pedal index and current Indonesian sales coverage, but their exact chronology and production boundaries remain Protocol B work. The Andra Ramadhan model is retained as a signature product rather than silently collapsing it into the core Arjuna/Arwana/Barong families.

## Photo workflow
Each inventory row has a stable product or catalog URL carrying product imagery or a persistent visual reference. Dedicated Effects Database pages provide direct visual evidence for Arjuna, Arwana and Barong; RockBoard and Indonesian retailer pages provide the later-catalog visual trail. None is treated as automatically licensed for republication.

## Scope discipline
Only gain, overdrive, distortion and fuzz products were promoted. The FM Pedals multi-effects product Zentuary is excluded from the central dirt inventory. No schematics, PCB layouts, gutshots, cloning instructions or complete BOM data are published.
