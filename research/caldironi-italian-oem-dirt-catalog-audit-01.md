# Caldironi Musica / Italian Export Pedal OEM Audit 01

## Status
Discovery/catalog-layer audit. Do not treat every marketed badge as a separate factory.

## Core identity signal
TONEHOME documents a 1972/73 Goldsound Distortion Whau-Whau Repeat as probably made by Caldironi Musica/Milano. The same source describes Caldironi as a prolific 1970s producer that marketed effects under multiple names including Silversound, Goldsound, G.I.S., GUN, EF-EL, JEI and Vox. The exact factory-to-badge mapping is not yet fully resolved, so the archive retains brand and manufacturing roles separately.

## Dirt products / relationships
- Goldsound Distortion Whau-Whau Repeat, 1972/73 corridor, manufacturer attribution: probably Caldironi Musica/Milano.
- Silversound SE/40, documented as a marketed equivalent of the Goldsound Distortion Whau-Whau Repeat.
- Cosmosound Distortion Whau-Whau Repeat, documented as another marketed version of the same product family.
- The broader Caldironi/Silversound/G.I.S./GUN/EF-EL/JEI/Vox relationship is recorded as an OEM/marketing hypothesis cluster, not collapsed into one public-facing brand.

## Research caution
The JEI connection is especially important because Jennings Electronic Industries is separately documented in the British census. The Italian Caldironi trail therefore must not automatically be merged with the British JEI corporate entity without product-specific evidence.
