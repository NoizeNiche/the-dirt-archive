# Phase One Batch 37 Breadcrumb

Date: 2026-09-16

## Batch
Batch 37 expands Phase One builder-first inventory coverage into Switzerland, the Netherlands, and Slovakia.

## Builders added
- Volume & Power Company — Renens, Switzerland; compact in-house boutique analog catalog with Power Lines Distortion plus silicon and germanium boost products.
- Greuter Audio — Zurich, Switzerland; handmade catalog centered on Tone Bender, Fuzz Face, Italian Big Muff and amplifier-style overdrive reinterpretations, including anniversary/collaborative states.
- RhPf Electronics — Basel, Switzerland; two-person original-circuit builder with a recurring MOSFET design language across boost, overdrive, distortion and fuzz.
- Iceberg Effects — Netherlands; handmade builder with clearly documented V1/V2 states for Stratum and Strijp plus the single-channel Strijp-S and two Miniwatt tube-overdrive presentation states.
- Salamander Pedals — Slovakia; artisanal builder associated with Jozef Kršák, with Smudge documented from overdrive into fuzz and a ten-unit 2024 anniversary run.

## Evidence / boundary notes
- Volume & Power's current site states that its pedals are hand-built in Renens and that PCB design, soldering, drilling, painting and laser engraving are performed in-house. Power Lines is classified as central dirt; the two booster models remain gain-adjacent but historically relevant.
- Greuter's current shop explicitly documents Germanium OD, Jumbo Fuzz, Moonlight, Star Bender, ToBias Bender and Fuller Drive. The Fuller Drive WAH 10th Anniversary combination product is retained as a commercial edition/combination state, not as two unrelated product families.
- RhPf's official products page lists 11 products, of which nine are dirt or gain-adjacent. The archive excludes the tremolo and delay from the central dirt inventory. The builder states the circuits are original and made in Basel.
- Iceberg's manuals provide explicit V1/V2 documentation for Stratum and Strijp, while the V2 bypass support page documents the linked footswitch architecture. These are useful later generation/state targets.
- Salamander evidence is thinner than the larger Swiss catalogs. The Smudge model is strongly documented through Reverb/retailer material as handmade in Slovakia; the 2024 anniversary run is explicitly ten individually numbered units. Keep future lifetime-catalog expansion conservative until additional first-party history is recovered.
