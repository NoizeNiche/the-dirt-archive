# Blackstone Appliances: 1998 Origin and Dirt Catalog Audit

## Scope
Builder-level historical audit focused on the origin of Blackstone Appliances and the MosFET Overdrive. This record stays at the identity, chronology, product, and evidence level. No circuit reconstruction, schematic, PCB, BOM, or cloning material is included.

## Builder identity
- **Entity:** Blackstone Appliances / Jon Blackstone Steele
- **Region:** USA
- **Founding anchor:** 1998
- **Primary dirt signal:** MosFET Overdrive
- **Status:** HISTORICAL CATALOG ANCHOR

## Evidence
Blackstone's current site states that Jon Blackstone Steele first offered the Mosfet Overdrive as a pedal in **1998** and that the same core pedal family has been built for roughly two decades. Effects Database independently quotes Jon Blackstone saying he started Blackstone Appliances in **1998** and that the Mosfet Overdrive evolved from an earlier late-1970s experiment.

## Catalog disposition
### MosFET Overdrive
- First offered as a pedal: **1998**.
- Historical product family retained as one builder lineage rather than splitting every enclosure/control revision into a new builder.
- The current 2S is a later production state and should not be back-projected into the 1998 specimen layer.

### 2S / modern compact state
- Current product family documents a two-channel compact version.
- Treat as a later generation/state under the MosFET Overdrive family.
- Do not infer that the 1998 pedal was physically identical to the present 2S.

## Historical significance
Blackstone provides a useful late-1990s boutique bridge: unlike vintage-recreation shops whose identities grew from restoration or modification businesses, Jon Blackstone describes a distinct original overdrive concept that began in 1998 while drawing on much earlier experimentation.

## Archive rules applied
1. Builder identity and product family remain separate from later enclosure/generation states.
2. 1998 is treated as the founding/product anchor, not as proof of exact first-unit production date.
3. Present-day product specifications are not back-projected onto 1998 specimens.
4. Circuit-level material is intentionally excluded.
