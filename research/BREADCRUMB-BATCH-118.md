## Information on the pedal

- Organic Sounds is an Aichi workshop led by Yusuke Watanabe; the company identifies one-by-one workshop construction. Its catalog spans original overdrives, RAT-derived distortion, Muff/Ram's Head-oriented fuzz and Buzzaround-derived fuzz products.
- Katanasound is documented as a Japanese handmade builder whose catalog includes overdrive, classic fuzz, multifaceted silicon fuzz and bass/octave fuzz products. Founder-origin material emphasizes hand production and selected parts, while exact lifetime chronology remains open.
- ZenZero's FuzU was introduced in 2017 as a hybridized Muff-family fuzz combining two era references with MOSFET stages and added mids control; launch coverage identifies Tokyo hand-building.
- Quiet Signal is a Matsusaka one-person builder, with founder "Italian" documenting direct circuit, PCB and enclosure design. Death Metal Slime is explicitly identified as a fuzz product.
- 320design's official documentation establishes Sunakake as an overdrive/booster and Brown Feather as a distortion; current site material also preserves Landmighty and collaboration-era products. Strung Fuzz remains a signature/collaboration relationship record rather than a separate core lineage.

## Normalization decisions

- Current dealer colorways and special presentation states are not promoted to independent conceptual models without evidence of substantive product differences.
- ZenZero's weaker secondary-only dirt names remain CENTRAL_PENDING rather than being treated as equally verified with FuzU.
- Katanasound's exact historical production periods remain open; the batch records product existence without inventing dates.
- 320design Strung Fuzz is retained as a relationship-only collaboration record.
- No schematics, PCB layouts, gutshots, complete BOMs or cloning instructions are collected.
