## Information on the pedal

- ALBIT's official effectors index separates Booster/Distortion/Overdrive/Fuzz families. GFD-1 is documented as a FET-based guitar/bass distortion with a February 2021 release; TB-1 is a 2015 TubeSound Booster/overdrive using six FETs; OD-1B is a discontinued bass overdrive with two gain paths; GWF-1 is a 2021 germanium-diode guitar fuzz; FZ-2B is a discontinued 2012 bass fuzz; MF-1 is a discontinued 2012 micro fuzz. These dated pages provide strong chronological anchors while weaker catalog-only models remain pending.
- The ALBIT manual trail also preserves the historical FZ-1A OverDrive Fuzz, a fuzz-driver combining fuzz and drive behavior with its own control/switching layout.
- Organic Sounds' Kerberos is explicitly described by the builder as its first original fuzz, using a three-mode rotary control and strong guitar-volume interaction. The Custom Limited Edition adds Master Volume and a different vintage-part selection, so it is retained as an edition relationship rather than a separate conceptual family.
- Orthros is documented as a Fuzz Face-oriented product; Japanese dealer evidence explicitly separates germanium and silicon versions. The archive preserves those as model-state relationships rather than unrelated designs.
- Small Tower extends the builder's Ram's Head-oriented fuzz work in a smaller enclosure; ORGAROUND and ORGA TONE belong to the builder's vintage-series reconstructions of BUZZAROUND and DIZZY TONE, while ORGA FACE 66 represents the Fuzz Face-oriented vintage series.

## Normalization decisions

- ALBIT models with official catalog existence but insufficient detailed historical information remain **CENTRAL_PENDING** rather than being presented as fully researched histories.
- Kerberos Custom is relationship-only because the evidence points to a limited variant of Kerberos with added Master Volume and component selection rather than a wholly separate product family.
- Orthros Germanium and Orthros Silicon are relationship-only production/component variants under Orthros.
- No schematics, PCB layouts, gutshots, complete BOMs or cloning instructions are collected.
