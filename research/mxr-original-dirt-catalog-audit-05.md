# MXR Original Dirt Catalog Audit 05

## Scope
This pass continues the original MXR Innovations-era catalog from the early 1970s through the company's 1984 closure, concentrating on dirt-relevant products and separating Reference, Commande, and Series 2000 identities.

## Findings

### Reference / original compact corridor
- **MX-103 / M-103 Blue Box**: period MXR lineage; retain as a distinct octave-fuzz/distortion family member rather than treating the later CSP reissue as the same production state.
- **MX-104 / M-104 Distortion+**: foundational original compact dirt model, with script-logo and later block-logo production states. A 1976 trade feature independently identifies MXR's Distortion+ and describes it as a fuzz/distortion-oriented unit. citeturn143090search38turn143090search40
- **M-133 Micro Amp**: keep as a scope-boundary clean-boost product, not a central dirt count.
- **M-142 Distortion II**: distinct original MXR product, separate from both M-104 Distortion+ and the later Series 2000 M-204 Distortion+. Historical lists place M-142 in the original MXR AC-powered catalog family. citeturn553512search29turn846092search2

### Commande Series
- **M-164 Commande Overdrive**: distinct dirt model introduced in the early 1980s Commande line. Reverb's product record gives a 1981-1984 United States production corridor; specialist historical coverage also places it in the Commande series. citeturn734817search4turn734817search6
- Preserve M-164 as a marketed retail model even where modern descriptions compare it with Tube Screamer-style overdrive. Do not merge it into Ibanez or Maxon catalog identity without direct manufacturing evidence.

### Series 2000
- **M-204 Distortion+**: separate Series 2000 product generation, with a 1982-1984 production corridor in surviving product records. The model number and chassis generation must not be collapsed into the earlier M-104 Distortion+. citeturn734817search8turn734817search11
- Series 2000 also included non-dirt models, but only the Distortion+ is central to this dirt-catalog pass. The broader 2000-series chronology is retained as a context layer.

## Important chronology rule
MXR's model-number reuse and series changes make simple model-number sorting unsafe. **M-104, M-142, M-164, and M-204 are separate catalog identities/states even though modern listings may use abbreviated names such as Distortion+ or Overdrive.** The archive therefore keys records on marketed product + series + production corridor, then links them by family where evidence supports it.

## Evidence notes
- MXR's Reference period is generally treated as 1973-1984, with script-logo and block-logo production states and later changes such as LED/DC-adapter-era revisions. citeturn143090search40
