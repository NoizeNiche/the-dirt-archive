# Prescription Electronics, 1993-2000 catalog audit

## Identity
Prescription Electronics was founded by Jack Brossart in Portland, Oregon, in the early 1990s. Period and retrospective sources place the company's commercial run beginning in 1993 and continuing until 2014. The brand should remain distinct from later reissue/revival activity.

## Dirt catalog anchors
- Experience: introduced 1993; fuzz/octave/swell product and one of the earliest Prescription products.
- Yardbox: introduced 1994; Tone Bender-oriented dirt product.
- Rx Overdriver: documented as a Colorsound Overdriver-inspired overdrive.
- C.O.B. / Clean Octave Blend: octave-fuzz/clean-blend product in the original catalog.

## Evidence disposition
The Experience and Yardbox have strong dated catalog/timeline evidence. Rx Overdriver and C.O.B. are securely part of the original Prescription dirt catalog, but exact first-production years require further period catalog work. Do not infer that later reissues are identical production specimens to the 1990s originals.

## Relationship policy
Prescription's products drew explicitly on 1960s British/psychedelic effects history, but the archive records inspiration separately from manufacturer identity. Reissue-era production should remain a separate generation/production-state layer.
