## Evidence notes
K.S. Aji Tone Workshop has unusually strong historical documentation. The workshop site is authored by Kintoko S. Aji and explains that the builder's work involves refining existing classic pedal concepts. Effects Database catalogs ten products, including Blues Creamer, Red Creamer, FU-223, Valve Creamer and Violet Drive. A surviving Reverb listing identifies Red Creamer as hand-made in Indonesia and documents its 2020 product year. Preserve early Blues Creamer and Blues Creamer 2 states as chronology clues until Protocol B can reconcile exact generation boundaries.

GFI System is documented by independent industry reporting as a Jakarta boutique venture begun around 2010 by Henry Widjaja. Effects Database records Eigendrive as a 2014 distortion/overdrive/boost product with multiple gain stages and dual boost sections. GFI's current first-party catalog is dominated by DSP, delay, reverb, modulation and utility pedals, so only Eigendrive is promoted in the central Phase One dirt inventory from this pass.

AMTECH-HANDWIRED was previously reviewed but deferred because its visible activity mixed custom, service and clone work with broader electronics. Current first-party evidence now directly identifies Sarinten Pedals as a handmade boutique line and names Destroyer analog distortion plus Drive Box analog overdrive/boost with product descriptions and pricing. This is sufficient for a canonical-candidate census record while retaining a strict separation between named products and the workshop's custom/clone/service activities.

## Boundary / scope notes
- K.S. Aji's classic-circuit refinement philosophy is historical context, not a reason to merge its products with original reference designs or other brands.
- Blues Creamer, Blues Creamer 2, White/Batik editions and other finish or market variants should be reconciled as model/generation/specimen relationships before final public counting.
- GFI System remains a builder record with one promoted historical dirt product; current non-dirt and DSP products stay outside the central dirt inventory.
- AMTECH-HANDWIRED custom builds, repairs and clone work remain separate from the Sarinten named-product line.
- No schematics, PCB layouts, gutshots, cloning instructions, or complete BOM data are published.
