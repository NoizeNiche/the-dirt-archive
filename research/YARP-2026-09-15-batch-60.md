# YARP 2026-09-15 Batch 60: Davoli / Krundaal Trem Dix

## Scope
Builder-first catalog expansion for Davoli's late-1960s external effect units, with the Trem Dix family separated from unrelated Davoli amplifiers and with specimen-dated evidence treated as corroboration rather than universal chronology.

## Builder identity
Davoli was an established Italian musical-instrument/electronics manufacturer active through the 1960s and 1970s. The external effect record is thinner than the amplifier record, so the archive treats Davoli as a canonical Italian manufacturer for the documented pedal/product state while keeping individual manufacturing and distribution relationships model-specific.

## Enumerated dirt catalog

### Krundaal Trem Dix
A documented Italian external unit combining tremolo with fuzz/distortion. A surviving example is stamped 30 October 1968, providing strong specimen evidence for the late-1968 corridor. Contemporary-style specialist coverage identifies it as a rare late-1960s Davoli pedal and specifically labels the Trem Dix as a fuzz/tremolo product.

### Krundaal / related external units
Additional Davoli external effects appear in surviving equipment references, but the dirt-bearing catalog is not yet sufficiently enumerated to assume every Krundaal-branded unit contained distortion. Future work should establish exact model numbers and catalog dates before promotion.

## Historical significance
The Trem Dix provides an important Italian counterpoint to the better-known JEN/Caldironi/Montarbo corridor. Its dated 1968 specimen demonstrates that Italian fuzz-related external pedals were present before many later-1970s Italian rebrands, while its combined tremolo/fuzz role belongs in the archive as a hybrid dirt product.

## Evidence state

**Evidence-qualified:** Davoli manufacturer identity; Krundaal Trem Dix existence; fuzz/distortion + tremolo role; specimen date 30 October 1968; Italian manufacture.

**Unresolved:** complete Davoli/Krundaal dirt catalog; exact commercial introduction; production endpoint; whether additional model numbers were factory-built or distributor-marketed states.
