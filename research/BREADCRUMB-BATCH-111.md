## Builders

### TRINITY EFFECTS
- Treated as a canonical candidate Japanese boutique builder.
- Tone Blue announced TRINITY EFFECTS as a new pedal brand in June 2025 and described its point-to-point construction, selected parts and one-of-a-kind fluid-art finishes.
- Current dealer evidence confirms ZENTAUR, LT FUZZ and FLUID DISTORTION as Japanese-made products, with serialized examples and exterior photography.

### Herbe&Chick
- Treated as canonical.
- First-party shop identifies Herbe&Chick as a Japanese handmade effects brand and separates OverDrive, Distortion, Booster and Preamp categories.
- First-party product page documents ORANGE with 16 switch combinations and broad drive use; current dealer evidence documents GAZON -Distortion- Rev.FINAL with low/high gain switching and three-band active EQ.

## Photos

- TRINITY EFFECTS: current Ishibashi dealer records include exterior product photography for ZENTAUR, LT FUZZ and FLUID DISTORTION; Tone Blue's 2025 launch post also contains product imagery.
- Herbe&Chick: first-party ORANGE page contains multiple product images; current dealer documentation supplies exterior imagery for GAZON Rev.FINAL.

## Information on the pedal

- LEGENDARY TONE is a one-knob point-to-point drive that can cover boost, crunch, overdrive and heavy fuzz-like saturation through player/amp interaction.
- ZENTAUR is described by dealer documentation as a transparent-style overdrive built around a Klon-based foundation with more gain than the original concept.
- LT FUZZ and FLUID DISTORTION are independently documented current Japanese-made Trinity products with serialized examples.
- ORANGE is documented by Herbe&Chick with 16 tone-character switch combinations.
- GAZON Rev.FINAL adds a final development pass, low/high gain switching and three-band active EQ.

## Normalization decisions

- TRINITY EFFECTS is not split into separate brands for its Fluid, Legendary Tone and LT families.
- LEGENDARY TONE is central dirt despite its ability to reach fuzz-like saturation because the builder's launch description presents it as a drive product with a broad gain range.
- Herbe&Chick boosters and preamp remain outside central dirt.
- No schematics, PCB layouts, gutshots or complete component inventories are collected.
