## Important research notes
- Pedaltrain's Daylight and Nightlight are documented as brand-new original circuits, designed/built/tested in Nashville. The First Edition matched-set page gives unusually good physical-production evidence: hand-soldered through-hole construction, Switchcraft jacks, Rainbow's End sparkle black finish and designer Aaron Wahl.
- Funny Little Boxes Skeleton Key is a 2023 collaboration with Let's Play All and Boneface artwork. FLB explicitly distinguishes it from the 1991 and later documented the 2025 Rated R cosmetic limited edition as the same circuit. Keep those identities separate.
- Intensive Care Audio Death Drive is an original all-analogue overdrive/distortion with EGO, DESTRUCTION, DRIVE and MOOD controls, built in London with relay-based soft switching.
- Intensive Care Audio Vena Cava Filter was already documented as V.1 in 2021, so its appearance in 2023 coverage must not be mistaken for its introduction date. This is a useful reminder that census-year discovery and product-year introduction are separate fields.
- Sentimental Bob Electronics Tephra is well documented in 2023 coverage as a Ukrainian builder's enhanced RAT-style distortion with octave, boost and six clipping modes, but introduction timing remains unresolved. Preserve the uncertainty rather than forcing a 2022 or 2023 start date.

## Catalog-integrity correction
Batch 21 used IDs MOD-0080 through MOD-0085. Batch 22 had accidentally reused those identifiers. Batch 22 has now been renumbered to MOD-0086 through MOD-0091, and public Batch 20-23 TSV files were added because the runtime bridges load public-path TSV assets. The Batch 23 bridge was folded into `public/catalog-extensions-73.js`, avoiding an additional script-load change while preserving the existing page architecture.

## Data rule reinforced
Never reuse a pedal, generation, distinguisher or claim identifier across separate catalog subjects. Discovery year is not introduction year. Edition, reissue, collaboration, artwork treatment, manual revision and generation remain distinct until evidence proves otherwise.

## Public catalog status
