# YARP Breadcrumb 136

## Focus
Continue hard dirt-device qualification and distinguish genuine builders from retailers, marketers, and unresolved product labels.

## Confirmed
- G. P. Electronics: promoted to VERIFIED_DIRT_MAKER. Fuzzboxes documents the Harmonic Generator as a G. P. Electronics fuzz and states that construction was carried out at the G. P. Electronics workshop. Public sales are documented by late 1965/early 1966.
- Seamoon Inc., Carlsbro, Rosac / Sierra Electronics, Aul Instruments, Chunk Systems, Montarbo, Lemon StudioSound, Jennings Electronic Industries, Morley / Tel-Ray Electronics, and fOXX / Ridinger Associates remain qualified under the same gate.

## Rejected / held
- Clifford Musical Supplies: rejected as a builder candidate because the evidence only establishes retail advertisement. Fuzzboxes explicitly leaves open whether the shop stocked another maker's product or was supplied by a small builder.
- Kelly Amplification: remains provisional because the 1966 fuzz advertisement does not resolve physical manufacture.

## Site work
- Public builder data now carries a dirt-maker qualification field for verified builders.
- Builder census UI now displays a DIRT-MAKER VERIFIED badge and includes the qualification evidence on builder cards.
- G. P. Electronics has been added to the public qualification layer without creating an OEM or brand duplicate.

## Editorial rule
A historical name earns verified-builder status through evidence of actually producing a dirt device. Selling, distributing, designing, importing, advertising, or appearing on an unlabeled/rebranded pedal is not enough by itself.

## Next queue
Keep screening obscure builders and micro-builders. Prioritize candidates where maker evidence can be established without solving the entire relationship graph. Once qualified, move that builder into product inventory rather than spending unlimited time on factory-family archaeology.
