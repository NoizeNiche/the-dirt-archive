### Findings

- Applied Audio now has stronger factory-operation evidence for the 1968-1970 South Plainfield period. Period research identifies Applied Audio as an associate organization housed with Trans-Tek and states that workers built amplifiers and effects pedals there, including the Applied Big Fuzz.
- Separate 2026 research adds workforce evidence indicating Applied staff built pedals and amplifiers while also handling component inspection associated with the Trans-Tek operation. This supports a real manufacturing workforce rather than a paper-only brand identity.
- The Applied/Trans-Tek relationship remains a shared-facility and associate-organization relationship. Trans-Tek is not promoted to pedal builder by default.
- Bowman Electronics Fuzz Maker Sound Kit is now isolated as a HOLD_FOR_REVIEW research case. Its 1970 catalog appearance and physical resemblance to early Applied hardware are leads, but there is still no concrete evidence proving Applied manufacture or an Applied circuit.
- Bowman and Trans-Tek's geographic proximity in Garwood, NJ is preserved as a research clue only.
