# YARP 2026-09-15 Batch 39

## Focus
BearFoot FX as the authorized U.S. production/brand branch for Björn Juhl designs, especially the Honey Bee family and related dirt models.

## Findings

### BearFoot relationship
Historical research states that an agreement was made in 2011 for BearFoot FX to produce BJFE Honey Bee pedals in the United States. BearFoot's own historical interview material describes its pedals as Björn Juhl designs and notes that many were drawn from BJFE regulars, customshop or research pedals.

### Dirt families
Documented BearFoot dirt families include Honey Bee, Model H, Model G, Honey Beest, Uber Bee and Sparkling Yellow Overdrive. These are preserved as a production/marketing branch of Juhl's design lineage rather than as a separate independent design house for every model.

### Archive interpretation
The graph is: Björn Juhl designer -> BJFE/BJF original Swedish hand-built product -> BearFoot FX U.S. authorized production/brand state. This is especially important for specimen research because a later BearFoot enclosure is not evidence of a Swedish BJFE production date.

### Future completion
A full BearFoot dirt catalog is still warranted, including discontinued and customshop products, but this pass establishes the builder-level lineage and enough model names to prevent duplicate counting in the master census.
