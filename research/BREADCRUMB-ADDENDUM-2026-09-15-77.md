## Findings
- UMI Wa is retained as a UMI-made product with a strong 1969 specimen anchor. A separate 1970s UMI Wa example is also documented.
- Fox Wa is kept separate from UMI. Shared enclosure appearance is treated as comparison evidence only and does not establish a common factory.
- Apollo No. 843 and Conrad Buzz Box & Volume Expander remain UMI-produced marketed-label states within the Buzz Tone/Volume Expander family.
- UMI Buzz Tone is retained as the UMI own-brand state in the later portion of the Kimberly/Applied/UMI production lineage.
- UMI Tone Booster remains its own UMI model and is not forced into the Kimberly/Applied Buzz Box lineage.
- UMI Treble-Bass Booster & Mute remains a UMI-made candidate with weaker chronology than the 1969 Wa and Buzz Tone records.
