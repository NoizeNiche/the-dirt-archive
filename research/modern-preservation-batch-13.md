# Modern Preservation Track — Batch 13

## Position
2023 census continues. This batch deliberately captures builder/product relationships that are easy to lose when annual roundups are treated as the catalog itself.

## Verified / strong secondary evidence

### Aclam — The Mocker
- 2023 launch.
- Limited edition of 200 units.
- Expanded interpretation of the Vox UL730-series fuzz family.
- Controls documented as Volume, Bias and Tone.
- Contemporary reporting identifies NOS Philips OA200 diodes as part of the design identity.
- Status: CATALOGED / VARIANT_MAPPED candidate.
- Important identity note: limited-edition status should remain an EDITION attribute, not a new generation unless later evidence demonstrates a product-generation change.

### Holy Island Audio + Mask Audio Electronics — STOP! Dual Fuzz
- 2023 collaboration.
- Combines Holy Island Audio Abracadaver octave-up fuzz and Mask Audio Electronics YES gated fuzz.
- Two independent footswitches.
- Order switch reverses the two fuzz stages.
- Shared enclosure contains two distinct product lineages.
- Status: CATALOGED / COMPOUND PRODUCT.
- Archive note: preserve both contributing builder identities rather than collapsing STOP! into a single-builder record.

### Oneder Effects — Old Blue Overdrive
- 2023 catalog entry / V2-era documentation.
- Low-to-medium gain overdrive with higher-gain saturation available.
- V2 adds a third clipping position and changes the Weight control behavior.
- Earlier V1 documentation indicates a two-position clipping control.
- Builder identity is Oneder Effects; Nick Diener describes Old Blue as his own faithful overdrive that later became part of the Oneder line.
- Status: VARIANT_MAPPED.

## Method note
Annual 'best of' lists are discovery indexes, not authority. Promote a product only after its builder/product identity, date, category, physical identity, and variant state can be supported. Preserve limited editions, collaborations, signatures, and cosmetic editions separately from generations.
