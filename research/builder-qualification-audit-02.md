# Builder Qualification Audit 02

## fOXX / Ridinger Associates Inc.
**Status:** VERIFIED_DIRT_MAKER

The Liverpool Fuzz Tone is directly attributed to Steve Ridinger / Ridinger Associates. Ridinger states he made early examples himself beginning around 1966, later using an assembly company. Period and specialist sources document commercial release and an estimated 500-1,000 units between 1966 and 1969.

**Editorial treatment:** upgrade the existing fOXX / Ridinger lineage. Do not create a duplicate builder for Ridinger Associates. Keep the Liverpool Fuzz Tone as an early product and preserve hand-built versus outsourced production as a manufacturing/generation distinction.

Sources: https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/liverpool/fuzztone ; https://www.tonemachinesblog.com/2024/04/the-liverpool-fuzz-box-foxx.html ; https://worldradiohistory.com/UK/Beat-Instrumental/6812-Beat-Instrumental-1968-12-OCR.pdf

## Paul Lucas / Tridents
**Status:** PROVISIONAL_CUSTOM_MAKER

A custom fuzz commission for Jeff Beck is documented, but the evidence currently establishes a single commission rather than a broader independent pedal-making operation.

**Editorial treatment:** retain as a custom-maker research lead. Do not promote as a commercial builder yet.

Source: https://fuzzboxes.org/features/sixtiesboutique

## Clifford Musical Supplies
**Status:** REJECTED_BUILDER_CANDIDATE

Fuzz boxes were advertised, but the surviving evidence does not establish manufacture. The source explicitly leaves open whether the shop merely stocked another maker's fuzz boxes.

**Editorial treatment:** retail/distribution history only unless direct manufacturing evidence emerges.

Source: https://fuzzboxes.org/features/sixtiesboutique

## Kelly Amplification
**Status:** PROVISIONAL_DIRT_MAKER

A Kelly Fuzz Box was advertised in 1966, but family testimony leaves open whether Kelly built it or Macari/Sola Sound supplied a rebranded product.

**Editorial treatment:** retain as provisional pending physical-manufacture evidence.

Source: https://fuzzboxes.org/kellyfuzzbox
