# ProCo Dirt Catalog Completion 03

Research date: 2026-09-14

Status: breadth-first RAT-family and adjacent dirt reconciliation. Exact lifetime completeness is not claimed.

## Canonical identity

Canonical builder: ProCo Sound. Keep ProCo distinct from later third-party RAT-style builders and from the JHS historical commentary used as a secondary evidence trail.

## RAT family: current working chronology

- Bud Box RAT development/prototype corridor (1977-1978; surviving evidence and unit counts require cautious treatment)
- 1979 Version 1 production RAT / Fringe Logo states
- 1979 Version 1 cosmetic revisions
- 1981 Version 2 Filter-control state
- 1984-1988 White Face / Black Face production states
- 1988 RAT2 era / slope-enclosure transition
- 1989 Turbo RAT
- 1991 Big Box Vintage Reissue
- R2DU rack-mount dual RAT
- 2001 Deucetone RAT development / release corridor
- 2003 25th Anniversary Silver RAT
- 2004 You Dirty RAT
- 2006 SOLO
- 2010 '85 Whiteface RAT Reissue
- 2014 FATRAT
- 2021-era Lil' RAT
- Later RAT2 production states, including outsourced Chinese slope-enclosure examples

JHS's detailed history is unusually explicit that simple V1/V2 labels hide important sub-versions and that collector chronology should use more granular visual/production identifiers. It dates the first production RAT to 1979, describes the 1981 Filter-control change, the 1989 Turbo RAT, the 1991 Vintage Reissue, and later major retail states. ## Non-RAT ProCo dirt catalog audit

The central queue should also verify ProCo products outside the RAT family, including:

- Brat
- Roadkill
- Deucetone RAT
- SOLO
- You Dirty RAT
- FATRAT
- limited/anniversary RAT editions
- compact RAT derivatives and later retail variants

Brat and Roadkill remain separate searchable retail identities but should be linked as an economy-market family rather than treated as unrelated ProCo lineages. The existing archive chronology preserves this relationship.

## Identification priorities

For public historical identification, prioritize visible enclosure/form-factor, graphics/logo treatment, control labeling, production-era dates, serial/date conventions, and documented manufacturing transitions. Avoid treating component folklore as a standalone identity rule.

The archive's editorial boundary remains in force: no schematics, PCB layouts, complete BOMs, gutshot galleries, cloning instructions, or circuit-reconstruction material are published even where secondary sources contain such details.

## Priority verification queue

1. Reconcile RAT version naming between the archive's public generation model and the granular JHS chronology.
2. Verify first-sale versus first-catalog versus prototype dates wherever sources disagree.
3. Separate RAT2 production states, enclosure transitions and geographic manufacturing states.
4. Complete non-RAT ProCo catalog enumeration from company catalogs and historical retailer literature.
5. Promote only evidence-reviewed records into the public pedal catalog.

## Disposition

ProCo remains CATALOG-INCOMPLETE at builder level, while the RAT family now has a substantially stronger breadth baseline and an explicit version-reconciliation queue.
