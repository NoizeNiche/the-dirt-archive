# Breadcrumb Addendum 72

Date: 2026-09-15

## Pass
Universal Amplifier / Astro Amp OEM neighborhood audit.

## Work recorded
- Astrotone remains a high-confidence Universal Amplifier Corp. manufactured product, with surviving examples and period documentation placing production in New York from 1967 into 1969.
- Sam Ash Fuzzz Boxx remains a high-confidence Universal Amplifier OEM / house-brand state. Early Fuzzz Boxx units share the Astrotone production platform and later surviving units show the same evolving factory states.
- Sam Ash Fuzz-Stainer remains explicitly unresolved and does not inherit Universal Amplifier attribution merely from the Sam Ash name.
- Sam Ash Fuzzola remains a separate multi-builder supply chain: Shin-Ei in Japan initially, then JEN in Italy.
- Sam Ash Fuzzola II / Jumbo Fuzz is kept in the later JEN production branch, estimated c. 1973-1975 by secondary historical research.
- No defensible third vintage dirt-pedal identity was found in this pass that warrants adding to the Universal Amplifier factory list. The negative result is recorded deliberately to prevent OEM expansion by visual similarity or generic New York provenance.

## Editorial decision
Do not promote additional Universal Amplifier lineage edges from this pass. Keep the UAC branch anchored to Astrotone and Sam Ash Fuzzz Boxx until model-specific factory evidence appears.
