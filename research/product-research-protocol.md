# The Dirt Archive — Product Research Protocol

## Purpose

Phase 44 marks a deliberate shift from builder enumeration to product-by-product catalog depth. The builder universe is broad enough to pause the independent-company discovery work and begin turning the discovery catalog into historically useful individual records.

## Research unit

Each product record should eventually receive, where evidence exists:

- normalized model name and family name
- earliest credible evidence / introduction window
- latest credible evidence / discontinuation window when applicable
- production status
- concise historical summary
- generation/version breakdown
- enclosure, artwork, control and labeling changes that help identify examples
- high-level component or circuit changes only when they are historically useful for identification
- OEM, rebrand, licensing, collaboration and derived-design relationships
- source records with URL, source type and confidence
- at least one front-image lead when feasible
- image rights status kept separate from discovery status

## Editorial boundary

Document the object, not the recipe. Do not add schematics, PCB layouts, complete bills of materials, gutshot libraries, or cloning instructions merely because a source contains them.

## Confidence discipline

Use evidence tiers rather than filling empty fields with guesses:

- **Verified:** supported by primary manufacturer documentation, period advertising/catalog material, or strong surviving-object research with clear provenance.
- **Probable:** supported by multiple independent specialist sources but not yet anchored by a primary source.
- **Discovery:** useful lead requiring additional verification.
- **Unresolved:** conflicting or insufficient evidence. Preserve the uncertainty rather than forcing a conclusion.

## Batch strategy

Work in manageable batches, prioritizing historically important and highly connected products first. Each batch should:

1. research the individual products;
2. add structured metadata to the site;
3. attach source records;
4. capture image leads separately from republishing rights;
5. make thumbnails visible only when the source is explicitly cleared for public rendering;
6. update this file and `research/catalog-breadcrumb.md` with the completed range and next queue.

## Thumbnail strategy

The builder page is the main browsing surface, so every product should eventually have a compact front thumbnail. A thumbnail record must distinguish:

- `image_url`
- `source_page`
- `credit`
- `license_or_permission`
- `rights_status`
- `republish_status`

Collector, marketplace and editorial images can be excellent identification references but remain non-public until permission/license is established.
