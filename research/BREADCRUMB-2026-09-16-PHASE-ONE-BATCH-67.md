## Evidence notes
KHP FX Audio's first-party About page states the Vietnamese brand is based in Da Nang and was established in 2022 after starting with pedal repair, classic-pedal modification and DIY kits. The current shop identifies a growing handmade guitar-effect line. Recent catalog evidence documents Ratten Distortion V4.1, while the shop and archived material expose earlier Ratten revisions plus Generator Distortion, Amnesia Fuzz/Distortion and Immoral Duality V3. Preserve the Ratten version trail for Protocol B rather than collapsing every revision into one undifferentiated model.

Blacksleep Audio's first-party site states the project began as a solo backyard effort during the pandemic, formed a team in 2021 and released its first line in 2023. The current catalog establishes Logicvirus overdrive/boost and Pitchbog fuzz as core products and lists additional named custom-shop dirt builds. Keep the Tasing x Blacksleep Audio limited run as a collaboration record rather than a new builder identity.

PedalPimps is credited by JCraft as a Philippines-based boutique guitar pedal designer and the design collaborator for the LTX Drive. That is enough to preserve the identity and relationship trail, but not enough to infer a complete independent PedalPimps product line. The archive therefore records the designer relationship without counting the collaboration as a standalone PedalPimps catalog.

## Boundary / scope notes
- KHP FX Audio repair, modification, clone/DIY activity and original named products remain separate research dimensions.
- Ratten Distortion V3 and V4.1 are chronology/version clues, not automatically distinct conceptual products.
- Blacksleep custom-shop names are retained as object-level records but should not inflate stable catalog counts until repeatability and chronology are established.
