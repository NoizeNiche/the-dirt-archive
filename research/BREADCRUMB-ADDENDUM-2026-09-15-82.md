### Findings

- The rare Nomad Super Fuzz is now explicitly isolated as an unresolved early-supplier question rather than inheriting the later Applied Audio attribution attached to Nomad Banshee and Rogue products.
- Tone Machines reports that the Nomad Super Fuzz contains a germanium Fuzzrite-type circuit matching late-1966/early-1967 Mosrite Fuzzrite examples, but no period evidence currently proves a Mosrite-to-Nomad OEM transaction or an Applied Audio build.
- The Nomad brand therefore now has three distinct supplier states in the research ledger: early Super Fuzz unresolved, later Applied Audio Banshee/Rogue, and UMI-linked Tone Booster.
- Gracin's Fubar remains a separate East Coast Fuzz family research case. Its shared-family evidence is stronger than its builder evidence, so it stays HOLD_FOR_REVIEW.
- The Gracin's Fubar must not be confused with the Foxx-made Gracin's & Towne Wa & Volume, which is a different product and supply chain.
