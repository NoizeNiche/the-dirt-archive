# Batch 138 Breadcrumb

## Governing Priority
**Builder → Pedals → Photos → Information on the pedal**

## Builder
Petla is treated as a canonical Japanese handmade effects builder, led by builder nanase. Guitar Magazine's 2022 Tokyo Pedal Summit coverage identifies nanase as the builder and highlights Noa. Later Shimamura coverage identifies the four-pedal core of Noa, Noa.err, Ive and Rev.

## Catalog
### Central
- Noa — transparent low-gain overdrive.
- Noa.err — fuzz/overdrive with chaotic character.
- Ive — mid-focused overdrive with saturation cut.
- Rev — drive with Resonance and Presence 2-band EQ.

### Central-pending
- OD4A — Petla builder workshop/event overdrive build.
- OD4ABSSCM — limited/event-associated build found in Japanese secondary-market records, awaiting stronger first-party reconciliation.

### Relationship-only
- Noa Atheist ver — anniversary/special version.
- Noa Pre / Archive-2024 — archive/pre-release state.
- Rev 3rd Anniversary Color — commemorative color edition.

## Photo layer
Exterior reference imagery is available through Japanese dealer and workshop listings, especially Noa and Ive. Secondary-market listings can document specimen appearance but are treated as reference trails, not automatic publication permissions.

## Research rules maintained
- Do not turn workshop builds, anniversary colors, or archive states into separate conceptual families without evidence.
- Keep model existence and builder identity separate from deeper technical claims.
- No schematics, PCB diagrams, gutshots, BOMs, or cloning instructions.
- Continue Japanese builder/OEM census before broadening regional scope.
