# 2013-2014 Microboutique Audit 12

## Purpose

This batch extends the builder-first census into the 2013-2014 boutique and mini-pedal corridor while preserving manufacturer, marketed-brand, collaboration, module, and scope-boundary distinctions.

## Greenhouse Effects

Greenhouse has a longer history than the 2014 Self-Titled publicity alone suggests. Effects Database records Roy Zichri's timeline as first working pedal in October 2004, first Goldrive sold in March 2005, first dealer in 2007, studio opening in April 2009, and quitting the day job in October 2009. The indexed catalog includes Goldrive, Goldrive XL, Finger Fuzz, Jurassic Fuzz, Outlier, Roots Fuzz, Double Edged and the 2014 Self-Titled modular analog platform. The Self-Titled can change circuit modules between functions including overdrive, booster and fuzz. It is therefore treated as one modular platform with documented module states rather than a pile of permanently distinct products.

## BearFoot FX

BearFoot's identity must remain separate from BJF Electronics and DonnerBox even though they are directly related. Effects Database identifies BearFoot FX as a collaboration between DonnerBox and BJF Electronics and states that Donner Rusk runs the company in St. Louis. The timeline gives a business opening of February 22, 2011. Dirt records in the indexed catalog include Honey Bee, Honey Beest, Dyna Red, Candy Apple, Arctic White, Gnarwahl and the 2014 Gated Apple, Candiru and Uber Bee states. Gated Apple and Candiru are linked to the Candy Apple family, but remain distinct retail products. Uber Bee is treated as a descendant of the Honey Bee line rather than an unrelated OD.

## Tom's Line Engineering

2013 Musikmesse provides a hard documentary anchor for the compact dirt family. AOD-1 is an overdrive/distortion product published April 11, 2013, while ADT-1 distortion is published the same day. The indexed catalog also lists AMD-1 Metal and ABS-1 Blues among the same Mini-series. Effects Database links the brand to Aroma. The archive therefore preserves Tom's Line Engineering as a searchable marketed/manufacturer relationship and avoids assuming that every later Aroma product is a one-to-one continuation without model-specific evidence.

## Hotone Audio

The 2013 Musikmesse/NAMM corridor provides the early commercial anchor for Hotone's mini-pedal story. The Skyline Fury is published April 10, 2013 as a compact fuzz with an additional push boost function and strong bass suitability. The later Hotone catalog is much broader, including modeling and digital products. Only dirt-relevant products are central here. Current catalog evidence also includes Dirty Boy, but the exact first-sale date remains unresolved and is not assigned a false precision.

Sources:
- https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/hotone
- https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/hotone/skyline/fury

## Mid-Fi Electronics

Mid-Fi requires chronological care because Doug Tuttle's history predates the visible 2010s catalog by years. Effects Database states that pedal building began in 1997, the Random Number Generator appeared in 2001, and profit sales began in 2003. The Demo Tape Fuzz has a clean May 10, 2011 documentary anchor. The indexed catalog also includes Fuzz Wall, Magick I, Overdrive and Peace Gun. These are catalog-enumeration records, not claims that every first-sale date has been recovered.

Sources:
- https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/midfi
- https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/midfi/demotape

## Evidence policy

Documentary publication date is not automatically first sale. NAMM or Musikmesse appearance is not automatically the first production date. Collaboration brands remain distinct from their design partners. Modular platforms are not multiplied into fake model counts. Scope-boundary products remain visible without inflating the central overdrive/distortion/fuzz catalog.
