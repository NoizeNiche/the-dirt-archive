# 2015-2017 Microbuilder Audit 14

## Purpose

This pass extends the builder-first census into the 2015-2017 boutique corridor. The goal is to capture builders and dirt catalogs that are documented strongly enough to become research targets without treating a modern catalog snapshot as proof of lifetime completeness.

## Abominable Electronics

Effects Database currently indexes 17 products under Abominable Electronics. The surviving media trail places Abominable activity in 2013, while the Hail Satan product has 2014 demo evidence and a later 2016 database publication. Hail Satan, Demon Lung, Don't Shred on Me, Oppressive Cult Destroyer, Toke Machine and Yeti Fuzz form a useful early dirt cluster. Hail Satan variants such as Deluxe, Russian and later Hail Santa/kit states are retained as distinct retail or production states. The builder's product description identifies Hail Satan as a modified Big Muff-derived design rather than a period Electro-Harmonix retail product. Evidence is sufficient for catalog enumeration but not for claiming a complete historical release sequence.

## IdiotBox

IdiotBox already has an earlier 2012 footprint in the archive. This pass extends its product map through the 2014-2015 corridor. The -D4 Distortion is documented May 14, 2012; Death Ray Frequency Mangler is documented March 31, 2012; Blower Box is documented with a first limited run of 15; Static Fuzz is documented by 2014 builder video and a March 24, 2015 database publication. The Blower Box is important because its initial run was tied to a specific artist/band association but subsequently proved useful as a broader bass OD/distortion. Do not convert its 15-unit first run into a lifetime production total.

## KMA Audio Machines

Effects Database indexes 24 KMA products. Fuzzly Bear is documented March 26, 2015 and explicitly states that KMA designed, assembled, silkscreened, tested and numbered pedals by hand while using externally manufactured PCBs. That is valuable manufacturing evidence and should remain distinct from later scale or supplier assumptions. Dead Stag is documented September 21, 2016 as a Muff-style fuzz/distortion evolution with active bass/treble and parametric mids. Wurm is documented January 30, 2017 as an HM-2 MIJ-inspired distortion. The later Wurhm limited edition is a distinct 2023-era state and is excluded from this corridor's central count.

## Greer Amplification

Greer is older than this batch and is not a newly founded 2015-2017 builder. The significance here is continuity and catalog development. Ghetto Stomp and Green Giant provide 2005 anchors; Burning Goat is documented July 16, 2007; Lightspeed is documented August 20, 2013; 390 Vintage OD Preamp March 9, 2014; Southland September 24, 2014. The 390 description explicitly says it began from a request for a DOD 250-like sound but that Greer changed elements of the design, so no OEM or identity merger with DOD is warranted. Southland is described as an evolution of the Lightspeed platform. This builder should receive a dedicated full-catalog completion pass later.

## Spruce Effects

Reverb's December 2016 interview with Brian and Katie of Spruce Effects says Brian built his first pedal in 2005 after finding a Supro-inspired overdrive kit. The interview frames the later transition from DIY hobby to a boutique fuzz business. Effects Database currently indexes 14 products, including Salt Water Fuzz, Old Growth Fuzz, Lichen Fuzz, Lichen Fuzz Overgrowth, Giganteum Drive and Treemolo Fuzz. Treemolo Fuzz is documented March 6, 2017 as a Tone Mob/Spruce Effects collaboration combining fuzz and tremolo. Exact first-sale dates for the older Spruce dirt products remain unresolved and should not be backfilled from later product pages.

## Daredevil Pedals

Chicago-based Daredevil now has 31 indexed products. The '77 IC Op-Amp Fuzz is documented January 4, 2014 as a Chicago Music Exchange exclusive and should retain the exclusive/collaboration retail context. Nova Fuzz is documented June 16, 2014 and is explicitly described as an original silicon fuzz rather than a Big Muff recreation. The 2015-2016 cluster includes Model 2015 Chicago Big Fuzz and Northern Creeper; Premium Hand-Crafted Transistor Overdrive is documented February 9, 2017. Later v4/v5 and anniversary states are separate production states and should not overwrite the early records.

## Vein-Tap

Effects Database indexes 15 products. The mapped dirt layer includes Angel Of Blues, Angel of Doom, Angel Of Rock, Holy Ghost Fuzz and Boost, Saint Doom, Dark Triad and Holy Roar. Dark Triad was documented April 6, 2016 with a stated April 24 release target. Holy Roar was documented July 15, 2016. The builder's geography and pre-2015 business history remain insufficiently documented, so Vein-Tap remains DISCOVERED_BASELINE rather than canonical. The catalog is nevertheless substantial enough to justify chronological completion work.

## Cross-builder cautions

1. Database publication date is not automatically first-sale date. The 2014 Hail Satan media trail predates its 2016 Effects Database publication, for example.
2. A limited first run is not a lifetime production quantity. IdiotBox Blower Box is an explicit example.
3. Builder claims about external PCB manufacture should be recorded as manufacturing-process evidence, not interpreted as OEM design outsourcing unless documented.
4. Collaboration and exclusive-retailer states should stay attached to the retail identity. Daredevil's '77 IC Op-Amp Fuzz is the clearest example in this batch.
5. A later revision or anniversary product should not overwrite the original model. KMA Wurhm and Daredevil later version states are intentionally separate.

## Next completion priorities

Highest priority for the next builder-first pass: KMA Audio Machines full dirt catalog chronology; Abominable Electronics complete pre-2017 dirt map; IdiotBox early catalog completion; Spruce Effects first-sale timeline; Daredevil pre-2015 origin and early catalog reconstruction; Vein-Tap identity/geography clarification. Greer should be revisited in a dedicated all-era completion audit rather than treated as complete from this batch.
