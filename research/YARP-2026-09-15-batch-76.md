# YARP 2026-09-15 Batch 76: Rotosound OEM Production States

Rotosound is preserved as a marketed British manufacturer/retailer with a documented OEM history rather than a single in-house fuzz factory. From 1966 into the early 1970s, Rotosound-branded Fuzz Boxes were supplied by Sola Sound across multiple enclosure and Tone Bender production states. Cast-metal blue and gold 1966-era examples, later silver examples, and pressed-steel late-1960s/early-1970s units are separate production states within one marketed Rotosound line.

A separate short-lived relationship began around November 1968 when Jennings supplied a Rotosound-branded Fuzz, with reports of additional Jennings-made Rotosound wah and Growler products. No surviving example currently confirms the latter two, so they remain catalog leads.

A further Rotosound-branded fuzz exists in a Liverpool-built enclosure with uncertain date and relationship. Keep it unresolved rather than assigning it to Zonk or another builder from appearance alone.

Source trail: Fuzzboxes Rotosound Fuzz Box; Fuzzboxes historical timeline.
