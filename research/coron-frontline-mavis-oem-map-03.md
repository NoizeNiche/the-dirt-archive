# Coron / Frontline / Mavis OEM Map 03

## Purpose
Cross-brand reconciliation for the Japanese budget pedal ecosystem around Coron and its marketed/export labels.

## Findings

Effects Database provides unusually explicit related-model groupings that are valuable for the archive's manufacturer-vs-brand model.

### Coron DC-851 Over Drive Box I family
Observed marketed names include:
- Coron DC-851 Over Drive Box I
- Cortez Overdrive
- Frontline Overdrive
- Frontline Overdrive Box I
- JHS OV-210 Overdrive
- Laser Overdrive
- Monacor Overdrive
- Tronix OV-210 Overdrive
- Yu Brother by Coron OV-210 Overdrive

### Coron DC-852 Over Drive II family

### Coron O-300 family
Observed marketed names include:
- Coron O-300 Over Drive
- Daytone O-300 Over Drive
- Frontline Series II Overdrive

### Coron HO-320 family
Observed marketed names include:
- Coron HO-320 Heavy Over Drive
- Daytone HO-320 Heavy Over Drive
- Frontline Series II Heavy Overdrive
- Mavis Over Drive
- Nighthawk NH-340 Electric Tube

## Interpretation
These relationships strongly support a Coron-centered OEM family model for the listed examples. The archive should not convert every marketed name into a separate manufacturer. Each badge should remain searchable as its own retail/marketed node while its OEM relationship points toward Coron where the evidence is explicit.

The Mavis connection is especially notable because separate historical discussion places Mavis in the broader Ishibashi store-brand ecosystem for guitars, while Effects Database independently places Mavis Over Drive in the Coron HO-320 family. These are different questions and should not be merged: Mavis may describe a retail/store brand while Coron describes a pedal manufacturing family.

## Date caution
The related-model mapping is stronger than exact production dating. Do not assign a single universal production range to every family member without period-specific evidence.
