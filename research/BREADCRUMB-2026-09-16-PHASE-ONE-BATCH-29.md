Evidence notes:
- Fieldfare's current catalog explicitly separates overdrive, fuzz and distortion collections and exposes six current analog dirt/bass products.
- Bionique's About page identifies Dan Poirier as builder, dates the commercial project to 2020, and identifies Chimera as the first marketed pedal. Treat Chimera MK3 as a generation/state relationship unless later evidence proves otherwise.
- Animal Factory's current shop exposes discontinued Baron Samedi and Pit Viper V2 alongside current Dirty Mirror, Godeater+ and Ozymandias. Historical articles document Chemical Burn as the first product and Godeater/Chemical Burn generations. Keep Eurorack modules separate from the central pedal inventory.
- OKKO states it has handbuilt pedals since 2003 in Leipzig. Current catalog evidence links Diablo, Diablo Dual, Diablo GH Signature, Dirty Diablo, The Dynamist, Twinsonic MKII, Motorbass, Holy Grit and BFGRMWB. Diablo 20th anniversary is explicitly limited to 50 pieces and should remain an edition relationship rather than a separate family.
