# Breadcrumb Addendum 67

Date: 2026-09-15

## Research batch

### Universal Amplifier Corp. / Astro Amp
- Added a dedicated research ledger for the Universal Amplifier Corp. New York fuzz line.
- Astrotone is treated as a distinct, high-confidence Universal Amplifier Corp. manufactured product.
- Surviving evidence anchors known Astrotone production to 1967-1969; an earlier late-1966 start remains possible but is not promoted to high confidence without a stronger dated source.
- The Astro Amp business address is recorded as 615 West 131st Street, New York City, from period literature as reproduced by specialist research.

### Sam Ash Fuzzz Boxx
- Treated as a Universal Amplifier Corp. house-brand/OEM state, not as a Sam Ash-manufactured pedal.
- Sam Ash's own historical account states that its house-branded Fuzzz Boxx was manufactured by Universal Amplifier Corp. and identifies 1968 as the renaming/introduction period.
- Specialist evidence supports overlapping production of black Astrotone and red Sam Ash versions.

### UMI separation
- UMI / United Musical Industries remains a separate New York builder associated with Richard Soloway and its Buzz Tone / Wa products.
- Geographic proximity and period component similarities are not used as evidence of common manufacture with Universal Amplifier Corp.
