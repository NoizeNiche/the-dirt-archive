# YARP 2026-09-15 Batch 12

## Focus

This batch returns to **JEN Elettronica / JEN elettronica, Pescara, Italy** for a fuller dirt catalog map. The previous pass established JEN as an Italian physical-builder/OEM node. This pass expands the dirt catalog beyond Double Sound and Jumbo Fuzz and separates own-brand products from VOX and private-label OEM states.

## Builder identity

Effects Database identifies Jen Elettronica as a company from Pescara, Italy and currently indexes 13 products under the JEN entity, including **Jen Double Sound, Jen FZ-III Fuzz and Jen Super Double Sound**. ToneHome likewise describes JEN as a major European OEM manufacturer for VOX, Gretsch and other brands, while noting that JEN also marketed its own pedal line. The exact corporate founding year remains inconsistent across specialist sources, so this batch does not force a single foundation date.

## Core JEN dirt catalog

### Jen Double Sound
The JEN-branded Double Sound is a core fuzz-wah product. Effects Database documents it as a late-1960s/early-1970s Italian-made combination effect, and ToneHome identifies a 1973 Pescara specimen. It should be retained as a distinct JEN retail model even though the broader production family also appears under VOX and other market labels.

Status: **INDEXED_RELATIONSHIP / cataloged**.

### Jen Super Double Sound
Effects Database has a dedicated JEN Super Double Sound model page. This is a separate catalog product from the basic Double Sound and carries a distinct fuzz-wah-volume identity. Related product indexing connects the family to Dallas-Arbiter Fuzz Wah Face, Electra 1245, Elka Fuzz Wah, JHS Triofex, Luxor Double Sound, Unicord Wah Fuzz and VOX by JEN Double Sound, but several of those relationship links remain marked as uncertain in Effects Database. They are retained as relationship leads, not universal OEM claims.

Status: **INDEXED_RELATIONSHIP / cataloged**.

### Jen Fuzz FZ-III
ToneHome explicitly identifies the Jen Fuzz FZ-III as made in Pescara in the mid-1970s and says it appeared under several brandings. Effects Database also lists Jen FZ-III as a standalone JEN product. This makes FZ-III a separate JEN retail family from the earlier Tone Bender/Vox lineage.

Status: **INDEXED_RELATIONSHIP / cataloged**.

### FBT Fuzz FF-III
ToneHome documents the FBT Fuzz FF-III as made by JEN Elettronica in Pescara in the mid-1970s and explicitly calls it a differently branded version of the JEN Fuzz FZ-III. This is a useful model-level OEM/rebrand relationship and should not be counted as a second physical-builder lineage.

Status: **INDEXED_RELATIONSHIP / OEM/rebrand**.

### Ricken's Fuzz FR-III
ToneHome documents Ricken's Fuzz FR-III as made by JEN in Pescara, Italy, in the late 1970s. This is another model-level rebrand of JEN's FZ-III family and should remain a separate marketed identity beneath the JEN physical-builder node.

Status: **INDEXED_RELATIONSHIP / OEM/rebrand**.

## VOX by JEN dirt family

### Vox Tone Bender V828 / V8281
Fuzzboxes documents grey V828 production in Italy around 1966-1968 and black V828 successor production around 1968 to the early 1970s. Specialist history distinguishes these Italian-built Vox pedals from British Sola Sound Tone Bender MK1.5 units even though the circuit lineage and enclosure appearance overlap. Fuzzboxes also reports an early EME-to-JEN manufacturing transition inside the Italian production history.

The archive therefore preserves:
- VOX V828 as a retail family node.
- Grey and black production states as historical sub-states.
- EME as an earlier Italian manufacturer/production role where documented.
- JEN as the later physical-builder role.
- Do not collapse the Italian V828 with the British Sola Sound MK1.5.

Effects Database has a separate **Vox by Jen V8281 Tone Bender** model page, while ToneHome's VOX-by-JEN catalog places the V8281 within the 1970s Pescara production corridor.

Status: **INDEXED_RELATIONSHIP / cataloged**, with factory succession preserved as a distinct research issue.

### Vox by JEN Double Sound
Effects Database and specialist histories identify a VOX-branded Double Sound produced in the JEN Pescara factory. Thomas Organ model 9-3701 Cry Baby Stereo Fuzz-Wah and VOX model 9-3700 Stereo Fuzz-Wah are linked as related marketed states. Because some Effects Database links are tentative, each retail model remains distinct and evidence-graded.

Status: **INDEXED_RELATIONSHIP / cataloged**.

## JEN OEM/private-label dirt network

### Elka
Effects Database associates the Elka Fuzz Wah and Luxor Double Sound with the JEN Double Sound family, but marks some specific factory relationships uncertain. Keep Elka as a marketed relationship candidate until model-specific evidence is stronger.

### Luxor
Effects Database explicitly lists Luxor Double Sound as a 1970s Italian-made fuzz wah built by JEN Elettronica in Pescara. This is sufficiently specific for an indexed OEM/rebrand relationship.

### Unicord
Effects Database explicitly documents Unicord Jumbo Fuzz as manufactured by JEN Elettronica in Pescara during the early-to-mid-1970s. The same record links the model to Jen, Dallas Arbiter and Sound City names. This confirms that the JEN Jumbo Fuzz family was materially broader than the own-brand JEN box.

### Dallas Arbiter / Sound City / Sam Ash
Specialist catalog coverage identifies JEN's 1973-75 Jumbo Fuzz family under Dallas Arbiter, Sound City and Sam Ash states. The JEN physical-builder relationship is strong for the family, while individual retail states should stay separate. The historical evidence is particularly useful because the Dallas Arbiter Jumbo Fuzz appeared in a 1973 Sound City price list and catalog.

## Catalog boundary notes

JEN's current Effects Database entity also includes **Jen Cosmic Sound, Jen Crier, Jen Cry Baby, Jen GS-3000 Syntar, Jen Mister Cry Baby, Jen Super Cry Baby, Jen TD-1 Platinum Drive, Jen Variospectra and Jen Volume**. Most of these are non-dirt or dirt-adjacent/unknown from the accessible summaries and should not inflate the central dirt count without model-specific classification. The GS-3000 Syntar is explicitly a guitar synthesizer and remains outside the core dirt total.

## Cross-source interpretation

1. **JEN is a true physical manufacturer/OEM node**, not merely a retail brand. Multiple model pages directly identify JEN Elettronica in Pescara as the maker.
2. **JEN's dirt catalog has at least three major families** in the current census: Double Sound/Super Double Sound fuzz-wah products, FZ-III-era fuzz products, and the Italian VOX V828/V8281 Tone Bender family.
3. **OEM brand duplication is substantial.** FBT, Ricken's, Luxor, Unicord, Dallas Arbiter, Sound City and selected VOX/Thomas Organ states connect to JEN production, but each remains a marketed identity rather than a duplicate builder.
4. **EME and JEN should remain separate historical manufacturer states** for the Italian VOX V828 corridor until corporate succession and exact model dates are fully reconciled. The archive should not backfill JEN onto every early grey V828 simply because JEN later produced the family.
5. **JEN's Jumbo Fuzz is a particularly high-value catalog anchor** because independent specialist sources place it in 1973-75 and document its rebrand network.
