# Catalog Completeness Phase 27

Status: **Discovery / Enumerated**

## Purpose

Continue the breadth-first builder pass. This batch maps builders first and enumerates a practical catalog footprint before returning later for production variations, generations, identification clues, design lineage, OEM relationships and other deeper research.

## Builders added

1. **JHS Pedals** — Josh Scott, Kansas City, USA. Effects Database currently lists 98 products. The catalog includes original designs, reworkings of classic effects, utility products and collaborations with companies including Boss, Electro-Harmonix and Keeley. The archive should later distinguish official JHS families from modifications, collaborations, signatures and collector naming. Sources: Effects Database brand page; official JHS materials.
2. **Walrus Audio** — USA. Effects Database currently lists 69 products. The official site also maintains a discontinued collection and separate current series, making this a strong future family/generation research target. Dirt families include Ages, Eons, Eras, Iron Horse, Jupiter, Kangra, Mayflower, Messner, Projector, RED, Silt, Voyager and Warhorn. Sources: Effects Database brand page; official Walrus Audio catalog.
3. **Old Blood Noise Endeavors** — USA. Effects Database currently lists 49 products. The catalog includes multiple explicit versions such as Black Fountain v2/v3, Dark Star v3, Excess v2 and Fault v2, plus artist/company collaborations and a Utility line. Sources: Effects Database brand page; official OBNE pedal catalog.
4. **Fairfield Circuitry** — Guillaume Fairfield, Hull, Québec, Canada. Effects Database currently lists 15 products. The official site says the company has been experimenting since 2008 and currently lists 10 pedals, while the historical database preserves earlier and discontinued models. This is a useful example of why current and historical snapshots must remain separate. Sources: Effects Database brand page; official Fairfield Circuitry About and Effects Pedals pages.
5. **Thorpy FX** — Adrian Thorpe, U.K. Effects Database currently lists 40 products. The official catalog contains current models plus special editions, and the historical record includes work associated with former Lovetone engineer Dan Coggins and collaborations such as Lazy J and Origin Effects. Dirt-heavy families include Fallout Cloud, Gunshot, Hanami, KUDU, Muffroom Cloud, Peacekeeper, Boneyard, Bunker, Dane, Electric Lightning, Field Marshal, Veteran and Warthog. Sources: Effects Database brand page; official Thorpy FX catalog.
6. **Mr. Black** — Jack Deville, USA. Effects Database currently lists 90 products. Mr. Black was founded as a special division of Jack Deville LTD. in early 2012, with a distinct look/features/manufacturing identity from Jack Deville Electronics. The line contains many historical LTD runs, mini pedals, special editions and dirt models. Sources: Effects Database brand page; official Mr. Black About, All Pedals, Built to Order and Limited & Discontinued pages.

## Discovery record scope

The public discovery batch contains a conservative enumeration of identifiable products from these builder catalogs. It intentionally preserves historically important non-dirt products where the source lineup exposes them, but the primary Dirt Archive navigation remains focused on fuzz, overdrive and distortion.

This batch is **not product verification**. Product names copied from secondary databases are treated as discovery evidence until individually reconciled against manufacturer documentation, period catalogs, interviews, archived pages or other high-confidence sources.

## Archive principle reinforced by Phase 27

**Builder first. Product universe second. Variations third. Relationships fourth.**

The purpose of this pass is to make the archive structurally capable of saying which people or companies made which products before spending disproportionate research effort on a handful of famous boxes.
