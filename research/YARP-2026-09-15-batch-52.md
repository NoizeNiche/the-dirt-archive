# YARP 2026-09-15 Batch 52: Applied Audio Products and the NJ/NY OEM Corridor

## Scope
Builder-first pass for the late-1960s American OEM layer around New Jersey and New York, centered on Applied Audio Products Inc. and its documented or strongly supported marketed-brand relationships.

## Applied Audio Products Inc.

### Identity
Applied Audio Products is best treated as a **New Jersey physical-manufacturer / OEM-builder candidate**, not simply as another marketed fuzz brand. A 1968 Billboard listing names Applied Audio Products, Inc.; a 1968 Cranford directory places Applied Audio Products Inc. at 111 Market St., Kenilworth, New Jersey, as an amplifier manufacturer. Tone Machines Blog's 2025 research additionally connects Applied Audio with a South Plainfield, NJ manufacturing facility and identifies it as the maker behind numerous branded fuzzes and effects.

The present archive status is **evidence-qualified / canonical candidate**, pending a fuller corporate-paper trail and complete catalog audit.

### Personnel / lineage
Tone Machines Blog associates Applied Audio with engineer Jack Gentul, whose earlier work included Sano and Hilgen amplifier manufacturing. The exact legal/corporate formation sequence between Hilgen, Goya work, Applied Audio, and the later Trans-Tek relationship is not yet sufficiently documented to merge those entities. Preserve the personnel/technical lineage as a relationship note, not a proven corporate continuity claim.

## Applied OEM and private-label states

### Goya
Tone Machines Blog's 2025 Applied investigation strongly attributes post-1966 Goya effects to Applied Audio based on repeated physical/build/layout/component correlations. Dirt-bearing states include:

- Goya Panther Fuzz, circa 1967-1968 corridor
- Goya Fury Box, 1968
- Goya Boom Box, 1968, dirt-adjacent
- Goya Power Box, 1969, boost/dirt-adjacent

Treat Goya as the marketed brand and Applied as the physical manufacturer. The evidence is strong but should remain evidence-qualified rather than overstated as a fully documented contract.

### Conrad / David Wexler & Co.
Tone Machines Blog reports a 1969 Conrad effects set built by Applied Audio and distributed by David Wexler & Co. Dirt-bearing models are:

- Conrad Fuzzy Fury
- Conrad Bass Hawk
- Conrad Sky Hawk

The Sky Hawk is catalog-documented in 1969 and 1970, but Tone Machines Blog notes that a physical specimen has not yet been located. Keep that one discovery-only until a surviving pedal or clearer primary source is found.

### Kimberly / LIMMCO transition
Effects Database separates the Kimberly Fuzz Box and Volume Expander into a v1 associated with LIMMCO and a v2 associated with Applied. Tone Machines Blog adds a plausible historical bridge in which LIMMCO's own warehouse-built first version was later outsourced, with Applied Audio fitting the documentary gap before later UMI production. This is a useful working hypothesis, but the exact handoff dates are not yet proven enough to publish as a seamless manufacturing chain.

### Nomad
Applied is strongly associated with Nomad-branded Banshee and Rogue fuzzes. LIMMCO is documented as a distributor in the Nomad/Applied relationship. The later "Nomad Super Fuzz" associated with Mosrite/Fuzzrite is a separate and unresolved identity problem and must not be merged with the Applied/LIMMCO Nomad line solely because the brand name matches.

### Lou Rose
Effects Database documents the Lou Rose Combo-Fuzz as an Applied-manufactured private-label/local-retail product from the Edison, NJ Lou Rose Music Center period beginning after its September 1968 opening. It shares the Applied Fuzz and Treble Booster lineage.

### Guild
Tone Machines Blog identifies Applied as the builder of several Guild effects around 1970, including the FW-3 Fuzz Wah. Only the FW-3 is central dirt; VW-1 Volume Wah, SP-5 Siren & Surf, and Dimensional Reverb System remain boundary records.

### Hohner
Tone Machines Blog reports a later Hohner/Applied manufacturing relationship within the Sound Modifier Series and specifically identifies the Hohner MF-50 Tri-Dirty Booster as part of that story. Keep Hohner as the marketed brand and Applied as the manufacturing relationship. Do not collapse Hohner's entire catalog into Applied.

## Additional Applied-related observations

Tone Machines Blog describes additional Applied-related branding including Vivant, Crown, Scott, and blank/unbranded units. These are not all separate retail models. The archive should treat them as private-label or factory-state records when evidence supports that role, rather than incrementing the builder's model count for every logo variant.

The Applied Rogue enclosure also overlaps visually with the Hohner MF-50 Tri-Dirty Booster enclosure. This establishes a useful physical-production lead, but enclosure reuse alone does not establish identical circuitry, chronology, or OEM ownership.

## LIMMCO and UMI boundary notes

LIMMCO (Long Island Musical Merchandise Co.) should remain a **distributor/house-brand entity**, not automatically a physical factory. Kimberly v1 is LIMMCO-associated while the later Kimberly state is Applied-associated.

United Musical Industries (UMI) is a separate New York manufacturing/brand candidate. Effects Database lists UMI Buzz Tone & Volume Expander, Tone Booster, Treble-Bass Booster & Mute, and Wa Pedal. The Kimberly relationship becomes especially important because Effects Database explicitly links the later Apollo no. 843 and Conrad Buzz Box states to UMI. Full UMI catalog completion should therefore be the next pass, rather than folding UMI into Applied.

## Evidence discipline

- **Verified/evidence-qualified:** Applied Audio Products existed as a New Jersey company/manufacturer; Applied Fuzz and Treble Booster; Applied Big Fuzz as a documented factory product; Applied Rogue; Applied Banshee; several Applied-built Goya, Conrad, Nomad, Lou Rose and Guild states.
- **Discovery/unresolved:** exact Applied corporate formation date; complete legal relationship to Trans-Tek; exact Kimberly handoff chronology; physical production status of Conrad Sky Hawk; full extent of Vivant/Crown/Scott private-label production.
- **Do not merge:** Mosrite/Sierra/Rosac Nomad material with Applied/LIMMCO Nomad without model-level evidence.
