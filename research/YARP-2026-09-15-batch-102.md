# YARP 2026-09-15 Batch 102: Lectrolab Fuzz Buzz Catalog State

## Scope
Builder-first audit of the Lectrolab Fuzz Buzz as an early U.S. regional fuzz product.

## Findings
- Lectrolab is retained as the builder/manufacturer, distinct from Allied Radio/Allied Electronics distribution and advertising.
- Surviving Fuzz Buzz examples carry 1966 component/date evidence, while contemporary Allied advertising places the product in the mid-1960s retail market.
- The product belongs to the same early U.S. fuzz commercialization corridor as Maestro, ACA, Sunn and other regional builders, but no evidence supports merging Lectrolab with those manufacturers.
- The catalog currently lacks evidence for a broader Lectrolab dirt family beyond the Fuzz Buzz. Further period catalog searching is warranted before declaring its dirt catalog complete.
