# YARP 2026-09-15 Batch 78: Sola Sound OEM Network Audit

Sola Sound's 1966-1970s position as both a retail brand and an OEM supplier is reaffirmed. The company supplied fuzz products to Marshall, Vox, Rotosound, Dallas/Rangemaster and Carlsbro, among others. The important archive distinction is that identical or near-identical housings and evolving Tone Bender generations do not make the customer labels independent builders.

The Rangemaster Fuzzbug is a documented 1966 Sola Sound-supplied Dallas product, with early surviving examples showing the MK1.5-era production and later examples reflecting subsequent Sola Sound fuzz development. Rotosound provides a particularly clear multi-generation OEM example, while Carlsbro shows a transition from an early independent Carlsbro product to later Sola Sound supply.

The Archive should therefore model Sola Sound as the physical manufacturer/OEM node, with Tone Bender as its own marketed product family and Marshall, Vox, Rotosound, Dallas/Rangemaster and Carlsbro as separate marketed/OEM relationship states.
