# YARP Breadcrumb 140

## Focus
Continue strict dirt-device qualification and add only builders with documented dirt production or construction.

## Confirmed
- Lectrolab: verified dirt maker via the Fuzz Buzz. Surviving examples carry 1966 component dates, and period Radio-Electronics material documents the Lectrolab/Allied Fuzz Box. Allied remains a marketing/catalog relationship.
- Amplifier Corporation of America (ACA): verified dirt maker via the 1967 Fuzz King / Fuzz Sound, documented in period advertising/catalogues and surviving U.S.-made examples.
- Sunn: verified dirt maker via the Sunn Buzz SB-1G, introduced in the 1966-1967 period and documented in period brochure material and surviving examples.
- Jürgen Peter: verified custom dirt maker added in the prior pass. The Haunted historical record explicitly identifies him as the maker of the homemade fuzz box used on “1-2-5”.

## Dedupe / exclusions
- Knight Electronics KG-389 remains outside the verified builder layer for now because the surviving record establishes a DIY kit product, not necessarily factory-produced assembled pedals.
- Clifford Musical Supplies and Kelly Amplification remain unresolved/non-verified because retail or advertising evidence does not establish physical manufacture.
- Existing Ridinger/fOXX lineage remains one builder identity after stronger Liverpool Fuzz-Tone production evidence. No duplicate Steve Ridinger node created.

## Editorial consequence
The archive now explicitly distinguishes a commercial dirt maker from a documented custom/micro-builder. Both can qualify as historical builder entities, but a one-off build must never be inflated into a fictional product line or production company.
