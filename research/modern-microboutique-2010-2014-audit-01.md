# Modern Micro-Boutique 2010-2014 Audit 01

## Scope
This audit extends the builder-first census into the 2010-2014 boutique/micro-builder layer. The goal is not to declare any builder complete. It records dated origin points, early dirt products, designer/build relationships, and production-state boundaries.

## One Control
One Control's Tomokaz Kawamura timeline states pedal making began March 11, 2010, with the Mosquite Blender first sold in September 2010. The catalog later developed a substantial BJF-designed dirt family, including Baby Blue OD, Baltic Blue Fuzz, Cranberry OverDrive, Golden Acorn Overdrive Special, Honey Bee OD, Persian Green Screamer, Purple Plexifier, Rocket Grey Fuzz, Silver Bee OD and Rebel Red Distortion. The archive keeps One Control as the retail/builder entity and Bjorn Juhl/BJF as the designer relationship rather than creating duplicate builders.
Source: https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/onecontrol

## Dreadbox
Effects Database identifies Dreadbox as an Athens, Greece builder and indexes Disorder as a boutique analog fuzz/filter product. The original Disorder is a distinct three-control state; a later V2 expanded the design with resonant filtering, envelope and CV/modular features. The Dreadbox catalog also contains other fuzz-family products such as Buzz and Jellyfish. Exact first-sale dates for those additional dirt models are not established in this pass.
Sources: https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/dreadbox ; https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/dreadbox/disorder

## EC Pedals / EC Custom Shop
Eldar Cohen's Effects Database timeline says EC Pedals started in late 2010 with a five-pedal line containing two distortion pedals, a bass fuzz, an overdrive and a compressor. The indexed catalog includes Angry Ape, Muff's Head, Michael Benson FuzzDriver, Orphaned Drive, Overlord, Overlord Bass Overdrive, Primestortion, Rusty Cooley Overdrive and Zombie Distortion. Signature products remain separate retail records, while artist names remain relationship metadata.
Source: https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/ecpedals

## Erafuzz
Rex Thornton provides an unusually precise chronology: Buzzaround prototypes January 2010, Erafuzz founded March 2010, Buzzaround May 2010, Grand Dragon July 2010, Italian V October 2010, Civil War April 2011, Revolution May 2011, Sacred Resonance December 2011 and Octavo Stone January 2012. LeBasse and RAM are also indexed. The archive should preserve the prototype/founding/product sequence as separate dates instead of treating March 2010 as the release date of every early product.
Source: https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/erafuzz

## Analog Alien
Jack and Joe Napoli's timeline gives a clean origin sequence: FuzzBubble-45 prototype completed December 2009, official internet release September 2010, first U.S. dealer January 2011, and Alien Twister officially released April 2012. The 2012 interview states the two brothers built pedals in-house, with professionally manufactured circuit boards and enclosures and hand wiring. This makes FuzzBubble-45 a strong example of a small builder moving from studio experimentation to documented commercial production.
Sources: https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/analogalien ; https://www.effectsdatabase.com/interviews/brands/analogalien

## Fromel Electronics
John Fromel's timeline records his first pedal in 2008, expansion from home workshop to garage and then a retail location in 2010, with Leading Tone opening October 1, 2010 as both retail showcase and manufacturing site. The indexed catalog contains D.I.G., Death Is Gain, Phuz Ge, Phuz Si and Velvet Vice among dirt-related models. The archive should not backdate every catalog model to 2008 simply because the first pedal was built then.
Source: https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/fromel

## Ben Adrian Audio
Ben Adrian's timeline records his first production pedals under the Ben Adrian Audio name in 2008, Bunny Drive and Fuzzy Bunny, followed by Kowloon Walled Bunny development in 2009. Commercial pedal production halted in 2011 after he joined Line 6. KWB is a separate distortion product and should not be collapsed into the Bunny Drive/Fuzzy Bunny family merely because of the shared name.
Sources: https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/benadrian ; https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/benadrian/kwb

## RogerThat
Rogier van Oudheusden states RogerThat began with first pedals in late 2009, was formally founded in January 2010 for repairs and custom work, and began its first pedal line with Distortion DX in early 2011. The indexed catalog includes Distortion, Distortion Deluxe, Fuzz, Overdrive and Bass OD/DIS. Repairs/custom work therefore precede the standardized product line in the chronology.
Source: https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/rogerthat

## Moody Sounds
Effects Database records the first Moody Mushroom pedal in March 2009, international interest in assembled pedals by June 2009, and Japanese retail distribution by June 2011. The current indexed nine-product catalog includes Zeygot Fuzz and Hjärt Müller Bass Drive. Exact individual launch dates remain unresolved, so these models are cataloged without fabricated release years.
Source: https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/moodysounds

## JMK Pedals
Jacob Kokura's timeline gives 2006 first pedal modification, 2009 first kit/first sale/custom build, 2011 release of the pedal line and website, and 2012 PCB launch. Effects Database indexes Custom Drive and Neutron Drive as the dirt products. This builder is useful for tracking the transition from hobby work to commercial pedal building and then to component/PCB sales.
Source: https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/jmk

## JerroStomp
Jerry Ernst's timeline records kit and breadboard experimentation from January 2012, Fuzzy Sunday becoming market-ready in June 2012, and development of Hot Fudge and Fuzzo from Fuzzy Sunday modifications beginning in January 2013. The indexed catalog includes Fuzzy Sunday, Hot Fudge Sundae, Fuzzo, Salamander, Fuzz Mace and Uppity Pup. Effects Database records the business ending in 2016. This is exactly the sort of short-lived micro-builder whose small production history should remain in the archive.
Source: https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/jerrostomp

## Purdy Tube Amps-FX
Effects Database identifies Purdy Tube Amps-FX as an Edmonton, Canada company and says the Fuzz was the first pedal built. Its indexed catalog includes Fuzz, Distortion and Overdrive. A firm founding chronology is not established here, so the census retains the builder as a discovered baseline rather than assigning an unsupported date.
Sources: https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/purdy ; https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/purdy/fuzz

## Research conclusions
The 2010-2014 layer shows several recurring historical patterns that matter for the archive:
1. Prototype, founding, first sale, first dealer, and first standardized product line can be different events by several years or months.
2. Designer relationships, especially BJF/One Control, should not be mistaken for separate manufacturer identities.
3. Tiny runs and short-lived businesses are historically significant even when the catalog has only one to six dirt products.
4. Product pages and later indexed snapshots should be used to enumerate the catalog, while builder-provided timelines get priority for chronology.
5. No builder in this batch is marked lifetime-complete. The next pass should reconcile these records with historical catalogs and look for missing 2009-2014 models and regional OEM relationships.
