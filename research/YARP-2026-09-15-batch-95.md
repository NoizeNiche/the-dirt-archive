# YARP 2026-09-15 Batch 95: Pro-Fuzz Distortion Box Discovery Record

## Scope
Discovery-only record for the obscure U.S. Pro-Fuzz Distortion Box referenced in 1966 contemporary print.

## Findings
- Specialist historical research identifies a January 30, 1966 Springfield News-Sun item using the generic term “distortion box.”
- Two months later, a contemporary advertisement is reported for a product explicitly called the “Pro-Fuzz Distortion Box.”
- The available indexed evidence does not yet establish the manufacturer, distributor, city, surviving specimen, or complete product catalog.
- Because the product name is documented before many later American fuzz labels, it is worth retaining as a candidate builder/product lead.
- The archive does not assign Pro-Fuzz to Maestro, ACA, Applied, Lectrolab, UMI, LIMMCO, or any Japanese OEM without model-specific evidence.
