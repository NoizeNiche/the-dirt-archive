# Cusack Music, 2002 onward OEM / builder audit

## Scope
Builder and manufacturing-layer audit for Cusack Music, emphasizing its significance as both a pedal brand and an industry manufacturing partner.

## Identity
Cusack Music's own history dates the company to 2002 in Holland, Michigan. It began as a garage side project and grew into a pedal manufacturing business serving both its own brands and other pedal companies.

## Dirt relevance
Cusack's importance to the archive is twofold:
1. Cusack-branded overdrive/distortion/fuzz products belong in its own product catalog.
2. Its manufacturing work for other pedal companies must be modeled as an OEM/production relationship, not used to reassign those brands to Cusack without product-specific evidence.

## Evidence disposition
- Company founding: PRIMARY, company history.
- Manufacturer/OEM role: PRIMARY, company history explicitly states pedals are built for its own brands and for many pedal companies.
- Lifetime dirt catalog: NOT ENUMERATED in this audit.

## Research queue
1. Enumerate every Cusack-branded OD, distortion and fuzz product.
2. Map known third-party brands manufactured by Cusack, preserving customer brand identity separately.
3. Find dated catalogs and discontinued-product pages to establish the 2002-2010 product spine.
4. Separate prototypes, collaborations and OEM-only production from Cusack retail models.

## Sources
- Cusack Music, About: https://cusackmusic.com/about/

## Archive boundary
Identity, manufacturing relationships, catalog chronology and evidence states only. No schematics, PCB layouts, complete BOMs, gutshot libraries, cloning instructions or circuit reconstruction material.
