# Modern Preservation Track — Batch 16

## Position
2023 census continues. This batch prioritizes high-confidence primary documentation for products that bridge historical circuit lineage and modern production identity.

## Verified primary evidence

### BOSS — BP-1W Booster/Preamp
- Introduced October 26, 2023; availability announced for November 2023.
- Waza Craft compact pedal based around analog preamp circuitry associated with the BOSS CE-1 and Roland RE-201.
- Three modes: CE, RE and NAT clean boost.
- Gain and Level controls; selectable standard/vintage input buffer.
- Made in Japan; buffered bypass.
- Archive identity: treat BP-1W as a distinct modern BOSS product, not as a CE-1 or RE-201 variant. Its historical lineage is functional/preamp ancestry, not product-family identity.
- Status: CATALOGED / VERIFIED.

### Fender — Shields Blender
- Limited-edition signature fuzz launched June 13, 2023 in collaboration with Kevin Shields.
- Fender describes more than four years of co-development and says the design was built around a circuit traced from Shields' personal 1970s Fender Blender.
- Two footswitchable fuzz channels, blend controls, octave functionality and reactive sag circuitry.
- Angled brushed-aluminum chassis with vinyl appointments, four footswitches, top-mounted jacks, true bypass and 9V power.
- Fender's launch documentation explicitly separates the limited-edition collector package from the underlying Shields Blender product identity.
- Archive identity: preserve the 2023 Kevin Shields collaboration as its own product record and retain its 1970s Fender Blender ancestry as lineage, not as a generation of the original Fender Blender.
- Status: CATALOGED / VERIFIED / SIGNATURE EDITION.

## Discovery context
GPX's 2023 overdrive roundup lists 29 selected boost/overdrive products, while its fuzz roundup lists 24 selected fuzzes plus a much larger longlist. The 2023 distortion roundup identifies 18 selected distortion products. These are discovery indexes rather than automatic verification authorities.

## Method note
This batch reinforces three separate identity layers: historical inspiration/ancestry, modern retail product identity, and edition/signature status. A modern product may deliberately reproduce or reinterpret an older circuit without becoming a generation of that older product.
