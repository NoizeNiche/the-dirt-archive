## Editorial consequence
A dirt product must be established before a candidate enters the verified builder layer. Relationship archaeology can remain unresolved without blocking a builder from qualifying, but an unsupported maker claim cannot be promoted merely because a name appears on a vintage pedal.

## Next queue

Do not spend the pass resolving OEM family trees unless doing so exposes a new builder who independently clears the dirt gate.
