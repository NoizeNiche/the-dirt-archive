# The Dirt Archive — Persistent Research Breadcrumb

## Current strategy
Builder enumeration is paused. Work product-by-product through the current catalog. Each research record captures concise history, production chronology, generation/version notes, identification clues, OEM/lineage relationships, sources and an image lead when useful. Discovery records remain separate from canonical history. Image discovery remains separate from republication rights.

## Batch 05 notes
- Deluxe Big Muff Pi: 2014 feature-expanded modern Big Muff branch.
- Double Muff: 2010–2020 compact dual-Muff product derived from the 1969 Muff Fuzz concept.
- Graphic Fuzz: 2008–2023 fuzz/distortion plus six-band EQ.
- Little Big Muff Pi: 2006 compact Big Muff branch.
- Hot Tubes: original 1978 overdrive with a modern reissue branch.

## Batch 06 notes
- SD-1: introduced 1981 as OD-1 successor and remains in production.
- OD-2 TURBO: 1985–1994, major post-OD-1 BOSS overdrive branch with Turbo mode.
- OD-3: later BOSS overdrive branch, introduced in the 1990s and still current.
- RAT 2: introduced 1988, flat enclosure first, sloped enclosure from 2003, offshore production from 2008 according to specialist histories.
- R2DU: 1980s rackmount product containing two RAT channels. Exact release chronology remains less certain, so the archive retains the specialist range rather than presenting an absolute factory date.

## Editorial rule
Document the object, not the recipe. Do not publish gutshots, schematics, PCB diagrams or complete circuit recipes. Do not write 2,000 bespoke essays. Use structured records and reserve deep dives for historically important/version-complex products.
