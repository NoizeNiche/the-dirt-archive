## Batch 07 notes
- **Marshall SupaFuzz:** 1966 origin as a Sola Sound-supplied Marshall product. A short-lived early MKI-derived/filter form is separated from the conventional 1966–1968 Professional MKII-era SupaFuzz. Marshall assumed production around 1968 and continued into the early 1970s. Fuzzboxes documents the enclosure and production split.
- **Sola Sound Tone Bender Professional MKII:** three-transistor Sola Sound model introduced in 1966 and used as the OEM basis for major Vox, Marshall and Rotosound branded products. The canonical Sola Sound record remains separate from those branded variants.
- **Tone Bender MKIII:** major 1968 redesign with a substantially different circuit family and expanded controls. Sola Sound, Vox, Rotosound and Park forms are retained as related but distinct catalog objects.
- **Vox Tone Bender Professional MKII:** British Sola Sound OEM branch supplied to Vox approximately 1967–1968. It is explicitly separated from the later Italian-made Vox Tone Bender V828 lineage.
- **Rotosound Fuzz Box:** Sola Sound-supplied branded family spanning MKII-related and later MKIII-era production. The archive avoids treating all Rotosound fuzz examples as one unchanging specification.

## Editorial rule
Document the object, not the recipe. Do not publish gutshots, schematics, PCB diagrams or complete circuit recipes. Do not write 2,000 bespoke essays. Use structured records and reserve deep dives for historically important/version-complex products.
