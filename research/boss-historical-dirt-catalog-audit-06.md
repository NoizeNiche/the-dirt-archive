# BOSS Historical Dirt Catalog Audit 06

## Purpose

This pass cross-checks the existing BOSS dirt ledger against BOSS's official compact-pedal chronology and recent BOSS historical material. It focuses on discontinued and transitional overdrive, distortion, fuzz, and dirt-relevant compact products rather than deep specimen archaeology.

## Primary chronology anchors

BOSS's official 40th Anniversary Compact Pedals history gives the following production corridors:

- OD-1 Over Drive: 1977-1988 in the official compact-series chronology.
- DS-1 Distortion: 1978-present.
- SD-1 SUPER OverDrive: 1981-present.
- HM-2 Heavy Metal: 1983-1992.
- DF-2 SUPER Feedbacker & Distortion: 1984-1993.
- OD-2 TURBO OverDrive: 1985-1994.
- DS-2 TURBO Distortion: 1987-present.
- MZ-2 Digital Metalizer: 1987-1992.
- OS-2 OverDrive/Distortion: 1990-present.
- MT-2 Metal Zone: 1991-present.
- FZ-2 HYPER Fuzz: 1993-1997.
- SD-2 DUAL Overdrive: 1993-1998.
- HM-3 HYPER Metal: 1993-1999.
- OD-2R TURBO OverDrive: 1994-1999.
- ODB-3 Bass OverDrive: 1994-present.
- BD-2 Blues Driver: 1995-present.
- PW-2 Power Driver: 1996-1997 in the 40th-anniversary chronology.
- XT-2 Xtortion: 1996-1998.
- FZ-3 Fuzz: 1997-1999.
- OD-3 OverDrive: 1997-present.

BOSS's more recent PX-1 historical releases independently describe the DF-2 as a 1984-1994 product, and the May 2026 PX-1 article identifies PW-2 and XT-2 as 1990s discontinued compact dirt models. These discrepancies are retained as source conflicts rather than silently normalized.

## Important corrections / reconciliation notes

### OD-1 dating conflict
The current BOSS 40th-anniversary chronology lists OD-1 as 1977-1988, while other historical references commonly use a shorter original-production window. The archive should preserve the official BOSS chronology as an anchor but keep exact end-date interpretation open until the service-history and catalog evidence are reconciled at year/specimen level.

### DF-2 dating conflict
The official 40th-anniversary chronology lists 1984-1993, while BOSS's newer PX-1 historical article states 1984-1994. Treat 1993/1994 as an unresolved endpoint conflict, not a reason to overwrite either source.

### PW-2 endpoint conflict
The official 40th-anniversary chronology gives PW-2 as 1996-1997. The existing BOSS ledger should therefore not automatically use 1998 as the endpoint without a stronger primary source. The May 2026 BOSS PX-1 article confirms the model as a historical 1990s compact dirt product but does not supersede the compact-history endpoint by itself.

### XT-2 endpoint
The official chronology gives 1996-1998, matching BOSS's May 2026 PX-1 historical article, which describes the XT-2 as a high-gain compact released in 1996 and produced briefly until 1998.

### 1990s fuzz corridor
The official BOSS chronology cleanly establishes FZ-2 (1993-1997) followed by FZ-3 (1997-1999). BOSS's own historical fuzz article places these compact fuzz products within a broader return of fuzz-oriented effects after the earlier compact-era gap.

## Research implications for the Dirt Archive

1. BOSS is not yet a specimen-complete builder, even though the core dirt model universe is substantially enumerated.
2. The strongest remaining BOSS work is reconciling official chronology conflicts with service-history records, period catalogs, and regional catalogs.
3. Discontinued models such as DF-2, FZ-2, HM-3, SD-2, PW-2, XT-2 and FZ-3 deserve separate retail-model records and should not be hidden inside broad family entries.
4. The archive should preserve BOSS's distinction between original models, later Waza Craft editions, anniversary reissues, and collaborations such as TB-2W.
5. No circuit-reconstruction material is required for this catalog stage.
