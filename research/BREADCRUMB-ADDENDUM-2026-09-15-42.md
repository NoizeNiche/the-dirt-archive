## Historical findings

- Hohner's Tri Dirty Booster is documented as USA-made, but the physical factory remains unknown. Similar enclosure evidence points toward a possible shared factory with Rogue Fuzz and possibly Guild/Goya models, but this remains a candidate rather than a promoted attribution.
- Hohner's mid/late-1970s Sound Modifier effects should not be assumed to have been built in Germany simply because Hohner's corporate base was in Trossingen; available evidence instead points toward third-party USA production for at least the Tri Dirty Booster and leaves the broader line unresolved.
- Echolette is best treated as a brand/product lineage with distinct manufacturing eras: early equipment associated with Artur Klemt, later production under Dynacord after the 1969 acquisition. Current evidence does not establish a specific vintage dirt-pedal model strongly enough to attribute to either manufacturer.
- Dynacord remains a German builder candidate for later Echolette production, but no standalone fuzz/overdrive model is promoted from the current evidence set.

## Validation posture

This batch reinforces the rule that a manufacturer's national identity cannot be inherited from the marketed brand. Hohner's USA-made effects and Echolette's Klemt/Dynacord production succession are retained as separate cases.
