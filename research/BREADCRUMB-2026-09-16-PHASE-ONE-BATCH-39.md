# Phase One Batch 39 Breadcrumb

Date: 2026-09-16

## Batch
Batch 39 expands builder-first Phase One coverage into South Korea, concentrating on builders with recoverable first-party or strong catalog evidence and keeping modifications/recreations separate from historical authorship claims.

## Builders added
- Moollon — Seoul, South Korea; boutique builder whose historical record begins with overdrive development in 2002 and whose documented dirt catalog includes overdrive, distortion, fuzz and boost models.
- LiberaToe — Seoul, South Korea; manufacturer-backed dual-channel analog pedal/module platform with software-controlled routing and a substantial gain catalog spanning overdrive, distortion, fuzz and bass drive.
- A3 Stompbox — Seongnam, Gyeonggi-do, South Korea; current boutique builder with ANGEL, AWESOME and GROOVIM product families, plus the bass and GROOVIM 808 variants surfaced through Korean retail/catalog evidence.

## Evidence / boundary notes
- Moollon's accessible catalog history is corroborated by Effects Database, which places completion of the original Moollon Overdrive prototype in 2002, company founding in 2003, and later BufferAge/VintAge development. This batch does not infer missing generation splits from enclosure or series names alone.
- LiberaToe's own product pages document the dual-channel architecture, analog effect processing, software-based signal control, relay true bypass and gain-group/dynamic-group catalogs. Its manufacturer/export listing identifies Dunicof Inc. in Seoul and the Republic of Korea as origin. The builder explicitly describes many gain products as modifications or amplifier-inspired implementations, so those relationships are preserved rather than rewritten as original circuit authorship.
- A3 Stompbox's first-party store confirms the Seongnam, Korea location and current ANGEL, AWESOME and GROOVIM listings. Korean retailer evidence adds AWESOME BASS and GROOVIM 808; GROOVIM 808 is retained as a likely variant until deeper chronology establishes whether it was a separate production family.
- LiberaToe's current all-products page also contains collaboration products associated with Vogart Audio. Those collaboration models are not duplicated in this batch because the archive should distinguish LiberaToe-branded platform products from co-developed external-brand relationships.

## Research discipline
No schematics, PCB layouts, complete BOMs, gutshot libraries, cloning instructions, or circuit-reconstruction material added. Variant, modification, collaboration and signature relationships remain explicit and separate from canonical product-family claims.

## Next action
Continue builder-first Phase One coverage into another underrepresented region, then return to high-value catalog reconciliation for builders whose earliest and latest dirt states are now sufficiently documented to justify chronology work.
