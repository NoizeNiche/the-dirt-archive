# YARP Breadcrumb 126

## Purpose
Model-level attribution audit of the Herald / Olson / Apollo / LRE / Melos / Mica / Monacor Japanese fuzz family, with emphasis on separating confirmed family relationships from unresolved factory identity.

## New research conclusions

- `VAR-113`: The AM-44A family remains an attribution hold. Current evidence supports Japanese manufacture and a broad private-label/product-family relationship, but no source reviewed in this pass identifies the physical Japanese factory with sufficient confidence.
- `VAR-114`: Olson X-78 and X-87 are retained as distinct products. X-78 belongs to the Herald/LRE/Apollo/Melos/Mica/Monacor family, while X-87 has a separate collector-observed relationship to the Ace Tone FM-1 lineage.

## Cross-reference / dedupe decisions

- Do not promote Shin-Ei, Ace Tone, Nisshin Onpa, Teisco, Matsumoku, Fujigen or another Japanese maker to the AM-44A factory role merely from circuit/layout, enclosure, knob or component resemblance.
- Do not collapse Olson X-78 and X-87 into one model or assume they share a factory solely because both carry Olson branding.
- Do not create new manufacturer records for Herald, Apollo, LRE, Melos, Mica or Monacor from the shared-family evidence alone. Their roles remain marketed, distributor, or private-label identities unless independent manufacturing evidence appears.

## Evidence notes

Effects Database identifies the Herald AM-44A as Japanese-made and links it with Apollo No.843, LRE Fuzz Sound, Melos FS-1, Mica FT-12, Monacor FS-1 and Olson X-78. A current Olson X-78 listing likewise describes Japanese OEM production and names Apollo and Melos equivalents. Separate collector evidence associates Olson X-87 with the Ace Tone FM-1 design lineage, but that is insufficient for a factory attribution. Secondary historical evidence also places Herald Electronics in a Chicago sourcing/wholesale relationship connected with Olson Electronics.

## Next queue

Continue the factory hunt through period Japanese exporter/importer records, catalogs and surviving model-specific labels. Priority remains the exact maker behind the AM-44A/X-78 family, followed by the relationship between the X-87/Ace Tone lineage and other early Japanese wedge fuzzes.
