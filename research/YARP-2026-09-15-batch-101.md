# YARP 2026-09-15 Batch 101: S. Hawk Distribution Reconciliation

## Scope
Refine the S. Hawk catalog with manufacturer/distributor separation.

## Findings
- S. Hawk Ltd. is retained as the marketed/manufacturing entity for the Hawk family.
- Hawk I is the principal dirt-bearing unit, with a documented 1973 corridor and multiple functions including overdrive/distortion/fuzz.
- Hawk II Tonal Expander and Hawk III Bass Expander remain separate dirt-adjacent products.
- Historical DiMarzio material documents Kaman distribution during the period; Kaman is not treated as the pedal manufacturer.
- The archive keeps the family distinct from later DiMarzio-branded products.
