# The Dirt Archive — Boutique & Defunct Fuzz Preservation, Phase 1

This phase prioritizes smaller, collectible, discontinued and historically interesting builders whose documentation may become harder to find. The archive is curated rather than exhaustive and should favor models with historical, identification or preservation value.

## D*A*M (Differential Audio Manifestationz)

Effects Database identifies D*A.M. as David Main's South Yorkshire company and notes its fuzz focus and October 25, 2004 timeline milestone. Its catalog is unusually centered on vintage fuzz families, making it a strong archive candidate.

### Important catalog targets
- Fuzz Sound family
- Fuzzrong / FR-70
- Fuzzaround
- Meathead family
- Tone Bender-related models
- Colorsound Fuzz Box reissue
- other limited runs

### Fuzz Sound MkIV
Effects Database preserves a D*A.M. statement that Fuzz Sound numbers #040 onward changed the model marking/typography, removed the "Professional" wording, used the MkIV designation, and changed the LED presentation. The builder also described a particular early run with a documented Mullard transistor combination. The Dirt Archive should publish the production/model-marking and other useful identification distinctions, not a complete component recipe.

### Fuzzrong / FR-70
D*A.M. describes FR-70 as the silicon-enhanced sister to FR-69 and a Rosac Nu Fuzz-derived design with its own tonal changes. Model FR-69 and FR-70 should be related records rather than one generic fuzz entry.

## Blackout Effectors

Effects Database documents Blackout Effectors as founded by Kyle Tompkins in Vancouver in 2008 and moved to Asheville in 2009. Its 17-product catalog includes several important dirt models.

### Fix'D Fuzz
Effects Database explicitly says the Poppy and Ant graphic versions have no functional/circuit differences. These should therefore be **graphic/edition variants**, not new models.

### Fix'D Fuzz Deluxe
A later, more tweakable product building on the earlier Fix'D Fuzz and the version used in Twosome. Treat as its own model.

### Musket Fuzz / Blunderbuss
Musket is a Big-Muff-family expansion. The Blunderbuss is described as an alternate take rather than simply Musket V3, so the archive should preserve that distinction.

### Twosome
A dual-fuzz product combining the Musket Fuzz and Deluxe Fix'D Fuzz. Model it as a combination product rather than as a revision of either component pedal.

### Fuzz Bear / FUBÄR
Fuzz Bear is described as Tone Bender MkII-oriented with added controls. FUBÄR is a later development of that idea. This makes a useful builder-level family history.

## Prescription Electronics

Prescription Electronics is a high-preservation candidate because many surviving records are now secondary documentation.

### Experience
An unusual multi-function product combining fuzz, octave and swell effects. It should be documented as a distinct product, with its unusual control/function layout preserved.

### Yardbox
A Tone Bender-family reproduction. The archived description explicitly distinguishes it from Jumbo, Supa and Vox Tone Bender products.

### Face Lift
Effects Database states that two versions existed using silicon or germanium transistors with octave functionality. Record the **silicon vs. germanium version distinction** because it can be diagnostically useful, without publishing a full parts list.

## Effector 13 / Devi Ever lineage

The Effector 13 period is historically useful because products can cross brand/manufacturer boundaries.

### Truly Beautiful Disaster
Effects Database lists both an original Devi Ever version and an OohLaLa Manufacturing version. That is a textbook example for the archive's builder/brand/production relationship model.

Devi Ever should ultimately have its own builder history record separating original designs, Effector 13-era work, later Devi Ever-branded products and subsequent licensed or reissued products.

## SkinPimp

SkinPimp is a strong discontinued-boutique preservation candidate.

### Face Plant
Effects Database describes the Face Plant as a two-germanium-transistor fuzz. Record the model and external identification characteristics; do not turn the entry into a circuit build guide.

### Buzzaround
SkinPimp's Buzzaround is explicitly linked by Effects Database to the much larger family of later Buzzaround reproductions and reinterpretations. This will be useful to the archive's genealogy system.

### SkinBender
Effects Database lists at least SkinBender Mk I and Mk II. These should be separate named generation/model records.

## Lovetone

Effects Database records Lovetone's founding by Dan Coggins and Vlad Naslas in 1995 and preserves a useful designer timeline. The timeline dates the Meatball prototype to 1994, founding/early designs to 1995, and RingStinger and Cheese Source to 1998. This is high-value preservation material.

### Dirt-related products
- Big Cheese
- Brown Source
- Cheese Source
- Ring Stinger

The Cheese Source is particularly useful to document because Lovetone's timeline describes it as a repackaging/combination of the Brown Source overdrive and Big Cheese fuzz. An Effects Database auction record also preserves a low serial-number Cheese Source specimen and an early finish variant, demonstrating the value of specimen-level provenance.

## Smallsound/Bigsound

A high-preservation candidate because the builder's catalog is now largely historical and modern references often rely on older product pages.

### Team Awesome! Fuzzmachine
Effects Database documents it as a fuzz with a clean blend and low-end retention, particularly aimed at bassists but useful for guitar. Its wider catalog, production periods and Mini/Team Awesome relationships deserve a dedicated future pass.

## Builder-selection rule reinforced

A small builder should not be included simply because a database lists it. High priority goes to builders where one or more of these apply:
- discontinued or disappearing documentation
- explicit revisions/editions
- important vintage-family reinterpretations
- licensing/OEM relationships
- collector confusion around versions
- sparse but recoverable historical evidence
