# Phase One Batch 38 Breadcrumb

Date: 2026-09-16

## Batch
Batch 38 expands builder-first Phase One coverage into New Zealand, South Africa, Canada and Estonia.

## Builders added
- G2D Guitar Pedals — Auckland, New Zealand; historical pedal lineage begins with the Thunder Chief in 1984 and continues through Standard, Cream-Tone, Classic, Custom and Morpheus.
- CraigAmps — Pietermaritzburg, South Africa; official site confirms a production range spanning overdrive, fuzz and distortion, while surviving community catalog evidence recovers named historical models and a custom dual-drive build.
- Bennett Custom Audio — St. John's, Newfoundland and Labrador, Canada; current small-batch catalog combines house/original designs with explicitly identified recreations and combination pedals.
- Spicetone Music Technology — Tallinn, Estonia; 6APPEAL is a digitally controlled but fully analog hexaphonic dirt processor with per-string overdrive, crunch and fuzz processing.

## Evidence / boundary notes
- G2D's own history is unusually useful for chronology: Thunder Chief is tied to 1984, Standard to 2000, Cream-Tone and Classic to 2002, and Morpheus to 2003. The Cream-Tone/Custom OD support pages preserve serial-range internal switch changes, so those are future generation/state targets rather than automatically separate models.
- CraigAmps is kept INDEXED_PARTIAL because the current official site confirms the builder and broad dirt scope, but the accessible historical product naming trail is fragmented across older community documentation. Custom-build evidence is not promoted to core catalog status.
- Bennett Custom Audio explicitly identifies Sea Eagle as a house distortion and separately describes Zendrive, Centaur, and OD808 + Boost as recreations or combinations. The archive preserves those relationship roles rather than treating inspiration as authorship of the original historical product.
- Spicetone's 6APPEAL remains a central dirt record despite its unusual architecture. The builder describes it as a pure-analog signal path with digital control, six per-string channels plus one mono channel, and three distortion types: overdrive, crunch and fuzz.

Continue builder-first Phase One coverage. Prioritize uncovered geographic regions and overlooked micro-builders, while periodically auditing existing candidate catalogs for omissions before promoting any builder to catalog-complete.
