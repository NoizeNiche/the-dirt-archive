## Historical product boundaries
- Naked Machine is preserved as a Camuro product with a documented Hiroshi Aoki signature/development relationship. The 2014 production record describes a 10-unit limited edition.
- Caboolture is preserved as a productized design that originated from a special-order project.
- BACI's compact and later large-enclosure forms are treated as product-generation/enclosure history, not separate builders.
- Custom commissions, modifications and one-off artist projects are not automatically promoted into the core catalog.

## Relationship boundary
Hiroshi Aoki is documented as an artist/collaborator on Naked Machine and related development work. He is not treated as a separate pedal builder entity on this evidence.

## Photo policy
Official exterior product imagery is identified as a future photo-provenance source. Final archival image-credit treatment remains a separate pass.

## Research exclusions
Do not publish gutshots, schematics, PCB diagrams, complete BOMs, cloning instructions, or circuit reconstruction material. Internal construction details encountered during research remain outside the public archive scope.

## Next direction
Continue the builder-first Japanese census. Prioritize independent small builders and unresolved OEM/manufacturer identities, then return to photo provenance and deeper product archaeology after builder coverage is strengthened.
