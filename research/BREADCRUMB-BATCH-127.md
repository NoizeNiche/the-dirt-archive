# The Dirt Archive — Phase One Breadcrumb — Batch 127

Date: 2026-09-16

## Governing priority

**Builder → Pedals → Photos → Information on the pedal**

This pass stays on the builder/OEM layer and adds only dirt products supported by surviving manufacturer-chain evidence.

## Builder covered

### HILLWOOD / FIRSTMAN

- Promoted as a **CANONICAL_OEM_MANUFACTURER** rather than a normal consumer-facing pedal brand.
- Historical sources connect Hillwood and Firstman in the same Japanese manufacturing lineage; one recent historical synthesis explicitly describes Hillwood as manufacturer, Firstman as brand/export identity, and Multivox as U.S. label/rebranding context.
- The archive therefore keeps the manufacturer relationship intact and does not create duplicate builder entities for Hillwood and Firstman without stronger documentary evidence.

## Pedal layer

Central dirt records added:

- Multivox Big Jam SE-8 Distortion
- Multivox Big Jam SE-13 Space Driver

SE-8 is documented as a Japanese-made distortion produced by Firstman for Nihon-Hammond and distributed in the United States under Multivox. SE-13 is documented as the Big Jam Space Driver, a boost/overdrive product in the same historical series.

The wider Big Jam catalog contains many non-dirt effects, but this batch deliberately did not import those into the dirt inventory.

## Photo layer

The SE-8 has a strong surviving exterior/specimen trail through Effects Database and vintage-market documentation. SE-13 likewise has surviving exterior/specimen records. These sources are evidence leads for future archive photography credits, not assumed republication rights.

## Information layer

Only identity, category, historical production relationship, and basic product framing were captured. No schematics, PCB diagrams, gutshots, BOMs, or cloning instructions were added.

## Identity / provenance rule reinforced

For vintage Japanese/OEM material, the archive should distinguish:

**physical manufacturer → contracting/marketing company → export/distribution brand → retail model**

The Big Jam family is a useful test case because the pedal may be encountered under Multivox branding even though surviving evidence identifies Firstman as the Japanese OEM and Nihon-Hammond as the contracting company.
