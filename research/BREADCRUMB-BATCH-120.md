## Information on the pedal

- KATANASOUND's history is now explicitly connected to Mitsuteru Takagi / Takagi Manufacturing, with F.C.G.R.'s 2021 termination notice providing the strongest surviving dated corporate relationship record used in this pass. The notice states the KATANASOUND pedal brand ended in 2021 while Takagi Manufacturing continued effects and amplifier work.
- SHIGEMORI's Stone Drive Custom is a core handmade Japanese overdrive family; Ver.3 is preserved as a documented production state with a rear volume-boost function.
- The ASAMA, SEIWA, BIAS and STORM records preserve early Japanese FET-overdrive retail identities associated with the Coron family without collapsing every marketed name into a duplicate builder.
- LUSTER PROSERIES DST-5 is retained cautiously because surviving documentation links it to multiple 1980s Japanese retail names but does not establish a sufficiently secure physical OEM attribution for this pass.

## Normalization decisions

- No separate conceptual models were created solely for cosmetic colorways or limited presentation states.
- SHIGEMORI generation/special records remain subordinate to the main Stone Drive Custom / Ruby Stone concepts.
- Coron-associated marketed names remain searchable relationships and are not falsely promoted to independent physical manufacturers.
- LUSTER PROSERIES is explicitly unresolved rather than merged with Coron or another factory on visual similarity alone.
- No schematics, PCB layouts, gutshots, complete BOMs or cloning instructions are collected.
