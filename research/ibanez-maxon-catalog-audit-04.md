# Ibanez / Maxon Catalog Audit 04

## Scope
Breadth-first catalog reconciliation for the 1980-1986 transition from the late compact/9-series era into Master Series and Power/10 Series. This pass prioritizes period catalog evidence and keeps Ibanez retail identity separate from physical-manufacturer/OEM attribution.

## Period anchors

### 1980-1982: late compact / transition corridor
Ibanez's 1980 catalog indexes the **OD850 Overdrive**, **OD855 Overdrive II**, **TS808 Tube Screamer**, **Double Sound (84)** and **Blubber (93)** alongside other compact effects. The 1981 catalog still lists **OD855**, **Double Sound**, and **Blubber**. The 1982 Sound Effects catalog lists **Double Sound**, **SD9 Sonic Distortion**, and **TS9 Tube Screamer**, showing the 9-series transition while legacy products remained in the catalog corridor.

### 1983-1984: 9-series dirt baseline
The 1983 Sound Effects catalog explicitly lists **TS9 Tube Screamer**, **SD9 Sonic Distortion**, and **84 Double Sound**. The 1984 Sound Effects catalog continues **TS9**, **SD9**, and adds **SM9 Super Metal** as a separately documented distortion product. The archive should therefore treat SM9 as a period-primary anchor rather than a merely secondary nine-series candidate.

### 1984-1985: ST-9 / Master Series transition
Historical Tube Screamer sources place the rare **ST-9 Super Tube Screamer** in the 1983-1984 corridor, with very limited European distribution, followed by the **STL Super Tube** in 1985's Master Series. Ibanez/Hoshino's catalog archive separately preserves 1985 Master Series compact-effects literature. These are related historical products but must remain separate retail identities and production states.

### 1986: Power Series / 10 Series launch
Ibanez's 1986 archive contains dedicated Power Series effects brochures, dealer sheets, and a pocket manual. The Power/10 Series dirt baseline includes **TS10 Tube Screamer Classic**, **DS10 Distortion Charger**, **FC10 Fat Cat**, **MS10 Metal Charger**, **LM7 L.A. Metal**, **MT10 Mostortion**, **SS10 Session-Man**, **SS20 Session-Man II**, and **WF10 Wah Fuzz**. Current historical indexes describe the 10 Series as a 1986-1989 Ibanez family and identify Maxon as the manufacturer for the line, but this OEM attribution should remain source-qualified at model level rather than automatically inherited by every Ibanez-branded product.

## Evidence notes

- The official Ibanez catalog support archive confirms surviving 1983, 1984, 1985, and 1986 effects literature, including the 1984 Sound Effects catalog, 1985 Master Series compact-effects brochure, and 1986 Power Series effects materials.
- The 1983 and 1984 catalog indexes directly enumerate TS9, SD9, and SM9. This strengthens the project's primary-source status for the core 9-series dirt catalog.
- Premier Guitar's Tube Screamer history places ST9 in the 1983-1984 corridor and STL in 1985, with TS10 returning in the 1986 Power/10 Series.
- Effects Database identifies the broader 10-series as an Ibanez family produced by Maxon during its historical production period. Because that is a secondary database-level attribution, the archive should use it as relationship evidence while still seeking model-specific factory documentation where available.
- Do not publish circuit-level reconstruction details even when secondary sources discuss them. This audit is limited to product identity, catalog appearance, chronology, retail naming, manufacturing relationship, and historical production-state questions.
