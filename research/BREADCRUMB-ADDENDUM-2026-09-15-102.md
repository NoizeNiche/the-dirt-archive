## Catalog completion targets

### Herald Electronics

The Herald Electronics dirt catalog currently resolves to the AM-44A Fuzz Master. Herald is historically important as a marketed/imported identity connected to Japanese OEM manufacture rather than as a standalone Japanese factory. Related marketed names in the same product corridor include Apollo, LRE, Melos, Mica, Monacor and Olson. This is an OEM reconciliation target and a strong future specimen-comparison candidate.

### Top Gear

The initial dirt catalog now records Top Gear Fuzz and Fuzz Sustain. Period 1975 evidence confirms the Fuzz Sustain in the UK market, while specialist research distinguishes a three-knob state and a two-knob state associated with the Guild Grizzly relationship. Top Gear's Series-70 enclosure transition is also flagged for future cross-model examination.

### Lemon StudioSound

The catalog baseline now records the rare Zuzz Thainer and the Sound Stretcher A from the surviving Karlsruhe catalog trail. Zuzz Thainer is a dirt-relevant hybrid combining fuzz, sustain and noise-gate functions. The wider Lemon catalog will remain separated from the central dirt count until each product's category is resolved.
