# Fulltone 1991-2005 Catalog Audit 03

## Scope
This pass focuses on the early Fulltone commercial corridor, especially 1993-2005, and separates early production states from later continuations, reissues, and Custom Shop products. The archive treats prototype, first-sale, and later standardized production as distinct evidence states where documentation supports it.

## Early company chronology

Fulltone's current company history states that Michael Fuller founded Fulltone in California in 1993, while surviving historical references commonly describe the earliest pedals as hand-built garage-era products. The current Fulltone store is a continuation/revival-era source and should not be used by itself to back-project every current model into the 1990s. citeturn671428search7turn671428search9

## Early Full-Drive chronology

The Full-Drive 2 is one of Fulltone's most important early products. Secondary historical coverage places the product's 1996 release in the early boutique-overdrive wave, while JHS provides unusually valuable specimen-level evidence for a February 1996 garage/label-maker build, serial 171. This should be modeled as an **early production/prototype-state record**, not as proof that every later FD2 looked or was made exactly the same way. citeturn357569search5turn357569search60

The later Fulltone Full-Drive 3 manual adds another useful historical anchor: a limited 20th-anniversary orange FD-3 run was 5,000 units, sold out within seven months, and was explicitly intended to capture the character of the circa-1996 orange FD2 while expanding the feature set. That is a modern anniversary state referencing an older production era, not the original 1996 retail model itself. citeturn357569search10

## Early fuzz chronology

The '70 is especially well documented. Fulltone's own material describes the pedal as the third or fourth Fulltone release and dates its debut to 1994. It also states that approximately 4,400 units were sold during the following 15 years. The later 70-BC is a smaller reintroduced form with BC108-C silicon devices and quiet switching, so it belongs in a later production state rather than being counted as a new unrelated family. citeturn357569search4turn357569search7

The original Soul-Bender is likewise a major chronology anchor. Fulltone's manual says the original came out in 1995, was produced until approximately 2006, and sold about 3,107 units. Fulltone's current SB-2 is explicitly a reissue/continuation following the original run. Preserve those as original-production and revival/reissue states. citeturn357569search6

## Newly separated later dirt products in the early-catalog corridor

### Distortion Pro (DP-1)
Distortion Pro is not part of the 1990s starter trio. Effects Database published the model page in 2005 and identifies it as a Fulltone distortion pedal with a six-control format. Other period user reports place it in active use by 2002-2005, supporting an early-2000s commercial corridor. Archive treatment: **VERIFIED_ANCHOR, 2000s**, separate from OCD and Full-Drive families. citeturn966103search1turn966103search4

### Bass-Drive
Bass-Drive is a distinct bass overdrive product. Effects Database published the model page in 2006, and Reverb identifies a later Bass-Drive MOSFET state. Because the earliest confirmed evidence in this pass is 2006, retain Bass-Drive as a **2000s boundary/verified product** rather than back-dating it into the 1990s without stronger paper evidence. citeturn966103search3turn966103search6

## OCD boundary

The OCD must be kept outside the earliest 1993-1995 group unless stronger period evidence is found. Current Fulltone material distinguishes the OCD product line, its V1.4 Custom Shop recreation, OCD-Germanium, and current OCDv2. Fulltone's present store also preserves a New Old Stock V1.4 listing. The archive therefore treats **OCD as a distinct later Fulltone family requiring its own production chronology audit**, rather than assuming its modern version history represents the first years of Fulltone. citeturn671428search0turn671428search3

## Scope exclusions / boundaries

- Bass-Drive is kept in the 2000s boundary unless earlier primary catalog evidence appears.
- Distortion Pro is included as a dirt product, but its exact first-sale year remains less certain than the 1994-1996 fuzz/Full-Drive anchors.
- Current Fulltone USA storefront categories are evidence for present product identity, not automatic evidence for historical first-release dates. citeturn671428search0turn671428search5
- Circuit schematics, PCB layouts, BOMs, gutshots, and reconstruction details are intentionally excluded.

## Research conclusion

The 1991-2005 Fulltone corridor now has a stronger historical spine: **'70 (1994) → Soul-Bender (1995) → Full-Drive 2 (1996)**, followed by the broader 2000s expansion including Distortion Pro and the later Bass-Drive boundary. The remaining high-value work is a dedicated OCD chronology and surviving Fulltone print-catalog/dealer-sheet search, not indiscriminate addition of modern Fulltone products to the 1990s catalog.
