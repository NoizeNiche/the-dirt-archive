# Big John Effects Historical Catalog Audit 01

## Research status
DISCOVERY_BASELINE

## Identity
Big John Music / Big John Effects was founded in Belgium in 2006 by bassist Jan Detremerie. Stijn Vandebril later helped build pedals, and Arnoud Faber took over the workshop in the Netherlands in 2013.

## Dirt chronology
- 2006: first pedal designs by Jan Detremerie.
- 2007: Hairy Balls v1, fuzz-only version.
- 2008: Hairy Balls v2, adding boost/gate/fuzz functions.
- 2008: Granny Puker, overdrive plus octave-down functionality.
- 2009: Obama Wah, filter/distortion design.
- 2012: Pocket Rocket, vintage-tube-style overdrive.
- 2014: Dirty Sandwich, boost/drive plus looper functionality.

## Catalog interpretation
The builder's own history provides unusually clean model-by-model dates. Hairy Balls v1 should remain a distinct early product state from Hairy Balls v2. Granny Puker should remain a separate model despite overlapping gain functionality. Obama Wah is retained as a hybrid/filter-plus-distortion product rather than forced into a pure distortion category.

Later Lil' John, BassDrive, AmpAmp, LoLife and TrebleJuice entries belong to the continuing company history but should not be backdated into the 2006-2009 founding corridor.
