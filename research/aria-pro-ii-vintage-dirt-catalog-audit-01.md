# Aria Pro II Vintage Dirt Catalog Audit 01

## Scope
Period-focused audit of Aria / Aria Pro II dirt products in the late-1970s through 1990s transition.

## Findings

The surviving indexed catalog shows Aria Pro II using compact Japanese-made effect lines across multiple generations. The 1985 XX-5 platform included at least **DT-5 Distortion** and **MP-5 Metal Pedal**, establishing a distinct mid-1980s Aria dirt family. The late-1980s **APE-4** and **AME-1** multi-effect units then carried combined overdrive/distortion sections. A later **AOD-1** appears in the 1990s secondary-market record and should remain a separate product-generation candidate until a dated period catalog is found.

## OEM / production caution

Matsumoku is strongly associated with Aria Pro II guitars and period Aria manufacturing, but the evidence available here does **not** justify assigning every Aria Pro II pedal to Matsumoku without a pedal-specific source. Keep Aria / Aria Pro II as the marketed product entity and physical manufacturer attribution conservative until specific pedal documentation confirms it.

The Matsumoku-era catalog archive is still useful as a chronological source because it preserves Aria dealer and product catalogs through the 1980s and up to the plant's closure period.
