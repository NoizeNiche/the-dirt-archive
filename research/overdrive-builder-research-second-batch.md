# Overdrive Builder Research: Second Batch

Source catalog: user-supplied Effects Database Overdrive export.

Research rule: Builder → Pedals → Photos → Information on the pedal.

## Maxon Effects
**Catalog entries:** 29

Maxon effects are associated with Nisshin Onpa, the Japanese manufacturer behind many Ibanez-era effects. Historical references identify Nisshin Onpa as an OEM and effects manufacturer, including the Tube Screamer lineage.

Primary source: https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Maxon_Effects

## JHS Pedals
**Catalog entries:** 27

JHS's official history traces the company to Josh Scott repairing a Boss BD-2 in 2007, followed by JHS Mods, original builds, and the move from small workshop production to a larger Kansas City operation. The history documents the Morning Glory as an early original design and the 2015 Steak & Eggs collaboration with Robert Keeley.

Primary source: https://jhspedals.info/pages/history

## Jetter Gear
**Catalog entries:** 26

Jetter Gear maintains a current store with a smaller active lineup; the supplied archive contains a larger historical Overdrive footprint, so discontinued models and production history should be treated as a core research target rather than inferred from today's catalog.

Primary source: https://www.jettergear.com/

## Barber Electronics
**Catalog entries:** 25

Barber states that its products are primarily unique designs rather than direct copies, are hand made, and emphasize clarity, note definition, tonal range, textures and flexibility. Its current material discusses the OD-1 lineage in the context of later designs.

Primary source: https://www.barberelectronics.com/about

## SMB Effects
**Catalog entries:** 25

SMB's current Russian-language price list identifies Sergei Bogucharov's effects catalog, including 100% Drive, Bass Master, Bass T, Deity Blues, Mustang and other effects. JHS Pedals has also documented the SMB 100% Drive and identified SMB as a Russian company.

Primary source: https://www.smb-effects.ru/prices.html

## Tech 21 NYC
**Catalog entries:** 25

Tech 21 states it was established in 1989 by Andrew Barta and introduced the SansAmp, describing it as an analog tube-amp emulator that helped pioneer direct recording. The company publishes a historical timeline including SansAmp Classic (1989), Bass Driver (1992), GT2 (1993) and PSA (1993).

Primary source: https://www.tech21nyc.com/about-us/

## Menatone
**Catalog entries:** 24

Menatone's official site describes its effects as handmade since 1996 and lists current standard, PTP/Deluxe, mini and other pedal families. Current standard-line examples include The King, Top Boost in a Can, Wreck'T, Fish Factory, MenaWatt, PiG and The Dirty Blonde.

Primary source: https://www.menatone.com/

## WEEHBO Guitar Products
**Catalog entries:** 24

WEEHBO is based in Hannover, Germany. Its current catalog includes multiple overdrive designs, including Plexface, Tackleberry, Dumbledore, Plexdrive, Helldrive and JTM Drive. The Tackleberry manual documents a Class-A JFET preamp with selectable clipping options and internal voltage/clipping controls.

Primary sources:
- https://shop.weehbo.com/collections/overdrive
- https://www.weehbo.com/fileadmin/manuals/tackleberry.pdf
- https://www.weehbo.com/contact/

## Catalinbread
**Catalog entries:** 23

Catalinbread says it was born in 2003 on Nic Harris's kitchen table in the Pacific Northwest, with the Super Chili Picoso as the first Catalinbread-branded pedal. The company moved to Portland in 2006 and states it manufactures pedals by hand.

Primary source: https://catalinbread.com/pages/about

## Electro-Harmonix
**Catalog entries:** 23

Electro-Harmonix states that Mike Matthews founded the company in New York City in 1968 and that it was among the first companies to offer affordable state-of-the-art guitar effects pedals. Its current catalog includes dedicated Overdrive and combined Overdrive/Fuzz/Distortion categories.

Primary sources:
- https://shop.ehx.com/about/
- https://shop.ehx.com/category/guitar-effects-pedals-overdrive-fuzz-distortion/
