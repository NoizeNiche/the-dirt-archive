# YARP Breadcrumb 119

## Purpose
Japanese manufacturer/OEM pass focused on separating Nisshin Onpa's physical manufacturing role from Maxon and export/retail identities such as Ibanez, Aria and Greco.

## New builder record

- `BUILDER-116`: Nisshin Onpa Co., Ltd.
- Status: DISCOVERED / HIGH confidence.
- Preserve Nisshin Onpa as the physical manufacturer; Maxon remains its marketed brand. Ibanez, Aria, Greco and other labels remain separate marketed/OEM identities unless product-specific evidence proves otherwise.

## Cross-reference / dedupe decisions

- Do not create a new builder for Maxon merely because Maxon appears as the front-panel brand. Maxon is documented as Nisshin Onpa's effects brand.
- Do not collapse Ibanez into Nisshin Onpa. The archive should record Hoshino/Ibanez as the marketed/export identity and Nisshin Onpa as manufacturer where supported.
- Pre-Maxon Japanese wedge-fuzz attribution remains a research trail. Recent scholarship argues some of these families may have been manufactured by Nisshin Onpa, but model-by-model attribution is not yet uniformly proven, so those relationships remain flagged rather than promoted.

## Evidence notes

A Premier Guitar interview with Nisshin Onpa designer Susumu Tamura states that Nisshin Onpa initially manufactured pedals as OEM projects and began using the Maxon brand from 1971. Kit Rae documents the Maxon OD-801 and Ibanez OD-850 relationship and identifies Nisshin Onpa as manufacturer. ToneHome documents pre-Maxon OEM fuzz and fuzz-wah production for multiple Japanese labels.
