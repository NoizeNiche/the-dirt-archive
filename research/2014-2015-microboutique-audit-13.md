# 2014-2015 Microboutique Audit 13

## Purpose
This batch extends the builder-first census into the 2014-2015 boutique expansion using dated weekly indexes, builder-origin material, and surviving product pages. The goal is to identify canonical or discovery-stage builder entities and enumerate documented dirt products without treating publication dates as automatic first-sale dates.

## Key findings

### Anasounds
Anasounds provides one of the clearest origin chronologies in the current sweep. Its company history separates 2013 aesthetic/design development from 2014 company formation and first engineering work. The same history identifies Feed Me, Centaur and Phase Lag among the first products and says the 2014 overdrive study led to Cerberus, described as the first true engineering/original creation. Feed Me is later documented as the first fuzz in the range. Keep the legal/company start, first prototype work, and later retail/product dates distinct. 

Source trail: https://anasounds.com/about-us/tone-quest/ ; https://anasounds.com/products/origins-pedals/feed-me/ ; https://anasounds.com/produit/cerberus/

### SviSound
The 2014 weekly archive provides a clean documentary cluster for OverZoid, MetalZoid and Si Fuzz. Current maker documentation shows the OverZoid family has multiple later variants, so oz01 and later forms should remain linked but not merged into one timeless specimen. The current OverZoid+ page explicitly calls itself a next version of OverZoid oz01. This is a good future specimen/generation target.

Source trail: https://www.effectsdatabase.com/updates/weekly/20140811 ; https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/svisound/overzoid ; https://svisound.com/product/overzoid/

### Tone Hungry Effects
The 2014 documentary cluster gives three dirt-adjacent products: Hunger Bender, Hot Molasses and Treble Master. Hunger Bender is specifically described as a modified MKIII Tone Bender lineage with tested NOS germanium transistors. The archive records that inspiration as a design relationship, not a manufacturer identity claim. Geography and full chronology remain unresolved.

Source trail: https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/tonehungry/hungerbender ; https://www.effectsdatabase.com/updates/weekly/20140331

### Pepper Pedals
The August 2014 weekly archive is the current clean anchor for Ancho, Chimayo and Pimiento. Ancho is indexed as IC Muff, while Chimayo is an Overdrive 250 and Pimiento an overdrive. Because the surviving record is largely index-based, these remain discovery-stage builder/catalog entries pending deeper primary-source identity work.

Source trail: https://www.effectsdatabase.com/updates/weekly/20140804

### Black Cat
Monster K-Fuzz is especially useful because its documentation says the Custom K-Fuzz became part of the regular line and then received additional voicing, clipping-diode and input-gain functions. The added features and cosmetic changes make this an explicit product-state boundary. The broader Black Cat catalog includes older D&S, Super Fuzz, N-Fuzz, OD-1, OD-Boost and related models, but this batch does not claim a lifetime-complete catalog.

Source trail: https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/blackcat/kfuzz/monster ; https://www.talkbass.com/threads/cat-got-your-tone-%E2%80%94-feline-effects-file.1258583/

### VauxFlores
Gold Standard is one of the better-defined collaboration records in this batch. The builder describes it as a collaboration with artist/composer Liz Meredith and says the circuit was designed by Travis Johns; the product was based on Number 24. Pedal of the Day documents Baltimore hand-built small-batch production and notes that the prototype was completed before the move back to the United States. Preserve the Costa Rica/Baltimore corridor as production history rather than collapsing it into a single location.

Source trail: https://vauxflores.com/electronics/gold-standard/ ; https://www.pedal-of-the-day.com/2014/05/11/vaux-flores-gold-standard-fuzz/

### Walker A&E
The August 2014 weekly archive gives an unusually dense six-product contemporary cluster: Black Guts, Cold Shot, Crotaline, Firebird, King Bee and Selfburner. Because the source does not establish a complete builder history, these remain discovery-stage catalog records. Do not infer circuit family relationships from names alone.

Source trail: https://www.effectsdatabase.com/updates/weekly/20140804

### Aural Dream
Five dirt products appear together in the March 2014 weekly archive: Bold Distortion, Compelling Drive, Intense Distortion, Purely Fuzz and Warm Drive. This is enough to establish a meaningful catalog cluster, but not enough to establish manufacturing identity, geography, or exact first-sale dates.

Source trail: https://www.effectsdatabase.com/updates/weekly/20140331

### Animal Factory Amplification
Chemical Burn is documented in the January 2014 new-brand archive and again in the January 2015 index as an octave-fuzz product. The repeat appearance is useful because it separates a dated documentary anchor from later catalog persistence. Builder origin still needs deeper work.

Source trail: https://www.effectsdatabase.com/updates/weekly/20140120 ; https://www.effectsdatabase.com/blog?page=65

### 2015 fringe builders
Clarenzio, Dolphin's Sound and L0/Rez are retained as discovery-stage entities because the 2015 weekly archive exposes multiple products under each name. Clarenzio is represented by Brine, Sandroun, Spenador and Stremne; Dolphin's Sound by David, Nettuno and later Purgatorio; L0/Rez by Cement Lunch and Mona Lisa. Names are preserved cautiously where accent/spelling normalization may otherwise distort the historical record.

Source trail: https://www.effectsdatabase.com/blog?page=64 ; https://www.effectsdatabase.com/blog?page=60

## Duplicate and boundary rules used
- Anasounds Feed Me's January 2015 Effects Database publication is treated as documentary confirmation of the same product, not a second model.
- Ge Boost is retained as a gain-adjacent boundary product rather than automatically inflating the central dirt count.
- Phase Lag is a catalog boundary because it is modulation rather than overdrive, distortion, or fuzz.
- Treble Master is likewise retained as a boundary/boost product.
- Later SviSound OverZoid variants remain related product states, not automatic duplicates.
- Gold Standard and Number 24 remain distinct because the builder explicitly describes Gold Standard as based on Number 24 but adapted into a separate collaboration/product.

## Research quality notes
Effects Database weekly archives are valuable chronological evidence, especially where small builders have disappeared or modern sites no longer preserve launch material. They are documentary anchors, not universal proof of first sale. Builder-origin pages are preferred for legal/entity chronology and production-role distinctions. Where only secondary index evidence survives, the archive uses discovery or baseline statuses instead of upgrading to canonical manufacture claims.
