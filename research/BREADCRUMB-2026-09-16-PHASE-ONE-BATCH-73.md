## Evidence notes
Limetone Audio has unusually clean builder provenance. Its first-party site identifies Yujin Imanishi as Sound Designer and Builder and describes the brand as a Japanese handmade effects company. The current catalog exposes JACKAL, JACKAL MIDNIGHT, JACKAL MIDNIGHT ADD CBF and fuzzhog proto, with JACKAL explicitly documented as a high-gain drive designed to retain picking nuance. A 2026 Guitar Magazine interview further places Imanishi and JACKAL in the current Japanese boutique builder scene.

A.Y.A Tokyo Japan has a useful historical-to-revival chain. Japanese product reporting documents DRIVESTA as a handmade OD/distortion with three drive modes, while current dealer catalogs list BM-FUZZ, FrontGate BassDrive and Super Atomic Distortion. A 2024 Japanese report states that A.Y.A returned to the pedal scene in a new Tokyo Funabori base using the same builder as its Nakano-era products, so the archive preserves one continuous builder lineage rather than inventing a successor brand.

Shin's Music is strongly attributable to Shinichi Suzuki through the official site and current Japanese retailer records. OD-X, Mk-2 Over Drive and DUMBLOIDSP are documented drive products, and the official site currently exposes FUNN FACE FUZZ. Relationship modeling matters here: the builder's MIGHTY BOOST page explicitly says production was outsourced to Dunlop in the USA, so that product should remain an OEM relationship record rather than evidence that all Shin's Music products were Japanese-made.

Nature Sound / Zahnrad should remain a connected lineage. Japanese retailer evidence identifies Kaoru Kunda as the builder behind nature sound and describes Zahnrad as the new brand by that builder. The Zahnrad product trail includes DYNAMIC DRIVER, FLUX and INVERSION, while Japanese reporting also preserves Nature Sound FUZZ and JanRay as historical catalog evidence. Keep public-facing brand names separate in object records, tied through the shared builder relationship.

LiberaToe is a Korean manufacturer and engineering project established in 2015. Its first-party history says the team spent years developing analog dual-channel effects and exhibited prototypes at NAMM in 2017 and 2019. The current Gain Group provides named overdrive/distortion products including Luther, Camus, Fire Sword, Wrecker Valve, Blue Crow, Darwin, Iron Rod and Lost Gerbil. Handmade models were discontinued in 2019 as production series replaced them, which is useful for generation and production-state tracking.

FengzaiPedals is a small Chinese independent builder based in Shijiazhuang. The builder's current Reverb profile explicitly identifies the maker as a professional Chinese pedal designer/researcher/handcraft builder. Current listings document the Fuzz Face Childlike Hand-Painted Series, Zeppelin Bendable and BASS LOVER OD. Custom transistor choices, hand-painted graphics and second-generation enclosure states should be captured as configuration/specimen data rather than separate builders.

## Boundary / scope notes
- Limetone irodori remains a booster/utility lineage; KarDiaN's irodori onboard is a documented collaboration and is not a new Limetone successor builder.
- A.Y.A R-COMP is intentionally omitted from central dirt inventory because it is a compressor, while FrontGate BassDrive remains because it is explicitly an overdrive.
- Shin's Music OEM products should retain manufacturer-of-record relationships and should not be used as proof of domestic production by default.
- Nature Sound and Zahnrad are public-facing identities under one documented builder relationship; do not double-count the lineage in headline builder statistics.
- LiberaToe modules and platform shells are system relationships, not separate manufacturers or independent dirt concepts.
- Fengzai custom transistor options and artwork are specimen/configuration states, not automatically separate model variants.
- No schematics, PCB layouts, gutshots, cloning instructions, or complete BOM data are published.
