# Sound Lab / Japanese OEM Dirt Catalog Audit 01

## Status
Catalog relationship audit. Manufacturer identity is treated separately from marketed OEM brands.

## Core family
Effects Database documents the Sound Lab MUM-7 Ultra Metal as a Japanese-made 1980s distortion pedal sold under multiple OEM names including Aria AUM-1, Ken Multi MUM-7, Axe and Monarch. The source describes the family as sharing an enclosure design and product identity across these labels.

## Related records
- Sound Lab MUM-7 Ultra Metal
- Aria AUM-1 Ultra Metal
- Ken Multi MUM-7 Ultra Metal
- Axe MUM-7 Ultra Metal
- Monarch MUM-7 / related Ultra Metal family record
- IMG Stage Line / Ken Multi / Monarch related Metal Master records remain adjacent rather than automatically merged.

## Archive treatment
The archive should model Sound Lab as the researched product-family node while preserving Aria, Ken Multi, Axe and Monarch as marketed-brand/OEM labels. This is exactly the type of 1980s Japanese export structure that can otherwise produce false builder counts.

## Source
Effects Database, Sound Lab MUM-7: https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/soundlab/mum7

## Research caution
Effects Database pages can contain technical descriptions beyond what the archive needs. Public Dirt Archive records should retain historical identity, chronology, marketed names and evidence status, while omitting circuit reconstruction, complete component information and cloning instructions.
