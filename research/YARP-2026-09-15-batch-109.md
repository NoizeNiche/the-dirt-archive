# YARP 2026-09-15 Batch 109: Knight Electronics KG-389 Scope Boundary

## Identity
Knight Electronics Inc. was an Allied Radio subsidiary specializing in electronic kits. Its KG-389 Fuzz Box was sold through Allied Radio mail-order catalogs from 1967 through 1972.

## Catalog treatment
The KG-389 is a documented fuzz product but a **build-it-yourself kit**, not evidence of a factory-assembled commercial pedal. It therefore belongs in the Dirt Archive's scope-boundary layer rather than the central manufactured-pedal count.

## Historical value
The record is useful because it demonstrates how quickly fuzz technology moved from commercial branded pedals into the mail-order electronics-kit market. It also creates a useful bridge between Allied's distribution infrastructure and the wider American fuzz boom.

## Evidence state
**Kit existence:** documentary/index evidence qualified.
**Commercial assembled production:** not established.
**Builder/factory attribution:** not applicable as a conventional assembled-pedal manufacturer.
**Catalog completeness:** Knight dirt-kit catalog remains incomplete pending additional Allied/Knight period catalogs.
