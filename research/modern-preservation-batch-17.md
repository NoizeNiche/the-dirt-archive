# Modern Preservation Track — Batch 17

## Position
2023 census continues. This batch prioritizes products where contemporary documentation gives unusually useful identification evidence: limited editions, compound products, and products whose physical or functional identity changed during the year.

## Verified / strong contemporary evidence

### Great Eastern FX Co. — Focus Fuzz
- Launched March 2023.
- Original fuzz design combining NOS germanium and modern silicon transistors.
- Limited to 250 units because of transistor availability.
- Three external controls: Focus, Fuzz and Level.
- The manufacturer describes the Fuzz control as simultaneously affecting gain and bias, while Focus changes the character and frequency emphasis.
- Archive rule: the 250-unit production cap is an EDITION / production-run attribute, not automatically a generation.
- Status: CATALOGED / VERIFIED / LIMITED PRODUCTION.

### Messiah Guitars — Billy Transparent Overdrive
- Released January 2023 as Messiah Guitars' first overdrive pedal.
- Four controls: Boost, Low, High and Level, plus a three-position Mode switch.
- Mode positions documented as silicon, germanium and an experimental fuzz setting.
- Standard silver version and limited Brown Sugar edition documented separately.
- Brown Sugar edition uses NOS germanium diodes in its germanium mode.
- Archive rule: Brown Sugar is an EDITION distinction unless later evidence demonstrates a broader production-generation change.
- Status: CATALOGED / VERIFIED / EDITION MAPPED.

### CopperSound — Kingpin
- Introduced July 2023.
- Mini two-stage FET overdrive with germanium clipping diodes.
- Three levels of overdrive and a Clip toggle.
- Mechanical true bypass; 9–18V operation; compact mini enclosure.
- Archive rule: germanium is part of the clipping implementation, not evidence that this is a vintage germanium-transistor fuzz lineage.
- Status: CATALOGED / VERIFIED.

### Flower Pedals — Lupine Dual Fuzz
- 2023 Lupine expanded the earlier Purple Lupine octave-up pedal into a dual-fuzz product.
- Purple side retains the octave-up lineage and gains an external Gain control; Cream side adds a JFET fuzz with Input, Bias and Output.
- Two independent sides can be combined; extra input/output jacks are part of the product identity.
- Startup switching provides a single-fuzz operating mode.
- Archive rule: preserve the earlier Purple Lupine as a predecessor product and the 2023 dual Lupine as a distinct compound product, rather than calling it merely a cosmetic V2.
- Status: CATALOGED / VERIFIED / PRODUCT-LINEAGE MAPPED.

### Bardic Audio Devices — 2 Stroke Beaver / Shelob's Lair
- 2023 reporting identifies the two names as the same circuit with different artwork.
- Product lineage is an extended-range Rat-style distortion/fuzz design.
- Archive rule: preserve both names as artwork / market identities pointing to one underlying product identity unless builder evidence later demonstrates separate manufacturing or circuit generations.
- Status: CATALOGED / VARIANT MAPPED.

## Method note
Contemporary roundup lists are discovery indexes only. Promote products into the verified catalog only when builder identity, product identity, date, category and variant status can be supported independently. Limited editions and artwork-only variants remain separate attributes from generations unless evidence demonstrates a manufacturing or functional change.
