# HomeBrew Electronics (H.B.E.) — 2001–2005 Dirt Catalog Audit

## Status
**CANONICAL BUILDER ORIGIN / EARLY BOUTIQUE CATALOG ANCHOR**.

## Origin
Joel Weaver developed HomeBrew Electronics after years of electronics repair and amp-tech work. Vintage Guitar reports that Weaver showed his pedals at the **2002 Winter NAMM** show and received enough orders that the venture became full-time.

## Early dirt spine
- **Power Screamer** — H.B.E.'s first pedal according to Joel Weaver; core early overdrive product.
- **Power Screamer/Compressor** — later combined/expanded product record; keep distinct from the earliest Power Screamer until exact release sequencing is established.

## Builder character
H.B.E. described its early manufacturing as hand-soldered and wired, with enclosures powder-coated and screen-printed in-house. This is useful provenance for the small-shop production context, but does not establish that every later H.B.E. model used identical processes.

## Evidence
- Vintage Guitar, HomeBrew Electronics Power Screamer/Compressor Retro: https://www.vintageguitar.com/3167/homebrew-electronics-power-screamercompressor-retro/

## Archive caution
The archive should use the 2002 NAMM appearance as a strong public-commercial anchor, while keeping the exact H.B.E. founding date and full 2001–2005 dirt catalog separate until primary catalog evidence establishes them. Later H.B.E. products should not be backdated into the opening catalog automatically.
