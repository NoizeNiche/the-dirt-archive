# Subdecay — 1999–2005 Dirt Catalog Audit

## Status
**CANONICAL BUILDER ORIGIN / EARLY PRODUCT TIMELINE VERIFIED**.

## Origin timeline
Brian Marshall's timeline places his first pedal build in 1999/2000, with the exact date uncertain. His first original design, the **Stupid Box**, was completed in summer 2003. By autumn 2003 he was building pedals for Brad Fee's online store.

## Early dirt spine
- **Stupid Box** — summer 2003; first original Subdecay design. Preserve as the foundational early product even though it is not necessarily the company's best-known later dirt model.
- **Liquid Sunshine** — introduced in 2004; class-A overdrive and one of the earliest clearly documented Subdecay dirt products.
- **F-Bomb / later Liquid Sunshine family records** — retain as later-generation/discontinued variants until exact transition evidence is established.

## Product-family caution
Subdecay's later catalog became substantially broader. The archive should not project the modern 40+ product catalog backward into 2003–2005. Early records should be tied to dated evidence.
