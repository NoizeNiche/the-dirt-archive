# Prescription Electronics, 1996-2000 product audit

Research status: HISTORICAL_BASELINE

## Identity
Prescription Electronics was founded in 1993 by Jack Brossart in Portland, Oregon. The company had a long interruption, later resumed production, and therefore original-era and revival-era products must be separated.

## Catalog
Effects Database currently indexes 14 products: C.O.B. Clean Octave Blend, Depth Charge, Dual-Tone, Experience, Face Lift, Frantic Fuzz, Germ, Outbox, Rx Overdriver, Static, Supernatural, Throb Tremolo, Vibe-Unit and Yardbox.

## 1993-1995 launch spine
- Experience: introduced in 1993.
- Yardbox: introduced in 1994.
- Rx Overdriver: early original catalog member following Yardbox.
- C.O.B. Clean Octave Blend: early original catalog member.

## 1996-2000 expansion
The surviving secondary catalog record identifies 2000 as the introduction of Germ, an original gain product that expanded the line beyond the first vintage-recreation wave. Later products such as Depth Charge, Dual-Tone, Face Lift and Outbox are retained in the broader company catalog but should not be assigned pre-2000 dates without stronger dated evidence.

## Catalog interpretation
Prescription Electronics is important because its early range mixes historically styled recreations with original boutique products. The archive should therefore record lineage intent and marketed description while avoiding any claim that all products are simply clones or all products are wholly original.
