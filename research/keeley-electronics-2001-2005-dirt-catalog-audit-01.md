# Keeley Electronics — 2001–2005 Dirt Catalog Audit

## Status
**CANONICAL BUILDER ORIGIN / EARLY MOD-TO-BOUTIQUE TRANSITION**. Initial product universe is split between modification work and Keeley-branded pedals.

## Origin
Keeley Electronics was founded by Robert Keeley in 2001 in Oklahoma City. Early work included modifications to existing Boss and Ibanez pedals before the company became known for its own branded products.

## Early dirt record
- **Keeley Compressor** — foundational Keeley-branded product; compressor, not dirt, but important to the company chronology.
- **Keeley modded Boss DS-1 / Ultra Mod** — early modification lineage; preserve as a modification/service offering rather than a new manufacturer model.
- **Super Phat Mod / Blues Driver modification lineage** — early Keeley overdrive-mod lineage; preserve separately from later Keeley retail designs.

## Archive interpretation
This is a critical example of why the archive distinguishes a builder's modification era from an original retail catalog. A modified Boss pedal remains a Boss model with a Keeley modification role unless Keeley marketed a distinct retail product as its own model.

## Archive caution
A complete 2001–2005 Keeley branded dirt catalog still requires primary advertisements/catalogs to establish exact release years, names, and transition points. This audit intentionally does not promote every later Keeley overdrive to the early-period catalog.
