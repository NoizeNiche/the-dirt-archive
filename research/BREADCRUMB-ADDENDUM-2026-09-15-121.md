# YARP Breadcrumb 121

## Purpose
Japanese OEM and American export-brand research pass, with emphasis on Walco-Linck, Keio Giken and Shin-Ei private-label relationships.

## New research record

- `BUILDER-119`: Walco-Linck Corporation / Walco Electronics, preserved as a U.S. company/marketed electronics node with Japanese-made effects and unresolved physical OEM factory.

## Cross-reference / dedupe decisions

- Do not promote Walco-Linck to a Japanese pedal factory. Period material identifies Walco Electronics as a division of Walco-Linck Corporation in Clifton, New Jersey, while surviving effects are documented as made in Japan.
- Do not create a new builder for Keio Giken separate from Korg. The Synthesizer Traveler F-1 is a Keio Giken-era product and belongs to the historical Korg corporate lineage.
- National FW939 is treated as a Shin-Ei OEM/marketed-brand relationship, not a new National manufacturer.

## Evidence notes

Effects Database lists seven Walco effects, including the Fuzz Tone Generator, Bass-Treble Expander, Signal Booster and Sound-Go-Round. Period trade material identifies Walco Electronics as a division of Walco-Linck Corporation in Clifton, New Jersey. Surviving examples are Japanese-made, so the U.S. company and physical Japanese factory should remain separate until the factory is identified.

Tokyo Guitar Center documentation describes the Keio Giken Synthesizer Traveler F-1 as a circa-1972 pedal manufactured by Keio Giken, now Korg. A Japanese dealer/history source documents the National FW939 as a 1971 Shin-Ei-made wah/fuzz, adding another concrete private-label example to the Shin-Ei manufacturer map.
