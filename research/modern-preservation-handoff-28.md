## Important research notes
- Unsound Circuitry Kato is a dual parallel fuzz/glitch product. Contemporary 2023 coverage distinguishes the two paths, the Amok/Drone glitch modes and the Kill/Gain/Sens/Volume/Drift/Depth control family. Current later V2 evidence must remain separate from the 2023 product identity.
- Redbeard's Hairy Squid was documented by the builder as its first dedicated fuzz, with Silicone, LED and Germanium clipping modes in a fully analogue true-bypass pedal hand built in the UK.
- Stone Deaf Rise & Shine launched in December 2023 after roughly three years of development. The audio path is analogue, while control logic adds multiple modes and the Octapulse tremolo/vibrato system. Preserve the digital-control layer without misclassifying the pedal as a digital effect.
- Hello Sailor / rnrmf Xenomorph is a valuable relationship record. Joe Halliday's larger custom build predates the standardised Australian production collaboration. Matched CV7351/NKT germanium transistors, Xen transformer/unbuffering and Morph control are useful external identifiers.
- Bispell Gleam V2 is kept at DISCOVERY because 2023 coverage gives the V2 label and rich control/voicing information but stronger primary documentation is still needed to establish the exact generation boundary.
- Palmer Solarist is kept at DISCOVERY. 2023 coverage documents both the very feature-dense V2 and the streamlined Green Edition. These should not collapse into a single Fuzz Face record.
- Reeves Facet IZ is distinct from the Facet FF. The IZ uses C1016Y yellow-dot silicon transistors and adds Tone to the standard Fuzz Face control footprint; hand screen printing and Northamptonshire workshop production add specimen-level evidence.
- JAM Octaurus ltd is a clean Custom Shop edition boundary. Its early-1960s NOS silicon transistor sourcing, turret point-to-point construction, olive-drab suede enclosure and sold-out limited status distinguish it from later standard Octaurus and Octaurus NOS products.

## Data rule reinforced
The Archive treats collaboration, custom run, edition, generation, production method and later rework as separate dimensions. Famous-circuit lineage is evidence about design family, not a substitute for product identity. Discovery coverage can establish that a product existed without establishing its first production date.
