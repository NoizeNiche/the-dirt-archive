## Builder work
### FACTORY OF THE APES
- Added Factory of the Apes as a **CANONICAL** one-person Japanese builder based on first-party writing by Saruwatari, who states that he lives on the Oshika Peninsula in Miyagi and handles effects design, manufacturing, design and sales himself.
- Preserved the builder's explicit fuzz-centered identity.
- Mapped the documented dirt family before attempting deeper pedal archaeology:
  - DOSUKOI
  - 轟哭之歪 (GOU-KOKU NO HIZUMI)
  - 不屈毛羽立 (FUKUTSU-KEBADACHI)
  - AMA
  - JUNAN
  - GOKUSOTSU (獄卒)
  - Deer Attack
- DOSUKOI remains CENTRAL_PENDING because the current shop's surviving listing trail is weaker than the evidence for later models, although the builder explicitly identifies it as the basis of later designs.
- Retained mandala as RELATIONSHIP_ONLY because its primary identity is a parametric EQ / preamp utility with an integrated fuzz channel.

## Pedal lineage notes
- The builder's shop states that 轟哭之歪 is derived from DOSUKOI with an additional gain stage and a Muff-style tone section.
- The builder's shop states that 不屈毛羽立 is a self-modified one-knob version of 轟哭之歪.
- GOKUSOTSU is a later fuzz/overdrive design centered on blending fuzz with the dry signal.
- Deer Attack is a current CMOS fuzz with a two-knob external interface.

## Photo evidence

## Archive discipline
- No schematics, PCB layouts, gutshots, BOMs or cloning instructions were added.
- Internal controls are only summarized when necessary to identify a public product and are not used to reconstruct its circuit.
- Related variants remain attached to their parent platform rather than creating artificial families.
