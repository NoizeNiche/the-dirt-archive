# Dirty Boy Pedals, early-2000s origin audit

## Scope
Builder identity and early dirt-catalog baseline. No circuit reconstruction.

## Identity
Current reporting describes Dirty Boy as a boutique pedal brand that first appeared in the early 2000s and was formed by Alex Saraceno, father of guitarist Blues Saraceno. The surviving 2025 historical reporting is useful as a modern corroborating source, but the exact founding year remains unresolved.

## Dirt catalog signal
The brand is strongly associated with fuzz/gain products and later artist-driven boutique production. Early product chronology should be separated from the present corporate era rather than backfilled from the current catalog.

## Evidence disposition
- Builder identity: SUPPORTED by current historical reporting.
- Early-2000s existence: SUPPORTED as a broad period, not yet month/year exact.
- Complete early catalog: NOT ENUMERATED.
- Present catalog: NOT used as proof of early-period availability.
