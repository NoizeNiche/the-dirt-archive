# Catalog Completeness Phase 67

## Scope

This pass deepens the builder census around four historically useful catalog families: BJF Electronics (BJFE), Stomp Under Foot, Fuhrmann, and Guild.

## BJF Electronics (BJFE)

Effects Database currently indexes 30 products. Björn Juhl's timeline places company founding at July 1, 1999 and first Baby Blue Overdrive deliveries at December 13, 2000. The catalog includes fuzz, overdrive, distortion, compressor, boost, EQ, vibe and filter products. Priority product-level research: Baby Blue, Honey Bee, Dyna Red, Bone Bender MkI/MkII, Arctic White, Face 109, Fuzz 68, Little Green Wonder, Model D/G/H/R, and the compressor family. Record version changes separately from retailer/reissue relationships.

## Stomp Under Foot

Effects Database currently indexes 30 products and identifies Matt Pasquerella as the builder in Haverhill, Massachusetts. His timeline dates the organized garage production phase to summer 2006. The catalog is unusually useful for preservation because many products explicitly document source inspirations and production distinctions. Priority research families include Dirty Rooster, Shag MkII/MkIII, Red Menace, Silver Foxx, Dirt Preacher, Mountain Goat, Rhinosaur, Green Russian, Ram's Head/Violet, Elk Big Mag Sustainar, and limited/exclusive collaborations.

Variation notes worth preserving at high level:
- Dirty Rooster: Jumbo Tonebender-inspired; documented use of BC184 transistors in a period description.
- Shag MkIII: 1968 Sola Sound Tonebender MkIII-inspired; documented NOS germanium transistor sourcing and limited production tied to parts availability.
- Dirt Preacher: documented hybrid silicon/germanium design and specific transistor selection, without publishing a circuit recipe.
- Silver Foxx: 1971 Foxx Tone Machine-inspired, with NOS germanium diodes and distinctive crushed-velvet finish.
- Red Menace: documented 3-knob and 4-knob versions, with the latter adding mids control.
- Mountain Goat: limited retailer-exclusive modified Muff-family pedal.
- Fuzz Honey: 2015 Blackbook Guitars limited run of 30, based on Steve Turner's Civil War Big Muff.

## Fuhrmann

Effects Database currently indexes 39 products. Current multimedia shows active Brazilian production in January 2026, including a new mini-pedal line. Preserve Fuhrmann as a living catalog, not a closed historical manufacturer. The current indexed lineup spans fuzz, overdrive, distortion, boost, modulation, delay, chorus, phaser, tremolo, envelope filter, utility and power products. Priority dirt families: Big Fuzz, Classic Fuzz, Bass Fuzz, Classic 800, Lead Drive, Metal Drive, High Gain, Hot Rod, Punch Box, Power Drive, Rocking Tube, Super Metal Drive and Tube Drive. Future passes should distinguish One series, mini series, older production, and signature/artist editions where evidence supports it.

## Guild

Effects Database currently indexes 15 Guild products. The important archival rule is that Guild is a brand/OEM lineage rather than a single manufacturer. The catalog includes Applied Audio, Aria, fOXX and Top Gear-built products. The Foxey Lady is the highest-priority family because versions were built by different manufacturers including Mosrite, AUL Instruments and Electro-Harmonix. Preserve each OEM lineage as a relationship rather than flattening it into one Guild model.

Guild products indexed include: Applied Fuzz Box, Applied FW-3 Distortion Wah Volume, Applied SP-5 Siren and Surf & Turf, Applied Stinger, Applied VW-1 Volume Wah, Aria MM-500 Tweedy Bird, fOXX VW-400 Wah & Volume, Top Gear Grizzly Fuzz, Top Gear Roadrunner, Brian May Power Booster, Brian May Treble Booster, Foxey Lady, RV-1 Rotoverb, Timbre Generator and Trioct.

## Research rule

Do not publish gutshots, schematics, PCB layouts, complete BOMs or cloning instructions. Component distinctions may be recorded when they materially establish a production/version difference, at a high historical level.
