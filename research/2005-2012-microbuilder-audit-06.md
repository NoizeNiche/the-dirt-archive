# 2005-2012 Microbuilder Audit 06

## Scope

This pass targets overlooked boutique-era builders whose surviving documentary trail reaches into the 2005-2012 transition. The emphasis is catalog breadth, chronology, builder identity, marketed-brand status, and documented product relationships rather than circuit reconstruction.

## Pigtronix

Pigtronix is a high-priority builder because the dirt story begins before its later compact format products. The OFO Disnortion is documented by Effects Database in March 2006 and is attributed to Howard Davis. It combines independent overdrive, fuzz, and octave functions, making it an important early example of a boutique multi-dirt architecture. Effects Database also describes the early OFO corridor as 2006-2008, while later documentation states the broader Disnortion remained in production through 2015. These statements are preserved as different historical scopes rather than collapsed into one date range.

PolySaturator is anchored to NAMM 2008 and should remain a separate commercial model. FAT Drive appears at NAMM 2012. The 2012 NOVA Disnortion is a Custom Shop / exclusivity state and must not replace the core Disnortion record. Philosopher's Rock and FAT Philosopher are hybrid compressor/dirt records and are retained because distortion is an explicit product function.

## Orion Effekte

Orion is unusually valuable for the German boutique record because Jan van Triest provides a direct timeline: first friend-built Fuzz Face clones in summer 2004, first Big Muff modification in autumn 2005, and official Orion Effekte start in winter 2005. The current Effects Database index contains 30 products, demonstrating that the brand should be treated as a catalog-completion target rather than as a single-pedal entry.

Motor Fuzz is documented in March 2006, Gold Fuzz in the same period, and the Gold Fuzz is explicitly limited to 100 units. The later catalog links Gold Fuzz to the Fabrik Fuzz naming state. De Luxe Motor Fuzz is documented in February 2010. Mufflon appears in October 2010 as a Ram's Head Big Muff-inspired design with three Orion operating modes. Lemming Fuzz is the first documented signature product in summer 2011, associated with Locas in Love. Russlon Fuzz is documented in November 2012 and draws on the green Russian Big Muff tradition.

Important historical rule: Orion's Big Muff-derived products remain Orion products. The source model is a design reference, not a manufacturer identity merge with Electro-Harmonix or Sovtek.

## DingoTone / LauriePedals relationship

DingoTone is a strong Canadian micro-builder lead because the chronology spans two names. Contemporary historical evidence says the mission began in 2007, while the DingoTone name first appeared in 2011. A secondary brand history describes the predecessor operation as LauriePedals from late 2009 to early 2011 before the DingoTone name and updated branding were introduced. This should remain a relationship record pending stronger primary documentary evidence.

Effects Database's December 19, 2011 weekly overview provides a very useful first catalog cluster: BSD Big Sky Drive, CSF Classic Seventies Fuzz, FDD Fat Dingo Distortion, GHF Germanium Hybrid Fuzz, HZD Boost, SQO Seventies Quad-Opamp Overdrive, TLM loop mixer, and VFZ Vintage Fuzz. The HZD is dirt-adjacent and belongs in the boundary layer; the other gain models belong in the main catalog.

A 2012 review of the BSD confirms DingoTone's appearance in 2011 and describes the Big Sky Drive as a germanium-based transparent overdrive. This creates a solid period corridor even where exact first-sale dates are not yet available for every model.

## Dr. No

Dr. No is another builder where the earliest model matters because the first documented product carries a licensing relationship. The Drive-O-Matic page states that it was the first and most successful early Dr. No stompbox and that the first generation was licensed under Blendomatic. The builder's operation then developed into a growing repertoire of original effects.

The November 2009 documentation cluster includes Drive-O-Matic, More Gary, Black Magic Bass Distortion, and Octofuzz. Eindhoven Rockcity Overdrive appears in December 2009 as an unusually small handmade limited edition. The surviving page contains conflicting quantity language, so the archive intentionally marks the exact edition size unresolved rather than selecting one number. Power Driver & Booster is documented in August 2011 and later Power Driver MkII is kept as a separate 2013 generation anchor for a later pass.

Dr. No's later Evil MotherFuzzer record also demonstrates why the archive should retain collaboration and limited-run states separately. The 2014 product was developed with Pablo van de Poel and had an explicitly limited run, but it falls outside this pass's 2005-2012 corridor.

## Archive decisions

1. Pigtronix's OFO Disnortion, later Disnortion variants, and Custom Shop editions remain linked but separate.
2. Orion's inspiration from vintage Big Muff designs is recorded as a design lineage note, not an OEM relationship.
3. DingoTone's 2007 origins and 2011 brand appearance are preserved as separate chronology facts.
4. DingoTone HZD is kept outside the central dirt count as boost/preamp.
5. Dr. No's early Blendomatic license is preserved instead of being erased into a generic Dr. No origin story.
6. Exact first-sale dates remain unresolved where only publication/index dates survive.
7. No schematics, PCB layouts, BOMs, gutshots, cloning instructions, or circuit reconstruction are included.
