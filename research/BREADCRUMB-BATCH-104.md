## Photos

Japanese retailer product pages and the ECB/NAMM builder page provide exterior imagery for the documented THE NEXT SOUND models. These are retained as source leads for eventual credit/licensing review.

## Information on the pedal

- MSS-06 is documented as a distortion model in the earlier MSS family.
- CH-01OD is a CAT HAND overdrive.
- CH-03 Meows Driver is a transparent-style drive derived from a classic reference concept and adapted for the compact CAT HAND format.
- CH-03 DS is a separate distortion product in the same series.
- CH-06 CAT is explicitly described by Japanese retail documentation as a RAT homage.
- CH-09 Nyazz is a dynamic fuzz designed for the compact CAT HAND format.
- CH-10 pNyas is a 2025 compact distortion built around another classic distortion reference concept.
- Current 2026 community documentation confirms that the earlier full-size lineup was moving into production-complete status while the builder continued developing the compact CAT HAND family.

## Normalization decisions

- CAT HAND numbered products are kept as distinct models when their function and documented identity differ.
- Special editions and finish changes do not create new conceptual counts.
- The one-off Mazda RX-7 enclosure is preserved as a custom relationship record rather than a separate standard production model.
- No schematics, PCB layouts, gutshots or complete component inventories are collected.
