# YARP 2026-09-15 Batch 106: Blackfield / StudioSound German Catalog Boundary

## Scope
Audit two obscure German dirt names while preserving their separate identities.

## Findings
- Blackfield Orchester-Elektronik remains a separate West German marketed/manufacturing candidate associated with the Flying Sound and Blackfield Fuzz products.
- StudioSound/Lemon remains a distinct Karlsruhe-area product line associated with the Zuzz Thainer fuzz.
- No evidence in this pass supports merging Blackfield, Lemon StudioSound, Allsound, or Schaller into one manufacturer.
- The surviving German record is fragmented, so the Archive favors product-level evidence over enclosure similarity or collector shorthand.
- Non-dirt studio products remain catalog boundaries and are not counted toward the central fuzz/OD/distortion totals.
