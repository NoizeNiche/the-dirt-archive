# YARP 2026-09-15 Batch 113: McPherson Australia Fuzz Prototype

## Builder node
**McPherson (Australia, Melbourne-area)**

## Status
**Prototype / one-off lineage; not yet a confirmed production pedal maker.**

## Evidence
A retrospective history of the McPherson amplifier operation records that Ron McPherson built what may have been the first Australian fuzz box for guitarist Lindsay Cook of The Mustangs around the time the Kinks-style distortion problem arose, roughly 1964-65. The device was described as a small circuit board mounted on or associated with a Goldentone amplifier and connected through a custom 5-pin DIN socket. A separate account from The Mustangs repeats the recollection and explicitly says Ron may have devised the first fuzz box in Australia, while acknowledging that the claim is not proven.

The evidence matters because this is earlier than the documented commercial Fi-Sonic Fuzz Blender advertisement of December 23, 1965, but it describes a custom amp-integrated unit rather than a normal standalone production stompbox. It should therefore remain a prototype/one-off builder record rather than being counted as a mass-produced pedal.

## Catalog state
- Custom Australian fuzz box, approximately 1964-65: prototype / one-off, associated with Goldentone amplifier infrastructure.
- No verified evidence yet of a separately marketed McPherson-branded fuzz pedal.
- No verified production run, model number, retail price, or quantity.

## Historical significance
McPherson expands the Australian prehistory of fuzz beyond Fi-Sonic and Claybridge. It also illustrates an important distinction for the archive: local builders could create distortion devices for individual musicians before a national commercial pedal catalog existed.

## Attribution discipline
Do not merge McPherson with Goldentone or Rose-Morris. The available evidence indicates that Ron McPherson built the fuzz device while working around the McPherson amplifier operation, and that it interfaced with a Goldentone amplifier. It does not establish Goldentone as the physical maker of the fuzz unit.
