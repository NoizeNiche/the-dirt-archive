# Breadcrumb Addendum 70

Date: 2026-09-15

## Batch

`research/universal-amplifier-sam-ash-first-use-and-fuzz-stainer-audit-34.tsv`

## Scope

Universal Amplifier Corp. / Astro Amp and Sam Ash fuzz identity chronology.

## Findings

- Sam Ash's 2014 U.S. trademark gazette entry for **SAM ASH FUZZZ BOXX** states first use and first use in commerce as **December 31, 1967**.
- Sam Ash's later historical article says the pedal originated as the **Astrotone in 1967** and was renamed the Sam Ash Fuzzz Boxx in **1968**. The archive preserves this as a source conflict rather than choosing one date without stronger evidence.
- Independent vintage research documents a **March 1967 Astrotone** and a **May 1967 Sam Ash Fuzzz Boxx** with the same board/component family, supporting concurrent production of the two marketed states by Universal Amplifier Corp.
- Astrotone production is independently anchored by surviving examples into **1969**, with Universal Amplifier Corp. identified in service documentation at 615 West 131st Street, New York City.
- The rare Sam Ash **Fuzz-Stainer** remains unresolved. Construction similarities and the `SA-FZ` board marking support Sam Ash association, but current evidence does not establish Universal Amplifier manufacture, employee assembly, or kit origin.
- Multiple Astrotone/Fuzzz Boxx circuit-board and transistor states remain cataloged as production revisions unless independent evidence establishes a factory change.

## Decision log

- Do not rewrite the public chronology to force 1968 as the first Sam Ash use date.
- Do not infer Universal Amplifier manufacture of the Fuzz-Stainer from physical similarity alone.
- No public lineage edges promoted in this batch.
