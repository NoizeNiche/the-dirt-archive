## Builder

- VEMURAM is a Japanese custom pedal brand based in Tokyo and operated under Tri-Sound.
- Surviving first-party and specialist dealer records document a long-running dirt catalog, including major overdrive, distortion, fuzz and bass-overdrive families plus artist collaborations.
- The archive preserves historical/discontinued products as historical records rather than deleting them when they leave the current catalog.

## Information on the pedal

- Jan Ray is documented around a 1960s Blackface-era Fender “Magic 6” concept.
- Galea is a medium-gain overdrive associated with Tweed-era/Tone-Master references.
- Karen is a higher-gain Marshall-style drive.
- SHANKS ODS-1 is a John Shanks collaboration with a broad transparent overdrive range.
- Butter Machine is a Michael Landau signature distortion.
- Oz is an Oz Noy signature silicon fuzz and is documented as a distinct product from Shanks 4K.
- Myriad Fuzz is a Josh Smith collaboration using germanium/silicon hybrid fuzz architecture; Myriad is preserved separately because its loop/mix format gives it a distinct product identity.
- Shanks II, Shanks 3K and Shanks 4K are preserved as a visible family, with Shanks 3K currently outside central dirt pending category reconciliation.
- DJ1 is a Darryl Jones bass overdrive and is counted as dirt rather than a booster because surviving sources identify it as bass overdrive.
- Las Lunas is a 2026 distortion built around late-1970s/early-1980s rock drive goals, with three-band passive EQ and two distortion characters.
- SPIRITONE is a 2026 Char collaboration overdrive, developed from research into Char-associated Klon, CE-1 and Copicat drive/boost behavior and presented with two switchable voices.

## Normalization decisions

- Jan Ray artist variations are not treated as new conceptual pedals.
- Shanks 3K remains relationship-only because surviving catalog records classify it primarily as a booster while other documentation describes substantial fuzz lineage.
- Budi, Budi-G and Neoplex remain outside central dirt because their primary identities are boost/preamp.
- Myriad and Myriad Fuzz remain separate product records because the Myriad adds a materially different loop/dry-mix product format rather than being merely a cosmetic revision.
- Discontinued models remain archived; discontinuation does not erase the historical record.
- No schematics, PCB layouts, gutshots or complete component inventories are collected.
