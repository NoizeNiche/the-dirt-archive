# Early Foundational Handoff 84

## Pass 36: 1990s boutique transition, early production objects, and builder-origin clocks

Pass 36 begins the systematic descent from the late-1990s boutique corridor into the early-1990s transition. The pass emphasizes builders and product families that show how the boutique pedal category moved from small-run recreations, modifications and hand-built originals into identifiable branded production lines.

### Newly documented

- **Prescription Electronics Experience**: 1993 documented introduction and early original production family.
- **Prescription Electronics Yardbox**: 1994 documented product-era anchor within Jack Brossart's early 1990s boutique catalog.
- **Prescription Electronics C.O.B.**: original 1990s octave-fuzz state kept separate from the later reissue.
- **Fulltone Fulldrive**: 1993 original raw-aluminum/label-maker production state documented from Michael Fuller's later account.
- **Fulltone Fulldrive 2**: second production state separated from the original Fulldrive, with the orange enclosure and boost control establishing a meaningful family transition.
- **Frantone Hep Cat**: late-1995 first-product production anchor.
- **Frantone Peachfuzz**: 1997 original production state, separated from later limited reissues.
- **Foxrox ProVibe**: approximately 150 units built through 1994, preserved as a tiny pre-incorporation Foxrox production story and chronology boundary.
- **Foxrox Captain Coconut**: summer-2000 production state, preserved as a fuzz-containing Hendrix multi-effect and scope-boundary object.
- **Real McCoy Custom RMC3**: winter-1994 release, 1995-1996 distribution/case transition evidence, retained as boutique builder chronology outside the core dirt index.
- **Analog Man early TS9/TS-808 modification business**: early-1990s modification and dealer activity recorded as a builder-origin clock separate from later branded pedal launches.
- **Klon Centaur**: late-1994 original production anchor after a multi-year prototype period.

### Key research findings

1. **The early boutique category did not emerge from a single template.** Prescription Electronics, Fulltone, Frantone, Foxrox, Analog Man and Klon show different routes: vintage recreation, modification, original small-run designs, multi-effect consolidation, and purpose-built overdrive.
2. **Physical design changes are becoming unusually valuable.** Fulltone's raw-aluminum Fulldrive and later orange Fulldrive 2 create an identifiable production boundary without requiring component-level claims.
3. **Tiny runs deserve their own historical layer.** Foxrox's roughly 150 ProVibes and Way Huge's previously documented three-unit early run demonstrate why the archive should preserve micro-production objects instead of forcing every builder into a conventional catalog model.
4. **Prescription Electronics gives the archive a clean early-1990s runway.** Experience in 1993 and Yardbox in 1994 make Jack Brossart an important bridge between vintage-effect recreation culture and the later boutique market.
5. **Frantone provides an unusually clear founder timeline.** A 1994 personal pedal experiment led to the Hep Cat by late 1995, followed by Peachfuzz in 1997 and later work at Electro-Harmonix. The archive keeps those clocks distinct.
6. **Analog Man's modification business is historically significant even when no new branded pedal is involved.** The early TS9/TS-808 work shows a market and information network developing before the later full-time handmade catalog.
7. **Klon belongs in the foundational 1990s overdrive layer.** The Centaur's late-1994 production anchor gives the archive an early purpose-built boutique overdrive object whose identity is not reducible to vintage recreation alone.

### Evidence discipline

- Founder interviews and manufacturer histories are used for origin and production anchors.
- Marketplace listings are specimen evidence, not universal release-date proof.
- Later reissues and limited editions remain separate from original-era states.
- Small-run products are preserved even when they sit outside the primary fuzz/overdrive/distortion index.
- Modification businesses and later branded pedal production remain separate chronology layers.
- No schematics, PCB layouts, complete BOMs, gutshot libraries or cloning instructions are added.

### Current lineage position

The archive now has a direct bridge from the late-1990s boutique corridor into the earliest identifiable 1990s boutique wave. Prescription Electronics supplies a 1993-1994 vintage-recreation foundation; Fulltone shows the transition from raw prototype packaging to a recognizable commercial overdrive line; Frantone demonstrates hand-built originals becoming a sustained catalog; Foxrox preserves micro-run craftsmanship; Analog Man documents the modification economy that preceded its mature pedal line; and Klon provides a seminal late-1994 boutique overdrive object.
