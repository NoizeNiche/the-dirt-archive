## Scope
Audited Sam Ash branded fuzz products as a supply-chain sequence rather than assuming one manufacturer across the brand's entire effects history.

## Locked findings
- Sam Ash Fuzzz Boxx / Astrotone state remains a Universal Amplifier Corp. OEM / house-brand production relationship, with the renaming chronology left explicitly disputed between surviving evidence and the later Sam Ash retrospective.
- Sam Ash Fuzz-Stainer remains HOLD_FOR_REVIEW. Current evidence supports an early-1970s Sam Ash identity, but not a verified factory. Employee-built or locally assembled production remains a live hypothesis, and differing surviving topologies require production-state caution.
- Sam Ash Fuzzola is separated from the Universal Amplifier branch. Current evidence supports an initial Shin-Ei Japan supply state followed by a later JEN Italy supply state.
- Sam Ash Fuzzola II / Jumbo Fuzz is treated as a JEN Italy OEM/rebrand state around 1973-1975, with period support from the broader JEN Jumbo family and Dallas Arbiter catalog evidence.
- Astrotone first-generation dating remains a source-conflict field rather than a forced single year.

## Editorial rule reinforced
A retailer's house brand can span multiple factories and countries over time. Sam Ash branding therefore cannot be used as factory evidence without model- and period-specific support.
