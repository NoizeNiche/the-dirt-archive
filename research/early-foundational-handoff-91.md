# Early Foundational Handoff 91

## Pass 43: 2003-2008 Canadian and European boutique expansion, first-shipment clocks, and production-method boundaries

Pass 43 continues the 2005-2008 boutique expansion while strengthening the archive's international coverage and its distinction between product launch, first shipment, company founding, and later generation changes.

### Newly documented or clarified

- **Diamond Drive**: official Diamond history dates hobby design work to 2003 and the first shipment of approximately 30 hand-built pedals to a Halifax music store to June 2004.
- **Diamond Drive design-to-production split**: 2003 is retained as a development corridor; June 2004 is the first documented shipment rather than an inferred launch month.
- **Diamond Compressor**: first shipment is documented in March 2005 as a builder chronology companion outside core dirt.
- **JAM Pedals**: founder history separates 2005 self-built development from 2006 commercial sales to friends and Greek shops, then 2007 international exhibitions and 2008 dealer expansion.
- **Big John Effects Hairy Balls v1**: official Belgian company history dates the fuzz-only version to 2007.
- **Big John Effects Hairy Balls v2**: official history dates the 2008 version and explicitly adds boost, gate and fuzz functions.
- **Big John Effects Granny Puker**: official history lists the overdrive/octave-down pedal as available in 2008.
- **Red Witch Moon Phaser**: 2003 Red Witch founding and Moon Phaser first-effect lineage are preserved as a modulation boundary rather than a dirt record.
- **Fairfield Circuitry Barbershop**: 2008 is established as the company/product-era starting point, with contemporary 2008 publication confirming the Barbershop as the first Fairfield pedal.
- **Fairfield Barbershop V1 to later Millennium boundary**: manufacturer FAQ documents the smaller later enclosure and added three-position tone switch while explicitly stating that the topology itself was not changed.
- **AMT Electronics Fatal Tube**: retained as an early Russian pedal family whose exact first production date remains unresolved; the 2001 AMT naming change is kept separate from product chronology.
- **BJFE Pale Green Compressor**: Bjorn Juhl's notes preserve a 2004 finish transition from flat-green nitrocellulose lacquer to a brighter oil-based color, useful as a specimen boundary outside core dirt.

### Key research findings

1. **First shipment is a powerful historical anchor.** Diamond's exact June 2004 shipment and 30-unit count are stronger than dating surviving Drive examples from marketplace listings.
2. **European boutique history is not one thing.** JAM, Big John, Red Witch and Fairfield show different pathways from self-built experimentation to small commercial production.
3. **Functional expansion can define a generation even without circuit reconstruction.** Hairy Balls v1 versus v2 and Barbershop V1 versus Millennium are useful external identity boundaries.
4. **Company names and product dates must remain separate.** AMT's 2001 naming transition does not automatically date Fatal Tube production; Diamond's 2002/2003 corporate chronology also precedes the first pedal shipment.
5. **The international network is now substantial.** Canada, Greece, Belgium/Netherlands, New Zealand, Québec, Russia and Sweden now sit alongside the established U.S., U.K. and Japanese nodes.
6. **Specimen clues can be material without becoming cloning information.** Finish chemistry, enclosure size, control changes, serial/shipment windows and production-method notes provide identification value while respecting the archive's technical boundary.

### Evidence discipline

- Manufacturer-authored legacy pages and builder interviews are preferred for chronology and production clocks.
- Contemporary articles are retained as independent public-circulation evidence.
- Company founding, design start, first shipment, first sale and later revision dates are never silently merged into one date.
- Non-dirt products remain scope-bound chronology companions where they materially illuminate a builder's history.
- No schematics, PCB layouts, complete BOMs, gutshot libraries or cloning instructions are added.

### Current lineage position

The archive now has a meaningful 2003-2008 international production map. Diamond anchors Canadian first-shipment history; JAM and Big John capture Greek and Belgian workshop-to-market expansion; Red Witch provides a New Zealand origin; Fairfield marks the 2008 Québec boutique wave; AMT preserves a Russian independent lineage; and BJFE contributes specimen-level finish chronology.
