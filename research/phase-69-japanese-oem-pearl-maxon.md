# Phase 69: Japanese OEM / Pearl / Maxon research

## Research targets

This pass prioritizes Japanese OEM relationships and catalog families that can otherwise be flattened into brand-only entries.

### Stafford Maxon
Effects Database documents a five-product Japan-only collaboration between Stafford and Maxon around 2011-2012: 1054TT HizMax, 1929T Dual Boostrex, 1944TT HizBender, 1964T Astro Echo, and 6348ST Koh Chorus. The relationship should be represented as Stafford + Maxon rather than treating the products as ordinary Maxon catalog entries.

Source: Effects Database, Stafford Maxon catalog.

### Pearl Sound Spice
Pearl's Sound Spice series contains 12 products: AD-08 Analog Delay, CH-02 Chorus, CO-04 Compressor, DS-06 Distortion, FG-01 Flanger, GE-09 9 Band Graphic EQ, OC-07 Octaver, OD-05 Overdrive, PE-10 Dual Band Parametric Equalizer, PH-03 Phaser, SU-19 Stereo Noise Suppressor, and TH-20 Thriller. The GE-09 is documented as a 1980s Japanese KLM-built unit, which makes the manufacturer relationship an important archival field.

Source: Effects Database, Pearl Sound Spice series and individual GE-09 entry.

### Maxon / Ibanez 9-series relationship
Effects Database lists 32 Maxon 9-series products and explicitly notes that these were also made for the Ibanez 9 series. This is a major OEM lineage and should be modeled as a relationship rather than duplicated as unrelated products. The catalog includes OD-9, SD-9, SM-9, ST-9, TOD-9, VOP-9 and numerous modulation, EQ and utility models.

Source: Effects Database, Maxon 9 series.

### Ibanez 10-series relationship
The Ibanez 10 series is documented as the Ibanez Power series, made by Maxon between 1986 and 1989. The 36-product family includes MT10 Mostortion, TS10 Tube Screamer Classic, WF10 Wah Fuzz, WH10, FC10 Fat Cat, DS10 Distortion Charger, and many modulation/digital/utility units.

Source: Effects Database, Ibanez 10 series.

### Ibanez 7 / Tone-Lok relationship
The Tone-Lok family is documented as the Ibanez 7 series and contains 17 products, including FZ7 Fuzz, DS7 Distortion, TS7 Tube Screamer, PD7 Phat-Hed, LF7 Lo Fi, DE7 Stereo Delay/Echo, and WD7 Weeping Demon. These deserve their own family-level historical record even where production details remain incomplete.

Source: Effects Database, Ibanez Tone-Lok series.

## Archival handling

Do not infer that every Maxon/Ibanez model is electronically identical merely because an OEM relationship exists. Preserve brand, series, manufacturer/OEM, enclosure generation, production period, and variant as separate fields. High-level circuit-family observations are appropriate; schematics and gutshots remain outside the public archive scope.

## Image / rights leads

Prioritize manufacturer catalogs, period advertisements, dealer scans, and clearly attributed collector photography. Record provenance and usage status separately from the product record. Avoid assuming that an image being indexed online means it is reusable.
