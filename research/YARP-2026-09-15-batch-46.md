# YARP 2026-09-15 Batch 46

## Focus
Foxx / Ridinger private-label and OEM family reconciliation.

## Findings
ToneHome identifies fOXX effects as made by Ridinger Associates Inc. in Chatsworth, California, in the early 1970s. Effects Database preserves multiple marketed states of the Fuzz & Wa & Volume family, including Sears Model 1481, Emmons, Footsystem, G&G, Hamilton Music, Musical Mexicana, Paraclete Productions, Regal RE-30, Turtle and Univox.

The archive treats these as marketed/private-label identities under the Foxx/Ridinger physical-builder lineage unless model-specific evidence shows a different factory. Sears Model 1481 is therefore not promoted to an independent Sears manufacturer.

ToneHome separately dates the Foxx Fuzz & Wa & Volume to the early 1970s and documents a 1971 retail price of $74.95. The archive keeps the retail-date evidence separate from exact production-end dating.

## Evidence boundary
Private-label identity does not equal physical manufacture. The relationship map records Foxx/Ridinger as the builder lineage and each label as a marketed/OEM state. No circuit reconstruction material added.
