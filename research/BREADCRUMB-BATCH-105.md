# The Dirt Archive · Breadcrumb · Batch 105

## Completed

Phase One Batch 105 deepened the THE NEXT SOUND / TNS lineage with additional historical MSS dirt products and explicit generation relationships.

- THE NEXT SOUND remains a canonical-candidate Japanese handmade builder lineage centered in Fukushima and associated with Katsuhiro Kijima.
- The surviving evidence now supports a multi-stage MSS development history rather than a single isolated MSS-06 pedal.

## Photos

ECB/NAMM and the official GWNN/TNS shop provide exterior product imagery for the newly documented MSS products and are retained as source leads for future credit/licensing review. Retail and Reverb records provide additional historical specimen imagery for MSS generations.

## Information on the pedal

- MSS-14 is documented by the builder as the successor-era mainline MSS distortion after the MSS06 family.
- AC127GF Guerrilla Fuzz is a compact dedicated fuzz with an AC127 germanium transistor and front-mounted jacks, documented for guitar or bass use.
- MSS-18 is a wide-gain distortion using an LM386 audio amplifier, with switchable mid emphasis/cut behavior.
- The MSS06 family is explicitly described as evolving through G/BA, RS, C, E and F production forms. These are preserved as lineage/generation records rather than inflated into unrelated conceptual models.

## Normalization decisions

- MSS-06 G/BA, MSS-06RS and MSS-06C remain relationship-only variants/generations of the existing MSS-06 family.
- MSS14B GODFROST remains a signature/special relationship record.
- CH-04DL, CH-07 and other non-dirt CAT HAND functions remain outside central dirt.
- No schematics, PCB layouts, gutshots or complete component inventories are collected.
