# Analog Man 1990s Catalog Audit

Research status: indexed historical baseline, not lifetime-complete.

## Identity
Analog Man is the effects business associated with Mike Piera. Piera's own history says he began studying and selling vintage effects after earlier work with vintage guitars, developed Tube Screamer modification work in the early 1990s, and began using the Analog Man name around 1993. He later moved full-time into Analog Man in 2000. The early business therefore belongs in the archive both as a pedal-modification lineage and as a handmade pedal-builder lineage, with those roles kept distinct.

## Dirt catalog signal
- Sun Face fuzz family: early Analog Man handmade fuzz lineage, ultimately tied to the Fuzz Face family.
- Peppermint Fuzz: 1990s/early-2000s era family; exact first-production date requires deeper period advertising.
- King Of Tone: later product, not a 1990s launch. Do not back-date it merely because the builder dates to the 1990s.
- Astro Tone: later Analog Man product, not a 1990s launch.
- Beano Boost: boost boundary for this dirt catalog; retain as adjacent gain product rather than automatically classifying as OD/distortion.

## Important historical distinction
The Analog Man origin story shows that the 1990s boutique scene grew partly from vintage-pedal restoration, resale and modification. The early TS9/TS808 modification work is a business/service lineage and should not be counted as a separate original Analog Man pedal model.
