# YARP 2026-09-15 Batch 75: WEM Rush Pep Box Lineage

WEM's Pep Box is retained as a Rush/WEM builder lineage rather than a single static WEM model. Early units were associated with Pepe Rush's workshop, with later marketing and sales under the WEM brand after Rush partnered with Charlie Watkins. Documentary evidence shows WEM demonstrating the Pep Box at the August 1965 British Musical Instrument Industries Trade Fair. Surviving production states include early germanium and later silicon versions, with the construction and electronics changing over the period.

Important catalog boundary: later red "Fuzzy" pedals are not assumed to be Pep Box generations. Specialist evidence indicates those red units were manufactured in Germany and post-date the silver Rush/WEM wedge era. Keep them as a separate discovery node until independent documentation establishes identity and maker.

Source trail: Fuzzboxes WEM Rush Pep Box; Fuzzboxes historical timeline.
