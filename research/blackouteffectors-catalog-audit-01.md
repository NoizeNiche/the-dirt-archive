# BlackOutEffectors Historical Catalog Audit 01

## Research status
DISCOVERY_BASELINE

## Identity
BlackOutEffectors is a boutique guitar-effects manufacturer that began in Vancouver, British Columbia, in 2008 and relocated to Asheville, North Carolina, in 2009.

## Early dirt catalog
The current historical site identifies several enduring BlackOutEffectors dirt families:

- Musket Fuzz — core Muff-family fuzz product; later Musket Fuzz v2 is a distinct revision.
- Fix'd Fuzz — multi-function fuzz/boost/overdrive family with later V3 and Deluxe states.
- Twosome Dual Fuzz — later two-pedal-family combination built from Musket and Fix'd Fuzz Deluxe identities.
- Blunderbuss Fuzz — later fuzz family.
- Fubar Fuzz / Blackout Fuzz Bear — later limited-edition fuzz family.
- Mantra Overdrive — later overdrive family.

## Chronology caution
The company start in 2008 is strongly documented, but the available current catalog does not provide reliable first-sale dates for every early dirt model. The archive therefore records the builder start and model-family existence without inventing launch years.

The original Musket and Fix'd Fuzz should be separated from their later V2/V3/Deluxe states. The archive should also preserve special editions and retailer-exclusive variants as editions rather than treating them as unrelated builders.
