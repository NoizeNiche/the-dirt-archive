# Early Foundational Handoff 87

## Pass 39: 1994-2003 boutique expansion, Japanese independent clocks, and modification-culture boundaries

Pass 39 pushes the archive across the point where late-1990s boutique construction becomes an identifiable early-2000s ecosystem. The pass emphasizes manufacturer-authored production clocks, corrections to previously broad chronology, and explicit separation between branded products and modification work.

### Newly documented or clarified

- **BIXONIC Expandora EXP-2000**: manufacturer chronology anchors the original production state to 1994.
- **BIXONIC EXP-2001**: manufacturer chronology dates the FET-based Expandora II to 1999, while period/database coverage preserves a later 2001 public-era corridor. The disagreement is retained rather than normalized away.
- **Lovepedal FAB 50 → COT-50 lineage**: manufacturer history places Sean Michael's design work in the early 1990s and establishes the FAB 50 as the core early product; specialist evidence supports the later FAB50-to-COT50 lineage.
- **Lovepedal Eternity**: retained as a family-level early handmade overdrive with multiple later variants, pending stronger first-production dating.
- **Menatone Red Snapper chronology correction**: current manufacturer text explicitly says the original three-knob point-to-point Red Snapper debuted in 1996. The earlier 1998 NAMM note is therefore retained as a later public milestone, not the product's birth.
- **Menatone Blue Collar**: manufacturer text confirms a 1997 debut as the second Menatone design.
- **Menatone King of the Britains**: early four-knob specimen evidence is preserved as a 1998-2000 market corridor, while a 2000 documentary product state provides a separate product-era anchor.
- **Providence A/B box chronology boundary**: Pacifix/Providence chronology is preserved from its 1996 establishment and pre-SOV-1 handcrafted switching work into the 1998 Stampede OD release already documented in the prior pass.
- **Keeley modification era**: third-party pedal modification work is explicitly separated from Keeley-branded products; Robert Keeley's firsthand timeline puts the first production Compressor in September 2001.
- **Catalinbread Super Chili Picoso**: manufacturer history gives a clean 2003 founding and first-branded-product anchor.

### Key research findings

1. **Chronology conflicts are useful data.** BIXONIC's manufacturer chronology and later public/database dating do not line up perfectly for the EXP-2001. The archive keeps both evidence classes visible instead of inventing a synthetic date.
2. **Menatone's Red Snapper needed a correction.** The strongest current manufacturer evidence puts the original product in 1996. The 1998 NAMM appearance should describe a later public milestone, not the product's birth.
3. **A builder can have three distinct historical layers:** modification work, first branded production, and later model families. Keeley makes this especially visible.
4. **Lovepedal demonstrates why names can drift without implying a new circuit.** FAB 50 and COT-50 appear as a documented lineage, but the exact rename date remains open.
5. **Japanese independent builders deserve equal footing.** BIXONIC and Providence provide manufacturer-level chronology that materially improves the global 1990s map.
6. **2003 becomes a useful boundary marker.** Catalinbread's first branded production on a kitchen table shows the boutique phenomenon moving from small shops and modification specialists into a new generation of internet-connected regional builders.

### Evidence discipline

- Manufacturer-authored chronology is preferred for launch and production clocks.
- Period press and specialist databases are preserved as independent corroboration, not silently promoted to primary evidence.
- Market listings are used as specimen evidence and date corridors, not universal factory histories.
- Conflicting dates remain flagged where the underlying evidence classes genuinely disagree.
- Third-party modifications never become substitute identities for the modified pedal's original manufacturer.
- No schematics, PCB layouts, complete BOMs, gutshot libraries or cloning instructions are added.

### Current lineage position

The archive now bridges the 1990s boutique pioneers into the early-2000s expansion with a stronger cross-Atlantic and Japan/U.S. chronology: BIXONIC and Providence anchor Japanese independent production; Menatone and Lovepedal show evolving U.S. boutique product families; Keeley records the modification-to-branded-product transition; and Catalinbread marks a 2003 Pacific Northwest emergence.
