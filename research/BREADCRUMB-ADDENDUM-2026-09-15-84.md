### Findings

- LIMMCO is best treated as a distributor, wholesaler, and house-brand owner rather than a universal factory attribution.
- The Kimberly Buzz Box has the clearest multi-stage production trail: an initial LIMMCO in-house state, a later Applied-associated state, and a still later UMI-associated state.
- Nomad remains a cross-supplier house brand. The Banshee and Rogue are tied to Applied Audio, the Tone Booster is tied to UMI, and the early Nomad Super Fuzz remains unresolved.
- The existence of a LIMMCO business relationship does not by itself establish physical manufacture by LIMMCO, UMI, Applied Audio, or another network participant.
- Goya effects remain an Applied Audio research case. LIMMCO is documented as an important distributor in the same trade network, but no direct LIMMCO-to-Goya factory claim was added.
