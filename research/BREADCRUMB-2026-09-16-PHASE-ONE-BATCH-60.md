## Evidence notes
Neutrino Labs MX's first-party site states that the company has designed and manufactured guitar and bass effects since 2017 and currently markets a 23-product collection. The storefront lists MARX WAR as a current Neutrino Labs MX product. A dedicated product listing describes MARX WAR as high-gain fuzz/distortion using 1N34A germanium diodes and a Fuzz War-derived design, with the product available in red and black finishes. Reverb independently lists MARX WAR as a 2026 product made in Mexico.

## Scope discipline
Only the newly observed central dirt model is added in this batch. Existing canonical products are preserved in their earlier files. No clone/custom product is promoted merely because it appears in a builder's current marketplace, and no schematics, PCB layouts, gutshots, cloning instructions or complete BOM data are published.
