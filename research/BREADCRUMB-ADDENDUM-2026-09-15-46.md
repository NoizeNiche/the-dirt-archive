## Historical findings

- The Applied Fuzz and Treble Booster is now treated as a distinct early Applied Audio OEM platform with multiple marketed identities including Crown, Goya and Lou Rose; early-1968 is the safest public start-window rather than assuming an earlier date from component codes alone.
- Kadent Treblefuzz is retained as a same-builder candidate but not yet promoted as a confirmed direct predecessor because documented examples show meaningful construction differences.
- Applied Rogue Fuzz is recorded as a separate Applied family, with Kadent and Nomad marketed states preserved independently.
- Guild FW-3 remains a high-confidence Applied OEM relationship; the Guild/Applied partnership is supported independently of the broader database family mapping.
- V-Jay FW-3 is retained as another high-confidence Applied marketed state.
- Shared enclosure appearance between Applied Rogue and Hohner MF-50 is explicitly not treated as proof of identical factory production.
