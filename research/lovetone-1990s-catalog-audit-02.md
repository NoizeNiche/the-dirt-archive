# Lovetone 1990s catalog audit

## Scope
Builder-level historical catalog pass for Lovetone, emphasizing period product chronology and original-company identity. This file intentionally excludes schematics, PCB layouts, BOMs and circuit reconstruction.

## Identity
Lovetone was officially founded 1 April 1995. Period-history research places its commercial pedal availability from 1995 through 2007, sometimes intermittently. The company released four pedals during 1995: Meatball (May), Big Cheese (July), Brown Source (July), and Doppelganger (September).

## Dirt catalog
- Big Cheese — fuzz — first released July 1995.
- Brown Source — overdrive — first released July 1995.

## Important non-dirt companion
- Meatball — envelope filter — May 1995.
- Doppelganger — phaser/vibrato — September 1995.

## Catalog interpretation
Lovetone should be modeled as its own British boutique builder. The large-format, highly distinctive enclosure/platform is a product-family identity marker, but enclosure appearance should not be treated as sufficient evidence for exact production generation.

The four 1995 launches are a strong period anchor. Big Cheese and Brown Source belong directly in the Dirt Archive's core dirt layer; Meatball and Doppelganger belong in the wider Lovetone catalog/relationship layer only.
