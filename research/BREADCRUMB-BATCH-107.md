## Information on the pedal

- Super Fats Drive is the flagship overdrive family.
- Fuzzy Voodoo was introduced in 2022 by extracting the high-gain mode of Super Fats Drive, retuning the low end and adding a Voo Doo bass-boost switch.
- The first-party catalog describes the fuzzy-overdrive concept as combining amplifier-like distortion with fuzz-style low-end response.

## Normalization decisions

- Fuzzy Voodoo is kept as a distinct product because the builder presents it as its own 2022 release, while its development relationship to Super Fats Drive is preserved.
- Fuzzy Voodoo 2 is retained as a documented later product presentation/family entry pending deeper chronology.
- Booster products remain outside central dirt.
- No schematics, PCB layouts, gutshots or complete component inventories are collected.
