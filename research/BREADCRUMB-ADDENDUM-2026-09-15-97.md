### Findings

- The Bowman Electronics Fuzz Maker Sound Kit is now anchored to a 1970 Burnstein-Applebee catalog record as a specific Bowman product.

- Bowman is therefore established as the marketed kit-maker/product identity, but the exact physical assembly source for the Fuzz Maker remains unresolved.
- The geographic proximity of Bowman and Trans-Tek in New Jersey is retained only as contextual evidence.
- Possible Applied Audio/Trans-Tek component supply, old-stock use, or other cooperation remains a low-confidence hypothesis. It is not treated as proof of Applied manufacture.
- Similar knobs, lamp hardware, or enclosure appearance do not override the absence of a model-specific manufacturing record.
