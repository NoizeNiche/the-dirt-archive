# YARP 2026-09-15 Batch 47

## Focus
Lafayette Radio Electronics catalog evidence.

## Findings
The Spring 1970 Lafayette catalog directly documents a Lafayette Fuzz Sound, a separate Super Fuzz, and a Deluxe AC Super Fuzz.

The catalog describes the Fuzz Sound as a transistorized, battery-powered fuzz with two controls. The Super Fuzz is described as a solid-state harmonic sustained-fuzz product with balance, expander, and tone controls.

The archive treats Lafayette Radio Electronics as a retail/import/private-label identity rather than assuming it physically manufactured these products. Factory attribution remains model-specific.

The same catalog advertises a separate Wah Wah Sound and Volume Control product. It remains catalog context and does not inflate the central dirt count.

## Evidence boundary
A period retail catalog establishes marketed product existence, but not necessarily physical factory identity. Imported status and factory relationships remain separate fields until model-specific evidence supports an upgrade.
