# YARP batch 93: MJM Guitar FX

## Identity

**Canonical builder:** MJM Guitar FX

**Also indexed as:** MJM Guitar Effects / MJM

**Region:** Canada / Montreal origin

**Era:** founded 1998; commercial catalog documented through the 2000s-present period.

**Identity state:** CANONICAL_CANDIDATE

**Catalog state:** INDEXED_BASELINE_MAPPED

## Why this builder enters the census

MJM Guitar FX is a boutique effects builder associated with Mike Milcetic. Equipboard's current brand record identifies the company as founded in 1998 and originally based in Montreal, with a product history centered on hand-built recreations and vintage-oriented fuzz, boost, overdrive and related effects. The builder is absent from the current master census, so it is added as a distinct candidate rather than inferred to be a branch of another builder.

The available evidence is strongest for product existence and family names, not exact year-by-year production boundaries. The archive therefore maps the indexed product universe without pretending that every finish, control-count change, or enclosure state is a separate generation.

## Dirt catalog baseline

The first catalog pass identifies these dirt-relevant products/families:

- London Fuzz family, including Germanium, Silicon and bias-oriented states
- London II Fuzz
- Brit Bender, including 2-knob and 3-knob states
- Foxey Fuzz
- China Fuzz
- Roctavios / Roctavious octave fuzz
- King Buzz fuzz
- Phantom Overdrive
- Luxora Overdrive
- Blues Devil overdrive

Dallas Boost / RM Booster is retained as a gain-adjacent boundary record rather than included in the central dirt count.

## Evidence notes

MJM's own FAQ directly supports the existence of Germanium and Silicon fuzz variants, a bias version of the London Fuzz, and the practical distinction between those production configurations. This is useful identification evidence, but it does not by itself establish exact manufacturing dates for each state.

Equipboard currently indexes 16 MJM products across fuzz, overdrive, boost, vibe and wah categories. Its MJM record supplies the builder's 1998 founding attribution and a broad product list including London Fuzz, Brit Bender, Phantom Overdrive, China Fuzz, Roctavios, Foxey Fuzz, Dallas Boost, London II, Luxora and King Buzz. Audiofanzine independently indexes MJM's fuzz and overdrive products, including Blues Devil and the London/Brit Bender families.

## Archive treatment

Do not merge MJM with another vintage-fuzz manufacturer merely because individual products emulate historical circuits. The builder identity is the company that marketed and built the MJM product, while the historical circuit inspiration belongs in the product relationship/evidence layer.

Do not split London Fuzz finishes or bias/germanium/silicon states into unrelated builder records. They belong under the MJM London Fuzz family until stronger evidence establishes separate retail models or production generations.

## Next pass

The next MJM research pass should prioritize primary MJM catalog pages, archived MJM product pages, dated dealer catalogs, and surviving specimens to establish:

1. exact London Fuzz variant chronology;
2. Brit Bender 2-knob versus 3-knob production boundaries;
3. whether London II is a successor, parallel model, or later revision;
4. first/last documented dates for Foxey, China Fuzz, Roctavios, King Buzz, Phantom, Luxora and Blues Devil;
5. enclosure/artwork/label changes that can identify production periods.

No circuit reconstruction or gutshot material is required for this pass.
