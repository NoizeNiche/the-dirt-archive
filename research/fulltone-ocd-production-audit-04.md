# Fulltone OCD Production Audit 04

## Scope
Product-level chronology for the Fulltone OCD family, emphasizing documented retail/version states, serial-number corridors, visual identification, limited editions, and current revival states. Circuit reconstruction details are intentionally excluded.

## Primary Fulltone position
Fulltone's current OCDv2 documentation states that the OCD was born in 2004 and that the numerous early revisions should be understood as one Version 1 family with minor variations, conventionally identified as V1.0 through V1.8. Fulltone explicitly distinguishes the later OCD V2 as a sufficiently different redesign. This corrects the common internet shorthand that labels every V1.x state as a separate major version. 

Source: Fulltone USA, OCDv2 product page: https://www.fulltoneusa.com/products/ocd-v2

## Major V2 break
The OCD V2 was introduced in 2017, with Fulltone's own historical wording identifying June 15, 2017 as the release point. This is the correct major retail-generation break for the family.

Fulltone describes V2 as sufficiently different to warrant a V2 designation, unlike the preceding V1.x changes. The V2 has its own enclosure/labeling and internal bypass-selection feature, and later retail listings identify it as a 2018 production product as well. 

Sources:
- Fulltone USA, OCDv2: https://www.fulltoneusa.com/products/ocd-v2
- Stars Music OCD V2 listing: https://www.stars-music.com/fulltone-ocd-v2_135200.html

## Limited and special OCD states

### Chicago Music Exchange black OCD V2
In July/August 2018 Fulltone and Chicago Music Exchange released a limited black-finish OCD V2 exclusive. Contemporary reporting states that the enclosure color changed while the underlying V2 design remained the same. This belongs in the edition layer, not as a new OCD generation.

Sources:
- MusicRadar, July 30, 2018: https://www.musicradar.com/news/fulltones-iconic-ocd-is-back-in-black
- Guitar.com, August 1, 2018: https://guitar.com/news/fulltone-unveils-limited-edition-colorway-for-the-ocd-v2/

### Red limited-edition OCD V2
A separate red V2 limited edition followed in 2019. Public secondary sources describe it as a limited-color run of the V2 rather than a new generation. Preserve the colorway as an edition/specimen relationship.

Source trail: Fulltone/secondary OCD chronology, with modern Fulltone product indexing retained.

### Custom Shop OCD v1.4 recreation
Fulltone USA currently sells a Custom Shop OCD v1.4 described as an exact recreation of the original hand-wired V1.4 specification. Fulltone states that this run is limited to 500 units and is a separate revival/recreation, not original 2007-era production. The current page states that production has paused after this run.

Source: Fulltone USA, Custom Shop OCD v1.4: https://www.fulltoneusa.com/products/custom-shop-ocd-v1-4

### OCD-Germanium
Fulltone USA currently identifies OCD-Germanium as a Custom Shop product based on the OCD platform, using an expanded germanium clipping implementation and a metallic teal finish. The archive treats this as a Custom Shop variant/derivative, not as another historical OCD V1 production generation.

Source: Fulltone USA, OCD-Germanium: https://www.fulltoneusa.com/products/ocd-germanium

### Gold Standard Limited Edition OCD
Fulltone USA currently lists a Limited Edition OCD with a metallic gold finish, serialized certificate of authenticity, and selectable OCD V2 or OCD-Ge configuration. It belongs to the modern Custom Shop/limited-edition layer and must not be merged with historical white-enclosure V1/V2 production.

Source: Fulltone USA, Limited Edition OCD: https://www.fulltoneusa.com/collections/all-products/products/ltdocd

## Identification hierarchy
For future specimen records, identify OCDs in this order:
1. Retail family: OCD V1-family vs OCD V2-family vs Custom Shop variant.
2. Serial number, when present.
3. Internal version marking only when available and documented.
4. Exterior graphics, knob-area markings, enclosure wording, and LED/colorway.
5. Edition relationship, such as CME black, red limited, Custom Shop v1.4, OCD-Ge, or Gold Standard.
6. Production date evidence from surviving paperwork or documented internal date marks.

Do not infer an exact production version solely from a modern reseller title. Contemporary and manufacturer evidence should outrank collector shorthand.
