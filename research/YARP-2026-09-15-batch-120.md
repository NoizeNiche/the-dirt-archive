# YARP 2026-09-15 Batch 120: CS.Cathey Mid-1970s Japanese Effects

## Builder identity
- **Cathey Co., Ltd. (株式会社キャシー / Cathey)**, Tokyo, Japan, marketed effects under the **CS.Cathey / C.S. Series** identity.
- Current specialist catalog evidence identifies CS.Cathey as a Japanese effects and electronic musical-equipment brand active during the mid-1970s.
- Do not confuse this company with unrelated namesakes. The pedal evidence specifically attributes the C.S. Series to Cathey Co., Ltd., Tokyo.

## Historical notes
- The AS-01 is described in period advertising as a “Soft Fuzz” product and as a contemporary popular effect.
- The AD-02 is described as a Distortion Box and explicitly framed around a return to the origins of fuzz, making it central to the Dirt Archive rather than merely a generic effect.
- The four-model series appears in *Music Life* during a short documented window from October 1975 through April 1976.
- The AS-01 became identifiable decades later because one appeared in the 2003 Yura Yura Teikoku “Tsumetai Gift” music video; that discovery is secondary evidence of survival, not the basis for the model chronology.

## Manufacturing / attribution boundary
- **CANONICAL_BUILDER_CANDIDATE / CORPORATE IDENTITY IDENTIFIED.** Cathey Co., Ltd. is identified as the producer in the current specialist Effects Database record.
- The present research does not establish whether Cathey manufactured every electronic subassembly in-house or contracted any portion to another Japanese factory.
- Do not infer a Melos, Elk, Maxon/Nisshin Onpa, Matsumoku, Teisco, or Fujigen OEM relationship from similar knobs, enclosures, or circuits without separate model-level evidence.
