# YARP 2026-09-15 Batch 84: Fi-Sonic Australian Fuzz Line

## Builder identity
Fi-Sonic is treated as a distinct Australian amplifier/electronics builder. Effects Database identifies the company as founded by Phil Dreoni in 1959 in Marrickville, Sydney, with the business sold in 1969 to a larger company that concentrated on consumer products.

## Dirt catalog
- Fuzz Blender: early Fi-Sonic fuzz product. Specialist newspaper research found advertisements in Australian publications from September and December 1965, establishing a strong documentary floor for the product. A surviving amp-top example is treated as an early family state, with exact introduction date still conservatively unresolved.
- Fuzz Blender MkIV: separate later production state documented as an Australian Fi-Sonic fuzz product. Existing specialist evidence identifies a surviving MkIV and dates the broader family to the 1960s.

## Chronology posture
The December 23, 1965 Sydney Morning Herald advertisement is particularly valuable because it is contemporary evidence that Fi-Sonic was selling a fuzz product under the Fuzz Blender name. Earlier owner recollections are retained as anecdotal rather than used to move the chronology earlier than the documentary evidence.

## Attribution posture
Fi-Sonic remains the marketed/manufacturing brand node for the family. No external OEM factory is asserted from design resemblance alone.
