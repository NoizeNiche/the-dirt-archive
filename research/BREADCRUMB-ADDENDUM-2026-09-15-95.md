### Findings

- Applied Fuzz and Treble Booster FT-100 has a stronger early-1968 catalog anchor through two St. Louis Music Supply advertisements.
- Lou Rose Combo-Fuzz is retained as a high-confidence Applied OEM/store-branded state around 1968, with the September 1968 Lou Rose Music Center opening providing a useful period anchor.
- Conrad Fuzzy Fury is separated from the Conrad Bass Hawk and Sky Hawk as individual marketed states, while all three remain linked to the Applied branch by model-level evidence.
- Giulietti Fuzz and Treble Booster remains MEDIUM / RESEARCH_ONLY because the present evidence establishes strong Applied-family characteristics but not a surviving model-specific factory document.
- Scott Music Supply remains MEDIUM / RESEARCH_ONLY because the surviving Scott-branded example may have acquired its logo later; the 1968 trade-show evidence identifies Scott Music Supply as a Louisiana wholesaler but does not itself prove pedal manufacture.
- The Applied / Trans-Tek relationship is recorded as a shared-facility or associate-organization relationship. The evidence does not justify attributing physical pedal assembly to Trans-Tek unless a model-specific source says so.
