# YARP 2026-09-15 Batch 118: Jujiya / Jeugia FB-1

## Builder / marketed identity
- **Jujiya Musical Instruments / Jujiya (十字屋)**, Kyoto, Japan.
- Current corporate history identifies the business as founded in Kyoto in 1898 and later renamed JEUGIA in 1990. This establishes Jujiya as a long-running Kyoto musical-instrument retailer, not by itself as a factory.
- A June 1968 issue of *Music Life* carries an advertisement for the **FB-1 Fuzz Box**, sold through Jujiya Musical Instruments for ¥10,000 and marketed as a professional fuzz box.

## Catalog signal
- **FB-1 Fuzz Box**: documented by June 1968.
- Two controls: Volume and Gain.
- Wedge-shaped housing strongly resembling the Mosrite Fuzzrite form factor.
- No second Jujiya-branded dirt product has been located in the present search.

## Manufacturing confidence
- **FACTORY_UNRESOLVED.** The available evidence supports Jujiya as the marketed/selling identity and a Kyoto-origin product, but does not identify the physical factory.
- Specialist analysis notes unusual switch, knob, and enclosure details that do not cleanly match the standardized construction of better-documented Shin-Ei, Maxon/Nisshin Onpa, or other Japanese export families.
- Do not assign the FB-1 to Maxon/Nisshin Onpa merely because of general late-1960s Japanese wedge-fuzz similarity.

## Historical significance
The FB-1 is valuable as evidence that Kyoto's established musical-instrument trade was offering professional-grade fuzz by mid-1968, while the manufacturing chain remained opaque. It should remain a marketed-brand/product node until physical production evidence appears.
