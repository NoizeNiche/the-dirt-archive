## Evidence notes
Pedal Tank's first-party site establishes the Thailand operation and current pedal catalog, while Effects Database documents Songpol Teeramanud, the 2008 founding date, and historically important hand-built dirt models such as BBlues and Delicate 2. Keep Delicate versus Delicate 2 and G-2 versus G2V5 unresolved until a fuller chronology audit is performed.

M.V. Electronics has unusually strong object-level evidence despite a thin surviving primary web presence. Contemporary Philippine documentation describes the Shredhead as hand built, numbered and signed; current Philippine retail coverage preserves the Shredhead LE Devastator name. Preserve Mark V as the builder attribution and M.V. Electronics as the commercial label pending primary-source identity evidence.

Sunless Effects is an active Taiwan-based shop with a first-party storefront currently listing Monolith, Dark Ages, Silencer, The Forest, Cold Lands, The Winter V2 and Astronaut V2. Earlier FXDB documentation records The Winter, Astronaut, Hybrid Machine, Green100 and the 2017 start date. Treat this as a strong continuity case with explicit model generations.

Jubilee Custom Shop is a Taipei one-person/small-run project by David. Effects Database documents Centurion, Taroko Gorge, Grand Hotel and Samurai; recent 2026 reporting adds unconventional custom effects built from vintage telephones. The archive should distinguish repeatable named models from one-off object conversions and custom pieces.

Goosoniqueworx is a historically significant Singapore one-man operation by Ravi Goose. Effects Database provides a builder interview and chronology from 2002 onward, including MangoJuice, Pixie, Fudge 73, Seventheaven / 7thvn and Kult. Later retrospective sources indicate the maker's most active period was in the 2010s and sourcing became difficult; catalog it as a historical builder rather than assume present activity.

## Boundary / scope notes
- Clone-derived products remain cataloged as products when the builder identity is genuine; circuit ancestry is recorded separately from builder identity.
- Custom and one-off object conversions are not allowed to inflate a stable model count without evidence of a repeatable product series.
- Utility hardware and non-dirt accessories stay outside the central dirt inventory unless the gain/fuzz/distortion role is intrinsic to the product.
- G4 Metal Gate and Grand Hotel remain candidate rows until category and product-series evidence improves.
- Preserve historical builders even when inactive because Phase One is a census, not an active-maker directory.

## Photo workflow
Each inventory row has a product/catalog or surviving marketplace/retail page carrying imagery or object-level documentation. These URLs are evidence trails for later specimen capture, crediting and rights review, not automatic republication permission.

## Scope discipline
No schematics, PCB layouts, gutshots, cloning instructions, or complete BOM data are published.
