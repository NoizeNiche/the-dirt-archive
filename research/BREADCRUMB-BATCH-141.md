# Batch 141 Breadcrumb

## Governing priority
**Builder → Pedals → Photos → Information on the pedal**

## Builder covered
URMA Sound, Japanese handmade effects brand established in 2024.

## Builder identity
Official URMA Sound About page states the brand was established in Japan in 2024 and describes the pedals as fully hand-wired in air without PCBs. It also states that acetone-transfer artwork creates intentional unit-to-unit exterior variation. This supports a canonical builder record rather than a mere retail/reseller identity.

## Pedals promoted
- Like A ENIGMA — central; current v4.5 is treated as a production state/version, not a new conceptual family.
- 16D-1A — central; overdrive/fuzz-border distortion inspired by the Red Llama concept.
- ZFZ-1A — central; silicon fuzz modernizing the Zonk-2 concept.
- Japanium Fuzz 312/100 — central-pending because the current official page identifies it as a prototype/development-stage product.

## Relationship-only / outside central dirt

- RGM-1A — Rangemaster-type booster.
- SHD-1A — Super Hard On-derived booster.
- Elec Dist — sold through URMA Sound shop but explicitly labeled CynicalPop fx; retained relationship-only pending clearer builder/brand separation.
