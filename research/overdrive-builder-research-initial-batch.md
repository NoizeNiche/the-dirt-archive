## Way Huge
**Catalog entries:** 41

Dunlop's current and legacy catalogs preserve Way Huge models, including overdrive, fuzz, and combination effects. Dunlop's WHE205OD page identifies the Way Huge Overdrive as the Saucy Box Overdrive in a new enclosure/skin and describes its blend of clean and overdrive paths.

Primary source: https://www.jimdunlop.com/way-huge-overdrive/

## Keeley Electronics
**Catalog entries:** 39

Keeley Electronics says it began with Robert Keeley at a kitchen table and grew into a larger Oklahoma manufacturing operation. Its history describes a mix of original circuit design, hand work, automated production steps, testing, repair, and continued refinement. The company explicitly groups its products into Boost + Drive and Distortion & Fuzz families.

Primary sources:
- https://robertkeeley.com/about-keeley-electronics/
- https://robertkeeley.com/

## Ibanez
**Catalog entries:** 36

Ibanez's official site maintains the Tube Screamer as a major product family with multiple versions. Independent historical sources document that early Ibanez effects were produced by Nisshin/Maxon and that Susumu Tamura designed the Tube Screamer circuit in 1979. This builder record should distinguish Ibanez branding from OEM/manufacturing lineage where evidence supports it.

Primary sources:
- https://www.ibanez.com/na/products/model/tube_screamer/
- https://www.ibanez.com/eu/products/detail/ts808_99.html
- https://www.vintageguitar.com/1762/ibanez-overdrives/

## J. Rockett Audio Designs
**Catalog entries:** 33

J. Rockett says Chris Van Tassel and Jay Rockett formed the company in 2006 after long careers as session musicians and recording-industry professionals. The company states that its pedals are built in California and emphasizes player-oriented design, steel enclosures, serviceability, and a mix of vintage inspiration with modern functionality. Its current store separately lists several overdrives.

Primary sources:
- https://rockettpedals.com/about-us/
- https://rockettpedals.com/shop-list/

## Wampler Pedals
**Catalog entries:** 32

Wampler states that Brian Wampler began by modding pedals in his garage and developed that work into a boutique effects company. The company describes its current products as including overdrive, distortion, and fuzz and provides manuals/quick guides for its pedal lineup. The archive should track signature relationships and model revisions as separate historical records when source evidence supports them.

Primary sources:
- https://www.wamplerpedals.com/about/
- https://www.wamplerpedals.com/downloads/

## Lovepedal
**Catalog entries:** 29

Lovepedal's official history identifies Sean Michael as the designer and traces his pedal-circuit work to the early 1990s. It highlights the FAB 50, COT 50/Church of Tone, and Eternity as important lineage points and notes the introduction of the Mini Line in 2009. Lovepedal explicitly describes a 'less is more' aesthetic and a hand-built boutique history.

Primary source: https://www.lovepedal.com/history/
