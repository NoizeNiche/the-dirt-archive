# Menatone, 1996-2000 catalog audit

## Identity
Menatone identifies itself as a handmade-effects company operating since 1996. Its own historical pages provide unusually useful launch dates for the earliest products.

## Early dirt catalog
- Red Snapper: original Menatone pedal; debuted in 1996 in three-knob form.
- Blue Collar: second Menatone design; debuted in 1997 after a request for a Red Snapper with more gain and compression.
- Red Snapper 4-knob: later generation adding a hi-cut control; keep distinct from original three-knob specimens.
- King of the Britains / The King lineage: British-amp-oriented gain family; retain later current versions separately from original-era production states.
- Top Boost in a Can and other later products should not be retroactively assigned 1990s production dates without period evidence.

## Evidence disposition
The 1996 and 1997 anchors are strong because they come directly from Menatone's own product-history pages. Current pedals may preserve an older design but still represent a later production state, so the archive should model original launch, intermediate revisions, and current revival separately where evidence permits.
