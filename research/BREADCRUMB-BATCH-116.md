## Photos

Every new inventory record carries a product or specimen photo trail. First-party pages are preferred where available; specialist Japanese dealer and historical database pages are retained where they materially improve object identification. Photo URLs are evidence trails, not automatic republication permission.

The strongest photographic corridors in this batch include HSW's surviving catalog pages, KGR HARMONY's current ARARE presentation, A.Y.A's current Drivesta specimens, Tanabe's official product pages and surviving Sunkudo specimen photography, and the current monica product record.

## Information on the pedal

- HONDA SOUND WORKS is represented as a historical boutique Japanese builder with a large surviving dirt catalog. Effects Database indexes 25 HSW products, including multiple Fujiyama generations, Fab Drive, Fab Cream and fuzz families. The archive keeps the Fujiyama variants connected as a family while preserving model-specific identities.
- KGR HARMONY's ARARE is documented as the first Nambu-tekki pedal release, following extended enclosure and circuit prototyping with Oitomi craftsmen. The distinctive cast-iron enclosure is part of the product's identification history rather than merely decoration.
- A.Y.A's Drivesta is documented as an overdrive/distortion with three-band EQ, clipping-mode switching and a buffer switch. Super Atomic Distortion is retained as a separate higher-gain product with three-way distortion/booster/overdrive behavior.
- TANABE.TV is a one-person builder operation. Toshihiko Tanabe's interview states that ZENKUDO began as a personal response to an unsatisfactory commercial drive in 2007, with hand-wired construction and individually varying natural-abalone tops. Official pages document ZENKUDO, DUMKUDO, May Queen, Santanabe and SUNKUDO FUZZ as order products.
- TANABE's DUMKUDO is a three-mode overdrive aimed at single-coil guitars; May Queen is voiced for Brian May-oriented tones; Santanabe is built around Carlos Santana-oriented voicing; SUNKUDO FUZZ uses three germanium transistors and is described as a controllable middle ground between fuzz and distortion.
- VIRTUES monica is retained as a current Japanese boutique overdrive with documented bipolar-transistor clipping and JFET-input architecture. Its product record is kept separate from dealer/distributor presentation states.

## Normalization decisions

- HSW Fujiyama variants are treated as a product family with separate model states rather than one undifferentiated pedal.
- A.Y.A SAD remains distinct from Drivesta because its switching, gain range and product identity differ materially.
- Tanabe customs, color variants and combined enclosures are not promoted to new conceptual models unless evidence establishes substantive circuit/product identity.
- Non-dirt KGR, Tanabe and A.Y.A products remain outside the central dirt list.
- No schematics, PCB layouts, gutshots or complete component inventories are collected.
