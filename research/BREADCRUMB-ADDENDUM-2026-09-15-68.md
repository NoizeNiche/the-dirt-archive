# Dirt Archive Breadcrumb 68

Date: 2026-09-15

## Batch

Added `research/universal-amplifier-astrotone-production-versions-32.tsv`.

## Scope

Audited the Universal Amplifier Corp. Astrotone / Sam Ash Fuzzz Boxx family for model-specific manufacture, parallel branding, production revisions, service documentation, and chronology.

## Findings

- Astrotone remains a high-confidence Universal Amplifier Corp. manufactured product. Surviving examples establish a February 1967 earliest known specimen and documented production evidence through 1969.
- The original Astrotone service documentation names Universal Amplifier Corp. at 615 West 131st Street, New York City, strengthening direct manufacturer attribution.
- Sam Ash Fuzzz Boxx is a high-confidence Universal Amplifier OEM / house-brand state. 1967 examples show the same PCB and production platform as Astrotone units.
- Astrotone and Sam Ash Fuzzz Boxx were produced in parallel during at least part of 1967. This is better represented as an OEM / marketed-label relationship than as a simple successor chain.
- The Sam Ash `Ashstrotone` / early enclosure state remains a separate production-state record because its precise position in the chronology is not fully resolved.
- February, March, May 1967, and 1969 evidence supports period-specific production revisions. The archive should preserve these states rather than flattening all Astrotone / Fuzzz Boxx examples into a single build.
- 1969 is currently the latest evidenced production year. The precise final production date and reason for discontinuation remain unresolved.
- No evidence from this pass justifies extending Universal Amplifier manufacture to unrelated East Coast fuzz labels.

## Editorial action

No public lineage edges promoted from this batch. Existing public lineage model remains unchanged.
