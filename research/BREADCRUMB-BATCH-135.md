## Research discipline
- Do not treat every finish, color, dealer exclusive or generation as a new conceptual pedal family.
- Preserve model names exactly where evidence supports them.
- Keep uncertain chronology and lineage in CENTRAL_PENDING rather than inventing dates.
- Photo references are exterior/reference evidence only. No gutshots, schematics, PCB diagrams, complete BOMs or cloning instructions.
- Japanese dealer pages were used for builder identity, product existence and exterior-photo trails; surviving specimen pages provide additional photographic evidence.

## Key external evidence
- Ishibashi: Pedal diggers handmade Japanese builder and Over Statement Special.
- Musicland KEY: 10 Ver.2, Perfect 5th, Over Statement Special relationship.
- Shimamura: maker index listing 10, 819 DLX, Cranked 45 Ver.2, Emotions, Super Natural and 819 finish variants.
- Reverb: 130 Fuzz specimen and Mouse specimen photography.
- Equipboard: broader builder/model corroboration, used cautiously as secondary evidence.
