# YARP Breadcrumb 135

## Focus
Expand the hard dirt-device qualification gate and verify that qualifying builders are represented in the public archive without collapsing brand/OEM relationships.

## Confirmed / promoted
- Seamoon Inc.: verified dirt maker via the 1973 Fresh Fuzz. Evidence supports an independent Berkeley effects company and hand-built early production.
- Carlsbro: verified dirt maker via the original 1966 Fuzz-Tone. Later Carlsbro fuzzes supplied by Sola Sound are kept as a separate manufacturing phase.
- Rosac / Sierra Electronics: qualification retained from the previous audit via Nu-Fuzz, Nu-Wa-Fuzz and Nu-Fuzz Distortion Blender.
- Aul Instruments: qualification retained via the Aul Instruments Fuzz and documented Guild Foxey Lady production.
- Chunk Systems: qualification retained via FZ002 and Brown Dog Gated Bass Fuzz.
- Montarbo: qualification retained via the Sinfhoton fuzz/distortion.
- Lemon StudioSound: qualification explicitly audited via Zuzz Thainer.
- Jennings Electronic Industries: qualification explicitly audited via the documented fuzz range.
- Morley / Tel-Ray Electronics: qualification explicitly audited via the Power Wah Fuzz PWF.
- fOXX / Ridinger Associates Inc.: qualification explicitly audited via the Tone Machine and Clean Machine lineage.

## Held back
- Kelly Amplification remains PROVISIONAL_DIRT_MAKER. A 1966 Kelly Fuzz Box advertisement exists, but physical manufacturing attribution remains unresolved between Kelly and a possible Macari/Sola Sound supply arrangement.

## Editorial consequence
The archive now treats dirt-maker qualification as a gate independent of relationship certainty. A builder may qualify even when OEM, distributor, designer, successor or marketed-brand relationships are unresolved, but a name cannot be promoted merely because it appears on a dirt pedal.

## Site consequence
A public qualification layer now adds verified dirt-maker metadata and missing qualifying builders without allowing duplicate builder names to be created from OEM or badge identities.

## Next queue
Continue screening obscure historical makers and micro-builders. Once a builder clears the dirt gate, stop digging its family tree until needed for catalog enumeration. Then move builder-by-builder into product inventory, where model families, generations, periods and specimens can be handled separately.
