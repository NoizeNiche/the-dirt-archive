# The Dirt Archive — Batch 140 Breadcrumb

## Governing priority
**Builder → Pedals → Photos → Information on the pedal**

## Batch focus
Japanese builder census, emphasizing a builder with a large documented dirt catalog rather than isolated flagship-pedal coverage.

## Builder added
**SHIN'S MUSIC** — canonical Japanese custom handmade effects builder, Tokyo.

## Core dirt catalog mapped
DUMBLOID; DUMBLOID TWIN; DOUBLE TROUBLE; FLOOD DRIVE; BROWN DRIVE; 308 DRIVE; MK-2 OVER DRIVE; PLATINUM DRIVE / PLD-1; 65 FUZZ; BOG FUZZ; FUNN FACE FUZZ; SPECTRON OCT FUZZ SPF-2; CLD1 CLEAN DRIVE (central-pending).

## Photo layer
Exterior/reference-photo trails are attached to product records through Effects Database, Japanese retailer listings, and catalog pages. Current searchable retail evidence includes DUMBLOID, MK-2 Over Drive, FUNN FACE FUZZ and 65 FUZZ. No internal/gutshot material is used.

## Normalization decisions
- DUMBLOID remains a product family, not a builder identity.
- Dealer customs, kimono/anniversary one-offs, color variants and signature finish records remain relationship-only unless substantive product changes are documented.
- BOOSTER models such as CLB1/Clean Boost, Mighty Boost and Platinum Boost remain outside central dirt.
- CLD1 Clean Drive is retained central-pending because its exact historical position relative to the DUMBLOID family needs stronger first-party chronology.

## Evidence anchors
- Effects Database DUMBLOID page identifies Shin's Music in Tokyo as builder and documents the model.
- Moridaira's 2025 Shin's Music catalog documents DOUBLE TROUBLE, FLOOD DRIVE, BROWN DRIVE and 308 DRIVE.
- Shin's 2020 catalog documents SPECTRON OCT FUZZ SPF-2.
- Ishibashi and Sound House current/searchable retail catalogs provide exterior product evidence and active/discontinued model trails.

## Exclusions
No schematics, PCB diagrams, gutshots, complete BOMs, cloning instructions, or circuit-reconstruction material added.

## Next target
Continue the Japanese builder/OEM census, favoring under-enumerated builders and manufacturer relationships where a whole dirt family can be mapped before deep individual pedal archaeology.
