## Evidence notes
Equipboard documents MP Custom FX as a boutique Belgrade builder operated by designer Milos Popovic, with hand-wired, largely analogue guitar and bass pedals and a Gainiac overdrive family. Reverb listings from the MP Custom FX shop explicitly identify Gainiac XL as hand soldered and made in Serbia, with independent controls for the low and high channels. Current Serbian marketplace listings document Gainiac Plus XL/Gold variants and attribute the products to Milos Popovic in Belgrade.

Soundwise's MP Custom FX catalog documents Gollum as a fully hand-built, dual-gain-stage overdrive with a Pat De Jonge signature relationship and also lists Gollum XL. A 2021 specialist article identifies Tesla Distortion Fuzz as an MP Custom FX product and reports a much earlier pedal-building history for Milos Popovic, saying he began making pedals in 1986 and hand-made circuit boards and metal casings. Because that claim is secondary and conflicts with the later MP Custom FX brand footprint, 1986 is recorded as a historical lead rather than a settled company founding date.

## Photo workflow
Every inventory row includes a product/catalog or marketplace source that can serve as a later photo/specimen breadcrumb. Reverb and Serbian marketplace listings provide direct specimen photography for several MP Custom FX variants; Soundwise and Equipboard provide catalog-level product imagery/links. These are evidence trails for later specimen capture, credit and rights review, not automatic permission to republish images.

## Scope discipline
Gainiac-family products are retained as overdrive/distortion records because their documented use spans clean boost, overdrive and higher-gain distortion. Gollum is retained as overdrive. Tesla Distortion Fuzz remains central dirt scope. Third-party clones, redesigned classics and custom copies are kept outside the original retail-family count.
