# The Dirt Archive — Batch 145 Breadcrumb

## Governing priority
**Builder → Pedals → Photos → Information on the pedal**

## Batch target
Japanese microbuilder / small-run manufacturer census, with emphasis on builders that have a real dirt product but limited public historical documentation.

## Added builders
**22ND CENTURY LLC / NIJUUNISEIKI** — retained as `CANONICAL_CANDIDATE` rather than fully canonical because available evidence is concentrated in Japanese retail/auction records rather than a substantial first-party history page.

**Fats Sound Laboratory** — retained as `CANONICAL_CANDIDATE`; first-party site identifies Hirokazu Kadowaki, a 30+ year analog circuit designer, and documents research, design and manufacture from a home workshop in Fujisawa, Kanagawa.

## Added pedals
**SILICON BABY** — central fuzz record. Surviving product documentation describes it as handmade in Japan and built to reproduce a deliberately depleted/dead-battery-style garage fuzz character. Exterior references exist from Yahoo Japan and eBay specimen listings.

**Fats Sound Laboratory** — central: Fats Drive, Super FatsDrive, Fats VooDoo, Fats Smoother, Fats Shonan Driver, Fats Accel, Fats Crunch DX. Central-pending: Fats Crunch. Relationship-only: Fats Boost and Fats Bullet.

## Evidence / normalization notes
- Do not treat retail sellers as manufacturers.
- `22nd century LLC` and `NIJUUNISEIKI LLC` are preserved as the same candidate identity because surviving listings explicitly equate the names.
- Fats Drive is preserved as a historical predecessor to Super FatsDrive, not as a duplicate family.
- Fats VooDoo is retained as its own production model while its documented derivation from Super FatsDrive is preserved as lineage.
- Fats Boost and Fats Bullet remain outside central dirt because the builder explicitly classifies them as booster/fixed-wah products.
- Image sources are exterior-only. No internal images are promoted.
- The 1960s/garage description for SILICON BABY is preserved as a product-design reference claim, not as a claim that the pedal itself dates to the 1960s.

## Next direction
Continue Japanese long-tail builder census and historical/OEM reconciliation before moving the primary frontier into Taiwan/Hong Kong/mainland China.
