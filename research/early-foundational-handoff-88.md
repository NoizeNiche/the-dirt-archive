# Early Foundational Handoff 88

## Pass 40: 1999-2004 boutique production clocks, early-2000s Japanese/U.S. bridges, and high-value specimen markers

Pass 40 advances the archive through the 1999-2004 boutique expansion while prioritizing production states that can be recognized from surviving pedals. The pass emphasizes manufacturer-authored chronology, explicit version boundaries, and builder-history layers that should not be confused with branded dirt models.

### Newly documented or clarified

- **Xotic AC Booster**: official history dates the first AC Booster production state to September 2002.
- **Xotic RC Booster**: official anniversary history places sale in December 2002, while noting the possibility of slightly earlier U.S. circulation.
- **Xotic RC Booster earliest specimen state**: engraved serials, early lettering, 16mm pot and a gray epoxy substrate are documented as early-state identifiers.
- **Xotic RC Booster early serial boundary**: Xotic states that the product changed gradually but not substantially until roughly serial 5,000, with another documented transition around 2005-2006.
- **Blackstone Mosfet Overdrive 2Sv1**: official documentation dates the rear-control 2Sv1 to 1999 and preserves its unusual lack of a top-panel version marking.
- **Blackstone Mosfet Overdrive 2Sv2**: official documentation dates the 2Sv2 to 2000 and distinguishes it by top controls and a 2SV2 nameplate.
- **Analog Man King of Tone**: first sold in November 2003; the original production state is kept separate from later versions.
- **Analog Man King of Tone Version 3**: late-2004 generation adds separate Drive controls and is preserved as a clear external-layout boundary.
- **Analog Man Sun Face**: late-2004 NKT275 tightening/brightening was made standard after customer response, giving the early Sun Face lineage a documented production-era setting change.
- **Hermida Audio Technology Mosferatu**: Alfonso Hermida identifies 2003 as the year of his first pedal.
- **Hermida Audio Technology Zendrive**: the first unit sent to Robben Ford in 2003 had three knobs, creating an important early Zendrive specimen state before the later Voice control.
- **Skreddy Pedals Mayonaise**: September 2004 is documented as the first Skreddy production state, with independent interview confirmation that the Mayonaise was the first Skreddy product.
- **Creation Audio Labs**: company begins in 2003 as a repair/restoration operation; the first pedal-market entry arrives later in 2005, so the founding is retained only as a scope-bound builder chronology record.

### Key research findings

1. **Specimen-level detail is becoming unusually strong.** Xotic and Blackstone both provide physical or control-layout markers that can separate early production states without exposing circuit reconstruction.
2. **The early-2000s boutique market is international.** Xotic's U.S./Japan development path and Providence's earlier Japanese chronology now sit alongside Japanese BIXONIC and Sobbat records.
3. **King of Tone has a clean production clock.** November 2003 first sale and the late-2004 Version 3 change give the archive a compact but useful early lineage.
4. **The modification/recreation culture continues into new brands.** Hermida's Mosferatu and Zendrive show how a builder could move from a first experimental pedal into a highly recognizable model family.
5. **The 2004 boundary is becoming a useful bridge.** Skreddy's first pedal appears in September 2004, while Creation Audio Labs is still in a pre-pedal company phase until 2005.
6. **Production dating must stay evidence-class aware.** Xotic's late-2002 RC Booster sale date is stronger than a marketplace listing but still allows earlier U.S. circulation; this is exactly the kind of uncertainty the archive should preserve rather than erase.

### Evidence discipline

- Manufacturer-authored histories are preferred for launch and production clocks.
- Builder interviews are treated as firsthand evidence for personal/product chronology.
- Specialist databases and period material corroborate but do not silently replace primary evidence.
- Physical markers are documented when useful for identification, but no schematics, PCB layouts, complete BOMs, gutshot libraries or cloning instructions are added.
- Scope-boundary builder records remain outside the central dirt count when the product itself is not dirt.

### Current lineage position

The archive now has a continuous bridge from 1990s boutique pioneers through the 2003-2004 acceleration: BIXONIC, Providence, Sobbat, Menatone and Lovepedal establish the earlier boutique field; Xotic, Blackstone, Analog Man and Hermida demonstrate mature early-2000s identity families; Skreddy marks the 2004 vintage-recreation resurgence; and Creation Audio Labs shows the continued overlap between pro-audio repair culture and later pedal manufacturing.
