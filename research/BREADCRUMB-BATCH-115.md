## Information on the pedal

- SHIGEMORI RUBY STONE is documented as a Japanese handmade overdrive and as a downsized evolution of STONEDRIVE; surviving dealer records also document its machined high-purity aluminum enclosure.
- SHIGEMORI MUSE is a lower-gain-focused drive with an added BASS control and is documented as an evolution of RUBY STONE.
- SHINOS Naughty Brain is a Marshall-style drive with broad gain range and TIGHT/DEEP voicing; SHINOS documents a 2023 refresh that reduced the enclosure size while retaining the original sound concept.
- SHINOS The Blast is a transparent-style natural overdrive with TIGHT/DEEP switching and sufficient gain for standalone distortion or amp boosting.
- SHINOS Alice The Goon is a British-style medium-to-high-gain drive covering natural drive through deep distortion.
- TDC-YOU 007 CREAMY DRIVE uses a documented JRC4558DV vintage-version op-amp and vintage germanium transistor matching, with three-band EQ.
- TDC-YOU 005 CLASSIC LEAD likewise documents premium/vintage parts, three-band EQ and a soft-distortion design target.
- TDC-YOU 001 CRUNCH BOOSTER HOT TONE is retained as a current dirt/boost catalog record but needs deeper historical and specimen work before chronology is treated as settled.

## Normalization decisions

- 007 CREAMY DRIVE RED is preserved as a relationship-only color/presentation record unless evidence demonstrates a substantive circuit or product-generation difference.
- SHINOS boost-only products are not being promoted into the dirt core merely because the builder also makes dirt pedals.
- TDC-YOU DI and utility products remain outside the dirt core unless their primary documented function is distortion/overdrive/fuzz.
- No schematics, PCB layouts, gutshots or complete component inventories are collected.
