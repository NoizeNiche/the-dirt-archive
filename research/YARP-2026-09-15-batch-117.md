# YARP 2026-09-15 Batch 117: Kelly Amplification Fuzz Box

## Builder node
**Kelly Amplification, United Kingdom**

## Identity
Small British amplifier manufacturer founded by John Kelly Sr., a former Selmer service/repair employee, with his son John Kelly Jr. and reportedly other former Selmer staff. The company operated as a relatively small, cottage-scale amplifier builder in the late 1960s and early 1970s.

## Dirt catalog
- **Kelly Amplification Fuzz Box**: advertised in Melody Maker on 21 May 1966 at £10.
- No surviving example is currently known in the principal historical reference used here.
- No verified model number, quantity, schematic identity, or complete production chronology.

## Manufacturer/OEM question
This is a critical attribution boundary. John Kelly Jr. recalled his father working with Larry Macari during the early Kelly period. Early Kelly amplifiers were reportedly boxed/branded through Macari's Musical Exchange, and the same arrangement may have applied to the fuzz box.

Two possibilities therefore remain open:
1. Kelly designed/built the fuzz and Macari marketed or distributed it.
2. The fuzz was a Macari/Sola Sound design supplied under the Kelly name.

No current evidence justifies selecting one explanation as fact.

## Historical significance
Kelly adds a genuine British micro-manufacturer to the 1966 fuzz explosion and helps connect the Selmer repair/service workforce to the emerging independent effects trade. The dated advertisement makes this more than a surviving-pedal attribution guess.
