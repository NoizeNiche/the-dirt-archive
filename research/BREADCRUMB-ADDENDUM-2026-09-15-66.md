# Breadcrumb Addendum 66

Date: 2026-09-15

## Research batch

File: `research/east-coast-fuzz-andre-chronology-30.tsv`

This batch separated the late-1960s Andre Fuzzier identity from the later formal Andre Audio-Tronics corporate/trademark record. The ANDRE trademark filing documents first use on August 1, 1973 and covers electronic sound-effect devices including fuzz tone, reverb, tremolo, phase shifter, vibrator and mixers. That evidence supports the existence and product scope of Andre Audio-Tronics in the 1970s, but it does not backdate the corporate identity onto the earlier East Coast Fuzzier. Gene Andre's earlier Ampeg, Oliver Sound and 1969 independent-workshop chronology remains a separate biographical/factory clue.

The Halifax ambiguity was also preserved rather than merged. East Coast sources place Halifax Fuzz inside the Manny's/Clark/E.U. Wurlitzer family, while a separate West German Höfner Buzz Tone line was also marketed as Halifax Buzz Tone. A specialist repair thread documents a 3-knob Halifax example closely matching the Manny's/Orpheum construction family, supporting that specimen's East Coast classification without establishing a factory.

Tim's Own received another dated-specimen chronology entry for 1968. MGI remains an East Coast family identity without a builder attribution. The East Coast Mica-Tone identity remains explicitly distinct from the Japanese FY-6-related Mica Tone product.

## Catalog discipline

- Do not backdate Andre Audio-Tronics corporate identity to the late 1960s.
- Do not merge East Coast Halifax with German Höfner Halifax-branded examples.
- Do not merge East Coast Mica-Tone with Japanese FY-6-derived Mica Tone.
- Family resemblance remains evidence of relationship, not proof of common physical manufacture.
- No public lineage edges promoted in this batch.
