# YARP 2026-09-15 Batch 85: French Baby Fuzz / Distor Wha

## Builder identity
The Baby Fuzz and Distor Wha are treated as a probable French micro-builder lineage with unresolved maker identity. Specialist historical research corrected an earlier Japanese attribution and places the products in France, most likely around 1970.

## Dirt catalog
- Baby Fuzz: rare French fuzz/distortion product, probably around 1970. The surviving example is too scarce to establish production quantity or exact commercial launch.
- Distor Wha: closely related French fuzz-wah product with matching typography, treadle construction details and other visible manufacturing characteristics. Specialist comparison strongly supports a common maker with Baby Fuzz.

## Evidence posture
Country of manufacture is evidence-qualified by the surviving Distor Wha marking and the relationship between the two products. Manufacturer identity remains unresolved. The products could represent a tiny independent production run, a local electronics shop, or an obscure French amplifier/electronics brand; no firm attribution is asserted.

## Historical significance
This lineage is valuable because it demonstrates that late-1960s/early-1970s French dirt production was not limited to the better-documented Garen ecosystem. The Archive preserves the pair as a distinct unresolved builder candidate rather than forcing them into Garen, JEN, or another known factory.
