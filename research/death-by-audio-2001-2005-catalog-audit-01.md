# Death By Audio — 2001–2005 Catalog Audit

## Status
**Builder entity:** CANONICAL
**Period:** 2001–2005 early workshop era
**Confidence:** HIGH for origin and early-business chronology; product-by-product dates remain specimen-sensitive.

## Historical spine
Death By Audio grew from Oliver Ackermann's early pedal-building and customization work. Death By Audio's own history dates the company to 2002, while contemporary interviews describe the pedal business opening around 2001–2002. Treat **2001–2002 as an origin window**, not a falsely precise founding day.

Premier Guitar reports that Ackermann began with a single pedal, **Total Sonic Annihilation**, then accepted custom orders and expanded the line. Death By Audio's own history describes the company as originally a web-based effects customization service before the dedicated pedal line developed.

In 2003 Ackermann moved to Brooklyn. In 2005 the operation moved into the large Williamsburg warehouse that became the well-known Death By Audio workspace/factory/venue. The warehouse period is an important production-context boundary for later specimen research.

- Fuzz War — early flagship fuzz/noise model; date should be treated separately from founding date until specimen evidence is attached.
- Supersonic Fuzz Gun — early noise/fuzz family; chronology requires period catalog/specimen evidence.
- Interstellar Overdriver — early drive/boost family; chronology requires period catalog/specimen evidence.
- Apocalypse — later early-era model; do not backdate to the founding year without evidence.

## Research cautions
Death By Audio is unusually vulnerable to date compression because the company began through custom work and word-of-mouth rather than a conventional catalog launch. Keep custom one-offs, modified customer pedals, named products, and later standardized production models as separate entities.

Do not treat the modern website's complete pedal archive as proof that every named model existed in 2001–2005. Future work should use period ads, Wayback captures, forum posts, interviews, dated dealer listings, and surviving specimens to establish earliest/latest bounds.
