# YARP 2026-09-15 Batch 26

## Focus
Arbiter's early compact dirt/boost catalog and the distinction between pre-merger Arbiter products and later Dallas-Arbiter corporate states.

## Findings

### Arbiter Fuzz Face
The surviving period record establishes the Fuzz Face as an Arbiter product beginning in 1966. Fuzzboxes documents multiple Arbiter/Dallas-Arbiter production states, including pre-Dallas Arbiter England branding and later Dallas-Arbiter England branding after the 1968 merger. The archive treats these as one product family with separate corporate/production-state records rather than separate pedal families.

### Arbiter Treble & Bass Face
A June 3, 1967 Melody Maker advertisement lists the **Treble & Bass Face** alongside the Fuzz Face and describes it as a three-way boost unit offering treble, bass and additional volume. The ad places the product at Arbiter's Gerrard Street, London address. Later historical coverage places the product in the 1966-1967 corridor and notes surviving examples through the pre-Dallas Arbiter branding period. Exact discontinuation remains unresolved, so the archive uses a broad 1966-1968 working corridor.

### Corporate/branding transition
Fuzzboxes documents the 1968 Arbiter/Dallas merger and the resulting Dallas-Arbiter branding transition. This is important for the archive because the Fuzz Face should not be interpreted as a Dallas-developed 1966 pedal simply because later examples bear Dallas-Arbiter branding. The development and early retail identity belong to Arbiter; the corporate/production state changes after the merger.

### Sato component supplier
Fuzzboxes identifies Sato Parts Co. in Japan as the source of the distinctive dial knobs used on some early Arbiter Fuzz Faces and Treble & Bass Faces. This is retained as a component-supplier relationship, not as evidence of Japanese pedal manufacture.

## Research boundary
The batch does not attempt to infer internal circuit ancestry, publish schematics, or assign undocumented factory labor. It records only historically supported product, branding and supply-chain relationships.
