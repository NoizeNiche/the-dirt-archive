# YARP 2026-09-15 Batch 48

## Focus
Foxx/Ridinger related marketed states that carry the dirt-bearing Fuzz & Wa family.

## Findings
Effects Database records the Ibanez Fuzz & Wa as made by Foxx and lists Frogg Fuzz Wa as a later Foxx-branded state. The same Foxx family index links these with the Fuzz & Wa & Volume variants sold under multiple labels.

The archive therefore keeps the dirt-bearing Ibanez and Frogg products as marketed/OEM relationship records under Ridinger Associates / fOXX, rather than creating separate physical manufacturers. Pure wah or wah/volume-only products are retained only as contextual family members unless a dirt function is documented.

ToneHome independently identifies fOXX effects as made by Ridinger Associates Inc. in Chatsworth, California, in the early 1970s.

## Evidence boundary
The presence of a Foxx-like enclosure or feature set is not by itself enough for universal factory attribution. The relationships above are retained because the cited specialist catalog explicitly identifies the Foxx manufacturing connection.
