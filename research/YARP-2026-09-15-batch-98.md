# YARP 2026-09-15 Batch 98: Sunn Buzz Catalog Audit

## Scope
Builder-first chronology audit for Sunn's Buzz fuzz/distortion product.

## Findings
- Sunn Buzz is an own-brand Sunn effects product, not an Applied, UMI, or Japanese OEM record.
- Specialist historical research places a small prototype run in 1966, followed by the SB-1G production redesign in 1967.
- Sunn's 1967 brochure lists the SB-1G, and historical catalog references continue into the 1969 period.
- The Archive treats the prototype and SB-1G as separate production states inside one Sunn Buzz family.
- Exact first shipment and final sales date remain less secure than the 1967 catalog anchor, so 1966 remains a prototype/early availability corridor rather than a blanket production date.
