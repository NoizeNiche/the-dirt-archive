## Organizational rules reinforced
- Original product introduction date, revision date, generation date and edition date are distinct fields.
- Separate appearance identity, production identity and electronics identity.
- Do not equate a V2 label with a circuit revision unless evidence supports it.
- A limited edition remains linked to its parent product/generation unless it is a genuinely distinct product.
- Compound pedals need parent/child relationships for underlying product families.
- Modern preservation is explicitly allowed to advance to variant depth before historical census work is complete when evidence is abundant but potentially fragile.

## Next target

Capture controls, enclosure, jacks, switches, firmware/hardware revisions, production dates, limited editions and other exterior identification clues. Preserve modern evidence before returning to older decades.
