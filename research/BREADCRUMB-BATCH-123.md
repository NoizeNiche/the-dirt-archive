## Information on the pedal

- The VIRTUES shop currently documents monica as the flagship overdrive and Stella as a distortion, including the builder's use of bipolar-transistor clipping and LED combination clipping in Stella.
- PEDAL SHOP CULT's Stella record explicitly identifies Virtues' 2019 debut as Monica and Lander, establishing Lander as the second core model and Stella as the subsequent new model. Stella dealer documentation identifies the early ProCo RAT / Fringe Logo units as an initial development inspiration.
- Lander is documented as a Fuzz Face-family pedal. CULT's Lander limited history distinguishes iss.1 as a 1966-68 germanium reference, iss.2 as a 1969-73 silicon reference, and iss.3 as a combined/refined version. These remain edition relationships rather than separate conceptual families.
- Current arca dealer documentation describes arca as a deliberately translucent overdrive rather than a fully transparent design, with a gain range extending from clean through classical distortion territory.

## Normalization decisions

- The master census's prior single-model VIRTUES signal was not treated as complete simply because monica was already present. The builder now has a four-model central dirt map.
- CULT limited editions are retained for historical/specimen identification but are subordinate to their parent products unless stronger evidence establishes an independent conceptual family.
- Artist signature and commemorative/presentation states are kept relationship-only.
- Carla remains a scope-boundary booster record and is not counted as central dirt.
- No schematics, PCB layouts, gutshots, complete BOMs or cloning instructions are collected.

## Master census note

The large master census file was not rewritten in this batch because the repository's currently exposed copy is too large for safe whole-file replacement through the available connector response limits. The canonical VIRTUES update is therefore recorded in `research/builder-census-batch-123.tsv` and should be merged into the master ledger during a controlled future reconciliation rather than risking truncation or accidental loss of prior rows.
