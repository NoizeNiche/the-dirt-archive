# YARP Breadcrumb 138

## Focus
Continue hard dirt-device qualification while separating genuine makers from brand, retail, distributor, and unresolved OEM identities.

## Confirmed
- fOXX / Ridinger Associates Inc. is a verified dirt maker through the Liverpool Fuzz-Tone. Steve Ridinger states that he made early units himself beginning around 1966 and later used an assembly company; specialist and period sources document commercial production.
- No new builder node was created for Ridinger Associates because the archive already carries the fOXX / Ridinger lineage. The relationship is treated as one builder identity with an early product phase.

## Held / rejected
- Paul Lucas / Tridents remains a provisional custom-maker lead because the present evidence supports a Jeff Beck commission but not a broader pedal-making operation.
- Clifford Musical Supplies remains rejected as a builder because advertising alone does not establish manufacture.
- Kelly Amplification remains provisional because the 1966 fuzz advertisement does not resolve physical manufacture.

## Research principle
The archive qualifies a builder from evidence of making/producing a dirt device. Design, sale, distribution, importation, branding, or association with an OEM product does not independently establish builder status.
