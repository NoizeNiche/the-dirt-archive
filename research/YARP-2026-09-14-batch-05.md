# YARP Batch 05 — Canonical Relationship Index

Research date: 2026-09-14

## Purpose
Convert scattered OEM, private-label, distributor, parent-company and manufacturing notes into an explicit relationship layer that can later drive public lineage displays without creating duplicate builders.

## Completed
- Added `canonical-relationship-index-01.tsv`.
- Recorded high-confidence manufacturer/marketed-brand transitions for the early British Tone Bender corridor.
- Recorded model-specific cautions for JEN-made Vox V828 versus UK Sola Sound-built Tone Bender states.
- Separated Mosrite-built and EHX-built Guild Foxey Lady production states.
- Added Japanese OEM relationship nodes for Shin-Ei, Honey, Univox, Nisshin Onpa/Maxon, Ibanez and Greco.
- Added corporate/ownership lineage treatment for DOD/DigiTech, BOSS/Roland and MXR/Jim Dunlop.

## Key editorial rules reinforced
1. A retail brand is not automatically the physical manufacturer.
2. One retail model can have more than one manufacturer over time.
3. One physical OEM can produce multiple marketed brands without those brands becoming duplicate builders.
4. A reissue or revival is a production-state relationship, not proof of uninterrupted original manufacture.
5. Factory attribution remains model-specific where the documentary record conflicts.

## Evidence highlights
- Sola Sound supplied Tone Bender-derived products for Vox, Marshall and Rotosound; Marshall later assumed Supa Fuzz manufacture.
- Vox V828 Tone Bender is documented as JEN-made in Italy and should not be collapsed into UK Sola Sound production.
- Rotosound's dirt history includes Sola Sound-built states and a brief Jennings relationship.
- Guild Foxey Lady has documented Mosrite-built and later Electro-Harmonix-supplied states; those must remain distinct.
