# Modern Preservation Track — Batch 20

## Position
2023 census continues. This batch prioritizes products with unusually strong primary-source evidence and deliberately separates historical lineage from product identity.

## Verified / strong primary evidence

### BOSS — BP-1W Booster/Preamp
- Official BOSS announcement dated October 26, 2023.
- New Waza Craft product, not a CE-1 or RE-201 variant.
- Three modes: CE, RE and a clean boost.
- Gain and Level controls.
- Rear selectable buffer types.
- Historical lineage is preserved as source inspiration: BOSS CE-1 Chorus Ensemble and Roland RE-201 Space Echo preamp sections.
- Archive rule: lineage/inspiration is not treated as parent-product identity.

### Fender — Shields Blender
- Official Fender launch announcement dated June 13, 2023.
- Limited Edition signature product developed with Kevin Shields over four years.
- Fender states it was built around a circuit traced from Shields' personal 1970s Fender Blender.
- Two footswitchable fuzz channels, blendable clean/fuzz or suboctave fuzz, octave control and reactive sag circuit.
- Brushed aluminum, 1970s-inspired chassis with top-mounted jacks.
- Archive rule: Limited Edition and signature status are preserved as edition/product attributes, not automatically as a Fender Blender generation.

### Great Eastern FX Co. — Focus Fuzz
- 2023 product documented in contemporary GPX coverage.
- Hybrid germanium/silicon fuzz architecture and three main controls: Fuzz, Level and Focus.
- GPX identifies the product as a 2023 Ge/Si Focus Fuzz and describes the Focus control as tuned gain/frequency shaping.
- A 250-unit production limit was reported in contemporary coverage; preserve as production-run evidence, not a generation.
