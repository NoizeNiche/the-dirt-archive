# Mosrite Electronics — Dirt Catalog Audit 01

## Scope
Builder/manufacturer-level catalog audit focused on overdrive, distortion, fuzz, and closely related effects. This record separates original Mosrite-era products from later reproductions/revivals and does not treat every Mosrite-branded enclosure as evidence of original Mosrite manufacture.

## Historical entity
Mosrite is associated with Semie Moseley and the Bakersfield, California guitar/electronics operation. The effects catalog is best treated as a distinct product branch within the broader Mosrite enterprise rather than assuming all Mosrite-branded effects share one continuous factory history.

## Core dirt catalog anchors

### Fuzzrite
- Retail model: Fuzzrite
- Family: fuzz
- Era: 1960s original Mosrite-era production
- Status: verified historical family
- Notes: The Fuzzrite is the principal Mosrite dirt product and one of the key American fuzz designs of the mid-1960s. It appeared in Mosrite-branded form and was also associated with other marketed/badged versions. The archive should retain marketed-brand, manufacturer/OEM, and later-reissue identities separately.

### Brass Music / Mosrite-branded variants and export/badge relationships
- Retail identity: Fuzzrite-related branded variants
- Family: Fuzzrite family / badge or OEM corridor
- Status: candidate-to-verified by specimen/source
- Notes: Surviving examples can differ in branding, graphics, controls, and enclosure details. These should be modeled as generation/specimen records rather than collapsed into one universal Fuzzrite appearance.

## Later revival boundary
Later Mosrite-branded reproductions and boutique recreations must remain separate from original 1960s Mosrite production. A later maker using the Mosrite/Fuzzrite name is not automatically evidence of original Mosrite manufacture.

## Identification cautions
1. Do not identify a Fuzzrite generation solely by enclosure color or graphics.
2. Do not infer an OEM relationship from similar circuitry or component layout.
3. Do not merge Mosrite, Ventures-associated branding, and later reproduction companies into one manufacturer entity.
4. Preserve conflicting dates rather than manufacturing a false exact production window.
5. Keep circuit-level reconstruction, schematics, PCB layouts, and complete BOM material out of the public archive.

## Research queue
- Locate primary Mosrite catalogs, dealer sheets, and advertisements from 1965–1970.
- Build a specimen chronology for original Fuzzrite enclosure/logo/control variants.
- Audit branded/export versions against contemporary advertisements.
- Separate original Mosrite production from later Hallmark, Filtron and other reproduction/reissue corridors where evidence supports the relationship.
- Check whether additional original Mosrite dirt models existed beyond Fuzzrite before marking the original dirt catalog complete.

## Evidence posture
Current catalog confidence: Fuzzrite is the clear verified core dirt anchor. Additional models/variants remain open until supported by period documentation or sufficiently strong specimen evidence.
