# YARP 2026-09-15 Batch 44

## Focus
Carlin Pedals and early Swedish micro-builder census.

## Findings
Carlin Pedals was founded in Sweden by Nils Olof Carlin in the early 1970s. The surviving catalog trail identifies a Carlin Compressor/Fuzz as a dirt-relevant product and separates it from the Carlin Phase Pedal and Ring Modulator.

The Carlin Compressor/Fuzz is retained as a core historical dirt record because its compression/sustain function includes a dedicated distortion control. Production was small and hand-built. The exact unit count remains an estimate and is not treated as factory documentation.

The Ring Modulator and Phase Pedal remain builder-census boundary products. Moody Sounds' later collaboration with Carlin is modeled as a revival/reissue relationship, not as original production continuity.

## Evidence boundary
Carlin is treated as a canonical Swedish micro-builder. Estimated quantities remain secondary evidence, and modern Moody Sounds production remains a separate production state.
