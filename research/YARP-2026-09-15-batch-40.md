# YARP 2026-09-15 Batch 40

## Focus
Jordan Electronics / Victoreen historical dirt catalog and production-state reconciliation.

## Findings
Jordan Electronics operated as a Victoreen Instrument Company division in Alhambra, California. Mid-1960s research documents the Jordan Juniors group: Boss Tone, Boss Boost, and Vico Vibe.

Boss Tone is retained as the core fuzz record. Research distinguishes Alhambra, Pasadena, and Nashville/Sho-Sound states. Jordan is the canonical original maker; Sho-Sound is retained as a licensed/marketed later state because the actual Nashville factory attribution is not sufficiently documented.

Boss Boost is retained as a separate Jordan dirt/boost catalog record. Vico Vibe, Gig Wah, Vibrasonic, and Creator remain non-core effect boundary records.

## Evidence boundary
Do not assign Sho-Bud manufacture merely from the Sho-Sound label. Keep Jordan, Sho-Sound, distributor, and factory roles distinct.
