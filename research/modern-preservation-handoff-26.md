## Important research notes
- Unsound Circuitry Woes is a highly distinctive Finnish-made discrete analog dual-band design with parallel gain circuits. High and Low simultaneously affect gain and bias, while Filter changes tonal emphasis and Input manages headroom/saturation. It can enter fuzz territory without being reduced to a fuzz-family label.
- Summer School Electronics Science Fair combines a Test Tube-style and Lab Rat-style drive section with shared Level and Blend controls. Contemporary 2023 sources identify maker Mark Turley, but the first-production date remains less certain. Preserve the lineage references without making the discovery year the introduction year.
- SkyShatter Audio's Shatter Drive was the brand-new Tulsa, Oklahoma company's debut pedal in March 2023. Its four-knob analog design and three-way symmetrical/asymmetrical/cross-asymmetrical clipping switch are strong specimen clues.
- Sentimental Bob Electronics Albert Overdrive + Boost is a separate product from Tephra despite both being enhanced takes on classic gain families. Albert uses an OP275 front end, selectable MOSFET/Soviet-germanium clipping, Fat and LP/HP voicing options, integrated boost, and internal voltage doubling. Preserve these as identification attributes rather than circuit recipes.
- Ohmless AKC-4 follows the builder's 2013 Lorena as a second Klon-oriented attempt. 2023 coverage documents four selectable clipping options plus pre/post boost placement and MIDI/footswitch control. Generation dating remains unresolved and is kept at discovery status.
- Tallon Electric Dual Drive was introduced in 2023 and combines breaker-style and K-style drive sides in one enclosure. The asymmetric control surfaces, separate footswitches and reversible signal order are unusually useful for specimen identification.

## Data rule reinforced
The Archive distinguishes product identity from famous-circuit lineage. A pedal can reference a Tube Screamer, Klon, Rat, OCD, Blues Breaker or other classic without becoming that product in the database. Keep builder, marketed brand, product, family lineage, generation, edition, and specimen evidence separate.
