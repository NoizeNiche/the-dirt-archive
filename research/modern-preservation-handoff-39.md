## Key 2024 historical/identification notes
- Uzhhorod Xtortion is explicitly documented by Drunk Beaver as a reworking of the Boss XT-2, with a distinct control arrangement and filtering/clipping changes. GPX reports an initial 25-unit run in November 2024.
- Formula B Eighty Master V2 is an explicit generation boundary from the original Eighty Master, with independent gain controls and dual-channel operation.
- JHS Hard Drive is historically significant as JHS's first original modern distortion design rather than a direct recreation of a classic pedal; JHS states the project took eight years and was the final design project of former lead engineer Cliff Smith.
- Lichtlaerm Medusa is explicitly described by the builder as the successor to Breathe//Die, making lineage a primary catalog field.
- Menatone MenaWatt's current builder page identifies V.III and adds Power Gain and Presence controls, so versioning must be preserved rather than flattened.
- Universal Audio ANTI 1992 and Knuckles '92 are digital amp/drive products, but they belong in the dirt archive because their primary identity is high-gain distortion/overdrive behavior. Their digital architecture, amp lineage, cab modeling and software-controlled features are identification fields.

## Audit method
Continue checking every year against: (1) annual GPX finalists, (2) GPX longlists and launch roundups, and (3) builder/manufacturer/contemporary specialist evidence. Existing products should be enriched, not duplicated. Explicit V2/V3/MKII/Edition labels remain independent where evidence supports a production boundary. Longlist-only products can enter the research layer when reconciliation supports them.
