### New or newly elevated builder records

- David Sperrin: independently assembled the Sperrin Fuzz Box in Southport during 1966. HIGH / RESEARCH_VALIDATED.
- Sierra Electronics: early producer associated with the 1969 Nu-Fuzz, treated as a distinct pre-Rosac builder state but kept MEDIUM / RESEARCH_ONLY while chronology is tightened.
- Rosac Electronics: physical manufacturer with its own facility and multiple effects products, including Nu-Fuzz, Nu-Wa, Nu-Wa-Fuzz and Distortion Blender. HIGH / RESEARCH_VALIDATED.
- Fi-Sonic: Australian Fuzz-Blender MKIV documented and founder/operator Phil Dreoni reported the example was one of his. MEDIUM / RESEARCH_ONLY because the surviving historical record is sparse.
- Electro-Harmonix: New York builder founded in 1968, with an early dirt/boost catalog including LPB-1, Muff Fuzz, Big Muff Pi, Screaming Bird, Mole and Axis. HIGH / RESEARCH_VALIDATED.

### Product-enumeration notes
