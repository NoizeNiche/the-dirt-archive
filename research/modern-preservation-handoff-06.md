# Modern Preservation Track Handoff 06

## Current position
Continue newest-to-oldest modern preservation work. The active window remains 2026 into 2024, with the next historical target 2023.

## New research in Batch 06
- Walrus Audio Voyager Preamp/Overdrive MKII: 2024-present. Documented functional expansion with footswitchable parametric mid EQ, Freq control, five-way diode/voicing selector, and multiple exterior artwork variants.
- Analog Man King Of Tone: V2/V3/V4 generation lineage. Manufacturer distinguishes the original four-knob architecture from V4's two independently controlled six-knob sides and documents optional external modes, four-jack configuration, higher-gain options and bypass options.
- MXR/Dunlop Dookie Drive: manufacturer currently distinguishes DD25, DD25V2, DD25V3 and DD25V4. V2 has explicit special-edition artwork evidence; V3/V4 technical differences remain unresolved and are intentionally not inferred.
- Electro-Harmonix Graphic Fuzz: manufacturer now records 2023 discontinuation, useful as a production-end marker.
- Electro-Harmonix Muff Overdrive: modern reissue of 1969 Muff Fuzz, now discontinued in 2023; preserve reissue identity separately from the original.

## Structural work
- Created `research/variant-backfill-queue-01.tsv` with the first ten high-value existing catalog records requiring variant/generation backfill.
- Variant research continues to distinguish appearance, controls, connectivity, function, production identity, electronics identity, edition status and collector-facing identification clues.
- Unresolved differences remain explicitly unresolved rather than being filled by inference.

## Evidence discipline
Primary manufacturer pages are preferred. Current manufacturer pages may preserve older product identities even after discontinuation, which is valuable historical evidence. A manufacturer listing separate model numbers is treated as evidence of distinct marketed identities, not automatic evidence of distinct circuitry.

## Next target
Continue into 2023 with the same pattern: discover revision, identify what actually changed, capture external identification clues, preserve uncertainty, and queue the result for linkage to the corresponding public pedal record.
