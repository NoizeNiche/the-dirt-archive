## Excluded / discovery-only
- Çokünlü Pedals was researched but not promoted into the conventional builder/product census. Its own site describes a low-volume, on-demand operation that manufactures hundreds of existing branded pedal models, including Klon Centaur and King of Pawn, so the evidence currently fits the archive's custom/OEM-style relationship layer better than an original-product catalog entity.
- AC Noises was also encountered in the Turkish search universe but correctly excluded because its own history identifies the company as an Italian duo based in Italy. It was not a Turkish builder despite Turkish-market search visibility.

## Evidence notes
ADD+ Pedals' official site states that it has produced handmade pedals since 2005 and places the workshop in Cankaya, Izmir. Its stompbox catalog lists Blues Player, Blues Player 2, Blues Player Pro, TubeDrive, Distortion, Soul, Ratortion, PI, PI Dry-Wet, Bullseye and related gain products. Individual pages document the clipping and control architecture of Blues Player, Blues Player Pro, Distortion, Ratortion3V2 and PI/PI Dry-Wet. FET-Boost is retained as gain-adjacent because the builder describes it as a light-crunch boost.

Cheesy Instruments is documented by an external builder catalog as an Istanbul-based independent maker producing handwired and handpainted audio gear. Its indexed pedal universe includes Valve Kong, Fuzzodharma, Bit Killer, Noiseswash and Alien Invasion. The broader 12-product entry also includes synthesizers, plugins, mixers, switching devices and microphones, which are outside the central dirt scope.

## Photo workflow
Every inventory row includes a stable builder product/catalog source as a photo-source breadcrumb. Cheesy Instruments currently lacks a stable first-party catalog endpoint in the reviewed evidence, so the external catalog source is preserved explicitly rather than inventing a missing manufacturer URL. These links are evidence trails for later specimen capture, source credit, rights review and generation mapping, not permission to republish images.

## Scope discipline
Boost-only and gain-adjacent models remain labeled as such. Bass dirt is retained in PI Dry-Wet because bass fuzz is in scope. Custom mods and third-party brand recreations from Çokünlü are not counted as original retail product families. No schematics, PCB layouts, gutshots, cloning instructions, or complete BOM data are published.
