## Important research notes
- DSM & Humboldt Black Clouds was introduced in February 2023 as a from-scratch high-gain distortion/preamp design with multiple gain stages, active three-band EQ, dual boost, three operating modes and the X-Gate noise-gate/crossover function. Preserve it as a distinct DSM & Humboldt product, not as a Simplifier clone.
- Fortin NATAS is the company's first dedicated distortion pedal and appeared in October 2023 as a pedal translation of the discontinued NATAS amplifier platform. Its cascading gain stages, active three-band EQ, Kill boost, Girth/Grind tone controls and Mid Shape switch are strong identifiers. The current builder documentation also preserves its unusual power/startup requirement and USA manufacture.
- TWA SH9 is a signature distortion developed with Scott Henderson and Susumu Tamura, reworking the 1981 SD-9 Sonic Distortion concept. Better bass/lower-mid response, increased output, optional 18V operation, USA manufacture and documented faceplate treatments make the product easy to distinguish from original SD-9 and Maxon SSD-9 lineage.
- ROSS Distortion belongs to the 2023 ROSS revival under JHS manufacture in Kansas City. It is a reissue-family product with its own current production identity, soft-touch switching and added mode behavior. Keep vintage ROSS branding, marketed brand identity and JHS manufacturing identity separately.
- UAFX Lion '68 is a 2023 UAFX amp-emulation product with a separate firmware chronology. UA release notes identify v1.1.5 as the initial Lion release and v1.1.6 as adding Ghost Notes and Bright Cap mods. Firmware revisions must remain separate from hardware-generation claims.
- Poly Effects Josh Smith Flat V is a 2023 collaboration built around two analog gain channels with selectable clipping, JFET amp-style gain, resonant filters, touch-sensitive sliders, MIDI and presets. Preserve the analog-audio/digital-control split as a core product characteristic.

## Data rule reinforced
The Archive must not flatten reissue, signature, collaboration, firmware, hardware and lineage into a single version field. A product can be contemporary and technically complex while still requiring separate market identity, manufacturing identity and evidence layers.
