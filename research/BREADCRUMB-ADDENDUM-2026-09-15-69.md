## Scope
Universal Amplifier Corp. / Astro Amp Astrotone and Sam Ash Fuzzz Boxx identity, naming, enclosure, and production-period audit.

## Findings

- Astrotone remains a high-confidence Universal Amplifier Corp. manufactured product, with surviving evidence placing production in 1967 and continuing into 1969.
- Sam Ash Fuzzz Boxx remains a high-confidence Universal Amplifier Corp. OEM / house-brand state.
- A 1967 Sam Ash unit in the original Astrotone-style enclosure is preserved as a separate historical state rather than being collapsed into the later red wedge enclosure.
- Secondary sources disagree on exact introduction wording and year, alternating among Astro Tone, Astrotone, 1966, and 1967. The archive preserves that discrepancy rather than forcing an exact launch year without a primary dated advertisement or catalog.
- Sam Ash's later corporate retrospective says the Fuzzz Boxx rebrand occurred in 1968, while surviving 1967 Sam Ash examples and specialist documentation show Sam Ash-branded units during 1967. This is retained as an explicit chronology conflict for future primary-source resolution.
- Production revisions are being modeled as historical states of one Universal Amplifier product family, not as evidence of different factories.

## Editorial status
No public lineage edge promoted. No factory attribution extended beyond the evidence-supported Universal Amplifier / Sam Ash relationship.
