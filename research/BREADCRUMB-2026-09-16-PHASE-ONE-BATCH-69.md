## Evidence notes
West Tokyo Waves' first-party site identifies the operation as a one-person workshop in West Tokyo and names Neil as founder. The site traces the brand's origin through Brutalist Fuzz and currently documents concrete-enclosed Brutalist Fuzz plus Structures. A 2026 Japanese industry feature also describes Noise Swash and Structures as handmade Tokyo products. Preserve the concrete finish as specimen/production evidence, not as separate models.

Animal Factory Amplification's first-party material places the operation in Mumbai and states it has been building since 2013. The current pedal catalog includes Godeater+, Ozymandias and Dirty Mirror, while the product archive preserves discontinued Pit Viper v2. The builder describes its current pedal circuits as original and separate from its Eurorack module catalog.

MASF Pedals has a substantial Japanese dealer trail covering dirt and experimental products including ORDO, Paranoid, POSSESSED, thornoscillator and Epilepsy. Because several MASF products blur fuzz, noise, glitch and unusual signal processing, retain the identity in the builder census while only promoting central dirt rows where the category is sufficiently clear.

ZenZero Electronics appears in the documented survey of lesser-known Japanese boutique builders. That source records FuzU plus Habu Heavy Metal Distortion and Red Crown among a compact Japanese catalog. Present activity and precise founding chronology remain open for a later primary-source audit.

## Boundary / scope notes
- MASF and West Tokyo Waves experimental/noise devices are not allowed to inflate central dirt counts merely because they pass guitar audio or create aggressive textures.
