# 2008-2012 Microbuilder Audit 03

## Scope

This pass targets overlooked boutique and micro-builder activity around 2008-2012, prioritizing builders with documented dirt products and meaningful historical chronology. The purpose is catalog breadth and identity reconciliation, not circuit reconstruction.

## Fairfield Circuitry

Fairfield Circuitry states that it has been experimenting with sound since 2008 and manufactures its pedals in Hull, Quebec. The Barbershop is explicitly described as an original design rather than a clone and is documented by Effects Database in October 2008. The Unpleasant Surprise appears as an experimental fuzz/gate in 2009. Randy's Revenge is retained as a non-dirt boundary because it is a ring modulator. The archive should later distinguish original Barbershop production from the V2/Millennium state and other later variants.

Sources: https://fairfieldcircuitry.com/pages/about ; https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/fairfield/barbershop ; https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/fairfield/unpleasantsurprise ; https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/fairfield/randysrevenge

## Stone Deaf FX

MusicRadar reports that Luke Hilton launched Stone Deaf as a solo venture in 2009 while remaining in other employment for years. Effects Database documents the PDF-1 Parametric Distortion Filter in June 2010. PDF-1 combines parametric filtering with switchable clean/distorted operation and is explicitly capable of overdriven, fuzz and velcro-fuzz textures. The current catalog contains later Fig Fumb, Kliptonite, Q Boost, Trashy Blonde and Warp Drive states, but their individual release dates should be assigned separately rather than inherited from the company launch.

Sources: https://www.musicradar.com/news/the-workshop-stone-deaf-fx ; https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/stonedeaf ; https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/stonedeaf/pdf1

## Mountainking Electronics

Mountainking's own history gives a clean origin date: founded in 2007 by Alan Pavlica. The initial project grew from breadboarding simple amplifier circuits for heavy bass/fuzz work, and the low-end booster became the basis for the original Behemoth. Effects Database documents Megalith in June 2010 as a heavy fuzz/distortion product, while Frequency~LSD provides a psychedelic synth/fuzz hybrid boundary. Keep Mountainking's product-origin narrative separate from exact commercial sale dates unless supported by product-specific evidence.

Sources: https://www.mountainkingelectronics.com/about ; https://www.mountainkingelectronics.com/megalith-order ; https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/mountainking/megalith ; https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/mountainking/frequencylsd

## VFE Pedals

VFE's timeline is unusually useful for defining builder-state chronology. Peter Rutter discovered BYOC kits in March 2008, began selling heavily modified pedals in summer 2008, designed his first circuit board in May 2009, founded VonRutter Family Effects in January 2010, went full time in June 2010, adopted relay switching in October 2010 and entered retail distribution with silkscreen graphics in April 2011. The Scream was documented in November 2009; the later catalog expanded into a large dirt universe. These milestones should be modeled as distinct identity, production and distribution transitions rather than one founding date.

Sources: https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/vfe ; https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/vfe/thescream

## Avondale

Effects Database introduced Avondale as a new brand in May 2009 and listed ADT-4 Distortion, AMR-5 Metal Driver and AOD-6 Overdrive among its launch-era products. The AMR-5 is connected in the database to a Daphon E20MT/F15MT OEM family. This is useful OEM evidence, but it should remain model-specific and evidence-qualified rather than becoming a blanket claim that all Avondale products were Daphon-made.

Sources: https://www.effectsdatabase.com/updates/weekly/20090504 ; https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/avondale ; https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/avondale/amr5

## Crews Maniac Sound

Crews Maniac Sound is the stompbox line associated with Tokyo-based Crews Corp. The catalog currently indexes 18 products, including SVD-001 Classic Overdrive, Addict OD, DIS-01 Distortion, G.O.D. Genius Over Drive, Quadrive, Texas Tornado and Valve Drive. SVD-001 has a clean 2009 documentary anchor from Effects Database. A later chronological pass should determine which of the indexed gain models predate 2010 and whether any product states represent revisions rather than separate models.

Sources: https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/crews ; https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/crews/svd001

## Archive interpretation

This batch reinforces three recurring rules. First, company formation is not necessarily the same event as first pedal sale. Second, OEM relationships must be attached to specific models when that is the level of evidence available. Third, a product with fuzz/drive behavior can be historically relevant to the dirt story even when its primary catalog category is hybrid or experimental, but it should not inflate the central dirt count when the effect is fundamentally outside the archive scope.

No schematics, PCB layouts, complete BOMs, gutshot libraries, cloning instructions, or circuit-reconstruction material are included.
