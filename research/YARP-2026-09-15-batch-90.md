# YARP 2026-09-15 Batch 90: Claybridge Sound Systems Fuzz Family

## Builder
Claybridge Sound Systems is retained as an Australian builder lineage centered on Ralph Bridges in Clayton, Victoria.

## Catalog state
- Early Claybridge Fuzz Box: advertisements from April-May 1966 describe a hardwired fuzz box with one to two controls and a 15-foot lead.
- Fuzzmaster: developed and advertised through 1966, with the first documented pedal carrying the Claybridge Fuzzmaster identity appearing later in the year.
- Wildcat / intermediate state: surviving examples and 1966-era advertising indicate a distinct presentation within the Fuzzmaster development sequence, but exact Mark numbering remains uncertain.
- Fuzzmaster Mark IV: documented surviving version, with three-control layout including volume, fuzz and treble; surviving examples are exceptionally scarce.

## Historical significance
Claybridge provides unusually rich evidence for rapid product evolution within one tiny Australian builder. The advertising trail lets the Archive distinguish preliminary fuzz-box products from the later Fuzzmaster family without backdating a single design across every surviving specimen.

## Evidence posture
The existence of multiple 1966 products is strongly supported by contemporary advertising and surviving examples. Exact Mark II/III numbering and the endpoint of production remain unresolved.

## Source trail
Tone Machines Blog, In Search of the Australian Fuzzmaster parts 1-3; contemporary Go-Set advertisements.
