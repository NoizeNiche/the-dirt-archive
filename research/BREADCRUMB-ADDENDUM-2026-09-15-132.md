# YARP Breadcrumb 132

## Purpose
Builder-discovery-only pass. The research scope is intentionally limited to identifying independent historical effects builders and preserving them as builder records. OEM/factory attribution work is deferred until the builder census is substantially complete.

## New builder

- `BUILDER-123`: David Sperrin / Sperrin Electronics, Southport, England. Independent builder of the Sperrin Fuzz Box, advertised in the UK music press in 1966. Family testimony records home assembly and shipping by David and Beryl Sperrin. David also built amplifiers and related musical-electronics equipment.

## Dedupe decisions

- Barnes & Mullins / Exel remains a distributor/marketed identity lead rather than a confirmed physical builder.
- Existing Sola Sound, Hornby Skewes, Jen, and other established builder records are not duplicated from this pass.
