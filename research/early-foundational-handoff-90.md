# Early Foundational Handoff 90

## Pass 42: 2005-2008 production expansion, online-DIY lineage, and early hand-built specimen context

Pass 42 advances the archive through the 2005-2008 period where the boutique pedal market becomes broader, more international, and increasingly shaped by online communities. The pass emphasizes first branded products, production-method transitions, and evidence that can help distinguish early specimens without reconstructing circuits.

### Newly documented or clarified

- **EarthQuaker Devices Fantasy Fuzz**: retained as the first pedal sold under the EarthQuaker Devices name during the early hobbyist period.
- **EarthQuaker Devices Hoof**: manufacturer history places the first full-production run in 2007.
- **EarthQuaker Devices early basement production**: the earliest Hoof and companion pedals were built by hand in Jamie Stillman's West Akron basement before the operation scaled.
- **Death By Audio**: the builder's own history separates its 2002 origin as an effects-customization service from the later recognizable pedal catalog.
- **Death By Audio Fuzz War**: March 2008 period discussion provides a documented public-production/preorder corridor, kept separate from any unverified prototype date.
- **Empress Effects**: 2005 custom tremolo request became a first production demand signal and company-origin boundary.
- **Dr. Scientist**: 2005 company founding in a two-person basement operation is retained as a builder-origin boundary pending stronger first-dirt-product dating.
- **Himmelstrutz FETTO Standard**: April 2006 specialist publication provides an independent early product-era anchor for a Scandinavian overdrive/distortion builder.
- **Wampler Pinnacle**: USPTO first-use documentation gives August 29, 2005 as a trademark first-use anchor; later builder interviews describe Pinnacle as one of the earliest Wampler-branded pedals.
- **Wampler / IndyGuitarist transition**: Brian Wampler's firsthand history keeps the 2000-era modification work separate from subsequent branded pedal production.
- **BJFE Honey Bee**: specialist builder-community evidence preserves the unusually small 2002 starting run and early serial-based lineage.
- **Pigtronix**: 2004 company founding retained as chronology; Philosopher's Tone is kept outside the core dirt count pending stronger first-production primary dating.
- **Xotic BB Preamp**: January 2005 first production state reinforced from the manufacturer's official chronology.

### Key research findings

1. **The boutique market is now visibly internet-shaped.** EarthQuaker's online one-off sales, Wampler's IndyGuitarist/DIY culture, and DBA's web-based customization work show how internet communities became part of the production ecosystem.
2. **Early production method is itself historical evidence.** EarthQuaker's basement hand-production is useful context for interpreting early specimens, while avoiding the claim that every early unit shares identical construction.
3. **First branded product and company founding are not interchangeable.** Empress and Dr. Scientist provide clear examples where the company-origin clock precedes or does not precisely date a core dirt model.
4. **Trademark dates can anchor product identity without proving manufacture dates.** Pinnacle's August 29, 2005 trademark first use is preserved as a legal/documentary anchor rather than a factory timestamp.
5. **The same builder can occupy multiple identities.** Wampler's IndyGuitarist modification work and later Wampler-branded production are intentionally separated.
6. **The international map keeps widening.** Canadian Dr. Scientist, Swedish Himmelstrutz and BJFE, Japanese/U.S. Xotic, and U.S. builders now form a genuinely cross-border boutique network.

### Evidence discipline

- Manufacturer-authored histories remain preferred for production clocks.
- Builder interviews and official legal records are retained as distinct evidence classes.
- Specialist publication dates are not silently converted into manufacturing dates.
- Marketplace/forum material is treated as specimen or public-circulation evidence rather than universal factory history.
- Scope-bound builder records remain outside the central dirt count when the product is not fuzz, overdrive, or distortion.
- No schematics, PCB layouts, complete BOMs, gutshot libraries, or cloning instructions are added.

### Current lineage position

The archive now crosses the 2005-2008 boutique expansion with three increasingly visible production patterns: tiny hand-built workshops, internet-driven modification/DIY businesses, and small manufacturers beginning to scale branded product lines. EarthQuaker, Wampler and Death By Audio preserve the DIY/internet route; Empress and Dr. Scientist show new regional boutique entrants; Himmelstrutz and BJFE strengthen the Scandinavian node; and Xotic supplies a well-dated continuing product family.
