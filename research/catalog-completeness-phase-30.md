# The Dirt Archive — Catalog Completeness Phase 30

## Objective
Breadth-first enumeration of nine historically useful boutique builders. Whole discoverable lineups are recorded, not just dirt products. Discovery records remain separate from verified product histories.

## Builders
- SkinPimp
- M.A.S.F.
- T. Jauernig Electronics
- Rastop Designs
- Compulsive Audio
- Main.Ace.FX
- GoosoniqueWorx
- 1969 Effects
- Pigdog

## Discovery snapshots and sources
### SkinPimp
Effects Database snapshot: 6 products. Premier Guitar's 2009 profile documents Ryan Kirkland's MKI, MKII, MKIII, Buzzaround, Fuzzface and custom variants; the public database now lists 3OD, Buzzaround, Face Plant and SkinBender MK I/II/III. This phase preserves the current database lineup while noting the older article as a historical catalog lead.
Sources: https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/skinpimp ; https://www.premierguitar.com/gear/skin-pimp-pedals

### M.A.S.F.
Effects Database snapshot: 17 products, including Biloma, Bloody Emily, Epilepsy, Halothan, Homicide Noodle, Kidnapper, Lavender Head, Live Convulsion, three OSC noise machines, Paranoid, PJ Fuzz, SCM, Starry Wisdom, Swan Song and Thornoscillator.
Source: https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/masf

### T. Jauernig Electronics
Effects Database snapshot: 22 products. Builder history records the 2004 Luxury Drive origin, Greg Koch's DGTM and Gristle King collaboration, a 2007 stop in pedal orders for Peavey work, a T-Rex licensing period, and renewed production in 2010. These are major future relationship-map candidates.
Source: https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/jauernig

### Rastop Designs
Effects Database snapshot: 16 products. Alexander Rastopchin's builder site lists Super Divider, Phaser, Gypsy Phaser, Tweedy, 4007, 393, Fuzz+Octave, optical compressor, Envelope Filter, 60's Fuzz, Tremolo, Ring Modulator and bass-focused products, plus later Ekasilicon and one-off fuzz work. Treat the website as a historical snapshot rather than a permanent live catalog.
Sources: https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/rastop ; https://www.rastopdesigns.com/effects.htm

### Compulsive Audio
Effects Database snapshot: 19 products. Founded by John De Luca in summer 2009; the company moved from kitchen-table builds to a garage shop in early 2010. Catalog spans dirt, boost, compression, modulation and utility/switching products.
Source: https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/compulsive

### Main.Ace.FX
Effects Database snapshot: 6 products. James Granuzzo's operation began with prototypes in June 2011, production in November 2011, a custom shop in May 2012 and a new laboratory workshop in October 2012.
Source: https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/mainace

### GoosoniqueWorx
Effects Database snapshot: 10 products. Ravi Goose's timeline documents MangoJuice, Pixie, Rapture, Citrus, Fudge 73, Tremor, Seventheaven, 7thvn and Kult, including a 50-unit silver Seventheaven run and a 100-unit black run.
Source: https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/goosonique

### 1969 Effects
Effects Database snapshot: 3 products: Blonde Boost, Bone Blender and Scarlet Storm. Bone Blender is documented as a hand-made French Tone Bender MkII Pro-derived fuzz using selected germanium transistors and external bias, making it a clear future relationship case.
Sources: https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/1969 ; https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/1969/boneblender

### Pigdog
Effects Database snapshot: 16 products. Steve Williams' Surrey/London operation began in 2006 with first pedals in 2007 and includes custom work, fuzz, treble boosters, drive and amp/mini-amp products. Electric Eye and Professional MkII are strong future photographic and production-detail targets.
Sources: https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/pigdog ; https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/pigdog/electriceye ; https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/pigdog/mk2

## Media/image discovery
Image leads were collected during the pass for SkinPimp, M.A.S.F. and T. Jauernig, with the media manifest preserving source-page URLs and rights state. SkinPimp has strong period visual sources in Premier Guitar and Reverb; M.A.S.F. has builder-store and modern retailer imagery; T. Jauernig has specimen imagery showing at least a 2011 Gristle King V.3. All third-party images remain reference-only until license or permission is established.

## Editorial notes
- SkinPimp illustrates why the archive must preserve historical article evidence in addition to a current database lineup. The 2009 article names custom and Fuzzface work not represented in today's six-record database snapshot.
- T. Jauernig is a priority lineage case because retail production, artist collaboration and T-Rex licensing are explicitly documented by the builder.
- GoosoniqueWorx has unusually precise limited-run counts for Seventheaven and should eventually receive a specimen/generation timeline.
- 1969 Effects and Pigdog belong in the Tone Bender/Buzzaround relationship map later, but this pass does not convert resemblance or stated basis into a full lineage claim.

## Status
All 115 records are discovery/Enumerated candidates only. No product is promoted to Canonical or Deep Research solely because it appears in an aggregator.
