# YARP 2026-09-15 Batch 97: Olson X-78 / Herald AM-44A OEM State

## Scope
Reconcile the Olson X-78 Fuzz Master with the broader Herald AM-44A Japanese export family.

## Findings
- Effects Database identifies the Herald Electronics AM-44A Fuzz Master as a late-1960s Japanese product distributed under multiple labels including Olson, Apollo, LRE, Melos, Mica, and Monacor.
- The Olson marketed state is cataloged separately as Olson X-78 Fuzz Master rather than as an independent U.S. or Japanese factory.
- The archive retains Herald Electronics as the manufacturing candidate and Olson as the marketed/export label state, while preserving the exact corporate identity of Herald as a separate research problem.
- This strengthens the earlier LRE Fuzz Sound relationship without collapsing the entire Japanese wedge-fuzz ecosystem into one anonymous OEM.
