# YARP 2026-09-15 Batch 123: Iwase Denshi / Voice Boundary Record

## Builder identity
- **Iwase Denshi / Voice (岩瀬電子 / VOICE)** was founded by Yukichi Iwase after he left Teisco around 1964.
- Iwase was an electronics and amplifier specialist and operated a small production business. Modern historical interviews describe him as a primarily one-man operation with unusually low production numbers.
- The company produced Voice guitars and amplifiers and continued into approximately 1970.

## Dirt relevance
- No confirmed commercial Voice stompbox or standalone fuzz pedal has been located in the present search.
- Iwase is nevertheless relevant to the Dirt Archive's builder genealogy because Japanese historical testimony places him among early independent electronics builders and describes his attention to reproducing and improving imported fuzz equipment.
- This record therefore remains **SCOPE_BOUNDARY_ONLY** and must not inflate the manufactured dirt-pedal count.

## Related products / boundary evidence
- Voice Frontier Custom 1000 and other Voice guitars.
- Voice amplifiers.
- A rare Voice/Iwase tape echo machine is documented from the late 1960s; this remains outside the dirt catalog except as a builder-history boundary record.

## Attribution boundary
- Do not infer that Voice made Mirano, Melos, Honey, Shin-Ei, or other Japanese fuzz products simply from regional proximity or Iwase's technical reputation.
- The relationship between Iwase/Voice and other Japanese electronics companies remains unresolved unless specific documentary evidence is found.
