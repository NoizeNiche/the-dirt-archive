# The Dirt Archive · Breadcrumb · Batch 100

## Completed

Phase One Batch 100 expanded the historical Soul Power Instruments dirt catalog using dated Japanese dealer archives.

## Builder

- Soul Power Instruments remains a canonical Japanese handmade builder lineage.
- Archived Shimamura material from 2013 and 2017 identifies SPI as a Shizuoka-area handmade effects brand and provides historical product-line evidence.

## Photos

The 2013 and 2017 Shimamura pages contain product imagery tied to the historical lineup and are retained as exterior-photo source leads for later rights/credit review.

## Information on the pedal

- Hands Of Death is described as a thick distortion whose low-gain setting retains defined attack while higher gain increases low-mid mass.
- Brown Sound Equation was built from a dealer request as a high-gain distortion intended to reshape transistor-amp response toward a brown-sound character, with a SHAPE control.
- The dealer archives establish that SPI had a broad dirt catalog well before the later products already mapped in the archive.

## Normalization decisions

- Third-party modified units such as OD-3 TK Mod, M-80 Bass DI+ MOD and similar work remain relationship records, not new SPI conceptual models.
- Names and spellings are preserved from dated dealer records where exact historical typography is not yet recoverable from a first-party source.
- No schematics, PCB layouts, gutshots or complete component inventories are being collected.

## Next target

Continue Japanese S historical reconciliation. Priority targets are additional dated SPI catalog material, historical Sound Wave Lab and Sound Dog models, STOMPROX chronology, and genuinely distinct remaining S builders before the geographic sweep returns to Taiwan / Hong Kong / mainland China.
