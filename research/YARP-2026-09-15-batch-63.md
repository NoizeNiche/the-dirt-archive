# YARP 2026-09-15 Batch 63: Meazzi / Caldironi Whau-Whau Repeat Family

## Scope
Expand the Italian Meazzi/Caldironi catalog beyond the already documented Whau-Whau Distortion, identifying repeat/distortion variants and separating the marketed labels from the probable physical manufacturing family.

## Catalog expansion

### Distortion Whau-Whau
The Meazzi, Goldsound, G.I.S., Mac, Gun and Cosmosound branded Distortion Whau-Whau family is documented as an Italian-made late-1960s to early-1970s fuzz-wah product family. Effects Database attributes the family to Caldironi Musica in Milan, while ToneHome treats Caldironi Musica as the probable factory behind a wider set of Italian labels.

### Distortion Whau-Whau Repeat
ToneHome documents a Goldsound Distortion Whau-Whau Repeat example from 1972/73 and cross-links the same product to Silversound SE/40 and Cosmosound Distortion Whau-Whau Repeat. This should be represented as a distinct product family/generation rather than simply an alternate logo on the earlier Distortion Whau-Whau.

### Silversound SE-20 Whau-Whau Distortion
Effects Database identifies the SE-20 as a 1970s Italian dual wah/fuzz-distortion pedal from the EF-EL family and links it to Cosmosound CSE-3 and Vox Wah-Wah Distortion. The exact commercial chronology and all label variants remain a deeper audit target.

## Relationship model
The current evidence supports a manufacturing cluster in which Caldironi Musica/EF-EL is the probable physical manufacturing layer and Meazzi, Goldsound, G.I.S., Gun, Mac, Cosmosound and Silversound are marketed/label states. Model-level identical construction strengthens the relationship, but the archive keeps each retail label as a relationship node instead of creating duplicate factories.

## Evidence state
**Evidence-qualified:** Whau-Whau Distortion family; multiple marketed labels; Italian manufacture; Distortion Whau-Whau Repeat 1972/73 example; Silversound SE-20 relationship to Cosmosound/Vox family.

**Unresolved:** exact corporate identity and endpoint of Caldironi Musica; complete 1960s-1970s product catalog; precise chronology of plastic versus metal chassis families; factory responsibility for every EF-EL-associated label.
