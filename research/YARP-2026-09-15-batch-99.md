# YARP 2026-09-15 Batch 99: ACA / Lafayette Fuzz Sound Reconciliation

## Scope
Separate the U.S.-made Amplifier Corporation of America Fuzz King from the Lafayette Fuzz Sound marketed state.

## Findings
- ACA Fuzz King was introduced through Merson/Unicord in summer 1967.
- Lafayette's fall 1967 catalog shows the same product family marketed as Fuzz Sound.
- The factory relationship is therefore ACA -> Fuzz King, with Lafayette -> Fuzz Sound as a retail/import/marketing state.
- The evidence does not justify identifying ACA as the factory behind the later Japanese LRE Fuzz Sound. The LRE Fuzz Sound is instead tracked separately in the Herald/Japanese export corridor.
- This distinction prevents the common catalog error of treating the words “Fuzz Sound” as a unique factory identifier.
