# 2011-2013 Microbuilder Audit 07

## Scope

This pass targets boutique and specialist pedal entities documented around 2011-2013, with emphasis on overdrive, distortion, fuzz, bass dirt, and historically important hybrid gain products. The purpose is catalog breadth and identity mapping, not circuit reconstruction.

## 1969 Effects

Effects Database's January 9, 2012 weekly overview records 1969 Effects Bone Blender and Scarlet Storm as newly indexed products. A later French pedal-builder survey describes the operation as a small classic-fuzz-focused line consisting of Blonde Boost, Bone Blender and Scarlet Storm. Bone Blender is treated as a Tone Bender MKII-inspired marketed product; Scarlet Storm as a Fuzz Face-inspired product. Founding date and physical manufacturing details remain unresolved, so the builder stays discovery-level rather than being promoted to a fully canonical historical manufacturer.

## Labo K Effects

Labo K Effects is a French/Paris-associated builder with a surviving first-party product site. The site identifies Fuzz Ultra as a high-gain fuzz with gate, colour selection and Fat/Ultra modes, and Warm Lady as a booster/distortion with saturation control. Effects Database's January 2012 weekly overview additionally records Fuzzy Lady and Miss Crunchy. The official site is preferred for present product identity while the 2012 database entry supplies historical catalog timing. The Power-Link power-sharing system is retained as production/context evidence, not as a dirt model.

## Cusack Music

Cusack Music already existed before this 2012 pass and has a broader catalog than the two models entered here. Premier Guitar's March 2012 NAMM coverage documents SubFuzz and Kingsnake together at the Cusack booth. SubFuzz is central fuzz evidence; Kingsnake is an overdrive entry. Exact first-sale dates and the full earlier Cusack dirt catalog should be refined in a dedicated builder-completion pass rather than inferred from the NAMM appearance alone.

## Dunwich Amps

Effects Database's January 2012 model page documents the Fac Off as a heavy fuzz with a six-position filter and switchable input transistor options. Because the same source's weekly overview lists Dunwich Amps among brands newly indexed in January 2012, 2012 provides a clean documentary anchor, but not necessarily the company's true founding date. Full early Dunwich dirt catalog remains an active queue.

## Custom 77

Custom 77 was a French guitar company expanding into pedals. Effects Database records Push Me Pull Me in January 2012. Contemporary Guitar World and French reviews describe the pedal as a three-mode overdrive, while the King For A Day booster was reviewed alongside it in 2012. The two products remain separate retail models. The archive does not assume Custom 77 manufactured every internal component itself without stronger documentation.

## FXdoctor

This pass produced one of the strongest version-history records. FXdoctor's current first-party history states that the original 8-Bit Fuzz began around 2002, went through several knob/enclosure and labeling states, had a serious retail-launch attempt in 2011, and received another retail presentation around 2013. The present source calls the later expanded model Super 8-Bit beginning in 2015, so the archive carefully distinguishes the long-running 8-Bit family from the later Super 8-Bit product name. Effects Database also indexes Super Bass Bit Fuzz in the January 2012 catalog overview.

## Nocturne Brain

Effects Database's January 2012 weekly overview records the Ubangi Stomp. Current Nocturne documentation still includes an Ubangi Stomp manual, while RockBoard describes it as Tavo Vega's interpretation of a late-1970s White Label Bud Box RAT-style platform, voiced for vintage Fullerton-era and British amplifiers. The archive records this as a Rat-family overdrive relationship, without treating that lineage as proof of physical manufacturing identity. Nocturne's broader early dirt catalog remains incomplete and should receive a dedicated completion pass.

## Scope boundaries

The following are not promoted into the central dirt count merely because they appeared in the same 2012 source set: Catalinbread kits, Overtōn TōnLink-DI, preamps, power supplies, and other non-dirt effects. They may remain useful boundary records. Luxor Fuzz is deliberately excluded from this builder batch because the 2012 Effects Database record concerns the vintage Italian Jen-made product rather than a clearly distinct 2012 boutique builder.

## Evidence hierarchy

Primary builder material is preferred where it exists, especially for FXdoctor and Labo K. Contemporary trade coverage and archived specialist databases are used for dated catalog appearances. A dated catalog appearance proves documentary presence by that date; it does not automatically establish founding date, first production, or physical manufacture.
