- Chicago Musical Instruments / Maestro: enumerated FZ-1, FZ-1A, FZ-1B, and the later FZ-1S Super-Fuzz as separate product states. FZ-1/FZ-1A/FZ-1B have especially strong model-level production evidence, including Kalamazoo and Lincolnwood factory changes.

- Selmer: added Buzz Tone as a distinct early British builder/product entry and logged the later Fuzz-Wah as a separate research case.
- Arbiter Electronics: added the Fuzz Face as an independently manufactured British product with its own Ashford factory evidence.
- Wilsic Electronics: added as the physical builder behind John Hornby Skewes' Zonk Machine. Zonk II is recorded separately at lower confidence pending stronger model-specific evidence.

### Research rule reinforced

A product-family resemblance, similar circuit, or related marketed identity is not enough to add a builder. The builder census records physical manufacturing only when supported by model-level or strong organizational evidence. Where that threshold is not met, the product stays research-only or unresolved.
