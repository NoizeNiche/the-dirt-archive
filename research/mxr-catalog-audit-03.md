# MXR Catalog Audit 03

## Scope
Historical reconciliation of the original MXR Innovations dirt catalog and the later Jim Dunlop stewardship/revival period. The archive keeps original MXR production separate from later MXR-branded releases unless a direct lineage is documented.

## Core historical anchors

- **Distortion+ M104**: foundational MXR dirt product, part of the early lineup after the Phase 90. Jim Dunlop's official history identifies Distortion+ as one of the first additions to the original core lineup. 
- **Blue Box M103**: early MXR octave-fuzz product and part of the original core catalog expansion.
- **Distortion II M145**: retain as a separate historical MXR dirt model rather than treating the later Super/Badass products as replacements for it.
- **Distortion III M115**: later MXR-branded distortion product, distinct retail identity.
- **Doubleshot Distortion M151**: later MXR-branded dual-voice distortion, separate model record.
- **Fullbore Metal M116**: high-gain MXR distortion model, separate product identity.

## Ownership and production-state rule

MXR Innovations is documented by specialist historical catalog sources as operating in Rochester, New York from approximately 1972-1984. Jim Dunlop's official history states that MXR later closed and that Dunlop acquired and resurrected the brand, with the first Dunlop-stewarded MXR releases including Distortion+, Dyna Comp, Blue Box and Phase 90. Therefore the archive must not treat every M-series pedal carrying an MXR name as one uninterrupted manufacturing era.

## Evidence from period catalog material

A surviving 1982 MXR catalog is especially useful because it preserves the original pre-Dunlop product environment. The catalog evidence should be treated as a primary-adjacent period snapshot rather than a lifetime catalog. It confirms Distortion+ in the original-era lineup and provides a fixed reference point for original MXR branding and presentation.

## Modern/revival dirt layer

The later MXR catalog includes:

- **Custom Badass '78 Distortion M78**
- **Super Badass Distortion M75**
- **Badass Modified OD M77**
- **Badass '77 Distortion CSP202**
- **Fullbore Metal M116**
- **Distortion III M115**
- **Doubleshot Distortion M151**
- signature/limited Distortion+ variants such as the Randy Rhoads models

These are retained as later MXR-branded products unless direct evidence establishes a historical-original relationship.

## Important catalog correction

The modern M-number system should not be assumed to be chronological. M104, M103 and other familiar numbers coexist with much later M75/M77/M78/M115/M116/M151 products. The archive indexes the retail product name, manufacturer/ownership state, and historical period separately rather than sorting by model number alone.
