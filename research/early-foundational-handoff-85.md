# Early Foundational Handoff 85

## Pass 37: deeper 1990s boutique transition, early Foxrox production, and the first-wave manufacturer network

Pass 37 extends the systematic 1990s descent with a focus on builders whose surviving histories preserve unusually strong production clocks, small-run evidence, or transitions between modification culture and branded pedal manufacturing.

### Newly documented

- **Prescription Electronics Experience**: 1993 product-era anchor preserved from timeline material.
- **Prescription Electronics Yardbox**: 1994 product-era anchor for the Tone Bender-inspired fuzz/overdrive family.
- **Prescription Electronics Experience specimen variance**: surviving original-run examples are hand-painted, and examples with and without external power jacks are documented; the factory-versus-owner modification question remains open.
- **Fulltone early Fulldrive corridor**: early-1990s company history and surviving raw-aluminium Fulldrive references are kept separate from later numbered Fulldrive revisions.
- **Frantone Hep Cat**: Fran Blanche's firsthand account places the first commercial Hep Cat in production by late 1995.
- **Frantone Peachfuzz**: firsthand history places the Peachfuzz in 1997.
- **Foxrox ProVibe 1992 original generation**: Dave Fox documents the first official Foxrox pedal, with around 40 large-box units plus rackmount examples.
- **Foxrox ProVibe 1993-1994 second generation**: manufacturer documentation explicitly distinguishes a smaller second version produced in 1993 and 1994.
- **Analog Man Sun Face early lineage**: early Sun Face history is preserved as an evolution from Fuzz Face modification work rather than treated as a single timeless model.
- **Real McCoy Custom RMC3**: 1994 public release and 1996 move into its own original case provide a clean non-dirt builder chronology boundary.
- **Real McCoy Custom RMC1**: 1998 first production state documented as a non-tunable wah derived from the builder's most requested modifications.
- **Black Cat original OD-Fuzz family**: 1993 founding and late-2007 end of the original company's production establish a long original-company envelope before the 2009 relaunch.
- **Foxrox Captain Coconut**: design discussion begins in November 1999 with Mike Piera, production starts in summer 2000, and owner-manual evidence brackets the original production window to August 2000-July 2001.

### Key research findings

1. **The early boutique era was a network, not a clean succession.** Prescription Electronics, Fulltone, Frantone, Foxrox, Analog Man, RMC and Black Cat emerged from overlapping modification, restoration, vintage-recreation and small-shop manufacturing cultures.
2. **Foxrox provides one of the strongest production clocks in the archive.** The ProVibe has explicit 1992 and 1993-1994 generations, while later Foxrox material provides a precise 2000-2001 Captain Coconut window.
3. **Tiny production runs matter.** Foxrox's original ProVibe and Prescription Electronics' hand-built examples demonstrate why early boutique identification cannot rely on modern model names alone.
4. **A model may have multiple identity layers.** Prescription Electronics Experience combines product-era evidence, hand-painted specimen variation and unresolved power-jack history, making it a useful specimen-identification case study.
5. **Builder chronology can include non-dirt companions.** RMC wah and Foxrox ProVibe are retained as scope-boundary records because they illuminate the same manufacturing culture that produced dirt pedals.
6. **The modification-business to branded-pedal transition is becoming visible.** Analog Man's early Fuzz Face work and later Sun Face lineage show how modification expertise could evolve into a recognizable retail product family.

### Evidence discipline

- Manufacturer and builder-authored histories remain the strongest evidence class for production counts and date windows.
- Surviving marketplace specimens are treated as object evidence, not universal production histories.
- Unresolved factory-versus-owner modifications remain unresolved.
- Scope-boundary records may remain in builder chronology without entering the central dirt count.
- Later relaunches and reissues are kept separate from original-company production states.
- No schematics, PCB layouts, full BOMs, gutshot libraries or cloning instructions are added.

### Current lineage position

The archive now has a stronger bridge from the 1990s boutique pioneers into the early-2000s expansion: Prescription Electronics and Foxrox show small-run vintage-recreation work; Fulltone, Frantone and Black Cat demonstrate the emerging branded-boutique production model; Analog Man preserves the modification-to-product transition; and RMC documents the parallel specialty-builder market.
