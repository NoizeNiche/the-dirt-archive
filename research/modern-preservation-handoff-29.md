# Modern Preservation Track Handoff 29

## Mission command: YARP
YARP means resume the full Dirt Archive mission: continue researching builders/companies and their overdrive, distortion and fuzz products; collect trustworthy historical/product/production/variant/edition/lineage/identification evidence; update the public catalog; backfill existing records; preserve uncertainty; and keep this breadcrumb current.

## Current position
2023 modern preservation continues. This pass moved deeper into the fuzz long tail, prioritizing products with unusually strong external identification surfaces, explicit generation boundaries, novel circuit-topology markers, unusual control layouts, and useful builder-level production evidence.

## Important research notes
- Rabbit Hole FX Pile of Sticks is a distinct product from the earlier Merkin. Its clean/fuzz blend architecture, Low/High EQ, Gain, Fuzz Volume, Clean Volume and Chaos control are particularly valuable identification markers. Contemporary 2023 coverage describes Chaos as progressively adding intermodulation and upper-octave material.
- Hungry Robot The Stampede is a compact 4069 CMOS glitch fuzz. Its two-knob layout, Sensitivity behavior and square-wave character make the physical enclosure unusually easy to identify. Contemporary coverage dates the unveiling to September 2023.
- Vahlbruch Octavia V2 is retained as a separate generation because 2023 coverage explicitly identifies V2 and adds Edge plus silent switching. Preserve the earlier V1 separately rather than treating V2 as artwork-only.
- Hiwatt Custom Shop Filter Fuzz MKII is a clean production-boundary case. The original large-format hand-wired Custom Filter Fuzz moved to a smaller board-friendly MKII enclosure while retaining hand-wired construction and adding the documented Thick, curated three-position Filter and internal Bias features.
- Collision Devices TARS is a relationship-rich product derived from the Singularity fuzz section of Black Hole Symmetry, but its analog MS-20-style filter, cutoff fader, serial/parallel routing and expression/CV implementation make it a distinct product identity rather than a simple Black Hole Symmetry variant.
- Sutton Instruments' Del Fuego is important at the builder level because contemporary coverage identifies it as the company's first pedal. Preserve its fuzz + independent boost structure and keep the later Bass version as a separate related product.

## Data rule reinforced
A product can be historically related to another circuit or builder without being the same catalog object. Preserve source-family lineage, product identity, generation, edition, production enclosure, and specimen clues as separate dimensions. V2 is a label that needs evidence, not an automatic claim of a circuit rewrite.
