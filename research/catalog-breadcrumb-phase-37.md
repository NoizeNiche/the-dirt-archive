# Phase 37 handoff supplement

Phase 37 added Caroline Guitar Company, Rainger FX, Red Panda, Pladask Elektrisk, drolo, and JPTR FX.

Discovery volume: 110 product records.

Media volume: 12 reference-only image leads, with rights status tracked separately from source discovery.

Primary research seams:
- Caroline: Wave Cannon lineage, Icarus/Haymaker/Blues/Olympia/Shigeharu families, current CROM and Aaron Graves Overdrive.
- Rainger: Freakenbender/Freakenstein lineage, Razor Fuzz, Pull Focus, Break Box, and unusual Igor/mini-pedal ecosystem.
- Red Panda: Bitmap, Particle, Raster, Tensor and current-generation product families.
- Pladask: Vrang plus a broad experimental catalog not fully represented in Effects Database.
- drolo: Lilac Cloud, Giant Hogweed, Kiwami, Moon Rabbit, Molecular Disruptor, Stamme[n], Strands, Stretch Weaver, Twin Peaks and related one-person experimental work.
- JPTR FX: Warlow, Jive, Silvermachine, Katastrophe, Bleach, Super Weirdo and current anniversary/special variants.

All six remain Discovery status. Product-by-product chronology, versions, physical identification and relationship mapping are deferred to the later deep-research pass.
