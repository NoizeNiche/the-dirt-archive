## 2020-2026 audit protocol
Cross-check each year against: (1) annual GPX finalists, (2) GPX longlists and launch roundups, and (3) builder/manufacturer/contemporary specialist evidence.

A finalist already present should be enriched rather than duplicated. Longlist-only products are legitimate discovery candidates. Explicit V2/V3/MKII/Edition labels remain independent. 2026 remains a live current-year queue rather than a finalized annual census.
