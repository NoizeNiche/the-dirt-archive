# Modern Preservation Track Handoff 33

## Mission command: YARP
YARP means resume the full Dirt Archive mission: continue researching builders and their overdrive, distortion and fuzz products; collect trustworthy historical, product, production, variant, edition, lineage and identification evidence; update the public catalog; preserve uncertainty; and keep this breadcrumb current.

## Current position
The preservation track has moved into **2020**. The project is now working through the beginning of the modern archive decade and increasingly finding products where collaboration, programmable control, generation boundaries and hybrid analog/digital architecture are central to identification.

## Important research notes
- Automatone Preamp MKII is a major collaboration record. Its motorized slider controls, presets and MIDI control make the control layer itself part of the product identity. Preserve Chase Bliss and Benson roles separately from the underlying analog gain system.
- Sunn O))) Life Pedal V2 is a textbook example of why version numbers cannot be translated automatically into circuit revisions. EQD explicitly documents V2 as using the same core circuit as the 2019 limited run while adding expression control and Flexi-Switch technology, plus a more compact enclosure.
- Bliss Factory is a collaboration-derived Fuzz Factory family object. Preserve ZVEX lineage separately from Chase Bliss digital-control, preset and limited-production identity.
- Demedash Spidola is retained as a distinct 2020 product record rather than being absorbed into a generic Demedash fuzz family. Later catalog status should not erase its original release identity.
- Redbeard Honey Badger is a heavily identifiable octave fuzz with switchable octave operation, DIVIDE control for blending -1 and -2 octaves, and two-band EQ. Preserve it separately from the builder's later Hairy Squid.
- Red Witch Fuzz God IV has an explicit fourth-generation label and a very strong identification footprint: three functional modes, three footswitches, Sputter bias control, Wrath oscillation control, internal DIP settings and NOS BC109 transistors. This is a real generation record.
- Spaceman Sputnik III is explicitly the third iteration of the Sputnik all-germanium fuzz. Earlier Sputnik generations must remain separately mappable.
- Nordland ODR-C should remain an ODR-family lineage record with its own builder identity and production history rather than being treated as a Nashville product clone.
- Wampler Pantheon Deluxe is a separate dual-channel product from the original single Pantheon. Preserve the Deluxe enclosure, independent controls and stacking behavior as production identity.

## Data rule reinforced
The archive is now encountering more programmable and hybrid pedals. The identification model must distinguish audio-path technology, control technology, firmware/preset behavior, enclosure generation and collaboration/manufacturing identity. Later firmware changes must not silently rewrite original hardware history.
