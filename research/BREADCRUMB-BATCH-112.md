# The Dirt Archive · Breadcrumb · Batch 112

## Completed

Phase One Batch 112 expanded the Japanese builder census with three strongly documented workshop/brands: Y.O.S. Guitar Workshop, E.W.S. (Engineering Work Store), and Tone Brew Club.

## Builders

### Y.O.S. Guitar Workshop
- Canonical Japanese builder/workshop based in Shizuoka.
- First-party documentation identifies Smoggy Overdrive as a handmade original overdrive and preserves its development/release history from 2015 onward.

### E.W.S. (Engineering Work Store)
- Canonical Japanese engineering/effects brand associated with PCI Japan.
- First-party history states E.W.S. began in 2006 as a repair/modification engineering section and released original pedals in 2009, including Fuzzy Drive, Brute Drive and Little Brute Drive.
- Current first-party catalog retains Fuzzy Drive, Brute Drive, Little Brute Drive, LBD2, Little Fuzzy Drive and Stormy Bass Drive.

### Tone Brew Club
- Canonical Japanese effects brand based in Obuse, Nagano.
- First-party shop separates a regular lineup from Kimagure Pedals and documents full-discrete drive/fuzz development as well as individual contemporary products.

## Information on the pedal

- Smoggy Overdrive: first-party description covers fat clean boost, crunch and dynamic drive with three controls; Y.O.S. describes it as an original handmade overdrive.
- E.W.S. Fuzzy Drive: first-party description covers fuzz, overdrive and in-between sounds and explains the op-amp-based design choice for stability.
- E.W.S. Brute Drive: smooth classic distortion with Volume, Gain, Tone and a boost switch.
- E.W.S. Little Brute Drive: compact one-knob drive covering heavy crunch to fiery distortion.
- E.W.S. LBD2: low/high gain voicings, three-stage mid switch and selectable 9V/16V operation.
- E.W.S. Little Fuzzy Drive: Fuzzy Drive and Fat Fuzzy modes with dynamic response.
- E.W.S. Stormy Bass Drive: first E.W.S. bass overdrive with Mid Boost, Normal and Mid Cut modes.
- Tone Brew Club 鎌鼬: full-discrete saturation-focused overdrive with fast pick response and two gain/bass states.
- Recipe "47" Goat Soup V2: Rams Head-inspired Muff-style fuzz with mid boost and controllable feedback.
- One Foot Fuzz: compact fuzz/drive/overdrive hybrid designed for fast guitar-volume cleanup.
- Recipe "79": broad-range overdrive from crunch through brown-sound territory with switchable open/high-gain modes.
- BC108 FUZZ and ver2: hand-selected silicon Fuzz Face-style lineage, with ver2 adding selectable low-end modes and improved high-frequency behavior.
- BOBD FUZZ #1: BC108 Fuzz Face-style fuzz combined with tremolo.

## Normalization decisions

- Y.O.S. Smoggy Onboard is treated as an onboard adaptation, not a separate central pedal model.
- E.W.S. Brute Drive Eric Gales signature is kept relationship-only because it is explicitly a limited edition of the Brute Drive platform.
- Tone Brew Club regular and Kimagure Pedals lines remain under the same builder rather than being split into separate builders.
- Recipe "79" Custom ver remains relationship-only as a one-off/custom variant.
- Booster products remain outside central dirt.
- No schematics, PCB layouts, gutshots or complete component inventories are collected.
