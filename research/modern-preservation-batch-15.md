# Modern Preservation Track — Batch 15

## Position
2023 census continues, with a deliberate focus on variant-rich products and builder/product relationships that are easy to lose when annual roundups become the de facto catalog.

## Verified / strong primary evidence

### Browne Amplification — Protein Dual Overdrive
- Protein is a dual overdrive developed by Dave Brown with guitarist/producer Adam Sniegowski.
- Manufacturer documentation distinguishes V2, V3 and V4.
- V2 uses side-mounted jacks and mechanical switching; documentation specifies two internal switches for split mode.
- V3 moves to top-mounted jacks and soft switching, with a single internal switch for split mode.
- Browne explicitly states that V2 and V3 were intended to sound the same, while component tolerances can produce minor sonic variation.
- V4 adds a hi-cut option on the Blue side and three internal Green-side clipping/voicing options; Browne also offers a V3-to-V4 board upgrade, available only for V3 and not V2.2 or earlier.
- Archive rule: V2/V3/V4 are genuine generation records because the manufacturer documents hardware, switching and feature changes. Do not infer that every enclosure/color change is a generation.

### Browne Amplification — The Carbon
- Carbon originated as the Blue side of the Protein and later became a standalone pedal.
- Carbon V2 adds a three-position High-Cut function around the original Carbon voice and adds adjustable startup state through the soft switch.
- Manufacturer documentation identifies left/center/right high-cut behavior and gives V2 power/physical data.
- Archive rule: preserve the Protein-side lineage separately from the standalone Carbon product family. Shared circuit ancestry does not make the products interchangeable records.

## High-value lineage lead

### PastFX — PX76 Punkifriar
- 2023 product explicitly developed as a revival/enhancement of the discontinued DOD FX76 Punkifier.
- PastFX added a fifth control, identified as the Mead/Contour control, expanding the original concept.
- Archive treatment: separate retail product record linked to a predecessor product lineage. Do not collapse PX76 into DOD FX76 as though they were the same production object.

## Compound / artwork identity lead

### Bardic Audio Devices — 2 Stroke Beaver / Shelob's Lair
- 2023 reporting describes the two names as the same circuit with different artwork.
- Product identity should therefore remain shared while artwork/edition identity is recorded separately.
- This is a useful test case for the Archive's Product Family vs Edition model.

## Method notes
- Effects Database late-2023 weekly indexes are discovery indexes, not final authority. Week 49 listed 152 new effects and week 50 listed 137; these numbers demonstrate why the census needs a discovery layer before verification.
- GPX's 2023 roundups are useful cross-checks, especially where multiple independent sources converge on the same product.
- Primary manufacturer evidence remains the promotion gate for VERIFIED status.
- Preserve uncertainty at the claim level. Do not turn a discovery listing into a manufacturing attribution without evidence.
- Continue prioritizing 2023 before moving to 2022.
