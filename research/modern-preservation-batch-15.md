# Modern Preservation Track — Batch 15

## Position
2023 census continues, with a deliberate focus on variant-rich products where the manufacturer's own documentation can preserve identification clues for later generations.

## Verified / strong primary evidence

### Browne Amplification — Protein Dual Overdrive
- Protein is a dual overdrive developed by Dave Brown with guitarist/producer Adam Sniegowski.
- Manufacturer documentation distinguishes V2, V3 and V4.
- V2 uses side-mounted jacks and mechanical switching; documentation specifies two internal switches for split mode.
- V3 moves to top-mounted jacks and soft switching, with a single internal switch for split mode.
- Browne explicitly states that V2 and V3 were intended to sound the same, while component tolerances can produce minor sonic variation.
- V4 adds a hi-cut option on the Blue side and three internal Green-side clipping/voicing options; Browne also offers a V3-to-V4 board upgrade, available only for V3 and not V2.2 or earlier.
- Archive rule: V2/V3/V4 are genuine generation records because the manufacturer documents hardware, switching and circuit-level feature changes. Do not infer that every enclosure/color change is a generation.

### Browne Amplification — The Carbon
- Carbon originated as the Blue side of the Protein and later became a standalone pedal.
- Carbon V2 adds a three-position High-Cut function around the original Carbon voice and adds adjustable startup state through the soft switch.
- Manufacturer documentation identifies left/center/right high-cut behavior and gives V2 power/physical data.
- Archive rule: preserve the Protein-side lineage separately from the standalone Carbon product family. The shared circuit ancestry does not make the products interchangeable records.

## Discovery / census context
Effects Database's December 2023 weekly indexes recorded unusually large numbers of new catalog entries, including 152 new effects in week 49 and 137 in week 50. These indexes are useful discovery maps, not automatic verification authorities.

## Method note
This batch reinforces the Archive's distinction between product family, generation, and specimen-level traits. Manufacturer-documented V2/V3/V4 changes should become identification evidence. Cosmetic variants, blemishes and color editions should remain separate unless evidence demonstrates a product or manufacturing change.
