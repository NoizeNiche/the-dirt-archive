# 2010-2014 Microboutique Audit 10

## Scope

This batch extends the 2010-2014 boutique-builder census using historical Effects Database indexes, builder-authored history where available, and contemporary event/archive traces. The emphasis is catalog breadth and identity separation, not circuit reconstruction.

## Shoe Pedals

CJM Venter's builder timeline is unusually valuable. The first Shoe prototypes were made in summer 2011 during an intense period of nightly experimentation. The Silver Apple emerged from that period; Pixel followed after Venter deliberately altered a Silver Apple prototype. Other early designs include Spitvalve, Frog, Kung Fuzz, Evil Rainbow, Legalizer, Tighty Whitey, Green Man and Black Samurai. Sporadic sales/consignment activity occurred in 2012, followed by a major aesthetic and construction transition in 2013. By late 2013 new products were PCB-based, while retaining off-board hardware and through-hole parts. The archive therefore separates early experimental/design states from later production/redesign states.

## Moollon

Moollon is a South Korean builder operated by Young Joon Park. Its history places Overdrive and Distortion prototypes in 2002, company formation in 2003, and subsequent BufferAge/VintAge product development from 2004 onward. The indexed catalog later includes Fuzz Thirty-Two, SLO-101, SLO-201, British Octah, Sol Fuzz and Z-O-D. The 2013 NAMM records for Distortion MkII and Overdrive Extra Headroom are retained as later product states rather than merged into the earlier 2006-documented models. Documentation date is not treated as first-sale date.

## Enormous Door FX

The January 2013 catalog record shows ICBM, BSO Burning Spirits Overdrive, PDX Distortion and SCUD Fuzz. The ICBM and SCUD are explicitly linked by a trademark-driven name change, so the archive treats them as successive retail names for the same documented product lineage rather than unrelated circuits. The July 2013 update adds FOAD Distortion and JINN Octave Fuzz. Austin, Texas is the documented builder location for the surviving records.

## 1969 Effects

The 2012 archive adds Bone Blender and Scarlet Storm together. These are modern builder products oriented around classic fuzz lineages. The archive keeps them strictly under the 1969 Effects identity and does not transfer vintage manufacturer status from their reference designs. A French association appears in secondary modern coverage but should remain provisional until primary company history is found.

## Myco Pedals

The January 2013 archive identifies four products: One and Two for guitar, Three and Four for bass, all described as tube overdrive products. Bass overdrive remains in scope because it materially belongs to the dirt catalog. Builder identity, region, first-sale dates, and later catalog history remain unresolved.

## Kinsman FX

The July 2013 weekly archive identifies Hi-Gain Lead and Crunch Distortion among a new-brand group. Both are cataloged as discovery-level products. The archive deliberately avoids inferring manufacturing geography or a larger catalog from the two surviving entries alone.

## Gimp FX

Filthy Little Pig is present in the January 2012 new-product index, while a January 2011 contemporary video description supplies independent evidence that the product existed by then. The builder's broader identity and production catalog remain thin, so the census stays at discovery baseline rather than claiming completion.

## Yu Brother / Profi-Sound

OV-200 Overdrive Preamp appears in the January 2012 archive under the composite name "Yu Brother (by Profi-Sound)." This is exactly the sort of label that can create false builder duplication. The current record retains the marketed identity and the possible maker relationship separately until physical manufacturing evidence supports canonicalization.

## Scope decisions

- No kit, schematic, PCB, BOM, or circuit-reconstruction material is published.
- Modern recreations are not assigned the historical manufacturer of the vintage design they reference.
- NAMM or database publication dates are evidence of public documentation, not automatic first-sale dates.
- Limited prototypes, consignment units, redesigns and renamed retail states remain separate when the evidence supports that distinction.
