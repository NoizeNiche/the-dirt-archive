**Builder → Pedals → Photos → Information on the pedal**

This batch continues the Japanese builder census rather than moving into site polish.

## Builder work
### Inner Bamboo / SUMO STOMP
- Promoted **INNER BAMBOO** to a canonical Japanese builder/manufacturer identity because SUMO STOMP's own About page states that SUMO STOMP, IBBI and Tone of Gold are Inner Bamboo brands.
- Kept **SUMO STOMP** as the visible pedal brand in product records so retail names remain searchable.
- Mapped a dirt-focused catalog from the current/historical SUMO STOMP shop:
  - SUMO #8
  - SUMO #8TEX
  - SUMO #9
  - Longtail #873
  - MINOTAUR
  - BIG MAGE
  - 73'COLORSOUND OVERDRIVER MOD / 松風 -MATSUKAZE-
- Preserved meaningful sub-states/limited variants separately:
  - SUMO #8 Final Edition
  - TEX limited
  - Longtail #873 Limited Milled Case
  - 73'COLORSOUND OVERDRIVER MOD / 松風 Telefunken Ver.
- Excluded SUMO TRON, SUMO COMP and SUMO PRE from central dirt because their primary functions are auto-wah, compression and preamp/other utility rather than OD/DS/FZ.

## Evidence notes
- First-party SUMO STOMP About page identifies the brand relationship to Inner Bamboo.
- First-party SUMO STOMP catalog supplies current and historical model names and product descriptions.
- Ishibashi and Musicland KEY provide exterior/product evidence and supporting descriptions for Matsukaze and Minotaur.
- Big Mage is documented by SUMO STOMP as a limited 1972 Triangle Big Muff clone; retain as product-history evidence without publishing internal construction material beyond high-level public descriptions.

## Archive discipline
- No schematics, PCB layouts, gutshots, BOMs or cloning instructions added.
- Variants are not promoted to separate families unless evidence supports substantive model identity.
- Builder/manufacturer identity is kept separate from visible retail brand naming.
- Photo evidence is catalogued as exterior/reference evidence with source trails, not assumed to equal image-license permission.

## Next direction
Continue the Japanese builder/OEM census, prioritizing incomplete catalogs and ambiguous manufacturer/brand/distributor chains before shifting the research center of gravity to Taiwan/Hong Kong/mainland China.
