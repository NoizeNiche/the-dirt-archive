# Wampler / Indyguitarist — 2002–2005 Origin Audit

## Status
**Builder entity:** CANONICAL LINEAGE
**Period:** 2002–2005 DIY/mod origin period
**Confidence:** HIGH for the 2002 first-built-pedal anchor; branding/product chronology needs later dated-source work.

## Historical spine
Brian Wampler describes his path as beginning with pedal modification. In a 2019 MusicRadar interview he states that the first pedal he built was in **2002**, a basic overdrive assembled on veroboard from an online DIY design. His early work was associated with the **Indyguitarist** identity before the later Wampler Pedals brand became established.

The early story matters to the archive because it captures the same DIY-to-boutique pipeline seen elsewhere in this period: repair/modification, online communities, small home-built runs, then increasingly formal commercial products.

## Dirt relevance
The early Wampler/Indyguitarist catalog should be reconstructed as a product-family layer rather than assuming that every later Wampler model belongs to the 2002–2005 period. The archive should specifically separate:
- early modified commercial pedals,
- DIY/experimental home builds,
- Indyguitarist-branded products,
- Wampler-branded products after the transition,
- later factory-produced models.

## Archive cautions
Do not use modern Wampler model pages to retroactively date the entire early catalog. The naming transition itself is historically important and should be preserved as a branding/organizational boundary.

## Key source
- MusicRadar interview with Brian Wampler, published 2019, including his statement that his first built pedal dates to 2002.
- Guitar.com interview with Brian Wampler discussing the 2000–2005 boutique transition.

## Archive decision
Promote **Wampler / Indyguitarist** as a canonical early-2000s boutique lineage with a 2002 first-built-pedal anchor. Keep the detailed 2002–2005 product list open for a dedicated catalog audit.
