# Dirt Archive Breadcrumb Addendum 63

## Scope
East Coast Fuzz identity collision and unresolved-builder audit.

## New research file
`research/east-coast-fuzz-micatone-halifax-andre-evidence-27.tsv`

## Findings
- Mica-Tone now explicitly splits into two catalog identities: the late-1960s/early-1970s East Coast Mica-Tone Fuzz, documented as a rebranded Manny's Fuzz, and the separate Japanese Mica-Tone Fuzz Master/Super Fuzz FY-6 variant dating from about 1970.
- Halifax Fuzz remains a distinct East Coast marketed state. Specialist evidence supports its close relationship to Manny's and a tone-control variant, but not its physical factory.
- Andre Fuzzier is upgraded from a generic family clue to a medium-confidence builder hypothesis because multiple construction details cross-match Andre effects and East Coast Fuzz units. The outside-OEM explanation remains explicitly open.
- An Orpheum example reportedly marked "Gene" inside the enclosure is retained only as a clue. Provenance and production timing are not independently established, so no builder promotion is made.

## Editorial action
No public lineage edges were promoted. The identity collision is resolved in research so future catalog work does not accidentally merge the East Coast Mica-Tone with the Japanese FY-6 Mica-Tone.

Also look for surviving examples with dated components or original documentation that can separate production batches.
