# The Dirt Archive · Breadcrumb · Batch 99

## Completed

Phase One Batch 99 continued the Japanese S corridor with one clearly established boutique lineage and one builder/brand family whose strongest evidence is clone-focused.

## Builder

- **SHINOS** is now mapped as a canonical Japanese builder lineage. Official SHINOS material identifies the Blue Tongue Series pedals as Naughty Brain, The BLAST and Alice The Goon. Reverb's builder interview identifies Masaru Shinohara as the master builder behind SHINOS.
- **Inner Bamboo / SUMO STOMP** is recorded as a canonical candidate because the official SUMO STOMP about page explicitly states that SUMO STOMP, IBBI and Tone of Gold are brands of Inner Bamboo.

## Pedals

### SHINOS

- Naughty Brain: Marshall-style overdrive with broad gain range and TIGHT/DEEP voicing.
- The BLAST: transparent/natural overdrive with TIGHT/DEEP voicing and substantial gain.
- Alice The Goon: British-style medium/high-gain overdrive extending the Blue Tongue family.

### Inner Bamboo / SUMO STOMP

- Longtail #873
- Short Tail #S430
- MINOTAUR
- SUMO #8
- SUMO #9
- SUMO #8TEX
- BIG MAGE

The SUMO STOMP records remain relationship-only because the builder's own catalog explicitly identifies several as reproductions/clones of specific vintage pedals or reference units.

## Photos

- SHINOS official product pages provide direct exterior product imagery for all three Blue Tongue pedals and are retained as primary photo-source leads.
- SUMO STOMP's official shop and Japanese retailers provide exterior imagery for its documented models; these remain source/credit leads for later rights review.

## Information on the pedal

- SHINOS documents a 2023 renewal of Naughty Brain with a smaller enclosure while maintaining the original sound lineage, so the archive should treat this as a generation/production update rather than a new conceptual model.
- The SHINOS official Blue Tongue page documents the three-pedal family together, making the family relationship unusually clear.
- SUMO STOMP's official catalog distinguishes original brand identity from its vintage-reference work. Longtail #873 and Short Tail #S430 reproduce specific Klon Centaur units; SUMO #8 and #9 reproduce specific vintage Tube Screamer units; BIG MAGE reproduces a 1972 Triangle Big Muff; #8TEX derives from a Tone of Gold specification.

## Normalization decisions

- Do not count SHINOS finish/enclosure presentations as separate concepts.
- Do not count Naughty Brain's 2023 smaller enclosure as a separate model.
- Do not count SUMO STOMP's limited enclosure or component variants as new conceptual models.
- Keep clone/reference-derived SUMO STOMP objects visible as documented products/relationships without allowing them to inflate the original-design dirt count.
- SUMO TRON, SUMO COMP and SUMO PRE remain outside the central dirt inventory because their documented functions are envelope filtering, compression or bass preamp duties rather than dedicated dirt pedals.
