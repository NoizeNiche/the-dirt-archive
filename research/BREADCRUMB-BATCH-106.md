## Pedals

Relationship-only generation/specimen records:

- MSS-06E
- MSS-06F
- MSS-06CP

The builder's own MSS14 history states that MSS-06 evolved G/BA → RS → C → E → F, with F identified as the final mass-production form.

## Photos

Reverb's MSS-06E and MSS-06F listings provide exterior imagery, while 2026 owner documentation provides an exterior photo trail for MSS-06CP. These are preserved as source leads for future rights/credit review.

## Information on the pedal

- MSS-06E is documented as a later MSS-06 form that omits functions present on earlier generations and leaves chassis space for customization.
- MSS-06F is documented as an updated MSS-06 generation with expanded gain and level range.
- MSS-06CP is a special color/presentation of the established family, not a new conceptual model.
- The official MSS14 narrative explicitly establishes the G/BA, RS, C, E and F progression.

## Normalization decisions

- E and F remain generation records under MSS-06.
- CP remains a specimen/edition relationship.
- No schematics, PCB layouts, gutshots or complete component inventories are collected.
