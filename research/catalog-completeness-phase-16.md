# The Dirt Archive — Catalog Completeness Phase 16

## Scope
This pass advances the archive from representative discovery toward full builder-lineup enumeration. Each selected builder is treated as a catalog entity whose complete Effects Database lineup is captured, including non-dirt products, discontinued models, collaborations, editions, and products that reveal important variations.

## Builders audited

### Black Cat — 24 products
Effects Database lists 24 Black Cat products. The builder history identifies Fred Bonte, with the original Texas company founded in 1993 and the original operation ending in late 2007 before a later rebuilt line. The archive should preserve the pre-2007 lineage and later rebuilt Black Cat entity/history without automatically treating the later line as continuous production. Source: https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/blackcat

The full snapshot is captured in `public/discovery-16.tsv`.

Important future research targets include the D&S / OD-Fuzz cluster, the Monster K-Fuzz and Monster K-Fuzz Stompbox distinction, the OD-Fuzz CME Edition, and the multiple Vibe formats. These are exactly the kinds of records where enclosure, branding, production era, or electronics may distinguish otherwise similar-looking products.

### BSM — 35 products
Effects Database lists 35 BSM products and identifies Bernd C. Meiser and Steffi Meiser with the company founded in 2002. The source describes BSM as a highly specialized German boutique operation focused on historical rock tones, artist-specific boosters, fuzzes, and overdrives. BSM ceased operations after Bernd C. Meiser's death in July 2024. Source: https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/bsm

The complete 35-product snapshot is captured in `public/discovery-16.tsv`.

Priority future research:
- RM family variants
- HS / HS-C / HS-S family and the germanium/silicon distinction
- BM-Q and BM-Q Special
- Galaxy 1011
- RPA / RPA Major / RPA California
- artist-specific models
- documented NOS transistor usage and other historically meaningful production distinctions

The archive should record the historical role of germanium/silicon changes where they distinguish models or periods, but should not publish full circuit recipes.

### Lovepedal — 62 products
Effects Database lists 62 Lovepedal products. The catalog contains fuzz, overdrive, boost, amp-simulation, modulation, delay, reverb, utility, and other products, so this is a critical example of why the archive must enumerate the whole builder catalog instead of only dirt models. Source: https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/lovepedal

The complete 62-product snapshot is captured in `public/discovery-16.tsv`.

Important future research targets:
- Lovepedal Mk12 Bender, including documented Chrome Bender versus Hammertone Bender production changes; Effects Database records period-correct OC76 transistors on one version and NKT275 on the later housing/version.
- Dragon Fuzz limited editions and transistor-family differences across versions.
- Tchula custom-origin history and later production runs.
- Super Sic Tone and its documented three-position tone selector.
- SuperLead, JTM, Purple Plexi and other repeatedly revised overdrive families.
- handwired versus later production forms and documented special editions.

Sources: https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/lovepedal and individual product records such as https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/lovepedal/bender/mk12

## Research rule reinforced

A catalog count is only the beginning. Every enumerated model must eventually be evaluated for:

- alternate names
- editions
- rehouses
- artist/signature variants
- enclosure and graphics changes
- production-period changes
- component-family changes that materially distinguish versions
- discontinuation and revival
- collaborations and OEM relationships
- documented commentary and user/collector observations
- photographs and image rights
- conflicting claims

The full product page should ultimately distinguish **what changed** and **why the change matters**, while keeping sensitive circuit-reconstruction information out of the public archive.
