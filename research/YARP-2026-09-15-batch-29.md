# YARP 2026-09-15 Batch 29

## Focus
Gary Stewart Hurst / Electronic Sounds catalog separation from later reissues and Italian OEM relationships.

## Findings

### Gary Hurst Electronics / Electronic Sounds
NAMM's oral history identifies Gary Stewart Hurst as the Tone Bender designer who continued producing and designing fuzz products beyond the 1960s, including the Marshall Supa Fuzz. Later historical research documents his move to Italy and the establishment of Electronic Sounds in the early 1970s.

### Electronic Sounds catalog baseline
Accessible historical references support a dirt-centered Electronic Sounds baseline containing the Fuzz King, UFO Octa-Fuzz / Octafuzz-family products, and related fuzz designs, while the wider range also included sustain/compression, phaser, wah-fuzz and other effects. The archive keeps the fuzz products central and records sustain/phaser items as related boundary/catalog records.

### Fuzz King
The Fuzz King is consistently documented as a Gary Hurst design in the Italian-made Electronic Sounds period. Secondary historical work describes the family as spanning Electronic Sounds, CBS/Arbiter and Meazzi UFO marketed states. Because exact factory attribution within Italy is not yet proven at the model level, the archive treats Gary Hurst/Electronic Sounds as the designer/marketed lineage and keeps physical Italian manufacture evidence-qualified.

### UFO / Meazzi relationship
Hylight's current Gary Hurst historical material explicitly describes the 1970s relationship among CBS/Arbiter, Meazzi UFO and Hurst's Electronic Sounds range. The archive records these as linked marketed/design states rather than creating three independent manufacturers.

### Catalog caution
Marketplace listings repeatedly assign a nominal 1970 date to Fuzz King specimens. The archive does not convert that listing date into a hard first-release year. The preferred period is early-to-mid 1970s, with exact model chronology still open for primary catalog verification.

## Evidence boundary
No schematics, PCB layouts, BOMs, gutshots, cloning instructions or circuit reconstruction material added.
