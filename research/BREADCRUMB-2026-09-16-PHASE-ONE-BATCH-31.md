## Evidence and handling notes
- Red Witch is documented by its own site as a New Zealand company founded by Ben Fulton in 2003. Fuzz God, II, IIC, III and IV are retained as separate generations or limited states because published sources explicitly distinguish them. Fuzz God III's first run is documented as 100 numbered hand-built pieces; Fuzz God IIC as a 99-piece hand-modified numbered run.
- Deep Trip's own about page dates the builder to 2007 and identifies Du Menegozzo as original designer, with units made one-by-one in downtown Sao Paulo. The current shop exposes both current models and historical/variant/B-stock states; those are preserved without inflating the core family count.
- Hungry Robot's own Classics archive identifies older discontinued products that can be rebuilt direct-only one-by-one. The dirt-focused records in this batch include Bumblebee, Dual Overdrive, The Hungry Robot [LG], [FZ], and The Collective Fuzz. Reverb/modulation-only products were intentionally excluded from the central dirt inventory.
