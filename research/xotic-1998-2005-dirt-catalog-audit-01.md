# Xotic Electronics — 1998–2005 Dirt Catalog Audit

## Status
**CANONICAL LINEAGE / STRONGLY VERIFIED PRODUCT TIMELINE** for the early Xotic period, with later-generation boundaries kept separate.

## Why this matters
Xotic is an important bridge from the late-1990s boutique ecosystem into the early-2000s overdrive/boost boom. Xotic states that its roots go back to 1996 in a San Fernando Valley garage and that the Robotalk 1 was the first pedal to carry the Xotic name. The brand was formally expanded under Prosound Communications Inc. in August 2000.

## Early pedal chronology
- **Robotalk 1** — 1998; first Xotic-branded pedal. Envelope-filter / arpeggiator product, not a dirt product, but it establishes the brand's pedal origin.
- **AC Booster** — introduced September 2002. Early Xotic gain/boost anchor.
- **RC Booster** — introduced November 2002. Early Xotic boost/gain anchor; the company later calls AC and RC its first distortion pedals.
- **BB Preamp** — introduced January 2005. Early Xotic overdrive flagship and the third member of the company's "Tremendous Trio."

## Classification note
For the archive's dirt taxonomy, preserve AC Booster, RC Booster, and BB Preamp as separate retail models. Do not collapse the later V2/V1.5 and anniversary/reissue variants into the original release records.

## Manufacturing / corporate notes
Xotic's history says Prosound Communications Inc. was established in August 2000 to acquire the Xotic brand and increase production/marketing. PCI Japan later supported importation and sales in Japan. This should be recorded as a corporate/distribution relationship, not automatically treated as a change in physical pedal manufacture.
