## Research rule
Use broad 2023 indexes to discover builders, then verify the builder, product identity, date, dirt category, physical markers, controls, revisions, editions and lineage individually. Do not infer manufacturer identity from a marketplace listing or a similar-looking enclosure.

## Backfill rule
Every promoted pedal must eventually receive variant/generation review and meaningful identification clues. A pedal with no known revision is still recorded as having its variant state evaluated, with uncertainty preserved.
