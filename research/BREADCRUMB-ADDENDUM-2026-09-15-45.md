## Historical findings

- Applied Audio Products Inc. is now documented as the U.S. physical manufacturer behind the FW-3 fuzz/wah/volume platform, with multiple marketed states including Guild, Crown, Kadent, Lou Rose, V-Jay and others.
- Hohner FW-10 is linked to the Applied FW-3 family, while Hohner ME-30 is separately documented as a 1977 Applied-made U.S. reintroduction of the Shin-Ei ME-7-derived Surf/Siren family.
- Hohner MF-50 remains a separate Japanese Shin-Ei/Companion production state from the later Applied Audio Hohner lineup.
- Hohner Tri Dirty Booster is retained as an Applied-built U.S. OEM state based on specialist historical research, preserving Hohner as the marketed identity.
- Goya Panther Box is retained as a medium-confidence Applied OEM candidate rather than promoted as a hard factory fact.

## Validation posture

Applied Audio is increasingly useful as a builder hub, but family resemblance alone is not being used to merge every related pedal. Japanese Shin-Ei production, U.S. Applied production, marketed branding, and later reissues remain separate evidence dimensions.
