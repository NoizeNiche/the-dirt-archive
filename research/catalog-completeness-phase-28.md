# Catalog Completeness Phase 28

## Objective
Breadth-first enumeration of five historically useful boutique builders. This phase deliberately records whole discoverable lineups rather than only fuzz/drive products.

## Builders
- BSM
- VFE Pedals
- Blackout Effectors
- Fredric Effects
- Basic Audio

## Discovery snapshots and sources
### BSM
Effects Database snapshot: 35 products. Builder history and complete product list captured from the BSM builder page. BSM was founded in 2002 by Bernd C. Meiser and Steffi Meiser; the business is now historical following Meiser's death in 2024.
Source: https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/bsm

### VFE Pedals
Effects Database snapshot: 42 products. Builder timeline notes BYOC discovery in 2008, heavy modified-pedal sales in summer 2008, first designed PCB May 2009, company founded January 2010 as VonRutter Family Effects, full-time June 2010, relay switching October 2010, and silkscreen/retail distribution April 2011.
Source: https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/vfe

### Blackout Effectors
Effects Database snapshot: 17 products. Founded by Kyle Tompkins in Vancouver in 2008 and moved to Asheville in 2009. Musket and related Blunderbuss/Special Twosome families deserve later generation mapping.
Source: https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/blackout

### Fredric Effects
Effects Database snapshot: 25 products. Run by Tim Webster and Stacey Hine in North London. The catalog is particularly useful for tracing compact-wedge formats and later expanded/family variants.
Source: https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/fredric

### Basic Audio
Effects Database snapshot: 27 products. John Lyons started making pedals in 1998. Product list includes several named Muff, Fuzz Face/Percolator, Tone Bender and Zonk-related families that should be treated as separate retail models until later evidence establishes relationships.
Source: https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/basicaudio

## Media/image leads collected during this phase
- Electro-Harmonix current official shop is an important future source for manufacturer imagery and current-vs-discontinued status: https://www.ehx.com/shop/ and https://www.ehx.com/category/pedals/
- VFE product imagery was found in a public Indiegogo campaign page showing multiple VFE/v(e) design products; retain as an image-discovery lead only until reuse rights are established.
- Fredric Effects product imagery was found in Guitar Pedal X's Birmingham Guitar Show coverage, including multiple Wedge-series products; retain as a reference lead unless the photographer/source license permits republication.
- Basic Audio has a broad catalog image in Guitar Pedal X's coverage showing many compact fuzz models; retain as a visual identification lead pending rights review.
- Effects Database product pages expose image/media links and are useful as discovery leads, but an image appearing there is not automatically licensed for redistribution.

## Audit notes
- BSM has unusually strong artist-specific/product-line naming. Later work should distinguish historical retail models from builder descriptions and possible internal revisions.
- VFE has a clear company-to-v(e) design transition that should be preserved as a builder/company relationship rather than flattening both into one undifferentiated catalog.
- Blackout Effectors has explicit named variants on product pages, including Musket alternate projects and FUBÄR derived from Fuzz Bear. These are ideal later family/lineage cases.
- Fredric Effects has many product families with repeated naming motifs and enclosure changes. Later research should map Wedge vs Mini Wedge generations without treating every enclosure revision as a new model automatically.
- Basic Audio has both model-level and family-level naming that will require careful later source reconciliation.

## Status
All 146 records are discovery/Enumerated candidates only. No product in this phase is promoted to Canonical or Deep Research solely because it appears in an aggregator catalog.
