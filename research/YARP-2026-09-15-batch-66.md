# YARP 2026-09-15 Batch 66: JEN Plug-In Distortion Booster Corridor

## Scope
Reconcile the compact guitar-output plug-in boosters sold under Jen, Vox, Meazzi, EUR, G.I.S., and GEM labels. These are dirt-bearing products but belong to a distinct plug-in product family from JEN's die-cast fuzz and fuzz-wah pedals.

## Builder and relationship structure
Jen Elettronica of Pescara, Italy is treated as the physical manufacturer. The surviving catalog trail shows multiple marketed labels sharing the Distortion Booster family. Vox V8162, Jen PE-402, Meazzi, EUR, G.I.S., and GEM are therefore relationship states, not automatically separate factories.

## Boundary
These direct-output-jack units are closer to boosters/distortion plug-ins than conventional floor pedals. They remain inside the dirt archive because the distortion function is explicit, but should be tagged as plug-in effects rather than counted as ordinary stompboxes.
