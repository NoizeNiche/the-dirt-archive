## Evidence notes
320design has a strong first-party archive. Its own About page describes 320design as a guitar-effects shop started by a graphic designer working with staff from an instrument-development division. The first-party lineup and store document Brown Feather distortion, Landmighty multifunction overdrive, Sunakake overdrive/booster and Canary Drive. The builder repeatedly states that units are handmade, and the 2026 store listing for Canary Drive notes that the current lot is expected to be the final production lot. The 2022 Strung Fuzz is an officially licensed JoJo's Bizarre Adventure Stone Ocean collaboration and should be preserved as a product/collaboration state under 320design.

Video Protone has a useful but incomplete trail. A June 2026 GearStrings review identifies it as a South Korean small-batch builder and documents the Essential Edition Dead Horse overdrive and Essential Edition Fuzz as later streamlined states of earlier boutique designs. Because a direct official builder storefront, dated identity page or archived first-party catalog was not recovered during this pass, the two products remain research candidates. Do not promote Video Protone to canonical builder status until primary identity evidence improves.
