# Phase One Batch 41 Breadcrumb

Date: 2026-09-16

## Batch
Batch 41 expands builder-first Phase One coverage across Finland, Sweden and Norway, emphasizing small independent builders and historical catalogs with unusually dirt-heavy or experimental product lines.

## Builders added
- Raato Custom Guitars — Finland; pedal sub-catalog includes Fuzzrocity, its bass-oriented Amalgam state, and STARLORD booster.
- Parasit Studio — northern Sweden; original experimental developer with a substantial lifetime fuzz/drive/synth project archive.
- Jamés Pedals — Örebro, Sweden; James Bennett's house-built pedal project with documented 2004 fuzz origins and later overdrive/fuzz/boost products.
- Locomofon AS — Norway; heavy-texture boutique catalog spanning fuzz, bass fuzz, overdrive, octave fuzz and development-stage distortion/fuzz products.
- Deaf Audio — Norway; historical one-person boutique operation associated with Magnus Nordbye, with surviving fuzz/boost catalog evidence.

## Evidence / boundary notes
- Raato's first-party pedal page explicitly describes Fuzzrocity as a modified Fuzz Face-style circuit using NPN silicon transistors, with controls taking it from crushing fuzz toward transparent overdrive. Fuzzrocity Amalgam adds blend and master-volume controls for bass and guitar. STARLORD is described as a Rangemaster-inspired booster with adjustable frequency range and boost intensity. These are central or gain-adjacent dirt records respectively.
- Parasit Studio's first-party pedals page and downloads archive together preserve both current and discontinued project states. The archive deliberately includes only projects materially involving fuzz, distortion or drive, while excluding guitar-synth, modulation and utility projects that do not belong in the central dirt layer. DIY-project availability is not treated as proof of a mass-produced retail run.
- Jamés Pedals is strongly anchored by Effects Database's interview/timeline: James Bennett made his first pedal, a fuzz, in 2004; registered a guitar-parts/repairs/modifications company in 2005; and says the pedal side had mostly taken over by 2007. The catalog records Ass Kickin' Overdrive, Fuzzy Pie Bass Fuzz, Treble Booster MkII/MkIII and Trebletastic Overdrive, while utility products remain separate.
- Locomofon's official current effects page provides explicit state labels. Fuz Completo, Everyscream, Calavera, Monito, Platanito and Radiator are available for order; Kindergarten Blitzkrieg and Kindergarten Panzerskräck are discontinued/in revision; Junkie Puke and Sandblaster are currently prototyping. The latter two remain BOUNDARY_REVIEW so an in-development product is not mistaken for a released catalog model.
- Deaf Audio evidence is archival rather than current. A 2009 account documents Faceplant Fuzz as handcrafted in Norway and associated with Magnus Nordbye, while later Scandinavian coverage preserves Little Voodoo Fuzz Mini and Tinnitus Boost. Lifetime catalog completeness remains unresolved.
