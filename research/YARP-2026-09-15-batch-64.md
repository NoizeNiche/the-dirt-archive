## Historical significance
The FS series now reads as an actual family rather than a famous-pedal orphan list. Dating disagreement is explicitly preserved: a 1967 FS-1 lead, a late-1960s FS-2 corridor, and a 1970s documentary record for FS-3. This is preferable to retroactively assigning every model the same date.
