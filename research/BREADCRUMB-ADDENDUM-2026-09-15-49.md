## Historical findings

- Applied Banshee Fuzz is retained as a high-confidence Applied family record, but its exact production chronology remains unresolved.
- Nomad Banshee is treated as a marketed Applied state, with LIMMCO preserved as the distribution/house-brand context rather than confused with the physical builder.
- Nomad Fuzz Wah is a high-confidence Applied FW-3 marketed state.
- Guild VW-1 is separated from FW-3 as a distinct Applied wah/volume family and is tied to the 1972-1978 Guild catalog window.
- Guild SP-5 is a high-confidence Applied-built U.S. reintroduction of the Shin-Ei Surf Wah family, with the surviving Guild catalog window centered on 1974.
- V-Jay EX-1 is retained as a medium-confidence related Applied marketed state because model-specific documentation is thinner.
- Hohner WW-20 remains medium-confidence research-only within the Applied wah family, while Hohner ME-30 stays high-confidence and explicitly separate from the Japanese Shin-Ei manufacturing state.
- The Applied wah family is now explicitly kept separate from the FW-3 three-effect family, even where labels and enclosures overlap.

## Validation posture

The main improvement this batch is family separation: Banshee/Wah, FW-3, and Surf/Siren reintroduction states are now treated as related but distinct research objects. This avoids turning an OEM catalog index into an automatic model-identity merger.
