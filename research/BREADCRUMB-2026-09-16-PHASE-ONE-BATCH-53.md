## Evidence notes
Flattley Guitar Pedals' official site states that the family-run business was established in 2016 and that products are hand-built, hand-wired and hand-finished in England, with PCB design/manufacture in Gloucestershire. The current catalog exposes nine overdrive results plus multiple fuzz and bass-gain products, including anniversary editions. The DG Fuzz and JK Fuzz pages document the builder's 1960s-style fuzz references and internal refinements.

JNS Inventions is a current Singapore entity founded in 2024. The official catalog currently shows Kleros, Exodus and Covenant. Kleros combines Tube Screamer and Klon-inspired stages with flexible routing; Exodus combines Bluesbreaker-derived and DRV-inspired sides. These references are preserved as product design relationships, not historical manufacturer identities.

Mojo Gear FX's current site lists Rust Ride, Mojo Fuzz Deluxe and Mojo Fuzz MKII variants, Stratosphere Sing&Drive and Tube Drive. Independent reference material documents additional named products including Tone Bender MKII Professional, Fuzz No. 8, Staccato Machine, Cosmic Ash, ODD Preamp 741 and Red Boost. Variant labels such as BC108, NKT275 and AC128 are retained as named product states rather than split into duplicate builders.

## Photo workflow
Every inventory row includes a stable builder product or catalog page as the photo-source breadcrumb. These links are evidence trails for later specimen capture, source credit, rights review, and generation mapping, not permission to republish images.

## Scope discipline
Boost-only models remain gain-adjacent rather than inflating the central dirt count. Flattley bass variants are retained because bass dirt is in scope. JNS Covenant is excluded from central inventory because its documented focus is compressor/boost/EQ rather than dirt. Mojo Gear FX Red Boost is retained as gain-adjacent. No schematics, PCB layouts, gutshots, cloning instructions, or complete BOM data are published.
