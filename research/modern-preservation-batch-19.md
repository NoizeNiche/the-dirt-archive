# Modern Preservation Track — Batch 19

## Position
2023 census continues. This batch prioritizes products that appear in contemporary year-end indexes but have enough evidence to establish product identity, chronology, or variant clues without turning a discovery mention into an overconfident catalog record.

## Verified / strong evidence

### Bardic Audio Devices — Rival
- 2023 Mesa Mark V-style preamp/distortion product.
- Contemporary documentation identifies Tim as the builder/sound designer and Dale Atkinson as the artwork designer.
- Controls include Gain, Solo, Presence, Volume and a five-band graphic EQ, with separate Gain and Solo footswitches.
- Handmade in Niles, Illinois; 9–15V operation; top-mounted jacks; soft-touch true bypass.
- Archive identity: standalone Bardic product, not a Mesa product or an OEM Mesa model.
- Status: CATALOGED / VARIANT_MAPPED candidate.

### Messiah Guitars — Eddie BoostDrive
- Contemporary 2023 documentation identifies a dual Boost + Drive pedal.
- Controls include Drive, Boost, Level, Tight, Silicon/LED mode selection and three-band EQ, with separate Boost and Drive footswitches.
- GPX's 2023 reporting described a further iteration as being in development, so the 2023 specimen should not automatically be relabeled as a later generation.
- Status: CATALOGED / VARIANT WATCH.

### PedalPalFX — PAL800 Gold Overdrive V4
- 2023 reporting identifies the PAL800 Gold Overdrive as V4.
- Contemporary feature set includes three voicing modes, active Presence, Gain, Bass, Middle, Treble and an internal Master Tone control; the Slash #34 mode is documented as part of the current identity.
- Status: VARIANT_MAPPED, with V4 treated as the documented generation rather than inferred from cosmetic changes.

### Electric Eye Audio — The Thrasher
- 2023 contemporary reporting documents the Thrasher as a high-gain distortion and shows both standard/red and black/stealth editions.
- Archive rule: standard versus Stealth/Black treatment is preserved as edition/specimen identity unless manufacturer evidence demonstrates a circuit or production-generation change.
- Status: CATALOGED / EDITION_MAPPED candidate.

### Intensive Care Audio — Vena Cava
- 2023 contemporary reporting identifies Vena Cava as a filter/distortion product combining distortion with filter, oscillation and ring-mod-oriented functions.
- Its unusual control/function identity makes it useful as an example of a dirt product that should not be forced into a conventional distortion-only taxonomy.
- Status: CATALOGED / FUNCTION_MAPPED candidate.

### Intensive Care Audio — Death Drive
- 2023 contemporary reporting identifies Death Drive as a distortion product from Intensive Care Audio.
- Status: CATALOGED / DISCOVERY-TO-CATALOG candidate; deeper manufacturer chronology remains required before assigning a generation.

### Fairfield Circuitry — Roger That
- 2023 contemporary documentation identifies Roger That as an FM/demodulating distortion/noise instrument.
- Archive identity should preserve its experimental signal-processing character instead of treating it as a conventional overdrive or fuzz.
- Status: CATALOGED / FUNCTION_MAPPED.

## Important uncertainty preserved
- GPX year-end lists are discovery evidence, not sufficient by themselves to establish exact introduction dates or generation numbers.
- Where 2023 reporting shows editions, signatures or artwork variants, those are not promoted to new generations without stronger evidence.
- Where a later iteration is mentioned as forthcoming, the earlier specimen remains historically separate from the later product until the later version is independently documented.

## Method note
The archive is using contemporary 2023 indexes as a census map, then promoting only products for which builder identity and product identity can be reasonably established. Variant mapping follows the rule: introduction date != revision date != generation date != edition date.
