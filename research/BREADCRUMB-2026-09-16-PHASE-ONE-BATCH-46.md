# Phase One Batch 46 Breadcrumb

Date: 2026-09-16

## Batch
Batch 46 continues builder-first Phase One with strict duplicate/OEM cross-reference before promotion. A fresh South American sweep expanded Peru and Uruguay coverage and retained photo-source trails with each product record.

## Duplicate-control notes
- Existing master census was checked before adding any builder in this batch.
- Iannucci Audio, BSQ Effects, SEHAT Effectors and other previously mapped entities were not duplicated.
- Candidate brands are only added when they are absent as canonical entities and the available evidence supports a distinct builder/brand node.

## Builders added
- MUSA Electronics — Peru; current handmade catalog includes ChavinScream, Inkadist, WankaFuzz, UyaFuzz and CaralBoost.
- AC Pedals — Peru; broad current gain catalog includes Fauno, Silverfish, Dual Dream, Zodiac, Black Box, Eleven, Revolt, StomPlex, Gecko, Amp Solver CL and Grunt plus deluxe/boost states.
- Inspira FX — Montevideo, Uruguay; current boutique catalog includes Hiss Orbiter, Bloody Fuzz, Tube Killer and Ninfa Overdrive.

## Evidence / relationship notes
- MUSA's current site states its pedals are handmade in Peru. ChavinScream is described as Tube Screamer-inspired, Inkadist as Distortion+-inspired, WankaFuzz as Big Muff-inspired, and CaralBoost as a three-band boost. Those inspirations remain attributed and are not treated as original historical inventions.
- AC Pedals states its products are designed and manufactured in Peru. Fauno is explicitly a Klon/Centaur-inspired overdrive; Fauno Deluxe expands the control set. Zodiac is a combination of Silverfish and Fauno with independently usable/stackable sides. Grunt is explicitly inspired by the Darkglass B3K family.
- AC Pedals' storefront currently exposes a large mixed gain range. The catalog contains both core dirt families and gain-adjacent boost/preamp/amp-sim products; those boundaries remain visible in the inventory rather than inflating the central dirt count.
- Inspira FX states its effects are designed and handmade in Montevideo. Tube Killer is an amp-in-a-box/fuzz/high-gain overdrive hybrid using BJT and JFET gain stages. Hiss Orbiter combines bass overdrive and fuzz behavior with octave-up/harmonic expansion. Bloody Fuzz and Ninfa Overdrive complete the current four-model dirt catalog baseline.
