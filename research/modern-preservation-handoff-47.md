## Major 2016 research findings
- MusicRadar's January 2016 NAMM survey describes 2016 as a refinement-heavy year and records the new Amptweaker, Stone Deaf and Way Huge dirt products. Its EarthQuaker coverage provides unusually strong dated ship information for Acapulco Gold, Bellows and Gray Channel.
- EarthQuaker's 2016 NAMM group also included Spires and Bows. Those remain research-tier until exact first-production chronology is reconciled with builder archives.
- Stone Deaf's Kliptonite is particularly useful for identification because contemporary reporting documents the Mirror Mix system and its combined fuzz/overdrive role. Warp Drive and PDF-2 similarly preserve distinct control architectures rather than being flattened into generic distortion records.
- Walrus Contraband Fuzz is preserved as a very simple one-knob silicon fuzz with Fat/Thin switching. The NAMM 2016 coverage provides direct contemporary identification evidence.
- Way Huge's 2016 MKIV Green Rhino and MKIIs Swollen Pickle demonstrate why the archive must treat enclosure/variant states as historically meaningful. The Green Rhino gained 100Hz/500Hz shaping while the Swollen Pickle moved to a smaller format with expanded controls.
- Friedman established a significant 2016 pedal expansion. BE-OD has strong dated introduction evidence from August 26, 2016, while Fuzz Fiend and Motor City Drive preserve the unusual tube-driven direction of the early line.
- Supro's first pedal trio is a useful maker-level chronology anchor: SP1303 Boost, SP1304 Fuzz and SP1305 Drive were introduced as the company's initial effects line in 2016. SP1304 additionally has direct contemporary evidence documenting its germanium/silicon hybrid behavior and amplifier-derived EQ.
- Reverb's 2016 new-pedal sales list identifies Friedman BE-OD and Keeley Monterey among the year's commercially visible new releases, useful as a secondary census checkpoint rather than a substitute for launch proof.
- MXR Sub Machine Fuzz is retained because a 2016 artist-rig interview describes it as relatively new and specifically calls out its blendable sub-octave, footswitchable upper octave and series/parallel routing behavior.
- Effects Database's 2016 NAMM streams provide additional discovery coverage for Orange Two Stroke, Ovaltone Fountain Overdrive, KHDK No. 1, Mode Machines AT1/AT2/MM-3 and other compact-format products.
- Gore Pedals' 2016 release coverage adds an independent boutique builder to the census with Filth Fuzz and confirms all three featured models were shipping.

## Data rule reinforced
The 2016 layer contains several strong examples of variant-aware preservation: PDF-2 as a successor to PDF-1, Green Rhino MKIV and Swollen Pickle MKIIs as distinct format states, and numbered Supro models as exact product identities. These should remain linked to family lineage without erasing their production-object distinctions.
