# Way Huge Electronics 1990s catalog audit

## Scope
Historical builder-level catalog pass for the original 1990s Way Huge run. Reissues and later Dunlop-era products are kept separate from original production.

## Identity
Original Way Huge pedals were hand-built in the 1990s. A surviving historical specimen listing identifies original pedals from 1992-1999 and distinguishes them from later Jim Dunlop reissues.

## Dirt catalog baseline from period-specimen evidence
- Blue Hippo — chorus, not core dirt.
- Aqua-Puss — delay, not core dirt.
- Saffron Squeeze — compressor, boundary.
- Foot Pig — original Way Huge dirt/gain family candidate.
- Purple Platypus — original Way Huge dirt/gain family candidate.
- Green Rhino — overdrive.
- Piercing Moose — distortion/fuzz-adjacent custom/gain product; preserve custom status.
- Red Llama — overdrive/fuzz-adjacent gain product.
- Swollen Pickle — fuzz.

The original 1990s run is explicitly documented as separate from Dunlop reissues. A surviving lot gives serial examples including Foot Pig SN019, Purple Platypus SN062, Green Rhino SN291, Piercing Moose Custom Green SN022, Red Llama SN315 and Swollen Pickle SN119, plus non-dirt companion pedals.

## Catalog interpretation
Use the surviving 1990s specimen lot as a discovery and specimen anchor, not as proof that the list is lifetime-complete. Exact first/last production dates for individual models remain a further archaeology target.

The Way Huge entity should remain separate from Jim Dunlop, with the later revival represented as a relationship/reissue era rather than a replacement of the original company history.
