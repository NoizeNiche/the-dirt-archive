# YARP Breadcrumb 114

## Purpose
North American and Australian builder-discovery pass, centered on missing OEM manufacturers and product-family succession.

## New builder records

- `BUILDER-108` Applied Audio Products Inc. — high-confidence New Jersey manufacturer/OEM identity. Research connects the company to Applied, Goya, Sano, Crown and Lou Rose branded fuzz/boost products and later Guild/Hohner OEM work.
- `BUILDER-109` Chunk Systems — high-confidence Australian builder lineage beginning in 1995, with FZ002 and Brown Dog treated as one product succession.

## Cross-reference / dedupe decisions

- **Applied Audio Products Inc.** is the manufacturer node. Goya, Sano, Crown, Lou Rose, Guild and Hohner remain marketed/OEM identities unless independent evidence establishes additional factory roles.
- **FZ002 and Brown Dog** remain products in one Chunk Systems lineage, not duplicate builders.
- **Ace Tone** remains a distinct manufacturer lineage from later Roland/BOSS despite Ikutaro Kakehashi's later founding of Roland.
- **Montarbo Sinfhoton** remains research-only because the product is well attested but its first-production date is still disputed by current historical research.

## Evidence notes

Recent historical research identifies Applied Audio Products Inc. as a significant New Jersey effects manufacturer. It documents the Applied Fuzz and Treble Booster, links the same manufacturing family to Goya effects, and reports a June 1968 factory announcement placing Applied Audio in a South Plainfield facility associated with Trans-Tek. The same research traces later Applied-built Guild and Hohner effects. Effects Database independently identifies the Lou Rose Combo-Fuzz as an Applied Audio product.

Chunk's own documentation says the FZ002 was handmade in Australia and later superseded by the Brown Dog; TalkBass-era discussion independently describes the Brown Dog as a re-engineered FZ002.
