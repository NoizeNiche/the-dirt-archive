## Information on the pedal

- Bracton OverDrive is documented by Atlas Pedal as a medium-compression overdrive using an op-amp gain stage with symmetric LED clipping. The builder explicitly identifies Sutherland OverDrive as its variation model.
- Sutherland OverDrive is a rebuilt four-knob prototype-derived variation of Bracton, with additional PreGain/Tone control behavior documented by Atlas Pedal.
- LSP-OD MK-2 is a discontinued amplike overdrive/fuzztortion with multi-stage JFET gain and silicon-diode clipping; the official page documents its 9–18V operation and characteristic fuzzy distortion transition.
- River Distortion survives in Japanese dealer documentation as an early-1980s-oriented Atlas Pedal distortion with a deliberately lower-gain, overdrive/fuzz-intermediate character.
- Cream Soda is retained conservatively because the surviving evidence in this pass is a Japanese retail specimen record describing it as an overdrive/preamp; stronger first-party historical documentation is still needed.
- Tenor is deliberately excluded from central dirt because Atlas Pedal defines it as a full-range booster/buffer.

## Normalization decisions

- Sutherland is retained as a meaningful variation/edition relationship to Bracton rather than a wholly unrelated family.
- Tenor remains outside central dirt.
- Cream Soda remains central-pending until the first-party historical trail is strengthened.
- No schematics, PCB layouts, gutshots, complete BOMs or cloning instructions are collected.
