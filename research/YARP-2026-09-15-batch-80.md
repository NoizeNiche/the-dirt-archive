# YARP 2026-09-15 Batch 80: Baldwin-Burns Buzzaround Catalog State

The Baldwin-Burns Buzzaround is retained as a distinct British manufacturer/product node. Documentary evidence places the model on a Burns price list in April 1966, with surviving potentiometer dates extending through early 1969. The Archive records the Buzzaround as a Burns/Baldwin-Burns product and does not collapse it into the later Sola Sound Tone Bender MkIII family.

Historical research now supports an important lineage caution: the Buzzaround was likely derivative of the earlier G. P. Electronics Harmonic Generator, while the later Sola Sound MkIII appears to have drawn from the Buzzaround design. The relationship is historically useful but does not establish identical manufacturing or corporate identity.
