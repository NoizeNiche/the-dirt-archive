## Information on the pedal

- Umbrella Company's Hitchhike Drive is an overdrive/preamp based around vintage American tweed-amp behavior; #24 is a hard-drive distortion; Mayonaise Fuzz is a fuzz/distortion with DOPING and 1979 switching.
- Chocolate Drive is an earlier core overdrive with broad EQ and internal voltage-boosted headroom. The current Chocolate Crunch, Melty Overdrive and Fuzzy Overdrive form a distinct newer white-panel dirt group. transistripper is retained as a separate high-gain fuzz.
- Flying Teapot's own 2019 interview is a major historical source: it documents 088 Over Drive, Omega Drive, FUZZ and Rum and Coke, plus the builder's development history. 088 uses Vintage/Fat/Bright modes; FUZZ moves between Fuzz Face-like and Tone Bender-like behavior through switching; Rum and Coke is derived from a specific late-Rams-Head reference.
- West Tokyo Waves documents itself as a one-person Tokyo builder. Brutalist Fuzz evolved from an earlier Void Fuzz prototype and uses a concrete enclosure; Structures is a seven-transistor overdrive/fuzz; Noise Swash is a separate experimental dirt identity.
- RoShi Pedals identifies ELF FUZZ SUSTAINER as its first pedal and documents Japanese triangle-fuzz lineage. GRUFF V1 and GRUFF result preserve separate historical product states. DEZZYNER follows the Elka DIZZY TONE lineage from the builder's own specimen. Plexition, Blacklon and Seji document later amplifier/drive-oriented work.

## Normalization decisions

- Product colors, dealer specials, collection-card packages and ADD CBF/Ginza variants are not promoted to separate conceptual models without stronger evidence.
- Flying Teapot preamps remain outside central dirt even when their designs are distortion-adjacent.
- Umbrella Company's Fusion Blender and Chocolate Electronics booster products remain outside central dirt by primary function.
- Internal construction details are recorded only at the level useful for historical identification; no schematics, PCB layouts, gutshots or complete BOMs are added.
