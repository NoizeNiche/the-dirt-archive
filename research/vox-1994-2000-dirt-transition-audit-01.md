# Vox: 1994-2000 Dirt Transition Audit

## Scope
Historical product chronology for the Vox dirt-pedal transition from the 1990s Tone Bender revival period into the V810/V830 generation.

## Key chronology
- **V829 Tone Bender Fuzz:** documented as a 1994-1997 Vox product.
- **V810 Valvetone:** introduced in summer 1998; included in the 1998-1999 and 1999-2000 Vox catalogs and a single-page sales flyer.
- **V830 Distortion Booster:** introduced alongside the V810 in summer 1998; included in the same catalog corridor and replaced the V829 in the product line.

## Identity / manufacturer disposition
Vox is retained as the marketed brand/product entity. The surviving historical source identifies the V810 and V830 country of origin as **China**, so the archive should not automatically carry the UK/1960s Vox manufacturing story into these later products.

## Product-class disposition
- V829 = fuzz / Tone Bender-marketed revival family.
- V810 = overdrive.
- V830 = distortion / fuzz-name terminology inherited from Vox's historical use of “Distortion Booster.”

## Archive significance
This is a useful example of a legacy brand re-entering dirt-pedal production in a later manufacturing geography. The 1998 catalog boundary also gives the archive a hard chronological bridge between the 1994-1997 V829 and the later V810/V830 generation.
