# YARP 2026-09-15 Batch 115: Sunn Buzz Builder Catalog Audit

## Builder
**Sunn Musical Equipment Co. / Sunn**

## Catalog state
The Sunn Buzz family is confirmed as an in-house Sunn effects product line, distinct from the later independent fuzz-pedal companies that merely copied its concept.

- **Sunn Buzz prototype / early unit**: first introduced in 1966 in a small run described by specialist historical research as prototype units.
- **Sunn Buzz SB-1G**: revised 1967 unit, documented in Sunn advertising/brochure material as a compact distortion device with a three-stage transistor circuit and direct plug-in format.
- Catalog appearances continue into approximately 1969, after which the Sunn effects line disappears from the surviving catalog trail.

## Chronology caution
The evidence supports a 1966 prototype phase followed by the better-documented 1967 SB-1G. Do not compress these into one timeless model. The early version and the 1967 redesign should be treated as separate production states until Sunn primary literature resolves whether the prototype carried the same model number.

## Builder / manufacturer attribution
Sunn is the marketed builder/manufacturer node for this product line. Unlike many Japanese export labels and East Coast private-label products in the same period, no third-party OEM assignment is necessary in the current catalog record.

## Historical significance
Sunn is useful as an American amplifier company entering the effects market during the 1966-69 fuzz boom. The Sunn Buzz also provides a documented example of a manufacturer taking the emerging Maestro-style three-stage fuzz vocabulary into a silicon-transistor product and packaging it as a compact instrument-plugging device.
