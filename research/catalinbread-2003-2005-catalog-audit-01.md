# Catalinbread — 2003–2005 Catalog Audit

## Status
**Builder entity:** CANONICAL
**Period:** 2003–2005 origin period
**Confidence:** HIGH for founding and first-branded-product chronology; complete early dirt catalog still requires period catalog reconstruction.

## Historical spine
Catalinbread's official history says the company was **born in 2003 on Nic Harris's kitchen table** in the Pacific Northwest. The first Catalinbread-branded pedal was **Super Chili Picoso**, produced exclusively for ToneFactor, a Seattle-area dealer. Sales of that pedal funded further designs.

Effects Database provides a more precise sequence: **June 2003** marks the Super Chili Picoso/ToneFactor event that effectively established Catalinbread as a pedal company; in **August 2003** Derek Moss joined the brand-building effort, including the tagline and visual identity. **Early 2004** saw the Semaphore Tremolo.

## Dirt-family relevance
The company did not begin as a dirt-only builder. For the Dirt Archive, the important point is to establish Catalinbread as an early-2000s boutique builder whose first branded product predates the later explosion of recognizable drive/fuzz models.

Early dirt products should be researched independently rather than back-projected from today's lineup. In particular, future work should establish the earliest documented production dates for models such as **Dirty Little Secret, Formula No. 5, Hyper Pak, Manx, Katzenkönig, and other dirt families** using period catalogs, ToneFactor listings, dated reviews, and surviving specimens.

## Archive cautions
Do not use the existence date of the Catalinbread company as the release date of every early pedal. Preserve the distinction between founding, first-branded product, dealer-exclusive production, and later catalog expansion.
