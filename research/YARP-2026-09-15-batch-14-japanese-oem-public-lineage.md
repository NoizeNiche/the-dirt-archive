# YARP 2026-09-15 Batch 14 — Japanese OEM Reconciliation and Public-Safe Lineage

## Purpose

Stress-test the Japanese OEM graph before public promotion. The archive separates canonical physical builders, marketed brand names, OEM identities, and current revivals.

## Findings

### Shin-Ei / Honey FY-6
Shin-Ei's current historical material states that Honey released the FY-6 Baby Crying Fuzz in 1967 and that Shin-Ei branded the FY-6 in 1968. Record Honey and Shin-Ei as distinct marketed states within the FY-6 lineage; do not treat the 1967 and 1968 labels as unrelated designs.

### Companion family
Effects Database lists Companion FY-2, Companion FY-6, WF-8 and WF-24 in the Shin-Ei Companion family. WF-8 and WF-24 should remain separate product/family states because enclosure/control architecture differs, while their marketed Companion names remain searchable relationships.

### Teisco Standard Fuzz OEM family
Effects Database explicitly groups the Teisco Fuzz Machine with Ibanez Standard Fuzz No. 59, Bruno Fuzz Machine, Mica Fuzz, Marlboro Wailer Fuzz, Antoria Fuzz Machine and Aria Diamond Fuzz. The archive therefore uses one physical-product-family node with multiple market-name relationships rather than counting every brand as a separate builder.

### JEN Italian Tone Bender lineage
Effects Database identifies Jen Fuzz as an Italian Jen Pescara product tied to Vox V828/V8281 and later Jen, Elka, Luxor, Nova and Unicord market states. The archive keeps these names separate while linking them at manufacturer/lineage level. Exact model-by-model production transitions still require primary-source reconciliation.

### Coron OEM family
Effects Database identifies Coron Distortion as a late-1970s/early-1980s Japanese product and KMD Distortion as a KMD-by-Coron market state. The family also includes Grant, Storm, Asama and Memphis names in the surviving secondary evidence. Treat the physical builder as Coron and retain each marketed name as an alias/OEM edge.

## Public-data rule

Only relationships with a stable evidence trail should enter the public lineage graph. A relationship edge means a documented identity or manufacturing/marketing relationship, not that two names are interchangeable in every physical specimen.
