# 2011-2014 Microboutique Audit 13

## Scope
This batch targets small and mid-sized builders visible in the 2013-2014 historical catalog/weekly-archive corridor. The priority is to capture builder identity and a useful dirt catalog baseline before deeper specimen archaeology.

## Recovery Effects
Recovery Effects has a firm May 4, 2011 documentary anchor for the Sound Destruction Device, described as a combined distortion/fuzz/glitch tool. Couple Skate follows in the January 2, 2013 documentary record as a square-wave fuzz/octave-down hybrid. The indexed Couple Skate record explicitly distinguishes a later MkII state, so the archive treats MkII as a generation/state rather than an unrelated family. Recovery remains a high-priority builder because the early catalog is experimental and may contain short-lived products omitted from modern storefronts.

Sources: https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/recovery/sdd ; https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/recovery/coupleskate

## Kor Pedals
The July 2013 Effects Database weekly overview exposes a surprisingly broad Kor Pedals catalog: DP9, DP9 Boost, Eden Drive, Eden Drive Boost, Gusdrive, Leadstar, Leadstar Boost, Oracle, Trilogy and more. Trilogy has independent video evidence dated May 2011, while Eden Drive and Eden Drive Boost are formally published July 6, 2013. The archive therefore treats 2011 as an evidence corridor, not automatically as universal first-sale date. Kor should remain a P0 catalog-completion target because the indexed family spans multiple gain categories and signature/collaboration products.

Sources: https://www.effectsdatabase.com/updates/weekly/20130708 ; https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/kor/trilogy ; https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/kor/edendrive

## Keisman Pedals
The July 2013 weekly overview records Anvil Fuzz, Early Bird, Juggy Boost, K-Buff, Midnight Drive, Thunderbird and Velvet Drive together. Thunderbird is separately published July 4, 2013 and explicitly combines Early Bird and Velvet Drive concepts. This is a useful example of a builder whose catalog includes both component products and a combined derivative model. Exact first-sale dates and full identity history remain open.

Source: https://www.effectsdatabase.com/updates/weekly/20130708

## Master Effects
Effects Database identifies Master Effects as a Calgary, Canada company. Its March 31, 2014 weekly overview provides a four-product catalog snapshot: Happy Trails Overdrive, Occam's Razor, Teh Brootz and Wolfy. This is enough to establish a canonical catalog baseline, but not enough to claim the lifetime catalog is complete.

Sources: https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/master ; https://www.effectsdatabase.com/updates/weekly/20140331

## Masterbuilt Amplification
The July 8, 2013 weekly overview records Dark Side of the Fuzz, Stone Groove, Texas Lone Star, Tritone Heavy Metal Distortion and Vicious. Dirt products are separated by function, and Vicious is retained as a boost boundary record rather than being counted as a distortion/fuzz pedal. The physical manufacturing identity and broader company history require additional research.

Source: https://www.effectsdatabase.com/updates/weekly/20130708

## Tone Hungry Effects
Tone Hungry is a Canadian small-batch builder in the available 2014 record. Hunger Bender and Treble Master were both published March 27, 2014. Hunger Bender is explicitly a modified Tone Bender MkIII-derived fuzz/overdrive hybrid, while Treble Master is a germanium treble boost derived from the Rangemaster concept. Hot Molasses appears in the March 31, 2014 weekly overview. Boost is retained as a gain/dirt boundary category.

Sources: https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/tonehungry/hungerbender ; https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/tonehungry/treblemaster ; https://www.effectsdatabase.com/updates/weekly/20140331

## Oddfellow Effects
Caveman Drive has an explicit July 20, 2014 documentary date and an indexed v2 state. The Bishop appears later, December 27, 2015, as a separate single-channel design developed with Scotty Smith of ProAnalog Devices. The archive keeps Bishop as continuity evidence rather than forcing it into the 2014 catalog window.

Sources: https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/oddfellow/cavemandrive ; https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/oddfellow/bishop

## Old Blood Noise Endeavors
Old Blood Noise Endeavors states that the company has been around since 2014. Haunt is documented in October-December 2014 material and is a core gated fuzz. The later Fault and Alpha Haunt are outside this initial corridor and are retained only as continuity evidence. This builder belongs in the P0 queue because its later catalog is large, but the archive should not backfill modern products into the 2014 origin record without evidence.

Sources: https://oldbloodnoise.com/about-obne-contact ; https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/oldbloodnoise/haunt ; https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/oldbloodnoise

## Magnetic Effects
The July 8, 2013 weekly overview captures Isotope and Solar Bender MkII. Later catalog evidence documents White Atom and Satellite/Sändare, but those later products are not used to imply 2013 availability. Solar Bender MkII is retained as a generation-labelled record, preserving the distinction between family and state.

Sources: https://www.effectsdatabase.com/updates/weekly/20130708 ; https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/magneticeffects/whiteatom/v3 ; https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/magneticeffects/satellite

## Cross-batch overlap / cleanup notes
- Kor Pedals and Keisman Pedals appear together in the July 2013 weekly archive and should be cross-checked against existing Japanese/European builder census records before any public-facing identity merge.
- Recovery Effects already has 2011 evidence and should not be re-dated to 2013 merely because Couple Skate is the easiest surviving index hit.
- Old Blood Noise Endeavors is a genuine 2014-origin entity according to its own current company statement; later catalog models are not retrojected into the founding year.
- Boost products are retained where historically relevant but marked as boundary/gain records so the core OD/distortion/fuzz counts are not inflated.

## Research status
This is a baseline census/catalog pass, not a declaration of lifetime completeness. The next layer should reconcile these builders against maker names, signatures, collaboration partners, and any surviving 2011-2014 web/store captures before doing individual specimen archaeology.
