# Earth Sound Research Catalog Audit 01

## Scope
Builder/product-level audit for Earth Sound Research (ESR), concentrating on historically documented dirt effects and keeping amplifier/equipment products outside the core pedal count.

## Builder identity
Effects Database identifies Earth Sound Research as an American maker founded in Farmingdale, New York in 1971, with later corporate activity under ISC Audio and a limited effects catalog. The surviving effects evidence is especially important because ESR sits outside the better-known Fender/Marshall/EHX/MXR/ProCo histories.

Source: https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/earth

## Graphic Fuzz chronology
Specialist sources consistently place the Graphic Fuzz in the 1970s, with 1976 the strongest surviving specimen/catalog anchor. Tone Machines Blog documents a 1976 example with original box and manual, while Effects Database describes it as a rare mid-1970s American fuzz. This should be treated as an evidence-backed production corridor rather than a claim that every surviving unit is from exactly one year.

Sources:
- https://www.tonemachinesblog.com/2011/10/earth-sound-research-graphic-fuzz.html
- https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/earthsound/graphicfuzz

## Product identity and scope
The Graphic Fuzz is the clear central ESR dirt product. The surviving catalog trail does not currently justify inventing additional ESR overdrive or distortion families. The archive should therefore record Graphic Fuzz as the primary dirt model and maintain Earth Wah Wah only as a scope-boundary companion.

## Research caution
Online clone/build pages frequently expose circuit-level material about the Graphic Fuzz. The Dirt Archive deliberately does not reproduce schematics, PCB layouts, BOMs, gutshot libraries or circuit-reconstruction instructions. Those pages are useful only as discovery leads for historical identification and specimen comparisons.
