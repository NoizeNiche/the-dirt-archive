# YARP 2026-09-15 Batch 91: ACA Fuzz King / Fuzz Sound Cross-Label State

## Builder
Amplifier Corporation of America (ACA) is retained as the physical manufacturer evidence node for the Fuzz King. Merson/Unicord is a distribution and marketing relationship, while Lafayette's Fuzz Sound name is a separate retail/private-label state for the same product.

## Catalog state
- ACA Fuzz King: introduced summer 1967; contemporary advertising and Effects Database support a North American market release through Merson/Unicord.
- Lafayette Fuzz Sound: fall 1967 Lafayette catalog markets the same ACA unit under a different retail name.

## Historical significance
The Fuzz King is a clean demonstration that one physical product can appear under multiple retail labels without creating multiple factories. The Archive should link ACA -> Fuzz King and ACA -> Lafayette Fuzz Sound rather than counting the Lafayette name as an independent builder.

## Evidence posture
Product existence and 1967 corridor are evidence-qualified. Exact ACA facility address, production volume, and full ACA dirt catalog remain open.

## Source trail
