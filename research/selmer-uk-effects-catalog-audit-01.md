# Selmer UK Effects Catalog Audit 01

## Scope
Manufacturer/distributor identity and dirt/effects products associated with Selmer London / Selmer UK, with emphasis on documented 1950s-1970s production and marketed effects. No circuit reconstruction.

## Entity
- Selmer London / Selmer UK: British musical-instrument and amplifier company and distributor, distinct from Selmer Paris.
- The company operated a London manufacturing operation in the mid-1960s; period research places Selmer equipment production at a Holborn factory.
- Selmer was also a major UK/Commonwealth distributor, so imported/rebranded products must not automatically be counted as Selmer-manufactured.

## Buzz-Tone chronology
- Selmer's own fuzz was already being presented by August 1966 / September 1966 trade-show period.
- Fuzzboxes.org describes the Buzz-Tone as an early British fuzz box likely assembled at Selmer's Holborn factory, while explicitly noting that the exact production duration is unknown.
- Surviving examples have surfaced in continental Europe, suggesting possible export distribution, but this remains unconfirmed.
- Later Selmer Fuzz-Wah documentation says the basic fuzz concept was redesigned into the more complex combined effects unit.

## Fuzz-Wah chronology
- First version: approximately 1968-1971.
- John Weir recalled that Selmer management asked him in 1968 to design a pedal combining Fuzz, Wah-Wah and a repeat/chop effect, and that development straddled the move to Braintree.
- A separate later version is documented from 1971 onward; its manufacturing origin should remain unresolved until primary catalog evidence identifies it.
- Do not collapse both into one undifferentiated Fuzz-Wah product.

## Manufacturing / distribution cautions
- Selmer's role as distributor is substantial. For archive purposes, brand ownership, distributor role, manufacturing site and OEM source must remain separate fields.
- Selmer's relationship with Hofner is relevant to the later Hofner Buzz Tone but does not by itself prove identical manufacture or circuit design.
- The Selmer Buzz-Tone has been discussed in connection with Syd Barrett/Pink Floyd. The archive records this only as equipment-use evidence with varying confidence, not as proof of a unique production variant.

## Important evidence conflicts
- Some secondary sources state that the Buzz-Tone used germanium transistors and give detailed circuit descriptions. The public Dirt Archive should not reproduce circuit details and should instead preserve only high-level product identity and period evidence.
- Sources disagree or remain uncertain about the precise end date of Buzz-Tone production.
- Selmer Fuzz-Wah version-2 manufacturing origin is not yet sufficiently documented for a definitive OEM attribution.

## Primary / high-value source anchors
1. Fuzzboxes.org, “Selmer Buzz Tone”: historical timeline, 1966 trade-show evidence, Holborn manufacturing context, survival/export discussion, and relationship to later Fuzz-Wah.
2. Vintage Höfner / VintageHofner, Selmer effects pages: catalog-level chronology for 1959 Tremolo, mid-1960s Buzz-Tone, 1967-71 Fuzz-Wah, and early-1970s Scintillation reverb.
3. VintageHofner, “Selmer Fuzz-Wah Effects Pedal”: first-version dating, 1968 design testimony from John Weir, and later-version boundary.
4. Vintage Guitar and Bass Selmer catalog archive: surviving Selmer catalog publication chronology, useful for future page-by-page primary-source extraction.
