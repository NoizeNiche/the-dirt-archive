# Sola Sound / Colorsound: 1970s Expanded Catalog Audit 04

## Scope
This pass expands the Sola Sound / Colorsound 1970s catalog beyond the best-known Tone Bender, Power Boost, Overdriver, Supa Tone Bender, and Jumbo Tone Bender families. The goal is to preserve period product identity and chronology without treating every effect in the line as a dirt pedal.

## Period advertising anchors

### 1970: initial Colorsound-era advertising
A March 1970 Beat Instrumental advertisement identifies a new Sola Sound effects range including **Wow-Fuzz**, **Wow-Swell**, and **Tone Bender**. The ad describes the range as designed and manufactured by Sola Sound and directs customers to Macari's Musical Exchange at 102 Charing Cross Road, London. Source: World Radio History, Beat Instrumental, March 1970.

### 1972: Jumbo-format expansion
A October 1972 Beat Instrumental advertisement announces new **Jumbo-size pedals** and names **Octivider**, **Sustain**, and **Wah-Fuzz-Swell**. It states that these were intended to extend the existing Colorsound range and specifically notes use by Hawkwind. Source: World Radio History, Beat Instrumental, October 1972.

### 1973: expanded Colorsound line
A December 1973 Beat Instrumental advertisement presents the Colorsound line as a broad effects system and visually identifies **Octivider**, **Colorsound Sustain**, and **Tone Bender** among the range. Source: World Radio History, Beat Instrumental, December 1973.

### 1974: catalog-level enumeration
A November 1974 Beat Instrumental buying guide lists a large Colorsound range including **Wah-Wah, Swell, Wah-Swell, Wah-Fuzz, Wah-Fuzz-Straight, Organ Wah-Swell, Supa Wah-Swell, Tone Bender, Tremelo, Overdriver, Ring Modulator, Wah-Fuzz-Swell, Fuzz, Phazer, Supa Tone Bender, Sustain Module, and Octivider**. The same source describes the Overdriver as a preamp with treble and bass boost. Source: World Radio History, Beat Instrumental, November 1974.

## Non-dirt controls that should remain in the builder catalog

The 1970s Colorsound catalog was substantially larger than its dirt families. **Swell, Wah-Swell, Organ Wah-Swell, Supa Wah-Swell, Tremelo, Ring Modulator, Sustain/Sustain Module, Octivider, and other modulation/utility products** should remain as catalog records but should not inflate the archive's OD/distortion/fuzz census.

## Important chronology caution

The ColorSound/Sola Sound paper trail uses overlapping product names, combined-function products, and changing enclosure formats. A period advertisement establishes that a marketed product existed, but it does not by itself prove a circuit identity, exact production dates, or a one-to-one relationship with another model. The archive therefore stores **marketed identity**, **period evidence**, and **family relationship** as separate claims.

## Archive boundary
