## Search priorities

1. Prefer owned Archive photography, builder-provided photography, permission-cleared images, or compatible open licenses.
2. Prefer photographs showing the complete exterior object clearly enough for identification.
3. Capture generation-specific images whenever a generation has meaningful cosmetic or enclosure changes.
4. Record the source page, direct image URL when known, credit, license/permission basis, generation ID, and confidence before promoting an image into `DIRT_MEDIA`.
5. Keep external visual leads available to researchers without treating them as republishable assets.
