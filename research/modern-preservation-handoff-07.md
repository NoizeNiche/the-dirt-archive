## Evidence strategy
Primary manufacturer pages and manuals are preferred. Contemporary independent sources are used for discovery and date anchoring when manufacturer pages do not expose historical launch information.

## Required data for each record
Capture product identity, builder/brand identity, introduction and revision dates separately, enclosure/artwork, controls, jacks, switches, operating modes, functional changes, power requirements when useful for identification, documented production geography, editions, lineage relationships, and uncertainty. Record the specific source supporting each claim.

## Important modern preservation finding
The 2023 field contains a large number of boutique builders that are easy to miss in broad manufacturer censuses. Sources such as Effects Database's dated weekly update and Guitar Pedal X's annual roundups are useful discovery indexes, but the Archive should promote a builder/product only after checking a primary or high-quality secondary source.

## Next target
Continue through 2023 and begin 2022 once 2023's OD/distortion/fuzz builder landscape has been swept. In parallel, continue backfilling existing public pedal records so every catalog record gains documented variant and identification data rather than only a name and category.
