## Evidence strategy
Primary manufacturer pages, manuals and dated builder posts remain preferred. Contemporary independent annual/monthly roundups are useful discovery indexes and date anchors, but do not override primary evidence. Preserve uncertainty when a source only establishes a year, a generation label, or a physical difference.

## Required interpretation rule
For every pedal, do not stop at "this product exists." Ask: **What changed? When did it change? How can a person holding the pedal recognize the change? What source proves that distinction?** Then connect that answer to the public product record.
