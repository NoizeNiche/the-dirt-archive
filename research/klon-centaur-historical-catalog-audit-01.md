# Klon Centaur Historical Catalog Audit

Research status: early historical baseline; not specimen-complete.

## 1990s anchor
Guitar World identifies the Klon Centaur as entering the market in 1994 and places Klon among the independent American pedal companies that emerged during the 1990s boutique resurgence.

## Archive treatment
Klon should be represented as its own builder/brand lineage rather than folded into Tube Screamer or other transparent-overdrive families. The Centaur is the core original product and should be separated from later Klon-branded variants and any third-party clones.

## Research gaps
- Establish earliest advertisements and exact first-production window.
- Separate original Centaur enclosure/production states from later Klon KTR-era continuation.
- Capture documented production quantities only where primary or highly reliable evidence supports them.
- Keep clone/copy products out of the Klon manufacturer catalog.

Sources:
- https://www.guitarworld.com/features/guitar-gear-of-the-90s
