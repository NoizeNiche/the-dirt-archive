## Photos

## Information on the pedal

- SPI's PRE MA DONNA shows that the builder's historical catalog extends into complex bass preamp products with substantial distortion capability, but its primary product identity is a preamp.
- SPI's 190 Notch Filter and 190 BoostBuffer were collaborations with bassist IKUO and are documented by Shimamura as filter/booster products.
- JMgeek OverDrive was shown as a prototype at Sound Messe 2023, with germanium-transistor gain and a multi-band EQ section, so it should not be promoted to a normal production model without stronger release evidence.
- Sound Dog's DOG BOOST is explicitly categorized as a booster, while SEASIDE MOON is a phaser. Both are retained for builder history without inflating dirt counts.

## Normalization decisions

- Continue separating central dirt models from preamps, boosters, filters, modulation, prototypes and third-party modifications.
- Keep historical variant names where they materially improve identification, but do not create new conceptual counts from cosmetic or presentation changes.
- No schematics, PCB layouts, gutshots or complete component inventories are collected.
