# YARP 2026-09-15 Batch 69: Carlsbro / Sola Sound Relationship Audit

Carlsbro is retained as a marketed British brand with multiple documented Colorsound/Sola Sound production states rather than as a single independent factory line.

Dirt-bearing catalog members found in the historical index:
- Carlsbro Fuzz-Tone, with a May 1966 advertisement as an early documentary anchor.
- Carlsbro Fuzz, a Sola Sound/Colorsound-built MkIII-family state.
- Carlsbro Fuzz-Wah, early 1970s, built by Sola Sound and branded for Carlsbro.
- Carlsbro Suzz-Phase, an analog fuzz/phase hybrid in the Carlsbro Alu family.

The Fuzz-Tone should remain separate from the later Sola/Colorsound MkIII-derived Fuzz. The Fuzz-Wah should likewise remain a marketed Carlsbro state, not a duplicate factory entry. This batch therefore strengthens the British OEM graph without inflating builder count.
