## Evidence notes
Kasleder's official site documents Dirty Little Thing and Sweet Little Thing, Vintage King and GE Vintage King, The Toad, JellyFish, Freaky Stone, Mean Bone, Sparkle Chili and other catalog families. The site identifies its products as handmade in Hungary and separately documents a historical Höfner Time And Again project by Albert Kasleder, supporting a relationship edge without conflating the brands.

SviSound's official review/archive material identifies Mark Svirkov and describes the Bulgarian builder's original pedal universe. The archive documents GeFuzz, FuzzoZoid, RetroZoid, SiMosFuzz, OverZoid, DistoZoid and Techno-OV families, with multiple version labels appearing in historical and current references. Independent and first-party references consistently place the builder in Bulgaria.

## Photo workflow
Every inventory row includes a product or catalog photo-source breadcrumb. These links are source trails for later specimen capture, credits, date/context, and rights review. They are not claims of republication permission.

## Scope discipline
Boost-only or boost-dominant functions remain gain-adjacent rather than inflating the central dirt count. Product families that span fuzz/overdrive/distortion remain single named product records unless evidence establishes a distinct retail generation or edition. No schematics, PCB layouts, gutshots, cloning instructions, or complete BOM data are published.
