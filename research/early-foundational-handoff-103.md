### Why this pass matters
Pass 55 extends the archive's chronological bridge from the 2022-2025 boutique diversification work into documented 2026 production history. It deliberately mixes several evidence classes rather than treating every new pedal as simply another model: a limited anniversary edition, a long-running grassroots UK fuzz with an approximate production corridor, an artist co-development project, a major-builder all-new circuit, a builder-partnership distribution line, and a clear V1-to-MKII revision.

### Key identification boundaries
- Cornerstone Nero is a first fuzz-generation anchor for the Italian builder. Keep it separate from the earlier Gladio overdrive family.
- The Sound Boutique Beefeater Fuzz has an approximate 2022 production corridor based on April 2024 reporting that it had been out for about two years. This is intentionally not a hard launch date.
- EQD Hoof 20th Anniversary is an edition-level state of Hoof, not a new family. The 400-unit count, Platinum Vein enclosure, original three-knob layout, hand-soldered through-hole construction and anniversary packaging are the useful external clues.
- Wampler Cryptid Fuzz is documented as an all-new Brian Wampler circuit launched March 4, 2025. Do not label it as a clone of a specific classic circuit.
- Benson Deep Sea Diver Fuzz-Echo is a three-year artist co-development project with Jessica Dobson. The commercial launch date and the longer development timeline are separate clocks.
- Great Eastern FX Focus Fuzz Deluxe is a successor production state to the original Focus Fuzz. The Boost/Drive/Fuzz selector and Overtone footswitch are strong external generation clues.
- Manson Guitar Works Supermassive Black Fuzz is a Manson-branded commercial model co-designed with Matt Bellamy and built in partnership with Thorpy FX. Keep those roles distinct.
- Mattoverse AirTrash MKII is a direct generation boundary after roughly seven years and more than 1,000 original units. The Clean-Blend-Trash and post-gain controls are useful external identifiers.
- Warm Audio / Dirty Boy Pedals Dirty Boy Jr. Fuzz is a distribution-era collaboration record. Preserve the original boutique Dirty Boy lineage separately from the Warm Audio Jr. production line.
- Buzzing Bugs Audio Devices Bolster is a 2026 builder/model record. No company-founding date is inferred from the product launch source.

### Editorial / evidence rule reinforced
Never convert an announcement date, review date, approximate product age, projected availability window, or first documented public appearance into a stronger claim than the source supports. Continue separating launch, shipment, production, edition, and specimen evidence.
