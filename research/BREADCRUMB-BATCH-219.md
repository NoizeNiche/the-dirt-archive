## Research method
This was a final discovery pass rather than another narrow alphabet block. The existing canonical builder index and live Scrape C census were used as duplicate gates, with manufacturer catalogs/manuals and established pedal directories used to surface additional builders and dirt products.

Representative source checks included:
- BBE and Behringer product material
- BIG EAR and Big Joe Stomp Box Company product catalogs
- Darkglass, Decibelics and Demeter product documentation
- Dophix, Flattley, Menatone and Pettyjohn current catalogs
- MadeByMike and Retro Mechanical Labs current catalogs
- Outlaw Effects current pedal catalog
- Lunastone manual
- KingTone current product pages
- RockBoard pedal listings for Fortin, EWS, Foxpedal and Guyatone

## Cleanup / gating
- **Brady Tone Electronics** was surfaced during discovery but was not admitted to the live census because the available source evidence did not provide clean, named catalog pedal records suitable for the archive.
- Existing canonical builders were not duplicated under alternate spellings.
- Products outside the Builder -> Dirt scope were not imported.
- The active census remains the live working layer.
