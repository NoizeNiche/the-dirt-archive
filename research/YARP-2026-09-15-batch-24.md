# YARP 2026-09-15 Batch 24

## Focus
Höfner and Schaller cross-catalog reconciliation, with special attention to the early Höfner fuzz products and the separate later Halifax-marketed states.

## Findings

### Höfner No. 540 Fuzz Tone
The Vintage Höfner archive states that Höfner introduced the No. 540 Fuzz Tone in 1967 and that pedals were also available with Schaller markings. The archive therefore retains Höfner as the marketed/catalog identity and Schaller as the likely physical-manufacturing relationship, rather than treating No. 540 as an independent Höfner factory product without qualification.

### Höfner No. 539 Distortion
The Höfner historical catalog places the No. 539 Distortion in the 1970-1971 corridor and describes it as a cheaper development of the earlier Fuzz Tone. Effects Database identifies it as a Schaller-built OEM pedal. This is catalog evidence, not a claim that every No. 539 specimen shares the same transistor or production state.

### Höfner Guitar Effect Pedal No. 1Z / No. 2Z
The Vintage Höfner archive documents the multi-effect Pedal No. 1 and No. 2 families, with the Z variants adding fuzz. These are important **hybrid/boundary products** because they combine wah/oscillation/multi-effect functions with a fuzz stage. They remain searchable in the catalog but are not used to inflate the central single-effect fuzz count.

### Höfner Z / Halifax
Effects Database records Höfner Z as a German-made wah-fuzz product with later Halifax-marketed production states. Because the archival evidence does not yet establish a simple factory handoff chronology, Halifax is kept as a marketed relationship node rather than a new canonical manufacturer.

### Schaller relationship
The Vintage Höfner archive explicitly notes that Schaller was heavily involved in Höfner pedal production and that several Höfner pedals closely match Schaller-branded equivalents. The archive therefore strengthens the Schaller <-> Höfner OEM relationship while preserving both retail identities for historical search.
