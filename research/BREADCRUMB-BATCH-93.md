The batch prioritizes Builder → Pedals → Photos → Information on the pedal.

## Important normalization decisions

- Sound Dog: BLUE DOG, YELLOW DOG, WARM DOG, HEAT DOG, DACHS-TORTION, CHOCO DUCHS and ORTHRUS are treated as the core dirt family. WARM DOG EVO and BLUE DOG EVO are retained because the builder documents them as evolved versions of the earlier models.
- Soul Power Instruments: original SPI overdrives, distortion and fuzzes are central. The 2022 仕事猫/Kumamine products are recorded as SPI-supervised collaborations, not a new builder lineage. Modifications and loop-switcher work remain relationships rather than separate canonical pedals.
- Sound Wave Lab: D-002, 4-knob Overdrive, Ultimate Drive, G-SLAM mk3, TUBE DRIVE and Shige Brass Master are cataloged as dirt products. The Miyazaki professional version is retained as a documented commercial variant of Ultimate Drive rather than a new conceptual family.
- Sunfish Audio: Lycoris, Forest, Tara, Summit and Skyscraper are central dirt records. Ikigai is excluded from central dirt because it is a multi-effect unit.
- STOMPROX: GHOST BUZZ is confirmed as the third STOMPROX product and therefore establishes the builder lineage, but the first two products remain unresolved research targets.
