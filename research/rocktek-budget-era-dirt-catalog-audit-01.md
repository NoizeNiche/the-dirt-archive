# Rocktek budget-era dirt catalog audit 01

## Scope
Builder-level pass for Rocktek's budget-era Japanese/Taiwanese pedal catalog, with emphasis on overdrive, distortion and adjacent products and on documented OEM/rebrand relationships.

## Catalog baseline
Effects Database indexes 16 Rocktek products. The dirt layer includes:
- DIOD-02 Distortion/OD
- DIR-01 Distortion
- MWR-01 Metal Worker
- ODR-01 Overdrive

Other indexed Rocktek products are effects outside the current dirt scope.

## Production / identity caution
Effects Database describes Rocktek as a late-20th-century budget pedal brand and separately records DIR-01 as originally manufactured in Taiwan during the late 1980s/1990s. This is important because the brand should not automatically be treated as a single Japanese factory across every production state.

## OEM / export relationships
The DIR-01 appears under other marketed names including Tronix and Omnitek, with additional related products documented under other budget brands. Preserve these as relationship records until factory attribution is independently supported.

## Dirt boundaries
- DIOD-02 is an explicit distortion/overdrive combination product.
- DIR-01 is a standalone distortion product.
- MWR-01 is a dedicated metal-oriented distortion product.
- ODR-01 is an overdrive product.

## Evidence disposition
- INDEXED_BASELINE: 16-product Rocktek category and four clear dirt models above.
- RELATIONSHIP_EVIDENCE: DIR-01 cross-brand trail including Tronix and Omnitek.
- UNRESOLVED: complete factory mapping for each Rocktek model, precise country-of-origin transition points, and lifetime catalog completeness.
