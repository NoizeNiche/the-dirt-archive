# YARP 2026-09-15 Batch 65: Ace Tone FM Family

## Scope
Builder-first catalog expansion for Ace Tone's early Fuzz Master family, separating the FM-1, FM-2, and FM-3 rather than collapsing them into one generic product.

## Related products
FW-1 Twin Ace and WM-1 Wah Master remain non-central boundary records. FM-2 should not be merged with the FY-6 export family merely because both are Japanese wedge-style fuzzes.

## OEM attribution rule
Similarity of enclosure, controls, or era is not sufficient to assign Ace Tone production to Nisshin Onpa, Maxon, or another Japanese factory. The archive records Ace Tone as the marketed/manufacturer entity for the FM family and leaves model-specific subcontracting open unless source evidence proves it.
