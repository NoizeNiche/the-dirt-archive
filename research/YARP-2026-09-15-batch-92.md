# YARP 2026-09-15 Batch 92: LIMMCO / Kimberly Manufacturing Transition

## Entity role
Long Island Musical Merchandise Co. (LIMMCO) is treated as a wholesale/distribution and house-brand entity, not as an enduring pedal factory.

## Dirt relationships
- Kimberly Buzz Box / Volume Expander: launched in fall 1966 as a LIMMCO house-brand product. Owner testimony says the earliest production was performed in the LIMMCO warehouse before the workload led to third-party manufacturing.
- Later Kimberly states: historical research points to United Musical Industries (UMI) as a later manufacturing partner, with the product undergoing another redesign before the end of its run.
- Nomad Rogue and Nomad Banshee: Nomad is treated as a LIMMCO house-brand state associated with Applied Audio manufacturing, not as a separate factory.

## Historical significance
LIMMCO is a major relationship node in the American 1960s fuzz market because it demonstrates the difference between house-brand ownership, wholesale distribution and actual physical production. The Kimberly line should therefore be modeled as a sequence of manufacturer states rather than one timeless builder.

## Evidence posture
The existence of LIMMCO's house-brand role and early in-house Kimberly production is evidence-qualified through owner testimony reproduced by specialist historical research. Exact dates for the transition from warehouse manufacture to each outside factory remain unresolved.
