## Evidence observations

Dédalo's own history states that Miguel Canel built effects for his band in the late 1990s and, in the early 2000s, worked with Santiago Canel to produce the pedals at larger scale. The initial production included fuzz-tortion. Current official catalog material documents Kamikaze as distortion + booster, B-Saurio as bass distortion and Revolver as overdrive + booster.

The Argentine foreign-ministry industry directory identifies Cluster Effects as an analog-effects manufacturer with more than twenty years of experience. This establishes builder identity, but not yet a complete lifetime dirt catalog.
