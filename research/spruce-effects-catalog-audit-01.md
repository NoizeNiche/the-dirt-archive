# Spruce Effects Historical Catalog Audit 01

## Research status
DISCOVERY_BASELINE

## Identity
Spruce Effects is the Bay Area husband-and-wife operation of Brian and Katie. Brian built his first pedal in 2005 while in college, then built kits, one-offs, and custom/mod work for friends and local musicians. The later professional Spruce line emerged after this experimental period.

## Dirt catalog signal
Spruce is strongly fuzz-oriented. Documented dirt products include:

- Salt Water Fuzz — three-knob Tonebender-derived fuzz, documented by Effects Database by 2016.
- Lichen Fuzz — germanium fuzz family.
- Rainier Fuzz — fuzz family with a historical JEN Jumbo Fuzz reference in builder/product coverage.
- Giganteum Drive — combined fuzz/boost drive product using the Lichen/Growth Ring concept.
- Additional historical fuzz/drive models in discontinued catalog coverage require a complete product-by-product enumeration pass.

## Chronology interpretation
The 2005 first-pedal date is a builder-origin anchor, not a commercial-launch date. Brian's own account says Spruce was an idea around that period and became a serious business later. Therefore no 2005 commercial product dates are assigned without dated evidence.

Salt Water Fuzz is securely represented by 2016-era documentation and should be treated as a later production product. The full commercial catalog requires further historical retailer, advertisement, and archived-site reconciliation.

## Evidence notes
Spruce's own site describes the company's beginnings as tinkering, kits, one-offs and custom work before the professional line. Reverb's 2016 interview supplies the 2005 first-pedal origin story and explains that the company name existed as an idea around that time. This distinction is useful for the archive's prototype/origin versus commercial-product hierarchy.
