# MI Audio — 1995–2005 Dirt Catalog Audit

## Status
**CANONICAL LINEAGE / EARLY DESIGN HISTORY VERIFIED**, with the pre-company Tube Zone history intentionally separated from the 2002 company formation.

## Origin
MI Audio says the company was founded in 2002 by Michael Ibrahim, after 15 years of interest in music and electronics. The Tube Zone Overdrive was the impetus for founding the company, although Ibrahim says he first began building Tube Zone devices in 1995 for himself and friends.

## Dirt spine
- **Tube Zone Overdrive** — design/build history begins 1995; became the founding commercial impetus for MI Audio in 2002.
- **Crunch Box** — early MI Audio distortion family; requires separate chronology verification before assigning a precise first-production date.
- **Blues Pro** — early MI Audio overdrive family; chronology should remain provisional until primary catalog evidence is located.

## Entity model
Treat Michael Ibrahim's pre-2002 home-built Tube Zone work as design antecedent. Treat **MI Audio** as the formal manufacturer/entity beginning in 2002. Do not backdate the legal/company entity merely because the design existed earlier.

## Evidence
- MI Audio official history: https://miaudio.com/about-us/

## Archive caution
The official history is exceptionally useful for the Tube Zone origin but does not enumerate the complete first-year product catalog. Crunch Box, Blues Pro, and other early models need catalog/advertisement evidence before being promoted to exact-year entries.
