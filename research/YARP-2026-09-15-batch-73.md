# YARP 2026-09-15 Batch 73: Höfner / Halifax Vintage Dirt Relationship

## Scope
Builder-first reconciliation of the vintage Höfner dirt products against the Schaller and Halifax relationship graph.

## Findings
- Höfner Buzz Tone: German-market fuzz product documented by specialist historical sources in the 1960s. The same product family is associated with the Halifax Buzz Tone name, so Halifax should be treated as a marketed/OEM relationship node rather than automatically as a separate factory.
- Höfner Fuzz / No. 539 Fuzz-Distortion: belongs to the separate Schaller-related German fuzz family already mapped in the Archive. Do not collapse it into the Buzz Tone solely because both carry Höfner branding.
- Höfner Z / Fuzz-Wah: later hybrid product, retained as a distinct family member and not assumed to share the exact manufacturer state of the earlier Buzz Tone.

## Attribution posture
Schaller is the strongest documented manufacturer relationship for the Höfner Fuzz/Distortion family. The Buzz Tone relationship to Selmer and Halifax is historically significant, but the exact physical factory for every early Höfner-labeled Buzz Tone specimen remains insufficiently proven. The Archive therefore preserves the marketed-brand and OEM layers separately.

## Sources
- VintageHofner historical equipment archive: Selmer Buzz Tone introduced at the September 1966 British Musical Instrument Trade Show and Höfner relationship context.
- Hewitts Garage Studio historical fuzz timeline: German Höfner Buzz Tone and the later Höfner/Schaller fuzz family.
- Effects Database: Schaller Fuzz and Kent 6400 Distorter relationship records.
