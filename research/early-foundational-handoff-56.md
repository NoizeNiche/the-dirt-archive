## Major findings
- Shin-ei's own history explicitly places Honey FY-6 Baby Crying in 1967 and the Shin-ei branded FY-6 in 1968, giving the Super Fuzz family a clear Honey → Shin-ei chronology.
- The Univox U-1095 record should be split into early stamped-steel and later die-cast specimen states. The external enclosure change is more useful for identification than pretending there is one exact transition date.
- Ace Tone FM-2 now has a much stronger March 1968 anchor from period advertising/catalog research than the older generic 1969-71 range.
- Guyatone FS-2, FS-3, FS-5 and FS-6 are retained as separate model identities. Current evidence supports catalog windows rather than unsupported exact first-sale dates.
- JEN, Unicord and Vox V828 remain in the Italian Tone Bender branch and are not merged with British Sola Sound production.
- Teisco Wau Wau Fuzz remains an OEM/fuzz-wah family lead requiring further alias reconciliation.

## Evidence rule
Where a source establishes an enclosure change, model sequence or period catalog appearance, record that fact without inventing a precise transition date. OEM and rebrand relationships remain relational claims unless primary evidence is strong enough to promote them.
