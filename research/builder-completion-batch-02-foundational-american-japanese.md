# Builder Completion Batch 02: Foundational American + Japanese Catalog Targets

Research date: 2026-09-14

Status: breadth-first catalog work. These records are **not** declared lifetime-complete unless explicitly marked otherwise. The purpose of this batch is to move major P0 builders from census-only toward structured catalog enumeration.

## DOD / DOD Electronics

### Identity
- Canonical builder/brand: DOD Electronics.
- Founded in 1974 in Salt Lake City, Utah, by John Johnson and David O. DiFrancesco, according to DigiTech's official history.
- The archive should keep DOD distinct from DigiTech while preserving the corporate/brand lineage.

### Dirt catalog baseline found in official historical material
- Overdrive Preamp 250
- FX10 BiFET Preamp (boost/preamp boundary; keep separate from central dirt count unless used as historically relevant gain product)
- FX69 Grunge
- Death Metal distortion
- Carcosa Fuzz

### Research notes
- The 250 is the priority historical anchor. DigiTech states that the original 250 was conceived in the 1970s by David O. DiFrancesco and describes it as providing distortion and boost.
- DigiTech's current 50th Anniversary 250 page states that the original 250 was introduced in 1974 and that 1,974 anniversary units were produced. Treat the anniversary edition as a later edition/reissue, not as the original production state.
- DOD's historical catalog is much larger than the small list above. This batch therefore moves DOD to a high-priority completion queue rather than claiming catalog completion.

### Evidence
- https://digitech.com/history/
- https://digitech.com/dp/overdrive-preamp-250/
- https://digitech.com/dp/overdrive-preamp-250-50th-anniversary/

## BOSS / Roland

### Identity
- Canonical builder/brand: BOSS, a Roland brand.
- BOSS compact effects began in 1977 with the OD-1, PH-1 and SP-1.
- The archive should treat BOSS as a builder/brand entity and retain Roland as the parent company rather than collapsing the two names.

### Dirt catalog baseline
Early and foundational dirt models to enumerate first:
- OD-1 OverDrive (1977)
- DS-1 Distortion (1978)
- OD-2 Turbo OverDrive
- SD-1 Super OverDrive
- DS-2 Turbo Distortion
- HM-2 Heavy Metal
- OS-2 OverDrive/Distortion
- BD-2 Blues Driver
- MT-2 Metal Zone
- MD-2 Mega Distortion

Later catalog completion must separately capture OD-3, ODB-3, FZ-2, FZ-3, FZ-5 and other bass/fuzz/drive products, plus limited/reissue/signature states where they are actual dirt products.

### Research notes
- Roland's BOSS compact history identifies the OD-1 as the first compact BOSS effects unit in 1977.
- Roland's 2017 40th-anniversary release confirms the original three compact models and describes the anniversary OD-1 as a one-time reissue produced in Japan with serial-numbered packaging.
- Roland's historical material places the DS-1 introduction in 1978.
- Do not merge anniversary/reissue OD-1 records with original 1977 production.

## Electro-Harmonix

### Identity
- Canonical builder: Electro-Harmonix.
- Founded by Mike Matthews in 1968 in New York City.
- The archive must preserve the company's multiple historical manufacturing/ownership eras instead of treating every later Big Muff or EHX-branded product as one uninterrupted physical production state.

## MXR

### Identity
- Canonical builder/brand: MXR, later acquired/resurrected by Jim Dunlop.
- Founders Keith Barr and Terry Sherwood are part of the historical builder story.
- Preserve original MXR production versus Dunlop-era continuation as separate production/ownership states.

### Foundational dirt catalog baseline
- Distortion+
- Blue Box Octave Fuzz

Later Dunlop-era dirt catalog requires enumeration of products such as Custom Badass '78 Distortion and subsequent MXR distortion/fuzz/overdrive releases.

### Research notes
- Dunlop's official MXR history says the original company grew from a small Rochester, New York operation and added Distortion+, Dyna Comp and Blue Box after the Phase 90.
- Dunlop states that MXR eventually closed and that Jim Dunlop acquired and resurrected the brand, initially reissuing Phase 90, Distortion+, Dyna Comp and Blue Box.
- Distortion+ is therefore a high-priority cross-era model: original MXR production, post-acquisition reissue, and later revisions should not be collapsed.

### Evidence
- https://lifestyle.jimdunlop.com/mxr-a-short-history/
- https://www.jimdunlop.com/mxr-distortion/

## ProCo

### Identity
- Canonical builder: ProCo Sound.
- Priority dirt lineage: RAT family.
- Keep ProCo's RAT family separate from later licensing/marketing relationships and from third-party historical reconstructions.

### RAT family baseline
- Bud Box RAT (1978-era prototype/custom production; commonly documented as 12 total including prototype in secondary historical research)
- Big Box RAT Version 1 (1979-1981)
- Big Box RAT Version 2 (1981-1983)
- Small Box RAT (1984-1988)
- R2DU (1984-1988)
- RAT 2 (1988-present lineage)
- Turbo RAT (1989-present lineage)
- Vintage Reissue RAT (1991-2005)
- Brat (1997-2001)
- Roadkill (1997-2001)
- 25th Anniversary Silver RAT (2003)
- Deucetone RAT (2002-present lineage)
- You Dirty RAT (2004-present lineage)
- SOLO (2006-present lineage)
- '85 Whiteface RAT Reissue (2010)
- FATRAT (2014)
- Lil' RAT (2021-era introduction)

### Research notes
- The RAT should be treated as a family with enclosure, control-label, logo, production and circuit-state boundaries rather than as one model with cosmetic variants.
- JHS's long-form RAT history explicitly warns that popular shorthand version labels can obscure important sub-versions and uses a more granular revision scheme.
- Griffin Effects independently provides a useful production-era index and enclosure chronology. These are secondary sources and should remain evidence trails, not substitutes for surviving ProCo primary documentation.
- The public archive should avoid publishing circuit reconstruction details even when secondary research contains them.
