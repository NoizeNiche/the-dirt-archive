## Evidence notes
Val Martins Amplification's first-party site describes its pedals as handmade and wired in France and maintains a dedicated pedal catalog. The current catalog lists 23 pedal products total, of which 21 are central or gain-adjacent dirt records in this batch; Reverb is a separate later verification path, not required for the basic builder/catalog promotion. The current category structure explicitly labels products with Original and Sobriety designations, so those labels are preserved instead of collapsing the families prematurely.

The Bullet Box product page identifies it as a handmade, 100% analog French overdrive/boost. The catalog also identifies a signature Michaal Benjelloun model, which should become a relationship node during Protocol B. What The Fuzz ? is retained in the central dirt universe because the builder catalogs it as fuzz/overdrive. Reverb is omitted from the central evidence set where the first-party catalog already establishes the product identity.
