## Historical findings

- The late-1960s East Coast Fuzz family is documented across at least 11 OEM brandings, including Manny's, Orpheum, Clark, Mica-Tone and Gracin's, but the physical manufacturer remains unidentified.
- A 1968 Manny's Fuzz sales example establishes a firm public-sales date, while not identifying the factory.
- Applied Audio, UMI and Universal Amplifier Corp. are treated only as regional component-sourcing peers in this context. Similar parts or construction do not justify assigning the East Coast Fuzz to any one of them.
- AUL Instruments is independently documented as the New York manufacturer contracted by Guild for the transitional grey Foxey Lady production after Mosrite. This is a confirmed AUL OEM relationship, but it is kept separate from the unresolved East Coast Fuzz family.
- Guild Foxey Lady v2 therefore remains a clean AUL record rather than being merged with Manny's, Orpheum or Clark merely because the designs share Fuzzrite ancestry.

## Validation posture

This creates a useful boundary between **known U.S. OEM production** and **unknown-builder U.S. OEM production**. A known builder nearby is not being used as a proxy for an unresolved pedal family.
