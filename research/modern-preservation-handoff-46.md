## Major 2017 research findings
- Guitar Pedal X's 2017 annual census is the broad discovery spine for this batch and explicitly identifies the year's notable overdrive, fuzz and distortion field, including Brothers, JB-2, Dracarys, Dirty Shirley, Rockaway Archer, D&M Drive, Filaments, Jupiter V2, Iron Horse V2, Warhorn and Tumnus Deluxe.
- BOSS provides an unusually strong chronology anchor for JB-2 Angry Driver: official announcement September 8, 2017, developed with JHS to mark the 40th anniversary of BOSS compact pedals. Its six-way routing between Blues Driver and Angry Charlie voices is a major identification feature.
- Chase Bliss contemporary January 2017 material documents Brothers as a March 20 release with two independent analog channels, six gain voices and programmable routing/presets. The archive treats its digital control layer as separate from its analog audio-path identity.
- J. Rockett's Rockaway Archer was announced in February 2017 as the Steve Stevens Q-Series collaboration, using the Archer overdrive foundation with a six-band graphic EQ. The signature artist relationship and visible slider array are high-value specimen clues.
- Wampler announced Dracarys on March 1, 2017 as Brian Wampler's modern high-gain release. Keep the dated manufacturer announcement separate from later reviews and annual surveys.
- Friedman's Fuzz Fiend and Motor City Drive are especially important 2017 tube-pedal records. Contemporary April coverage documents the 12AX7-based designs and impending market release, while the official Fuzz Fiend demo documents the Rage control and required high-current supply.
- KMA Minos is retained as a 2017 fuzz anchor because contemporary catalog evidence identifies it as a germanium fuzz drawing on Tone Bender MkII and Superfuzz territory. Wurm remains a distinct 2017 high-gain identity rather than a generic HM-2 clone field.
- Walrus Jupiter V2 and Iron Horse V2 are explicit revisions with user-visible changes. Jupiter V2 adds increased gain, revised volume/tone behavior and an internal gate control; Iron Horse V2 changes the gain range, tone range and unity-gain behavior. These are generation-level clues rather than cosmetic-only notes.
- Keeley Filaments is preserved with a stronger distinction than a generic annual listing because Premier Guitar explicitly identifies it in 2017 as Keeley's first metal-oriented distortion effort.

## Data rule reinforced
2017 contains several important examples of why the archive separates model, generation, edition and collaboration. JB-2 is a co-developed product, Brothers separates programmable control from analog audio, Jupiter and Iron Horse carry explicit V2 identities, and the JHS/Wampler/Friedman artist relationships are provenance fields rather than replacements for the builder/product identity.
