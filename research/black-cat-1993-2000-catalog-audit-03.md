# Black Cat Pedals, 1993-2000 catalog audit

Research status: HISTORICAL_BASELINE

## Identity
Black Cat Pedals was founded in Texas by Fred Bonte in 1993, placing it near the beginning of the modern boutique-pedal era. The original company ceased production in late 2007 and was later restructured, so original Black Cat and later revival production must remain separate production eras.

## Dirt catalog signal
Effects Database currently indexes 24 Black Cat products. The dirt-relevant universe includes OD-1 / Freddie Fuzz, OD-Fuzz, Super Fuzz, Bass Octave Fuzz, D&S, D-II Distortion, OD-Boost, N-Fuzz, Monster K-Fuzz, RGDP (Really Great Dirt Pedal), Psychedelic Machine and Wee Buzz. Not every indexed product belongs to the 1993-2000 period, so these are catalog candidates rather than a claimed period-complete list.

## High-confidence historical anchors
- OD-1 / Freddie Fuzz: original Black Cat design, associated with Fred Bonte and later also sold as Freddie Fuzz in the Custom Audio Electronics context. Preserve the CAE relationship as a marketed/model relationship rather than merging identities.
- Super Fuzz: Black Cat version of the Univox/Shin-ei Super Fuzz family. Preserve this as a Black Cat recreation/product lineage, not as an original Shin-ei product.
- OD-Fuzz: Black Cat two-channel dirt product. Later silicon, germanium and hybrid versions are distinct production states.

## Chronology / interpretation
The founding date of 1993 is well supported. The currently available sources do not justify assigning exact launch years to every individual Black Cat model in the 1993-2000 window. The archive therefore records the builder-era anchor and product candidates without inventing year-by-year dates.

## Relationship rules
Keep Fred Bonte's Black Cat company separate from Custom Audio Electronics and from the 2009-onward Black Cat relaunch. Keep Black Cat Super Fuzz separate from the original Shin-ei/Univox Super Fuzz family while recording the explicit recreation relationship.
