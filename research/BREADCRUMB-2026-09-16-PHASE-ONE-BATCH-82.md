## Evidence notes
BJPress has a useful Korean product trail even though its direct corporate-history page is difficult to surface. Current Korean dealer records identify BJPress as the manufacturer and Korea as origin. Music Force reproduces BJPress product copy describing the builder's analog-tone research and lists the Range87 Special Run; the same record identifies BJPRESS as both manufacturer and importer with Korea as origin. ToneQuest's BJPress catalog preserves Range84, Range84 Hot Rod, Range87, Blue Tonic, Blue Tonic92 and MorningStar as named products. A 2021 Delicious Audio report describes Range84 as a handmade Korean Rat-inspired distortion, and a Reverb listing states that BJ Press pedals are handmade in Korea.

The Range family should be kept as one lineage. Range84, its Hot Rod state and the later Range87 are distinct named product states, but their continuity is more useful than treating each enclosure/version as a new builder. Blue Tonic and Blue Tonic92 likewise remain related overdrive states, with MorningStar retained as a separate named drive product.

## Boundary / scope notes
- BJPress buffer/interface products are outside the central dirt inventory.
