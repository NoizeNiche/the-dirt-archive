## Evidence notes
Goran Custom Guitars' official site lists Fat Boy Overdrive as a pedal product and names Fat Boy Wah-Wah as the other documented effect. Historical Effects Database material dates the Fat Boy Drive entry to August 2012, while Premier Guitar's Lance Lopez interview documents a handmade two-channel Fat Boy Drive built in Belgrade. Marketplace specimens from 2016-2017 show later V1/V2 styling, which should be treated as generation/specimen evidence rather than separate builders.

Sitek Guitar Electronics' official site identifies Andrzej and Hania Sitek as founders and gives Gdynia, Poland as the workshop location. The current catalog lists Caffeine Dirty Booster v2, Fuzzie Germanium Fuzz v2, Vixen Germanium Booster, Pandora Octave Fuzz, Freya Overdrive/Booster, Wuffy Fuzzy Distortion, Nursie Transparent Booster, and Cocoa Distortion. Guitar.com independently documented the Wuffy launch in 2019 and identified the surrounding four-pedal dirt roster at that time.
