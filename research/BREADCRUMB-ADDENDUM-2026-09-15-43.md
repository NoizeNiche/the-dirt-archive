## Historical findings

- Hohner MF-50 Tri Dirty Booster is documented as U.S.-made and as third-party manufactured; the contractor remains unknown. The Rogue Fuzz enclosure resemblance is retained only as a candidate clue, not a factory attribution.
- The broader Hohner Sound Modifier series is therefore not assumed to be German-made simply because the marketed brand is Hohner.
- Echolette's Klemt-era production is distinct from the post-1969 Dynacord ownership era. Echolette was the trade brand applied to Klemt-made amplification and effect equipment in the earlier period.
- Dynacord 5010 G is useful census evidence for a German-made integrated fuzz/tremolo amplifier effect, but it is not treated as evidence of a Dynacord standalone stompbox line.
- Klemt Echolette tape devices are retained as effect-device context because their preamps can generate strong saturation, but they are not entered as standalone dirt pedals.

## Validation posture

The German sweep reinforces the need to distinguish marketed brand, physical builder, ownership transition, and integrated effect architecture. No new public lineage edges were promoted.
