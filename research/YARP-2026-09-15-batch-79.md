# YARP 2026-09-15 Batch 79: JHS Zonk / John Hornby Skewes Boundary Audit

The John Hornby Skewes Zonk Machine is retained as a distinct marketed British product lineage. Historical timeline evidence places the Zonk Machine in the press by early 1966 and in production at least through 1968. It is not collapsed into Sola Sound's Tone Bender even though it belongs to the same early British fuzz ecosystem and is widely described as derived from the Tone Bender family.

The archive should preserve JHS as a marketed/distribution entity and keep any physical manufacturing attribution model-specific until stronger primary evidence is located. This prevents the common collector shorthand of treating all early British wedge fuzzes as products of one factory.

Source trail: Fuzzboxes historical timeline; historical JHS/Zonk references.

No schematics, PCB layouts, complete BOMs, gutshot libraries, cloning instructions, or circuit reconstruction material included.
