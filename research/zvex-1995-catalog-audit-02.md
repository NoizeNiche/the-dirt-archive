# ZVEX Effects 1995 catalog audit

## Scope
Builder-level historical pass focused on the company's first year and foundational dirt products. This file is not a circuit-reconstruction resource.

## Identity and founding
Zachary Vex says he built his first Z.Vex pedal on his kitchen table in June 1995. He took the prototype to Willie's American Guitars in St. Paul, where the shop ordered three units. Vex identifies the Octane as the first Z.Vex pedal released. He reports approximately 12 Octanes sold between June and October 1995 before the dealer asked for another design, leading to the Fuzz Factory.

ZVEX's current official site states that the company has been making effects pedals and amps since 1995 and identifies its Minneapolis/St. Louis Park operation, hand-painted/custom products and Vexter series as distinct manufacturing/presentation layers.

## Foundational 1995 dirt catalog
- Octane — octave-up fuzz — first commercial Z.Vex offering, 1995.
- Fuzz Factory — fuzz — developed after the initial Octane run in 1995.

## Later production-state distinction
The Fuzz Factory later gained a Vexter Series version in December 2004. The archive should represent the hand-painted original family and Vexter production state as distinct generations/editions rather than treating Vexter as merely a cosmetic duplicate.

## Evidence interpretation
The 1995 origin story is unusually well documented by first-person retrospective testimony and corroborating current company history. Exact production counts and serial chronology require separate specimen-level research. The Fuzz Factory should not be treated as proof of every later ZVEX fuzz/distortion product; lifetime catalog enumeration remains a separate task.
