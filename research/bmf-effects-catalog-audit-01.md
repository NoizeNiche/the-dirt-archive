# BMF Effects Historical Catalog Audit 01

## Research status
DISCOVERY_BASELINE

## Identity
BMF Effects is the boutique pedal operation associated with Scott Kiraly in Southern California. Effects Database records the first commercial BMF pedal as sold on September 17, 2005, followed by a distributor relationship in April 2006. The current BMF site describes the company as founded in 2005 and handcrafted in Southern California.

## Dirt catalog spine
The indexed BMF universe contains 17 products. Dirt-relevant models include:

- Fat Bastard — boost/gain boundary; first official BMF pedal.
- El Jefe — overdrive.
- Aries Fuzz — fuzz; builder describes it as a Big Muff variant but not a Ram's Head circuit specifically.
- Ge Spot MkI — germanium fuzz/gain.
- Ge Spot F2 — germanium fuzz/gain.
- Rocket 88 — fuzz.
- Sisyphuzz — silicon fuzz.
- The Great Wide Open — fixed distortion.
- Purple Nurple — overdrive/gain.

Other BMF products such as Decho Box, Liquid Sky, Little Red Compressor, Holy Balls, GB Boost and Squawk Box remain in the broader builder catalog but outside the central dirt count where appropriate.

## Chronology anchors
17 Sep 2005: first commercial BMF pedal sold.
Apr 2006: first distributor and first large-scale order.
Aug 2007: first set of pedals delivered to Marc Ford.
Jul 2008: Marc Ford connection to Jeff Massey begins.

## Archive interpretation
The strongest evidence supports a builder-level start in 2005 and a rapid transition from one-off/home workshop production to wider distribution during 2006-2008. Individual dirt-model launch years beyond the Fat Bastard require dated advertisements, catalog pages, serial specimens, or contemporaneous retailer evidence.

The archive should preserve Scott Kiraly's distinction between his own designs and products that intentionally reference historical families. Do not convert a builder's own statement that a pedal is a variant into an assertion that the product is a direct commercial clone unless the historical documentation supports that wording.
