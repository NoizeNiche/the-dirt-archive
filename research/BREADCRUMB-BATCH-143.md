## Batch focus
Japanese builder census, prioritizing a distinct current/historical catalog over isolated famous pedals.

## Builder added
**YUKI** — canonical Japanese handmade effects brand associated with builder Makoto Kawai. Current Japanese documentation identifies YUKI as the sister brand of AYA tokyo japan, established after Kawai's long AYA career and relocation to Sapporo in 2019. Preserve YUKI as a separate public builder/brand identity while retaining the AYA relationship as lineage metadata.

## Dirt catalog captured
Central: 3OD / Three EQ Overdrive; DAW; Blues1903; THE FUZZ; 初月 -UIZUKI-; 十五夜 -JUGOYA-.

Relationship-only: 究極, 十六夜 -IZAYOI-, 弓張月 -YUMIHARIZUKI- because they are primarily bass preamp / DI products rather than central dirt pedals.

## Photo policy
Use official or dealer exterior product photography as reference/credit trails. Do not publish gutshots, schematics, PCB images, or internal construction material.

## Key normalization decisions
- YUKI remains separate from AYA tokyo japan despite direct builder lineage.
- Current 2026 bass dirt releases are included as first-class YUKI products rather than treated as side notes.
- Preamp/DI products remain relationship-only unless a future source establishes them as central dirt models.
- The Fuzz is a current YUKI fuzz model and should not be merged into unrelated historical fuzz labels.
