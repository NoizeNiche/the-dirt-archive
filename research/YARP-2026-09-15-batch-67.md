# YARP 2026-09-15 Batch 67: PUE Elektronika Muzyczna

## Scope
Catalog-first treatment of the Polish PUE Elektronika Muzyczna dirt-bearing treadle family documented from the late 1970s into the 1980s.

## Builder identity
PUE Elektronika Muzyczna is retained as a distinct Polish manufacturer/brand node. The surviving product documentation describes PUE-made multi-effect treadle pedals rather than a distributor-only label.

## Historical significance
PUE broadens the archive's Eastern European coverage beyond East Germany, Czechoslovakia, and the Soviet Union. These products demonstrate that combined fuzz/wah/treadle designs continued as practical analog products in Poland into the late 1970s and 1980s.

## Evidence boundary
The current surviving documentation is strongest for the Wah-Fuzz Beat and Tone & Volume Beat products. Do not infer a single common internal design for every PUE model without model-level evidence.
