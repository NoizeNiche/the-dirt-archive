# YARP Breadcrumb 133

## Focus
Builder census qualification gate.

## What changed
- Established a hard editorial rule: a candidate is not promoted as a Dirt Archive builder until at least one fuzz, distortion, overdrive, or clearly dirt-oriented booster product is documented as made/produced by that entity.
- Separated builder qualification from later relationship archaeology. OEM, distributor, importer, designer, licensee, marketed badge, successor, and revival relationships remain separate research questions.
- Qualified Rosac / Sierra Electronics as a verified dirt maker based on direct evidence for the Nu-Fuzz, Nu-Wa-Fuzz, and Nu-Fuzz Distortion Blender and evidence that Rosac built effects.
- Confirmed that Rosac already exists in the discovery layer, so this pass does not create a duplicate builder node.

## Research principle
Builder discovery first. Product inventory second. Relationship and OEM graph later.

## Next queue
Continue screening candidate names against the dirt-device gate before adding or promoting them. Exclude dirt-only-unsupported brands, distributors, retailers, and unresolved labels from the builder census even when they appear in product histories.
