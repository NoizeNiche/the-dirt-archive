# Catalog Completeness Phase 29

## Scope
Breadth-first builder and product enumeration focused on Electro-Harmonix, Prescription Electronics, D*A*M and Abominable Electronics.

## Discovery sources
- Effects Database Electro-Harmonix builder page: 133 products in current discovery snapshot.
- Electro-Harmonix official discontinued catalog: 94 discontinued entries visible in current snapshot; official pedal category shows a much larger current catalog.
- Effects Database Prescription Electronics builder page: 14 products.
- Effects Database D*A*M builder page: 28 products.
- Effects Database Abominable Electronics builder page: 17 products.
- Product-level pages were used as visual/reference leads where available.

## Editorial treatment
This phase is enumeration, not generation verification. Effects Database records are preserved as discovery evidence and do not automatically establish chronology, authorship, OEM relationships or official version names.

Electro-Harmonix is deliberately treated as a historical catalog universe. Duplicate-looking entries such as Bad Stone variants, LPB-1 entries, Memory Man entries and Octave Multiplexer entries remain separate discovery candidates until product-level research determines whether they represent distinct retail models, packaging eras, database splits or naming variants.

D*A*M records include both D*A*M-branded products and documented D*A*M reissues of Colorsound/Sola Sound products because those manufacturing relationships are historically important and should be preserved for later relationship research.

Prescription Electronics is a priority historical builder. Effects Database identifies Jack Brossart as founder in 1993, closure in 2014, a brief 2019 return and disappearance following Brossart's death that summer. This chronology should be separately verified before becoming canonical.

Abominable Electronics is included as a builder-first discovery record even though some products are described as clones or collaborations. Those relationships become later research targets, not conclusions of this phase.
