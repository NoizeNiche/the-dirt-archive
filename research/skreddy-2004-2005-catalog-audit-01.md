# Skreddy Pedals — 2004–2005 Catalog Audit

## Status
**Builder entity:** CANONICAL
**Period:** 2004–2005 origin and first-production period
**Confidence:** HIGH for first products and dates from Marc Ahlfs's timeline; individual specimen chronology remains a later task.

## Historical spine
Skreddy Pedals identifies Marc Ahlfs and Cynthia Ahlfs as the core operation and states that they have been designing and building guitar effects since **2004**.

Effects Database preserves a detailed timeline from Marc Ahlfs:
- **September 2004:** first pedal, **Mayonaise**, described as a period-oriented Big Muff-derived design.
- **January 2005:** **Zero**, a redesign using different transistors and a smaller enclosure.
- **September 2005:** **Screw Driver**, identified as the first original design in the Skreddy line.
- **2005–2007:** further BMP-derived models including **Top Fuel** and the later **Lunar Module**.

## Dirt catalog spine
- Mayonaise — 2004 first Skreddy pedal.
- Zero — January 2005 redesign/variant stage.
- Screw Driver — September 2005 original-design milestone.
- Top Fuel — 2005–2007 period; exact first production date should be narrowed later.
- Lunar Module — falls inside the 2005–2007 derivative/original-design transition and should receive its own dated product record later.

## Archive cautions
Do not flatten Mayonaise, Zero, Top Fuel, and later Muff-related Skreddy releases into one generic 'Big Muff clone' entry. The archive should preserve product identity, release sequence, and design lineage as separate layers.

The Mayonaise is especially useful as a documentary bridge between the older collector/clone culture and the early boutique period. That historical role should be noted without turning the archive into a circuit-analysis document.

## Key sources
- Skreddy Pedals official About page: https://skreddypedals.com/about
- Effects Database Skreddy timeline/interview
- Marc Ahlfs interview at Pedais & Efeitos

## Archive decision
Promote Skreddy into the **2004–2005 boutique builder census** with Mayonaise, Zero, and Screw Driver as firmly anchored milestones. Keep later 2005–2007 models in a provisional sequence until period specimens or dated catalogs tighten the chronology.
