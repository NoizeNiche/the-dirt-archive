# YARP 2026-09-15 Batch 61: FBT Italian Dirt / JEN-Market Relationship

## Scope
Catalog the documented FBT dirt-bearing products and distinguish FBT as a marketed Italian manufacturer/brand from the better-supported JEN Elettronica OEM relationship where evidence is model-specific.

## Enumerated products

### FBT FF-III Fuzz
A 1970s Italian fuzz product documented by surviving examples and specialist references. ToneHome identifies the FF-III as made by Jen Electronica in Pescara and sold as a differently branded version of the JEN Fuzz FZ-III. Reverb examples also identify the FBT FF-III as an Italian-made 1970s pedal and record the FBT/JEN/FZ-III relationship.

### FBT Distortion A-608 B
A distinct mid-1970s FBT distortion product documented by a surviving example. The seller reports Italian manufacture, foot-controlled gain, Level and Tone controls, and a period specimen date corridor. Because this evidence is specimen-level and not a factory catalog, retain the product as evidence-qualified without asserting that all A-608 B units used the same manufacturing state.

### FBT broader dirt catalog
Specialist historical references state that FBT produced several fuzz and wah products in the 1970s and sometimes rebranded JEN designs. The archive therefore treats FBT as a canonical historical marketed/manufacturing node with an explicit relationship to JEN, while avoiding the stronger claim that every FBT dirt product was made by JEN.

## Relationship model
FBT FF-III should cross-link to the JEN FZ-III family as a marketed/rebranded state. The A-608 B should remain a separate FBT model until a primary catalog establishes whether it was a JEN OEM, a parallel FBT design, or another contract state.

## Evidence state

**Evidence-qualified:** FBT FF-III existence and Italian manufacture; JEN FZ-III cross-brand relationship; FBT Distortion A-608 B existence and Italian manufacture; broader 1970s FBT fuzz/distortion activity.

**Unresolved:** complete FBT period catalog; corporate history/endpoints; exact factory responsibility for A-608 B; dates of each FBT/JEN cross-branded model.

## Source trail
1. ToneHome, FBT Fuzz FF III / JEN relationship reference.
2. Reverb historical examples for FBT FF-III and Distortion A-608 B.
3. Existing Dirt Archive JEN catalog and Italian OEM research for cross-indexing.
