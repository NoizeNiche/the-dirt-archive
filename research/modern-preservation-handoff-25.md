## Important research notes
- Crazy Tube Circuits HI POWER has a dated December 2023 launch and combines Colorsound Power Boost / Overdriver lineage with Hiwatt DR103/DR504-inspired amp voices. The passive series effects loop, independent footswitches, PWR/ODR switch, 103/504 switch and top-mounted jacks are high-value identification clues.
- Formula B EIGHTY Master is well documented in 2023 coverage with its 50W/100W power-mode toggle, three-position optical compression control, three-band EQ, Gain, Volume and Presence modeling. The actual introduction year remains less certain than the 2023 census date, so preserve that distinction.
- TWA Krytical Mass was unveiled at NAMM 2023 and has an unusually dense identification surface: Sensitivity, Dry/Fuzz levels, HP/LP filtering, three-way mids, Edge, Bias and transformer-polarity controls. Its Brassmaster lineage is separate from TWA product identity.
- Laowiz Cyst Mode is explicitly identified by the builder as V2 of the earlier tiny-run Cystech Filter Drive. The exact V2 introduction date is unresolved. Preserve the builder's version statement without converting the 2023 roundup appearance into a false launch date.
- Hoopla Tinnitus is documented in 2023 as a Mesa/Boogie Mark V-inspired preamp/distortion with Pre, Gain, Master, Bass, Mid and Treble controls. The Mark V relationship is an inspiration/lineage claim, not the product identity itself.
- Way Huge Stone Burner Sub Atomic Fuzz is a 2023-era compact Way Huge Smalls product with separate fuzz/sub-octave levels and selectable Sub 1/Sub 2 ranges. Keep the Atreides-related lineage and product identity distinct.
- Old Blood Noise Endeavors Beam Splitter has 2023-era public evidence and a current V2 production state. OBNE firmware documentation distinguishes early V2 beta/demo units from V2.0C first production. Do not collapse firmware/prototype/production labels into an invented generation history.
