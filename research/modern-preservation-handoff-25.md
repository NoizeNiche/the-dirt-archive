## Important research notes
- Great Eastern FX Co. Focus Fuzz was a limited 250-unit original hybrid Ge/Si design launched in March 2023. Focus Fuzz Silicon followed in July 2023 after the original sold out and is an all-silicon, all-original design. Preserve these as distinct variant/product identities, not merely finish changes.
- KMA Mandrake is an explicitly documented reworking and improvement of the Shriek circuit from MOAI MAEA. Its discrete-transistor upper-octave fuzz, pre-gain filter, separate DRY/OCT+ controls, top jacks and relay soft switching provide a strong specimen fingerprint.
- Rainger FX states that Chop Fuzzz is the latest installment in the Dr Freakenstein dynasty and retains the core fuzz circuit used across earlier Dr Freakenstein versions while adding extensive modulation/control functions. The three-Z spelling distinguishes it from the prior Chop Fuzz naming.
- JAM Octaurus is present in the 2023 GPX census and is currently documented by the builder as a versatile octafuzz with Volume, Tone and Gain plus two voice/clipping toggles. Historical launch dating still needs stronger primary enrichment.
- Spaceman Charon is documented as a germanium/silicon hybrid fuzz with a simple three-control layout and a low 100K input impedance. Current Spaceman documentation establishes a later Charon v2 Mod as a reworked take, so original and v2 must remain separate identities.

## Data rule reinforced
A later redesign that changes the circuit remains a new generation or variant candidate; a limited transistor supply can also create a meaningful production boundary without changing the marketing name. Never infer that two products are identical merely because the name is similar, and never infer a generation solely because the manufacturer changed components, documentation or artwork.
