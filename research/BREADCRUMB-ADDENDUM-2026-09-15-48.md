## Historical findings

- Guild VW-1 Volume Wah is retained as a high-confidence Applied Audio OEM product, separate from the FW-3 platform.
- Guild SP-5 Siren Surf & Turf is retained as a high-confidence 1974 Applied-built U.S. production state and is explicitly separated from the earlier Japanese Shin-Ei-related Guild HA-9P Surf Wah state.
- Guild Dimensional Reverb System remains a medium-confidence Applied relationship pending stronger model-specific documentation.
- Nomad Banshee Fuzz is now cross-indexed as an Applied-built New Jersey OEM state distributed by LIMMCO. Its exact factory chronology remains unresolved.
- Applied-branded Banshee is preserved alongside the Nomad marketed state without assuming that Banshee preceded the Applied FT-100.
- Kimberly Buzz Box and Volume Expander is treated as a multi-stage production history: LIMMCO initial production, later outsourced manufacture, and a specialist reconstruction that places UMI in a later redesign and Applied in the intermediate period. No single-builder simplification is made.
- Nomad Super Fuzz is explicitly isolated from the later Applied/LIMMCO Nomad products. Its possible Mosrite/Fuzzrite origin remains unresolved and is not inherited from the Banshee/Rogue lineage.
- Earlier Guild HA-9P Surf Wah is preserved as a separate pre-Applied production state so Guild branding does not imply continuous manufacture by the same builder.

## Validation posture

The Applied Audio record now distinguishes physical manufacturing, marketed branding, distribution relationships, and successive production states. Applied-built products are not automatically treated as one circuit family, and earlier products carrying the same marketed identity are not merged without model-specific evidence.
