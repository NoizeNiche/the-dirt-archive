Date: 2026-09-16

## Duplicate-control gate

## Batch
Batch 45 expands Phase One coverage into the Philippines, Thailand and Vietnam, with a focus on boutique builders having recoverable dirt catalogs and useful product-level visual/source trails.

## Builders added
- M.V. Electronics — Philippines; Shredhead family and limited-edition states.
- PedalPimps — Philippines; Nephilim Overdrive and the collaborative JCraft LTX Drive.
- Maxz Pedals — Thailand; MZ-4, Hi Gain Monster, Quicksand 2, Warning, White Pearl, Black Pearl and Khao Neow V2.
- KHP FX Audio — Da Nang, Vietnam; Ratten, Generator, Amnesia and Immoral Duality V3.

## Evidence / boundary notes
- M.V. Electronics evidence is primarily retail/community and historical rather than first-party. Shredhead is strongly anchored as a Philippines-made high-gain British-style distortion product with a documented history reaching back roughly a decade. The Gold and green Devastator variants remain edition/state records, not assumed new families.
- PedalPimps is treated as a designer/builder entity distinct from JCraft. The LTX Drive is explicitly documented by JCraft as a collaboration with PedalPimps, so authorship/manufacturing roles remain separate.
- Maxz's first-party about page identifies Maxz as a Thailand-based handmade pedal builder whose pedal work began after electrical-engineering studies. Japanese catalog indexing and Thai player documentation recover several distinct dirt products, including the period White Pearl and Black Pearl names. Khao Neow V2 is retained as a later state with its own documented control/power behavior.
- KHP FX Audio's first-party pages identify a fully handmade Da Nang operation founded in 2022 after an earlier repair/modification phase. Its current site explicitly surfaces Ratten Distortion, Generator Distortion, Amnesia Fuzz/Distortion and Immoral Duality V3.

## Photo-source layer
Every inventory record in this batch carries a visual/product source URL. These are source trails for the later image/specimen archive, not claims of image ownership or permission to republish the image itself.

## Research discipline
No schematics, PCB layouts, complete BOMs, gutshot libraries, cloning instructions or circuit-reconstruction material added. Limited editions, collaborations and modified/recreation relationships are kept distinct from independent original product families.
