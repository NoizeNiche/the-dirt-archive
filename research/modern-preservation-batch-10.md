## Research cautions
1. A 2023 roundup is evidence that a product existed in the 2023 market cycle, not necessarily proof of first introduction date.
2. Preserve product introduction, revision, generation and edition as separate fields.
3. Do not infer OEM/manufacturer identity from enclosure similarity.
4. Signature, collaboration and retailer editions require separate edition lineage when evidence supports it.
5. Do not promote a discovery lead to VERIFIED without product-level evidence.
