## Evidence notes
VZtec's current first-party site states that the company has focused on designing and developing technology for musicians since 2016 and is based in Buenos Aires. Its hardware catalog documents a dedicated Fuzz pedal and a dedicated Overdrive pedal. The Fuzz uses silicon transistors and includes Octafuzz, Face/Muff voice selection, a tilt-style tone control, buffered output and Bias control for open-to-gated textures. The Overdrive combines transparent and mid-forward drive voices, a stackable hard-clipping stage, and active treble/bass EQ.

## Boundary / variant notes
- VZtec also sells Dual Control, Delay and footswitch controllers; these remain outside the central dirt inventory.
- VZtec offers digital plugins that replicate its Fuzz and Overdrive hardware. These are retained conceptually as digital twins/related software products rather than duplicate pedal models.
