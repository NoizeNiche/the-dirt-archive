## Evidence notes
KGR HARMONY is unusually valuable as a craft-history record. First-party material identifies Keijiro Fukushima, handmade production in Iwate, joint Nanbu-tekki development with Oitomi beginning in 2019, and the ARARE release in 2020. Current Japanese and U.S. store material documents the continuing ARARE/MIZORE/SHIZUKU/KUROFUNE/IRON808 family. Preserve Nanbu-tekki enclosure provenance as part of the object history rather than treating the enclosure maker as a pedal builder.

Lemon & Ginger is explicitly a one-person Tokyo operation. The current store lists handmade dirt products including muzz, puzz, daidarabocchi and CENTAROU, while the historical store preserves Cashew Nuts FUZZ. The builder's Fuzzman (1st) page is especially useful for version policy because it states the first version was limited to 50 units and that future Fuzzman versions can return with changed appearance and specifications. That is ideal for model-generation tracking instead of variant inflation.

Studio Daydream has a substantial historical catalog. Current first-party material documents JRM-OD V5.0 as a Blackface-era concept overdrive, while older catalog reporting preserves Dhyana, KCM-OD Gold, KMC-OD Silver and LDM-OD states. Keep the historical versions attached to one builder lineage. HBOD Version 2 is retained only as a candidate because the strongest surviving object-level evidence found in this pass is secondary-market documentation.

Shigemori has strong repeated Japanese retailer/catalog evidence for its handmade identity and dirt products, including RUBY STONE, RUBY STONE PREMIUM, MUSE and G.O.T BASS DRIVE. The product family is recognizable through the machined high-purity aluminum enclosure and recurring model documentation. Because surviving direct historical builder pages are harder to surface, maintain a partial-primary provenance note and plan a dedicated first-party archive hunt later.

KarDiaN is a clean canonical addition. First-party material identifies the Shiga builder identity and current dirt catalog. The brand states it was born in 2017, and current product pages document Serotonin, Nitroglycerin, titania v2 and Vitamin C. Serotonin Origin S.T. also carries an explicit collaboration with Susumu Tamura, while Vitamin C preserves research ancestry from a vintage Guild Ram's Head Big Muff. The archive should store those relationships without collapsing the products into clones or OEM entries.

Petla was established in 2022 by composer/builder nanase. Retailer and official-store evidence document Noa, Ive and Rev as its core drive family. The archive should preserve Petla as a builder identity while keeping workshops, DIY kits and collaboration events as relationship/event records instead of separate manufacturer identities.

## Boundary / scope notes
- KGR SHIZUKU remains dirt-adjacent because it is primarily presented as a booster/gain-stage utility.
- Lemon & Ginger Fuzzman generations should be modeled as one lineage with versioned states where evidence supports continuity.
- Studio Daydream HBOD V2 remains a candidate pending stronger first-party chronology.
- Shigemori has strong product evidence but thinner surviving direct historical-source material, so chronology remains open.
- KarDiaN collaborations, signature editions and limited colorways are relationship or specimen data unless they constitute a repeatable conceptual model.
- Petla Noa, Ive and Rev remain separate named products, while DIY kit/collaboration appearances remain relationship records.
- No schematics, PCB layouts, gutshots, cloning instructions, or complete BOM data are published.
