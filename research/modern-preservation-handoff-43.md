## Duplicate-control rule
Batch 43 was constructed after checking the existing 2024 and 2025 census structure. Explicitly distinct model, edition, rehoused, V2, signature, retro and named-variant states are retained when the source treats them as separate products. The archive should continue enriching an existing parent record where a new source merely describes the same production state.

## Preservation boundary
The archive records product identity, historical relationships, production periods, variant states and externally useful identification clues. It does not publish schematics, PCB layouts, gutshot libraries, complete bills of materials or cloning instructions.
