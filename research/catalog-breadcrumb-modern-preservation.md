# Modern Preservation Handoff

## New operating priority
For the current research cycle, work **newest to oldest** so modern pedal variations are captured while manufacturer documentation, manuals, current product pages, revision notes, dated retailer material, high-quality photographs and firsthand owner knowledge remain abundant.

Preferred order:
- 2025–2026
- 2020–2024
- 2015–2019
- 2010–2014
- 2000–2009
- 1990s
- 1980s
- 1970s
- 1960s

This is a preservation track running in parallel with the long-term builder census. It does not invalidate the census or the historical breadth-first plan.

## Architectural lessons to preserve
- A revision number does not automatically mean a new circuit.
- Cosmetic editions, production revisions, hardware revisions and electronics revisions must remain separate axes.
- Record **revision date** separately from original product introduction date.
- Preserve limited/anniversary editions as editions linked to the parent product when appropriate.
- Modern documentation can prove physical identification markers that later secondary sources may lose.
- Keep marketed brand, manufacturer/OEM, designer and distributor separate.
- Build identification records around what a person can observe on a physical specimen, while recording what the evidence says about its underlying production identity.

## Current pickup point
Continue backward through the **2024–2025 modern/recent window**, prioritizing builders with explicit version histories, current/manual archives and documented enclosure or hardware revisions. Cross-check official manufacturer evidence against high-quality secondary sources, but do not promote repeated community claims into verified facts without evidence.

Next research emphasis: modern V2/V3/MKII revisions from established boutique manufacturers, followed by 2020–2024 generation maps for builders with substantial dirt catalogs.
