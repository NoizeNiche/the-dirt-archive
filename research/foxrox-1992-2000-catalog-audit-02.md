# Foxrox Electronics: 1992-2000 Historical Catalog Audit

## Scope
Builder-level chronology for Foxrox Electronics before its later 2001-era expansion. Focus is original product identity, dates, and production states, not circuit reconstruction.

## Builder identity
- **Entity:** Foxrox Electronics
- **Builder/designer:** Dave Fox
- **Region:** USA
- **Founding/product anchor:** 1992
- **Historical status:** ORIGINAL BUILDER / EARLY BOUTIQUE NODE

## ProVibe
Foxrox's own historical archive identifies the **ProVibe** as the first pedal made after the Foxrox Electronics name was registered. Dave Fox states that he first encountered a Uni-Vibe in 1987, later built and sold custom units, and in summer **1992** registered the Foxrox Electronics name after a distributor, Tom Lanik of North Star Audio, encouraged him to turn the work into a product.

Foxrox's current historical page states that approximately **40 large-box ProVibes** were made, in addition to rack-mount units. It lists the last manufacture date as **1994**.

## Other 1990s catalog implications
The Foxrox historical catalog currently emphasizes ProVibe as the first branded pedal. Later best-known products such as Captain Coconut belong to the subsequent product era and should not be retroactively placed in the 1992-1994 catalog without evidence.

## Archive significance
Foxrox is a useful bridge case because the brand begins with a small run of an original builder product before the company later develops a broader recognizable boutique catalog. The archive should therefore retain:
- 1992 = brand/product launch anchor.
- 1992-1994 = ProVibe early production corridor.
- Rack and floor versions = product states under one family, not separate builders.
- Later Foxrox products = separate chronological product layer.
