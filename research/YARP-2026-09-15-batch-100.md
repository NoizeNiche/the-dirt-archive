# YARP 2026-09-15 Batch 100: LRE Super Fuzz / Super Drive Catalog Reconciliation

## Scope
Audit Lafayette Radio Electronics (LRE) dirt products where surviving catalogs overlap Japanese OEM relationships.

## Findings
- Specialist historical research dates the first LRE-branded Super Fuzz to 1968 and identifies it as a Honey Baby Crying / FY-6 Super Fuzz family product.
- The 1968 LRE state is associated with a gray enclosure and oval label plate. This remains a marketed/import state rather than proof that Lafayette manufactured the pedal.
- LRE catalogs show a black Super Fuzz state around 1970 and orange/blue Super Fuzz marketing around 1971. These should be modeled as separate marketed production states pending physical specimen reconciliation.
- The 1971 LRE catalog also advertises a Super Drive, described by specialist research as LRE's version of the Univox Uni-Drive. No confirmed surviving LRE-branded specimen has yet been located in the cited research.
- A 1971 Lafayette catalog independently documents the Super-Drive as an imported, fully solid-state foot-pedal boost/clean-distortion product. The catalog does not identify a factory, so factory attribution remains unresolved.
- The archive keeps LRE as a retailer/import/private-label identity and preserves Univox, Shin-Ei, Honey, and other manufacturer relationships at model level.
