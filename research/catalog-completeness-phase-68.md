# Catalog Completeness Phase 68

## Discovery census

This pass adds 75 product-level discovery records across four builder/manufacturer catalogs:

- BJF Electronics (BJFE): 30 products.
- Guild: 15 products, including explicit OEM/manufacturing relationships.
- MJM Guitar Effects: 15 products.
- DMB Pedals: 15 products.

## BJFE

Effects Database currently indexes 30 BJFE products and dates the company foundation to July 1, 1999, with the first Baby Blue Overdrives delivered December 13, 2000. The catalog includes fuzz, overdrive, distortion, boost, compression, modulation, EQ and filter products. High-value follow-up targets include Bone Bender MkI/MkII, Dyna Red, Little Green Wonder, Honey Bee, Pine Green Compressor, Arctic White Fuzz, Pink Purple Fuzz, and the Model-series products. The BJFE/One Control/Mad Professor/Bearfoot relationships should be modeled as related designer/product lineages rather than flattened into one builder.

## Guild

Guild is especially important because the 15-product catalog contains multiple manufacturing relationships: Applied Audio, Aria, fOXX and Top Gear entries appear alongside Guild-branded products. Foxey Lady should be treated as a family with manufacturing/production phases rather than as a single undifferentiated model. The Timbre Generator is also historically important because Guild's abandoned synthesizer work connected directly to Mike Beigel and later Musitronics development.

## MJM Guitar Effects

The 15-product catalog is strongly vintage-oriented. Immediate variation targets are the 2-knob versus 3-knob Brit Bender, London Fuzz germanium versus London Fuzz II silicon, Foxey Fuzz, King Buzz, Roctavios and Dallas Boost. These should be researched for dates, enclosure changes, transistor-material distinctions where publicly documented, and relationships to the vintage products that inspired them.

## DMB Pedals

DMB Pedals was founded by Mike Blakemore and Daniel Minton in September 2007. Its documented timeline includes a modification shop, Stellar Drive, Volumizer, Cosmic Boost/Cosmic Crunch naming evolution, Loopy, Lunar Echo, Americana, Lexi, Foxy Pirate, Bumble Bass Fuzz, and Spankenstein Compressor. The catalog is useful preservation material for the 2007-2012 boutique wave and should be treated as a historical builder even where current production status is unclear.

## Evidence rule

These are discovery-level catalog records. Product-level dates, production counts, image rights, specimen evidence and version distinctions remain pending unless separately verified. No gutshots, schematics, PCB layouts or reconstruction instructions are being added.
