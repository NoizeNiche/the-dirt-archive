# Phase One Batch 36 Breadcrumb

Date: 2026-09-16

## Batch
Batch 36 expands Phase One builder-first inventory coverage into California, France, Chile and Turkey.

## Builders added
- BREÑA FX — Simi Valley, California; boutique vintage-fuzz / distortion / germanium-boost operation active since 2006. Current evidence includes several numbered, limited and prototype states.
- Teos Music — France; current boutique gain builder with a deliberately coherent modern overdrive/distortion catalog. British Drive, Gladiator and other families are preserved as functional product relationships rather than mode-level duplicates.
- Richón — Chile; Ricardo Guzmán's pedal operation documented from 2007. A 2015 profile says seven pedal products existed, but only Ketchup, Mustard and BBQ are securely recoverable from the accessible source, so the remaining catalog is left unresolved.
- Emma Dangers — Chile; independent hand-built guitar/bass pedal maker. Muff 70 is securely recoverable from the accessible catalog snapshot, while broader lifetime catalog enumeration remains open.
- Heartbreaker Audio — Turkey; current handmade Vintage Drive overdrive/boost with documented Class-A topology, asymmetric silicon/germanium clipping and JFET input.

## Evidence / boundary notes
- BREÑA FX current shop evidence exposes 11 fuzz products, 3 boosts, and 3 distortion/overdrive products. The batch uses a conservative subset for which product names are recoverable from current shop pages and product pages. Numbered states such as BP-3 #012 and Tone Slayer #001 are retained as catalog/state records, not automatically treated as separate families.
- Teos Music's current overdrive index lists at least 19 entries, with the visible first page providing 1987 Distortion, 45 Overdrive, Black Label Dual Overdrive, Black Pearl Dual Overdrive, British Drive, Centauride, Dirty Shire, Erebor, Gladiator and others. The builder's own comparison guide also establishes the Gladiator, British Drive, Erebor, Dirty Shire, Silver Monster MKII and T-Express gain hierarchy. This batch records those names without claiming the full lifetime catalog is complete.
- Richón is deliberately marked INDEXED_PARTIAL because the accessible 2015 source explicitly says seven pedal products existed but names only three. This is a catalog-gap record, not a reason to invent four missing names.
- Emma Dangers is likewise INDEXED_PARTIAL. The surviving current snapshot clearly identifies Muff 70 and the maker as a Chilean independent hand-built pedal operation, but the accessible catalog is too thin for a lifetime-complete list.
- Heartbreaker Audio is marked baseline mapped because the first-party page currently exposes one named pedal and detailed production/specification information.

## Next action
Continue builder-first Phase One coverage, especially in underrepresented regions and among micro-builders with recoverable catalogs. Keep partial catalogs visibly partial and return to lifetime catalog reconciliation when stronger primary or historical evidence becomes available.
