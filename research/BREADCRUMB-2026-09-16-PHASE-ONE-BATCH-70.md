## Evidence notes
Moollon is owned and operated by Young Joon Park in Seoul. Effects Database documents the company launch in 2003 after earlier prototype development, plus a catalog spanning fuzz, distortion, overdrive and boost. Retain historical product states even where current availability is unclear.

Blackberry JAM is documented by Kikutani as a Japanese effects brand making handmade guitar and bass effects, with a focus on vintage-oriented overdrive, fuzz and boosters. Effects Database provides object-level documentation for Lemongrass and describes its TS-derived concept with a dedicated boost stage. Keep the broader Blueberry/Raspberry naming trail pending deeper chronology verification.

Flying Teapot has strong first-party documentation. Its interview preserves the 088 Over Drive development philosophy and its three-mode Vintage/Fat/Bright control concept; current pages document the 080 Over Drive Pro, monolith fuzz, FOG BOX Silicon Fuzz and Gold Axe. Gold Axe is explicitly described by the builder as more than a simple clone, with multiple clipping choices and broader voicing.

Sobbat's first-party site identifies Kyoto as the handmade origin and states each effect is individually hand-built and inspected. The Drive Breaker and Fuzz Breaker families have extensive product-level documentation, including later S-series miniaturized revisions and limited reissues. Preserve these as connected generation/specimen states rather than unrelated products where the evidence supports continuity.

Katanasound / mt'Lab is a single documented builder lineage led by Mitsuteru Takaki. Effects Database dates mt'Lab to 1997 and Katanasound to 2003, and Takaki's interview explains the brand split: Katanasound became the standard-production identity while mt'Lab handled experimental and customized work. The archive therefore uses one lineage record with explicit brand/role relationships.

## Boundary / scope notes
- Blackberry JAM Blackcurrant remains a candidate until its exact product/category documentation is strengthened.
- Flying Teapot's Switch Audio sub-line remains a brand relationship under the same builder lineage, not a new manufacturer.
- Sobbat S-series and R-series units are generation/production-state evidence and should not be blindly counted as independent conceptual models.
- Katanasound and mt'Lab should remain separate public-facing names where object branding differs, but tied by a shared builder/relationship record.
- No schematics, PCB layouts, gutshots, cloning instructions, or complete BOM data are published.
