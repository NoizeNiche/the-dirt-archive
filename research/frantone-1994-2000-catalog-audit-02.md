# Frantone Electronics, 1994-2000 catalog audit

## Identity
Frantone Electronics was founded by Fran Blanche in Pennsylvania during the mid-1990s. Frantone's own history states the company has delivered effects since 1994 and that its pedals are designed and manufactured in the USA by Fran. The company website and interviews distinguish Frantone's original-effects identity from later revival/reissue states.

## Early dirt catalog
- Hep Cat: first commercial Frantone product; first units appeared in January 1996 according to Fran Blanche's Effects Database interview.
- Peachfuzz: introduced in 1997; a major early Frantone fuzz product.
- Cream Puff: later Frantone fuzz lineage, with revival activity documented separately from the 1990s founding era.
- Brooklyn Overdrive: distinct later overdrive lineage/reissue state; do not back-date the later 2017 limited reissue to the original 1990s production without separate evidence.

## Historical significance
Fran Blanche later worked at Electro-Harmonix and designed its 2000 Big Muff reissue after her Frantone period. This is a designer/industry relationship, not a reason to merge Frantone and Electro-Harmonix entities.

## Evidence disposition
Use 1994 as company-history anchor, 1995 as the period in which the first pedal was developed/launching, and January 1996 for Hep Cat production. Peachfuzz receives a 1997 anchor. Later Cream Puff and Peachfuzz revival products require separate production-state records.
