# YARP 2026-09-15 Batch 53: United Musical Industries (UMI)

## Scope
Builder-first catalog completion for United Musical Industries, while separating UMI's own products from Apollo, Conrad, Kimberly, and LIMMCO branded states.

## Builder identity
United Musical Industries (UMI) should be treated as a distinct late-1960s New York-area manufacturer/brand entity. Effects Database's UMI catalog lists four products: Buzz Tone & Volume Expander, Tone Booster, Treble-Bass Booster & Mute, and Wa Pedal. The surviving evidence does not justify merging UMI with Applied Audio Products or LIMMCO.

A surviving 1969 UMI Wa example carries a CTS pot dated week 18 of 1969, giving a useful production-era anchor. A 1973 Electronic Servicing reader request also names the UMI Buzz Tone and Volume Expander, confirming the product remained historically relevant in the period.

## Enumerated dirt catalog

### 1. Buzz Tone & Volume Expander
UMI's central dirt product. It belongs in the archive as fuzz plus volume-boost hybrid rather than as a simple one-function fuzz. The product is listed in the UMI manufacturer catalog, and surviving examples are commonly dated to the late 1960s. Contemporary surviving-unit evidence includes examples advertised as 1968 and 1967, but those seller dates should remain secondary dating rather than hard introduction claims.

The family relationship is unusually important: Effects Database links UMI's Buzz Tone & Volume Expander to the later Apollo No. 843 and Conrad Buzz Box & Volume Expander states, while also placing it in the wider Kimberly family that passes through LIMMCO and Applied production. These links are model-level manufacturing clues, not proof that every visually similar unit was made by the same factory at the same time.

### 2. Tone Booster
Listed in the UMI manufacturer catalog. Treat as a dirt-adjacent clean/treble boost product. Exact introduction and production span remain unresolved in this pass.

### 3. Treble-Bass Booster & Mute
Listed in the UMI manufacturer catalog. Treat as boost/mute boundary hardware and retain it for catalog completeness without inflating the archive's central fuzz count. Exact dating remains unresolved.

### 4. Wa Pedal
Listed in the UMI manufacturer catalog. This is a wah product rather than a central dirt pedal, but it is relevant because UMI's own history associates the Wa with the company's earliest product period. A surviving 1969 example with a week-18-1969 CTS pot provides a concrete dating anchor for the model family.

## OEM/private-label relationship map

### Apollo No. 843 Buzz Box & Volume Expander
Effects Database explicitly connects Apollo No. 843 to the UMI Buzz Tone family. Archive treatment: Apollo = marketed/private-label identity; UMI = manufacturing relationship.

### Conrad Buzz Box & Volume Expander
Effects Database explicitly connects the Conrad Buzz Box & Volume Expander to UMI. Keep this as a distinct Conrad-marketed state, not as a second UMI retail model.

### Kimberly later production
The Kimberly Fuzz Box and Volume Expander history is split in Effects Database into v1 by LIMMCO and v2 by Applied. The broader relationship graph also reaches UMI through the later Apollo and Conrad states. The exact Kimberly production handoff from Applied to UMI is therefore still unresolved and should not be presented as a continuous single-factory run without additional primary evidence.

## Historical significance
UMI is a particularly useful builder node because it sits inside the same New York-area fuzz manufacturing/distribution ecosystem as LIMMCO, Applied Audio Products, and retail/private-label companies. Rather than treating all of those as one lineage, the archive now has a cleaner separation:

**LIMMCO:** distributor/house-brand layer

**Applied Audio Products:** New Jersey physical-manufacturer/OEM layer

**UMI:** separate New York-area manufacturer/brand layer

**Apollo / Conrad / Kimberly:** marketed/private-label states whose manufacturing relationships changed over time

This separation should prevent future duplicate counting and false OEM certainty.

## Evidence state

**Evidence-qualified:** UMI exists as a distinct manufacturer/brand entity; four UMI catalog products are enumerated; Buzz Tone & Volume Expander is a late-1960s core dirt product; UMI Wa Pedal is supported by a 1969 surviving specimen; Apollo No. 843 and Conrad Buzz Box & Volume Expander belong to the UMI relationship graph.

**Unresolved:** exact UMI incorporation/start date; complete address chronology; exact start/end dates for Tone Booster and Treble-Bass Booster & Mute; exact Kimberly-to-UMI handoff chronology; complete catalog of any additional UMI dirt-adjacent products not represented in the current Effects Database index.
