# Aria / Japanese OEM Transition Audit 02

## Scope
Follow-up to the Aria catalog work, focused on the 1980s-to-1990s transition where Aria-branded products were also distributed under other labels.

## Firm product anchors
Effects Database dates the Aria XX-10 Super / Dual Stage series to 1983-85 and identifies DT-10 Distortion and OD-10 Overdrive within that platform.

Effects Database also documents the Aria DT-5 Distortion and MP-5 Metal Pedal in the 1985 XX-5 old series. The series used a shared mechanical platform, making product-family identification useful when identifying surviving examples.

The late-1980s/early-1990s TDR-5 Tube Driver family is documented under Aria's Series 10 branding and under Axtron, Columbus, Vision and Profile+ labels. Effects Database groups these as the same product family while keeping the distributor/brand names distinct.

The AFZ-1 fuzz also appears under the Pick Boy MFZ-7 label, documented by Effects Database as an OEM/rebrand relationship. Preserve the Aria AFZ-1 as the product-family anchor and Pick Boy as the marketed OEM label.

## Archive disposition
- Aria: canonical Japanese marketed manufacturer/brand entity.
- Aria XX-10: historical product platform, 1983-85.
- Aria XX-5: historical product platform, 1985.
- Aria Series 10 TDR-5: late-1980s/early-1990s family.
- Axtron / Columbus / Vision / Profile+: marketed labels attached to the TDR-5 family.
- Pick Boy MFZ-7: marketed OEM version of Aria AFZ-1.

## Caution
Do not infer that every Aria-era pedal came from one physical factory. The available evidence is stronger for product-family and branding relationships than for a universal factory assignment.
