# YARP 2026-09-15 Batch 96: Kadent Treblefuzz / Applied Pre-1968 Candidate

## Scope
Relationship record for the rare Kadent Treblefuzz in the Applied Audio Products manufacturing lineage.

## Findings
- Tone Machines specialist research identifies the Kadent Treblefuzz as clearly related to the later Applied Fuzz and Treble Booster family while being physically and functionally distinct.
- The surviving Kadent example is a two-control variant with a hard-wired output cable and front-panel input, features that may indicate an earlier production state.
- The same research notes that other Kadent examples exist without the power-switch function in the fuzz control, so a single linear chronology should not be assumed.
- The relationship to Applied Audio Products is strong at the manufacturing-family level, but the exact introduction date of Kadent remains unresolved.
- The Goya Panther Box Fuzz-Tone and Treble Boost, documented in spring 1967, is a useful dated comparison point, but similarity alone does not prove that Kadent predates it.
