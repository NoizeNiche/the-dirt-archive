# Automation Run 07 — Builder Catalog Research

Date: 2026-09-14

## Scope
This pass targets builders not found in the repository's searchable catalog, prioritizing boutique, one-person, historical, and complete-lineup candidates. Product lists are catalog-discovery records, not claims that every model had identical production runs.

## Dominion Electric Effects Co.
- Builder: Dominion Electric Effects Co.
- Builder/operator: Aaron Sabourin; Canada.
- Character: boutique, hand-assembled, fuzz/gain focused.
- Catalog candidates: The Faceless Fuzz; Firebox Treble Booster; Twin Step Fuzz; High Note Octave Fuzz; The Marksman Fuzz; The Knoz Machine Fuzz.
- High-level component distinction: Faceless family is documented in germanium, silicon, and hybrid Ge/Si forms; NOS germanium/silicon selection is part of the builder's stated design character.
- Historical/product research leads: verify release dates and individual production variants before assigning years; preserve Faceless variants as related records rather than collapsing them.
- Image/rights lead: builder/product imagery found in the Equipboard catalog; treat as discovery leads only and seek permission/original builder images before republication.
- Source: https://equipboard.com/brands/dominion-electric-effects-co

## Pine-Box Customs
- Builder: Pine-Box Customs / Pine Box Customs.
- Builder/operator: Jordan Clayton; Memphis, Tennessee.
- Character: boutique, handmade, experimental dirt/noise.
- Catalog candidates: Ahab Overdrive; Tall; Deep; Wide; Far; Sirens v.2; Free Shit; Bloodline (Farm Pedals collaboration); Fever Dream (Mask Audio Electronics collaboration); plus historically cataloged fuzz/tremolo/multi-effect releases requiring model-level verification.
- Catalog signal: Equipboard currently lists 12 products and identifies seven fuzz, two tremolo, one chorus, one multi-effect and one overdrive category entries.
- Relationship note: preserve collaboration relationships for Bloodline and Fever Dream rather than assigning them solely to Pine-Box Customs.
- Image/rights lead: Reverb shop and boutique retailer imagery are discovery leads; do not assume reuse rights.
- Source: https://equipboard.com/brands/pine-box-customs

## Chicago Stompworks
- Builder: Chicago Stompworks / Chicago Stomp Works.
- Location: Chicago, Illinois.
- Character: boutique vintage-circuit recreation builder; full-size handbuilt units and smaller machine-assembled formats.
- Catalog candidates: The Green Thing; Black Fudge; Fat Bald Guy; Mister Vermin; Ram's Head Big Muff Clone; Squeezey Compressor; Angry Bear; Octave Up Harmonizer; TS-808; Mööse Pie; The Pyramid Principle; The Pained Guy; Purple Bear; Angry Green Bear; Legitimate Beef; The Otterdrive.
- High-level circuit lineage leads: Mister Vermin is described as a ProCo RAT recreation; Legitimate Beef as a Way Huge Red Llama recreation; Black Fudge as a Maestro MFZ-1 recreation; Gray Speck is separately described as a DOD OD250 recreation and should be added in the next verification pass.
- Construction distinction: source describes through-hole/true-bypass/hand-wired construction for full-size units versus SMD construction in smaller formats. Preserve this as a format-level distinction, not a universal claim for every historical production run.
- Image/rights lead: Equipboard and current retail listings are discovery sources only.
- Source: https://equipboard.com/brands/chicago-stompworks
