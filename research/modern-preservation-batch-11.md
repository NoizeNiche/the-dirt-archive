## Census observations

The 2023 discovery layer continues to show that the modern dirt ecosystem contains three overlapping classes that must not be conflated:
1. genuinely new product designs;
2. revisions of established products;
3. limited/color/showcase editions of established products.

A useful example is the Death By Audio showcase material: a different finish or event-specific presentation does not by itself constitute a new circuit generation. The Archive should preserve the edition relationship instead.
