# The Dirt Archive · Breadcrumb · Batch 102

## Completed

Phase One Batch 102 added the Chocolate Electronics builder lineage after confirming first-party historical and current catalog documentation.

## Builder

- Chocolate Electronics is treated as a canonical Japanese handmade effects builder lineage.
- The official shop states the business began in 2013 and specializes in handmade electric-guitar effects pedals made in Japan.
- Guitar Magazine's 2026 builder profile identifies Tetsuro Horino as the founder and documents the workshop in Chiba.

## Pedals

Central dirt records:

- Chocolate Drive
- Chocolate Crunch
- Melty Overdrive
- Fuzzy Overdrive

Chocolate Booster remains relationship-only because its primary documented function is boost/pre-preamp rather than dedicated dirt.

## Photos

First-party Chocolate Electronics product pages provide exterior product imagery for the current dirt lineup and Chocolate Drive. These are retained as primary photo-source leads for eventual credit/licensing review.

## Information on the pedal

- Chocolate Drive is an earlier signature model whose official description emphasizes punchy mids, thick low end and internal voltage boosting from 9V to approximately 17V.
- Chocolate Crunch is a 2026 overdrive with Rich/Crispy character switching and selectable mid/bottom voicing.
- Melty Overdrive is a 2026 discrete drive design using MOSFET and bipolar-transistor gain stages, voiced toward warm, glossy overdrive.
- Fuzzy Overdrive grew from development work aimed at combining upper/lower-octave fuzz ideas and is documented as a normal/upper-octave dirt pedal ranging from grainy overdrive to heavy fuzz.

## Normalization decisions

- Chocolate Booster is excluded from central dirt because its documented identity is a booster/pre-preamp.
- Current 2026 Chocolate Crunch, Melty Overdrive and Fuzzy Overdrive are treated as distinct conceptual dirt models because the builder presents them as separate products with different circuit/voicing concepts.
- No schematics, PCB layouts, gutshots or complete component inventories are collected.
