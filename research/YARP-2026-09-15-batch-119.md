# YARP 2026-09-15 Batch 119: Vanished British / Boutique Fuzz Leads

## Purpose
Capture identifiable and semi-identifiable small-scale British fuzz activity that sits between private builders, retailers, and conventional manufacturers. These records must not be promoted to canonical factories without stronger evidence.

## Clifford Musical Supplies, Yorkshire
- Advertised fuzz boxes in *Banjo Mandolin Guitar* magazine in August and September 1966.
- The shop was based in Yorkshire and therefore sits in the same regional ecosystem as John Hornby Skewes / Zonk, but the advertisements do not establish that Clifford manufactured the products.
- Possible states: stocked major-brand product, contracted small builder, or shop-supplied house product.
- **Identity status: RETAILER / FACTORY UNRESOLVED.**

## Essex unnamed fuzz-box seller
- *Melody Maker*, 9 April 1966, advertised fuzz boxes in Essex with minimal specification: two jack sockets and a footswitch.
- No maker name has yet been tied to the surviving documentary lead.
- **Identity status: UNRESOLVED MICRO-BUILDER LEAD.**

## West Sussex unnamed fuzz seller / maker
- *Melody Maker*, 11 November 1968, carried an advertisement for new fuzz units from West Sussex.
- The maker/model name is not currently recoverable with sufficient confidence from the evidence reviewed.
- **Identity status: UNRESOLVED MICRO-BUILDER LEAD.**

## Anglo fuzz
- A surviving 1960s fuzz box described as “Anglo” is reported by its original owner to have been purchased from a small advertisement in the back of a music magazine.
- Exact publication, maker, production date, and relationship to known British builders remain unknown.
- **Identity status: MARKETED BRAND / FACTORY UNRESOLVED.**

## Interpretation
These leads demonstrate that the British 1965-1968 fuzz market included a substantial classifieds / small-advertisement layer outside the better-known Sola Sound, Arbiter, Vox, WEM, Burns, Selmer, and JHS networks. Absence of a surviving product or factory record must not be interpreted as proof that the seller did not manufacture the device.
