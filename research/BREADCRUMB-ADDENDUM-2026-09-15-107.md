# YARP Breadcrumb 107

## Purpose
Catalog-enrichment pass focused on existing Japanese, European and American-era builder records, with explicit duplicate suppression.

## Cross-reference / dedupe decisions

- **Pearl Musical Instrument Co.** already exists in the canonical census. Added CF-1 and the Sound Spice OD-05 / DS-06 family as catalog enrichment only.
- **Firstman / Hillwood** already exists as a single canonical manufacturer lineage. Multivox Big Jam is retained as a marketed/distribution/OEM relationship, not a second builder.
- **Caldironi Musica** already exists. G.I.S., Goldsound, Cosmosound, Meazzi and related labels remain marketed identities within the Caldironi family until stronger factory-separation evidence appears.
- **VEB Musikelektronik Klingenthal** already exists. Böhm and plain Verzerrer/VEB-labeled specimens are tracked as branding/specimen states, not duplicate manufacturers.
- **Arion** already exists. Japanese and Sri Lankan production are treated as a production-origin axis, not separate builders, because model-level cutoff evidence is incomplete.

## Evidence notes

Pearl's 1970s CF-1 is documented by TC Gakki; the later Sound Spice catalog includes OD-05 and DS-06. Official Pearl catalog archives also document extensive period catalogs, supporting future chronological enumeration. Arion's official lineup confirms a substantial dirt catalog, while Effects Database records the Japan-to-Sri-Lanka manufacturing corridor. Firstman's/Hillwood's Big Jam relationship is independently documented for Multivox/Nihon-Hammond distribution. Caldironi's multi-label distortion/wah family is corroborated by Effects Database and ToneHome. VEB Klingenthal's alternate Böhm/Verzerrer labeling is preserved as specimen history.
