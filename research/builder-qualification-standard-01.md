# Builder Qualification Standard 01

## Purpose

The Dirt Archive is builder-first. Before a company, workshop, person, or historical identity is promoted as a dirt-device builder, the archive must first establish that the entity actually produced at least one relevant dirt device.

## Dirt qualification gate

A builder candidate qualifies when reliable evidence documents at least one of the following as a product the entity made, built, manufactured, hand-built, or produced:

- fuzz
- distortion
- overdrive
- dirt-oriented booster / boost device when it is clearly functioning as a gain/distortion product

A source that only says an entity was an importer, distributor, retailer, licensee, designer, or owner does not by itself satisfy the builder gate.

## Evidence hierarchy

1. Period catalog, advertisement, manual, trade publication, or surviving company documentation naming the maker and dirt product.
2. Builder/company history or first-person testimony that explicitly identifies the dirt product as made by the entity.
3. Strong specialist historical reference that directly attributes the dirt product to the entity.
4. Collector/database evidence used as supporting evidence when stronger documentation is unavailable.

## Editorial treatment

Passing the dirt gate does not automatically settle every relationship. OEM manufacture, designer credit, distributor identity, marketed badge, license, collaboration, successor company, and revival/reissue history remain separate research fields and are intentionally deferred until the builder identity itself is established.

A candidate can therefore be recorded as a verified dirt maker while its relationship graph remains unresolved.

## Exclusions

Do not promote a name to the builder census merely because it:

- appears on a pedal faceplate;
- imported or distributed a pedal made elsewhere;
- is a retailer or shop label;
- is a related company in an unresolved OEM family;
- is associated with a designer who made dirt devices;
- made only non-dirt effects such as wah, tremolo, delay, reverb, or accessories.

## Current research rule

Builder discovery comes first. Once a builder passes the dirt gate, later passes can inventory the builder's dirt catalog and map relationships. This keeps the archive from spending early research time on factory archaeology before the underlying builder census is complete.
