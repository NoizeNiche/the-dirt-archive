## Evidence notes
Adrian Gutierrez R. posted to the Effects Database builder forum on December 27, 2025 that he had started his analog effects pedal project in 2020 in the Dominican Republic. He named Mid Booster, OD85 Full Range Overdrive, Cuervo Muerto Fuzz Bender, FZR912 Hybrid Muff Fuzz Deluxe, and DS2610 Vintage Distortion among his releases. Effects Database subsequently added AGR Pedals as a new brand in its January 5, 2026 weekly update and documented four models: Cuervo Muerto, DS2610, FZR912 and OD85.

## Catalog discipline
Mid Booster is retained because it is explicitly identified by the builder as a released effect, even though a dedicated Effects Database product page was not located in this pass. The other four models have independent Effects Database product records. No speculative additional projects are promoted into the central inventory.

## Photo workflow

## Scope discipline
AGR's stated work includes overdrive, fuzz, Velcro fuzz and bass-fuzz experimentation. Only the five specifically released products named by the builder or independently catalogued here are included. No schematics, PCB layouts, gutshots, cloning instructions or complete BOM data are published.
