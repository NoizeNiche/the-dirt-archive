# YARP Batch 03 — Major American / British Catalog Expansion

Research date: 2026-09-14

## Purpose

Continue the builder-first catalog-completion program without promoting incomplete builders to lifetime-complete status. This batch adds structured catalog ledgers for Fulltone, Wampler, ZVEX Effects, EarthQuaker Devices, JHS Pedals, and Sola Sound / Colorsound.

## Research disposition

### Fulltone
Current and historical dirt families were enumerated from the present Fulltone catalog and manuals. OCD, Full-Drive, PlimSoul, 69, 70, Soul-Bender, Octafuzz, Ultimate Octave and other dirt/gain products are represented. Current Custom Shop and revision names are retained as separate product-state records where they are explicitly surfaced by the manufacturer. Boost products such as 2B remain scope boundaries.

### Wampler
The historical/manual layer and current manufacturer catalog were cross-indexed for Euphoria, Pinnacle, Plextortion, Plexi-Drive, Dracarys, SLOstortion, Triple Wreck, Sovereign, Clarksdale, Belle, Moxie, Pantheon, Tumnus, Ratsbane, Velvet Fuzz, Cryptid Fuzz, Fuzztration and Leviathan Fuzz. Wampler remains a catalog-completion target because the manufacturer has a larger historical product universe than the current storefront alone exposes.

### ZVEX Effects
Current manufacturer product pages were used to map Fuzz Factory family states, Woolly Mammoth, Mastotron, Box of Rock, Distortron, Double Rock, Box of Metal, Machine, Super Duper, Fuzz Probe and related products. Finish/enclosure/product-state distinctions are retained for later specimen work. The ZVEX site explicitly describes the builder as active since 1995 and distinguishes Vexter, hand-painted and custom forms.

### EarthQuaker Devices
Current official device, fuzz and overdrive/distortion pages were used to map Acapulco Gold, Plumes, Blumes, Special Cranker, Zoar, Westwood, Palisades, Talons, Hoof, Hizumitas, Barrows, Bellows, Fuzz Master General, Octave Fuzz Blaster, Gary and Life Pedal family states. Legacy/reissue products remain identifiable separately from current releases.

### JHS Pedals
Current official catalog pages were used to map Morning Glory, Bonsai, Angry Charlie, Charlie Brown, Kilt, PackRat, Muffuletta, Cheese Ball, Violet, 3 Series dirt models, Hard Drive, Colour Box, Crayon, Coyote, Double Dragon, Moonshine and PG-14. Collaboration products remain relationship records rather than duplicate builders. Newly released 2026 models are dated to the current catalog snapshot and are not treated as historical evidence beyond their existence.

### Sola Sound / Colorsound
A historical British catalog layer was added around Tone Bender Mk I, Mk 1.5, Professional Mk II, Mk III, Mk IV, Tone-Bender Fuzz, Supa Tone Bender, Jumbo Tone Bender, Power Boost and Overdriver. The ledger deliberately separates model names from physical production states and preserves marketed names such as Vox, Rotosound, Park and Marshall as relationship nodes elsewhere in the archive. The Power Boost/Overdriver transition remains a key identification corridor.

## Evidence rule

Manufacturer storefronts and manuals establish existence and current marketed identity. Historical secondary sources are retained as evidence trails for older production corridors, but they do not automatically promote a record to verified historical state. The archive continues to avoid schematics, PCB layouts, complete BOMs, gutshot libraries, cloning instructions and circuit-reconstruction material.
