## Information on the pedal

- Fujiyama Drive American and British are explicitly differentiated by the builder as American- and British-voiced discrete drive variants.
- Fujiyama C is the bass version of the family.
- Stone is documented as a higher-gain Fujiyama-family drive using vintage transistors.
- Fab Drive is a long-running HSW flagship with Pre Gain and Real Tube switching.
- Roza is derived from the Fab Drive family and adds a four-control drive format.
- Dragon, Lenny and Memphis are documented as named members of the FAB family, while exact chronology remains under reconciliation.

## Normalization decisions

- American Fujiyama naming is normalized to the first-party family title Fujiyama Drive American; duplicate Effects Database naming is not counted separately.
- Drive-F is kept once as the named production model; later duplicated references are not separate models.
- Breaker remains relationship-only because surviving evidence is secondary and identifies it as a Fujiyama-derived variant.
- DHARMA remains relationship-only because it is explicitly a limited FAB DRIVE-based anniversary modification.
- Boosters and delays remain outside central dirt.
- No schematics, PCB layouts, gutshots or complete component inventories are collected.
