# MXR Original Dirt Catalog Audit 04

## Scope
This pass focuses on the pre-Dunlop MXR Innovations period, especially the Reference, Commande, and Series 2000 eras. It does not merge later Dunlop MXR products into the original-company catalog merely because a model name or number was reused.

## Verified or strongly indexed dirt products

### M-104 Distortion+
The original MXR Distortion+ belongs to the foundational Reference Series dirt catalog. Contemporary and later historical sources place the product in the 1970s, with early script-logo and later block-logo production states. Jim Dunlop's own MXR history distinguishes the later resurrection of the Distortion+ from the original MXR company. The archive therefore treats original M-104 production and later Dunlop-era Distortion+ revival as separate production entities/states.

### M-103 Blue Box
The Blue Box belongs to the original MXR Reference Series and combines fuzz/distortion with octave effect. It should remain a core vintage MXR dirt record rather than being treated as a generic modern reissue lineage.

### M-142 Distortion II
The M-142 is a distinct original MXR distortion product, not an alias for Distortion+. Historical catalogs and period advertising identify it as a four-control distortion product with Drive, Resonance, Filter, and Output. A November 1981 trade advertisement describes Distortion II as a new MXR product and gives period wording for its harmonic-distortion positioning. Effects Database independently indexes M-142 as a separate MXR model. Keep this as a dedicated model/family record.

### M-164 Commande Overdrive
The M-164 is a distinct Commande Series overdrive introduced around 1981 and produced through the original MXR company's 1984 closure. Period and surviving-product documentation identify it as the Commande Overdrive. It must not be collapsed into M-104 Distortion+, even though contemporary descriptions place it in the same broad gain ecosystem. The Commande series used a different enclosure/product family from the Reference Series.

### M-204 Distortion+
The Series 2000 M-204 Distortion+ is a separate historical product state from the Reference-Series M-104. Surviving product documentation places M-204 production in the 1982-1984 corridor. The Series 2000 was a complete redesign of the earlier Reference and Commande product lines, so the archive should model M-204 as a separate generation/state rather than simply another enclosure variant of M-104.

## Important model-number and chronology corrections

MXR's original numbering is not a single continuous modern-style SKU system. The historical company used Reference/early numbering, Commande-series numbering, and Series 2000 numbering. Therefore:

- M-104 Distortion+ and M-204 Distortion+ are distinct original-company products/states.
- M-142 Distortion II is a separate product from both Distortion+ generations.
- M-164 Commande Overdrive is a separate Commande product.
- A later Dunlop MXR model number should not be back-projected into the original 1970s-1984 company without evidence.

## Evidence hierarchy

Primary/period evidence is strongest for M-142 and M-204 because period advertising and surviving catalog material place them directly in the original company's early-1980s product environment. Reverb product records can help anchor surviving specimen dating, but are retained as secondary evidence. Jim Dunlop's historical page is used for ownership/revival chronology, not as proof that every later MXR product was manufactured by the original MXR company.

## Research boundary
This file records product-level historical identity, production corridors, family relationships, and evidence trails only. It intentionally excludes schematics, PCB layouts, BOMs, gutshots, cloning instructions, and circuit reconstruction.
