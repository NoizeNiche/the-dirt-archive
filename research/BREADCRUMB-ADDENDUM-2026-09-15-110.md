# YARP Breadcrumb 110

## Purpose
West German OEM/marketed-identity archaeology with a linked UK sustainer corridor. Strict duplicate suppression was applied before promotion.

## Cross-reference / dedupe decisions

- **Schaller** remains the physical West German manufacturer lineage. Kent 6400/6406, Höfner, Blackfield and Van Hall are retained as separate marketed/product identities rather than duplicate factories.
- **Kent 6400 vs Kent 6406** are separate product states within the Kent/Schaller relationship. The archive does not collapse them into one model merely because they share a marketing corridor.
- **Dharma Sound** is preserved as a historical marketed identity, not a confirmed independent factory. Period advertising establishes a small product family, while current evidence points toward the Sola Sound/Colorsound ecosystem.
- **Futuristic Sounds** and **Orange** are retained as research-only marketed identities for the late-1970s sustainer corridor. No independent factory is asserted.
- **Van Hall** is treated as a Schaller-associated marketed/OEM identity, not a second manufacturer.
