## Evidence notes
Apollo Approved is documented as an Indonesian boutique maker associated with builder Agus. Current secondary catalog coverage identifies Skyscraper, Buckartie-Boo, Buckartie-Boo V3, Thurston Drive and Sawdust, while separate product and used-market records establish Anchor Jubilee, Unicorn and Steam Roller. Steam Roller has especially useful historical evidence: 2023 specimens are documented with OC140 germanium transistors, while a 2025 V2 silicon specimen establishes a later generation state. Buckartie-Boo likewise has documented V1 and later variants. Preserve these as generation/specimen clues for Protocol B.

XFX Electronics' current Malaysian storefront identifies the brand as Made in Malaysia and lists Tikus V2 distortion and Nine O Nine V2 overdrive as analog, pre-order / made-to-order products.

## Boundary / scope notes
- Apollo Approved product names with classic-circuit inspirations remain separate commercial products in the archive; circuit inspiration alone does not make them OEM duplicates.
- Buckartie-Boo V3 is retained as a named catalog state but should be reconciled against V1 and any V2 evidence before Protocol B assigns formal generation semantics.
- Steam Roller germanium and silicon versions should become generation/specimen distinctions rather than separate builders.
- AMTECH-HANDWIRED was reviewed but not promoted in this batch because the currently available evidence emphasizes custom builds, clones and broad custom work rather than a sufficiently stable original named dirt catalog.
- Digital products, controllers, repairs and non-dirt utility hardware remain outside the central dirt inventory.
