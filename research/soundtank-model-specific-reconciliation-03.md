# SoundTank Model-Specific Reconciliation 03

## Purpose
Keep SoundTank as an umbrella marketed family while preventing blanket manufacturer inheritance from earlier Maxon/Ibanez relationships.

## Current model-level treatment

### SF5 60's Fuzz
Retain as a distinct SoundTank/5-Series retail model. The 1989 SoundTank catalog is a period anchor. Do not infer Maxon manufacture solely from the broader Ibanez/Maxon relationship.

### FZ5 Fuzz
Retain as a distinct later 5-Series retail model linked to SF5 at family/model level. Exact generation boundary remains a separate chronology task.

### CR5 Crunchy Rhythm / TM5 Thrashmetal
Retain as distinct dirt products from the same SoundTank market family. No blanket factory field should be inherited from TS/OD lineage.

### TS5 Tube Screamer
Treat as a special attribution case. Existing specialist evidence points toward non-Maxon manufacture for at least this model-era corridor, while Maxon's corporate history makes broad SoundTank manufacturing claims. Public data should therefore say manufacturer attribution is model-specific/contested rather than choose a universal factory.

## Public-data rule
For the runtime layer, the SoundTank parent family can be displayed safely. Factory attribution should only appear where model-specific evidence is stronger than the umbrella-brand relationship.

## Evidence trail
- Ibanez official catalog archive lists a 1989 SoundTank catalog.
- Maxon's current company history describes broad Ibanez manufacturing involvement.
- Specialist historical sources dispute blanket SoundTank/Maxon attribution and identify Taiwan/Daphon as a candidate for TS5.

## Editorial boundary
No circuit reconstruction, schematics, PCB material, or cloning guidance is included. This record exists only to preserve historical identity and evidence state.
