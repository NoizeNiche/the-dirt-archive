# YARP 2026-09-15 Batch 23

## Focus
British marketed/OEM products in the 1970s, with emphasis on Charles Summerfield Limited (CSL) and the Sola Sound Tone Bender MKIII/MKIV product network.

## Findings

### Charles Summerfield Limited (CSL)
A 1977 Beat Instrumental effects survey identifies British-made CSL effects including Wah-Fuzz, Super Fuzz, and Power Boost. Specialist historical research identifies the CSL Super Fuzz as a relabeled Sola Sound Tone Bender MKIII family product, with other CSL products likewise corresponding to Sola Sound-made products.

CSL is therefore retained as a **marketed-brand/distributor relationship**, not automatically as a physical manufacturer. This matters because catalog searches can otherwise create a false additional British manufacturer count.

### Sola Sound MKIII/MKIV corridor
The historical relationship now gets an explicit bridge:
- Sola Sound Tone Bender MKIII: core three-transistor British fuzz family.
- Park Fuzz Sound: Sola Sound-made marketed state.
- Carlsbro Fuzz: related Sola Sound-made marketed state.
- CSL Super Fuzz: later marketed state of the Sola Sound family.
- Sola Sound Tone Bender MKIV / Tone-Bender Fuzz: smaller enclosure and later graphic/product state.

The archive should keep these retail identities searchable while using Sola Sound as the physical-manufacturer lineage where evidence supports it.

### Scope boundary
CSL Wah-Wah and later effects are not inflated into the central dirt count. CSL Super Fuzz and Wah-Fuzz remain relevant because they materially document the migration of the Sola Sound dirt catalog into other British retail channels.

## Evidence caution
The surviving historical record supports the manufacturer relationship more strongly than it supports exact launch dates for each retail state. Dates are therefore stored as corridors unless a period catalog or dated surviving specimen supplies a tighter anchor.

## Research boundary
No schematics, PCB layouts, BOMs, gutshots, cloning instructions or circuit reconstruction material added.
