## Historical findings

Confirmed high-confidence period-qualified manufacturer relationships for:
- Rotosound Fuzz Box: Sola Sound supplied production, followed by a separate Jennings/JEI-made period beginning in November 1968.
- Marshall SupaFuzz: Sola Sound supplied early production, with Marshall taking over production in 1968 and continuing into the 1970s.
- John Hornby Skewes Zonk II: early Sola Sound-supplied production, followed by later Wilsic Sound production; an intermediate 1969 alternate-builder phase remains unresolved.
- WEM Rush Pep Box: early Rush/WEM partnership history followed by WEM production, with the later long-red enclosure marking the WEM-built phase.

These are research-layer findings only. No new OEM-switch relationships were promoted into `public/lineage.json` during this batch.

## Why this matters

The archive now has a reusable pattern for documenting manufacturer switches by production period rather than assigning a single manufacturer to an entire branded model. This is especially important for vintage pedals whose branding remained constant while the physical builder changed.
