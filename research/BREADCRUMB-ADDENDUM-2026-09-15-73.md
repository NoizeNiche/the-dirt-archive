# Breadcrumb Addendum 73

Date: 2026-09-15

## Batch
UMI / United Musical Industries dirt catalog and OEM audit.

## Added research
- UMI Buzz Tone & Volume Expander: high-confidence U.S. physical-manufacturer record, with late-1960s to early-1970s period scope and 1968 surviving-example anchor.
- UMI Tone Booster: high-confidence U.S. physical-manufacturer record, with a surviving example explicitly dated 1968 and identified as made in USA.
- UMI Treble-Bass Booster & Mute: medium-confidence UMI physical-manufacturer record from period-focused historical documentation; exact production dates remain approximate.
- UMI Wa Pedal: high-confidence U.S. physical-manufacturer record, with a surviving example carrying an original CTS pot dated week 18 of 1969.
- Apollo No. 843 Buzz Box & Volume Expander: high-confidence UMI OEM / marketed-label state.
- Conrad Buzz Box & Volume Expander: high-confidence UMI-related marketed-label state, kept distinct from the UMI-branded product identity.
- Kimberly Buzz Box and Volume Expander: multi-stage factory transition preserved as LIMMCO v1 -> Applied Audio v2 -> later UMI state. UMI is not back-projected onto the original Kimberly production.
- UMI Tone Booster related branded variants: held for review because historical documentation confirms different branded versions exist but does not identify the physical manufacturer.

## Editorial decisions
- UMI is now treated as a distinct New York-area physical manufacturer, not a synonym for Applied Audio or LIMMCO.
- Shared topology, enclosure family, or component resemblance does not create an OEM edge by itself.
- Kimberly remains a period-specific multi-builder record. No single-factory simplification.
- Apollo and Conrad remain marketed identities under the UMI production branch rather than separate factories.

## Public promotion
No public lineage edges promoted in this batch. Public lineage remains unchanged.
