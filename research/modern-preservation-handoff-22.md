## Important research notes
- ATOM is a standalone Browne product whose green-side lineage connects to Protein. Browne explicitly says its starting point is the ODR-1 family but that ATOM is not an ODR-1 clone. Keep standalone product identity intact.
- Gritador is Browne's June 2023 Tube Screamer-family reinterpretation, with builder-documented midrange, clipping and low-end changes. Treat the 2023 announcement as the introduction anchor rather than assuming an undocumented generation.
- Lollar Overdrive was a 100-unit 2023 collaboration with KGR Harmony Effects, using a cast-iron enclosure and three-band EQ. Preserve Lollar as the marketed brand and KGR as collaboration/manufacturing participant until stronger attribution evidence appears.
- Nemetone Distiny is currently a launch-coverage record. It is Canadian hand-built and has distinct Soft/Hard OD-distortion modes, but primary builder documentation still needs enrichment.
- Colosseum is a distinct Cornerstone dual-overdrive product. Its explicit relationships to Blues Breaker and Klon-style voices should become typed lineage/product-family links, not a collapsed identity.
- White Light Legacy Reissue is a 2023 legacy reissue/edition. Do not treat the reissue date as the original White Light introduction or as a new generation by default.

## Data rule reinforced
Every promoted record needs both a research-layer entry and a catalog-layer connection. Record what changed, when it changed, how a collector can recognize it, which source proves the distinction, and what remains unresolved. Product with no documented revision still gets a variant-state evaluation.
