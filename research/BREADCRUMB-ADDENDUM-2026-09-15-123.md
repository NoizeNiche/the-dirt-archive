# YARP Breadcrumb 123

## Purpose
Japanese early-fuzz discovery pass focused on tiny manufacturers/brands that sit outside the better-known Ace Tone, Shin-Ei and Nisshin Onpa factory histories.

## New research record

- `BUILDER-120`: Yack, preserved as a historical Japanese builder/brand lineage with multiple distinct fuzz products: YF-1, YF-2 and DA-1.

## Cross-reference / dedupe decisions

- Yack is kept separate from Ace Tone. The YF and DA product family is documented as its own marketed/build lineage even though collector research notes similarities to Ace Tone-era fuzz designs.
- No factory attribution is assigned to Yack beyond Japan. The underlying legal company and production facility remain unidentified.
- YF-1, YF-2 and DA-1 are not collapsed into one model. Available evidence supports distinct product identities, while chronology remains partly provisional.
- The Ideal Fuzz Master remains an OEM/marketed-identity case rather than a new manufacturer. Tone Machines Blog links it to the same broad late-1960s Japanese wedge-fuzz manufacturing corridor as Sekova, while explicitly leaving the ultimate factory unresolved.
- Jujiya FB-1 remains a retailer/marketed product lead, not a confirmed Jujiya manufacturing operation.

## Evidence notes

Effects Database documents the Yack DA-1 as a late-1960s Japanese fuzz and identifies YF-1/YF-2 as related Yack products. A surviving YF-2 listing identifies it as a 1967-1969 Japanese Yack Fuzz Box, while Freestompboxes preserves the collector chronology as provisional rather than primary-source dating.

Tone Machines Blog's 2025 Ideal Fuzz Master investigation strengthens the wider Japanese wedge-fuzz OEM hypothesis but explicitly says the actual factory remains unknown. That makes the distinction between product-family evidence and factory attribution important for future model-by-model research.

## Next queue

Continue auditing rare Japanese export brands and early domestic makers around the wedge-fuzz corridor, with special attention to model-specific factory evidence for Ideal, Sekova, Greco, Shaftesbury, Apollo and related labels. Keep every uncertain factory attribution flagged rather than promoted.
