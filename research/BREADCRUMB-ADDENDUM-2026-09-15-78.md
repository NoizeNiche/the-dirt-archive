### Locked findings

- Gearphoria's 2014 UMI feature identifies United Musical Instruments as founded in 1967 by Richard Soloway. It says UMI first made amplifiers for Gracin's Music in Freeport, New York, then made pedals including the Buzz Tone & Volume Expander, Treble-Bass Booster & Mute, and Wa Pedal.
- Effects Database explicitly catalogs a `Nomad (by UMI) Tone Booster`. This is sufficient to retain a direct UMI relationship in research, but not sufficient for public-grade promotion because no period primary-source scan was found in this pass.
- UMI Buzz Tone & Volume Expander remains a high-confidence UMI own-brand product, with surviving 1967-1968 examples supporting chronology.
- UMI Treble-Bass Booster & Mute and UMI Wa remain separate own-brand UMI products, not automatically folded into the Buzz Tone lineage.
- The UMI branch remains separate from Applied Audio and LIMMCO production states. Related circuit topology is not being used as factory evidence by itself.

### Public-promotion decision

- Nomad Tone Booster remains `RESEARCH_ONLY`.
