# Chicago Stompworks — Mööse Pie

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Mööse Pie
- **Builder:** Chicago Stompworks
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Chicago Stompworks Mööse Pie, a highly faithful reproduction of a 1973 Ram's Head Big Muff #18.

## What this pedal is
Effects Database documents the Mööse Pie as a 100% faithful recreation of the **Ram's Head 73 #18** schematic. Chicago Stompworks states that the build follows definitive historical schematics from the Big Muff History archive. [1]

## Colorways
No complete factory colorway sequence was established.

## Versions and factory options
The documented model uses:
- Modern-equivalent **2N5088** transistors.
- Modern carbon and metal-film resistors.
- Hand-fabricated construction.
- True bypass.
- 9V center-negative power. [1]

No numbered factory version sequence was established.

No reliable numbered revision history was established.

## Transistor
The builder explicitly documents **2N5088** transistors as modern equivalents for the 1973 circuit. [1]

## Diode
The exact diode part numbers were not established in the checked source.

## Sound
The Mööse Pie is intended to reproduce the thick, saturated and mid-scooped character of the 1973 Ram's Head circuit while preserving the specific #18 voicing that Chicago Stompworks selected as its reference. [1]
