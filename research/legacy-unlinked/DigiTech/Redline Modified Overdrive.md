# Unlinked Legacy Record — DigiTech — Redline Modified Overdrive

## Correction status
This record is retired from the active Dirt Archive catalog because the reviewed evidence identifies **Redline Modified Overdrive** as a DigiTech digital/e-pedal effect model, not a distinct standalone DigiTech dirt-box product.

DigiTech's official manuals place Redline inside the distortion-model lists of the BP355, RP500, RP1000 and related processors. DigiTech's iStomp documentation and contemporary reviews describe Redline as an e-pedal loaded into the programmable iStomp hardware. Effects Database likewise catalogs the iStomp as the physical DigiTech product and describes Redline as one of the downloadable effects.

## Archive decision
- Retire **DigiTech — Redline Modified Overdrive** from the standalone pedal census.
- Do not treat the iStomp hardware plus a loaded Redline algorithm as a separate Redline pedal identity.
- Preserve this note as an audit breadcrumb rather than leaving the model in the active pedal catalog.
- A previously archived Redline image is quarantined because the active image asset may represent the iStomp/e-pedal presentation rather than a standalone Redline hardware model.

## Evidence checked
1. DigiTech BP355 Owner's Manual: Redline is one of 18 distortion models.
2. DigiTech RP500 Owner's Manual: Redline is listed as a DigiTech Redline Overdrive model with Gain, Low, High and Level parameters.
3. DigiTech RP1000 Owner's Manual: Redline appears in the built-in distortion/overdrive model list.
4. DigiTech iStomp documentation: Redline is an e-pedal loaded into iStomp hardware.
5. Effects Database — DigiTech iStomp: identifies iStomp as a programmable multi-effect pedal and lists Redline among its downloadable effects.

## Scope note
This correction does not remove the real **DigiTech iStomp** hardware from consideration as a historical product. It only prevents the software/e-pedal identity **Redline Modified Overdrive** from being misrepresented as its own standalone dirt box.
