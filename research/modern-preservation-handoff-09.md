## Research priorities
1. Keep sweeping 2023 for OD, distortion and fuzz builders/products.
2. Promote discovery leads only after checking primary or strong secondary evidence.
3. Record builder identity separately from brand, OEM, distributor and designer identity.
4. Record product family, manufacturing family, generation, revision, edition and specimen clues separately.
5. For every promoted pedal, ask: what changed, when, how can someone identify it, and what source proves it?
6. Backfill previously cataloged pedals with variant/generation evaluation and identification clues.
7. Preserve uncertainty claim-by-claim rather than inventing certainty.
8. Continue newest-to-oldest into 2022 after the 2023 census is sufficiently swept.

## Editorial boundary
Document the object, history, lineage, production identity and identification evidence. Do not publish schematics, PCB layouts, complete circuit recipes or cloning instructions.

## Important discovery-source note
Effects Database's dated weekly 2023 updates are proving particularly valuable as a census index. Guitar.com, Guitar Pedal X, Delicious Audio and specialist reviews are useful secondary discovery/date sources. Primary builder pages remain preferred for promotion.
