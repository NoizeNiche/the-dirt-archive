# YARP 2026-09-15 Batch 124: Australian Rose-Morris / Goldentone Boundary Audit

## Builder identity
- **Rose-Morris & Co. (Australia) / Rose-Morris Australia**, Melbourne, manufactured the Goldentone amplifier range locally during the 1950s-1970s.
- Goldentone historical documentation states that Rose-Morris Australia manufactured the Goldentone range in Melbourne and that the Australian company separated from Rose-Morris England in 1965, subsequently operating as Rose Music.
- By the late 1960s Rose-Morris/Rose Music was increasingly importing Japanese equipment as Australian import tariffs eased.

## Dirt relevance
- No standalone Goldentone-branded fuzz stompbox has been verified in the present documentary sweep.
- A mid-1960s Australian oral-history trail places Ron McPherson's custom fuzz box as an add-on to a Goldentone amplifier for Lindsay Cook/The Mustangs, but this is a McPherson prototype/custom-build record, not evidence that Goldentone manufactured a commercial fuzz pedal.
- Goldentone remains **AMP-MANUFACTURER / DIRT-GENEALOGY BOUNDARY**, not a confirmed commercial stompbox builder in the current catalog.

## Important relationship distinction
- Rose-Morris Australia manufactured Goldentone amplifiers locally; the later importing of Japanese products does not make those Japanese effects Rose-Morris products.
- Daison/Goldentone amplifier relationships remain an amplifier-manufacturing issue and should not be extended to stompbox OEM attribution without model-specific evidence.
