# YARP 2026-09-15 Batch 42

## Focus
Ampeg Scrambler and Ampeg historical effects catalog boundary.

## Findings
Ampeg introduced the Scrambler SC-1 in 1969 as an octave-fuzz/distortion effect. Period-focused historical sources place the original production in a short early run, with commonly cited output around 2,000-2,500 units. The Archive records this quantity only as a secondary estimate, not a factory-certified count.

The Scrambler is a core dirt record because its primary function is distortion/fuzz with upper-octave content. Later Ampeg effects such as Phazzer are cataloged separately as non-dirt effects. The 2000s Scrambler reissue is treated as a revival/production-state record rather than merged with the 1969 original.

## Evidence boundary
Original 1969 Scrambler and later reissue are separate production states. Quantity estimates remain evidence-qualified.
