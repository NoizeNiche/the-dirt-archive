# YARP 2026-09-15 Batch 36

## Focus
Swedish bass-dirt builder EBS, extending the 1960-present census into a well-documented regional manufacturer whose effects history begins in the 1990s.

## Findings

### EBS company baseline
EBS Sweden states that the company was founded in 1988 by Bo L. Engberg and Mats Kristoffersson. The company says its first pedal debuted in 1992. This establishes EBS as an independent Swedish builder/manufacturer identity rather than a later imported brand.

### Dirt catalog signals
The historical EBS effects index includes the MultiDrive, MetalDrive, ValveDrive and FuzzMo families, while EBS's current catalog includes Black Haze 2 and other distortion products. The Black Haze is explicitly described by EBS as an analog fuzzy overdrive suitable for bass and guitar. The FuzzMo was introduced in 2013 as a bass-oriented vintage-style fuzz intended to fill the gap between EBS distortion pedals and vintage fuzz effects.

### Production-state caution
Current EBS materials distinguish historical product families and legacy editions from current products. Secondary EBS history notes that legacy editions of pedals were manufactured in Sweden while current pedals may be manufactured elsewhere. The archive therefore treats EBS design/development identity separately from the manufacturing country of individual production states.

### Catalog priority
EBS should be treated as a P1 builder for the archive's bass-dirt layer. It is not a vintage-1960s builder, but it fills a genuine Swedish 1990s-present gap and has a documented dirt catalog large enough to justify dedicated reconciliation rather than a single generic "EBS fuzz" record.
