# YARP 2026-09-15 Batch 72: S. Hawk Ltd.

## Builder identity
S. Hawk Ltd. is treated as a distinct early-1970s U.S. builder rather than merely a retail label. Surviving Hawk I, Hawk II and Hawk III material identifies a coordinated product family associated in specialist documentation with Harry Kolbe and New York production.

## Dirt catalog
- Hawk I: switchable overdrive/distortion device with linear preamp, fuzz, headphone amp and volume-control functions. Surviving examples and original-manual material support a 1973 corridor.
- Hawk II Tonal Expander: three-band booster/expander introduced around 1973. Retained as a dirt-adjacent boost/EQ boundary rather than counted as a core fuzz.
- Hawk III Bass Expander: bass-oriented expander/booster from the same early-1970s family. Retained as a boundary pending stronger period catalog evidence.

## Evidence posture
Hawk I existence and the 1973 corridor are evidence-qualified. The Hawk II/III family relationship is supported by surviving manual material and specialist historical records. Exact production endpoint and complete distribution history remain unresolved.
