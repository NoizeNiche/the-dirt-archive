## Builder work
### MASF PEDALS / AXCNOX
- Added MASF Pedals as a canonical Japanese extreme-music pedal builder/brand founded in 2008 by ENDON noisemaker AXCNOX, based on current Japanese distributor/retailer documentation.
- Preserved MASF Pedals as the visible product identity and AXCNOX as the named founder/designer rather than flattening the relationship into ENDON.
- Mapped the documented dirt catalog before deeper pedal archaeology:
  - Epilepsy
  - Kidnapper
  - Lavender Head
  - Life Convulsion
  - Paranoid
  - ORDO
  - STARRY WISDOM

- Retained POSSESSED, Kollaps, RAPTIO, SCM, Ma.S Model OSC03 and thornoscillator as relationship-only because their primary identities are noise, tremolo, glitch/hold or oscillator functions rather than central OD/DS/FZ.

## Catalog reconciliation
- Current MASF Store shows 2026-visible products including RAPTIO, POSSESSED, 13u22130x and Lavender Head.
- NINEVOLT/LEP preserve a broader historical/current distributor catalog, allowing the archive to keep discontinued and less-visible MASF models rather than treating the current storefront as exhaustive.
- ORDO is explicitly described as a wave-shaper drive capable of booster, overdrive, distortion and fuzz textures, so it qualifies for the central dirt layer.
- Paranoid is explicitly categorized by NINEVOLT as an overdrive and historically described as a fuzz/drive with multiple modes; it remains central as a drive-family record.

## Photo evidence
- MASF Store pages provide multiple exterior images for current products such as Lavender Head, POSSESSED and 13u22130x.
- NINEVOLT pages provide exterior image sets for historical MASF products including Epilepsy and Kidnapper.
- Reverb preserves specimen photography for Epilepsy and Life Convulsion.
- These image trails are catalog evidence and sourcing leads, not automatic permission to republish.

## Archive discipline
- No schematics, PCB diagrams, gutshots, BOMs or cloning instructions added.
- Internal controls are not reproduced as reconstruction guides; they are used only where necessary to distinguish a product's public identity.
- Collaboration/custom and utility/noise models remain distinct from central dirt families.

## Next direction
Continue the Japanese long-tail builder census, especially brands with historical dealer catalogs that can be reconciled against current maker pages and exterior photo trails. Maintain the Japanese-first corridor before moving into the Taiwan/Hong Kong/mainland-China long tail.
