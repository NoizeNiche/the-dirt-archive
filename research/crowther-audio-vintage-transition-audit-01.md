# Crowther Audio: Vintage Transition Audit 01

Research status: VERIFIED_BASELINE_WITH_VARIANTS
Scope: historical product and production chronology, 1970s through 2000s transition. No circuit reconstruction.

## Identity
Paul Crowther is the designer and builder behind Crowther Audio in New Zealand. The Hotcake name dates to 1977. Early production was small-scale and direct from Crowther's workshop.

## Dirt catalog anchors
- Hotcake: originated in the 1976 design period and named in 1977. It is the core Crowther distortion/overdrive lineage.
- Early Hotcake variants: the original design lacked Presence and used a switch for the sound selection. Later production introduced changes in switching/control and tone options.
- Bluesberry: a named Hotcake variant that emerged after two Hotcake versions were circulating, created to distinguish a modified version from the earlier/original circuit state.
- Double Hotcake: later dual-unit product state.
- Prunes & Custard: introduced from Crowther's 1995 experimentation; first examples were informally called Google Box before the Prunes & Custard name was adopted. It is a separate Crowther dirt/harmonic-generator product family, not simply a Hotcake revision.

## Dating evidence
Crowther's serial system began in 1994 and encodes year/date/month production information. This is unusually valuable for specimen-level future work because a surviving unit can potentially be assigned a manufacturing date from the serial format.

The 1994 serial-number transition should not be mistaken for the beginning of Hotcake production. The Hotcake predates it by many years.

## Distribution relationship
A major 1990s distribution node is Human Gear's Hiroshi Yagi. Crowther recounts an initial Japanese order of 10 Hotcakes, followed by larger repeat orders, establishing a strong exporter/distributor relationship without changing Crowther Audio's manufacturer identity.

## Evidence disposition
Primary/first-person historical source: AudioCulture interview with Paul Crowther, published 2022.
Corroborating historical source: Aion FX tracing journal, 2020.

## Archive rule
Do not merge Hotcake, Bluesberry, Double Hotcake, or Prunes & Custard into one undifferentiated model. Treat each as a family/product or production-state node and reserve exact specimen dates for future serialized evidence.
