## Key determinations
- UMI direct pedal catalog now explicitly enumerates Buzz Tone & Volume Expander, Tone Booster, Treble-Bass Booster & Mute, and Wa Pedal.
- Buzz Tone & Volume Expander remains the strongest dirt-product UMI record, supported by manufacturer identification and a dated 1968 specimen.
- Tone Booster remains medium confidence because the UMI attribution is documented while alternate branded examples remain unidentified by maker.
- Treble-Bass Booster & Mute is retained as a direct UMI product based on Gearphoria's focused UMI catalog feature and Effects Database's four-product UMI census.
- Wa Pedal is retained as a direct UMI product, with a surviving example carrying an original CTS pot dated week 18 of 1969.
- Apollo No. 843 Buzz Box & Volume Expander and Conrad Buzz Box & Volume Expander remain UMI-related marketed states, not independent factories.
- Kimberly remains separate because its documented manufacturing history includes LIMMCO and Applied production stages.
- Applied Audio products remain separate from UMI absent model-specific UMI evidence.

## Boundary rule
Do not infer UMI manufacture from enclosure or circuit similarity alone. Treat the four direct UMI pedal models as the current manufacturer-grounded census and keep neighboring products in separate evidence classes.
