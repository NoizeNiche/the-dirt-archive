## Evidence notes
Effects Database currently catalogs 18 EC Pedals products and identifies Eldar Cohen as the builder in Netanya, Israel. A 2013 founder interview states that EC Pedals began in late 2010 with a seven-pedal lineup centered on Zombie Distortion, Mystical Sustainer, Angry Ape, Overlord, Hot Rodded and related products, and states that Zombie Distortion was the first pedal produced and sold under the EC brand. The same interview discusses wide-gain design intent and later international distribution plans.

The historical roster was expanded using the Effects Database product list and 2013 coverage that documents Fat Rock and TighMetal Pro. Non-dirt products in the 18-product database, including compressor, switching-system and delay/reverb records, were excluded from central dirt inventory.

## Photo workflow

## Scope discipline
Boost-only Hot Rodded remains gain-adjacent but retained because it is part of the original documented gain lineup. Signature products stay under EC Pedals with artist relationship metadata. No schematics, PCB layouts, gutshots, cloning instructions, or complete BOM data are published.
