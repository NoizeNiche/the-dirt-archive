## Evidence notes
GIGAGAIN's official site identifies the company address in Shenzhen, Guangdong, China and presents the brand as a guitar-effects and audio-hardware manufacturer. Official product pages document BLUEGIANT as a low-gain overdrive, GIGA SCREAMER as a TS-style overdrive, GOLDEN LEGACY as an analog Marshall-style distortion, and SILVER LEGACY as an analog Dumble-style overdrive. The first-generation catalog also lists GAINSTER as a drive product and SUPERKICK as a transparent booster.

The timing is historically useful for the archive. GIGAGAIN announced its first public brand debut at Music China in October 2025 and was exhibiting again at NAMM in February 2026. Taiwan distributor material from July 2026 describes the first Taiwanese product arrival and lists the same gain family. Preserve those events as launch/distribution chronology rather than treating Taiwan as the manufacturing origin.
