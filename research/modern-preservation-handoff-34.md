## Major research notes
- Guitar Pedal X's 2024 category surveys provide a broad discovery spine: 32 fuzz finalists, 34 distortion finalists and 35 boost/overdrive finalists. This is census evidence, not a claim that every finalist first appeared in 2024.
- 29 Pedals Toki is especially important to preserve as an original product identity. Contemporary coverage describes a two-gain-stage fuzz/driver with three highly interactive controls and explicitly distinguishes it from a direct vintage-circuit recreation.
- Horrothia Lütz is a major modern fuzz reference point: three selected silicon transistors, Fuzz Face and Tone Bender territory, harmonic/octave possibilities, oscillation and an unusually deep external control surface. It deserves later specimen/image work.
- Cornerstone Nero demonstrates the archive's lineage rule well: it combines a Fuzz Face-style section with Cornerstone's Gladio-style Dumble overdrive ideas, but Nero remains its own retail product.
- Danelectro Nichols 1966 is historically interesting because the modern pedal is tied to a teenage Steve Ridinger design concept from the 1960s. The archive should preserve the historical design story separately from the 2024 production object.
- Death By Audio Octave Clang belongs in the experimental octave-fuzz branch. Its importance is less about genealogy with older octave boxes and more about preserving a distinct builder/product identity.
- Wampler Mofetta is a strong lineage-and-identity record. Wampler describes it as a tribute to the Ibanez MT10 Mostortion, retaining the original's broad low/mid-gain voice and three-band EQ while adding a Texture mode with actual MOSFET gain stages. The first run was 600 units.
- Tavysh HARM, Poly Effects Ample 56 and Tubesteader ROY show a strong 2024 tendency toward multi-domain gain, amp/preamp modeling or multi-channel tube implementations. The database should preserve those architecture/control distinctions instead of reducing them to one traditional circuit label.
- LPD Dutch Overdrive '24 is a useful revision record. The Hi/Low footswitch, changed enclosure/jacks, soft-touch switching with last-state memory and 9-18V operation are meaningful physical identifiers, while the underlying Dutch family remains continuous.

## Data rule reinforced
The archive must separate four kinds of historical statements: discovery/census year, introduction year, revision/generation year and current availability. A product appearing in a 2024 roundup is not automatically a 2024 introduction. Generation labels such as II remain meaningful, but technical differences should not be invented without primary evidence.
