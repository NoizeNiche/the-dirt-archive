# MXR Original Dirt Catalog Audit 06

## Scope
Original MXR / MXR Innovations dirt products and adjacent model-family states, with emphasis on the 1973-1984 original-company period. Later Jim Dunlop MXR products are retained only as chronology boundaries, not silently merged with the original company.

## Catalog negative evidence / boundary finding

The surviving high-level original MXR model chronology identifies the major Reference-series and Commande-series dirt products above, but does not currently surface a second independent original-company fuzz/overdrive/distortion family beyond Blue Box, Distortion+, Distortion II and Commande Overdrive. The Series 2000 adds a distinct Distortion+ generation rather than a new named dirt family.

The 1982 Series 2000 lineup is documented as M-201 Phaser, M-202 Dyna Comp, M-203 Stereo Flanger, M-204 Distortion+, M-205 Stereo Chorus and M-206 Time Delay. A later Junior digital unit is a scope-boundary record, not central dirt.

## Important chronology corrections

1. **M-104 vs M-204:** These are not the same retail production state. M-104 belongs to the original Reference-series Distortion+ lineage; M-204 belongs to the later Series 2000 redesign. Historical product databases independently list the Series 2000 as a seven-product family including M-204 Distortion+.
2. **M-142 Distortion II:** historical coverage identifies it as a distinct MXR distortion with Drive, Resonance, Filter and Output controls. Effects Database explicitly lists M-142 separately from M-104 and M-204.
3. **M-164 Commande Overdrive:** the Commande line was introduced around 1981 and M-164 is its dirt model. Reverb's historical product record places M-164 at 1981-1984.
4. **Distortion III is not original-company MXR:** current Dunlop documentation presents M-115 Distortion III as a later proprietary design. It should remain in the post-acquisition / revival-era MXR catalog, not be back-projected into the 1970s-1984 original-company catalog.

## Evidence trail

- MXR historical chronology: Reference series lists MX-103 Blue Box, MX-104 Distortion+, MX-142 Distortion II and related original models; Commande series lists M-164 Overdrive; Series 2000 lists M-204 Distortion+.
- Effects Database: M-142 Distortion II and M-204 Distortion+ are separate catalog entries, reinforcing separate retail identities.
- Reverb: M-164 Commande Overdrive is cataloged as a 1981-1984 U.S.-made product.
- Jim Dunlop: current M115 Distortion III documentation describes it as a later original design and does not place it in the original MXR company catalog.

## Research conclusion

The original MXR dirt catalog is now substantially bounded at the named-model level. The remaining uncertainty is mostly **production-state chronology, logo/enclosure generations, regional catalog appearances, and surviving paperwork**, not a large hidden second catalog of named dirt pedals. Further work should therefore shift toward historical catalog/specimen reconciliation and the original-to-Dunlop ownership transition rather than continuing to invent new model candidates from modern MXR lists.
