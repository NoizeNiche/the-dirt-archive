# YARP 2026-09-15 Batch 27

## Focus
Vox-branded fuzz/wah production-state reconciliation across JMI/Vox Sound Limited, Erith, Hastings, JEN and Sola Sound relationships.

## Findings

### Vox Wow Fuzz / Wah-Fuzz
VoxSupreme documents three important historical states of the Vox Wow Fuzz corridor: an early Sola Sound-marketed unit carrying a Vox Sound Equipment Limited distribution label, a Vox Sound Limited unit marked Erith, and a later Vox Sound Limited/Hastings state. The source dates the Erith state to roughly 1970-1971 and notes that Vox's manufacturing moved from Erith to Hastings in 1971. This allows the archive to preserve a production-state chronology without pretending that the name on the pedal alone identifies the physical factory.

### Sola Sound relationship
VoxSupreme and FuzzFaced both document Sola Sound manufacture of Vox-branded Wow Fuzz / Wah-Fuzz products in the 1970s. The 1975 Dallas-Arbiter catalog corridor is especially useful because it places Vox Tonebender Mark 3 beside Italian-made and Sola Sound/Colorsound-made effect products, showing that Vox's catalog was a mixed manufacturing ecosystem rather than a single-factory line.

### Vox 9-3700 Fuzz-Wah
The Vox Showroom identifies the 9-3700 Fuzz-Wah as a specific North American Vox/Thomas Organ product and argues from the base-plate marking that the documented specimen was made in California rather than Italy. Because the site also notes that the model is absent from surviving Thomas Organ/Vox catalogs through February 1970, the archive records a mid-1970 to early-1970s working introduction corridor rather than inheriting a generic JEN attribution from other Vox fuzz-wah products.

### JEN boundary
The JEN relationship remains strong for other Vox fuzz/wah products, including Italian Vox Stereo Fuzz Wah and Double Sound families, but this batch deliberately does **not** transfer that attribution to every Vox-branded fuzz-wah. The archive's model-specific OEM rule is reinforced here.

## Research boundary
No circuit reconstruction or internal construction instructions are recorded. The purpose is production chronology, retail identity, and manufacturer/OEM relationship mapping only.
