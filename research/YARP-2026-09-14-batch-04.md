# YARP Batch 04 — OEM / Rebrand Reconciliation

Research date: 2026-09-14

## Purpose

Convert the growing builder/product census into a safer historical graph by reconciling marketed brands, OEM manufacturers, contract suppliers, and distinct production corridors before public promotion.

## Completed

- `oem-reconciliation-batch-01.tsv`
- Cross-checked historical Tone Bender / Foxey Lady / Tube Screamer relationship nodes.

## Key findings

### Sola Sound / Colorsound web
The Tone Bender family cannot be treated as a single retail identity. Historical sources document Sola Sound manufacturing for branded products associated with Vox, Marshall, Rotosound and Park, while the Vox V828 Tone Bender represents a separate Italian JEN manufacturing corridor. The archive therefore keeps the retail brand searchable while attaching the physical manufacturer to the specific historical product state.

### Guild Foxey Lady web
Foxey Lady is an important duplicate-prevention case. Surviving evidence shows Guild-branded pedals were produced through multiple supplier states, including Mosrite, AUL/All Instruments and later Electro-Harmonix production. A Foxey Lady record therefore needs production-state lineage rather than a single immutable manufacturer field.

### Ibanez / Maxon
The existing Maxon/Ibanez work is reinforced as an OEM/retail split: the archive should not turn Ibanez and Maxon into duplicate physical builders, but it should also not erase their separate marketed identities. Tube Screamer family relationships remain model-specific and historically bounded.

## Editorial outcome

The central public identity should be the canonical physical/historical builder entity. Marketed brands, distributors and OEM customers remain searchable relationship nodes. A single retail model may therefore have multiple production states without creating duplicate builders.
