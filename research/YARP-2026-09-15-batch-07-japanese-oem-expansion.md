## Evidence observations

Shin-Ei's current site explicitly states the historical relationship between Honey Baby Crying Fuzz (1967) and Shin-Ei FY-6 Super Fuzz (1968), and it now also documents modern revival products. These modern products are not to be mistaken for original historical production states.

Effects Database currently groups a broad family of Japanese OEM products around the Shin-Ei WF-8 and Teisco/Bruno/Mica/Ibanez/Guyatone/Elk marketed identities. The archive retains these as related marketed products, not duplicate physical builders.

The Teisco vintage record must also be kept separate from the post-2017 Teisco revival. Modern Teisco returned as a pedal brand in 2018-2019, meaning present-day Teisco products are a new production state and not evidence of uninterrupted vintage Teisco manufacturing.

Sobbat's official pages give strong production-state evidence for DB-1, DB-1S, DB-2S and DB-4R, including explicit discontinuation/reissue wording. The archive will use these official pages for product existence and state boundaries before deeper specimen work.
