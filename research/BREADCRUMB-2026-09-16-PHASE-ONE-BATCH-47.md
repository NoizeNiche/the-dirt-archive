## Evidence notes
Dawner Prince's official site currently lists DIKTATOR MKII and RED ROX in its effects catalog; its DIKTATOR pages document the original unit and the MKII as a complete redesign while retaining the predecessor's tonal character. Premier Guitar provides a dated 2013 introduction for RED ROX.

DryBell's official The Engine page and manual place the builder in Croatia and document the pedal as a two-section analog overdrive/distortion plus EQ/boost foundation platform.

Couch Electronics' official About page identifies Slobodan as the builder in Slovenia and says he designs and builds the pedals himself, including custom enclosures, knobs, and switches. The current catalog exposes Baker's Dozen and Blue Whale.

Polarbear Effects' official Drowner page identifies the fuzz as the first effect in the chain and documents the combined fuzz, bitcrusher, reverb/delay architecture. The current site identifies POLAR AUDIO, vl. Darko Vajda, in Hvar, Croatia. Earlier community documentation found an alternate historical stylization, PLBR, so that alias should be preserved for future specimen searches rather than treated as a separate builder.
