# ELKA vintage dirt catalog audit 02

## Scope
Follow-up catalog pass for ELKA / ELK products and the Italian/Japanese export relationship layer. This file focuses on product identity, period chronology, and manufacturer uncertainty without publishing circuit reconstruction material.

## Period anchors
Tone Machines Blog's catalog research reports ELK catalogs and brochures from 1970-1976 and identifies these key period products:
- ES-960 Super Fuzz Sustainer, documented by 1973.
- WF-880 Wau Fuzz, documented beside the ES-960 in the 1973 catalog.
- WF-1100 Wau-Fuzz, replacing the WF-880 in the following year's catalog.
- WS-1400 Wau & Sustainer, appearing in the following year's catalog.

Effects Database independently indexes ELK WF-1100 Wau Fuzz and WS-1400 Wau & Sustainer, providing a useful cross-check for the catalog trail.

## Elka relationship layer
Effects Database indexes Elka Dizzy Master, Elka Fuzz Wah and Elka Wah Wah as distinct marketed products. Historical collector evidence also identifies Elka Dizzy Tone as a surviving vintage fuzz product. These should remain separate model records unless primary-period evidence proves a single model-number lineage.

## Important OEM finding
The ELK Wau-Fuzz products are especially important to the archive's Japanese OEM map. Tone Machines Blog notes that the same broad enclosure/product family occurs under Guyatone FS-5 as early as 1971 and under multiple Ibanez/Maxon/Greco/Elk-era names. This supports an OEM/shared-supplier relationship record, but it does **not** justify assigning every marketed version to one named manufacturer without product-specific evidence.

## Canonical treatment
Keep ELK / ELKA as the marketed brand entity. Keep Japanese OEM/manufacturing attribution as a relationship layer with confidence states. Do not collapse ELK, ELKA, Guyatone, Ibanez, Teisco, Shin-Ei, Maxon or other names into one builder merely because an enclosure or product family appears similar.

## Dirt catalog baseline
- ELK ES-960 Super Fuzz Sustainer — c.1973 onward anchor.
- ELK WF-880 Wau Fuzz — documented by 1973.
- ELK WF-1100 Wau-Fuzz — documented as the successor listing in the following catalog year.
- ELK WS-1400 Wau & Sustainer — documented in the following catalog generation.
- Elka Dizzy Tone — vintage fuzz record; production chronology unresolved.
- Elka Dizzy Master — vintage foot-controlled gain-related record; exact relationship to Dizzy Tone requires further evidence.
- Elka Fuzz Wah — vintage fuzz-wah record.

## Next evidence targets
- Direct scans of the ELK 1970-1976 brochures where available.
- Period advertisements establishing retail model numbers and market regions.
- Product photographs and surviving boxes/labels to distinguish ELK from ELKA naming states.
- Product-specific evidence for Japanese OEM responsibility.
