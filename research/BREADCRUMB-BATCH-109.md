## Photos

The first-party NERDS page supplies exterior images for the documented products. These are retained as primary photo-source leads for later credit/licensing review.

## Information on the pedal

- The NERDS page gives individual product descriptions, controls, pricing and reference lineage for the dirt subset.
- Distortion models include RAT-, Maestro MFZ-, DeArmond-, Ross- and Ibanez MT10-derived/tuned concepts.
- Overdrive models include tuned DOD FX50, Hot Cake, TS9, Zen Drive, Ibanez Dirt Box, Box of Rock, tube-like CD4049, bass overdrive and discrete transistor/FET designs.
- Fuzz models include Colorsound, Maestro FZ-1, LM386, Little Muff, Foxx Tone Machine, Octavia, Fuzz Face, AstroTone, Mad Fuzz and bass-tuned derivatives.

## Normalization decisions

- NERDS products are preserved as individual historical product records because the first-party archive gives each a distinct model identifier and product description.
- Component identifiers in the model names are preserved because Honda Sound Works itself uses them to distinguish the historical designs.
- Booster, preamp and modulation products remain outside the central dirt inventory.
- The archive does not publish schematics, PCB layouts, gutshots or complete component inventories.
