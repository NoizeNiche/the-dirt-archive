# Big Ear - Black Betty

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** Black Betty
- **Builder:** Big Ear
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Big Ear's Black Betty fuzz.

## What this pedal is

Big Ear's current product documentation identifies **Black Betty** and **Betty White** as the same pedal in different finishes. The two versions use the same PCB, transistors and part values and are intended to sound identical.

## Controls

- **Volume:** The single external face control acts as master output.
- **Bass trim:** Side-mounted trim pot provides the bass adjustment.
- **Gain:** Effectively fixed at maximum in the documented design.

## Circuit / components

- The builder states that the pedal is based on a famous fuzz known for its single-knob layout, but the current product page does not name the source circuit.
- The builder explicitly says Black Betty and Betty White share the same PCB, transistors and part values.
- Exact transistor part numbers and diode details remain undisclosed.

## Sound

The builder describes the circuit as a deliberately simple fixed-gain fuzz with bass adjustment available through the side trim. No more specific classic-circuit attribution is added because the builder does not name the source.

## History / finish

- **Black Betty:** Black finish.
- **Betty White:** White finish.
- Current Big Ear documentation treats them as the same circuit and product.
