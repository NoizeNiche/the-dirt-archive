# Big Ear - Black Betty

## PRP identity

- **Identity:** Big Ear's Black Betty, a minimalist one-knob fuzz with an internal/side bass trim.

## What this pedal is
Black Betty is documented as a very simple fuzz with gain fixed at maximum. The primary top-mounted control is Volume, while a side-mounted trim controls the bass response.

## Controls
- **Volume:** Main output control.
- **Bass trim:** Side-mounted internal/trim control for low-frequency response.
- **Gain:** Fixed/maxed rather than externally adjustable.

## Sound
The fixed-gain architecture makes Black Betty a deliberately simple, high-fuzz design. The bass trim provides the main tonal adjustment beyond output level.
