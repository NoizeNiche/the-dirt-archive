# Bias - BDS-1 Distortion

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** BDS-1 Distortion
- **Builder:** Bias
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** Vintage Bias BDS-1, a Japanese-market distortion pedal associated with Coron's OEM pedal designs.

## What this pedal is
Effects Database lists Bias BDS-1 Distortion among Bias products and links it to the Coron DC-808 Distortion III family. The BDS-1 is documented as a three-knob distortion. Denhaku's 1980 museum archive independently lists the BIAS BDS-1 as a distortion model.

## Circuit / lineage
- **OEM family:** Coron DC-808 Distortion III.
- **Controls:** Three knobs.
- Exact BDS-1 control labels, transistor part numbers and diode arrangement were not safely established from the checked sources.

## Sound
The BDS-1 belongs to the compact Japanese solid-state distortion family produced under multiple OEM brand names. The archive keeps its description at the documented family level rather than assigning an unsupported modern circuit equivalent.
