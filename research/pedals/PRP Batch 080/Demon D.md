## What this pedal is
Demon D is an overdrive from Brazilian builder BFFX Boutique Pedals. Equipboard identifies BFFX as a boutique builder based in Ribeirão Preto, São Paulo, and catalogs Demon D as an overdrive. The available product description identifies gain, tone, and level-style controls and describes an asymmetrical clipping design. A 2015 Brazilian user discussion also identifies Demon D as one of the builder's distortion-focused pedals.
