## What this pedal is

Demon D is a BFFX overdrive model from the builder's Brazilian boutique catalog. Current secondary product data describes it as an aggressive overdrive/distortion pedal with a three-control interface and asymmetrical clipping.

## Controls

- **GAIN:** Drive amount.
- **TONE:** Tone shaping.
- **LEVEL:** Output.
These controls are reported consistently in the current product listing reviewed.

## History

Equipboard records the model as available since **May 2009**. A 2015 Cifra Club discussion documents a user identifying and buying the Demon D after seeing a BFFX review, confirming the model's presence in the builder's mid-2010s catalog/community.

## Sound

The available model description emphasizes gritty, dynamic overdrive that can be pushed toward more aggressive distortion. A Cifra Club user characterized it as a distortion pedal in personal use, but that subjective comment is not treated as a definitive technical or tonal classification.
