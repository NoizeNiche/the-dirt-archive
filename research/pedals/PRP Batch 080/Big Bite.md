# Big Bite Fx - Big Bite

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Big Bite
- **Builder:** Big Bite Fx
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive
- **Identity:** The Big Bite, a rare UK-built two-channel boost/overdrive/distortion pedal.

## What this pedal is
The Big Bite is documented as a hand-built UK pedal from the 2000s. Surviving listings describe two channels, one boost and one overdrive, that can be combined to cover boost through bluesy overdrive and heavier distortion. Reverb listings identify four controls: Boost, Tone, Gain and Bite.

## Controls
- **Boost**
- **Tone**
- **Gain**
- **Bite**
- Two channels: boost and overdrive.

## Power / construction
- 9V battery or optional power supply.
- Hand-built in the United Kingdom.
- Heavy metal enclosure; surviving listings describe the unit as roughly 1kg.

## Sound
The documented design spans low-gain boost, bluesy overdrive, punk-style distortion and heavier rock gain. The archive keeps those descriptions attributed to surviving product/listing material rather than treating them as laboratory measurements.

## Historical notes
- The company appears to have been a UK operation and is now generally treated as defunct in surviving resale documentation.
- Contemporary resale listings associate the pedal with Tom DeLonge, Hawthorne Heights and Taking Back Sunday.
