# Big Bite Fx - Big Bite

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Big Bite
- **Builder:** Big Bite Fx
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive
- **Identity:** Rare UK-built Big Bite dual-channel boost/overdrive pedal.

## What this pedal is
Surviving Reverb listings identify the Big Bite as a **hand-built UK pedal from the 2000s/2011 era** with two channels: one boost and one overdrive. The channels can be combined, allowing the pedal to span boost, bluesy overdrive and heavier distortion.

## Controls
- **Boost**
- **Tone**
- **Gain**
- **Bite**

The documented layout contains four external controls. Surviving listings do not expose an internal schematic or a control-by-channel map.

## Power / construction
- **Power:** 9V battery or external power supply, according to surviving listings.
- **Construction:** Hand-built in the UK.
- Listings describe a heavy metal enclosure.
- No exact current draw was reliably established.

## Sound
A surviving listing describes the pedal as moving from vintage-style boost through bluesy overdrive to punk/heavy-rock distortion. These are preserved as product/listing characterization rather than measured response data.

## History
- Reverb records examples from the **2000s** and a **2011** listing.
- Multiple surviving listings describe production as extremely limited; one seller estimates roughly 100 examples, but that quantity is not independently verified.
- The pedal is repeatedly associated in resale listings with Tom DeLonge of Blink-182 and with Hawthorne Heights and Taking Back Sunday.
