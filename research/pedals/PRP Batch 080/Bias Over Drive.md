# Bias - Bias Over Drive

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Bias Over Drive
- **Builder:** Bias
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Late-1970s/early-1980s Japanese Bias-branded overdrive associated with Coron Musical Instrument Co.

## What this pedal is
Effects Database documents the Bias Over Drive as a historical Japanese pedal built by Coron Musical Instrument Co. and distributed under the Bias name. It is part of a family of early Coron FET overdrive designs also sold under several other brand names.

## Controls
- **Level:** Output level.
- **Tone:** High-frequency/brightness control.
- **Over Drive:** Gain/drive amount.
- The documented version uses three controls.

## Circuit / power
- **Circuit:** Discrete single-stage JFET design.
- **Power:** 1.5V AA / UM-3 battery according to the historical reference.
- Exact JFET part number was not established.

## Sound
The historical reference describes a dynamic, touch-sensitive overdrive with mild compression and an open harmonic character, positioned between a gritty boost and light fuzz-style breakup.
