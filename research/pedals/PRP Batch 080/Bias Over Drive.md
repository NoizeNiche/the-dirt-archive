# Bias - Bias Over Drive

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Bias Over Drive
- **Builder:** Bias
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Late-1970s/early-1980s Japanese Bias-branded overdrive associated with Coron Musical Instrument Co.

## What this pedal is
Effects Database documents the Bias Over Drive as a historical Japanese overdrive built by **Coron Musical Instrument Co.** and distributed under the Bias name. It belongs to an early Coron FET-overdrive family also represented by Coron, Asama, Seiwa and Storm variants.

## Controls
- **Level:** Output level.
- **Tone:** High-frequency/brightness shaping.
- **Over Drive:** Gain/drive amount.

## Circuit / power
- **Circuit:** Discrete single-stage JFET design.
- **Power:** The historical reference specifies a **1.5V AA / UM-3 battery**.
- Exact JFET part number is not documented.

## Technical notes
The same historical source reports 500 kOhm input impedance, 50 kOhm output impedance and dimensions of 120 x 60 x 35 mm. Because that page also contains a contradictory advertising note mentioning a 006P 9V battery, the archive treats the **1.5V AA specification as the stated operating supply while preserving the advertisement inconsistency rather than silently choosing both**.

## Sound
Effects Database characterizes the circuit as dynamic and touch-sensitive, with mild compression and an open harmonic texture between gritty boost and light fuzz-style overdrive.
