# Bias - Bias Distortion

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Bias Distortion
- **Builder:** Bias
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** Vintage Bias-branded Japanese distortion pedal, distinct from Positive Grid's later BIAS Distortion hardware.

## What this pedal is
Effects Database documents this Bias Distortion as a **late-1970s to early-1980s Japanese-made analog distortion** from the Coron OEM family. The surviving description identifies it as a compact two-knob design with Level and Distortion controls.

## Controls
- **Level:** Output level.
- **Distortion:** Amount of distortion.

## Circuit / lineage
- **Circuit family:** MXR Distortion+ style op-amp clipping circuit, according to the exact Effects Database entry.
- Related to Coron Distortion models and several other Coron-branded OEM variants.
- Exact op-amp part number, transistor complement and diode values are not documented in the checked sources.

## Construction / power
- **Power:** 9V battery only on stock versions, according to the historical reference.
- **Bypass:** Mechanical footswitch.
- **Connections:** 1/4-inch input and output.
- **Made in:** Japan.
- Exact stock adapter provision was not established; historical documentation describes the stock version as battery-only with no external DC input.

## Sound
The exact historical entry describes the pedal as covering light overdrive/boost-like textures at lower Distortion settings and thicker, compressed vintage distortion at higher settings. Those descriptions are retained as source characterization, not as independent measurements.

## Identity separation
This record must not be merged with the **Positive Grid BIAS Distortion**. Positive Grid's later product is a programmable, MIDI/USB-connected hardware platform from a different manufacturer and era.
