# Big Ear - LOAF

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** LOAF
- **Builder:** Big Ear
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Big Ear's original silicon fuzz, sold under both Big Ear Pedals and the earlier Big Ear n.y.c. branding.

## What this pedal is
LOAF is an original silicon fuzz rather than a direct Muff or Fuzz Face emulation. Reverb and current Big Ear coverage describe it as warm, bass-friendly and darker in character. Later coverage identifies a three-knob Fuzz/Tone/Volume layout.

## Controls
- **Fuzz**
- **Tone**
- **Volume**

## Circuit / components
- **Device family:** Silicon fuzz.
- Exact transistor and diode part numbers were not safely established.
- The archive does not assign a specific classic circuit lineage.

## Sound
The available descriptions characterize LOAF as a smooth, warm, bass-friendly fuzz with a darker voice. Community and review coverage notes that lower fuzz settings can read more like overdrive, while higher settings produce the more pronounced fuzz character.

## Branding
Reverb's product record notes that the Loaf was produced under both Big Ear Pedals and the earlier Big Ear n.y.c. names, with no documented sonic difference between the versions.
