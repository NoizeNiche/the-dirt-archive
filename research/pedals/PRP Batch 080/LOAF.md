# Big Ear - LOAF

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** LOAF

- **Identity:** Big Ear LOAF, the original silicon fuzz.

## What this pedal is
Big Ear and Reverb documentation describe LOAF as an **original silicon fuzz**, explicitly not a direct emulation of a Muff or Fuzz Face. Reverb records the model under both Big Ear Pedals and the earlier Big Ear n.y.c. branding.

- **Fuzz**
- **Tone**
- **Volume**

## Circuit / components
- **Device family:** Silicon fuzz.
- Exact transistor and diode part numbers were not publicly established in the checked sources.
- No specific classic circuit clone is assigned.

## Sound
Surviving product and community descriptions characterize LOAF as warm, bass-friendly and relatively dark. Community reports also note that lower fuzz settings can behave more like a medium-gain overdrive while retaining the fuzz circuit's basic character.

## Branding / production
Reverb records the LOAF across the earlier Big Ear n.y.c. and later Big Ear Pedals branding and does not identify a documented sonic difference between those versions.
