# BFFX Boutique Pedals - Guitarlab1

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** Guitarlab1
- **Builder:** BFFX Boutique Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive
- **Identity:** BFFX GL1, a fully analog 3-in-1 pedal combining booster, overdrive and distortion.

## What this pedal is

Contemporary Brazilian product coverage describes the Guitarlab1 as three effects in one enclosure: Booster, Overdrive and Distortion. Each effect can be used by itself or in cascade, so the unit can produce stacked combinations from the same pedal.

## Controls / operation

- **Booster:** Dedicated effect section.
- **Overdrive:** Dedicated effect section.
- **Distortion:** Dedicated effect section.
- Each section can operate independently or in cascade.
- Exact factory knob labels and ranges were not recovered from the surviving product write-ups.

## Construction

- **Fully analog** design.
- Aluminum enclosure with **anodized finish**.
- High-performance components, described as mostly imported.
- Components are mounted individually on industrial PCBs and tested under stated quality-control procedures.
- **100% true bypass**.

## Power

- **9V** supply.
- Product coverage specifies **BOSS-style polarity with positive exterior / negative center**.
- Approximate current draw: **300 mA with all three effects engaged**.

## Circuit / components

- Fully analog.
- Exact transistor, diode and op-amp part numbers were not established.
- No circuit-family or clone attribution is added without direct evidence.

## History

A Brazilian product announcement published **April 8, 2015** introduced the Guitarlab1 to the market. The BFFX catalog entry is therefore independently anchored to the mid-2010s.

## Sound

Because the booster, overdrive and distortion sections can be used separately or cascaded, the design's principal documented feature is its ability to move from boost through drive and distortion and combine those stages. Detailed circuit-specific tonal claims remain undocumented.
