# BFFX Boutique Pedals - Guitarlab1

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Guitarlab1
- **Builder:** BFFX Boutique Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive
- **Identity:** BFFX GL1, a three-effect analog pedal combining booster, overdrive and distortion.

## What this pedal is
Guitarlab1 is a three-in-one analog pedal. Contemporary Brazilian coverage describes the booster, overdrive and distortion sections as usable independently or cascaded, allowing the effects to be combined. The pedal uses an anodized aluminum enclosure and was described as assembled with high-quality components.

## Controls and operation
- Three effect sections can operate alone or in cascade.
- Exact individual knob names and section control ranges were not reliably recoverable from the checked sources.
- **Bypass:** 100% true bypass according to the contemporary product write-up.
- **Power:** 9V, Boss-style polarity with positive exterior/negative center as reported in the Brazilian source.
- **Current draw:** Approximately 300mA with all effects engaged.

## Circuit / components
- Fully analog.
- Source material describes precision resistors, multilayer capacitors and Alpha potentiometers.
- Exact transistor and diode part numbers were not established.

## Sound
Because the three sections can be used separately or cascaded, the GL1 covers boost, overdrive, distortion and stacked combinations. The archive avoids assigning a named circuit lineage without documented evidence.
