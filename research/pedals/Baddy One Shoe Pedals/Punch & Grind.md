# Baddy One Shoe Pedals — Punch & Grind

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Punch & Grind
- **Builder:** Baddy One Shoe Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Dual-fuzz pedal combining an updated JunkPuncher-derived Grind section with a separate brighter Punch section; both sides can be used independently or cascaded. [1][2]

## Controls / architecture
- **PUNCH:** Dedicated brighter fuzz side.
- **GRIND:** Updated JunkPuncher-derived fuzz side.
- **TONE**
- **LEVEL**
- Two independent footswitches. [1]

## What this pedal is
Punch & Grind evolved the earlier JunkPuncher concept by widening the Grind section's usable range and adding a separate brighter Punch fuzz. The sections can be stacked for a denser, more saturated result. [1][2]

## Circuit lineage
- **Grind:** Derived from **The Junk Puncher**.
- **Punch:** Separate brighter fuzz stage.
- No exact transistor or diode recipe is publicly documented.

## Version history
- Punch & Grind is the documented evolution of JunkPuncher.
- The defining change is the addition of the Punch fuzz and expansion of the Grind range, not a numbered electronic revision. [1][2]

## Sound
The Grind section spans sputtery/gated and velcro-like textures. Punch is brighter and simpler. Stacking both sections yields a denser, more saturated fuzz sound. Baddy One Shoe also positioned the design for bass use. [1]
