# Baddy One Shoe Pedals - Punch & Grind

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Punch & Grind
- **Builder:** Baddy One Shoe Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive / Fuzz
- **Identity:** Dual-fuzz pedal combining an updated JunkPuncher-derived Grind circuit with a separate brighter Punch fuzz.

## What this pedal is
Punch & Grind is effectively two fuzzes in one enclosure. The Grind side updates the JunkPuncher fuzz with a wider usable fuzz range, while the Punch side is a simpler, brighter one-knob fuzz that can be used independently or cascaded into the Grind.

## Version changes
Punch & Grind is the evolved successor to the original JunkPuncher design. The Grind control was widened for more usable range, and the new Punch section added a separate brighter fuzz stage; the stock version kept the two fuzzes independently accessible.

## Sound
The Grind side ranges from sputtery, gated textures through strong velcro-like fuzz, while the Punch side is brighter and simpler. The two sections can be cascaded for a denser, saturated fuzz sound, and the builder specifically designed the pedal with bass players in mind.
