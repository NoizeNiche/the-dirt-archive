# Baddy One Shoe Pedals - The Junk Puncher

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** The Junk Puncher
- **Builder:** Baddy One Shoe Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Early Baddy One Shoe fuzz that became the basis for the later Grind section of Punch & Grind.

## What this pedal is
The JunkPuncher is an early Baddy One Shoe fuzz whose design was later updated into the Grind half of Punch & Grind. The builder's historical archive preserves dedicated JunkPuncher sound clips and identifies it alongside the Honeycomb as an early small-run production pedal.

## Version changes
Punch & Grind is the documented evolution of the JunkPuncher: its Grind section widened the usable fuzz range and added more extreme gated/velcro-like settings, while Punch became a separate brighter fuzz.
