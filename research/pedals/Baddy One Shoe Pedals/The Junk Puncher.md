# Baddy One Shoe Pedals — The Junk Puncher

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** The Junk Puncher
- **Builder:** Baddy One Shoe Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Early Baddy One Shoe fuzz that later became the foundation for the Grind section of Punch & Grind. [1][2]

## What this pedal is

The Junk Puncher is one of Baddy One Shoe's early fuzz designs. Later Punch & Grind documentation explicitly identifies the Grind side as an update of the JunkPuncher circuit with a wider usable range and more extreme gated/velcro textures. [1]
