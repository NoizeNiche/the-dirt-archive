# Baddy One Shoe Pedals - Heartbreaker

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Heartbreaker
- **Builder:** Baddy One Shoe Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Low-gain overdrive introduced in 2012 as the successor to the Honeycomb Overdrive.

## What this pedal is
The Heartbreaker was the next-generation Baddy One Shoe low-gain overdrive, retaining the Honeycomb's responsive low-drive character while adding a more souped-up gain control. The builder described it as equally suited to guitar and bass and suitable for both humbuckers and single coils.

## Colorways

## Version changes
The Heartbreaker replaced the Honeycomb in the 2012 lineup. Baddy One Shoe explicitly described it as occupying the same sonic neighborhood as Honeycomb, but with a slightly increased gain range and a move to the builder's newer PCB-based production process.

## Sound
The builder describes the Heartbreaker as a low-gain, dynamic and harmonically rich overdrive with organic breakup. It was designed for both guitar and bass and intended to preserve the responsive feel of an amp being pushed while offering more available grit than the Honeycomb.
