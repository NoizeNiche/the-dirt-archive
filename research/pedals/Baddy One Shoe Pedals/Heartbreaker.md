# Baddy One Shoe Pedals — Heartbreaker

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Heartbreaker
- **Builder:** Baddy One Shoe Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Low-gain overdrive introduced in 2012 as the successor to the Honeycomb, with a three-control layout and expanded gain range. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
Baddy One Shoe introduced Heartbreaker when retiring the Honeycomb. The builder positioned it in the same low-gain, amp-like territory but with somewhat more available drive. It was intended for both guitar and bass and for both humbuckers and single coils. [1]

## Controls
- **VOLUME**
- **TONE**
- **DRIVE**
The surviving documentation describes the pedal as a low-gain overdrive rather than a multi-mode dirt box. [1][2]

## Sound
Heartbreaker is described as dynamic, harmonically rich and responsive, with organic breakup and a slightly wider gain range than Honeycomb. Baddy One Shoe explicitly positioned it for both guitar and bass. [1][2]
