# Baddy One Shoe Pedals — Wayfarer

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** Wayfarer
- **Builder:** Baddy One Shoe Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Silicon/germanium hybrid overdrive using a silicon transistor and germanium diodes, with a Dirty boost switch and a documented transition from early veroboard toward PCB production. [1][2]

## What this pedal is

Wayfarer combines a solid-state silicon gain stage with germanium clipping, aiming for a warm, gritty small-tweed-amp-style overdrive. The builder explicitly documented both guitar and bass use. [1]

## Controls

- **DRIVE**
- **DIRTY** toggle
- Two-control core plus boost/voicing function. [1][2]

## Circuit architecture

- **Silicon transistor gain stage.**
- **Germanium diodes** in the clipping section.
- Dirty switch functions as an additional boost/drive state. [1]
- Exact transistor and diode part numbers are not documented.

## Version history

- Early construction used **vero-board**.
- In 2012 Jim McConnell announced a move toward **PCB** production and expected to tweak the design.
- The reviewed sources do not establish a final PCB revision or exact component changes. [1][2]

## Sound

Wayfarer is described as warm, gritty and amp-like, with the Dirty switch taking the pedal from a slight gritty edge toward stronger drive while retaining the core response. [1]
