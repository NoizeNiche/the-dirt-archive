# Baddy One Shoe Pedals - Wayfarer

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Wayfarer
- **Builder:** Baddy One Shoe Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Silicon/germanium hybrid overdrive designed around the warm, gritty response of a small tweed amplifier.

## What this pedal is
The Wayfarer combines a silicon transistor with germanium diodes and uses a deliberately simple two-control interface. The builder designed it to provide amp-like grit without overtaking the fundamental character of the guitar or bass.

## Version changes
No numbered factory revision was verified. In 2012 Jim McConnell said he was moving the Wayfarer design from vero-board to PCB and expected to tweak it, so later PCB builds may differ, but the checked sources do not establish a final production revision or exact component changes.

## Transistor
- **Technology:** Hybrid silicon/germanium overdrive.
- **Documented device:** Silicon transistor, exact part number not publicly documented.

## Diode
- **Documented device:** Germanium diodes.
- Exact part number and arrangement were not publicly documented.

## Sound
The Wayfarer is voiced for warm, gritty small-tweed-amp-style breakup with a dynamic response. The Dirty switch works as a boost, moving the pedal from a slight gritty edge toward more aggressive drive while preserving the core tone.
