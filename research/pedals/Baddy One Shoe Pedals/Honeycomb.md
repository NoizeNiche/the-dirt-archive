# Baddy One Shoe Pedals — Honeycomb

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** Honeycomb
- **Builder:** Baddy One Shoe Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Early Baddy One Shoe low-gain overdrive later retired in favor of the Heartbreaker. [1][2]

## What this pedal is

Honeycomb was voiced around the feel of a small tube amplifier being pushed into breakup. Baddy One Shoe deliberately retained substantial low end with the Nature control backed down and marketed the pedal for both guitar and bass. [1]

## Controls

- **VOLUME**
- **NATURE:** Tone/low-frequency shaping.
- **HONEY:** Gain amount. [1][2]

## Version history

- **2010:** Honeycomb documented in the builder's archive.
- **January 2012:** Builder announced retirement of Honeycomb in favor of Heartbreaker.
- Heartbreaker retained the general low-drive role with somewhat more gain. [1]

## Sound

The builder described Honeycomb as warm, responsive and low-gain, with a clean-amp-like feel and bass-friendly low-end retention. The source material specifically associates it with warm, Motown-leaning drive tones. [1]
