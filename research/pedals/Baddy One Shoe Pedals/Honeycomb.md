# Baddy One Shoe Pedals - Honeycomb

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Honeycomb
- **Builder:** Baddy One Shoe Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Original low-gain Baddy One Shoe overdrive later replaced by the Heartbreaker.

## What this pedal is
The Honeycomb was a low-gain overdrive designed around the feel of a tube amp being pushed into breakup. Baddy One Shoe specifically voiced it to retain bass when the tone control is backed off, making it usable for bass as well as guitar.

## Colorways
- Documented examples use **painted metal enclosures with custom water-slide decal artwork**.
- No complete factory colorway chronology was verified.

## Versions and factory options
### Honeycomb Overdrive
- Volume
- Nature, tone control
- Honey, gain
- Low-gain overdrive
- Bass-friendly voicing
- Small-run hand-built production

## Version changes
In January 2012, Baddy One Shoe announced that the Honeycomb would be retired in favor of the Heartbreaker. The Heartbreaker kept the same general low-drive character but added a slightly greater gain range; the earlier Honeycomb therefore remains a distinct historical model.

## Sound
The Honeycomb was voiced as a warm, responsive low-gain drive with a strong clean-amp-like feel. With the Nature control fully backed off it was designed to retain substantial low end, and the builder explicitly presented it as suitable for bass and capable of warm, Motown-leaning drive tones.
