## What this pedal is

Dark Horse is a VFE Pedals distortion model with a three-band active Baxandall tone stack and a compressor-style control that changes the dynamics and sustain of the gain stage.

## Sound

VFE describes the Dark Horse as capable of low-gain grit through much heavier distortion. The active Baxandall tone stack provides broad tonal shaping, while the COMP control changes the dynamic response from longer sustain toward more compressed, saturated behavior.
