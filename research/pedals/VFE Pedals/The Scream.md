# VFE Pedals — The Scream

## PRP identity

## What this pedal is
The Scream is VFE's extended take on the Tube Screamer-style overdrive format. The model keeps the familiar mid-focused drive concept but adds substantial low-frequency and filter control.

## Colorways
- VFE The Scream has appeared in multiple enclosure colors, including green/silver examples documented in exact-model listings.
- These finish differences are treated as cosmetic variants unless the circuit/product changes.

## Versions and factory options
- **Version 2** is documented.
- Added **FAT** control changes the bass content ahead of the drive stage.
- **FILTER** changes a low-pass section before the tone circuit.
- The FAT range is documented from approximately **65 to 720 Hz**.
- LED clipping is referenced in surviving model documentation.

## Version changes
- V2 expands the tonal range beyond the original mid-hump response by adding FAT and FILTER controls.
- No additional numbered factory revision was established from the available exact-model records.

## Transistor
- **Exact production transistor/device:** Not established.
## Diode
- **Exact clipping device:** LED clipping is documented; the exact production part number is not established.

## Sound
The Scream retains the focused, mid-forward overdrive behavior associated with the Tube Screamer family but can reduce the traditional bass cut with FAT and reshape the high end with FILTER. Those controls let it move from a conventional mid-humped overdrive into broader, less compressed tonal shapes.
