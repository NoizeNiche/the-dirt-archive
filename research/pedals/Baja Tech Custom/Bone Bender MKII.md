# Baja Tech Custom - Bone Bender MKII

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Baja Tech Custom
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Historical Bone Bender MKII.

## What this pedal is
A historical Baja Tech Custom product list explicitly names **Bone Bender MKII** as a separate model from the original Bone Bender and Bone Bender 1.5. The original Bone Bender is documented by Effects Database as a germanium Vox Tone Bender-style fuzz.

## Circuit / components
Exact MKII schematic and semiconductor complement were not independently verified. The archive does not assume that the MKII retained every component choice of the original Bone Bender.

## History
Bone Bender MKII is retained because the historical product list identifies it separately.

## Research confidence
- **Identity:** High.
- **Fuzz family:** High.
- **Exact MKII revision changes:** Unknown.

## Sound

The original Bone Bender's documented voicing ranges from traditional early Tone Bender fuzz through more splatty/gated textures using the KILL control. This is retained strictly as family context ; exact MKII voicing remains unresolved.

## Deep research verification

This pass separates the **Bone Bender MKII** identity from the original Bone Bender and records only the historical relationship that can be supported.

### Verified description

Baja Tech Custom's surviving historical product list explicitly names **Bone Bender MKII** as a separate product from Bone Bender and Bone Bender 1.5. Effects Database documents the original Bone Bender as a germanium, two-transistor Vox Tone Bender-style fuzz with a temperature-stabilization approach and KILL control.

### Verified version references

- Bone Bender MKII is a distinct named historical product in Baja Tech Custom's catalog.
- The surviving sources do not establish the exact revision date or a complete list of electrical changes from the original Bone Bender.
- The archive therefore does not copy the original model's circuit, controls, or components onto MKII.

### Verified transistor/device terms

- **Original family context:** germanium, two-transistor Bone Bender design.
- **Exact MKII transistor part number:** Unknown.

### Verified diode terms

- **Exact MKII clipping/rectifier diode:** Unknown.

### Verified sound evidence

The original Bone Bender's documented voicing ranges from traditional early Tone Bender fuzz through more splatty/gated textures using the KILL control. This is retained strictly as **family context**; exact MKII voicing remains unresolved.
