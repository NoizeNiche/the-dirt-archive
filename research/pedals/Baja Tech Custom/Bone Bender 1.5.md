## Deep research verification

This pass separates the **Bone Bender 1.5** identity from the original Bone Bender rather than inheriting the original circuit specification.

### Verified description

Baja Tech Custom's surviving historical product list explicitly names **Bone Bender 1.5** as a separate product from Bone Bender and Bone Bender MKII. The original Bone Bender documentation identifies the Bone Bender family as a germanium, two-transistor Vox Tone Bender-style fuzz. That family relationship is useful context, but the archive does not assume the 1.5 uses the identical circuit or component set.

### Verified version references

- Bone Bender 1.5 is documented as its own named Baja Tech Custom product.
- The historical product list places it between the original Bone Bender and the later Bone Bender MKII.
- No direct exact-1.5 documentation was located that establishes the revision's circuit changes, controls, or transistor part numbers.

### Verified transistor/device terms

- **Family/device context:** germanium is established for the Bone Bender family.
- **Exact Bone Bender 1.5 transistor part number:** Unknown.

### Verified diode terms

- **Exact Bone Bender 1.5 clipping/rectifier diode:** Unknown.

### Verified sound evidence

The original Bone Bender is described by Effects Database as a traditional-voiced germanium Vox Tone Bender-style fuzz with a KILL control that can move from gain-boost-like behavior through splatty textures to the stock-style fuzz character. Those sonic details are retained as **family context only** and are not presented as proof of the 1.5's exact voicing.
