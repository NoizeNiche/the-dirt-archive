# Baja Tech Custom — Das Fuzz Ge

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Das Fuzz GE
- **Builder:** Baja Tech Custom
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Handmade germanium fuzz with Volume, Fuzz and KILL controls, positive-center power and a bias-shifting KILL function. Audiofanzine documents it explicitly as the Das Fuzz GE. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
The Das Fuzz GE is a compact, handmade germanium fuzz rooted in the Fuzz Face family. Audiofanzine's user reviews describe the basic sound as fat, creamy and vintage-oriented, with strong response to guitar-volume changes. [1]

## Controls
- **FUZZ:** Fuzz/distortion amount.
- **VOLUME:** Output level.
- **KILL:** Bias-related control that acts as a boost at part of its rotation and can starve the circuit toward a dying-battery-style texture. [1][2]

Audiofanzine describes the earlier two-knob state as the baseline, with KILL added on the later GE presentation. [1]

## Sound
The Das Fuzz GE is described as fat, creamy and vintage-styled, with useful cleanup from the guitar's volume control. The KILL circuit changes the bias and can produce more strained, starved or dying-battery-like textures. [1][2]
