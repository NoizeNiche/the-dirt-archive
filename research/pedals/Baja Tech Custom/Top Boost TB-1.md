# Baja Tech Custom — Top Boost TB-1

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Top Boost TB-1

- **Identity:** Baja Tech Custom's recreation of the beefier treble-boost/overdrive approach associated with Brian May's modified Vox Top Boost setup. [1]

## What this pedal is
Effects Database describes the historical TB-1 as a recreation of the beefier treble-booster approach developed around Brian May's Vox AC-30 rig. Baja Tech's version adds a **Level control** and **true bypass**, turning the circuit into a more flexible boost/overdrive pedal rather than a simple fixed treble booster. [1]

## Controls / operation
- **LEVEL:** Added output-level control.
- Treble-boost/overdrive circuit derived from the original Top Boost concept.
- True bypass. [1]

## Circuit lineage
