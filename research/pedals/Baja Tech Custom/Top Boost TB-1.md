# Baja Tech Custom - Top Boost TB-1

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Top Boost TB-1
- **Builder:** Baja Tech Custom
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Brian May-inspired treble-boost/overdrive recreation based on the Vox Top Boost/treble-boost concept.

## What this pedal is
The Top Boost TB-1 recreates the beefier treble-boost approach associated with Brian May's early Vox AC-30 setup. Baja Tech modified the basic recreation with an added Level control and true-bypass switching, making it function as an overdrive as well as a boost.

## Sound
The design is aimed at the bright, sustaining boost-to-overdrive behavior associated with a treble booster pushing a Vox-style amp. Baja Tech describes the TB-1 as having more body and gain than a conventional treble-only booster, with enough output control to function as an overdrive.
