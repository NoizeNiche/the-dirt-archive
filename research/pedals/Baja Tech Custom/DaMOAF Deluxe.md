## Deep research verification

This pass verifies the Deluxe model's historical identity and its relationship to the standard Da MOAF without inventing undocumented Deluxe-specific changes.

### Verified description

Effects Database lists **Da MOAF Deluxe** separately from **Da MOAF** in Baja Tech Custom's historical catalog. A surviving Audiofanzine review of the standard Da MOAF identifies the model as a Big Muff-style fuzz, with three large controls: **Volume, Sustain, and Tone**, true-bypass switching, and 9V battery operation. The review also describes the standard pedal as a relatively versatile fuzz ranging from fat sounds toward more aggressive textures as Tone is advanced.

### Verified version references

- Da MOAF Deluxe is a separate named product in the historical Baja Tech Custom catalog.
- The surviving standard-model review does not document the electrical differences of the Deluxe.
- The archive therefore does not assume the Deluxe has identical controls or internal topology to the standard Da MOAF.

### Verified transistor/device terms

- **Exact Deluxe transistor/device:** Unknown.

### Verified diode terms

### Verified sound evidence

The standard Da MOAF is independently described as a Big Muff-style fuzz with strong control response and a wide tonal range. That description is retained as **family context**, not as a substitute for a Deluxe-specific sonic record.
