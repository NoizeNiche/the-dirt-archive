# Baja Tech Custom - DaMOAF Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** DaMOAF Fuzz
- **Builder:** Baja Tech Custom
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Fuzz / Overdrive
- **Identity:** Big Muff-family fuzz with Triangle/Ram's Head lineage and multiple gain textures.

## What this pedal is
DaMOAF, or “Da Moaf,” is a Baja Tech Custom fuzz derived from the Big Muff family. Historical descriptions characterize it as a cleaner, more versatile Muff-style design that can move from overdrive-like textures through sweet fuzz and into palm-muted crunch and sustained wall-of-fuzz distortion.

## Versions and factory options
### DaMOAF V1
- Volume
- Sustain
- Tone
- True bypass
- 9 V battery operation
- Big Muff-family topology

### DaMOAF Deluxe / V2
- Added gain/boost footswitch
- Fine Tune control
- Dual low-/high-gain stages

## Version changes
The Deluxe V2 added a dedicated gain/boost switch and Fine Tune control, allowing the low- and high-gain stages to be balanced more precisely. These additions distinguish the Deluxe from the earlier three-control DaMOAF.

## Sound
Historical descriptions place DaMOAF across a wide Muff-derived range, from blues/rock overdrive and sweet 1960s-style fuzz through palm-muted crunch and dense sustained fuzz. The Deluxe's added boost/gain section extends the usable range toward both lower-gain drive and more saturated textures.
