# Baja Tech Custom - Bone Bender

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Bone Bender
- **Builder:** Baja Tech Custom
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Two-transistor germanium Vox Tone Bender-style fuzz with temperature stabilization and a KILL control.

## What this pedal is
The Bone Bender is a germanium Vox Tone Bender recreation that uses hand-selected transistors and a stabilization method intended to reduce the temperature drift common to germanium fuzzes. Baja Tech added modern conveniences including true bypass, external DC power, and the KILL control.

## Version changes
Historical coverage documents an earlier/discontinued compact Bone Bender circuit and a later form with the KILL control. The checked sources do not provide a complete official revision chronology, so only the documented KILL-control-era production is described here.

## Sound
The traditional voicing targets early Vox Tone Bender sounds, while KILL can move the response from a more stock character toward added gain and the splatty, mosquito-like textures of early fuzzes. The stabilization approach is intended to keep the character more consistent as the pedal heats and cools.

### Verified sound evidence
Our transistors are hand picked to get you that magic tone of the original two transistor Vox Tone Bender (Not to be confused with the Sola Sound Tone Bender MKII or the MKI.
The Bone Bender gets the early Tone Bender tones of yesteryear but with modern conveniences.
