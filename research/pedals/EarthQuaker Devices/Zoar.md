## What this pedal is
Zoar is a medium-to-high-gain discrete distortion built entirely around transistors. EarthQuaker Devices describes it as a touch-sensitive, amp-like distortion with a hi-fi response and extensive tonal control.

## Versions and factory options
- Discrete transistor-only gain architecture.
- EarthQuaker's Flexi-Switch technology.
- Passive and active tone-shaping controls provide extensive frequency adjustment.
- Designed for guitar, bass, and extended-range instruments.
- Standard 9V DC negative-center operation.

## Sound
Zoar covers medium to high gain with a responsive, less-compressed feel. EarthQuaker emphasizes a modern hi-fi character combined with older discrete-circuit behavior, making it suitable for riffing, lead work, and lower-tuned instruments.
