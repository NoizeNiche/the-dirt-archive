## Sound

The Hoof keeps the thick sustain and fuzz weight of that general family, but adds one control that changes the whole experience: Shift .
Instead of living with one baked-in mid character, you can move the response from more scooped to more mid-forward.
That turns the Hoof from “another Muff-inspired fuzz” into a much easier pedal to fit around different amps, guitars and rooms.
