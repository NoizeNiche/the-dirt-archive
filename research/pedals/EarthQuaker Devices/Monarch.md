## What this pedal is
The Monarch is an all-discrete, FET-based overdrive designed around the input stage of a vintage Orange amplifier. EarthQuaker Devices' historical description emphasizes a natural, relatively uncompressed response and a large low end with a bright top.

## Sound
The Monarch is voiced for higher-gain overdrive while retaining a relatively open, natural response. Its Bass and Treble controls operate as frequency boosters rather than conventional cut-only tone controls. Historical descriptions emphasize strong low-end retention and a bright top end.
