## Sound

The White Light is a hard clipping, vintage-voiced overdrive that takes its cues from the vintage dirt pedals that defined the sounds of heavy rock.
Think OD250 or Distortion+ without being overly derivative, a new voice to an olde sound if you will.
We think you’ll find that it is much more dynamic, open and harmonically rich than these classic overdrives it shares its lineage with.
