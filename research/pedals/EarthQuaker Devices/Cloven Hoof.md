## What this pedal is

The **EarthQuaker Devices Cloven Hoof** is a **Fuzz Grinder** derived from the Hoof concept. EarthQuaker Devices describes the design as keeping the tightness and clarity associated with the Hoof while replacing its earlier architecture with **four specially selected silicon transistors** for higher gain, improved temperature stability, and a grittier fuzz voice. [1]

The archive keeps **Fuzz** as the primary taxonomy.

## Versions

The reviewed evidence references **V2**. No complete factory revision chronology was established, so V2 is preserved as an observed version reference rather than expanded into a complete sequence.

## Technical evidence

The manufacturer explicitly identifies **four specially selected silicon transistors** as part of the Cloven Hoof design. [1]

The archive does not infer exact transistor part numbers or a complete production BOM from that statement.

## Catalog naming

The archive also contains a record named **Cloven Hoof - Fuzz Grinder**. Because EarthQuaker Devices itself uses “Cloven Hoof Fuzz Grinder” as the product name, the second record is likely an expanded naming alias rather than a separate pedal. [1]
