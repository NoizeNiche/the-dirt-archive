## What this pedal is
The **Dr. Scientist Frazz Dazzler** is a fuzz pedal whose documented design goal includes retaining useful response with bass instruments. A 2013 Bass Musician Magazine review specifically discusses the problem of fuzz pedals with active basses and presents Frazz Dazzler as a fuzz that addresses that use case. [1]

The evidence also documents a **Mix** control, allowing the processed fuzz signal to be blended with the original signal. [1]

## Historical context
