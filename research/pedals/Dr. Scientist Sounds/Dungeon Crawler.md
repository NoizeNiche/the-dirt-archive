## What this pedal is
The **Dr. Scientist Dungeon Crawler** is an **extreme-metal, high-gain distortion** designed around unusually high-gain MOSFET clipping stages. Contemporary coverage reports **three very-high-gain 2N7000 MOSFET clipping stages**, producing heavy saturation and long sustain. [1][2]

The product also includes **active gyrator EQ** plus additional **Grind, Rectifier, and Boost** functions, according to contemporary coverage. [2]

## Indicator and component evidence
The existing evidence packet notes two LEDs that shift from **amber toward brighter red with increasing input gain**. That is retained as a documented hardware/indicator behavior, not a claim about clipping components. [2]

The **2N7000** references are exact-model secondary technical evidence. The archive does not extrapolate them into a complete universal BOM without factory documentation. [2]
