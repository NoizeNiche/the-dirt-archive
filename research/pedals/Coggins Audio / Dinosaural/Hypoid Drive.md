# Coggins Audio / Dinosaural — Hypoid Drive

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Hypoid Drive
- **Builder:** Coggins Audio / Dinosaural
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Dinosaural Hypoid Drive, the standalone Drive section of Cogmeister.

## What this pedal is
Coggins Audio states that Hypoid Drive is identical to the central **Drive** section of Cogmeister. Effects Database describes it as a transistor-based overdrive with symmetrical and asymmetrical transistor clipping rather than diode clipping. [1][2]

## Versions and factory options
- Volume.
- Sustain.
- Tone.
- Three-position Shift toggle.
- Transistor-only clipping architecture. [1]

The Shift positions provide flat mids, a moderate scoop, and a deeper scoop. [1]

## Version changes
Hypoid Drive is a standalone deconstruction of Cogmeister's central Drive section, and therefore inherits that section's circuit identity. [2]

## Transistor
The Drive section uses a **transistor-only** gain/clipping architecture. [1][2]

## Diode
No diode clipping is used as the principal clipping mechanism. [2]

## Sound
Hypoid covers subtle breakup through saturated drive and fuzz-flavored distortion. The Tone control is a passive bass/treble balance and Shift alters the midrange contour. [1]

## Historical note
Coggins Audio's current site presents Hypoid as part of the standalone-gain-stage family derived from Cogmeister. [2]
