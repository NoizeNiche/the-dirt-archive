# Coggins Audio / Dinosaural — Cogmeister

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Cogmeister
- **Builder:** Coggins Audio / Dinosaural
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive / Boost / Fuzz
- **Identity:** Dinosaural Cogmeister, a three-stage gain-staging pedal combining Push, Drive, and Solo sections.

## What this pedal is
Cogmeister is a triple gain-stager developed by Dan Coggins and later produced commercially with ThorpyFX assistance. The fixed series chain comprises a late-1960s-style Push booster, a transistor Drive section, and a Solo midrange booster. [1]

## Versions and factory options
- Push: single-transistor upper-mid/treble booster with GAIN and LOW bandwidth controls.
- Drive: SUS, VOL, TONE, and three-position SHIFT voicing.
- Solo: PEAK and LEV controls.
- Three independent mechanical bypass switches.
- 9V battery or 9V DC input, up to 12V.
- 7–16 mA at 9V. [1]

## Version changes
Coggins Audio states that Cogmeister supersedes the discontinued Tube Bender and OPA-101 with a new Drive circuit and adds Push and Solo sections around it. [2]

## Transistor
The complete circuit is **all-discrete, Class-A, silicon transistor** circuitry. [1]

## Diode
The Drive section does not use diode clipping. Dan Coggins describes transistor-based symmetrical and asymmetrical clipping; the Push stage also uses transistor clipping. [3]

## Sound
Cogmeister covers a very wide range from boost and transparent-ish drive through overdrive, distortion, and fuzz-like sounds. The Drive section is a reworked Tube Bender-derived design, while the Push stage can drive the subsequent stages harder and the Solo stage adds focused upper-mid gain. [1][3]
