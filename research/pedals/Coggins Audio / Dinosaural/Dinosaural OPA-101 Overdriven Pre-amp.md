# Coggins Audio / Dinosaural — OPA-101 Overdriven Pre-amp

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Dinosaural OPA-101 Overdriven Pre-amp
- **Builder:** Coggins Audio / Dinosaural
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Dinosaural OPA-101, a rare overdrive/preamp developed as an updated Tube Bender.

## What this pedal is
Aion FX's traced documentation identifies OPA-101 as a rare Dan Coggins overdrive originally released in November 2012, with production estimated at only a few hundred units. It was developed from the earlier Tube Bender. [1][2]

## Versions and factory options
The traced circuit preserves the middle two-transistor amplifier stage from Tube Bender but substitutes op-amp input and output stages. The original OPA-101 used a fixed treble cut rather than a Tone potentiometer. It also included a JFET buffered-bypass option. [2]

## Version changes
OPA-101 is documented as an updated Tube Bender rather than a numbered Tube Bender revision. Coggins Audio's current history states that Cogmeister later superseded both OPA-101 and Tube Bender. [3]

## Transistor
Aion FX's trace identifies:
- Q1: **J113 JFET**
- Q2: **BC549C**
- Q3: **BC549C**
- IC1: **TL072CN** op-amp. [2]

## Diode
The traced circuit identifies **1N914** signal diodes and a **1N4001** protection diode. These are trace-derived component identifications, not a factory-published parts list. [2]

## Sound
The OPA-101 combines the Dinosaural transistor-clipping core with op-amp input/output stages. Its heritage is the Tube Bender, but with a more modern preamp/buffer architecture and a fixed treble cut. [2]

## Historical note
Aion FX estimates only a few hundred original OPA-101 units were made. [1]
