# Coggins Audio / Dinosaural — Tube Bender

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Dinosaural Tube Bender
- **Builder:** Coggins Audio / Dinosaural
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive / Distortion
- **Identity:** Dinosaural Tube Bender, an all-discrete Class-A transistor overdrive capable of reaching fuzz.

## What this pedal is
Dan Coggins described Tube Bender as a purpose-designed low-noise Class-A discrete transistor circuit designed to provide tube-like harmonic response without a tube. Effects Database records its release in late 2003. [1][2]

## Versions and factory options
- Drive.
- Tone.
- Level.
- Tone-range press switch.
- Mechanical true bypass.
- 9V battery or external DC.
- Tip-positive 3.5 mm mini-jack input in the documented original specification. [1]

## Version changes
The OPA-101 was later developed as an updated version of Tube Bender. Cogmeister subsequently superseded both earlier models. [3]

## Transistor
The circuit is **all-discrete Class-A transistor** circuitry. The checked manufacturer/database sources do not securely publish every original transistor part number.

## Diode
No diode-clipping stage is documented as the principal distortion mechanism.

## Sound
Tube Bender ranges from subtle overdrive and amp boosting through rich sustaining fuzz. The Drive control crosses that range gradually, while Tone and the guitar controls shape the harmonic balance. Coggins specifically designed it to retain the character of the guitar. [1]
