## What this pedal is
Creamy Fuzz is a handmade analog silicon fuzz from French builder SBGO Effects. SBGO explicitly describes it as an original fuzz rather than an attempt to clone a germanium circuit, using more stable standard silicon components.

## Colorways

## Versions and factory options
- Two fuzz modes:
  - **Soft**
  - **Hard**
- Controls:
  - **Input Gain**
  - **Fuzz**
  - **Volume**
- True bypass.
- Standard SBGO power is 9 V battery or standard BOSS-style adapter.

## Sound
SBGO describes the Creamy Fuzz as rounded and balanced rather than sharp or muddy, with a stable response that avoids the temperament of some vintage germanium designs. The Soft/Hard modes provide two fuzz characters while Input Gain, Fuzz, and Volume determine the amount and output.
