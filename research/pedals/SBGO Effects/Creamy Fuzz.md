## What this pedal is
Creamy Fuzz is an analog silicon fuzz designed by French builder SBGO Effects. The manufacturer describes it as an original fuzz rather than a germanium clone, using standard silicon components for a more stable response.

## Colorways
- Standard SBGO handmade enclosure with etched/painted artwork.
- SBGO notes that unique finishes can be requested on its pedals, but those cosmetic finishes are not separate catalog models.

## Versions and factory options
- **Creamy Fuzz:** two switchable fuzz modes, **Soft** and **Hard**.
- Controls: **Input Gain**, **Fuzz**, and **Volume**.
- **True bypass**.
- Standard SBGO power: 9V battery or standard BOSS-style adapter.

## Sound
SBGO describes the Creamy Fuzz as rounded and balanced, intentionally avoiding the sharper or unstable behavior associated with some vintage germanium designs. The Soft/Hard modes provide two levels of fuzz response, while Input Gain, Fuzz, and Volume shape the amount and output.
