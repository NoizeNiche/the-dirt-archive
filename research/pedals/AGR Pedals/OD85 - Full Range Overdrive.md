## What this pedal is
OD85 is designed to preserve the complete character of the instrument signal while adding controllable overdrive. Its three-control layout provides Gain, Tone and Volume, with the circuit emphasizing clarity, definition and response to playing intensity. [1]

## Sound
OD85 is voiced toward transparent boost and full-range overdrive rather than strong mid coloration. Its dynamic response is intended to let picking intensity change the amount and character of saturation without obscuring articulation. [1]
