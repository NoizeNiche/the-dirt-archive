## What this pedal is
Effects Database describes OD85 as an analog overdrive intended to preserve the full character of the instrument signal while adding controllable drive. Rather than selectively emphasizing or compressing one part of the spectrum, its design is presented as a full-range, clarity-oriented boost/overdrive with a strong dynamic response. [1]

The control layout is Gain, Tone, and Volume, providing a straightforward way to set drive amount, tonal balance, and output level. [1]

## Sound
OD85 is aimed at transparent boost and full-range overdrive, keeping the instrument's natural character prominent instead of heavily imposing a fixed mid-focused coloration. Its dynamic response is intended to let picking intensity affect the amount and character of saturation while keeping articulation and definition intact. [1]
