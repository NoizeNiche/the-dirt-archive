## What this pedal is
Effects Database describes DS2610 as an analog distortion whose character changes noticeably with playing intensity and the instrument's volume control. The design is intended to move from restrained, touch-sensitive breakup toward heavier, more saturated distortion while retaining some of the interactive behavior associated with classic fuzz circuits. [1]

Its four controls are Attack, Tone, Volume, and a Heavy Landing switch. Effects Database describes the switch as changing how firmly the signal is grounded and shaped, expanding the distortion response toward heavier landing characteristics. [1]

## Sound
DS2610 is voiced for a broad distortion range, from restrained and touch-sensitive breakup to heavier, more saturated sounds. Its response is intentionally interactive with picking dynamics and the guitar's volume control, giving the player a direct way to shape saturation, texture, and clarity. [1]

The Heavy Landing switch provides another axis of response, changing the grounding/weight behavior of the distortion rather than simply acting as a second gain control. [1]
