## What this pedal is
DS2610 is an analog distortion designed to react strongly to playing intensity and guitar-volume changes. Its control set combines Attack, Tone and Volume with a Heavy Landing switch that changes the grounding/response behavior of the distortion stage. [1]

## Sound
DS2610 is designed to cover a broad range from restrained, touch-sensitive breakup through heavier saturation. Its response is intentionally interactive with picking dynamics and the guitar's volume control, giving it some of the behavior associated with classic fuzz circuits while remaining cataloged as distortion.
