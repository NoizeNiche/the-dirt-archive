## What this pedal is
Effects Database describes FZR912 as an analog fuzz inspired by classic Muff Fuzz Deluxe and Fuzz Face circuits. AGR uses selected silicon transistors and custom component values, tuning each unit for its response and intended tonal range rather than treating every build as electrically identical. [1]

The four controls are Attack, Bias, Tone, and Volume. The Bias control changes the transistor operating point and therefore affects texture, gating, and sustain, while Attack controls the intensity and character of the fuzz response. [1]

## Sound
FZR912 is designed to span aggressive, gated, Velcro-style fuzz through warmer, smoother vintage-oriented fuzz tones. Its soft-clipping description emphasizes a balanced response that avoids excessively harsh compression, while Bias and Attack provide direct control over gating, sustain, intensity, and overall fuzz character.
