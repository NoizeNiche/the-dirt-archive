## What this pedal is
FZR912 is an individually tuned silicon fuzz. AGR describes the design as covering aggressive gated/Velcro textures through warmer vintage fuzz, with Attack, Bias, Tone and Volume providing control over intensity, transistor operating point, tonal balance and output. [1]

## Sound
FZR912 is designed to range from gated, Velcro-like fuzz through smoother vintage saturation. Bias changes gating and sustain by shifting the transistor operating point, while Attack changes the intensity and character of the fuzz response. [1]
