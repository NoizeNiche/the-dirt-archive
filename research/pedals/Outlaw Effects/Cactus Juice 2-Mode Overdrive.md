## What this pedal is
Cactus Juice is a two-mode overdrive from Outlaw Effects. The documented factory modes are **Normal** and **Juiced**, with the latter adding more gain and drive character.

## Sound
The Normal mode provides the base overdrive voice, while Juiced adds more gain and a thicker, more saturated response. The three-knob control layout keeps the pedal straightforward while the two-mode switch supplies the main shift in character.
