# Axewerx - Glass Drive

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Glass Drive
- **Builder:** Axewerx
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Fuzz / Overdrive
- **Identity:** Hand-built dual-stage octave dirt pedal combining octave-up harmonic distortion with selectable -1/-2 octave subharmonic processing.

## What this pedal is
Axewerx describes Glass Drive as a custom-made analog pedal with one digital chip, built around two independent octave dirt paths. The Drop side provides -1/-2 octaves, while Lift provides octave-up harmonic distortion with a bypassable octave function.

## Versions and factory options
### Glass Drive production
- Dual-stage octave dirt
- Drop side with selectable -1 and -2 octaves
- Lift side with octave-up harmonic distortion
- Independent or stacked operation
- Buffered input
- Active mixer
- FILTER tone shaping
- FOCUS path control and clean blending
- SMASH master output
- Buffered bypass
- 9 VDC center-negative power

### Internal option
Pedal of the Day reports an internal DIP switch for selecting the Drop side's -1 or -2 octave behavior.

## Sound
Glass Drive combines octave-up harmonic dirt with sub-octave rumble and allows the two paths to be used independently or stacked. The Filter, Focus, and Smash controls shape the combined result from octave fuzz textures to heavier layered octave distortion.
