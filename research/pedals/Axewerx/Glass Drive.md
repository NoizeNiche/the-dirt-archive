# Axewerx — Glass Drive

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** Glass Drive
- **Builder:** Axewerx
- **Catalog type:** Octave / Overdrive / Distortion / Fuzz
- **Identity:** Hand-built dual-stage octave dirt pedal combining an octave-down DROP section with selectable -1/-2 octaves and an octave-up LIFT section, with buffered input and active mixing. [1][2]

## What this pedal is

Axewerx describes Glass Drive as an analog pedal with one digital chip, built around two different octave dirt paths. DROP produces -1 or -2 octaves; LIFT produces octave-up harmonic distortion and can bypass the octave function while retaining dirt. [1][2]

## Controls / architecture

- **DROP:** Octave-down path.
- Internal DIP selects **-1 or -2 octaves**.
- **LIFT:** Octave-up harmonic distortion.
- LIFT octave can be toggled on/off.
- **FILTER:** Tone shaping.
- **FOCUS:** Trims each effect path and blends clean signal.
- **SMASH:** Master output level.
- Buffered input.
- Active mixer stage.
- The two channels can run independently or together. [1][2]

## Construction / power

- Breadboarded, refined and assembled by hand.
- Custom PCB.
- Separate power/I/O board from effect circuit.
- UV-printed graphics.

The builder states individual units are measured and tested before shipment. [1]

## Sound

The pedal combines octave-up and sub-octave dirt, allowing either path independently or both stacked. FILTER sets tone, FOCUS restores definition/clean blend, and SMASH controls the final output. Recent 2026 demonstrations emphasize its ability to layer an octave-up voice over a -1/-2 octave foundation. [1][2]
