# Celestial Effects — Capricorn - Rock Distortion

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Capricorn - Rock Distortion
- **Builder:** Celestial Effects
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** Celestial Effects Capricorn Rock Distortion, a dual-gain-stage boost/overdrive/distortion pedal with selectable clipping.

## What this pedal is
Celestial Effects describes the Capricorn as a versatile boost, overdrive, and distortion pedal using two independently controllable gain stages. The first is a transistor-based gain stage with a fuzz-like character, while the second adds further gain and clipping. [1][2]

The Capricorn is true bypass, uses no buffers or inline tone circuits, and was hand built by Celestial Effects. [1]

## Colorways

## Versions and factory options
The Effects Database listing documents four controls: **Vol, Limit, Edge, and Gain**. [2]

The pedal also has a three-position clipping selector:
- Symmetrical silicon diodes.
- Asymmetrical MOSFET clipping.
- No clipping diodes. [1]

No formal numbered V1/V2 sequence was established.

## Version changes
No reliable numbered production revision history was found in the checked sources.

## Transistor
Celestial Effects explicitly describes the first gain stage as **transistor based**, but the checked sources do not securely establish a transistor part number. [1][2]

## Diode
The clipping selector uses **symmetrical silicon diodes** in one position and **asymmetrical MOSFET clipping** in another; the center position removes the clipping diodes from the signal path. [1]

## Sound
Celestial Effects positions the Capricorn from creamy boost and overdrive through very high-gain distortion. The transistor gain stage contributes a bright, edgy, slightly fuzz-like character, while the clipping selector changes compression and openness. [1][2]
