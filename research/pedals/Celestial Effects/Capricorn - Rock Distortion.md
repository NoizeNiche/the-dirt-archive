# Celestial Effects — Capricorn - Rock Distortion

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** Capricorn - Rock Distortion
- **Builder:** Celestial Effects
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** Dual-gain-stage analog rock distortion with selectable symmetrical Silicon, asymmetrical MOSFET or no-diode clipping. [1][2]

## Controls

- **VOL**
- **LIMIT**
- **EDGE**
- **GAIN**
- Three-position clipping selector. [1][2]

## Circuit / topology

- First gain stage is transistor-based and contributes a fuzz-like edge.
- Second gain stage adds further gain/clipping.
- Three clipping modes:
  1. **Symmetrical silicon diodes**
  2. **Asymmetrical MOSFET clipping**
  3. **No clipping diodes** [1]

## Sound

Celestial Effects positions Capricorn from creamy boost/overdrive into high-gain rock distortion. The clipping selector changes compression and openness, while the Edge/Limit controls shape the feel around the two gain stages. [1][2]
