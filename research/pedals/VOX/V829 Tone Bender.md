The VOX Showroom - The Vox V829 Tone Bender .....
The Vox V829 Tone Bender "Fuzz" was one of first products introduced under the Korg ownership of the Vox brand.
The V829 was based on the circuitry of the V828 Tone Bender that was produced during the Thomas Organ ownership period of Vox (1965 - 1971).
