## What this pedal is
The Vox V828 Tone Bender is a germanium fuzz pedal produced in Italy by Jen for Vox and distributed by Thomas Organ in the United States during the late 1960s and early 1970s.

## Colorways
- Some of these pedals were finished in black "crinkle coat" paint while others were adorned with gray hammertone.
