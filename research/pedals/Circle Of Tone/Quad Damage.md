# Circle Of Tone — Quad Damage

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Quad Damage
- **Builder:** Circle Of Tone
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive
- **Identity:** Circle Of Tone Quad Damage, a medium-to-high-gain analog overdrive with three main controls and midrange shaping.

## What this pedal is
A 2025 review documents the exact Circle Of Tone Quad Damage as a compact medium-to-high-gain overdrive with an amp-in-a-box character. The reviewer identifies Volume, Gain, and Tone controls plus a separate Mid control that can sweep or boost the midrange. [1]

The same review identifies the pedal as made in Canada. [1]

## Sound
The 2025 review characterizes Quad Damage as medium-to-high-gain, warm, tight, punchy, and capable of a “wall of sound” type response, with strong string definition. These are attributed reviewer observations rather than a manufacturer technical specification. [1]
