## What this pedal is
One Thousand Pedals documents Circle Of Tone Distortion as an analog **Hello Kitty Series** pedal with Distortion, Volume, and Tone controls. [1]

## Versions and factory options

- Distortion control.
- Volume control.
- Tone control.
- Single footswitch.
- True bypass.
- Mono input/output.
- 9V DC center-negative.
- 50 mA current draw.
- Mini enclosure, approximately 9.5 x 4 x 3 cm. [1]
