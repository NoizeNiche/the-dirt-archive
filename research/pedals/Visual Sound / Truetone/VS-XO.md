## What this pedal is
The VS-XO is a true dual-overdrive pedal with two independent overdrive sections in one enclosure. The manufacturer manual describes it as two separate pedals sharing a housing, with independent Drive, Tone, and Volume controls plus additional bass, clean-mix, and clipping controls.

## Colorways
- Standard Visual Sound VS-XO enclosure and graphics.
- No distinct factory colorway split was established in the sources reviewed.

## Versions and factory options
- Two independent overdrive channels.
- One channel includes a **Clean Mix** control that blends from clean boost to full overdrive.
- A **Clipping** selector offers three documented voices: no diodes, two LEDs, or three signal diodes.
- A three-position **Bass** selector changes low-frequency emphasis.

## Diode
- The manufacturer documents three clipping options on the VS-XO: no diode clipping, LED clipping, and signal-diode clipping. Exact production part numbers are not specified in the manual.

## Sound
The two channels cover distinct overdrive roles, while the clean-mix, bass, and clipping controls let the pedal move from low-gain organic drive into brighter or more saturated voices. The manual's clipping selector ranges from a low-gain no-diode character through LED clipping to higher-gain asymmetrical signal-diode clipping.
