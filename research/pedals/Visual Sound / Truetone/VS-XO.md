# Visual Sound / Truetone — VS-XO

## What this pedal is
The VS-XO is a true dual-overdrive pedal that places two independent overdrive circuits in one enclosure. The manufacturer manual treats the two sides as separate pedals sharing the same housing, with independent Drive, Tone, and Volume controls plus additional bass, clean-mix, and clipping controls.

## Colorways
- Standard black VS-XO enclosure with Visual Sound graphics.
- No separate factory colorway established as a distinct model.

## Versions and factory options
- Two independent overdrive sections.
- **Overdrive 1** adds a Clean Mix control that moves from fully clean boost to fully overdriven.
- **Clipping** selector:
  - A: no diode clipping
  - B: two LEDs
  - C: three signal diodes
- **Bass** selector provides three low-frequency settings:
  - A: slight bass cut
  - B: slight bass boost
  - C: larger bass boost
- True-bypass switching.

## Controls
- Overdrive 1: Drive, Tone, Volume, Clean Mix.
- Overdrive 2: Drive, Tone, Volume.
- Shared/additional controls: Bass, Clipping selector, Bass selector.

## Sound
The two channels cover different overdrive roles inside the same pedal, with one side providing a continuously adjustable clean-to-drive blend. The three-way clipping and bass controls let the VS-XO move from low-gain organic drive through brighter LED clipping into smoother, higher-gain asymmetrical diode clipping.

## Deep research verification
The manufacturer manual explicitly describes the VS-XO as two separate overdrive pedals in one housing and documents the Clean Mix, Clipping, and Bass controls and their three-position ranges. It also identifies true-bypass switching and the three clipping diode configurations.
