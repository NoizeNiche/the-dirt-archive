# ColdCraft Effects — Sweet Kiwi MkII

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Sweet Kiwi MkII
- **Builder:** ColdCraft Effects
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz / Overdrive
- **Identity:** ColdCraft Effects Sweet Kiwi MkII, a revised saturated OD/fuzz derived from the 2012 Sweet Kiwi Custom Shop fuzz.

## What this pedal is
Effects Database documents Sweet Kiwi MkII as the revised version of a limited-edition 2012 fuzz. The original Kiwi used cascading power-amp designs with op-amp clipping and power-amp stages; MkII adds an active inverting treble control and input-gain trimming. [1]

## Versions and factory options
- Active inverting treble control.
- Internal input-gain trimmer.
- Relay-based true bypass.
- Soft-touch momentary switch.
- Enhanced power filtering. [1][2]

## Version changes
MkII retains the original gain architecture and adds:
- Active inverting treble control.
- Input-gain trimmer that moves the op-amp stage from smoother OD toward fuzz.
- Non-inverting overall signal orientation.
- Expanded stacking applications. [1]

The original Sweet Kiwi remains a separate historical identity. [1]

## Transistor
Exact Sweet Kiwi MkII transistor part numbers were not established. The underlying architecture uses cascading power-amp stages.

## Diode
Op-amp clipping is documented in the underlying Kiwi architecture; exact diode parts were not established. [1]

## Sound
Lower input gain produces smoother overdrive behavior; higher input gain moves toward fuzz and stronger saturation. The treble stage gives additional control of high-frequency bite. [1][2]
