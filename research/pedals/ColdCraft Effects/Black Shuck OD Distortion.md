# ColdCraft Effects — Black Shuck OD/Distortion

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Black Shuck OD/Distortion
- **Builder:** ColdCraft Effects
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive / Distortion
- **Identity:** ColdCraft Effects Black Shuck, an amp-stack-like overdrive/distortion designed to clean up with guitar-volume changes.

## What this pedal is
ColdCraft describes Black Shuck as an amp-like overdrive/distortion intended to clean up well from the guitar's volume control. Effects Database lists it among the original/reworked ColdCraft 2013 products. [1][2]
