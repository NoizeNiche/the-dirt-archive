# ColdCraft Effects — Sweet Cream OD

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Sweet Cream OD

- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** ColdCraft Effects Sweet Cream, a low-to-medium-gain dual-stage overdrive with MOSFET first stage and CMOS second stage.

## What this pedal is
Effects Database documents Sweet Cream as a low-gain preamp and the smaller sibling of Cascade, using two distinct stages. The first is a discrete MOSFET boost with Structure control; the second is a custom rail-to-rail CMOS amplifier configured as layered semi-clean and dirty paths. [1]

## Versions and factory options
- Structure.
- Drive.
- Tone.
- Contour.
- 3-way Compression toggle.
- Volume/output.
- Discrete MOSFET first stage.
- Custom rail-to-rail CMOS second stage.
- Internal EQ shaping. [1]

Structure changes frequency emphasis and first-stage gain. Compression changes clipping type and threshold relative to the clean path. Tone cuts treble counter-clockwise and bass clockwise; Contour adds mids clockwise. [1]

## Transistor
Discrete **MOSFET** first stage and **CMOS** second stage. Exact device part numbers were not established. [1]

## Diode
Clipping is controlled by the three-position Compression system; exact diode parts were not established.

## Sound
Sweet Cream covers low-to-medium gain and can shift from chimey/mid-treble boost toward fuller punchier drive. The second stage layers semi-clean and dirty signals for an amp-like response. [1]
