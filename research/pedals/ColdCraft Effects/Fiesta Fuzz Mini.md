# ColdCraft Effects — Fiesta Fuzz Mini

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Fiesta Fuzz Mini
- **Builder:** ColdCraft Effects
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** ColdCraft Effects Fiesta Fuzz Mini, a hybrid MOSFET fuzz with a Tone Bender-like gain range and bias control.

## What this pedal is
ColdCraft describes Fiesta Fuzz Mini as a “no fuss” hybrid fuzz using MOSFETs, with a gain range similar to a Tone Bender. The Bias control can push the circuit into gated, sputtery textures. [1]

## Colorways
No complete factory colorway sequence was established.

## Versions and factory options
Documented controls/features:
- Fuzz/gain control.
- Bias control.
- Hybrid MOSFET fuzz architecture.
- Compact ColdCraft enclosure.
- 9V-era ColdCraft switching architecture. [1][2]

## Version changes
The ColdCraft lineup later included a more feature-rich **Fiesta Fuzz MkII** and a custom-shop version, but those are retained as separate identities rather than merged into Fiesta Fuzz Mini. [1]

## Transistor
The circuit uses **MOSFETs**. Exact transistor part numbers were not established. [1]

## Diode
No exact diode specification was established.

## Sound
The Fiesta Fuzz Mini covers Tone Bender-like gain levels and can move toward tightly gated, sputtery sounds by manipulating Bias. [1]
