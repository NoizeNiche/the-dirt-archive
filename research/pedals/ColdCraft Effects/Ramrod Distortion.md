# ColdCraft Effects — Ramrod Distortion

## PRP identity

## What this pedal is
ColdCraft describes Ramrod as a classic-rock distortion associated with 1980s and 1990s sounds, with controls for **Presence, Compression, and Voice**. [1][2]

## Versions and factory options
Documented functions:
- Distortion/gain.
- Tone shaping.
- Presence.
- Compression.
- Voice.
- 2013 relay-based bypass architecture.
- Switchable buffered bypass.
- 9V DC. [1]

## Sound
Ramrod is voiced for classic-rock distortion and explicitly associated with 1980s/1990s gain. Presence, Compression, and Voice provide additional shaping beyond a basic distortion layout. [1][2]
