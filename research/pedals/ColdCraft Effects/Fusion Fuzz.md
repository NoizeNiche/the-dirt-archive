# ColdCraft Effects — Fusion Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Fusion Fuzz
- **Builder:** ColdCraft Effects
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** ColdCraft Effects Fusion Fuzz, a four-stage MOSFET fuzz combining Fiesta and Fuzzrite-style modes.

## What this pedal is
ColdCraft's Fusion Fuzz combines Fiesta mode with a Fusion mode inspired by the Mosrite Fuzzrite while adding substantially more adjustment. A surviving MkII listing documents a four-stage MOSFET architecture and a Texture control that manipulates dry and filtered distorted signals. [1][2]

## Versions and factory options
- Volume.
- Fuzz.
- Texture.
- Fiesta / Fusion mode.
- Internal Gain trimmer.
- Internal FAT trimmer.
- Two internally switchable low-pass filters.
- JFET input buffer.
- BJT output buffer.
- 4-stage MOSFET fuzz circuit. [2]

## Version changes
Fusion is an expansion of the Fiesta Fuzz MkII concept. ColdCraft's historical product description documents the two-mode relationship; the later listing documents the expanded implementation. [1][2]

## Transistor
- MOSFETs for the four-stage fuzz core.
- JFET input buffer.
- BJT output buffer.
Exact part numbers were not established. [2]

## Diode
No exact-model diode specification was established.

## Sound
Fiesta mode provides the hybrid MOSFET voice; Fusion mode moves toward a modern Fuzzrite family. Texture can create octave, synth, and brass-like fuzz textures through cancellation of dry and filtered distorted signals. [1][2]
