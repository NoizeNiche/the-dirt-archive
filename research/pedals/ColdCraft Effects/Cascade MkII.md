# ColdCraft Effects — Cascade MkII

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Cascade MkII
- **Builder:** ColdCraft Effects
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** ColdCraft Effects Cascade MkII, a dual-stage overdrive combining Class-A-inspired and Class-A/B-style gain structures.

## What this pedal is
ColdCraft and contemporary coverage describe Cascade MkII as two distinct gain stages stacked in series. Drive A is warm and Class-A-inspired; Drive B is punchier and Class-A/B-style. [1][2]

## Colorways
ColdCraft used powder-coated, screen-printed die-cast enclosures in its MkII generation. No complete finish sequence was established. [1]

## Versions and factory options
- Drive A.
- Drive B.
- Cut.
- Volume.
- Internal Mid and Bass EQ trimmers.
- MOSFET input preamp in MkII.
- Relay-based switching.
- Switchable buffered bypass.
- 9V DC. [1][2]

## Version changes
MkII updates the original Cascade with a MOSFET input preamp, internal Bass/Mid trimmers, wider gain and tonal ranges, and relay-based switching. [1]

## Transistor
The MkII explicitly adds a **MOSFET input preamp**. The exact device part number was not securely established in the historical sources. [1]

## Diode
No exact diode clipping part number was established. The pedal's central design identity comes from its two cascaded gain structures. [1]

## Sound
Each gain stage can provide moderate drive independently, but stacking them multiplies harmonic content and saturation. The external Cut control and internal Mid/Bass trimmers tailor the overall response. [1][2]
