## PRP identity

- **Identity:** Compact analog high-gain distortion with a Vintage/Modern voicing switch.

## What this pedal is
Aural Dream's Intense Distortion uses Distortion, Tone and Volume controls plus a two-position VI/MO switch. The builder description says VI ("Vintage") emphasizes more midrange for a warmer, more mellow response, while MO ("Modern") adds low-frequency weight for a tighter, harder-hitting sound.

## Sound
Intense Distortion is designed around a compact high-gain distortion range, with the VI setting filling out the mids and the MO setting pushing more low-end weight and punch. The switch therefore changes the apparent voicing more than the basic distortion identity, giving the same pedal a warmer vintage-leaning or tighter modern-leaning presentation.
