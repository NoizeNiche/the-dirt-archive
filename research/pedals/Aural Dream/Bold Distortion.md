# Aural Dream — Bold Distortion

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Bold Distortion
- **Builder:** Aural Dream
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** Compact high-gain distortion with Distortion, Tone and Volume controls and switchable LED/silicon clipping. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
Effects Database and secondary product documentation describe Bold Distortion as a high-gain distortion intended for clean amplifiers, with a simple three-knob interface and two clipping modes. [1][2]

## Controls
- **DISTORTION**
- **TONE**
- **VOLUME**
- **LED / SI clipping switch**
- True bypass. [1][2]

## Circuit architecture
- Analog distortion circuit.
- Two selectable clipping families: LED and silicon.

## Sound
The documented design is aimed at dense high-gain distortion while keeping the external interface simple. The LED versus silicon clipping selection changes the clipping behavior and tonal feel without changing the basic three-control layout. [1][2]
