# Aural Dream — Bold Distortion

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Bold Distortion
- **Builder:** Aural Dream
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** Compact high-gain distortion with Distortion, Tone and Volume controls plus a two-position LED/silicon clipping switch.

## What this pedal is
Effects Database describes Bold Distortion as a high-power, high-gain distortion for clean amplifiers, with three main controls and an LED/SI clipping switch. Product listings document 9V operation, true bypass and a compact aluminum enclosure.

## Sound
Bold Distortion is intended to supply dense modern high-gain distortion while retaining a simpler three-control layout. The LED and silicon modes shift the low-end/midrange emphasis, giving the player a broader or deeper distortion character from the same basic circuit.
