## What this pedal is
Effects Database documents Compelling Drive as an asymmetric-clipping overdrive with Drive, Tone and Volume controls. Its SH/SL switch is described as switching between a sharper attack and a smoother "sleek" response.

## Sound
Compelling Drive is voiced around a bright, smooth overdrive response with the SH/SL switch changing the feel from sharper and more cutting to smoother and more fluid. Low-gain settings can function as an ordinary overdrive or boost, while higher Drive settings increase saturation.
