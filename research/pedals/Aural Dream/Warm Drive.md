# Aural Dream — Warm Drive

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Warm Drive
- **Builder:** Aural Dream
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Compact asymmetric-clipping overdrive with Drive, Tone and Volume controls and a Sharp/Sleek voicing switch.

## What this pedal is
Effects Database documents Warm Drive as an asymmetric-clipping overdrive with a bright, smooth presentation. Secondary sources compare its circuit lineage to other familiar low-to-medium-gain drives, but those clone comparisons are not treated here as factory-confirmed identity.

## Versions and factory options
### Warm Drive
- Drive
- Tone
- Volume
- SH / SL mode switch
- True bypass
- Analog circuit
- 9V DC, center-negative operation
- Approx. 9mA current draw

### SH / SL mode
- **SH / Sharp:** more cutting, cliffy attack while retaining a sweet drive character.
- **SL / Sleek:** smoother, more flowing response.

## Sound
Warm Drive is aimed at bright-but-smooth overdrive rather than heavy saturation, with the SH/SL switch changing attack and smoothness. The pedal can work as a low-gain drive or boost, and its 3-knob layout keeps the voicing intentionally simple.
