- **Identity:** Kazoo-based electro-acoustic fuzz pedal.

## What this pedal is
The Fuzzoo routes the guitar signal through a small amplifier and speaker mounted beneath a kazoo membrane, then captures the vibrating membrane with a microphone before bringing the signal back to line level. Alameda describes the result as fuzz generated by the physical distortion of the speaker and kazoo membrane rather than a conventional clipping-only pedal. [1]

## Sound
The Fuzzoo is intentionally harsh, buzzy, and lo-fi, with the sound coming from speaker distortion plus the mechanical distortion of the kazoo membrane. The three-position tone switch and gain control shape that already unconventional signal path rather than turning it into a conventional smooth fuzz. [1]
