# Charlie Pedals — Troubadour MKI.5

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Troubadour MKI.5
- **Builder:** Charlie Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Charlie Pedals Troubadour MKI.5, a limited three-unit Tone Bender-style fuzz made in 2023.

## What this pedal is
The exact-model Reverb listing states that Charlie Pedals made only **three Troubadour MKI.5 units**, and that these were the only non-MKI units at the time to carry the Troubadour name. The pedal is described as loud, raw, and highly cleanup-friendly. [1]

## Colorways
- The documented unit uses a **deep grey hammertone** finish with light relicing. [1]

## Versions and factory options
The documented MKI.5 build includes:
- Attack control with a wide usable range.
- Internally mounted bias pot.
- Vintage-spec vero construction.
- Brimar AC113 yellow-jacket germanium transistors.
- Real 1960s Bulgin UK knobs.
- Carling switches.
- Neutrik jacks. [1]

## Version changes
MKI.5 is explicitly presented as a distinct transitional build between the standard MKI and later Troubadour development. The listing says only three were made and that they were the only non-MKI Troubadours at that point. [1]

## Transistor
The exact-model listing documents **Brimar AC113 germanium transistors**. [1]

## Diode
No exact-model diode specification was established.

## Sound
The listing describes the MKI.5 as loud and raw, with strong cleanup from the guitar's controls and a broad Attack range that can move the pedal toward a lower-drive sound. [1]
