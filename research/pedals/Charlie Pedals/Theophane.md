## What this pedal is
The exact-model listing describes Theophane as a mid-1970s Muff-style circuit built in the UK. The documented build uses NOS components, a Hammond 1590J enclosure, and a set of three controls: Sustain, Tone, and Volume. [1][2]

## Sound
Theophane is designed around a mid-1970s Muff family sound, with the builder emphasizing vintage-spec components and construction. The documented architecture points toward thick, sustaining Muff-style fuzz rather than a low-gain overdrive voice. [1][2]
