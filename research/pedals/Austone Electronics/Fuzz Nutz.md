# Austone Electronics — Fuzz Nutz

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Fuzz Nutz
- **Builder:** Austone Electronics
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Hand-built 1960s-style fuzz using discrete components, with a deliberately large low end and a simple Fuzz/level control scheme.

## What this pedal is
Austone's Fuzz Nutz was built to retain classic 1960s fuzz character while addressing the radio interference and noise associated with some period fuzz boxes. The builder's documented control scheme uses FUZZ for rise time/saturation and NUTZ for output level.

## Sound
Fuzz Nutz is intentionally thick and bottom-heavy, with a smooth, fat fuzz response that can clean up when the guitar volume is reduced. The two-control design keeps the focus on saturation and output level rather than tone-sculpting, preserving the blunt, old-school fuzz character.

This pass adds only claims supported by the newly admitted exact-model evidence. Earlier archive research is retained unchanged.

### Verified description
The FUZZ NUTZ is famous for it's big bottom-end and smooth, fat fuzz that cleans-up when guitar volume control is reduced slightly.

### Verified sound evidence
Many of the fuzz effects from the 60's received radio stations and were notoriously noisy.
Special care has been taken in the design and building of the Fuzz Nutz to solve both of these problems, yet retain the classic fuzz tone character.
The FUZZ control sets the rise time and saturation of the signal while the NUTZ control sets the output level.
