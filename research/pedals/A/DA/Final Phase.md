# A/DA — Final Phase

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Final Phase
- **Builder:** A/DA (Analog/Digital Associates)
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive
- **Identity:** A/DA Final Phase, a voltage-controlled analog phaser with an integrated distortion section.

## What this pedal is
The Final Phase combines a deep, voltage-controlled analog phaser with an integrated overdrive/distortion circuit. Effects Database's historical material states that the original design was developed by **Lee Powell in 1977**, with an initial Vactec opto-coupled design later replaced by a switched-capacitor approach for greater stability and manufacturability in 1978. [1]

Effects Database also identifies the Final Phase as a Berkeley, California A/DA design and preserves historical magazine references, later reissue references, manuals and schematics. [1]

## Controls
The documented control set includes:
- **Sweep Rate**
- **Sweep Modulation**
- **Range**
- **Intensity**
- **Overdrive**
- external control-voltage input
- separate phaser and overdrive footswitch functions. [1]

## Original design / later reissues
The original design used Vactec opto-coupled resistors in its phase-shifting/modulation system. The later 1978 production design replaced those with switched-capacitor technology while retaining the high-Q character and broad sweep. [1]

The archive also retains the documented **2009 reissue** as a later production form with modernized bypass, PCB and control-voltage handling. Exact details for every intermediate reissue are not split into separate archive parents because the surviving documentation does not establish a clean complete revision map. [1]

## Circuit / components
Effects Database describes a multi-stage analog phase-shifting network and an onboard overdrive section. The historical source specifically identifies Vactec opto-coupled resistors in the early design and switched capacitors in the later 1978 implementation. [1]

- **Transistor:** Exact production devices not verified.
- **Diode:** Exact overdrive clipping devices not verified.
- **Schematic:** Effects Database links to schematics, but this record does not infer component substitutions beyond the documented Vactec-to-switched-capacitor change.

## Sound
The Final Phase is characterized by a **high-Q, deep and resonant** phaser response with an integrated overdrive that can add its own harmonic texture. The modulation section can produce broad automatic sweeps, and the additional Sweep Modulation control allows more complex movement than a basic fixed-rate phaser. [1]

## Historical production
Effects Database states that the original pedal had a short 1978-79 production period and was later reissued, including a 2009 version. Because the source also contains both original and reissue material, the archive treats “Final Phase” as the parent model with version-specific notes rather than assuming every pictured unit is identical. [1]
