# A/DA — Final Phase

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Final Phase
- **Builder:** A/DA (Analog/Digital Associates)
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive
- **Identity:** A/DA's analog phaser with integrated overdrive section.

## What this pedal is
Final Phase is a **voltage-controlled analog phaser** with a built-in overdrive. The original design dates to the late 1970s, with later reissues preserving the core phase-shifting architecture while adding modernized bypass, power and control refinements. [1][2]

## Colorways
- Original/reissue examples use a **black enclosure with silver control panels** and the A/DA logo.
- The exact finish shown in the archive photo is a 2009-era reissue example.

## Versions and factory options
### Original Final Phase
- Sweep Rate
- Sweep Modulation
- Range
- Intensity
- Overdrive
- voltage-control input
- analog phaser architecture with regenerative feedback
- separate overdrive footswitch [1]

### 2009 reissue
- preserves the original feature set
- hardwire/true bypass
- modernized through-hole PCB
- revoiced overdrive with more bass resonance
- expanded control-voltage range
- all-analog signal path [1]

Other reissues existed, but the sources checked do not establish every production change cleanly enough to split them into separate archive identities.

## Version changes
The significant documented change is the move from the original late-1970s production into later reissues. The 2009 reissue kept the core Final Phase sound and controls while modernizing bypass, PCB layout, overdrive response and control-voltage handling. [1]

## Sound
Final Phase combines a deep, resonant phase sweep with a built-in overdrive that can be used independently or layered onto the modulation. The phase section can move from slow, subtle motion into much more dramatic, swirling movement, while the overdrive adds a second layer of gritty harmonic texture. [1][2]
