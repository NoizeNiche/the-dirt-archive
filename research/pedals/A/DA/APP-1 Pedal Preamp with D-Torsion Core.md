## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** APP-1 Pedal Preamp with D-Torsion Core
- **Builder:** A/DA (Analog/Digital Associates)
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Two-channel all-analog pedal preamp built around A/DA's D-TORSION CORE technology.

## What this pedal is
The APP-1 is a **guitar preamp rather than a conventional stompbox overdrive**. It provides two independent channels, each with clean/OD/distortion voicings, passive three-band tone shaping, a D-TORSION dynamics control, an effects loop, and a final boost stage. [1][2]

## Colorways
- The documented production enclosure is **black with blue-and-white graphics** and two banks of controls.

## Versions and factory options
### APP-1 production
- two channels
- D-TORSION dynamics control
- independent voicing selection on each channel
- passive Bass / Middle / Treble tone stacks
- Master controls
- dual-buffered effects loop
- post-master boost
- up to approximately +16 to +20 dB of boost depending on documentation
- true bypass / tuner functions documented in the manual [1][2]

The user manual is version 2, but this is a manual revision, not proof of a separate hardware generation. [2]

## Sound
APP-1 is intended to respond to playing dynamics rather than only knob position. The D-TORSION system is designed so harder picking can move the signal from clean into overdrive and distortion, while the passive tone stacks and separate channels allow a large range of amp-like tones. [1][2]
