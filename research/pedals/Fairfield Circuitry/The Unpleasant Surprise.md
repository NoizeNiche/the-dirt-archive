## What this pedal is
The Unpleasant Surprise combines fuzz with a gate-like onset control. The circuit is designed to move from conventional fuzz into abrupt, gated and unstable textures.

## Sound
The Unpleasant Surprise is designed for fuzz that can become abruptly gated and compressed as Onset and Crush are increased. The circuit can jump between sustaining fuzz and broken-up, splintered textures.
