# AED — Blue Bee

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Blue Bee
- **Builder:** AED
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** AED overdrive originally associated with Chuya Online's original pedal brand. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
Blue Bee is an unusually simple overdrive using only Volume and Gain controls, with no Tone control. Contemporary coverage describes it as a TS-style overdrive with a high-frequency-responsive, natural character. [1][2]

## Colorways
- A later **Paint it Blue Limited Edition** was documented in 2016.
- The original production finish is distinct from the limited blue reissue. [2]

## Versions and factory options
### Original Blue Bee
- Volume
- Gain
- no Tone control [1]

### Paint it Blue Limited Edition
A blue-finish revival was released in 2016. The surviving documentation describes it as a limited-color version of Blue Bee rather than a separately redesigned circuit. [2]

## Version changes
The 2016 Paint it Blue edition is treated as a documented finish/reissue distinction. No circuit change was verified.

## Transistor

## Sound
Blue Bee is described as a high-response, natural overdrive with a TS-style mid-focused behavior despite its unusually simple Volume/Gain control set. With gain reduced and volume increased, contemporary coverage describes a thicker, mid-boost-like core. [1][2]
