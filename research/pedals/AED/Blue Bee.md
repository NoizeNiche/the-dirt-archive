# AED — Blue Bee

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Blue Bee
- **Builder:** AED
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** AED Blue Bee, an overdrive associated with Chuya Online's original AED pedal brand. [1][2][3]

## What this pedal is
A 2008 contemporary Japanese review describes Blue Bee as an unusually stripped-down overdrive with only Volume and Gain controls, no Tone control, no LED indicator, and no DC input. The same review reports that the original unit had no input buffer and used ordinary symmetrical clipping. [1]

Effects Database separately catalogs AED Blue Bee as an overdrive and records the model in November 2011. It also preserves an AED Blue Bee demo from Chuya Online dating to August 2008. [3]

## Colorways
### Original Blue Bee
- Surviving 2008 coverage describes the original production unit without a documented colorway specification. [1]

### Paint it Blue Limited Edition
- A **Paint it Blue Limited Edition** reissue was offered in 2016 in a limited blue finish.
- The 2016 coverage identifies it as a revival of the Blue Bee rather than a new model name. [2]

## Versions and factory options
### Original Blue Bee
- Volume
- Gain
- No Tone control
- Battery-only operation in the documented original form
- No LED status indicator
- No DC input
- True-bypass switching was not documented for the original unit in the 2008 source. [1]

### Paint it Blue Limited Edition
The 2016 revival retained the simple Volume/Gain control layout but added an LED, true-bypass switching, and a DC input. The article presents these as changes from the original model. [2]

## Version changes
The 2016 Paint it Blue Limited Edition is a documented hardware revision/reissue rather than merely a color statement: the surviving source specifically notes the added LED, true-bypass switching, and DC input. The archive does not infer any additional circuit changes beyond those documented. [2]

## Transistor
- **Exact transistor/device:** Not publicly documented.
- The 2008 review discusses the internal circuit and clipping behavior but does not identify a production transistor part number. [1]

## Diode
- The 2008 review characterizes the original circuit as using symmetrical clipping, but does not identify the exact clipping diode/device.
- **Exact clipping diode/device:** Not publicly documented. [1]

## Sound
The 2008 review describes Blue Bee as a high-response, natural-sounding overdrive and characterizes it as a very simple, bufferless design. The 2016 coverage describes the drive as having a thick mid-boost-like core at lower gain with increased volume, while higher gain adds a slightly gritty texture without losing its thickness and sheen. [1][2]

The 2008 writer also compared its general behavior to TS-family ideas and other overdrives, but those comparisons are presented as the reviewer's impression rather than as a factory circuit claim. [1]

## Historical documentation notes
Effects Database records Blue Bee as an AED overdrive and preserves a Chuya Online demo from 2008. A separate 2016 article documents the Paint it Blue revival and explicitly identifies the added LED, true-bypass switching, and DC input. [2][3]
