# AMT Electronics — SS-11B

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** SS-11B
- **Builder:** AMT Electronics
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Studio Series three-channel tube guitar preamp with CLEAN, OVERDRIVE, and LEAD sections. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
The SS-11B is a high-gain-oriented tube preamp built around two 12AX7-class tubes and a four-stage overdrive channel. It includes separate clean and drive controls, an effects loop, a guitar-amp output, and a cabinet-simulation output for direct/recording use. [1][2]

## Colorways
- Black chassis with a yellow central vented panel is documented for SS-11B examples.
- Complete finish history is not established.

## Versions and factory options
### SS-11 family
- CLEAN / OVERDRIVE / LEAD channels
- Separate gain sensitivity and output controls
- Low, Mid, High EQ on the clean channel
- TREBLE SHIFT control
- FX LOOP
- Guitar-amp OUT plus CAB SIM output
- Two 12AX7-class tubes [1][2]

### SS-11B Modern
- Modern high-gain voicing
- Bright/TREBLE SHIFT functionality
- Designed for heavy riffing, drop tunings, seven-string guitars and lead work [2]

## Version changes
The documented SS-11B Modern revision adds a Bright switch to the Crunch section and changes the drive-channel indication to a two-color indicator. The B version is positioned as the more modern/high-gain member of the SS-11 family, with more distortion than the SS-11A Classic. [2]

## Transistor
- Solid-state buffers are used at the preamp outputs, and the cabinet-emulation processing is described as field-effect-transistor based.
- Exact transistor/FET part numbers are not publicly documented. [1]

## Diode
- Exact clipping diode/device is not publicly documented.

## Sound
The LEAD channel is described as thick and viscous for chunky rhythms and singing solos, while the OVERDRIVE channel is more open and saturated, ranging from light crunch toward bright lead-like gain. The overall voicing is deliberately modern and high-gain, with the dynamic feel of a tube preamp rather than a conventional low-gain solid-state overdrive. [1][2]
