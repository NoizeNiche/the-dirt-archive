# Amzel Electronics — Cheshire Cat Tone Evaporator

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Cheshire Cat Tone Evaporator
- **Builder:** Amzel Electronics
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive
- **Identity:** 100% analog distortion pedal built around Amzel's Tone Evaporator concept, which filters and shapes the distorted portion of the waveform separately from the base guitar sound. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
The Cheshire Cat uses six interactive controls: DISTORT, VOLUME, INJECT, RESONANCE, FREQUENCY and ACCENT. INJECT determines how strongly the Tone Evaporator's resonance/frequency filter and dynamic accent section act on the distorted portion of the signal. [1][2]

## Colorways
- Early examples are documented in a blue enclosure.
- The updated 2015 design used a smaller housing; complete finish/color history is not established.

## Versions and factory options
### Original Cheshire Cat Tone Evaporator
- 100% analog distortion
- Distort and Volume
- Inject control
- Resonance and Frequency filter controls
- Accent dynamic control
- 9 V battery/adapter operation [2]

### Updated Cheshire Cat (2015)
- Same Tone Evaporator electronics
- Sonic tweaks
- Higher-quality controls
- Approximately 35% less pedalboard area
- Patent-pending circuit description retained [1]

## Version changes
The May 2015 update retained the Tone Evaporator concept but introduced sonic tweaks, higher-quality controls and a smaller housing. Contemporary coverage describes the redesign as using 35% less pedalboard area than the earlier enclosure. [1]

## Sound
With INJECT low, the Cheshire Cat can behave like a conventional distortion/boost, while raising INJECT brings the resonance, frequency and accent controls further into the distorted waveform. The documented range runs from usable overdrive through compressed, fuzzy textures and more unusual filtered, vowel-like or choked distortion sounds. [1][2]
