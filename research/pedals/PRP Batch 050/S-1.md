# AMT Electronics — S-1

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** S-1
- **Builder:** AMT Electronics
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Legend Amp series single-channel JFET preamp designed around the drive-channel character of Soldano-style amplifiers. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
S-1 is a compact analog preamp/overdrive with LEVEL, GAIN, LOW, MID, and HIGH controls. It provides a direct amplifier output plus a separate CAB.SIM output for mixer or recording use, with passive true bypass in the Legend Amp series. [1][2]

## Sound
S-1 is described as an open, wide, tough and dynamic overdrive voiced around the drive-channel character of Soldano amplifiers. AMT's Legend Amp documentation emphasizes dense, tube-like response while retaining useful midrange information rather than heavily scooping it out. [1]
