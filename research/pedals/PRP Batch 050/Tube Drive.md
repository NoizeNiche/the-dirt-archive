## What this pedal is
Tube Drive is described by the builder as a flexible overdrive that can cover several familiar overdrive voicings using a three-way toggle. The builder positions it as a drive/booster for guitar and documents 9 V operation, battery or adapter use, a glass knob intended to brighten/twang the response, and true bypass. [1]

## Sound
AMTECH describes Tube Drive as a flexible overdrive intended to cover traditional and more modern flavors, with the builder specifically comparing its range to familiar TS, SD-1, Maxon and Voodoo-style overdrive territory. The three-way switch is intended to move between those broader response characters rather than lock the pedal to a single clipping voice. [1]
