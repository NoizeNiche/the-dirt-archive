# Analog Alien — Rumble Seat

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Rumble Seat
- **Builder:** Analog Alien
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Three-effect pedal combining overdrive, delay and reverb. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
Rumble Seat houses three independently switchable sections: Rumble Drive overdrive, delay, and reverb. The Rumble Drive is described as a simulation of a vintage 1969 Marshall Plexi, the delay spans approximately 25-650 ms, and the reverb is voiced around the sound of a vintage Fender Blackface amp. [1][2]

## Colorways
- Original/current classic enclosure: orange sparkle finish is documented.
- Analog Alien announced a 2026 new-look Rumble Seat with the same three-effect architecture and new pedal artwork. [1]

## Versions and factory options
### Original Rumble Seat
- Rumble Drive: Output, Tone, Gain
- Delay: Delay, Repeats, Mix
- Reverb: Reverb
- Three independent footswitches
- True bypass
- 9 V operation, no battery
- Rumble Drive 100% analog; delay/reverb use digital clocking with analog input/output stages [2]

### 2026 new-look Rumble Seat
- New enclosure/artwork
- Same overdrive, slapback delay and reverb concept
- Current builder page identifies the redesign as a visual update rather than a new effect architecture. [1]

## Version changes
The builder's 2026 announcement describes a new-look Rumble Seat with updated visual treatment while retaining the familiar overdrive, slapback-delay and reverb combination. The earlier documented Rumble Seat is the orange-sparkle form represented by the archived image. [1][2]

## Sound
Rumble Drive is intended to move from smooth enhancement and boost into warm, tube-like overdrive while keeping definition and bite. The delay covers short slapback through long repeats, and the reverb is deliberately dark, rich and spring-like, giving the pedal a broad classic-rock/surf/rockabilly range. [1][2]
