# AMT Electronics — SS-20

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** SS-20
- **Builder:** AMT Electronics
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Studio Series tube guitar preamp with CLEAN and DRIVE channels, including CRUNCH and LEAD modes. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
The SS-20 combines a 12AX7 tube stage with high-voltage operation and specially designed semiconductor amplification stages. Its CLEAN and DRIVE channels have their own gain/volume controls and EQ sections, while the drive side switches between CRUNCH and LEAD modes. [1][2]

## Colorways

## Versions and factory options
- CLEAN and DRIVE channels
- DRIVE channel CRUNCH and LEAD modes
- B-channel gain-character switch
- Effects loop SEND/RETURN
- Guitar-amplifier OUTPUT plus OUT CAB.EMUL.
- One 12AX7/ECC83 tube
- 12 V DC / approximately 500 mA operation [1][2]

## Sound
AMT describes the SS-20 as a tighter, crunchier and more aggressive counterpart to the SS-11, with clean, overdrive, distortion and lead textures from the same compact tube-preamp format. The architecture is designed to preserve guitar dynamics while using the tube and high-voltage stages to create a dense, aggressive response. [2]
