# Analog Fox — Tone Machine

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Tone Machine
- **Builder:** Analog Fox
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Russian-made clone of the 1970s Foxx Tone Machine octave-fuzz circuit. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
Tone Machine is a thick, high-gain fuzz with a separate footswitch for adding an upper octave. The documented controls are Sustain, Tone and Volume. [1][2]

## Sound
The Tone Machine is described as a thick, saturated fuzz with substantial gain and sustain, while the octave switch adds the characteristic upper-octave component associated with the Foxx Tone Machine family. Its voice sits in the aggressive octave-fuzz tradition rather than behaving like a low-gain overdrive. [1]
