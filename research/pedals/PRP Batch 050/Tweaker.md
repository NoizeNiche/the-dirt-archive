## What this pedal is
Tweaker combines a high-gain fuzz circuit with a controllable feedback loop that can move from conventional fuzz and boost use into self-oscillation and noise-generator territory. The documented controls include Fuzz, Volume, Feedback, a Treble/Bass selector, a Feedback On/Off switch and a momentary feedback switch. [1]

## Version changes
After two years of use, amukaT redesigned the circuit and layout for Ver.2 and moved it to a horizontal aluminum enclosure. The momentary switch was also replaced with a sturdier part for repeated use. [1]

## Sound
Tweaker can produce classic or gnarly fuzz at ordinary settings, but its defining behavior is the playable feedback circuit that can produce dub-siren-like oscillation and sustained noise textures. The pedal can also be used as a booster into a tube amplifier, with the Treble setting emphasizing cut and the Bass setting retaining a fatter low-end fuzz character. [1]
