# Analog Alien — Joe Walsh Double Classic

## PRP identity

## What this pedal is
The Joe Walsh Double Classic combines a studio-style compressor and an analog Classic Amp circuit in one enclosure. The two effects are independently switchable, and a PRE/POST switch changes whether compression occurs before or after the amp section. [1][2]

## Sound
The compressor is voiced more like a natural studio/rack compressor than a heavily colored stompbox, while the Classic Amp section moves from warm, clean response into natural amp-like breakup as Gain rises. The PRE/POST switch adds another tonal dimension by letting the compression shape the amp section or sit after it. [1][2]
