# Crews Maniac Sound — Texas Tornado

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Texas Tornado

- **Identity:** Crews Maniac Sound's mid-focused overdrive from the company's earlier Japanese production period.

## What this pedal is
Texas Tornado is a three-control overdrive built around a Tube Screamer-style tonal concept while offering a wider gain range and a more flexible tone sweep. Archived retail and used-market references identify it as a Japan-made, discontinued Crews model. [1][2]

## Colorways
- Documented examples include red and burgundy metallic finishes.
- Finish differences are treated as colorways unless a circuit change is documented.

## Versions and factory options
### Texas Tornado production
- Gain
- Tone
- Level
- Made in Japan
- 9V negative-ground external power [1][2]

The Tone control is documented as having a wide range that can move from a pronounced mid emphasis toward a more scooped response. [1]

## Version changes
No reliable numbered V1/V2 sequence was verified. Crews later explained that the tone concept was carried forward into the company's G.O.D. and Quadrive development, while Texas Tornado itself was discontinued. [3]

## Sound
Texas Tornado combines familiar mid-focused overdrive behavior with a wider gain range than a basic Tube Screamer-style pedal. Its Tone control can move from a prominent honky mid character toward a more open, scooped presentation, and higher gain settings can become distinctly rougher and more fuzz-like. [1][2]
