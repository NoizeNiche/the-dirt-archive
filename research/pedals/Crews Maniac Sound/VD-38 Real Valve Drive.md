## What this pedal is
VD-38 Real Valve Drive is a rackmount overdrive unit from Crews Maniac Sound, documented by Effects Database as a **rackmount unit** rather than a conventional floor pedal. The database links it directly to Crews' own VD-38 product page and records a 2007 publication date for the model. [1]

## Sound
VD-38 is designed around a tube-driven, amplifier-like overdrive character rather than a compact transistor stompbox voice. The surviving demo record and Crews product classification place it in the company's more studio/rack-oriented approach to tube gain and saturation. [1][2]
