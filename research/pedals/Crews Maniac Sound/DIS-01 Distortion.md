# Crews Maniac Sound — DIS-01 Distortion

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** DIS-01 Distortion
- **Builder:** Crews Maniac Sound

- **Identity:** Crews Maniac Sound's original-design distortion pedal built around a multi-stage drive/preamp approach.

## What this pedal is
DIS-01 is an original Crews Maniac Sound distortion design intended to capture the saturation and response of a large tube amplifier in pedal form. The manufacturer/distributor description emphasizes a complex harmonic structure, strong pick response, and a gain range that moves naturally from crunch into heavier distortion. [1]

## Sound
DIS-01 is voiced for a punchy, articulate distortion that keeps complex upper harmonics from becoming buried. The documented design goal is a progression from smoother crunch into high gain while retaining pick attack, a firm tonal center, and useful response to the guitar's controls. [1]
