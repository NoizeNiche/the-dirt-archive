# Crews Maniac Sound — Quadrive

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Quadrive
- **Builder:** Crews Maniac Sound
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Crews Maniac Sound's four-op-amp overdrive/preamp with an integrated boost section.

## What this pedal is
Quadrive was announced for release in late 2011 as an amplifier-like overdrive/preamp designed to preserve the character of the guitar and amplifier. The name refers to its use of four operational amplifier stages for input impedance, drive, EQ/output, and output-impedance functions. [1][2]

## Colorways

- Cosmetic editions are treated as colorways unless a circuit change is documented.

- Drive footswitch
- Boost footswitch
- 9–15V DC operation, with a dedicated 15V adapter documented for the model [2][3]

The Boost section is integrated after the main drive and is designed for additional level shaping rather than independent operation as a standalone effect. [2]

## Sound
Quadrive is voiced as a relatively natural, amplifier-like overdrive with a large amount of control over gain, mid character, EQ, output impedance, and boost. Crews' own presentation emphasizes preserving the instrument's character while changing the way the signal interacts with the amplifier, with the voltage and impedance controls influencing headroom and feel. [1][2]
