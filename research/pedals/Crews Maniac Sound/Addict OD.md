# Crews Maniac Sound — Addict OD

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Addict OD
- **Builder:** Crews Maniac Sound
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Crews Maniac Sound's compact overdrive introduced in 2013 as a simpler continuation of the company's G.O.D./Quadrive approach.

## What this pedal is
Addict OD was introduced by Crews as a smaller, simpler overdrive intended to preserve the player's instrument, amplifier and picking dynamics rather than create a heavily colored effect. The official Crews blog lists 9V operation, an active output buffer, compact size, and simple controls as core design goals. [1]

## Sound
Addict OD is intended to produce natural low-gain through crunch overdrive while retaining picking dynamics and the character of the guitar and amplifier. Its LED clipping is described as giving the drive a strong, present character, and the output-impedance control lets the user alter how the pedal interacts with the following gear. [1][2]
