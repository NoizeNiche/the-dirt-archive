## What this pedal is
Valve Drive is a tube-based overdrive/preamp built around a 12AX7. Contemporary Crews coverage identifies Gain, Bass, Treble, and Level controls and AC-powered operation, with the pedal intended to provide an amplifier-style tube response in a floor unit. [1][2]

## Versions and factory options
### Valve Drive production
- 12AX7 vacuum tube
- Gain
- Bass
- Treble
- Level
- AC-powered operation [1]

The pedal was also described as useful as a preamp in a larger gain chain, with compression and enhancement alongside its overdrive function. [2]

## Version changes
No reliable numbered V1/V2 production sequence was verified. Crews later stated that production ended because maintaining the required tube quality became difficult. [3]

## Sound
Valve Drive is intended to add tube-style gain, compression, and harmonic enhancement rather than simply create a hard-edged pedal distortion. The two-band tone section gives broad control over bass and treble while the gain and level controls cover a range from relatively mild enhancement to heavier overdrive. [1][2]
