## What this pedal is
SVD-001 is a bass overdrive designed around a vintage amplifier-style response. Reverb and Effects Database references describe it as a unit with a broad EQ section, gain, mix, and volume controls, intended to add overdrive while retaining a solid low-frequency foundation. [1][2]

## Versions and factory options
### SVD-001 production
- Gain
- Mix
- Volume
- Three-band tone section
- 9–15V DC operation
- Bass-oriented voicing
- External power operation documented around the Crews 15V ecosystem [1][3]

Used-market descriptions note that the lower-voltage setting can feel more compressed and distorted, while higher operating voltage provides more clarity and headroom. [2]

## Sound
SVD-001 is voiced for bass-friendly overdrive with a controlled low end and enough EQ range to reshape the instrument's mid and treble presence. The gain and mix controls allow anything from relatively subtle amplifier-like grit to a denser, more compressed drive sound, with supply voltage affecting available headroom and feel. [1][2]
