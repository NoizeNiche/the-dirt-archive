## What this pedal is
Taco Fuzz is an octave fuzz from Crews Maniac Sound. The builder's April 2010 material describes it as producing a strong upper-octave effect with a sustained, bending response, controlled by Gain, Tone, and Volume. [1][2]

## Versions and factory options
### Taco Fuzz production
- Gain
- Tone
- Volume
- Upper-octave fuzz character
- Standard external pedal operation

The original production was later discontinued. Crews' own interview explains that production ended because a specific component used in the pedal became difficult to source consistently, making continued production impractical. [3]

## Sound
Taco Fuzz is built around a strong upper-octave voice that becomes especially pronounced when the player uses sustained notes and bends. The Tone and Gain controls let the effect move from a more cutting octave-fuzz texture toward thicker, dirtier and more sustained fuzz. [1][2]
