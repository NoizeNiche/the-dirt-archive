## What this pedal is
Genius Over Drive, commonly abbreviated **G.O.D.**, is a two-channel drive pedal built to provide distinctly voiced A/B gain settings with extensive shared tone shaping. Crews describes it as an amplifier-like overdrive and the available documentation shows it as a core Crews model alongside Quadrive. [1][2]

## Colorways
- Standard examples are documented in black and other production/custom finishes.
- Limited color editions are documented separately as cosmetic variants rather than new pedal identities. [3]

## Versions and factory options
### G.O.D. production
- Drive A
- Drive B
- Contour
- Output
- Bottom
- Middle
- Treble
- Bottom Shift switch
- Treble Shift switch
- Footswitching for bypass and A/B gain selection
- 9–15V DC operation, with a dedicated 15V adapter documented for the model [1][2]

The A/B channels share the main EQ and output section while providing different gain character, allowing cleaner-to-crunch and heavier lead-oriented setups from one pedal. [1][3]

## Sound
G.O.D. is designed around thick, amplifier-like drive with two independently set gain levels, backed by active three-band EQ, Contour shaping, and frequency-shift switches. The documented response ranges from comparatively clean/crunchy sounds to high-gain drive, with a strong relationship to guitar volume and picking dynamics. [1][2]
