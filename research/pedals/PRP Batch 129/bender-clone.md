# Build Your Own Clone — Bender Clone

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Build Your Own Clone
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** DIY recreation of the Sola Sound Tone Bender MKII.

## What this pedal is
The Bender Clone is a recreation of the Sola Sound Tone Bender MKII, emphasizing thick woolly fuzz, long sustain and controllable feedback. [1]

## Colorways
- DIY kit; finished enclosure appearance depends on the builder.

## Versions and factory options
- Three selected NOS Philips/Mullard OC75 germanium transistors
- Tone Bender MKII circuit basis
- DIY enclosure/footswitch implementation [1]

## Sound
The Bender Clone is intended for large, woolly vintage fuzz with plenty of sustain. Its Tone Bender MKII basis places it in the classic British fuzz family, with a thick response rather than a bright, tight modern fuzz character. [1]
