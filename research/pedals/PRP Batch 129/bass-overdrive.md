# Build Your Own Clone — Bass Overdrive

## PRP identity

- **Builder:** Build Your Own Clone
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** BYOC original bass-oriented overdrive with blend and two-band EQ.

## What this pedal is

Bass Overdrive is a BYOC original design with Bass, Treble, Volume, Gain and Blend controls. The blend ranges from fully wet to fully dry, allowing the dry signal to retain punch while the overdrive adds warmth, growl and grind. [1]

## Sound

The two-band EQ gives broad control over the bass and treble around the drive circuit, while the Blend control can preserve the original instrument attack and low-end punch. BYOC also describes substantial output, allowing the unit to work as a clean or dirty boost. [1]
