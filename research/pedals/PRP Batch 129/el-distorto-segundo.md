The El Distorto Segundo is a unique and interesting distortion pedal that is not widely known or highly priced in the vintage market.
It offers two completely different distortion/overdrive circuits, with the ‘resonance’ knob blending the second circuit into the mix.
The pedal provides plenty of opportunities for tinkering and customization, with various clipping mods, RC filters, and op amp gain structures to experiment with.
