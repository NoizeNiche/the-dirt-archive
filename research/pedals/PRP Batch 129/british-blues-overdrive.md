# Build Your Own Clone — British Blues Overdrive

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Build Your Own Clone
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** British-voiced DIY overdrive kit.

## What this pedal is
British Blues Overdrive is a BYOC overdrive kit. Its preserved build instructions specify a TL072 op amp and a four-diode 1N914/1N4148 clipping set. [1]
