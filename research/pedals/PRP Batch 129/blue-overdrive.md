# Build Your Own Clone — Blue Overdrive

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Build Your Own Clone
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** DIY recreation of the BOSS BD-2 Blues Driver.

## What this pedal is
The Blue Overdrive is based on the BOSS BD-2 Blues Driver. Effects Database describes it as a FET-based overdrive with enough gain to move from overdrive into substantial distortion. [1]

## Colorways

## Sound
Blue Overdrive follows the open, responsive family associated with the Blues Driver, moving from lower-gain overdrive toward harder distortion as the gain is increased. The FET topology is part of its amp-like response. [1]
