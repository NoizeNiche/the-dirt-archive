# Build Your Own Clone — Crown Jewel

## PRP identity

- **Builder:** Build Your Own Clone
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive
- **Identity:** Original analog drive/boost platform, not a direct clone.

## What this pedal is

Crown Jewel combines a three-band EQ with parametric mids, multiple clipping choices and a separate modular boost channel. The drive section can run at 9V or 18V, and the boost can be placed before or after the drive. [1]

## Colorways

- DIY kit; finished enclosure appearance depends on the builder.
- Finished examples exist with different cosmetic treatments.

## Versions and factory options

- Parametric midrange
- Three-way Mid Q switch
- Hard and soft clipping combinations
- 9 documented clipping textures
- 9V/18V drive supply selection
- Modular boost choices including JFET, MOSFET, LPB, germanium treble booster and other modules
- Independent drive and boost switching
- Selectable boost order [1]

## Version changes

The interchangeable boost modules are functional options rather than evidence of a single numbered circuit revision.

## Transistor

The platform includes JFET and MOSFET boost configurations, but no single transistor set applies to every Crown Jewel build. [1]

## Diode

The drive channel uses Germanium, Silicon and LED clipping options. BYOC also documents a combined germanium-diode/MOSFET clipping path in one implementation. [1]

## Sound

Crown Jewel is intentionally broad, moving across many overdrive and distortion textures rather than one fixed voice. Parametric mids, clipping selection and the independent modular boost provide most of that range. [1]
