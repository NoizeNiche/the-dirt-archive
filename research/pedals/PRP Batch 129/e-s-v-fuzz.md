# Build Your Own Clone — E.S.V. Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Build Your Own Clone
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Extra Special Vintage Fuzz Face-style DIY kit.

## What this pedal is
E.S.V. Fuzz is a BYOC Fuzz Face recreation updated with an LED, DC jack, adjustable bias and true bypass. It is offered in Germanium and Silicon configurations. [1]

## Colorways
- DIY kit; finished enclosure appearance depends on the builder.

## Versions and factory options
- **Germanium:** AC128 germanium transistors made by New Jersey Semiconductor
- **Silicon:** BC108 or BC109C
- Adjustable bias
- Positive- or negative-ground PCB configuration using jumpers
- Standard negative-tip adapter jack [1]

## Version changes
BYOC updated the PCB so it can be configured for either positive-ground PNP or negative-ground NPN transistor operation. The core Fuzz Face identity remains the same. [1]

## Transistor
- **Germanium:** AC128.
- **Silicon:** BC108 or BC109C. [1]

## Diode
Exact clipping diode/device is not separately documented in the product description.

## Sound
BYOC describes the germanium configuration as darker, warmer and woollier with strong cleanup from the guitar volume. Silicon offers more gain, sustain and brightness, while the adjustable bias expands the range of the Fuzz Face-style circuit. [1]
