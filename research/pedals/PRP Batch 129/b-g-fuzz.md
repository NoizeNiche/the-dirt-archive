# Build Your Own Clone — B.G. Fuzz

## PRP identity

- **Builder:** Build Your Own Clone
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** DIY recreation of the Roland AF-60 Bee Gee fuzz.

## What this pedal is

The B.G. Fuzz recreates the Roland AF-60 Bee Gee. The documented circuit uses Toshiba TA7504M metal-can op amps and 2SC1000 transistors, with the guitar volume serving as the main way to control fuzz because there is no dedicated fuzz knob. [1]

## Colorways

- DIY kit; finished enclosure appearance depends on the builder.

## Versions and factory options

- TA7504M metal-can op amps
- 2SC1000 transistors
- No dedicated fuzz control
- Optional 1N60 germanium-diode or red-LED clipping modifications [1]

## Version changes

No numbered BYOC revision was verified.

## Transistor

- **Technology:** Silicon transistor.
- **Device:** 2SC1000. [1]

## Diode

- Optional clipping modifications include 1N60 germanium diodes or red LEDs. These are documented kit modifications, not the stock component claim. [1]

## Sound

The reference describes the B.G. Fuzz as woolly, sustaining and articulate, with a slight gate-like response on the pick attack. Guitar-volume control is central to the pedal's cleanup and fuzz-range behavior. [1]
