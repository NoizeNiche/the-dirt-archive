# Cicognani Engineering — Speciale DD 1959

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Speciale DD 1959
- **Builder:** Cicognani Engineering
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Cicognani Engineering Speciale DD 1959, an analog tube overdrive and preamp that evolves the Double Decker into a multi-channel recording/live platform.

## What this pedal is
Cicognani documentation describes Speciale DD 1959 as an evolution of the famous Double Decker. It uses a **12AU7** tube preamplifier with professional IC stages and provides two channels plus boost in amplifier mode, and three channels plus solo in Rec Out mode. [1][2]

## Colorways
- The checked exact-model sources establish the custom metal chassis and PMMA front panel but do not establish a complete factory colorway sequence. [1]

## Versions and factory options
Documented controls and functions include:
- Channel 1 Gain and Volume.
- Channel 2 Gain and Volume.
- Three-band tone stack for both channels.
- Boost level.
- Serial/Parallel FX loop control.
- Dry/Wet mix.
- Remote switching for Bypass, Ch1, Ch2, and Boost.
- Rec Out speaker simulation. [1]

The standard tube is **12AU7 / ECC82**, with compatible double triodes listed by the manufacturer including 12AX7/ECC83, 7025, 12AT7, and 5814. [1]

Power is 9-12V DC negative tip and consumption is approximately 300-500 mA. [1]

## Version changes
Speciale DD 1959 adds an extra gain stage on Channel 2, a clean channel in Rec Out mode, and a redesigned FX loop with series/parallel mixing compared with the earlier Double Decker. [1][2]

## Transistor
No discrete transistor stage is documented as the primary gain element. The pedal uses a **12AU7 tube** with professional IC processing stages. [1]

## Diode
No exact-model diode specification was established.

## Sound
Cicognani describes the pedal as covering edge-of-breakup through high-gain British lead sounds. The additional Channel 2 gain stage is intended to provide more detail, headroom, and a broader transition from rhythm to solo sounds rather than simply maximizing raw gain. [1][2]
