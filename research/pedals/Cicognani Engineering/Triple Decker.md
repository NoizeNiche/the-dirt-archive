# Cicognani Engineering — Triple Decker

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Triple Decker
- **Builder:** Cicognani Engineering
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Cicognani Engineering Triple Decker, a three-channel analog tube preamp/overdrive with MIDI switching, boost, mute, FX loop, and Rec Out speaker simulation.

## What this pedal is
Cicognani Engineering describes Triple Decker as an evolution of the Double Decker, designed as a compact **three-channel MIDI guitar preamp**. It can be used into an amplifier, into a power amplifier as a preamp, or directly to a mixer/recording device through Rec Out. [1]

## Colorways
- The official current model is documented with a metal chassis and acrylic/PMMA-style front presentation, but no complete factory colorway sequence was established.

## Versions and factory options
Documented features:
- Three channels plus boost and mute.
- MIDI-relay switching with storable presets.
- Channel 1 and 2 three-band tone stacks.
- Channel 3 three-band tone stack.
- Boost level.
- Serial/parallel FX loop.
- Dry/wet FX control.
- Rec Out speaker simulator.
- External control via MIDI. [1]

The tube type is **12AU7**. Power is 9-12V DC negative tip, with 500-700 mA consumption and an internal voltage-doubler circuit. [1]

## Version changes
Cicognani explicitly describes Triple Decker as an evolution of Double Decker rather than a cosmetic successor. Channel count increases to three and the switching/preset system is expanded. [1]

## Transistor
No discrete transistor stage is documented as the primary gain element. The core tube is **12AU7** with high-impedance IC stages. [1]

## Diode
No exact-model diode specification was established.

## Sound
The pedal is designed around Plexi-style overdrive, with channel 1 offering lower-gain/cleaner territory and channel 2 and 3 carrying progressively heavier saturation. The builder specifically emphasizes dynamic, wall-of-sound overdrive on Channel 2 and high-gain capability on Channel 3. [1]
