# Gamechanger Audio — Plasma Pedal

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Plasma Pedal

- **Identity:** Gamechanger Audio's Plasma Pedal.

## What this pedal is
The Plasma Pedal is a high-voltage distortion effect built around a xenon-filled gas-discharge tube. The audio signal is converted into high-voltage electrical energy and produces a visible electrical arc between electrodes, which is then translated back into the audio signal.

## Colorways
- The standard Plasma Pedal uses the signature **blue arc display**.
- The arc is part of the circuit's operation, not a conventional LED effect indicator.

## Versions and factory options
- High-voltage gas-discharge distortion architecture.
- Visible electrical arc between electrodes.
- **Level** controls output.
- **Volume/Drive** controls shape the distortion intensity and response.
- True bypass.
- Standard 9V DC pedalboard power.

## Version changes
- The standard Plasma Pedal is distinct from the later **Plasma Coil** and **Plasma Drive** products.
- Those related products are not merged into the core Plasma Pedal record.

## Transistor
- The defining distortion mechanism is the gas-discharge tube, not a transistor-only clipping stage.
- **Exact semiconductor complement:** Unknown.
## Diode
- The visible blue arc comes from the xenon gas-discharge tube rather than an LED clipping element.
- Exact clipping/rectifier diode information was not established.
- **Exact part:** Unknown.

## Sound
The Plasma Pedal creates an unusually hard-edged, high-energy distortion with aggressive harmonics and compression. The visible arc responds directly to the signal, making the pedal's high-voltage processing part of both its behavior and visual identity.
