# Compulsive Audio - Texas Holdem ZZ

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Compulsive Audio
- **Catalog identity:** Texas Holdem ZZ
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Compulsive Audio's versatile overdrive highlighted by John De Luca as the company's most popular product in his 2012 interview.

## What this pedal is
John De Luca identified Texas Holdem ZZ as Compulsive Audio's most popular pedal at the time of the 2012 interview. He described it as a versatile overdrive that works with both Telecaster and Les Paul setups and covers sounds from Texas blues through classic rock. [1]
