# Compulsive Audio - Dimed Boogie

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Compulsive Audio
- **Catalog identity:** Dimed Boogie
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** Discontinued high-gain distortion designed by John De Luca as Compulsive Audio's first dedicated Heavy Metal pedal.

## What this pedal is
Compulsive Audio's John De Luca described the Dimed Boogie as his first attempt at building a pedal aimed at a **Heavy Metal** sound. He later discontinued it because the design was too complicated and replaced it with the Thor's Hammer. [1]

Effects Database's 2010 brand introduction separately lists the Dimed Boogie Distortion as one of Compulsive Audio's initial cataloged effects. [2]
