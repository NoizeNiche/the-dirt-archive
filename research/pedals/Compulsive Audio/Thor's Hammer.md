# Compulsive Audio - Thor's Hammer

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Compulsive Audio
- **Catalog identity:** Thor's Hammer
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** Heavy-metal-oriented Compulsive Audio design that replaced the discontinued Dimed Boogie.

## What this pedal is
John De Luca explained that Thor's Hammer replaced the Dimed Boogie after the latter was retired. In his interview, he described the Dimed Boogie as his first attempt at a Heavy Metal pedal and stated that Thor's Hammer became its successor. [1]

Effects Database lists Thor's Hammer as one of Compulsive Audio's original cataloged models. [2]
