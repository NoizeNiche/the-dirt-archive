# Compulsive Audio - Valve Howler Jr.

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Compulsive Audio
- **Catalog identity:** Valve Howler Jr.
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Tube Screamer TS-808-derived overdrive with switchable clipping and boutique component options.

## What this pedal is
Effects Database describes Valve Howler Jr. as a TS-808 overdrive clone with boutique modifications, including switchable clipping. [1]

## Controls
The verified functional controls are:
- **Clipping:** adjustable/switchable clipping behavior.
- **Tone**
- **Overdrive**
- **Volume** [1]

## Colorways
Compulsive Audio's pedals were made to customer paint/finish specifications, so cosmetic variation is possible without implying a circuit revision. [2]

## Versions and factory options
- True bypass.
- Based on the TS-808 design with boutique modifications.
- Can be "chipped" with either **RC4558P** or **Burr Brown OPA2134PA** op-amps, according to Effects Database. [1]
- No numbered hardware revision sequence was established.

## Version changes
No reliable dated Valve Howler Jr. V1/V2 chronology was found. The documented distinction is the available clipping and op-amp options. [1]

## Construction
Effects Database lists high-quality Vishay metallized-film capacitors, tight-tolerance resistors, silver solder, and military-spec wire; Compulsive Audio's builder interview confirms hand wiring and through-hole construction for many pedals. [1][2]

## Transistor
- **Exact transistor/device:** Not publicly documented for the model in the checked sources.

## Diode
- **Clipping:** switchable/adjustable clipping is documented, but the exact diode complement is not identified in the checked source. [1]

## Sound
Effects Database describes the pedal as covering blues through vintage hard rock within the TS-808-style overdrive family. [1]

## Photo provenance
Effects Database provides an exact Valve Howler Jr. model record and an eBay product-image lead. [1]
