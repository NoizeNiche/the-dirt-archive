# Compulsive Audio - Tone Oracle

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Compulsive Audio
- **Catalog identity:** Tone Oracle
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive / Distortion / Preamp
- **Identity:** JFET-based discrete Marshall 1968 Plexi JMP emulation.

## What this pedal is
Effects Database describes Tone Oracle as an overdrive pedal emulating the sound of a Marshall '68 Plexi JMP. The circuit is fully discrete and uses JFETs to emulate tube behavior. [1]

## Controls
The verified control set is:
- **Level**
- **Master Level**
- **Bass**
- **Mid**
- **Treble**
- **Gain** [1]

## Colorways
Compulsive Audio built pedals to customer specifications, including painted enclosures and optional decals. [1] The archive treats finish choices as cosmetic customization rather than circuit revisions.

## Versions and factory options
- True bypass.
- 9V battery or Boss-style 9V adapter.
- Handmade to customer paint specifications. [1]
- No numbered hardware revision sequence was established.

No reliable dated Tone Oracle hardware revision chronology was found.

## Construction
Effects Database specifies high-quality Vishay metallized-film capacitors, tight-tolerance resistors, silver solder, and military-spec wire. The builder's interview confirms hand wiring and attention to component quality. [1][2]

Tone Oracle covers a wide gain range from nearly clean through heavy-rock distortion while retaining the Marshall '68 Plexi JMP target. [1]
