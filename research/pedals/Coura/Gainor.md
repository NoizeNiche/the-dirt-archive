# Coura - Gainor

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Coura
- **Catalog identity:** Gainor
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** Exact Coura Gainor.

## What this pedal is
Gainor is a Coura distortion pedal from the same early Coura electronics family as the Raizor. Effects Database documents the exact model as a distortion pedal shown at Musikmesse Frankfurt 2007.

## Relationship to Raizor
A contemporaneous Musiker-Board account describes the Gainor as essentially the Raizor with an additional volume control that can raise the signal slightly while the effect is bypassed. This is retained as period user testimony rather than treated as a complete factory specification.

## Controls
- **Distortion section:** the exact-model archive classifies Gainor as a distortion pedal, but a complete verified control-label/value transcription was not found.
- **Additional volume:** period testimony documents a second volume function intended to provide a slight signal lift when the effect is switched off.
