# COG Effects — Tarkin Fuzz

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** Tarkin Fuzz
- **Builder:** COG Effects
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Four-transistor Green Russian-inspired Muff-family fuzz with In, Tone, Out and Mid Shift. [1]

## Controls

- **IN:** Input/gain interaction.
- **TONE:** Tonal balance.
- **OUT:** Output.
- **MID SHIFT:** Alters the Muff-style midrange profile. [1]

## Circuit lineage

- **Primary reference:** Green Russian / Big Muff family.
- COG describes Tarkin as a bass-oriented four-transistor fuzz adapted for both bass and thick guitar. [1]

## Transistor

- Four-transistor Muff-family architecture is documented at the lineage level.
- Exact production transistor part numbers are not published.

## Diode / clipping

- Exact clipping-device part numbers are not established.

## Power / construction

- **9V DC**.
- True bypass.
- Die-cast aluminum enclosure. [1]

## Version history

- **Mk1 / original Tarkin**
- Later superseded by **Tarkin Mk2**, which is retained separately because it adds LPF/HPF and internal clipping options. [1][2]

## Sound

Mid Shift moves the voice away from a fixed Muff scoop toward flatter/more forward mids. Tone spans deeper low-frequency fuzz through brighter settings, while the input and output stages remain responsive to guitar-volume changes. [1]
