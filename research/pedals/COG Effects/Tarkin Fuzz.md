# COG Effects — Tarkin Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Tarkin Fuzz
- **Builder:** COG Effects
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** COG Effects Tarkin, a Green Russian-inspired fuzz voiced for bass and thick guitar.

## What this pedal is
COG Effects describes the original Tarkin as a Green Russian-based four-transistor fuzz tuned for bass and thick guitar sounds. [1]

## Colorways
COG's standard presentation uses engraved aluminium enclosures, with custom finishes available. No complete Tarkin colorway sequence was established. [1][2]

## Versions and factory options
- In.
- Tone.
- Out.
- Mid Shift switch.
- True bypass.
- 9V DC.
- Die-cast aluminium enclosure. [1]

## Version changes
The original **Mk1** was superseded by the smaller, expanded **Mk2**. The Mk1 remains available historically/custom-order according to COG's archived page. [1][3]

## Transistor
The circuit is a four-transistor Green Russian-derived fuzz, but exact transistor part numbers were not published in the checked manufacturer sources. [1]

## Diode
The original model's exact diode complement was not established.

## Sound
The Mid Shift switch changes the core Muff-style mid profile from scooped toward flatter mids for mix cut. Tone spans deep low-end fuzz through brighter, more cutting settings, and the pedal responds to guitar-volume changes with usable clean-up. [1]
