# COG Effects — Grand Tarkin Mk2

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Grand Tarkin Mk2
- **Builder:** COG Effects
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** COG Effects Grand Tarkin Mk2, an expanded bass fuzz with two switchable clean/fuzz level presets.

## What this pedal is
The Mk2 retains the Grand Tarkin's bass-fuzz architecture while replacing the original Blend and Level arrangement with separate Fuzz and Clean level controls and an A/B footswitch. [1]

## Colorways
COG documents CNC-engraved aluminium enclosures; no complete finish sequence was established. [1][2]

## Versions and factory options
- Sustain.
- Tone.
- Mids.
- Two Fuzz Level controls.
- Two Clean Level controls.
- A/B footswitch switching the two level presets.
- Filtered clean blend.
- True bypass.
- 9V DC.
- North-mounted jacks.
- Die-cast aluminium enclosure. [1]

## Version changes
The **Mk2** is explicitly the second iteration of Grand Tarkin, adding the dual preset level architecture while retaining the core sound. [1]

## Transistor
The pedal retains the Green Russian-derived fuzz lineage; exact transistor part numbers were not established for the Mk2.

## Diode
No exact diode part number was established.

## Sound
The Mk2 is built for large bass fuzz sounds with low-end retention and increased punch. The A/B controls allow two clean/fuzz blend presets to be recalled during a performance. [1]
