# COG Effects — TK-421-X Bass Distortion

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** TK-421-X Bass Distortion
- **Builder:** COG Effects
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** COG Effects TK-421-X, a high-gain bass expansion of TK-421 with filtered low-end control and switchable clipping.

## What this pedal is
COG Effects describes TK-421-X as the bass-oriented, higher-gain expansion of TK-421. It trades some dynamics for aggressive, biting bass distortion. [1]

## Versions and factory options
- Dirt.
- LPF.
- Level.
- Internal Range switch for LPF frequency.
- Internal clipping selection.
- Symmetrical LED standard.
- Optional asymmetrical LED.
- Optional symmetrical Silicon.
- Optional asymmetrical Silicon.
- Relay true bypass.
- 9V DC.
- Die-cast aluminium enclosure. [1]

## Version changes
COG's discontinued page says an MK2 was planned; finished Mk2 specifications were not established. [2]

## Transistor
Exact transistor complement not published.

## Diode
Documented options are symmetrical LED, asymmetrical LED, symmetrical Silicon, and asymmetrical Silicon. [1]

## Sound
TK-421-X emphasizes aggressive bass distortion, with LPF and clipping choices used to tailor the low end and clipping texture. [1]
