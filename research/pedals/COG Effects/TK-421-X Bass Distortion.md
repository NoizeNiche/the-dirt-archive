# COG Effects — TK-421-X Bass Distortion

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** TK-421-X Bass Distortion
- **Builder:** COG Effects
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** Higher-gain bass expansion of TK-421 with LPF control and multiple internal clipping options. [1][2]

## Controls / architecture
- **DIRT**
- **LPF**
- **LEVEL**
- Internal **RANGE** switch for the LPF frequency.
- Internal clipping-selection switches.
- Symmetrical **LED** stock clipping.
- Optional asymmetrical LED, symmetrical Silicon and asymmetrical Silicon configurations. [1]

## Circuit lineage
- **Primary lineage:** COG TK-421 / RAT-family distortion.
- The X version is a bass-oriented/high-gain expansion of the original TK-421.

## Transistor
- Exact production transistor/device part number is not publicly documented.
## Diode / clipping
Documented clipping options include:
- Symmetrical LED
- Asymmetrical LED
- Symmetrical Silicon
- Asymmetrical Silicon [1]

## Power / construction
- **9V DC**.
- Relay true bypass.
- Die-cast aluminum enclosure. [1]

## Version history
- COG's discontinued archive says an **MK2 was planned**, but no finished Mk2 specification is established. [2]
- No other numbered electronic revision established.

## Sound
TK-421-X is intended for aggressive bass distortion. LPF and the internal clipping choices let the player tailor both low-frequency response and clipping texture. [1]
