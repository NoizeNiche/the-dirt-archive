# COG Effects — TK-421-X Bass Distortion

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** TK-421-X Bass Distortion
- **Builder:** COG Effects
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** COG Effects TK-421-X, a high-gain bass expansion of the TK-421 with filtered low-end control and switchable clipping.

## What this pedal is
COG Effects describes TK-421-X as the bass-oriented, higher-gain expansion of TK-421. It trades some of the Knightfall family's dynamics for aggressive, biting, barking distortion and huge bass-gain tones. [1]

## Version changes
The original TK-421-X is documented as the expanded bass member of the TK-421 family. COG's discontinued page states that an MK2 was planned, but the checked sources do not establish the finished Mk2 specifications. [1][2]

## Transistor
The exact transistor complement was not published.

## Diode
The documented clipping options are symmetrical LED standard, asymmetrical LED, symmetrical Silicon, and asymmetrical Silicon. [1]

## Sound
The TK-421-X emphasizes aggressive bass distortion. Its low-pass filtering and internal clipping choices allow the user to tailor the amount of low end and clipping texture. [1]
