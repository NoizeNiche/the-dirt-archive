# COG Effects — Grand Tarkin Bass Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Grand Tarkin Bass Fuzz
- **Builder:** COG Effects
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** COG Effects Grand Tarkin, a Green Russian-inspired bass fuzz with filtered clean blend and adjustable mids.

## What this pedal is
COG Effects describes Grand Tarkin as a bass-focused development of its Tarkin Green Russian-inspired fuzz. It adds adjustable Mids and a filtered clean blend while retaining Sustain, Level, and Tone. [1]

## Versions and factory options
- Sustain.
- Tone.
- Mids.
- Level.
- Filtered clean blend.
- Relay true bypass.
- North-mounted jacks.
- 9V DC.
- Die-cast aluminium enclosure. [1]

## Version changes
The original Grand Tarkin was later replaced by **Grand Tarkin Mk2**. [2]

## Transistor
The circuit lineage is a Green Russian-style four-transistor fuzz design, but exact transistor part numbers were not established.

## Diode
No exact diode part number was established.

## Sound
The filtered clean blend is designed to retain low-end punch and integrate with the fuzz rather than sounding like a separate full-range clean signal. Adjustable mids reduce the classic Muff-style scoop. [1]
