# COG Effects — Grand Tarkin Bass Fuzz

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** Grand Tarkin Bass Fuzz
- **Builder:** COG Effects
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Bass-focused Green Russian-inspired fuzz with filtered clean blend, adjustable mids and sustain/tone/level controls. [1]

## Controls / architecture

- **SUSTAIN**
- **TONE**
- **MIDS**
- **LEVEL**
- **FILTERED CLEAN BLEND**
- Relay true bypass.
- North-mounted jacks. [1]

## Circuit lineage

- **Primary reference:** Green Russian / Big Muff family.
- COG presents Grand Tarkin as a bass-oriented evolution of the Tarkin fuzz, using a filtered clean blend to retain low-end punch. [1]

## Transistor

- Green Russian/Muff-family four-transistor architecture is the lineage reference.
- Exact production transistor part numbers are not established.

## Diode / clipping

- Exact diode part numbers are not established.

## Power

- **9V DC**. [1]
- Current draw not established.

## Construction

- Die-cast aluminum enclosure.
- Bass-focused clean blend and adjustable mids. [1]

## Version history

- Original Grand Tarkin was later replaced by **Grand Tarkin Mk2**. [2]
- Mk2 is maintained as a separate archive record.

## Sound

The filtered clean blend is intended to retain bass punch underneath the fuzz without simply mixing an untouched full-range dry path. Mids can reduce the classic Muff-style scoop, while Sustain and Tone shape the fuzz layer. [1]
