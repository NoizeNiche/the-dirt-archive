# COG Effects — Tarkin Mk2

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Tarkin Mk2
- **Builder:** COG Effects
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Compact second-generation Tarkin Green Russian-inspired fuzz in a Hammond 1590A enclosure, with LPF/HPF filter switching and internal clipping options. [1][2]

## Controls / architecture
- **FUZZ**
- **TONE**
- **LEVEL**
- **LPF** switch.
- **HPF** switch.
- Internal clipping-stage switches.
- Internal options can remove either clipping-diode pair. [1]

## Circuit lineage
- **Primary lineage:** COG Tarkin / Green Russian / Big Muff family.
- Mk2 is the smaller successor to the original Tarkin and changes the Tone-filter corner behavior while adding clipping options. [1]

## Transistor
- Four-transistor Green Russian-derived design.
- Exact production transistor part numbers are not established.

## Diode / clipping
- Internal switches change the clipping configuration or remove either clipping-diode stage.
- Exact diode part numbers are not established. [1]

## Power / construction
- **9V DC**.
- **Hammond 1590A** enclosure.
- True bypass. [1]

## Version history
### Tarkin Mk1
Original larger-format design with Mid Shift.

### Tarkin Mk2
- Smaller 1590A enclosure.
- LPF and HPF switches.
- Expanded internal clipping options.
- Retains Green Russian/Muff-family core. [1][2]

## Sound
LPF and HPF change the Tone-filter behavior and can move the classic Green Russian scoop toward more cutting or fuller responses. Internal clipping choices provide smoother or more biting saturation. [1]
