# COG Effects — Tarkin Mk2

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Tarkin Mk2
- **Builder:** COG Effects
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** COG Effects Tarkin Mk2, a compact Green Russian-inspired fuzz with switchable filter shapes and internal clipping options.

## What this pedal is
COG Effects describes the Mk2 as the smaller successor to the Tarkin Mk1, placed in a Hammond 1590A enclosure and expanded with LPF and HPF switches around the Tone circuit. [1]

## Colorways
The documented stock enclosure is engraved aluminium. Custom finishes are offered by COG. [1]

## Versions and factory options
- Fuzz.
- Tone.
- Level.
- LPF switch.
- HPF switch.
- Internal clipping-stage switches.
- Internal options to remove either clipping-diode pair.
- True bypass.
- 9V DC.
- Hammond 1590A enclosure. [1][2]

## Version changes
Compared with Mk1, Mk2 adds LPF/HPF switches that alter the Tone-filter corner frequencies and adds internal clipping options, including diode removal. [1][3]

## Transistor
The circuit remains a Green Russian-derived four-transistor fuzz design; exact transistor part numbers were not established.

## Diode
Internal switches can alter clipping style or remove either clipping-diode stage. Exact diode part numbers were not established. [1]

## Sound
LPF and HPF allow the familiar Green Russian mid scoop to be reshaped toward more cutting midrange. The internal clipping switches move between smoother and more biting responses. [1]
