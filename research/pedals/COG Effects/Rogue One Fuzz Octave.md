# COG Effects — Rogue One Fuzz Octave

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Rogue One Fuzz Octave
- **Builder:** COG Effects
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz / Octave
- **Identity:** COG Effects Rogue One, a dual-fuzz bass pedal combining two Grand Tarkin channels with a parallel analogue octave-down channel.

## What this pedal is
COG Effects describes Rogue One, later renamed **R-1**, as two full-featured Grand Tarkin fuzz channels plus a parallel analogue octave channel. [1]

## Colorways
COG offers custom engraved finishes; no complete Rogue One finish sequence was established.

## Versions and factory options
Each Grand Tarkin channel provides:
- Fuzz.
- Level.
- Tone.
- Mids.
- Clean Level.
- Internal clipping-stage switches.

The octave channel adds:
- Octave-down level.
- Filter.
- Clean Kill toggle.
- A/B switching between fuzz channels.
- True bypass.
- North-mounted jacks.
- 9V DC.
- Die-cast aluminium enclosure. [1][2]

## Version changes
The original **Rogue One** name was later changed to **R-1**. The circuit architecture documented on the surviving COG page is the same family identity. [1]

## Transistor
The two fuzz channels are discrete Grand Tarkin circuits, but exact transistor part numbers were not established.

## Diode
Each fuzz channel has switches that can remove the clipping diodes from either clipping stage. [1]

## Sound
The two fuzz channels cover different Big Muff-era voicings, while the octave channel can be added in parallel and filtered to shape the octave-down tone. [1] The manufacturer demos include bass use. [2]
