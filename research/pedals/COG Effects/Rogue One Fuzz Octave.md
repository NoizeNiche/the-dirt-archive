# COG Effects — Rogue One Fuzz Octave

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** Rogue One Fuzz Octave
- **Builder:** COG Effects
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz / Octave
- **Identity:** Two full-featured Tarkin/Grand-Tarkin-derived fuzz channels plus a parallel analog octave-down section; later renamed **R-1**. [1][2]

## Architecture / controls

Each fuzz channel provides:
- **FUZZ**
- **LEVEL**
- **TONE**
- **MIDS**
- **CLEAN LEVEL**
- Clipping-stage options.

The octave section provides:
- **OCTAVE LEVEL**
- **FILTER**
- **CLEAN KILL**

An **A/B footswitch** selects the two fuzz channels. [1]

## Circuit lineage

- **Primary lineage:** COG Tarkin/Grand Tarkin Green Russian/Muff-derived fuzz.
- Parallel analogue **octave-down** circuit is added to the two fuzz channels. [1][2]

## Transistor

- Exact production transistor part numbers are not publicly documented.
- The fuzz channels retain Tarkin/Grand Tarkin lineage.

## Diode / clipping

- Each fuzz channel has clipping-stage switching that can remove clipping diodes from either stage. [1]
- Exact diode part numbers are not established.

## Power / construction

- **2.1 mm 9V input**.
- True bypass.
- North-mounted jacks.
- Die-cast aluminum enclosure. [1]

## Version history

- Original model: **Rogue One**.
- Later renamed **R-1**. [1]
- No other numbered electrical revision established.

## Sound

The two fuzz channels cover distinct Muff-family voicings, while the parallel octave-down section can be filtered and mixed underneath them. The Clean Kill function removes the octave section's clean path for more isolated octave processing. [1]
