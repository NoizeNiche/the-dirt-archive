# COG Effects — TK-421 Distortion

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** TK-421 Distortion
- **Builder:** COG Effects
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** COG Effects TK-421, a rat-style guitar/bass distortion without the original LM308 as standard.

## What this pedal is
COG Effects describes TK-421 as a more aggressive, clearer alternative within the Knightfall/Tarkin family. The design omits the classic LM308 as standard, retaining a socket so users can experiment with one if desired. [1]

## Colorways
No complete TK-421 finish sequence was established.

## Versions and factory options
- Dirt.
- Tone.
- Diode Selection.
- Range.
- Externally accessible clipping-diode choice.
- Three internal Tone ranges.
- Relay true bypass.
- 9V DC.
- Die-cast aluminium enclosure. [1]

## Version changes
COG's discontinued archive states that a **MK2** was planned. The archive does not infer its final circuit changes because the checked source does not document the finished Mk2. [2]

## Transistor
The central gain IC is intentionally **not the LM308 as standard**; the socket permits LM308 experimentation. [1]

## Diode
External clipping selection is a defining feature, with two clipping options described by COG. [1]

## Sound
TK-421 aims for aggressive upper-mid bite with more clarity and low-end retention than a traditional 308-based RAT. Tone, Range, Dirt, and Diode Selection interact across guitar and bass applications. [1]
