# COG Effects — TK-421 Distortion

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** TK-421 Distortion
- **Builder:** COG Effects
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** RAT-style guitar/bass distortion with external clipping selection and three internal Tone ranges, deliberately not using an LM308 as standard. [1][2]

## Controls

- **DIRT**
- **TONE**
- **DIODE SELECTION**
- **RANGE**
- External clipping selector.
- Three internal Tone ranges.
- Relay true bypass. [1]

## Circuit lineage

- **Primary reference:** RAT family.
- The standard gain IC is **not an LM308**; the circuit includes a socket/experimental provision that permits LM308 use. [1]

## Active device

- Standard active IC: exact part number not securely established by the reviewed source.
- **LM308:** optional/exploratory, not the stock device. [1]

## Diode / clipping

- External **DIODE SELECTION** is a defining factory feature.
- Exact selectable device list is not fully established from the reviewed source. [1]

## Power / construction

- **9V DC**.
- Relay true bypass.
- Die-cast aluminum enclosure. [1]

## Version history

- COG's discontinued archive says an **MK2 was planned**.
- No final Mk2 specification is established, so the archive does not invent one. [2]

## Sound

COG positions TK-421 as an aggressive RAT-family distortion with more clarity and low-end retention than the darker traditional 308/RAT presentation. [1]
