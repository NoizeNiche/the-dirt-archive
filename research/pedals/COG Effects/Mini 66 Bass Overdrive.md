# COG Effects — Mini 66 Bass Overdrive

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** Mini 66 Bass Overdrive
- **Builder:** COG Effects
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Compact one-channel derivative of Knightfall 66, using filtered clean blend with three-position Fat and Cut controls. [1][2]

## Controls

- **GAIN**
- **CLEAN LEVEL**
- **FAT:** Changes low-frequency content entering the gain stage.
- **CUT:** Changes post-clipping high-frequency content.
- Filtered clean blend.
- True bypass. [1]

## Circuit lineage

- **Primary lineage:** COG Knightfall 66.
- Mini 66 keeps the filtered-clean concept while reducing the dual-channel format to one drive path. [1][2]
