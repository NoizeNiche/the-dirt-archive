# COG Effects — Mini 66 Bass Overdrive

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Mini 66 Bass Overdrive
- **Builder:** COG Effects
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** COG Effects Mini 66, the compact one-channel descendant of Knightfall 66.

## What this pedal is
The Mini 66 is a streamlined version of Knightfall 66 in a Hammond 1590A enclosure. It keeps the filtered clean blend and replaces the larger pedal's Voice and Tone controls with three-position Fat and Cut switches. [1]

## Colorways
COG uses engraved aluminium enclosures and custom finishes. No complete Mini 66 finish sequence was established. [1][2]

## Versions and factory options
- Gain.
- Clean Level.
- Three-position Fat switch.
- Three-position Cut switch.
- Filtered clean blend.
- True bypass.
- 9V DC.
- Hammond 1590A die-cast aluminium enclosure. [1]

## Version changes
Mini 66 is a compact single-channel derivative of Knightfall 66. [1][2]

## Sound
Fat changes the amount of low end entering the gain stage; Cut controls post-clipping high-frequency content. The filtered clean path integrates with the driven signal for a larger bass overdrive sound. [1]
