# COG Effects — Dual Knightfall

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** Dual Knightfall
- **Builder:** COG Effects
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive
- **Identity:** Two independent Knightfall circuits in one COG enclosure, with selectable MOSFET, Silicon or LED clipping and internal bass options. [1]

## Controls / architecture

- Each side has **GAIN**, **BASS**, and **DIODE SELECT** functions.
- Three clipping choices:
  - **MOSFET**
  - **SILICON**
  - **LED**
- Internal Bass switch.
- Two independent footswitchable channels.
- Relay true bypass.
- North-mounted jacks. [1]

## Circuit lineage

- **Primary lineage:** COG Knightfall.
- Dual Knightfall packages two Knightfall circuits rather than introducing an unrelated fixed topology.

## Transistor / clipping

- Exact gain-transistor part number is not documented.
- Clipping modes explicitly include **MOSFET, Silicon and LED**. [1]

## Power

- **9V DC**. [1]
- Exact current draw is not established.

## Construction

- Die-cast aluminum enclosure.
- Relay true bypass.
- Dual-channel format. [1]

## Version history

- COG's discontinued-pedal archive states Dual Knightfall was later **replaced by Knightfall 66**. [2]
- No additional numbered electronic revision established.

## Sound

Each side can be used independently or stacked, while the clipping selector changes the saturation character and the internal Bass option broadens low-frequency weight. [1]
