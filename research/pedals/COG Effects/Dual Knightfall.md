# COG Effects — Dual Knightfall

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Dual Knightfall
- **Builder:** COG Effects
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** COG Effects Dual Knightfall Distortion, two independent Knightfall circuits in one enclosure.

## What this pedal is
COG Effects describes Dual Knightfall as two Knightfall distortion circuits sharing one pedal enclosure. Each side can be used independently or stacked. [1]

## Colorways
COG used engraved aluminium enclosures and custom finishes. No complete Dual Knightfall finish sequence was established.

## Versions and factory options
- Two independent Knightfall channels.
- Gain, Bass and Diode Selection.
- Internal Bass switch.
- Three-position diode selection: MOSFET standard, Silicon, LED.
- Custom diode selection available.
- Relay true bypass for both circuits.
- North-mounted jacks.
- 9V DC.
- Die-cast aluminium enclosure. [1]

## Version changes
COG's discontinued archive states that Dual Knightfall was later **replaced by Knightfall 66**. [2]

## Transistor
No exact transistor part number was established.

## Diode
Stock options are MOSFET, Silicon, and LED clipping. [1]

## Sound
The two channels can be used separately or stacked, with the internal Bass switch broadening low-frequency options. [1]
