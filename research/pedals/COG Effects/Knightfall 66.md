# COG Effects — Knightfall 66

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Knightfall 66
- **Builder:** COG Effects
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** COG Effects Knightfall 66, a dual-preset bass overdrive with filtered clean blend.

## What this pedal is
Knightfall 66 provides fat cleans, mid-heavy growl, and higher-gain overdrive, with two footswitchable gain settings. [1]

## Versions and factory options
- Gain A and Gain B.
- Level A and Level B.
- A/B footswitch.
- Voice control for pre-gain low end.
- Tone control for post-clipping high end.
- Filtered clean blend.
- True bypass.
- 9V–18V operation.
- North-mounted jacks.
- Die-cast aluminium enclosure. [1][2]

## Version changes
Knightfall 66 superseded Dual Knightfall in the COG range. [3]

## Sound
The filtered clean path integrates with the drive channel, while Voice and Tone provide low/high shaping and A/B recalls two gain/level setups. [1]
