# Bigfoot Engineering - Bigfoot King Fuzz XL

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Bigfoot Engineering
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive

## Research
Deluxe two-in-one design combining the Thunder Pup overdrive and King Fuzz distortion. Separate controls provide Overdrive Gain, Overdrive Volume, Fuzz Gain and Fuzz Volume, plus independent mode/engagement switching for the two sections. The overdrive side is described as Marshall/Angus Young-style grit, while the King Fuzz side targets a high-gain Dan Auerbach-inspired saturation. It is all-analogue, hand built in England, uses a 9VDC supply with no battery option, and has buffered bypass.
