# Bigfoot F.X. - Spaghetti Western Fuzz

## PRP identity

- **Builder:** Bigfoot F.X.
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz

## Research

Distinctive fuzz designed for maximum buzz and honk, aimed at 1960s pop, surf and spaghetti-western textures rather than smooth Fuzz Face or Big Muff sounds. Effects Database describes its character as closer to early Fuzzrite and Companion-style fuzzes and notes that it works best with single coils. Controls are Volume and Fuzz; the Fuzz control changes both gain and the character/type of fuzz as it is turned. Published April 6, 2009.
