# Bigfoot F.X. - The Humongous Box

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Bigfoot F.X.
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Fuzz

## Research
Unique Bigfoot F.X. design rather than a modified clone. Effects Database describes a very large alternative/grunge-oriented sound that moves from subtle distortion at minimum gain to smooth fuzz at maximum. Controls are Volume, Tone and Gain. The circuit is all analog with true bypass, and the builder specified high-quality components and a powder-coated enclosure with baked-on print. Power is 9V battery or 9V+ adapter, center-negative barrel jack. Published November 6, 2016.
