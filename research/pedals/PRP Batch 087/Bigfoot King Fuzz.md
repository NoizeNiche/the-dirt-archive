# Bigfoot Engineering - Bigfoot King Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Bigfoot Engineering
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Fuzz

## Research
Flagship high-gain guitar fuzz designed to cover a broad range of fuzz and distortion tones. Controls are Fuzz Gain and Fuzz Volume. The builder states there are no EQ controls, with the voicing designed around dynamics and guitar-volume interaction. Power is 9VDC or an internal PP3 battery; construction is all-analogue and hand built in England. Output is buffered bypass. The builder describes the sound as covering medium-gain blues through fully saturated rock.
