# BigNoise Amplification - Bulls Eye Overdrive

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** BigNoise Amplification
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive

## Research
Dynamic overdrive/boost covering clean boost through Bluesbreaker territory and hotter Marshall-style gain. Controls documented by the builder include Gain, Tone, Shape and Fat. Shape is an active mid EQ that can scoop or boost mids; Fat adds low-mid grind. The builder describes broad usable gain response and strong cleanup/drive interaction across the Gain range.
