# Bigfoot F.X. - Fuzz Hog

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Bigfoot F.X.
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz

## Research
New fuzz design, not a clone or modified circuit. Effects Database describes it as responsive to picking dynamics, useful for both leads and rhythm, with fat chords and good cut through its square-wave character. Silicon transistors are described as carefully biased, with useful cleanup from the guitar volume control. Controls are Volume and Gain. The circuit is all analog with true bypass, can run from a 9V battery or 9V+ adapter, and uses a center-negative barrel power jack.
