# Bigfoot Engineering - Bigfoot Germanium Fuzz (+Buffer)

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Bigfoot Engineering
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz

## Research
Special edition based on the King Fuzz circuit and re-biased around a gold-pin NOS germanium transistor. Controls are Gain and Volume, with an output toggle selecting true bypass or buffered bypass. It can be placed anywhere in the chain, including after buffers, active pickups and wireless systems. Power is 9VDC with no battery option; current draw is stated as greater than 5mA. Construction is all-analogue and hand built in England.
