# Bigfoot Engineering - Bigfoot King Fuzz Mini

## PRP identity

- **Builder:** Bigfoot Engineering
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz

## Research

Mini version of the King Fuzz. The builder states that its circuit and tones are identical to the standard King Fuzz, but it omits the internal battery option. Controls are Fuzz Gain and Fuzz Volume. There are no EQ controls. It uses a standard 2.1mm centre-negative 9VDC supply, has no battery option, is all-analogue and hand built in England, and uses true bypass.
