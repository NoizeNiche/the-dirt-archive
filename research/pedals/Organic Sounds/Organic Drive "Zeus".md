## What this pedal is
Organic Sounds introduced the Zeus in September 2020 as an overdrive following the company's Hydra and Ares models. The builder describes buffered bypass, a natural low-gain/preamp character, and a fat drive that retains its sense of speed as gain is increased. [1][2]

## Sound
The builder describes Zeus as a natural-sounding overdrive with a preamp-like character at low gain, good response to the guitar's volume control, and a fat, focused drive character at higher gain. Independent and retailer descriptions place its range from nearly clean through higher-gain overdrive, with clear note separation, moderate thickness, and a responsive crunch texture. [1][3][4]
