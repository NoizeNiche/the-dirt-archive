# Catalinbread Effects Antichthon

- **Builder:** Catalinbread Effects
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Catalog identity:** Antichthon
- **What this pedal is:** An experimental fuzz/tremolo pedal using guitar-volume interaction and LFO-style behavior to create dynamic fuzz tremolo, self-generating tones, and harmonic fuzz sounds.
- **Colorways:** The original production finish is documented along with Limited Edition Gold and a 2024 Circles Limited Edition.
- **Versions:** Original Antichthon; Limited Edition Gold; 2024 Circles Limited Edition. Antichthon Deluxe is a separate, expanded model and is not conflated here.
- **Version changes:** The documented special editions are limited cosmetic/production variants rather than numbered circuit revisions of the original model.
- **Transistor type:** Exact transistor/device types are not documented in the reviewed product records.
- **Diode type:** Not documented.
- **Controls / switching:** Volume, Gravity, Time, and Space controls, with operating behavior that changes across the pedal's distinct modes.
- **Power:** Period coverage documents 9-18V operation; an exact current manufacturer supply specification was not established in the reviewed sources.
- **Sound:** Antichthon behaves like a fuzz that can become a tremolo, an oscillating tone generator, or a more conventional harmonic fuzz depending on how it is driven. The guitar's volume control is part of the performance interface, affecting how quickly the pedal travels through its unusual textures.
- **Research confidence:** High for the model identity, operating concept, and documented special editions; moderate for power and component specifics.
