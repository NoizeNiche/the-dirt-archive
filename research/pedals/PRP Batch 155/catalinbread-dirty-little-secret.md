# Catalinbread Effects Dirty Little Secret

- **Builder:** Catalinbread Effects
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** Dirty Little Secret
- **What this pedal is:** A Marshall-style foundation overdrive designed around the sound and response of classic Plexi-era Super Lead and Super Bass amplifiers.
- **Colorways:** Standard Dirty Little Secret production finish; red Dirty Little Secret variants are separate DLS Red models rather than the standard target record.
- **Versions:** Original Dirty Little Secret; MKII; MKIII; later DLS Red variants are related but separately named products and are not merged into the standard record.
- **Version changes:** MKII introduced Super Lead and Super Bass voicings, redesigned controls, an internal presence trimmer, and a wider gain range. The MKIII further revised the gain structure and added a three-band EQ tailored to the two voicings, while retaining the internal mode selection and presence adjustment.
- **Transistor type:** Current manufacturer documentation does not specify an exact transistor part; period coverage describes the design as a solid-state Marshall-style preamp rather than a tube stage.
- **Diode type:** Not documented.
- **Controls / switching:** Current model documentation lists an internal Super Lead/Super Bass mode switch and internal presence control; the current front-panel control set is represented as the foundation/preamp-style DLS interface.
- **Power:** 9-18V DC, 5mA, center-negative supply.
- **Sound:** The DLS is voiced as an always-on British foundation pedal, moving from Plexi-style drive into higher-gain Super Lead territory while the Super Bass voicing has lower gain and a different midrange emphasis. The two voicings are designed to respond like distinct Marshall-era tone stacks and can serve as a platform for fuzzes, boosters, and other dirt pedals.
- **Research confidence:** High for the model family and version history; moderate for exact internal component details.
