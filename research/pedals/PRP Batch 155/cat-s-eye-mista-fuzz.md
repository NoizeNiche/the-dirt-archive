# Cat's Eye ESP Mista' Fuzz

- **Builder:** Cat's Eye ESP
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Catalog identity:** Mista' Fuzz
- **What this pedal is:** A Fuzz Face-style germanium fuzz focused on classic Jimi/Hendrix-era response, using hand-selected AC128 transistors and additional design changes intended to improve gain, output, stability, and EQ.
- **Colorways:** Surviving examples show more than one cosmetic treatment, but a complete factory colorway history is not established.
- **Versions:** Mista' Fuzz production model.
- **Version changes:** No numbered production revisions were established from the surviving record.
- **Transistor type:** Hand-picked AC128 germanium transistors.
- **Diode type:** Not documented.
- **Controls / switching:** Volume and Fuzz controls; true bypass switching.
- **Power:** Not documented in the reviewed archival product record.
- **Sound:** The Mista' Fuzz is voiced for classic Jimi-style fuzz and is intended to clean up from the guitar's volume control. Its higher gain and output, reshaped EQ, and attention to biasing were presented as improvements over the operating quirks of early Fuzz Face-style designs.
- **Research confidence:** High for the AC128, control set, and documented design goals.
- **Sources checked:**

## Sound

Thru careful component selection, including the hand picked AC128 germanium transistors (for that classic Hendrix sound), and most of all, proper biasing, we've created a kickass fuzztone.
We added true bypass switching, a Hi Z input to drive the front end of any guitar amp, increased both the gain and the output levels, eliminated radio interference and oscillation, and reshaped the EQ for a better overall tone.
Of course the Mista' Fuzz responds to a players own individual style and touch, and cleans up nicely using only your guitars volume knob, making subtle tone changes effortless.
