# Cat's Eye ESP Hop Hed Fuzztone

- **Builder:** Cat's Eye ESP
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Catalog identity:** Hop Hed Fuzztone
- **What this pedal is:** An all-germanium fuzz designed in the spirit of the Tone Bender Mk III, with a voiced tone circuit and an additional gain trimmer for more dynamic control.
- **Colorways:** Surviving examples include purple finishes; a complete factory colorway history is not established from the surviving documentation.
- **Versions:** Hop Hed Fuzztone production model.
- **Version changes:** No numbered production revisions were established from the surviving record.
- **Transistor type:** Germanium; exact transistor part numbers are not documented.
- **Diode type:** Not documented.
- **Controls / switching:** Volume, Tone, and Fuzz controls, plus a gain trimmer.
- **Power:** Not documented in the reviewed archival product record.
- **Sound:** The Hop Hed is voiced toward the thick, singing character associated with Mk III Tone Bender fuzz, with useful treble range from its tone circuit. The gain trimmer adds another way to shape the feel and dynamics without changing the basic three-knob control set.
- **Research confidence:** High for the model identity and germanium/Mk III description; lower for undocumented production details.
