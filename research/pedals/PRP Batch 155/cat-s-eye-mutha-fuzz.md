# Cat's Eye ESP Mutha' Fuzz

- **Builder:** Cat's Eye ESP
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Fuzz
- **Catalog identity:** Mutha' Fuzz
- **What this pedal is:** A more versatile, musical distortion/fuzz design using an RC4558 wideband op-amp, a tuned EQ section, and germanium clipping diodes.
- **Colorways:** The surviving catalog record does not establish a complete factory colorway history.
- **Versions:** Mutha' Fuzz production model.
- **Version changes:** No numbered production revisions were established from the surviving documentation.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented.
- **Diode type:** Germanium.
- **Controls / switching:** Volume and Effect controls; true bypass switching.
- **Power:** Not documented in the reviewed archival product record.
- **Sound:** Cat's Eye positioned the Mutha' Fuzz as a rounded, output-forward alternative to harsher classic distortion boxes, with enough range to move between purr and scream. The tuned EQ and germanium clipping are central to its described character.
- **Research confidence:** High for the RC4558, germanium diodes, control set, and documented design intent.
