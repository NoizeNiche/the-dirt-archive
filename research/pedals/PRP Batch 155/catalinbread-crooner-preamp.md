# Catalinbread Effects Crooner Preamp

- **Builder:** Catalinbread Effects
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Fuzz / Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** Crooner Preamp
- **What this pedal is:** A BA-31-inspired studio-preamp pedal using a germanium-and-transformer-equipped preamp with a UREI-inspired discrete op-amp stage ahead of it.
- **Colorways:** A mustard-yellow production finish is documented in the surviving product imagery.
- **Versions:** Crooner Preamp production model.
- **Version changes:** No numbered hardware revision was established in the reviewed documentation.
- **Transistor type:** The circuit is described as germanium-equipped, but exact transistor/device part numbers are not publicly specified.
- **Diode type:** Not documented.
- **Controls / switching:** Push, Volume, Filter, and Trim controls.
- **Power:** The original BA-31 was a 30V device; Catalinbread states that the pedal loves 18V and gains additional headroom and output behavior at that supply voltage.
- **Sound:** At lower settings the Crooner works as a clean, dynamic preamp with mild saturation, while higher Push and Trim settings move into compressed overdrive, distortion, and full fuzz. Catalinbread's own examples range from tape-era warmth to thick, blown-out and Velcro-like fuzz.
- **Research confidence:** High for the preamp inspiration, control set, germanium/transformer architecture, and stated sonic range.
