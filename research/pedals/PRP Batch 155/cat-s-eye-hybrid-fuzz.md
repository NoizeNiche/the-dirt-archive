# Cat's Eye ESP Hybrid Fuzz

- **Builder:** Cat's Eye ESP
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Catalog identity:** Hybrid Fuzz
- **What this pedal is:** A fuzz that combines germanium devices for its tonal character with silicon for higher gain, emphasizing volume, harmonics, and sustain.
- **Colorways:** The surviving catalog record does not establish a complete factory colorway history.
- **Versions:** Hybrid Fuzz production model.
- **Version changes:** No numbered production revisions were established from the surviving documentation.
- **Transistor type:** Germanium and silicon; exact transistor part numbers are not documented.
- **Diode type:** Not documented.
- **Controls / switching:** Volume and Fuzz controls.
- **Power:** Not documented in the reviewed archival product record.
- **Sound:** Cat's Eye describes the Hybrid Fuzz as fat, loud, harmonic-rich, and sustain-heavy, with the germanium/silicon pairing intended to combine tone with higher gain. The emphasis is on a large, forward fuzz sound that remains prominent even through bigger amplification.
- **Research confidence:** High for the model identity and hybrid germanium/silicon description; lower for undocumented production details.
