# Catalinbread Effects Airstrip Console Pre

- **Builder:** Catalinbread Effects
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** Airstrip Console Pre
- **What this pedal is:** A discrete console-style preamp inspired by Trident A-Range recording consoles, combining a germanium-and-transformer-equipped preamp with a UREI-inspired discrete op-amp gain stage and treble filtering.
- **Colorways:** Standard Airstrip finish is documented; Catalinbread also sells a separate red-finish Airstrip Console Pre variant.
- **Versions:** Airstrip Console Pre production model.
- **Version changes:** No numbered hardware revision was established for the standard Airstrip Console Pre from the current product documentation.
- **Transistor type:** The circuit is described as germanium-based, but the exact transistor/device part numbers are not publicly specified by the manufacturer.
- **Diode type:** Not documented.
- **Controls / switching:** Push, Volume, Filter, and Trim controls.
- **Power:** Center-negative DC, 9-24V. The manufacturer states that higher voltage increases headroom, volume, and clarity.
- **Sound:** The Airstrip can move from warm, mastered-style clean enhancement into preamp saturation, distortion, and collapsing fuzz as Push and Trim interact. Its transformer stage and filter give the circuit a studio-preamp character that can also function as a very loud clean boost.
- **Research confidence:** High for the circuit description, controls, power, and stated tonal range.
