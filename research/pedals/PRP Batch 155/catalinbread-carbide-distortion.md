# Catalinbread Effects Carbide Distortion

- **Builder:** Catalinbread Effects
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Catalog identity:** Carbide Distortion
- **What this pedal is:** A heavy distortion built from the Boss HM-2 "chainsaw" concept but extensively reworked, with parallel clean-boost and distorted paths and a dedicated Emphasis filter.
- **Colorways:** Standard production finish; a separate Purple Gaze limited-edition finish is documented.
- **Versions:** Carbide Distortion production model, plus documented special-finish editions.
- **Version changes:** The manufacturer describes refinements to clipping, filter values, and transistor selection as part of the Carbide design, but no numbered hardware revision was established.
- **Transistor type:** A changed transistor selection is documented by the designer, but the exact transistor type/part number is not publicly specified in the reviewed manufacturer material.
- **Diode type:** Not documented.
- **Controls / switching:** Dry and Wet level controls and an Emphasis control; the Dry and Wet paths run in parallel and are mixed together.
- **Power:** Center-negative DC, 9-15V.
- **Sound:** Carbide delivers the boosted, abrasive frequency emphasis associated with an HM-2 chainsaw sound while adding a parallel clean path for blendable low-end and punch. Catalinbread's revisions are aimed at more crunch, thud, and palm-mute response rather than simply reproducing the stock HM-2 circuit.
- **Research confidence:** High for the signal architecture, controls, power, and design intent; lower for exact component identities.
