# Catalinbread Effects Clean Little Secret

- **Builder:** Catalinbread Effects
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** Clean Little Secret
- **What this pedal is:** A low-gain Marshall/Blues Breaker-style foundation overdrive with an overhauled tone section, separate Treble and Bass controls, and a broader low-to-medium gain range.
- **Colorways:** The current production finish is documented; a complete factory colorway history is not established.
- **Versions:** Clean Little Secret production model.
- **Version changes:** No numbered hardware revision was established in the reviewed current documentation.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented; Catalinbread specifies a designer-selected op-amp rather than a named transistor device.
- **Diode type:** Not documented.
- **Controls / switching:** Treble, Master, Bass, and Pre-Amp controls. The Bass and Treble controls interact with each other to shape the midrange response.
- **Power:** Center-negative DC, 9-18V.
- **Sound:** The Clean Little Secret is designed to stay subdued as an always-on enhancer at lower Pre-Amp settings, then move into gritty edge-of-breakup and hairier low-to-medium overdrive as gain is increased. Its separate Bass and Treble controls provide a more flexible foundation than a single global tone control.
