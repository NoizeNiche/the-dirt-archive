# Black Mass Electronics - The First Herald

## PRP identity

- **Builder:** Black Mass Electronics
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** The First Herald, a Black Mass Electronics fuzz with a modernized tone stack and selectable clipping modes.

## What this pedal is

The First Herald is one of Black Mass Electronics' early fuzz designs. The builder describes it as a fuzz built around a modernized tone stack and three distinct clipping modes.

## Deep research verification

### Controls and voicing
The builder's exact-model documentation confirms a conventional **Tone** control that moves from more bass/less treble counter-clockwise toward less bass/more treble clockwise. At the lowest setting, the pedal produces a full, present fuzz; increasing the control introduces the builder's characteristic midrange scoop.

### Clipping architecture
Three distinct clipping modes are documented by the exact-model material. The reviewed sources do not establish the individual diode/device types for those modes, so no specific semiconductor is assigned.

### Version history
The surviving source set references **V2 / MKII / V3** forms of The First Herald, indicating multiple historical presentations of the design. A complete electrical revision map is not established well enough to merge those references into a definitive revision table.

### Component evidence
Exact production transistor/device part numbers are not established in the reviewed exact-model sources. An LED is documented as part of the pedal hardware.
