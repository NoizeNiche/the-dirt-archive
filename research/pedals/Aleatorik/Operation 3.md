# Aleatorik — Operation 3

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Operation 3
- **Builder:** Aleatorik
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Hand-built Aleatorik overdrive/distortion associated with Human Gear engineer Jin Terada. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
Operation 3 is documented as an overdrive/distortion with an output transformer and tagboard wiring. A Japanese review describes a warm, slightly fuzzy character with tight low end and good note separation, moving from normal-gain overdrive toward distortion. [1]
