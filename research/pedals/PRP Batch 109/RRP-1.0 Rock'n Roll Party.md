# Boot-Leg Hand Made Effects - RRP-1.0 Rock'n Roll Party

## PRP identity

- **Builder:** Boot-Leg Hand Made Effects
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive

## Research

RRP-1.0 Rock'n Roll Party was designed around Boot-Leg's idea of a “real overdrive,” aiming for a mellow, rich and clear response while also functioning as a gain booster. The maker emphasizes a fat low end, delicate but punchy attack and a touch-sensitive response.

The original RRP-1.0 predates the RRP-2.0 and is kept separately in the archive because the later version changes both the controls and internal construction.

## Features

- Mellow, rich and clear overdrive
- Gain-boosting use
- Touch-sensitive attack
- Fat low-frequency response
- 9V operation
- Early Boot-Leg architecture
