# Boot-Leg Hand Made Effects - HZM-1.2 Hizumi

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Boot-Leg Hand Made Effects
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive

## Research
HZM-1.2 is the second major Hizumi version and keeps the two-stage serial drive architecture of HZM-1.0 while changing the device selection and voicing. Boot-Leg describes the version as using germanium elements for softer, richer distortion while retaining the solid character of the original, with higher output than typical germanium designs.

The Range control shifts the peak frequency. Level sets output and Hizumi controls the overall amount of the two-stage distortion.

## Controls
- Level
- Range
- Hizumi
- Two serially connected drive circuits
- 9V operation
