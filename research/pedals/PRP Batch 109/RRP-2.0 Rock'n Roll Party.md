# Boot-Leg Hand Made Effects - RRP-2.0 Rock'n Roll Party

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Boot-Leg Hand Made Effects
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive

## Research
RRP-2.0 is the second major Rock'n Roll Party version and adds the RAVE control to the original ALL/NIGHT layout. RAVE is not a simple treble-cut tone control: it is designed to preserve high frequencies as it is reduced while maintaining a thicker low end.

The model uses a germanium-oriented internal design and remains a compact three-control drive. Published specifications identify ALL as level, RAVE as tone/voicing and NIGHT as gain. It can also be used as a booster.

## Controls
- ALL (Level)
- RAVE (Tone/voicing)
- NIGHT (Gain)
- Germanium-oriented circuit
- 9V operation
- 82 x 110 x 44 mm
