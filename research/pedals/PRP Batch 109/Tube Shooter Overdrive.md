## Research
Tube Shooter is a Bootlegger Guitar Tube Screamer clone. Surviving listings document the model as an analog overdrive used with Bootlegger's tube amplifier packages, while Effects Database lists it as one of the Bootlegger Guitar pedals added to its catalog in 2016.

The available surviving source material does not establish a complete authoritative control list or a unique circuit modification beyond its Tube Screamer basis, so the archive does not fill in undocumented circuit claims.

## Deep research verification

The surviving exact-product evidence identifies Tube Shooter as a **Bootlegger Guitar Tube Screamer clone**. A 2018 Reverb listing paired a Tube Shooter with a Bootlegger hand-wired Vox AC15-style amplifier and explicitly identifies the Tube Shooter as a Tube Screamer clone. Effects Database's October 17, 2016 weekly overview independently lists **Bootlegger Guitar Tube Shooter Overdrive**, establishing the named pedal in the builder's catalog by that date.

The reviewed sources do not establish a complete authoritative control list, unique modification, or exact component complement for the Tube Shooter, so those details remain unknown.
