# Boot-Leg Hand Made Effects - HZM-2.0 Hizumi Quattro Valvole

## PRP identity

- **Builder:** Boot-Leg Hand Made Effects
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive

## Research

HZM-2.0 Hizumi Quattro Valvole is the higher-spec Hizumi variant in the early Boot-Leg catalog. Surviving listings and reviews identify it as an overdrive/distortion pedal derived from the Hizumi family, while the available primary documentation is sparse.

A surviving review documents the compact Level, Bottom and Hizumi control arrangement and 9V center-negative operation. The model is retained as its own catalog identity rather than merged into HZM-1.0 or HZM-1.2.

## Controls

- 9V battery or adapter
