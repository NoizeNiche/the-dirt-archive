# BOSS - BD-2 Blues Driver

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** BOSS
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive

## Research
The BD-2 Blues Driver is BOSS's amp-style overdrive introduced in 1995. BOSS describes its circuit as a multi-stage clipping design intended to reproduce interactive tube-amp-like gain and compression behavior. The pedal responds strongly to picking dynamics and guitar volume, moving from mild breakup and clean boost through more saturated overdrive.

The standard BD-2 uses Gain, Tone and Level controls, true-bypass-style switching architecture within the BOSS compact format, and supports a 9V battery or PSA-series AC adapter.

## Controls and specifications

- 9V battery or PSA-series AC adapter

- 73 x 129 x 59 mm
- Analog multi-stage clipping circuit
