# Bootlegger Guitar - Zapped Up Yours Analog Tube Distortion

## PRP identity

- **Builder:** Bootlegger Guitar
- **Catalog type:** Distortion

## Research

Zapped “Up Yours” is an analog tube distortion pedal built around an Electro-Harmonix 12AT7. The tube stage receives 15V rather than the lower internal voltage common to many tube pedals, and an active EQ adds Bass, Middle, Treble and a separate Middle Frequency control.

Level sets output and Distortion moves from lower drive into much heavier saturation. The pedal is true bypass and uses an external 15V power supply.

## Controls and architecture

- Level
- Distortion
- Bass
- Middle
- Treble
- Middle Frequency
- Electro-Harmonix 12AT7
- 15V DC tube-stage supply
- True bypass
- No battery operation
- 1 input / 1 output
