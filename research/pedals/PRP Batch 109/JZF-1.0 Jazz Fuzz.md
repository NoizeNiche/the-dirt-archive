# Boot-Leg Hand Made Effects - JZF-1.0 Jazz Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Boot-Leg Hand Made Effects
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz

## Research
JZF-1.0 Jazz Fuzz was developed from an initial goal of giving bass players clear mids and lows, then expanded for guitarists who wanted a fuzz with distortion-like control response. The pedal uses a Vintage control that does more than a conventional tone cut, moving between metallic fuzz and much fatter vintage-style sounds.

Boot-Leg designed the fuzz for smooth adjustment and claims that it can also function as a booster at low settings.

## Controls
- Volume
- Vintage
- Fuzz
- 9V operation
- Guitar and bass oriented voicing
