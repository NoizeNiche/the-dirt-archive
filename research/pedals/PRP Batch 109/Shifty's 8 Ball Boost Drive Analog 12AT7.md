# Bootlegger Guitar - Shifty's 8 Ball Boost Drive Analog 12AT7

## PRP identity

- **Catalog type:** Overdrive

## Research
Shifty's 8 Ball Boost Drive is an analog tube boost/drive pedal built around an Electro-Harmonix 12AT7. The pedal provides a Drive control, Tone control, two-position Mode switch and Master volume, with the tube stage receiving 15V from the supplied power supply.

The mode switch changes the voicing between a more Tube Screamer-like response and a fatter, more compressed bass-heavy mode. It is true bypass and is not battery operated.
