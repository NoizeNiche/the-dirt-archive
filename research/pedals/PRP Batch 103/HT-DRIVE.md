# Blackstar Amplification - HT-DRIVE

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Blackstar Amplification
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive

## Research
The HT-DRIVE is a high-voltage ECC83 / 12AX7 valve overdrive pedal. Its Gain control moves from a clean boost at low settings through crunch and into a full distorted lead tone at maximum. Tone shapes the treble response, while Level controls output.

The pedal also provides a speaker-emulated output for direct recording or mixing-desk use. Blackstar's suggested settings describe the relationship between gain and level as part of the pedal's response: lower gain with more level gives a more dynamic response, while higher gain with less level gives a more compressed, fluid response.

## Controls and connectivity
- Gain
- Tone
- Level
- Output
- Speaker-emulated output
- ECC83 / 12AX7 valve
- 22V DC / 1.1A supplied adapter
