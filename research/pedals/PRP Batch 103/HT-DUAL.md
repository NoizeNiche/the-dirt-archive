# Blackstar Amplification - HT-DUAL

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Blackstar Amplification
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive

## Research
The HT-DUAL is a two-channel high-voltage valve distortion pedal built around an ECC83 / 12AX7. Channel 1 is a lower-gain clean/crunch voice, while Channel 2 extends into stronger crunch and lead distortion. Each channel has its own Gain and Level controls, with shared Bass, Middle, Treble and ISF tone shaping.

The pedal includes independent footswitching for the two channels and a speaker-emulated output for recording or direct use.

## Controls and connectivity
- Gain 1 / Level 1
- Gain 2 / Level 2
- Channel 1 Clean/Crunch
- Bass / Middle / Treble
- ISF
- Channel 1 and Channel 2 footswitches
- Standard output
- Speaker-emulated output
- ECC83 / 12AX7 valve
- 22V DC / 1.1A supplied adapter
