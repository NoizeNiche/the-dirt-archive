# Blackstar Amplification - Dept. 10 Dual Drive

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Blackstar Amplification
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive

## Research
The Dept. 10 Dual Drive is the overdrive counterpart to Blackstar's Dept. 10 Dual Distortion. It uses a high-voltage ECC83 triode and two independently switchable channels. Channel 1 moves between clean and crunch, while Channel 2 moves between crunch and overdrive. Each channel has its own gain and level controls, while Bass, Middle, Treble and ISF are shared.

Blackstar also integrates Cab Rig speaker simulation, USB recording, XLR DI, headphones and an effects loop, making the unit usable as a conventional valve drive, a preamp, or a recording/DI interface.

## Controls and connectivity
- Gain 1 / Level 1
- Gain 2 / Level 2
- Channel 1 Clean/Crunch
- Channel 2 Crunch/OD
- Bass / Middle / Treble
- ISF
- Cab Rig
- USB audio
- XLR output
- Headphone output
- Effects loop
- ECC83 / 12AX7 valve
