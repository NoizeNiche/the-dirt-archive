# Blackstar Amplification - HT-METAL

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Blackstar Amplification
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive

## Research
The HT-METAL is a two-channel high-voltage valve pedal designed around an ECC83 / 12AX7. Channel 1 can be used as clean, boost or overdrive, while Channel 2 moves from heavy crunch into high-gain metal distortion. Shared Bass, Middle, Treble and ISF controls provide the main tone-shaping network.

Blackstar includes separate channel footswitches, a speaker-emulated output, and the same high-voltage valve architecture used in the HT family. The ISF control moves the tone network from a tighter American response toward a more British, mid-rich response.

## Controls and connectivity
- Gain 1 / Level 1
- Gain 2 / Level 2
- Bass / Middle / Treble
- ISF
- Channel 1 Clean/OD switching
- Channel 1 / Channel 2 footswitches
- Standard output
- Speaker-emulated output
- ECC83 / 12AX7 valve
- 22V DC / 1.1A supplied adapter
