# Blackstar Amplification - HT-DISTX

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Blackstar Amplification
- **Catalog type:** Distortion

## Research
The HT-DISTX is the expanded version of Blackstar's high-voltage valve distortion platform. It uses an ECC83 / 12AX7 valve and adds more detailed tone shaping around the Gain, Bass, Middle and Treble controls. The ISF control continuously changes the character of that EQ network between American and British responses.

Compared with a basic three-knob drive, the HT-DISTX is designed for aggressive high-gain tones and includes a speaker-emulated output for direct recording. Blackstar describes the high-voltage valve stage as providing dynamic, natural distortion.
