# Blackstar Amplification - Dept. 10 Dual Distortion

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Blackstar Amplification
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive

## Research
The Dept. 10 Dual Distortion is a two-channel high-voltage valve distortion/preamp pedal built around an ECC83 triode operating at more than 200V internally. Blackstar positions it as both a drive/distortion pedal and a recording preamp. The two channels provide three crunch/overdrive voices alongside a clean channel, with shared Bass, Middle, Treble and ISF controls.

The rear/recording system adds Cab Rig speaker simulation, XLR DI, low-latency USB audio, headphones and an effects loop. The pedal is powered from a 9V external supply while generating the higher internal valve voltage.

## Controls and connectivity
- Channel 1 Gain / Level
- Channel 2 Gain / Level
- Shared Bass / Middle / Treble
- ISF (Infinite Shape Feature)
- Channel 1 Clean/Crunch switching
- Channel 2 Crunch/OD switching
- Cab Rig speaker simulation
- USB audio
- XLR output
- Headphone output
- Effects loop
- ECC83 / 12AX7 valve
