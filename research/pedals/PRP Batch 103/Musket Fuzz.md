# BlackOutEffectors - Musket Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** BlackOutEffectors
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz

## Research
The Musket Fuzz is BlackOutEffectors' highly tweakable Muff-type fuzz. Effects Database describes it as a four-stage circuit expanded with three controls that address common Big Muff limitations: PRE, FOCUS and MIDS. PRE adds input/preamp push, FOCUS shapes the bass entering the fuzz, and MIDS alters the midrange response. The design is intended to cover multiple eras and variants of four-stage Muff-style fuzz rather than simply reproduce one original unit.

## Controls and architecture
- Volume
- Tone
- Fuzz
- PRE
- FOCUS
- MIDS
- Four-stage Muff-type fuzz foundation
- Versions include the original Musket, v2 and v3 revisions

The BlackOutEffectors site calls the Musket a core Blackout design and identifies the v2 as a highly tweakable, fuzz-heavy iteration.
