# BlackOutEffectors - Special Twosome Deluxe

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** BlackOutEffectors
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz

## Research
The Special Twosome Deluxe is the later limited-run Twosome design that combines Fix'd Fuzz Deluxe with either the Musket Fuzz or Blunderbuss version. The Deluxe generation replaces the earlier order-selector toggle with a third footswitch, allowing the stacking hierarchy to be changed from the floor. A color-changing LED indicates which circuit is first in the stack. Effects Database documents both Musket and Blunderbuss versions.

## Features
- Fix'd Fuzz Deluxe section
- Musket or Blunderbuss section, depending on run
- Three-switch stacking control
- Footswitchable hierarchy/order
- Color-changing LED for stack order
- Limited run
- 9V operation
