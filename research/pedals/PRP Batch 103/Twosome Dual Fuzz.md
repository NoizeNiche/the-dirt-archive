# BlackOutEffectors - Twosome Dual Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** BlackOutEffectors
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz / Overdrive

## Research
The Twosome Dual Fuzz combines the Fix'd Fuzz and Musket Fuzz in one enclosure. Effects Database describes the original Twosome as two independent circuits in an A/B configuration, while the later production version externalizes the Fix'd fuzz controls and keeps the Musket's dedicated PRE, FOCUS and MIDS controls. The Fix'd side can move from clean boost through multiple fuzz stages, and the Musket supplies a dense Muff-family fuzz voice.

## Features and architecture
- Fix'd Fuzz side
- Musket Fuzz side
- A/B operation of the two circuits
- External Fuzz 1 and Fuzz 2 controls on later versions
- Four independently bypassable Fix'd stages
- 9V DC operation
- No battery operation on documented production version
