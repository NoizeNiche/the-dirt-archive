## What this pedal is
The Geiger Counter Pro is a digital destruction/bit-crushing pedal that combines a digital sample-rate and bit-depth reduction engine with wavetable processing, filtering, modulation, and performance-oriented control. WMD describes the Pro as the final evolution of the Geiger Counter concept, adding presets, MIDI, expanded filtering, compression/noise-gate/tremolo functions, and hands-on control over parameters that were previously hidden.

## Versions and factory options
- **Original Geiger Counter Pro:** first production era, followed by a pause in production.
- **2024 re-release:** WMD's manual documents a later return of the Pro after redesign/rework of the project.
- Major controls/features include analog preamp, Level/Master, sample-rate reduction, bit-depth/Gate, wavetable Bank/Table controls, Dry/Wet mix, filter, randomization, presets, MIDI, and assignable CV/expression inputs.
- WMD's own tutorial identifies the internal signal-flow sections and the additional Dry/Wet and low-pass filtering controls on the Pro.

## Version changes
- WMD's current manual explicitly describes the 2024 return as a re-release after the original production was shelved and the enclosure/board design was revised.
- The archive keeps the re-release history attached to the Pro record rather than treating it as an unrelated pedal.

## Sound
The Geiger Counter Pro can move from harsh sample-rate reduction and gated bit-crushed textures to complex wavetable-driven digital distortion and hybrid noise sounds. The Dry/Wet mix and filter controls let the player retain more of the dry instrument while shaping the destructively processed path.
