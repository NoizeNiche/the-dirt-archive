# Celebrity Pedals — Djent Reznor

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Djent Reznor
- **Builder:** Celebrity Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Celebrity Pedals Djent Reznor, a handmade analog overdrive combining a BB Preamp-style foundation with Tube Screamer-style clipping options.

## What this pedal is
The exact-model Reverb listing describes the Djent Reznor as a combination of a BB Preamp and a Tube Screamer, with a switch selecting symmetrical or asymmetrical clipping. The same listing identifies the pedal as a UK-made Celebrity Pedals overdrive and boost model. [1]

Guitar.com independently repeats the BB Preamp + Tube Screamer description and the symmetrical/asymmetrical clipping switch. [2]

## Versions and factory options
### Djent Reznor
The photographed exact-model example shows four main controls: **Level, Drive, Bass, and Treble**. The documented clipping switch provides a choice between asymmetrical clipping associated by the listing with an SD-1 style response and symmetrical clipping associated with a TS-9 style response. [1]

No formal numbered factory V1/V2 sequence was established.

## Sound
The documented circuit concept combines a BB Preamp-style overdrive character with Tube Screamer-style clipping choices. The clipping switch changes the harmonic/clipping behavior between asymmetrical and symmetrical modes, giving two distinct response families within the same pedal. [1][2]
