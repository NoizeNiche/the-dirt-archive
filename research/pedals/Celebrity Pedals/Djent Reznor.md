# Celebrity Pedals — Djent Reznor

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Djent Reznor
- **Builder:** Celebrity Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Handmade analog overdrive combining a BB Preamp-style foundation with Tube Screamer/TS-9-style clipping choices. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
The exact-model Reverb listing describes Djent Reznor as a **BB Preamp + Tube Screamer** combination with a selectable symmetrical/asymmetrical clipping switch. Guitar.com repeats that description in its 2026 coverage. [1][2]

## Controls
- **LEVEL**
- **DRIVE**
- **BASS**
- **TREBLE**
- **CLIPPING:** Symmetrical/asymmetrical selection. [1]

The documented asymmetrical position is associated by the source with an SD-1-style response, while the symmetrical position is associated with TS-9-style clipping. [1]
