## What this pedal is

Celebrity Pedals describes Fuzzbulla as a handmade analog fuzz for guitar and bass. The builder cites **Big Muff** and **Way Huge Pork & Pickle** inspiration while emphasizing clarity, punch and useful low-end retention. [1]
