## What this pedal is
Celebrity Pedals describes the Fuzzbulla as a handmade analogue fuzz designed for guitar and bass. The builder cites Big Muff and Way Huge Pork & Pickle inspiration, with changes intended to retain clarity, punch, and low-end definition. [1]

## Sound
The builder positions the Fuzzbulla as a thick, harmonically rich fuzz with a blend control that can retain clean signal and low-end definition. The documented inspirations place it in the Big Muff/Pork & Pickle family of saturated fuzz sounds, with modern emphasis on clarity and bass retention. [1]
