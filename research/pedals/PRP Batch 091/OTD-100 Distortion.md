# Biyang - OTD-100 Distortion

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Biyang
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Series:** Tonefancier

## Research
The Biyang OTD-100 is an all-tube distortion pedal built around a selected 6AX7/12AX7 vacuum tube. Contemporary documentation describes three versions: Value with a Chinese 6AX7, Standard with a Chinese 12AX7, and Professional with a Russian 12AX7EH. The pedal uses Gain, High, and Bass controls and is designed to cover a range from warm overdrive through sustained tube distortion to heavier metal-oriented distortion. Biyang's product documentation also describes an AC adapter and transformer supply for the tube, relay-based true bypass, and a Biyang Interplay jack. The model was documented in 2006 and is associated with Biyang's Tonefancier series.
