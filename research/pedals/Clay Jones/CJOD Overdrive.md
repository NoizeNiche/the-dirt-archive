# Clay Jones — CJOD Overdrive

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** CJOD Overdrive
- **Builder:** Clay Jones
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Clay Jones Overdrive, a Landgraff Dynamic Overdrive / Tube Screamer-derived circuit associated with the OPA2134 and red LED clipping arrangement.

## What this pedal is
Historical circuit analysis identifies the original Clay Jones Overdrive as a close implementation of the Landgraff Dynamic Overdrive, itself a modified Tube Screamer 808-style circuit. A published teardown/schematic discussion identifies an **OPA2134 Burr-Brown op-amp** and **5mm red LED clippers**. [1]

These component details are documented as historical circuit-analysis findings, not a manufacturer-published current parts list.

## Colorways
The checked historical sources do not establish a complete factory colorway sequence.

## Versions and factory options
The documented CJOD concept retains a compact Tube Screamer-style control architecture with switching/clipping options associated with the Landgraff design. [1]

No reliable factory numbered version sequence was established for the original Clay Jones build.

## Version changes
Historical builders and DIY reproductions have implemented the CJOD with alternate clipping options, but those reproductions are not treated as factory Clay Jones revisions.

## Transistor
The cited circuit analysis identifies the **OPA2134** dual op-amp as the principal active gain IC. [1]

## Diode
The cited circuit analysis identifies **5mm red LEDs** in the clipping section. [1]

## Sound
The CJOD belongs to the dynamic, transparent-to-medium-gain Tube Screamer/Landgraff overdrive family, with clipping changes used to move between a more open response and more compressed drive. The exact original commercial control arrangement should remain version-specific where evidence is incomplete.
