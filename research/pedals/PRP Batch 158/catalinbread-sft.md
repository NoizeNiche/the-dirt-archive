# Catalinbread Effects SFT

- **Builder:** Catalinbread Effects
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Fuzz / Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** SFT
- **What this pedal is:** A Catalinbread foundation overdrive inspired by Ampeg amplifier designs, intended to cover both the Stones-era pushed-amp sound and the heavier desert/stoner side of the same basic platform.
- **Colorways:** Standard SFT artwork/finish; documented Blackout Series and Fireworks Collection cosmetic editions.
- **Versions:** Earlier SFT versions; current SFT; Blackout and Fireworks cosmetic editions.
- **Version changes:** Catalinbread currently describes the latest SFT as having the same insides with new artwork. The Blackout and Fireworks editions are cosmetic collections of the SFT rather than separate core circuit identities.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented.
- **Diode type:** Not documented.
- **Controls / switching:** Treble, Stoner, Bass, Volume, Gain.
- **Power:** 9-18V DC, center-negative; 4mA.
- **Sound:** The SFT spans cleaner, bright edge-of-breakup tones through thicker overdrive and higher-gain distortion, with the Stoner control pushing the circuit toward the huge low-end-heavy side of its range. The Baxandall-inspired tonal approach and broad bass control make it possible to keep the pedal tight or deliberately let the low end bloom.
- **Research confidence:** High for the Ampeg-inspired concept, current control set, power range, and cosmetic-edition relationship; moderate for undocumented component details.
