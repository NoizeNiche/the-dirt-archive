# Catalinbread Effects Sabbra Cadabra

- **Builder:** Catalinbread Effects
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** Sabbra Cadabra
- **What this pedal is:** A Catalinbread distortion built around the combination of a modified Rangemaster-style treble booster and a Laney Supergroup-style preamp concept, aimed at early Black Sabbath / Tony Iommi-inspired tones.
- **Colorways:** Standard black/red Sabbra Cadabra finish; 10th Anniversary red edition documented as a limited cosmetic/anniversary edition.
- **Versions:** Original Sabbra Cadabra; 10th Anniversary Edition.
- **Version changes:** The 10th Anniversary edition retains the core four-control layout while adding anniversary-specific finish and graphics.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented in the reviewed manufacturer material.
- **Diode type:** Not documented.
- **Controls / switching:** Presence, Vol4, Gain, Range.
- **Power:** 9-18V DC, center-negative; 5mA; 9V battery supported.
- **Sound:** Range adjusts the input-frequency emphasis of the permanently engaged treble-booster section, while Gain drives the British-style preamp from crunch toward saturated distortion. Presence then shapes the post-gain response, allowing the pedal to tighten or fatten the character for different rigs and tuning choices.
- **Research confidence:** High for the control set, power range, circuit concept, and anniversary distinction; moderate for undocumented component details.
