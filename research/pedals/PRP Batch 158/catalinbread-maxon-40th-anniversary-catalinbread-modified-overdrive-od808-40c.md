# Catalinbread Effects Maxon 40th Anniversary Catalinbread Modified Overdrive (OD808-40C)

- **Builder:** Catalinbread Effects
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** Maxon 40th Anniversary Catalinbread Modified Overdrive (OD808-40C)
- **What this pedal is:** A limited-edition Maxon OD808 modified by Catalinbread chief engineer Nicholas Kula using Catalinbread's “ODin808” modification. Maxon and Catalinbread describe the mod as changing a substantial number of components to push the classic 808 platform toward higher gain, higher output, and a flatter mid response.
- **Colorways:** Green Maxon 40th Anniversary enclosure.
- **Versions:** OD808-40C production units 41-400; special units 1-40 signed by Nicholas Kula.
- **Version changes:** All units are individually numbered and supplied with a signed certificate of authenticity. Units 1-40 received additional component substitutions, different Drive and Tone potentiometer values, an OPA2134 op amp, and Catalinbread knobs.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented in the reviewed manufacturer material.
- **Diode type:** Not documented in the reviewed manufacturer material.
- **Controls / switching:** Drive, Balance, Tone; bypass footswitch.
- **Power:** Listing documentation specifies a 9V battery or Maxon AC adaptor; DC requirement is listed as 9-10V.
- **Sound:** The ODin808 modification keeps the familiar 808 response while reducing the amount of clean signal blended into the drive and flattening the mid hump. The result is described by Maxon/Catalinbread as a thicker, higher-output drive that reaches further toward distortion while retaining the recognizable 808 character.
- **Research confidence:** High for the collaboration, ODin808 modification, 400-unit run, signed 1-40 distinction, controls, and broad tonal description; moderate for power details because the most accessible detailed electrical specifications came from retailer documentation.
