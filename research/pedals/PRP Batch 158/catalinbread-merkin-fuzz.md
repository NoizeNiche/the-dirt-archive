# Catalinbread Effects Merkin Fuzz

- **Builder:** Catalinbread Effects
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Catalog identity:** Merkin Fuzz
- **What this pedal is:** A Catalinbread fuzz that draws on the gain-stage mixing idea associated with the Mosrite Fuzzrite while also taking tonal inspiration from the Jordan Bosstone. Catalinbread's current product is a reissue of an earlier Merkin design.
- **Colorways:** Current standard Merkin Fuzz finish.
- **Versions:** Original Merkin Fuzz; current Merkin Fuzz (Reissue).
- **Version changes:** The current manufacturer listing identifies the product as a reissue but does not document a specific internal revision history in the reviewed material.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented in the reviewed manufacturer material.
- **Diode type:** Not documented.
- **Controls / switching:** Volume and Texture.
- **Power:** 9-18V DC, center-negative.
- **Sound:** Merkin uses three gain stages, with Texture acting as a mixer over the first stages, creating a broad range of fuzz textures. With Texture around the middle, Catalinbread notes a mild phase-cancellation effect that adds another vintage-leaning texture.
