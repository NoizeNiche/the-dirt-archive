# Catalinbread Effects RAH

- **Builder:** Catalinbread Effects
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** RAH
- **What this pedal is:** A Catalinbread amp-style dirt pedal designed around the sound and dynamic response associated with Jimmy Page's modified Hiwatt setup at the 1970 Royal Albert Hall performance.
- **Colorways:** Current standard RAH finish.
- **Versions:** Original RAH; current cataloged RAH.
- **Version changes:** No distinct manufacturer-documented hardware revision was found in the reviewed current product page and user-guide material.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented in the reviewed manufacturer material.
- **Diode type:** Not documented.
- **Controls / switching:** Treble, Middle, Bass, Gain, Master/Volume.
- **Power:** 9V DC, 5mA; 9V battery supported.
- **Sound:** The three-band tone circuit is based on the Hiwatt-style EQ described in the user guide, while Gain and Master shape the amp-like response. The pedal is intended to move from cleaner, touch-responsive settings into singing British crunch as the gain is increased, with useful cleanup from the guitar volume.
- **Research confidence:** High for the concept, five-knob layout, power, and tone-control behavior; moderate for internal component details.
