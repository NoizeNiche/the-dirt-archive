# Catalinbread Effects Perseus DIO

- **Builder:** Catalinbread Effects
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Catalog identity:** Perseus DIO
- **What this pedal is:** A three-voice all-analog octave-down fuzz device that expands on Catalinbread's original Perseus concept with separate one-octave-down, two-octave-down, and dry boosted-distortion paths.
- **Colorways:** Current standard finish.
- **Versions:** Original Perseus; Perseus DIO successor.
- **Version changes:** Perseus DIO expands the concept with three mix voices and separate low-pass filtering for the octave-down paths.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented.
- **Diode type:** Not documented.
- **Controls / switching:** Down 1, Filter 1, Down 2, Filter 2, Dry.
- **Power:** 9V DC.
- **Sound:** With the octave filters open, the octave-down voices retain harmonically rich square-wave character with an old-school digital or video-game edge. Filtering the voices progressively smooths the harmonics into cleaner octave-down tones, while the Dry control supplies a boosted distortion path that can stand on its own.
