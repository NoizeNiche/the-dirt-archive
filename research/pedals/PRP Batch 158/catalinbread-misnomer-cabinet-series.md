# Catalinbread Effects Misnomer (Cabinet Series)

- **Builder:** Catalinbread Effects
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** Misnomer (Cabinet Series)
- **What this pedal is:** Catalinbread's Cabinet Series take on the early Systech Overdrive concept, retaining its hard clipping, high-gain op-amp character, and resonant band-pass “EQ” while adding a clean blend and flexible filter routing.
- **Colorways:** Current yellow Cabinet Series finish.
- **Versions:** 2026 Cabinet Series release.
- **Version changes:** This version adds a clean blend and a Wet/Mix routing switch around the Systech-derived filter while preserving the underlying character of the source circuit.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented.
- **Diode type:** Not documented.
- **Controls / switching:** Frequency, Volume, Blend, Gain, Wet/Mix toggle.
- **Power:** 9V DC, center-negative.
- **Sound:** The high-Q band-pass filter can sweep from roughly 122Hz to 900Hz, producing a narrow, vocal or fixed-wah-like response that can become synth-like at extreme settings. Blend lets the player restore the dry signal and can also be used to route upstream effects through the filter when the Wet/Mix switch is set appropriately.
- **Research confidence:** High for the current Cabinet Series identity, source circuit, controls, routing, frequency range, and power.
