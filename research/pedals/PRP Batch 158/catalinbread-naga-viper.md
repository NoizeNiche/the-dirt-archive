# Catalinbread Effects Naga Viper

- **Builder:** Catalinbread Effects
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** Naga Viper
- **What this pedal is:** Catalinbread's expanded take on the Dallas Rangemaster treble booster, adding control over both frequency range and gain so the booster can be shaped for different amplifiers and guitars.
- **Colorways:** Standard Naga Viper finish; limited gold MKI edition documented separately as a cosmetic/limited edition of the same original platform.
- **Versions:** Original Naga Viper / MKI; limited gold MKI edition; later Naga Viper MKII is a separate catalog identity.
- **Version changes:** The later MKII replaced the transistor and reworked the circuit, adding an Attenuate control, so those changes are not assigned to this original Naga Viper record.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented for the original/MKI in the reviewed manufacturer material.
- **Diode type:** Not documented.
- **Controls / switching:** Range, Boost, Heat; bypass footswitch.
- **Power:** 9-18V DC or 9V battery.
- **Sound:** Naga Viper extends the classic Rangemaster-style treble boost from a fixed high-frequency response toward fuller-range boosting with Range, while Heat controls how clean or saturated the boost becomes. It can stay close to the gritty traditional treble-booster role or act as a more general-purpose boost into cleaner amplifiers.
- **Research confidence:** High for the original control concept and power range; high for the distinction from MKII; moderate for undocumented internal component details.
