# Comodoro Pedales - Rat Bass

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Comodoro Pedales
- **Catalog identity:** Rat Bass
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** Bass-focused Spanish ProCo Rat derivative with dry/wet blend and selectable clipping configurations.

## What this pedal is
Rat Bass is based on the ProCo Rat and modified to increase gain in the low-frequency range for electric bass. It can also be used with guitar. The original signal is buffered by a JFET op-amp before being blended with the distorted path, preserving bass body and note definition. [1][2]

## Controls
- **Blend:** mixes original and distorted signal.
- **Filter:** passive low-pass control.
- **Distortion:** amount of distortion.
- **Volume:** output level of the distorted signal.
- **3-position clipping switch:** **LEDs**, **4148 silicon diodes**, or **No Clipping**. [1]

## Colorways
- Reverb examples document white-production finishes.
- Other cosmetic states may exist, but are not treated as circuit revisions without supporting evidence. [2]

## Versions and factory options
- **2021 version:** documented with top-mounted jacks. [1]
- 9V DC negative-center supply.
- Hand assembled individually by Comodoro in Spain. [1][2]

## Version changes
The 2021 redesign moved the input/output jacks to the top of the enclosure while retaining the core Rat Bass concept. [1]

## Construction
Comodoro documents the model as hand assembled with a JFET op-amp buffer on the clean path to facilitate the blend. [1][2]

## Transistor
- **JFET buffer:** documented at the signal input, but the exact device part number is not stated in the checked sources. [2]

## Diode
- **Clipping options:** LED clipping devices and **1N4148/4148 silicon diodes**, plus a no-clipping position. [1]

## Sound
Rat Bass is voiced for more low-frequency gain than a standard Rat, with the Blend control allowing a dry signal to retain low-end body. The clipping selector trades distortion amount and output level for different responses. [1]
