# Comodoro Pedales - Botfly Fuzz

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Comodoro Pedales
- **Catalog identity:** Botfly Fuzz
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz / Boost
- **Identity:** Hand-assembled Spanish fuzz with a dedicated pre/post booster and two fuzz modes.

## What this pedal is
Botfly Fuzz combines two functional blocks in one enclosure: a fuzz section and a booster that can be placed before or after the fuzz. The current manufacturer/retailer documentation describes it as a bass-friendly fuzz that can move from vintage-leaning tones to aggressive, lead-oriented fuzz while retaining a useful booster stage. [1][2]

## Controls and switching
- **Volume:** output level.
- **Tone:** fuzz tone control.
- **Fuzz Level:** amount of fuzz.
- **Booster Gain:** booster level/gain.
- **Booster Pre/Pos:** places the booster before or after the fuzz.
- **Fuzz Switch:** selects **Huge Fuzz** or **Unspeakable Botfly Fuzz** mode.
- **Sublime Booster:** booster on/off. [1]
