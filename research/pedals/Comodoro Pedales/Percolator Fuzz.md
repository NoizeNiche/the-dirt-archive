# Comodoro Pedales - Percolator Fuzz

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Comodoro Pedales
- **Catalog identity:** Percolator Fuzz
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz / Overdrive
- **Identity:** Spanish Harmonic Percolator interpretation using the original-style 2N404A germanium transistor reference with modified voicing.

## What this pedal is
Comodoro describes Percolator Fuzz as its version of the Interfax Harmonic Percolator from the 1970s. The design retains the simple two-control concept while modifying the circuit to make the response more versatile, spanning vintage overdrive through dirty but defined distortion with strong harmonic content. [1]

## Controls
- **Harmonics:** controls the input signal, affecting gain and distortion.
- **Volume:** output level. [1]

- 9V DC negative-center power.

## Transistor
