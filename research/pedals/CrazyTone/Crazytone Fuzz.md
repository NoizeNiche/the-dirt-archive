## What this pedal is
Crazytone Fuzz is a vintage Japanese fuzz associated in surviving historical references with the Fuzz Face/Crazy Face family. Analog Man's vintage inventory describes a 1970s Crazytone fuzz as a Japanese Fuzz Face copy, while technical discussion at Freestompboxes treats Crazy Tone/Crazy Face as a related Japanese fuzz design with differences from a literal Fuzz Face clone.
