## What this pedal is
Crazytone Fuzz is a vintage Japanese fuzz associated with the Fuzz Face/Crazy Face family. Historical references describe examples as Japanese Fuzz Face-related designs, while technical discussion indicates that the circuit should not automatically be treated as a component-for-component Fuzz Face clone.
