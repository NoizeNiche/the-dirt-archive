# Cajita Stompboxes - FBASSDRIVE

- **Builder:** Cajita Stompboxes
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Product page:** https://pedalescajita.mitiendanube.com/productos/fbassdrive-overdrive-de-bajo/
- **Identity:** FBASSDRIVE, Cajita's bass overdrive/distortion pedal.
- **Description:** Cajita describes it as a bass distortion/overdrive with substantial low-frequency content, three saturation modes, and a controllable booster. The builder identifies the design as based on the Fulltone Bassdrive.
- **Controls:** TONE, VOL, GAIN, BOOST.
- **Mode switch:** VIN adds more distortion and mids; FM lowers mids at similar distortion; CC removes the distortion section. The builder describes the BOOST control as adding additional controllable distortion.
- **Switching / I/O:** True bypass; mono input and mono output.
- **Power:** 9V center-negative adapter or battery.
- **Construction:** Aluminum enclosure, high-quality jacks, durable vinyl graphics.
- **Circuit note:** The Fulltone Bassdrive relationship is a builder description. No more specific circuit topology is asserted here.
