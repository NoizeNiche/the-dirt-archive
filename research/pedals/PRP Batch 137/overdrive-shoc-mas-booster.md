# Cajita Stompboxes - Overdrive Shoc más booster

- **Builder:** Cajita Stompboxes
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Product page:** https://pedalescajita.mitiendanube.com/productos/overdrive-shoc-mas-booster/
- **Identity:** SH.OC, a combined overdrive/distortion and booster pedal.
- **Description:** Cajita describes two pedal sections in one enclosure that can be used together or separately. The builder calls the sections SH (Super HO) and OC (OB.D), and says their combination provides three distinct sounds.
- **Controls:** VOL/CRCK, GAIN, TONE, plus an HP/LP switch.
- **Tone behavior:** VOL/CRCK controls volume and gain; TONE controls the pedal's tone; the HP/LP switch changes low-pass/high-pass behavior and therefore how much low end is cut or passed.
- **Switching / I/O:** True bypass; mono input and mono output.
- **Power:** 9V center-negative adapter or battery.
- **Construction:** Aluminum enclosure, high-quality jacks, durable vinyl graphics.
- **Circuit note:** The Super HO and OB.D section names are reported as Cajita's own descriptions. No further circuit equivalence is asserted.
