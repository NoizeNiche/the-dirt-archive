# Cameltone Electronics The Nard

- **Builder:** Cameltone Electronics
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** The Nard
- **Colorways:** Black-finish examples are documented in archived listings; the reviewed sources do not establish a formal factory colorway catalog.
- **Versions:** Archived examples include older Nard units and a 2022 reappearance of the model after an extended absence. The 2022 source treats it as the returning Nard rather than a differently named design.
- **Version changes:** The reviewed 2022 description says the design had undergone six years of road testing, but it does not document a numbered circuit revision. No specific factory V1/V2 hardware change was confirmed.
- **Transistor type:** FET-based.
- **Diode type:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.
- **Controls / switching:** Full control labeling was not confirmed in the reviewed sources.
- **Sound:** The Nard is a FET-based distortion designed to push an amplifier into high-gain territory while retaining the natural character of the guitar and amp. Cameltone's description associates it with the sound of an early-1990s blue-colored album and emphasizes an organic, not overly compressed texture with a gentle presence lift.
- **Research confidence:** High for FET topology, intended voicing, and the 2022 return; medium for historical/version details beyond those documented by the archived listings.
