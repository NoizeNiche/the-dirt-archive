# Cameltone Electronics Freak Scene

- **Builder:** Cameltone Electronics
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Catalog identity:** Freak Scene
- **Colorways:** Purple finish is documented in archived Reverb examples.
- **Versions:** No numbered production version was confirmed in the reviewed sources.
- **Version changes:** The defining PAIN switching function is part of the Freak Scene identity. No separate factory circuit revision was established.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.
- **Diode type:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.
- **Controls / switching:** PAIN switch is documented as switching between the splattering character and the period-correct Ram's Head character. The complete control labeling was not confirmed from the reviewed text sources.
- **Sound:** Freak Scene began as Cameltone's attempt to build a very accurate recreation of J. Mascis's legendary "#2" Muff. An accidental component-loss event produced a dramatic splattering texture, which became the PAIN mode alongside the more period-correct Ram's Head-style sound.
- **Research confidence:** High for the model identity, purple finish, PAIN function, and Ram's Head/J. Mascis design story; medium for component-level construction because the reviewed sources do not provide a pedal-specific schematic or verified parts list.
