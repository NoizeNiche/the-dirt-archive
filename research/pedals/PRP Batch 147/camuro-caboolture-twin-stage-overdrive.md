# Camuro Caboolture Twin-Stage Overdrive

- **Builder:** Camuro
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** Caboolture Twin-Stage Overdrive
- **Colorways:** A blue finish is documented in Camuro photography, including a special-color example.
- **Versions:** The defining factory options are the two-stage BOOST function plus selectable clipping modes.
- **Version changes:** No numbered factory revision was confirmed in the reviewed sources.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.
- **Diode type:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.
- **Controls / switching:** Volume, Gain, Tone, and BOOST. A side slide switch selects VINTAGE, COMP CUT, or FLAT MIDS clipping behavior. A top toggle selects STANDARD or MOSFET clipping. Camuro specifies a JRC4558DV op amp.
- **Sound:** Caboolture is a TS-based overdrive developed around a compressed, heavy, edged guitar sound associated by the builder with Keith Urban. The one-stage/two-stage setup, three clipping modes, and STANDARD/MOSFET selection let it move from fuller TS-like overdrive toward deeper or more open textures, with MOSFET mode used when more low end is desired.
- **Research confidence:** High for topology description, controls, switching, and JRC4558DV use because these are specified by Camuro.
