# Camuro Baci Overdrive/Distortion

- **Builder:** Camuro
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** Baci Overdrive/Distortion
- **Colorways:** A blue finish is documented in Camuro's official product photography. No formal alternate-color lineup was confirmed.
- **Versions:** Compact-enclosure and later large-enclosure builds are documented.
- **Version changes:** Camuro states that the compact and large versions retain the same basic configuration and circuit, while the large enclosure allowed greater freedom in component selection because of the additional space.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.
- **Diode type:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.
- **Controls / switching:** The overdrive side has Volume, Gain, Tone, and High. The distortion side has Volume, Gain, and Tone. A toggle changes the distortion-side mode. The two sections can operate independently or together, with the signal path arranged overdrive -> distortion.
- **Sound:** Baci combines two deliberately different dirt characters in one enclosure. Camuro describes the overdrive as a rich vintage-combo-style sound and the distortion as an 1980s-flavored character, with the two sections usable separately or combined for additional gain and texture.
- **Research confidence:** High for architecture, controls, switching, and documented enclosure evolution; component-level details remain undocumented in the reviewed source.
