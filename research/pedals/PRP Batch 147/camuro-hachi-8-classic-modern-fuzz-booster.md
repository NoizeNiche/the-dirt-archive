# Camuro hachi “8” Classic Modern Fuzz/Booster

- **Builder:** Camuro
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Catalog identity:** hachi “8” Classic Modern Fuzz/Booster
- **Colorways:** No formal factory colorway list was confirmed in the reviewed source.
- **Versions:** Tokyo Effector and Camuro developed hachi “8” as a collaboration over more than a year.
- **Version changes:** The reviewed product description does not establish numbered hardware revisions.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.
- **Diode type:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.
- **Controls / switching:** The pedal combines separate fuzz and booster functions. A mode switch selects fuzz or booster, and a tone switch selects a fat or trebly character. The booster uses part of the fuzz circuit, so both functions cannot be operated simultaneously.
- **Sound:** hachi “8” was developed around Japanese vintage fuzz traditions, with Camuro explicitly citing research into ACE TONE/Fuzz Master FM-1 through Roland AF-100 Bee Baa. The booster is intentionally voiced in the style of pre-clean-booster-era effects, while the fuzz is presented as a distinctly Japanese vintage-inspired sound with modern pedalboard practicality.
- **Research confidence:** High for the collaboration, functional layout, and stated Japanese-fuzz design goal; component-level details were not documented.
