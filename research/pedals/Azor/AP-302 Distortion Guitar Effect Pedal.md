## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** AP-302 Distortion Guitar Effect Pedal

- **Identity:** Compact Azor distortion with three selectable voicings and a simple Level/Gain/Tone control layout.

## What this pedal is
Azor documents the AP-302 with Level, Gain, and Tone controls plus Natural, Tight, and Classic modes. The builder specifies true bypass, 9V DC center-negative power, a metal enclosure, approximately 3 mA current draw, 470K input impedance, and 1K output impedance.

## Sound
The three modes are designed to move the AP-302 between more natural, tighter, and classic distortion voicings while the Gain and Tone controls set amount and spectral balance. Azor's own description compares the pedal's tonal territory to familiar compact hard-clipping distortion designs, but the archive does not treat that as proof of a specific clone topology.
