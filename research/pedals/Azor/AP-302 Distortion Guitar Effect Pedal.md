# Azor — AP-302 Distortion Guitar Effect Pedal

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** AP-302 Distortion Guitar Effect Pedal
- **Builder:** Azor
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** Azor AP-302, a compact three-mode distortion with LEVEL, TONE and GAIN controls. Effects Database independently catalogs the black AP302 as part of the Azor Mini series. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
Azor describes AP-302 as an all-around high-gain distortion intended to make a small clean tube amplifier sound fuller while preserving the original guitar/amp structure. The pedal offers three voicings: **Natural, Tight and Classic**. [1][2]

## Controls / modes
- **LEVEL:** Effect output volume.
- **TONE:** Tonal balance.
- **GAIN:** Distortion intensity.
- **Natural / Tight / Classic:** Three operating modes. [1][2]

## Sound
Azor's stated design target is full-spectrum, high-gain distortion with strong dynamic response and definition. The three modes are intended to change the feel/voicing between Natural, Tight and Classic settings. [1][2]
This is a manufacturer characterization, not a measurement.
