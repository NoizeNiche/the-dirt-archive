- **Identity:** Azor AP-507 Dog Distortion, part of the Azor Zodiac series, with TONE, DIST and VOL controls. Effects Database independently catalogs the model as AP-507. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
Azor's Dog Distortion is a compact distortion intended to cover a broad range from Texas-blues crunch through thrash and metal. Azor also positions the volume control as capable of providing enough boost to push an already-dirty tube amp. [1][2]

## Controls
- **DIST:** Distortion/gain amount.
- **TONE:** Overall EQ balance.
- **VOL:** Output level/boost.
- **LED:** Operating-status indicator.
- **True bypass:** Standard bypass path. [1]

## Sound
Azor describes the pedal as covering sizzling blues, hair metal, heavy metal and intermediate gain territory. The DIST control spans from Texas-blues crunch to full thrash/metal, while the tone section is voiced broadly from dark to bright and the VOL control can provide a useful boost. [1][2]
