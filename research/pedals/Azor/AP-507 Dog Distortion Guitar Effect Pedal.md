## What this pedal is
Azor documents the AP-507 as a compact distortion with Tone, Dist, and Volume controls, true bypass switching, 9V DC power, and a metal/aluminum-alloy enclosure. The builder documents a compact 100 x 50 x 25 mm format and presents the pedal as part of its small-form-factor distortion series.
