## What this pedal is

AP-308 is a compact low-gain overdrive with four controls: DRIVE, HIGH, LOW and LEVEL. Azor describes it as transparent and singing, suitable both for adding gain to a crunchy amplifier and for light overdrive on a clean amplifier. [1][2]

## Controls

- **DRIVE:** Gain/drive amount.
- **HIGH:** High-frequency EQ.
- **LOW:** Low-frequency EQ.
- **LEVEL:** Output level.
- **LED:** Status indicator.
- **True bypass:** Yes. [1][2]

## Sound

Azor describes AP-308 as transparent and singing, intended to add gain to a crunchy amp or provide light overdrive through a clean amp. The HIGH/LOW controls allow independent broad EQ shaping around DRIVE and LEVEL. [1][2]
