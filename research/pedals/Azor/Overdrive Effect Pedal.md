## What this pedal is
Azor's AP-308 is a compact low-gain overdrive with aluminum-alloy construction and true bypass. The factory page lists DRIVE, HIGH, LOW, and LEVEL and describes it as transparent and singing, for adding gain to a crunchy amp or light breakup into a clean amp.

## Sound
Azor describes a transparent, singing low-gain overdrive with enough range to add gain to an already-crunchy amp or provide light breakup on a clean amp. The HIGH and LOW controls give two-band EQ shaping around DRIVE and LEVEL.
