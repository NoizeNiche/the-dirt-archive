## What this pedal is
Azor's AP-303 is a compact traditional fuzz in an aluminum-alloy mini enclosure with true bypass. The factory page specifies LEVEL, FUZZ, and TONE, 9V DC center-negative power, 3 mA current draw, 470 kOhm input impedance, and 1 kOhm output impedance.
