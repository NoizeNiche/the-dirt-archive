# Azor — Fuzz Effect Pedal

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Fuzz Effect Pedal
- **Builder:** Azor
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Azor AP-303, independently cataloged by Effects Database as the grey Azor Mini fuzz. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
Azor AP-303 is a compact three-control fuzz with a traditional, rich and creamy voicing. Effects Database describes it as a classic fuzz with a “violin-like” tonal character and identifies it as part of the Azor Mini series. [1][2]

## Controls
- **LEVEL:** Output volume.
- **FUZZ:** Fuzz intensity.
- **TONE:** Fuzz tone depth/brightness. [1][2]
- **LED:** Operating indicator.
- **True bypass:** Yes. [1]

## Circuit lineage

## Sound
Azor's description emphasizes full, sweet and creamy fuzz with strong sustain and a violin-like character. The simple three-control interface focuses on fuzz amount, tonal balance and level. [1][2]
