## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** SP75 Sessionette:75 Guitar Amp Sound
- **Builder:** Award-Session
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive
- **Identity:** Sessionette:75 preamp/drive pedal intended to capture the character of Stewart Ward's Sessionette amplifier.

## What this pedal is
The SP75 is described as effectively the preamplifier section of the Sessionette 75 amplifier. It combines Award-Session's FlexiDrive distortion with PAFtone humbucker emulation, three-band EQ and switchable Heavy/Crunch behavior.

## Sound
SP75 is designed around the Sessionette amp's drive character rather than a single fixed fuzz or distortion topology. Heavy/Crunch shifts the gain character, while PAF/Normal changes the way the pedal presents a humbucker-emulation curve into the three-band EQ.
