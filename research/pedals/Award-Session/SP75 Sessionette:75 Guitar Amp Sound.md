# Award-Session — SP75 Sessionette:75 Guitar Amp Sound

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** SP75 Sessionette:75 Guitar Amp Sound
- **Builder:** Award-Session
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive
- **Identity:** Sessionette-75-derived preamp/drive pedal using Award-Session's FlexiDrive distortion, PAFtone humbucker emulation and three-band EQ. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
The SP75 is intended to capture the character of Award-Session's Sessionette amplifier in pedal form. The documented architecture adds a PAFtone humbucker-emulation function and the company's FlexiDrive touch-sensitive distortion. [1]

## Sound
Award-Session positions the SP75 as an amp-derived drive capable of moving from Crunch into heavier distortion. The Heavy/Crunch switch changes the gain character, while PAF/Normal changes the front-end response by engaging the humbucker-emulation function. [1][2]
