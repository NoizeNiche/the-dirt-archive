# Award-Session — JD20 Jerry Donahue Twin Channel Direct Recording Guitar PreAmp And Overdrive/Distortion Pedal

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** JD20 Jerry Donahue Twin Channel Direct Recording Guitar PreAmp And Overdrive/Distortion Pedal
- **Builder:** Award-Session
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive
- **Identity:** Twin-channel Jerry Donahue direct-recording preamp with FlexiDrive touch-sensitive distortion, G12T speaker simulation and enhanced Super-T EQ.

## What this pedal is
The JD20 develops the JD10 concept into a two-channel pedal/preamp. The official manual describes an enhanced Super-T EQ, FlexiDrive touch-sensitive distortion, G12T speaker simulation and a footswitchable Heavy/Crunch function with Balance control.

## Version changes
No numbered factory revision was verified. The major design change from JD10 is the move to a twin-channel format with dedicated Heavy/Crunch switching and expanded EQ/speaker-simulation functionality.

## Sound
JD20 is designed to move from touch-sensitive clean-to-overdrive response in Classic/Crunch territory into much heavier rock and metal distortion through the Heavy setting. The speaker simulator permits direct recording while keeping the output useful as a stompbox or preamp.
