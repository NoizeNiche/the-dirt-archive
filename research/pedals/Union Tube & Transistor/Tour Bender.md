## Sound
Our pursuit of great tone is its own reward.
This unit is made with silicon transistors, providing a less temperamental version of this classic fuzz circuit.
Like the Sone Bender, the Tour Bender takes aim at creating the legendary 60s tone of the Tone Bender and giving it an ability to clean up like a Fuzz Face, combining the best of both classics in a modern, reliable device.
