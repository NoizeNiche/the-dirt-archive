# Big Game Pedals - Nothing Is Dead Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Nothing Is Dead Fuzz
- **Builder:** Big Game Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Customizable Big Game Pedals fuzz with selectable diode and tone options.

## What this pedal is
Nothing Is Dead Fuzz was documented as a customizable fuzz where buyers could choose modifications. A reviewed example used Tone Bypass, a diode selector and a “Creamy Dreamer” higher-gain modification.

## Controls
- Volume
- Tone
- Sustain
- Mids
- Optional Tone Bypass switch
- Optional diode-selection switch
- Optional Creamy Dreamer gain modification

## Circuit / components
- Muff-derived design, described in contemporary community discussion as based around the old Sovtek Green Muff.
- Optional diode selection can switch among silicon, LED and no-diode configurations.
- Earlier versions were also offered with NOS KD521A diode options.

## Sound
The reviewed unit was described as loud, raw and powerful while retaining clarity at higher levels. The selectable clipping options substantially broaden the available fuzz textures.
