# Big Ear NYC - Frank

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Frank

- **Identity:** Big Ear NYC FRANK, a low-gain boost/overdrive.

## What this pedal is
FRANK uses an LED clipping-based low-gain boost/overdrive stage around a Burr Brown op-amp. Effects Database and historical Big Ear coverage describe it as a clean boost at low gain that moves into natural amplifier-like breakup as gain rises.

## Controls / internal trim
- **Gain:** Drive amount.
- Output/level control is part of the documented pedal layout.
- Internal saturation/body trim provides additional voicing control.
- Exact factory knob labeling beyond the documented Gain/boost behavior was not independently reconstructed.

## Circuit
- Burr Brown op-amp.
- LED clipping.
- Internal saturation/Body trim.
- Exact op-amp part number and other device values were not established.

## Sound
Low gain can function as a clean boost; increasing gain moves toward saturated amplifier-like breakup. The internal trim can bias the voicing toward thicker or brighter British-style attack.
