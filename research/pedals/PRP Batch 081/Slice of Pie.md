# Big Ear - Slice of Pie

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Slice of Pie
- **Builder:** Big Ear
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** BIG EAR x The Tone Mob Podcast Slice of Pie fuzz.

## What this pedal is
SLICE OF PIE was originally released in **2022** as a collaboration between BIG EAR pedals and The Tone Mob Podcast. Big Ear's current documentation explicitly calls it a fuzz pedal and identifies the cheese and pepperoni artwork versions as the same underlying circuit.

## Controls
- **Gain:** Amount of distortion/fuzz.
- **Tone:** Bright/dark EQ shaping.
- **Volume:** Output level.

## Power / construction
- **9VDC**, 2.1mm barrel, **center negative**.
- Approximately **2mA** current draw.
- Bent-steel pizza-slice enclosure, approximately **5.2 x 3.4 x 1.4 in (132 x 85 x 35 mm)**.
- Weight reported by the builder: **11.2 oz**.
- No battery snap.

## Circuit
- Fuzz circuit.
- The official product page does not disclose exact transistor or diode part numbers.
- No unsupported classic-circuit attribution is added.

## Sound
Big Ear describes the effect as a large, fuzz-forward voice; a 2022 demo and later descriptions present it as a dedicated fuzz rather than the unusual movie sound effect used in Spinal Tap II.

## Colorways
- **Pepperoni**
- **Cheese**
The underlying circuit is documented as the same between the two artwork variants.

## History
The first production run sold out after the 2022 release. Big Ear later reintroduced the product.
