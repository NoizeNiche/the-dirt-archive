# Big Ear - Slice of Pie

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Slice of Pie
- **Builder:** Big Ear
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Big Ear's pizza-shaped collaboration fuzz with The Tone Mob Podcast.

## What this pedal is
SLICE OF PIE is a silicon fuzz originally released in 2022 as a collaboration between BIG EAR pedals and The Tone Mob Podcast. Cheese and pepperoni versions use the same underlying circuit. The pedal is hand-assembled in the USA.

## Controls
- **Gain:** Amount of fuzz/distortion.
- **Tone:** Bright/dark EQ shaping.
- **Volume:** Output level.

## Power / construction
- 9VDC, 2.1mm barrel, center negative.
- Approximately 2mA current draw.
- Bent-steel pizza-shaped enclosure.

## Sound
Coverage describes the circuit as warm, relatively open and dynamic for its inspiration, with a lower-gain character than many classic fuzz references.
