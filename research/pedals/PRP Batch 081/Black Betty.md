## What this pedal is
Black Betty is a minimalist fixed-gain fuzz. Surviving product documentation describes the top control as Volume, with a side-mounted bass trim; gain is fixed at maximum.

## Controls
- **Volume:** Output.
- **Bass trim:** Side-mounted bass EQ trim.
- **Gain:** Fixed/maxed.
