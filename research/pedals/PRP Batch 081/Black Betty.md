# Big Ear NYC - Black Betty

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** Black Betty
- **Builder:** Big Ear NYC
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Historical Big Ear n.y.c. Black Betty / Betty White fuzz.

## What this pedal is

Reverb documents the **Big Ear n.y.c. Betty Fuzz** as a 2013-2018 product made in the USA. Black Betty and Betty White are the same finish family and the two colorways are identical.

## Controls / architecture

The important historical distinction is that the older **Big Ear n.y.c. version did not carry the later side-mounted bass trim**. Reverb describes the later Big Ear Pedals version as the design that added the bass-control trim pot.

Therefore this NYC record is documented as:
- **External control:** One front-panel volume control.
- **Bass trim:** Not established for the NYC production version; evidence indicates the later Big Ear design added it.
- **Gain:** Fixed in the simple fuzz design.

## Circuit / components

- Exact transistor part numbers are not disclosed.
- Reverb identifies the NYC and later Big Ear versions as the same Betty family but with the later bass-trim addition; the archive does not infer exact internal component continuity beyond that evidence.

## History

- **2013-2018:** Big Ear n.y.c. production range in Reverb's historical record.
- The NYC version was reportedly produced for artists and friends and was not a normal standard-production pedal.
- Later Big Ear Pedals production added the bass trim pot.

## Sound

The surviving record supports a simple fuzz architecture, but no exact named classic source circuit is assigned because the builder's current page does not identify it.
