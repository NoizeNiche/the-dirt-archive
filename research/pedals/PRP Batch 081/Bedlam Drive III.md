# Big Game Pedals - Bedlam Drive III

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Bedlam Drive III
- **Builder:** Big Game Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Third-generation Bedlam Drive, a dynamic low-gain overdrive capable of reaching compressed distortion/fuzz territory.

## Controls
- **Volume:** Output.
- **Mids:** Mid-frequency boost/cut.
- **Gain:** Clipping/distortion amount.
- **Bass:** 40-120Hz boost/cut.
- **Treble:** 4-10kHz boost/cut.
- LO/HI setting changes the amount of signal hitting the front of the circuit.

## Sound
The LO setting emphasizes dynamic low-gain overdrive. HI adds more front-end signal and produces more compressed, distorted output. The three-band EQ and semi-parametric mid section provide broad tonal shaping.
