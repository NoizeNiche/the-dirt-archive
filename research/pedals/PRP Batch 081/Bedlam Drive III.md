## Controls

- **Volume:** Output level.
- **Mids:** Midrange boost/cut.
- **Gain:** Drive amount.
- **Bass:** 40-120 Hz boost/cut.
- **Treble:** 4-10 kHz boost/cut.

## Circuit / architecture

The available builder discussion documents an analog gain design with a broad three-band EQ and semi-parametric midrange shaping. Exact semiconductor part numbers and schematic topology were not established.

## Sound

The LO setting is described as more dynamic and lower-gain, while HI pushes the front end harder for more compression and distortion. The EQ controls allow substantial low, mid and high-frequency shaping.
