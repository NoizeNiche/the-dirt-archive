# Big Ear - Woodcutter

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Woodcutter
- **Builder:** Big Ear
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** Big Ear's LM308-based RAT-family distortion.

## What this pedal is
WOODCUTTER is Big Ear's homage to a 308-loaded Kalamazoo-era classic. Current Big Ear documentation describes it as covering low-gain overdrive through high-gain distortion/fuzz and suitable for guitar, bass and other sources.

## Circuit
- **Core:** Motorola LM308N-based RAT-family design.
- Exact production revisions were not established beyond the documented LM308 lineage.

## Controls
- **Gain**
- **Tone**
- **Volume**

## Power
- Standard 9V supply, center negative.
- Later production versions omit the battery snap.

## Sound
The pedal is described as louder and gnarlier than the source RAT-style voice while retaining the core distortion character.
