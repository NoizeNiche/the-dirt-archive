# Big Ear - Woodcutter

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** Woodcutter
- **Builder:** Big Ear
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** Big Ear WOODCUTTER, a 308-loaded RAT-family distortion.

## What this pedal is

Big Ear's current product page calls WOODCUTTER an homage to a **308-loaded Kalamazoo classic** and describes a range from low-gain overdrive through high-gain distortion/fuzz.

## Circuit / lineage

- **Core:** LM308-loaded RAT-family architecture.
- Community comparison identifies the Woodcutter as a RAT-style pedal with LM308 lineage.
- Exact PCB/revision differences were not established.

## Controls

The surviving product records document a three-control layout:
- **Gain**
- **Tone**
- **Volume**

## Power / construction

- Later production documentation states that from **November 2018** the pedal no longer included a battery snap and required a standard **9V, 100mA, 2.1mm center-negative** supply.
- Big Ear states its pedals are hand-assembled in the USA.
- Exact current draw of the Woodcutter circuit itself was not separately verified.

## Sound

The builder positions WOODCUTTER as a broad-range RAT-family distortion capable of low-gain overdrive through high-gain distortion/fuzz. Community descriptions compare it directly with other LM308 RAT-style designs.

## History

The name is an homage to a RAT-associated Kalamazoo builder nickname, but the archive does not infer any deeper historical connection beyond the builder's stated homage.
