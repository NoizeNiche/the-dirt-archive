## What this pedal is
Effects Database identifies the **Industrialectric Squarewave Generator** as a fuzz pedal derived from the modified fuzz section of the Schumann PLL. The source describes it as a 9V unit designed for massive squarewave fuzz and notes that the circuit was engineered and designed by Kevin from KO Amps.

## Controls
- **Squarewave:** output level.
- **Waveshape:** tone/shape control ranging from darker low-pass sounds toward modulating squarewave textures.
- **Generate:** gain.
- **Regulate:** texture control implemented as a voltage-divider function, with different settings producing gated squarewave behavior.
