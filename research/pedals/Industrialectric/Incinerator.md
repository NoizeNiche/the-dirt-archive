# Industrialectric — Incinerator

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Industrialectric
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Industrialectric Incinerator, the builder's dual-channel fuzz with dual chaos oscillators.

## What this pedal is
The reviewed exact-model record identifies the **Industrialectric Incinerator** as a dual-channel fuzz built around rare silicon transistors, with separate signal paths, voltage-starvation control, and two chaos oscillators. Effects Database's exact-model page was published July 22, 2013 and identifies it as Industrialectric's first main original pedal.

## Controls
- **Input:** overall input/drive of the first fuzz channel.
- **Output 1:** level of the first fuzz channel.
- **Output 2:** level of the second fuzz channel.
- **Voltage:** starves power to the first fuzz channel.
- **Oscillation Range 1 / 2:** separate oscillator range controls.
- **Oscillation Switch 1 / 2:** engage or disengage the corresponding oscillators.
- **Mode:** two oscillation modes on fuzz channel 2.
- **NPN/PNP:** switches between two transistor variations.
- Separate input and output jacks are provided for the two channels.
- A large analog voltage meter indicates the amount of starvation applied.

## Circuit / hardware evidence
The exact-model description specifies a **pure rare silicon transistor** design and states that the Incinerator contains **26 transistors**, five of which are described as special rare silicon transistors. The archive records those counts as builder/source claims rather than inferring exact semiconductor part numbers.

The separate channel outputs, oscillator controls, and voltage-starve circuit make this substantially more than a conventional single-stage fuzz. The archive does not infer undocumented schematic topology or component values.

## Power
A current retailer description documents **9V DC, center-negative** operation. Exact current draw was not established in the reviewed sources.

## Sound
The exact-model source describes the unit as capable of full-blown transistor fuzz through smoother fuzz textures, and says the gain stages can also be used as overdrive or boost. It also describes highly textured, heavy and harsh sounds alongside smoother musical operation. The archive retains this as source-described operating range rather than as an independent subjective review.

## Versions / historical context
The original Incinerator is documented separately from the later **Incinerator Deluxe** presentation. A 2022 10th-anniversary Deluxe re-release is documented by trade and dealer sources, but this record is intentionally limited to the original **Incinerator** identity and does not merge the Deluxe into the same canonical model name.

This pass replaces the mistaken Death By Audio attribution with the verified **Industrialectric Incinerator** identity. The exact-model Effects Database record and independent retailer/trade descriptions agree on the builder and fuzz identity. The source material documents the dual-channel architecture, dual oscillators, voltage starvation, transistor-switching behavior, and the 26-transistor construction claim. The archive deliberately leaves exact transistor and diode part numbers unresolved.
