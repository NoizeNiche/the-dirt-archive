## What this pedal is
The Microtubes B7K is a bass preamp built from Darkglass's dynamic Microtubes saturation circuit, adding a four-band EQ and balanced line driver.

## Colorways
- Black anodized aluminum enclosure is documented.
- Limited artwork editions exist, including Black Beast.

## Versions and factory options
- **V2** is documented as a later version.
- Four-band equalizer.
- Balanced line output/DI.
- Analog saturation circuit based on the B3K platform.
