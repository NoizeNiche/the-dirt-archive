## Colorways
- One foot switch bypasses the effect and another foot switch selects between the green and red channels (low gain and high gain).
- Boost Foot switch: Switch with dual-color LED to select between gain modes: standard mode (Green) and volume/drive Boost (Red).
- Red drive: This controls the high gain channel and there is more available gain on this control compared to the Green Drive control.
