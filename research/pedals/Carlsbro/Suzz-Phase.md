# Carlsbro - Suzz-Phase

## PRP identity

- Builder: Carlsbro
- Catalog type: Distortion / Fuzz
- Identity: Carlsbro Suzz-Phase, a historical hybrid Suzz/Phase pedal in the 1977 Effects Series.

## What this pedal is

The Suzz-Phase is listed in Carlsbro's historical "Alu" Effects Series, introduced in 1977. It combines the Suzz dirt identity with phase modulation.

## Controls / circuit

Exact control legend and circuit topology remain unresolved in this surface record.

## History

Carlsbro's 1977 Effects Series documentation lists Suzz-Phase as its own product rather than merely a colorway or accessory.

## Sound

Effects Database documents the exact Carlsbro Suzz Phase as a historical Alu series hybrid combining the Suzz fuzz and a phaser in one enclosure. Verified controls are Gain for the fuzz section and Speed for the phase section. The two effects have independent footswitches. The unit uses analog fuzz and phase circuits, 9V battery power, and a metal enclosure. Effects Database records publication on October 10, 2005.
