## What this pedal is
The modern Triangle Big Muff Pi is EHX's recreation of the original **Version 1** Big Muff, the early circuit commonly identified by the triangular arrangement of the three controls.

## Sound
The Triangle Big Muff provides thick, sustaining fuzz with the broad low end and compressed character associated with the earliest Big Muff family. The current reissue is intended to reproduce the original Version 1 character.
