## Sound
The English MUFFn is used to create the sound of 1960s British guitar amps pre-amp and tone circuits.
The English MUFFn consists of both a gain stage and a tone control section.
The tone control section contains high (treble), midrange, and low (bass) tone controls, which form a traditional tone stack, as used in vintage pre-amp sections of 60s British guitar amps.
