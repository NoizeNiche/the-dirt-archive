## What this pedal is
The **Electro-Harmonix Hot Wax Dual Overdrive** combines two EHX overdrive concepts in one pedal:
- **Crayon**, the company's full-range overdrive.
- **Hot Tubes**, the reissue of the classic 1970s CMOS overdrive. [1]

The archive keeps **Overdrive** as the catalog type.

## Historical/circuit relationship
The important historical point is that Hot Wax is not merely a generic dual overdrive. EHX specifically presents it as a fusion of two named EHX circuits, giving the pedal a documented lineage inside the company's own product family. [1]
