## What this pedal is
The standard Big Muff Pi is EHX's classic three-control fuzz, known for thick sustain, compression, and a broad, singing distortion character. This record refers to the standard Big Muff identity, not the separate Deluxe Big Muff Pi or Op-Amp/Tone Wicker models.

## Colorways
- The standard production pedal uses the familiar **NYC Big Muff** styling.
- Special editions and artist versions are cataloged separately when they change the identity.

## Versions and factory options
- **Sustain:** controls fuzz amount/sustain.
- **Tone:** adjusts the characteristic Big Muff tone balance.
- **Volume:** output level.
- True bypass on the current production version.
- Standard 9V battery/DC operation.

## Version changes

- The archive does not pretend that one exact schematic represents all vintage units.

## Sound
The Big Muff Pi is defined by dense sustain, compressed fuzz, a strong low end, and the characteristic mid-scooped tone network. Compared with many overdrive pedals, it produces a more enveloping and heavily saturated texture.
