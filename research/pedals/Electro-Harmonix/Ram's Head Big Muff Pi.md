## What this pedal is

The Ram's Head Big Muff Pi is Electro-Harmonix's modern recreation of the classic 1970s Ram's Head Big Muff sound. The current model is based on the historical triangle-and-Ram's Head Muff family but is not treated as electrically identical to every original vintage revision.

## Colorways

- Standard production uses the classic **white and violet** Ram's Head styling.
- The J Mascis special edition uses its own documented white/violet artist finish and is cataloged separately where applicable.

## Versions and factory options

- Classic Big Muff-style four-control interface.
- **Volume**
- **Tone**
- **Sustain**
- True bypass.
- 9V battery or standard 9V DC operation.
- The current reissue uses modern production while targeting the historical Ram's Head voicing.

## Version changes

- Historical Ram's Head Big Muffs existed in multiple circuit variations.
- The current EHX reissue is treated as its own production design rather than a claim that one exact 1970s schematic was universal.

## Sound

The Ram's Head Big Muff delivers thick, sustained fuzz with a more pronounced mid scoop than many overdrive/fuzz designs. EHX's current version is intended to preserve the large sustain and dense harmonic character associated with the classic Ram's Head family.
