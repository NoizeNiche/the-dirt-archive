## Sound

It operates with two 12AX7EH vacuum tubes, integrating a moving filter circuit with a gain structure that allows both pre-filter and post-filter distortion shaping.
The envelope follower responds to input dynamics, producing filter sweeps that track playing intensity, while the tube stages introduce harmonic coloration and distortion that can be used independently or in conjunction with the filter.
The pedal provides selectable upward or downward envelope sweep directions, altering whether the filter initially moves toward higher or lower frequencies in response to playing dynamics.
