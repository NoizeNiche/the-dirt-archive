## What this pedal is

## Sound
The ’74 Pi iteration replaced the four-transistor gain stage with three cascaded transistor stages and introduced the now-famous op-amp-based tone stack—a design that delivered tighter low-end response and enhanced midrange articulation.
Even the potentiometer tapers (logarithmic for Volume and Tone, linear for Sustain) were validated using oscilloscope sweeps of original units.
