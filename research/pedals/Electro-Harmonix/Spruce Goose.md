## What this pedal is
The Spruce Goose is an elevated take on a **Bluesbreaker-style overdrive**, with a smooth, rich breakup that retains clarity and dynamics. EHX adds a three-position LIFT function for changing the input gain and expands the EQ/output range.

## Sound
Spruce Goose covers clean preamp/boost territory, gritty bass and rhythm overdrive, and more driven stacked sounds. Its multiple LIFT settings change the amount of input gain while preserving the core Bluesbreaker-derived response.
