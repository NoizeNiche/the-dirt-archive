## Sound

Volume and Overdrive let you go from a gentle rasp that caresses your notes to a full-blown gain monster with a powerhouse punch.
Sculpt your sound with the Tone control, or take a detour around the tone circuit with the Tone On/Off switch.
Quick Specs - CMOS overdrive - Sensitive to your touch and guitar’s volume - True Bypass - Tone bypass switch completely removes tone circuit - 9 Volt battery included - Optional 9.6DC-200 power supply available Demo Video User Manual: https://www.ehx.com/hot-tubes-manual.pdf Shipping Weight: 0.65 lbs.
