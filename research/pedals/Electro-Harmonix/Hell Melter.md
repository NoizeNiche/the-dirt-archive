## What this pedal is
The **Electro-Harmonix Hell Melter** is an **Advanced Metal Distortion**. EHX describes it as the company's take on the cult-classic “chainsaw” distortion sound and positions it for extreme, heavy distortion. [1]

The archive keeps **Distortion** as the primary taxonomy.

## Operating context
EHX's published sound examples include multiple modes and stacking/use cases, including:
- **Burn Mode with Boost**
- **Gilmour-style lead**
- **Doomed Out**, with volume rolled up
- **Bass + Guitar**
- stacking with other drive pedals. [1]

These are manufacturer example names and use cases, not claims that the pedal is restricted to those styles.
