## What this pedal is
The **Electro-Harmonix Flatiron Fuzz** is an **op-amp powered fuzz/distortion**. EHX's product description and the exact-model evidence packet identify it as a dedicated fuzz rather than a conventional overdrive. [1]

## Circuit and clipping evidence
The reviewed technical evidence describes:
- a first gain stage providing up to approximately **38 dB of gain at 1 kHz with Fuzz at 3 o'clock** in the cited measurement,
- a second stage using dynamic compression and soft clipping,
- a proprietary dual-junction clipping network rather than simply standard 1N4148 or LED clipping. [6]

These measured/technical observations come from secondary exact-model analysis. The archive does not convert them into a universal factory BOM or claim every production revision has identical component values.

## Version context
The evidence packet references **V3** and contains device terms including **2N5088**, silicon-transistor language, LED, and 1N4148. Because those component terms conflict with the specific dual-junction description in the same packet, the archive does not treat them as a complete universal clipping BOM.

## Sonic/product context
EHX presents Flatiron as a classic fuzz/distortion voice, while the secondary evidence includes sound clips covering ratty distortion, full/clear drive, compressed overdrive, and more extreme fuzz textures. These are product/review descriptions, not measured frequency-response classifications. [1][6]
