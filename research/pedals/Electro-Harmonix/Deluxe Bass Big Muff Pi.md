## What this pedal is
The Deluxe Bass Big Muff Pi is EHX's bass-oriented expansion of the classic Big Muff circuit, adding filtering, clean blending, and bass-specific controls around the Muff fuzz core.

## Versions and factory options
- Bass Big Muff-style fuzz core.
- **Volume**
- **Tone**
- **Sustain**
- **Blend** control for clean signal mixing.
- **Bass Boost/Cut** and additional filter controls.
- Footswitchable crossover/filter functions.
- True bypass.
- 9V DC operation.

## Version changes
- This record is the original Deluxe Bass Big Muff Pi.
- The later **Deluxe Bass Big Muff Pi 2** uses the dual-op-amp Bass Big Muff Pi 2 platform and is cataloged separately.

## Sound
The Deluxe Bass Big Muff retains the huge, sustaining Muff character while adding practical bass controls. Clean blending helps preserve fundamental attack, while its filters provide more control over low and high frequency content than a standard three-knob Muff.
