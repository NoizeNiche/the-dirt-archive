# CopperSound Pedals - Mystique Germanium Fuzz & Boost

## Research identity
- **Builder:** CopperSound Pedals
- **Catalog identity:** Mystique Germanium Fuzz & Boost
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Exact 10-year anniversary limited-edition Mystique model.

## What this pedal is
Mystique is a hand-built germanium fuzz and boost pedal released by CopperSound Pedals as a limited 10-year anniversary edition. CopperSound states that only 50 units were made.

## Circuit and controls
- Fully discrete germanium fuzz section.
- Independent germanium boost section.
- A dedicated order switch places the boost before or after the fuzz.
- Two-band EQ with **Low** and **High** controls.
- CopperSound states that the pedal uses three individually tested, hand-measured germanium transistors.

## Versions and factory options
- **10-Year Anniversary Limited Edition:** 50 units.
- No numbered production revision was established beyond this limited-edition designation.

## Transistor
- **Type:** Germanium.
- **Exact part numbers:** not publicly specified on the checked manufacturer page.

## Diode
- No exact diode type was established from the checked sources.

## Sound
CopperSound describes Mystique as spanning smooth, creamy sustain through rawer, more aggressive germanium fuzz. The independent boost and switchable order make it practical both as a standalone fuzz/boost and as a stacking tool.

## Construction and provenance
- Hand-built by CopperSound Pedals.
- Official material says the design draws inspiration from vintage fuzz circuits including the Fuzz Face and Tone Bender.
