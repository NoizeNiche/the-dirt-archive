# CopperSound Pedals - Strategy Overdrive & Preamp

## Research identity
- **Builder:** CopperSound Pedals
- **Catalog identity:** Strategy Overdrive & Preamp
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Version documented here:** Strategy V2

## What this pedal is
Strategy V2 is a two-channel, all-analog preamp and overdrive built around a Burr-Brown op amp. Preamp mode provides unity gain through up to 30 dB of boost; Overdrive mode adds adjustable grit and saturation. CopperSound presents it as an always-on-style preamp/overdrive and part of its Pickguard Series.

## Controls and switching
- **Master/Volume:** output level, from unity through heavy boost.
- **Tone:** wide-sweep tone shaping.
- **Mode:** rear-panel switch selects Preamp or Overdrive operation.
- **Footswitch:** CopperSound SMART BYPASS supports normal latching and momentary operation.

## Circuit and components
- Burr-Brown op amp.
- Fully analog preamp/overdrive architecture.
- Exact Burr-Brown part number was not established in the checked sources.

## Power and specifications
- 9V or 18V operation.
- 40 mA at either 9V or 18V according to CopperSound's current technical specifications.
- Input impedance: 1 MΩ.
- Output impedance: 10 kΩ.
- Dimensions including hardware: 4.7 x 4.1 x 2.25 in.

## Switching behavior
SMART BYPASS is relay-based true bypass. CopperSound documents latching and momentary operation, with momentary engagement available by holding the footswitch. A fail-safe behavior returns the signal to bypass if power is lost.

## Research notes
CopperSound's current page identifies the current form as Strategy V2. Earlier launch coverage from Guitar World and Premier Guitar describes the V2 as the updated Strategy with the added Overdrive mode and 30 dB boost. Do not collapse earlier Strategy revisions into V2-specific specifications without version evidence.
