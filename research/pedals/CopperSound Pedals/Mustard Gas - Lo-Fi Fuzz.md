## What this pedal is
Mustard Gas is a deliberately lo-fi fuzz machine built to reproduce old-radio and broken-speaker textures while also offering increasingly heavy low-end fuzz as the three-position switch is moved away from its center setting.

## Controls
- **Volume:** overall output.
- **Texture:** lo-fi distortion amount; active with the switch centered.
- **Switch:**
  - **Center:** normal lo-fi response.
  - **Down:** restores low-end response and overloads the circuit for thicker fuzz.
  - **Up:** adds still more low-end emphasis and aggression.
- True bypass.
