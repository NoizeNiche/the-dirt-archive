## What this pedal is
ION is a compact PLL-style laser fuzz built around an optical filter network. CopperSound describes it as a streamlined approach to frequency-sweep fuzz, designed to keep the effect recognizable and musical rather than requiring extensive multi-control adjustment.

## Controls
- **Mode toggle:** changes optical-filter response.
  - **Up:** high-frequency resonance and maximum filter sensitivity.
  - **Middle:** balanced sensitivity for responsive sweeps.
  - **Down:** sub-octave square-wave grit and the most traditional octave-down presentation.

## Circuit and sound
CopperSound describes a sophisticated optical filter network that produces resonant frequency sweeps in response to playing dynamics, including pick scrapes and heavy attack. The pedal is presented as a PLL-style design, but the exact oscillator/filter IC implementation is not publicly documented in the checked sources.
