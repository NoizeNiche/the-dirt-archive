- **Identity:** CopperSound's 10th-anniversary limited edition Mystique.

## What this pedal is
Mystique is a hand-built Germanium fuzz and independent boost platform created for CopperSound Pedals' 10th anniversary. The limited edition was announced as a 50-unit run.

## Controls and functions
- Independent Germanium fuzz and boost sections.
- Boost can be placed before or after the fuzz with the order switch.
- Two-band EQ with Low and High controls.
- Separate fuzz/boost level controls are part of the platform; exact full control labeling should be confirmed from the manual before treating every knob name as archival fact.

## Circuit and components
- Fully discrete Germanium fuzz.
- Three hand-measured Germanium transistors.
- Exact transistor part numbers are not publicly identified on the checked manufacturer page.
- The manufacturer positions the circuit as drawing inspiration from classic Fuzz Face and Tone Bender designs, but those references are lineage/inspiration rather than proof of a specific circuit clone.
