# CopperSound Pedals - Foxcatcher - Overdrive & Boost

## Research identity
- **Builder:** CopperSound Pedals
- **Catalog identity:** Foxcatcher - Overdrive & Boost
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive / Boost
- **Identity:** Exact first-generation Foxcatcher documented by CopperSound and Effects Database.

## What this pedal is
Foxcatcher is a two-part dirt platform built from a Bluesbreaker starting point. CopperSound documents changes to the tone section, addition of the Nature control, an active output buffer, an independent boost section derived from the Gravity Bomb, and an effect-order switch that places the boost before or after the drive section.

## Controls
- **Volume:** overall output.
- **Tone:** overall brightness.
- **Nature:** presence/voicing control used with Tone and Drive.
- **Drive:** overdrive amount.
- **First toggle:** boost before the drive for added gain or after the drive for added volume.
- **Boost:** boost amount.

## Signal and switching
- Analog overdrive with an independent clean boost.
- Mechanical true bypass is documented.
- Output buffer is active when the effect is engaged.
- Internal polarity protection is documented by the manufacturer/retailer material.

## Power
- 9-18V operation.
- 2.1 mm x 5.5 mm center-negative supply.
- Effects Database notes 18V as recommended for increased clarity/headroom.

## Construction
CopperSound documentation describes the model as handmade in the USA. The circuit uses a Burr-Brown audiophile-grade op amp in the final documented design.

## Versions and factory options
- This is the first-generation Foxcatcher identity. CopperSound separately labels a V1 product page and a later V2 overdrive.
- V2 should remain a separate catalog identity because it changes the control architecture, gain modes, tone network, and switching system.

## Sound
CopperSound presents Foxcatcher as a light-to-medium clipping overdrive with a transparent Bluesbreaker foundation, expanded tone/presence control, and an independent boost that can be stacked before or after the drive.
