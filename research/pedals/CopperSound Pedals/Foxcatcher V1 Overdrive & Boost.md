# CopperSound Pedals - Foxcatcher V1 Overdrive & Boost

## Research identity
- **Builder:** CopperSound Pedals
- **Catalog identity:** Foxcatcher V1 Overdrive & Boost
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive / Boost
- **Identity:** CopperSound's explicitly labeled V1 product page identifies this as the first-generation Foxcatcher platform.

## What this pedal is
Foxcatcher V1 is a two-in-one Bluesbreaker-derived overdrive and clean boost. CopperSound's V1 documentation describes changes to the tone section, the Nature presence control, an engaged-state output buffer, and an independent clean boost section.

## Controls and features
The V1 platform documents:
- **Volume**
- **Tone**
- **Nature**
- **Drive**
- **Boost**
- Boost-order switching for pre/post placement around the drive circuit

The unit uses a mechanical true-bypass arrangement and an engaged-state output buffer.

## Power
- 9-18V operation.
- 2.1 mm x 5.5 mm center-negative supply.
- CopperSound documentation and surviving listings identify increased headroom/clarity at higher supply voltage.

## Versions and relationship
- CopperSound explicitly labels this product page **Foxcatcher V1**.
- The archive keeps the generic **Foxcatcher - Overdrive & Boost** identity and the explicitly labeled **Foxcatcher V1** identity separate in the catalog because both identities are present in the source corpus.
- The later Foxcatcher V2 is a distinct design with a new gain/shape control system and SMART Bypass.
