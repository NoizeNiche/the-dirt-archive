## What this pedal is
Renegade is a compact fuzz derived from the Tone Bender MK1.5 concept but implemented with high-gain silicon transistors and a switchable bias arrangement. CopperSound describes the design as a simple, no-frills fuzz with two distinct bias responses.

## Controls
- **Master Volume:** overall output and gain staging.
- **Bias toggle:**
  - **Up:** full-bodied, conventional fuzz.
  - **Down:** more modern, gated fuzz with an electric-sizzle character.

## Circuit
- High-gain silicon transistors.
- The exact transistor part numbers are not publicly documented.
- CopperSound states that each unit is biased to a precise voltage for consistency.

## Power and switching
- 9-18V operation.
- 9V draw: 5mA.
- 18V draw: 10mA.
- 2.1 x 5.5 mm center-negative power.
- Mechanical true bypass.
- Input impedance: 1M.
- Output impedance: 30K.
- Dimensions: 3.65 x 1.8 x 2.19 inches.
- No battery option is stated in the current manufacturer technical data.

## Versions and factory options
- No numbered revision established.
- Bias selection is a core factory feature.

## Transistor
- **Technology:** high-gain silicon.
- **Exact part numbers:** not publicly documented.

## Diode
- **Exact diode/clipping device:** not publicly documented.

## Sound
The up position provides a fuller, more vintage-oriented fuzz; the down position introduces a gated response and a pronounced electric-sizzle decay. CopperSound also notes long sustain and increased headroom at 18V.
