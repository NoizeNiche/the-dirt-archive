## What this pedal is
Foxcatcher V2 is the later evolution of the Foxcatcher platform. CopperSound describes it as retaining a Bluesbreaker-based foundation while adding broader gain and low-end shaping, a revised tone network, and SMART Bypass switching.

## Controls and switching
- **Volume:** output level.
- **Tone:** expanded tone sweep.
- **Gain:** drive amount.
- **Gain mode switch:** Low / Medium / High.
- **Shape control/switch:** three low-end shaping positions.
- **SMART Bypass:** latching or momentary operation from the footswitch.
- Configurable power-up state via internal DIP switch.
- Fail-safe bypass behavior on loss of power.

## Signal and power
- Analog overdrive.
- True bypass through CopperSound's relay-based SMART Bypass system.
- 9-18V operation.
- 9V draw about 50mA; 18V about 60mA.
- 2.1 x 5.5 mm center-negative power input.
- Input impedance 2.2M; output impedance 100K.
- Dimensions 4.8 x 2.6 x 2.25 inches.

## Versions and relationship
- V2 is a distinct catalog identity from the first-generation Foxcatcher / V1 because its gain architecture, tone network, low-end shaping, and bypass system were materially revised.

## Sound
CopperSound describes three gain ranges from touch-sensitive low-gain breakup through medium-drive saturation to a higher-gain setting while preserving note definition. The Shape system changes low-end tightness versus bloom, and the Tone network has a wider sweep than the earlier platform.
