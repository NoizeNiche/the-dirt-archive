# CopperSound Pedals - Kingpin Germanium Clipper

## Research identity
- **Builder:** CopperSound Pedals
- **Catalog identity:** Kingpin Germanium Clipper
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Current CopperSound product page for Kingpin.

## What this pedal is
Kingpin is a compact overdrive that uses Germanium clipping diodes rather than germanium transistors. CopperSound places a symmetrical pair of Ukrainian Germanium diodes between two FET gain stages, with a MOSFET first stage and JFET second stage.

## Controls
- **Volume:** overall level/gain-use control.
- **CLIP toggle:** switches between lower and more aggressive Germanium clipping behavior.
- Variable-voltage operation from 9V to 18V increases available headroom at the higher supply voltage.

## Circuit
- Two FET gain stages.
- First stage uses a MOSFET.
- Second stage uses a JFET.
- Symmetrical pair of Ukrainian Germanium clipping diodes sits between the FET stages.
- Exact transistor part numbers are not publicly identified in the checked source.

## Power and switching
- 9-18V operation.
- Center-negative 2.1 x 5.5 mm input.
- 10mA at both 9V and 18V in the manufacturer's current technical specification.
- Input impedance: 2.2M.
- Output impedance: 30K.
- Mechanical true bypass.
- Dimensions: 3.65 x 1.8 x 2.19 inches.

## Versions and factory options
- No numbered production revision was established.
- Germanium diode clipping is a core factory design feature, not an aftermarket modification.

## Transistor
- **Technology:** MOSFET + JFET gain stages.
- **Exact part numbers:** not publicly documented by the checked manufacturer source.
## Diode
- **Type:** Ukrainian Germanium, used as a symmetrical clipping pair.

## Sound
CopperSound describes Kingpin as an always-on-style gain stage capable of moving from touch-sensitive grit into more aggressive garage-rock overdrive. The CLIP toggle increases the intensity of the Germanium clipping while retaining depth and definition.
