# Cause & Effect Pedals (CE Pedals) — FET Dream

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** FET Dream
- **Builder:** Cause & Effect Pedals (CE Pedals)
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive
- **Identity:** The company's first production pedal, introduced in 2009 as a hand-built, individually tuned overdrive/distortion design.

## What this pedal is
The FET Dream is a boutique overdrive/distortion pedal from Cause & Effect Pedals, developed around a tube-like FET input stage and intended to retain guitar dynamics while adding warm, harmonically rich drive. Contemporary coverage describes it as hand-built and individually tweaked, with controls designed to cover a wide range from clean boost into substantial clipping. [1][2]

## Colorways
- Documented production examples use a **blue/purple powder-coated enclosure** with white graphics.
- The archive treats finish differences as cosmetic unless Cause & Effect identifies a circuit or production change. No separate production colorway/version family was established from the sources checked.

## Versions and factory options
### FET Dream production model
- Four controls: **Girth, Drive, Lean, and Level**.
- **Girth** provides pre-distortion low and lower-mid shaping.
- **Lean** provides post-distortion treble shaping with variable filter Q.
- **Drive** ranges from low-gain clean-boost behavior to substantially higher gain.
- **True-bypass** switching with a bright blue indicator LED.
- 9V operation with a standard 2.1 mm center-negative DC connection and provision for a 9V battery. [1][3]

### Factory tuning
Cause & Effect says individual FET Dream pedals use **hand-selected and individually tuned components**. This is a production/tuning characteristic rather than a separately named V1/V2 hardware version. [2][4]

## Sound
The FET Dream is designed around clear, articulate overdrive and distortion with a tube-like response, warm harmonic content, and preserved pickup dynamics. The Girth control lets the player add or reduce low/lower-mid content before distortion, while Lean changes upper-treble emphasis after distortion, so the pedal can move from relatively clean boost and open crunch toward smoother or more aggressive higher-gain sounds. [1][2][3]
