# AWOL Pedals — Virus Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Virus Fuzz
- **Builder:** AWOL Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Compact single-knob silicon fuzz, handmade in Nashville, designed around strong pickup/dynamics sensitivity and interactive feedback at higher output levels. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
AWOL describes Virus as a silicon fuzz whose entire external control scheme is a single output-volume knob. The circuit is intentionally highly interactive with different pickups, playing attack and instrument volume, producing aggressive but smooth clipping that can become glitchy and feedback-rich as level rises. [1]

## Controls
- **Single knob:** Output volume.
- True bypass latching footswitch. [1][2]

## Sound
AWOL describes Virus as aggressive yet smooth, with velvety and glitchy clipping qualities. The harmonic content varies strongly with pickup choice, and higher volume can create unruly feedback that interacts with guitar or bass. [1]
