## What this pedal is
AWOL describes Virus as a hand-made silicon fuzz whose single knob controls output volume. The builder emphasizes that the harmonic response changes strongly with pickup choice and playing dynamics.

## Sound
Virus is designed to be aggressive yet smooth, with a fuzzy texture that can become glitchier as the signal and level increase. AWOL specifically notes strong harmonic interaction with guitars and basses and pronounced feedback at louder settings.
