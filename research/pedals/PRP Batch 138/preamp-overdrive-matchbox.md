# Cajita Stompboxes - Preamp Overdrive Matchbox

- **Builder:** Cajita Stompboxes
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Product page:** https://pedalescajita.mitiendanube.com/productos/preamp-overdrive-matchbox/
- **Identity:** MATCHBOX, Cajita's light overdrive / preamp-style pedal.
- **Description:** Cajita describes it as a light overdrive with flexible tone control and a simulator of the classic Matchless DC30.
- **Controls:** TONE, VOL, GAIN.
- **Voicing switch:** Six-position switch. Cajita describes the THIN setting as brighter with less distortion and useful for rhythm guitar, while FULL adds distortion and bass. The switch works separately from the TONE control.
- **Switching / I/O:** True bypass; mono input and mono output.
- **Power:** 9V center-negative adapter or battery.
- **Construction:** Aluminum enclosure, high-quality jacks, durable vinyl graphics.
- **Circuit note:** The Matchless DC30 relationship is a builder description. No component-level topology is asserted.
