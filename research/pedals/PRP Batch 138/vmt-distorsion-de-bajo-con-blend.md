- **Catalog type:** Distortion

- **Identity:** VMT, a bass-focused overdrive/distortion with clean-blend control.
- **Description:** Cajita describes a CMOS-based distortion combined with FET-transistor saturation. It is voiced for bass and described as having an Ampeg-style character with strong low and low-mid content.
- **Controls:** BL, GAIN, VOL, ERA.
