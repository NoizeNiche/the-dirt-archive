# Copilot FX - Orbit

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Copilot FX
- **Catalog identity:** Orbit
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz / Oscillator
- **Identity:** Copilot FX frequency-modulated fuzz with VCO.

## What this pedal is
Copilot FX describes Orbit as a frequency-modulated fuzz in which the incoming note and a voltage-controlled oscillator interact to create changing frequency relationships. [1]
The manufacturer documents a six-control version with independently adjustable fuzz and VCO voices, dry-signal control, gating and expression/CV control. [1][2]
Effects Database independently catalogs Orbit as a fuzz/oscillator pedal. [3]

## Controls
The manufacturer documents:
- **FRQ**: VCO center-frequency range.
- **EXP**: fine frequency adjustment.
- **GATE**: input level/sustain and VCO triggering.
- **DRY**: dry-signal volume.
- **VOL**: fuzz volume.
- **VCO**: VCO volume. [1][2]

The manufacturer manual also documents sync functions and an expression/CV jack for external VCO control. [2]

## Versions and factory options
- Six-knob version added broader gate range, independent VCO/fuzz volume controls and dry control compared with the earlier five-knob version. [1]
- **Power:** 9V DC center-negative adapter, minimum 15mA. [2]
- **Expression/CV:** TRS expression or 0-5V CV. [2]
- **Enclosure:** powder-coated aluminum with silk-screened artwork. [1]

## Version changes
Copilot FX explicitly distinguishes the earlier five-knob and later six-knob Orbit versions. [1]

## Construction
Powder-coated aluminum enclosure with electronic VCO/fuzz architecture and external expression/CV control. [1][2]
