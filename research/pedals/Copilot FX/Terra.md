# Copilot FX - Terra

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Copilot FX
- **Catalog identity:** Terra
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Copilot FX dual-voice fuzz with a standard fuzz voice and a scramble/octave-like fuzz voice.

## What this pedal is
Copilot FX describes Terra as a six-control fuzz machine with multiple tonal behaviors ranging from swelling and gated sounds through octave-like and tremfuzz effects. The newer version provides two independent fuzz voices. [1]
The manufacturer describes one standard classic fuzz voice and a second scramble-y/octavish fuzz voice, with shared gain, bias, tone and input controls. [1]

## Controls
The manufacturer documents:
- **Bass Toggle**: three-way bass selection.
- **Bias**: voltage-starve control.
- **Gain**: gain amount.
- **Tone**: tone control.
- **Volume**: standard fuzz voice volume.
- **Octave**: octave/scramble fuzz voice volume.
- **Input**: input gain.
- **Exp/CV jack**: external control of octave-voice volume. [1]

## Versions and factory options
- **Two independent voices:** classic fuzz and scramble-y/octavish fuzz. [1]
- **Power:** 9V DC center-negative adapter only; no battery. [1]
- **Expression/CV:** TRS expression or 0-5V CV for the octave fuzz voice volume. [1]
- **Enclosure:** powder-coated aluminum with silk-screened artwork. [1]

## Version changes
The manufacturer describes the newer version as having two independent voices. [1]

## Construction
Powder-coated aluminum enclosure with parallel fuzz voices and voltage-starve/bias control. [1]
