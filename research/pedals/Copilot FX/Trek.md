# Copilot FX - Trek

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Copilot FX
- **Catalog identity:** Trek
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz / Oscillator
- **Identity:** Copilot FX variable pulse-width fuzz and oscillator pedal.

## What this pedal is
Copilot FX describes Trek as a variable pulse-width fuzz pedal and oscillator. The incoming signal is converted to a square wave with adjustable duty cycle, while a parallel dry path adds clarity. An oscillation footswitch introduces additional chaotic modulation. [1]

## Controls
The manufacturer documents:
- **Oscillation toggle**
- **Gate**
- **Width**
- **Amount**
- **Vol**
- **Dry**
- **Tone**
- **Exp/CV jack**
- **Momentary oscillation switch** [1]

The controls shape pulse width, fuzz level, dry balance, gate behavior, tone and oscillator pitch. [1]

## Versions and factory options
- **Power:** 9V DC center-negative adapter only; no battery. [1]
- **Expression/CV:** external pulse-width control through TRS expression or 0-5V CV. [1]
- **Enclosure:** powder-coated aluminum with silk-screened artwork. [1]
- The manufacturer also documents later custom Trek variants with additional controls, but the archive preserves the canonical Trek identity separately. [2]

## Version changes
A later custom 10-knob Trek variant used parallel overdrive/distortion and pulse-width-fuzz paths plus additional oscillator controls. [2]
That custom variant is not substituted for the canonical Trek entry.

## Construction
Electronic pulse-width fuzz/oscillator design with dry parallel path and external CV/expression control. [1]

## Sound
The pedal can move from pulse-width fuzz into oscillating, Morse-like and more chaotic textures, with dry-signal blending used to retain clarity. [1]
