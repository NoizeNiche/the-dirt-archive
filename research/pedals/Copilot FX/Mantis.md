# Copilot FX - Mantis

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Copilot FX
- **Catalog identity:** Mantis
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz / Octave Fuzz
- **Identity:** Copilot FX octave-down PWM fuzz pedal.

## What this pedal is
Copilot FX's product catalog identifies Mantis as an **Octave Down PWM Fuzz**. [1]
The manufacturer's Mantis manual documents a multivoice design combining a fuzz voice with one- and two-octave-down voices, with pulse-width controls and external expression/CV control. [2]
Effects Database independently places Copilot FX Mantis in its octave-fuzz catalog. [3]

## Controls
The manufacturer manual documents:
- **RANGE ▼**: pulse-width control for the one-octave-down voice.
- **▼**: one-octave-down voice volume.
- **▼▼**: two-octave-down voice volume.
- **GATE**: input sensitivity and sustain/decay behavior of the octave and fuzz voices.
- **VOL**: octave-fuzz volume. [2]

The two octave voice pulse-width controls can be externally controlled through expression/CV jacks. [2]

## Colorways
- The manufacturer describes silk-screened artwork on a powder-coated aluminum case for the Mantis family. [2]
- Cosmetic variations are not treated as circuit revisions.

## Versions and factory options
- **Power:** 9V adapter, center-negative; the manufacturer manual specifies at least 15mA. [2]
- **Expression/CV:** external control of pulse width for the octave voices. [2]
- The manufacturer later documents a revamped Mantis II, but the archive retains Mantis as its own catalog identity. [1][4]

## Version changes
Copilot FX's designer states that Mantis later received a second version, Mantis II. [5]
The Mantis II is therefore not collapsed into the original Mantis entry.

## Construction
The manufacturer describes powder-coated aluminum enclosure construction and electronic control over octave pulse width. The checked manual does not provide a complete component-level circuit schematic. [2]

## Transistor
- **Exact transistor/device:** Not publicly documented in the checked sources.
## Diode
- **Exact clipping/rectification diode/device:** Not publicly documented.

## Sound
The Mantis combines straight fuzz with one- and two-octave-down voices. The manufacturer manual notes that the pedal is monophonic and recommends allowing each note to track cleanly; mixing the voices is central to its operation. [2]
