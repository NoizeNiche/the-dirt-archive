## What this pedal is

Surviving Guitar Center and Musician's Friend records identify an Azur OVERDRIVE Effect Pedal with Drive, Tone and Level controls. The surviving retail evidence describes it as a compact overdrive suitable for applications from light grit through more saturated drive. [1][2]

- Footswitch for engagement.

## Sound

Musician's Friend characterizes the pedal as warm, classic overdrive with Drive, Tone and Level controls and smooth cleanup from the guitar volume. Guitar Center describes the surviving unit as touch-responsive from light grit to saturated lead tones. These are retailer descriptions rather than measured circuit behavior. [1][2]
