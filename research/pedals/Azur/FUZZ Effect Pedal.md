# Azur — FUZZ Effect Pedal

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** FUZZ Effect Pedal
- **Builder:** Azur
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Exact retail identity sold under the **Azur** spelling. No independent factory model number has been established. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
Surviving Guitar Center and Musician's Friend records identify an **Azur FUZZ Effect Pedal** as a compact fuzz for guitar and bass. The surviving descriptions agree on the basic three-control arrangement and center-negative 9V operation. [1][2]

## Controls
- **LEVEL**
- **TONE**
- **FUZZ**
- Standard 1/4-inch input/output.
- True bypass.
- LED/status switching is documented in the retailer record. [2]

## Circuit lineage
- A specific Fuzz Face, Big Muff or other circuit lineage is **not established** for the Azur-labeled unit.
- The archive does not merge the pedal with Azor AP-303 even though AP-303 is a superficially similar three-knob fuzz; the surviving retail branding is explicitly **Azur**. [1][2]

## Sound
Guitar Center describes the surviving Azur fuzz as vintage-style, thick and saturated with responsive cleanup from the instrument's volume control. Another surviving Guitar Center listing emphasizes gritty fuzz, strong sustain and harmonic bite. These are retailer descriptions, not measured frequency-response claims. [1][2]
