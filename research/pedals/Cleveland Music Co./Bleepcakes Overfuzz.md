# Cleveland Music Co. — Bleepcakes Overfuzz

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Bleepcakes Overfuzz

- **Identity:** Cleveland Music Co. Bleepcakes Overfuzz, an all-silicon Harmonic Percolator-inspired fuzz/overdrive with diode-lift and fat controls.

## What this pedal is
Cleveland Music Co. describes Bleepcakes as its spin on the Harmonic Percolator, expanded with tone/wave shaping and an adjustable diode-lift function. The builder uses modern silicon transistors rather than the original PNP germanium/NPN silicon combination to improve stability while retaining a Percolator-based voice. [1]

## Colorways
The official product page establishes the exact model and standard powder-coated/UV-printed enclosure but does not document a complete factory colorway sequence. [1]

## Versions and factory options
Controls:
- **LEVEL**
- **HARMONICS**
- **LIFT**
- **FAT**
- **- / +** clipping selector [1]

The LIFT control blends between the clipping diodes being engaged and lifted out of circuit. The clipping selector chooses:
- Vintage asymmetrical silicon clipping.
- Modern symmetrical silicon clipping. [1]

The builder also describes the design as loosely based on “Albini-esque” Percolator specifications, with changes for the all-silicon implementation. Additional hardware includes true bypass, top-mounted jacks, 125B enclosure, and no battery snap. [1]

## Version changes
No formal numbered factory revision sequence was established.

## Transistor
The builder explicitly specifies **genuine Fairchild silicon transistors**. [1]
## Diode
Two documented clipping configurations use **asymmetrical silicon** or **symmetrical silicon** diodes. The LIFT control can remove those clipping diodes from the signal path. Exact diode part numbers were not established. [1]

## Sound
Bleepcakes spans transistor overdrive and rich harmonic fuzz. Lower LIFT settings emphasize the Percolator clipping character, while lifting the diodes makes the sound louder, more open, and less compressed. FAT adds low-end content and can produce thicker, splattier fuzz at extreme settings. [1]
