## What this pedal is
Woollybear is a creamy, squishy, Bender-like fuzz that can move from thin, gated textures to thick, sustaining fuzz. Cleveland Music Co. specifies an all-silicon design for greater temperature stability.

## Versions and factory options
- **LEVEL**: output volume.
- **BIAS**: changes the operating point of one silicon transistor, from gated/Velcro-like textures toward thicker and more overdrive-like settings.
- **FUZZ**: saturation amount.
- **TONE**: treble content.
- **FAT**: low-frequency content.
- True bypass.
- Top-mounted audio/DC jacks.
- 125B enclosure.
- No battery snap.

## Sound
Woollybear spans gated, thin fuzz through thick, mid-heavy saturation. The Bias, Fuzz and Fat controls are deliberately interactive, and the circuit can clean toward overdrive as guitar volume is reduced.
