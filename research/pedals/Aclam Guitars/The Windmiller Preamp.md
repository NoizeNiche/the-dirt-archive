# Aclam Guitars — The Windmiller Preamp

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** The Windmiller Preamp
- **Builder:** Aclam Guitars
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Aclam's recreation and modernization of its own Grampian Reverberation Type Unit 636 S/N 1138 preamp circuit.

## What this pedal is
The Windmiller Preamp is based on Aclam's own Grampian Reverberation Type Unit 636 S/N 1138. Aclam kept the distinctive preamp character but developed the pedal for modern use with a substantially improved signal-to-noise ratio, added treble response and simple high/low-cut tone shaping. [1]

## Versions and factory options
### Windmiller Preamp production
- Grampian 636-based preamp/booster
- Gain control
- Overload indicator
- Hi Cut and Lo Cut controls
- Preamp/clean-boost use
- Relay true bypass
- 9V DC center-negative
- Approx. 38 mA current draw [1]

During development, Aclam tried germanium and then silicon transistor prototypes, and produced two or three prototypes under the earlier “Grampian Overdrive” concept before settling on the 636 preamp approach. This is documented development history, not a verified list of factory production revisions. [2]

## Version changes
The major documented development change was a move away from the early overdrive concept toward a dedicated Grampian 636 preamp. Aclam also added noise reduction and two simple cut controls to make the pedal quieter and easier to fine-tune while keeping the basic character of the original circuit. [2]

## Sound
The Windmiller can operate as a clean boost or as a gritty preamp that pushes an amp or another drive harder. Its two cut controls let the player trim low and high frequencies around the core Grampian-style character, making it useful for both cleaner boost duties and rawer overdriven sounds. [1][2]
