# Aclam Guitars — Dr. Robert

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Dr. Robert
- **Builder:** Aclam Guitars
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** Aclam's pedal recreation of the character of the rare 1966 VOX UL730 amplifier associated with the Beatles-era sounds on Revolver and Sgt. Pepper's.

## What this pedal is
Dr. Robert is designed around the distinctive preamp/distortion character of the 1966 VOX UL730. Aclam specifically based the design on its own 1966 UL730, serial number 3042, to bring that unusual British solid-state amp voice into a compact pedal format. [1]

## Versions and factory options
### Dr. Robert V3
- 1966 VOX UL730-inspired preamp/distortion design
- Bass and Treble controls on the front panel
- Mach Schau extra-saturation gain control on the front panel
- Global Lo/Hi Gain mode
- 3-band EQ
- Relay true bypass
- 9V DC center-negative
- Approx. 10 mA current draw [1]

Aclam's current product page identifies the present release as **V3**. The page also provides separate manuals for serial-number ranges, but the exact serial-to-feature mapping was not established from the reviewed material, so those ranges are not treated as independently verified circuit versions here. [1]

## Version changes
Aclam states that V3 keeps the sound and character of earlier Dr. Robert versions while making the pedal more versatile. The important documented change is that Bass, Treble and Mach Schau gain controls that were previously internal are now on the front panel, and V3 adds a global Lo/Hi Gain mode. [1]

## Sound
Dr. Robert is aimed at the unusual, compressed and gritty character of the VOX UL730 rather than a generic modern distortion voice. Aclam's current V3 keeps that vintage British coloration while expanding the control range and gain options. [1]
