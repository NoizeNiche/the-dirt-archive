# Aclam Guitars — Dr. Robert

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Dr. Robert
- **Builder:** Aclam Guitars
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** Aclam's pedal recreation of the character of the rare 1966 VOX UL730 amplifier associated with the Beatles-era sounds on Revolver and Sgt. Pepper's.

## What this pedal is
Dr. Robert is designed around the distinctive preamp/distortion character of the 1966 VOX UL730. Aclam specifically presents the pedal as a way to bring that unusual British solid-state amp voice into a compact pedal format. [1]

## Sound
Dr. Robert is aimed at the unusual, compressed and gritty character of the VOX UL730 rather than a generic modern distortion voice. Its appeal is the combination of aggressive breakup and vintage British coloration associated with the amplifier Aclam used as its reference. [1]
