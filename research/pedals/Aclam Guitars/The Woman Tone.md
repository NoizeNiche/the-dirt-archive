# Aclam Guitars — The Woman Tone

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** The Woman Tone
- **Builder:** Aclam Guitars
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Aclam's Cream-era tribute pedal combining a vintage humbucker pickup simulation with a Plexi-inspired overdrive and switchable Woman Tone shaping.

## What this pedal is
The Woman Tone combines a custom humbucker pickup simulation circuit with a touch-sensitive Plexi-inspired overdrive using discrete components. A dedicated Woman Mode footswitch activates the Woman Tone shaping, making the Clapton-style frequency emphasis available without reaching for the guitar's tone control. [1][2]

## Colorways

## Versions and factory options
### Current production
- Custom humbucker pickup simulation
- Woman Tone control
- Gain and Volume controls
- Woman Mode footswitch
- Relay true bypass
- Smart Track fastening system
- 9V DC center-negative, 40 mA minimum
- Approx. 10 mA current draw [1]

The current Aclam store page uses product reference **AP0120**. An older indexed Aclam store entry used **AP0040**, but no Aclam document reviewed states that the reference change corresponds to a circuit revision, so the archive does not treat those reference codes as verified separate electronic versions. [1]

## Version changes
A 2022 Guitarist review documents an internal DIP switch for a darker tone-capacitor setting and a Woman Mode footswitch that engages the Woman Tone control while slightly increasing gain and low end. The current Aclam store description still documents the same core pickup-simulation and Woman Tone concept, but a specific circuit change tied to AP0040 versus AP0120 was not verified. [1][2]

## Sound
The Woman Tone is voiced around a rounded, sustaining Cream-era overdrive with a simulated vintage humbucker response feeding a Plexi-style drive. Its dedicated Woman Mode shifts the tonal balance toward the darker, vocal-like character associated with Clapton's Cream-era setup while retaining a usable overdrive range outside the preset. [1][2]
