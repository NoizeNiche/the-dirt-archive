# Aclam Guitars — Go Rocky Go - White Album Edition

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Go Rocky Go - White Album Edition
- **Builder:** Aclam Guitars
- **Catalog types:** Distortion / Fuzz / Overdrive
- **Identity:** Limited White Album Edition of the Go Rocky Go, using the same circuit with an exclusive finish.

## What this pedal is
The White Album Edition is Aclam's special-edition version of Go Rocky Go. Aclam states directly that it shares exactly the same circuit as the original Go Rocky Go and that the change is the exclusive finish. [1]

## Colorways
- **White Album Edition:** exclusive special-edition finish.
- **First edition:** 68 individually numbered pedals, matching the 1968 theme. [1]

## Versions and factory options
### Go Rocky Go - White Album Edition
- Same Go Rocky Go circuit
- Clean / Normal mode
- Distortion mode
- Three-frequency M.R.B. mid boost
- Crunch mode
- 9V to 18V operation
- Relay true bypass
- Certificate included
- First edition limited to 68 individually numbered pieces [1]

## Version changes
Aclam explicitly states that the White Album Edition does **not** change the circuit or sound. The distinguishing change is the exclusive finish, with the first run limited to 68 individually numbered units. [1]

## Transistor
- **Technology:** Solid-state analog preamp/distortion circuit.

## Sound
Because Aclam states the circuit is unchanged, the White Album Edition has the same clean, fuzzy distortion, mid-boost and added crunch range as the standard Go Rocky Go. The special edition changes the visual presentation rather than the way the circuit sounds. [1]
