# Aclam Guitars — Cinnamon Drive - Dreamer Edition

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Cinnamon Drive - Dreamer Edition
- **Builder:** Aclam Guitars
- **Catalog types:** Distortion / Overdrive
- **Identity:** A special-edition presentation of Aclam's Cinnamon Drive dual-stage overdrive.

## What this pedal is
The Dreamer Edition uses the Cinnamon Drive platform, with Aclam describing the same dual-stage clipping approach, dynamic response and broad range from overdrive into distortion. The official Dreamer product page lists the same core Cinnamon Drive feature set and power requirements. [1]

## Colorways
- **Dreamer Edition:** special-edition finish/artwork.
- The edition is cataloged separately because Aclam presents it as its own named product.

## Versions and factory options
### Cinnamon Drive - Dreamer Edition
- Dual-stage clipping circuits
- Dynamic, natural response
- Relay true bypass
- 9V DC center-negative or 9V battery
- Approx. 35 mA current draw
- Smart Track fastening system [1]

No separate factory circuit revision was verified.

## Version changes
No circuit change was documented on the official Dreamer product page reviewed. It is treated as a named edition of the Cinnamon Drive platform rather than a newly numbered circuit revision. [1]

## Transistor
- **Technology:** Solid-state analog overdrive.
- **Exact transistor/device:** Not publicly documented in the official product material reviewed.

## Diode
- **Type:** The official material describes dual-stage clipping but does not identify the production diode types.

## Sound
The Dreamer Edition is built around the Cinnamon Drive's flexible, amp-like response, moving from clean boost and lighter overdrive into thicker distortion. The core design emphasizes dynamics and natural-feeling clipping instead of a fixed one-voice drive sound. [1]
