# Aclam Guitars — The Mocker

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** The Mocker
- **Builder:** Aclam Guitars
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Expanded recreation of the built-in fuzz circuit from Aclam's own 1966 VOX UL730, serial number 3042.

## What this pedal is
The Mocker is based on the built-in fuzz circuit of Aclam's own 1966 VOX UL730 S/N 3042. Aclam says a component in the original amp had drifted out of specification and produced a gated, spluttery response, so that behavior was deliberately preserved and made adjustable with a Bias control. [1]

## Colorways
- **Original release:** Grey Hammerite finish with 1960s British fuzzbox styling. [2]
- The current enclosure keeps the same general visual identity, with an updated enclosure that no longer has the side Smart Track holes. [1]

## Versions and factory options
### Original limited run
- First production run planned around a batch of NOS Philips OA200 diodes.
- 200 units in the original run.
- Bias and Tone controls.
- Smart Track fastening system.
- Relay true bypass. [2]

### Current production
- Aclam later found another batch of genuine NOS Philips OA200 diodes.
- Aclam states that every Mocker produced since then also uses NOS Philips OA200 diodes.
- Current enclosure removes the side Smart Track mounting holes; Aclam says the electronics and sound remain the same. [1]

## Version changes
The documented production change is primarily enclosure-related after the original 200-unit run. Aclam states that the later version still uses genuine NOS Philips OA200 diodes and that the electronics and sound remain unchanged. [1]

## Transistor
- **Exact production transistor/device:** Not publicly documented in the official material reviewed.

## Diode
- **Type:** New Old Stock Philips OA200.
- **Use:** Aclam identifies these as the same diode type used in the original VOX UL series. [1][2]

## Sound
The Mocker can be biased from the original amp's gated, spluttery character toward a more open 1966 fuzz response, with the Bias control also taking it into other textures. The Tone control broadens the usable range across different guitars and rigs, while the circuit remains centered on the distinctive UL730 fuzz character. [1]
