# Aclam Guitars — Cinnamon Drive

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Cinnamon Drive
- **Builder:** Aclam Guitars
- **Catalog types:** Distortion / Overdrive
- **Identity:** Aclam's original dual-stage overdrive design built to cover clean boost, low-gain overdrive and saturated distortion.

## What this pedal is
Cinnamon Drive is an original Aclam circuit designed from the ground up around the feel of pushed 1960s and 1970s British tube amps. Aclam describes a combination of soft and hard clipping stages with cascading gain that lets the pedal move from clean preamp sounds through overdrive into heavy saturation.

## Sound
Cinnamon Drive starts around clean boost and low-gain overdrive, then adds a second gain stage for thicker and more saturated distortion. The cascading stages and soft/hard clipping are intended to keep the response dynamic and amp-like rather than turning every setting into the same block of gain. [1]
