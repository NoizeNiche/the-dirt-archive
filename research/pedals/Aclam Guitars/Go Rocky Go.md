# Aclam Guitars — Go Rocky Go

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Go Rocky Go
- **Builder:** Aclam Guitars
- **Catalog types:** Distortion / Fuzz / Overdrive
- **Identity:** Aclam's recreation of the Brilliant Channel of a specific Vox Conqueror, expanded with switchable distortion, mid boost and crunch functions.

## What this pedal is
Go Rocky Go is a faithful recreation of the Brilliant Channel of Aclam's Vox Conqueror S/N 2004. It recreates the clean preamp character, the built-in fuzzy distortion and the original mid-range boost, then adds a separate crunch mode for a lower-gain voice. [1]

## Colorways
- The standard edition uses the Rocky Strat-inspired artwork/finish documented by Aclam. [1]

## Versions and factory options
### Go Rocky Go production
- Normal clean/preamp mode
- Independent distortion section and volume control
- M.R.B. mid-range boost with three selectable frequencies
- Crunch mode with internal Crunch Gain control
- 9V to 18V operation
- Relay true bypass
- Ultra-silent switching
- Artwork inspired by George Harrison's Rocky Strat [1]

## Version changes
The standard Go Rocky Go is the base production model. The later White Album Edition is documented by Aclam as the same circuit with a special finish and was initially produced as 68 individually numbered pieces. [2]

## Sound
The clean mode keeps the character of the Vox Conqueror preamp, while the distortion mode delivers the original amp's fuzzy vintage breakup. The M.R.B. boosts selectable mid frequencies, and the added crunch mode sits between clean and distortion, giving the pedal a broader range than the original circuit alone. [1]
