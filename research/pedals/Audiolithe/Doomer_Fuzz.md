## What this pedal is
Doomer Fuzz is a two-stage cascaded fuzz with an ENGAGE stage and a second ROAR stage that adds gain and volume. Its TONE tilt EQ, ANGER mid contour, SCOOP switch and switchable impedance adaptation make it unusually controllable for a very heavy fuzz. [1][2]

## Versions

### Doomer Fuzz — original generation
The original production established the two-stage fuzz, ROAR boost, tilt EQ and impedance-adaptation concepts.

### Doomer Fuzz v2
**Builder/designated current revision**

The Guitar Division and Audiolithe catalog now identify the model as **Doomer Fuzz v2**. The current version retains:
- ENGAGE first fuzz stage
- ROAR second fuzz stage with gain/volume boost
- TONE tilt EQ
- ANGER mid contour
- FLAT/SCOOP EQ switch
- switchable impedance adaptation
- 9V DC, true bypass [1][2]

The available sources do not document every circuit change from the original to v2, so the archive does not invent missing changes.

## Sound
Doomer Fuzz is deliberately enormous and thick, with the first fuzz stage already producing heavy saturation and the ROAR stage adding more gain and output on demand. The tilt EQ and ANGER control give the player a surprising amount of control over the mass of the sound, while the SCOOP switch can carve the mids when a wider, heavier wall is desired. [1][2]
