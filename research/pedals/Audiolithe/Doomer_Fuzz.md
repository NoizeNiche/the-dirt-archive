# Audiolithe — Doomer Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Doomer Fuzz
- **Builder:** Audiolithe
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Current production:** v2 is the documented current generation.
- **Identity:** Two-stage cascaded fuzz with a first ENGAGE stage and second ROAR stage adding gain and volume, plus tilt EQ, midrange contour and switchable impedance adaptation. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
Audiolithe describes Doomer Fuzz as a saturation machine capable of very heavy, thick fuzz while preserving note definition. The second fuzz stage creates the larger “wall of fuzz” effect by adding both gain and level. [1]

## Controls / signal path
- **ENGAGE:** Activates the first fuzz stage.
- **ROAR:** Activates the second fuzz stage.
- **ROAR control:** Adjusts the amount of added boost from the second stage.
- **TONE:** Tilt EQ balancing lows and highs.
- **ANGER:** Midrange contour / bite.
- **SCOOP:** Flat versus scooped response.
- **Switchable impedance adaptation:** Designed to keep the pedal's behavior consistent when placed before or after other pedals. [1]

## Sound
Audiolithe describes Doomer Fuzz as extremely heavy, saturated and grainy while retaining definition. The first fuzz stage gives the base sound; ROAR adds both gain and output, and the TONE/ANGER/SCOOP controls let the player reshape the frequency balance and midrange bite. [1]
