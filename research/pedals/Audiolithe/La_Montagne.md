# Audiolithe — La Montagne

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** La Montagne
- **Builder:** Audiolithe
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Compact two-stage cascaded fuzz with three-position MORE switching, tilt-style Tone, Scoop and switchable impedance adaptation. [1][2]

## What this pedal is

La Montagne is designed to recreate the sound of an amplifier pushed beyond comfortable volume, using two cascaded fuzz stages. The first stage is always available; the second stage is introduced by the MORE switch in two preset intensities. [1]

## Controls / signal path

- **FUZZ**
- **VOLUME**
- **TONE:** Tilt EQ balancing low and high frequencies.
- **MORE:** OFF / MID / HIGH. OFF uses only the first fuzz stage; MID and HIGH engage the second stage with different preset amounts of added gain/level/thickness.
- **SCOOP:** Midrange reduction.
- **Impedance adaptation:** Switchable circuit intended to improve compatibility with pedals placed before or after the fuzz. [1][2]

## Circuit architecture

- Two cascaded fuzz stages.
- Second stage provides additional gain and volume.
- Tilt EQ.
- Switchable impedance adaptation.
- True bypass. [1]

## Sound

The builder describes La Montagne as thick, grainy and extremely heavy. The first stage is already substantial; activating MORE adds preset levels of gain, volume and thickness. The Tone tilt and Scoop controls can move the sound from full and dark toward more mid-carved textures. [1]
