## What this pedal is
La Montagne is a compact two-stage fuzz designed around the sound of an amplifier being pushed beyond comfortable volume. Its MORE switch engages the second stage in two preset intensities, while the TONE control works as a tilt EQ and SCOOP removes midrange; a switchable impedance-adaptation circuit is intended to keep the pedal behaving consistently anywhere in a signal chain. ## Sound
La Montagne is deliberately thick, grainy and aggressive, with the first fuzz stage already capable of a substantial wall of saturation. The second stage adds preset amounts of gain, volume and thickness, while the tilt-style Tone and Scoop controls can shift the sound from fuller and darker toward a more carved, cutting fuzz texture. [1][2]
