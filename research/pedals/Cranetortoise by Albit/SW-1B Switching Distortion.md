# Cranetortoise by Albit - SW-1B Switching Distortion

## Research identity

- **Identity:** Exact Cranetortoise / Albit SW-1B.

## What this pedal is
SW-1B is the bass-oriented version of the Cranetortoise SW-1 switching distortion. NoiseGuide identifies it as the “for bass” version and documents adjustable pulse width and speed for machine-like switching textures.

## Controls and operation
- **SPEED:** changes the switching rate; NoiseGuide describes machine-gun, helicopter and siren-like results at different settings.
- **Pulse width:** adjustable switching pulse width is documented in the exact-model description.
- The surviving sources do not provide a reliable full panel-label/value transcription beyond these confirmed functions.

## Versions and factory options
- **SW-1 Switching Distortion:** standard version.
- **SW-1B Switching Distortion for Bass:** distinct bass catalog identity.
- The archive keeps SW-1 and SW-1B separate.

## Sound
The switching section is designed for rhythmic, chopped textures. Exact-model documentation specifically mentions machine-gun, helicopter and siren-like sounds, with pulse width and Speed shaping the switching character.
