# Cranetortoise by Albit - DD-1 Dual Distortion

## Research identity

## What this pedal is
DD-1 is a dual-distortion design from Cranetortoise by Albit. The documented architecture mixes separate high-frequency and low-frequency distortion paths, allowing the player to control the contribution of each path while preserving attack and definition.

## Controls and switching
- **DRIVE 1:** controls the high-frequency distortion contribution.
- **DRIVE 2:** controls the low-frequency distortion contribution.
- **GAIN:** moves from dry-like crunch toward hard distortion.
- **TONE COLOR switch:** selects between documented European- and American-style distortion voicings.
- **SOUND BALANCE switch:** works with TONE COLOR to provide four documented distortion combinations.
- The exact output-level control labeling is not sufficiently established by the checked sources, so it is not guessed.

## Versions and factory options
- **DD-1B Dual Distortion for Bass** is a separate bass-oriented catalog model and is not merged with DD-1.
- The original DD-1 is separately documented in Effects Database from February 14, 2006.

## Circuit
- Dual Distortion Driver Circuit.
- Separate high- and low-frequency distortion contributions are mixed by DRIVE 1 and DRIVE 2.

## Sound
Manufacturer-derived descriptions reproduced by Japanese retailers emphasize a defined, attack-preserving distortion with less objectionable high-frequency character. The dual-path mixer allows the relative high- and low-frequency distortion content to be balanced for different sounds.
