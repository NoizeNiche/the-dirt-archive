## What this pedal is
HM-1 is a high-gain distortion pedal from the Cranetortoise by Albit line. Effects Database dates the documented model to February 14, 2006, and the surviving ALBIT manual identifies it as an all-handmade high-gain distortion with a dedicated Heavy Metal switch.

## Controls and switching
- **GAIN:** distortion/gain amount.
- **VOLUME:** output level.
- **HEAVY METAL SW:** switches the additional heavy-metal active circuit.
- **ON/OFF:** bypass control.

The manual's worked examples show the Heavy Metal switch changing the character and the Gain control then determining the amount of distortion in that mode.
