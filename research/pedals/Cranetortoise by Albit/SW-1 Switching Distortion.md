## What this pedal is
SW-1 is an analog switching-distortion effect from the Cranetortoise/ALBIT range. Surviving exact-model documentation describes selectable gain-overdrive/distortion and a separate switching/tremolo-style sound that can also be combined with the distortion.

## Controls and switching
- The exact-model Reverb listing describes selectable:
  - gain overdrive or distortion
  - tremolo / “scatter gun” delay-echo style switching effects
  - or a combination of both
- A complete verified knob-label/value transcription was not established.

## Versions and factory options

## Sound
SW-1 combines conventional gain-based distortion with a switchable rhythmic/switching effect. The checked period listing specifically describes machine-like scatter-gun, delay-echo and tremolo-style textures when the switching section is engaged.
