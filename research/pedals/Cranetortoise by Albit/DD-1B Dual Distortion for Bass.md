## What this pedal is
DD-1B is the bass-oriented member of the Cranetortoise Dual Distortion family. Albit's support documentation lists DD-1B among its discontinued Cranetortoise effects, while current and recent Japanese listings identify it as a bass distortion model.

## Controls and operation
The verified retail documentation describes the DD-1B as a dual-mode / dual-channel distortion with independent gain/tone adjustment for the two channels. The exact physical panel naming and complete control-value set are not fully preserved by the checked sources, so unverified labels are not added.

## Versions and family relationship
- **DD-1 Dual Distortion:** standard model for general instrument use.
- **DD-1B Dual Distortion for Bass:** separate bass-oriented catalog identity.
- The archive keeps both models distinct rather than treating the B suffix as a cosmetic change.

## Circuit and sound
The DD-1B is designed to retain bass low end while providing a thick, wide-ranging distorted sound. Current product documentation describes the model as capable of moving from cleaner sounds to thick distortion with independent channel/gain shaping.
