# Avora — FY-6 Super Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** FY-6 Super Fuzz
- **Builder:** Avora
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Rebranded Japanese FY-6 / Super Fuzz-family octave fuzz from the late 1960s or early 1970s.

## What this pedal is
Effects Database identifies the Avora FY-6 as one of many brand names applied to the same Japanese OEM Super Fuzz circuit family. Its control layout is Expander, Balance, a two-position Tone switch and a top-mounted bypass switch.

## Versions and factory options
### FY-6 production
- Expander fuzz/intensity control
- Expander also serves as the power switch
- Balance output control
- Two-position Tone switch
- Top-mounted bypass switch
- 9V battery operation

### Tone switch
- One position for a more focused/mid-forward fuzz sound
- One position for thicker, more sustained low-end emphasis

## Sound
The Avora FY-6 belongs to the classic Super Fuzz sound family: aggressive, cutting fuzz with pronounced octave content and a thicker sustained setting from the Tone switch. The simple two-control plus switch layout keeps the circuit focused on fuzz intensity, output and the two main voicings.
