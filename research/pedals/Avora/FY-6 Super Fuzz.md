# Avora — FY-6 Super Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** FY-6 Super Fuzz
- **Builder:** Avora
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Japanese OEM FY-6 / Super Fuzz family rebrand from the late 1960s/early 1970s. Effects Database identifies Avora as one of many brand names applied to the same OEM circuit family. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
The Avora FY-6 Super Fuzz belongs to the well-documented family of Japanese OEM octave-fuzz pedals sold under numerous names. The circuit is associated with the aggressive, octave-rich fuzz sound of early heavy rock. [1]

## Controls
- **EXPANDER:** Fuzz intensity; its knob also incorporates the main power switch.
- **BALANCE:** Overall output volume.
- **TONE switch:** Two-position slide switch:
  - Position 1: more focused / mid-forward fuzz.
  - Position 2: thicker, more sustained response with greater low-end emphasis.
- **Top-mounted push-button:** Effect bypass. [1]

## Circuit lineage
- **OEM family:** FY-6 / Shin-Ei Super Fuzz family.
- Effects Database explicitly lists the Avora as one of many branded versions of this Japanese OEM circuit. [1]
- The archive therefore records **Super Fuzz family** as the circuit lineage and does not invent an Avora-specific schematic.

## Sound
Effects Database describes the FY-6 family as delivering snarling, octave-infused fuzz with aggressive attack. The Tone switch broadens the sound from a focused, cutting voice to a thicker, sustaining setting. [1]
