# Carlsbro Carlsbro Suzz

- **Builder:** Carlsbro
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Catalog identity:** Carlsbro Suzz
- **What this pedal is:** A compact analog fuzz/distortion from Carlsbro's Alu-series modular effects line.
- **Colorways:** The documented Suzz uses the distinctive flat metal Alu-series enclosure with a black/orange graphic treatment. No formal alternate factory colorway list was confirmed.
- **Versions:** No numbered factory revisions were confirmed.
- **Version changes:** No documented hardware revision was established in the reviewed sources.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented.
- **Diode type:** Not documented.
- **Controls / switching:** Gain and Sustain. Sustain affects the compression/decay character while Gain controls the amount of distortion/fuzz.
- **Power:** 9V battery.
- **Sound:** Suzz spans gritty breakup through fuller saturation, with the Sustain control extending the note envelope as the Gain control increases distortion. Its compact, simple interface is typical of the Carlsbro Alu-series effects.
- **Research confidence:** High for controls, analog format, power, and basic function; medium for exact production dating.
