# Carlsbro Carlsbro Fuzz

- **Builder:** Carlsbro
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Catalog identity:** Carlsbro Fuzz
- **What this pedal is:** The later Carlsbro-branded fuzz supplied by Sola Sound/Colorsound in the 1970s, associated with the Tone Bender MkIII family.
- **Colorways:** Surviving examples are commonly blue/teal metal enclosures with Carlsbro branding. No formal factory colorway list was confirmed.
- **Versions:** Distinct from the short-lived 1966 Carlsbro Fuzz-Tone. This record represents the later Sola Sound-built Carlsbro Fuzz.
- **Version changes:** Fuzzboxes.org documents the early-1970s Sola Sound version as a later replacement for the 1966 wedge-shaped Fuzz-Tone, with surviving original date stamps showing manufacture at least into 1976.
- **Transistor type:** Original surviving examples are reported with germanium transistors; exact production consistency across all units was not established.
- **Diode type:** Not documented.
- **Controls / switching:** Volume, Treble-Bass, and Fuzz on the classic three-knob Sola Sound-built form; footswitch below.
- **Sound:** This Carlsbro Fuzz is part of the Sola Sound/Tone Bender MkIII-era family, giving it a broad vintage fuzz character with a separate treble-bass tone control. The Fuzz control can move from darker, more overdrive-like settings toward a brasher fuzz, while the output control can provide substantial level.
- **Research confidence:** High for model identity and Sola Sound/Tone Bender family relationship; medium for universal component details.
