# Carlin Carlin Compressor/Fuzz

- **Builder:** Carlin
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Catalog identity:** Carlin Compressor/Fuzz
- **What this pedal is:** A Swedish early-1970s Carlin design by Nils Olof Carlin that combines compression/sustain with a variable distortion stage.
- **Colorways:** Surviving originals are associated with red-painted enclosures and the distinctive Carlin Compressor label; custom/hand-built enclosure variation is possible, so no formal factory colorway catalog was established.
- **Versions:** Original 1970s Carlin Compressor/Fuzz; later Moody Sounds reissue/clone versions are separate and are not merged into this factory-history record.
- **Version changes:** Moody Sounds later recreated the circuit in cooperation with Carlin, adding modern usability changes. The original pedal had the input on the left and no effect LED; the later clone adds right-side input, LED, and true bypass.
- **Transistor type:** Original component type not confirmed from a factory source in the reviewed material.
- **Diode type:** Not documented.
- **Controls / switching:** Sustain, Dist, and Volume. The Dist control is the defining feature, allowing drive/fuzz to be mixed into the compressed signal. Original bypass implementation is documented as a simple on/off footswitch; modern reissues are not the same hardware.
- **Power:** Original factory power specification not confirmed in the reviewed sources.
- **Sound:** With Dist low, the circuit provides mild, natural compression and sustain; increasing Dist adds progressively stronger overdrive/fuzz while retaining the compressed envelope. The design was part of Sweden's small 1970s progressive-rock pedal scene and was built in very small numbers.
- **Research confidence:** High for origin, controls, hybrid compression/drive behavior, and separation from later Moody Sounds versions; medium for exact original production quantity and power details.
