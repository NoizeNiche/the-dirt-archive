# Caroline Guitar Company CROM

- **Builder:** Caroline Guitar Company
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Fuzz / Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** CROM
- **What this pedal is:** A BC184 Supa Tone Bender-style fuzzstortion with a tweed-amplifier-style tone control and a Turbo gain/clipping switch.
- **Colorways:** STANDARD BLACK and SPECIAL BRONZE are documented.
- **Versions:** Standard Black and Special Bronze factory variants are documented on Caroline's current product page.
- **Version changes:** No separate numbered hardware revision was established from the current documentation.
- **Transistor type:** BC184.
- **Diode type:** Not documented.
- **Controls / switching:** HELMET output, SWORD gain, MOUNTAIN tone, and side-mounted TURBO. An internal trimpot sets device bias/gate and can affect noise and response.
- **Power:** 9V DC center-negative or battery operation per the manual.
- **Sound:** CROM uses the Supa Tone Bender-style gain structure but replaces the usual highly scooped Muff-family tone behavior with a tweed-amp-style MOUNTAIN control that is more immediate and punchy. TURBO changes clipping response and increases the amount of later-stage distortion.
- **Research confidence:** High.
