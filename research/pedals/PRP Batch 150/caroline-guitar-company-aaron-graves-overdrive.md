# Caroline Guitar Company Aaron Graves Overdrive

- **Builder:** Caroline Guitar Company
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Fuzz / Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** Aaron Graves Overdrive
- **What this pedal is:** A preamp/power-amp-style drive based on the preamp concept from Caroline's Parabola & Météore platform, expanded with a second gain stage and a recovered Mode B voice.
- **Colorways:** Green and “Baltim-Orange” are documented factory colorways.
- **Versions:** Current production model; no numbered V1/V2 naming was confirmed.
- **Version changes:** Caroline says the pedal brings back the Mode B circuit from the older Haymaker and Icarus V1 and adds a second gain stage. A bass voicing selector was also added for studio, humbucker, and neck-pickup use.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented.
- **Diode type:** Not documented.
- **Controls / switching:** The production pedal has the core drive controls plus a Mode/B voicing switch and a bass voicing selector. Exact knob labels were not exposed in the reviewed current product text, so unverified labels are left out.
- **Power:** Current source does not expose a complete power specification.
- **Sound:** Caroline positions the Aaron Graves as a high-headroom preamp/drive with both clarity and heft, able to function alone or stacked with other drives. The second gain stage lets the two drive styles balance from cleaner preamp-like tones to much gnarlier saturation.
- **Research confidence:** High for architecture, colorways, and stated voicing; medium for exact control labeling and power details.
- **Sources checked:**
  - https://carolineguitar.com/product/graves_drive/
