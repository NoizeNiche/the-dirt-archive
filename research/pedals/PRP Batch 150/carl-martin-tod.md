# Carl Martin TOD

- **Builder:** Carl Martin
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** TOD
- **What this pedal is:** Classic Series high-gain overdrive originally developed as the Limited Edition Dizzy Drive for Dizzy Mizz Lizzy guitarist Tim Christensen.
- **Colorways:** Classic Yellow is the documented production finish. The original Limited Edition Dizzy Drive was the earlier form of the same project.
- **Versions:** The original Limited Edition Dizzy Drive had a run of 500 units for the band's reunion tour; Carl Martin later released the compact Classic Series TOD in yellow.
- **Version changes:** The TOD keeps the core concept but moves it into a compact die-cast enclosure. The official manual describes four knobs: Level, Drive, Tone, and Edge.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented by Carl Martin in the reviewed sources.
- **Diode type:** Not documented by Carl Martin in the reviewed sources.
- **Controls / switching:** Level, Drive, Tone, Edge, and an on/off bypass switch. Tone is a high-cut filter; Edge is a midrange/presence filter. The bypass is buffered.
- **Power:** 9V battery or regulated 9V DC external power; maximum consumption 11 mA.
- **Sound:** High-gain overdrive built around Tim Christensen's early-1990s Turbo Overdrive/Marshall-stack sound, with thick sustain and strong harmonic content. The Drive moves from light crunch to high saturation, while Tone and Edge provide unusually broad control over high-frequency rolloff, mids, and presence.
