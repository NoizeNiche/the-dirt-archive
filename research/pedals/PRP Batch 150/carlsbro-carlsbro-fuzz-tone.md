# Carlsbro Carlsbro Fuzz-Tone

- **Builder:** Carlsbro
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Catalog identity:** Carlsbro Fuzz-Tone
- **What this pedal is:** A short-lived British fuzz made in 1966 by Carlsbro in the Midlands, distinct from the later Sola Sound-built Carlsbro Fuzz.
- **Colorways:** The surviving/period examples documented are blue wedge-shaped metal enclosures.
- **Versions:** 1966 Fuzz-Tone is the early Carlsbro design; it should not be combined with the later three-knob Sola Sound/Colorsound Carlsbro Fuzz.
- **Version changes:** Fuzzboxes.org describes production as short-lived and notes a May 1966 advertisement. The later Carlsbro fuzz was a separate Sola Sound-supplied model.
- **Transistor type:** Three-transistor design is documented. Individual transistor type was not treated as universal without a factory parts source.
- **Diode type:** Not documented.
- **Controls / switching:** The original front/top-panel layout uses Volume and Amplifier controls, with the input/output connections and switching mounted around the wedge enclosure.
- **Power:** 9V operation is documented for the original-style unit; exact original battery compartment details were not independently confirmed.
- **Sound:** The early Fuzz-Tone is not simply a Tone Bender or Maestro Fuzz-Tone clone despite the familiar name. Surviving circuit documentation shows a focused, distinctive fuzz character, and the three-transistor circuit has its own topology rather than being folded into Carlsbro's later Sola Sound design.
- **Research confidence:** High for 1966 identity, enclosure, three-transistor architecture, and separation from the later Carlsbro Fuzz; medium for power/control documentation.
