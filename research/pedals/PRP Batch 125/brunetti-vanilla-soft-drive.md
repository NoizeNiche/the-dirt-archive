# Brunetti — Vanilla - Soft Drive

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Brunetti
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Soft, creamy overdrive developed from the Mercury Box.

## What this pedal is
Vanilla Soft Drive is the milder counterpart to the Mercury Box. Brunetti's modeling documentation describes it as a soft overdrive derived from the Mercury Box, with FET technology intended to reproduce tube characteristics and dynamics. [1]

- FET technology
- Smooth/sensitive control response
- Intended for rhythm and blues through rock applications [1][2]

## Sound
Vanilla is voiced toward smoother and creamier overdrive rather than the harder attack of Brunetti's high-gain products. The documented target is warm, soft drive that can add character without excessive distortion. [1]
