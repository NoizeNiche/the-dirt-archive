# Bruce Bennett Music Labs — Lowball

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Bruce Bennett Music Labs
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** Rare octave-down fuzz/distortion and ring-modulator pedal.

## What this pedal is
Lowball is an extremely rare Bruce Bennett pedal centered on an octave-down divider and aggressive distortion/fuzz behavior. Effects Database records it as an octave-down octaver with ring modulation, and the builder's surviving notes say fewer than 50 units were made. [1]

## Colorways
- Orange finish is documented on a surviving early-00s Reverb example. [2]

## Versions and factory options
- Sensitivity controls input volume
- 15 dB Boost switch
- Octave Off control
- Output volume
- Octave-down operation
- Ring-modulator behavior [1]

## Version changes
The builder's 2006 note describes the same pedal as a very small production run of fewer than 50 units and emphasizes use with low tunings and bass. [1]

## Transistor
Exact active devices are not publicly documented.

## Diode
Exact clipping devices are not publicly documented.

## Sound
Lowball is intentionally extreme: the builder describes it as nasty, glitchy and very loud, with strong behavior on low-tuned guitar and bass. The octave divider creates a deep lower-register effect while the boost can hit an amplifier hard. [1]
