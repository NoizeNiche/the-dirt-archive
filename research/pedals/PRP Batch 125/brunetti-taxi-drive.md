# Brunetti — Taxi Drive

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Brunetti
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Versatile FET-DIODE boost/overdrive.

## What this pedal is
Taxi Drive is a Brunetti boost/overdrive pedal designed to move from discreet, subtle drive to fat, crunchy overdrive. Brunetti's authorized modeling documentation describes it as preserving playing nuances and responding cleanly to lighter attack. [1]

## Sound
Taxi Drive is intended to stay touch-responsive across a wide range of gain. Lower settings work as a restrained boost/drive, while higher Drive settings become fuller and more harmonically rich without losing the instrument's playing nuances. [1]
