# Bruce Bennett Music Labs — Brown Sound

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Bruce Bennett Music Labs
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive
- **Identity:** Boutique Brown Sound drive/distortion pedal.

## What this pedal is
Brown Sound is a boutique drive/distortion pedal associated with Bruce Bennett Music Labs. Historical coverage describes Bennett's design as aiming at Hendrix-era tones built around a touch of fuzz and substantial amplifier distortion. A surviving Reverb example identifies the model as Brown Sound Overdrive and reports rich harmonics and grind. [1][2]

## Colorways

## Sound
Brown Sound is documented as a harmonically rich drive/distortion pedal rather than a simple clean boost. Historical coverage ties its design goal to touch-sensitive rock tones with a fuzz component, while the surviving Reverb example describes rich harmonics and grind. [1][2]
