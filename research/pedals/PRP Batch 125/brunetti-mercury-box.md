# Brunetti — Mercury Box

## PRP identity

- **Builder:** Brunetti
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive
- **Identity:** FET-based stompbox derived from the Mercury amplifier's Channel 2.

## What this pedal is

Mercury Box is Brunetti's overdrive/distortion stompbox derived from Channel 2 of the CustomWork Mercury amplifier. Brunetti introduced it in 2009 and used FET technology to emulate tube behavior and dynamics. [1]

## Colorways

Surviving imagery documents a metallic enclosure.

## Versions and factory options

- Interactive gain/drive and tone controls
- FET technology
- 9V stompbox operation
- Designed around Mercury Channel 2 voicing [1]

No numbered factory revision was verified for the base model.

## Version changes

Mercury Box 2 is a later, separate catalog identity and is not collapsed into the original Mercury Box. Effects Database dates Mercury Box 2 to 2014. [1][2]

## Transistor

- **Technology:** FET.
- Exact factory device part numbers are not publicly documented.

## Diode

Exact production clipping devices are not publicly documented.

## Sound

Mercury Box moves from clear, defined crunch into harder-rock distortion while remaining relatively smooth and articulate at higher gain. Brunetti describes it as preserving the character of the instrument rather than becoming shapeless. [1]
