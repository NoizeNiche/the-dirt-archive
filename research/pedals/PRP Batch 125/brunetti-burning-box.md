# Brunetti — Burning Box

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Brunetti
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** High-gain FET distortion stompbox.

## What this pedal is
Burning Box is a Brunetti high-gain distortion stompbox introduced in 2009. Brunetti describes it as using FET technology to reproduce tube behavior and dynamics, with the pedal aimed at extreme, aggressive and highly compressed distortion. [1]

## Colorways
