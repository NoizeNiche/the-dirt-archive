# Bruno — BOD-1

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Bruno Guitars
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Japanese-made low-to-medium-gain overdrive.

## What this pedal is
BOD-1 is a Bruno Guitars overdrive built around a restrained but deliberate amount of compression. Bruno describes its goal as a superb overdrive with an "exquisite" compression that remains responsive to the input. [1]

## Sound
BOD-1 emphasizes smooth, controlled compression rather than a hard, heavily clipped response. Bruno describes the pedal as dynamically responsive to the input, suggesting an overdrive intended to retain touch rather than flatten picking differences. [1]
