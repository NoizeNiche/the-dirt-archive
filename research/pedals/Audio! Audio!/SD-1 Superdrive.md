# Audio! Audio! — SD-1 Superdrive

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** SD-1 Superdrive
- **Builder:** Audio! Audio!
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive
- **Identity:** Flexible boost/overdrive/distortion pedal derived from a Talons-style starting point and expanded with selectable clipping, EQ and bypassable buffers.

## What this pedal is
Effects Database documents the Superdrive as beginning as a clone of the EarthQuaker Devices Talons, then being substantially modified by Audio! Audio! with additional tone-shaping and clipping options. The pedal can operate as a clean boost, soft-clipping overdrive or hard-clipping distortion.

### Clipping diode options

- MOSFETs
- Silicon 1N4148

### Clipping modes

- Soft Clipping

- **Documented clipping options:** Red LEDs, MOSFETs and silicon 1N4148.

## Sound
The Superdrive is designed to span clean boost, smoother overdrive and harder distortion without changing pedals. Its six-knob EQ/presence section plus selectable clipping devices lets the player move from open and less-compressed clipping toward tighter, more compressed distortion.
