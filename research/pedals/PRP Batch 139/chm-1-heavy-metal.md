# California (by Eleca) - CHM-1 Heavy Metal

- **Builder:** California (by Eleca)
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Primary research sources:** Eleca California CHM-1 product page; Effects Database NAMM 2012 listing; 2016 GS Fanatic used listing for an exact CHM-1.
- **Identity:** Eleca's current California collection and product page identify CHM-1 as a California Guitar Effects Heavy Metal pedal.
- **Colorways:** No verified production colorway list located from the checked manufacturer material.
- **Versions:** No numbered production versions verified.
- **Version changes:** No documented production revision found.
- **Controls:** Pedal switch, Gain, Level, Middle, Mid Freq, High and Low.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented by the checked exact-model sources.
- **Diode type:** Not documented by the checked exact-model sources.
- **Sound description:** The manufacturer specifies a six-knob EQ/gain control layout, while a 2016 owner listing describes the exact CHM-1 as a high-gain mini distortion with a powerful bass control and a warm character. That owner also reports that it can move from metal-oriented sounds toward Santana/Queen-style tones when the highs are restrained and mids are boosted, and that a permanent built-in noise gate was present.
- **Construction / dimensions:** The official Eleca page lists a 60 x 96 x 55 mm enclosure and 0.28 kg weight.
- **Power / electrical notes:** The exact-model owner listing reports battery operation; exact factory current draw and adapter specification were not verified from the official CHM-1 page.
- **Photo status:** The manufacturer page contains exact-model imagery, but no direct image file was promoted into the local archive in this batch.
- **Research confidence:** High for identity, control set and dimensions; medium for the sonic/noise-gate observations because those details come from a historical owner listing rather than factory documentation; low for internal component specifics.
