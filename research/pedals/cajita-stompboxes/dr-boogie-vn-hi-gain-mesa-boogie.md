## PRP identity
- Builder: Cajita Stompboxes
- Catalog type: Distortion

## Research notes
Cajita describes Dr.Boogie VN as high-gain Mesa/Boogie-style distortion using FET transistors to simulate valves. Four-band EQ: HI, MID, LOW, PRESENCIA. Separate red/green GAIN and VOL settings with footswitchable CH selection and bicolor LED. True bypass, mono I/O, 9V center-negative adapter or battery, aluminum enclosure, 12 x 8 cm.
