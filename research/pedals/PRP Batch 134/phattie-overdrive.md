# Byron Amplification — Phattie Overdrive

## PRP identity

- **Builder:** Byron Amplification
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Bass/body-forward overdrive with a strong low-end and midrange character.

## Research

Byron's published material describes the Phattie as having a distinctive mid-range and bass boost. A Harmony Central review quoted on Byron's site describes it as useful both as a boost and for pushing an amp into heavier chugging while retaining low end.

## Research limitation

The reviewed sources do not establish a definitive circuit topology or component list, so those details are not asserted.
