# Byron Amplification — Jabberwocky Distortion

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Byron Amplification
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** Byron's tribute to the classic RAT/“rodent” distortion circuit.

## Circuit
Byron describes the Jabberwocky as an exacting tribute to the original “rodent” distortion pedal and documents a **NOS LM308** op amp. Its modification adds a **Clip** switch with two additional clipping diodes.

## Controls / switching
The documented modification is the Clip switch. Byron describes the pedal as producing fat crunch and late-1970s/early-1980s-style distortion.

## Construction
Handmade in Columbia, Missouri.
