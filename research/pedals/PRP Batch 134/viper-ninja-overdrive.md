# Byron Amplification — Viper Ninja Overdrive

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Byron Amplification
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Original medium-gain overdrive designed as an amp-and-guitar extension rather than a clone of a specific circuit.

## Controls
- Loud = volume
- Dirt = gain
- Phat = bass
- Bright = treble

## Circuit / clipping
Byron describes the clipping section as using MOSFET transistors for a smoother, dynamic response. The company explicitly states that the Viper Ninja is an original design, not based on another pedal.

## Sound
The documented range covers loud clean boost, light drive, and heavy crunch, with good cleanup from the guitar volume control.

## Construction / power
- Standard 9V input.
- True bypass.
- Handmade in Columbia, Missouri.
- Super-bright LED.
