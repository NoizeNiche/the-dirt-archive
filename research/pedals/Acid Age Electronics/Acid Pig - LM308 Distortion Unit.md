# Acid Age Electronics — Acid Pig - LM308 Distortion Unit

## PRP identity
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** LM308 Rat-style distortion with selectable clipping and drive modes.

## What this pedal is
Acid Pig is a boutique LM308-based Rat-style distortion. Premier Guitar documents selectable silicon, silicon+LED and diode-bypassed clipping, plus an overdrive/distortion mode switch. [1]

## Colorways
- Documented finishes include orange and other limited graphic/color treatments.

## Versions / options
- Gain, Tone, Level.
- Three-way clipping selection.
- Overdrive/distortion mode.
- 9V battery or external supply.
- No numbered revision verified.

## Transistor
- Exact transistor/device: **Unknown.**
- LM308 is an op-amp IC, not a transistor.

## Diode
- Silicon, silicon+LED, or diode-bypass clipping.
- Exact silicon diode part: **Unknown.** [1]

## Sound
Acid Pig covers chunky overdrive through saturated Rat-style distortion, with the clipping switch changing compression and output character. The LED-assisted and diode-bypass positions provide louder/rawer alternatives to the classic silicon clipping voice. [1]
