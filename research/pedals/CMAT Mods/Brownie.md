# CMATMODS — Brownie

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Brownie
- **Builder:** CMATMODS
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** CMATMODS Brownie, a licensed BSIAB-based high-gain distortion.

## What this pedal is
Effects Database documents Brownie as an officially licensed **BSIAB (Brown Sound in a Box)** circuit with Gain, Level, and Tone controls. CMATMODS describes it as a high-gain Marshall-style distortion with strong bass, attack, gain, and sustain. [1]

## Colorways
No complete factory colorway sequence was established.

## Versions and factory options
Documented controls/features:
- Gain.
- Level.
- Tone.
- Bright blue LED.
- True bypass.
- Boss/Ibanez-style footprint. [1]

## Version changes
The preserved demo history documents a meaningful FET population change across Brownie runs:
- Older units are shown with **J201** devices.
- Later units are shown with **MPF102**.
- A later high-gain treatment used **2N5457** replacements. [1]

These are documented component-era examples, not assumed to be a single universal factory revision chronology.

## Transistor
The Brownie uses FET gain stages. Surviving documentation identifies **J201, MPF102, and later 2N5457** examples across the production/modified lineage. [1]

## Diode
No exact-model diode specification was established.

## Sound
The Brownie is designed for saturated Marshall-style gain, with strong bass and attack response and very high sustain. [1]
