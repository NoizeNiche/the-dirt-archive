# CMATMODS — Brownie

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** Brownie
- **Builder:** CMATMODS
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** Licensed **BSIAB (Brown Sound in a Box)** high-gain distortion with Gain, Level and Tone controls. [1]

## Controls

- **GAIN**
- **LEVEL**
- **TONE**
- Bright blue LED.
- True bypass. [1]

## Circuit lineage

- **Primary lineage:** BSIAB / Brown Sound in a Box.
- Effects Database explicitly identifies Brownie as an officially licensed BSIAB circuit. [1]

## Transistor / active device

- FET gain stages are documented in the surviving Brownie literature.
- Production examples and revisions include **J201**, **MPF102**, and later **2N5457** examples.
- These devices are retained as documented production/modified examples, not claimed as one universal BOM across every Brownie. [1]

## Sound

Brownie is voiced for saturated Marshall-style high gain, with strong bass/attack, sustain and harmonic density. [1]
