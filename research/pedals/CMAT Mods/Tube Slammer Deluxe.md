# CMATMODS — Tube Slammer Deluxe

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Tube Slammer Deluxe
- **Builder:** CMATMODS
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** CMATMODS Tube Slammer Deluxe, an expanded member of the Tube Slammer overdrive family.

## What this pedal is
Effects Database records Tube Slammer Deluxe as a distinct CMATMODS overdrive model published alongside the original Tube Slammer in 2007. [1]

The surviving exact-model page does not publish a complete control or schematic description. The archive therefore keeps the Deluxe as its own identity without borrowing specifications from the standard Tube Slammer.

## Version changes
Signa Drive was later described by CMATMODS as likely replacing both Tube Slammer and Tube Slammer Deluxe, establishing the Deluxe as a preceding model in the builder's OD lineage. [2]
