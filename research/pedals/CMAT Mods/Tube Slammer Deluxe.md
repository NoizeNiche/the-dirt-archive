# CMATMODS — Tube Slammer Deluxe

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Tube Slammer Deluxe
- **Builder:** CMATMODS
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Distinct 2007-era CMATMODS Tube Slammer-family overdrive that preceded the later Signa Drive. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
Effects Database records Tube Slammer Deluxe as a separate CMATMODS overdrive alongside the standard Tube Slammer in 2007. Later Signa Drive documentation states that Signa Drive replaced the Tube Slammer and Tube Slammer Deluxe, establishing the Deluxe as a predecessor rather than a cosmetic Signa variant. [1][2]
