# CMATMODS — Signa Comp-Drive

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Signa Comp-Drive
- **Builder:** CMATMODS
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive / Compressor
- **Identity:** CMATMODS Signa Comp-Drive, a dual-effect pedal combining Signa Drive overdrive with Signa Comp compression.

## What this pedal is
Effects Database documents Signa Comp-Drive as a new hand-built design combining the builder's Signa Comp and Signa Drive circuits. The result provides an independent compressor side and overdrive side in one enclosure. [1]

## Colorways
No complete factory colorway sequence was established.

## Versions and factory options
Compression side:
- Level.
- Sustain.
- CA3080E-based compressor section. [1]

Overdrive side:
- Tone.
- Level.
- Drive.
- Three-way clipping/gain-structure toggle.
- True bypass on the OD side. [1]

Construction notes include Panasonic poly-film capacitors, 1% metal-film resistors, Alpha pots, military-grade wiring, EH 3PDT switching, and double-sided plated-through PCBs. [1]

## Version changes
No formal numbered factory revision sequence was established.

## Transistor
The documented compressor active device is the **CA3080E** IC. No discrete transistor complement was established. [1]

## Diode
The overdrive clipping choices are selectable, but exact diode part numbers were not securely established.

## Sound
The compression side ranges from moderate sustain to pronounced squish, while the OD side can move from clean boost through Marshall-style crunch. The two sections can be combined for more sustain and a footswitchable boost effect. [1]
