# CMATMODS — Signa Comp-Drive

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Signa Comp-Drive
- **Builder:** CMATMODS
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive / Compressor
- **Identity:** Hand-built combination of the Signa Comp compressor and Signa Drive overdrive circuits in one enclosure. [1]

## What this pedal is
Effects Database documents the Signa Comp-Drive as a dual-function design containing separate compressor and overdrive sections. The compressor side uses a CA3080E, while the overdrive side retains the Signa Drive control and clipping architecture. [1]

## Controls / sections
### Compressor
- **LEVEL**
- **SUSTAIN**
- CA3080E-based compressor. [1]

### Overdrive
- **TONE**
- **LEVEL**
- **DRIVE**
- Three-position clipping/gain-structure switch. [1]

The two sections can be combined, making the compressor capable of feeding the overdrive for greater sustain and density.

## Sound
The compressor section ranges from moderate sustain to pronounced squish. The overdrive section moves from cleaner boost through Marshall-style crunch, and running the two sections together can create more sustained and saturated drive. [1]
