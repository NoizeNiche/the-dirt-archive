## What this pedal is
Droog combines the Cult Overdrive with a momentary feedback loop. External pedals can be inserted into the loop and driven toward controlled self-oscillation with the momentary control.

## Function
- Cult Overdrive section.
- Momentary feedback-loop control.
- External effects can be routed through the loop.
- Intended for controlled oscillation, swells, glitches and resonant textures.
