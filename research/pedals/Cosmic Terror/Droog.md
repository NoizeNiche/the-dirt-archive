## What this pedal is
Droog combines the Cult Overdrive with a momentary feedback looper. The feedback section lets external pedals be placed in a dedicated loop and driven toward self-oscillation with a momentary control.

## Function
- Cult Overdrive section for the main dirt voice.
- Momentary feedback-loop control.
- External effects can be routed through the feedback loop.
- Designed for controllable oscillation, swells, glitches, resonances and other extreme feedback textures.

## Sound
Droog extends the Cult overdrive into a performance-oriented feedback tool, allowing a player to bring external delays, reverbs, modulation and filters into controlled self-oscillation. Its momentary feedback control makes those extreme sounds playable in short bursts rather than requiring a permanent feedback state.
