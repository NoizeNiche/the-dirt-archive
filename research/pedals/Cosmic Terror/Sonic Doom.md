# Cosmic Terror - Sonic Doom

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Cosmic Terror
- **Catalog identity:** Sonic Doom
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Exact Cosmic Terror Sonic Doom.

## What this pedal is
Sonic Doom is an extreme-volume fuzz designed to produce long sustain, strong string articulation and substantial low-end without immediately collapsing into feedback or mush. The current product page documents Version 4.

## Controls
- **Gravity:** fuzz/sustain amount, with lower settings capable of overdrive/distortion territory.
- **Sonority:** overall fuzz character/tone.
- **Focus:** midrange emphasis.
- **Master:** output volume.

## Versions and factory options
- **Version 4:** current documented version, with increased low end, 9V or 18V operation, chemically etched/patina-treated enclosure, black soft-click footswitch, and smooth glossy finish.
- **Version 3:** documented as adding enhanced low end and 9V/18V operation before the current V4 presentation.
- Earlier examples are documented in Reverb listings from 2022/2023. Do not assume their enclosure appearance represents a separate circuit revision without supporting evidence.
- Cosmic Terror produces the Sonic Doom in limited yearly batches.

## Power
- Fully isolated **9V DC center-negative** operation is documented.
- The current V4 page also documents **18V operation** for additional headroom.

## Sound
The Sonic Doom is built around very high output, long sustain, strong articulation and substantial low-end. The Gravity control can move from cleaner drive/distortion into very long fuzz sustain; Sonority and Focus reshape the fuzz character and midrange. Independent coverage also describes the current V4 as retaining clarity and low-frequency power while producing very high volume.
