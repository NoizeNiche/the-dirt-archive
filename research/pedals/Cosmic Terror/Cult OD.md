## What this pedal is
Cult Overdrive is Cosmic Terror's heavy reinterpretation of the Zendrive-style overdrive concept. The builder positions it for low-tuned guitar and bass use, emphasizing substantial low end while retaining definition.

## Construction
- Hand-built in limited runs.
- Chemically etched, black-patina-treated enclosure with a smooth oiled finish.
- Lifetime warranty against normal abuse is stated by the builder.

## Function and response
Cosmic Terror describes the Cult as optimized for lower tunings, with tight lows, prominent mids and warm highs. The builder also emphasizes touch-sensitive operation and response to guitar-volume changes.
