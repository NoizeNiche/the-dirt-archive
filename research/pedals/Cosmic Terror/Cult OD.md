## What this pedal is

Cult OD is Cosmic Terror's heavy reinterpretation of a Zendrive-style overdrive concept. The builder positions it for low-tuned guitar and bass use, emphasizing tight lows, prominent mids and warm highs.

## Sound

Cult OD is a large, saturated overdrive with substantial low-end weight and defined mids. Cosmic Terror specifically presents it as a lower-tuning-oriented drive with strong touch response and guitar-volume interaction.
