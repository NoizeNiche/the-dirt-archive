# Cosmic Terror - Sonic Rat

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Cosmic Terror
- **Catalog identity:** Sonic Rat
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** Exact Cosmic Terror Sonic Rat.

## What this pedal is
Sonic Rat combines a Rat-derived distortion section with the tone-shaping and output architecture of Cosmic Terror's Sonic Doom. Cosmic Terror states that the Rat section is based on a modded Rat using the LM308 chip, with asymmetric gain clipping and extra low-end power.

## Controls
The documented Sonic Doom-derived tone/output section provides:
- **Master:** output volume.
- **Mid:** midrange control.
- **Tone Filter:** tone shaping.
- **Distortion:** distortion/gain amount.

## Power and use
- **9V DC center-negative only.**
- Cosmic Terror documents Sonic Rat for both guitar and bass.

## Versions and factory options
- Cosmic Terror documents small annual production batches.
- A 2025 run was described as limited to 10 pedals.
- Current enclosures are chemically etched, black-patina treated and oiled; individual surface character varies.

- Do not infer other IC/transistor/diode part numbers from this single documented component.

## Sound
Cosmic Terror describes Sonic Rat as a tighter, high-gain distortion with increased low-end, good string separation and articulation, capable of moving into harsh/evil high-gain and Sunn-style drone territory. The Sonic Doom output stage contributes the pedal's unusually high-volume capability.
