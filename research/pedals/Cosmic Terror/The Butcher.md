## What this pedal is
The Butcher is a fuzz circuit tuned for heavy, doom-oriented use. Cosmic Terror describes it as a Fuzz Face-style design with substantially increased low end and loudness, plus a three-way resistor switch that changes the circuit's response.

## Controls and switch
- **Volume / Gain:** master output/gain control.
- **Three-way resistor switch:** changes the fuzz character by altering how the circuit is loaded.
- The current builder description gives three positions:
  - **Up:** more gated, sputtery and lower-gain behavior.
  - **Middle:** balanced, vintage Fuzz Face-style response.
  - **Down:** louder, tighter and more focused response with more gain/clarity.

## Versions and factory options
- The current builder page documents **Version 3**.
- A limited cream-white version is documented from 2022, with only three units reported in that specific finish listing.
- A later **Heavy Meditative Doom** artwork is described as a 20-piece limited enclosure run.
- These documented finish/artwork variants are treated as cosmetic editions unless a circuit change is established.

## Power and intended use
- Requires **9V DC center-negative only**.
- Cosmic Terror specifically recommends the pedal for guitar and notes that it behaves differently on bass.

## Transistor
- The current builder description refers to the circuit starving the transistors in one switch position, but it does not establish exact transistor part numbers.
- Exact transistor type is therefore not established.

## Diode
- No exact diode type or part number was established from the checked sources.

## Sound
The Butcher covers sputtery, gated fuzz at the most starved setting, balanced vintage Fuzz Face-style fuzz in the center, and louder/tighter high-gain doom fuzz in the third position. Cosmic Terror emphasizes sustain, large low end, fast dynamics and interaction with the guitar volume control.

## Construction and provenance
- Made to order in small batches.
- V3 enclosures can be chemically etched and hand-finished; individual surface character varies.
