## What this pedal is
The Butcher is a Fuzz Face-style fuzz tuned for heavy, doom-oriented use, with substantially increased low end and loudness. A three-position resistor switch changes the circuit's loading and response.

## Controls and switch
- **Volume / Gain**
- Three-way resistor switch:
  - **Up:** more gated, sputtery and lower-gain.
  - **Middle:** balanced, vintage Fuzz Face-style response.
  - **Down:** louder, tighter and more focused.

## Versions and factory options
- Current builder documentation identifies **Version 3**.
- Limited artwork/finish runs are treated as cosmetic editions unless an electrical change is documented.

## Power and intended use
- 9V DC center-negative only.
- Cosmic Terror recommends the design primarily for guitar and notes different behavior on bass.

## Sound
The Butcher covers sputtery gated fuzz, balanced Fuzz Face-style fuzz, and louder, tighter high-gain fuzz depending on the resistor-switch position.
