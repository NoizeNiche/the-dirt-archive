# Cosmic Terror - Low Frequency Destroyer

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Cosmic Terror
- **Catalog identity:** Low Frequency Destroyer
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Fuzz

## What this pedal is
Low Frequency Destroyer is a high-gain distortion/fuzz designed around heavy low-frequency use. Cosmic Terror identifies it as its take on the D.A.M. Ezekiel 25:17 circuit lineage, with a clean blend and four selectable clipping modes.

## Controls and clipping
- **Drive:** amount of drive/distortion.
- **Blend:** mixes the affected and clean paths.
- **Filter:** high-frequency/tone shaping.
- **Level:** output volume.
- **Voice:** four-way clipping selector.
- The documented Voice positions include germanium, silicon-diode, LED, and op-amp clipping approaches.

## Versions and factory options
- Current product listings document separate **Black Knob** and **Silver Knob** versions for a production batch.
- Cosmic Terror states the effect is made in tiny limited batches.
- Enclosure finishes are chemically etched/patina-treated on documented examples; these are cosmetic options unless a separate circuit revision is established.

## Version changes
No full historical revision matrix was established in the checked sources. The current product page documents batch/color differences rather than a separate named circuit revision.

## Transistor
- Exact transistor part numbers were not established from the checked sources.
## Diode
- The clipping selector explicitly documents silicon-diode and LED modes, but exact diode part numbers were not established.
- The germanium mode is documented as a germanium clipping voice without a specific semiconductor part number.

## Sound
The Low Frequency Destroyer is voiced for deep, authoritative distortion while preserving the fundamental through its clean blend. Cosmic Terror describes a range from tight drive to aggressive speaker-pushing distortion, with each clipping position changing the character, headroom and perceived strength.

## Construction and provenance
- Cosmic Terror states that the pedal is handcrafted in limited runs.
- Current product information lists 9V center-negative power.
