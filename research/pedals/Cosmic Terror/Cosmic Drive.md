# Cosmic Terror - Cosmic Drive

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Cosmic Terror
- **Catalog identity:** Cosmic Drive
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive / Preamp
- **Identity:** Exact Cosmic Terror Cosmic Drive.

## What this pedal is
Cosmic Drive is an overdrive / preamp based on the Matamp GT 120, with additional tone-shaping and drive-staging changes. Builder-supplied text reproduced in exact-model listings describes four drive stages that can stack for dynamic, amp-like saturation.

## Controls and internal adjustments
- **Gain:** primary drive control.
- **Master:** output level.
- **Bass / Treble:** tone-stack controls.
- **F.A.C. (Frequency Attenuation Control):** adjusts frequency attenuation for different guitar/bass tunings and is intended to keep low-tuned signals focused.
- **Boost:** an additional boost section that can be engaged with the drive.
- Two internal PCB trimmers are documented on early/2022 examples:
  - one adjusts added midrange.
  - one adjusts the overall feedback range.

## Versions and factory options
- Exact-model listings document **2022** production examples, including an early **v0** listing.
- Finish examples include black, black/white, and black/purple presentations. Finish is treated as cosmetic rather than a separate circuit model unless a distinct circuit revision is documented.
- Cosmic Terror states that the Cosmic Drive is produced in small batches, approximately once or twice a year.

## Version changes
The checked sources establish an early/v0 2022 example and later 2022/2024-era examples, but do not provide a complete revision history. Do not infer circuit changes solely from enclosure finish or listing date.

## Sound
The builder describes a warm, growly vintage overdrive that can cover classic low-to-medium gain sounds and heavier doom-oriented drive. The multi-stage drive architecture, low-tuning F.A.C., boost, and internal feedback control allow the pedal to move from focused overdrive toward thick, highly sustained and more aggressive textures.

## Construction and provenance
- Cosmic Terror production is associated with Germany/Dortmund in the checked listings.
- Small-batch construction and chemically etched/patina enclosure finishes are documented on later examples.
