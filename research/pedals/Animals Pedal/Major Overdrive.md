## What this pedal is
Major Overdrive is an Animals Pedal / Skreddy Pedals collaboration designed by Marc Ahlfs to deliver British plexi and late-1970s brown-sound-style overdrive. [1]

## Versions and factory options
- VOL, TONE and GAIN controls.
- True bypass.
- 9V battery or standard center-negative 9V adapter. [1]

## Version changes
- The current version has updated footswitch placement, artwork and circuit refinement for a wider range of guitars and amplifiers. [1]

## Sound
Major Overdrive ranges from lower-gain plexi-style crunch to more compressed, sustaining brown-sound distortion. The builder emphasizes touch response, harmonic detail and the ability to clean up through guitar volume and picking dynamics. [1]
