# Animals Pedal — In Oct,3 Foxes talking of dreamy FUZZ

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** In Oct,3 Foxes talking of dreamy FUZZ
- **Builder:** Animals Pedal
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Octave-up fuzz based on the intense “velvet” style of 1970s octave fuzz, refined for easier operation while retaining the underlying octave-fuzz character.

## What this pedal is
In Oct,3 Foxes talking of dreamy FUZZ is an octave-up fuzz that combines a standard silicon-based fuzz voice with a switchable upper-octave circuit. Animals Pedal says the design keeps the core sound of 1970s octave fuzzes while improving usability compared with some historically difficult vintage implementations. [1][2]

## Controls
- **LEVEL:** overall output volume.
- **SUSTAIN:** strength/amount of distortion and sustain.
- **TONE:** high-frequency-centered tone control.
- **OCT:** switches the octave-up section on or off. [1][3]

## Circuit lineage
- **Primary lineage:** 1970s “velvet” style octave fuzz.
- Animals Pedal describes the pedal as preserving the basic sound of 1970s octave fuzz while refining operation. [1]
- Independent coverage places it in the same general family as vintage West Coast-style octave fuzzes and notes the switchable octave as a modern usability refinement. A specific original circuit such as the Foxx Tone Machine is sometimes suggested by secondary sources, but the manufacturer does not make that exact circuit attribution, so the archive does not assign one. [4]

## Octave behavior
- The **OCT** switch activates the upper-octave voice.
- Animals Pedal describes the octave as capable of intense, high-register sounds, especially with neck pickup, higher fret positions, and a rolled-back guitar tone control. [1]
- The standard fuzz remains available with the octave switched off. [1][2]

## Power / electrical specifications
- **Supply:** 9V battery or standard **center-negative DC 9V** adapter.
- **Current draw:** **1mA**.
- **Input impedance:** **500kΩ**.
- **Output impedance:** **8.2kΩ**.
- **Switching:** true bypass.
- **Dimensions:** approximately 64 × 112 × 50mm including protrusions.
- **Weight:** approximately 384g. [1][3]
- Battery storage guidance requires unplugging the input jack to avoid battery drain. [1]

## Version / edition history
- The documented production version is associated with **October 2021** product documentation.
- Animals Pedal later produced **Custom Illustrated** editions of the same design. Those are treated as cosmetic/edition variants unless the source establishes a circuit change. [1][5]
- No numbered electronic revision was established from the reviewed sources.

## Sound
With OCT off, the pedal provides a recognizable 1970s-style silicon fuzz voice. Engaging OCT adds the pronounced upper-octave character associated with vintage octave fuzz. Animals Pedal emphasizes that the revised design is more stable and easier to operate than the original-style vintage implementations while retaining the unusual octave-fuzz response. [1][2]
