## What this pedal is
This is Animals Pedal's octave-up fuzz based on the intense octave-fuzz designs that emerged in the late 1960s and evolved through 1970s silicon octave fuzzes. [1]

## Versions and factory options
- LEVEL, SUSTAIN and TONE controls.
- OCT switch activates the octave-up effect.
- True bypass. [1]

## Version changes
- Animals Pedal describes the design as retaining the basic vintage octave-fuzz sound while refining operation for modern use.
- Custom illustrated editions exist, but are treated as cosmetic editions rather than separate circuit models. [1][2]

## Transistor

## Sound
With OCT off, the pedal supplies a stable 1970s silicon-fuzz flavor. Engaging OCT produces the characteristic intense octave-up response, especially on higher single-note lines and the neck pickup. [1]
