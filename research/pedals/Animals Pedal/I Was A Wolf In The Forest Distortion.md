## What this pedal is
I Was A Wolf In The Forest Distortion is a British-stack-style distortion voiced around the thick, warm and edgy character of large UK amplifier rigs. Animals Pedal dates its original release to 2015. [1][2]

## Sound
The pedal targets British high-gain distortion while retaining lower-gain settings that move toward crunch and overdrive. Independent owner reporting also describes a JCM800-like character, but that is treated as user experience rather than a builder-stated circuit identity. [1][3]
