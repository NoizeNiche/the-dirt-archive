## What this pedal is
Diamond Peak Hybrid Overdrive is a lower-gain, higher-headroom overdrive based on the Animals Pedal Major Overdrive platform. [1]

## Versions and factory options
- VOL, TONE and GAIN controls.
- Current version moved the footswitch to the front-right area and includes updated artwork plus circuit refinement. [1]

## Version changes

## Sound
The circuit reduces gain and expands headroom relative to Major Overdrive, aiming for a light-gain, transparent-to-crunch response with strong note separation. The TONE control can darken the upper range for brighter rigs. [1]
