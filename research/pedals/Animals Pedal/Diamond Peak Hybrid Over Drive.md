# Animals Pedal — Diamond Peak Hybrid Over Drive

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Diamond Peak Hybrid Over Drive
- **Builder:** Animals Pedal
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Collaboration:** Animals Pedal × Skreddy Pedals
- **Identity:** Low-gain British/plexi-style overdrive designed by Marc Ahlfs of Skreddy Pedals and based on the Skreddy Major Overdrive, with reduced gain and increased headroom. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
Diamond Peak Hybrid Over Drive is a low-gain overdrive focused on the “edge of breakup” character of 1960s–1970s British tube amplifiers. Animals Pedal and independent database coverage identify it as a Skreddy collaboration based on the Major Overdrive, with the gain reduced and headroom expanded for a more open, less compressed response. [1][2]

## Versions and factory options
- The **latest version** moved the footswitch to the front-right position and made battery access easier.
- The enclosure artwork was upgraded and the circuit was fine-tuned/expanded for a wider range of guitars and amplifiers.
- No separate numbered electronic revision was established beyond this documented latest-version change. [3]
- Custom Illustrated editions exist, including an over print / Gyoshi collaboration, but these are treated as cosmetic/edition variants rather than a separate circuit family unless the source establishes a circuit change. [4]

## Controls
- **VOL:** overall output volume.
- **TONE:** balances the frequency response.
- **GAIN:** amount of overdrive.
- **Footswitch:** true bypass. [1]

## Transistor / circuit technology
- The pedal is an **analog** overdrive.
- The design is based on the Skreddy Major Overdrive.
- Independent schematic/layout documentation identifies **Q1 as 2N5210** and **Q2/Q3 as 2N5172** in a published Diamond Peak circuit reference. These part numbers are retained as supporting circuit evidence rather than presented as factory-verified BOM data. [5]
- The reviewed sources do not establish a single transistor material classification such as germanium or silicon from the manufacturer's published product specification, so the archive does not force one.

## Diode / clipping

## Power / electrical specifications
Animals Pedal's product documentation specifies:
- **Supply:** 9V DC, center-negative, or 9V battery.
- **Current draw:** approximately **1mA**.
- **Input impedance:** **220kΩ**.
- **Output impedance:** **10kΩ**.
- **Switching:** true bypass.
- **Dimensions:** approximately 64 × 112 × 50mm including protrusions.
- **Weight:** approximately 383g. [1]
- A current Animals Pedal US listing also documents the same 9V/battery power arrangement. [6]

## Sound
The pedal is voiced for a low-gain British/plexi-style drive that sits between clean and full distortion. The design emphasizes harmonic clarity, pick response, midrange presence, and usable headroom rather than maximum saturation. Independent documentation describes it as a mild cranked-amp voice derived from the Skreddy Major Overdrive with less gain and more headroom. [2][3]

## Version / edition history
- Original Diamond Peak established the low-gain British overdrive platform.
- A later/latest version improved footswitch placement and battery accessibility, upgraded the artwork, and fine-tuned/expanded the circuit for broader instrument/amplifier compatibility. [3]
- Custom Illustrated editions represent collaborative artwork/limited visual editions; the reviewed source does not establish a distinct core circuit change. [4]
