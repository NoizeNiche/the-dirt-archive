## What this pedal is
Alien Tone Dragon combines a clean boost, a Dragon Fire overdrive/fuzz circuit and a three-band active tone circuit. The builder specifies 1–18 dB of boost, In/Out/Bias controls for Dragon Fire, and separate Low/Mid/High controls with independent cut/boost capability. [1][2]

## Sound
Dragon Fire can travel from warm, expressive rock/blues overdrive into a dense fuzz wall by changing its In, Out and Bias controls. The independent active EQ can then reshape the result without overlapping bands, while the clean boost can be used on its own or to push the Dragon Fire and the rest of a pedalboard. [1][3]
