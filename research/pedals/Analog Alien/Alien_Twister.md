## What this pedal is
Alien Twister is an all-analog fuzz, distortion and overdrive pedal inspired by the Diaz Texas Square Face, with three primary controls: Input, Output and Stab. It also includes a footswitchable buffer designed to preserve signal strength through long cables and complex pedal chains. [1][2][3]

## Sound
Alien Twister can move from thick vintage fuzz through defined distortion into expressive overdrive using only Input, Output and Stab. Lower Stab settings reduce voltage at the final transistor stage and make the sound more splattery and less defined, while higher settings increase sustain and articulation; the buffer adds flexibility without becoming a separate dirt mode. [1][2]
