# Analog Alien — Alien Bass Station

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Alien Bass Station
- **Builder:** Analog Alien
- **Catalog types:** Distortion / Fuzz / Overdrive-related multi-effect
- **Introduction/shipping:** 2014
- **Identity:** One parent multi-effects pedal containing three independently footswitchable sections. The builder also abbreviates it as ABS.

## What this pedal is
The Alien Bass Station combines a Limiter/Compressor, Amp Generator, and Gamma Fuzz in one all-analog enclosure. It was designed specifically for bass, while Analog Alien notes that it can also produce a different set of sounds with guitar. [1][2]

## Versions and factory modifications
### Alien Bass Station — original production
- Limiter/Compressor with Limit-Comp, Sensitivity and Out
- Amp Generator with Bass, Treble, Gain and Out
- Gamma Fuzz with In and Out
- independent footswitches for all three sections
- true bypass
- 100% analog circuitry
- external 9V supply only; no battery operation [2][3]

## Sound
The Alien Bass Station is essentially a compact bass signal-processing chain: compression controls dynamics, the Amp Generator adds a vintage tube-bass-amp character, and Gamma Fuzz becomes more pronounced as stronger and lower-frequency content reaches it. The sections can run independently or together, covering controlled clean bass, amp-like grit and unusually low-frequency-heavy fuzz. [1][3]
