- **Identity:** Dual-circuit parent containing separate Pete/OD and Jimi/Fuzz sections.

## What this pedal is
FuzzBubble-45 was Analog Alien's first pedal and combines two independent dirt circuits in one enclosure. The Pete side provides vintage-style overdrive, while the Jimi side is a Fuzz Face-inspired fuzz; the two sections were intentionally designed so they could be used independently or together. ## Sound
The Pete side moves from light, touch-sensitive vintage overdrive into richer distortion as the Year control advances, while the Jimi side ranges from chunky fuzz to dense, saturated textures through its Input and Haze controls. Because the two circuits can be stacked, the pedal can cover clean-ish grit, crunch, fuzz and lead textures while remaining strongly responsive to the guitar's volume control. [1][2][4]
