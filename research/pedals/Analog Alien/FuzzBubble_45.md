# Analog Alien — FuzzBubble-45

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** FuzzBubble-45
- **Builder:** Analog Alien
- **Catalog types:** Distortion / Fuzz / Overdrive
- **Introduction:** 2010
- **Identity:** Dual-circuit parent containing separate Pete/OD and Jimi/Fuzz sections.

## What this pedal is
FuzzBubble-45 was Analog Alien's first pedal and combines two independent dirt circuits. The Pete side provides vintage-style overdrive and the Jimi side is a Fuzz Face-inspired fuzz; the sections can be used independently or together.

## Sound
The Pete section moves from light vintage overdrive into richer saturation, while the Jimi section provides chunky to dense fuzz. Stacking the two expands the range from grit and crunch into heavier fuzz and lead textures.
