# Analog Alien — Bucket Seat

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** Bucket Seat
- **Builder:** Analog Alien
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Introduction:** 2015–2016 product generation
- **Identity:** Separate parent from Rumble Seat, despite using the same Rumble Drive circuit.

## What this pedal is

Bucket Seat is Analog Alien's single-effect version of the Rumble Drive circuit. Analog Alien describes the circuit as targeting the feel of a vintage 1969 Marshall Plexi and emphasizing touch-sensitive overdrive.

## Sound

Bucket Seat covers light grit through saturated overdrive while retaining strong pick and guitar-volume sensitivity. Its voicing is intended to provide cranked British-style amp character.
