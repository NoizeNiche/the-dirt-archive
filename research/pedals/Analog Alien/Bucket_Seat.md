## What this pedal is
Bucket Seat is Analog Alien's first single-effect unit and uses the same Rumble Drive circuit found in the larger Rumble Seat. Analog Alien describes that circuit as simulating a vintage 1969 Marshall Plexi, with Output, Tone and Gain controls and a strong emphasis on touch-sensitive overdrive. [1][2]

## Sound
Bucket Seat covers light grit through saturated, harmonically rich overdrive while retaining a strong sense of pick and volume-control dynamics. Its tone control can add body or sharpen the attack depending on the guitar and amplifier, and the circuit becomes increasingly complex and harmonically dense as Gain is pushed higher. [1][4]
