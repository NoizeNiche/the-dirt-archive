## What this pedal is

Effects Database's October 17, 2011 weekly update lists **D&M** as a newly added brand and **D&M Distortion** as one of the newly added effects. A separate 2008 FreeStompBoxes repair discussion is explicitly titled **D&M - Distortion** and documents photographs of the outside, inside, and schematic.

## Historical / construction evidence

In the 2008 FreeStompBoxes discussion, forum member **analogguru** examined the photographed unit and reported:
- a TI 741 integrated circuit dated to 1979,
- Radiohm potentiometers associated with the 1980s,
- Siemens MKT film capacitors from the late 1970s or early 1980s,
- a Philips electrolytic,
- Piher and probably Vitrohm resistors,
- **AA119 germanium diodes**,
- Japanese jacks and footswitches described as typical of 1970s equipment.

The same post concludes that the unit was neither US- nor Japan-made and was **most probably made in England in the early 1980s**, based on the observed construction. That geographic/date conclusion is retained as the forum analyst's assessment, not as an independently established factory record.

## Controls / circuit

The surviving discussion includes a schematic reference and reports a **741-based circuit with AA119 germanium diodes** on the photographed unit. The archive does not reconstruct or republish a schematic that it could not independently inspect.
- **Reported active device:** TI 741.
- **Reported clipping device:** AA119 germanium diodes.
- **Exact component values:** Not independently verified in this pass.
- **Complete circuit topology:** Not independently reconstructed.

## Sound

The only direct sound comment located in the surviving repair discussion is the owner's brief subjective assessment that the unit was not sonically remarkable. The archive does not elevate that personal opinion into a product-level tonal characterization.
