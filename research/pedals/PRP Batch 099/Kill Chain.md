# Black Mass Electronics - Kill Chain

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Black Mass Electronics
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz

## Research
Kill Chain is an aggressive fuzz designed around a deliberately simple control set: Level plus Bleed and Depth toggles. A fixed output filter avoids a conventional tone control, Depth selects how much low end enters the fuzz, and Bleed changes treble attenuation, compression and output level. Black Mass also designed the input to tolerate buffers, active pickups and different pedal-order positions, using a pickup simulator and input filtering before the fuzz circuit.
