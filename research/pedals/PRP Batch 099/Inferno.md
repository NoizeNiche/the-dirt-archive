# Blackbox Music Electronics - Inferno

## PRP identity

- **Builder:** Blackbox Music Electronics
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz

## Research

The Inferno is a full-range hybrid germanium/silicon fuzz designed to retain bass and low mids that many fuzz circuits discard. Hand-selected transistors combine germanium warmth with silicon gain, and the circuit responds strongly to picking strength and the instrument's volume control. The three controls, Smoke, Fire and Heat, cover input/fuzz level, output and additional gain/compression, allowing the pedal to move from mild overdrive through dense saturated fuzz.

## Sound

All components are hand selected and auditioned for the best sound resulting in the warmth of vintage germanium transistors combined with the high gain of silicon.
A lot of distortion/fuzz units cut a significant amount of bass and low mids out of your tone, giving at best, a thin sound or at worst, that lovely fingernails on chalkboard tone.
The Inferno is very responsive to picking strength and to the volume knob on your guitar or instrument and will give a range of tones from mild overdrive to full on sick fuzz.
