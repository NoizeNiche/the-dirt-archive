## Research

The Dual 1312 packages two 1312 distortion circuits in one enclosure, allowing independent channel use, stacking and routing between the two stages. Each 1312 uses the eight-way clipping architecture of the V3, with the same RAT-derived foundation and internal headroom strategy. The dual format is aimed at creating layered distortion and flexible A/B or stacked sounds while saving pedalboard space.
