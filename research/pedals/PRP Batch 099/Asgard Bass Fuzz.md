# Blackhawk Amplifiers - Asgard Bass Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Blackhawk Amplifiers
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz

## Research
The Åsgard Bass Fuzz is a cascaded-transistor high-gain fuzz that can move from heavy overdrive to large, saturated fuzz. Its control set includes volume, gain, tone/low-pass, clean blend and a defeatable notch filter for a large mid boost, with diode/MOSFET clipping options and a small amount of gating in the second gain stage. Blackhawk redesigned the Åsgard as V2 after oscillation issues, using a new layout, double copper planes and shielded cables.
