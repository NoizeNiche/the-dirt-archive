## PRP identity

## Research
The Ceremonial Show of Force is an all-analog discrete overdrive built from cascading transistor gain stages rather than opamps or diode clipping. Its Level, Drive, Tone, Body and Bias controls move from pushed-clean and vintage textures to thicker modern saturation, with Body controlling low-end entry and Bias shifting the gain-stage character. Black Mass also specifies an internally boosted input voltage, JFET input buffer, transformer isolation, relay true bypass and overvoltage protection.
