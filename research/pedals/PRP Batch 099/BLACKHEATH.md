# Black Country Customs - BLACKHEATH

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Black Country Customs / Laney
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive

## Research
BLACKHEATH is a bass-focused distortion pedal in Laney's Black Country Customs line with three modes: DIST 1, DIST 2 and OVERDRIVE. Gain, Volume, Range and Colour controls cover the gain, low-frequency range and high-frequency character, while a three-colour indicator shows the selected mode. The design is fully buffered and uses silent switching.
