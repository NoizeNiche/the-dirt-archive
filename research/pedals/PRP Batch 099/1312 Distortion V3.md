# Black Mass Electronics - 1312 Distortion V3

## PRP identity

- **Builder:** Black Mass Electronics
- **Catalog type:** Distortion

## Research

The 1312 Distortion V3 is a RAT-family distortion with an eight-position clipping selector covering vintage germanium, LEDs, silicon, MOSFET, mixed and asymmetric combinations. V3 adds an internal charge pump for 18V headroom from a 9V supply and retains Level, Filter and Gain controls. Black Mass describes the 1312 as a project tied to Black Lives Matter and, in this iteration, Trans Rights are Human Rights, with half of profits allocated among supported organizations.
