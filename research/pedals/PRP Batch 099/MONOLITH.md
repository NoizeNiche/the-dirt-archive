# Black Country Customs - MONOLITH

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Black Country Customs / Laney
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive

## Research
MONOLITH is an analog three-mode distortion covering heavily compressed D1, more expressive D2 and lower-gain Overdrive territory. Distortion, Range, Volume and Tone controls shape gain, low-end filtering, output and treble response, with the circuit designed to remain articulate under hard picking and complex chords. The pedal uses a three-position mode switch and silent switching.
