# Black Mass Electronics - The First Herald (Big Box)

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Black Mass Electronics
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz

## Research
The First Herald (Big Box) is Black Mass's modernized take on the 1973 Rams Head Big Muff idea, expanded into two independent, reversible circuits. The fuzz side adds a modern tone stack, Scoop control and three clipping modes: MOSFET/LED hybrid, silicon, or diode bypass. A separate discrete boost can run before or after the fuzz, allowing thicker saturation before the fuzz or a louder amp-driving boost after it.
