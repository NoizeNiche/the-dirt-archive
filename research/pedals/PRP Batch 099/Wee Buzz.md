# Black Cat Pedals - Wee Buzz

## PRP identity

- **Builder:** Black Cat Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz

## Research

The Wee Buzz is a compact, simplified version of the Black Cat Bee Buzz, both based on the Roland Bee Baa AF-100. It removes the Bee Buzz's boost channel and hardwires the fuzz fully on, while using a higher-gain first-stage transistor for slightly more edge. The circuit keeps the Bee Baa's two switchable tone settings and was built with true-bypass switching, hand-wiring and 9V battery or Boss-style power capability.
