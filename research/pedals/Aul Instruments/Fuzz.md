# Aul Instruments — Fuzz

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** Fuzz
- **Builder:** Aul Instruments
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Very early fuzz boxes made by Aul Instruments for Mike Matthews in 1967, before Electro-Harmonix, closely derived from the Mosrite Fuzzrite family and associated historically with the Guild Foxey Lady replacement program. [1][2]

## What this is

Effects Database places the Aul fuzzes in the early history of Mike Matthews' pre-Electro-Harmonix work. Fuzzboxes documents the surviving Aul examples as close to the early Fuzzrite design, including the characteristic compact folded-metal construction. [1][2]

## Historical context

- Produced around **1967**.
- Built for Mike Matthews before the Electro-Harmonix brand launched.
- Connected to Guild's replacement for the Foxey Lady after Mosrite's bankruptcy.
- Related Aul-built examples later appeared under the **Guild Foxey Lady** name. [1][3]

## Controls / hardware

- Surviving Aul/Guild-family documentation supports a simple vintage fuzz control arrangement.
- The Guild Foxey Lady AUL version is documented with **Volume** and **Tone**, with the Volume control also acting as the switching control. [3]
- Exact control layouts are not fully preserved for every unbranded Aul example.

## Circuit lineage

- **Primary lineage:** Mosrite Fuzzrite.
- The surviving evidence supports a close Fuzzrite-derived design rather than an unrelated original topology. [2]

## Transistor

- **Technology:** Silicon.
- Surviving Aul examples are documented with unmarked metal-can silicon transistors.
- Exact transistor part numbers are not established.

## Diode / clipping

- Exact clipping diode/device is not publicly documented.

## Construction / finish

- **Greenish-grey wrinkle finish** is documented on surviving Aul examples.
- Folded metal enclosure.
- Some units have Manny's stickers reflecting period distribution. [2]

## Version history

- The important historical distinction is the transition from Aul Instruments production to the later Guild/Electro-Harmonix Foxey Lady lineage.
- No numbered Aul hardware revision sequence is established.

## Power

- The reviewed historical sources point to vintage **battery-powered** operation, but do not preserve a complete standardized electrical specification for every surviving example.

## Sound

The Aul design belongs to the early Fuzzrite sound family, with a direct two-transistor silicon fuzz character. Exact response can vary between surviving examples because individual component identities and bias conditions are not fully documented. [1][2]

## Deep research verification

Effects Database, Fuzzboxes and the historical Guild Foxey Lady record were cross-checked. The evidence supports Aul Instruments as an early Mike Matthews production source, establishes the close Fuzzrite lineage and preserves the greenish-grey wrinkle-finish/silicon-transistor context. The archive avoids assigning exact transistor part numbers where the surviving evidence does not identify them. [1][2][3]
