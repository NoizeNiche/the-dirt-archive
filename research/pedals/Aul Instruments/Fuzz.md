# Aul Instruments — Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Fuzz
- **Builder:** Aul Instruments
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Very early Mike Matthews-era fuzz boxes made by Aul Instruments, derived from the Fuzzrite family and supplied before the launch of Electro-Harmonix.

## What this pedal is
Effects Database records Aul Instruments fuzzes as being made for Mike Matthews in 1967, before Electro-Harmonix, in connection with a Guild replacement for the Foxey Lady after Mosrite's bankruptcy. Fuzzboxes documents surviving Aul fuzzes as greenish-grey wrinkle-finish boxes based closely on the early Fuzzrite design.

## Colorways
- **Documented finish:** greenish-grey wrinkle paint.
- Some surviving units carry Manny's stickers, reflecting distribution through Manny's.
- Units supplied to Guild appeared under the Foxey Lady name.

## Versions and factory options
### Aul Instruments fuzz
- Very early production before Electro-Harmonix
- Fuzzrite-derived design
- Folded metal enclosure
- Battery-powered vintage construction
- Surviving unbranded Aul examples are associated with the same family of circuit later sold as Guild Foxey Lady

### Related Guild Foxey Lady AUL version
Archival research on the AUL-built Guild version documents Volume and Tone controls, with Volume also functioning as a switching pot.

## Version changes
The important historical change is lineage rather than an internal numbered revision: Aul-built fuzzes preceded the later Electro-Harmonix Guild Foxey Lady versions. The later EHX models retained the basic Fuzzrite-derived concept while changing enclosure aesthetics and construction.

## Transistor
- **Technology:** Silicon.
- **Exact devices:** Surviving Aul fuzzes are documented with unmarked metal-can silicon transistors.

## Diode
- **Type:** Exact clipping-diode arrangement is not publicly documented.

## Sound
Aul fuzzes are closely tied to the early Fuzzrite sound family, with a relatively direct two-transistor vintage fuzz character. The surviving evidence supports a Fuzzrite-derived identity, while the exact component-level voicing of an individual Aul example can vary with the undocumented parts.
