## What this pedal is
The 112+ is a high-voltage preamp/distortion pedal based on a clipping scheme found in early-1990s solid-state amplifiers. Demedash describes multiple clipping thresholds followed by smoothing of the sharp edges.

## Colorways

## Versions and factory options
- Three gain modes.
- Fully active three-band EQ.
- Internal voltage/headroom architecture reaches approximately 32V.
- 9V DC external supply.
- The checked sources reference **V2**.

## Version changes
- V2 is treated as a documented revision reference.
- The pedal is discontinued.

## Sound
The 112+ provides high-headroom distortion with multiple gain structures and broad active EQ. It is designed to stack with other drives or feed an amplifier's drive channel.
