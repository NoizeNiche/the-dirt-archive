# Case Study Effects - Aspen Overdrive

## PRP identity
- Builder: Case Study Effects
- Catalog type: Overdrive / Distortion / Fuzz
- Identity: Aspen Overdrive, the four-mode dirt pedal from Case Study Effects.

## What this pedal is
Aspen Overdrive combines four dirt modes: Crunch, Overdrive, Distortion and Fuzz. It reworks an Expandora / Kilt-style concept with improved post-drive volume and a Bass control.

## Controls / switching
- Gain
- Tone
- Bass
- Toggle switches select four dirt modes: Crunch, Overdrive, Distortion, Fuzz.
