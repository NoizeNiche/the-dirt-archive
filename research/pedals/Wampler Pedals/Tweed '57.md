## Sound

With their warm and woolly sound and pushed volume tube break-up, the tone of tweed amps have found their way on to numerous historic recordings of some legendary artists such as Keith Richards, Eric Clapton, Neil Young, Billy Gibbons, and Joe Bonamassa.
Responsive Touch With a 3 band eq similar to a vintage tweed amp’s, you’re able to sculpt the tonality, feel, compression and overall gain structure just like you would on the real tube amplifier it’s emulating.
Normal, Bright, and Linked input options cooperate with EQ and gain adjustments just like “jumpering inputs” on a vintage Bassman style amp.
