## Sound
The Dual Fusion pedal from Wampler Pedals is, we think, the first overdrive pedal specifically voiced to the new breed of fusion guitarists – but it’s so versatile it can find a place on ANY board of ANY genre.
The Wampler Pedals Dual Fusion is a versatile overdrive pedal designed in collaboration with guitarist Tom Quayle, specifically for fusion music.
It offers two independent circuits, based on the Euphoria and Paisley Drive , providing a wide range of tones suitable for various genres.
