## What this pedal is
The original Wampler Plextortion is a British-voiced distortion pedal designed around the character of hot-rodded JCM800-style amplifier tones. Wampler's historical demo material describes the design as intended to emulate a JCM800 with Celestion Greenbacks.

## Sound
The Plextortion is voiced toward hot-rodded British amplifier distortion, with a tighter, more preamp-like character than a looser power-amp-style distortion. Its design target is the JCM800 family of sounds rather than the later Super Plextortion's separate multi-structure design.
