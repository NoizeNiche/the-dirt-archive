## Sound
The Wampler Hot Wired v2 is a versatile overdrive and distortion pedal designed in collaboration with Brent Mason, offering a wide range of tones.
It provides two channels: Channel 1 delivers a warm overdrive, while Channel 2 offers a 'British Plexi' flavored distortion.
This two-in-one pedal allows for diverse sounds, from gentle breakup to full crunch and fat, creamy distortion.
