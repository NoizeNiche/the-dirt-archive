## Sound
Gearbox features modified versions of two of Brian Wampler’s best loved drive circuits: the Tumnus and the Pinnacle.
The Wampler Gearbox is a signature overdrive pedal designed in collaboration with guitarist Andy Wood.
Channel 1 is inspired by the Tumnus, offering a boost or light overdrive, while Channel 2, based on the Pinnacle distortion, delivers a 'Brown Sound' with a three-band EQ and noise gate.
