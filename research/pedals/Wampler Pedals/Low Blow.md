## Sound
By liam-carter June 13, 2026 The Wampler Low Blow is a high-headroom, transparent bass-boost pedal designed for players who need low-end reinforcement without muddiness or loss of clarity—especially guitarists using single-coils, low-output pickups, or extended-range instruments, and bassists seeking subtle foundational lift rather than distortion or overdrive.
It delivers a focused +12 dB boost centered at 80 Hz with fully variable control over frequency (40–160 Hz), gain (+0 to +12 dB), and blend (0–100% wet/dry).
Unlike many ‘bass boosters,’ it preserves transients and articulation, making it viable for clean rhythm work, dynamic lead passages, and studio layering.
