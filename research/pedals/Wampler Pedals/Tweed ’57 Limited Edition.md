## Sound
The Wampler Tweed '57 is a limited-edition overdrive pedal designed to emulate the warm, woolly tones of classic tweed-style amplifiers, celebrated by blues, country, and rock musicians.
With their warm and woolly sound and pushed volume tube break-up, the tone of tweed amps have found their way on to numerous historic recordings of some legendary artists such as Keith Richards, Eric Clapton, Neil Young, Billy Gibbons, and Joe Bonamassa.
Guitarists were impressed with the Wampler Tweed '57's sound quality and versatility, appreciating the ease of achieving the desired 'chewy, gooey, woolly' tones.
