## Sound
The Wampler Moxie is a versatile overdrive pedal inspired by the Ibanez® Tube Screamer® TS10®, offering a wide range of tones from light boost to gritty drive.
It features unique controls like a Voice switch for clarity and a Fat switch with MOSFET clipping for added gain and saturation.
This pedal is praised for its flexibility, warmth, and rich sound, making it a standout standalone Tube Screamer style effect.
