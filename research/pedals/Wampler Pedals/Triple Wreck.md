## Sound

It delivers tight lows, massive mids, and soaring highs, with controls for Gain, Volume, and a three-band EQ for precise tone shaping.
The pedal includes a "Hard/Brutal" toggle switch for two distinct voices: warmer, high gain, and full audio spectrum.
Furthermore, it has a boost switch with a knob to change the pedal to a Big Muff type tone, ideal for David Gilmour tones.
