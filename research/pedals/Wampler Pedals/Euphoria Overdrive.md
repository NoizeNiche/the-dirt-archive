## What this pedal is

The Euphoria is a versatile Wampler overdrive built around multiple clipping/gain states. Wampler describes it as capable of everything from gentle clipping and transparent boost-like sounds to thick, aggressive crunch.

## Versions and factory options

- **Smooth** mode: warm, fat, compressed overdrive with strong sustain.
- **Open** mode: clearer, more open overdrive with less coloration.
- **Crunch** mode: more aggressive, thicker response and capable of approaching fuzzy textures.
- Controls: **Volume, Gain, Bass, Treble**.
- 9V to 18V operation.
- Relay true bypass.
- Top-mounted input and output jacks.

## Sound

The Euphoria covers clean-boost territory, low-gain overdrive, and more compressed high-gain crunch. Its three gain states change the clipping character, while the Bass and Treble controls shape the response before and around the gain stages.
