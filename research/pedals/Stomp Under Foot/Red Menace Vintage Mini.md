## What this pedal is
The Red Menace Vintage Mini is a limited-edition Stomp Under Foot fuzz that combines the **Red Army** overdrive/fuzz voice with a **1972 Triangle** Big Muff-style circuit.

## Colorways
- Stomp Under Foot lists the Vintage Mini as a **Limited Edition**.

## Versions and factory options
- Custom 4 x 5 inch enclosure.
- True bypass.
- NOS through-hole components.
- 9V battery or 2.1 mm 9V adapter.
- Added **Mids** control provides additional midrange shaping.

## Sound
The Red Menace Vintage Mini combines the grittier character of Stomp Under Foot's Red Army design with the Triangle-style fuzz voice. The Mids control lets the player move beyond the classic scooped Muff response toward a more forward midrange.
