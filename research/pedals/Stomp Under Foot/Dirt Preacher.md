## Sound
With the gain control around 9-10 o'clock you can get an overdriven tone.
When turning the gain between 11-1 o'clock you get a smooth, muff-like fuzz and with the gain all the way up you get a gnarly, gritty, wooly, fuzz that sounds like you are pushing a maxed out Tweed amp.
With the gain control around 9-10 o'clock, you can get an overdriven tone.
