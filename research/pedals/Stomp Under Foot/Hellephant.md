## What this pedal is

The Hellephant is a Stomp Under Foot fuzz built around a vintage Fuzz Face-style foundation, with changes intended to make the circuit respond usefully to the guitar's controls. The surviving exact-model sources establish it as a distinct Stomp Under Foot fuzz rather than a generic Fuzz Face entry.

## Colorways

No complete factory colorway sequence was established. Individual listings show different finishes, but those examples are not enough to define a universal production colorway history.

## Versions and factory options

The reviewed evidence references **MkII**, but it does not establish a complete numbered production chronology. The archive therefore avoids treating the version label as a fully reconstructed revision history.

## Transistor

The Hellephant uses **silicon transistors**. An exact-model Premier Guitar review identifies **NOS metal-can 2N222A transistors** in the reviewed Hellephant unit. Because individual builds can vary, the archive treats 2N222A as documented unit-level evidence rather than an unconditional production-wide BOM.

## Controls

The documented control set centers on **Volume** and **Fuzz**. The circuit also includes an internal bias adjustment used during setup.

## Sound

The Hellephant combines the familiar response of a Fuzz Face-style circuit with increased control over gain and cleanup from the guitar. Lower Fuzz settings can move toward crunchy overdrive, while higher settings produce a thicker, more saturated fuzz. The reviewed unit is also described as dynamic with strong response to picking and guitar-volume changes.
