## Sound
Unlike conventional overdrive or clean boost pedals, the Halo combines two fully independent tube gain stages (ECC83 and 12AU7), a 30-watt Class AB analog power amp, and a balanced XLR DI with ground lift and pre/post-EQ switching.
At $1,299 USD, it sits in the premium tier alongside brands like Aguilar, Darkglass, and Tech 21—but distinguishes itself by delivering true tube saturation, dynamic headroom, and full-range amplification without requiring an external cab or power amp.
For gigging bassists seeking studio-quality tone in a single floor unit, the Halo isn’t just another pedal—it’s a paradigm shift.
