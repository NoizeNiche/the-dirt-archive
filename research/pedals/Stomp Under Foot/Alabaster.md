## Sound
The Stomp Under Foot Alabaster is a boutique analog overdrive pedal built entirely by hand in Portland, Oregon, using discrete JFET transistors and point-to-point wiring on turret board.
Released in late 2021 as a spiritual successor to the legendary Klon Centaur—but with intentional departures in voicing and dynamics—it delivers a rich, touch-sensitive overdrive that preserves pick attack while adding harmonic complexity without compression or fizz.
Unlike mass-produced clones, each Alabaster undergoes 90 minutes of individual calibration, including DC biasing of the 2N5457 and J201 JFETs, and verification of gain staging across three internal trimmers.
