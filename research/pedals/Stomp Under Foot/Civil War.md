## What this pedal is

The Stomp Under Foot Civil War is a handmade recreation of the Russian **Civil War** Big Muff family. Stomp Under Foot identifies the Civil War as one of its faithful recreations of classic Big Muff-era circuits. The archive treats the name as a pedal identity, not as a claim that every historical Sovtek unit was electrically identical.

## Sound

The Civil War is voiced toward the large low end, smooth sustain, and broad fuzz texture associated with Russian Big Muff designs. It is intended to preserve the thick character of that circuit family while using Stomp Under Foot's modern handmade construction.
