## What this pedal is
La Scatola Nero is a fuzz pedal with an added octave function. Contemporary exact-model records identify it as an octave-fuzz design developed after a request from Ben McLeod of All Them Witches for a different fuzz with an octave added.

The model combines a fuzz voice with an added octave function, giving it a more aggressive upper-register texture than a conventional fuzz alone.
