# Chamber Of Sounds — Green Genie

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Green Genie
- **Builder:** Chamber Of Sounds
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Chamber Of Sounds Green Genie, a handmade Foxx Tone Machine-style octave fuzz.

## What this pedal is
Effects Database documents Green Genie as a handmade version of the then-discontinued Foxx Tone Machine. The listing specifically notes an octave footswitch, true-bypass switching, top-quality components, and an enclosure approximately half the size of the original. [1]

## Versions and factory options
The exact-model source documents:
- Fuzz.
- Switchable octave.
- Footswitchable octave function.
- True bypass.
- Reduced enclosure size. [1]

Green Genie is an octave-up fuzz in the Foxx Tone Machine tradition, with the octave function footswitchable for live use. [1]
