# Chamber Of Sounds — Green Genie

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** Green Genie
- **Builder:** Chamber Of Sounds
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz / Octave
- **Identity:** Handmade Foxx Tone Machine-style octave fuzz with footswitchable octave and true-bypass switching. [1]

## What this pedal is

Effects Database identifies Green Genie as a handmade recreation of the then-discontinued **Foxx Tone Machine**. The builder's implementation is packaged in an enclosure approximately half the size of the original and adds live-friendly footswitching for the octave. [1]

## Circuit lineage

- **Primary reference:** Foxx Tone Machine.
- The archive records this as a Foxx-derived reproduction rather than claiming every original Foxx component was retained.

## Controls / features

- Fuzz section.
- **OCTAVE** footswitch.
- Footswitchable octave engagement.
- True bypass. [1]
