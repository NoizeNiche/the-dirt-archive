## Sound
This latest incarnation of our top selling Beta Series is designed to capture the warmth and grit of favorite vintage tube amps.
You know, that distinctive drive and tone that seems to be lacking from much of today’s modern bass amplification.
The LPF switch has three settings: Mid Boost (Position 1/Left), LPF Bypass (Position 0/Center), and Mid Cut (Position 2/Right).
