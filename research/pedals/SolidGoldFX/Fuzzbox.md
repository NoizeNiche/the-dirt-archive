## Sound
Published on September 20, 2026 SolidGoldFX fuzz pedal Information The SolidGoldFX Fuzzbox is a modern take on the Tone Bender Mk II, the circuit developed in mid-1960s England that went on to define the sound of British rock.
That original was thick and harmonically rich, and responsive enough to a player's touch that it worked as an extension of the guitar rather than simply a distortion, cleaning up as the volume knob rolled back and opening into singing sustain when pushed.
Rather than copying one particular vintage unit, the Fuzzbox rebuilds the idea around carefully selected low-gain silicon transistors, which hold onto the warmth and harmonic texture of the original design while behaving consistently from one pedal to the next.
