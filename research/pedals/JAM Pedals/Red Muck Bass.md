## Sound
A Fuzz-Distortion pedal, inspired from the circuits of the ’71 “triangle” and the later “Red Army / Civil War” Muff.
Along with our individual touch, the end result boasts a smoother sound, fatter low end, pronounced midrange and added tonal flexibility through the expanded range of the tone knob.
The onboard MIX control allows you to discover your sweet- spot of the Dry / Wet signal ratio, while the second footswitch turns it into a 2-gain-stage pedal!
