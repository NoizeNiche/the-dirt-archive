# Spaceman Effects — Sputnik I

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** Sputnik I
- **Builder:** Spaceman Effects
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Spaceman Effects's Sputnik I.

## What this pedal is

Sputnik I is Spaceman Effects' return to the original Sputnik fuzz concept, updated with **NOS silicon transistors** and a smaller enclosure. Spaceman describes it as a stable, higher-gain interpretation of the original circuit.

## Colorways

Documented limited editions include:
- **Olive Drab**
- **White**
- **Black**
- **Blue** dealer-exclusive edition

The Olive Drab edition uses a Cyrillic-engraved faceplate and custom knobs.

## Versions and factory options

- Original **v1** circuit concept.
- **DRIFT** mode for wild oscillation, octave, and 8-bit-like artifacts.
- **FILTER** switch provides two voicings for the Calibration tone control.
- True bypass via soft-click switching.
- Discrete analog circuit using through-hole components.
- Hand-soldered and wired in Portland, Oregon.
- 9V battery or external power.

## Version changes

- Sputnik I is a distinct silicon-transistor return of the Sputnik concept.
- Later Sputnik revisions and models are not merged into this record.

## Sound

Sputnik I retains the unstable, highly interactive character of the original Sputnik while using silicon devices for greater consistency and additional gain. DRIFT expands the pedal into oscillation, octave, and artifact territory, while the FILTER switch changes the voicing around the Calibration control.
