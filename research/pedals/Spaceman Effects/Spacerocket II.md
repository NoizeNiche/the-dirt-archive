# Spaceman Effects — Spacerocket II

## PRP identity

- **Identity:** Spaceman Effects's Spacerocket II.

## What this pedal is

The Spacerocket II is Spaceman Effects' updated version of the original Spacerocket octave/intermodulation fuzz. Spaceman describes it as an octave-fuzz design with a minimalist control layout and a three-position voice switch.

## Colorways

- First-edition colors were **Blue**, **Black**, and **White**, offered as signed and numbered runs.
- Spaceman later added a **White Light Edition**.
- Color editions are finish editions unless a circuit change is specifically documented.

## Versions and factory options

- Three-position toggle changes compression, gain, and bass simultaneously.
- Discrete, all-analog, through-hole circuit.
- True bypass.
- Hand-soldered and wired in Portland, Oregon.
- Standard 9V DC negative-center operation.
- Spaceman states that the Spacerocket II reduced the original's noise floor by an average of 15 dB, strengthened the octave effect in the center switch position, and refined the tonal structure.

## Version changes

- Spacerocket II is explicitly the sequel to the original Spacerocket released in 2011.
- The archive treats the original Spacerocket and Spacerocket II as separate models.

## Sound

The Spacerocket II covers traditional octave-fuzz textures through thick, layered fuzz. Spaceman describes a fat, compressed character with harmonic octave behavior on sustained notes and a strongly interactive response to playing technique and instrument controls.
