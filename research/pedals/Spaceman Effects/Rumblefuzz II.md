## What this pedal is
From transparent amp-like overdrive to thick sustain-laden wall of fuzz, the Rumblefuzz II offers a wide palette of harmonic distortion.

## Colorways

## Sound
From transparent amp-like overdrive to thick sustain-laden wall of fuzz, the Rumblefuzz II offers a wide palette of harmonic distortion.
This pedal acts as an extension of your instrument, with a natural dynamic interaction to your playing and instrument controls.
The RUMBLE control tailors the bass response of the circuit, from tight overdrive tones to incredibly fat fuzz.
