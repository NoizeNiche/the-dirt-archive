## What this pedal is
This is Crazy Rock'n'Roll Effecter's interpretation of a classic British Tone Bender Mk I / Mk I.V-style fuzz, developed around the builder's goal of retaining the character of early British fuzz while making the response practical with modern recording and amplifier setups.

The builder documents extensive experimentation with germanium transistor families from multiple manufacturers and identifies the final transistor choice as Mullard OC72.

## Controls
- **LEVEL:** output level.
- **ATTACK:** fuzz/attack character control.

## Switching and power
- True bypass.
- **9V battery only**.
- Input and output instrument terminals.

- **Mullard OC72 germanium transistor:** explicitly documented by the builder.
- The builder states that OC43, OC44, OC45, OC72, OC77, OC81, AC-series, NKT-series and Hitachi 2SB germanium families were evaluated before settling on the OC72.
- Exact individual transistor matching values are not documented.

## Sound
The builder's target is the classic British fuzz character associated with early Tone Bender sounds, while avoiding excessive boom. The stated tuning emphasizes useful compression, satisfying attack and enough definition to preserve the character of different amplifiers. The ATTACK control is the principal user-facing tone/response adjustment in the documented two-control layout.

## Construction and provenance
