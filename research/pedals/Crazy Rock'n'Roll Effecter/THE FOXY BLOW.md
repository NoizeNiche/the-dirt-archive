## What this pedal is
THE FOXY BLOW is a distortion pedal inspired by the Landgraff distortion family and early RAT designs. The builder's exact-model product description says it centers on an LM308 device and was voiced by changing the tone curve, minimum gain and IC operating voltage to produce a more refined distortion character with richer low end.
