## What this pedal is
Nasty Drive is an overdrive designed around a wide gain range and a Hiwatt-style driven character. The builder describes it as moving from clean sounds through creamy or aggressive overdrive while keeping the instrument's character intact.

## Controls
- **QUANTITY:** volume.
- **STRAIN:** gain.
- **VEIL:** tone.
- **Oldy / Modern:** tone-mode switch.
- **1 / 2:** high-cut/normal response switch.

## Sound
The builder targets the driven feel of a 1970s Hiwatt stack while preserving a broad gain range, moderately fat low end and enough high-end clarity for ensemble playing. The Oldy/Modern and 1/2 switches alter the tone response.
