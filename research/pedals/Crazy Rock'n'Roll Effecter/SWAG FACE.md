# Crazy Rock'n'Roll Effecter - SWAG FACE

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Crazy Rock'n'Roll Effecter
- **Catalog identity:** SWAG FACE
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Exact Crazy Rock'n'Roll Effecter SWAG FACE.

## What this pedal is
SWAG FACE is the builder's Fuzz Face-derived fuzz platform, developed from long-term study of vintage and later Fuzz Face variants. The 2024 Limited model used selected New Market NKT278 germanium transistors and a circular enclosure.

## Controls
- **Volume**
- **FUZZ**
- **swag knob**

## Switching and power
- True bypass.
- 9V battery only for the documented 2024 Limited model.

## Versions and factory options
- The builder documents multiple SWAG FACE variants over time.
- **2024 SWAG FACE Limited:** NKT278 germanium transistor model with circular enclosure.
- A separate NKT278 2025 production listing is also documented.
- Historical NKT275 versions are distinct examples and should not be conflated with NKT278 variants.
- Finish variants, including red/silver/hammer-tone presentations, are cosmetic unless a component or circuit change is documented.

## Transistor
- **NKT278 germanium:** explicitly documented for the 2024 Limited and later NKT278 listing.
- Earlier NKT275 examples are separately documented and should remain historically distinct.
- Exact matching/specification of individual vintage transistors is not established beyond the builder's model-level statements.

## Diode
- Exact diode type or part number: **not established**.

## Sound
The builder's design goal is a Fuzz Face-derived sound with enough presence to remain useful both in a band mix and for solos. The 2024 Limited NKT278 version is described as thicker and richer than earlier NKT275-associated builds.

## Construction and provenance
- Handmade by Crazy Rock'n'Roll Effecter.
- Point-to-point construction is explicitly documented for the NKT278 2025 version.
- The builder describes selecting parts individually.
