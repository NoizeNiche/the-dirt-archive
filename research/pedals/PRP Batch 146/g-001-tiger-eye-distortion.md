# Caline G-001 Tiger Eye Distortion

- **Builder:** Caline
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Catalog identity:** G-001 Tiger Eye Distortion
- **Colorways:** Black mini enclosure with tiger-stripe graphics and the four labeled controls is documented in exact-model listings.
- **Versions:** G-series mini distortion design; no distinct production version was confirmed in the reviewed sources.
- **Version changes:** No confirmed hardware revision or circuit change was found.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.
- **Diode type:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.
- **Controls / switching:** Volume, Gain, Lo Mid, Treble; single true-bypass footswitch.
- **Sound:** Caline describes the Tiger Eye as a touch-responsive distortion with a useful EQ section, while independent demonstrations describe it as a British-style/classic-rock distortion with substantial gain available. The dedicated Lo Mid and Treble controls make it possible to shape the low-mid body and upper-end cut rather than relying on a single tone control.
- **Research confidence:** High for controls, format, power, and general voicing; medium for the British-style lineage because that comparison comes from demonstration/review material rather than a schematic.
