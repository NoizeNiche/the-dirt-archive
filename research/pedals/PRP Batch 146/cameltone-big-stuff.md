# Cameltone Electronics Big Stuff

- **Builder:** Cameltone Electronics
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Catalog identity:** Big Stuff
- **Colorways:** Purple finishes are documented, including a 2014 purple version and a 2015 purple-sparkle edition.
- **Versions:** Multiple dated/cosmetic examples exist; the reviewed 2014/2015 listings show the same Big Stuff identity with the characteristic three-knob layout and PAIN control/switch.
- **Version changes:** The documented PAIN feature is part of the model's sound concept. A 2015 listing describes a limited-edition purple-sparkle example; no confirmed circuit revision between the dated examples was established from the available evidence.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.
- **Diode type:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.
- **Controls / switching:** Volume, Tone, Sustain, plus a PAIN control/switch for the alternate splattery character.
- **Sound:** Cameltone describes Big Stuff as a carefully developed recreation of the builder's interpretation of J. Mascis's famous Ram's Head-era Big Muff sound, with an accidental component-loss event inspiring the PAIN texture. The resulting pedal can switch between a more period-style vintage Ram's Head character and a harsher, splattering, heavily blooming fuzz voice.
- **Research confidence:** High for the identity, three-knob layout, PAIN feature, and documented Ram's Head/J. Mascis design story; medium for exact component-level construction because the reviewed listings do not provide a complete schematic or verified parts list.
