# Black Cat Pedals - Bee Buzz Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Black Cat Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz

## Research
Bee Buzz Fuzz is a dual-channel fuzz/boost based on the Roland Bee Baa. The Bee setting delivers a sharp 1960s fuzz, while Buzz is fuller and more aggressive with a Muff/Fuzz Face-like weight; Premier Guitar documents Candy Red, Candy Blue, Denim Blue, Coppertone and Limonade finishes among production variants.
