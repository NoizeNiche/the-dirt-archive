# Black Cat Pedals - Monster K-Fuzz Stompbox

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Black Cat Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz

## Research
The Monster K-Fuzz Stompbox is the 2016 pedalboard-friendly form of the Monster K-Fuzz platform. The launch announcement lists expression control for Fuzz Frequency, three-way clipping and voicing switches, input gain, 3PDT true bypass, Switchcraft jacks, through-hole construction and hand-built production in the USA.

## Diode
- **Inherited platform selection:** Germanium, silicon, and a selected Schottky diode pair are documented for Monster K-Fuzz.
