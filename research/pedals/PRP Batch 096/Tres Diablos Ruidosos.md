# Black Arts Toneworks - Tres Diablos Ruidosos

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Black Arts Toneworks
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Fuzz / Overdrive

## Research
Tres Diablos Ruidosos uses three cascading gain stages and three master volumes. Black Arts Toneworks describes a range from slight boost and edgy overdrive through 1970s rock distortion and proto-metal crunch, with all three masters pushed producing saturated fuzz; it also responds to guitar-volume changes for layered gain.

## Source
- https://www.blackartstoneworks.com/pedal/tres-diablos-ruidosos/

- **Archive status:** **No Photo Archived**

## Sound

This one can go from a slight sweet boost, edgy OD, classic 70s rock distortion to classic 70s and early 80s proto metal crunch.
Dime all three masters and get a gnarly oversaturated fuzz.
The Diablos respond well to guitar knob input so you can create layered gain working with your guitar and amplifier.
