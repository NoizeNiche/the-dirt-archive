# Black Cat Pedals - Monster K-Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Black Cat Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz

## Research
Monster K-Fuzz is Black Cat's hot-rodded take on the 1960s Kay Fuzztone. The 2014 version added three-way voicing and clipping selectors, input gain and the Fuzz Frequency control; the documented clipping choices are germanium, silicon and a selected Schottky diode pair, while Premier Guitar describes a smoother, dynamic octave-up fuzz.
