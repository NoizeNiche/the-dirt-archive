# Black Cat Pedals - D&S

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Black Cat Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz

## Research
Black Cat D&S recreates the 1974 Maxon D&S, itself from the Ram's Head Big Muff family. Black Cat redesigned the tone network for more usable low-end clarity and EQ range while retaining the original component character, including documented Toshiba 2SC1815-BL transistors.

## Transistor
- **Documented:** Toshiba 2SC1815-BL
