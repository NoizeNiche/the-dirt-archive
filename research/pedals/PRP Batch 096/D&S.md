## Research
Black Cat D&S recreates the 1974 Maxon D&S, itself from the Ram's Head Big Muff family. Black Cat redesigned the tone network for more usable low-end clarity and EQ range while retaining the original component character, including documented Toshiba 2SC1815-BL transistors.

## Deep research verification

Effects Database identifies Black Cat D&S as a recreation of the **1974 Maxon D&S**, itself part of the Ram's Head Big Muff family. Black Cat redesigned the tone network for greater low-end clarity and a wider usable EQ range while retaining the original component character. The reviewed source documents **Toshiba 2SC1815-BL** transistors in the circuit; the clipping-diode type remains unknown.
