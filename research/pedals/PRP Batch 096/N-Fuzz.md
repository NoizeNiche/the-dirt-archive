# Black Cat Pedals - N-Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Black Cat Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz

## Research
N-Fuzz is based on the fuzz section of Black Cat OD-Fuzz and was modified with Japanese components and a wide Bias control. The original was made in limited numbers from 2001 to 2003 for the Asian market; the documented reissue uses N-channel NPN transistors, with Toshiba 2SC1815 at Q1 and Panasonic 2SC1384 at Q2.

## Versions
- **Original:** 2001-2003, limited Asian-market production.
- **Legacy reissue:** 2015-era reissue using Japanese components and the wide-ranging Bias control.

## Transistor
- **Documented:** N-channel NPN devices; Q1 Toshiba 2SC1815, Q2 Panasonic 2SC1384

- **Not reliably documented in the reviewed sources**
