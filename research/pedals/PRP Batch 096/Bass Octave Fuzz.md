# Black Cat Pedals - Bass Octave Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Black Cat Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz

## Research
Bass Octave Fuzz is Black Cat's compact recreation of the Maestro Bass Brassmaster, with an octave-up fuzz voice aimed at bass but usable on guitar. It has Bass, Drive and Fuzz controls plus Filter and Harmonic switches, with the Bass control blending clean and effected signal; Effects Database dates it to 2004 and lists a 2009 reissue.
