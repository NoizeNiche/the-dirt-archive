# Black Arts Toneworks - Witch Burner MkII

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Black Arts Toneworks
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive

## Research
Witch Burner MkII is a deliberately high-gain overdrive. Black Arts Toneworks describes excessive gain, mids and highs that combine into a screaming voice; it runs from standard 9VDC and has no battery connection. Reliable public sources reviewed did not document the production MkII's transistor or diode types.
