## Research
DJENTTONIC is a high-gain metal distortion with a saturated, harmonic-heavy voice. The current manufacturer page describes a Standard version with built-in lithium-ion power, USB-C charging and 9V adapter operation, plus a Light Edition without the lithium/USB-C system; it provides Low, Mid and High controls and a listed 32 mA draw.
