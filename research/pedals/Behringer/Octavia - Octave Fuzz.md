### Verified description

The **Behringer OCTAVIA OCTAVE FUZZ** is an analog, silicon-based octave fuzz with a pronounced octave-up character. Behringer describes its sound as distinct and sometimes unpredictable, with strong response to picking dynamics.

### Verified controls and hardware

- **VOLUME:** output level.
- **BOOST:** amount of fuzz distortion.
- Footswitch and status LED.
- 9V DC / 100 mA power input.
- Single 9V battery operation is also documented.

### Verified sound evidence

Behringer describes an octave-up response that can be chirpy and unpredictable, with useful octave harmonics emerging around the 12th fret and neck-pickup settings. The builder emphasizes that the pedal responds strongly to picking dynamics and works with both single-coil and humbucker guitars.
