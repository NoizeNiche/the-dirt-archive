## What this pedal is
The SF300 is a three-mode fuzz and distortion pedal with two fuzz voices plus a dedicated boost mode. Its control set provides separate bass and treble shaping alongside Gain and Level.

## Sound
Fuzz 1 emphasizes a classic fuzz response, while Fuzz 2 is fuller and more scooped in the midrange with stronger harmonic bite; the third position becomes a gain-boost mode. The dedicated Bass and Treble controls make the SF300 unusually easy to tailor around a particular amplifier or cab response.
