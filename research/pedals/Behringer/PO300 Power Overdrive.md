### Verified description

The **Behringer POWER OVERDRIVE PO300** is a tube-like overdrive designed for thick drive and extended sustain, with a voicing system aimed at hard-rock and heavy-metal applications.

### Verified controls and hardware

- **LEVEL:** output volume.
- **PHAT:** broad frequency shaping for a fatter response.
- **POWER:** changes the tonal strength; higher settings emphasize the mids while lower settings reduce some midrange energy.
- **DRIVE:** amount of overdrive.
- 9V supply input.
- 9V battery compartment.
- LED indicates effect/battery status.
- Footswitch activation/deactivation.
- Input jack remains part of the battery disconnect arrangement.
