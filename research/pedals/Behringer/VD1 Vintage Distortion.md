### Verified description

The **Behringer VINTAGE DISTORTION VD1** is an early compact distortion/sustainer designed around a warm vintage-style distortion response.

### Verified controls and hardware

- **VOLUME:** output.
- **TONE:** high-frequency emphasis.
- **SUSTAIN:** sustain amount.
- 9V DC regulated power.
- 9V battery.
- Hardwired historical bypass design.
- Footswitch activation/deactivation.
- Approximately **100 kOhm input impedance** and **25 kOhm output impedance** are listed in the surviving manual.
- Approximate current consumption: **30 mA**.

### Verified sound evidence

Behringer describes VD1 as producing warm, emotive vintage-style distortion and sustaining tones while retaining natural playing dynamics.
