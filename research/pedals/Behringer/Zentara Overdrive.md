# Behringer - Zentara Overdrive

## Surface catalog record

## What this pedal is

The **ZENTARA OVERDRIVE** is Behringer's transparent boost/overdrive pedal with a dual-ganged Gain control, Treble control, Output control, and germanium-diode clipping.

## Versions and history

Behringer identifies the current product as ZENTARA OVERDRIVE. Public retailer documentation also describes it as a rendering of the classic 1990s transparent overdrive concept. The reviewed sources do not establish a separate factory revision history for the Zentara itself.

## Sound

The pedal is designed to move from clean boost through transparent overdrive and heavier saturation. Behringer emphasizes high headroom and strong picking-dynamic response.

## Deep research verification

### Verified controls and hardware

- **GAIN:** dual-ganged potentiometer that blends clean signal and overdrive.
- **TREBLE:** approximately +/-12 dB of adjustment at 3 kHz.
- **OUTPUT:** final output level.
- Footswitch for effect on/off.
- Buffered bypass.
- 1/4-inch TS input and output.
- Input impedance documented at approximately 1.3 MOhm.
- Output impedance documented at approximately 0.56 kOhm.
- 9 V DC power and 9 V battery operation are documented.

### Verified semiconductor terms

- **Diodes:** Behringer explicitly describes soft-clipping germanium diodes.
- Exact diode part number: Unknown.
- Exact active-device/transistor part numbers: Unknown.

### Verified sound evidence

Behringer describes the Zentara as capable of clean boost, edge-of-breakup overdrive, and full saturation. Its high headroom and picking sensitivity are central to the documented design intent.
