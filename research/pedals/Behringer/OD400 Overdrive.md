## Deep research verification

This pass uses the surviving Behringer OD400 manual documentation.

### Verified description

The **Behringer OVERDRIVE OD400** is an overdrive/distortion pedal intended to boost an amplifier into an overdriven state.

### Verified controls and hardware

- **LEVEL** controls output.
- **TONE** shapes the overall sound.
- **DRIVE** controls overdrive/distortion amount.
- ON/BATT LED shows activation and battery condition.
- 9V DC input.
- 9V battery compartment.
- Footswitch activation/deactivation.
- The input jack also serves as the battery-disconnect mechanism when removed.
