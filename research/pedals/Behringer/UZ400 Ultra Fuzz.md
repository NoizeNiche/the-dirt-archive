## What this pedal is
The UZ400 Ultra Fuzz is a Behringer fuzz/distortion pedal voiced around aggressive vintage-style grit. Behringer describes it as a classic fuzz-distortion effect with a three-knob control set and 9V battery or PSU-SB power.

## Colorways

## Sound
Behringer describes the UZ400 as supercharged vintage grit with buzz-saw attack and sustained lead tones. Fuzz, Tone, and Level cover the basic range from raw garage-like fuzz into more aggressive distortion textures.
