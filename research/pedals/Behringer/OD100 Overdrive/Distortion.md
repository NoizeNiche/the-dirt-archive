## Deep research verification

This pass uses Behringer's surviving English manual for the exact OD100.

### Verified description

The **Behringer OVERDRIVE DISTORTION OD100** combines overdrive and distortion in one pedal, with a continuous mode control that moves between the two effects.

### Verified controls and hardware

- **LEVEL:** output level.
- **TONE:** overall tone shaping.
- **DRIVE:** overdrive/distortion intensity.
- **MODE:** balances overdrive and distortion, from pure overdrive at one extreme to pure distortion at the other.
- ON/BATT LED.
- Footswitch activation/deactivation.
- 9V DC power input.
- 9V battery compartment.
- The input jack disconnects the battery when removed.
