# Behringer - OD300

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** OD300
- **Builder:** Behringer
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive
- **Identity:** Two-mode blend pedal that continuously mixes overdrive and distortion.

## What this pedal is
OD300 combines overdrive and distortion in one analog effects pedal. Its Mode control moves from pure overdrive at one end to straight distortion at the other, with continuously variable blends between those extremes.

## Sound
OD300 is designed as a continuum rather than a hard either/or choice: rotate Mode toward one side for conventional overdrive, toward the other for straight distortion, or stop in the middle for a hybrid. Tone, Drive, and Level then shape the chosen blend.
