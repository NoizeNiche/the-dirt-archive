# Behringer — OD300

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** OD300
- **Builder:** Behringer
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive
- **Identity:** Analog overdrive/distortion blender with continuously variable Mode control between pure overdrive and straight distortion. [1][2]

## Controls
- **LEVEL**
- **TONE**
- **DRIVE**
- **MODE:** Continuously blends the overdrive and distortion paths. [1][2]

## Circuit / topology
- Analog overdrive/distortion.
- **Mode fully left:** pure overdrive.
- **Mode fully right:** straight distortion.
- Intermediate settings blend the two behaviors rather than selecting only one. [2]

## Transistor
- Exact production transistor/device part numbers are not publicly documented.
## Diode / clipping
- Exact clipping/protection device and part number are not publicly documented.

## Power
- **9V battery** operation.
- Optional **PSU-SB** external DC supply. [1]

## Version history
- No numbered electronic revision established.
- Standard production enclosure is the canonical presentation.

## Sound
OD300 is designed as a continuum from conventional overdrive through hybrid settings into straight distortion. Drive and Tone shape the selected blend, while Level sets output. [1][2]
