## What this pedal is
The **DISTORTION-X XD300** is Behringer's historical compact distortion pedal aimed at thick, tube-like distortion, long sustain, hard rock, and heavy metal.

## Versions and history
A Behringer price list from 2007 lists the XD300 as a new product. No verified factory revision split was recovered in this pass.

### Verified controls and hardware

- **LEVEL:** output level.
- **CONTOUR:** shapes the signal from traditional toward super-modern voicings. The manual describes more aggressive/heavier settings at the counter-clockwise end and a milder response toward the clockwise end.
- **PUNCH:** adds aggressive mid-frequency punch for more distinctive distortion.
- **DIST:** amount of distortion.
- LED indicates effect/battery status.
- Footswitch activates or deactivates the effect.
- 1/4-inch TS input and output.
- 9 V DC input.
- 9 V battery compartment.
- The input jack is also the battery disconnect, so the input cable should be removed when the pedal is not in use.
