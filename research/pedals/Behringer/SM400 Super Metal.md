## Deep research verification

This pass uses the surviving Behringer SM400 manual documentation.

### Verified description

The **Behringer SUPER METAL SM400** is a high-gain distortion/sustain pedal with independent low and high frequency controls.

### Verified controls and hardware

- **LEVEL:** output level.
- **LOW:** boosts/cuts low-frequency range.
- **HIGH:** boosts/cuts high-frequency range.
- **DIST:** amount of distortion and resulting sustain length.
- ON/BATT LED.
- 9V DC input.
- 9V battery operation.
- Footswitch activation/deactivation.
- The input jack disconnects the battery when removed.

### Verified sound evidence

Behringer states that higher DIST settings can produce extremely long sustaining tones.
