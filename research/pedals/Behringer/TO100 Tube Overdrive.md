This pass uses the surviving Behringer TO100 manual documentation.

### Verified description

The **Behringer TUBE OVERDRIVE TO100** is an analog overdrive pedal marketed around tube-like distortion, smooth sustain and a fat response.

### Verified controls and hardware

- **DRIVE:** overdrive intensity.
- **LEVEL:** output level.
- **TONE:** overall tone shaping.
- ON/BATT LED.
- 9V DC input.
- 9V battery compartment.
- Footswitch switching.
- The input jack disconnects the battery when no instrument cable is inserted.

### Verified sound evidence

Behringer describes TO100 as ranging from smooth overdrive to more extreme, screaming tube-like sounds, with smooth sustain and a fat tonal character.
