## What this pedal is
KMA Machines' WURHM is a heavy distortion pedal derived from the builder's interpretation of the classic HM-2-style Swedish death-metal distortion circuit. The design extends that foundation with a six-band active EQ section and additional frequency controls for shaping the extreme low- and mid-frequency response. [1][2]

## Sound
WURHM is designed around the aggressive, heavily filtered distortion character associated with HM-2-type circuits. The expanded EQ controls let the player push or cut the low, low-mid, high-mid, and high-frequency regions while the Terror control changes the intensity of the distortion. [1]
