## What this pedal is
**Queequeg 2** is KMA Machines' update of the original Queequeg. RockBoard's PedalPedia and KMA product references describe the version 2 as an analog monophonic sub-octave device with an additional lower octave, selectable and mixable sub-octave modes, updated enclosure/jack layout, and relay-based switching. [1][2][3][4]

The archive currently carries this record under its existing "Fuzz" catalog taxonomy. That taxonomy is preserved here rather than reclassified in the research pass.

## Controls and operation
The documented version-2 design provides a **MIX** control for blending the clean and sub-octave signals and three selectable modes for one octave down, two octaves down, or both together. RockBoard also identifies analog circuitry, 9V DC center-negative power, and approximately 60mA current draw. [1][2][3]

## Versions and factory options
- Queequeg 2 is explicitly documented as an update to the original Queequeg, released after the original 2016 model. [1]
- The update adds an additional sub-octave and the ability to switch and mix between the available sub-octave signals. [1][2]

## Sound
KMA and independent product references describe Queequeg 2 as a fast-tracking analog sub-octave generator for guitar, bass, and synth. The documented controls allow the player to blend the generated octave-down signal with the clean signal and choose between the available sub-octave modes. [1][2][3][4]
