## What this pedal is
The LOGAN Desert is KMA Machines' limited Desert White edition of the Logan Transcend Drive overdrive. KMA introduced the Desert edition as a limited colorway of its Logan platform rather than as a new circuit family. The underlying Logan architecture combines a gain stage with an active three-band EQ, a switchable parametric mid section, a PRE/POST gain-position switch, a dedicated mids footswitch, and an expression input for sweeping the mid frequency. [1][2]

## Sound
The Logan platform is designed as a flexible overdrive/preamp with low- to mid-gain drive, up to roughly 25 dB of boost, active bass and treble control, a parametric mid EQ, PRE/POST placement for the mid section, and expression control over the mid frequency. KMA describes the response as open and touch-responsive, with the gain range extending from cleaner boost and breakup into heavier rock-oriented drive. [1]
