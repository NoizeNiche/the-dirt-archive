## Sound
His massive blast of distortion will tear down even the thickest boondock wilderness and set the world on fire.
The Dead Stag is based on one of the most classic discrete fuzz/distortion circuits in pedal history.
Beneath the active Bass/Treble EQ with a 15db cut/boost, KMA designed a parametric mid EQ with a 12db cut/boost between 400Hz and 2.4kHz.
