# Colorsound / Sola Sound - B&M Champion Fuzz Unit

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Colorsound / Sola Sound
- **Catalog identity:** B&M Champion Fuzz Unit
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Late-1970s Barnes & Mullins private-label fuzz built by Sola Sound/Colorsound, from the silicon Jumbo Tone Bender family.

## What this pedal is
The B&M Champion Fuzz Unit is one of the late-1970s private-label versions of the Sola Sound/Colorsound silicon Jumbo Tone Bender family. Contemporary and retrospective sources group the B&M Champion, B&M Fuzz Unit, CMI Fuzz Unit, G.B. Fuzz variants, Pro'Traffic Fuzz Unit, and the smaller Eurotec Black Box Fuzz Module within this lineage. [1][2][3]

## Controls
Surviving examples show three external controls:
- **Volume**
- **Sustain**
- **Tone** [1][3]

## Colorways and labeling
- Orange examples are documented.
- Black examples are also documented.
- The product name itself varies in surviving units, including B&M Fuzz Unit and B&M Champion Fuzz Unit. [1][3]
- These finish and label differences are recorded as historical cosmetic/branding variants rather than assumed circuit revisions.

## Versions and factory options
Sources describe production differences between surviving examples, including enclosure color and component batches, while retaining the same basic fuzz family. [1]
No complete dated production-revision chronology was established.

## Version changes
The documented differences are mainly labeling, enclosure finish, and component-era variation. A specific unit should not be assigned a revision number unless its construction can be directly documented. [1]

## Construction
The original pedals were built in London by Colorsound/Sola Sound for Barnes & Mullins. The surviving family is associated with the later silicon Jumbo Tone Bender circuit lineage. [1][2]

## Transistor
- **Typical device family:** Silicon transistors.
- A commonly documented example of the original family uses **BC184C** transistors, although surviving units can differ by production period. [1]
- Exact transistor fit for every B&M Champion unit should therefore not be generalized from one specimen.

## Diode
- **Exact clipping diode/device:** Not reliably documented for the specific cataloged unit in the checked sources.

## Sound
The circuit family is characterized by sustained, saturated fuzz with a comparatively firm low end and more open midrange than earlier germanium Tone Bender designs. It belongs to the late-1970s silicon branch of the Tone Bender lineage. [1][2]

Reverb and historical pedal-reference sources provide exact B&M/Champion Fuzz Unit imagery. [3][4]
