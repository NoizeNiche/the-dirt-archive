# Colorsound / Sola Sound - Tone Bender MKIII

## Research identity

- **Builder:** Colorsound / Sola Sound
- **Catalog identity:** Tone Bender MKIII
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Late-1960s Sola Sound Tone Bender generation that introduced the treble-bass tone control and later evolved through multiple circuit/enclosure states.

## What this pedal is

The MKIII arrived in the late 1960s as the next major Sola Sound Tone Bender generation. Sources describe it as an evolving family rather than a single unchanging circuit; different MKIII-era examples can contain materially different electronics. [1][2]

## Controls

A classic documented MKIII layout uses:
- **Fuzz**
- **Treble-Bass**
- **Volume** [1]

Some short-lived variants and later reissues differ, so exact panel configuration must be matched to the photographed unit.

## Colorways

- Historical MKIII examples exist in multiple enclosure/graphic states.
- Later Colorsound yellow/germanium and other reissue variants should not be silently merged into one original-production revision. [1]

## Versions and factory options

The documented lineage includes:
- original late-1960s MKIII
- subsequent MKIII circuit changes
- later MKIV-era descendants and Colorsound-era variants/reissues. [1][2]

## Version changes

Fuzzboxes notes that Sola Sound continued MKIII production for Vox into the 1970s and that the fuzz circuit changed over time. Effects Database likewise documents multiple MKIII-era circuit/finish states. [1][2]

## Construction

Original MKIII examples moved into pressed-steel style enclosures as Sola Sound production evolved. [1]

## Transistor

- Mid-1970s Colorsound MKIII-related examples are documented with **three silicon transistors**.
- Other MKIII-branded generations used different device types, so transistor type must be tied to the specimen. [2]

## Diode

- **Exact clipping diode/device:** Not asserted as universal across the MKIII lineage.

## Sound

The MKIII family ranges from the aggressive classic British fuzz associated with the late-1960s Tone Bender line to later silicon versions with a different, tighter response. [1][2]

## Photo provenance

Effects Database provides exact-model MKIII records and historical product images. Fuzzboxes provides generation-by-generation visual identification material. [1][2]

### Verified sound evidence
Vox Tone Bender MKIII Overview Introduced in 1968, the Tone Bender MKIII was the latest incarnation of the series of Tone Benders that Sola Sound had been supplying to Vox (among other brands) since 1966.
By the time that Sola Sound were producing the MKIII, their fuzz boxes were no longer being built into cast aluminium enclosures, and instead, the MKIII appeared in a pressed steel casing.
Sola Sound continued to supply Vox with the Tone Bender MKIII until well into the 1970s, with the fuzz circuit undergoing various further changes over the years.
