## What this pedal is
The Supa Wah-Fuzz-Swell is a large early-1970s multi-effect pedal combining a wah, fuzz, and swell/volume function in one treadle enclosure. Historical sources place it in the early-1970s British Colorsound product line. [1][2]

## Controls and switching
The documented unit uses:
- a **wah treadle**
- a **swell/volume treadle function**
- separate footswitches for the effect sections. [2][3]

Surviving examples show the large yellow metal chassis characteristic of the period. [1][3]

## Construction
The pedal uses a very large metal treadle enclosure, two input/output jacks, and two footswitches. It does not provide true bypass; historical testing notes a small bypass volume drop. [2]

## Sound
The three functions cover a wide filter sweep, raw period fuzz, and volume swells without attacking the string's note envelope in the same way as a conventional hard switch. The result is a distinctly early-1970s multi-effect concept rather than a modern compact pedal. [1][2]
