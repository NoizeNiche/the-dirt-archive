# Colorsound / Sola Sound - Pro Traffic Fuzz Unit

## Research identity

- **Catalog identity:** Pro Traffic Fuzz Unit
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Late-1970s private-label Sola Sound/Colorsound fuzz from the silicon Jumbo Tone Bender family.

## What this pedal is
Pro Traffic Fuzz Unit is one of the third-party branded versions of the late-1970s Sola Sound/Colorsound Jumbo Tone Bender family. Historical references group it with B&M Champion Fuzz, B&M Fuzz Unit, CMI Fuzz Unit, G.B. Fuzz variants, and the Eurotec Black Box Fuzz Module. [1][2]

## Controls
The private-label family commonly presents the fuzz with **Volume**, **Sustain**, and **Tone** controls, though exact enclosures can differ. [1]
