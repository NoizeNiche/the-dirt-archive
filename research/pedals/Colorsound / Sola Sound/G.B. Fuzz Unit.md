# Colorsound / Sola Sound - G.B. Fuzz Unit

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Colorsound / Sola Sound
- **Catalog identity:** G.B. Fuzz Unit
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** G.B.-branded private-label unit from Sola Sound/Colorsound's late-1970s silicon Jumbo Tone Bender family.

## What this pedal is
G.B. Fuzz Unit is a named private-label presentation of the same late-1970s Sola Sound/Colorsound family represented by B&M Champion Fuzz, B&M Fuzz Unit, CMI Fuzz Unit, G.B. Fuzz, Pro'Traffic Fuzz Unit, and Eurotec Black Box Fuzz Module. [1][2]

## Controls
The surviving family commonly uses:
- **Volume**
- **Sustain**
- **Tone**

Because enclosure labeling and controls varied across OEM/private-label examples, the archive treats the exact front panel of each specimen as the final photographic identity check. [1]

## Colorways
- G.B. branding is the primary identifying feature.
- Finish and enclosure differences are historical variants unless supported by evidence of a circuit change.

## Versions and factory options
No complete numbered G.B. Fuzz Unit revision sequence was established.
The important distinction from the companion catalog identity G.B. Fuzz is the documented model naming rather than a proven separate circuit generation.

## Version changes
No reliable date-based production chronology was established for G.B. Fuzz Unit.
Period construction differences are documented across the wider Jumbo family. [1]

## Construction
Sola Sound/Colorsound manufactured these British private-label effects for third-party distribution. The circuit lineage is the later silicon Jumbo Tone Bender family, which by this period was closely related to Big Muff-style architecture. [1][2]

## Transistor
- **Typical family:** Silicon transistors.
- Exact transistor selection for a specific G.B. Fuzz Unit was not established.

## Diode
- **Exact clipping diode/device:** Not publicly documented in the checked sources.

## Sound
The family produces saturated, sustaining fuzz with the weight and low-end authority associated with the Jumbo Tone Bender period. [1][2]
