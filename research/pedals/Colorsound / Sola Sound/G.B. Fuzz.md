# Colorsound / Sola Sound - G.B. Fuzz

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Colorsound / Sola Sound
- **Catalog identity:** G.B. Fuzz
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Private-label British fuzz produced by Sola Sound/Colorsound in the late-1970s Jumbo Tone Bender family.

## What this pedal is
G.B. Fuzz belongs to the group of late-1970s third-party labels that Sola Sound used for the same broad silicon Jumbo Tone Bender family. Historical references place G.B. alongside B&M Champion Fuzz, B&M Fuzz Unit, CMI Fuzz Unit, G.B. Fuzz Unit, Pro'Traffic Fuzz Unit, and Eurotec Black Box Fuzz Module. [1][2]

## Controls
Surviving G.B. examples and related family units use the characteristic fuzz controls of the enclosure variant, commonly **Volume**, **Sustain**, and **Tone**. Exact front-panel configuration should be verified against an individual G.B. specimen before importing a photograph as canonical. [1]
