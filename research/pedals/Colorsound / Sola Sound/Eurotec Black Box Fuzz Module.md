# Colorsound / Sola Sound - Eurotec Black Box Fuzz Module

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Colorsound / Sola Sound
- **Catalog identity:** Eurotec Black Box Fuzz Module
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Compact private-label enclosure carrying the late-1970s Sola Sound/Colorsound Jumbo Tone Bender family.

## What this pedal is
The Eurotec Black Box Fuzz Module is a smaller-enclosure presentation of the late-1970s Jumbo Tone Bender family. Historical sources group it with B&M Champion Fuzz, B&M Fuzz Unit, CMI Fuzz Unit, G.B. Fuzz, G.B. Fuzz Unit, and Pro'Traffic Fuzz Unit as private-label versions of the same broad Sola Sound/Colorsound design lineage. [1][2]

## Controls
The compact module format does not establish a universally documented external control set across surviving examples. Historical family references indicate that the underlying fuzz design was based around the later silicon Tone Bender/Jumbo family rather than the earlier germanium Tone Benders. [1][2]

## Construction
Sola Sound/Colorsound produced these late-1970s third-party/private-label fuzz units in England. The Eurotec version is associated with the compact enclosure noted in historical family surveys. [1][2]

## Sound
The family is known for thick sustained fuzz, strong low-end presence, and a more open midrange than the earlier germanium Tone Bender designs. [1][2]
