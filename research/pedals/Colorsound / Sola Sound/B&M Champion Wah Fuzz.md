# Colorsound / Sola Sound - B&M Champion Wah Fuzz

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Colorsound / Sola Sound
- **Catalog identity:** B&M Champion Wah Fuzz
- **Catalog type:** Wah / Fuzz
- **Identity:** Late-1970s Barnes & Mullins private-label wah/fuzz pedal produced by Sola Sound/Colorsound.

## What this pedal is
The B&M Champion Wah Fuzz combines a Colorsound-style wah circuit with a separate fuzz section in a single treadle enclosure. The Effects Database identifies the fuzz section with the Jumbo Tone Bender family and the unit as a late-1970s Barnes & Mullins product built in England. [1]

## Controls and switching
- Wah is controlled by the treadle.
- Independent footswitches allow wah and fuzz to be engaged separately or together.
- Three internal trimmers are documented for the fuzz section: volume, tone, and sustain. [1]

## Sound
The wah supplies the familiar broad filter sweep of the Colorsound family, while the fuzz section provides dense, sustaining distortion. The combined unit can run wah-only, fuzz-only, or both together. [1]
