# Chunk Systems — FZ002

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** FZ002

- **Identity:** Chunk Systems FZ002, an obsolete gated bass fuzz that preceded the Brown Dog.

## What this pedal is

Chunk Systems describes FZ002 as a bass-specific fuzz with a gating system intended to keep bass lines punchy and defined. The pedal can move from lightly crusty fuzz through heavy, synth-like bass fuzz. [1]

The builder states that FZ002 was later superseded by Brown Dog. [1]

## Versions and factory options

Documented features:
- Gate control.
- Drive control.
- Independent fuzz/wet control.
- Independent bass/dry control.
- CV send and return.
- 9V battery compartment.
- Optional 9-24V regulated DC power. [1]

## Version changes

FZ002 is the predecessor to Brown Dog. Brown Dog added a soft mode and easier mixer controls while keeping the core gated-bass-fuzz concept. [1]

## Sound

FZ002 is voiced for bass, using gating to keep the low end punchy while the fuzz section adds progressively heavier saturation and synth-like textures. The independent dry/bass control is specifically intended to retain low-end definition under fuzz. [1]
