# Chunk Systems — Brown Dog

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Brown Dog
- **Builder:** Chunk Systems
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Chunk Systems Brown Dog gated bass fuzz, a bass-specific fuzz with two fuzz modes, clean blend, and a unique gating circuit.

## What this pedal is
Chunk Systems describes Brown Dog as a gated bass fuzz based on its earlier FZ002. It adds separate hard and soft fuzz modes plus an easier mixer control while retaining the gating concept. [1]

Effects Database documents the exact control set and true bypass design. [2]

## Colorways
No complete factory colorway sequence was established.

## Versions and factory options
Controls:
- Dirty Level.
- Clean Level.
- Gate.
- Drive.
- Soft / Hard mode. [2]

Chunk Systems also documents an envelope output and an optional envelope connector used to interface the Brown Dog with Agent 00Funk. [1][3]

## Version changes
Brown Dog explicitly evolved from FZ002, adding a soft fuzz mode and easier-to-use mixer controls. [1][2]

## Sound
Brown Dog is designed to produce thick, aggressive bass fuzz while retaining the original bass signal for punch and definition. The Gate tightens the attack and reduces noise, while Hard/Soft modes cover different fuzz textures. [1][2]
