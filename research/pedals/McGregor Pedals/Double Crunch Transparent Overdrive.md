## What this pedal is
The Double Crunch puts complete **Original Crunch** and **Extra Crunch** circuits into one enclosure. The two sections are independently switchable and can also be run together as a stack.

## Versions and factory options
- Two independent transparent-overdrive circuits.
- **Original Crunch:** lower-gain voicing.
- **Extra Crunch:** higher-gain voicing.
- Three operating states: Original only, Extra only, or both.
- Both engaged uses **Extra → Original** routing by default.
- Volume, Tone and Gain controls on each side.
- True bypass on each circuit.
- 9VDC center-negative power.
- Hand-soldered in British Columbia.

## Sound
The Double Crunch is voiced as a transparent overdrive for both guitar and bass. The Extra side provides more saturation, while stacking both sections produces a thicker cranked-amp-style drive.
