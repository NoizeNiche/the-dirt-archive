## What this pedal is

The Cozmic Fuzzball cascades three dirt stages: **overdrive → distortion → fuzz**. A single **Attack** control determines how much of the later stages enter the signal path.

## Versions and factory options

- Attack control.
- Full cascade is available at higher Attack settings.
- At minimum Attack, the pedal operates as overdrive only.
- Hand-soldered in British Columbia.
- Designed for guitar and bass.

## Sound

The Attack control morphs the pedal from relatively open overdrive through layered distortion and finally full fuzz. It is designed as a continuous dirt stack rather than three unrelated presets.
