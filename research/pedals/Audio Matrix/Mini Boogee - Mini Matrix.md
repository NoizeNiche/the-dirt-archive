# Audio Matrix — Mini Boogee / Mini Matrix

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** Mini Boogee / Mini Matrix
- **Builder:** Audio Matrix / B.K. Butler
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Early B.K. Butler tube overdrive/preamp sold under the Mini Boogee name and later renamed Mini Matrix after a request from Mesa Engineering. The two names refer to the same historical pedal family rather than separate circuits. [1][2]

## What this is

Effects Database links Mini Boogee and Mini Matrix as the same pedal. Historical technical research by Kit Rae identifies the family as an early five-knob incarnation of Butler's Tube Driver work, made under the Audio Matrix brand from approximately 1980–1984, with two preamp tubes and no op-amps. [1][2]

## Controls / architecture

Historical references document:
- **Five-knob architecture** on the Mini Boogee/Mini Matrix family.
- A **Bright** switch was part of the mini-amplifier-style layout.
- The circuit used **two preamp tubes**, not op-amps. [2]
Exact knob labels for every enclosure revision are not completely preserved in the accessible sources, so the archive does not invent a universal control legend.

## Active devices

- **Tube-based**, not transistor-only.
- **Two preamp tubes** are documented for the Mini Boogee/Mini Matrix family. Kit Rae's historical account identifies them as **12AX7** tubes. [2]
- No op-amp stage is documented for this family. [2]

## Diode / clipping

- The defining distortion mechanism is the valve preamp circuit.
- No specific clipping-diode network is established in the reviewed historical sources.

## Version / naming history

Kit Rae documents multiple enclosure/name stages:
1. Bone-white cast-metal Mini Boogee, circa 1980.
2. Metal enclosure with imitation wood end panels, circa 1980.
3. Sheet-metal black/white version with wood end panels, circa 1981.
4. Essentially the same enclosure with the name changed to **Mini Matrix**, circa 1981.
Slight circuit and PCB changes occurred during production. The Mini Matrix name continued into the later part of the family's 1980–1984 production window. [2]

## Renaming

The name changed after Randall Smith of Mesa Engineering objected to the use of “Boogee” because of its similarity to Mesa's Boogie brand. The historical sources describe the resulting Audio Matrix label as **Mini Matrix**. [1][2]

## Power / construction

- Historical accounts describe an **internal power transformer**.
- The product was hand-built by B.K. Butler under Audio Matrix.
- Exact mains specifications for every surviving unit are not established in the reviewed sources.

## Sound

The Mini Boogee/Mini Matrix is an early Butler tube overdrive/preamp platform, preceding the later Tube Driver family. Historical descriptions emphasize its two-tube architecture and high-gain, tube-driven distortion rather than a conventional diode-clipping pedal. [2]

## Deep research verification

Effects Database was cross-checked against Kit Rae's detailed historical Tube Driver research and a surviving 1980s Mini Matrix/Boogee listing. The combined evidence establishes the naming history, approximate production period, multiple enclosure revisions, two-12AX7 preamp architecture and relationship to Butler's later Tube Driver designs. The archive keeps exact unit-level component details unresolved. [1][2][3]
