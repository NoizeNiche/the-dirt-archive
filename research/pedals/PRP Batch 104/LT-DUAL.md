# Blackstar Amplification - LT-DUAL

## PRP identity

- **Builder:** Blackstar Amplification
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive

## Research

The LT-DUAL is a two-channel compact distortion pedal. Each channel has its own Gain and Level controls, while Tone and Blackstar's ISF are shared, allowing the two channels to cover different levels of drive while using the same overall tonal shape. The pedal is built around Blackstar's LT low-voltage platform rather than the ECC83 high-voltage architecture used by the HT family.

## Controls and architecture

- Channel 1 Gain
- Channel 1 Level
- Channel 2 Gain
- Channel 2 Level
- Tone
- ISF
- Channel switching/on-off
- 9V DC or PP3 battery operation
- Input and output
