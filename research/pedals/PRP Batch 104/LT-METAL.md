# Blackstar Amplification - LT-METAL

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Blackstar Amplification
- **Catalog type:** Distortion

## Research
The LT-METAL is the LT-series high-gain distortion pedal. Blackstar designed it for aggressive modern metal tones and uses the patented ISF circuit to reshape the tone network between American and British responses. The main controls are Gain, Level, Tone and ISF.

The pedal uses a low-voltage 9V DC or PP3-battery format, making it distinct from the high-voltage ECC83 HT-METAL pedal.

## Controls
- Gain
- Level
- Tone
- ISF
- Effect on/off
