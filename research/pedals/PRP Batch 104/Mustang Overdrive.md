# Blakemore Effects - Mustang Overdrive

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Blakemore Effects
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive

## Research
Mustang Overdrive was one of Blake Hickey's original Blakemore Effects designs, created before the brand began selling pedals commercially. Hickey named it after the Mustang mascot of Southern Methodist University, and the pedal was developed as a core low-to-higher-gain overdrive platform.

The Bi-Polar Octadrive was later developed from the Mustang by adding a footswitchable octave section. Hickey describes the Blakemore pedals as hand-built, with custom PCB layouts and externally manufactured boards/enclosures.

## Features
- Overdrive platform
- Related to the later Bi-Polar Octadrive
- Handmade construction
- 2010s boutique design
