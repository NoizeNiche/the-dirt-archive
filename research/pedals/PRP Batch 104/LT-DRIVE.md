# Blackstar Amplification - LT-DRIVE

## PRP identity

- **Builder:** Blackstar Amplification
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive

## Research

The LT-DRIVE is the compact LT-series overdrive pedal. It uses a conventional low-voltage pedal format with Gain, Tone and Level controls, and is designed to move from lower-gain boost/drive sounds into stronger overdrive. The unit accepts a 9V DC adapter or 9V PP3 battery and has standard input and output connections.

## Controls

- Gain
- Tone
- Level
- Effect on/off
