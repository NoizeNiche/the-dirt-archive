# BLAMMO! Electronics - Buzzaround

## PRP identity

- **Builder:** BLAMMO! Electronics
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz

## Research

BLAMMO!'s Buzzaround is a modern reproduction of the rare 1966-68 Baldwin-Burns Buzzaround, a British fuzz historically associated with early King Crimson recordings. The maker states that the circuit itself follows the original component values while adding a master volume and modern power/footswitch treatment.

The circuit uses three NOS germanium NPN transistors. BLAMMO! says each transistor is hand-tested for gain and leakage before placement. The pedal adds relay-based true bypass, upgraded power filtering and a pedalboard-friendly 125B enclosure.

## Features

- Three NOS MP38A germanium NPN transistors
- Original-style component values
- Added master volume
- Relay true bypass
- 125B enclosure with top-mounted jacks
- 9V center-negative
- 38mA current draw
- Handmade in Portland, Oregon

Electronics Model: Buzzaround Condition: New Description: Known to some as the “mythical fuzz”, this wedge shaped pedal officially named the Buzzaround was built by hand on tag board from 1966 to 1968 in England by Baldwin – Burns Ltd.
For quite some time conventional wisdom said Tone Bender guy Gary Hurst designed it but actually it’s an unauthorized copy of a much more obscure circuit called the Harmonic Generator by Gerry Pope of G.P.
