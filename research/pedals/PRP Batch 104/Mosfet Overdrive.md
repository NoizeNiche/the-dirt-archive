## PRP identity

- **Catalog type:** Overdrive

## Research
The Blackstone Mosfet Overdrive began as Jon Blackstone Steele's pedal circuit in 1998 and remains a core Blackstone design. The current builder describes four gain stages, each contributing a small amount of soft clipping, rather than relying on a conventional pair of clipping diodes.

The pedal uses two footswitchable channel/preset settings with recessed controls. The EQ control is a post-distortion mid cut rather than a conventional treble-cut tone control. The red channel's drive control uses separate ranges for different pickup types, and an internal switch can select buffered mode for use later in a pedal chain.

## Features
- Four gain stages with distributed soft clipping
- Two footswitchable channel/preset settings
- Recessed flush-mounted controls
- Post-distortion mid-cut EQ
- Pickup-sensitive drive ranges
- Internal buffered-mode switch
- MXR-sized enclosure
- Built in New York City
