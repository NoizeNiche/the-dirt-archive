# BLAMMO! Electronics - Blues Blaster

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** BLAMMO! Electronics
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive

## Research
BLAMMO!'s Blues Blaster is based on the classic Marshall Blues Breaker-era low-gain drive concept. The maker offers two modes: STOCK for the vintage low-gain voicing and MOD for a higher-gain distortion character capable of heavier crunch and feedback.

The pedal adds a relay-based soft-switching true-bypass system, stronger power filtering, polarity protection, a discrete output buffer and a smaller 125B enclosure with top-mounted jacks.

## Features
- STOCK / MOD modes
- Low-gain boost through mid-gain overdrive/distortion
- Relay true bypass with momentary or latching behavior
- Discrete output buffer
- Panasonic film capacitors
- 125B enclosure with top-mounted jacks
- 9V center-negative
- 36mA current draw
- Handmade in Portland, Oregon
