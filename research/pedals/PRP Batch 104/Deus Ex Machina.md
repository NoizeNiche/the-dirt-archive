# Blakemore Effects - Deus Ex Machina

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Blakemore Effects
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz

## Research
Deus Ex Machina is Blakemore Effects' op-amp fuzz and was the builder's first pedal design. Contemporary descriptions emphasize a thick, highly tweakable fuzz with a clean blend and mid boost, allowing the saturated signal to retain articulation and occupy a more useful position in a band mix.

Blake Hickey's interview identifies the pedal as one of the original three Blakemore designs, alongside Mustang Overdrive and Bi-Polar Octadrive. The brand was later discontinued.

## Features
- Op-amp fuzz architecture
- Clean blend
- Mid boost
- Broad fuzz/tone adjustment
- Handmade construction
