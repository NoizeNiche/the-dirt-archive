# Blackstar Amplification - LT-DIST

## PRP identity

- **Builder:** Blackstar Amplification
- **Catalog type:** Distortion

## Research

The LT-DIST is Blackstar's compact solid-state/classic distortion pedal from the LT series. Blackstar describes it as a classic distortion design with its patented ISF (Infinite Shape Feature), allowing the tone network to move continuously between American and British responses. The control set is Gain, Level, Tone and ISF.

The pedal runs from a standard 9V supply or 9V PP3 battery and uses a conventional input/output layout rather than the valve and speaker-emulated architecture of the older HT pedals.

## Controls

- Gain
- Level
- Tone
- ISF
- Effect on/off
