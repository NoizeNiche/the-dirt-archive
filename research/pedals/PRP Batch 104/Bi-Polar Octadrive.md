# Blakemore Effects - Bi-Polar Octadrive

## PRP identity

- **Builder:** Blakemore Effects
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz / Overdrive

## Research

The Bi-Polar Octadrive is a dual overdrive/octave pedal that can run the drive and octave effects separately or together. Reverb describes it as a 2010s Blakemore design, while the builder's interview explains that the octave section was added to the Mustang platform and required extensive transistor and diode experimentation.

The builder also documented the use of some NOS transistors and diodes in this model. The pedal was hand-built by Blake Hickey, with PCB production handled externally.

## Features

- Overdrive section
- Footswitchable octave section
- Separate or combined operation
- NOS transistors/diodes documented in the build history
