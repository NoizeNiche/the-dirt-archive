# Build Your Own Clone — Li'l Beaver (Opamp)

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Build Your Own Clone

- **Identity:** Small-format Op-Amp Big Muff-family kit.

## What this pedal is
Li'l Beaver (Opamp) is the op-amp-designated member of BYOC's Li'l Beaver family. It is maintained as a distinct public catalog version because the op-amp designation identifies a materially different Big Muff circuit family from the transistor-based versions.
