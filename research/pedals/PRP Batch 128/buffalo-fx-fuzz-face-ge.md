# Buffalo FX — Fuzz Face Ge

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Buffalo FX
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Hand-wired germanium Fuzz Face-style fuzz.

## What this pedal is
Buffalo FX's Fuzz Face Ge keeps the original Fuzz Face circuit concept while adding an LED, 9V DC operation, internal Bias and Pre-Gain trimmers, and matched germanium transistors. The surviving documentation also records a Hammond 1590BB enclosure and true bypass. [1]

## Sound
The pedal is designed as a hot, vintage-voiced germanium Fuzz Face implementation with added control over bias and input gain. The internal trimmers allow the response to move from smoother vintage behavior toward more aggressive, cooked fuzz textures. [1]
