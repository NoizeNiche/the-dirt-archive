# Buffalo FX — Patriot

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Buffalo FX
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Civil War-style Russian Big Muff-derived fuzz.

## What this pedal is
The Patriot is based on the early-1990s Russian “Civil War” Big Muff. Buffalo FX describes it as a large, loud and heavy fuzz with Distortion, Tone and Level controls, plus an internal low-frequency trim and an optional Burr Brown buffer. [1]

## Versions and factory options
- Distortion
- Tone
- Level
- Internal low-frequency roll-off trimmer
- Optional Burr Brown switchable buffer
- True bypass

A later Patriot MkII is documented in Buffalo FX video coverage, but the archive keeps the catalog identity unified unless a materially distinct public version is separately represented. [1][2]

## Sound
Patriot is a huge, low-end-heavy Muff-style fuzz aimed at thick chord work and singing lead tones. The internal low-frequency trim exists specifically because the stock voicing can produce substantial bottom end, while the core circuit is intended to retain smooth treble and long saturation. [1]
