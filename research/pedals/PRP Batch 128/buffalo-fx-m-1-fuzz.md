# Buffalo FX — M-1 Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Buffalo FX
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Six-stage Ram's Head-derived fuzz with active bass and treble EQ.

## What this pedal is
The M-1 is a heavily reworked Ram's Head-style Muff circuit. Buffalo FX adds active Treble and Bass controls and uses six gain stages, with modifications intended to retain substantial gain while keeping noise low. [1]

## Sound
M-1 is built around a large, saturated Ram's Head voice but gives the player much more control over bass and treble than a conventional Muff layout. Its EQ can move from thick, mid-present fuzz toward a more focused lead sound while the six-stage architecture provides substantial gain and sustain. [1]
