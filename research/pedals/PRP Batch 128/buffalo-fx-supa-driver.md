# Buffalo FX — Supa Driver

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Buffalo FX
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Expanded Colorsound Power Boost-style overdrive with extra gain stage.

## What this pedal is
Supa Driver is an evolution of the classic Colorsound Power Boost circuit. Buffalo FX added an extra gain stage and pre-gain control to extend the design beyond its original boost/overdrive range and into more compressed fuzz-like saturation. [1][2]

## Colorways
- Black-finish examples are documented in surviving Reverb listings. [1]

## Sound
Supa Driver keeps the broad EQ and feel of the Power Boost family but adds substantially more gain and compression. Its extra pre-gain stage can push the pedal from booster-like response toward saturated fuzz, while the Bass and Treble controls retain the familiar two-band shaping. [1][2]
