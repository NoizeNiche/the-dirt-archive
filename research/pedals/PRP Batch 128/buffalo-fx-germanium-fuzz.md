# Buffalo FX — Germanium Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Buffalo FX
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Multi-generation germanium Fuzz Face-derived fuzz.

## What this pedal is
Buffalo FX's Germanium Fuzz is a hand-built Fuzz Face-based design with external Pregain and Bias controls. The documented production history includes a limited V1 using Matsushita 2SB175 transistors, followed by V2 and V3 using matched NOS Russian and Texas Instruments 2N404 germanium transistors. [1]

## Sound
The external Pregain control moves the input stage from tighter, overdrive-like behavior toward thicker fuzz, while Bias ranges from smoother sustain into gated or sputtering textures. The result is a broader range than a fixed vintage Fuzz Face, while the different germanium transistor generations are intended to preserve the family character across revisions. [1]
