# Buffalo FX — Stiletto Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Buffalo FX
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Germanium fuzz with a Fuzz Face-to-Rangemaster-style range.

## What this pedal is
Stiletto is a germanium-powered fuzz that Buffalo FX designed to move from Fuzz Face-like tones toward Dallas Rangemaster-style treble boost territory. An external Cut control shifts the frequency emphasis, and a Bias control changes the germanium response from smooth to gated or choked. [1]

## Sound
At one end of its range Stiletto produces warm germanium fuzz, while the Cut control can push it toward brighter treble-boost territory. The Bias control provides additional movement from smooth sustain toward fat, choked or thin gated textures. [1]
