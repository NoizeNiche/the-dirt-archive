# Build Your Own Clone — 855 Drive

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Build Your Own Clone
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** DIY recreation of the Ibanez OD855 Overdrive II.

## What this pedal is
The BYOC 855 Drive is a DIY kit based on the Ibanez OD855, a close relative of the TS808 but with more gain, less mid emphasis and more low end. BYOC describes its version as a near-original recreation with a 4558D chip, 1N60 germanium diodes, tantalum capacitors in the original positions, upgraded passive components and true mechanical bypass. [1]

## Sound
The 855 Drive is intended to reproduce the more open, higher-gain voice of the OD855 rather than simply being a TS808 clone. Its lower-mid emphasis and greater headroom in the bass region help it move toward a fuller overdrive response while retaining the familiar family resemblance of the Japanese compact overdrive format. [1]
