# Audio Monk — Fuzzbeard

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Fuzzbeard
- **Builder:** Audio Monk
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Early bass-oriented fuzz/overdrive first developed under the Nina Electronics name before the Audio Monk rebrand.

## What this pedal is
Effects Database dates the first production Fuzzbeard to the early 2000s and identifies it as the precursor-era pedal made by Anthony Pelosi before the Audio Monk name was adopted. Pelosi later described Fuzzbeard as a smooth overdrive/fuzz designed for bass.

## Sound
Anthony Pelosi described Fuzzbeard as an unusually smooth bass overdrive/fuzz. The intent was to retain musical bass response while adding fuzz coloration rather than simply producing uncontrolled clipping.
