# Audio Monk — Fuzzpotion - Bass Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Fuzzpotion - Bass Fuzz
- **Builder:** Audio Monk
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Bass-oriented fuzz with an internal octave generator and two switchable harmonic modes, developed from the builder's study of a notorious Japanese fuzz-box design. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
Audio Monk's Anthony Pelosi described Fuzzpotion as deliberately over-the-top in clipping and distortion. The circuit adds an octave generator so that the note receives both hard clipping and additional upper harmonics. The design was specifically engineered to work on guitar and bass. [1]

Historical discussion identifies the Japanese reference as the Univox Super-Fuzz family, but the archive treats that as lineage/reference context rather than asserting that Fuzzpotion is an identical schematic clone. [2]

## Controls / switching
- **ON/OFF**
- **TWIGGY / WIGGY** footswitchable harmonic mode.
- **WIGGY:** Heavier harmonic generation.
- **TWIGGY:** Reduced harmonic content for more defined lead work. [1][2]
True bypass is documented in historical product descriptions. [2]

## Circuit architecture
- Hard-clipping fuzz.
- Internal **octave generator**.
- Switchable harmonic density.
- Bass-oriented voicing and design. [1][2]

## Sound
Pelosi describes Fuzzpotion as “over the top” in its clipping behavior. WIGGY adds extremely heavy harmonic generation; TWIGGY reduces that extra harmonic density to improve note definition. Bass was a deliberate target of the design rather than an afterthought. [1][2]
