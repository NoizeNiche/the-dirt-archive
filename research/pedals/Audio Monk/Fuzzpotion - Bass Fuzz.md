# Audio Monk — Fuzzpotion - Bass Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Fuzzpotion - Bass Fuzz
- **Builder:** Audio Monk
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Bass-oriented fuzz with octave generation and switchable harmonic emphasis, developed from a Japanese Super-Fuzz-type reference.

## What this pedal is
Anthony Pelosi described Fuzzpotion as an intentionally over-the-top bass fuzz with an octave generator in the circuit. Contemporary discussion connects its clipping behavior to the Univox Super-Fuzz family, while Pelosi emphasized that the pedal was engineered specifically to work on bass as well as guitar.

## Versions and factory options
### Fuzzpotion production
- On/off switching
- Footswitchable TWIGGY / WIGGY harmonic setting
- Octave generator within the circuit
- 9V battery or pedalboard/wall-wart operation
- True bypass

### TWIGGY / WIGGY behavior
WIGGY emphasizes very heavy harmonic generation. TWIGGY reduces the harmonic content for more defined lead playing.

## Sound
Fuzzpotion is deliberately extreme. The heavy setting combines hard clipping with added octave harmonics, while the alternate setting reduces the extra harmonic density to make individual lines easier to follow. Owners specifically noted the large low-end response in the heavy mode.
