# Coda Effects — Dolmen Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Dolmen Fuzz
- **Builder:** Coda Effects
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Coda Effects Dolmen Fuzz, a Russian Big Muff-inspired fuzz with a mid control, clipping switch, and feedback/oscillation footswitch.

## What this pedal is
Coda Effects states that Dolmen Fuzz is inspired by the Russian Big Muff, particularly the Green Russian version, and was modernized with additional controls and a second footswitch. [1][2]

## Colorways
No complete factory colorway sequence was established.

## Versions and factory options
Documented features include:
- Core fuzz/sustain control.
- Clipping switch changing compression and dynamics.
- Mid control.
- Solstice footswitch for feedback, heavy drone, and experimental sounds.
- Relay true bypass with anti-pop switching.
- Analog signal path.
- Optimized for guitar and bass.
- 9V center-negative operation.
- Less than 50 mA current draw.
- Optional battery operation. [1]

The documented enclosure is approximately 12.1 × 6.6 × 3.5 cm and weighs about 312.9 g. [1]

## Version changes
Coda describes the production pedal as the result of multiple prototypes. The historical crowdfunding announcement established a small assembled series alongside kit and PCB formats. [2]

## Transistor
No exact-model transistor complement was published in the checked manufacturer documentation.

## Diode
The clipping switch changes clipping behavior and compression, but exact diode part numbers were not published.

## Sound
The Dolmen is deliberately low-heavy and aggressive, with strong sustain and a mid control for mix cut. The Solstice switch can push the fuzz into feedback, noise, drone, and experimental territory. Coda explicitly positions it for both guitar and bass. [1][2]
