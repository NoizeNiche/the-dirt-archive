## What this pedal is
MOOER describes Black Secret as a vintage-style distortion with smooth clipping and a band-cut filter for shaping the overall voice. It has two operating modes, Vintage and Turbo. [1]

## Controls and modes
- **Distortion:** drive amount.
- **Filter:** overall tonal shaping.
- **Volume:** output level.
- **Vintage / Turbo:** Turbo provides more than twice the maximum output of Vintage and a more powerful distortion character. [1]
