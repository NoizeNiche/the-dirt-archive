## What this pedal is
MOOER describes Green Mile as its take on the original Japanese green overdrive circuit, with smooth gain and a distinct mid-hump character. [1]

## Controls and modes
- **GAIN**
- **TONE**
- **VOLUME**
- **WARM / HOT** mode. [1]

Warm is voiced as a smoother, more compressed tube-drive style response. Hot raises output and produces a more forceful crunch. [1]
