## What this pedal is
MOOER describes Hustle Drive as an ultra-wide dynamic, open, tube-like drive with two selectable response modes. [1]

## Controls and modes
- **Drive**
- **Tone**
- **Volume**
- **HP / LP** peak mode switch. [1]

HP boosts the bottom end and increases output with a slight upper-mid emphasis around 3.5 kHz. LP adds comparatively little coloration and provides a smoother, lower-output response suited to clean boosting. [1]

## Sound
MOOER describes HP as fuller and more aggressive, with additional output and upper-mid emphasis, while LP keeps the original tone more open and works well as a cleaner boost. [1]
