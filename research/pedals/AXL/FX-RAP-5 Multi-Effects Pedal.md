- **Identity:** Multi-section floor unit combining echo, chorus, distortion with four-band EQ, tube overdrive, and a built-in chromatic tuner.

## What this pedal is
Effects Database describes the FX-RAP-5 as an all-in-one performance floor unit with four main effect sections and per-section level controls. Its documented functions are Analog Echo Delay, Distortion with 4-Band EQ, Tube Overdrive, Chorus, plus a built-in chromatic tuner and padded carrying bag.

## Sound
FX-RAP-5 is intended as a single-footboard solution for several classic dirt and modulation/time-based jobs, including distortion, tube overdrive, chorus, and echo. The separate sections and per-effect level controls make it useful for layering or using each effect independently.
