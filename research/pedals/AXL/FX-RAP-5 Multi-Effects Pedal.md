# AXL — FX-RAP-5 Multi-Effects Pedal

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** FX-RAP-5 Multi-Effects Pedal
- **Builder:** AXL
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive / Multi-effect
- **Identity:** Analog floor multi-effect combining echo/delay, chorus, distortion with EQ, tube-style overdrive and a built-in chromatic tuner. [1][2]

## What this is

The FX-RAP-5 was marketed as a compact multi-effect board replacement rather than a single dirt pedal. Effects Database identifies four major effects sections plus a tuner, with separate level controls for the effect sections. [1]

## Dirt sections

### Distortion
- Distortion control.
- Four-band EQ in the Effects Database description.
- Surviving user documentation expands this to a three-band EQ plus parametric Mid frequency control. [1][2]

### Tube Overdrive
- Dedicated overdrive section.
- User report documents **Hot / Turbo** mode selection.
- Separate Drive, Tone and Level controls. [2]

## Other sections

- **Analog Echo Delay**
- **Chorus**
- **Built-in chromatic tuner**
- Individual level controls per effect.
- Padded carrying bag included. [1][2]

## Routing / outputs

A 2011 user report documents two mono outputs, including a bypass/dry output and affected/wet output, and says the unit can blend multiple sections simultaneously. This is retained as user-report evidence rather than a manufacturer schematic claim. [2]

## Active device / clipping

- The complete internal topology is not published in the reviewed primary sources.
- Exact transistor/op-amp and diode part numbers are unknown.

## Power

- **9V DC** standard pedal power is documented in surviving market/user records. [2]

## Version history

- Effects Database published the model in **2010**.
- Premier Guitar's 2007 new-products page identifies the FX-RAP-5 as a then-new AXL multi-effect, suggesting the broader product was already announced before the later database entry. [1][3]
- No numbered circuit revision established.

## Sound

The FX-RAP-5 is designed for layering multiple analog-style effects rather than one narrowly defined voice. The dirt sections can be used independently or combined with modulation and delay for broader performance textures. A 2011 user report characterizes the distortion as usable 1980s-style high gain and the overdrive as a Tube Screamer-style section with Hot/Turbo modes. [2]

## Deep research verification

Effects Database, Premier Guitar and a surviving 2011 user report were cross-checked. The sources confirm the FX-RAP-5 as an AXL multi-effect with analog echo, chorus, distortion/EQ, tube overdrive and tuner. The archive keeps user-reported control/routing details labeled as such and does not infer internal semiconductor details. [1][2][3]
