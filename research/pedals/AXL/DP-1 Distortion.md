# AXL — DP-1 Distortion

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** DP-1 Distortion
- **Builder:** AXL
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** Compact AXL distortion with Distortion, Tone and Level controls plus a Warm/Heat voicing switch on documented examples. [1][2]

## What this pedal is

Effects Database identifies DP-1 as a distinct AXL distortion model first cataloged in 2007. A surviving exact-model retail listing describes the Warm/Heat switch as changing the tonal character toward a hotter/more aggressive response. [1][2]

## Controls

- **DISTORTION**
- **TONE**
- **LEVEL**
- **WARM / HEAT** voicing switch on the documented unit. [2]

## Sound

The pedal is a compact high-gain distortion with an additional Warm/Heat voicing option. A 2026-used-market listing describes the switch as moving the sound from a conventional distortion setting toward a hotter, more aggressive one. That is recorded as source-described behavior rather than a measured response. [2]
