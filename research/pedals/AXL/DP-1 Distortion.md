## What this pedal is
Effects Database lists the AXL DP-1 as a dedicated distortion pedal. An exact secondary-market photograph also shows the model's three main controls and a front-panel Warm/Heat switching control, providing model-specific visual evidence.

## Sound
The DP-1 is a conventional compact distortion with tone and output level controls around the main distortion setting. The Warm/Heat switch on the exact-model example provides an additional voicing change without turning the pedal into a multi-effect unit.
