# AXL — TOD-9 Tube Overdrive

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** TOD-9 Tube Overdrive

- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** AXL-branded tube-overdrive stompbox documented in the AXL effects catalog and kept separate from the unrelated Maxon TOD-9 True Tube Overdrive. [1]

## What this pedal is

The AXL product catalog identifies TOD-9 as a dedicated tube-overdrive pedal. Because another manufacturer, Maxon, used a similar TOD-9 model name, the archive keeps the AXL record separate and does not merge the two products. [1]
