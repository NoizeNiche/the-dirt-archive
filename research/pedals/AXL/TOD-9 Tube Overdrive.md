## What this pedal is
The AXL catalog identifies TOD-9 Tube Overdrive as one of its dedicated stompbox effects. It is distinct from the separately documented Maxon TOD-9 True Tube Overdrive, despite sharing the model name, and the archive keeps those products separate.
