# Airis Effects — The Savage Drive

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** The Savage Drive
- **Builder:** Airis Effects
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Aggressive, tight overdrive developed for clarity and control with downtuned/high-gain guitar rigs. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
The Savage Drive is a high-output overdrive whose design emphasizes tight low end, clarity, bite and dynamic response. Airis states that the earlier design removed clipping diodes to increase headroom and reduce compression, while the current V5.6 design uses a broader six-control layout with dedicated low-mid and high-mid shaping. [1][2]

## Colorways
- A **Black Skull Graphic** finish is documented in a 2020 Reverb listing. [3]
- Special artwork editions exist, but a complete production colorway history was not reliably documented.

## Versions and factory options
### Version 2.0
- Added the **Tight** control to the earlier four-control design.
- Volume / Dirt / Tight / Bite controls. [1]

### Later/current design
- The current manufacturer page identifies the model as **V5.6**.
- Volume / Savage / Tight / Lo-Mid / Hi-Mid / Bite controls.
- 9-18 V operation, true bypass, hand-built in Canada. [2]

## Version changes
The documented 2.0 revision added the Tight knob to the earlier Savage Drive. Later manufacturer documentation identifies a V5.6 design with a substantially expanded six-control EQ/gain interface; this is retained as a version history of the same catalog model rather than a separate public pedal card. [1][2]

- **Clipping diodes:** Airis states that the Savage Drive design removed clipping diodes to increase clarity, reduce compression and add headroom. [1]

## Sound
Savage Drive is built for tight, aggressive overdrive with enough output and bite to stay articulate in low tunings. The Tight control focuses the low end, while Bite and the later Lo-Mid / Hi-Mid controls shape attack, body and presence for controlled rhythm gain or more cutting high-gain tones. [1][2][3]
