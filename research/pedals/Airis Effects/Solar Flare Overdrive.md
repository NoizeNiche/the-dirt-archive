# Airis Effects — Solar Flare Overdrive

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Solar Flare Overdrive
- **Builder:** Airis Effects
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Dual-mode analog overdrive built around a Klon Centaur-inspired concept, with altered clipping and tone-stack behavior. [1]

## What this pedal is
Solar Flare is a transparent-to-gritty overdrive with two voicings, Gamma and Plasma. Airis describes the design as a Klon-style departure from the more common Tube Screamer approach, with an internal charge pump used in the circuit. [1]

## Sound
Solar Flare is voiced toward transparent, organic overdrive rather than a Tube Screamer-style mid hump. Gamma is described as the more original Centaur-like setting, while Plasma tightens the low end and adds more upper-frequency edge and grit. [1][2]
