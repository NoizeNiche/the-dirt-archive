- **Identity:** Airis Effects Solar Flare, a dual-mode analog overdrive built around a Klon Centaur-inspired concept with altered clipping and tone-stack behavior. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
Solar Flare is an overdrive with two operating voices, Gamma and Plasma. The available documentation describes it as an Airis interpretation of the Klon-style overdrive family rather than a Tube Screamer-style mid-focused design, and notes an internal charge-pump supply section. [1]

The front panel provides Gain, Tone, and Volume controls, with the Gamma/Plasma selection supplying the principal voicing change. A 2015 black-finish Reverb listing provides an independent period specimen for the product identity and appearance. [1][2]

## Sound
Solar Flare is voiced toward transparent, organic overdrive rather than the pronounced mid emphasis commonly associated with Tube Screamer-style designs. Gamma is described as the more original Centaur-like setting, while Plasma tightens the low end and introduces more upper-frequency edge and grit. [1][2]
