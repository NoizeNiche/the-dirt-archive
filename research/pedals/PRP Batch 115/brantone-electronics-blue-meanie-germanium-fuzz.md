## What this pedal is
Brantone describes Blue Meanie as a germanium fuzz based on the 1970s MK3 Tone Bender. Effects Database characterizes it as a large, fat, sustaining germanium fuzz with a central bass-cut control. [1]

## Sound
Blue Meanie is voiced for thick, sustaining germanium fuzz rather than a tight modern distortion texture. The bass-cut control lets the player trim low end as the fuzz moves from broad vintage saturation toward a more focused sound. [1]
