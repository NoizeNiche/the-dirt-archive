# Brantone Electronics — Tonemaster Mk1.5 Germanium Fuzz

## PRP identity

- **Builder:** Brantone Electronics
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Modified recreation of the 1966 Sola Sound Tone Bender MKI.5.

## What this pedal is

Brantone's Tonemaster Mk1.5 is based on the 1966 MKI.5 Tone Bender circuit, modified and tweaked for its preferred sonic result. The pedal uses two matched/tested NOS Mullard OC75 germanium transistors and is built on tagboard in a Hammond 1590BB enclosure with a bullion-gold powder coat. [1]

## Sound

The MK1.5 is deliberately thick and aggressive, with a large wall of fuzz at higher settings. Brantone says the modified circuit was tuned for stronger sonic performance than a stock recreation, pushing the pedal toward extreme saturation and amp-overdriving textures. [1]
