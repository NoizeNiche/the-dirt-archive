# Brantone Electronics — Vintage Tweed Overdrive

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Brantone Electronics
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Three-voice Fender-style tweed amp overdrive/boost.

## What this pedal is
Brantone's Vintage Tweed Overdrive combines three amplifier voices with a separate 10 dB boost. Surviving descriptions identify Fender Twin, Bassman and 5E3-style voices, with an aged Fender tweed tolex finish. [1][2]

## Sound
The pedal is designed to move from light, touch-sensitive tweed-style overdrive into singing sustain, with the three amp voices changing the basic response and the boost providing additional level. Surviving product descriptions position it for blues, rock and country applications. [1]
