## What this pedal is

Brantone designed Green Manalishi to capture the bluesy overdriven tones associated with Peter Green and his early Marshall-era sound. Effects Database documents Volume, Tone and Gain controls plus a high/low power switch and a compact Hammond 1590BB enclosure. [1]

## Sound

The circuit is voiced toward dynamic, British-style overdrive rather than compressed modern distortion. Brantone emphasizes response to pick attack and the ability to work with guitar volume, with the hi/lo power switch used to change the sustain response. [1]

Brantone Electronics Green Manalishi Overdrive
In tribute to Peter, it has been a labour of love and a real pleasure spending hours listening to his tone and playing style, from his days with John Mayall's Bluesbreakers, trying to recapture that magical tone and put it all into a compact Hammond 1590BB enclosure.
The pedal controls feature a simple but very effective volume, tone and gain with an added hi and lo power switch, for the ultimate in sustain.
