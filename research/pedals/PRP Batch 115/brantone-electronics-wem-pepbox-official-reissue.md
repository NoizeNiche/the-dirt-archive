## What this pedal is
Brantone's WEM Pepbox is an authorized reissue made with Charlie Watkins. The documented production used paired vintage transistors and was presented as a small-batch revival of the original WEM Pepbox design. [1]

## Sound
The reissue is intended to retain the rough, saturated “garage” character associated with the Pepbox while using selected vintage components. The surviving description places it in medium-to-saturated fuzz territory. [1]
