# Brantone Electronics — Woodstock - Silicon Fuzz Face

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Brantone Electronics
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Silicon Fuzz Face-style pedal inspired by the sound associated with Woodstock 1969.

## What this pedal is
Brantone's Woodstock is a silicon take on the classic Fuzz Face sound associated with Woodstock-era guitar tones. The documented design uses a matched pair of silicon transistors. [1]

## Sound
Woodstock is intended to provide singing fuzz and sustain without the darker character often associated with some vintage germanium circuits. Brantone describes it as responsive to both pickup type and the guitar's volume control, allowing cleaner textures as the volume is rolled back. [1]
