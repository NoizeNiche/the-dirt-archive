# Braking Train Pedals — Frequency Control Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Braking Train Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Shin-ei Companion FY-2-inspired fuzz with sweepable mid frequency and optional clean blend.

## What this pedal is
The Frequency Control Fuzz is inspired by the Shin-ei Companion FY-2. It keeps the FY-2's pronounced mid scoop while adding control over the mid frequency, from roughly 100 Hz to above 1 kHz, and a mode that bypasses the tone control for a fuller-range fuzz. [1]

## Sound
The circuit preserves the aggressive, scooped character associated with the Companion FY-2 while making the center of the mid scoop adjustable. The clean-blend version can retain low-end fundamentals while the full-range mode removes the normal tone-control limitation. [1]
