# Brantone Electronics — ToneMaster Mk2 Germanium Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Brantone Electronics
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Recreation of the 1966 Sola Sound Tone Bender MK2 Professional.

## What this pedal is
Brantone's Tonemaster MK2 recreates the 1966 Sola Sound Tone Bender MK2 Professional, with the original circuit used as the starting point and a biasing trimmer added. It uses three selected NOS Mullard germanium transistors. [1]

## Colorways
No single factory colorway was reliably established.

## Versions and factory options
- Three selected/tested NOS Mullard germanium transistors
- Added biasing trimmer
- PP3 9V battery or standard power brick
- No numbered factory revision verified [1]

## Version changes
The added biasing trimmer is a documented design change from the original reference circuit. [1]

## Transistor
- **Technology:** Germanium.
- **Device:** NOS Mullard germanium transistors; MusicRadar identifies the review example as 1967 NOS Mullard OC84. [1][2]
## Diode
Exact clipping diode/device not publicly documented.

## Sound
The Tonemaster MK2 is voiced as a classic British germanium fuzz with long sustain and a warm, saturated response. MusicRadar places it in the classic MKII/Sola Sound family and notes its strong vintage character. [1][2]
