## Sound
The Vixen includes Treble, Bass, and Presence controls for precise tonal shaping, suiting various playing styles with its clean boost, fat leads, and medium crunch capabilities.
What Foxpedal say about the Vixen Out of Production - all remaining units sold The Vixen is a stunning dimensional overdrive with loads of flexibility and an incredible dynamic range.
At low gain in the N3 mode, with volume at unity and all other settings at noon, The Vixen works very nicely as an “amp-tone sparkle booster.” It enhances your amp to simply become “more” of itself by giving it a slightly beefed up tonal profile by clarifying, but not coloring, your amp's sound.
