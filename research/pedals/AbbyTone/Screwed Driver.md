# AbbyTone — Screwed Driver

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Screwed Driver
- **Builder:** AbbyTone
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** AbbyTone Screwed Driver, a Brooklyn-made overdrive from the brand's 2004-2005 production period.

## What this pedal is
Screwed Driver is a compact overdrive built around **Gain** and **Level** controls plus a three-position **Treble / Normal / Full** switch. Effects Database's source describes the Normal position as capable of clean boost at low gain and smooth natural overdrive as gain rises; Treble emphasizes the upper range; Full gives a thicker full-spectrum overdrive. [1]

## Controls
- **Gain**
- **Level**
- **Treble / Normal / Full** three-position switch. [1]

The pedal is also described as responding strongly to pick attack and guitar volume changes. [1]

## Production history
Effects Database identifies AbbyTone pedals as products of **Jonathan Segura in Brooklyn, New York**, made during **2004-2005**. The brand was named after Segura's English Bulldog, Abby. [2]

This production window is the brand's documented period, not a claim that every individual Screwed Driver unit was manufactured on a known date within that range.

## Sound
The Normal setting can cover clean boost through smooth natural overdrive as Gain is increased. Treble adds a more upper-frequency-focused character, while Full produces a thicker, broader drive. The source also emphasizes touch and guitar-volume responsiveness. [1]
