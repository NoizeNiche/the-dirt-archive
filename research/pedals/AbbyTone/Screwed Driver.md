## What this pedal is
Screwed Driver is a compact overdrive with Gain and Level controls plus a three-position **Treble / Normal / Full** switch. Effects Database's surviving description says the switch changes both the tonal emphasis and the effective gain range, letting the pedal work as a clean boost, smooth overdrive, treble-leaning drive or thicker full-spectrum drive. ## Sound
Screwed Driver can behave almost like a clean boost with the gain reduced, then become a smooth natural overdrive as gain rises. The Treble setting emphasizes upper frequencies, while Full opens the sound into a thicker full-spectrum drive, and the circuit is notably responsive to how hard the player picks and how the guitar volume is set. [1]
