# CBC Pedals — FY-2 Companion

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** FY-2 Companion
- **Builder:** CBC Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** CBC Pedals reproduction of the Shin-Ei FY-2 Companion fuzz.

## What this pedal is
CBC Pedals produced a version of the Shin-Ei FY-2 Companion as part of its classic-series recreations. In CBC Pedals' own 2013 demonstration, the builder describes the unit as an FY-2 Companion clone and identifies original 2SC536 transistors, an internal boost circuit, and an additional Scoop control on that build. [1][2]

## Sound
The FY-2 Companion is a compact, saturated fuzz voice derived from the Shin-Ei design. CBC's added Scoop control and boost stage are intended to give the reproduction more flexibility than a strictly minimal recreation, while the underlying character remains the aggressive, compressed Companion-style fuzz. [1][2]
