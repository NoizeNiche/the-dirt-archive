# CBC Pedals — FY-2 Companion

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** FY-2 Companion

- **Catalog type:** Fuzz

## What this pedal is
CBC's own demonstration identifies the pedal as an **FY-2 Companion clone** and documents additional design work beyond a strict original-form recreation, including an internal boost circuit and a Scoop control. [1]
