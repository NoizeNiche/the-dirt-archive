# CBC Pedals — The Drive

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** The Drive
- **Builder:** CBC Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Handwired CBC Pedals exact-circuit clone of the Hermida Audio Zendrive. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
Surviving Reverb listings describe The Drive as a **Zendrive clone**, with the original four-control arrangement: Volume, Drive/Gain, Tone and Presence/Voice. One listing documents a socketed op-amp and an **AD712** installed in the demonstrated build. [1]

## Controls / hardware
- **VOLUME**
- **DRIVE / GAIN**
- **TONE**
- **PRESENCE / VOICE**
- Socketed op-amp in the documented build.
- 3PDT true bypass.
- Neutrik jacks.
- Alpha pots.
- Boss-style center-negative 9V supply. [1][2]
