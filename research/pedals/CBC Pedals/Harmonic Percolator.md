# CBC Pedals — Harmonic Percolator

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Harmonic Percolator
- **Builder:** CBC Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** CBC Pedals reproduction of the classic Harmonic Percolator fuzz/distortion circuit.

## What this pedal is
CBC Pedals is documented in the archive as a builder of recreations of vintage and out-of-production effects. Its Harmonic Percolator is cataloged as a builder-specific reproduction of the classic Percolator design rather than as an original Interfax/Oberheim production unit. [1][2]

## Historical design context
The Harmonic Percolator is known for an asymmetric, intermodulating fuzz/distortion response that can move from comparatively compressed, warm textures into more aggressive and chaotic harmonic behavior. The archive's historical Percolator research describes hybrid silicon/germanium implementations and selectable clipping as characteristics of one modern recreation, but those details are **not** assigned to CBC Pedals without CBC-specific evidence. [2][3]
