# CBC Pedals — Harmonic Percolator

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Harmonic Percolator
- **Builder:** CBC Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz / Distortion
- **Identity:** CBC Pedals reproduction of the Interfax Harmonic Percolator, with surviving CBC demonstration material specifically identifying a “Percolator” clone. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
CBC's builder record places the company among handwired recreators of vintage effects and lists the Interfax Harmonic Percolator among its reproduced circuits. A CBC demonstration also identifies its own **Percolator clone**. [1][2]

## Circuit lineage
- **Primary reference:** Interfax Harmonic Percolator.
- The historical Percolator is an unusual asymmetric fuzz/distortion with intermodulating harmonic behavior.
- Historical component details are intentionally not copied into the CBC record without CBC-specific evidence.

## Sound
The historical Percolator family is harmonically complex, asymmetric and capable of moving between compressed distortion and rough fuzz. CBC's reproduction is intended to preserve that identity, while the documented diode-lift option can change the clipping behavior on equipped units. [1][2]
