## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** ODR1

- **Catalog type:** Fuzz / Overdrive
- **Identity:** Skrydstrup R&D ODR1, later associated with Caveman Audio's founder Steen Skrydstrup.

## What this pedal is
The ODR1 is a Skrydstrup R&D overdrive pedal originally developed to add a Plexi-style feel to the company's OD50 amplifier, then released as a standalone stompbox. Effects Database describes it as a dynamic, tube-like overdrive with a touch of fuzz and an unusual tone control that functions as high-frequency damping rather than a conventional treble/bass control. [1][2]

## Versions and factory options
### ODR1 production model
- Controls include **Level, Drive, and Tone**. [1]
- The **Tone** control is described as a high-frequency damping control; fully open, the damping is effectively inactive. [1]
- The circuit uses **old germanium diodes** according to Effects Database. [1]
- ODR1 was part of Skrydstrup R&D's high-end Danish effects line used by professional touring and studio players. [2][3]

## Sound
The ODR1 is voiced as a dynamic, amp-like overdrive with a Plexi-oriented character and a small amount of fuzz texture at higher drive settings. Its tone control is primarily a high-frequency damping control, so the pedal can move from open and bright toward smoother, more controlled top end without behaving like a conventional guitar-pedal tone stack. [1][2]
