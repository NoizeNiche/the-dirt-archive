## What this pedal is
The **Drunk Beaver Kavun MOSFET Drive** is a MOSFET-based overdrive with a dedicated three-band EQ and separate Level and Drive controls. Contemporary coverage identifies the control set as **Bass, Mid, Treble, Level, and Drive**. [1]

The product is associated with **Denys Toporchuk** as a signature model. The archive preserves that signature relationship as part of the model context without treating it as a sonic or technical claim. [1]

## Controls
- **Bass:** controls the low-frequency portion of the signal.
- **Mid:** controls the midrange.
- **Treble:** controls the high-frequency portion.
- **Level:** overall output.
- **Drive:** amount of overdrive/gain. [1]

The three-band EQ is a defining part of the interface. Contemporary coverage describes the pedal as a particularly shapeable MOSFET overdrive, but those are reviewer/player observations rather than laboratory measurements. [1]
