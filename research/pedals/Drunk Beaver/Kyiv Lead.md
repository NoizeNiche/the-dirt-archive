## What this pedal is

The **Drunk Beaver Kyiv Lead** is an evolved take on the **Boss SD-2 Lead channel**. Drunk Beaver describes it as moving beyond the original's single tone control with a broader EQ section. Contemporary coverage identifies controls for **Gain, Treble, Mid, Bass, and Presence**. [1][2]

The archive keeps **Overdrive** as the catalog type because the exact-model product page places Kyiv Lead in Drunk Beaver's overdrive/dirt catalog and describes it as an overdrive derived from the SD-2 Lead channel. [1]

## Circuit and version context

Contemporary technical coverage reports **five cascaded gain stages**, JRC4580 op-amps, and a hybrid **Germanium / Silicon / LED** clipping arrangement. These are secondary technical observations from the exact model and are retained as such rather than presented as a universal factory BOM for every production unit. [4]

The product page identifies Kyiv Lead as a **Pedal Drop** model and currently marks it **discontinued**. [1]
