# Drunk Beaver — Mykolaiv Soft Blooming

## Surface catalog record

- **Catalog type:** Overdrive

- **Deep research status:** Identity/alias audit
- **Identity basis:** Contemporary Drunk Beaver coverage identifies a **Pedal Drop #35** called **Mykolaiv Soft Blooming / Mykolaiv Broadcast Attack**, described as a soft-blooming magnetic saturation overdrive. The archive's current shorter name may be an alias of that product, but the naming relationship should be verified before the catalog is consolidated.

## Identity finding
Later Drunk Beaver coverage lists **PD #35: Mykolaiv Soft Blooming Magnetic Saturation Overdrive** and identifies it as **discontinued**. Another contemporary article refers to the product as **Pedal Drop #35 The Mykolaiv Broadcast Attack soft blooming magnetic saturator**. [1][2]

Because the archive record is named simply **Mykolaiv Soft Blooming**, these sources provide strong evidence of the product family but do not prove whether “Broadcast Attack” was a subtitle, an alternate catalog name, an earlier name, or a separate edition.

The archive therefore preserves the current record and flags the relationship for a future catalog-identity decision rather than silently renaming or merging it.

## Product character
Contemporary coverage describes the pedal as a **soft-blooming magnetic saturation** design, with a natural, soft-attack breakup behavior. Guitar Pedal X specifically groups it among Drunk Beaver's overdrives and notes its discontinued status in the 2026 selection. [1][2]

Those sound descriptions are reviewer observations, not measured frequency-response data.

## Historical status
Guitar Pedal X's 2026 Drunk Beaver overdrive list marks **PD #35 Mykolaiv Soft Blooming** as **discontinued**. This is useful lifecycle evidence, but it is not a manufacturer discontinuation bulletin. [1]

The same source places it in Drunk Beaver's Pedal Drop series. That series history should remain separate from the mainline Drunk Beaver catalog because the Pedal Drop designation is part of the product's historical identity. [2]

## Technical evidence boundary
The reviewed sources do not establish a complete schematic, exact magnet/material specification, transistor or op-amp inventory, or factory BOM. The phrase **magnetic saturation** is retained as the product's documented design description rather than converted into an unsupported component claim.
