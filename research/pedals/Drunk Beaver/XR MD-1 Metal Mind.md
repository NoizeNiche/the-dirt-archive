## What this pedal is

The **Drunk Beaver XR MD-1 Metal Mind** is a high-gain distortion in the XR series. Drunk Beaver describes it as a modernized take on the **Exar MD-03 and MD-04**, which were Polish interpretations of the dual-gain-stage Metal Zone-style architecture. [1][2]

The current product description identifies modern low-noise op-amps, robust power-supply filtering, true-bypass switching, and an active three-band EQ with fixed midrange voicing. [1]

## Controls

The documented control set includes:
- **Metal:** gain.
- **Gyrator Lows:** approximately 86 Hz.
- **Mids:** approximately 533 Hz.
- **High-Pass Filter:** approximately 7.2 kHz.
- **Level:** output. [1][2]

These values are preserved as the product's documented control frequencies, not as a claim about a laboratory sweep under every hardware condition.
