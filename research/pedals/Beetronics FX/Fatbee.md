# Beetronics FX - Fatbee

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Fatbee
- **Builder:** Beetronics FX
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Original JFET overdrive designed with Howard Davis for amp-like breakup and touch response.

## What this pedal is
Beetronics describes Fatbee as the first Babee Series pedal and an original JFET overdrive designed in collaboration with Howard Davis. The current version uses three controls: Weight, Flavor, and Honey.

## Colorways
- Standard production and multiple Custom Shop/limited finishes are documented.
- Cosmetic editions are treated as colorways rather than separate circuits.

## Versions and factory options
### Fatbee
- Weight (volume)
- Flavor (tone)
- Honey (gain)
- Original JFET circuit
- 9V DC center-negative
- True bypass
- Approximately 36mA current draw

## Sound
Fatbee is designed for natural breakup, smooth drive, and dynamic pick response rather than extreme distortion.
