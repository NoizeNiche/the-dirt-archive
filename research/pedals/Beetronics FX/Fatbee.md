# Beetronics FX — Fatbee

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** Fatbee
- **Builder:** Beetronics FX
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Original JFET overdrive developed with Howard Davis, using Weight, Flavor and Honey controls. [1][2]

## What this pedal is

Beetronics describes Fatbee as the first Babee Series pedal and an original JFET design developed with Howard Davis. The circuit is aimed at natural amp-like breakup and touch response rather than extreme distortion. [1]

## Controls

- **WEIGHT:** Output/volume.
- **FLAVOR:** Tone/voicing.
- **HONEY:** Gain. [1]

## Circuit / active device

- **Technology:** JFET-based overdrive.
- Exact JFET part number is not publicly documented.

## Diode / clipping

- Exact clipping/protection device and part number are not publicly documented.

## Power

- **9V DC, center-negative**.
- Approximately **36 mA** current draw is documented in the catalog material. [1]

## Sound

Fatbee is intended for dynamic, natural breakup. Weight controls level, Flavor shapes the tonal profile and Honey increases JFET gain, taking the pedal from warmed-clean sounds through fuller overdrive. [1][2]
