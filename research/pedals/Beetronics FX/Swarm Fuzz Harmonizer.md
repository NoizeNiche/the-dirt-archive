# Beetronics FX — Swarm Fuzz Harmonizer

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** Swarm Fuzz Harmonizer
- **Builder:** Beetronics FX
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz / Harmonizer
- **Identity:** Fuzz-based pitch harmonizer that generates square-wave harmonies at nine selectable intervals spanning two octaves, with independent upper/lower harmony and modulation controls. [1][2]

## Controls / architecture

- **WORKER:** Input/fuzz level.
- **SPECIES:** Selects one of nine harmony intervals.
- **QUEEN:** Upper-harmony level.
- **DRONE:** Lower-harmony level.
- **FLIGHT:** Modulation/glide behavior.
- **STING:** Additional modulation/response control.
- **MASTER:** Overall output.
- Fuzz square-wave core with pitch-shifted harmony generation. [1]

## Circuit architecture

- Input is converted to a square-wave fuzz signal.
- Harmony generation provides **nine interval choices over two octaves**.
- Queen and Drone independently set the relative harmony levels.
- Flight and Sting alter the motion/response of those harmony layers. [1][2]

## Power

- **9V DC, center-negative**.
- Approximately **7 mA** current draw is documented.
- True bypass.
- Handmade in California. [1]

## Version history

- Earlier production and current Royal Series presentations retain the same basic Worker/Species/Queen/Drone/Flight/Sting/Master concept.
- Bee Stock and Custom Shop editions are treated as cosmetic variants.
- No numbered electrical revision established.

## Sound

Swarm combines a fuzzy square-wave source with harmonized pitch layers. It can produce usable octave-like reinforcement but is also capable of synthetic, unstable and highly animated textures as the harmony intervals and Flight/Sting controls are pushed. [1][2]
