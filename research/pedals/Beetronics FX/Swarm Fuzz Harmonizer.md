# Beetronics FX - Swarm Fuzz Harmonizer

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Swarm Fuzz Harmonizer
- **Builder:** Beetronics FX
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Analog square-wave fuzz harmonizer with nine interval choices across two octaves and interactive modulation.

## What this pedal is
Swarm turns the input into a square-wave fuzz signal and creates pitch-shifted harmony layers from it. Beetronics provides nine interval choices across two octaves and separate controls for the upper and lower harmony voices.

## Colorways
- Standard Royal Series and multiple Bee Stock/Custom Shop finishes are documented.
- Custom finishes are treated as cosmetic editions unless a circuit difference is established.

## Versions and factory options
### Swarm Fuzz Harmonizer
- Worker input/fuzz level
- Species selects nine harmony intervals
- Queen and Drone harmony levels
- Flight and Sting modulation controls
- Master output
- 9V DC center-negative
- Approximately 7mA current draw
- True bypass
- Handmade in California

## Version changes
The Swarm has appeared in earlier production presentations and the current Royal Series. The available sources preserve the same core Worker/Species/Queen/Drone/Flight/Sting/Master control concept; no numbered factory circuit revision was established.

## Sound
Swarm combines a fuzzy square-wave core with pitch-shifted harmonies, ranging from usable octave-like reinforcement to deliberately unstable, synth-like textures. Queen and Drone set the relative harmony levels, while Flight and Sting make the harmonies glide, wobble, or react more abruptly to playing.
