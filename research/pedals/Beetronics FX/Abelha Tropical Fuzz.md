# Beetronics FX — Abelha Tropical Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Abelha Tropical Fuzz
- **Builder:** Beetronics FX
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Tropicália-inspired fuzz with three voices, two-band EQ, and a high-pass Tropical Mode. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
Beetronics names three fuzz voices: **Polen, Nectar and Honey**. Tropical Mode applies a high-pass filter intended to reproduce the sharper response associated with running fuzz directly into a recording-console input. [1][2]

## Controls / modes
- **BUZZ:** Fuzz amount.
- **LOUD:** Output level.
- **HI:** High-frequency EQ.
- **LO:** Low-frequency EQ.
- **POLEN / NECTAR / HONEY:** Three fuzz voices.
- **TROPICAL MODE:** High-pass/console-style voicing.
- Footswitch behavior can be configured for the Tropical function. [1]

## Circuit / topology
- Analog fuzz platform.
- Three selectable fuzz voices inside one circuit design.
- Tropical Mode changes the input/low-frequency response rather than representing a separate pedal.

## Sound
Polen is presented as the more sputtery/saggy voice, Nectar as fuller and rounder, and Honey as more contained. Tropical Mode sharpens the response with a high-pass effect, while Hi/Lo provide broad tonal control. [1][2]
