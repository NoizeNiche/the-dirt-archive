# Beetronics FX - Abelha Tropical Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Abelha Tropical Fuzz
- **Builder:** Beetronics FX
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Modern tropical fuzz with three distinct fuzz voices, two-band EQ, and a switchable high-pass “Tropical Mode.”

## What this pedal is
Abelha means “bee” in Portuguese. Beetronics describes the Abelha as a Tropicália-inspired fuzz with three voices: Polen, Nectar, and Honey. A separate Tropical Mode adds a high-pass filter intended to recreate the sharp, raw feel of plugging fuzz directly into a recording console. citeturn703842view4turn320193youtube111

## Sound
Polen is the most sputtery and saggy voice, Nectar is rounder and larger, and Honey is somewhat more contained while retaining versatility. Tropical Mode adds a treble-forward, direct-to-console edge, while the Hi and Lo controls can radically reshape the fuzz's frequency balance. citeturn703842view4turn320193youtube111
