## What this pedal is
Effects Database describes FZ-BRK as a **CMOS fuzz** designed to defy conventional volume cleanup and dynamic fuzz behavior. It is intentionally brutal, compressed, fuzzy, and robotic, with gritty square-wave textures. [1]
