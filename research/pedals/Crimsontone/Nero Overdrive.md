# Crimsontone — Nero Overdrive

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Nero Overdrive
- **Builder:** Crimsontone
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Crimsontone's hand-wired overdrive based on an LM741 op-amp design.

## What this pedal is
Nero is a hand-wired overdrive/distortion using an **LM741 op-amp**. Crimsontone describes the circuit as a hybrid of the 1970s script-logo MXR Distortion+ and the MXR Micro Amp, with no diode clipping. [1][2]

## Colorways
- Effects Database documents standard examples with **black or white knobs**. [1]
- Cosmetic knob or enclosure differences are treated as variants unless a circuit change is documented.

## Versions and factory options
### Standard Nero
- Volume
- Gain
- Tone
- Hand-wired circuitry
- LM741 op-amp
- No diode clipping [1][2]

### Nero Dual Gain
A later Dual Gain version added a second gain control and A/B switching while retaining shared Volume and Tone controls. Used-market coverage documents separate A and B gain settings within the same Nero family. [3]

## Version changes
The archive keeps Standard Nero and Dual Gain as documented factory variations under one parent identity. Effects Database separately identifies the original product information and later Dual Gain examples. [1][3]

## Diode
- **Type:** **No diode clipping** in the documented Nero design. [1][2]
- **Exact part:** Not applicable to the primary clipping method.

## Sound
Nero is designed to move from a warm, relatively transparent boost into progressively stronger overdrive and distortion. The interactive Gain and Volume controls provide broad level/gain combinations, while the Tone control is described as a low-pass filter that can reduce high-frequency content. [1][2][3]
