## What this pedal is
Nero is a hand-wired overdrive/distortion using an **LM741 op-amp**. Crimsontone describes the circuit as a hybrid of the 1970s script-logo MXR Distortion+ and the MXR Micro Amp, with no diode clipping. [1][2]

- Volume

- Tone

- LM741 op-amp
- No diode clipping [1][2]

### Nero Dual Gain
A later Dual Gain version added a second gain control and A/B switching while retaining shared Volume and Tone controls. Used-market coverage documents separate A and B gain settings within the same Nero family. [3]

## Version changes

## Sound
Nero is designed to move from a warm, relatively transparent boost into progressively stronger overdrive and distortion. The interactive Gain and Volume controls provide broad level/gain combinations, while the Tone control is described as a low-pass filter that can reduce high-frequency content. [1][2][3]
