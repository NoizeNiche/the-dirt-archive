# Crimsontone — Rosso Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Rosso Fuzz
- **Builder:** Crimsontone
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz / Overdrive
- **Identity:** Crimsontone's three-stage silicon fuzz with soft diode clipping.

## What this pedal is
Rosso is a three-stage **silicon transistor fuzz** designed to cover a wide range from mild overdrive and dirty boost through thick fuzz. Crimsontone describes the circuit as hand-wired and specifically identifies soft diode clipping. [1][2]

## Sound
Rosso is designed to remain usable as an overdrive when the Gain is reduced, then expand into thick, sustained and articulate fuzz as the gain rises. The Tone control smooths harsh highs or brightens a darker rig, while the Volume control can operate as a cut/boost stage rather than simply reaching silence at its minimum setting. [1][3]
