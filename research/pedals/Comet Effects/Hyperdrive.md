## What this pedal is
The Comet Hyperdrive is a dark-finished boutique overdrive from Comet Effects, a small English builder based in Devon. A 2008 MusicRadar review describes it as a versatile, relatively restrained overdrive that remains articulate when used into an already driven amplifier. [1]

## Controls
- **Level**
- **Tone**
- **Drive** [1]

The pedal also supports an internal 9V battery and an optional external power supply. [1]
