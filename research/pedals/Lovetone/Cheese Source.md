# Lovetone - Cheese Source

## PRP identity

- **Builder:** Lovetone
- **Pedal:** Cheese Source
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz / Overdrive
- **Identity:** Lovetone two-in-one combining Big Cheese fuzz and Brown Source overdrive.

## What this pedal is

The Cheese Source is Lovetone's two-in-one dirt pedal combining the complete Big Cheese and Brown Source circuits in one enclosure. Effects Database identifies it as a fuzz/overdrive.

## Controls

### Cheese section
- **Curds**
- **Tone**
- **Off / 1 / 2 / Cheese**
- **Whey**

### Source section
- **Drive**
- **Tone**
- **Volume**
- **Off / 1 / 2 / 3**

## Circuit / architecture

- Dual analog dirt architecture.
- Fuzz side derives from Big Cheese.
- Overdrive side derives from Brown Source.
- Exact semiconductor part numbers were not established.

## Power

RockBoard PedalPedia documents the Cheese Source as **9V DC, center negative**.

## History

Lovetone introduced Cheese Source as a two-for-one offering of the Big Cheese and Brown Source circuits. Effects Database's timeline places it in **1998**. After Lovetone ceased normal production, Dan Coggins later built additional small runs through Dinosaural-era servicing.

## Sound

The combined pedal retains the Big Cheese's wide fuzz range and the Brown Source's dynamic, warm overdrive character, while allowing the two sections to operate independently or together.
