# Lovetone - Big Cheese

## PRP identity

- **Builder:** Lovetone
- **Pedal:** Big Cheese
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Original Lovetone Big Cheese fuzz.

## What this pedal is

The Big Cheese was one of Lovetone's founding dirt pedals, developed in the 1990s and sold as part of the company's early flagship range. Effects Database preserves the model as a fuzz with four controls.

## Controls

- **Curds**
- **Tone**
- **Off / 1 / 2 / Cheese**
- **Whey**

The mode control provides multiple fuzz voices, including the named Cheese setting.

## Circuit / lineage

Lovetone describes the Big Cheese as an original early fuzz rather than a direct clone of a single commercial circuit. Exact semiconductor part numbers were not established in the reviewed sources.

## History

Lovetone was founded in **1995** by Dan Coggins and Vlad Naslas. The Big Cheese was one of the company's first four pedals and remained in the range through the company's eventual discontinuation.

## Sound

Lovetone describes the Big Cheese as harmonically rich, buzzy and capable of moving from gated breakup to heavy fuzz. It can also be used as a synth-like fuzz texture.
