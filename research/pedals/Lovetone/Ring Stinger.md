# Lovetone - Ring Stinger

## PRP identity

- **Builder:** Lovetone
- **Pedal:** Ring Stinger
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz / Distortion
- **Identity:** Lovetone transformer-matrix ring modulator with octave-up fuzz and germanium distortion functions.

## What this pedal is

Effects Database documents Ring Stinger as a ring modulator, octave-up fuzz and dirt box with foot control, LFO control and voltage-control options.

## Circuit / architecture

Lovetone describes the Ring Stinger as using a **transformer-matrix modulator** rather than a generic modulator IC. Effects Database documents **germanium distortion** and octave-fuzz operation. Exact transistor and diode part numbers were not established.

## History

Lovetone's timeline places Ring Stinger in **1998**, following the company's first-generation products.

## Sound

The design combines ring modulation with octave-up fuzz and dirt, and the extensive oscillator/LFO controls allow everything from pitched octave textures to metallic ring-modulated sounds.
