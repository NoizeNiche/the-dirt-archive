# Lovetone - Brown Source

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Lovetone
- **Pedal:** Brown Source
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Original Lovetone Brown Source overdrive.

## What this pedal is
Brown Source was one of Lovetone's original early pedals. Effects Database documents it as an overdrive with a four-control interface.

## Controls
- **Drive**
- **Tone**
- **Off / 1 / 2 / 3**
- **Volume**

The mode control provides multiple voicing/response settings.

## Circuit / lineage
The Brown Source is an original Lovetone design associated with a late-1960s/early-1970s "brown sound" concept. It is not documented as a direct clone of one specific commercial pedal. Exact semiconductor devices were not established.

## History
Lovetone was founded in 1995. Brown Source was among the first four pedals released by the company and remained part of its historical catalog.

## Sound
Lovetone describes the Brown Source as dynamic, touch-sensitive and warm, with low-fizz amplifier-like breakup. The circuit is also documented as responsive to filtering and useful with wah.
