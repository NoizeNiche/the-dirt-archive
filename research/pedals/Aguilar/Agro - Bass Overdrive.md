# Aguilar — Agro - Bass Overdrive

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Agro - Bass Overdrive
- **Builder:** Aguilar
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Aguilar bass overdrive designed to reproduce the saturation character of the AG 500 amplifier while retaining bass low end. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
AGRO is a bass-focused overdrive with Level, Saturation, Presence and Contour controls. Aguilar describes it as ranging from warm, tube-like overdrive through heavier distortion, with bass-specific EQ controls designed to preserve low-end weight. [1]

## Colorways
- The original/current black enclosure is documented.
- A redesigned black AGRO V2 was introduced in 2023 with a new chassis and appearance. [2][3]

## Versions and factory options
### AGRO
- Level
- Saturation
- Presence
- Contour
- Gig-saver bypass
- 9 V DC power [1][2]

### AGRO V2
- New chassis/look introduced in 2023
- same core overdrive concept
- Level, Saturation, Presence and Contour controls [3]

## Version changes
Aguilar and contemporary coverage document the 2023 AGRO V2 as a new look/chassis version while retaining the core overdrive function. The archive keeps the base AGRO and V2 distinction explicit rather than treating a V2 image as a base-model photo. [3]

## Sound
AGRO ranges from warm, tube-like bass overdrive to heavier distortion while retaining low-end content. Presence works around 4 kHz and Contour provides broad midrange shaping, allowing either a more scooped or more aggressive response. [1][2]
