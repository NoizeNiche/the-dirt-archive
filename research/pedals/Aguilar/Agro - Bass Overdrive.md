## What this pedal is
AGRO is a bass-focused overdrive centered on four controls: Level, Saturation, Presence, and Contour. Aguilar describes it as capable of warm, tube-like overdrive through more aggressive distortion, while the bass-oriented voicing is intended to keep the low end intact. [1]

The Presence control works in the upper-frequency region, while Contour provides broad midrange shaping. Together they give the pedal more control over the balance between a fuller/scooped response and a more forward, aggressive sound. [1][2]

## Sound
AGRO ranges from warm, tube-like bass overdrive into heavier distortion while retaining substantial low-end content. Aguilar's Presence and Contour controls provide complementary high-frequency and midrange shaping, allowing the player to move between a broader, more scooped response and a more aggressive forward character.
