## What this pedal is
Storm King is a compact bass dirt pedal whose all-analog circuit is derived from Aguilar's Tone Hammer Drive section. Aguilar describes the result as covering warm, tube-like saturation, cutting distortion, and more aggressive fuzz while keeping the form factor small. [1]

The four front-panel controls are Gain, Master, Shape, and Kick. Shape is used to alter the character of the distortion, while the Kick switch adds presence and punch to the response. [1][2]

## Sound
Storm King progresses from warm, tube-like saturation into cutting distortion and then more heavily compressed, aggressive fuzz as Gain increases. Shape changes the overall distortion character, while Kick is used to add extra presence and punch rather than functioning as a conventional continuous tone control. [1][2]

## Historical documentation notes
Premier Guitar's introduction describes Storm King as an Aguilar distortion/fuzz aimed at bass applications, while Aguilar's current product material emphasizes its Tone Hammer-derived all-analog drive architecture. [1][3]
