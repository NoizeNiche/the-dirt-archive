## What this pedal is
Storm King is a compact bass distortion/fuzz pedal built around an all-analog circuit derived from Aguilar's Tone Hammer Drive section. Aguilar describes it as covering a broad range from warm, tube-like saturation through cutting distortion and aggressive fuzz. [1]

## Sound
Storm King moves from warm, tube-like saturation into cutting distortion and then heavily compressed, aggressive fuzz territory as the Gain control is advanced. The Shape control sweeps the character of the distortion, while the Kick switch adds presence and punch to the output. [1][2]
