# Cajita Stompboxes — Bdi.ts with Overdrive

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Cajita Stompboxes
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Bass preamp/overdrive unit combining a BDI-style preamp with a separate TS-style distortion/overdrive section.

## Architecture
Cajita describes the unit as a bass preamp with a separate distortion section. The distortion section is described as a **Tube Screamer-type circuit modified for bass**, usable independently as a booster, overdrive, or heavier distortion.

## Controls
The documented distortion controls are:
- Gain
- Tone
- Vol

The BDI preamp section has:
- Hi
- Low
- Presencia
- Mid
- Drive
- Blend
- Vol

## Connections / power
- Mono input
- Mono output plus XLR output
- 9V battery or transformer
- 48V phantom power through XLR
- Metal enclosure
