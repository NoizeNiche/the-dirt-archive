# BYW Audio — Gros Buzz

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** BYW Audio
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** High-gain fuzz / vintage-distortion design.

## Circuit
BYW describes the Gros Buzz as using **five bipolar transistors** and **two phases of soft-clipping diodes**. The builder says the design is strongly inspired by the Big Muff family while offering selectable silicon, germanium, and Schottky clipping combinations.

## Controls / switching
- T (Tone)
- S (Sustain)
- B (Boost)
- V (Volume)
- Two mini toggle switches providing four clipping choices
- Blue LED

## Power / switching
- True bypass
- 9V DC BOSS-style input, center negative
- Approx. 6 mA current draw
- 120 × 94 × 33 mm
