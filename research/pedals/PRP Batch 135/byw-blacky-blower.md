# BYW Audio — Blacky' Blower

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** BYW Audio
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** Bass-oriented distortion design by BYW Audio, associated with Voïvod bassist Jean-Yves “Blacky” Thériault.

## Circuit / architecture
BYW describes the Blacky' Blower as a simple design using **five silicon JFET transistors and op-amp stages in three stages**. The builder also documents multiple low-pass and high-pass filters intended to reshape the harmonics and cut treble for direct-recording use.

## Controls / functions
The product page documents **Drive, Voice, Level, Sustain, Volume, Treble, Mid, and Bass**. BYW also describes a two-knob opto-compressor section using a dual op amp, with sustain/dynamics and booster use.

## Power / switching
- True bypass
- 9V DC BOSS-style input, center negative
- Approx. 50 mA current draw

## Construction
BYW lists the enclosure at 119 × 93 × 30 mm. The builder says the pedal was designed to work with bass and also performs well with guitar and keyboard.
