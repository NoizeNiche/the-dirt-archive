# BZZT Electronics — SELEKTOR 16

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** BZZT Electronics
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz / Overdrive
- **Identity:** Multi-transistor fuzz/overdrive with selectable circuit combinations.

## Circuit / architecture
Contemporary coverage describes the SELEKTOR 16 as containing **four pairs of transistors** sourced from the USA, Europe, Russia, and Japan. The four transistor circuits can be mixed through the selector controls for **16 circuit modes**.

## Controls / functions
Documented controls include **GAIN, BIAS, and VOLT** starving controls plus the two selector controls. The voltage-starving function can produce lower-bit / sagging effects, while the circuit can also be used with lower-wattage amplifiers for additional clipping.
