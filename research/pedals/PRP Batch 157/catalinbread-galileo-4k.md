# Catalinbread Effects Galileo 4K

- **Builder:** Catalinbread Effects
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** Galileo 4K
- **What this pedal is:** A later Galileo generation that keeps the Rangemaster-style treble-booster into Vox AC30 concept while expanding the tone section into a two-band Baxandall EQ.
- **Colorways:** Standard production finish. Cosmetic editions are not treated as separate circuit versions unless Catalinbread names and markets them separately.
- **Versions:** Galileo; Galileo 4K; Galileo Red Special is a separately named limited colorway and remains its own catalog identity in the archive.
- **Version changes:** The major documented change from the earlier Galileo is the EQ section. The original's single Tone control acted primarily as a treble control, while the 4K adds separate Treble and Bass controls based on a Baxandall-style two-band section. The Gain control continues to drive the treble-booster stage while the amp-emulation gain remains fixed.
- **Transistor type:** Exact transistor family is not specified in the reviewed manufacturer documentation.
- **Diode type:** Not documented.
- **Controls / switching:** Treble, Bass, Volume, Gain.
- **Power:** 9-18V DC, center-negative; over 5mA supply capability.
- **Sound:** The Galileo 4K preserves the focused, harmonically rich attack of a treble booster feeding a pushed AC30 but gives the player much more freedom to shape the frequency response. Its gain structure is intended to move from cleaner, jangly Vox-like tones into searing lead and aggressive focused rhythm sounds as the treble-booster stage is pushed.
- **Research confidence:** High for the documented Galileo 4K design change, controls, and power requirements; moderate for undocumented internal component details.
