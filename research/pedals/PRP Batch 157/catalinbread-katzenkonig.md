# Catalinbread Effects Katzenkönig

- **Builder:** Catalinbread Effects
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Fuzz
- **Catalog identity:** Katzenkönig
- **What this pedal is:** A hybrid fuzz/distortion that combines the front half of a Tone Bender MkII concept with a Rat-style distortion/filter approach. Catalinbread designed it to keep the singing quality of a classic fuzz while tightening the response.
- **Colorways:** Standard production finish. Catalinbread has also released special cosmetic editions, including the Fireworks Collection, without presenting them as new circuit versions.
- **Versions:** Standard Katzenkönig; documented cosmetic editions such as the Fireworks Collection.
- **Version changes:** No major circuit revision was established in the reviewed official material. The named special editions change presentation rather than the core pedal function.
- **Transistor type:** Catalinbread does not specify the exact semiconductor complement of the finished pedal in the reviewed product documentation. The product explanation discusses the germanium transistor arrangement of the original Tone Bender MkII as circuit inspiration, not as a direct component claim about the finished Katzenkönig.
- **Diode type:** Not documented.
- **Controls / switching:** Input, Gain, Filter, Volume.
- **Power:** 9-18V DC, center-negative; 9V battery operation is also documented; approximately 6mA.
- **Sound:** The Katzenkönig is designed to sit between unruly fuzz and tighter distortion, with the Filter providing a broad final-voicing sweep from smoother and creamier toward brighter and cutting. Input sensitivity accommodates lower-output single coils through hotter humbuckers, while Gain controls the second gain stage for a wide, responsive range.
- **Research confidence:** High for the documented Tone Bender/Rat design relationship, control set, and power specification; moderate for internal component details.
