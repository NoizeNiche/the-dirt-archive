# Catalinbread Effects Manx Loaghtan Fuzz

- **Builder:** Catalinbread Effects
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Catalog identity:** Manx Loaghtan Fuzz
- **What this pedal is:** A Catalinbread Muff-style fuzz with a substantially expanded Baxandall EQ. The current product is sold as a reissue of a model Catalinbread had previously stopped making.
- **Colorways:** Standard Manx Loaghtan finish.
- **Versions:** Original Manx Loaghtan Fuzz; current Manx Loaghtan Fuzz (Reissue).
- **Version changes:** Catalinbread's current reissue preserves the defining four-control concept of the original: separate high and low shelving controls plus Level and Sustain. The defining circuit treatment is the replacement of the classic Muff-style single dual-filter tone approach with a more flexible Baxandall EQ.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented in the reviewed manufacturer product material.
- **Diode type:** Not documented.
- **Controls / switching:** Highs, Level, Lows, Sustain.
- **Power:** 9-18V DC, center-negative; Catalinbread recommends 9V.
- **Sound:** Manx Loaghtan is a thick, sustaining fuzz that can be shaped from crisp highs to booming lows instead of being locked to the familiar mid-scooped Muff balance. The surrounding circuit is deliberately voiced to support chunky chords, long sustain, and even lower-gain overdrive textures when the fuzz amount is backed down.
- **Research confidence:** High for the current reissue identity, Muff lineage, EQ architecture, controls, and power range; moderate for undocumented internal component details.
