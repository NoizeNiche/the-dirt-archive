# Catalinbread Effects Giygas 2K Fuzz

- **Builder:** Catalinbread Effects
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Catalog identity:** Giygas 2K Fuzz
- **What this pedal is:** A streamlined reworking of Catalinbread's Giygas fuzz, brought back in a two-knob format. Catalinbread describes it as a heavily modified Muff-style fuzz engine that avoids the original's lossy dual-filter tone circuit.
- **Colorways:** Standard compact Giygas 2K finish.
- **Versions:** Original Giygas; Giygas 2K. The 2K is a separately named compact version rather than being merged into the earlier Giygas identity.
- **Version changes:** The 2K reduces the control surface to Loud and Fuzz and redistributes the gain structure so the initial boost and clipping stages provide the intended extra gain without reproducing the original's extended multi-EQ-stage arrangement.
- **Transistor type:** Not specified in the reviewed manufacturer documentation.
- **Diode type:** Not documented.
- **Controls / switching:** Loud and Fuzz.
- **Power:** 9V DC, center-negative; Catalinbread recommends 9V for normal instrument interaction. The current product text does not give a dependable current-draw figure.
- **Sound:** The Fuzz control moves from a warm, slightly gritty boost through thick, rounded square-wave fuzz, while the circuit avoids the pronounced mid loss associated with a conventional Muff tone stage. Catalinbread also emphasizes useful guitar-volume cleanup and a punchy response that stays connected to the surrounding rig.
- **Research confidence:** High for the two-knob architecture and stated voicing; moderate for undocumented internal component details.
