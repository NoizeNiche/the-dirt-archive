# Catalinbread Effects Little Secret

- **Builder:** Catalinbread Effects
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** Little Secret
- **What this pedal is:** A compact version of the Dirty Little Secret concept, reduced to the core Marshall Plexi-style preamp voice in a small enclosure.
- **Colorways:** Standard compact finish.
- **Versions:** Dirty Little Secret family relationship is the main documented lineage; Little Secret is its own named compact product rather than a cosmetic version of DLS.
- **Version changes:** Compared with the full Dirty Little Secret, Catalinbread strips away the larger control and voicing section and leaves a two-knob preamp-style format centered on the Master and Pre-Amp controls.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented in the reviewed manufacturer product page. Catalinbread describes the gain path as two mu-amp stages rather than publishing a discrete transistor bill of materials.
- **Diode type:** Not documented.
- **Controls / switching:** Master and Pre-Amp.
- **Power:** 9-18V DC, center-negative; extremely low current draw.
- **Sound:** The Little Secret is meant to behave like the raw preamp side of a driven Plexi, giving low-to-medium gain, compression, and growl from a very simple control set. It can sit before other dirt pedals for gooey amp-like compression or later in the chain so a clean amplifier's EQ section can shape the result.
- **Research confidence:** High for the compact DLS relationship, control behavior, and power range; moderate for undocumented internal component details.
