# Catalinbread Effects Knight School Overdrive

- **Builder:** Catalinbread Effects
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** Knight School Overdrive
- **What this pedal is:** A touch-sensitive dirt circuit derived from Craig Anderton's Tube Sound Fuzz lineage, with the Hot Tubes and Red Llama families as further reference points and a Matchless Chieftain-style mids section added by Catalinbread.
- **Colorways:** Standard Knight School finish.
- **Versions:** DIY kit and fully assembled Knight School Overdrive. The public product identity remains one circuit.
- **Version changes:** The major defining addition is the adjustable mids-scoop section. Catalinbread describes the circuit as moving from sparkling clean boost at low Drive settings to meaty distortion when driven hard.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented as a transistor-based gain topology in the reviewed manufacturer material; the circuit is centered on the hex-inverter Tube Sound Fuzz concept.
- **Diode type:** Not documented.
- **Controls / switching:** Volume, Mids, Drive.
- **Power:** 9V DC, center-negative; at least 0.5mA.
- **Sound:** The Knight School Overdrive is designed to be highly touch-sensitive, with the Drive control able to function almost like a threshold for picking dynamics. The Mids control creates a pronounced scoop when turned down, which can help the pedal sit against mid-heavy British amplifiers or compensate for more mid-scooped Fender-style amps.
- **Research confidence:** High for the circuit lineage, control behavior, and power requirements; moderate for undocumented internal components.
