# Catalinbread Effects Galileo Red Special

- **Builder:** Catalinbread Effects
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** Galileo Red Special
- **What this pedal is:** A limited Red Special colorway of the Catalinbread Galileo, created to commemorate Brian May's documented association with the pedal and styled after his Red Special guitar.
- **Colorways:** Red Special finish patterned after Brian May's signature Red Special guitar.
- **Versions:** Galileo Red Special is a limited cosmetic edition of the Galileo design; it is separate from the later Galileo 4K in the public catalog.
- **Version changes:** Catalinbread's product description presents the Red Special difference as the enclosure/color treatment, not as a new circuit. The underlying Galileo control set remains Volume, Tone, and Gain.
- **Transistor type:** Exact transistor family is not specified in the reviewed manufacturer documentation.
- **Diode type:** Not documented.
- **Controls / switching:** Volume, Tone, Gain.
- **Power:** 9-18V DC, center-negative.
- **Sound:** Because Catalinbread describes the Red Special as a limited Galileo colorway rather than a new circuit, its sonic reference is the original Galileo: a Rangemaster-style treble-booster character driving a Vox AC30-style voice, moving from jangly lower-gain sounds to focused, saturated leads as Gain is increased.
- **Research confidence:** High for the limited-edition/colorway relationship and control set.
