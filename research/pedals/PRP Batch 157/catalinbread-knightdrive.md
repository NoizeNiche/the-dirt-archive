# Catalinbread Effects Knightdrive

- **Builder:** Catalinbread Effects
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** Knightdrive
- **What this pedal is:** A lower-gain adaptation of the Tube Sound Fuzz concept, voiced as a buttery drive and paired with a Matchless Chieftain-inspired mids control.
- **Colorways:** Standard Knightdrive finish.
- **Versions:** Knightdrive is a separate named product from Knight School Overdrive even though both draw from the Tube Sound Fuzz lineage.
- **Version changes:** The documented change from the original Tube Sound Fuzz concept is the lower-gain voicing and addition of the external Mids control. No further production revision was established in the reviewed material.
- **Transistor type:** The circuit is based around the Tube Sound Fuzz's hex-inverter IC approach rather than a transistor-only gain stage; exact finished-pedal semiconductor details are not separately documented.
- **Diode type:** Not documented.
- **Controls / switching:** Volume, Mids, Drive.
- **Power:** 9V DC, center-negative; at least 0.5mA.
- **Sound:** Knightdrive is intended to cover a wide range from sparkling clean boost through meaty distortion, with the Mids control able to create a substantial scoop around the guitar's body frequencies. Its lower-gain starting point gives it a smoother drive profile than a full-gain fuzz interpretation of the same lineage.
- **Research confidence:** High for the documented design lineage, control set, and power; moderate for internal component details.
