# Catalinbread Effects Knight School Fuzz

- **Builder:** Catalinbread Effects
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Catalog identity:** Knight School Fuzz
- **What this pedal is:** A Catalinbread adaptation of the Jordan Boss Tone fuzz, originally developed as an instructional pedal-building project and later offered as a DIY kit and as a fully built pedal.
- **Colorways:** Standard Knight School finish. The reviewed source does not establish a separate factory colorway with a different sound.
- **Versions:** Knight School Fuzz DIY Kit; fully assembled Knight School Fuzz. The public product identity remains the same circuit.
- **Version changes:** Catalinbread describes the design as a Boss Tone-inspired circuit with component value changes, different transistor choices for a more modern sound, and a Bias control not found on the original concept.
- **Transistor type:** The supplied design uses a 2N5088 NPN silicon transistor as its principal gain device; Catalinbread notes that the circuit is bias-adjustable and permits other NPN substitutions in the DIY context.
- **Diode type:** Silicon clipping diodes are supplied for the stock build. Catalinbread also documents that the clipping diode position can be modified experimentally.
- **Controls / switching:** Volume, Bias, Gain.
- **Power:** 9V DC, center-negative; at least 0.5mA.
- **Sound:** Knight School Fuzz moves from full, throaty fuzz toward sputtery and gnarly textures as Bias is advanced. The Bias control deliberately destabilizes the transistor operating point, and at extreme settings it can produce controlled sputter, synth-like behavior, and drone textures.
- **Research confidence:** High for the Boss Tone relationship, control behavior, and the documented stock transistor/diode types.
