# Catalinbread Effects Karma Suture GE

- **Builder:** Catalinbread Effects
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Catalog identity:** Karma Suture GE
- **What this pedal is:** The original germanium version of Catalinbread's Harmonic Percolator-inspired Karma Suture, designed to generate prominent even-order harmonics while extending the old circuit with additional control over clipping and frequency response.
- **Colorways:** Standard purple Karma Suture graphics, with documented limited-edition finishes such as the early gold edition. Cosmetic editions do not change the main GE circuit identity.
- **Versions:** Karma Suture GE; Karma Suture SI followed in 2016 as a separate all-silicon version rather than replacing the GE model.
- **Version changes:** Catalinbread added Density and Diodes controls to the two basic Harmonic Percolator-derived functions, Input and Output. Aion FX's later tracing work identifies the original GE circuit as distinct from the later silicon version.
- **Transistor type:** One NOS PNP germanium transistor paired with one NPN silicon transistor.
- **Diode type:** Germanium and silicon diodes are used for the clipping network.
- **Controls / switching:** Input, Output, Density, Diodes.
- **Power:** 9-18V DC, center-negative; approximately 1mA according to surviving product/database documentation.
- **Sound:** The GE version can move from a lively, harmonically rich boost into open overdrive and saturated fuzz while retaining strong note definition. Density changes the frequency character ahead of the core clipping engine, and Diodes controls the amount of clipping and compression, giving the pedal a much wider range than the two-control Harmonic Percolator concept that inspired it.
- **Research confidence:** High for the GE/SI distinction, transistor pairing, control layout, and circuit ancestry because the historical documentation and later circuit tracing agree.
