# Cole Music Co. - ParaDrive Parametric Overdrive

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** ParaDrive Parametric Overdrive
- **Builder:** Cole Music Co.
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Cole Music Co. ParaDrive Parametric Overdrive, a medium-gain overdrive with a four-knob parametric EQ and switchable clipping.

## What this pedal is
Cole Music Co. introduced ParaDrive in 2019 as the first release in its Parametric Pedal Series. The design combines Drive and Level controls with Frequency and EQ Gain controls that form a parametric EQ section. The selectable frequency spans 100 Hz to 4 kHz and the EQ can boost or cut by up to 15 dB. [1][2]

## Versions and factory options
- Drive.
- Level.
- Frequency.
- EQ Gain.
- Two clipping modes.
- True bypass.
- Side-mounted jacks.
- Two Burr-Brown/TI OPA2604AP chips. [1][2]

The two clipping modes use either four N914 diodes or two BAT41 diodes. [2]

## Version changes
No formal numbered factory revision sequence was established.

## Transistor
The gain/equalization architecture uses **two Burr-Brown/TI OPA2604AP op-amps**. [1]

## Diode
Published circuit/review documentation identifies:
- **Four N914 diodes** in one mode.
- **Two BAT41 diodes** in the other mode. [2]

## Sound
The parametric EQ can be used for treble-boost style shaping, feedback notching, midrange emphasis, or cocked-wah-like voicing. The N914 mode provides more output and a more organic low-gain response in the reviewed example, while BAT41 mode is described as more compressed and fuzzier with less output. [2]
