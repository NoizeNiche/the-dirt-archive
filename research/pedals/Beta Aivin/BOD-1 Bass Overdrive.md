# Beta Aivin — BOD-1 Bass Overdrive

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** BOD-1 Bass Overdrive
- **Builder:** Beta Aivin
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Bass-specific dual-overdrive pedal with independently adjustable Overdrive and Gain, plus two-band EQ and a separate Tone control. Effects Database dates the catalog entry to **April 26, 2006**. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
Beta Aivin describes BOD-1 as a pedal designed specifically for electric bass. The defining architecture is a **dual overdrive circuit**, allowing the player to move from warm/fat overdrive through much higher-gain fuzz-like saturation while preserving separate control over the overdrive character and gain. [1][2]

## Controls
- **LEVEL:** Overall output.
- **EQ:** Two-band bass/treble-style equalization.
- **TONE:** Overdrive tone shaping.
- **GAIN:** Overall gain/saturation.
- The documented housing uses space-saving dual-concentric controls. [2][3]

A historical retailer listing explicitly describes the controls as **Level, EQ, Tone and Gain**, with separately adjustable overdrive and gain. [2][3]

## Sound
Effects Database describes BOD-1 as covering **warm, fat overdrive through high-gain fuzz**. A surviving retailer listing says the overdrive and gain are independently controllable, allowing the player to tailor dynamics while retaining the fuzz character. [1][2]

The Beta Aivin BOD-1 Effects Database entry and exact-model Reverb/retailer records were cross-checked.
