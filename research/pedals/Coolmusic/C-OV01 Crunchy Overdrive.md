# Coolmusic - C-OV01 Crunchy Overdrive

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Coolmusic
- **Catalog identity:** C-OV01 Crunchy Overdrive
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Compact Coolmusic overdrive pedal documented by the manufacturer's manual and Effects Database.

## What this pedal is
Coolmusic documents C-OV01 as the **CRUNCHY Overdrive** pedal. The manufacturer specifies 9V DC center-negative power and a compact aluminum enclosure. [1]
Effects Database catalogs the same model with Tone and Gain controls. [2]

## Controls
The manufacturer manual documents:
- **Output**
- **Gain**
- **Tone** [1]

Effects Database also identifies Tone and Gain as the core control pair. [2]

## Colorways
- Black anodized aluminum enclosure is documented for the C-series. [2]
- Cosmetic variation is not treated as a circuit revision.

## Versions and factory options
- **Power:** 9V DC. [1]
- **Input impedance:** 1MΩ.
- **Output impedance:** 1KΩ.
- **Enclosure:** aluminum alloy.
- **Dimensions:** approximately 4.5 × 2.4 × 0.8 in. [2]

## Version changes

The checked sources identify the product as an analog overdrive but do not provide component-level evidence sufficient to name a specific topology.

## Sound
Coolmusic describes the Tone/Gain combination as producing smooth, warm, classic overdrive while controlling the output level separately. [2]
