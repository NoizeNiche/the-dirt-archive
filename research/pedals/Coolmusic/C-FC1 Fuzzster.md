# Coolmusic - C-FC1 Fuzzster

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Coolmusic
- **Catalog identity:** C-FC1 Fuzzster
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Compact Coolmusic fuzz pedal documented by the manufacturer's guitar-effects manual. The manual labels the product C-FC01 FUZZSTER Distortion; the archive preserves the catalog identity as C-FC1 Fuzzster to match the canonical catalog.

## What this pedal is
Coolmusic's official manual identifies the product family as **C-FC01 / FUZZSTER Distortion**, a dedicated fuzz-oriented pedal in the compact C-series. [1]
Effects Database lists Coolmusic C-FC1 Fuzzster in its catalog. [2]

## Controls
The official manual documents:
- **Tone**
- **Sustain**
- **Fuzz**
- **Clean** [1]

Instrument input, amplifier output, power and switching/indicator hardware are also shown in the manual. [1]
