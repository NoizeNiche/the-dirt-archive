# Coolmusic - C-DI02 Classic Distortion

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Coolmusic
- **Catalog identity:** C-DI02 Classic Distortion
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** Compact Coolmusic distortion pedal documented in the manufacturer's guitar-effects manual and cataloged by Effects Database.

## What this pedal is
Coolmusic's official manual identifies C-DI02 as the **CLASSIC Distortion** pedal. The unit uses a 9V DC center-negative supply and the same compact C-series enclosure format. [1]
Effects Database independently lists C-DI02 among Coolmusic's pedals. [2]

## Controls
The official manual documents:
- **Output**
- **Distortion**
- **Tone** [1]

The panel also includes instrument input, amplifier output, power and switching/indicator hardware shown in the manufacturer documentation. [1]

## Colorways
- The C-series documentation shows the model in an anodized aluminum enclosure format. [1]
- Cosmetic variation is not treated as a circuit revision without evidence.

## Versions and factory options
- **Power:** 9V DC center-negative. [1]
- **Enclosure:** aluminum alloy.
- **Size:** approximately 115 × 66 × 35 mm in the official manual. [1]
- **Input impedance:** 1MΩ.
- **Output impedance:** 1KΩ. [1]
