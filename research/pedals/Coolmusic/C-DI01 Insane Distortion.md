# Coolmusic - C-DI01 Insane Distortion

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Coolmusic
- **Catalog identity:** C-DI01 Insane Distortion
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** Compact Coolmusic distortion pedal documented in the manufacturer's guitar-effects manual and cataloged by Effects Database.

## What this pedal is
Coolmusic's official manual identifies C-DI01 as the **INSANE Distortion** pedal. It provides a 9V DC center-negative supply and a compact aluminum-enclosure format. [1]
Effects Database independently catalogs the C-DI01 under Coolmusic as an Insane Distortion model. [2]

## Controls
The official manual documents:
- **Level**
- **Low**
- **Gain**
- **Fat**
- **Tone** [1]

The pedal also has instrument input, amplifier output, power connection and a footswitch/indicator arrangement shown in the manufacturer manual. [1]

## Colorways
- Black anodized aluminum enclosure is documented for the C-series format. [1]
- Cosmetic variations are not treated as circuit revisions without supporting evidence.

## Versions and factory options
- **Power:** 9V DC center-negative. [1]
- **Enclosure:** aluminum alloy; the manual gives approximately 115 × 66 × 35 mm for the C-series format. [1]
- The official manual lists input impedance at 1MΩ and output impedance at 1KΩ. [1]
