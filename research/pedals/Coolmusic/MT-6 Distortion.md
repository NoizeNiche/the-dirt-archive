# Coolmusic - MT-6 Distortion

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Coolmusic
- **Catalog identity:** MT-6 Distortion
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** Early Coolmusic digital heavy-metal distortion pedal with six selectable distortion modes.

## What this pedal is
Effects Database documents the MT-6 as a digital metal distortion effect with six distortion modes selected through a mode control. It uses a high-precision A/D-A/D conversion and a high-speed DSP processor. [1]

## Controls
The model description explicitly documents a **mode selector** and describes six selectable distortion modes. [1]
The exact complete panel control list is not asserted here because the checked model description does not enumerate every knob.
