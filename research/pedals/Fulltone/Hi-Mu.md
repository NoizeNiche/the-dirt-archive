## What this pedal is
Fulltone describes the **Custom Shop Hi-Mu** as a high-gain distortion design built with full-size components and a handful of matched JFETs. The manufacturer states that it is hand-soldered and uses no opamps, diodes, or digital circuitry. [2]

The Fulltone manual documents the front-panel controls as **LEVEL, SUSTAIN, EQ**, plus a **BASS** switch. The manual describes the BASS switch as working together with the EQ control to raise or reduce low frequencies. [1]
