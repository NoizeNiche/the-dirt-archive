## What this pedal is
The Octafuzz 2 is Fulltone's recreation of the classic Tycobrahe Octavia-style circuit. It combines fuzz with an octave-up effect and retains the distinctive ring-modulated, steel-drum-like character of the original family.

## Sound
The Octafuzz 2 combines saturated fuzz with a strong octave-up character. The octave mode can produce the pronounced upper-harmonic, ring-modulated texture for which the Tycobrahe lineage is known.
