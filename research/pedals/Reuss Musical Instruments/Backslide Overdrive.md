## What this pedal is

The Reuss Backslide Overdrive is a boutique overdrive based on the builder's own mid-1980s Tokai TOD-1 Overdrive. Reuss rebuilt that circuit as a handmade pedal using the same unusual vintage semiconductor types the builder identifies inside the original Tokai reference.

## Colorways

- Metallic baby-blue powder-coated enclosure.
- Real silk-screen printed graphics.

## Versions and factory options

- First production batch: **30 pedals**.
- Internal clipping switch:
  - stock Tokai/Boss-style asymmetrical clipping
  - symmetrical clipping
- Internal **Fat** switch extends the low-frequency response by approximately one octave downward.
- Standard 9 V supply or 9 V battery.
- Reuss states the pedal can accept up to **18 V** from an external supply for additional headroom.
- Completely handmade in the European Union.

## Version changes

- No separate factory revision was established from the manufacturer documentation reviewed.
- The 30-pedal figure refers to the first production batch, not a numbered revision.

## Transistor

- Reuss identifies **C2021E** transistors as part of the vintage semiconductor set used in the Backslide.

## Diode

- Reuss identifies **1S2473** clipping diodes.
- The stock arrangement is asymmetrical; the internal switch provides a symmetrical alternative.

## Circuit and build

- Based on the **Tokai TOD-1** overdrive circuit.
- Reuss identifies an unusual **Sanyo LA6458D** dual op-amp in the vintage reference circuit.
- Mechanical true-bypass switching replaces the FET switching used in the builder's reference pedal.
- High-quality Neutrik jacks and an upgraded footswitch are specified by the manufacturer.

## Sound

The Backslide keeps the punchy, dynamic character Reuss associates with the Tokai TOD-1 while reducing noise and refining the response. The internal clipping switch moves between a sharper, more dynamic asymmetrical response and a softer, more compressed symmetrical response, while the Fat switch adds substantial low-end extension.

## Specifications

- **Type:** boutique overdrive
- **Base circuit:** Tokai TOD-1
- **Clipping:** asymmetrical / symmetrical internal switch
- **Fat:** internal low-end extension switch
- **Power:** 9 V battery or standard 9 V pedal supply; manufacturer allows up to 18 V for increased headroom
- **Construction:** handmade in EU
- **Enclosure:** metallic baby blue

## Deep research verification

The manufacturer's current product page explicitly identifies the Backslide as a discontinued model based on the builder's mid-1980s Tokai TOD-1. It also names the vintage semiconductor set, the 1S2473 clipping diodes, the internal asymmetrical/symmetrical clipping switch, the Fat switch, the 9 V/18 V power behavior, the first 30-pedal production batch, and the metallic baby-blue enclosure.
