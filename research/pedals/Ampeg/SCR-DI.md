# Ampeg - SCR-DI

## PRP identity
- Builder: Ampeg
- Catalog type: Overdrive
- Identity: Ampeg SCR-DI bass preamp/DI with Scrambler overdrive.

## What this pedal is
SCR-DI combines Ampeg bass preamp/DI functionality with a footswitchable Scrambler-style overdrive section. It is part of Ampeg's later bass-pedal lineup.
