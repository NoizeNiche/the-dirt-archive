# Ampeg - A-1 Distortion

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Ampeg
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** Ampeg A-1 Distortion from the early-1980s A Series.

## What this pedal is
The **A-1 Distortion** was one of Ampeg's Japanese-made A-Series effects, produced in **1982 and 1983** during MTI ownership. Effects Database records it as a dedicated three-control distortion pedal.

## Controls
- **Level:** Output level when the effect is engaged.
- **Distortion:** Amount of distortion.
- **Brilliance:** Tone control, covering bass-to-treble emphasis.

## Power / switching / hardware
- **Power:** 9V battery or Ampeg AC adaptor.
- **Switching:** Ampeg's "Noiseless" solid-state switching system.
- Dimensions: **68 x 53 x 120 mm**.
- Made in Japan.

## Circuit / lineage
A separate TalkBass historical discussion identifies the A-Series as a Japanese MTI-era product family and notes that the pedals were made by **Guyatone** and also appeared under Rozz, Westbury and Tora branding. This establishes the OEM family context, but no exact A-1 component complement is assigned without a model-specific schematic.

## Historical A-Series scope
The documented A-Series consists of:
- A-1 Distortion
- A-2 Compressor
- A-3 Over Drive
- A-4 Phaser
- A-5 Flanger
- A-6 Chorus
- A-7 Multi Octaver
- A-8 Analog Delay
- A-9 Parametric Equalizer
- SB-5 System Board

The Dirt Archive's dirt-box census is intentionally retaining the A-1 as the dirt-bearing entry while the other A-Series effects remain outside this dirt-focused catalog unless independently represented by a qualifying dirt circuit.

## Sound
The exact historical page identifies the A-1 as a distortion but does not provide a detailed modern tonal analysis. The archive therefore keeps the description at the documented function level.
