# Bad Cat — X-Treme Tone

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** X-Treme Tone
- **Builder:** Bad Cat
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive / Distortion / Tube Preamp
- **Identity:** Dual-gain-stage tube preamp pedal using two 12AX7 tubes, with separate gain-stage operation, active Bass/Treble controls, Edge texture control and selectable half/full boost behavior. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
Bad Cat's operating manual describes X-Treme Tone as an **X-Treme pre-amp pedal** built around **two 12AX7s** and dual gain. Stage 1 provides transparent volume matching, interactive Bass/Treble control and smooth gain boost; Stage 2 adds another gain stage for thicker overdrive/distortion. [1]

## Controls / modes
- **LEVEL:** Stage-1 output / level matching.
- **GAIN:** Stage-2 gain control.
- **MASTER:** Overall output / level matching.
- **EDGE:** Changes attack and texture from softer/velvety to sharper/aggressive.
- **BASS**
- **TREBLE**
- **MODE:** Stage 1 versus Stages 1+2.
- True bypass. [1]

The manual's LED readout identifies:
- **Green:** bypass.
- **Yellow:** Gain Stage 1.
- **Red:** Gain Stages 1+2. [1]

## Tube architecture
- **2 × 12AX7**.
- **Dual gain stages**.
- Stage 1: transparent matching/boost with tone shaping.
- Stage 1 + 2: additional gain stage for thicker overdrive/distortion.
- Point-to-point construction is documented in independent coverage. [1][2]

## Active device
- **2 × 12AX7:** Explicitly documented.
- No discrete transistor/op-amp gain stage is identified as the defining circuit.

## Diode / clipping
- The distortion mechanism is tube-stage clipping/gain behavior.
- Exact clipping/protection diode components are not established as the core gain mechanism.

## Power / high-voltage architecture
- The available schematic/manual evidence shows an internal high-voltage tube supply, including approximately **+235V** rail in the schematic transcription. [3]
- This is not a standard 9V transistor stompbox circuit.
- Bad Cat warns that incorrect voltage can damage the tubes/pedal. [1]

## Version history
- No numbered electronic revision established.
- The distinct mode is a gain-stage configuration rather than a separate revision.

## Sound
Bad Cat describes Stage 1 as smooth, transparent and capable of clean-to-crunch boost. Engaging Stage 2 adds substantial gain, thickness and distortion. Edge specifically changes bite and attack, while the active Bass/Treble controls are interactive across the two stages. [1][2]
