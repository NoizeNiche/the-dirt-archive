# Bad Cat - Burmese Drive - Dual Vintage Overdrive

## PRP identity

- **Builder:** Bad Cat
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Bad Cat Burmese Drive, a dual-channel vintage overdrive released in November 2025.

## What this pedal is

The Burmese Drive contains **two independent, identical BB-style overdrive circuits**. Each channel can be run by itself or stacked with the other for greater saturation and harmonic density.

## Controls

Each channel has:
- **Gain**
- **Tone**
- **Output / Level**
- **Bright / Warm** toggle

The two channels therefore provide eight channel controls/switches in total, split across the two sides.

## Circuit / architecture

- **Two BB-style overdrive circuits**, one per channel.
- The builder describes the pedal as rooted in the classic BB/Bluesbreaker-style overdrive family.
- Channels are independently switchable and stackable.
- Exact transistor, op-amp and clipping-device part numbers are not publicly documented in the checked sources.

## Power / construction

- **9-12V DC, center negative.**
- **True bypass.**
- Hand-crafted in Southern California.
- Black sparkle powder-coated enclosure with aluminum knobs and Bad Cat graphics.
- Dimensions: **5.75 x 4.5 x 2.75 in**.
- Weight: approximately **1 lb**.
- No battery.

## History

Effects Database records the product on **November 11, 2025**, matching contemporary launch coverage. Surviving retail/manufacturer descriptions present it as a new Bad Cat dual-overdrive product rather than a legacy reissue.

## Sound / behavior

Bad Cat and launch coverage describe the Burmese as a transparent, dynamic overdrive with vintage-style breakup. The two channels can be configured independently for rhythm and lead roles or cascaded for more saturated drive.
