### Verified description

Effects Database documents the **Siamese Drive** as a dual-channel overdrive introduced at NAMM 2013. It contains two identical drive circuits in one enclosure. Each channel has independent **Gain, Tone, and Output/Volume** controls, and the gain can be set to zero so a channel functions as a clean boost. The two sides can be run independently or cascaded for a combined overdrive sound.

### Verified version references

- No numbered Siamese Drive revision was established in the reviewed exact-model sources.

- The Effects Database product documentation describes a pearl-white enclosure with royal-blue graphics and the Bad Cat tiger motif as the documented finish for the model.

### Verified circuit / architecture

- Effects Database describes the circuit as a **dual K-style overdrive**.
- The two channels are identical and stackable.
- True-bypass switching and 9-12V DC center-negative power are documented in the exact-model specification.
