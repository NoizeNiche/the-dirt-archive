# Axis Guitar Effects — Axis Face MkII

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Axis Face MkII
- **Builder:** Axis Guitar Effects
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Fuzz Face-based fuzz redesigned with modern passive components, added power filtering and reverse-polarity protection for lower noise and improved reliability. [1]

## What this pedal is
Effects Database describes the Axis Face MkII as a modernized version of the classic Arbiter Fuzz Face circuit. The builder specifically changed resistor/capacitor choices and added protection/filtering components instead of treating vintage component age as an essential part of the design. [1]

## Circuit architecture
- Fuzz Face-based two-transistor topology.
- **1% metal-film resistors**.
- Stacked metal-film / silver-mica capacitors.
- Added **power-supply filtering capacitor**.
- Added **reverse-polarity protection diode**. [1]
The exact transistor part numbers are not identified in the reviewed source.

## Transistor
- Two-transistor Fuzz Face architecture.
- Exact transistor devices are not published in the reviewed Axis source.
## Diode
- **Reverse-polarity protection diode:** Verified.
- This should not be treated as a clipping diode.
- Exact protection-diode part number is not given in the Axis source. [1]

## Version history
- **Axis Face MkII** is itself the documented modernized generation.
- No later numbered revision established.
- The MkII's main change from the older Fuzz Face concept is component modernization and power/noise protection. [1]

## Sound
Axis positions the MkII as a quieter, more reliable Fuzz Face implementation. The builder's published rationale specifically rejects carbon-composition resistor “mojo” as a desirable noise contribution and emphasizes modern component consistency. [1]
