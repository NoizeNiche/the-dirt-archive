# Axis Guitar Effects - Axis Face MkII

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Axis Face MkII
- **Builder:** Axis Guitar Effects
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Modernized Fuzz Face-based fuzz designed around the original circuit concept with component and power-protection upgrades.

## What this pedal is
Effects Database describes the MkII as a Fuzz Face-based circuit whose parts were changed to improve reliability and reduce noise. The documented upgrades include 1% metal-film resistors, stacked metal-film/silver-mica capacitors, a power-supply filtering capacitor, and a reverse-polarity protection diode.

## Sound
Axis positions the MkII as a quieter, more reliable interpretation of the Fuzz Face circuit rather than a radically different fuzz topology. The modernized passive components and added power protection are intended to preserve the familiar Fuzz Face behavior while reducing unwanted noise and improving consistency.
