# Concrete Sound Lab - SLAB

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Concrete Sound Lab
- **Catalog identity:** SLAB
- **Catalog type:** Boost / Preamp / Overdrive / Distortion / Fuzz
- **Identity:** All-transistor lo-fi gain pedal with JFET preamp, James tone stack, silicon bipolar output stage, and footswitchable JFET boost.

## What this pedal is
SLAB is Concrete Sound Lab's gain pedal built to cover clean-ish boost and preamp textures through overdrive, distortion, and fuzz. The manufacturer describes a tube-like JFET preamp feeding a James tone stack and a silicon bipolar transistor output stage. [1][2]

## Controls
The documented control architecture includes:
- **Pre-Gain Low:** low-frequency emphasis before the gain stage.
- **Pre-Gain High:** high-frequency emphasis before the gain stage.
- **Gain:** main saturation amount.
- **Boost:** amount of the footswitchable additional gain stage.
- **Tone:** passive post-gain low-pass filter.
- **Level:** final output level.
- **Main Bypass:** effect bypass footswitch.
- **Boost:** independent boost footswitch. [1][2]

## Colorways
- Current examples use acid-etched or hand-finished enclosure treatments.
- Purple/marbled and other finishes are documented in contemporary examples. [3]
- Finish variation is treated as cosmetic rather than a separate circuit revision unless hardware evidence says otherwise.

## Versions and factory options
- 9V center-negative operation only. [1]
- Mono input/output. [1]
- No numbered hardware revision sequence was established.

## Version changes
No documented V1/V2 circuit chronology was established. The current product remains the same core all-transistor concept described by Concrete Sound Lab. [1]

## Construction
Concrete Sound Lab states that SLAB is hand-soldered, all transistor, and through-hole except for the surface-mount JFETs. Every transistor is tested before soldering, and each completed pedal is calibrated and tested. [1]

## Transistor
- **JFET preamp:** documented.
- **Silicon bipolar output stage:** documented.
- Exact individual JFET/bipolar transistor part numbers are not publicly specified in the manufacturer product description. [1]

## Diode
- **Exact clipping diode/device:** Not publicly documented in the checked sources.

## Sound
The manufacturer describes SLAB as dirty and lo-fi, with a range from clean-ish gain through overdrive and fuzz. The JFET boost drives the preamp harder for additional saturation and harmonic density, while the pre-gain and post-gain tone stages provide broad control over texture and harshness. [1][2]
