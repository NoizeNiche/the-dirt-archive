# AXiom Effects - Fuzz Preamp FP-1

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Fuzz Preamp FP-1
- **Builder:** AXiom Effects
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Handmade analog fuzz/preamp combining a silicon transistor fuzz stage, electronically emulated germanium behavior, and a 4558-based overdrive preamp.

## What this pedal is
The FP-1 uses a silicon transistor-based fuzz stage with a sliding-bias GAIN control and transistor current sourcing to emulate the response of germanium fuzz devices. A cascaded 4558-based preamp section lets the same box move from classic fuzz into amp-like overdrive and higher-gain fuzz/preamp sounds.

## Versions and factory options
### Builder-documented 2025 versions
AXiom's May 2025 build update documents two FP-1 versions:
- **Vintage fuzz version:** approximately the fuzz level of a vintage Fuzz Face.
- **Maximum fuzz version:** maximum fuzz the circuit can provide without squealing.

### Core controls and features
- FUZZ
- GAIN with sliding-bias behavior
- PREAMP
- MIDs
- 100% analog circuitry
- 4558-based overdrive preamp
- True bypass
- Input and output buffers

## Version changes
The two 2025 FP-1 versions are documented by the builder as different fuzz-level implementations. The checked sources do not provide separate transistor part numbers for the two versions.

## Transistor
- **Technology:** Silicon transistor-based fuzz stage with electronically shifted bias behavior.
- **Exact device:** AXiom does not publish the actual transistor part number in the checked product documentation.
- **Important distinction:** The builder uses BC108 and NKT275 behavior as tonal reference targets for the Si and Ge modes; those are not presented as the installed device part numbers.

## Diode
- **Type:** Exact clipping-diode arrangement is not publicly documented.

## Sound
The FP-1 can deliver compressed silicon-style fuzz, warmer germanium-like response, or a fuzz-into-amp sound through its preamp section. AXiom also says the pedal can be backed down into medium-gain overdrive while retaining cleanup from the guitar volume control.
