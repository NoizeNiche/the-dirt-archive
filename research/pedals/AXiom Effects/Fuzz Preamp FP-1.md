# AXiom Effects — Fuzz Preamp FP-1

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Fuzz Preamp FP-1
- **Builder:** AXiom Effects
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Handmade 100% analog fuzz/preamp combining a silicon transistor fuzz stage with electronically shifted bias behavior, a 4558-based preamp and separate MID soft-clipping. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
AXiom describes FP-1 as a fuzz/preamp intended to reproduce both classic silicon and germanium-style fuzz responses without changing to actual germanium transistors. The builder's method uses a **sliding-bias GAIN control** and transistor current sourcing to alter the behavior of the silicon fuzz stage. [1]

## Controls / architecture
- **FUZZ:** Fuzz amount.
- **GAIN:** Adds up to **16dB** of gain to the preamp input while shifting fuzz-transistor bias.
- **PREAMP:** Cascaded preamp amount.
- **MIDS:** Adds soft clipping to mid/high-mid frequencies for amp-like drive.
- Input and output buffers.
- True bypass.
- 100% analog circuitry. [1]

## Silicon / germanium modes
AXiom describes:
- **Silicon mode:** Intended to replicate the compression and “exploding amp” character associated with BC108 silicon transistor fuzzes.
- **Germanium mode:** Intended to replicate the smoother, glassier response associated with NKT275 germanium transistor fuzzes.
These are **tonal-reference modes**, not claims that BC108 or NKT275 devices are installed in the FP-1. [1]

## Circuit architecture
- Silicon transistor fuzz stage.
- Sliding-bias behavior.
- Transistor current sourcing.
- Cascaded **4558-based** overdrive preamp.
- MID soft-clipping stage.
- Input/output buffers. [1][2]

## Transistor
- **Technology:** Silicon transistor fuzz.
- Exact installed transistor part number is not published.
- AXiom's BC108/NKT275 mentions refer to reference behavior, not confirmed installed components. [1]

## Diode / clipping
- No exact clipping-diode part number is published.
- The **MIDS** section is explicitly described as adding soft clipping to mid/high-mid frequencies. [1]

## Version history
AXiom's May 13, 2025 update documents two FP-1 versions:
- **Vintage fuzz version:** approximately vintage Fuzz Face-level fuzz amount.
- **Maximum fuzz version:** maximum fuzz before squealing.
The checked update does not publish separate semiconductor part numbers for the two versions. [2]

## Sound
AXiom describes FP-1 as spanning thick silicon-style fuzz, smoother germanium-like fuzz, fuzz-driven-amp sounds and medium-gain overdrive. The buffers are designed to coexist with other pedals while the fuzz still cleans up from the guitar volume control. [1]
