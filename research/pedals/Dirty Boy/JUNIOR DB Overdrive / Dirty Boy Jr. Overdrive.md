## What this pedal is

Warm Audio's official product page identifies the **Dirty Boy Jr. Overdrive Preamp** as an amp-like overdrive with **Variac-style touch sensitivity** and an **emulated balanced output**. The page describes the design as the original Dirty Boy “Preamp” overdrive and T.A.E. amp-sim circuit rebuilt into a pedalboard-friendly format.

The same official product page gives an **MSRP of $169** and identifies the Dirty Boy line as a legacy boutique design associated with Alex Saraceno.
