## What this pedal is
Pet Yeti is a 100% analog distortion/fuzz pedal with a very wide gain range, selectable clipping voices, an analog bit-crusher section, and a parallel clean path. The manufacturer describes it as suitable not only for guitar but also bass, drum machines, synthesizers and other instruments.

## Controls and switching
- **GAIN:** overall gain range.
- **STRENGTH:** selects the gain/headroom range.
- **VOICE:** three distinct voicing/clipping configurations.
- **BIT:** activates the analog bit-crusher section.
- **SHAPE:** controls which input frequencies reach the distortion section.
- **TONE:** final EQ balance of the distorted signal.
- **LEVEL:** effect-signal output level.
- **CLEAN:** output level of the parallel unaffected path.
- True bypass.

The manufacturer documents op-amp, germanium and silicon clipping approaches across the VOICE configurations, including symmetric and asymmetric behavior.

## Switching and power
- 100% analog signal path.
- True bypass.
- Standard 9V DC center-negative Boss-style supply.
- Approximately 15 mA current draw.
- Standard 1590BB enclosure, approximately 3.7 x 4.7 inches.
- Handmade in Massachusetts, USA.

## Versions and factory options
- Two documented production colorways: **Neon Nuclear Wasteland** and **Dark Doomy Deathscape**.
- **DEV MODE:** one-of-a-kind custom-artwork units are sold separately. Cosmodio states these are identical in sound and function to the standard Pet Yeti, with the individual artwork being the distinguishing feature.
- Colorways and DEV MODE artwork are cosmetic editions rather than separate main catalog models.

## Sound
The Pet Yeti spans a broad range from near-clean boost through crunch, overdrive, heavy distortion and fuzz into glitchy, hard-clipped textures. The analog bit crusher can drive the signal toward square-wave hard clipping, while the parallel CLEAN control can retain body and clarity when the effect is pushed hard.
