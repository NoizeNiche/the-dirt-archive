# Xotic Effects — BB Preamp V1.5 — Boost / Overdrive

- **Builder:** Xotic Effects

## What this pedal is

The BB Preamp V1.5 is Xotic's updated BB Preamp format, retaining the core boost/overdrive design while changing the enclosure presentation. Xotic describes it as capable of more than **30 dB of clean boost** and a broad range of overdrive tones through its active two-band EQ.

## Colorways

- Vermillion-orange enclosure.
- White control knobs on the V1.5 presentation.
- Xotic describes the V1.5 as the same core BB Preamp sound in a new look.

## Sound

The BB Preamp V1.5 can move from a clean, high-headroom boost into smooth, compressed overdrive using its pre-gain stage and gain control. Its active Bass and Treble controls provide a wide tonal range, while the low-noise, true-bypass format keeps it practical as a board utility pedal.

## Specifications

- **Boost:** 30 dB+
- **EQ:** active Bass/Treble, approximately ±15 dB
- **Bypass:** True bypass
- **Power:** 9 V battery or regulated DC adapter
- **Current draw:** 6 mA
- **Dimensions:** 112 × 60 × 50 mm
- **Weight:** 280 g
