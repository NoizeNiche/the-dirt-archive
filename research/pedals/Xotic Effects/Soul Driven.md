## What this pedal is
Released late last year in collaboration with Allen Hines, the Soul Driven is based on Xotic’s highly popular AC-Comp Booster , albeit with enough tweaks to give it an entirely different feel.

## Colorways
- While Xotic Effects pedals aren’t known for their creative or unique designs, the lustrous chrome finish on the Soul Driven is actually pretty nice.
- I had the choice of red, blue or silver.

## Sound
After putting the Soul Driven for a long drive, I can say that this is a unique little guitar pedal, made to deliver a customized experience that you won’t get from a typical booster, compressor or overdrive.
Yes, the Volume and Gain knobs are still there and perform similarly, but gone are Treble and Bass.
Instead, the Soul Driven replaces them with Tone and Mid Boost controls.
