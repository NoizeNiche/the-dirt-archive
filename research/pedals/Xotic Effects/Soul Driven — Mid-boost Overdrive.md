## Sound
It's got a lot of tone variations." Paul Jackson Jr.
The Soul Driven is a compact overdrive built around a transparent, dynamic drive concept with dedicated Mid Boost and Tone controls.
Xotic positions it as a pedal that can function as a clean boost or as a richer, creamier overdrive while preserving the guitar's natural character.
