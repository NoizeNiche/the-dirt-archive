## What this pedal is
The Soul Driven is a compact overdrive built around a transparent, dynamic drive concept with dedicated Mid Boost and Tone controls. Xotic positions it as a pedal that can function as a clean boost or as a richer, creamier overdrive while preserving the guitar's natural character.

## Versions and factory options
- Main controls: **Gain, Tone, Volume, Mid Boost**.
- Internal Bass Boost DIP switch offers four documented settings: flat, +2.4 dB, +3.6 dB, and +6 dB centered around 125 Hz.
- True bypass.
- Xotic documents operation at 9 VDC and 18 VDC, with higher voltage providing additional headroom.

## Sound
The Soul Driven covers transparent boost through smooth, harmonically rich overdrive, with the Mid Boost and Tone controls shifting the center and contour of the drive response. The internal Bass Boost switch provides additional low-frequency weight while the higher-voltage option gives the circuit more headroom and a less compressed response.
