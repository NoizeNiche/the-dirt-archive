## What this pedal is
The AH Booster is Allen Hinds' signature Xotic overdrive, based on the legacy AC-Comp design and updated with additional EQ, compression, and voltage-headroom controls.

## Colorways
- The checked retail source documents a **Silver Sparkle** presentation.
- No complete finish chronology was established.

## Versions and factory options
- **Bass** and **Treble** active EQ, each with approximately ±15dB range.
- Three compression settings.
- Internal DIP switches for treble/presence character.
- 9-18V operation.
- True bypass.
- 4mA at 9V or 5mA at 18V.

## Version changes
- The AH Booster is a modernized version of Allen Hinds' favored AC-Comp design rather than a generic Xotic overdrive.

## Sound
The AH Booster is voiced for tight, smooth overdrive with selectable compression and active EQ. Xotic notes that higher operating voltage provides additional headroom and dynamics.
