Unlike most tape plugins or simpler saturation pedals, Deco models two distinct tape machines—one for saturation-only processing (using a custom-modified Ampex ATR-102 circuit) and another for true stereo doubletracking with variable delay, pitch shift, and mechanical instability modeling.
