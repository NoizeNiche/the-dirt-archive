Drive Core Deluxe MKII Gallery / Spec / Support 8 iconic drive circuits in one pedal The NUX DRIVE CORE DELUXE MKII (WASABI DRIVE) is an overdrive pedal that combines 8 distinct circuits, offering a wide range of tones.
