## What this pedal is
CHIMAERA was introduced in 2008 as Barbarossa's modern overdrive flagship alongside GARGOYLE. Barbarossa's design goal is a dynamic, wide-range, amp-like overdrive with strong response to playing touch and guitar/amp volume, built using hand wiring and a CNC-machined aluminum enclosure. [1][2]

## Sound
CHIMAERA is designed around dynamic, wide-range overdrive rather than a compressed one-note gain voice, with strong pick response and substantial low-end body. Contemporary player testing describes it as a mid-forward, highly responsive overdrive that can move from articulate crunch into heavier drive while retaining the sense of the guitar and amp underneath it. [2][3]
