# Barbarossa — CHIMAERA

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** CHIMAERA
- **Builder:** Barbarossa
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Barbarossa's long-running flagship modern overdrive, launched in 2008 and documented with a three-control Volume/Level, Tone and Drive/Gain layout. [1][2]

## What this pedal is

Barbarossa developed CHIMAERA as a hand-built modern overdrive emphasizing playing dynamics and interaction with the guitar and amplifier. Exact-model review evidence shows that the pedal has substantial output headroom, a wide Gain range and a control philosophy that works especially well when the guitar's volume knob is used as part of the gain system. [1][2]

## Controls

- **LEVEL / VOLUME:** Overall output, with enough range for substantial amplifier boosting.
- **TONE:** Strongly interactive tonal control; the review notes that increasing it can also make the output seem louder and that very high settings emphasize upper frequencies.
- **DRIVE / GAIN:** Wide gain range, extending from lower-gain crunch into sustained higher-gain overdrive. [1][2]

## Circuit / topology

- Barbarossa identifies CHIMAERA as a **Modern Over Drive**.
- The accessible manufacturer/retailer documentation confirms point-to-point construction but does not publish a complete schematic or exact active-device topology. [1]
- A 2020 exact-model review characterizes it as broadly **TS-family / Tube Screamer-style** in tonal behavior, specifically noting a familiar low-cut response. This is reviewer analysis, not a manufacturer schematic claim. [2]

## Sound

The exact-model 2020 review describes CHIMAERA as living in the TS-style overdrive family, with a familiar low-cut tendency but unusually effective Volume, Tone and Gain controls. The reviewer found a particularly useful operating range with the amp turned up and the pedal's Gain/Tone kept relatively restrained, then using the guitar volume to move between cleaner crunch and fuller overdrive. [2]
This is a player's analysis and should not be read as laboratory measurement.
