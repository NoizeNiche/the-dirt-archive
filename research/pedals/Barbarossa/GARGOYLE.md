## What this pedal is
GARGOYLE is Barbarossa's high-end distortion/overdrive flagship, designed around the response and feel of a quality tube amplifier rather than a heavily compressed generic distortion. The builder uses a CNC-machined aluminum enclosure and point-to-point construction, with a simple Level, Tone and Drive control set. [1][2]

## Sound
GARGOYLE sits between overdrive and distortion, with strong compression and a pronounced upper-mid/high presence while retaining a defined low end. Retail descriptions emphasize a tube-amp-like response with enough saturation for distortion territory but enough articulation for chords and lead playing. [1][3]
