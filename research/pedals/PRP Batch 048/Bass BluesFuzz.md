# Amptweaker — Bass BluesFuzz

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Amptweaker
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Bass-oriented BluesFuzz derived from Amptweaker's low-gain BluesFuzz platform.

## What this pedal is
The Bass BluesFuzz belongs to Amptweaker's BluesFuzz family, centered on a low-gain germanium fuzz character intended to cover cleaner, vintage-style fuzz and fuzzy overdrive territory. Amptweaker's later Bass BluesFuzz Jr documentation confirms the bass version's core design priorities: lower-gain germanium operation, midrange shaping, boost, and a Dry Low blend to retain bass fundamentals. [1][2]

## Colorways
- Full-size BluesFuzz/Bass BluesFuzz examples use the Amptweaker steel enclosure format and period BluesFuzz artwork.
- No complete historical colorway list for the Bass BluesFuzz was established.

## Versions and factory options
- Bass-specific voicing
- Low-gain germanium fuzz circuit
- Boost function
- Mid EQ
- Bass-oriented Dry Low blend
- The later Bass BluesFuzz Jr is a smaller-format descendant with a 3-position Fat/Normal/Tight attack switch and simplified controls. [1][2]

## Version changes
The Bass BluesFuzz Jr was introduced in 2017 as the compact bass counterpart to the earlier BluesFuzz platform. Amptweaker retained the low-gain germanium approach, added/retained bass Dry Low blending and mid control, and moved the attack shaping to a 3-position Fat/Normal/Tight switch in the Jr format. [1][2]

## Transistor
- **Low-gain germanium transistor** is documented for the BluesFuzz family and the Bass BluesFuzz Jr. [1][2]
- Exact transistor part number is not publicly documented.

## Diode
- Exact clipping diode/device is not publicly documented.

## Sound
The Bass BluesFuzz is aimed at a less extreme, more amp-like fuzz response than a high-gain bass fuzz, with the low-gain germanium circuit supporting cleaner and bluesier textures. The bass-specific EQ and Dry Low approach are intended to keep fundamental low end present while adding fuzz and midrange character, allowing the pedal to move between fuzzy boost, overdrive-like breakup, and fuller fuzz. [1][2]
