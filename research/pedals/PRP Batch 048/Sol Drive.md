# AmpMojo — Sol Drive

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** AmpMojo
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Tube-based D-style overdrive/preamp using a genuine Tung-Sol 12AX7 preamp tube.

## What this pedal is
The Sol Drive is a tube overdrive designed around the feel and harmonic character of classic D-style amplifiers from the 1970s and 1980s. AmpMojo specifically identifies a genuine Tung-Sol 12AX7 as the heart of the pedal. [1][2]

## Sound
The Sol Drive is voiced for warm, dynamic D-style overdrive, with gain ranging from subtle tube warmth into creamy saturated drive. Its Voice control moves the character from American-style warmth toward British edge, while the real 12AX7 contributes harmonic complexity, sustain, and touch-sensitive response. [1]
