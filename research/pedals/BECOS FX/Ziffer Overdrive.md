# BECOS FX - Ziffer Overdrive

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Ziffer Overdrive
- **Builder:** BECOS FX
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Jordan Ziff signature mini overdrive derived from the TS8-MS platform with wet/dry mixing and multiple clipping/frequency options.

## What this pedal is
Ziffer Overdrive was developed for Jordan Ziff and is primarily based on the vintage 808/Tubescreamer concept. BECOS adds three clipping options, two frequency-response modes, a dry/wet mix, and dry-signal EQ trimming while keeping the pedal in a compact enclosure. citeturn789352search1turn789352search2

## Sound
Ziffer keeps the familiar 808-style mid-focused drive core but broadens the response dramatically with selectable clipping, a Deep mode, and wet/dry shaping. The extra controls can move it from tight, articulate low-gain drive into warmer, larger-feeling tones without abandoning the compact Tube Screamer-centered architecture. citeturn320193search4turn320193search9
