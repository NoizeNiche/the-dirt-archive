# BECOS FX — Ziffer Overdrive

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Ziffer Overdrive
- **Builder:** BECOS FX
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Jordan Ziff signature mini overdrive derived from BECOS' TS8-MS platform and heavily modified around the vintage 808 concept, with selectable clipping, Deep low-frequency injection and parallel clean processing. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
BECOS developed Ziffer for Jordan Ziff after he had used the TS8-MS with his 1976 Marshall JMP. The Ziffer removes the TS8-MS MIDI-switching functions and instead adds compact analog tone-shaping features intended for guitar and bass. [1][2]

## Controls / features
- **GAIN**
- **TONE**
- **LEVEL**
- **CLIPPING:** Classic / Asymmetric / LEDs
- **DEEP:** Adds low-frequency content.
- **DRY/WET MIX:** Blends clean dry signal with overdriven wet signal.
- **DRY EQ:** Trims/tilts the dry signal frequency response.
- **Dry boost/cut** capability.
- True analog bypass. [1]

## Circuit lineage / architecture
- **Primary reference:** Vintage **Tube Screamer 808**.
- Derived from BECOS **TS8-MS** architecture.
- Parallel clean/dry processing plus wet overdrive path.
- Dry line processing uses a tilting equalizer concept.
- BECOS replaced ordinary one-transistor buffers with **audio-grade op-amps** for the Ziffer. [1][2]

## Diode / clipping
- **Classic:** 808-style clipping.

## Construction / hardware
BECOS specifies:
- Low-tolerance metal-film resistors.
- Panasonic/Kemet/Cornell Dubilier film capacitors in the audio path.
- Gold-plated PCB.
- Gold-plated microswitches.
- Mini-size genuine Hammond aluminum enclosure.
- Hand-assembled in Vienna, Austria. [1]

## Power / electrical specifications
- **9–18VDC**, center-negative.
- **Input impedance:** 1MΩ.
- **Output impedance:** <100Ω.
- **Current consumption:** <11mA at 9VDC and <15mA at 18VDC.
- 5.1/2.1mm barrel plug, 12mm long. [1]
BECOS also documents that 4–7VDC can be used for a different tonal character, while 9–18VDC is the intended normal range. [1]

## Version history
- Released in **2021** as the Jordan Ziff signature mini pedal. [2]
- No numbered electronic revision established.
- The important product-generation change from TS8-MS is removal of MIDI switching and addition of Deep/Mix/Dry-EQ functions. [1][2]

## Sound
BECOS describes the Classic mode as close to familiar 808-style compression, while Asymmetric adds articulation and sweetened compression. Deep increases low-end definition, and Dry/Wet lets clean signal remain underneath the driven path. A Guitar World review highlighted the practical usefulness of Deep, Dry EQ and wet/dry mixing. [1][3]
