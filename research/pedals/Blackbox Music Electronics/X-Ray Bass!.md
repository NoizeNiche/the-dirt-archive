# Blackbox Music Electronics - X-Ray Bass!

## PRP identity

- Builder: Blackbox Music Electronics
- Catalog type: Distortion / Overdrive
- Identity: Blackbox X-Ray Bass, the bass-optimized X-Ray model.

## What this pedal is

X-Ray Bass! is the bass-oriented version of Blackbox's full-range boost/overdrive/distortion design. The core concept preserves the instrument's low-frequency content while adding controllable gain and saturation.

## Controls

- More
- Drive
- Louder

## Circuit / behavior

The More control moves from distortion toward clean boost/overdrive. Drive provides additional post-boost gain and can itself become mildly distorted at higher settings.

## History

Effects Database dates the Blackbox X-Ray family to the 2001 product generation and lists X-Ray Bass! as its own model.

## Sound

Effects Database documents X Ray Bass! as the bass oriented member of the Blackbox X Ray family, preserving low frequency content while covering boost, overdrive and distortion. Verified controls are More, Drive, Louder . More spans cleaner boost through overdrive/distortion, while Drive adds post boost gain and can itself distort at higher settings. The model belongs to the 2001 Blackbox generation and is listed separately from standard X Ray.
