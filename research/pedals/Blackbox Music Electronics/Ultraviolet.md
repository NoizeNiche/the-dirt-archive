# Blackbox Music Electronics - Ultraviolet

## PRP identity
- Builder: Blackbox Music Electronics
- Catalog type: Fuzz
- Identity: Blackbox Ultraviolet dual-channel fuzz.

## What this pedal is
Ultraviolet is a dual-channel fuzz with an active filter stage. Effects Database describes two parallel fuzz paths mixed before a low-pass active filter.

## Controls
- Gain
- Volume
- Fuzz 1
- Fuzz 2
- Filter
- Boost switch
- Bypass

## Circuit / architecture
- Two parallel fuzz channels, separately voiced for lower and higher frequencies.
- Active low-pass filter after the mixing stage.
- Approximate 30dB boost is documented.

## History
Effects Database dates the design to 2001 and indexes the model from 2006. OohLaLa later manufactured an associated version.
