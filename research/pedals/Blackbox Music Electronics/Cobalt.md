# Blackbox Music Electronics - Cobalt

## PRP identity
- Builder: Blackbox Music Electronics
- Catalog type: Overdrive
- Identity: Blackbox Cobalt clean boost/overdrive.

## What this pedal is
Cobalt is a flexible guitar/bass boost and overdrive from Blackbox Music Electronics. Effects Database dates the model to 2001 and records its later OohLaLa manufacturing relationship.

## Controls
- Gain
- Drive
- Bias
- Volume

## Circuit / behavior
The Bias control changes headroom, compression, waveform symmetry and crunch. The pedal can move from clean boost into open, high-gain tube-like overdrive.

## Power
- 9V battery.
- DC input with 2.1mm tip-negative supply on the documented OohLaLa version.
