# Blackbox Music Electronics - Ultraviolet 2

## PRP identity

- Builder: Blackbox Music Electronics
- Catalog type: Fuzz
- Identity: Blackbox Ultraviolet 2 / UV2 quad-channel fuzz.

## What this pedal is

Ultraviolet 2 expands the Ultraviolet idea into four parallel fuzz channels with three active filter sections. One channel remains unfiltered and can be mixed back into the filtered paths.

## Controls

- Fuzz
- Low Freq
- Mid Freq
- Hi Freq
- Mix
- Volume

## Circuit / architecture

- Four parallel fuzz channels.
- Three active filter sections.
- Multi-channel mixing and feedback-harmonic shaping are documented features.

## History

Effects Database records Ultraviolet 2 as a separate historical model and dates its indexed catalog appearance to 2006.

## Sound

Effects Database documents Ultraviolet 2 as a separate historical model, indexed from 2006 , expanding the original Ultraviolet architecture to four parallel fuzz channels and three active filter sections .
