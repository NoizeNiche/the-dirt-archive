# Blackbox Music Electronics - X-Ray

## PRP identity
- Builder: Blackbox Music Electronics
- Catalog type: Distortion / Overdrive
- Identity: Blackbox X-Ray full-range boost/overdrive/distortion.

## What this pedal is
X-Ray is a full-range dirt design intended for guitar and bass. It preserves low frequencies and responds strongly to picking dynamics and instrument volume.

## Controls
- More
- Louder
- Shape

## Circuit / behavior
The More control blends clean boost/overdrive toward heavier distortion. Shape controls the onset and waveform character of clipping. Effects Database describes the clipping as asymmetric and tube-like in behavior.

## History
Effects Database dates the Blackbox design to 2001 and records both V1 and V2 control layouts in the historical page.
