# Coron - Coron Distortion

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Coron
- **Catalog identity:** Coron Distortion
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** Exact first-series Coron Distortion, distinct from the separately cataloged Distortion 10 and Distortion 15.

## What this pedal is
Coron Distortion is a Japanese-made distortion pedal from the late 1970s and early 1980s. It was also sold under related OEM branding including Grant, Storm, Asama and Memphis.

## Controls
- **Volume:** output level.
- **Distortion:** amount of gain and clipping.

## Circuit and power
- Analog solid-state design closely related to the MXR Distortion+ family.
- Effects Database describes a silicon-transistor implementation.
- Original units use a 9V battery.
- Surviving examples may have later DC-jack or bypass modifications.

## Versions and factory options
- **Distortion 10** and **Distortion 15** are related first-series models and remain separate catalog identities.
- Distortion 15 was marketed with substantially higher output than comparable units.
- OEM-branded units should remain related identities rather than being merged into the Coron record.

## Transistor
- **Technology:** Silicon transistor implementation is documented.
- **Exact part numbers:** not established from the checked sources.

## Diode
- No exact diode type was established from the checked sources.

## Sound
The basic circuit covers a wide range from lighter overdrive and boost-style operation to compressed, fuzz-like distortion at higher settings. Its character sits in the raw, direct family of early Japanese Distortion+ derivatives.
