# Coron - Coron Distortion 15

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Coron
- **Catalog identity:** Coron Distortion 15
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** Exact early Coron Distortion 15, distinct from Distortion 10.

## What this pedal is
Coron Distortion 15 is the higher-output sibling in Coron's early Distortion series. The original brochure says its output was roughly three times that of ordinary comparable units, allowing it to serve both as a distortion effect and, at lower distortion settings, as a boost.

## Controls
- **Volume:** output level.
- **Distortion:** amount of distortion.

The brochure describes low settings as softer precision distortion, middle settings as tube-amplifier-like breakup, and high settings as hard fuzz-like distortion.

## Circuit and power
- Japanese-made analog pedal from the late 1970s / around 1980.
- 9V battery operation in original configuration.
- Surviving examples can contain later DC and bypass modifications.
- Silicon-transistor / MXR Distortion+ lineage is documented for this Coron family.

## OEM lineage
Related Coron-family and OEM versions include Grant, Storm, Asama, Memphis and other rebadged variants. Keep these as related models rather than collapsing them into the Coron 15 identity.

## Research notes
A surviving used pedal may have replacement switches, LEDs, DC power, or other service modifications. Record those as unit-level provenance, not as factory specifications.
