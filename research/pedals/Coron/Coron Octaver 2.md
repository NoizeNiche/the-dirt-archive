# Coron - Coron Octaver 2

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Coron
- **Catalog identity:** Coron Octaver 2
- **Catalog type:** Octave Fuzz
- **Identity:** Exact first-series Coron Octaver 2.

## What this pedal is
Coron Octaver 2 is an early Japanese octave-fuzz pedal that generates a sub-octave voice and blends it with the original signal. Effects Database identifies it as a first-series Coron unit and cites a 1981 Coron brochure.

## Controls
- **Volume:** output level.
- **Blend:** mixes the octave signal with the direct signal.

## Function
The brochure describes the unit as an electronic synthesizer-style effect generating a deep octave voice and blending it with the fundamental. The pedal uses a footswitch for effect bypass.

## Specifications
- Dimensions: approximately 4 3/4 x 2 3/8 x 1 3/8 in (12 x 6 x 3.5 cm).

## Versions and factory options
- A **rare orange version** is documented by Effects Database imagery.
- Related OEM identities include Asama Engineering Octave Box, HMI Octaver 2, Memphis Bi Octave, Sanox 15SX Octaver and Storm Octaver 2.

## Sound
The blend control makes it possible to move from the original guitar voice toward a deeper, more synthetic octave texture. The effect belongs to the rough, monophonic-oriented octave-fuzz family of early Japanese pedals.
