## What this pedal is
D-500 Distortion belongs to Coron's last series, a family that Effects Database notes was also sold through the Frontline Series II line.

## Controls
- **Level:** output level.
- **Distortion:** amount of distortion.

## Versions and lineage

The two-control layout keeps the D-500 focused on output level and distortion amount. Available period examples place it firmly in the compact Japanese distortion-pedal family of Coron's later catalog.
