# Coron - Coron Over Drive

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Coron
- **Catalog identity:** Coron Over Drive
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Exact first-series Coron Over Drive.

## What this pedal is
Coron Over Drive is a late-1970s to early-1980s Japanese overdrive designed to reproduce the effect of driving an amplifier beyond clean operation. Effects Database identifies it as a first-series Coron model and reproduces a 1981 brochure.

## Controls
- **Level:** output level.
- **Emphasis:** driving intensity of the amplification.

## Circuit and power
- Coron's 1981 brochure describes an **F.E.T. circuit**.
- Power is specified as one 1.5V UM-3 / AA dry battery.

## Versions and related identities
- Related branded versions include Asama Engineering, Seiwa, Storm and Bias-family models.
- The related OEM identities should remain separate from the Coron-branded catalog record.

## Transistor
- **Technology:** FET.
- **Exact transistor part number:** not established from the checked sources.

## Sound
The low-voltage FET design is intended to give an amplifier-like overdrive response, ranging from gritty drive into more saturated, slightly fuzzy breakup as the Emphasis control is advanced. It is a notably simple early Japanese approach to compact overdrive.
