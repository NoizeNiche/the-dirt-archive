# Coron - Coron Distortion 10

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Coron
- **Catalog identity:** Coron Distortion 10
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** Exact early Coron Distortion 10, including the original HC-branded family.

## What this pedal is
Coron Distortion 10 is a Japanese-made early solid-state distortion/overdrive from the late 1970s and early 1980s. Effects Database documents it from a 1981 Coron brochure and relates the circuit family to the broader MXR Distortion+ lineage.

## Controls
- **Volume:** output level.
- **Distortion:** amount of distortion.

The published brochure describes a progression from softer, precision distortion at lower settings to harder, fuzz-like distortion at higher settings.

## Circuit and power
- Japanese-made analog pedal.
- Original stock units use a 9V battery.
- Some surviving units have later modifications such as DC input or modern bypass systems.
- Original units did not necessarily include modern conveniences such as LED indicators or true bypass.

## Dimensions
Approximately 4 3/4 x 2 3/8 x 1 3/8 in / 12 x 6 x 3.5 cm.

## OEM lineage
Effects Database links the Coron Distortion family to other Japanese OEM-branded versions including Grant, Storm, Asama, Memphis and related names. These should be treated as related OEM identities, not merged catalog records.

## Research notes
Do not backfill every later modification found on used examples into the original specification. Reverb examples can document surviving physical variants, but a modified individual unit is not evidence of the original factory configuration.
