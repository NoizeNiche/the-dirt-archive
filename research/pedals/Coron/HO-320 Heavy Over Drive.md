## Research identity

## What this pedal is
HO-320 Heavy Over Drive is a later Coron overdrive from the last series. Reverb examples identify the pedal as Japanese-made.

## Controls
- **Low Boost:** low-frequency boost.
- **High Boost:** high-frequency boost.
- **Level:** output level.
- **Drive:** amount of overdrive.

## Sound
