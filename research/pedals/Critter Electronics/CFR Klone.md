# Critter Electronics — CFR Klone

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** CFR Klone
- **Builder:** Critter Electronics
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Critter Electronics' Klon-style overdrive with selectable fat and higher-clipping modes.

## What this pedal is
CFR stands for Critter's "Classic Family Recipe." The builder describes it as a Klon-style circuit with its own flavor, including a toggle that can select classic Klon operation, added low-end/fat, or a more aggressive clipping mode. [1][2]

## Colorways
- Critter builds its enclosures by hand and powder-coats them individually, so CFR examples exist in multiple finishes.
- Documented examples include black and blue sparkle versions, as well as road-worn/custom cosmetic runs. [2][3]
- Finish differences are treated as colorways unless a circuit change is documented.

## Versions and factory options
### CFR Klone production
- Treble
- Gain
- Volume
- Three-position option toggle
- Buffered bypass
- Internal charge pump for higher operating voltage
- Standard 9V center-negative power [1][2]

The builder describes the option switch as providing the classic Klon position, a bass/fat position, and a more aggressive clipping mode. [1][2]

### Later CFR generation
A later builder description documents a TLE2072 op-amp, a refined treble control, slightly more available gain, and a fat switch. The lower switch position restores an additional clipping-diode option for a more distorted voice. [4]

## Version changes
The CFR has had documented production changes while retaining its parent identity. A later generation moved from TL072 to TLE2072 and revised the treble response and gain range, while keeping the classic/fat/aggressive concept. [4]

## Transistor
- **Technology:** Op-amp based Klon-style drive with an internal charge pump. [1][4]
- **Exact transistor/device:** Not applicable to the primary gain stage.

## Diode
- **Type:** Later CFR documentation identifies **1N34A** diodes and a selectable extra clipping-diode path. [4]
- **Exact standard clipping set:** The accessible documentation does not fully specify every clipping device used in every CFR production generation.

## Sound
CFR is designed to cover classic clean-boost to low/medium-overdrive behavior, then add two alternate voices through its option switch: more low-end body or a more aggressive clipping mode. Later revisions refined the treble response and added a little more maximum gain without changing the overall role of the pedal. [1][4]
