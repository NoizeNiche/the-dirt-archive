# Critter Electronics — 1978R Distortion

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** 1978R Distortion
- **Builder:** Critter Electronics
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** Critter Electronics' Rat-style distortion with selectable clipping and a noise-reducing EQ adjustment.

## What this pedal is
1978R is Critter's reworked take on the classic ProCo Rat circuit. The builder describes a slight EQ change intended to reduce noise, a toggle for standard versus LED clipping, and potentiometer choices selected for a useful control range. [1]

## Colorways
- Current Critter examples are hand-made and powder-coated in small runs, so cosmetic finish variation is expected. [1]
- Finish differences are treated as colorways unless a circuit change is documented.

## Versions and factory options
### 1978R production
- Rat-style distortion architecture
- Standard/LED clipping toggle
- Slight EQ change intended to reduce noise
- Hand-built enclosure
- 9V pedal power [1][2]

Used examples identify the model as a "Critterified" Rat-style distortion and confirm the clipping switch. [2][3]

## Sound
1978R is voiced around the aggressive, compressed distortion of the Rat family, with Critter's EQ adjustment intended to reduce noise and its clipping toggle offering a different gain/compression character. The builder's control choices are intended to keep the useful range broad without changing the pedal's core Rat-style identity. [1][2]
