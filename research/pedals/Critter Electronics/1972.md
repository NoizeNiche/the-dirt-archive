# Critter Electronics — 1972

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** 1972
- **Builder:** Critter Electronics
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Critter Electronics' recreation of the Univox Super Fuzz family.

## What this pedal is
Critter's 1972 is an intentionally old-school recreation of the Univox Super Fuzz. Critter describes it as the company's first fuzz and says the circuit was not modernized, with hand-built metalwork and hand-assembled electronics. [1][2]

## Colorways
- Critter makes individual enclosures by hand and powder-coats them in small runs, so surviving 1972 examples show cosmetic variation. [1][2]
- Finish differences are treated as colorways rather than separate models unless a circuit change is documented.

## Versions and factory options
### 1972 production
- Univox Super Fuzz recreation
- Mid-cut toggle
- 9V center-negative power
- Hand-built enclosure and hand-assembled circuit [1][2]

A Reverb listing documents an early "First Edition" example with the mid-cut feature. [2][3]

## Version changes
The current builder description identifies 1972 as a direct recreation without modernization. Some used examples are identified as First Edition, but no reliable numbered circuit revision was established beyond that production-era label. [1][3]

## Transistor
- **Technology:** Super Fuzz-family transistor fuzz circuit.
- **Exact transistor/device:** Not publicly established by Critter's accessible documentation.

## Diode

## Sound
1972 is intended to reproduce the large, cutting and octave-rich Super Fuzz character of the early 1970s. The mid-cut toggle provides an additional tonal choice around the Super Fuzz voice without changing the model's core identity. [1][2]
