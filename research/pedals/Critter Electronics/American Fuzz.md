# Critter Electronics — American Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** American Fuzz
- **Builder:** Critter Electronics
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Critter Electronics' high-headroom hybrid between a Big Muff-style fuzz and original Critter gain stages.

## What this pedal is
American Fuzz, also called CAF, is Critter's hybrid fuzz design. Critter describes it as "half original, half old school muff": two transistor stages are combined with two original op-amp gain stages, and the circuit runs internally at 18V from a normal 9V supply. [1]

## Colorways
- Critter builds and powder-coats the enclosures individually, so American Fuzz examples appear in multiple finishes. [1][2]
- A Reverb example documents a red/white/blue sparkle finish. [2]
- Finish differences are treated as colorways unless a circuit change is documented.

## Sound
American Fuzz is designed for a larger, beefier fuzz voice than a straightforward Muff recreation. The hybrid gain architecture and internal 18V operation are intended to preserve clarity and headroom while still delivering thick, sustained fuzz, with the Volume, Tone, and Sustain controls covering the core range. [1][2]
