# ARC Effects - Klone V2

## PRP identity
- Builder: ARC Effects
- Catalog type: Overdrive
- Identity: ARC Effects Klone V2 (K2), the later compact Klon recreation.

## What this pedal is
Klone V2 is ARC's smaller revision of its Klone, recreating the original Klon-style circuit and adding an internal bass-boost option.

## Controls / switching
- Klon-style Volume / Tone / Gain architecture.
- Internal DIP switch for additional bass boost.
- Buffered bypass.
- Standard Boss-style 9V DC jack.

## Construction
- Compact enclosure: 4.67 x 3.68 x 1.18 in.
- Matte black or matte white powder-coated enclosure in current documentation.

## History
ARC explicitly describes Klone V2 as preserving the original Klone circuit while adding the internal bass boost and a smaller chassis.
