## Sound

The cascaded six-stage tube circuitry is precision-biased to generate highly symmetrical clipping distortion.
The sound is immense, harmonically-saturated and super-smooth, able to go from mild bluesy breakup to full-on blistering distortion.
The pedal allows you to fine-tune the character by swapping tubes to change gain and tonal response.
