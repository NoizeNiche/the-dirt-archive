## What this pedal is
The CBS-Arbiter Wah-Fuzz-Swell is a combined wah, fuzz, and swell effect from the CBS-Arbiter plastic-pedal family associated with Gary Hurst. Effects Database lists the exact model in the CBS-Arbiter series and places it alongside the CBS-Arbiter Wah Wah, Wah-Swell, and Fuzz Phazer. The broader CBS-Arbiter series is documented as a 1975-1979 family sold under CBS-Arbiter, Electronic Sounds, and Electronic Sounds UFO branding. [1]

ToneHome documents the exact Wah Fuzz Swell as a CBS/Arbiter pedal made in Italy in the 1970s. [2] A Reverb listing for the exact model describes it as an original 1970s Gary Hurst design for CBS/Arbiter. [3]
