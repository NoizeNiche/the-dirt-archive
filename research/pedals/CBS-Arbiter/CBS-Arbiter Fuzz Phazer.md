## What this pedal is
The CBS-Arbiter Fuzz Phazer combines fuzz with a phasing/modulation section. Effects Database catalogs the exact model as a CBS-Arbiter fuzz/phaser and describes it as foot-controlled and LFO-controlled, with a treadle used to control the modulation rate. [1]

The exact-model record links the CBS-Arbiter Fuzz Phazer to earlier and related Electronic Sounds models, including the Electronic Sounds Fuzz Phazer and later UFO Fuzz Phazer entries. The same record identifies the CBS-Arbiter version as part of the Gary Hurst lineage and notes a plastic enclosure. [1]

A separate Effects Database family record dates the wider CBS-Arbiter series to 1975-1979. Because that range applies to the series rather than establishing a model-specific first/last production date, the archive does not force a single production year for the Fuzz Phazer. [2]

## Versions and factory options
### CBS-Arbiter Fuzz Phazer
The exact-model Effects Database record documents:
- **Treadle:** controls the speed/rate of the LFO wave.
- **Fuzz:** controls the amount of fuzz.
- **Volume:** controls output level.
- **Intensity:** controls the depth/amplitude of the phasing modulation.
- Foot-controlled, LFO-controlled modulation section. [1]

No separately numbered factory V1/V2 sequence was established in the checked sources.

## Sound
The Fuzz Phazer is documented as a combined fuzz and phasing effect. The Fuzz and Volume controls provide the fuzz/output section, while Intensity sets the depth of the LFO-driven modulation and the treadle changes modulation rate in real time. [1]

The result is a pedal intended for moving, phase-shifted fuzz textures rather than a conventional static fuzz alone. Comparisons to related Electronic Sounds/UFO Fuzz Phazer models are treated as lineage context, not as proof that every internal circuit detail is identical. [1]
