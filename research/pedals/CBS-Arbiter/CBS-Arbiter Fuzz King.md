# CBS-Arbiter — CBS-Arbiter Fuzz King

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** CBS-Arbiter Fuzz King
- **Builder:** CBS-Arbiter
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Rare 1970s CBS/Arbiter-branded Fuzz King associated with Gary Hurst and the Electronic Sounds production lineage.

## What this pedal is
The CBS-Arbiter Fuzz King is a rare British-market fuzz stompbox from the CBS/Arbiter family of products associated with Electronic Sounds and Gary Hurst. Effects Database catalogs the CBS-Arbiter Fuzz King as its own model and places it in a related family that also includes the Electronic Sounds Fuzz King. [1][2]

The checked dating sources are not completely consistent. A Reverb listing identifies a specific CBS/Arbiter Fuzz King example as a 1973 pedal, while Effects Database dates the wider CBS-Arbiter series to 1975-1979. This record therefore avoids assigning one production year as a universal fact. [2][3]

## Colorways
- A documented surviving example is listed in **White** finish by Reverb. [3]
- Effects Database shows multiple period photographs of the model, but the checked sources do not establish a complete factory colorway sequence.
- Finish differences are therefore treated as cosmetic unless stronger model-specific documentation establishes a distinct product version.

## Versions and factory options
### CBS-Arbiter Fuzz King
The photographed/documented control layout has:
- Fuzz
- Volume
- Filter
- footswitch
- input and output connections

The three-control layout is important because the CBS-Arbiter Fuzz King is distinct from the unrelated Amplifier Corporation of America Fuzz King, which is a different product. A period-effects discussion specifically calls out the CBS-Arbiter model's Fuzz, Volume, and Filter controls. [1][4]

No separately numbered factory V1/V2 sequence was established in the checked sources.

## Version changes
No verified numbered factory revision history was established. Surviving examples may differ in component population or physical condition, but the available evidence is not sufficient to turn those unit-level differences into a formal factory-version sequence.

## Transistor
A Reverb listing for a specific original example documents a **matched quartet of BC109 transistors**. [3]

That evidence is unit-specific. The archive therefore does not treat BC109 as a universal transistor specification for every CBS-Arbiter Fuzz King without broader factory documentation.

## Diode
The exact factory diode type is **not securely documented** in the checked sources.

Circuit-analysis discussions exist, but the available evidence is not strong enough to assign a universal diode type to the factory CBS-Arbiter Fuzz King.

## Sound
The documented Fuzz King example is described as a large, sustaining, aggressive fuzz with a tonal character reaching into Colorsound Tone Bender territory. [3] The presence of a dedicated Filter control gives the original three-knob unit a wider tone-shaping interface than the two-control Fuzz King design discussed elsewhere in the historical record. [4]

Later circuit-history discussions place the CBS-Arbiter Fuzz King in the broad family of highly sustaining multi-stage fuzz designs associated with the Supa Tone Bender and Big Muff era. Those comparisons are useful tonal/circuit context, but they are not treated here as exact factory circuit documentation. [4][5]
