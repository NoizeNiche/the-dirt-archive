## What this pedal is
Mad Hatter is a Russian-made boutique distortion/fuzz unit built around two independent sections: a Sunn-derived preamp channel and a Muff-derived fuzz channel. Reverb listings identify the pedal under the exact Mad Hatter High Precision Distortion Machine name.

## Channels
- **Sunn Preamp:** amplifier-style preamp section.
- **Muff Fuzz:** fuzz/distortion section.
- The two sections are independently controllable and can be used as a combined distortion rig or as separate gain stages.

## Sound
Mad Hatter combines the response of a Sunn-style preamp with the denser clipping texture of a Muff-style fuzz section. The two independent channels make it suitable as a standalone high-gain unit or as a source of separate preamp and fuzz stages.
