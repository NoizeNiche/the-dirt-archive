# Correct Sound - Eclipse

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Correct Sound
- **Catalog identity:** Eclipse
- **Catalog type:** Bass Amp Simulator / Preamp
- **Identity:** Exact Correct Sound Eclipse pedal.

## What this pedal is
Eclipse is a bass-oriented preamp / distortion pedal based on the preamplifier of the Sunn Concert Lead. Correct Sound's description says the output-stage frequency correction was modified while retaining the core Concert Lead reference.

## Controls
- **Bass:** low-frequency EQ.
- **Mid:** midrange EQ.
- **Treble:** high-frequency EQ.
- **Gain:** amount of gain.
- **Dist:** distortion amount.
- **Level:** output level.

## Switching and power
- True bypass.
- Input impedance: 1M ohm.
- Output impedance: 100k ohm.
- Correct Sound Eclipse is specified for an approximately **18V AC** supply; surviving examples may document a wider AC range, so the exact factory supply should be treated as source-specific.

## Versions and factory options
Audiofanzine documentation describes the Eclipse as a custom-shop, made-to-order Correct Sound pedal. The checked review reports that customers could choose enclosure and cosmetic details such as knobs, color and graphics. These are cosmetic options rather than separate catalog models.

## Sound
Eclipse is designed to bring the Sunn Concert Lead-style preamp character into a compact pedal, with a low-frequency-friendly response suited to bass and enough gain range to move from overdrive into heavier distortion. Contemporary user documentation emphasizes strong dynamics, high output capability and retention of low-end detail.
