## What this pedal is
M1 Tube Preamp is a two-channel tube preamp built around two ECC83 / 12AX7 tubes. Correct Sound describes it as an evolution of its MT preamp, with a revised middle stage and separate clean and distortion channels.

## Channels and controls
### Channel 1
- Gain
- Tone
- Level

### Channel 2
- Gain
- Bass
- Mid
- Treble
- Level

A master arrangement is also documented for the overall unit.

## Tubes and power
- **Tubes:** 2 x JJ ECC83-S / 12AX7.
- **Power:** 12V AC, minimum 1000mA.
- Dimensions: 144 x 120 x 58 mm at the knob level.
- Power jack: 5.5 x 2.1 mm.

## Sound
Channel 1 provides the cleaner preamp voice with simple Gain, Tone and Level controls. Channel 2 adds additional EQ shaping and distortion-oriented gain for a fuller, more aggressive preamp sound.
