# Correct Sound - Tube Beta Hybrid Mk-II

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Correct Sound
- **Catalog identity:** Tube Beta Hybrid Mk-II
- **Catalog type:** Preamp
- **Identity:** Exact Correct Sound Tube Beta Hybrid Mk-II.

## What this pedal is
Tube Beta Hybrid Mk-II is a large hybrid preamp combining a tube channel based on the 1973 Sunn Model T preamp with a solid-state channel based on the Sunn Beta preamp. Unlike the first version, the two sections are designed to operate in parallel.

## Channels and controls
- **Tube channel:** Model T-derived preamp with passive EQ.
- **Beta channel:** Sunn Beta-derived preamp with active EQ; available as Bass or Lead variant.
- Each channel has Drive, Bass, Mid, Treble and Level controls.
- Master Volume controls the overall output.

## Switching and connections
- Footswitch functions include tube, beta, combined operation and bypass.
- The published Mk-II specification includes a balanced XLR DI output with pre/post-master and ground-lift switching.
- True bypass is offered.

## Tubes and power
- Tube channel uses two 12AX7EH / ECC83 tubes.
- Published power requirement: 12V AC, 1200mA.
- Dimensions: 187 x 120 x 38 mm.

## Versions and factory options
- **Beta channel:** Bass or Lead variant.
- Some units were made without the XLR DI output as custom configurations. Do not treat a single used-unit configuration as universal factory specification.

## Transistor
- No exact transistor type or part number was established for the Beta section from the checked sources.

## Diode
- No exact diode type was established from the checked sources.

## Sound
The two preamps provide distinctly different voicings, with the Model T side covering the tube-derived response and the Beta side supplying a more aggressive solid-state preamp character. The parallel architecture allows the two channels to be combined rather than forcing the signal through them only in series.
