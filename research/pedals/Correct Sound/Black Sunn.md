# Correct Sound - Black Sunn

## Research identity

- **Builder:** Correct Sound
- **Catalog identity:** Black Sunn
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Preamp
- **Identity:** Exact Correct Sound Black Sunn preamp.

## What this pedal is

Black Sunn combines two preamp sections in one enclosure: a tube channel based on the preamp section of a 1973 Sunn Model T and a solid-state channel based on the RAT distortion design.

## Channels

- **Stone channel:** RAT-based distortion section.
- **Tube channel:** based on the 1973 Sunn Model T preamp using two JJ ECC83-S tubes.
- The channels can operate separately or together.
- When both are active, the signal passes through the RAT-based section and then the Model T-based tube section.

## Switching and power

- True bypass when the effect sections are bypassed.
- Published dimensions: 146 x 120 x 38 mm.
- Published power requirement: stabilized 12V DC, 1200mA.

## Transistor

- The stone section is described as RAT-based, but the checked source does not establish exact transistor part numbers for the Black Sunn unit.

## Diode

- No exact diode type was established from the checked sources.

## Sound

The Black Sunn is designed around the contrast between a solid-state distortion/preamp stage and a tube Model T-style preamp. Running the sections together allows the RAT-based stage to drive the warmer tube stage, while either section can also be used independently.
