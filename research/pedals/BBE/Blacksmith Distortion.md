# BBE - Blacksmith Distortion

## PRP identity

- Builder: BBE Sound
- Catalog type: Distortion
- Identity: BBE Blacksmith analog distortion.

## What this pedal is

Blacksmith is a Paul Gagon-designed high-output distortion intended to push tube amplifiers harder while retaining a Marshall-style tonal balance.

## Controls

- Gain
- Volume
- Passive 3-band PLEX-EQ

## Circuit / architecture

- LED-based distortion circuit.
- PLEX-EQ tone stack derived from a 1969 Plexi-style passive EQ.
- 1MOhm input impedance; BBE specifies 1kOhm output impedance when engaged.

## History

Effects Database dates the modern Blacksmith to January 25, 2013 and notes that the design concept traces to Gagon's 1980s work.

## Sound

BBE positions Blacksmith as a high output distortion that can push tube amps while preserving a Marshall/Plexi style balance. The PLEX EQ is intended to provide the familiar passive response associated with that amplifier family.
