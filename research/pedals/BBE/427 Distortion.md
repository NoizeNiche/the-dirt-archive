# BBE - 427 Distortion

## PRP identity
- Builder: BBE Sound
- Catalog type: Distortion
- Identity: BBE 427 Distortion, Paul Gagon-designed analog distortion from the 2013 product generation.

## What this pedal is
The 427 is a high-output distortion built around Paul Gagon's LED clipping approach. BBE describes it as a muscular, American-voiced distortion with a sweepable EQ section.

## Controls
- Gain
- Level
- Continuously sweepable EQ/Tone

## Circuit / architecture
- LED-based distortion circuit.
- Sweepable EQ section is described by BBE as a CSEQ section inspired by an early-1970s Big Muff Pi.
- Exact semiconductor part numbers are not documented here.

## History
Effects Database dates the model to January 25, 2013. Guitar World reviewed it in June 2013.
