# BBE - Free Fuzz

## PRP identity
- Builder: BBE Sound
- Catalog type: Fuzz
- Identity: BBE Free Fuzz.

## What this pedal is
The Free Fuzz is a 2006-era BBE fuzz designed around a vintage silicon-transistor fuzz concept. BBE describes it as dynamic, creamy and sustain-rich.

## Power / bypass
- Hardwire bypass.
- 9V battery compartment.
- Included external power supply in the original product documentation.

## Circuit / lineage
- Silicon-transistor fuzz concept.
- BBE describes the inspiration as a rare 1970s/late-1960s style fuzz associated with Jimi Hendrix-era sounds.
- Exact transistor and diode part numbers are not established.

## History
Effects Database dates the model to March 6, 2006 and preserves a prototype marker in the historical record.
