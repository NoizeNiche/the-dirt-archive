# BBE - AM64 American Metal

## PRP identity
- Builder: BBE Sound
- Catalog type: Distortion
- Identity: BBE AM-64 American Metal.

## What this pedal is
The AM-64 is a dedicated distortion pedal from BBE's late-2000s catalog. It uses a conventional three-control interface and true bypass.

## Controls
- Volume
- Tone
- Gain

## Power / bypass
- 9V DC input.
- True bypass.
- Blue LED status indicator.

## History
