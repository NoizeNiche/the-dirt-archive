# BBE - Windowpane - Silicon Fuzz

## PRP identity
- Builder: BBE Sound
- Catalog type: Fuzz
- Identity: BBE Windowpane Silicon Fuzz.

## What this pedal is
Windowpane is a silicon fuzz from BBE's 2013 generation, voiced around a mellowed vintage-fuzz character with consistent factory biasing.

## Circuit / architecture
- Two sequentially wired silicon transistors.
- Factory-biasing is part of the design concept.
- BBE specifies a 50kOhm low-impedance input and 10kOhm output impedance when engaged.
- Exact transistor part numbers are not documented.

## Power / bypass
- 9V battery compartment.
- True hardwire bypass.

## History
Effects Database dates the model to January 25, 2013. It is associated with NAMM 2013 and later BBE demonstrations.
