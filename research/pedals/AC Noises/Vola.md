# AC Noises — Vola

## PRP identity
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Transparent boost/preamp/overdrive.

## What this pedal is
Vola is a transparent, dynamic overdrive that can also work as a clean boost or preamp. Its range goes from subtle signal enhancement to denser overdrive for chords and clear solos. [1][2]

## Colorways
- Documented finish: pale mint green with red knobs and dragonfly artwork.

## Versions / options
- Volume, Tone and drive/gain control.
- True bypass.
- 9V DC negative.
- Hand-built in Italy.
- No numbered production revision verified.

## Sound
Vola is touch-responsive and transparent, adding clarity and presence without heavily rewriting the guitar/amp character. Higher drive settings become denser while retaining an articulate response. [1][2]
