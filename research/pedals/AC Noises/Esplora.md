# AC Noises — Esplora

## PRP identity
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Silicon-transistor fuzz with two tonal-option switches.

## What this pedal is
Esplora is a silicon-transistor fuzz with Level, Tone and Fuzz controls. One switch can bypass the tone control for a full, mid-heavy voice; the other adds treble brightness and a more aggressive edge. [1][2]

## Colorways
- Documented finish: blue enclosure, yellow knobs, astronaut artwork.

## Versions / options
- Level, Tone, Fuzz.
- Tone-bypass switch.
- Treble-brightness switch.
- True bypass, 9V DC.
- No numbered production revision verified.

## Transistor
- Type: **Silicon transistors.**
- Exact part: **Unknown.** [2]

## Diode
- Exact diode/clipping device: **Unknown.**

## Sound
The core sound is classic silicon fuzz, but the tone-bypass switch makes it much fuller and more mid-forward. The second switch adds treble presence for a brighter, more cutting fuzz voice. [1][2]
