# AC Noises — Urla

## PRP identity
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** CMOS fuzz followed by a synth-based dual resonant filter.

## What this pedal is
Urla combines a CMOS fuzz with a dual resonant filter. The filter section provides resonant shaping and expression control, making the unit a fuzz/filter/synth-like instrument rather than a conventional dirt box. [1][2]

## Sound
Urla is aggressive, gated and experimental, with the CMOS fuzz feeding a resonant synth-style filter. It can move from harsh fuzz into sweeping, unstable and highly filtered textures, especially when the filter is controlled externally. [1][2]
