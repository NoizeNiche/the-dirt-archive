# AC Noises — Combatti

## PRP identity
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** LM308N-based distortion with three selectable clipping modes.

## What this pedal is
Combatti uses an LM308N and offers three clipping modes: open/dynamic, compressed mid-cut, and a more aggressive low-end-defined mode. Lowering the distortion control can move it into warm overdrive territory. [1][2]

## Colorways
- Documented finish: light orange/salmon with illustrated artwork.

## Versions / options
- Volume, Tone, Distortion.
- Three-position clipping selector.
- True bypass, 9V DC.
- No numbered production revision verified.

## Transistor
- Exact transistor/device: **Unknown.**
- LM308N is an op-amp IC, not a transistor.

## Diode
- Three clipping modes are documented.
- Exact diode parts: **Unknown.**

## Sound
The middle clipping position is open and dynamic, while the upper mode is more compressed and mid-scooped. The lower mode emphasizes defined low end and heavier rhythm textures, with maximum drive entering gritty/fuzz-like territory. [1][2]
