# AC Noises — Arpiona

## PRP identity
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Limited-edition Xabier Iriondo collaboration combining octa-synth, gated fuzz and boost.

## What this pedal is
Arpiona contains three independent effect sections and five circuits, with up to 30 combinations. The octa-synth provides -2, -1 and +1 octave controls; the fuzz adds bass boost and hold-to-oscillate behavior; the boost provides up to +25 dB and a noise switch. [1][2]

## Sound
Gated, aggressive fuzz can be combined with sub-octave and octave-up synth tones, then pushed by the boost. The hold-to-oscillate function and noise filtering make it capable of unstable, synth-like and noise-oriented textures. [1][2]
