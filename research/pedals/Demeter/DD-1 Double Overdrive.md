## What this pedal is
The DD-1 Double Overdrive combines two Demeter overdrive circuits in one chassis: the **DRV-1 Overdrivulator** and the **DRV-2 Uberdriveulator**. The result is a dual-drive pedal intended for independent use or stacking.

## Sound
Used independently, the two sections cover different overdrive flavors. When stacked, the DD-1 can move from light drive into thicker and more saturated overdrive while preserving the ability to switch each section separately.
