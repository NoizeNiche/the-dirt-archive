## What this pedal is
BIG EAR n.y.c. More More More was a historical boost/overdrive product in the Brooklyn-era Big Ear catalog. Contemporary 2015 coverage describes **three independent boost channels** that can also be stacked, producing **seven possible combinations**. A volume-drop control allows the output to be pulled back after stacking.

## Controls / operation
- **Three boost channels:** Each channel can be used independently.
- **Stacking:** The three channels can be combined in different orders/sets for up to seven total boost combinations.
- **Volume drop:** Additional output adjustment for reducing the level after boosting.
- Exact factory knob labels for the three channels were not recovered with enough confidence to invent a control map.
