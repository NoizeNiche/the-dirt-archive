# Big Ear NYC - Chaka - Octave Fuzz

## PRP identity

- **Builder:** Big Ear NYC
- **Pedal:** Chaka - Octave Fuzz
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Big Ear NYC CHAKA, an octave-up fuzz introduced at Summer NAMM 2014.

## What this pedal is

Effects Database identifies CHAKA as a 1-octave-up fuzz with a dedicated footswitch for the octave effect. With the octave section off, it is also intended to function as a standalone fuzz.

## Controls / switching

- Dedicated **octave footswitch**.
- Full factory knob labeling was not preserved in the surviving exact-model source material, so no additional controls are invented.

## Circuit / components

- **Hand-matched germanium diodes** on the octave side.
- **NOS metal-can transistors** in the fuzz circuit.
- **LED clipping** is documented.
- Premier Guitar's 2014 NAMM coverage independently describes the fuzz section as LED-clipping based, while Effects Database specifies the hand-matched germanium diodes on the octave side.

## Power

- Standard **9V power supply only**.
- **No internal battery connector**.

## Sound / operation

Big Ear positioned CHAKA as an octave-up fuzz designed to retain low-end while adding the octave effect through a separate footswitch. Effects Database documents it as a Summer NAMM 2014 product.
