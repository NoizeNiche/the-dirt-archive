# Carl Martin Purple Moon

- **Builder:** Carl Martin
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Catalog identity:** Purple Moon
- **Colorways:** Standard Purple Moon finish is documented; no formal alternate factory colorway catalog was confirmed.
- **Versions:** Combined fuzz-and-vibe pedal. No numbered production revision was confirmed in the reviewed sources.
- **Version changes:** No documented factory hardware revision was established.
- **Transistor type:** Silicon fuzz is documented by Carl Martin's current support/product material.
- **Diode type:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.
- **Controls / switching:** Fuzz, Fuzz Level, Level, and two-speed modulation controls, with the pedal's dual-speed vibe selection. One bypass footswitch.
- **Power:** Fully analog, true bypass, regulated 9V DC; battery operation is not supported.
- **Sound:** Purple Moon combines a vintage-style silicon fuzz with Uni-Vibe-like modulation. Carl Martin describes it as a late-60s/70s psychedelic-oriented design, with fuzz supplying organic saturation while the vibe section adds the chewy moving texture and selectable speed character.
- **Research confidence:** High for the combined fuzz/vibe architecture, silicon fuzz, controls, analog/true-bypass format, and stated voicing.
