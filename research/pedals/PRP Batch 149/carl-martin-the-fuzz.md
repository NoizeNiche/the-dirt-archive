# Carl Martin The Fuzz

- **Builder:** Carl Martin
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Catalog identity:** The Fuzz
- **Colorways:** Standard Carl Martin production appearance is documented; no formal alternate factory colorway catalog was confirmed.
- **Versions:** No numbered factory version was confirmed in the reviewed sources.
- **Version changes:** No documented factory hardware revision was established.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.
- **Diode type:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.
- **Controls / switching:** Three-band EQ works with the wave-guide section's Deep and High controls. The reviewed current source describes a dedicated bypass footswitch but does not provide a complete current specification table.
- **Power:** Built-in regulated ±12V power supply for high headroom. Detailed external power specifications were not exposed in the current source.
- **Sound:** The Fuzz is designed to cover a remarkably broad gain range by combining its wave-guide Deep and High controls with three-band EQ. Carl Martin documents the result as spanning old blues fuzz, mid-80s hard-rock tones, and harder modern distortion, with the wave-guide controls helping clean up and reshape the saturated voice.
- **Research confidence:** High for the wave-guide/Deep/High concept, three-band EQ, ±12V architecture, and documented range of sounds; medium for the complete control legend.
