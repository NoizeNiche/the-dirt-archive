# Carl Martin Panama

- **Builder:** Carl Martin
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** Panama
- **Colorways:** Standard production finish is documented in current Carl Martin product photography; no formal alternate factory colorway catalog was confirmed.
- **Versions:** No numbered factory version was confirmed in the reviewed sources.
- **Version changes:** No documented factory hardware revision was established.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.
- **Diode type:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.
- **Controls / switching:** Gain, Tone, Level, and Damping.
- **Power / circuit:** Analog design with an internal DC/DC converter that supplies ±12V from a regulated 9V input.
- **Sound:** Panama is a hot-modded British-style drive with strong mids and substantial gain. Its unusual Damping control changes the response of the low end under gain, moving from tight and focused attack at higher settings toward a thicker, looser, deeper feel as Damping is reduced.
- **Research confidence:** High for the four controls, analog/±12V architecture, and documented low-end behavior; medium for broad historical amp-lineage comparisons.
