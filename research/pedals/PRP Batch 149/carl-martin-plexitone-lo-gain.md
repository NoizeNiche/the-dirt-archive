# Carl Martin PlexiTone Lo-Gain

- **Builder:** Carl Martin
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** PlexiTone Lo-Gain
- **Colorways:** Standard compact Carl Martin finish is documented; no formal alternate factory colorway catalog was confirmed.
- **Versions:** Distinct low-gain member of the PlexiTone family. It is not treated as the same public product as the original multi-channel PlexiTone.
- **Version changes:** The Lo-Gain incorporates a DC/DC converter so a regulated 9V supply feeds the same ±12V operating level used by the original AC-powered Pro Series PlexiTone approach.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.
- **Diode type:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.
- **Controls / switching:** Level, Gain, and Tone. Tone is a high-cut filter. Buffered bypass.
- **Power:** Regulated 9V DC, maximum 62 mA; battery operation is not supported; internal operating level is ±12V.
- **Sound:** Lo-Gain is intentionally the lower-gain Plexi voice, meant to push an amplifier into its sweet spot or add British color without the saturation of the full PlexiTone. Carl Martin specifically describes it as working well for single coils and humbuckers and as a useful stacking pedal.
- **Research confidence:** High for the compact layout, controls, power architecture, buffered bypass, and intended lower-gain Plexi voicing.
