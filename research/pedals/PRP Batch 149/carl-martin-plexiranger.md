# Carl Martin PlexiRanger

- **Builder:** Carl Martin
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** PlexiRanger
- **Colorways:** Standard production finish is documented in current Carl Martin photography; no formal alternate factory colorway catalog was confirmed.
- **Versions:** No numbered factory version was confirmed in the reviewed sources.
- **Version changes:** No documented factory revision was established.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.
- **Diode type:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.
- **Controls / switching:** Treble-booster-inspired boost section with adjustable Boost/Range behavior, plus a gain section and high-cut Tone control. True bypass.
- **Power:** Regulated 9V DC, maximum 100 mA; battery operation is not supported.
- **Sound:** PlexiRanger combines an upper-frequency treble-booster concept with a dedicated gain channel. The boost can push upper mids and treble into an amplifier, while the drive section adds body, saturation, and sustain; the Range and Tone controls help focus the low end and trim the high-frequency edge.
- **Research confidence:** High for the boost/drive architecture, true bypass, power specification, and stated British-rock voicing; medium for exact knob naming where the current page uses section labels rather than a conventional single-line control list.
