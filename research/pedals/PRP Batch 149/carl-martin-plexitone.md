# Carl Martin PlexiTone

- **Builder:** Carl Martin
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** PlexiTone
- **Colorways:** Standard Pro Series finish is documented; no formal alternate factory colorway catalog was confirmed.
- **Versions:** The original full-size PlexiTone is a three-section platform with Crunch, High Gain, and independent clean Boost.
- **Version changes:** Later PlexiTone-family compact products are related designs rather than being silently folded into the parent record. PlexiTone Lo-Gain and PlexiTone Single remain distinct catalog entries because their feature sets and intended gain ranges differ.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.
- **Diode type:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.
- **Controls / switching:** Separate Crunch, High Gain, and clean Boost sections, with dedicated controls and footswitches. The Boost can provide up to 20 dB according to the current Carl Martin specification.
- **Power:** Analog, true bypass, regulated 9V input with internal ±12V operation.
- **Sound:** PlexiTone is designed as a British/Plexi-style gain platform covering edge-of-breakup crunch through saturated lead distortion. The Crunch channel supplies the lower-gain side, High Gain handles heavier saturation, and the independent clean Boost can lift the amp or push the gain stages further.
- **Research confidence:** High for the three-section platform, boost function, analog/±12V architecture, and stated Plexi-style voicing.
- **Sources checked:**
  - https://carlmartin.com/products/plexitone/
