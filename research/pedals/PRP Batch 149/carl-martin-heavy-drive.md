# Carl Martin Heavy Drive

- **Builder:** Carl Martin
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Catalog identity:** Heavy Drive
- **Colorways:** Standard production appearance is documented in Carl Martin product imagery; no formal alternate factory colorway catalog was confirmed.
- **Versions:** No numbered factory version was confirmed in the reviewed sources.
- **Version changes:** No documented factory revision was established.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.
- **Diode type:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.
- **Controls / switching:** Attack and Edge are the defining tone-shaping controls in addition to the main distortion controls; the exact full control legend was not reproduced where the current product page omits a detailed specification table. True bypass.
- **Power:** 9V battery or 9V external power supply.
- **Sound:** Heavy Drive is aimed at high-gain heavy-rock distortion with a large, tight low end while retaining pick articulation and the guitar's underlying character. Attack and Edge let the player move the distortion's character from tighter modern aggression toward more saturated heavy voices.
- **Research confidence:** High for the heavy-rock voicing, tight low end, Attack/Edge concept, and bypass/power information; medium for undocumented control-label details.
