# Carl Martin DC-Drive

- **Builder:** Carl Martin
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** DC-Drive
- **Colorways:** The current compact pedal is documented in Carl Martin's current product photography; no formal alternate factory colorway catalog was confirmed.
- **Versions:** The current compact DC-Drive is distinct from the earlier full-size DC Drive platform in enclosure and feature arrangement. The original full-size DC Drive had independent clean-boost functionality; the compact current model centers on the Drive section and Regular/Fat voicing.
- **Version changes:** Carl Martin's current documentation describes the compact format as a refinement of the Vintage Series rather than a separate circuit concept. The 2026 manual documents the Regular/Fat voicing and regulated 9V operation.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.
- **Diode type:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.
- **Controls / switching:** Drive, Tone, Level, and Regular/Fat mini-toggle, with true bypass.
- **Power:** Regulated 9V DC; battery operation is not supported on the current compact version.
- **Sound:** DC-Drive is a responsive general-purpose overdrive that cleans up with lighter playing and gets thicker as the Drive rises. Regular mode is the tighter, more neutral voice, while Fat adds gain and low-mid weight for a fuller, more saturated response.
- **Research confidence:** High for the current controls, Regular/Fat behavior, power arrangement, and documented voicing; medium for historical distinctions from the earlier full-size DC Drive.
