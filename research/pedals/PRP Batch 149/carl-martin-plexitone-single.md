# Carl Martin PlexiTone Single

- **Builder:** Carl Martin
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** PlexiTone Single
- **Colorways:** Standard compact Carl Martin finish is documented; no formal alternate factory colorway catalog was confirmed.
- **Versions:** Compact single-channel member of the PlexiTone family, developed in collaboration with Pete Thorn.
- **Version changes:** PlexiTone Single is based on the original PlexiTone High Gain channel but was refined for a compact enclosure with tighter low end and smoother highs. It remains a distinct public model from both the full PlexiTone and Lo-Gain.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.
- **Diode type:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.
- **Controls / switching:** Level, Drive, and Tone with one true-bypass footswitch.
- **Power:** Regulated 9V DC; internal operation at ±12V; battery operation is not supported.
- **Sound:** PlexiTone Single delivers the saturated British-stack character of the original High Gain channel in a smaller format. Pete Thorn's requested refinement tightens the bottom end and softens the top end somewhat, making the high-gain voice easier to integrate across different amps while retaining dynamic response.
- **Research confidence:** High for the Pete Thorn collaboration, compact derivation, control layout, power architecture, and documented tonal refinement.
