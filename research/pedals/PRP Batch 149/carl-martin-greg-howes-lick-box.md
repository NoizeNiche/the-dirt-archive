# Carl Martin Greg Howe's Lick Box

- **Builder:** Carl Martin
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** Greg Howe's Lick Box
- **Colorways:** The documented production pedal uses the standard Carl Martin Pro Series appearance; no formal alternate factory colorway catalog was confirmed.
- **Versions:** Signature pedal developed with Greg Howe. No numbered production revision was confirmed in the reviewed current documentation.
- **Version changes:** No verified factory V1/V2 hardware change was established.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.
- **Diode type:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.
- **Controls / switching:** Three independent sections: High Gain, Crunch, and clean Boost. High Gain and Crunch each have Level, Gain, and Tone; Boost has its own Level control. Each section has a dedicated footswitch.
- **Signal / power architecture:** High Gain comes before Crunch internally, while the clean Boost can be used independently or with either drive section. The internal DC/DC converter lets the pedal run from regulated 9V while operating internally at ±12V. True bypass.
- **Sound:** Lick Box is a multi-stage signature gain platform rather than a single fixed distortion voice. High Gain supplies extended lows and high-gain saturation, Crunch is more mid-focused and less compressed, and the clean Boost can raise level on its own or on top of the gain stages, making stacked tones possible.
- **Research confidence:** High for the three-section architecture, controls, stacking behavior, power conversion, and signature collaboration.
