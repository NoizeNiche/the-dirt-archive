# Big Joe Stomp Box Company - B-313 FREAKSHOW FUZZ (GERMANIUM)

## PRP identity

- **Builder:** Big Joe Stomp Box Company
- **Pedal:** B-313 FREAKSHOW FUZZ (GERMANIUM)
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Big Joe's germanium Freakshow Fuzz, also labeled B-313(G) in the manufacturer's current page.

## What this pedal is

The Germanium Freakshow is the fat-sounding, responsive version of Big Joe's Freakshow pair. The manufacturer states that it uses two NOS matched transistors and contrasts it with the brighter, higher-gain silicon version.

## Controls / switching

- **Tone**
- **Fuzz**
- **Output**
- **Bias**
- **Trim**
- **Selectable input-impedance Buffer**

Bias and Trim provide adjustment for symmetry, gain structure and clipping.

## Circuit / components

- **Two NOS matched germanium transistors**, according to the manufacturer.
- Exact transistor part numbers are not disclosed.
- Exact diode/clipping-device part numbers are not disclosed.

## Power / hardware

- 9V battery or 9VDC supply.
- 2.1 x 5.5 mm barrel connector, center negative.
- True bypass.
- Status LED.
- 6.3 mm mono input/output.
- Dimensions: 4.80 x 2.91 x 2.12 in.
- Weight: 19 oz.

## Sound

Big Joe describes the Germanium Freakshow as highly responsive, fat and harmonically rich, with a vintage-style fuzz character. The archive retains those as manufacturer descriptions.
