# Big Joe Stomp Box Company - B-312 FREAKSHOW FUZZ (SILICON)

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Big Joe Stomp Box Company
- **Pedal:** B-312 FREAKSHOW FUZZ (SILICON)
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Big Joe's silicon Freakshow Fuzz.

## What this pedal is
Big Joe documents the Silicon Freakshow as the brighter, more modern and higher-gain counterpart to its Germanium Freakshow. Both were designed as fuzz pedals with external bias and trim controls and a selectable input-impedance buffer.

## Controls / switching
- **Fuzz**
- **Tone**
- **Output**
- **Bias**
- **Trim**
- **Selectable input-impedance Buffer**

The Bias and Trim controls provide external adjustment of symmetry, gain structure and clipping.

## Circuit / components
- Silicon fuzz version.
