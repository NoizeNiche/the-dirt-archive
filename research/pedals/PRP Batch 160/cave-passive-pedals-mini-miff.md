# Cave Passive Pedals Mini Miff

- **Builder:** Cave Passive Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz / Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** Mini Miff
- **What this pedal is:** A passive three-mode drive pedal offering two overdrive characters and a clean boost setting.
- **Colorways:** Historical Cave Passive Pedals presentation documented.
- **Versions:** Mini Miff.
- **Version changes:** No separate production version was confirmed in the reviewed sources.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented.
- **Diode type:** Not documented.
- **Controls / switching:** One rotary selector with three positions: Overdrive 1, Clean boost, and Overdrive 2; true bypass footswitch.
- **Power:** Passive design; no battery or external power required.
- **Sound:** Overdrive 1 is described as a fuzzier, 1960s-like voice, the center position provides clean volume/tone boost, and Overdrive 2 gives a smoother, more 1970s-style crunch or distortion. Response varies with pickup type and signal-chain position.
- **Research confidence:** High for the three-mode layout, passive operation, and documented tonal behavior; moderate for undocumented circuit components.
