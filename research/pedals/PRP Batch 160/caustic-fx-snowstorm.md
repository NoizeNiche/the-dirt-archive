# Caustic FX Snowstorm

- **Builder:** Caustic FX
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Catalog identity:** Snowstorm
- **What this pedal is:** An open-source DSP white-noise drone and ring-modulated distortion pedal that combines a noise source, oscillator/carrier routing, filtering, and multiple digital distortion algorithms.
- **Colorways:** White finish documented by the current product presentation.
- **Versions:** Snowstorm.
- **Version changes:** No separate production version was confirmed in the reviewed sources.
- **Transistor type:** Not applicable to the documented DSP architecture.
- **Diode type:** Not applicable to the documented DSP architecture.
- **Controls / switching:** Exact physical control labels were not documented in the reviewed product description.
- **Power:** Not documented in the reviewed product description.
- **Sound:** The pedal ring-modulates a white-noise source with a free-running sine or rising-saw oscillator, then adds a dry noise layer. External audio can substitute as a carrier. The signal can pass through low-pass, high-pass, or comb filtering and then one of six distortion algorithms, producing drones, industrial textures, bass-heavy noise, and heavily degraded tones.
- **Research confidence:** High for the documented signal architecture and six distortion algorithms; moderate for the undocumented physical control and power specifications.
