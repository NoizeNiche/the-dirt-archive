# Cave Passive Pedals Grunt MkII

- **Builder:** Cave Passive Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** Grunt MkII
- **What this pedal is:** A second-generation version of the original Grunt passive bass overdrive/boost.
- **Colorways:** Standard Grunt MkII enclosure.
- **Versions:** Grunt MkII; it is documented as the successor to the original Grunt and predates the Super Grunt.
- **Version changes:** The same basic Grunt circuit is retained, with Level added to tame output and variable Drive added to set the amount of grit.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented.
- **Diode type:** Not documented.
- **Controls / switching:** Level and Drive are the added controls documented for MkII alongside the underlying Grunt behavior.
- **Power:** Passive, no battery or external power required.
- **Factory modifications:** None documented.
- **Sound:** MkII adds practical control over output and grit to the warm, powerful Grunt voice. It can produce valve-like overdrive and can fatten and sustain bass notes.
- **Research confidence:** High.
- **Sources checked:**
  - https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/cavepassive/grunt/mk2
  - https://www.talkbass.com/threads/cave-passive-pedals.808351/
