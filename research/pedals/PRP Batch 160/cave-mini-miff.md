# Cave Passive Pedals Mini Miff

- **Builder:** Cave Passive Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz / Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** Mini Miff
- **What this pedal is:** A three-mode passive drive pedal offering two overdrive characters plus a clean boost setting.
- **Colorways:** Standard Mini Miff enclosure.
- **Versions:** Mini Miff; no materially distinct production revision established by the sources checked.
- **Version changes:** Not documented.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented.
- **Diode type:** Not documented.
- **Controls / switching:** Three positions: OD1, Clean boost, and OD2. OD1 is described as fuzz-like; OD2 is smoother.
- **Power:** Passive, no battery or external power required.
- **Factory modifications:** None documented.
- **Sound:** OD1 moves toward a 1960s/fuzzy character, the center setting provides a clean volume/tone boost, and OD2 gives a smoother distortion-like sound. The pedal is true bypass according to period review coverage.
- **Research confidence:** High for the three-mode behavior and passive operation.
