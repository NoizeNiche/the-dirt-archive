# Cave Passive Pedals 60's Break Up

- **Builder:** Cave Passive Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** 60's Break Up
- **What this pedal is:** A passive overdrive designed to create three selectable breakup/crackle tones associated with an older amplifier being pushed hard.
- **Colorways:** Historical Cave Passive Pedals presentation documented.
- **Versions:** 60's Break Up.
- **Version changes:** No separate production version was confirmed in the reviewed sources.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented.
- **Diode type:** Not documented.
- **Controls / switching:** Three tone settings are documented; the complete physical control layout is not documented in the reviewed source.
- **Power:** Passive design; no battery or external power required.
- **Sound:** The three settings are intended to simulate an older amplifier at high volume, ranging from distinctive breakup/crackle toward fuzzier textures. The source notes operation can be placed before or after other powered pedals.
- **Research confidence:** High for the three-tone-setting concept and passive operation; moderate for undocumented component and control details.
