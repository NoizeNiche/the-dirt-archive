# Cave Passive Pedals Super Grunt

- **Builder:** Cave Passive Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** Super Grunt
- **What this pedal is:** A higher-output development of Cave's Grunt bass overdrive family.
- **Colorways:** Standard Super Grunt enclosure.
- **Versions:** Super Grunt; related directly to original Grunt and Grunt MkII.
- **Version changes:** The original Grunt overdrive/boost circuit is retained, with a toggle that engages a higher-boost "super" circuit.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented.
- **Diode type:** Not documented.
- **Controls / switching:** Grunt-family controls plus a toggle for the higher-boost circuit; a mechanical on/off indicator replaces the usual LED.
- **Power:** Passive, no batteries or external power required.
- **Factory modifications:** None documented.
- **Sound:** Super Grunt retains the warm Grunt voice while adding a stronger boost option. Its passive architecture and mechanical indicator are part of the design.
- **Research confidence:** High for the version relationship and passive operation.
