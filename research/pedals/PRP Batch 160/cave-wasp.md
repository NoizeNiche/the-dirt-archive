# Cave Passive Pedals The Wasp

- **Builder:** Cave Passive Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Catalog identity:** The Wasp
- **What this pedal is:** A passive bass fuzz with a harsher overdrive character than the Grunt.
- **Colorways:** Standard Wasp enclosure.
- **Versions:** The Wasp; the MkII is a separate later identity.
- **Version changes:** Not documented for the original Wasp beyond the existence of the later MkII.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented.
- **Diode type:** Not documented.
- **Controls / switching:** A rotary depth control is documented; the source does not establish the complete control naming.
- **Power:** Passive, no batteries or external power required.
- **Factory modifications:** None documented.
- **Sound:** Cave described the Wasp as harsher than the Grunt and closer to fuzz tone. Period commentary also described a range from mild synth-like/envelope-filter character with fuzz toward harder rock textures.
- **Research confidence:** High for the passive bass-fuzz identity; moderate for exact control details.
- **Sources checked:**
  - https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/cavepassive/wasp
  - https://www.talkbass.com/threads/cave-passive-pedals.598605/page-6

## Sound

More musical than it's digital counterparts, this analogue fuzz for bass has a rotary control for depth which creates a variety of tones from 60's hippy to hard rock.
