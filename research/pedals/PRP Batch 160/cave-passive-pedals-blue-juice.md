# Cave Passive Pedals Blue Juice

- **Builder:** Cave Passive Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** Blue Juice
- **What this pedal is:** A passive overdrive voiced for warm, classic 1960s hard-blues tones, with controls intended to vary bite and the firmness of the drive.
- **Colorways:** Historical Cave Passive Pedals presentation documented.
- **Versions:** Blue Juice.
- **Version changes:** No separate production version was confirmed in the reviewed sources.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented.
- **Diode type:** Not documented.
- **Controls / switching:** Bite and Squash controls are documented; the complete control layout is not confirmed in the reviewed source.
- **Power:** Passive design; no battery or external power required.
- **Sound:** The pedal is described as covering edgy lead and hard-driving rhythm tones. Turning Bite counter-clockwise adds more drive, while Squash tightens or loosens the drive character.
- **Research confidence:** High for the historical voicing, Bite/Squash functions, and passive design; moderate for undocumented component and control details.
