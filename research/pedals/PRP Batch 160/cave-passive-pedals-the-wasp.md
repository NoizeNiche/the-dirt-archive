- **Builder:** Cave Passive Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Catalog identity:** The Wasp
- **What this pedal is:** A passive analog bass fuzz based on a harsher overdrive character than the Cave Grunt and described as a fuzz-tone effect.
- **Colorways:** Historical Cave Passive Pedals presentation documented.

- **Transistor type:** Not documented.

- **Sound:** The source describes a harsher overdrive than the Grunt, classed as fuzz tone, with the Depth control covering tones from 1960s-style sounds toward harder rock. It is intended for bass and uses an analog approach.
