# Cave Passive Pedals The Wasp MkII

- **Builder:** Cave Passive Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Catalog identity:** The Wasp MkII
- **What this pedal is:** A souped-up second-generation version of Cave's original Wasp fuzz.
- **Colorways:** Standard Wasp MkII enclosure.
- **Versions:** Wasp MkII; successor to the first-generation Wasp.
- **Version changes:** The MkII adds direct control over fuzz amount and thickness through the Sting and Aggression controls.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented.
- **Diode type:** Not documented.
- **Controls / switching:** Sting controls the amount of fuzz; Aggression controls thickness. A mechanical indicator is used instead of a powered LED.
- **Power:** Passive, no battery or external power required.
- **Factory modifications:** None documented.
- **Sound:** Wasp MkII keeps the original Wasp's harsher fuzz direction while adding direct control of fuzz quantity and thickness. The all-passive design gives it the unusual ability to remain connected without conventional pedal power.
- **Research confidence:** High.
