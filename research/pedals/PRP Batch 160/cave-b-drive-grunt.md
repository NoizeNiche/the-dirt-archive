# Cave Passive Pedals B-Drive Grunt

- **Builder:** Cave Passive Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** B-Drive Grunt
- **What this pedal is:** A passive bass overdrive/boost based on the Grunt design family.
- **Colorways:** Standard B-Drive enclosure; cosmetic differences are not separate variants.
- **Versions:** B-Drive Grunt; later B-Drive Mk2 examples are distinct version records elsewhere in the archive.
- **Version changes:** Not documented for this exact B-Drive Grunt record.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented.
- **Diode type:** Not documented.
- **Controls / switching:** Drive and Level. The Drive control increases grit while Level manages output; period user documentation notes that Cave's control directions run opposite the convention used by many common pedals.
- **Power:** Passive, no external power required.
- **Factory modifications:** None documented.
- **Sound:** With Drive low the B-Drive Grunt can provide subtle thickening and slight breakup; increasing Drive adds more bass-oriented overdrive. Level can provide a small signal boost at low Drive.
- **Research confidence:** High for controls, passive operation and general response.
