# Cave Passive Pedals Grunt

- **Builder:** Cave Passive Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** Grunt
- **What this pedal is:** A passive bass boost/overdrive with a simple Clean/Dirty selector.
- **Colorways:** Historical Cave Passive Pedals presentation documented.
- **Versions:** Grunt.
- **Version changes:** The Grunt is the original model later followed by the Grunt MkII and Super Grunt.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented.
- **Diode type:** Not documented.
- **Controls / switching:** Two-position selector labeled Clean / Dirty plus footswitch.
- **Power:** Passive design; no battery or external power required.
- **Sound:** Clean provides a frequency-boost function, while Dirty produces an intentionally strong 1960s-style overdriven bass-amp character. The amount of effect also depends on the signal level feeding the pedal.
- **Research confidence:** High for the Clean/Dirty operation and passive bass overdrive identity; moderate for undocumented circuit components.
