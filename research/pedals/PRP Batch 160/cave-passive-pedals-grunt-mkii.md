# Cave Passive Pedals Grunt MkII

- **Builder:** Cave Passive Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** Grunt MkII
- **What this pedal is:** A second-generation passive bass overdrive based on the original Grunt circuit, adding variable drive and output-level control.
- **Colorways:** Historical Cave Passive Pedals presentation documented.
- **Versions:** Grunt MkII.
- **Version changes:** Compared with the original Grunt, the MkII adds a Level control to tame output and a variable Drive control to set the amount of grit.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented.
- **Diode type:** Not documented.
- **Controls / switching:** Variable Drive and Level controls; complete physical layout not independently confirmed.
- **Power:** Passive design; no battery or external power required.
- **Sound:** The MkII retains the warm, boosted character of the original Grunt while allowing the player to dial the desired amount of grit. It is described as capable of warm valve-like overdrive plus added body and sustain in bass notes.
- **Research confidence:** High for the second-generation relationship and added Drive/Level functions; moderate for undocumented component details and complete control labeling.
