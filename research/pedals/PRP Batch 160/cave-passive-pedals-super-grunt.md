# Cave Passive Pedals Super Grunt

- **Builder:** Cave Passive Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** Super Grunt
- **What this pedal is:** An expanded version of the Cave Grunt bass overdrive that keeps the original Grunt/boost circuit and adds a higher-boost “Super” setting.
- **Colorways:** Historical Cave Passive Pedals presentation documented.
- **Versions:** Super Grunt.
- **Version changes:** The Super Grunt retains the original Grunt overdrive/boost circuit and adds a toggle-controlled higher-boost circuit. It also introduced a mechanical on/off indicator to the general-production Cave range.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented.
- **Diode type:** Not documented.
- **Controls / switching:** Grunt circuit plus a toggle for the higher-boost Super circuit; complete control labeling not documented.
- **Power:** Passive design; no battery or external power required.
- **Sound:** The base Grunt character can be pushed into a stronger boost mode, expanding the passive bass-drive concept without adding a battery or external supply.
- **Research confidence:** High for the Grunt relationship, Super boost function, and passive operation; moderate for undocumented component and complete control details.
