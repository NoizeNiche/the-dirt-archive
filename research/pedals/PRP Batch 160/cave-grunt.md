# Cave Passive Pedals Grunt

- **Builder:** Cave Passive Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** Grunt
- **What this pedal is:** Cave Passive Pedals' original passive bass overdrive/boost, launched in 2009.
- **Colorways:** Standard Grunt enclosure.
- **Versions:** Original Grunt; Grunt MkII and Super Grunt are separate identities in the archive.
- **Version changes:** This record represents the original Grunt. Cave's documented timeline places the launch of the original Grunt in 2009.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented.
- **Diode type:** Not documented.
- **Controls / switching:** A Clean/Dirty choice is documented. Clean functions as a frequency boost, while Dirty produces an overdriven bass-amplifier sound.
- **Power:** Passive, with no battery or external power required.
- **Factory modifications:** None documented.
- **Sound:** The Grunt can act as a clean frequency boost or move into a distinctly 1960s-flavored overdriven bass tone. Its passive architecture lets it remain connected without a conventional power source.
- **Research confidence:** High.

## Sound

The folks at Cave say that “The Grunt has the ability for you to choose either Clean for frequency boost or Dirty for an outrageous 60's sounding overdriven bass amplifier.” Well, does it actually work?
Depending on the position of the switch, you get either a volume boost (clean), or a nice 60’s/70’s overdriven crunch (dirty).
It really is a miracle, and can transform a dull bass tone into something that would make any rock or funk band happy.
