# BIXONIC - EXP2000 Expandora

## PRP identity
- Builder: BIXONIC
- Catalog type: Distortion / Overdrive / Fuzz
- Identity: Original BIXONIC EXP2000 Expandora.

## What this pedal is
The original Expandora is a multi-gain dirt pedal with internal DIP-switch settings for Crunch, Overdrive and Distortion. Effects Database also describes a wide range extending toward Fuzz Face-style tones.

## Controls / modes
- Gain
- Tone
- Level
- Internal DIP switches selecting Crunch, Overdrive and Distortion gain structures.

## Circuit / behavior
The design is dynamically responsive to the instrument volume control and preserves note definition across its gain range.

## History
BIXONIC's later EXP-2000R was a redesigned reissue with front-access drive control, increased low-frequency response, true bypass and a new chassis. The archive keeps the original EXP2000 separate from the later reissue.
