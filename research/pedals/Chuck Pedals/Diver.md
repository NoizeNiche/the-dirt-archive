# Chuck Pedals — Diver

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Diver
- **Builder:** Chuck Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Chuck Pedals Diver, a Green Russian Bubble Font Big Muff-derived fuzz with three Creature Modes and dedicated mid shaping.

## What this pedal is
Effects Database introduced Diver as a **Green Russian Bubble Font Big Muff Pi**-based fuzz. [1]

Equipboard's current catalog description documents the Diver as a handmade USA fuzz with mid-gain transistor stages, a dedicated Mid scoop control, and three Creature Modes: crab, jellyfish, and sea urchin. [2]

## Versions and factory options
Documented features:
- Dedicated Mid scoop control.
- Three Creature Modes: crab, jellyfish, sea urchin.
- True bypass.
- 9V center-negative power.
- Hand-assembled in the USA. [2]

## Sound
Diver is designed around the thick Green Russian Big Muff family while adding more midrange control and mode-dependent voicing. The Creature Modes are intended to shift the response from vintage warmth toward more aggressive bite. [1][2]
