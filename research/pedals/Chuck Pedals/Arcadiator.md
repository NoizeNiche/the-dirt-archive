# Chuck Pedals — Arcadiator

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Arcadiator
- **Builder:** Chuck Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Chuck Pedals Arcadiator, an NES 8-bit-inspired digital/experimental fuzz and octave/LFO texture pedal.

## What this pedal is
Effects Database introduced the Arcadiator in January 2017 and identifies it specifically as an **NES 8-bit fuzz**. [1]

Thermionic Studios' Arcadiator documentation records a control architecture built around octave selection, LFO switching, Level, and Rate controls, with modes that alternate the fundamental and octave signals and can add chopping/pulse-width behavior. [2]

## Sound
The Arcadiator is built for lo-fi, clocked, octave, chopped, and pseudo-8-bit fuzz textures rather than a conventional analog fuzz voice. The LFO and mode controls can alternate fundamental and octave content, creating repeating pitch and pulse textures. [1][2]
