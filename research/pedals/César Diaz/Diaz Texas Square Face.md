# César Diaz — Diaz Texas Square Face

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Diaz Texas Square Face
- **Builder:** César Diaz
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** César Diaz Texas Square Face Fuzz, a compact Fuzz Face-derived design originally developed as a more reliable replacement for Stevie Ray Vaughan's failing Fuzz Face pedals.

## What this pedal is
Effects Database documents the Texas Square Face as a fuzz pedal originally designed as a more reliable replacement for Stevie Ray Vaughan's Fuzz Faces. The production pedal uses a sturdy metal enclosure with Volume and Fuzz controls and an on/off switch. [1]

The Stevie Ray Vaughan archive likewise describes it as a small MXR-sized box using the Fuzz Face concept, with Volume and Fuzz controls. [2]

## Colorways
- A documented collector listing shows an **orange case with blue graphics and blue chicken-head knobs**. [3]
- Other colors/production appearances may exist, but a complete factory sequence was not established.

## Versions and factory options
The documented production control set is:
- Volume
- Fuzz
- On/Off switch [1]

No formal numbered V1/V2 sequence was established from the strongest checked sources.

## Version changes
Collector documentation distinguishes early hand-built examples from later production examples, but the checked evidence does not provide a sufficiently rigorous factory revision chronology to assign a numbered version sequence. [3]

## Transistor
The strongest exact-model sources available for the archive establish the circuit family as Fuzz Face-derived but do not securely establish a universal factory transistor type for every production unit.

Some secondary technical sources describe germanium/NPN transistor configurations, but these are not used as universal factory specifications here.

## Diode
No secure exact-model diode specification was established.

## Sound
The Texas Square Face is designed around the response of a Fuzz Face while prioritizing reliability. Its simple Volume/Fuzz interface keeps much of the shaping in the guitar and amplifier, and the pedal's documented purpose was to provide a dependable fuzz replacement for Vaughan's unreliable Fuzz Face units. [1][2]
