## What this pedal is
Fission Drive v2 is a two-topology preamp/overdrive/distortion pedal. Balaguer describes the second version as re-voiced for improved clarity and attack and updated with top-mounted jacks, while retaining the ability to cover clean boost, mid-gain, high-gain distortion, and fuzz-like settings.

## Version changes
V2 was re-voiced by Joe Balaguer to improve clarity and attack and added top-mounted jacks. The official V2 catalog also describes separate Modern and Vintage modes with different gain/topology behavior.

## Sound
Modern mode is voiced tighter and more focused for higher-gain articulate distortion, while Vintage mode adds bass and blooms more, extending from rock and doom textures into fuzz-like territory when the gain stages are pushed. The Pre, Post, Master, Bass, and Treble controls allow the pedal to move from pushed-clean tones through dense high gain.
