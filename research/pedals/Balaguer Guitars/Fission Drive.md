# Balaguer Guitars - Fission Drive

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Balaguer Guitars
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Fuzz / Overdrive
- **Identity:** Original Fission Drive, predecessor to the documented Fission Drive v2.

## What this pedal is
Balaguer's product catalog identifies Fission Drive v2 as the **second version** of the Fission Drive. The v2 was re-voiced for clarity and attack and changed to top-mounted jacks, establishing that an original Fission Drive existed as a distinct earlier production version.

## Controls / architecture
The original unit's exact control labeling is not fully preserved in the reviewed sources. The v2 uses two gain stages labeled Pre and Post, Master, Bass, Treble and a Modern/Vintage mode, but those specifications are not automatically attributed to the original.

## Circuit
The v2 is described as a preamp overdrive/distortion with two gain topologies and a Vintage/Modern voicing system. Exact original Fission Drive topology and component values remain unresolved.

## History
- **Original Fission Drive:** first version.
- **Fission Drive v2:** second version, re-voiced and relocated to top-mounted jacks.

## Research confidence
- **Original product identity:** High.
- **Distinct v1/v2 history:** High.
- **Original technical details:** Unknown.
