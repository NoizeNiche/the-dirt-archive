## What this pedal is
ADV Systems describes #distortion as an analog distortion designed to cover territory from heavy overdrive into dense, mid-rich distortion while preserving dynamics. The documented technical characteristics include 1 MOhm input impedance, 65 mA current consumption, and a 2.1 mm power socket. [1]
