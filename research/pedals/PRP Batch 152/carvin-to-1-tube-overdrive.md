# Carvin TO-1 Tube Overdrive

- **Builder:** Carvin
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** Carvin TO-1 Tube Overdrive
- **What this pedal is:** An early Carvin overdrive/distortion pedal marketed around tube-style response without an actual vacuum tube inside.
- **Colorways:** The reviewed surviving example is the original Carvin finish; a complete factory color list was not established.
- **Versions:** TO-1 production model.
- **Version changes:** No reliable numbered hardware revisions were established in the reviewed sources.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented.
- **Diode type:** Not documented.
- **Controls / switching:** LEVEL, COLOR, and DRIVE. A footswitch and external DC jack are documented.
- **Power:** 9V battery or DC adapter; a surviving specification lists approximately 3.5mA current draw and 37dB maximum gain.
- **Sound:** The TO-1 is intended to cover tube-emulation overdrive and heavier distortion rather than being a literal tube circuit. Its COLOR and DRIVE controls provide the main tone/gain shaping.
- **Research confidence:** Medium, based mainly on surviving retail/product documentation rather than a current Carvin factory archive.
