# Carruthers Dyna-Soar

- **Builder:** Carruthers
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Catalog identity:** Dyna-Soar
- **What this pedal is:** A rare New York-made fuzz from the 1980s built in the general lineage of the Big Muff/Ram's Head circuit family.
- **Colorways:** Surviving examples are documented in black with yellow lettering; a blue example is also reported from a 1993 Urge Overkill board.
- **Versions:** Original Dyna-Soar; Dyna-Soar Lite is a related but separately cataloged lower-output/lower-gain version.
- **Version changes:** The Dyna-Soar and Dyna-Soar Lite share the same broad circuit family, but the Lite uses a different transistor configuration that changes gain and response. A traced comparison by Stomp Under Foot places the family in the 1974 Ram's Head lineage with subtle circuit changes.
- **Transistor type:** Exact transistor part numbers are not securely documented in the reviewed public sources.
- **Diode type:** Exact diode type is not securely documented.
- **Controls / switching:** Surviving examples appear to use a compact three-control Muff-style layout; exact factory labels are not consistently documented.
- **Power:** Exact factory power specification is not securely documented.
- **Sound:** The original Dyna-Soar is described as exceptionally loud, high-gain, heavy, and full, with a large wall-of-fuzz response. Modern tracing work places its voice near the Ram's Head family while retaining distinct mid and low-end character.
- **Research confidence:** High for lineage and rarity; medium for exact production details because surviving documentation is sparse.
