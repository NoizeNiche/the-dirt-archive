# Cascade Pedals Hosstortion

- **Builder:** Cascade Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** Hosstortion
- **What this pedal is:** Cascade Pedals' first production model, a modern reworking of the Ibanez MT-10 Mostortion using a modern BiMOS chip.
- **Colorways:** The documented production enclosure is sour-apple green sparkle with printed artwork.
- **Versions:** Production Hosstortion.
- **Version changes:** Cascade replaced the obsolete original Mostortion CMOS CA3260 with a modern BiMOS device and tuned the circuit for more output and headroom while preserving the original-style EQ behavior.
- **Transistor type:** MOSFET/BiMOS technology is documented at the active-device level; exact discrete transistor part numbers are not documented.
- **Diode type:** Not documented.
- **Controls / switching:** LEVEL and DIRT plus a three-band EQ, with top-mounted input/output jacks and true bypass.
- **Power:** 9V DC, standard external jack, no battery.
- **Sound:** Cascade describes the Hosstortion as having more headroom and output than the original Mostortion, with warm mids and substantial low end. Its three-band EQ and broad gain range take it from lower-gain drive through high-gain distortion.
- **Research confidence:** High.
