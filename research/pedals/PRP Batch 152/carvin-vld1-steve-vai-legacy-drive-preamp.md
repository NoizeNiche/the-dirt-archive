# Carvin VLD1 Steve Vai Legacy Drive Preamp

- **Builder:** Carvin
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** Carvin VLD1 Steve Vai Legacy Drive Preamp
- **What this pedal is:** A full tube preamp pedal designed with Steve Vai to reproduce the channel architecture of Carvin's Legacy amplifiers in a pedal format.
- **Colorways:** The documented production finish is seafoam/foam green with the Legacy Drive graphics.
- **Versions:** VLD1 Legacy Drive production model.
- **Version changes:** No numbered production revision was established from the reviewed documentation.
- **Transistor type:** The core signal path is tube based; transistor details are not documented as a defining part of the preamp signal stages.
- **Diode type:** Not documented.
- **Controls / switching:** Drive channel has VOLUME, DRIVE, BASS, MID, TREBLE, PRESENCE and GAIN; clean channel has VOLUME, BASS, MID, TREBLE and PRESENCE switch; MASTER and BYPASS are also provided. Footswitches select BYPASS, GAIN, and DRIVE/CLEAN.
- **Power:** 12V DC, 1A, center-positive regulated adapter.
- **Sound:** The VLD1 uses two 12AX7 tubes and four gain stages to cover clean Legacy tones through harmonic-rich drive and a higher-gain mode. It also includes cabinet voicing for direct/headphone/recording use.
- **Research confidence:** High.
