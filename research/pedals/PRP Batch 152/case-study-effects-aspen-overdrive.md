# Case Study Effects Aspen Overdrive

- **Builder:** Case Study Effects Co.
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** Aspen Overdrive
- **What this pedal is:** Case Study's expandora-style drive, developed as a broad-range overdrive that can extend into fuzz territory.
- **Colorways:** Current production is documented in Alpine White and Midnight Black.
- **Versions:** Current Alpine White and Midnight Black production finishes.
- **Version changes:** Case Study reworked the original concept with more post-drive volume and added a bass control. The later Aspen One further revised the fuzz mode for stability and added the boost section.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented.
- **Diode type:** Current standard clipping is the silicon version; the separate Germanium Aspen uses vintage germanium diodes.
- **Controls / switching:** Gain, Tone, Bass, and Volume.
- **Power:** Exact current power specification was not exposed in the reviewed product text.
- **Sound:** Aspen covers a wide gain range from crunchy boost and edge-of-breakup sounds through saturated, fuzz-like drive. The added bass control lets the user shape the low end independently of the gain character.
- **Research confidence:** High.
