# Case Study Effects Lastlight Overdrive

- **Builder:** Case Study Effects Co.
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** Lastlight Overdrive
- **What this pedal is:** A transistor-based boost and overdrive rooted in a classic overdriver-style circuit.
- **Colorways:** Documented finishes include Midnight Black and Twilight Blue.
- **Versions:** Current 125B-format Lastlight production model.
- **Version changes:** The current design reworks the gain control for a smoother sweep and moves the circuit into a pedalboard-friendly 125B enclosure with top-mounted jacks.
- **Transistor type:** Transistor-based; exact part numbers are not documented in the reviewed manufacturer material.
- **Diode type:** Not documented.
- **Controls / switching:** Gain, Level, Treble, and Bass; top-mounted input and output.
- **Power:** Exact current power specification was not exposed in the reviewed product text.
- **Sound:** Lastlight is voiced primarily for low- to medium-gain boost and overdrive, with a smoother transition through the gain range than the earlier design. At higher settings it can become fuzz-like and more heavily distorted.
- **Research confidence:** High.
