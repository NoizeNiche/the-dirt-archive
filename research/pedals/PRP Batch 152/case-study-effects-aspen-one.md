# Case Study Effects Aspen One - Boost + Overdrive

- **Builder:** Case Study Effects Co.
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** Aspen One - Boost + Overdrive
- **What this pedal is:** A dual-section analog pedal combining the Aspen overdrive with a transparent MOSFET boost.
- **Colorways:** Current production is offered in Silicon and Germanium clipping versions.
- **Versions:** Silicon Aspen One; Germanium Aspen One.
- **Version changes:** The Germanium version substitutes hand-sourced vintage germanium diodes for the silicon clipping stage, giving softer clipping and smoother highs. The Aspen One also refines the original Aspen fuzz mode for greater stability.
- **Transistor type:** MOSFET boost section; exact MOSFET part number not documented.
- **Diode type:** Silicon or vintage germanium, depending on version.
- **Controls / switching:** Gain, Tone, Bass, Volume, and Boost, plus an order toggle for boost into drive or drive into boost and a TRS remote-control connection.
- **Power:** 9VDC only, 150mA.
- **Sound:** The Aspen side ranges from edge-of-breakup overdrive to fuzz, while the boost remains mostly clean until pushed. Changing the order of the two stages creates substantially different gain structures from the same pedal.
- **Research confidence:** High.
