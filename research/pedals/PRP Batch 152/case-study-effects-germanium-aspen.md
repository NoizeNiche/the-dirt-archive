# Case Study Effects Germanium Aspen Overdrive

- **Builder:** Case Study Effects Co.
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** Germanium Aspen Overdrive
- **What this pedal is:** A custom-shop reworking of the Aspen overdrive that replaces the standard silicon clipping stage with rare vintage germanium diodes.
- **Colorways:** Exact special-run enclosure finishes are not consistently documented; the defining distinction is the germanium clipping build.
- **Versions:** Germanium Aspen limited/custom-shop run.
- **Version changes:** The germanium diodes were initially used in a very small five-unit experiment and later returned in another limited supply run. This is a distinct component variant of the Aspen platform.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented.
- **Diode type:** Vintage germanium.
- **Controls / switching:** Aspen's four-control layout with Gain, Tone, Bass, and Volume.
- **Power:** Exact special-run power specification was not established in the reviewed sources.
- **Sound:** Compared with the standard silicon Aspen, the germanium version is described as having softer clipping, smoother highs, and a more organic response. It retains the Aspen platform's ability to move from clean boost through heavy overdrive and fuzz-like saturation.
- **Research confidence:** High for the diode distinction and product relationship; medium for the exact size and chronology of later runs.

  - https://casestudyeffects.co/products/aspen-one
