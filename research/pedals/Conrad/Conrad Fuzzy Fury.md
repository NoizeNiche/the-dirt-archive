# Conrad - Conrad Fuzzy Fury

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Conrad
- **Catalog identity:** Conrad Fuzzy Fury
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Late-1960s Conrad-branded fuzz unit associated with the Applied Audio Products OEM family and distributed by David Wexler & Co.

## What this pedal is
Effects Database catalogs the Conrad Fuzzy Fury as a fuzz pedal and places it in its 1968 catalog section, alongside the related Conrad Bass Hawk and Conrad Sky Hawk models. [1]
Tone Machines Blog documents that David Wexler & Co. released Conrad-branded effects in 1969, including the Fuzzy Fury, and attributes their construction to Applied Audio. [2]
A January 10, 1970 Billboard catalog notice identifies the Conrad Fuzzy Fury as a footswitch fuzz-tone unit and states that it was factory powered by a nine-volt battery. The same notice gives a suggested retail price of $29.50. [3]
