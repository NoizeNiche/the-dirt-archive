# Conrad - Conrad Buzz Box & Volume Expander

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Conrad
- **Catalog identity:** Conrad Buzz Box & Volume Expander
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Late-1960s/early-1970s Japanese OEM fuzz marketed by Conrad and built by UMI/Applied-era manufacturing.

## What this pedal is
Effects Database identifies the Conrad Buzz Box & Volume Expander as a fuzz pedal and links it to the same product family as Kimberly and Apollo Buzz Box & Volume Expander units. Its catalog relationship indicates a Japanese OEM/private-label product rather than an isolated proprietary Conrad design. [1]
