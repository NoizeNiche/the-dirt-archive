# Conrad - Conrad Sky Hawk - Fuzz and Treble Booster

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Conrad
- **Catalog identity:** Conrad Sky Hawk - Fuzz and Treble Booster
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz / Treble Boost
- **Identity:** Late-1960s Conrad private-label fuzz/treble booster from the Applied Audio Products OEM family.

## What this pedal is
Effects Database documents Conrad Sky Hawk as a late-1960s fuzz and treble booster designed by Applied Audio Products Inc. of New Jersey and distributed under Conrad by David Wexler & Co. Its placement in contemporary 1968 catalogs links it to the same Applied-built ecosystem as related Crown, Sano and Lou Rose effects. [1]

## Controls
The documented control set is:
- **Volume**
- **Fuzz**
- **Treble** [1]

The pedal uses separate switching to operate the fuzz/boost functions. [1]

## Sound
The Sky Hawk combines fuzz saturation with a separate treble-boost function, producing a sharper, more cutting response when the high-frequency boost is increased. The archive keeps this as a documented functional description rather than assigning an unsupported circuit topology. [1]
