# CDB Pedals — Darkwater

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** Darkwater
- **Builder:** CDB Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive
- **Identity:** High-gain two-channel distortion/overdrive/preamp based on the **red channel of the Revv Generator** amplifier, with a three-position gain switch and three-band EQ. [1][2]

## What this pedal is

CDB Pedals describes Darkwater as a distortion, overdrive or preamp based on the **red channel of the Revv Generator**. Its control architecture combines a three-band EQ with a three-position gain-stage selector and an alternate channel voicing footswitch. [1]

A documented **Version 2.0** redesign shrinks the footprint, uses a 125B enclosure and adds battery operation. [2]

## Controls

### Version 2.0
- **LOW / MID / HIGH:** Three-band EQ.
- **GAIN:** Three-position selector:
  - **CALM**
  - **ROUGH**
  - **ANGRY**
- **CHANNEL:** Footswitch for the alternate circuit/channel voicing.
- Channel/output control functions are retained within the V2 compact design. [1][2]

## Circuit lineage

- **Primary lineage:** Revv Generator **red channel**.
- The pedal is intended as a compact pedal-format translation of that high-gain amplifier voice rather than a generic metal distortion. [1]

## Sound

Darkwater is designed for high-gain amp-style dirt spanning distortion, overdrive and preamp use. Calm, Rough and Angry provide progressively more aggressive gain-stage voicings, while the three-band EQ shapes the overall response. [1][2]
