# CDB Pedals — Darkwater

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Darkwater
- **Builder:** CDB Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive
- **Identity:** CDB Pedals Darkwater, a two-channel distortion/overdrive/preamp based on the red channel of the Revv Generator amplifier.

## What this pedal is
CDB Pedals describes the Darkwater as a distortion, overdrive, or preamp with a three-band EQ, a three-position gain-stage toggle, and a channel footswitch that changes the circuit's voicing. The builder states that the main circuit is based on the red channel of the Revv Generator amplifier. [1]

A current Etsy listing identifies the documented **Version 2.0** update as a pedalboard-friendly redesign with a smaller footprint and a battery option. It also identifies the enclosure as a 125B size. [2]

## Sound
The Darkwater is presented as a high-gain amp-style dirt circuit that can cover distortion, overdrive, and preamp duties. Its two channel voicings and three-position gain-stage switch are intended to provide substantially different saturation responses, while the three-band EQ handles tonal shaping. [1][2]
