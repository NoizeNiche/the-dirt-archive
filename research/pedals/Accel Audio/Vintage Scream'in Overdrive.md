# Accel Audio — Vintage Scream'in Overdrive

## PRP identity
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Analog overdrive with Normal/Bright/Warm voicing switch.

## What this pedal is
Accel's Vintage Scream'in Overdrive is an analog overdrive voiced for natural, tube-like "Scream'in" tones. Its three-position toggle selects Normal, Bright or Warm. [1][2]

## Colorways
- Documented finish: bright green with black controls and Accel branding.

## Versions / options
- Volume, Tone, Drive.
- Normal/Bright/Warm toggle.
- True bypass.
- 9V DC center-negative.
- Analog circuit.
- No numbered revision verified. [1]

## Sound
The three modes shift the response between the core overdrive, a brighter/punchier voice and a fuller/warm voice. The pedal is intended to retain a natural, amp-like overdrive feel rather than a heavily saturated distortion character. [1][2]
