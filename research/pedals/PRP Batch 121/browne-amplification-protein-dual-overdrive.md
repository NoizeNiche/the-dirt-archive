# Browne Amplification — Protein Dual Overdrive

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Browne Amplification
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Protein dual-overdrive family entry represented by the V2.2 product page.

## What this pedal is
The Protein is a dual overdrive developed by David Brown and Adam Sniegowski. The blue side is derived from a classic low-gain overdrive, while the green side is based on Browne's Nashville-oriented drive. The two sides can be cascaded for a third combined drive texture. [1]

## Colorways
No single permanent finish is assumed for the family entry because multiple Protein finishes and later versions are documented.

## Versions and factory options
- Dual overdrive
- Blue and Green sides
- Green into Blue cascading
- Split/loop capability on the documented V2/V3 architecture
- V2.2 product page identifies side-mounted jacks and internal split-mode switching [1]

## Version changes
The current Browne store separately documents V2.2, V3 and V4. V3 adds top-mounted jacks and changes the green-side identity to Nashville-inspired ATOM; V4 retains the dual architecture and adds a three-way hi-cut plus additional clipping/low-cut controls. [1][2][3]

## Transistor
Exact active devices not publicly documented.

## Diode
Exact factory clipping devices are not publicly documented for the family entry.

## Sound
Protein is designed around complementary low-gain and Nashville-style overdrives that can be used separately or stacked. Browne emphasizes usable cleanup, clearer low end and added fullness when the green side cascades into the blue side. [1]
