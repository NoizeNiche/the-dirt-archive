# Browne Amplification — Ethane Dual Overdrive

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Browne Amplification
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Current normalized name for the Ethane dual-overdrive product.

## What this pedal is
Browne's current product page names the pedal **Ethane Dual Overdrive**. It combines Carbon V2 and Gritador drive circuits, with the two sides intended to work independently or stacked. [1]

## Colorways
- Black Silver Vein powder coat is documented on the current manufacturer page. [1]

## Versions and factory options
- Carbon V2 side
- Gritador side
- 3-way high-cut on Carbon V2 side
- Internal split mode
- Soft switching
- Adjustable startup state
- 9V DC, approximately 100mA [1]

## Version changes
The tracker also contains the spelling/hyphen variant **Ethane - Dual Overdrive**. Both records point to the same current Browne product page, so this naming duplication should be normalized later rather than silently treated as different hardware. [1]

## Transistor
Exact active devices not publicly documented.

## Diode
Asymmetrical clipping is documented on the Gritador side, but exact diode parts are not specified. [1]

## Sound
The Carbon V2 side is described as an open, high-headroom Bluesbreaker-inspired drive, while the Gritador side is more vocal and mid-forward. Stacking the two expands the range from boost and rhythm drive into saturated lead textures. [1]
