# Browne Amplification — Gritador Overdrive

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Browne Amplification
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Browne's modified Tube Screamer-style overdrive.

## What this pedal is
Browne describes Gritador as its version of a classic Tube Screamer-style circuit. The company says it refined the midrange to reduce nasal character, made clipping slightly less symmetrical, and added more fullness while keeping the low end tight. [1]

## Colorways
- Green and black front-panel finishes are shown on the current manufacturer page. [1]

## Versions and factory options
- Classic Screamer-derived architecture
- Adjusted midrange
- Less-symmetrical clipping
- Fuller but tight low end
- Blemished finish examples may exist, but blem status is a finish issue rather than a circuit version [1]

## Diode
- **Clipping:** Less symmetrical than the reference Screamer-style circuit.
- Exact diode parts not documented. [1]

## Sound
Gritador is built around a familiar mid-forward overdrive voice, but Browne deliberately softens the nasal quality and reinforces the lows so the result stays fuller and punchy. The pedal is intended to remain vocal and articulate as drive increases. [1]
