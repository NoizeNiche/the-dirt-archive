# Colortone Pedals - Parasite Overdrive

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Colortone Pedals
- **Catalog identity:** Parasite Overdrive
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Sydney-built full-spectrum JFET overdrive with active post-drive EQ and pre-drive bass/treble boosts.

## What this pedal is
Parasite is a harmonically rich, touch-sensitive JFET overdrive designed to cover mild always-on breakup through compressed, saturated amp-like drive. Colortone explicitly describes it as a full-spectrum overdrive and documents its ability to clean up from the guitar volume control. [1][2]

## Controls
The current design documents:
- **Drive:** gain/drive amount.
- **Level:** output volume.
- **Bass:** active post-drive bass EQ.
- **Treble:** active post-drive treble EQ.
- **Bright:** pre-drive treble boost.
- **Cab:** pre-drive bass boost, with the current 2026 implementation offering selectable 1x12 or 4x12-style resonance. [1][3]

Earlier 2024/2022 versions used the same core circuit and tone but a larger enclosure and a two-state bass-boost concept. Colortone states that the 2024 retooling did not change the circuit or tone. [1]

## Colorways
- Colortone offers black and white powder-coated enclosure options in its documented production information. [2]
- Deep-etched stainless-steel faceplates are part of the current visual design.
- Cosmetic finish is treated separately from circuit revision.

## Versions and factory options
- **2022/early production:** larger enclosure with the six-function control architecture documented by Colortone and reviewers.
- **2024:** smaller pedalboard-friendly enclosure; Colortone states the circuit and tone did not change. [1]
- **2026:** current release retains the full-spectrum JFET architecture and updates the Cab control to provide 1x12/4x12-style bass resonance selection. [3]

## Version changes
The documented 2024 change is an enclosure retooling rather than a circuit revision. The 2026 release adds a revised cabinet-resonance control presentation. [1][3]

## Construction
Colortone states that its pedals are designed and individually handcrafted in Sydney. The current Parasite documentation specifies hand-matched JFETs, true-bypass switching, and a custom etched stainless-steel faceplate. [1][2]

## Transistor
- **JFET devices:** Current 2026 documentation specifies hand-matched dual **5457 and J201 JFETs**. [3]
- Earlier versions are described as hand-matched JFET designs, but the exact transistor complement was not established for every historical production state.

## Diode
- **Clipping diode:** Not publicly documented in the checked sources.

## Sound
At low Drive settings Parasite functions as an always-on texture/breakup stage. Increasing Drive adds tube-like compression and harmonic density, while the active EQ and pre-drive boosts allow the response to be tightened, brightened, or pushed toward fuller cabinet-like lows. [1][2]
