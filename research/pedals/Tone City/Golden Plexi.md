## What this pedal is

The Golden Plexi is a Plexi-style overdrive/distortion pedal. It was later refined into the Golden Plexi2.

## Sound

In 2022, we refined the gain structure of the Golden Plexi to provide a more focused and consistent high-gain dynamic response, renaming it the Golden Plexi2.
An added toggle switch allows users to switch the tone from a right side tightened gain ( Golden Plexi 2) to left side more high gain and bass (low gain).
This version also optimizes the high frequencies in TONE.
