## What this pedal is
Basstortion is ZVEX's bass-focused distortion designed around the response of a driven Ampeg SVT-style tube amplifier.

Basstortion is designed to reproduce the growl and dynamic response associated with a driven Ampeg SVT-type bass amp. ZVEX emphasizes touch response and strong low-end distortion.
