## What this pedal is

The Octane 3 is a hand-built fuzz/upper-octave effect with a distinctive ring-modulator-like character. ZVEX describes it as combining fuzzy gain with a high octave and a mid-scoop control.

## Sound

The Octane 3 produces dense fuzz, a strong upper octave, and ring-modulator-like textures. The mid-scoop control changes the voicing while the nonlinear circuit can generate especially aggressive octave and harmonic tones.
