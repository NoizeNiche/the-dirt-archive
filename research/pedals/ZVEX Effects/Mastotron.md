The Mastotron by ZVEX Effects is an absolute sleeper of a fuzz pedal.
It runs on a silicon circuit rather than the germanium of ZVEX’s better known Fuzz Factory, built around a sub-heavy voice with an input impedance control and a pulse width knob that ZVEX bills as more flexible than any silicon fuzz to date.
