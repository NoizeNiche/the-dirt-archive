# Audile — Bass TOAD - Tone Overdrive And Distortion for Bass

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Bass TOAD - Tone Overdrive And Distortion for Bass
- **Builder:** Audile
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive
- **Identity:** Bass-focused analog overdrive/distortion with pre- and post-gain tone shaping and modular TADPOLE boards.

## What this pedal is
Audile describes BASS TOAD as a bass-specific tone-shaping tool that preserves low-frequency thickness while adding overdrive or distortion. It uses two gain stages, selectable clipping symmetry, multiple tone controls and the modular TADPOLE system.

## Versions and factory options
### Bass TOAD production
- PRE TONE tilt EQ
- GAIN
- NATURAL / DARK clipping selector
- CHOKE post-distortion high-pass filter
- POST TONE adaptive low-pass filter
- LEVEL
- 9V DC center negative
- 15mA current draw
- 115 x 66 x 49 mm
- 260 g
- Made in Australia

### TADPOLE system
Standard configuration includes Neutral, Bright and Mid Scoop boards plus blank boards for customization. Audile states that changing the boards does not void the warranty.

- **Type:** NATURAL is symmetrical clipping; DARK is asymmetrical clipping.

## Sound
The pedal is intended to add overdrive growl without losing the bass fundamental. Lower gain can function as a clean enhancer or boost, while clipping mode and TADPOLE choices expand the sound toward more saturated distortion.
