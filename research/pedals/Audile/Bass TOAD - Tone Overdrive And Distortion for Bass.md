# Audile — Bass TOAD - Tone Overdrive And Distortion for Bass

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** Bass TOAD - Tone Overdrive And Distortion for Bass
- **Builder:** Audile
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive
- **Identity:** Bass-focused version of Audile's TOAD concept, with two gain stages, selectable clipping symmetry, pre- and post-gain tone shaping, and a modular TADPOLE system designed to retain low-frequency depth. [1][2]

## What this pedal is

Audile describes BASS TOAD as a bass-specific tone-shaping tool that adds overdrive or distortion without sacrificing bottom-end thickness. Its architecture mirrors the main TOAD concept but is voiced around the lower frequency range of bass instruments. [1]

## Controls / signal path

- **PRE TONE:** Shapes the instrument before gain.
- **GAIN:** Two-stage overdrive section.
- **NATURAL / DARK:** Symmetrical versus asymmetrical clipping.
- **CHOKE:** Post-distortion filtering.
- **POST TONE:** Post-distortion adaptive low-pass behavior.
- **LEVEL:** Final output. [1]
Audile's TADPOLE system provides interchangeable tonal boards including Neutral, Bright and Mid-Scoop configurations plus blank boards for custom experiments. [2]

## Circuit architecture

- **Two gain stages.**
- Pre-gain tone shaping.
- Post-gain filtering and tone shaping.
- Swappable TADPOLE board system. [1][2]

## Sound

The builder emphasizes preserved low-end thickness while moving from subtle enhancement/boost into stronger overdrive and distortion. The Mid-Scoop TADPOLE is specifically described as attenuating mids for a darker, more aggressive high-gain profile. [2]
