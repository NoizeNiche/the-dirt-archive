# Daredevil Pedals — L.S.D

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** L.S.D
- **Builder:** Daredevil Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Logan Square Destroyer (L.S.D.), a Daredevil Fuzzrite-inspired fuzz.

## What this pedal is
Premier Guitar's 2013 review identifies the L.S.D. as the Logan Square Destroyer, inspired by the Mosrite Fuzzrite and expanded into a higher-gain modern fuzz. Effects Database independently documents the same two-knob pedal as a raw 1960s-style fuzz with heavier modern gain.

## Controls / circuit
- **Level:** output level.
- **Gain:** fuzz/gain control; the two controls interact.
- Premier Guitar describes a small circuit board with relatively few components, but does not publish a factory schematic.
- Effects Database documents hand wiring, true bypass, and a 9V DC jack.
- **Exact transistor/device:** Unknown.
- **Exact clipping/diode device:** Unknown.
- **Power:** 9V DC jack documented.

## Sound
Premier Guitar reports a broad range from mellow, volume-controlled overdrive to thick, destructive high-gain fuzz, spanning garage rock, classic rock, stoner and doom textures.
Effects Database similarly describes retro garage sounds at lower gain and thick modern fuzz at higher gain.

## History / variants
- Premier Guitar reviewed the pedal in September 2013 and recorded a $120 street price at that time.
- Guitar Center currently lists an **LSD Logan Square Destroyer Fuzz V2**, showing that a later version exists in the market record, with Level/Gain controls, 9V DC power, and true bypass.
- The archive keeps the model identity intact rather than collapsing the original and V2 evidence into one revision.
