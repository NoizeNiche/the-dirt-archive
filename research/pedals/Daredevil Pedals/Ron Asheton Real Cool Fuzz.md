# Daredevil Pedals — Ron Asheton Real Cool Fuzz

## PRP identity

- **Builder:** Daredevil Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Limited Ron Asheton edition of Daredevil's Real Cool Fuzz.

## What this pedal is

Daredevil documents a limited wedge-enclosure edition based on a late-1960s Vox Tonebender circuit, with tweaks for gain, cut, and operation.

## Controls / circuit

- Pair of vintage new-stock Mullard OC139 germanium transistors, NPN, tested and biased for each pedal.
- Hand wired, through-hole construction, true bypass.
- Power: 9V battery or DC power brick.
- Lowering voltage can produce a starving-battery effect.
- **Exact clipping/diode device:** Not documented.

## Sound

The builder describes biting, sustaining fuzz designed around Ron Asheton and the first two Stooges albums, with voicing intended to work with both humbuckers and Stratocasters.

## Versions / edition history

- A 2017 Daredevil demo documents an earlier limited run of 20 hand-wired germanium fuzzes using a Tone Bender-based design.
- A later limited wedge edition is signed and numbered and uses a Hammond wedge enclosure.
- The archive does not assume identical components across every edition.
