## What this pedal is
The Premium Overdrive is a hand-built transistor overdrive from Daredevil Pedals, inspired by the classic Colorsound Power Boost family. Daredevil describes it as a large, natural, open-sounding drive with more gain and low end than a straightforward vintage boost.

## Sound
The Premium Overdrive is voiced for a broad, open overdrive with substantial low end and a bright control capable of adding extra bite. Daredevil specifically notes that the circuit can work well for bass as well as guitar.
