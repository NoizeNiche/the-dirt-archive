## What this pedal is

Daredevil and Chicago Music Exchange describe the Chicago Rat as an original two-stage op-amp gain circuit, created with Rob Scallon, with selectable high-gain distortion and over-clipped fuzz modes. It is explicitly not a Rat circuit.

## Controls / circuit

- **Normal:** high-gain distortion mode.
- **Bleed:** starve control associated with the fuzz setting and defeated in Normal.
- **Rabid:** the alternate over-clipped fuzz mode.
- Two op-amp gain stages.
- Through-hole components, true bypass, Chicago construction, external 9V DC power.
- **Exact op-amp part numbers:** Unknown.
- **Exact clipping/diode part numbers:** Unknown.

## Sound

Daredevil describes Normal as hot-rodded crunchy high-gain distortion with substantial output. This record is restricted to that mode and does not borrow the Rabid fuzz description.
