## What this pedal is

The Wolf Deluxe is an original Daredevil fuzz designed for a warm, vintage character with a large amount of sustain. Daredevil describes it as capable of producing a singing neck-pickup-style tone while retaining bridge-pickup cut and output.

## Sound

The Wolf Deluxe emphasizes smooth, warm fuzz and long sustain while keeping enough upper-mid cut for bridge-pickup use. Its design is intended to make a bright bridge pickup behave more like a singing, saturated lead tone without losing definition.
