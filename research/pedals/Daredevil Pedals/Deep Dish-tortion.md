## What this pedal is
Born from Riot Fest artist Dan Wade's idea and designed by Johnny at Daredevil, the Deep Dish-tortion is described by Daredevil as an original, hand-wired-in-Chicago circuit. It uses multiple op-amp gain stages with two modes, Tavern for mid gain and Deep Dish for higher gain. [1]
