# Classic Audio Effects — Stumphouse Drive

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** Stumphouse Drive
- **Builder:** Classic Audio Effects
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Classic Audio Effects Stumphouse Drive, a medium-gain overdrive with a foot-operated gain roller.

## What this pedal is

Effects Database documents Stumphouse Drive as a medium-gain overdrive using **three cascading op-amp stages** and **symmetrical silicon clipping**. Its distinguishing feature is a gain roller wheel that can be adjusted with the foot while playing. [1][2]

## Colorways

- A **brown** exact-model example is documented by Pedal of the Day. [2]
- No complete factory colorway sequence was established.

## Versions and factory options

Documented controls/features:
- Gain roller wheel.
- Tone.
- Volume.
- Soft-touch relay bypass.
- Reinforced drive belt.
- Fully sealed bearing drive system.
- 270° roller rotation. [1][2]

The documented power requirement is 9V at approximately 45 mA, tip negative. [1]

## Version changes

No formal numbered factory revision sequence was established.

## Transistor

The active gain stages are documented as **op-amp based**. Exact op-amp part numbers were not established. [1]

## Diode

Effects Database explicitly describes **symmetrical silicon clipping**. Exact diode part numbers were not established. [1]

## Sound

The builder positions Stumphouse as a medium-gain primary overdrive with a balance of clarity and grit. The roller control is intended to make gain changes continuously accessible during performance. [1][2]
