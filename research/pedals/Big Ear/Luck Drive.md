# Big Ear - Luck Drive

## What this pedal is
LUCK DRIVE is a limited-edition guitar/bass overdrive created exclusively for artists performing at Willie Nelson's **Luck Reunion** festival on March 14, 2019. Big Ear states that **45 units** were made, handmade in Nashville, Tennessee.

## Circuit / development
Big Ear documents that the project began as an intended modification of an existing overdrive, but Zach Broyles of Mythos Pedals instead designed a **modified Tube Screamer-style circuit** for the project. Grant Wilson states that he changed only a single capacitor and the gain-pot taper from Broyles's circuit before the final version was built.

## Sound
The project was intentionally aimed at a broadly useful overdrive for a group of different performers. Big Ear describes the finished pedal as a successful refinement of the TS-style concept, with the final circuit changes limited to one capacitor and the gain-pot taper.

## History
- **March 14, 2019:** Luck Reunion festival date for which the pedals were made.
- **45 units total.**
- Handmade in Nashville by Grant Wilson and Karen Schierhorn with assistance from friends.
- Enclosure finishing included a clear powder coat and drilling/printing support from F5 Metalworks.
