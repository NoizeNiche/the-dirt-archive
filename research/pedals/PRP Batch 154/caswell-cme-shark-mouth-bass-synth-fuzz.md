# Caswell Modern Electronics (CME) Shark Mouth - Bass Synth Fuzz

- **Builder:** Caswell Modern Electronics (CME)
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Catalog identity:** Shark Mouth - Bass Synth Fuzz
- **What this pedal is:** A bass fuzz/synth effect that converts the input toward a sharp sawtooth waveform, emphasizing sustain, compression, smooth glissando, and strong sub-bass.
- **Colorways:** A complete factory finish history was not established.
- **Versions:** Original Shark Mouth production model.

- **Transistor type:** Matched MPSA18 transistors are explicitly documented.
- **Diode type:** Not documented.
- **Controls / switching:** A passive low-pass filter control is documented to soften the harsher edges of the sawtooth character.
- **Power:** Exact factory power specification was not exposed in the reviewed source.
- **Sound:** The Shark Mouth is aimed at turning bass into a smooth, compressed, synth-like sawtooth while preserving unusually strong low-end content. Its passive low-pass filter lets the player tame the sharper high-frequency edge of the synthesized fuzz voice.
