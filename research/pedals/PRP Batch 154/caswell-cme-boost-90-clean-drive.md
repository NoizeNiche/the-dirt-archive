# Caswell Modern Electronics (CME) Boost 90 Clean Drive

- **Builder:** Caswell Modern Electronics (CME)
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** Boost 90 Clean Drive
- **What this pedal is:** A one-control clean boost/low-gain overdrive designed to add level while gradually introducing its own gain as the control is advanced.
- **Colorways:** The reviewed 2016 production record documents a vintage-style witch-hat knob and amber indicator LED; a complete enclosure-finish history was not established.
- **Versions:** Original Boost 90 production model.
- **Version changes:** No numbered hardware revisions were established.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented.
- **Diode type:** Not documented.
- **Controls / switching:** One numbered boost/gain control; 3PDT true-bypass footswitch. High-quality Switchcraft jacks are documented.
- **Power:** 9V battery or 9V DC negative-center supply.
- **Sound:** Pedal of the Day describes the Boost 90 as clean at lower settings and progressively more overdriven as it rises, with a blended clean/dirty character around the upper part of the range. It is intended as both a level lift and a tone/gain enhancer rather than a conventional multi-knob overdrive.
- **Research confidence:** High for controls, power, and the documented sonic behavior; medium for construction details.
