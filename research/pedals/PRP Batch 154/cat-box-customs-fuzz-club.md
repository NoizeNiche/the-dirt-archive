# Cat Box Customs Fuzz Club

- **Builder:** Cat Box Customs
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Catalog identity:** Fuzz Club
- **What this pedal is:** A Fuzz War-inspired fuzz using a seven-transistor architecture with selectable silicon or germanium clipping.
- **Colorways:** Custom-painted editions are documented, so individual finishes vary.
- **Versions:** Original Fuzz Club production model.
- **Version changes:** No numbered hardware revision was established in the reviewed sources.
- **Transistor type:** Seven-transistor Fuzz War-style topology is documented; exact transistor part numbers are not.
- **Diode type:** Silicon/germanium clipping selection.
- **Controls / switching:** Volume, Gain, Tone, plus a clipping selector.
- **Power:** Exact factory power specification was not established from the reviewed sources.
- **Sound:** The Fuzz Club is deliberately very loud and aggressive, with the silicon setting providing a raspier, more gated character and germanium producing a fatter, warmer voice. Its broad tone control and high output make it suitable for massive, raw fuzz textures.
- **Research confidence:** High.
