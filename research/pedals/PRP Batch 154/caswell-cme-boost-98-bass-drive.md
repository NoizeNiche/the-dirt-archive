# Caswell Modern Electronics (CME) Boost 98 Bass Drive

- **Builder:** Caswell Modern Electronics (CME)
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** Boost 98 Bass Drive
- **What this pedal is:** A one-control bass-focused boost/drive that adapts the Boost 90 concept to emphasize low-frequency support.
- **Colorways:** No complete factory finish history was established from the reviewed source.
- **Versions:** Original Boost 98 production model.
- **Version changes:** It is documented as a bass-focused reworking of the same basic one-knob Boost concept used in the Boost 90.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented.
- **Diode type:** Not documented.
- **Controls / switching:** One control; the reviewed source describes it as the direct, simple control architecture of the Boost family.
- **Power:** Exact power specification was not established from the reviewed source.
- **Sound:** Pedal of the Day describes the Boost 98 as concentrating the Boost 90's basic principle on low-end frequencies, giving bass a substantial lift without abandoning the drive character. Its design is intentionally immediate rather than highly interactive.
- **Research confidence:** High for its relationship to the Boost 90 and intended bass voicing; medium for exact technical specifications.
