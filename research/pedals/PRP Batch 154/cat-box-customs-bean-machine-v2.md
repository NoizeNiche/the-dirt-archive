# Cat Box Customs Bean Machine V2

- **Builder:** Cat Box Customs
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Catalog identity:** Bean Machine V2
- **What this pedal is:** A deluxe expansion of the original Bean Machine's Harmonic Percolator-inspired fuzz with additional bias, voltage-sag, germanium-clipping, and feedback controls.
- **Colorways:** Custom hand-painted finishes are a major part of the model's production, with individual examples carrying distinct artwork and colors.
- **Versions:** Bean Machine V2, treated as the materially expanded successor to V1.
- **Version changes:** V2 turns the earlier intensity control into a Bias control, adds Volts voltage-starve, a feedback-loop footswitch and control, and a dedicated germanium-clipping adjustment.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented in the reviewed source.
- **Diode type:** Germanium, no-clipping, and silicon selections are documented; specific examples use D310 germanium and Schottky BAT41 silicon options.
- **Controls / switching:** Volume, Fuzz/Gain, Volts, Bias, Germ, Feedback, diode selector, gating/octave-related switching, and footswitchable feedback.
- **Power:** 9V center-negative power supply only; no battery snap.
- **Sound:** V2 expands the original percolator voice into gated, starving, oscillating, and feedback-driven textures. The voltage and bias controls can push it into spitty, broken sounds while the feedback circuit produces controllable squeal and self-interaction.
- **Research confidence:** High.
