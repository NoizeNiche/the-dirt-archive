## What this pedal is
The Swarm is an **analog fuzz harmonizer** that creates a square-wave fuzz signal and stacks it with pitch-shifted harmony voices. Beetronics documents nine selectable harmony intervals across two octaves, with controls for blending the upper and lower voices and for interactive modulation.\n\n## Catalog source

## Deep research verification

### Verified description

The **Swarm Fuzz Harmonizer** is an analog fuzz harmonizer that converts the incoming signal into a square-wave representation and creates pitch-shifted harmonies around it. Beetronics documents **nine selectable harmony intervals across two octaves**.

### Verified controls and architecture

- **Worker:** input/fuzz level.
- **Species:** selects among nine harmony intervals.
- **Queen** and **Drone:** blend the upper and lower harmony voices, which remain one octave apart.
- **Flight:** adjusts modulation response/tracking behavior.
- **Sting:** controls modulation rate/tracking behavior.
- **Master:** output volume.
- With the harmony voices reduced, Beetronics says the unit can function as a gated, aggressive fuzz.
- Fully analog signal path.
- Standard **9V DC center-negative** power; approximately **7 mA**.
- True bypass.

### Verified sound evidence

Beetronics describes the Swarm as ranging from vintage-inspired octave fuzz through buzzing synth-like textures and broken-machine sounds. The modulation can move from comparatively precise tracking toward unstable, detuned and glitch-like behavior.
