## Deep research verification

This pass uses Beetronics' exact-model Wannabee documentation.

### Verified description

The **Wannabee Beelateral Buzz** is a dual overdrive inspired by **Bluesbreaker and Klon-style** circuits. Its defining architecture is flexible routing: the two sides may run independently, **in series**, or **in parallel**.

### Verified controls and architecture

Each side has:
- Three-way voicing switch.
- **Honey** gain.
- **Taste** tone.
- **Volume**.
- Dedicated footswitch.

The documented voicing behavior is:
- Bluesbreaker side: **Mid Boost / Flat / Low Boost**.
- Klon-style side: selectable clean-signal blend of **None / Medium / Full**.

In series mode, the user can choose which circuit comes first.

- 9V DC center-negative power.
- True bypass.
- Standard model plus limited/custom finish editions are retained under the parent product identity unless hardware differences are established.

### Verified sound evidence

Beetronics describes the Bluesbreaker side as smoother and dynamic and the Klon-style side as tighter and punchier. Series routing adds saturation, while parallel routing is intended to retain clarity, depth, and separation between the two voices.
