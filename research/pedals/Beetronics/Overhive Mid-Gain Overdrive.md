## Deep research verification

This pass uses Beetronics' exact-model Overhive documentation.

### Verified description

The **Overhive Mid-Gain Overdrive** is a mid-gain overdrive designed to retain a direct connection to the amplifier while offering substantial voicing and gain flexibility. Beetronics documents separate **Body** and **Hive** voicing switches plus Honey gain, tone and master-volume controls.

### Verified controls and architecture

- **Body** switch: adds/subtracts low-end emphasis for guitar changes or added weight.
- **Hive** switch: alters the overall voicing between bigger/fatter and tighter/thinner responses.
- **Honey:** gain.
- **Tone:** tone shaping.
- **Master Volume:** output.
- Beetronics describes the circuit as an overdrive with a warm, musical breakup and singing sustain.
