## Deep research verification

This pass uses Beetronics' current exact-model documentation.

### Verified description

The **Fatbee Overdrive** is the first model in Beetronics' smaller **Babee Series**. Beetronics describes it as an amp-like overdrive built around an original **JFET circuit**, developed in collaboration with **Howard Davis**.

### Verified controls and hardware

- **Weight:** volume.
- **Flavor:** tone.
- **Honey:** gain.
- Sloped compact enclosure with top-mounted jacks.
- Standard **9V DC center-negative** power.
- True-bypass switching.
- Beetronics describes the circuit as a fully original JFET-based design rather than a named vintage clone.

### Verified version references

- Beetronics identifies Fatbee as the **first Babee Series** model.
- Custom Shop examples exist, but the archive does not treat individual Custom Shop serial-number finishes as separate product versions unless the hardware identity differs.

### Verified transistor/device terms

- **JFET-based circuit:** explicitly documented.
- Exact JFET part number: Unknown.

### Verified diode terms

- Exact clipping/rectifier diode: Unknown.

### Verified sound evidence

Beetronics describes Fatbee as producing tube-style breakup, smooth drive, natural compression/saturation and touch-sensitive response. The builder also says it can be used with guitar, bass and synth when warmth or saturation is desired.
