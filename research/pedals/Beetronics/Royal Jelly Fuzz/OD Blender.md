## Deep research verification

This pass uses Beetronics' exact-model Royal Jelly documentation and surviving limited/custom editions.

### Verified description

The **Royal Jelly Fuzz/OD Blender** is a dual-path overdrive/fuzz blender developed by **Filipe Pampuri in collaboration with Howard Davis**. Beetronics describes it as a design that can move continuously between overdrive and fuzz while retaining a dry-signal blend.

### Verified controls and architecture

- Two fuzz/overdrive blend presets: **Queen** and **King**.
- **Dry** control blends clean signal with the affected signal.
- **HI / LO** EQ provides approximately **10 dB of cut/boost**.
- **BUZZ** switch adds high-frequency emphasis to the fuzz side.
- The pedal can operate as separate overdrive/fuzz voices or blend them together.
- Standard **9V DC center-negative** power.
- True bypass.
- Hand-built in California.

### Verified sound evidence

Beetronics describes Royal Jelly as spanning silky overdrive/fuzz blends through gritty, high-frequency fuzz textures. The Dry control is explicitly intended to retain articulation and is useful on bass, drums, and synth as well as guitar.
