# Beetronics - Zzombee Filtremulator

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Beetronics
- **Pedal:** Zzombee Filtremulator
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz / Overdrive
- **Identity:** Beetronics Zzombee, an analog multi-effect that includes fuzz and overdrive alongside filtering, low-octave and modulation functions.

## What this pedal is
Beetronics describes Zzombee as an analog filtremulator with a broad effect set that includes **Overdrive, Fuzz and Low-Octave** plus filter, wah, LFO, randomized filter, tremolo and swell functions. Because it contains documented dirt functions, it belongs in the Dirt Archive while retaining its broader multi-effect identity.

## Functions
Documented functions include:
- Filter
- Wah
- LFO
- Randomized Filter
- Tremolo
- Swells
- **Overdrive**
- **Fuzz**
- **Low-Octave**

The pedal also includes tap tempo, CV control, external tap, expression output and preset storage.

## Circuit / architecture
Beetronics describes the audio architecture as **analog**, with digital control features layered onto the instrument effect system.

## Power
Beetronics' current FAQ specifies **100mA** current draw for Zzombee and states that the company's pedals generally use standard center-negative pedal power.

## Sound / behavior
The wide function set lets Zzombee move between dirt, octave, filter and modulation textures. The archive treats the overdrive/fuzz functions as part of the documented Zzombee architecture rather than reducing the product to a simple fuzz.

## History
Zzombee is part of Beetronics' Royal Series and is documented alongside the company's recent standard/catalog models.
