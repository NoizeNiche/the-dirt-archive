## Deep research verification

This pass uses Beetronics' exact-model product documentation.

### Verified description

The **Abelha Tropical Fuzz** is a psychedelic fuzz built around three selectable fuzz voices: **Polen, Nectar, and Honey**. Beetronics adds a dedicated **Tropical Mode** high-pass filter for a bright, direct-to-console-style response.

### Verified controls and architecture

- **Buzz:** fuzz/gain control.
- **Loud:** output volume.
- **HI / LO:** two-band EQ.
- Center toggle selects **Polen, Nectar, or Honey** fuzz flavor.
- **Tropical Mode** is a high-pass filter.
- Tropical Mode is accessed by double-tapping the footswitch or holding it for momentary operation.
- Three footswitch-profile options control the response timing of the momentary function.
- Requires **9VDC center-negative** power.
- Hand-built in California.
- Beetronics lists dimensions of 7 x 3.25 x 2.5 inches and weight of approximately 1.1 lb.

- Exact transistor/device complement: Not publicly specified in the reviewed builder documentation.

### Verified sound evidence

Beetronics describes Polen as sagged and spitty, Nectar as round and huge, and Honey as more contained with broader versatility. Tropical Mode adds a sharp, treble-forward edge inspired by plugging fuzz directly into a recording console.
