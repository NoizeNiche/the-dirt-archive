# Collision Devices - Black Hole Symmetry

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Black Hole Symmetry
- **Catalog identity:** Black Hole Symmetry
- **Builder:** Collision Devices
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz / Delay / Reverb
- **Identity:** Collision Devices Black Hole Symmetry, a three-section pedal combining modulated delay, pitch-shifted space reverb, and a destructive fuzz section.

## What this pedal is
Effects Database documents Black Hole Symmetry as three distinct sections: **Ergosphere** is a modulation delay based on the PT2399; **Event Horizon** is a space reverb based on the FV-1 with pitch shifting; and **Singularity** is the destruction fuzz section with a single Disintegrate control. [1]

## Controls
**Ergosphere / modulation delay**
- Mix
- Time, from 0 s to 1 s
- Feedback
- Modulation
- Speed
- Internal triangle-oscillator speed trimmer
- Activate switch [1]

**Event Horizon / space reverb**
- Mix
- Echo, up to 15 s
- Radiancy, controlling pitch-shifter proportion
- Pitch
- Activate switch [1]

**Singularity / destruction fuzz**
- Disintegrate
- Activate switch [1]

## Versions and factory options
Effects Database links multiple documented variants, including standard Black Hole Symmetry, Cream LTD, LTD Pastel Party, Mirror Polished, and related special finishes. Reverb's current product record lists **Black, Blackout, Polished Mirror, and White** finishes and gives the model range as 2018-2026. [1][2]

## Power and construction
The pedal uses a **9V DC negative-center** supply and is not battery-powered. Collision Devices states that each pedal is designed, assembled, tested, and numbered by hand in its workshop in France, with UV printing done by a local French partner. The pedals carry a lifetime warranty. [1]

## Transistor
**Not established.** The available model documentation identifies digital ICs for the delay and reverb sections but does not publish a discrete transistor list for the fuzz section. [1]

## Diode
**Not established.** No trustworthy model-specific clipping-diode part number or complete diode topology was located in the accessible documentation.

## Sound
The pedal is designed as a single multi-effect environment rather than three unrelated effects. The Ergosphere produces warm, modulated echoes; Event Horizon adds long spatial reverb with pitch manipulation; and Singularity supplies the destructive fuzz layer. The three sections can be used individually or together, allowing ambient, degraded, and heavily saturated textures from the same unit. [1][3]
