# Collision Devices - Singularity

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Singularity
- **Builder:** Collision Devices
- **Catalog identity:** Singularity
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Fuzz
- **Identity:** Collision Devices Singularity, a one-knob destruction fuzz with a three-position low-pass-filter toggle.

## What this pedal is
Collision Devices describes Singularity as a one-knob fuzz / distortion and a “destruction device.” The single knob controls the master fuzz volume, while a toggle selects among three low-pass filter cutoff frequencies that substantially change the fuzz response. [1]

## Controls
- Disintegrate: master fuzz volume.
- Three-position low-pass-filter toggle.
- Bypass footswitch.
- Input, output, and 9V power connections. [1][2]

## Power and construction
- 9V DC, negative center.
- Maximum current draw: 25 mA.
- Input impedance: 1 MOhm.
- Output impedance: 1 KOhm.
- Size: 118 x 118 x 56 mm.
- Weight: 150 g.
- True bypass.
- Lifetime warranty.
- Handmade in France. [1]

## Circuit
The manufacturer describes the pedal at the functional level and does not publish exact transistor or diode part numbers on the current product page. The Singularity fuzz section is also the basis for the fuzz portion of the later Black Hole Symmetry and TARS designs. [1][3]

## Versions and colorways
Collision Devices has documented alternative finishes, including black and white versions. The current shop listing identifies a black finish with black knob. These should be retained as finish variants rather than treated as different circuits unless documentation establishes a circuit change. [1][2]

## Sound
The three filter positions alter the cutoff frequency of the fuzz signal, moving the character of the one-knob circuit across different degrees of low-pass filtering. Collision Devices positions Singularity as a high-output destructive fuzz suitable for placing after a wide range of instruments. [1]

## Photo provenance
The manufacturer maintains a dedicated Singularity product page with model-specific imagery and a current shop listing identifying the model and black finish. Retail photography also clearly shows the one-knob enclosure, filter toggle, and single footswitch. [1][2]
