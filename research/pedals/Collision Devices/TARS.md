# Collision Devices - TARS

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** TARS
- **Builder:** Collision Devices

- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Fuzz
- **Identity:** Collision Devices TARS, a fuzz pedal with an analog low-pass filter inspired by the Korg MS-20.

## What this pedal is
Collision Devices describes TARS as a concept pedal combining guitar and synth approaches. Its fuzz section derives from the Singularity section of Black Hole Symmetry, while an analog MS-20-style filter creates additional fuzzy-noisy textures. [1]

## Controls
- Gain: fuzz gain, after a JFET input stage and followed by silicon gain/clipping stages.
- Master: master output volume.
- CutOff: MS-20-style low-pass filter cutoff, approximately 15 Hz to 44 kHz.
- Resonance: boosts intensity around the cutoff frequency.
- LPF VOL: VCA output level for the filter.
- 2p / 4p switch: selects -12 dB/octave or -24 dB/octave attenuation.
- Add Fuzz switch: adds a second unfiltered fuzz signal.
- S / P switch: serial or parallel routing of fuzz and filter.
- Expression / CV: linked to the CutOff control. [1][2]

## Circuit
Collision Devices specifies a JFET input stage and silicon gain/clipping stages in the fuzz path. The analog low-pass filter is based on an LM13700 stage and is inspired by the Korg MS-20 filter architecture. Exact transistor and diode part numbers for the fuzz stages are not published in the accessible manufacturer documentation. [1]

## Power and construction
- +9V DC, negative center.
- Maximum current draw: 150 mA.
- Input impedance: 1 MOhm.
- Output impedance: 1 KOhm.
- 120 x 90 x 55 mm.
- 150 g.
- True bypass.
- Isolated power supply recommended.
- Lifetime warranty.
- Hand-built in France. [1]

## Versions and finishes
The manufacturer documents a white TARS with silver custom-made knobs and faders, plus an alternative black/white finish sold as a distinct TARS Black & White version. The archive should retain these as documented finish variants rather than infer separate circuit revisions. [3][4]

## Sound
TARS spans grainy low-gain sounds through round, powerful fuzz/distortion. The resonant low-pass filter can sweep the spectrum for noisy textures, while serial/parallel routing and the Add Fuzz control allow filtered and unfiltered fuzz layers to be combined. [1][2]

## Photo provenance
