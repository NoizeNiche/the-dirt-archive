# Collision Devices - FZ - Gated Fuzz / Distortion

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** FZ - Gated Fuzz / Distortion
- **Builder:** Collision Devices
- **Catalog identity:** FZ - Gated Fuzz / Distortion
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Fuzz
- **Identity:** Collision Devices FZ, the first pedal in the builder's Sparks Series, built as a gated fuzz/distortion inspired by the Boss Tone and the fuzz section of the Black Hole Symmetry.

## What this pedal is
Collision Devices presents the FZ as a straightforward gated fuzz that spans low-gain distortion through aggressive fuzz. The manufacturer says it is inspired by the Boss Tone and by the fuzz circuit used in the Black Hole Symmetry, and identifies it as the first Sparks Series pedal. [1]

## Controls and features
- Volume / output level.
- Gain, covering lower-gain distortion through full fuzz.
- Gate, controlling the bias and degree of gating.
- High-frequency toggle for changing the top-end response.
- True bypass.
- Four silicon-transistor gain stages are documented by Effects Database. [1][2]

## Power and construction
- 9V DC, center negative.
- Designed and assembled by Collision Devices in France.
- Available as an assembled pedal or DIY kit through the Sparks Series. [1][2]

## Circuit
The FZ is documented as using four silicon-transistor gain stages. The exact transistor part numbers and diode part numbers are not published in the accessible manufacturer documentation. [2]

## Sound
At lower gain settings the FZ can deliver rounder overdrive/distortion textures; higher gain settings extend into dense fuzz, including gated and sputtering responses. The Gate control changes the biasing of the signal, while the high-frequency switch changes the amount of top-end energy entering the gain stages. [2][3]

## Versions and factory options
The Sparks Series is offered in three assembly paths: a workshop build with Collision Devices, a home DIY kit, or a finished pedal assembled and tested by Collision Devices. [1]

## Photo provenance
The manufacturer’s FZ information page identifies the pedal as the Sparks Series FZ and provides model-specific imagery. Retail listings also show the distinctive compact black enclosure with large white FZ graphics, three stacked controls, a top high-frequency toggle, and a single footswitch. [1][4]
