# Browne Amplification — Carbon X Dual Overdrive

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Browne Amplification
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Dual-Carbon overdrive, cataloged under the normalized product name used by Browne's current store.

## What this pedal is
The current Browne product page names this model **Carbon X Dual Overdrive** and describes it as a dual Carbon pedal. The archive retains the separately named tracker row **Carbon X - Dual Overdrive** as well; both records point to the same current manufacturer product page, so this naming duplication should be reviewed during a later catalog-normalization pass rather than silently merged here. [1]

## Colorways
Selectable color options are shown by the manufacturer; no single permanent finish is assumed.

## Versions and factory options
- Carbon V2 left side with 3-way hi-cut
- New grittier Carbon-derived right side
- Soft switching
- Adjustable startup state
- Internal looper/split-use switch [1]

## Version changes
Browne states that from February 2025 the right-side label became LEVEL/DRIVE. The underlying right side has lower output and more drive. [1]

## Sound
The two sides provide complementary Carbon-family responses, with the left side preserving greater headroom and the right side emphasizing a grittier, more raw drive character. [1]
