# Browne Amplification — Alkene - Nashville Drive

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Browne Amplification
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Nashville-style overdrive with expanded clipping and low-cut options.

## What this pedal is
The ALKENE is Browne Amplification's Nashville-inspired overdrive. Browne describes it as combining musical clipping with gentle amp-like compression, with tightened low end, restored mids, and a lower overall gain range intended to keep the control sweep usable. [1]

## Colorways
The current manufacturer page presents a selectable color option but does not establish a single permanent finish in the accessible text.

## Versions and factory options
- Volume/Level, Gain and Tone controls
- External symmetrical/asymmetrical clipping switch
- Internal More Clip switch with an alternate set of hard-clipping diodes
- Internal Low Cut switch
- The More Clip mode adds bite, aggression and gain while reducing output slightly
- Low Cut tightens low end [1]

## Version changes
The current ALKENE documentation preserves the expanded clipping/tone architecture, including the internal More Clip and Low Cut switches. The archived product lineage is the successor naming for the Nashville-oriented drive previously represented by the ATOM in Browne's older material. [1]

## Sound
ALKENE is voiced for clear crunchy rhythm tones and expressive lead overdrive rather than maximum-gain saturation. Browne emphasizes low-mid fullness, tightened bass, touch response and a subtle compressed/sagging feel, with asymmetrical clipping available for a wider and more amp-like response. [1]
