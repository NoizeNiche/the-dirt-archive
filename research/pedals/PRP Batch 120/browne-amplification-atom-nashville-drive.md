# Browne Amplification — Atom - Nashville Drive

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Browne Amplification
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Nashville-inspired overdrive predecessor to the current ALKENE.

## What this pedal is
Browne's surviving product documentation identifies the ATOM as a Nashville-inspired drive. The current ALKENE page preserves ATOM-specific audio examples and dimensions, indicating the older ATOM identity in the same Nashville-drive lineage. [1]

## Colorways
- A matte Flat Dark Earth powder coat is documented for the ATOM. [1]

## Versions and factory options
- 4.8 x 2.64 x 1.6 inches, with 2.25 inches including knobs
- 9V power
- 75mA draw
- Soft switching
- Adjustable startup state
- Nashville-drive voicing [1]

## Version changes
The current Browne site identifies ALKENE as the evolved Nashville overdrive and preserves ATOM references in its audio material. The archive keeps the ATOM as its own historical catalog identity rather than collapsing it into ALKENE. [1]

## Sound
ATOM is part of Browne's Nashville-oriented overdrive family, aimed at articulate drive and amp-like response rather than extreme saturation. The surviving manufacturer material provides audio examples but does not publish a complete technical circuit description. [1]
