# CastleRock Bass Overdrive

- **Builder:** CastleRock
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** CastleRock Bass Overdrive
- **What this pedal is:** A budget bass overdrive with separate control of distortion in the high- and low-frequency portions of the signal and a wet/dry-style tone control.
- **Colorways:** The documented production finish is the standard CastleRock metal enclosure; no stable alternate factory finish list was established.
- **Versions:** CR Bass Overdrive production model.
- **Version changes:** No numbered hardware revisions were established.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented.
- **Diode type:** Not documented.
- **Controls / switching:** Stacked dual LEVEL/GAIN controls, TONE, and stacked HIGH/LOW DISTORTION controls; dual outputs support mono or stereo operation.
- **Power:** 9V battery or 9V DC, 300 mA supply.
- **Sound:** The pedal lets bass players emphasize grit in the upper or lower frequency ranges independently, then blend the processed result with the tone control. Contemporary user reports compare its fuzzy/buzzy character to an ODB-3-style bass drive, but those reports are user impressions rather than manufacturer specifications.

## Sound

Because the electric bass has an extended frequency range, the CR Bass Overdrive incorporates a dual stacked distortion control where the inner knob controls the distortion amount for the higher frequencies and the outer knob controls the distortion for the lower frequencies.
The CR Bass Overdrive also uses a dual stacked control knob for Level and Gain.
A separate Tone knob allows you to control the timbre by mixing the wet and dry signals.
