# CAST Engineering Mike Zito Peace Drive

- **Builder:** CAST Engineering
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** Mike Zito Peace Drive
- **What this pedal is:** A two-channel gain pedal combining a dedicated overdrive with a separately footswitchable boost, developed with Mike Zito.
- **Colorways:** Reverb documents an orange production finish; other surviving examples may vary.
- **Versions:** Original Mike Zito Peace Drive production model.
- **Version changes:** The core design combines an independently usable boost and overdrive with order switching. No numbered hardware revision was established.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented.
- **Diode type:** Not documented.
- **Controls / switching:** Overdrive side uses LEVEL, GAIN, and TONE; boost side uses LEVEL and GAIN. A center pushbutton reverses the order of boost and drive, with two footswitches controlling the channels independently.
- **Power:** Standard 9V negative-tip supply; manufacturer coverage also documents battery operation for the broader CAST line.
- **Sound:** The red overdrive side covers thick, chunky drive with enough gain for a pushed clean amplifier, while the green boost can stand alone or stack before or after it. Both sections together move from boosted drive toward very saturated lead tones.
- **Research confidence:** High.
