# CAST Engineering Texas Flood

- **Builder:** CAST Engineering
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** Texas Flood
- **What this pedal is:** A three-control overdrive developed from the Cesar Diaz/SRV signal-chain concept and widened beyond a conventional Tube Screamer-style response.
- **Colorways:** Reverb documents a purple finish; surviving examples and special runs may differ.
- **Versions:** Original Texas Flood production model.
- **Version changes:** CAST states that the design retains the core Diaz-inspired character while adding bass balance, clarity, and more available gain. No numbered revision was established.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented.
- **Diode type:** Not documented.
- **Controls / switching:** DRIVE, TONE, and LEVEL with true bypass. The pedal can run from 9V to 18V.
- **Power:** 9V battery or standard negative-tip supply; CAST also documents operation up to 18V.
- **Sound:** Texas Flood is voiced around a slightly darker, focused drive with more low-end balance and gain than the circuit that inspired it. It can operate as a clean boost, low-gain growl, or more saturated overdrive while remaining highly stackable.
- **Research confidence:** High.
