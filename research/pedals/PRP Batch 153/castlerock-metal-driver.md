# CastleRock Metal Driver

- **Builder:** CastleRock
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Catalog identity:** CastleRock Metal Driver
- **What this pedal is:** A dedicated high-gain distortion aimed at metal, with a filtered midrange and more aggressive top end than CastleRock's general distortion.
- **Colorways:** Standard CastleRock enclosure; a complete factory finish list is not established.
- **Versions:** CRGMD Metal Drive production model.
- **Version changes:** No numbered hardware revision was established.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented.
- **Diode type:** Not documented.
- **Controls / switching:** LEVEL, FILTER, and GAIN.
- **Power:** 9V battery or 9V DC adapter, with contemporary retailer documentation listing a 300 mA supply.
- **Sound:** The design emphasizes tight low end, semi-scooped mids, and aggressive highs for heavy riffing. Contemporary retailer descriptions characterize it as a straightforward high-gain metal distortion rather than a flexible low-gain drive.
- **Research confidence:** High for controls, power, and intended voicing.
- **Sources checked:**
