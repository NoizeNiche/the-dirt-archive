# Casey Gooby Organ Donor

- **Builder:** Casey Gooby
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Catalog identity:** Organ Donor
- **What this pedal is:** A more extreme development of Casey Gooby's Bag Of Dicks fuzz, retaining the handmade Bosstone-derived character while increasing low end, output, and intensity.
- **Colorways:** Surviving examples vary; no reliable complete factory finish list is established.
- **Versions:** Original Organ Donor production model.
- **Version changes:** No numbered hardware revision was established. The model is documented as a crazier extension of the Bag Of Dicks rather than a separate unrelated design.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented.
- **Diode type:** Not documented.
- **Controls / switching:** Exact factory control labels are not consistently documented in the surviving catalog source.
- **Power:** Exact factory power specification not established.
- **Sound:** Organ Donor pushes the Bag Of Dicks idea toward more low end, volume, and instability. The surviving description specifically recommends rolling the guitar treble down to explore its more dramatic textures.
- **Research confidence:** High for the relationship to Bag Of Dicks and stated sonic behavior; medium for construction details.
