- **Builder:** Casey Gooby
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Catalog identity:** Bag Of Dicks

- **Controls / switching:** Fuzz is the defining gain control; surviving descriptions identify the pedal as a compact handmade fuzz, but a complete factory control list is not established from the reviewed source.
- **Power:** Exact factory power specification not established.
