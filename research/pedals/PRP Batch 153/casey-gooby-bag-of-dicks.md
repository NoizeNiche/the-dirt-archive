# Casey Gooby Bag Of Dicks

- **Builder:** Casey Gooby
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Catalog identity:** Bag Of Dicks
- **What this pedal is:** A boutique fuzz based around the Jordan Bosstone family, with a broader response than a standard Fuzz Face or Big Muff.
- **Colorways:** Surviving examples have varied artwork and finishes; a single stable factory color specification is not established.
- **Versions:** Original Bag Of Dicks production model.
- **Version changes:** No reliable numbered hardware revisions were established from the surviving catalog material.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented.
- **Diode type:** Not documented.
- **Controls / switching:** Fuzz is the defining gain control; surviving descriptions identify the pedal as a compact handmade fuzz, but a complete factory control list is not established from the reviewed source.
- **Power:** Exact factory power specification not established.
- **Sound:** The B.O.D. moves from a lower-gain, Octavia-like flavor with the fuzz barely raised through throaty mid settings and into a thick, cutting full-fuzz sound as it is turned up. Its sustain and ability to stay prominent in a live mix are emphasized in the surviving description.
- **Research confidence:** High for circuit lineage and sound description; medium for exact production details.
- **Sources checked:**
  - https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/caseygooby/bagofdicks
  - https://www.effectsdatabase.com/updates/weekly/20110502

## Sound

fuzz pedal has captured the sound of the Jordan Bosstone and so much more.
You can obtain a cool octavia vibe with the fuzz control just barely up, subtle throaty tones at the midpoint and full on in your face fuzz when dimmed.
The BOD really cuts through in live situations and the sustain will travel for days.This is a unique fuzz tone; it is not a Fuzz Face, it is not a Big Muff.
