# Casimir Effects Pedals Entropy Fuzz

- **Builder:** Casimir Effects Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Catalog identity:** Entropy Fuzz
- **What this pedal is:** A boutique handwired fuzz designed around strong picking and guitar-volume sensitivity, with a single gain control.
- **Colorways:** The reviewed production record documents a compact aluminum enclosure rather than a stable cosmetic edition list.
- **Versions:** Original Entropy Fuzz production model.
- **Version changes:** No numbered revisions were established from the reviewed source.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented.
- **Diode type:** Not documented.
- **Controls / switching:** A single Treble-Boosted Gain control; true bypass footswitch and 2.1 mm DC input are documented.
- **Power:** Boss-style 2.1 mm DC power input; exact voltage/current specification is not given in the reviewed record.
- **Sound:** The Entropy Fuzz is designed to stay touch-sensitive, ranging from a smooth, defined growl to a rich and thick wall of fuzz. Its single control makes the interaction with guitar volume and picking dynamics a major part of the design.
