# CAST Engineering Gypsy Haze

- **Builder:** CAST Engineering
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Catalog identity:** Gypsy Haze
- **What this pedal is:** An octave-up fuzz with a dedicated switching system that can make the octave momentary, latching, or part of a tap-tempo stutter mode.
- **Colorways:** The documented production finish uses a Gypsy-themed enclosure treatment; exact factory colorway history is not established.
- **Versions:** Production Gypsy Haze.
- **Version changes:** CAST describes the design as beginning as a solid octave-fuzz circuit and then adding its special switching system. The stutter mode converts the octave switch into a tap-tempo control for rhythmic octave gating.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented.
- **Diode type:** Not documented.
- **Controls / switching:** INTENSITY and LEVEL, separate fuzz and octave footswitches, plus a stutter/tap-tempo switching system.
- **Power:** 9V battery or standard 9V negative-tip supply.
- **Sound:** Gypsy Haze combines a vintage-leaning fuzz with octave-up harmonics and rhythmic switching. The momentary, latching, and stutter modes let the octave behave as either a conventional effect or a deliberately chopped, animated texture.
- **Research confidence:** High.
