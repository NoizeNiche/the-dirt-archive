# Axess Electronics — OBViouS Boost/OD

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** OBViouS Boost/OD
- **Builder:** Axess Electronics
- **Catalog type:** Boost / Overdrive
- **Identity:** Lower-gain transparent boost/overdrive with independent gain, treble, clipping and bass-response options, intended primarily for driving an already-overdriven amplifier. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
Axess positions OBViouS as a refinement tool rather than a high-gain distortion. It is designed to tighten the low end, shape the treble and add selectable clipping character while preserving the basic character of a pushed amp. [1]

## Sound
Axess positions OBViouS as a transparent lower-gain tool to push an amplifier, with bass tightening and treble shaping as core functions. Its clip selector extends the sound beyond a simple clean boost. [1]
