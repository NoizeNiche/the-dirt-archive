# Axess Electronics - OBViouS Boost/OD

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** OBViouS Boost/OD
- **Builder:** Axess Electronics
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Lower-gain transparent boost/overdrive designed to push an already overdriven tube amplifier while giving the player separate bass, treble, and clipping controls.

## What this pedal is
Axess describes OBViouS as a straightforward lower-gain pedal with Level, Gain, and Treble controls, a three-position clip selector, and a three-position bass-response selector. The design is explicitly intended to tighten and tailor the response of pushed tube amplifiers, especially plexi-style circuits.

## Sound
Axess positions OBViouS as a transparent, lower-gain tool for pushing already-overdriven tube amps while tightening the bass and shaping the treble. Its clip and bass-response switches broaden the voice beyond a one-knob boost without turning it into a high-gain distortion box.
