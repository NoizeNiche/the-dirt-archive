## What this pedal is
The updated taper of the “Cleanup” control is smoother than the older version, while the “Fuzz” control is more usable for setting post-gain and shaping the sound or establishing the maximum fuzz level when Cleanup is bypassed in Raw mode.

## Sound
The treble shape subtly rolls off the high end for a softer fuzz tone.
second footswitch - engages RAW mode bypassing the cleanup knob and tone controls to bring you back to full raw fuzz.
This makes it a sort of dual fuzz to get a huge variety of sounds.
