## What this pedal is
Effects Database lists the **Collins COD-1 Pro Over Drive** as a member of the Collins XXX-1 Pro Series, alongside the CBB-1 Pro Best Bass and CSD-1 Pro Super Dust. The available historical Collins discussion also distinguishes the Pro-series naming from the cheaper Collins COD-1/DT-1-era pedals. [1][2]
