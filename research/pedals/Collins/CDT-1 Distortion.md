# Collins - CDT-1 Distortion

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** CDT-1 Distortion
- **Catalog identity:** CDT-1 Distortion
- **Builder:** Collins
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** Collins CDT-1 Distortion, an inexpensive compact stompbox documented in the late-2000s and cataloged by Effects Database as part of the DT-1 family.

## What this pedal is
Effects Database lists **Collins CDT-1 Distortion** and groups it with the Aria DT-1, D.B. Goods DT-1, Dinosaur DDT-1, Electra ECDT-1, Legion Sound DDT-1, and Route 101 DT-1 as related models. The available Collins-specific historical discussion describes the CDT-1 as one of Collins' inexpensive Chinese belt-clip-style pedals. [1][2]

The available evidence is strongest for the pedal's family identity and physical category. An exact Collins-specific schematic or internal component list was not located, so the archive does not assign an unverified circuit clone or semiconductor part number to the Collins unit itself.

## Colorways
No reliable Collins-specific factory colorway sequence was established. Do not infer the finish of a sibling DT-1 family pedal as a Collins factory colorway.

## Controls
The related DT-1 family records use three controls:
- **Level**
- **Tone**
- **Distortion** [3][4]

Because the Collins-specific Effects Database page was not directly retrievable during this pass, these controls are recorded as **family-reference data**, not independently confirmed Collins-only specifications.

## Power and connections
Related DT-1 family records specify 1/4-inch mono input and output with 9V battery or 9V DC adapter operation. The exact Collins power implementation was not independently documented in the sources located for this pass. [3][4]

## Technical specifications
The DT-1 family records publish the following common specifications:
- Input impedance: **300 kOhm minimum**
- Output impedance: **200 Ohm maximum**
- Maximum gain: **50 dB**
- Signal-to-noise ratio: **85 dB**
- Frequency response: **70 Hz to 5 kHz**
- Size: **123 x 72 x 50 mm**
- Weight: **305 g** [3][4]

These figures are retained as **related-family reference specifications**, not as Collins-specific measurements, because the exact Collins model page was not accessible for independent confirmation during this pass.

## Enclosure
Related DT-1 family records describe a compact molded-plastic enclosure. The Collins-specific enclosure material was not independently confirmed. [4]

## Sound
The available family documentation characterizes the related DT-1 design as an analog distortion with a broad gain range, smooth sustain, and a three-control Level/Tone/Distortion interface. The Collins-specific historical sources found for this pass do not provide enough evidence to assign a more detailed voicing description without importing assumptions from sibling models. [3][4]

## Provenance notes
The Effects Database taxonomy explicitly lists Collins CDT-1 Distortion alongside its DT-1-related sibling models. A 2007-2008 Freestompboxes discussion independently documents the Collins CDT-1 as an inexpensive Chinese pedal and pairs it with the Collins COD-1 in a discussion of inexpensive Collins effects. [1][2]
