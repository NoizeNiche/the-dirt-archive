# Crestwood — Crestwood 8-Tr Fuzz Wah Pedal

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Crestwood 8-Tr Fuzz Wah Pedal
- **Builder:** Crestwood
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Crestwood-branded example of the Japanese Shin-Ei Companion WF-8 eight-transistor fuzz-wah family.

## What this pedal is
The Crestwood 8-Tr Fuzz Wah is documented as a 1970s Shin-Ei OEM combination pedal, rebranded under Crestwood and several other names. Its fuzz section is the eight-transistor Shin-Ei 8Tr design, paired with an onboard wah and two selectable fuzz voices. [1]

## Colorways
- Period Crestwood-branded examples are documented in the characteristic Japanese OEM enclosure format.
- Finish and graphic differences are treated as cosmetic colorways unless a circuit change is documented.

## Versions and factory options
### Crestwood 8-Tr Fuzz Wah production
- Fuzz Volume
- Fuzz Adjust
- Two-position fuzz voice switch
- Wah treadle
- Wah footswitch
- Fuzz footswitch
- 1/4-inch input and output
- 9V battery operation [1]

The pedal can be used as fuzz alone, wah alone, or both effects together. Effects Database identifies the Crestwood unit with the narrow-box WF-8 family rather than the wider WF-24 enclosure. [1]

## Version changes
No numbered Crestwood V1/V2 sequence was verified. The main archive distinction is between the six-transistor Crestwood model and this eight-transistor WF-8 family model.

## Transistor
- **Technology:** Eight-transistor fuzz circuit. [1]
- **Exact transistor/device:** Not established for the Crestwood-branded unit.

## Diode
- **Type:** **Germanium**, with two germanium diodes reported for the Shin-Ei 8Tr circuit. [1]
- **Exact diode/device:** Unknown.

## Sound
The 8-Tr fuzz is derived from the Super Fuzz family and provides two selectable fuzz textures, while the integrated wah supplies a narrow, expressive sweep. The combined effect is capable of thick, octave-rich and aggressive tones, with independent engagement of the fuzz and wah sections. [1]
