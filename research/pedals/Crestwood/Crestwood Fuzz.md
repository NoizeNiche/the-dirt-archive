# Crestwood — Crestwood Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Crestwood Fuzz
- **Builder:** Crestwood
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Crestwood-branded Japanese FA-II-family fuzz associated with the Apollo and Sekova versions of the same production lineage.

## What this pedal is
Crestwood Fuzz is a compact vintage fuzz from the Japanese OEM market. Effects Database identifies the Crestwood version as a black-enclosure **FA-II circuit**, while historical circuit research identifies the Apollo and Crestwood versions as members of the same FA-II lineage descended from the Japanese Shin-Ei/Sekova family. [1][2][3]

## Colorways
- The documented Crestwood version uses a black enclosure. [1]
- Other visual differences found among related FA-II-branded examples are not automatically assigned to the Crestwood parent.

## Versions and factory options
### Crestwood Fuzz
- Compact wedge-style enclosure
- Fuzz/volume control arrangement associated with the FA-II family
- 9V battery operation
- Positive-ground germanium transistor circuit is reported for the FA-II family. [2][3]

Historical examples within this lineage show that circuit boards and components could vary over production, so the archive keeps the Crestwood model as one parent identity rather than inventing a numbered revision sequence without direct Crestwood documentation. [2]

## Version changes
The broader FA-II family went through PCB and component revisions during its production life. Those documented family-level changes do not establish separate Crestwood catalog models by themselves, so they remain historical variant notes rather than new top-level identities. [2]

## Transistor
- **Technology:** Germanium, PNP, for the documented FA-II family. [2][3]
- **Exact transistor/device:** **2SB173** is documented in a traced example of the FA-II family; this is not treated as a guarantee for every Crestwood unit. [2]

## Diode
- **Type:** Germanium diodes are documented in the traced FA-II example. [2]
- **Exact part:** Unknown.

## Sound
The FA-II family is described as an aggressive vintage fuzz with a strong, compressed response and a character distinct from the earlier FZ-1A-style wedge fuzzes. Historical circuit research also notes substantial tonal variation between component choices and production revisions, which helps explain why surviving examples can feel different from one another. [2][3]
