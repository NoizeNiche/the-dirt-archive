# Crestwood — Crestwood 6-Tr Fuzz Wah

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Crestwood 6-Tr Fuzz Wah
- **Builder:** Crestwood
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Crestwood-branded example of the Japanese Shin-Ei/Companion six-transistor fuzz-wah family.

## What this pedal is
The Crestwood 6-Tr Fuzz Wah is documented by Effects Database as part of the **Companion 6-Tr** family, a Japanese OEM design sold under numerous names. The family combines a transformer-based wah with a six-transistor fuzz circuit and a two-position fuzz voicing switch. [1][2]

## Colorways
- Crestwood examples are documented with the period Crestwood-branded enclosure and graphics.
- Finish differences are treated as cosmetic colorways unless a circuit change is independently established.

## Versions and factory options
### Crestwood 6-Tr Fuzz Wah
- Wah treadle
- Wah on/off footswitch
- Fuzz on/off footswitch
- Two-position fuzz tone selector
- 1/4-inch input and output
- 9V battery operation [1][2]

Effects Database groups the Crestwood version with other Companion 6-Tr rebrands and distinguishes it from the separate eight-transistor WF-8 family. [1]

## Version changes
The surviving documentation establishes the Crestwood 6-Tr as a brand variant of the common Shin-Ei OEM design, not a separately documented circuit generation. No reliable numbered V1/V2 Crestwood sequence was established.

## Sound
The 6-Tr family is known for raw, high-gain fuzz with pronounced upper-octave content and a selectable softer or harder voicing, paired with a narrow, vocal-style wah sweep. Period examples can also exhibit a noticeable level change when the fuzz is engaged. [2]
