## What this pedal is
Effects Database records the Dragon-71 as a Cluster Effects pedal submitted in 2006 and classifies it under distortion and sustain. The database also preserves manufacturer and demo links for the model. [1]

The surviving catalog evidence places Dragon-71 in Cluster Effects' Big Muff-related family tree, but the checked record does not establish a factory statement that the pedal is a direct Big Muff clone. The archive therefore records the relationship as lineage context rather than a clone claim. [1]
