## What this pedal is
Effects Database identifies the DS-50 as a guitar distortion pedal in a die-cast metal case. The accompanying Chord manual documents Level, Tone, and Dist controls and a 9V battery or adapter power system. [1][2]

## Versions and factory options
The manual documents:
- **LEVEL**: output level.
- **TONE**: frequency balance from low to high.
- **DIST**: distortion amount and sustain.
- 6.3 mm input/output.
- 9V battery or optional mains adapter.
- Footswitch and LED. [2]

Specifications:
- Input impedance: 470 kOhm.
- Output impedance: greater than 10 kOhm.
- Current draw: 8 mA. [2]

## Sound
Chord describes the DS-50 as a conventional guitar distortion pedal whose Tone control shifts the frequency balance and whose Dist control changes the amount of distortion and sustain. [2]
