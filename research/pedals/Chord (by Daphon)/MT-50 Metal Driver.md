## What this pedal is
Effects Database describes the MT-50 as a high-gain distortion pedal aimed specifically at hard-hitting metal tones. Its Filter control shapes the tonal accent while Gain sets the amount of distortion/overdrive. [1]

The official Chord manual confirms the Level, Filter, and Gain controls and 9V battery/adapter operation. [2]

## Versions and factory options
The manual documents:
- **LEVEL**: output level.
- **FILTER**: high-frequency response.
- **GAIN**: distortion/overdrive amount.
- 9V battery or optional mains adapter.
- 1 MOhm input impedance.
- 10 kOhm output impedance.
- 11 mA current draw. [2]
