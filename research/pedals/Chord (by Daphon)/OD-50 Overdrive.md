# Chord (by Daphon) — OD-50 Overdrive

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** OD-50 Overdrive
- **Builder:** Chord (by Daphon)

- **Identity:** Chord OD-50 Overdrive, a three-control natural amp-style overdrive from the Daphon 50-series.

## What this pedal is
Effects Database describes the OD-50 as a guitar overdrive intended to produce a natural driven-amp sound ranging from mild crunch to higher-energy saturation. [1]

The official Chord manual documents Level, Tone, and Drive controls. [2]

## Colorways

## Versions and factory options
The manual documents:
- **LEVEL**: output.
- **TONE**: overall brightness.
- **DRIVE**: gain and overdrive amount.
- 9V battery or optional mains adapter.
- 1 MOhm input impedance.
- 10 kOhm output impedance.
- 4 mA current draw. [2]

## Sound
Chord describes the OD-50 as an overdrive designed for natural amp-like drive, covering mild crunch through stronger saturation. The three-control interface provides direct output, brightness, and gain adjustment. [1][2]
