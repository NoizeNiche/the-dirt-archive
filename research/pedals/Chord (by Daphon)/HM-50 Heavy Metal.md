## What this pedal is
The official Chord/AVSL manual documents HM-50 as a heavy-metal distortion pedal with separate low, middle, high, mid-frequency, and distortion controls. [1]

Effects Database lists it in the Chord-by-Daphon product family. [2]

## Sound
The HM-50 is specifically designed for hard-hitting metal tones. Its four-band-style shaping gives direct control over low, high, mid amount, and the frequency selected by the middle band, while DIST controls distortion and sustain. [1]
