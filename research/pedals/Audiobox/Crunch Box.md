## What this is
Effects Database lists Audiobox Crunch Box as an overdrive stompbox and preserves period product imagery. Audiobox's product family also included Crystal Box, Metal Box and Smash Box. [1]

The database's Related section connects Crunch Box with other historical drive designs, including the Eleca Warp Driver, Aria WD-1 and Collins COD-1, but this is a catalog relationship rather than proof of a shared schematic. [1]
