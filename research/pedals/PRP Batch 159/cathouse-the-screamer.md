# Cathouse Pedals The Screamer

- **Builder:** Cathouse Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** The Screamer
- **What this pedal is:** A compact distortion/overdrive with a broad gain range and selectable clipping profiles.
- **Colorways:** Standard enclosure finish documented by period coverage.
- **Versions:** The Screamer; no materially distinct circuit version established.
- **Version changes:** Not documented.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented.
- **Diode type:** Germanium and silicon clipping modes are documented.
- **Controls / switching:** Gain, Tone, Volume, plus a three-position clipping selector for unclipped boost, germanium clipping, or silicon clipping.
- **Power:** Not documented by the sources checked.
- **Factory modifications:** None documented.
- **Sound:** The Screamer ranges from unclipped boost through clipping-based overdrive and distortion. The selectable clipping profiles give it a wider tonal range than its simple three-knob front panel suggests.
- **Research confidence:** High for controls and clipping modes; moderate for broader circuit description.
