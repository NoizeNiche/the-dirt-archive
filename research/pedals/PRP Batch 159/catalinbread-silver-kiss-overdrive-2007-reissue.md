# Catalinbread Effects Silver Kiss Overdrive (2007 Reissue)

- **Builder:** Catalinbread Effects
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** Silver Kiss Overdrive (2007 Reissue)
- **What this pedal is:** A reissue of the Silver Kiss Mk. II, combining op-amp overdrive, clean-boost capability, and active three-band EQ.
- **Colorways:** Current 2007 Reissue presentation documented by Catalinbread.
- **Versions:** 2007 Reissue.
- **Version changes:** Catalinbread describes the reissue as a recreation of its original flagship overdrive, retaining the Silver Kiss Mk. II architecture with active Treble, Mid, and Bass controls and a wide gain range.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented in the reviewed manufacturer material.
- **Diode type:** Not documented in the reviewed manufacturer material.
- **Controls / switching:** Treb, Vol, Mid, Bass, Gain.
- **Power:** 9-18V DC, center-negative.
- **Sound:** The reissue is voiced as a glassy, dynamic drive with natural decay. Gain can be reduced toward clean boost, while the active EQ can remain flat at noon or be used aggressively for broad tonal shaping.
- **Research confidence:** High for the current five-control layout, power range, and reissue identity; moderate for undocumented component details.
