# Catalinbread Effects Silver Kiss Overdrive (2007 Reissue)

- **Builder:** Catalinbread Effects
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** Silver Kiss Overdrive (2007 Reissue)
- **What this pedal is:** A reissue of Catalinbread's original flagship Silver Kiss Mk. II overdrive. Catalinbread describes it as three functions in one: clean boost, medium-gain overdrive and a powerful active three-band EQ.
- **Colorways:** Silver Kiss reissue finish shown by Catalinbread.
- **Versions:** 2007 Reissue; Catalinbread's current listing identifies the circuit as Silver Kiss MK2.
- **Version changes:** The current source explicitly identifies the product as the 2007 Reissue and Silver Kiss MK2. No additional dated circuit change is assigned.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented by the primary current source.
- **Diode type:** Not documented by the sources checked.
- **Controls / switching:** Treble, Volume, Mid, Bass, Gain. The three EQ controls are active and flat around noon; Gain can be reduced to provide a clean boost.
- **Power:** 9-18V DC, center-negative.
- **Factory modifications:** None documented by the sources checked.
- **Sound:** Catalinbread describes a glassy drive with natural note decay, gentle clipping and an unusually flexible active EQ. With Gain down and the EQ flat, it can function as a clean boost; higher gain settings provide medium-gain overdrive.
- **Research confidence:** High.
