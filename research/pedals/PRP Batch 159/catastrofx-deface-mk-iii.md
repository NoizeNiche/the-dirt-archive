# CatastroFX Deface Mk.III

- **Builder:** CatastroFX
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Catalog identity:** Deface Mk.III
- **What this pedal is:** A later CatastroFX take on the classic Fuzz Face circuit, expanded with additional controls for more shaping and cleanup behavior than the original two-knob format.
- **Colorways:** Multiple small-batch finishes documented in used and builder listings; exact cosmetic editions are not treated as separate circuit identities here.
- **Versions:** Deface; Deface Mk.II; Deface Mk.III.
- **Version changes:** Earlier Deface versions are documented as two-transistor Fuzz Face-style circuits with increased output and, on later builds, an Input control. Contemporary discussion identifies the Mk.III as the four-control version with Input and Bias additions.
- **Transistor type:** Version-dependent. Deface listings document BC108C or BC549 implementations; the exact Mk.III transistor part number was not independently confirmed in the reviewed sources.
- **Diode type:** Not documented.
- **Controls / switching:** Fuzz, Volume, Input, Bias.
- **Power:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.
- **Sound:** The Deface family retains a Fuzz Face-style response while extending cleanup, output, and bias control beyond the original circuit. The Mk.III expands the range into more gated and splattery textures while retaining traditional fuzz sounds.
- **Research confidence:** Moderate. The Fuzz Face family identity and four-control Mk.III configuration are supported, but exact Mk.III component values and power specification remain undocumented in the reviewed sources.
