# Catalinbread Effects SideArm Overdrive

- **Builder:** Catalinbread Effects
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** SideArm Overdrive
- **What this pedal is:** A modern reworking of the 1979-style “green” overdrive circuit, with expanded gain range and additional frequency-shaping controls.
- **Colorways:** Standard '70s Collection green finish documented.
- **Versions:** SideArm Overdrive; current product revision documented by Catalinbread.
- **Version changes:** The current Catalinbread documentation describes the SideArm as a fresh take on the classic '79 green overdrive, with a 1KHz-centered hump/filter arrangement, an added 720Hz Mids control, a tilt-style Tone control, expanded gain range, and a reworked clipping arrangement.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented in the reviewed manufacturer material.
- **Diode type:** Not documented in the reviewed manufacturer material.
- **Controls / switching:** Mids, Volume, Tone, Drive.
- **Power:** 9-18V DC, center-negative.
- **Sound:** Designed to retain the familiar pushed-green-overdrive character while opening the midrange and extending the usable gain and EQ range. Mids boosts or scoops the 720Hz band; Tone acts as a tilt EQ centered at 1KHz; Drive ranges from clean to substantially dirty.
- **Research confidence:** High for the current control set, operating range, circuit concept, and 720Hz/1KHz frequency behavior; moderate for undocumented component details.
