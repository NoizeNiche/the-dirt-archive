# Cathouse Pedals Rat Fink

- **Builder:** Cathouse Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Catalog identity:** Rat Fink
- **What this pedal is:** A Rat-inspired distortion designed around the character of an early-1980s Rat v2 while emphasizing mid- and low-frequency harmonics.
- **Colorways:** Contemporary Cathouse finish documented in review and database coverage.
- **Versions:** Rat Fink.
- **Version changes:** No separate production revision was confirmed in the reviewed material.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented.
- **Diode type:** Not documented.
- **Controls / switching:** Distortion, Tone, Volume.
- **Power:** Not documented in the reviewed material.
- **Sound:** Cathouse describes the circuit as using a hard-to-find older op-amp with slow response and asymmetric frequency gain to emphasize growling mids and lows. The Tone control is used to retain or reduce high-frequency content while the distortion range provides sustained, rhythmic drive.
- **Research confidence:** High for the Rat-inspired design, control set, and op-amp description; moderate for undocumented component and power details.
