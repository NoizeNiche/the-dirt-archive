# Cause & Effect Pedals (CE Pedals) FET Dream

- **Builder:** Cause & Effect Pedals (CE Pedals)
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** FET Dream
- **What this pedal is:** A hand-built overdrive/distortion pedal using a tube-like FET input stage, pre-distortion low/mid shaping and post-distortion treble shaping.
- **Colorways:** Powder-coated and silk-screened cast-aluminum enclosure.
- **Versions:** FET Dream; no materially distinct production version established by the sources checked.
- **Version changes:** Not documented.
- **Transistor type:** Individually selected JFET transistors are documented in the input stage.
- **Diode type:** Not documented.
- **Controls / switching:** Girth, Drive, Lean, Level. Girth shapes low and low-mid content before distortion; Drive controls gain; Lean shapes upper treble after distortion; Level controls output.
- **Power:** 9V DC, center-negative; approximately 6mA. 9V battery support is documented.
- **Factory modifications:** None documented.
- **Sound:** The FET Dream is designed for articulate, harmonically rich overdrive and distortion with a tube-like feel. Its pre/post distortion EQ arrangement can fatten or tighten the low end and move the high-frequency response from smoother to more aggressive.
- **Research confidence:** High for the JFET stage, controls, power and switching.
