# Cathouse Pedals Fuzzy Tongue

- **Builder:** Cathouse Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Catalog identity:** Fuzzy Tongue
- **What this pedal is:** A simple Fuzz Face-inspired fuzz using matched and biased germanium transistors.
- **Colorways:** Pink finish documented in period review coverage.
- **Versions:** Fuzzy Tongue; no materially distinct circuit version established.
- **Version changes:** Not documented.
- **Transistor type:** Germanium, matched and biased.
- **Diode type:** Not documented.
- **Controls / switching:** Fuzz and Volume.
