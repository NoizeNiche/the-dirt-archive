# Cathouse Pedals The Screamer

- **Builder:** Cathouse Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** The Screamer
- **What this pedal is:** A flexible drive pedal combining clean boost, overdrive, and distortion behavior with selectable clipping profiles.
- **Colorways:** Contemporary Cathouse finish documented in review and database coverage.
- **Versions:** The Screamer.
- **Version changes:** No separate production revision was confirmed in the reviewed material.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented in the reviewed material.
- **Diode type:** Germanium and silicon clipping options are documented; exact diode part numbers are not.
- **Controls / switching:** Gain, Tone, Volume, three-position clipping toggle.
- **Power:** Not documented in the reviewed material.
- **Sound:** The Screamer provides a broad gain range from unclipped boost through Germanium or silicon clipping, with a large Tone range and enough gain for both guitar and bass applications.
- **Research confidence:** High for the control set and clipping options; moderate for undocumented component and power details.
