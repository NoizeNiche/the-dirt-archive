# Cathouse Pedals The Bender

- **Builder:** Cathouse Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Catalog identity:** The Bender
- **What this pedal is:** A modern, more reliable take on the three-knob Tone Bender format.
- **Colorways:** Contemporary Cathouse finish documented in review and database coverage.
- **Versions:** The Bender.
- **Version changes:** No separate production revision was confirmed in the reviewed material.
- **Transistor type:** Three matched and biased germanium transistors.
- **Diode type:** Not documented.
- **Controls / switching:** Fuzz, Tone, Volume.
- **Power:** 9V DC.
- **Sound:** The Bender is intended to preserve the warm, unruly character of a vintage three-knob Tone Bender while improving consistency and reliability. The three controls cover fuzz amount, tone shaping, and output.
- **Research confidence:** High for the Tone Bender relationship, germanium construction, controls, and 9V operation; moderate for undocumented component details.
