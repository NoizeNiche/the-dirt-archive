# Catalinbread Effects Silver Kiss Overdrive

- **Builder:** Catalinbread Effects
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** Silver Kiss Overdrive
- **What this pedal is:** The original Silver Kiss family identity, centered on a dynamic low-to-medium-gain overdrive combined with active three-band EQ and clean-boost operation.
- **Colorways:** Historical Silver Kiss finishes; later reissue and special editions exist separately in the catalog.
- **Versions:** Original Silver Kiss family; Silver Kiss Mk. II documentation.
- **Version changes:** Effects Database describes the Mk. II as a three-function design combining dynamic overdrive, a three-band equalizer, and clean boost, while keeping the EQ fully flat and gain nearly out for transparent boost use.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented in the reviewed material.
- **Diode type:** Not documented in the reviewed material.
- **Controls / switching:** Treble, Mid, Bass, Gain, Volume.
- **Power:** Not documented in the reviewed material.
- **Sound:** The Silver Kiss Mk. II is described as clear, detailed, dynamic, and amp-like, moving from edge-of-breakup grit toward full overdrive. Its active Treble, Mid, and Bass controls can scoop or reinforce broad frequency regions while remaining flat at noon.
- **Research confidence:** High for the Mk. II control architecture and functional description; moderate for historical version boundaries and undocumented component details.
