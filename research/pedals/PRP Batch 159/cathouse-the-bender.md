# Cathouse Pedals The Bender

- **Builder:** Cathouse Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Catalog identity:** The Bender
- **What this pedal is:** A three-knob fuzz loosely based on the classic three-knob Tone Bender format.
- **Colorways:** Standard enclosure finish; cosmetic differences are not treated as separate variants.
- **Versions:** The Bender; no materially distinct circuit version established.
- **Version changes:** Not documented.
- **Transistor type:** Three matched and biased germanium transistors.
- **Diode type:** Not documented.
- **Controls / switching:** Fuzz, Tone, Volume.
- **Power:** 9V operation documented.
- **Factory modifications:** None documented.
- **Sound:** The Bender is described as warm and smooth, moving from lightly overdriven fuzz to much heavier saturation. The matched/bias-treated germanium transistor set is part of its period-documented design.
- **Research confidence:** High.
