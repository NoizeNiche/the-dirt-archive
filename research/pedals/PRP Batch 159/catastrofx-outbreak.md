# CatastroFX Outbreak

- **Builder:** CatastroFX
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** Outbreak
- **What this pedal is:** A compact dual-voiced overdrive combining CatastroFX's High Velocity and Southerly Harmonic overdrive circuits.
- **Colorways:** Current compact-enclosure artwork/finish documented.
- **Versions:** Outbreak; 2025-era launch documented in independent coverage.
- **Version changes:** No later materially distinct version established by the sources checked.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented by the sources checked.
- **Diode type:** Not documented by the sources checked.
- **Controls / switching:** Level, Tone, Drive, plus a stage selector choosing High Velocity or Southerly Harmonic voicing. The selector changes the circuit voice rather than creating a separate pedal identity.
- **Power:** Not documented by the sources checked.
- **Factory modifications:** None documented by the sources checked.
- **Sound:** Outbreak covers two distinct overdrive voices in one enclosure, from the High Velocity side to the Southerly Harmonic side. Independent coverage describes both voices as dynamic, textured overdrives intended to preserve the character of the source circuits.
- **Research confidence:** High for the dual-voice concept and control layout; unknown for undocumented power/component details.
