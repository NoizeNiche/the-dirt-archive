# CatastroFX Outbreak

- **Builder:** CatastroFX
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** Outbreak
- **What this pedal is:** A dual-voiced compact overdrive combining CatastroFX's take on the Greer Lightspeed-style high-velocity drive and the Southland-style harmonic overdrive in one enclosure.
- **Colorways:** Silver sparkle enclosure documented.
- **Versions:** Outbreak.
- **Version changes:** The 2025 Outbreak combines the two drive voices on one circuit board, with a single Stage control selecting between them and engaging the harder-clipping gain stage associated with the Southland side.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.
- **Diode type:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.
- **Controls / switching:** Level, Tone, Drive, Stage.
- **Power:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.
- **Sound:** Stage selects between the lower-gain High Velocity/Lightspeed-style voice and the higher-harmonic Southland-style voice. The combined enclosure is designed to retain the dynamic response associated with both source circuits.
- **Research confidence:** High for the dual-circuit identity and control set; moderate for undocumented component and power details.
