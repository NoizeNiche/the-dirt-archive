# Catalinbread Effects StarCrash Fuzz

- **Builder:** Catalinbread Effects
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Catalog identity:** StarCrash Fuzz
- **What this pedal is:** A refined silicon Fuzz Face-style fuzz for the '70s Collection, adding bias and low-cut controls rather than simply reproducing the stock circuit.
- **Colorways:** Standard '70s Collection finish documented.
- **Versions:** StarCrash Fuzz; '70s Collection editions.
- **Version changes:** Catalinbread presents the StarCrash as a purpose-built refinement of the '70s silicon Fuzz Face concept, removing the traditional Fuzz control and adding Bias plus Low-Cut.
- **Transistor type:** Silicon Fuzz Face-style circuit; exact transistor part number not documented in the reviewed manufacturer material.
- **Diode type:** Not documented.
- **Controls / switching:** Volume, Low-Cut, Bias.
- **Power:** 9V DC.
- **Sound:** Bias changes the character from fuller, distortion-like fuzz toward a sputtery, Velcro-like response by starving the second transistor. Low-Cut operates as a high-pass filter that can progressively tighten the low end.
- **Research confidence:** High for the circuit concept, control set, and power requirement; moderate for undocumented component details.
