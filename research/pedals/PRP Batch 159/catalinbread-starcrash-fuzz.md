# Catalinbread Effects StarCrash Fuzz

- **Builder:** Catalinbread Effects
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Catalog identity:** StarCrash Fuzz
- **What this pedal is:** A silicon-era reinterpretation of the Fuzz Face concept, designed around the 1970s version of that circuit. Catalinbread removes the conventional Fuzz control and adds Bias and Low-Cut controls.
- **Colorways:** Standard StarCrash finish.
- **Versions:** StarCrash Fuzz; documented as part of the Catalinbread '70s / StarCrash Collection.
- **Version changes:** No materially distinct circuit version was established by the sources checked.
- **Transistor type:** Silicon; Catalinbread specifically discusses the 1970s transition to silicon Fuzz Face componentry and describes the Bias control as acting on the second transistor.
- **Diode type:** Not documented by the sources checked.
- **Controls / switching:** Volume, Low-Cut, Bias. Catalinbread says Low-Cut acts as a high-pass filter and Bias changes the texture from distortion-like to Velcro/sputtery fuzz.
- **Power:** 9V DC, center-negative. Catalinbread warns against AC or center-positive supplies.
- **Factory modifications:** None documented by the sources checked.
- **Sound:** StarCrash covers traditional Fuzz Face territory but adds control over low-frequency buildup and transistor bias. Bias can move the response from full and throaty toward sputtery Velcro textures, while Low-Cut removes low-frequency mud.
- **Research confidence:** High.
