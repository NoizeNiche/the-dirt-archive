## What this pedal is
Godeater+ is a programmable, multi-path distortion pedal with separate clean and dirty paths, filtering, bass control and crossover-style routing. [1]

## Versions and factory options
- GAIN, TONE, DIRTY, CLEAN and BASS controls.
- PREGAIN and CUTOFF input-stage controls.
- XOVER and SWAP functions alter which frequency regions feed the dirty and clean paths. [1]

## Version changes
- Animal Factory documents a Rev. 1.0 and later firmware behavior. January 2026 firmware notes include fixes for intermittent freezing/clean-channel loss, relay startup behavior, LED changes and improved LFO sub-mode. [2]

## Sound
Godeater+ can split the guitar into clean and distorted frequency regions, creating controlled parallel distortion rather than simply increasing broadband gain. Its PREGAIN, CUTOFF, XOVER and SWAP controls make the response highly shapeable. [1]
