## What this pedal is
Godeater+ is the current large-format development of Animal Factory's Godeater concept, built around extreme saturation with a filter-keyed sidechain, advanced routing, MIDI integration and an advanced crossover section. Animal Factory states that the circuit internally operates at up to 24V while accepting 9–12V external power at a high current requirement. [1][2]

## Version relationship to Godeater
Godeater+ is intentionally kept separate from the archive's **Godeater** parent because Animal Factory treats the plus-sign product as a distinct current design rather than merely a cosmetic or minor revision. Earlier Godeater production history includes a 2020 edition and a 2023 v3 evolution, but those belong to the Godeater parent record, not Godeater+. [2][3]

## Version changes
The documented difference is architectural: Godeater+ adds much more sophisticated routing, filter-keyed sidechain processing, MIDI and crossover functions than the earlier Godeater family. The archive does not invent a V2/V3 number for Godeater+ without builder documentation.

## Sound
Godeater+ is designed around enormous, saturated distortion while allowing the player to control how that saturation interacts with frequency ranges and routing paths. The filter-keyed sidechain and crossover system are especially important to its character, making it possible to sculpt aggressive distortion without simply turning the entire signal into a uniform wall of gain. [1][2]
