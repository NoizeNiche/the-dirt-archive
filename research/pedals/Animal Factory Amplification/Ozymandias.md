# Animal Factory Amplification — Ozymandias

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Ozymandias
- **Builder:** Animal Factory Amplification
- **Catalog types:** Distortion / Overdrive
- **Current generation:** 2024 launch generation; current production continues in 2026.
- **Identity:** One parent containing two independent channels, CROOK and FLAIL. The earlier Ozymandias concept existed before the 2024 product, but the current pedal is a substantially developed production realization rather than a cosmetic reissue.

## What this pedal is
Ozymandias is a dual overdrive/distortion pedal with independent CROOK and FLAIL channels. CROOK is fuller, darker and low-end-heavy, while FLAIL is more mid-focused, compressed and aggressive; each channel has its own gain, tone, volume and clipping/range controls. [1][2]

## Colorways
- Current production is the black geometric AFA enclosure.
- Cosmetic changes are not treated as versions.

## Versions and factory development
### Ozymandias — current production generation
The 2024 production design includes:
- two independent channels
- GAIN, TONE and VOLUME per channel
- GRIND / OFF / GRIT clipping choices
- RULE / CONQUER low-end options
- FULL / MID range voicing
- independent inputs and outputs for both channels
- series or parallel routing
- input and output buffers
- MIDI control
- USB-C firmware connection
- internal 22V operation from 9–12V DC supply [1][2]

### Historical development
Animal Factory's own manual explains that Ozymandias was shelved after its original development, with the FLAIL channel later simplified into the Pit Viper. In 2024 the builder returned to Ozymandias and developed the current two-channel product. This history is recorded as development lineage rather than invented V1/V2 pedal numbers. [2]

## Version changes
The major historical change is the revival and complete production realization of Ozymandias in 2024, with two full channels, independent routing, buffers, MIDI and 22V internal operation. The current manual is explicitly versioned 1.0 from October 2024, but that manual version number is not treated as a pedal V1 because it is a document revision. [2]

## Diode
- **Technology:** selectable soft clipping, natural clipping, and asymmetrical LED limiting through the GRIT/OFF/GRIND controls.
- Exact diode type is not published. [1][3]

## Sound
Ozymandias is essentially two different gain voices in one enclosure: CROOK provides fat, articulate, low-end-heavy drive, while FLAIL pushes the mids and compression harder for a more cutting voice. Running them in series produces a stacked gain chain, while parallel routing can treat them as independent channels for two amplifiers or even two instruments. [1][2]
