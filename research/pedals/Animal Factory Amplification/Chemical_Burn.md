## What this pedal is
Chemical Burn is Animal Factory Amplification's first pedal and a modern, deliberately irreverent take on the Shin-Ei Superfuzz/FY-6 family. The builder describes later development as a sequence of increasingly expanded versions rather than a one-and-done recreation. [1][2]

## Versions
### Chemical Burn — original generation
The original pedal established the Superfuzz-inspired core and the characteristic Animal Factory treatment of the circuit. Exact surviving control details vary across historical documentation, so the archive does not invent a complete schematic-level version history where the builder has not published one. [1]

### Chemical Burn v3
Animal Factory has documented a **third iteration (v3)** with expanded controls, switchable level boost, added tone-shaping control and octave-related capabilities. This is a builder-designated version and therefore belongs explicitly inside the parent record. [2]

## Factory modifications

- No post-sale DIY modifications are included.

## Version changes
The v3 development broadens Chemical Burn from its original Superfuzz-inspired core into a more controllable multi-function fuzz, adding control over level, tone and octave behavior. Because the builder does not expose every circuit change between each historical step in the public material reviewed, only the documented v3 changes are recorded rather than guessing the missing intermediate details. [2]

## Sound
Chemical Burn starts from the harsh, gated, octave-rich world of Superfuzz-style circuitry but pushes it toward a more unstable and aggressive modern fuzz voice. The later v3 control expansion makes it easier to move from splattery, broken-up textures into stronger, more focused fuzz and boosted sounds without turning the pedal into a generic distortion box. [1][2]
