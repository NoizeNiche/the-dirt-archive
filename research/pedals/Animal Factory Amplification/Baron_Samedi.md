# Animal Factory Amplification — Baron Samedi

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Baron Samedi
- **Builder:** Animal Factory Amplification
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Introduction:** mid-2010s
- **Identity:** High-gain fuzz derived from the Jordan Bosstone concept, expanded with an unusual passive tone network, octave/lower-frequency shaping, and gating controls.
- **Important distinction:** The pedal is a guitar/bass effect. The later **Baron Samedi II** is a separate Eurorack module and is not a pedal revision.

## What this pedal is
Animal Factory describes Baron Samedi as a Jordan Bosstone-derived fuzz that deliberately moves away from a literal recreation. The manufacturer states that added gain and a strange tone control increase aggression, fullness, and bite, with the pedal capable of conventional vintage-fuzz sounds as well as synth-like textures with faux-portamento-style decay. [1] Contemporary coverage describes the design as a more aggressive fuzz aimed at Bosstone-like character but with a substantially different control set. [3]

## Versions and factory modifications
### Original Baron Samedi pedal
The production pedal is built around five named controls:
- **Voodoo:** output volume.
- **Rum:** fuzz/gain amount.
- **Life:** unusual passive notch-filter tone control.
- **Death:** gating/bit-reduction-style control.
- **Burial:** lower-frequency/octave shaping control. [1][3]

### Later development
- Animal Factory announced work on a **Baron Samedi v2** during the development of its next generation of products, describing additional CV-controlled feedback and envelope sensitivity as planned features. [4]
- Current official store material lists the original **Baron Samedi Pedal as discontinued**. [1]
- The current **Baron Samedi II** is a 12HP Eurorack module with a separate architecture and power scheme, so it remains outside this pedal's version history. [2]

## Version changes
A numbered production revision with a confirmed final feature set was not established from the reviewed sources. The manufacturer documented a v2 development concept, but its final production identity remains insufficiently established to create a separate canonical pedal record. [4]

## Circuit lineage
- **Derived from:** Jordan Bosstone.
- The manufacturer and independent coverage agree on the Bosstone relationship while emphasizing that Baron Samedi is substantially modified rather than a one-to-one clone. [1][3]
- Supporting circuit documentation identifies a Bosstone-derived front section followed by a recovery/output stage after the Life tone network. This is retained as supporting evidence; undocumented component materials are not inferred from it. [5]

## Controls and behavior
### Voodoo
Overall output level. [1][3]

### Rum
Main fuzz/gain control, with more gain than the Bosstone starting point. [1][3]

### Life
A distinctive passive notch-filter tone control. Contemporary coverage identifies it as a mid-scooping network whose extremes also cut treble. [3]

### Death
Gate/texture control. Turning it up can reduce bass and progressively produce gated, AM-radio/bit-reduced textures; the exact behavior depends on the other controls. [3]

### Burial
Lower-frequency shaping control that can emphasize the lower-octave region and alter decay character. [3]

## Sound
The manufacturer describes Baron Samedi as an idiosyncratic fuzz capable of vintage-fuzz sounds, aggressive saturated tones, and synth-like textures. Independent coverage emphasizes short, bit-crushed tones and strong interaction among the controls. [1][3]
