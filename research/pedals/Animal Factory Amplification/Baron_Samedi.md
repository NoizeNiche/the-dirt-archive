# Animal Factory Amplification — Baron Samedi

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Baron Samedi
- **Builder:** Animal Factory Amplification
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Introduction:** early 2010s
- **Identity:** One pedal parent with a five-control high-gain fuzz architecture. Do not confuse it with Animal Factory's Eurorack **Baron Samedi II** module.

## What this pedal is
Baron Samedi pays homage to the Jordan Bosstone but changes the concept substantially, with more gain and a much stranger response range. Animal Factory describes it as capable of fuzz, gated/synth-like textures and unstable, highly interactive sounds controlled by Voodoo, Rum, Life, Death and Burial. [1][2]

## Sound
Baron Samedi is an intentionally unruly fuzz that moves from dense, saturated distortion into gated, synth-like and unstable textures as the five controls interact. Its Bosstone inspiration provides the historical starting point, but the builder deliberately pushes it toward stranger harmonic and envelope behavior rather than recreating the vintage circuit literally. [1][2]
