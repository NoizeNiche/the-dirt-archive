# Animal Factory Amplification — Pit Viper

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Pit Viper
- **Builder:** Animal Factory Amplification
- **Catalog types:** Overdrive / Distortion
- **Introduction:** 2015
- **Identity:** One parent containing the original 2015 production and the builder-designated 2019 revision / v2.

## What this pedal is
Pit Viper is a solid-state overdrive derived from the FLAIL channel of the first Ozymandias concept. The original pedal was designed to go from gritty boost territory to full amp-like roar, while the later revision expanded the tonal range without changing the core circuit identity. [1][2]

## Colorways
- Original 2015 examples used the earlier graphic/finish treatment.
- The builder's **2019 revision** introduced a laser-etched black finish with a new graphic by Aniruddh Mehta.
- The finish change alone is not the reason for the version designation: the 2019 revision also added functional control changes. [2]

## Versions

### Pit Viper — original 2015 version
- Gain / Tone / Volume
- earlier control labels and voicing
- solid-state overdrive
- hard/soft clipping architecture
- true bypass / 9V operation [1][2]

### Pit Viper v2 / 2019 revision
**Builder-designated revision**

Documented changes:
- **MODE** switch changes the minimum gain range, extending clean/low-gain use.
- **MIDS** switch expands the frequency response from the original low-cut behavior toward full-range/deep-bass response.
- control labels were made more functional/clear.
- laser-etched black finish and new graphic.
- HARD/SOFT switches provide different diode clipping/compression textures. [2][3]

## Factory modifications
- No separate factory MOD beyond the documented v2 revision was verified.
- No post-sale modifications are included.

## Version changes
Animal Factory explicitly says the 2019 revision kept the base circuit and tone of the original while adding the MODE and MIDS switches to extend its range. This is a textbook PRP version: meaningful functional change plus a builder-designated revision. [2][3]

## Sound
Pit Viper starts as a gritty, responsive boost and can become a raw, high-gain overdrive with a strong amp-like character. The 2019 MODE and MIDS controls make the same core circuit more usable across guitar, bass and electronic sources by changing its gain floor and low-frequency response, while the HARD/SOFT controls alter clipping feel and compression. [2][3]
