## What this pedal is
The BOSS HM-2 Heavy Metal is the original compact distortion pedal produced from 1983 to 1991. It was designed to reproduce the character of early-1980s British stack amplifiers and later became strongly associated with Swedish death metal. The later HM-2W Waza Craft is a separate reissue and should not be used as the description for the original production model.

## Sound
The original HM-2 is known for its highly aggressive distortion and focused midrange. BOSS describes the later HM-2W as reproducing the original's distinctive "chainsaw" character, the sound that became closely associated with Swedish death metal. The original model was discontinued after its 1983–1991 production run.
