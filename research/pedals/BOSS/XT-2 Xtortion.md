## Sound
Designed for rock, metal, grunge, and other high-intensity styles, the XT-2 delivers a tight, punchy distortion with clear articulation and minimal noise.
The pedal responds well to both single-coil and humbucking pickups and is optimized for riff-heavy playing, with strong note definition even under heavy gain.
