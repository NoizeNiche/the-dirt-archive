# BOSS - FZ-2 Hyper Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** BOSS
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz

## What this pedal is
The BOSS FZ-2 Hyper Fuzz is a three-mode fuzz/boost pedal from the 1990s. Its architecture provides two distinct fuzz voices plus a gain-boost mode.

## Deep research verification

### Controls and modes
The documented interface includes:
- **LEVEL**
- **TONE**
- **FUZZ**
- Three-position mode selection for the two fuzz characters and the **GAIN BOOST** mode.

The two fuzz modes provide deliberately different characters, while Gain Boost uses the pedal as a high-output drive/boost stage.

### Circuit and sound
BOSS describes the FZ-2 as using a discrete gain architecture. The pedal can move from heavy, saturated fuzz toward a more aggressive boosted/distorted response, with the two fuzz positions providing contrasting textures.

### History
The FZ-2 is a distinct BOSS model and is kept separate from the later FZ-3. The archive does not infer a numbered factory revision from the surviving documentation.

### Component evidence
Exact production transistor and clipping-diode part numbers are not established in the reviewed sources.
