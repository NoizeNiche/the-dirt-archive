# Coffee Shop Pedals — Portafilter Dual Overdrive

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Portafilter Dual Overdrive
- **Builder:** Coffee Shop Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Coffee Shop Pedals Portafilter, a dual programmable analog overdrive with Tube Screamer, Bluesbreaker, and Klon-style voices on each side.

## What this pedal is
Coffee Shop Pedals describes Portafilter as two fully customizable overdrive channels. Each side can select among **TS, BB, and KC/Klon-style** circuit voices, independent EQ settings, and clipping options. [1][2]

## Versions and factory options
Each side provides:
- Bass.
- Treble.
- Volume.
- Gain.
- Style: TS / BB / KC.
- Mid: Flat / Scoop / Boost.
- Clip: Soft / Hard.

Additional controls/functions:
- Channel order switch for OD1 → OD2 or OD2 → OD1.
- Independent OD1 and OD2 footswitches.
- Preset storage for six sounds onboard.
- Up to 20 presets via MIDI.
- Full MIDI control of knobs, presets, and switches.
- Hard clipping option for additional saturation, sustain, and compression. [1][2]

## Sound
Each channel can be configured as a Tube Screamer-style mid-focused drive, an open Bluesbreaker-style overdrive, or a transparent Klon-style voice. The Mid control offers flat, scooped, or boosted response, while the Order switch changes how the two channels interact when stacked. [1][2]

## Specifications
- Analog signal path.
- True bypass.
- 9V DC.
- MIDI support.
- Six onboard presets, expandable to 20 via MIDI. [2][3]
