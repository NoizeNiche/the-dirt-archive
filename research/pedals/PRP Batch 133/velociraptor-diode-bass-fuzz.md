# Bulinski Effect Pedals — Velociraptor Diode Bass Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Bulinski Effect Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Bass-oriented fuzz with switchable diode character.

## Controls and versions
The current Bulinski product page describes the pedal as a simple design with a **Volume** control and a diode toggle selecting modern versus vintage glass-diode tones. A separate Made For Bass database entry distinguishes **Version 1 and Version 2**, describing the 2023 update as adding **Fuzz, Tone, and Volume** controls while retaining the germanium/silicon clipping choice.

## Circuit / clipping
- Vintage germanium and modern silicon clipping diodes are documented.
- The manufacturer describes the pedal as 100% hand-wired with no printed circuit board on the current product page.
- No more specific circuit topology is asserted here because the reviewed sources do not establish one consistently across versions.

## Power and construction
- 9V DC, 2.1 mm center-negative, or 9V battery.
- Current manufacturer page identifies hand-wired construction in Orlando, Florida, USA.

## Research limitation
The available sources describe multiple production versions with different control layouts. The archive therefore preserves the model identity without collapsing version-specific details into one universal specification.
