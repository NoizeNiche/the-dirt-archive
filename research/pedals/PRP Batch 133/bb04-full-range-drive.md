# Buzzing Bugs Audio Devices — BB04 Full Range Drive

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Buzzing Bugs Audio Devices
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Saturated, non-transparent overdrive with four op-amp gain stages and a switchable BODY EQ range.

## Controls
- Gain
- Tone
- Body
- Volume

The BODY control changes the EQ parameter, moving between thinner and thicker responses. Buzzing Bugs describes the interaction of GAIN, TONE, and BODY as covering tones from edge-of-breakup through tube-like distortion.

## Circuit and construction
- Four op-amp gain stages.
- Soft clipping.
- Diecast enclosure.
- True bypass.
- Top-mounted jacks.

## Power
- 9V DC, center-negative.
