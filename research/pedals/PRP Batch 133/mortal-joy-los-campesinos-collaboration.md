# Buzzing Bugs Audio Devices — Mortal Joy - Los Campesinos! Collaboration

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Buzzing Bugs Audio Devices
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Collaborative dirt and modulation pedal created with Los Campesinos! around the sounds of the album *All Hell*.

## Signal sections
- DRIVE / dirt section
- TAPE modulation section
- Fixed-band CUT filter

The DRIVE side is a transistor overdrive with low- and high-gain options. The TAPE section combines compression and warped vibrato, with two LFOs producing rhythmic or randomized swells and vibrato.

## Controls
- Gain
- Tone
- Cut
- Sat
- Sens
- Rate/Depth controls for the two LFOs

## Switching and construction
- Three main footswitch functions engage dirt, tape-style processing, and the narrow band-pass filter.
- True bypass.
- Diecast enclosure.
- Top-mounted jacks.

## Power
- 9V DC center-negative.
