# Buzzing Bugs Audio Devices — Bolster

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Buzzing Bugs Audio Devices
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Two-channel fuzz combining a silicon-transistor main channel with a separate op-amp fuzz channel.

## Controls
- Volts
- Shift
- Gain
- Tone
- Master
- More switch

The main channel uses VOLTS to starve or over-supply the transistor stage, SHIFT to alter the input-stage fullness, GAIN for drive level, TONE for a modified Muff-style EQ response, and MASTER for output. MORE engages the second fuzz channel.

## Circuit and construction
- Main channel: silicon-transistor high-gain fuzz.
- MORE channel: one-knob op-amp fuzz.
- Hand-built.
- True bypass.
- Top-mounted jacks.

## Power
- 9V center-negative DC.

## Sound
The documented range runs from Tone Bender-style drive into synth-like, gated buzzsaw textures, with the second channel adding a separate high-sustain fuzz voice.

## What this pedal is

Pricing & Availability The Bolster Giant Fuzz is available now, priced at £179 including VAT.

### Verified sound evidence
Said to pay homage to the myths and legends of the company’s home county of Cornwall, the new effect has been named after a folklore giant that lived near the cliffs of Chapel Porth and terrorised the people of the nearby village — an appropriate title for a fuzz pedal!
Its main channel employs a silicon transistor circuit, and includes a Volts control that makes it possible to over- or under-supply the circuitry, allowing it to cover everything from Tone Bender-style sounds to “synthy buzzsaws”.
Plenty of tone-shaping control is available, with a Shift dial interacting with the input circuitry and a Tone control providing classic Big Muff-style EQ.
