# Buzzing Bugs Audio Devices — Bolster

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Buzzing Bugs Audio Devices
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Two-channel fuzz combining a silicon-transistor main channel with a separate op-amp fuzz channel.

## Controls
- Volts
- Shift
- Gain
- Tone
- Master
- More switch

The main channel uses VOLTS to starve or over-supply the transistor stage, SHIFT to alter the input-stage fullness, GAIN for drive level, TONE for a modified Muff-style EQ response, and MASTER for output. MORE engages the second fuzz channel.

## Circuit and construction
- Main channel: silicon-transistor high-gain fuzz.
- MORE channel: one-knob op-amp fuzz.
- Hand-built.
- True bypass.
- Top-mounted jacks.

## Power
- 9V center-negative DC.

## Sound
The documented range runs from Tone Bender-style drive into synth-like, gated buzzsaw textures, with the second channel adding a separate high-sustain fuzz voice.
