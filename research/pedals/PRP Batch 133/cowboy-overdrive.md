# Byron Amplification — Cowboy Overdrive

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Byron Amplification
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Mid-hump overdrive with clean blend and a three-position Phat switch.

## Controls and switching
- Volume
- Gain
- Blend
- Tone
- Three-position Phat switch

The manufacturer describes the center switch position as stock, with the other positions progressively adding bass and gain. The BLEND control restores dry guitar signal for attack and definition.

## Clipping
Byron's description documents LED and silicon diode clipping for higher gain, with less compression and greater touch sensitivity than the stock circuit being modified.

## Sound
The maker describes the pedal as covering clean boost through fat crunch and as a classic mid-hump drive.

## Construction
- Handmade in Columbia, Missouri, USA.
- Small-form-factor enclosure.
