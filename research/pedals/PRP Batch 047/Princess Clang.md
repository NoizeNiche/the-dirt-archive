# Alexander Pedals — Princess Clang Royal Overdrive

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Alexander Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive
- **Identity:** Limited 2015 British-voiced overdrive derived from an earlier Alexander design.

## What this pedal is
Princess Clang is a tweaked version of an Alexander overdrive dating to 2002. The 2015 version adds a passive two-band Bass/Treble stack and an active midrange Contour control, while increasing gain and output so it can cover clean boost, overdrive, and distortion territory. [1][2]

## Colorways
- Pink finish is documented for the production Princess Clang. [3]
- The production run was a limited edition, with contemporary documentation giving 30 or 31 units depending on the source. [1][2]

## Versions and factory options
- Level
- Drive
- Bass
- Treble
- Contour
- true-bypass latching relay / soft-touch switching
- 9-18V operation; no battery [1][2]

## Version changes
The Princess Clang is itself a revision of the 2002 Alexander design, replacing the original single Tone control and Brilliance switch with a two-band tone stack and active Contour control while increasing gain and output. [1]

## Sound
Princess Clang is voiced around British-style overdrive but stretches from clean boost through heavier overdrive and distortion. The Contour control shifts the midrange independently of the passive Bass/Treble stack, giving the pedal a broad range of British-flavored EQ shapes. [1][2]
