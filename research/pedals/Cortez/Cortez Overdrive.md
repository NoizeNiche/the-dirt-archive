# Cortez - Cortez Overdrive

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Cortez
- **Catalog identity:** Cortez Overdrive
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Exact Cortez Overdrive family of early Japanese analog pedals.

## What this pedal is
Cortez Overdrive is an early Japanese analog overdrive that appears in more than one physical/control configuration. Effects Database relates a one-knob Cortez version to the Bias overdrive family and a two-knob version to the Coron DC-851 family.

## Versions and factory configurations
- **One-knob version:** documented as an uncommon 1980s Japanese Cortez Overdrive.
- **Two-knob version:** documented by Effects Database as a related version with the Coron DC-851 family.
- Treat the one- and two-knob layouts as distinct physical configurations rather than assuming they are the same revision.

## Controls
### One-knob version
- A single overdrive control is documented by the surviving Reverb example; the exact panel name is not reliably established by the checked sources.

### Two-knob version
- Effects Database documents the related configuration with **Level** and **Drive** controls.

## Circuit and power
- Reverb identifies the one-knob example as an **analog** pedal made in Japan in the 1980s.

## Related OEM lineage
Effects Database places Cortez entries among early Coron/Bias-related Japanese overdrive families. Related OEM models should remain separate catalog identities even where the underlying family relationship is documented.
