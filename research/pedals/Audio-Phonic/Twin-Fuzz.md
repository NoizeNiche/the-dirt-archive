## PRP identity

- **Identity:** Historical Audio-Phonic fuzz pedal documented through 1970s Argentine advertising and later archival cataloging.

## What this pedal is
Effects Database preserves Audio-Phonic Twin-Fuzz advertisements from Pelo issue 40 in 1973 and lists the model as a fuzz pedal. Tone Machines Blog later assessed the photographed circuit as appearing to be a Japanese Super-Fuzz-type design, possibly related to the Royal Fuzz family, but that circuit identification remains an external assessment rather than verified factory documentation.
