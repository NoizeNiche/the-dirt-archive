## What this pedal is
The project's earlier Chocolate Electronics Phase One inventory describes Chocolate Crunch as a 2026 handmade overdrive with **Rich/Crispy character switching** and selectable **mid/bottom voicing**. The same inventory describes it as dynamically responsive to playing and the guitar's volume control. [1]
