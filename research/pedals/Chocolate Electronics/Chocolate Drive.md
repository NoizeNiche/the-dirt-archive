# Chocolate Electronics — Chocolate Drive

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Chocolate Drive
- **Builder:** Chocolate Electronics
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Chocolate Electronics Chocolate Drive, an original handmade overdrive with internally boosted operating voltage and broad Treble/Bass shaping.

## What this pedal is
The project's earlier Chocolate Electronics inventory describes Chocolate Drive as an original handmade overdrive from the builder's early catalog. It specifically notes **internally boosted operating voltage** and a wide **Treble/Bass** range. [1]

## Version changes
The project history treats Chocolate Drive as an early catalog model and distinguishes it from Chocolate Crunch, Melty Overdrive, and Fuzzy Overdrive in the later 2026 lineup. [1][2]
