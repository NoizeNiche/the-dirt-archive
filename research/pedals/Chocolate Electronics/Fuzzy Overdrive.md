# Chocolate Electronics — Fuzzy Overdrive

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** Fuzzy Overdrive

- **Identity:** Chocolate Electronics Fuzzy Overdrive, a hybrid dirt pedal derived from a Ram's Head-style fuzz development with normal and upper-octave modes.

## What this pedal is

The project's earlier Chocolate Electronics inventory describes Fuzzy Overdrive as a 2026 hybrid dirt pedal derived from a **Ram's Head-style fuzz** development. It documents both **normal** and **upper-octave** modes and a range from grainy overdrive to heavy fuzz. [1]

## Sound

The Fuzzy Overdrive is designed to span grainy low-gain dirt through heavier fuzz, with a dedicated upper-octave mode extending the harmonic range. The documented Ram's Head lineage is treated as design ancestry, not proof of a part-for-part clone. [1]
