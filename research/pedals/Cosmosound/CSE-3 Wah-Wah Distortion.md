# Cosmosound - CSE-3 Wah-Wah Distortion

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Cosmosound
- **Catalog identity:** CSE-3 Wah-Wah Distortion
- **Catalog type:** Wah / Distortion
- **Identity:** Exact Cosmosound CSE-3 Wah-Wah Distortion.

## What this pedal is
CSE-3 is a vintage treadle-format combination wah and distortion pedal. Effects Database describes the wah as a smooth, vocal-like sweep and the distortion as a mid-focused range running from overdrive toward fuzz.

## Controls and switching
- **Wah treadle:** controls the filter sweep.
- Separate switching allows the **wah** and **distortion** sections to be used individually or together.
- The checked exact-model page does not provide a trustworthy full panel control-value transcription.

## Versions and factory options
Effects Database groups the CSE-3 with the **Silversound SE-20 Whau-Whau Distortion** and a **Vox by EF-EL Wah-Wah Distortion** as metal-housed members of the same Italian family. This is a documented family relationship, not permission to merge the branded catalog entries.

## Circuit and components
- The checked sources do not establish exact transistor or diode part numbers for the Cosmosound-branded CSE-3.
- A Freestompboxes technical discussion of the closely related Vox by EF-EL unit reports MPS5172 transistors in its fuzz section and BC209C transistors in its wah section, but this is not treated as a confirmed Cosmosound CSE-3 bill of materials.

## Sound
The exact Cosmosound description emphasizes a smooth, vocal wah sweep and a mid-focused distortion that reaches fuzz-like territory. The two sections can be used separately or together.

## Construction and provenance
- Vintage Italian manufacture.
- Metal treadle enclosure.
- The wider Cosmosound/Silversound/Goldsound family is documented as part of Sandro Marchetti's Italian effects work.
