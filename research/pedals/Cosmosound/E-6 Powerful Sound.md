# Cosmosound - E-6 Powerful Sound

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Cosmosound
- **Catalog identity:** E-6 Powerful Sound
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Fuzz
- **Identity:** Exact Cosmosound E-6 Powerful Sound.

## What this pedal is
E-6 Powerful Sound is a Cosmosound E-series distortion pedal in the distinctive extruded-aluminium Fender-Blender-inspired enclosure. Effects Database describes it as saturated, harmonically rich and sustained, while the model is also grouped with the wider Italian E-series family.

## Controls
The exact model is documented as having a simple control set for gain and level, but the checked source does not preserve a reliable panel-label transcription. The archive therefore records the function without inventing names or potentiometer values.

## Versions and family relationships
Effects Database relates E-6 directly to **Silversound SE/60** and to Cosmosound E-7/E-8. It also lists related Italian-branded distortion/fuzz models. These relationships are retained as lineage rather than merged records.

## Version history
Sandro Marchetti's interview states that in **1976** the group adopted an extruded-aluminium Fender Blender-style case and created the E-6 Powerful Sound and E-8 Wild Sound distortions; E-7 was the distortion-with-tremolo variant.

## Sound
E-6 is described as saturated, harmonically rich distortion with strong sustain, intended for thick rhythm and lead work. Effects Database classifies the surviving example under distortion and octave-fuzz-related metadata.

## Construction and provenance
- Vintage Italian production.
- Extruded aluminium E-series enclosure inspired by the Fender Blender form.
