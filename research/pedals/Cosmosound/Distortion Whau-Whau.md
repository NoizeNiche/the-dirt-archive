## What this pedal is
The Cosmosound Distortion Whau-Whau is a vintage Italian treadle pedal combining a distortion/fuzz circuit with a wah filter. Effects Database places the Cosmosound-branded unit in the plastic-housed Whau-Whau family.

## Versions and family relationships
Effects Database groups this exact Cosmosound model with Goldsound, G.I.S., Gun, Mac, Meazzi and other related branded Whau-Whau units. These are documented family relationships and should remain separate catalog identities.

## Circuit and components
- No exact Cosmosound-branded transistor or diode part numbers were established from the checked sources.
- Related Italian family documentation describes four-transistor fuzz circuits in early examples, often with BC270 germanium devices, with later silicon variants; this is treated as family-level context rather than a confirmed CSE-branded parts list.

## Sound
The family is associated with a vocal, mid-forward wah character paired with gritty fuzz/distortion. The archive keeps that as family context and does not invent a narrower Cosmosound-specific voicing claim than the available evidence supports.
