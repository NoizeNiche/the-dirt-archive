## What this pedal is
E-7 is the Cosmosound E-series combination of distortion and tremolo. Effects Database describes the distortion section as similar to E-6 Powerful Sound and the tremolo as an onboard modulation section for dynamic, pulsating drive tones.

## Controls and operation
The exact-model source documents separate adjustment of:
- **Tremolo speed**
- **Distortion gain**

The checked source does not provide enough evidence for a complete additional control/value transcription.

## Versions and family relationships
Effects Database relates E-7 to the E-6 Powerful Sound and E-8 Wild Sound, plus sibling Italian-branded distortion models.

## Version history
Sandro Marchetti's interview identifies E-7 as the **distortion-with-tremolo** member of the 1976-era E-series, alongside E-6 Powerful Sound and E-8 Wild Sound. The tremolo effect was also made as a separate E-5 Shaking Sound.

## Sound
The documented design combines E-6-like distortion with tremolo, yielding a pulsating drive texture whose movement can be varied with the tremolo-speed control.

## Construction and provenance
- Vintage Italian E-series construction.
- Distinctive Fender-Blender-inspired extruded-aluminium housing.
- Associated with Sandro Marchetti's Cosmosound/Silversound/Goldsound effects work.
