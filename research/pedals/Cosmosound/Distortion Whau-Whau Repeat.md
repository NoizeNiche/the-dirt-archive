# Cosmosound - Distortion Whau-Whau Repeat

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Cosmosound
- **Catalog identity:** Distortion Whau-Whau Repeat
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz / Wah / Tremolo
- **Identity:** Exact Cosmosound Distortion Whau-Whau Repeat.

## What this pedal is
This Cosmosound model is the expanded Whau-Whau design documented as a treadle fuzz-wah with an LFO-controlled repeat/tremolo function. Effects Database also preserves a schematic for the exact model.

## Controls and operation
The checked exact-model page establishes:
- foot-controlled treadle operation
- fuzz/distortion
- wah/filter
- LFO-controlled tremolo/repeat behavior

A complete trustworthy front-panel control transcription was not established, so individual knob labels are not guessed.

## Versions and family relationships
Effects Database groups the exact model with the **Silversound SE/40 Distortion Whau-Whau Repeat** and **Goldsound Distortion Whau-Whau Repeat**. These are related branded versions, not duplicate catalog records.

## Circuit and components
- An exact-model schematic is documented by Effects Database.
- The schematic and a related Freestompboxes analysis show that this family uses a transistor fuzz section, but no exact branded Cosmosound transistor inventory is asserted here.
- Exact diode type: **not established**.

## Sound
The combined architecture produces a vintage fuzz/distortion voice through a wah filter, with the added low-frequency modulation producing the repeating/tremolo movement associated with the model's extended name.
