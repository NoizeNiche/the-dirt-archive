# Cosmosound - E-8 Wild Sound

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Cosmosound
- **Catalog identity:** E-8 Wild Sound
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz / Distortion
- **Identity:** Exact Cosmosound E-8 Wild Sound.

## What this pedal is
E-8 Wild Sound is the higher-gain Cosmosound E-series distortion/fuzz model. Effects Database describes it as more aggressive and brighter than E-6 Powerful Sound, aimed at cutting lead sounds and heavily driven rhythm work.

## Circuit and components
- **Transistors:** 2 x **BC209C**, as documented on the exact-model Effects Database entry.
- Exact diode type: **not established**.

## Controls
The exact-model source does not preserve a reliable complete panel-label transcription, so control names and values are not guessed.

## Versions and family relationships
Effects Database relates E-8 to E-6 Powerful Sound and E-7 Fearfully-Sound and to several sibling Italian distortion/fuzz products. Sandro Marchetti's interview identifies E-8 as one of the distortion models developed in the 1976 Fender-Blender-style E-series.

## Version history
The 1976 interview places E-8 alongside E-6 as an E-series distortion created after adoption of the extruded-aluminium Fender Blender-style enclosure.

## Sound
Compared with E-6, E-8 is documented as more aggressive and brighter, with emphasis on cutting lead tones and heavily driven rhythm sounds.

## Construction and provenance
- Vintage Italian production.
- E-series extruded-aluminium enclosure.
- Part of the Cosmosound/Silversound/Goldsound ecosystem.
