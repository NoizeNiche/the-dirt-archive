# Build Your Own Clone — Orange Distortion

## PRP identity

- **Builder:** Build Your Own Clone
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** Recreation of the original 1980s BOSS DS-1 circuit using the Toshiba TA7136P architecture.

## What this pedal is

The BYOC Orange Distortion is documented as a recreation of the original DS-1 circuit rather than the later production circuit that changed to dual op amps.

## Controls and modifications

- Adds a Pre-Gain control to regulate the transistor boost feeding the distortion stage.
- Includes spaces and parts for a Big Muff-style modification to the input boost stage.
- Includes parts for popular DS-1 modifications involving gain, high-frequency response, and clipping.
- True mechanical bypass.

## Active devices

- Genuine N.O.S. Toshiba TA7136P is documented.
- Three 2N5088 transistors are listed in the BYOC parts checklist.

## Diodes

The BYOC parts checklist documents 1N914/1N4148 diodes, 1N4001 diodes, and red LEDs for the kit's clipping/modification options.

## Sound

The archive records the original-DS-1 circuit identity and the documented modification range without importing later DS-1 circuit revisions.
