# Build Your Own Clone — Shredder

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Build Your Own Clone
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive
- **Identity:** Compact Marshall ShredMaster-family kit.

## What this pedal is
The BYOC Shredder is documented as a compact recreation of the Marshall ShredMaster circuit, voiced for high-gain distortion while retaining enough dynamics for overdrive use.

## Controls
- Volume
- Gain
- Bass
- Treble
- Contour

## Construction
- Compact enclosure, approximately half the size of the original pedal.
- True bypass.

## Sound
The documented design targets the 1980s Marshall high-gain distortion character while allowing lower-gain overdrive operation.

No numbered factory revision is established in the reviewed source.
