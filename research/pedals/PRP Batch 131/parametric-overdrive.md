# Build Your Own Clone — Parametric Overdrive

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Build Your Own Clone
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Clone of the Pearl OD-05 with an expanded parametric tone section.

## What this pedal is
The BYOC Parametric Overdrive is documented as an exact clone of the Pearl OD-05, with true bypass and a Q modification for the parametric equalizer.

## Controls and options
- Parametric tone control covering approximately 100 Hz to 4 kHz.
- Three-way Q switch: narrow, original-spec medium, or wide.
- Extra PCB provisions for alternate overdrive circuits and common clipping/gain modifications.

## Version changes
The documented BYOC version preserves the OD-05 identity while adding the Q modification and optional circuit modification points.

## Sound
The source emphasizes low-frequency retention and a transparent overdrive response with broad frequency control.
