# Build Your Own Clone — Li'l Modified Overdrive

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Build Your Own Clone
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Miniature TS-808-based overdrive kit.

## What this pedal is
The Li'l Modified Overdrive is a compact BYOC version of the TS-808 Tube Screamer with documented modifications to input impedance, gain, clipping, bass response, treble response, and the drive control.

## Controls and construction
- Drive, tone, and volume controls are documented.
- All through-hole components.
- Panel-mounted hardware.
- True bypass.
- Limited enclosure space requires knobs with skirts no larger than 16 mm.

## Version changes
The documented design is specifically the modified Li'l version rather than a plain TS-808 clone.

## Active devices
The reviewed source identifies the TS-808 circuit family but does not establish a complete component list in the catalog entry.

## Sound
The documented modifications provide more gain, asymmetrical clipping, more low and high frequency content, and a cleaner low-drive range suitable for boost use.
