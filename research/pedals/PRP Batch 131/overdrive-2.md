# Build Your Own Clone — Overdrive 2

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Build Your Own Clone
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Expanded Tube Screamer-based overdrive with an independent boost channel.

## What this pedal is
The BYOC Overdrive 2 combines a Tube Screamer-derived overdrive section with a second independent boost channel in a compact 125B enclosure.

## Controls and options
- Multiple Tube Screamer modifications are documented.
- Internal trimmers allow precise adjustment of the documented More and Less modifications.
- Second boost can be configured as silicon or MOSFET.
- Three-way clipping switch.
- Three-way EQ switch.
- Separate boost and bypass footswitches.
- True bypass.

## Active devices
- Socketed Burr Brown OPA2134 and JRC4558D op amp options are documented.
- A MOSFET conversion kit is also documented as an optional modification.

## Sound
The design covers stock-style Tube Screamer operation plus broader gain, clipping, EQ, and boost configurations.
