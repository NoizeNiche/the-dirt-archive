# Build Your Own Clone — Silver Pony

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Build Your Own Clone
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Silver-era Klon Centaur-based overdrive kit.

## What this pedal is
The BYOC Silver Pony is documented as being based on the silver-era Klon Centaur.

## Bypass
Unlike BYOC's usual preference for true bypass on cloned circuits, the Silver Pony retains the original-style pop-free buffered bypass system.

## Versions
The reviewed source identifies the silver-era Centaur basis. No numbered BYOC revision is established in the reviewed source.

## Sound
The archive records the Klon Centaur circuit family as the design reference without adding unsupported component or voicing claims.
