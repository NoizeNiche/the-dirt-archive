# Build Your Own Clone — Li'l Yellow OD

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Build Your Own Clone
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Compact clone of the first Made-in-Japan BOSS OD-1 circuit.

## What this pedal is
Li'l Yellow OD reproduces the earliest Made-in-Japan BOSS OD-1 circuit in a much smaller enclosure. The documented design uses the single-quad-op-amp version rather than the later dual-op-amp/transistor-buffer arrangement.

## Construction
- All through-hole components.
- Full-sized, panel-mounted hardware.
- Compact enclosure.
- Knobs with skirts larger than 16 mm are not suitable for the limited enclosure space.

## Active device
- MC3403 is documented for the signal circuit.
- BYOC documentation also identifies RC3404 as an alternative compatible chip.

## Clipping
The documented kit uses three 1N4148 diodes.

## Sound
The archive treats the pedal as the early OD-1 circuit rather than attributing later OD-1 revisions to it.
