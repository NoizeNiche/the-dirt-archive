## Sound

Signal-to-noise ratio is measured at ≥98.2 dB (A-weighted, 1 kHz, unity gain), and THD+N remains below 0.012% up to +12 dBu input level — verified using Audio Precision APx555 test suite.
Lunastone’s proprietary 'SilentSwitch' algorithm monitors input signal amplitude and only triggers relay actuation when signal level drops below −42 dBFS for ≥12 ms, eliminating audible pop even during high-gain solo passages.
The unit ships pre-loaded with 128 user-programmable patches (organized into 8 banks of 16), each storing complete state data: channel selection, gain (0–100%), tone (−12 dB to +12 dB shelving EQ), level (−20 dB to +15 dB), and all active modulation parameters.
