## What this pedal is
The Sun Beam is documented as a transparent/light overdrive and booster with Volume, Tone, Dense, and Drive controls. Dense thickens and compresses the response and adds low end, while Drive moves from light transparent gain to smoother, denser overdrive. [1][2]

## Sound
Sun Beam is designed to remain relatively transparent at lower Drive settings and become thicker and denser as Drive and Dense rise. Builder and historical descriptions position it as a flexible booster/overdrive covering light, bright gain through smoother, more compressed drive textures. [1][2]
