# Craftros Pedals - Musica Rev.D

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Craftros Pedals
- **Catalog identity:** Musica Rev.D
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Exact CRAFTROS musica Rev.D.

## What this pedal is
musica Rev.D is the revised CRAFTROS overdrive developed from the earlier musica platform with input from several guitarists. CRAFTROS describes the Rev.D as both a usable booster and a standalone overdrive, with a wider-range drive response than the earlier mid-focused Rev.B.

## Controls
- **GAIN:** controls the amount of overdrive.
- **TREBLE:** adjusts the high-frequency balance.
- **VOLUME:** output level.

## Switching and power
- True bypass.
- 9V power from a 006P battery or AC adapter.
- Documented adapter specification: 9V DC, 2.1 mm, center negative.
- Current consumption: approximately 7 mA.
- Exact enclosure dimensions vary slightly among retailer specifications, but the documented format is a compact hand-built aluminum enclosure.

## Versions and factory options
- **musica Rev.B:** documented earlier version and direct predecessor.
- **musica Rev.D:** redesigned/tuned successor.
- The archive keeps Rev.B and Rev.D as distinct catalog identities because the builder explicitly documents a different tuning and drive response.

## Version changes
Compared with the earlier mid-emphasized musica, Rev.D shifts the tonal center downward toward the low and low-mid ranges, and changes both the quantity and character of distortion. CRAFTROS describes the result as more three-dimensional and wide-range. Independent review coverage confirms that the two revisions have clearly different output character.

## Sound
The Rev.D retains the booster-to-overdrive versatility of the musica concept but emphasizes low and low-mid response rather than the stronger mid focus of the earlier version. Contemporary reviews describe it as touch-responsive, articulate and capable of clean boost through medium-gain overdrive.

## Construction and provenance

- CRAFTROS emphasizes selected audio-grade parts and design focused on both sound quality and usability.
