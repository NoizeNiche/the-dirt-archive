# Chellee Guitars — Odie - Classic Overdrive

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Odie - Classic Overdrive
- **Builder:** Chellee Guitars
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Chellee Odie Classic Overdrive V2, a symmetrical-clipping TS808-based overdrive with silicon, LED, and clean-boost modes.

## What this pedal is
Chellee describes the Odie Classic as a TS808-based overdrive with expanded voicing, designed for more vintage-inspired compression and a rounder attack than the Odie Modified. [1]

## Colorways
No complete factory colorway sequence was established.

## Versions and factory options
Documented features:
- Three drive textures: silicon diode clipping, LED clipping, clean boost.
- EQ voicing shift.
- True bypass / relay soft-switch in V2.
- 9-18V operation. [1][2]

## Version changes
Chellee explicitly identifies **Odie Classic V2** as the next generation and states that V2 uses relay soft-switch bypass plus a dimmer/diffused LED indicator. [1]

## Transistor
No discrete transistor stage is documented. Premier Guitar identifies a **Texas Instruments 4558** op-amp in the Odie Classic circuit. [3]

## Diode
The pedal provides selectable **silicon diode**, **LED**, and clean-boost texture modes. Exact diode part numbers were not established. [1][3]

## Sound
Chellee positions the Classic as the more vintage-feeling Odie flavor, with more compression and a rounder attack. The switching options add cleaner boost and broader EQ responses while retaining the TS808-derived foundation. [1][3]
