# Chellee Guitars — Odie - Overdrive

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Odie - Overdrive
- **Builder:** Chellee Guitars
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Chellee Odie Overdrive, the 2014 redesign of the earlier Odie/ Odelya family, using an RC4558P dual op-amp and three drive-texture settings.

## What this pedal is
Chellee and Premier Guitar document the Odie Overdrive as a handmade USA-built Tube Screamer-style overdrive with additional clipping and EQ-voicing options. [1][2]

## Colorways
No complete factory colorway sequence was established.

## Versions and factory options
The original Odie uses:
- Drive
- Level
- Tone
- Texture switch: asymmetrical diode / MOSFET / clean boost
- Voicing switch: flat / mid bump / low-end boost [2]

Chellee documents **RC4558P** dual op-amp construction and true bypass switching. [1]

## Version changes
Chellee's 2014 lineage article explains that the new Odie simplified Odelya by removing the module socket and voltage doubler, retaining three practical clipping configurations and redesigning the PCB to reduce switching noise. [3]

## Transistor
No discrete transistor stage is documented. The primary active gain device is the **RC4558P dual op-amp**. [1]

## Diode
The texture circuit includes **asymmetrical diode clipping**, alongside an asymmetrical MOSFET option and clean-boost mode. Exact diode part numbers were not established. [1][2]

## Sound
Odie spans conventional TS-style overdrive, cleaner boost, and more aggressive MOSFET-driven textures. The Voicing switch adds flat, mid-forward, and low-end-boosted responses, including a modern heavier rhythm setting. [2]
