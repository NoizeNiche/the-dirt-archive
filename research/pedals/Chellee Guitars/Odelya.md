# Chellee Guitars — Odelya

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Odelya
- **Builder:** Chellee Guitars
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Chellee Odelya, an expanded TS-clone overdrive with modular clipping, EQ, internal voltage doubling, and a socketed op-amp.

## What this pedal is
Chellee's own history describes Odelya as the company's more elaborate successor to its original early Odie TS-clone. It retained many of the original Odie's components but added a swappable clipping module, bass/EQ switching, an internal voltage doubler, and a socketed op-amp. [1]

## Colorways
No complete factory colorway sequence was established.

## Versions and factory options
Chellee documents:
- Interchangeable clipping-diode module.
- Bass switch that alters the overall EQ curve.
- Internal voltage doubler.
- Socketed op-amp.
- True bypass.
- MXR Phase 90-sized enclosure. [1]

The pedal was sold beginning in December 2009. [1]

## Version changes
Odelya was explicitly created to separate the expanded modular design from the earlier Odie. Chellee later redesigned it, removing the module socket and voltage-doubler circuitry and simplifying the PCB, which produced the later Odie design while retaining the core tonal character. [1]

## Transistor
No exact-model discrete transistor specification was established. The active gain core is an op-amp-based TS-style design.

## Diode
The pedal used interchangeable clipping-diode modules, but the checked source does not establish a single universal diode set for every Odelya unit. [1]

## Sound
Chellee describes Odelya as retaining TS-style overdrive character while providing a large range of clipping and EQ options. The design was intended to cover more tonal situations than the basic Odie, with high clarity and substantial flexibility. [1]
