# Chellee Guitars — Ponyboy - Overdrive

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Ponyboy - Overdrive
- **Builder:** Chellee Guitars
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Chellee Ponyboy Overdrive V3, a Klon Centaur-style overdrive with switchable germanium and Schottky clipping.

## What this pedal is
Chellee describes Ponyboy V3 as a Klon Centaur-based overdrive that combines a buffered clean boost with transparent overdrive. [1]

## Colorways
No complete factory colorway sequence was established.

## Versions and factory options
V3 documents:
- Gain
- Treble
- Bass
- Volume
- Vintage/Modern clipping switch
- Buffered soft-switch bypass. [1]

The clipping switch selects **1N34A germanium diodes** for Vintage and modern Schottky diodes for the Modern setting. [1]

## Version changes
Chellee calls V3 the newest evolution of the original Ponyboy. The builder also documents renamed controls and the addition of a Bass control through a 120 Hz band-pass stage placed after the main gain/blend circuit. [1][2]

## Transistor
No exact-model transistor part number was established in the checked sources.

## Diode
V3 explicitly uses **1N34A germanium diodes** in the Vintage position and **Schottky diodes** in the Modern position. [1]

## Sound
The Ponyboy is designed for a clear clean boost and transparent low-gain overdrive. V3 expands higher-gain voicing with the germanium/Schottky clipping selector and adds Bass control for additional low-frequency shaping. [1]
