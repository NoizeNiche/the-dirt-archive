# Chellee Guitars — Odie - Modified Overdrive

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Odie - Modified Overdrive
- **Builder:** Chellee Guitars
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Chellee Odie Modified Overdrive V2, the renamed and updated form of the original Odie Overdrive.

## What this pedal is
Chellee documents the Odie Modified Overdrive as the next generation of the original Odie Overdrive. Its core remains Tube Screamer-based, but the voicing is more open and less compressed, with an asymmetrical diode mode, asymmetrical MOSFET mode, and clean boost mode. [1]

## Colorways
No complete factory colorway sequence was established.

## Versions and factory options
Documented features:
- Three drive textures: asymmetrical diode, asymmetrical MOSFET, clean boost.
- EQ voicing shift switch.
- True bypass.
- 9-18V operation.
- Handmade in the USA. [1][2]

## Version changes
Chellee renamed the original Odie Overdrive to **Odie Modified Overdrive** in the Version 2 product line to distinguish it from the Odie Classic. Version 2 adds relay soft-switch bypass and a dimmer/diffused LED indicator. [1][3]

## Transistor
No discrete transistor stage is documented; the gain section is based on the Tube Screamer op-amp architecture. Exact op-amp part number for this V2 listing was not established.

## Diode
The pedal uses asymmetrical diode clipping in one texture position and asymmetrical MOSFET clipping in another. Exact diode part numbers were not established. [1][2]

## Sound
The Modified is intentionally more open and less compressed than a traditional TS-style overdrive. The MOSFET setting adds a denser, higher-gain response, while the voicing switch can add low mids without becoming loose. [1]
