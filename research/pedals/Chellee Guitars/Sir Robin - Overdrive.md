## What this pedal is
Chellee describes Sir Robin as a Blues Breaker-based overdrive with more headroom and a more flexible EQ structure than the classic design. [1]

## Sound
The Tone and Presence controls provide broader high-frequency shaping than a conventional Blues Breaker. Presence at zero is close to the classic family, while the Fat switch adds low mids and early-circuit gain for a fuller response. [1][2]
