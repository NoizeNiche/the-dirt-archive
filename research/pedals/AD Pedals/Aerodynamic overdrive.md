# AD Pedals — Aerodynamic overdrive

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Aerodynamic overdrive
- **Builder:** AD Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Compact low-to-mid-gain overdrive with three clipping modes.

## What this pedal is
Aerodynamic Overdrive uses Gain, Tone and Volume controls with a three-way toggle that changes the clipping arrangement. The documented low-gain mode uses Bat41 diodes symmetrically, the high-gain mode uses Germanium 1N34A diodes asymmetrically, and the center position uses the main circuit without either alternate clipping mode. [1][2]

## Diode
- **Low-gain mode:** Bat41 diodes, symmetrical clipping.
- **High-gain mode:** Germanium 1N34A diodes, asymmetrical clipping.
- **Center position:** Main circuit without the alternate clipping mode. [1][2]

## Sound
The Aerodynamic Overdrive is a low-to-mid-gain drive that can also provide a clean boost by lowering Gain and raising Volume. The three-position clipping system gives it distinct responses, from a more symmetrical low-gain sound to a more asymmetric, transparent-feeling higher-gain mode. [1][2]
