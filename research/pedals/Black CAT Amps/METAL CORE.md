# Black CAT Amps - METAL CORE

## PRP identity
- Builder: Black CAT Amps
- Catalog type: Distortion
- Identity: Black CAT Amps METAL CORE, the company's first guitar pedal.

## What this pedal is
METAL CORE was the first Black CAT Amps guitar pedal, developed specifically for metal-oriented guitar use.

## History
Black CAT's official company history states that the pedal was developed beginning in November 2016 and that the first METAL CORE pedal came out on **November 30, 2017**.
