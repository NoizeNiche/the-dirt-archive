## What this pedal is
.Ritual. is an extended-range RAT-style distortion with active three-band EQ, range boost, sweepable clipping-era control and an independent boost.

## Controls and features
- **Bass, Mids, High** active EQ, approximately ±18dB.
- **Boost** with selectable range.
- **Era** control from White Face RAT toward Turbo RAT voicing.
- **Low-Cut** control.
- **Gain** and **Level**.
- Footswitchable boost and bypass.
- **Comp** clipping switch adds additional diodes to the first gain stage.

## Sound
.Ritual. is intended for post-metal and extended-range distortion. It can move from white-face RAT-style sounds through higher-gain Turbo RAT territory, with deep EQ control and a strong optional boost.
