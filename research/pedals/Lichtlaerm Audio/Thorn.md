## What this pedal is

The Thorn is Lichtlaerm Audio's signature high-gain distortion for Lennart Bossu and is associated with Amenra, Oathbreaker, Predatory Void, and Living Gate. It combines multiple voicing options inspired by classic SDT-style heavy distortion with a more contemporary Lichtlaerm setting.

## Sound

Thorn covers saturated post-metal and modern high-gain distortion while retaining a transparent response. The SDT-derived settings provide more traditional heavy distortion voices, while the Laerm setting is the more contemporary Lichtlaerm interpretation.
