## What this pedal is
Gehenna is a highly flexible metal distortion designed to cover classic NWOBHM, early-2000s core, black/death metal and tighter modern djent-oriented sounds.

## Versions and factory options
- Two internal gain levels.
- Footswitchable second gain setting.
- Presence and Treble provide broad high-frequency shaping.
- Designed to work as a distortion or high-output preamp.
- Handmade in Berlin.

## Sound
Gehenna spans aggressive British-style distortion through very tight modern high-gain voices. Its broad EQ and second gain level make it suitable both before a clean amp and as a preamp-like drive source.
