## What this pedal is
Twin Pro is a 100% analog overdrive with Volume, Tone and Gain controls and a WRATH switch that changes the character of the pedal. A&M documents a socketed op amp and point-to-point wiring. [1]

## Colorways
- The documented chassis is **silver polychrome die-cast aluminum** with black chicken-head knobs and red indicator LEDs. [1]

## Sound
Twin Pro is aimed at warm, old-school overdrive rather than very high gain. The WRATH switch changes the character of the circuit, while the simple Volume/Tone/Gain layout keeps the pedal focused on broad classic-rock and blues overdrive sounds. [1]
