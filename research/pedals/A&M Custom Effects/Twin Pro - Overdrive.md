## What this pedal is

A&M Custom Effects's Twin Pro is a hand-built analog overdrive with Volume, Tone and Gain controls plus a WRATH switch that changes the pedal's character. The preserved product specification also notes true bypass, 9V battery/DC operation, a socketed op amp and point-to-point construction. [1]
