## What this pedal is\n\nA&M Custom Effects's Crazyboy - Double Fuzz is a dual-voice fuzz pedal that lets the player switch between Germanium and Silicon transistor fuzz circuits. The preserved product specification describes separate Volume and Fuzz controls, point-to-point wiring, true-bypass switching and 9V operation. [1]\n\n## Colorways
