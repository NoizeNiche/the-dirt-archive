## What this pedal is
CrazyBoy is a **dual fuzz** with two different transistor technologies in one enclosure: one Germanium channel and one Silicon channel. An A/B switch selects between the two fuzz circuits, with separate controls for each side. [1]

## Colorways
- The documented production chassis is **silver polychrome die-cast aluminum**, with black pointer knobs and red LEDs. [1]

## Sound
CrazyBoy gives two contrasting fuzz responses in one box. The Germanium side is intended to provide the softer, older-style fuzz character, while the Silicon side is designed to be more direct and aggressive; the A/B switch makes the contrast immediate without changing the pedal chain. [1]
