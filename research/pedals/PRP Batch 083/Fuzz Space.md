# Big Knob Pedals - Fuzz Space

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Big Knob Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz

## What this pedal is
Fuzz Space is a hand-wired recreation of the classic Hendrix-era fuzz family using matched NOS Russian military germanium transistors.

## Controls
- Level
- Attack

## Components
- Two matched NOS Russian military germanium transistors

## Construction / power
- Fully hand-wired analog
- True-bypass 3PDT
- 9V battery clip; standard pedal supply support is listed by the builder

## Sound
Creamy, sustaining vintage fuzz that cleans up when the guitar volume is rolled back.
