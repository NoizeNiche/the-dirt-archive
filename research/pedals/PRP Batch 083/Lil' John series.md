# Big John Effects - Lil' John series

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Big John Effects
- **Catalog type:** Boost / Fuzz
- **Identity:** Lil' John mini pedal series, consisting of the **Lil' John Boost** and **Lil' John Fuzz**.

## What this pedal is
The Lil' John series was a compact mini-pedal line designed by Stijn Vandebril and produced by Big John. Big John's official history dates the series to **2011** and describes it as pre-order only.

## Versions and history
- 2011: Lil' John series, Boost and Fuzz, designs by Stijn Vandebril.
- Contemporary 2012 coverage separately identifies Lil' John Boost and Lil' John Fuzz as compact pedals.
- A contemporary small-pedal inventory also lists both models as 1590A-format pedals.

## Controls / functions

### Lil' John Boost
Contemporary coverage documents:
- **MORE:** overall boost/output level.
- **DIRT:** adds gain/drive character.

The reviewer describes the Boost as capable of substantial level increase, with added warmth above the midpoint and a dirt control that can move toward a crunchy, tube-like character.

### Lil' John Fuzz
Contemporary coverage documents:
- **LOUDER:** fuzz output level.
- **GATE:** controls the gated/synth-like character of the fuzz.

The reviewer describes a compact fuzz with an aggressive, raspy character and a Gate control that changes its broken/synth-like behavior.

## Circuit / construction
- Big John's official history identifies the series as designs by Stijn Vandebril.
- INFOMUSIC describes the Big John pedals reviewed in Belgium as hand-made units emphasizing quality components and external 9 V power.
- Exact circuit topology, transistor part numbers, and diode types were not recovered for either Lil' John model.

## Sound
The Lil' John Boost is documented as a bright, clean-to-dirty boost with substantial level increase and optional added grit. The Lil' John Fuzz is documented as an aggressive compact fuzz with gate control for more synth-like and broken textures.

## Deep research verification

### Verified identity
The official history and contemporary coverage establish the Lil' John line as two distinct mini pedals: **Lil' John Boost** and **Lil' John Fuzz**.

### Verified circuit evidence
No reliable exact schematic or semiconductor part list was recovered. The archive does not assign a guessed topology to these pedals.

### Verified power and construction
Contemporary review coverage states that the tested Big John pedals used a single 9 V external supply and did not support battery operation.
