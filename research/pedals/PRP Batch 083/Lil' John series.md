# Big John Effects - Lil' John series

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Big John Effects
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz

## What this pedal is
The Lil' John series is a small-format family of Big John designs by Stijn Vandebril, documented by the builder as pre-order-only designs from 2011. The archive catalog groups the series under one parent identity.

## Series information
- Builder history identifies the series as **Boost and Fuzz** designs.
- Designed by Stijn Vandebril.
- Pre-order-only in the builder's historical timeline.

## Circuit / construction
Big John states its pedals were hand-wired point-to-point and built in-house.
