# Big Knob Pedals - Green Russian

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Big Knob Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz

## What this pedal is
Green Russian is Big Knob's recreation of the Sovtek Green Russian fuzz circuit.

## Controls
- Volume
- Tone
- Sustain

## Circuit
- Russian-made Sovtek Green Russian circuit
- Silicon-based fuzz topology

## Construction / power
- Hand-wired analog
- True-bypass 3PDT
- 9V adapter
- 9V battery clip

## Sound
Smooth, dark and mid-forward fuzz with the characteristic Russian Muff-family response.
