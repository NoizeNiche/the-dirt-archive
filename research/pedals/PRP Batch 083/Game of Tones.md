# Big Knob Pedals - Game of Tones

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Big Knob Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive

## What this pedal is
Game of Tones is Big Knob's modified take on the early-'90s Marshall BluesBreaker overdrive concept.

## Circuit / design
- Modified stock BluesBreaker-style circuit.
- Big Knob positions it as its interpretation of the dual-overdrive concept associated with KOT/POT/DOT designs.

## Controls / construction
The accessible primary listing establishes the overdrive identity and circuit lineage, but does not expose enough final control-label detail to safely add undocumented knob names.
- Hand-wired analog
- True-bypass 3PDT
- 9V adapter / battery support

## Sound
BluesBreaker-derived overdrive intended for dependable stage and studio use rather than exact clone-for-clone matching.
