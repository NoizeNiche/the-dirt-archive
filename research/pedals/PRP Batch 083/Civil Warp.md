# Big Knob Pedals - Civil Warp

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Big Knob Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz

## What this pedal is
Civil Warp is a recreation of the rare Russian-produced Sovtek Civil War fuzz/Muff variant.

## Controls
- Volume
- Tone
- Fuzz

## Circuit
The builder identifies it as a replica of the Russian Civil War model, with the characteristic component-value differences from the Green Russian family.

## Construction / power
- Hand-wired analog
- True-bypass 3PDT
- 9V adapter
- 9V battery clip

## Sound
Described as smoother and slightly darker, with more midrange and a musical fuzz response.
