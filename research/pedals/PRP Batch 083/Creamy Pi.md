# Big Knob Pedals - Creamy Pi

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Big Knob Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz

## What this pedal is
Creamy Pi is a Sovtek Green Russian fuzz circuit with a Creamy Dreamer modification.

## Controls
- Volume
- Tone
- Sustain

## Circuit / components
- Sovtek Green Russian circuit
- Creamy Dreamer mod
- The mod increases fuzz and sustain by grounding the emitters of the two gain-stage transistors.

## Construction / power
- Hand-wired analog
- True-bypass 3PDT
- 9V adapter
- 9V battery clip

## Sound
Higher-gain and more sustaining than a stock Muff-style circuit, targeted toward thick fuzz textures.
