# Big Knob Pedals - Guv'nator

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Big Knob Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Distortion

## What this pedal is
Guv'nator is Big Knob's recreation of the 1988 version 1 UK Marshall Guv'nor-style distortion.

## Controls
- Volume
- Bass
- Middle (internal trim)
- Treble
- Gain

## Circuit / architecture
- Recreation of the 1988 version 1 UK circuit
- Middle control moved to an internal trim pot to fit the smaller enclosure

## Construction / power
- Hand-wired analog, no PCB
- True-bypass 3PDT
- 9V adapter
- 9V battery clip

## Sound
Marshall-style distortion ranging from bluesier drive through stronger lead-oriented gain.
