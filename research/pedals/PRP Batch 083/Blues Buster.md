# Big Knob Pedals - Blues Buster

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Big Knob Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive

## What this pedal is
Blues Buster is a hand-wired reproduction of the early-'90s UK Bluesbreaker-derived overdrive, specifically the 1991 version 1.1 style.

## Controls
- Volume
- Tone
- Gain

## Construction / power
- Hand-wired analog, no PCB
- True-bypass 3PDT
- 9V adapter
- 9V battery clip

## Sound
Voiced around the smooth, rich character associated with the 1962 Bluesbreaker amplifier while preserving the particular response of the early UK pedal version.
