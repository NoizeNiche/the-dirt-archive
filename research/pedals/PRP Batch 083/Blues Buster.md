# Big Knob Pedals - Blues Buster

## PRP identity

- **Builder:** Big Knob Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive

## What this pedal is

Blues Buster is Big Knob Pedals' hand-wired reproduction of the early-1990s UK overdrive, specifically the 1991 version 1.1 style.

## Controls

- Volume
- Tone
- Gain

## Construction / power

- Hand-wired analog, no PCB
- True-bypass 3PDT
- 9V adapter
- 9V battery clip

## Sound

Voiced around the smooth, rich character associated with the Bluesbreaker amplifier and the early UK pedal version.
