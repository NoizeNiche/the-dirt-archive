# Big John Effects - Hairy Balls

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Big John Effects
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Fuzz / Overdrive

## What this pedal is
Hairy Balls is Big John Effects' original fuzz and boost design. Big John's current site says the design has remained unchanged since 2008 and describes it as a hairy fuzz with a tube-like boost.

## Controls / functions
- Boost
- Volume
- Gate
- Internal booster-start-volume trim

## Circuit / construction
- Point-to-point soldered construction.
- Big John states that the design is 100% original and unchanged since 2008.
- Intended for guitar, bass and keys.
- Version history from Big John's official timeline: **v1 in 2007, fuzz only; v2 in 2008, boost + gate + fuzz.**

## Sound
Big John describes a vintage-character fuzz with a gated mode capable of synth-like textures. The boost can move from clean enhancement toward added gain and grit. The builder notes a preference for passive instruments.

## Deep research verification

### Verified identity and history

Big John's official history places the first Hairy Balls design in **2007** and the expanded boost/gate/fuzz version in **2008**. The current product page states that the design has not changed since 2008.

### Verified controls and functions

- **Boost:** adjustable boost level, from subtle enhancement through aggressive overdrive.
- **Volume:** fuzz output level.
- **Gate:** controls the gated/synth-like character of the fuzz.
- An internal trim controls the booster's starting volume.

### Verified construction and power

- Point-to-point soldered construction.
- Big John's standard pedal power guidance is **9 V DC, negative tip / positive ring**.
- Big John describes its pedals as being built without routine 9 V battery operation.

### Verified transistor/device terms

- The reviewed builder documentation does not identify exact transistor part numbers or a complete semiconductor complement.

### Verified diode terms

- Exact clipping/rectifier diode information is not documented in the reviewed sources.

### Verified sound evidence

The builder describes a hairy fuzz with vintage character, gated/synth-like possibilities, and a boost stage that can add brightness, drive, gain and grit.
