# Big John Effects - Hairy Balls

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Big John Effects
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Fuzz / Overdrive

## What this pedal is
Hairy Balls is Big John Effects' original fuzz/boost design. The builder describes it as an original design dating to 2007-2008, with a fuzz section plus boost and gate functions.

## Controls / functions
- Boost
- Volume
- Gate
- Internal booster-start-volume trim

## Circuit / construction
- Hand-built, point-to-point construction.
- Designed for guitar, bass and keys.
- Version history: v1 fuzz-only; v2 added boost and gate.

## Sound
The builder describes a hairy fuzz with synth-like gated textures and a clean-to-aggressive boost that can add drive and grit.
