# Big Knob Pedals - Black Metallic

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Big Knob Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Distortion

## What this pedal is
Black Metallic is a boutique high-gain distortion modeled on the broad family of modern high-gain pedals. Big Knob describes it as tight and high intensity without excessive compression.

## Controls / architecture
- High-gain distortion architecture
- Exact front-panel control names were not sufficiently exposed in the accessible listing.
- Hand-wired analog
- True-bypass 3PDT
- 9V adapter / battery support

## Sound
Tight, aggressive high-gain distortion intended to avoid a mushy compressed response.
