# Big Knob Pedals - I.C.B.M.

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Big Knob Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz

## What this pedal is
I.C.B.M. is Big Knob's recreation of the 1977 op-amp Big Muff variant associated with the Siamese Dream-era circuit.

## Controls
- Volume
- Tone
- Sustain

## Circuit / components
- LM741 op-amp
- JRC4558 op-amp
- Two-op-amp architecture rather than the traditional four-transistor Muff gain structure

## Construction / power
- Fully hand-wired analog
- True-bypass 3PDT
- 9V adapter
- 9V battery clip

## Sound
Crunchier, higher-gain Muff character associated with the op-amp variant.
