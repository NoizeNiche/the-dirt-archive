# Big Knob Pedals - Appetite for Distortion

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Big Knob Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Distortion

## What this pedal is
Appetite for Distortion is Big Knob's Marshall-in-a-box style distortion aimed at the JCM800/Appetite-era sound. The builder states that backing down the gain can produce Plexi-like tones.

## Controls / architecture
- Gain
- Tone / EQ controls are part of the amp-style voicing; exact front-panel control labeling was not sufficiently exposed by the current primary listing.
- Hand-wired analog construction
- True-bypass 3PDT
- 9V adapter and battery support

## Sound
High-gain Marshall-style distortion with a lower-gain range intended for Plexi-like tones.
