# Big Knob Pedals - D OverDrive 250

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Big Knob Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive

## What this pedal is
D OverDrive 250 is Big Knob's recreation of the classic overdrive/boost associated with Yngwie Malmsteen and Randy Rhoads.

## Controls
- Gain
- Level

## Circuit / components
- Original LM741 op-amp chip
- Hand-wired analog circuitry

## Construction / power
- True-bypass 3PDT
- 9V adapter
- 9V battery clip

## Sound
Articulate, biting and clear boost/overdrive intended to drive tube amplifiers into classic distortion.

## Deep research verification
The Big Knob catalog describes D OverDrive 250 as a hand-wired recreation of the classic DOD 250-type boost/overdrive. Effects Database identifies the build with an **original LM741** op-amp, **Gain** and **Level** controls, true-bypass 3PDT switching, and 9V adapter plus battery support. The builder positions it as a boost for driving Marshall-type tube heads into classic distortion. Source: Effects Database.
