# Big Knob Pedals - Colour Jumbo

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Big Knob Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz

## What this pedal is
Colour Jumbo is a silicon fuzz based on the early-1970s Colorsound Tone Bender family, with a hybrid Bender/Muff circuit.

## Controls
- Volume
- Tone
- Fuzz

## Circuit / components
- Three-stage silicon gain hybrid Bender/Muff circuit.

## Construction / power
- Fully hand-wired analog
- True-bypass 3PDT
- 9V adapter
- 9V battery clip

## Sound
Rawer and grittier than modern Muff-style fuzzes, with a pronounced vintage character and usefulness on bass as well as guitar.
