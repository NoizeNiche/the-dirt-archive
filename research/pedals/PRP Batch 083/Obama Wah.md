# Big John Effects - Obama Wah

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Big John Effects
- **Catalog type:** Distortion

## What this pedal is
Obama Wah combines a fixed wah/filter with a distortion circuit. Effects Database describes both effects as independently usable.

## Controls / functions
- Distortion volume
- Distortion gain / voltage control
- Sweepable wah-filter control

## Circuit / operation
The distortion gain control also acts as a voltage control capable of creating unusual textures. The filter functions as an alternative tone-shaping control.

## Compatibility
Designed for guitar and bass; the builder also targeted synth users across the Big John line.
