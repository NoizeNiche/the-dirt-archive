# Biltworth - BW-1

## PRP identity

- **Builder:** Biltworth
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive

## Research

Multi-effects pedal combining overdrive, delay and reverb in series. Each section has its own controls and switching: Drive uses Volume, Gain and Tone; Delay uses Level, Speed and Repeats; Reverb uses Mix, Decay and Boost. The pedal provides true bypass between individual effects and a true-bypass master. Premier Guitar describes a 7.38 x 4.7 inch enclosure, one 9V DC input, and hand assembly in California. Gearnews describes the three effect sections as analogue.
