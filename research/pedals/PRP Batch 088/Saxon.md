# Bispell Audio - Saxon

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Bispell Audio
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive

## Research
JTM45 and Super Lead Plexi-style amp-in-a-box. V1.5 controls are Bass, Middle, Treble, Volume, Presence and Gain, plus Bass Boost and Bright Boost switches. Bass Boost selects JTM45 versus Super Lead character, while the Bright Boost follows the same two-position architecture. The builder also documented internal Fender/Marshall and DI/Amp switches for additional frequency shaping. Published coverage identifies the V1.5 as a refined revision with more gain and a recalibrated tone stack.
