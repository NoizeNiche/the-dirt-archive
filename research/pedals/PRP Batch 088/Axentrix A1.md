# BIXONIC - Axentrix A1

## PRP identity

- **Builder:** BIXONIC
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Fuzz / Overdrive

## Research

Digital multi-drive successor to the Expandora concept. Four drive modes are available: Crunch, Over Drive, Distortion and Fuzz. DREP technology adjusts expansion/compression response through the Accent control. Features include a typical Tone control plus 3-band EQ, switchable true or buffered bypass, adjustable buffered-bypass sound, three memories, guitar/bass instrument selection, tunable illumination and a tuning meter. The builder positions it as a modern digital distortion design rather than a direct reissue.
