# Bixonic - EXP2000DR Clean & Drive

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Bixonic
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive

## Research
Next-generation Bixonic drive pedal combining a Clean mode with Crunch, Distortion and Forbidden drive modes. It adds a Nuance function for shaping picking-dynamic response, Modern and Classic styles, a 3-band EQ, selectable true or buffered bypass and three storable presets. The Classic setting is designed around the 1994 original Expandora sound. The builder emphasizes digitally repeatable control while retaining a warm, organic-feeling response.
