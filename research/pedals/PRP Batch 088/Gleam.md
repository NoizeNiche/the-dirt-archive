# Bispell Audio - Gleam

## PRP identity

- **Builder:** Bispell Audio
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz

## Research

Silicon multi-fuzz designed to recreate several classic germanium fuzz voices: Tone Bender MKI, Tone Bender MKII, Fuzz Face and Maestro FZ-1. The V2 version exposes Bass, Mid Cut, Treble, Volume, Bias and Gain, plus a three-position Impedance switch and three-position Tone switch. The internal design uses a 9V-to-25V conversion and carefully biased silicon circuitry to emulate germanium behavior. External Bias can be used for gated/choppy textures and 8-bit sounds. Enclosure is hand built with true bypass and 9V centre-negative power.

## Sound

Its 2 x 3 way Impedance and Tone switches select those profiles, while 9V to 25V conversion helps Silicon mimic OC81D dynamics.
The manual includes an 8 Bit Fuzz preset; GPX’s favourite uses both switches DOWN.
I’m not usually big on hyperbole while this V2 Gleam might just be the most potent silicon fuzz pedal made to date where its 8 controls incredibly deliver pretty authentic FZ 1, MKI, MKII and FF voicings and then some.

## What this pedal is

This is my second Gleam, where for the V1 edition the Bias control was internal, and the Impedance and Tone controls were slightly less granular 2 way switches.
