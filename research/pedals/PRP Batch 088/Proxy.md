## PRP identity

## Research
Vox UL730 / Conqueror-inspired transistor amp-in-a-box aimed at Beatles and related 1960s tones. Controls: Bass, Middle, Treble, Volume, Presence and Gain, plus Mid Boost and Bright switches. No ICs are used in the audio path; the design uses transistors and passive circuitry. An internal 9V-to-18V conversion is documented for additional dynamics, along with an internal DIP switch that alters Presence behavior for Fender or Marshall-style response. True bypass, 9V centre-negative power; 27mA maximum consumption.
