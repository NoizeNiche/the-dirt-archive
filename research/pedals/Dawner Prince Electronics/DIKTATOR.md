## Sound

Diktator™ is a unique two channel combination of clean, transparent preamp and full, thick overdrive / distortion that operate independently.
Two toggle switches are responsible for different tonal and gain shapes, and with two-band Baxandall active tone control, any desired frequency is easily cut or boosted.
Another original feature is a Dynamic-O-Meter™ (Diktator’s eyes), which is a visual indicator of the drive’s dynamic response.
