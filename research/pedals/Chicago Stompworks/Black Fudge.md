# Chicago Stompworks — Black Fudge

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Black Fudge
- **Builder:** Chicago Stompworks
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Chicago Stompworks Black Fudge, a Maestro MFZ-1 clone using the scarce TL022 op-amp.

## What this pedal is
Chicago Stompworks describes Black Fudge as a recreation of the Maestro MFZ-1, a dirty and grungy 1970s fuzz. The builder states that it uses the same scarce TL022 op-amp as the original circuit. [1]

## Colorways
- The current builder catalog documents an unprinted barebox version and the standard Black Fudge, but does not establish a complete historical colorway sequence. [1]

## Versions and factory options
The builder documents:
- True bypass.
- Point-to-point wiring.
- 1590B enclosure.
- Standard 9V center-negative power or internal battery. [1]

## Version changes
No formal numbered factory revision sequence was established.

## Transistor
The documented active device is the **TL022 op-amp**, not a discrete transistor. [1]

## Diode
No exact-model diode specification was established.

## Sound
The Black Fudge aims at the dirty, warm, grungy fuzz character associated with the original Maestro MFZ-1. Contemporary discussion also describes a substantial heavy-overdrive component alongside the fuzz. [1][2]
