# Chicago Stompworks — Black Fudge

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Black Fudge
- **Builder:** Chicago Stompworks
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Point-to-point Maestro MFZ-1 recreation using the scarce **TL022** op-amp, housed in a 1590B enclosure with true bypass and battery/9V supply. [1][2]

## Circuit lineage
- **Primary reference:** Maestro **MFZ-1**.
- Chicago Stompworks explicitly describes Black Fudge as an MFZ-1 clone. [1]

## Active device
- **TL022 op-amp**, matching the scarce device associated with the MFZ-1 circuit. [1]
- No discrete transistor architecture is assigned.

## Controls / construction
- Exact-model control map is not fully preserved in the reviewed sources.
- **Point-to-point wiring**
- **1590B enclosure**
- **True bypass**
- Internal battery support. [1]

## Sound
The builder targets the dirty, warm and grungy fuzz associated with the Maestro MFZ-1, with enough gain to push toward heavy overdrive textures. [1][2]
