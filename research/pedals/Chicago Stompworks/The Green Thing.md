## PRP identity

- **Identity:** Chicago Stompworks The Green Thing, a hand-built TS-808-style overdrive.

## What this pedal is
Reverb describes The Green Thing as a handmade boutique clone of the TS-808 circuit. [1]

Chicago Stompworks' own catalog also documents the Green Thing as one of its Screamer-style builds. [2]
