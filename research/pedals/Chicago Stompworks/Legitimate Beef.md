# Chicago Stompworks — Legitimate Beef

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Legitimate Beef
- **Builder:** Chicago Stompworks
- **Catalog type:** Boost / Overdrive
- **Identity:** Close recreation of the Way Huge **Red Llama**, using a CD4049 CMOS IC and a large 330uF capacitor, with 1590B, true bypass and battery/9V power. [1][2]

## Circuit lineage
- **Primary reference:** Way Huge **Red Llama** / Tube Sound Fuzz family.
- Chicago Stompworks states that the circuit is intended to be as close to the original as possible without modifications. [1]

## Active device
- **CD4049 CMOS IC** is documented. [1]
- No discrete transistor complement is assigned.

## Controls / construction
- Exact control labeling follows the simple Red Llama-style boost/drive concept.
- **1590B enclosure**
- Hand-wired jacks.
- True bypass. [1]

## Power
- **9V center-negative** or internal battery.
- Approximately **5 mA** draw is documented in independent power data. [2]

## Sound
The builder positions Legitimate Beef as a warm, rumbly and unusually loud boost/overdrive that preserves low end and is intended to push an amplifier's front end. [1]

## Version history
- No numbered electronic revision established.
- Barebox/knob/LED differences are cosmetic production variations.

## Research confidence
- **Identity:** High
- **Red Llama lineage:** High
- **CD4049:** High
- **1590B/true bypass:** High
- **5mA:** High as independent power-reference data
- **Exact clipping diode:** Unknown

## Deep research verification
Chicago Stompworks' exact product page was cross-checked with independent power/catalog evidence. The builder source supports the close Red Llama recreation and CD4049 architecture; independent data supports the 5mA/9V operating reference. [1][2]
