# Chicago Stompworks — Legitimate Beef

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Legitimate Beef
- **Builder:** Chicago Stompworks
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Chicago Stompworks Legitimate Beef, a close recreation of the original Way Huge Red Llama / Craig Anderton Tube Fuzz Sound family.

## What this pedal is
Chicago Stompworks describes Legitimate Beef as a recreation of the original Red Llama, emphasizing very large output, warm crunch, and preservation of low end. The builder explicitly states that the circuit is intended to be as close to the original as possible, without modifications. [1]

## Colorways
- The standard builder finish is documented, along with an unprinted barebox variant. [1]
- Knob and LED colors may vary according to the builder. [2]

## Versions and factory options
The builder documents:
- **CD4049** chip.
- Large 330uF capacitor.
- 1590B enclosure.
- True bypass.
- Hand-wired jacks.
- Standard center-negative 9V power or internal battery.
- Approximately 5mA current draw. [1][2]

## Version changes
No formal numbered factory revision sequence was established.

## Transistor
The documented active gain device is the **CD4049 CMOS IC**, not a discrete transistor. [1]

## Diode
No exact-model diode specification was established.

## Sound
The Legitimate Beef is intended to be a warm, rumbly overdrive/boost with unusually strong output and preserved low end. Chicago Stompworks positions it directly for driving the front end of a tube amplifier. [1][3]
