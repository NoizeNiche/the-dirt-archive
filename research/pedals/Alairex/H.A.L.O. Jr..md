# Alairex — H.A.L.O. Jr.

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** H.A.L.O. Jr.
- **Builder:** Alairex
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Single-channel harmonic amp-like overdrive derived from the high-gain channel of the full-size H.A.L.O.

## What this pedal is
The H.A.L.O. Jr. is the compact single-channel version of Alairex's H.A.L.O., built around the high-gain character of channel 2. Contemporary documentation describes it as a touch-sensitive solid-state overdrive with controls for gain, tone, level, bass, contour, and clipping shape. [1][2]

## Colorways
- **White enclosure with black front panel/graphics** is documented in surviving product photography. [1][3]
- No verified circuit revision is tied to finish.

## Versions and factory options
### H.A.L.O. Jr.
- Gain
- Tone
- Level
- Bass
- Contour
- Clip Shape selector
- single-channel implementation of the H.A.L.O. high-gain path [1][2]

Documented clip-shape operation includes two clipping modes and a clean-boost mode. [2]

## Version changes
The Jr. is a distinct single-channel model based on H.A.L.O. channel 2, not a numbered factory revision of the full-size pedal. [1][2]

## Sound
The Jr. is documented as responsive to picking dynamics, with a full, amp-like overdrive character and strong output even at moderate gain. The two clip shapes provide contrasting responses, including a darker/hairier mode and a cleaner boost-oriented mode, with the result becoming more aggressive as gain is increased. [2]
