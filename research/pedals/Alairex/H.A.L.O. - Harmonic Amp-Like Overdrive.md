# Alairex — H.A.L.O. - Harmonic Amp-Like Overdrive

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** H.A.L.O. - Harmonic Amp-Like Overdrive

- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Dual-channel solid-state harmonic amp-like overdrive.

## What this pedal is
The Alairex H.A.L.O. is a two-channel overdrive built around separately controlled Gain and Level paths, with additional Bass, Contour, Presence, Tone, Shape, and Saturation controls. The documented Shape switch provides symmetrical clipping, asymmetrical clipping, or clean boost modes, while the Saturation switch adds a further layer of distortion/compression. [1]

## Colorways

## Versions and factory options
### H.A.L.O.
- Gain 1 and Gain 2
- Level 1 and Level 2
- Tone, Bass, Contour, Presence
- Shape: symmetrical clipping / asymmetrical clipping / clean boost
- Saturation: off / Gain 2 only / both gain channels
- 9–18V DC operation; the documentation notes increased headroom at 18V [1]

## Version changes
No numbered factory revision was verified. The H.A.L.O. Jr. is documented as a separate single-channel model rather than a revision of this unit. [1]

## Sound
The H.A.L.O. is documented as producing warm, rich, multidimensional overdrive with strong touch sensitivity and adjustable compression. Shape can move the response from a cleaner boost through brighter symmetrical clipping to darker, more harmonically complex asymmetrical clipping, while Saturation adds smoother sustain and extra density. [1][2]
