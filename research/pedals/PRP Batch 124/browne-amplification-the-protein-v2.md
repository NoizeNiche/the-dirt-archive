# Browne Amplification — The Protein v2

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Browne Amplification
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Protein Version 2.

## What this pedal is
Protein V2 is the second major documented Protein hardware generation. Browne's V2.2 page identifies side-mounted jacks and an internal split-mode switch, while preserving the two-drive architecture and green-to-blue cascade. [1]

## Colorways
Multiple finishes were produced; no single finish is assumed.

## Versions and factory options
- Dual overdrive
- Blue and Green sides
- Green cascades into Blue
- Side-mounted jacks
- Internal split/loop mode [1]

## Version changes
Browne's archived V2.2 page documents the side-mounted-jack layout. Later V3 changes to top-mounted jacks and V4 adds further tonal controls. [1][2][3]

## Transistor
Exact active devices not publicly documented.

## Diode
Exact factory clipping devices not publicly documented.

## Sound
V2 combines high-headroom low-gain drive on the blue side with Nashville-inspired drive on the green side. Cascading the two gives a thicker, more saturated sound without losing note definition. [1]
