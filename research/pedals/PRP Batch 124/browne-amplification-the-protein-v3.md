# Browne Amplification — The Protein v3

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Browne Amplification
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Naming variant of Protein Dual Overdrive V3.

## What this pedal is
The current Browne product page names this version **Protein Dual Overdrive V3**. The tracker also contains **The Protein v3**, which is retained as a separate naming record until catalog normalization. [1]

## Colorways
Multiple finishes exist; no single permanent finish is assumed.

## Versions and factory options
- Carbon blue side
- Nashville-inspired ATOM green side
- Top-mounted jacks
- Soft switching
- TRS-based split/loop capability with internal switch [1]

## Version changes
V3 is documented with top-mounted jacks and the ATOM-based green side. Browne later replaced that architecture with V4's additional hi-cut and clipping controls. [1][2]

## Transistor
Exact active devices not publicly documented.

## Diode
Exact factory clipping devices not publicly documented.

## Sound
The blue side emphasizes high headroom and clear low-gain drive, while the green ATOM side adds a more vocal Nashville-style response. Cascading green into blue increases gain and fullness. [1]
