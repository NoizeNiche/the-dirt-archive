# Browne Amplification — The Protein - Dual Overdrive

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Browne Amplification
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Naming variant of the Protein dual-overdrive product.

## What this pedal is
Browne's current store uses **Protein Dual Overdrive V2.2** for this product family. The archive also contains the historical naming form **The Protein - Dual Overdrive**, which is treated here as the same product lineage rather than a separate circuit. [1]

## Colorways
Multiple Protein finishes exist; no single permanent finish is assumed.

## Versions and factory options
- Dual blue/green overdrive architecture
- Blue side based on Browne's refined low-gain reference circuit
- Green side based on Nashville-style overdrive
- Green into blue cascading
- V2.2 side-mounted jacks and internal split-mode switch [1]

## Version changes
Browne separately documents later V3 and V4 versions. V3 moves to top-mounted jacks and the Nashville-inspired ATOM identity; V4 adds additional high-cut and clipping options. [1][2][3]

## Transistor
Exact active devices not publicly documented.

## Diode
Exact factory clipping devices not publicly documented.

## Sound
The Protein combines a clearer, high-headroom blue-side drive with a fuller Nashville-style green side. Running green into blue creates extra gain and fullness while preserving note clarity. [1]
