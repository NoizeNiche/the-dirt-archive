# Browne Amplification — The Protein v4

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Browne Amplification
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Naming variant of Protein Dual Overdrive V4.

## What this pedal is
Browne's current store names this generation **Protein Dual Overdrive V4**. The tracker also contains **The Protein v4**, which is treated as a naming variant pending later catalog normalization. [1]

## Colorways
The current product page offers selectable finishes; no single permanent colorway is assumed.

## Versions and factory options
- Dual overdrive
- 3-way hi-cut on blue side
- Green-side Low Cut
- Green-side heavier clipping
- Green-side asymmetrical clipping
- 9V / 130mA
- Optional MIDI upgrade [1]

## Version changes
V4 adds the hi-cut and green-side low-cut, heavier-clipping and asymmetrical-clipping options to the Protein architecture. [1]

## Transistor
Exact active devices not publicly documented.

## Diode
Browne documents alternate clipping configurations but does not publish exact part numbers. [1]

## Sound
V4 broadens the Protein's range from clear low-gain drive to fuller Nashville-style overdrive and more aggressive stacked sounds. The added filtering and clipping options allow the player to tighten lows, reshape highs and increase saturation. [1]

## Deep research verification

This pass adds only claims supported by the newly admitted exact-model evidence. Earlier archive research is retained unchanged.

### Verified description
Browne Amplification's The Protein v4 is cataloged as an overdrive pedal.

### Verified color/finish evidence
- The Blue side is based on the classic overdrive heard on records like John Mayer’s "Continuum." Dave addressed issues like limited output, revoiced the tone control, and refined the circuit to what we consider our favorite version of this beloved design—true to its roots, but undeniably improved.
- The Green side is inspired by the classic “Nashville” overdrive that’s been a staple for session greats like Tim Pierce and Jerry McPherson.
- But it’s the stacked mode —running the Green into the Blue—that continues to be our favorite.

### Verified version references
- The evidence references: V2, V4, v4.

### Verified sound evidence
The Protein Dual Overdrive V4 is a collaboration between David Brown and guitarist/producer Adam Sniegowski.
After years on the road and in the studio using unpredictable backline amps, Adam approached Dave with a goal: create a dual overdrive that would sound great no matter what it was plugged into.
