## Sound
The middle range of the Gain control delivers tight, stinging overdrive that covers anything from blues to modern metal with plenty of attitude.
Cranking it into the upper one-third of the Gain control in HI unleashes a blistering tone with searing high-end harmonics.
Like the original, this expanded Throttle Box includes two Modes, but on this version they're controlled by a HI/LO footswitch that provides two Gain regions to choose from during live performance.
