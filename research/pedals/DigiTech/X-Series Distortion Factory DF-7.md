# DigiTech — X-Series Distortion Factory DF-7

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** DigiTech
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** DigiTech X-Series Distortion Factory DF-7.

## What this pedal is
The **DF-7 Distortion Factory** is a physical DigiTech X-Series distortion-modeling stompbox. Effects Database dates its product record to **August 10, 2005** and identifies it as a DigiTech X Series distortion pedal. DigiTech's manual describes it as a pedal containing seven modeled distortion sounds, with additional EQ control for shaping each model. 

## Controls
The physical DF-7 uses four top-panel control positions, with the first three implemented as dual concentric controls:
- **Level / Low:** Level controls output; Low boosts or cuts the selected model's low frequencies.
- **Mid Freq / Mid:** Mid Freq sweeps the midrange center frequency from **200 Hz to 5 kHz**; Mid boosts or cuts the selected model's midrange.
- **Gain / High:** Gain controls the distortion/overdrive amount for the selected model, while High boosts or cuts high frequencies. For some modeled circuits, High functions as the original pedal's tone/filter control rather than a generic treble control.
- **Distortion Model:** selects one of the seven modeled distortion circuits.

The manual also documents a footswitch for bypass/on operation and multiple output modes.

## Modeled circuits
DigiTech's DF-7 documentation identifies seven modeled dirt circuits:
1. Ibanez TS-9 Tube Screamer
2. DOD Overdrive/Preamp 250
3. Boss DS-1 Distortion
4. Pro Co RAT
5. Boss MT-2 Metal Zone
6. DigiTech Metal Master
7. Electro-Harmonix Big Muff Pi

The archive records these as **models contained in the DF-7**, not as seven separate DigiTech catalog products.

## Output / routing
The DF-7 has one input and two outputs. DigiTech documents three flexible output modes:
- **Mode 1:** one amp output and one mixer output, mono/default.
- **Mode 2:** stereo dual mixer outputs.
- **Mode 3:** stereo dual amp outputs.

The device also supports a stereo spectrum selection in its flexible-output system. The archive records these routing features as hardware functionality, not as separate product identities.

## Power / specifications
The DF-7 can operate from a **9V battery** or an optional **DigiTech PS200R power supply**. A published specification sheet gives approximately **3 1/8 × 4 15/16 × 2 1/8 inches** and approximately **2 lb**.

## Circuit / component evidence
This is a digital/modeling pedal. The reviewed sources document the modeled circuits and the digital effect behavior but do not establish an exact analog clipping semiconductor set for the DF-7's internal signal path. The archive therefore does not assign transistor or diode part numbers.

- **Transistor/device:** Not established from the reviewed model-specific documentation.
- **Diode:** Not established from the reviewed model-specific documentation.

## Historical status
The DF-7 is a historical X-Series model. A manufacturer-distribution discontinuation notice identifies the **DF-7 Distortion Factory** among DigiTech discontinued models. Current used-market and historical dealer records continue to identify the physical pedal by its exact DF-7 model name.

## Sound
The DF-7 is intended to provide seven distinct modeled distortion voices while adding broad EQ control around each model. DigiTech's own material describes the unit as a way to obtain a wide spectrum of distortion sounds from several well-known reference circuits without using separate stompboxes. Contemporary review coverage likewise describes it as a seven-in-one distortion pedal.

## Deep research verification

This pass verifies that **DigiTech X-Series Distortion Factory DF-7** is a genuine physical standalone distortion pedal rather than a processor-only effect model. Effects Database identifies the exact product as an X-Series distortion pedal and dates its record to August 10, 2005. The model-specific manual documents the pedal hardware, its dual-concentric EQ controls, seven distortion models, battery/adapter power, and flexible output modes. B&H independently identifies it as a physical foot-pedal with the same core control layout and dimensions.

The archive deliberately keeps the seven modeled circuits as internal modes of one canonical pedal. It does not promote the modeled reference pedals into duplicate DigiTech identities, and it does not infer unsupported transistor/diode part numbers from the modeled circuit names.
