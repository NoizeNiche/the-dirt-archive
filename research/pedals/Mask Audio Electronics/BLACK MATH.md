## What this pedal is
Mask Audio Electronics describes Black Math as a fuzz inspired by the more compressed and scooped character of an early-2000s NYC Big Muff reissue designed by Fran Blanche. The builder developed the circuit as a one-box route to that blown-out, saturated garage-rock character. [1]

## Controls
- **BOOST:** pre-Muff op-amp boost used to push the fuzz section harder. [1]
- **GAIN:** adjusts fuzz amount and changes how much treble passes at lower settings. [1]
- **TONE:** Muff-style tone control, moving between woolly bass and brighter treble. [1]
- **LOUD:** master output level. [1]
- **BLOWOUT:** removes an early diode-clipping section for a more ragged, higher-gain response. [1][4]
- **Internal MIDS:** adjusts the midrange dip/boost. [4]

## Circuit / architecture
The builder describes Black Math as a Muff-family fuzz with a **pre-fuzz op-amp boost** and a **BLOWOUT** control that changes the early clipping structure. The exact production schematic and complete semiconductor bill of materials are not disclosed in the verified sources. [1][4]
