## What this pedal is

Mask Audio Electronics describes Hot Water as a high-gain fuzz that uses the **same number of transistors normally found in a Big Muff** while extracting roughly four times the gain from them. The builder characterizes the result as heavy, synthy, and compressed while retaining enough dynamics to respond to guitar-volume changes. [1]

## Controls

- **MORE:** gain/fuzz amount; higher settings increase compression. [1]
- **LOUD:** master volume. [1]
