## What this pedal is
Part Garden is a collaboration between **Mask Audio Electronics** and **Collector//Emitter** built around the idea of stacking two fuzz stages. The first stage is relatively warm, while the second is more harmonically rich, and the controls can move the combined circuit from smooth overdrive into extreme fuzz.

## Sound
Part Garden is designed around stacked fuzz stages, with the first side contributing warmth and the second contributing richer harmonics. Its control interaction allows settings ranging from relatively smooth drive to wild, loud fuzz.
