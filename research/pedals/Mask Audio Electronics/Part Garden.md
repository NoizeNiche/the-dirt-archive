## Sound
We spent a long time trying to find the perfect, versatile combination, with the ability to work as a smooth overdrive, a wild fuzz, and anything in between.
The first fuzz is fairly warm and the second is harmonically rich, though this will change as you adjust the controls.
This is a very dynamic pedal, so be sure to use your volume control to get even more range out of it.
