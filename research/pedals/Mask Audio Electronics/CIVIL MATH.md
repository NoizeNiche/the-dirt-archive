## Sound

The first generation of Russian big muffs had less gain and more of a round character then it's American cousins.
I gave the muff side a lower gain transistor set to really give it a different texture and emphasize the roundness.
And then in true MAE fashion, I then took the boost section and completely reconfigured it with double the gain of the normal boost to completely smash it to bits.
