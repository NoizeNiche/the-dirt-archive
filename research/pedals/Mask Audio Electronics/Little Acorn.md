## What this pedal is

Little Acorn is a no-knob fuzz with a deliberately dark voicing and a large amount of gain. Mask Audio describes its tone network as more mid-forward than a conventional scooped fuzz.

## Sound

Little Acorn is intentionally dark, high-gain and mid-forward. Its no-knob format is designed around the builder's chosen fixed voicing rather than live tone adjustment.
