Collector//Emitter and Mask Audio Electronics are excited to introduce Part Garden Part Garden grew from the idea of stacking fuzz pedals.
We spent a long time trying to find the perfect, versatile combination, with the ability to work as a smooth overdrive, a wild fuzz, and anything in between.
