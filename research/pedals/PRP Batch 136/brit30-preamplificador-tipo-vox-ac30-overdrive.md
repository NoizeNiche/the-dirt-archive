# Cajita Stompboxes — BRIT30 preamplificador tipo Vox AC30 overdrive

## PRP identity
- Builder: Cajita Stompboxes
- Catalog type: Overdrive

## Research notes
Cajita describes BRIT30 as a Vox-style preamp/light-to-medium overdrive. Controls: HI, LOW, BRIGTH (Presence), GAIN, VOL. True bypass, mono I/O, 9V center-negative adapter or battery, aluminum enclosure, 12 x 8 cm. The builder says it can be pushed with a booster or used clean for 1960s-style sounds.
