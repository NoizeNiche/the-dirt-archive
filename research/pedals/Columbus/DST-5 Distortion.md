## What this pedal is
Columbus DST-5 is part of a Japanese-made OEM pedal family that also appears under Axtar, Axtron, Cutec, Pick Boy, Polaris, Profile+, Session and Vision branding. The shared platform is documented as an analog distortion pedal for guitar and keyboard, ranging from mild overdrive to saturated distortion with some fuzz-like texture. [1]
