# Columbus - MTL-5 Ultra Metal

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Columbus
- **Catalog identity:** MTL-5 Ultra Metal
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** Japanese compact high-gain distortion from the shared 1980s MTL-5 Ultra Metal OEM family.

## What this pedal is
Columbus MTL-5 belongs to an OEM family that includes Axtar, Axtron, Cactus, Cutec, Pick Boy, Polaris, Profile+, Sakura, Series 10 and Vision examples. The platform is documented as a high-gain analog distortion capable of smooth overdrive through heavier metal-oriented saturation. [1]

## Controls
- **Distortion:** gain/saturation amount.
- **Level:** output volume.
- **Lo:** low-frequency boost/cut.
- **Hi:** high-frequency boost/cut. [1]
