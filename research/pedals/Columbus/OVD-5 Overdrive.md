# Columbus - OVD-5 Overdrive

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Columbus
- **Catalog identity:** OVD-5 Overdrive
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Japanese compact overdrive from the shared OVD-5 OEM family sold under several contemporary labels.

## What this pedal is
The Columbus OVD-5 is part of an OEM family that also includes Loco Box, Axtron, Cactus, Cutec, Hakata HOD-1, Image IOD-9, Luster Proseries, Pick Boy, Profile+ and Vision variants. Effects Database identifies the shared family as Japanese-produced OVD-5 overdrive units. [1][2]

## Controls
A sibling Profile+ OVD-5 record documents the family control set:
- **Level**
- **Tone**
- **Drive** [1]

The Columbus-specific panel should be checked before asserting any cosmetic or labeling differences.
