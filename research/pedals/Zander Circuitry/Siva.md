## Sound

Zander Circuitry describes the Siva as an op-amp take on the classic 1970s Big M-style fuzz circuit. It adds a 3-band EQ, an eight-position clipping selector, and a Blast footswitch that increases drive and activates a second volume setting. Zander’s product page also notes the circuit’s association with the heavier, grittier sound of the op-amp version and its later connection with 1990s alternative-rock tones. [1]
