## Sound

Zander Circuitry describes the Terra Firma as a heavy power-amp distortion built around the LM386 power-amp chip. The builder voices it for slower, heavier music and down-tuned guitars, emphasizing large low-end, fuzzy high-gain textures and substantial output volume. The cited page also documents an optional momentary-switching provision for later builds. [1]
