## Sound

Zander Circuitry describes the American Geek as its transistor-based take on the classic Big M-style fuzz/distortion circuit. It combines a 3-band EQ with an eight-position clipping selector and a Blast footswitch that increases gain and provides a second volume setting. The selectable clipping options include germanium, silicon, LED, asymmetrical variants, transistor/MOSFET, and no-diode operation. [1]
