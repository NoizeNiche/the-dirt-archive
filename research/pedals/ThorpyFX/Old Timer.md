## Sound
The first effect in the chain is the Octave Fuzz based on the Tycobrahe Octavia.
Old school cool with transformer and the ability to lift the octave and use it to create walls of fuzz.
The second effect is our take on the venerable fuzz face in the form of the TACIT BLUE.
