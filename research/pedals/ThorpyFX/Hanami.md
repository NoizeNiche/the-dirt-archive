## What this pedal is
The HANAMI is a germanium fuzz designed as a sonic successor to ThorpyFX's Boneyard. ThorpyFX emphasizes saturated, smooth fuzz tones from a compact control set.

## Sound
Hanami is voiced for smooth, saturated fuzz with substantial sustain and a broad range of textures from its control set. The circuit is intended to respond musically to playing dynamics rather than behaving like a rigid fixed-gain fuzz.
