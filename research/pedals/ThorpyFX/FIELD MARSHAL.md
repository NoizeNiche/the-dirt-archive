## What this pedal is
The FIELD MARSHAL is ThorpyFX's modern take on the **Lovetone Big Cheese** fuzz circuit. ThorpyFX describes the MKII as a refined version of that design.

## Sound
The FIELD MARSHAL is voiced around the unusual, sustaining, octave-rich fuzz character associated with the Lovetone Big Cheese family. ThorpyFX's version emphasizes aggressive fuzz textures and flexible filtering.
