## What this pedal is

Achieving articulate, touch-sensitive performance characteristics via a unique mixture of germanium, LED and silicon diodes arranged to produce asymmetrical clipping, Gamma Drive offers overdrive rich in 2nd order harmonics, which creates sweet, blooming tone.
