## What this pedal is

The Star Eater is an analog fuzz with a downstream boost and filter section. A dedicated filter switch provides two voicings that change the behavior of the Sweep control.

## Sound

The fuzz feeds a booster stage that drives the filter section. A rocker switch selects **Scoop** or **Bump** filter voicings, substantially changing the frequency response of the Sweep control.
