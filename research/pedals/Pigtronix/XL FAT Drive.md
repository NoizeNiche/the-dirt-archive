## Sound

Your new XL FAT Drive is an all analog distortion and boost pedal designed to impart warm, tube sound overdrive along with a powerful clean boost, while remaining transparent and true to your original guitar tone.
The XL FAT Drive is sensitive to your playing dynamics and will respond musically no matter how soft or hard you push it.
Both sections of this pedal are true bypass, allowing you to use either circuit independently or combine both for a massive tone.
