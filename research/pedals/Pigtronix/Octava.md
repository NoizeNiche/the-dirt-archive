## What this pedal is
The Octava is an all-analog octave fuzz and frequency doubler. Pigtronix built it around a front-end JFET drive stage, fuzz circuit, and octave-up section, with a low-pass Filter and Blend control for shaping and mixing the octave effect.

## Versions and factory options
- **Volume:** overall output.
- **Blend:** mixes the octave-up effect.
- **Filter:** low-pass cutoff for the octave circuit.
- **Drive:** adds front-end JFET overdrive.
- **Fuzz:** activates the fuzz circuit.
- True bypass.
- 9-18V DC operation.
- Pigtronix specifies 100mA current draw.

## Sound
The Octava moves between singing octave-up tones, warm front-end overdrive, and thick fuzz. The Filter and Blend controls allow the octave effect to be softened and mixed with the underlying drive/fuzz voice, and Pigtronix specifically notes its usefulness for shoegaze-style fuzz textures.
