## Sound
Compressed overdrive is a sound you can get from your amp, or from pedals, with many variations in tone depending on what order those pedals are in.
The Philosopher’s Tone 2 is a compression sustainer with optional grit, kicking it into high gear when you need it the most.
A Treble control located just before the master Volume provides 6db of cut or boost at 2kHz for fine-tuning the frequency response of the effect.
