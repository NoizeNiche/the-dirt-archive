## What this pedal is
The Philosopher's Tone 2 is a compression/sustainer with a footswitchable **Grit** function that adds an overdriven/distorted edge. Pigtronix pairs the sustaining/compression section with tone shaping so the pedal can function as both a clean sustain tool and a dirtier compressed drive.

## Sound
The compression stage produces sustained, even notes while the Grit switch adds a dirtier harmonic layer when more edge is needed. Pigtronix's ±6 dB Treble control around 2 kHz lets the user fine-tune the brightness of the compressed/overdriven signal.
