## What this pedal is

The Philosopher's Gold is a variation on Pigtronix's Philosopher's Tone optical sustainer, adding an optional layer of germanium-enhanced distortion to the optical compressor/sustainer engine.

## Colorways

- The documented enclosure is **Gold**.
- Finish is treated as part of the model identity, not as a circuit revision.

## Versions and factory options

- Optical compression/sustain section.
- Optional germanium distortion layer.
- Designed to combine sustain/compression with driven harmonic content.
- The checked sources identify the model as a V1-era production pedal.

## Sound

Philosopher's Gold combines the compressed sustain and optical response of the Philosopher's Tone concept with an optional germanium distortion layer. The result can move from sustained clean compression toward thicker, harmonically richer driven tones.
