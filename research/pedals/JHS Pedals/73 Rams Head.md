# JHS Pedals - 73 Rams Head

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** JHS Pedals
- **Pedal:** 73 Rams Head
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz / Distortion
- **Identity:** Limited-run JHS replica of the 1973-style Rams Head Big Muff.

## What this pedal is
JHS describes the 73 Rams Head as a limited-run pedal originally released in October 2013 and re-released in March 2014 exclusively through Chicago Music Exchange in small batches. The design recreates the lower-gain, mid-scooped character of the early-1970s Rams Head Big Muff.

## Controls
- **Volume**
- **Tone**
- **Sustain**

## Circuit / components
- Replica of a 1973-era Rams Head Big Muff circuit.
- JHS states that its unit uses **5088 transistors**, matching the original 1973 Rams Head example JHS owns.
- Exact diode part numbers were not disclosed in the reviewed source.

## History
- Original limited release: **October 2013**.
- Re-release: **March 2014**.
- Chicago Music Exchange exclusive, small-batch production.

## Sound
JHS describes the 73 Rams Head as having somewhat less gain and a pronounced mid scoop compared with other Big Muff eras.
