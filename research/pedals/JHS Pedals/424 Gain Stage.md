## What this pedal is

The 424 Gain Stage is inspired by plugging a guitar directly into a vintage Tascam Portastudio-style 424 recorder. JHS designed it to recreate the elastic, lo-fi gain and compressed character of that recording environment.

## Sound

The 424 Gain Stage ranges from rubbery clean-ish lo-fi tones through compressed overdrive and smashed fuzz-like saturation. JHS designed it specifically around the unusual response of a four-track recorder input.
