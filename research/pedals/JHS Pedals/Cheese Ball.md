## What this pedal is

The Cheese Ball is JHS's tribute to a rare British fuzz/distortion circuit from the 1990s. It uses a four-position mode control to change the gain and EQ character.

- In the bypassed tone-control mode, Tone is disengaged for a brighter, harsher fuzz/distortion voice.

## Sound

The Cheese Ball ranges from British-style fuzz/distortion into brighter, more aggressive textures depending on the mode. The additional mode control is the central part of its tonal range.
