# JHS Pedals - Sweet Tea V3

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** JHS Pedals
- **Pedal:** Sweet Tea V3
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive / Distortion
- **Identity:** Third-generation Sweet Tea 2-in-1 gain pedal combining Moonshine V2 and Angry Charlie V3.

## What this pedal is
JHS describes Sweet Tea V3 as an "Everything Medium-to-High gain 2-in-1" pedal covering light breakup through heavy grind. The circuits can run independently or be stacked in either order.

## Architecture / controls
- **Moonshine V2 side:** overdrive with Volume, Drive, Tone and Clean blend / Proof voicing controls as documented by JHS.
- **Angry Charlie V3 side:** Volume, Drive, Bass, Mid and Treble.
- **Order toggle:** Selects circuit order when stacked.

## Version history
- **V1:** Different icon, no toggles.
- **V2:** Order Toggle and Gain Toggle added.
- **V3:** Updated Moonshine V2 + Angry Charlie V3; top-mounted jacks.

## Power
- **9V DC center negative**
- Approximately **51mA**
- Dimensions: **4.7 x 3.7 x 1.66 in**

Two independent JHS overdrive/distortion circuits. Exact semiconductor part numbers were not established.

The V3 covers medium-to-high gain, from lighter breakup to high-gain distortion, with stack order adding further combinations.
