## Sound
The upper octave is pure bonus: an octave-up distortion that lands somewhere between an Octavia and a Superfuzz.
We've done octave fuzz but we never landed on a sub octave circuit that felt at home in our brand — until now.
CONTROLS VOLUME controls the master output level with extra boost available.
