# JHS Pedals - Calhoun V2

## PRP identity

- **Builder:** JHS Pedals
- **Pedal:** Calhoun V2
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz / Overdrive
- **Identity:** Mike Campbell signature 2-in-1 dirt pedal.

## What this pedal is

JHS describes Calhoun V2 as a collaboration with Mike Campbell containing a vintage overdrive and fuzz circuit for rhythm and lead use.

## Architecture / controls

- **Fuzz side:** Volume, Fuzz, Bias, EQ.
- **Overdrive side:** Volume, Drive, Treble, Bass.
- **Order toggle:** Selects fuzz -> overdrive or overdrive -> fuzz.
- Two independent footswitches permit separate or stacked operation.

## Circuit / lineage

- Fuzz side is based around vintage **Tone Bender** fuzzes.
- Overdrive side is based on the vintage **Silvertone Twin Twelve** amp circuit.
- Exact semiconductor part numbers were not established.

## Sound

JHS describes the fuzz as ranging from lower-gain tones to saturated fuzz via Bias and Fuzz controls. The Twin Twelve side provides vintage overdrive through heavier drive, with dedicated Treble and Bass controls.

## History

Calhoun V2 is the Mike Campbell collaboration version documented by JHS.
