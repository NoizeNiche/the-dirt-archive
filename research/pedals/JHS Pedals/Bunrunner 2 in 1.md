# JHS Pedals - Bunrunner 2 in 1

## PRP identity

- **Builder:** JHS Pedals
- **Pedal:** Bunrunner 2 in 1
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz / Overdrive
- **Identity:** JHS two-in-one Bunrunner combining Astro Mess fuzz and a modified germanium Tone Bender-style circuit.

## What this pedal is

JHS's surviving official Bunrunner documentation identifies the pedal as a two-in-one dirt design developed for Switchfoot's Drew Shirley. One side is the Astro Mess fuzz and the other is a heavily modified Tone Bender-style germanium fuzz.

## Architecture / controls

- **Tone Bender side:** Volume, Tone, Fuzz, Bias.
- **Astro Mess side:** Same control architecture as the standalone Astro Mess, including its gate functionality.
- Later **V2** adds an **Order Toggle**.

## Circuit / components

- Tone Bender-style circuit uses germanium fuzz architecture.
- JHS states the Tone Bender side is a positive-ground circuit internally, but the pedal uses voltage-reversal circuitry so it can run from a normal 9V supply.
- Exact transistor part numbers were not established.

## Version history

- **V1:** Same circuits/enclosure as V2; some units lack the Bunrunner icon.
- **V2:** Adds Order Toggle.

## Sound

The Astro side covers gated/high-gain fuzz; the Tone Bender side ranges from lush overdrive through thick fuzz. The two circuits can be stacked.

## Power

- Standard **9V supply** enabled by internal voltage-reversal circuitry.
