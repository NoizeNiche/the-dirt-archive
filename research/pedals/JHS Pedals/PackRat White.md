## What this pedal is
The PackRat White is the white-finish version of JHS's multi-mode PackRat, a tribute to the evolution of the ProCo RAT. It uses JHS's multi-mode switching architecture to reproduce nine historical RAT-era circuits in one analog signal path.

## Colorways
- **PackRat White:** white enclosure finish.
- The white version is a finish variant of the PackRat design, not a different RAT circuit.
- No other finish chronology is asserted here.

## Versions and factory options
The PackRat's rotary Mode control selects nine documented RAT-family modes.
- **Volume**
- **Distortion**
- **Filter**
- **Mode** rotary selector
- JHS uses a digital switching system to route different fully analog circuit paths.
- Standard 9V DC operation.

## Version changes
- The historical RAT modes represented in the PackRat include different enclosure eras and circuit variants.
- The **White Face** mode is explicitly represented as a historical RAT version; JHS notes that its circuit lineage overlaps with other RAT revisions while its filter behavior differs.

## Sound
The PackRat is designed to cover a broad slice of RAT-family sounds, from overdrive through aggressive distortion and fuzz-like saturation. Switching modes changes the modeled historical circuit behavior while retaining the same core four-control interface.
