## What this pedal is
The Charlie Brown V4 is an amp-in-a-box overdrive inspired by the character of a Marshall JTM45. V4 adds an active three-band tone stack to the earlier Charlie Brown concept.

## Colorways
- V4 is documented with a yellow enclosure.
- Earlier revisions used other finishes.

## Versions and factory options
- **Bass, Middle and Treble** active EQ.
- Version history includes V1, V2, V3 and V4.
- V4 has five controls and a U-shaped control layout.

## Version changes
- V1 used a larger enclosure.
- V4 changed the enclosure color and added the active three-band tone stack.
- The archive does not invent further internal changes without documentation.

## Sound
Charlie Brown V4 is voiced for chewy, British-style amp overdrive with active EQ for shaping the response around different amplifiers and guitars.
