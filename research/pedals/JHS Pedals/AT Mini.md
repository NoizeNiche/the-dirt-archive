## Sound
In 2016 we released Andy Timmons' signature overdrive, the AT — or as everybody ended up calling it, "the @." It started in 2013 when Andy bought a JHS Angry Charlie and made it his main lead channel, beating out the distortion channels of the boutique amps he's known for.
The AT Mini is the original V1 AT circuit, permanently set in Andy's preferred 100-watt mode — the open, loud, less-compressed voicing with the most volume and the most amp-like crunch.
If you need the most authentic British distortion lead and rhythm sound you can find — one that perfectly replicates the punch, saturation, and unmistakable feeling of standing in front of a stack of loud amplifiers — this is it.
