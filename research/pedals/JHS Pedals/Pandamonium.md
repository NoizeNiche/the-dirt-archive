# JHS Pedals - Pandamonium

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** JHS Pedals
- **Pedal:** Pandamonium
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz / Overdrive
- **Identity:** JHS two-channel bass dirt pedal combining 4 Wheeler Fuzz and an SVT-style preamp/overdrive.

## What this pedal is
JHS describes Pandamonium as a two-channel bass pedal. The right side is the 4 Wheeler Fuzz, while the left side is a preamp/overdrive based on the character of an SVT-style circuit.

## Controls / architecture
- **Preamp/overdrive side:** Volume, Drive, Bass, Treble.
- **4 Wheeler Fuzz side:** dedicated fuzz controls plus a Hi/Low switch in V3.
- **Order toggle:** Added in V3 to select which circuit comes first when stacked.
- Two circuits can operate separately or together.

## Version history
- **V1:** Larger enclosure; brown transparent finish.
- **V2:** Current color; Panda Drive section adjusted for more clean headroom.
- **V3:** Smaller enclosure, Order Toggle added, 4 Wheeler side gains Hi/Low switch.

## Circuit / lineage
- Left side described by JHS as SVT-style preamp/overdrive.
- Right side is JHS 4 Wheeler Fuzz.
- Exact semiconductor part numbers were not established.

## Sound
Designed for bass, with a preamp/enhancement side ranging into tube-flavored distortion and a separate fuzz side capable of broad bass fuzz tones. Both may be stacked.
