# JHS Pedals - Kilt V1

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** JHS Pedals
- **Pedal:** Kilt V1
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive / Distortion / Fuzz
- **Identity:** Original first-generation Kilt built with Stu G.

## What this pedal is
JHS's current Kilt history identifies the original Kilt as a 2015 dirt pedal developed with Stu G. The original V1 uses a larger enclosure, two footswitches, an independent boost side, four knobs and four toggles.

## Version history
- **Kilt V1:** Larger enclosure, two footswitches, independent boost on the right side, four knobs and four toggles.
- **Kilt V2:** Smaller enclosure, three knobs and three toggles, Red Remote system.
- **Kilt 10:** 2025 anniversary revision with updated clipping and other changes.

## Circuit / controls
JHS's current history establishes the original control architecture but does not publish a complete V1 knob-by-knob label set on the reviewed page.
- Independent boost section.
- Four-knob main dirt control set.
- Four toggles controlling the original Kilt's gain/voicing functions.

## Circuit / sound
The Kilt spans overdrive, distortion and gated-fuzz territory. Exact semiconductor part numbers were not disclosed.
