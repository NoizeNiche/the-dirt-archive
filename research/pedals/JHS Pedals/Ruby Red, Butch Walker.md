# JHS Pedals - Ruby Red

## PRP identity

- **Builder:** JHS Pedals

## What this pedal is

JHS documents Ruby Red as a collaboration with Butch Walker. The design combines a SuperBolt overdrive section with a custom two-stage boost, and the order can be reversed.

## Architecture / controls

- **SuperBolt side:** Volume, Tone, Drive.
- **Boost side:** Boost.
- **SuperBolt "+" toggle:** Adds sag, compression and gain.
- **Order toggle:** Selects SuperBolt -> Boost or Boost -> SuperBolt.
- Two footswitches operate the two sections independently.

## Circuit / lineage

- SuperBolt overdrive side uses JHS's Supro-inspired architecture.
- Right side is a custom two-stage boost circuit.
- Exact semiconductor part numbers were not established.

## Sound

JHS describes the combination as covering tone enhancement, boost, overdrive, distortion and fuzz, depending on section settings and stacking order.
