# JHS Pedals - SuperBolt V2

## PRP identity

- **Builder:** JHS Pedals
- **Pedal:** SuperBolt V2
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Second-generation SuperBolt, a Supro-inspired overdrive with switchable higher-gain voice.

## What this pedal is

JHS describes the SuperBolt V2 as a 1960s Supro-inspired overdrive. The circuit's Drive control and internal voltage conversion provide a wide gain range, while the front toggle or Red Remote activates the higher-gain setting.

## Controls / switching

- **Volume**
- **Tone**
- **Drive**
- **Gain toggle**
- Red Remote jack for remote switching of the Gain state.

## Version history

- **V1:** Three controls; Hi/Lo toggle.
- **V2:** Hi/Lo toggle replaced by Gain toggle; Red Remote system added.

## Power

- Standard **9V DC center negative**.
- Internal conversion raises the circuit to **18V**.
- Approximately **3mA**.
- Dimensions: **2.2 x 4.8 x 1.6 in**.

## Circuit / lineage

- Inspired by 1960s Supro tube amplifiers.
- Exact semiconductor part numbers were not disclosed in the reviewed documentation.

## Sound

JHS describes clear and gritty Supro-style tones with tube-rectifier-like sag increasing as Drive rises; lower settings can act as a boost while higher settings become substantially more saturated.
