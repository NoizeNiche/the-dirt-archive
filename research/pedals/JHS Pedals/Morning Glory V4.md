## What this pedal is
The Morning Glory V4 is a transparent, low-to-medium gain overdrive designed to preserve the character of the guitar and amplifier while adding edge-of-breakup grit through thicker drive.

## Colorways
- The original Morning Glory used a larger white enclosure.
- **V2:** changed to the smaller gold enclosure.
- The V4 retains the current compact enclosure format and adds the documented V4 feature set.

## Sound
The Morning Glory is designed for transparent-to-mild overdrive, from barely clipping clean tones through thicker rock-oriented breakup. The Gain Toggle provides a second gain level, while the Bright Cut helps match the pedal to brighter amplifiers or pickups. The Red Remote system allows the boost state to be controlled externally.
