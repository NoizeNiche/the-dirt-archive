## What this pedal is
The Angry Charlie V3 is a high-gain British amplifier-style overdrive/distortion pedal designed around JHS's JCM800-style voice. V3 added a Marshall-style three-band tone stack and uses the current red enclosure.

## Colorways
- **V3:** red enclosure.
- JHS identifies the color change as part of the V3 revision.
- No broader custom-finish chronology is asserted for this record.

## Versions and factory options
- **V1:** larger enclosure, three controls, no Presence control.
- **V2:** current-size enclosure, diamond knob layout, Presence control added.
- **V3:** red enclosure, five-control layout with Bass, Middle, and Treble tone stack.
- 9-18V negative-center operation.
- JHS specifies 18V operation for additional headroom and dynamics.

## Version changes
- The V3 revision is explicitly distinguished from V1 and V2 by its red enclosure and three-band tone stack.

## Sound
The Angry Charlie V3 is voiced toward hot-rodded British amplifier distortion. Its Drive and Volume controls interact in an amp-like way, while the active Bass, Middle, and Treble controls provide broad shaping. Lower Drive settings can move toward bluesier breakup.
