# JHS Pedals - Double Barrel V4

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** JHS Pedals
- **Pedal:** Double Barrel V4
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Fourth-generation Double Barrel 2-in-1 overdrive combining Morning Glory V4 and Moonshine V2.

## What this pedal is
JHS describes Double Barrel V4 as a low-to-medium-gain 2-in-1 overdrive. Its two independent circuits can be used separately or stacked, with an order toggle selecting which side comes first.

## Architecture / controls
- Left side: **Morning Glory V4** with Volume, Tone and Drive, plus the gain toggle / Red Remote capability.
- Right side: **Moonshine V2** with its own Volume, Tone and Drive functions plus the added Clean blend control.
- **Order toggle** selects Morning Glory -> Moonshine or Moonshine -> Morning Glory.

## Version history
- **V1:** Slightly darker cream finish; no toggles; six knobs.
- **V2:** White finish; Order Toggle and Morning Glory Bright Cut switch added.
- **V3:** 808 Gain Toggle added.
- **V4:** Updated Moonshine V2 + Morning Glory V4, top-mounted jacks.

## Power
- **9V DC center negative**
- Approximately **108mA**
- Dimensions: **4.7 x 3.7 x 1.5 in**

## Circuit
Two independent JHS analog overdrive circuits. Exact semiconductor part numbers were not established from the reviewed product page.

## Sound
JHS positions the V4 for low-to-medium gain, with stacking options ranging from open transparent overdrive to thicker mid-forward drive.
