## What this pedal is
The RAIL is a full-range fuzz from Pettyjohn Electronics and the first release in its CORE Series. Pettyjohn describes it as a highly versatile fuzz built around a broad gain range and substantial tone-shaping capability.

## Colorways
- Black compact enclosure with the Pettyjohn RAIL artwork.
- Other presentation differences are treated as variants unless the product itself changes.

## Versions and factory options
- Documented source records reference **V1** and **V2** production.
- The GAIN control uses an **eight-position rotary switch** to change the gain structure and overall response.
- Pettyjohn's RAIL documentation covers the model as a standard fuzz and a later production version.

## Sound
RAIL is intended as a full-range fuzz rather than a narrow vintage recreation. The eight-position gain control gives the circuit multiple drive/fuzz behaviors while retaining the broad tone-shaping philosophy Pettyjohn applies across its pedals.
