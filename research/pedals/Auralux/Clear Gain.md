# Auralux — Clear Gain

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Clear Gain
- **Builder:** Auralux
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Transparent boost/preamp designed to add up to about 20 dB of level without deliberately reshaping the instrument's natural frequency balance.

## What this pedal is
Auralux describes Clear Gain as a self-contained preamp that avoids a conventional tone control so the instrument and amplifier retain their own tonal controls. An Intensity control ranges the amount of effect, while the high headroom can be used to push tube-amp preamp stages into natural breakup.

## Sound
Clear Gain is designed to add level without obvious compression or tonal narrowing, so the major distortion character usually comes from the amplifier being pushed rather than from the pedal itself. Rolling the guitar volume back is specifically described as allowing the setup to clean up again.
