# Black Arts Toneworks - Raw Heart OD

## PRP identity

- **Builder:** Black Arts Toneworks
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive

## Research

The Raw Heart OD combines a low-gain version of the Witch Burner with the Quantum Mystic in a dual pedal developed around Mike Scheidt's YOB rig. The Quantum Mystic side provides active three-band EQ and the Witch Burner side is tuned for lower gain, less compression and better dynamics. Each circuit can be used independently or both can be stacked, with a toggle selecting Witch Burner > Quantum Mystic or the reverse order.
