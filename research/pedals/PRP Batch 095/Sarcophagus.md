# Black Arts Toneworks - Sarcophagus

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Black Arts Toneworks
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Fuzz

## Research
The Sarcophagus combines complete Pharaoh and LSTR circuits in one enclosure. A toggle reverses their order, allowing Pharaoh into LSTR or LSTR into Pharaoh. Black Arts Toneworks positions the design for players wanting more gain, low end and volume from the two fuzz circuits together.
