# Black Arts Toneworks - Pharaoh Supreme

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Black Arts Toneworks
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Fuzz

## Research
The Pharaoh Supreme expands the Pharaoh platform with a variable Pre control that sets front-end saturation from 1 to 10 and a six-position clipping selector. The clipping options are BAT41, asymmetrical BAT41, silicon, MOSFET, LED and bypass. Together, Pre and Clip broaden the range from softer, lower-saturation sounds through aggressive fuzz/distortion.
