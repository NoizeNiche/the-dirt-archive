# Black Arts Toneworks - Quantum Mystic

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Black Arts Toneworks
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive

## Research
The Quantum Mystic was developed with Mike Scheidt of Yob as an updated take on a favored early-YOB dirt sound. It is an op-amp-based distortion/overdrive with a three-band active EQ and germanium clipping. The active EQ can cut for older-style sounds or boost the gain stage for heavier saturation, while lower distortion and higher volume settings can produce clear boost tones.
