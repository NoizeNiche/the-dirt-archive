# Black Arts Toneworks - Son of Pharaoh

## PRP identity

- **Builder:** Black Arts Toneworks
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Fuzz

## Research

The Son of Pharaoh is the compact version of the Gold Standard Pharaoh platform. Black Arts Toneworks states that it uses the same parts and components as the Gold Standard Pharaoh and Pharaoh Supreme and retains the same sonic abilities, while shrinking the enclosure to 2.75 × 5 inches. It is handwired and operates from standard 9VDC power.
