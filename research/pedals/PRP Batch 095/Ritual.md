# Black Arts Toneworks - Ritual

## PRP identity

- **Builder:** Black Arts Toneworks
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz

## Research

The Ritual is a deliberately minimal one-knob fuzz. Black Arts Toneworks described it as responsive to the guitar's volume and tone controls: backing the guitar volume down cleans and thins the sound into crunch, while turning it up moves into higher-gain fuzz. The original Ritual was also used as one half of the later Destroyer alongside the Oath.
