# Black Arts Toneworks - Skyboost

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Black Arts Toneworks
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Fuzz

## Research
The Skyboost is a boost that can move from gentle full-range boosting through gritty treble emphasis and into aggressive fuzz. Its controls are Gain and Volume, with Contour switching between bassy and trebly voicings and Range switching between lower- and higher-gain ranges. It runs on 9VDC with negative-center polarity and uses true-bypass switching.
