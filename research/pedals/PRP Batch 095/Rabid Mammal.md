# Black Arts Toneworks - Rabid Mammal

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Black Arts Toneworks
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Fuzz

## Research
The Rabid Mammal V2 combines a Witch Boost-derived boost section with a partially stripped Supreme fuzz section. The boost adds a lower-gain GRN mode, while the fuzz uses Pharaoh-style mids with LED or MOSFET clipping and two RED/GRN toggles for additional mid shaping. Black Arts Toneworks developed it with Matt Pike of Sleep/High on Fire. The current archive listing marks the model out of production.

### Verified sound evidence
The boost section is a Witch Boost, now with added GRN low gain switch.
This is a little less compressed giving a more solid and larger sonic boost.
The fuzz section is a partially stripped Supreme, utilizing the Pharaoh tonestack for blasted mids and LED or mosfet clipping.
