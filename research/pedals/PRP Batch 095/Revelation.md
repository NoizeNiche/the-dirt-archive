# Black Arts Toneworks - Revelation

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Black Arts Toneworks
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive

## Research
The Revelation is a stripped-down amp-pusher with Pre Gain, Gain and Volume controls. Pre Gain sets the overall gain level, Gain controls distortion and the high-output Volume stage is intended to push an amplifier's front end. Black Arts Toneworks also documented SuperLead and SuperBass versions, with the former voiced more aggressively and toward mids, and the latter rounder, bassier and more dynamic. The archive listing marks it out of production.
