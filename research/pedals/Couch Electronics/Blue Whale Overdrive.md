# Couch Electronics - Blue Whale Overdrive

## What this pedal is
Blue Whale is a boutique low-gain overdrive built around a Bluesbreaker-style concept. Couch Electronics and the external Effects Database record it as a touch-sensitive drive that adds volume, brightness and a measured midrange presence while retaining clarity.

## Controls
- **Gain:** amount of overdrive.
- **Tone:** overall brightness/frequency balance.
- **Volume:** output level.

## Switching and power
- True bypass relay switching with optical activation.
- Internal power-up state selector can set the pedal to default **ON or OFF** when power is applied; factory default is OFF.
- 9V DC center-negative power.
- Current draw under 50 mA.
- Dimensions: 70 x 105 x 40 mm including knobs.

## Sound
The verified external record describes a lively, articulate low-gain overdrive with increased gain over a traditional Bluesbreaker, while retaining touch sensitivity and dynamic control. It is intended to enhance the guitar rather than obscure its natural character.
