## What this pedal is
Baker's Dozen is a compact low-gain overdrive from Couch Electronics in Slovenia. The documented design takes its cues from the classic 1990s Marshall BluesBreaker and combines that lineage with a modified clean op-amp boost architecture.

## Controls
- **Volume:** master output level.
- **Tone:** shapes the high-frequency response and can tame treble.
- **Gain:** moves the pedal from clean boost through medium-gain overdrive.
- **Footswitch:** true-bypass relay switching with optical activation.

## Switching and power
- True bypass relay circuit with optical switching.
- Internal power-up state selector lets the pedal default to ON or OFF when power is applied; the documented factory/default state is OFF.
- 9V DC center-negative supply.
- Current draw is documented at approximately 30 to 50 mA depending on the source/version specification.
- Compact enclosure: approximately 70 mm wide x 100 mm deep x 40 mm high, or 105 mm deep including jacks.
- Solid aluminum knobs.

## Versions and factory options

No distinct circuit revision matrix was established from the checked sources. The anniversary edition is documented as a named edition, while the base model is consistently described as the same low-gain BluesBreaker-derived design.

## Sound
The documented response runs from transparent clean boost to medium-gain overdrive, with a comparatively flat EQ response and a tone control focused on smoothing high frequencies. Independent coverage emphasizes touch sensitivity, dynamic response and retention of harmonic detail.

## Construction and provenance
- Hand-built in Slovenia.
- Enclosure and solid aluminum knobs were custom-designed for the pedal.
- Couch Electronics founder Slobodan is identified in the product coverage as the designer/builder.
