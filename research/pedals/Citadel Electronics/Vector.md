# Citadel Electronics — Vector

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Vector
- **Builder:** Citadel Electronics
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Citadel Electronics Vector, a hybrid silicon/germanium Univox Super-Fuzz-inspired octave fuzz with voltage-sag control.

## What this pedal is
Citadel Electronics describes Vector as an octave fuzz using both silicon and germanium semiconductor stages, inspired by the Univox Super-Fuzz with modifications. The product uses a **Wear** control to simulate a dying battery by lowering internal voltage. [1][2]

## Colorways
No complete factory colorway sequence was established.

## Versions and factory options
Documented controls:
- Tone.
- Octave.
- Level.
- Fuzz.
- Wear. [1][2]

Wear ranges the internal voltage from approximately 9V toward 5V, creating more gated and unstable textures. A true-bypass relay with configurable startup state is also documented. [1]

## Version changes
Vector debuted in Citadel's initial Guitar Summit 2025 pedal series. No numbered hardware revision sequence was established. [2][3]

## Transistor
The builder documents a **hybrid silicon/germanium** octave-fuzz circuit. Exact transistor part numbers were not established in the checked sources. [1][2]

## Diode
No exact-model diode specification was established.

## Sound
Vector spans warm vintage fuzz through more aggressive, square-wave-like textures. The Octave control adds an upper octave, while Wear simulates battery sag to make the response progressively more gated and unstable. [1][2]
