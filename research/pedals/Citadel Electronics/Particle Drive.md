# Citadel Electronics — Particle Drive

## PRP identity

## What this pedal is
Citadel Electronics describes Particle Drive as an overdrive designed to capture the essence of a modern British high-gain amplifier. The gain staging ranges from mild overdrive to full high gain and combines distortion with adaptive noise-gate technology from the company's Dynazero. [1]

## Versions and factory options
Documented controls:
- **Level**: output.
- **Gate**: threshold at which the noise gate begins muting/reducing lower-level signals.
- **Tone**: high-frequency response. [1]

Citadel states that the gate detector is placed before the distortion circuit. [1]

Current manufacturer dimensions are approximately 62 mm wide and 58 mm high, with a 250 g weight. [1]

## Sound
Particle Drive is designed to span mild overdrive through full high-gain distortion while keeping noise controlled with the adaptive pre-distortion gate. The gate threshold lets the player decide how much low-level signal is retained or suppressed, while Tone adjusts the post-distortion treble response. [1]
