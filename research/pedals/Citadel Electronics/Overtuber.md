# Citadel Electronics — Overtuber

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Overtuber
- **Builder:** Citadel Electronics
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Citadel Electronics Overtuber, a high-voltage tube overdrive/preamp with automatic bias and startup-state switching.

## What this pedal is
Citadel Electronics describes Overtuber as its interpretation of a classic Tube Driver-style pedal, adding a high-voltage Boost mode and automatic-bias topology. The manufacturer states that the design uses a preamp tube and is intended to provide consistent harmonics and dynamics without requiring manual bias adjustment. [1][2]

## Colorways

## Versions and factory options
Documented controls/features include:
- Level.
- Tone.
- Body.
- Girth.
- Gain.
- True bypass with Initial State switching. [1]

The Initial State switches can determine startup bypass/engaged state and whether boost is active on power-up. [2]

Citadel states that Overtuber is centered around an **ECC83 / 12AX7** tube, while ECC81/12AT7 and ECC82/12AU7 can also operate in the circuit. [2]

The manufacturer's current specifications list 9V center-negative power, approximately 500 mA current draw, and input/output impedances of 1 MOhm / 5 kOhm. [1]

## Transistor
The core preamp stage is tube based, centered on an **ECC83 / 12AX7**. No discrete transistor complement was established. [2]

## Diode
No exact-model diode specification was established.

## Sound
Citadel positions Overtuber as a Tube Driver-style overdrive/preamp with added control over low-frequency body and saturation. Girth interacts with Gain to change the amount of low-frequency signal entering the distortion stage, while Body shapes the post-distortion low end. [1][2]
