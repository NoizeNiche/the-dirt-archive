# Citadel Electronics — Plutonium Overdrive

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Plutonium Overdrive

- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Citadel Electronics Plutonium Overdrive, an analog op-amp bass distortion/overdrive with clean blend and active EQ.

## What this pedal is
Citadel Electronics describes Plutonium Overdrive as an analog op-amp distortion pedal engineered for bass, with a **Crush** control that changes the low-frequency response before distortion and a clean blend. [1][2]

## Colorways
No complete factory colorway sequence was established.

## Versions and factory options
Documented controls:
- Level.
- Crush.
- Blend.
- Drive.
- Bass, ±18 dB.
- Treble, ±15 dB. [1][2]

The pedal uses a true-bypass relay system with configurable startup state. Citadel documents 9V center-negative power and approximately 50 mA current draw. [1]

## Version changes
The pedal debuted as part of Citadel Electronics' first series at Guitar Summit 2025. No numbered hardware revision sequence was established in the checked sources. [3]

## Transistor
The active circuit is documented as **op-amp based**, with no discrete transistor complement published in the checked sources. [1][2]

## Diode
No exact-model diode specification was established.

## Sound
The Crush control transitions the low end ahead of distortion from tight, punchy responses into thicker, warmer fuzz-like saturation. The clean blend retains unprocessed low end and attack, while the active Bass/Treble EQ provides broad post-drive shaping. [1][2]
