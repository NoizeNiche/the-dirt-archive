## What this pedal is
The Box is a dual-channel high-gain distortion pedal developed by Crazybox Pedals after an extended prototyping period. The two channels are independently voiced and share a three-band active EQ.

## Channels and controls
- **Classic Mode:** open, crunchy high-gain distortion associated with hard rock through heavy metal.
- **Viking Mode:** higher-gain distortion extending into modern extreme-metal territory.
- The two distortion channels are independent but share a **3-band active tone stack/EQ**.
- The surviving exact-model source does not preserve a complete reliable knob-label transcription, so individual control names and values are not guessed.

## Sound
Classic Mode is described as a crunchy, open, harmonically rich high-gain distortion, while Viking Mode moves toward denser 1990s-style and modern extreme-metal gain. The pedal is designed to respond to guitar-volume changes with a warm, thick feel rather than harsh high-gain fizz.
