## What this pedal is
The Tone Box is the **Vargas Blues Band Dual Overdrive**, a two-channel overdrive built for Spanish guitarist Javier Vargas and associated with the Vargas Blues Band.

## Controls and operation
The checked historical sources establish the dual-overdrive design and independent channel behavior, but do not preserve a sufficiently reliable complete knob/value transcription. Individual control names and settings are therefore not guessed.

## Sound
The documented design is intended as a flexible dual overdrive for blues and rock applications. Contemporary demonstrations cover both single-coil and humbucker setups and show the pedal into Fender/Bassman-style amplification. The archive avoids attributing a narrower circuit topology without a surviving technical source.
