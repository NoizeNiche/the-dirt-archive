## What this pedal is
The Bass TightDrive Jr. was introduced in June 2016 as one of three bass-specific additions to Amptweaker's JR distortion series, alongside the Bass TightRock Jr. and Bass TightMetal Jr. Amptweaker stated that the Tone and Tight controls were shifted down an octave for bass or extended-range guitars, while the Dry Low control was moved to the side of the small enclosure to preserve low-end weight without blending the entire clean signal back into the distorted path. [1][2][3]

### 1. Bass TightDrive Jr. — original production
**Introduced:** 2016

The original Bass TightDrive Jr. is the compact bass overdrive model introduced with the Bass Jr. series. Its documented controls/features include Gain, Tone, Volume, side-mounted Dry Low, a three-position Fat/Normal/Tight switch for attack, a Plexi/Normal/Smooth EQ switch, an adjustable noise gate, true-bypass switching, and 9–18V operation. ## Version changes
- **Original Bass TightDrive Jr.:** the defining factory configuration already includes the Dry Low blend, rather than requiring the external/full-size Dry Low MOD associated with the larger Bass TightDrive. [1][2]
- The bass-specific voicing moves the Tone and Tight response down an octave compared with the guitar JR counterpart. This is a designed part of the Bass TightDrive Jr.'s identity rather than a later revision. [1][2]
- The Tight control is represented as a **3-position Fat / Normal / Tight switch**, changing note attack from thicker/heavier toward more aggressive/tighter response. [1][2]
- The EQ switch provides **Plexi / Normal / Smooth** settings on the Bass TightDrive Jr. [1][2]
- The adjustable Noise Gate acts on the distorted signal and disappears from the signal path when the pedal is bypassed. [2]

## Controls and functional design
- **Volume:** output level.
- **Tone:** bass-oriented version of the TightDrive Jr. tone control, with its frequency response shifted down an octave for bass/extended-range use. [1][2]
- **Gain:** adjusts the amount of drive.
- **Fat / Normal / Tight:** three-position attack control, from thick/heavy toward aggressive/chunky. [1][2]
- **Plexi / Normal / Smooth:** EQ voicing switch. [1][2]
- **Noise Gate:** manually adjustable gate for controlling noise and hard-stop behavior in the driven signal. [2]
- **Dry Low:** side-mounted blend that brings clean low-frequency content back into the sound while avoiding a full-range clean blend. [1][3]
- **Power:** 9–18VDC; published descriptions note different feel/dynamics at the two voltages, with 9V described as heavier/more compressed and 18V as more open/less distorted. [3]
- **Switching:** true bypass. [1][2]

## Sound
The Bass TightDrive Jr. is a flexible bass overdrive that can move from thick, comparatively restrained drive into tighter and more aggressive distortion, with the EQ and Fat/Normal/Tight controls changing the character of the attack and frequency balance. Its defining bass-oriented feature is the Dry Low blend, which lets the player restore clean low-end weight while keeping the driven upper signal tight and avoiding the phase-shift character of a conventional full-range parallel blend. [1][2][3]

Anecdotal user reports are mixed in emphasis: some players describe it as a useful, amp-like bass overdrive with a strong low-mid focus, while others find its voicing can emphasize low mids or perceive low-end loss in particular rigs. These are user-specific experiences and are retained as listening context rather than treated as universal properties. [4][5]
