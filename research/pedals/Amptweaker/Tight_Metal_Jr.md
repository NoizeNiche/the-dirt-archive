## What this pedal is

The TightMetal Jr is the compact member of Amptweaker's Jr distortion family, derived from the larger TightMetal design and built to retain its high-gain, tight-response character in a smaller enclosure. It combines three main controls with adjustable EQ, attack-shaping and an integrated noise gate. [1][2]

## Controls and architecture

- **Gain, Tone, Volume** controls.
- **EQ switch:** Thrash / Normal / Smooth voicings on the TightMetal Jr. [1][2]
- **Tight switch:** three-position Fat / Normal / Tight attack shaping. [1][2]
- **Noise Gate:** manually adjustable control for controlling residual noise and hard stops. [1][2]
- True-bypass switching. [1][2]
- 9V to 18V operation; the pedal can run from a 9V battery or external DC supply. [2]
- Published current draw is approximately 13mA at 9V and 15mA at 18V. [2][3]

## Versions and factory options

- **TightMetal Jr:** compact guitar-oriented version of the TightMetal family.
- The Jr design replaced the larger model's separate Tight and Fat controls with a three-position Fat / Normal / Tight switch and uses a three-position EQ voicing switch with Thrash on the TightMetal Jr. [1]
- Custom versions and artist/limited graphics exist in the used market, but those cosmetic editions are not treated as separate circuit revisions without evidence.

## Version changes

The Jr version reduced the footprint and control set of the larger TightMetal family while retaining the core high-gain distortion, EQ shaping, attack control and noise-gate functions. The reviewed documentation does not establish a complete component-level revision history for every production run. [1][2]

## Sound

The TightMetal Jr is documented as a dense, high-gain distortion with strong note definition and controllable low-end response. Contemporary testing reports that the pedal remains articulate at higher gain settings, while the Tight control helps keep the response focused for lower-tuned and palm-muted playing. The EQ switch moves between Normal, Thrash and Smooth voicings, and the noise gate can provide fast, hard stops without affecting the clean signal when the pedal is bypassed. [2][4] Other coverage describes the pedal as ranging from thick Fat-oriented distortion to more aggressive Tight settings, with enough flexibility to move beyond strictly modern metal into heavier rock textures. [1][5]
