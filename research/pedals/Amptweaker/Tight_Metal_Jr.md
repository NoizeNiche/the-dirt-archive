## What this pedal is
The Tight Metal Jr was introduced in Amptweaker's first JR distortion series in December 2015 for the 2016 NAMM season. It preserves much of the larger TightMetal/TightRock feature philosophy while eliminating the effects loops, illuminated controls, battery switching, and boost features that consume enclosure space. [1][2]

## Colorways and factory editions
- Standard production is black with the JR-series graphics.
- **Misery Index Limited Edition (2018):** factory-limited cosmetic edition with custom Misery Index graphics. Amptweaker explicitly described it as having the same core tones and dynamics as the regular TightMetal Jr.
- The Misery Index edition is recorded as a **factory special-edition/colorway note**, not a separate pedal or V2, because the documented difference is custom graphics rather than a functional circuit change. [3]

## Versions and factory modifications

### Tight Metal Jr — original production
Documented features:
- Gain, Tone, Volume
- Fat / Normal / Tight 3-position attack control
- Thrash / Normal / Smooth EQ switch
- manually adjustable Noise Gate
- true bypass
- 9–18V operation
- thumbscrew-removable bottom plate [1][2]

## Version changes
The JR's major change from the larger TightMetal family is its compact, simplified format. The continuously variable Tight control and individual Fat configuration were reorganized into a 3-position Fat/Normal/Tight switch, while the EQ became Thrash/Normal/Smooth. [1][2]

The 2018 Misery Index version does not receive a separate parent or numbered version because Amptweaker documented the custom graphics as the distinguishing change. [3]

## Sound
Tight Metal Jr compresses much of the original TightMetal/Pro philosophy into a compact, pedalboard-friendly format, with a hard, immediate attack and a broad high-gain range controlled by the Fat/Normal/Tight and Thrash/Normal/Smooth switches. The built-in gate can be adjusted from subtle noise reduction to very abrupt stops, while 9V operation is more compressed and 18V is more open. [1][3]
