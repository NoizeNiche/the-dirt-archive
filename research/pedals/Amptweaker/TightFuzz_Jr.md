## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** TightFuzz Jr

- **Introduction:** 2017
- **Identity:** Separate compact JR parent derived from the Pro/full-size TightFuzz family.

## What this pedal is
TightFuzz Jr was introduced in 2017 with Bass TightFuzz Jr, BluesFuzz Jr, and Bass BluesFuzz Jr. Amptweaker retained the central TightFuzz architecture while removing the larger pedal's effects loops, illuminated controls, battery switching, and other space-consuming features. [1][2]

## Versions and factory modifications
### TightFuzz Jr — original production
- Volume, Tone, Fuzz
- 60s / 70s / Now EQ switch
- Germanium / Silicon selector
- 3-position Fat / Normal / Tight attack switch
- automatic Fuzz biasing
- true bypass
- 9–18V operation
- thumbscrew removable bottom plate [1][2]

### Factory modifications
- No builder-documented factory MOD or numbered revision verified.

## Version changes
Amptweaker's own release notes state that the JR keeps virtually the same core circuitry as the bigger TightFuzz but replaces the continuous Tight control with the 3-position switch and adds the "Now" EQ position. The smaller enclosure also removes the original effects loop and other full-size hardware conveniences. [3]

## Sound
TightFuzz Jr preserves the core Amptweaker fuzz character while making the EQ and attack choices faster to access on a compact board. Its 60s/70s/Now voicing, Germanium/Silicon selection, and auto-biasing Fuzz control can move from cleaner, softer fuzz through brighter and more aggressive distortion without the full-size model's effects loop and routing hardware. [1][2]
