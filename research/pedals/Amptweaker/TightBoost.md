# Amptweaker — TightBoost

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** TightBoost
- **Builder:** Amptweaker
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive / Boost
- **Introduction:** 2010
- **Identity:** Lower-gain TightDrive-family pedal and separate parent from TightDrive.

## What this pedal is
TightBoost was developed immediately after the TightDrive and was intended to provide less gain, more output, and a cleaner way to push an amplifier. Its unusual feature is the Mid control, which gradually adds a parked-wah-style mid voice selected from two factory-chosen frequency options. [1][2]

## Versions and factory modifications
### TightBoost — original production
Documented features:
- Boost, High, Mid, Tight controls
- 2-position Mid/Wah frequency switch
- Pre/Post selectable effects loop
- SideTrak-style bypass loop behavior
- true bypass when off; buffered operation when on
- battery on/off switch
- illuminated controls on adapter power
- magnetic sliding battery door
- 14-gauge steel housing [1][2]

### Factory modifications
- No separately documented builder MOD or numbered production revision was verified.

## Version changes
The Mid/Wah frequency choices were finalized through the builder's user-feedback process at the 2010 Nashville Amp Expo. Amptweaker selected two preferred parked-wah frequencies, one more forward/screaming and one more classic-throaty, and placed them on a dedicated switch. This is part of the original production design, not a later user mod. [1]

## Sound
TightBoost is the cleaner, lower-gain side of the TightDrive family, intended to push an amplifier or another gain stage while keeping plenty of output headroom. The Tight control can sharpen the attack and low end, while the Mid/Wah section adds a controllable vocal-like push that can move from a strong mid-forward boost toward a throatier parked-wah character. [1][2]
