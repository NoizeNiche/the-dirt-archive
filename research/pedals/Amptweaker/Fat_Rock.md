# Amptweaker — Fat Rock

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Fat Rock
- **Builder:** Amptweaker
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Historical alias:** FatRock Distortion
- **Introduction:** 2013 announcement
- **Identity:** Fat Rock and FatRock Distortion are treated as one parent pedal. The catalog name "FatRock Distortion" is retained as an alias, not a second pedal.

## What this pedal is
Fat Rock was introduced as a lower-end-extended variation of TightRock, aimed at players who wanted the TightRock concept with more low-frequency mass and a permanently available front-end Fat boost. The builder also retained the SideTrak loop and expanded the low range of the Tone and Tight controls. [1][2]

## Colorways

## Versions and factory modifications
### Fat Rock / FatRock Distortion — original production
Documented features include:
- Gain, Tone, Volume
- Tight control with bass-extended range
- Tone control with extended low range
- fixed front-end Fat boost
- gain-tracking noise gate
- SideTrak loop
- true bypass
- LED/battery management of the full-size generation [1][3]

### Factory modifications
- No separately documented builder MOD or numbered revision was verified.
- The "FatRock Distortion" naming is treated as an alias for the same parent.

## Version changes
Fat Rock's key differentiation from TightRock is functional: its Tone and Tight controls were extended downward, a fixed Fat boost was built into the front end, and the output EQ received added low-end emphasis. These design changes are why Fat Rock is preserved as a separate parent from TightRock even though it is explicitly described as a variation of that platform. [1]

## Sound
Fat Rock is a warmer, bigger low-end interpretation of the TightRock concept, with the added front-end Fat boost making the distortion feel thicker before the main gain stages. It is designed to keep aggressive rock/metal attack while carrying more low-frequency body than the tighter original. [1][3]
