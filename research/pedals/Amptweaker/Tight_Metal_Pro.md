## What this pedal is
TightMetal Pro expanded the original TightMetal into a larger performance platform with dual boost stages, three-band EQ, Mid Boost, multiple voicing switches, a gain-tracking gate with Chomp, and three independent effects loops. It was announced at Summer NAMM 2013 as the two-button Pro counterpart to TightMetal. [1][2]

## Version changes
The Pro adds independent pre- and post-distortion boost staging to the TightMetal platform. One boost raises distortion gain while the other provides a post-distortion level lift, and the Boost loop can be used for effects that should appear only when the boost footswitch is active. [1]

## Sound
TightMetal Pro is a dense, articulate high-gain distortion that can move from aggressive rhythm crunch into very saturated metal while keeping the attack unusually controlled. The Tight control, three-band EQ, dual boost architecture, and gate/Chomp system make it a highly configurable high-gain rig rather than a one-voice metal box. [1][2]
