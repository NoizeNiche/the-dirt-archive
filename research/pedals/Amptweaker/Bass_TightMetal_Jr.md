## What this pedal is
Bass TightMetal Jr was one of three bass-specific JR pedals introduced in 2016. Amptweaker retained the TightMetal's basic high-gain architecture but shifted the Tone and Tight responses downward for bass and added the side-mounted Dry Low blend to preserve low-frequency weight. [1][2]

## Versions and factory modifications
### Bass TightMetal Jr — original production
Features documented by the builder and contemporary product material include:
- Gain, Tone, Volume
- Dry Low side control
- Fat/Normal/Tight attack switch
- Thrash/Normal/Smooth EQ
- manually adjustable noise gate
- true bypass
- 9–18V operation
- removable bottom plate [1][2]

### Factory modifications
- No separate builder-documented factory MOD or numbered revision was verified.
- The Dry Low circuit is part of the production bass model.

## Version changes
The defining bass changes are the lower-frequency Tone and Tight response and the added Dry Low blend. The TightMetal Jr's gate only affects the distorted path and drops out of the signal when the pedal is bypassed, leaving the clean signal unaffected; the gate also does not clamp the Dry Low path. [1]

## Sound
Bass TightMetal Jr is aimed at aggressive, high-gain bass distortion while retaining enough low-end weight to keep the instrument substantial in a mix. The Dry Low control lets the player add clean low frequencies underneath the tightly gated distortion, while the Thrash/Normal/Smooth and Fat/Normal/Tight controls move the character from hard-edged grind toward thicker, heavier distortion. [1][3]
