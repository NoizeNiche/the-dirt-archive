## What this pedal is
The Bass BluesFuzz is Amptweaker's bass-oriented fuzz based on the BluesFuzz design. Verified references document a low-gain germanium fuzz circuit with boost, Mid EQ, a Dry Low control, and a Fat/Normal/Tight attack selector.

## Controls and features
- **Volume, Tone, Fuzz, Mid, Dry Low**
- **Boost** for additional input drive
- **Fat / Normal / Tight** attack selection
- Automatically adjusting bias is documented for the fuzz controls
- True bypass

## Sound
The verified references describe the design as capable of thicker fuzz tones through its boost and of cleaner, more controlled bass fuzz through the Mid, Dry Low, attack, and bias controls. The circuit is documented as using a low-gain germanium transistor, which is central to the design's intended response.
