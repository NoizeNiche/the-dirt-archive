## Versions
1. **BluesFuzz Limited (2014)** — original 49-piece limited model that established the BluesFuzz design.
2. **Bass BluesFuzz Jr. (2017)** — bass-oriented JR derivative.

## Version changes
- The Bass BluesFuzz Jr. was derived from the limited BluesFuzz design and moved it into Amptweaker's smaller JR format.
- The bass version adds **Dry Low**, allowing clean low frequencies to be blended back into the fuzzed signal.
- The JR version uses a three-position **Fat / Normal / Tight** attack switch rather than the larger pedal's continuously adjustable Tight control.
- The BluesFuzz design uses a **low-gain germanium output transistor** and a Mid control. Amptweaker described the bass version as a cleaner, more vintage-small-bass-amp-oriented variation.
- These are functional/version changes, not cosmetic revisions.

## Sound
The Bass BluesFuzz Jr. sits between a warm, low-gain fuzz and an overdrive-like pushed amplifier sound rather than behaving like an extremely saturated fuzz. Its low-gain germanium character is intended to stay relatively smooth and dynamic, while the Mid control can add presence and the Dry Low control keeps the bass foundation intact. User reports describe it as clear and dynamic, with enough gain for heavier sounds when the boost is engaged, but still capable of cleaner, fatter tones.
