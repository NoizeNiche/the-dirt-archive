# Amptweaker — Tight Rock

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Tight Rock
- **Builder:** Amptweaker
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive
- **Original introduction:** 2011
- **Identity:** TightRock is one parent pedal record with documented production generations. It was designed to bridge the original TightDrive and TightMetal concepts.

## What this pedal is
The original TightRock was positioned between TightDrive and TightMetal: more gain than TightDrive, less extreme than TightMetal, with a PlexEQ-style tone approach, gain boost, Tight control, and gain-tracking gate. Amptweaker later returned to the TightRock name in the compact Performance Series with a reduced control set and the modern compact enclosure. [1][2][3]

## Colorways
- Standard legacy production used a red full-size enclosure.
- Amptweaker discussed a special pink charity edition of TightRock in 2011. This is recorded as a factory historical colorway/special edition, not a separate version, because the documented distinction is appearance/charity packaging rather than a functional circuit change. [1]
- Current compact production uses the standard compact Amptweaker styling.

## Versions and factory modifications

### TightRock — legacy full-size generation
**Period:** 2011-era
- Gain, Tone, Volume
- Tight control
- Gain Boost
- PlexEQ/tone shaping
- gain-tracking Noise Gate
- Effects loop / SideTrak architecture
- true bypass and full-size Amptweaker hardware [1][2]

### Tight Rock — current compact Performance Series generation
**Period:** 2023-era Performance Series
- Gain, Tone, Volume
- 3-way tone stack
- Fat & Tight switching
- built-in Noise Gate
- true bypass
- compact enclosure
- 9–18V operation [3][4]

### Factory modifications / special editions
- No numbered factory MOD was verified.
- The pink charity version is a cosmetic/historical special edition only.

## Version changes
The compact Performance Series Tight Rock is documented separately within this parent record because the physical format and control architecture changed substantially from the legacy full-size model. It should **not** be assigned an invented "V2" label unless Amptweaker itself eventually documents one; the archive should describe it as the later compact/Performance Series generation. [3][4]

The original TightRock was a bridge between TightDrive and TightMetal, while the later compact version carries forward the TightRock name and core distortion concept with a simplified control set. [1][3]

## Sound
Legacy TightRock occupies the middle ground between Amptweaker's smoother overdrive and its harder metal distortion, keeping a tighter attack but avoiding the extreme saturation of TightMetal. The later compact generation is deliberately simpler, with a 3-way tone stack and Fat/Tight switch that preserve the punchy, dynamic rock character in a much smaller footprint. [1][3]
