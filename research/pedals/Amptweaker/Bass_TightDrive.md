## What this pedal is
The Bass TightDrive was developed after bassist Greg Weeks tried the original guitar TightDrive at the January 2010 NAMM show. James Brown then altered the design for bass, extending the control ranges into lower frequencies while retaining the TightDrive concept and its adjustable attack/tone shaping. The pedal was announced for release in May 2010. [1][2]

## Versions and variants

### 1. Bass TightDrive — original full-size version
**Period:** 2010-era introduction and subsequent production.

The original Bass TightDrive is the bass-specific version created from the guitar TightDrive platform. It uses the full-size Amptweaker enclosure and includes the core TightDrive controls/features, including the Tight control and rear effects loop with selectable Pre/Post placement. [1][2]

### 2. Bass TightDrive Dry Low MOD
**Period:** Documented by the early 2010s; commercially documented as a MOD version by 2018.

The Dry Low MOD adds a control that blends filtered clean low-frequency content back into the driven signal. Amptweaker's James Brown explained that the added dry signal is intentionally rolled off in the highs before blending, so the player can recover low-end weight without creating the unwanted phase-shifted high-frequency character of a full-range clean blend. [3][4]

**Classification:** Variant/modification of the Bass TightDrive, not a separate parent pedal.

### Separate pedal: Bass TightDrive Jr.
The Bass TightDrive Jr. was introduced in 2016 as part of Amptweaker's Bass Jr. series. It uses the smaller JR enclosure and has its own product identity and feature set, including a built-in Dry Low blend. For the archive, it should remain a separate parent pedal and receive its own PRP record later. [5][6]

## Version changes
- **Original -> Dry Low MOD:** adds the Dry Low control and the associated clean-low blend circuit. The purpose was to restore some bass foundation lost when using the Tight control, especially at more aggressive settings. [3][4]
- **Bass TightDrive -> Bass TightDrive Jr.:** not treated as a version change inside this record. The JR was introduced as a separate named product in a later JR product series, with a smaller enclosure and several reconfigured controls/features. [5][6]
- No evidence was found in this pass supporting a numbered V1/V2 designation for the full-size Bass TightDrive itself.

- **Archive status:** Unknown.

## Sound
The Bass TightDrive is a bass-focused overdrive designed to move from relatively restrained drive and amp-like thickening into much heavier, pick-responsive distortion while keeping control over the low-end attack. The Tight control can shift the feel from a bigger, rounder bottom end toward a tighter and more aggressive response, while the Dry Low MOD can restore clean low-frequency weight when stronger Tight settings thin the foundation. [1][2][3]
