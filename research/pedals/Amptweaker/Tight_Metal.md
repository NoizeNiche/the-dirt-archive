# Amptweaker — Tight Metal

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Tight Metal
- **Builder:** Amptweaker
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Original introduction:** January 2011
- **Identity:** The original full-size Tight Metal is the parent record. Later JR, Pro, and Pro II products are separate parent records in the catalog.

## What this pedal is
Tight Metal was Amptweaker's first purpose-built high-gain metal pedal, developed from the TightDrive/TightBoost family with additional gain stages and a more aggressive metal voicing. The original full-size model paired a Tight control with a Mid/Thrash EQ, gain-tracking noise gate, and the larger Amptweaker hardware system. [1][2]

## Colorways
- Early prototypes used shiny black and later textured black finishes; the builder has discussed those changes as development/styling history rather than circuit revisions.
- Standard production is associated with the black full-size enclosure.
- Cosmetic finish differences are not counted as versions.

## Versions and factory modifications

### Tight Metal — legacy full-size production
Documented features include:
- Volume, Tone, Gain
- continuously adjustable Tight control
- Mid/Thrash EQ switching
- gain-tracking Noise Gate with Chomp behavior
- effects loop with Pre/Post switching
- illuminated controls on external power
- battery on/off management and magnetic battery access
- true bypass
- 9–18V operation [1][2]

### Factory modifications
- No separately documented factory MOD or numbered revision was verified for the original Tight Metal.
- Later Tight Metal Pro, Tight Metal Jr, and Tight Metal Pro II products are kept as separate parent records.

## Version changes
The defining historical change for the Tight Metal family is the expansion into Pro and JR formats, not an internally documented V1/V2 change to this original parent. The original pedal itself was based on the TightDrive platform but added extra gain stages, a metal-oriented EQ, and a gate/Chomp system for aggressive playing. [1]

The current compact Tight Metal sold by Amptweaker is presented as a later compact product using the Tight Metal Pro II distortion circuitry, so it is documented elsewhere rather than silently rewriting this 2011 full-size record. [3]

## Sound
Original Tight Metal is a focused, high-gain distortion built around fast note attack, aggressive pick response, and a controllable low end rather than loose vintage fuzz behavior. The Tight control, Mid/Thrash EQ, and gate/Chomp system make it especially suited to precise palm-muted metal and rhythm work while retaining enough gain for saturated lead tones. [1][2]
