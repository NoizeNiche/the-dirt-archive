# Amptweaker — Bass TightFuzz Jr

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Bass TightFuzz Jr
- **Builder:** Amptweaker
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Introduction:** 2017
- **Identity:** Separate JR parent pedal derived from the full-size Bass TightFuzz. It is not treated as a mere enclosure-size version of the large pedal.

## What this pedal is
The Bass TightFuzz Jr was part of Amptweaker's 2017 Fuzz Jr expansion. The JR retains most of the big pedal's tone-shaping ideas while eliminating the large pedal's effects loop, illuminated controls, battery switch, and other space-consuming features; the bass version retains the Dry Low blend. [1][2]

## Colorways
- Documented examples use the compact silver/tan Amptweaker JR enclosure.

## Versions and factory modifications
### Bass TightFuzz Jr — original production
Factory-documented features include:
- Volume, Tone, Fuzz
- automatic biasing tied to the Fuzz control
- Germanium/Silicon selector
- 60s/70s/Now EQ/gain switch
- Fat/Normal/Tight attack switch
- side-mounted Dry Low control
- true bypass
- 9–18V operation
- removable bottom plate with thumb screw [1][2][3]

### Factory modifications
- No separate numbered factory revision or builder MOD was verified.
- Dry Low is part of the production Bass TightFuzz Jr specification.

## Version changes
Compared with the larger Bass TightFuzz, the JR substitutes a 3-position Fat/Normal/Tight switch for the large pedal's continuously variable Tight control and adds the 'Now' EQ position. It also drops the larger pedal's effects loop, illuminated knobs, and battery switch to fit the JR enclosure, while retaining the important bass-oriented Dry Low blend. [1][2]

## Transistor
- **Technology:** selectable Germanium / Silicon output transistor.
- Exact transistor part numbers are not documented in the sources checked.
- The factory describes germanium as warmer/lower gain and silicon as brighter/higher gain. [1]

## Diode
- **Type:** Unknown.

## Sound
The Bass TightFuzz Jr preserves the unusually broad range of the larger TightFuzz, from cleaner, lightly biased fuzz textures through aggressive silicon sounds, but packages it in a much smaller chassis. Its Dry Low control lets the bass foundation stay substantial while the attack and EQ switches move the fuzz between thicker vintage character and tighter modern response. [1][3]
