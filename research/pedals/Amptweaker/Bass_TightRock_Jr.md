## Sound
Amptweaker's Dry Low blend knob is also included….a very popular feature from the Bass TightFuzz™, which helps maintain the lowend while allowing the distortion to be chunky and tight.
An EQ switch similarly provides Plexi(Thrash on TMJR)/Normal/Smooth tone settings, which helps the pedals work with a broad range of rigs.
In addition to Gain, Tone and Volume knobs, there's even a manually-adjustable Noise Gate which can be cranked to stop notes hard and fast….this gate only afects your distorted tone and automatically goes away when the pedal is bypassed.
