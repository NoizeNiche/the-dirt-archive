# Amptweaker — Bass TightRock Jr

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Bass TightRock Jr
- **Builder:** Amptweaker
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Introduction:** 2016
- **Identity:** Separate bass-specific JR pedal based on the TightRock platform.

## What this pedal is
Bass TightRock Jr was introduced with the Bass JR series in 2016. It keeps the compact JR format while moving the Tone and Tight controls down an octave for bass and adding the side-mounted Dry Low blend. [1][2]

## Versions and factory modifications
### Bass TightRock Jr — original production
Documented features:
- Gain, Tone, Volume
- Dry Low
- Fat/Normal/Tight attack switch
- Plexi/Normal/Smooth EQ
- manually adjustable noise gate
- true bypass
- 9–18V operation
- thumbscrew battery access [1][3]

### Factory modifications
- No separate numbered factory revision or builder MOD was verified.
- Dry Low is part of the production bass model, not an aftermarket modification.

## Version changes
The bass model shifts Tone and Tight response downward from the guitar JR version and incorporates Dry Low to retain the low end. Amptweaker also retained the 3-position Fat/Normal/Tight attack control and Plexi/Normal/Smooth EQ arrangement from the JR architecture. [1][2]

## Sound
Bass TightRock Jr emphasizes a thick, harmonically dense distortion that can stay articulate while producing a pronounced low-mid growl. The Plexi mode can pull the voicing away from that mid emphasis, while Dry Low allows a substantial clean low-frequency foundation beneath the distorted signal. [3][4]
