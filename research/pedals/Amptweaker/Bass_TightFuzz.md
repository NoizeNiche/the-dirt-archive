# Amptweaker — Bass TightFuzz

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Bass TightFuzz
- **Builder:** Amptweaker
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **First documented introduction:** November 2012 press announcement for the 2013 NAMM season.
- **Identity:** Bass-specific adaptation of the TightFuzz platform. It remains a distinct parent pedal from the guitar TightFuzz and from Bass TightFuzz Jr.

## What this pedal is
Amptweaker introduced the Bass TightFuzz as a carefully retuned version of the TightFuzz for bass. The builder moved the Tight, Edge, and Tone controls down an octave and added the Dry Low control so players could tighten the fuzz while restoring clean low-frequency weight. [1][2]

## Versions and factory modifications
### Bass TightFuzz — original full-size production
The original production model includes:
- Volume, Tone, Fuzz, Tight, and Dry Low controls
- 60s/70s voicing switch
- Germanium/Silicon transistor switch
- Edge/Smooth switch
- Effects loop with Pre/Post switching
- illuminated controls when externally powered
- battery disconnect/access system
- true bypass
- 9–18V operation [1][2]

### Factory modifications
- No separate builder-documented factory MOD or numbered revision was verified in this pass.
- The Dry Low feature is part of the original Bass TightFuzz production design, not a later post-sale user modification.

## Version changes
The principal change from the guitar TightFuzz platform was bass-specific retuning: Tone, Tight, and Edge were shifted down in frequency, and Dry Low was added to retain the low end while allowing the fuzz section to be tightened. The Germanium/Silicon selector changes the output device from a softer, lower-gain germanium stage to a heavier, higher-gain silicon stage; the builder also used automatic biasing to make the Fuzz control cover cleaner through heavier sounds. [1][3]

## Transistor
- **Technology:** selectable Germanium / Silicon output transistor.

## Sound
The Bass TightFuzz covers everything from lightly hairy, tube-like bass drive to thick, square-edged fuzz, while the Tight control focuses the attack and low end. The Dry Low control is central to its bass design, restoring weight when aggressive Tight settings or heavier fuzz would otherwise make the foundation feel thinner. [2][4]
