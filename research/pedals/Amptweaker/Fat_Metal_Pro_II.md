# Amptweaker — Fat Metal Pro II

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Fat Metal Pro II
- **Builder:** Amptweaker
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Product generation:** Performance Series Pro II generation, introduced around 2020
- **Identity:** Separate parent from Fat Metal Pro. The Pro II is a substantially redesigned preamp/distortion platform, not a cosmetic revision.

## What this pedal is
Fat Metal Pro II is a high-gain rock/metal preamp designed around controlled low-end response, especially for extended-range guitars and styles that need substantial chunk and chug. The later Pro II platform adds Depth Finder and DeFizzerator processing, a balanced DI, cab simulation, headphone output, and three independent loops while retaining dual boost controls. [1][2]

- Smooth Edge and Thrash switching
- Noise Gate
- Depth Finder with Resonance and Presence
- DeFizzerator
- dual boost controls, one before and one after distortion

## Version changes
The major change from the earlier Fat Metal Pro is the move to the Pro II platform, with analog Depth Finder power-amp-style shaping, DeFizzerator high-frequency control, direct-output tools, and expanded routing. The product remains explicitly aimed at controlled low-end high gain, including Black Metal and Doom applications. [1][2]

## Sound
Fat Metal Pro II is built for thick, forceful high-gain distortion with unusually deliberate control over low-end weight and high-frequency harshness. Depth Finder adds resonance/presence-style shaping, while DeFizzerator trims excessive high-end fizz, making the pedal suitable as either a front-end effect or a direct preamp for recording and live rigs.
