# Amptweaker — Tight Metal Pro II

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Tight Metal Pro II
- **Builder:** Amptweaker
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Introduction:** 2020/2021 Pro II generation
- **Identity:** Major redesign and separate parent from the earlier TightMetal Pro.

## What this pedal is
TightMetal Pro II moves the TightMetal Pro concept into Amptweaker's later Performance Pro II platform. It adds analog Depth Finder power-amp-style shaping, DeFizzerator high-frequency control, direct-recording tools, and expanded routing while retaining dual boosts and the three-loop architecture. [1][2]

- DeFizzerator
- dual Boost controls, one pre- and one post-distortion
- Global, Boost, and Side Track loops

## Version changes
The move from TightMetal Pro to Pro II is substantial enough to preserve separate parent records: the later platform adds Depth Finder, DeFizzerator, cab simulation, balanced DI, headphone output, and a redesigned physical/electronic architecture. The Pro II was already being discussed as a new product in late 2020 and appears in 2021-era launch material. [1][3]

## Sound
TightMetal Pro II is designed as a complete high-gain preamp/recording tool rather than simply a distortion stompbox, combining tight low-end metal gain with power-amp-style depth shaping and high-frequency fizz reduction. Its direct outputs and multiple loops make it especially useful for recording or complex live rigs where the distortion needs to function as the entire front end. [1]
