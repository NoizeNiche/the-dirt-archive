# Amptweaker — TightFuzz

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** TightFuzz
- **Builder:** Amptweaker
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Introduction:** 2012
- **Identity:** Original full-size TightFuzz parent. Bass TightFuzz, TightFuzz Jr, TightFuzz Pro, and Tim Lefebvre TightFuzz remain distinct named products where the builder gave them separate identities.

## What this pedal is
TightFuzz was Amptweaker's 2012 response to requests for a more controllable classic transistor fuzz. It combines a Fuzz Face-style transistor section with Amptweaker's Tight low-cut/attack control, auto-biasing Fuzz control, selectable germanium/silicon output stage, 60s/70s voicing, Edge/Smooth shaping, and a Pre/Post effects loop. [1][2]

## Colorways
- The final production finish was shiny silver. Amptweaker says NAMM attendees selected the shiny silver treatment during the prototype process.
- Prototype component/finish experiments are documented as development history, not separate production versions. [3]

## Versions and factory modifications
### TightFuzz — original production
- Volume, Tone, Fuzz, Tight
- 60s / 70s tone switch
- Germanium / Silicon transistor selection
- Edge / Smooth switch
- automatic Fuzz biasing
- Pre/Post effects loop
- illuminated controls on adapter power
- battery on/off system
- magnetic battery access
- true bypass [1][2]

### Factory Boost MOD
Amptweaker demonstrated a **Boost MOD** for TightFuzz in 2013. It is documented as a builder-installed modification to the TightFuzz platform, so it belongs inside this parent record rather than becoming a separate pedal. The existence of the MOD is established; the exact production population of factory-modified units is not established in the sources checked. [4][5]

## Version changes
The production TightFuzz itself went through prototype voicing work before release, including resistor/capacitor experimentation, auto-bias refinement, Edge/Smooth tuning, and final color selection. Aion FX's trace of a production TightFuzz specimen found a two-transistor NPN Fuzz Face-style section: Q1 and Q3 are NEC 2SD1616 silicon devices and Q2 is an AC127 germanium device; the switch changes the active output transistor between silicon and germanium. [6]

## Transistor
- **Technology:** NPN transistor fuzz stage with selectable silicon/germanium output.
- **Exact traced parts:** Q1/Q3 = NEC 2SD1616; Q2 = AC127 germanium.
- **Evidence limitation:** these values are from Aion FX's traced production specimen, not an Amptweaker parts list proving every production unit used identical parts. [6]

## Diode
- **Type:** No diode was established as a defining clipping device in the pedal-specific trace reviewed.
- **Archive status:** Unknown / not a defining documented diode stage.

## Sound
TightFuzz combines vintage Fuzz Face-style behavior with unusually modern control over the low end and attack. The Germanium setting is softer and lower gain while Silicon is brighter and higher gain; the auto-biasing Fuzz control allows the range to move from unusually clean boost-like territory into heavy fuzz, and the Edge/Smooth and 60s/70s controls move the texture from sharper/tighter to thicker and smoother. [1][6]

### Verified sound evidence
While most vintage fuzz boxes have few controls, the TightFuzz includes a Tone control to tame the fuzzy edges and the Tight control, a mainstay of Amptweaker pedals, which varies the pick attack from smooth and thick to tight and aggressive.
This is particularly helpful for tweaking a fuzz tone, since many can be too flubby on the lowend.
With requested Boost and Germanium/Silicon switches, this cool pedal also has the ability to get lower gain tones easily, with an Auto Bias that adjusts appropriately with the Fuzz control, to get the cleanest and nastiest distortions possible with one control.
