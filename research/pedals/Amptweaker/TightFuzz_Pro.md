# Amptweaker — TightFuzz Pro

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** TightFuzz Pro
- **Builder:** Amptweaker
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Introduction:** 2015
- **Identity:** Separate Pro-series parent derived from the full-size TightFuzz.

## What this pedal is
TightFuzz Pro takes the original TightFuzz concept into Amptweaker's expanded two-button Pro architecture. It retains Germanium/Silicon switching, 60s/70s voicing, Smooth Edge, the Tight control and automatic Fuzz biasing, then adds a three-band EQ, dual boost controls, selectable octave-up, three effects loops, an adjustable gate and an internal More Fuzz switch. [1][2]

## Versions and factory modifications
### TightFuzz Pro — original production
- Volume, Low, Mid, High, Fuzz, Tight
- Boost Volume and Boost Fuzz
- 60s / 70s switch
- Germanium / Silicon selector
- Smooth Edge
- automatic Fuzz biasing
- selectable blended Octave-Up in Boost
- internal More Fuzz switch
- adjustable Noise Gate
- Universal, Boost and SideTrak loops
- illuminated controls
- true bypass
- 9–18V operation [1][2]

### Factory modifications
- No separately documented numbered revision or builder MOD verified.
- The More Fuzz and Octave-Up functions are factory features of the Pro, not aftermarket modifications.

## Version changes
The Pro is a substantial expansion rather than a cosmetic revision of TightFuzz. Amptweaker added the three-band EQ, dual boost staging, octave-up blend, three-loop routing, gate and More Fuzz control while preserving the core TightFuzz architecture. [1]

## Transistor
- **Technology:** Germanium / Silicon selectable output-transistor architecture.
- Exact TightFuzz Pro production parts were not independently established.
- The original TightFuzz trace identifies 2SD1616 silicon and AC127 germanium in one traced specimen, but that does not prove identical parts across all Pro units. [3]

## Sound
TightFuzz Pro keeps the responsive, Fuzz Face-style foundation of TightFuzz but adds enough EQ and gain staging to operate as a complete live/studio fuzz platform. The Tight control focuses attack and low end, the Germanium/Silicon selector moves between softer and brighter/higher-gain behavior, and the boost/octave system can push the sound into a more cutting psychedelic lead range. [1][4]
