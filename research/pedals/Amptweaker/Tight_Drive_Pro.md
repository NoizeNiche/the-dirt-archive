# Amptweaker — Tight Drive Pro

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Tight Drive Pro
- **Builder:** Amptweaker
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Introduction:** 2014
- **Identity:** Separate two-button Pro-series parent derived from Tight Drive, with substantially expanded boost, EQ, gate, and loop architecture.

## What this pedal is
Tight Drive Pro takes the original Tight Drive concept into Amptweaker's Pro format, adding independent pre- and post-distortion boost controls, a 3-band EQ, multiple voicing controls, a gain-tracking gate, and three effects loops. Contemporary review material describes the Pro as capable of everything from light overdrive to heavy distortion, which is broader than the original pedal's simpler control set. [1][2]

## Version changes
The Pro is a distinct product because it adds an entire dual-boost/routing architecture rather than merely changing the enclosure. One boost stage sits before the distortion for more saturation, while the other can increase output after the distortion; the boost system can also activate its dedicated loop for lead effects. [1]

## Sound
Tight Drive Pro starts in the territory of articulate low-to-medium overdrive and can be pushed into much heavier distortion by stacking its gain and boost controls. The Tight control changes attack and tonal emphasis significantly, while the expanded EQ and three-loop routing make it a much more programmable live and studio distortion platform than the original Tight Drive. [1][2]
