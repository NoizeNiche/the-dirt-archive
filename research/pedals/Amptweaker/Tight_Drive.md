# Amptweaker — Tight Drive

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Tight Drive
- **Builder:** Amptweaker
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Introduction:** NAMM 2010; first Amptweaker product
- **Identity:** Foundational full-size Tight Drive from the original Amptweaker product generation. Later compact/reworked products are documented separately where the builder presents them as separate named products.

## What this pedal is
Tight Drive was Amptweaker's first product and established James Brown's approach to adjustable attack, gain range, and tone shaping. Contemporary records place its introduction at NAMM 2010, with shipping beginning shortly afterward. [1][2]

## Colorways
- Early production was associated with the original full-size Amptweaker enclosure and green/industrial finish treatments.
- Builder interviews document that prototype finishes changed during development, but those appearance changes are not treated as separate versions.
- No meaningful colorway version was verified.

## Versions and factory modifications
### Tight Drive — original full-size production
Key documented features:
- Gain, Tone, Volume
- continuously variable Tight control
- Fat switch
- 3-way EQ/Tone stack
- built-in noise gate
- effects loop with Pre/Post switching
- illuminated controls on adapter power
- battery on/off management
- magnetic/removable battery access
- true bypass
- 9–18V operation [1][3]

### Factory modifications
- No builder-documented factory MOD was verified as a separate production variant in this pass.

## Version changes
The original Tight Drive platform was later developed into additional Pro and JR products, but those are separate parent records when the builder gave them distinct product identities. The guitar Tight Drive's documented circuit philosophy includes multiple gain stages, variable low-cut behavior, clipping, a Big Muff-style tone stage, and gain recovery, but those details come from a pedal-specific Aion trace rather than from the builder's published component list. [4]

## Diode
- **Type:** Zener diodes are documented in the Aion trace of the guitar Tight Drive clipping section, including 1N4733A 5.1V zeners; however, this is technical trace evidence from a specific guitar Tight Drive specimen rather than a builder parts list. [4]
- **Archive status:** 5.1V zener clipping is a usable technical note for the traced guitar Tight Drive, but exact production-wide diode population remains uncertain.

## Sound
Tight Drive was designed to move far beyond a mild boost, covering nuanced amp pushing through heavy overdrive while keeping the attack adjustable with the Tight control. Its 3-way tone structure and responsive gain range make it a flexible rock overdrive whose character can move from softer and darker to brighter and more immediate. [2][3]
