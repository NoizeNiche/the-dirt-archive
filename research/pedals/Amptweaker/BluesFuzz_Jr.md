# Amptweaker — BluesFuzz Jr

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** BluesFuzz Jr
- **Builder:** Amptweaker
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Introduction:** 2017
- **Identity:** Compact JR descendant of the 49-unit BluesFuzz Limited Edition.

## What this pedal is
Amptweaker brought the limited BluesFuzz concept into the JR line in 2017, retaining the low-gain germanium character while adding a Mid control and Boost switch. Like the other Fuzz Jr models, it uses a smaller enclosure and drops the larger models' effects loops, illuminated controls, battery switch, and other size-consuming features. [1][2]

## Versions and factory modifications
### BluesFuzz Jr — original production
Documented features include:
- low-gain germanium transistor
- Fuzz, Tone, Volume
- Mid control
- Boost switch
- 3-position attack control
- 60s/70s/Now EQ
- automatic biasing on the Fuzz control
- true bypass
- 9–18V operation
- thumbscrew battery access [1][2]

### Factory modifications
- No separate numbered factory revision or MOD was verified.
- The Boost function is part of the factory BluesFuzz Jr design.

## Version changes
Compared with the full-size limited BluesFuzz, the JR shrinks the platform and removes the larger model's effects-loop and accessory features. It retains the low-gain germanium output stage and Tight/Boost concept while converting the attack control to a 3-position Fat/Normal/Tight arrangement and adding the Mid control to shape the important midrange region. [1][3]

## Sound
BluesFuzz Jr is the cleaner, softer-spoken member of this fuzz group, favoring low-gain germanium breakup that can approach a pushed small tube amp at low Fuzz settings. The Mid control gives the player more authority over the center of the sound, while Boost adds extra thickness and the attack switch can move the response from heavy to more aggressive. [1][2]
