# Amptweaker — BluesFuzz

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** BluesFuzz
- **Builder:** Amptweaker
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Introduction:** 2014
- **Production:** Limited run of 49 units.
- **Identity:** Distinct limited-edition parent pedal that later served as the direct basis for the BluesFuzz Jr family.

## What this pedal is
The BluesFuzz was created after an Amptweaker NAMM tuning session showed a preference for lower-output germanium transistors for blues-oriented sounds. James Brown and the company chose a 49-pedal limited run as a deliberate special model tied to the Browns' personal Highway 49 history and the blues tradition. [1][2][3]

## Colorways
- Documented production examples use the tan/gold enclosure with brown graphics.
- The limited run's unit numbers are individual production identities, not separate versions.
- Cosmetic differences are not treated as versions.

## Versions and factory modifications
### BluesFuzz Limited Edition — 49-unit production
The documented design includes:
- low-gain germanium output transistor
- Fuzz, Tone, Volume
- Tight control
- auto-bias tied to the Fuzz control
- Boost Switch MOD
- effects loop with Pre/Post switching
- magnetic battery access
- true bypass [1][2]

### Factory modification
**Boost Switch MOD** is documented by Amptweaker as part of the limited model's factory feature set. It provides roughly 10 dB of additional gain, useful for thicker fuzz or for compensating for low-output vintage pickups. This is a builder-supplied modification, not a post-sale user modification. [2]

## Version changes
The BluesFuzz is essentially a specialized lower-gain germanium take on the TightFuzz family rather than a sequence of numbered revisions. The major documented feature distinction is the builder-installed Boost Switch MOD and the low-gain germanium output stage, which shifts the pedal toward cleaner and more blues-oriented behavior. [1][3]

## Transistor
- **Technology:** low-gain Germanium output transistor.
- Exact part number was not established in the sources checked.
- Contemporary Amptweaker material specifically distinguishes this lower-output germanium choice from the higher-output transistor used in the broader TightFuzz development. [1][2]

## Diode
- **Type:** Unknown from builder/technical sources checked.
- A user report states a germanium diode, but that is not being promoted to archive fact without pedal-specific technical documentation.

## Sound
BluesFuzz is a lower-gain, touch-responsive fuzz aimed at worn-in, amp-like blues textures rather than maximum saturation. The germanium output stage and auto-biasing make the Fuzz control unusually usable at lower settings, while the Tight control can push it toward more focused, classic fuzz attack. [1][2]
