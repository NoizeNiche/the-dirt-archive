## What this pedal is
Tight Drive Jr was introduced in the 2016 Amptweaker JR series as the small-format counterpart to the larger Tight Drive family. The JR design removes large-enclosure features such as the effects loops, illuminated controls, battery switching, and some boost functions while retaining the essential TightDrive response and adding a 3-position attack switch. ## Version changes
The most important change from the larger Tight Drive is structural and control-based rather than cosmetic: the continuously variable Fat/Tight arrangement becomes the 3-position Fat/Normal/Tight switch, and the JR keeps a simplified signal/routing system to achieve its smaller footprint. Running at 18V is documented as providing more headroom and less compression than 9V; this is operating behavior, not a version change. [1][3]

## Sound
Tight Drive Jr keeps the core Amptweaker idea of a highly responsive overdrive that can move from amp-like push toward heavier drive, but packages the attack and EQ choices into compact 3-position switches. The result is designed to stay tight and articulate while giving thicker Fat settings and smoother or more Plexi-like tonal paths. [1][3]
