- **Identity:** Compact JR parent derived from the Pro-series TightRock architecture. It is separate from the full-size Tight Rock parent.

## What this pedal is
The Tight Rock Jr was introduced as part of Amptweaker's 2016 JR Distortion Series, alongside TightDrive Jr and TightMetal Jr. Amptweaker specifically designed the JR format by removing the larger pedals' effects loops, illuminated controls, battery switches, and boost features, then reorganizing Tight/Fat into a three-position Fat/Normal/Tight attack switch. [1][2]

## Version changes
The defining change from the larger TightRock/Pro platform is the compact control architecture rather than a new circuit identity: Fat and Tight become a 3-position attack switch, the EQ becomes Plexi/Normal/Smooth, and the large routing/boost hardware is removed. Amptweaker's own description says the JR units retain most of the tone-tweaking features of the larger counterparts. [1]

## Sound
Tight Rock Jr is voiced for the broad zone between classic Plexi-style crunch and modern high-gain rock, with the three-way EQ providing unusually broad midrange shaping for a three-knob compact pedal. The Fat/Normal/Tight switch changes the attack from thicker and more singing to aggressive and chunky, while the adjustable gate can tighten high-gain passages without affecting the clean signal when bypassed. [1][3]
