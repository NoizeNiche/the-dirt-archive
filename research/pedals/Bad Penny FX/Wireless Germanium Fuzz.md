# Bad Penny FX - Wireless Germanium Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Wireless Germanium Fuzz
- **Builder:** Bad Penny FX
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Handbuilt germanium fuzz following a Tone Bender MkIII circuit structure, with additional gate and bass controls.

## What this pedal is
The Wireless is a three-transistor germanium fuzz built around a Tone Bender MkIII-style structure. Bad Penny FX and contemporary coverage document individually selected germanium devices, internal trimming, and a control set of Fuzz, Tone, Master, Gate, and Bass.

## Colorways
- A documented production build uses a **brushed/etched silver aluminum face with an English ash hardwood frame**.
- No complete factory colorway chronology was verified.

## Versions and factory options
### Wireless Germanium Fuzz
- Fuzz
- Tone
- Master
- Gate switch
- Bass switch
- Internal transistor-balance and gate adjustment trimmers
- Neutrik mono jacks
- 22 AWG cloth wiring
- True-bypass switching
- Internal Faraday shielding
- Individually selected germanium transistors

## Version changes
No numbered factory revision was established in the checked sources. A 2021 serial-numbered Wireless example and the contemporary 2021 review both identify the same Wireless model.

## Transistor
- **Technology:** Germanium fuzz, PNP, three-transistor circuit.
- **Documented devices:** NKT275-equivalent germanium devices are explicitly documented; Gear Hero also notes possible vintage CV7005 or ASY37SV devices depending on the build.
- Devices are selected for gain profile and audio response.

## Diode
- **Exact clipping/protection diode:** Not publicly documented in the checked sources.

## Sound
The Wireless is described as highly responsive to guitar input level, moving from a sweeter, lower-gain response toward high-gain fuzz as the controls are opened up. The Gate and Bass switches further shape the attack and low-frequency content while the Tone and guitar volume controls provide additional cleanup and contouring.
