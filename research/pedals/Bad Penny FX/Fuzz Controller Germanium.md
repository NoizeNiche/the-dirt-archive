# Bad Penny FX - Fuzz Controller Germanium

## PRP identity

- **Builder:** Bad Penny FX
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Fuzz Controller Germanium, the germanium member of Bad Penny FX's Fuzz Controller line.

## What this pedal is

Bad Penny FX's historical range explicitly lists a **Fuzz Controller Germanium** separately from the silicon-oriented Fuzz Controller V2. The archive therefore keeps it as its own named product rather than folding it into the broader Fuzz Controller family.

## Controls / circuit

Exact external control layout and a dedicated Germanium-version schematic were not reliably recovered. The archive does not copy the control set or semiconductor complement from the documented silicon Fuzz Controller V1/V2 into this model.

## Sound

The surviving exact-product catalog evidence identifies the pedal as the Germanium version of the Fuzz Controller family, but it does not preserve enough Germanium-specific tonal text for a more precise claim.

## History

Bad Penny FX was a UK builder active from 2017 until retirement. A historical British-builder survey lists **Fuzz Controller Germanium** as a distinct product in the company's range alongside Fuzz Controller V2 and Wireless Germanium Fuzz.

## Deep research verification

### Exact-product evidence

The historical Bad Penny FX range listing names **Fuzz Controller Germanium** as its own product. This establishes the model as a distinct catalog identity, not merely a colorway or undocumented modification of the standard Fuzz Controller.

### Family evidence kept separate

The documented 2019 Fuzz Controller uses a modern high-gain silicon transistor and a selectable clipping network. The later V2 is documented as a redesigned silicon fuzz with additional power filtering and boost circuits. Those details are intentionally **not** assigned to Fuzz Controller Germanium because the reviewed sources do not prove that the Germanium version shares the same circuit.

Bad Penny FX's separate **Wireless Germanium Fuzz** is also a distinct product and is not treated as interchangeable with Fuzz Controller Germanium.
