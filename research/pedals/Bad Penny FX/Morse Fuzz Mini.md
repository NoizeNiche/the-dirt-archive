## Deep research verification

This pass ties the archive's **Morse Fuzz Mini** identity to the surviving builder/retailer record, which is sold under the shorter model name **Morse**.

### Verified description

The surviving Bad Penny FX product description identifies **Morse** as the "miniest version" of the Fuzz Controller, with a **toggleable dual-stage boost** stacked in front of the fuzz circuit. The product is described as a **high-gain silicon fuzz** with a rear gate switch.

### Verified controls and architecture

- One external knob: **Volume**, which also behaves as the fuzz control and rolls off some high end as it is reduced.
- Rear toggle: gate switch, taking the pedal between deep fuzz and a more extreme gated setting.
- Internal trimmers govern the gain and total volume of the two boost stages.
- The unit uses a compact board and is explicitly described as not using SMD construction.
- The surviving Reverb specification lists the product simply as **Morse**; the archive retains its canonical catalog identity as **Morse Fuzz Mini** because that is the historical catalog name being archived.

### Verified transistor/device terms

- **Silicon fuzz:** explicitly documented.
- **Exact transistor part number:** Not documented.

### Verified diode terms

- **Exact clipping/rectifier diode:** Not documented.

### Verified sound evidence

The product documentation describes the fuzz as **ridiculously high gain**, with the gate switch moving from deep/brutal fuzz toward a more extreme gated character. The volume/fuzz control also reduces high-end content as it is backed down.
