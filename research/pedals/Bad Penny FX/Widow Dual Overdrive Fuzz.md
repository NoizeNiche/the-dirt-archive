# Bad Penny FX - Widow Dual Overdrive/Fuzz

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** Widow Dual Overdrive/Fuzz
- **Builder:** Bad Penny FX
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Fuzz / Overdrive
- **Identity:** A historical Bad Penny FX dual-dirt pedal combining overdrive and fuzz functions.

## What this pedal is

Guitar Pedal X's 2023 British pedal-builder survey lists the **Widow Dual Overdrive/Fuzz** in Bad Penny FX's range. A 2020 Goliath FX demo describes the Widow as an overdrive with stackable fuzz, providing independent real-world confirmation of the combined dirt role.

## Historical context

- Guitar Pedal X places Bad Penny FX in Newhaven, East Sussex and dates the builder from 2017 until retirement.
- A Reverb listing documents a Bad Penny FX Widow Fuzz from 2021, finished in Blue & Black, made in the United Kingdom. The seller describes it as a hand-built high-gain fuzz with an onboard boost.
- The Reverb listing is catalog evidence for a Widow-branded production unit, but it does not by itself establish the full factory revision history of every Widow variant.
