# Bad Penny FX — The Fuzz Controller V1

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** The Fuzz Controller V1
- **Builder:** Bad Penny FX
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Original 2019 Fuzz Controller design using a high-gain silicon transistor fuzz stage, externally adjustable bias, supply-starve control and selectable 1N4148/red-LED clipping. [1]

## What this pedal is

The 2019 Fuzz Controller is the first documented Bad Penny FX original pedal. Its control system exposes bias/gain, master level, voltage-starve behavior and frequency-cut switches so the player can move from conventional fuzz through unstable, highly compressed or overdrive-adjacent textures. [1]

## Controls / circuit

- **GAIN / BIAS** control.
- **MASTER VOLUME**.
- **STARVE** switch using a 47k supply-resistance path.
- **DIODE:** 1N4148 or red LED.
- **BASS CUT** switch.
- **TREBLE CUT** switch.
- True bypass. [1]

## Transistor

- **Technology:** High-gain silicon fuzz.
- Exact transistor part number is not published in the reviewed listing.

## Diode / clipping

- **1N4148** or **red LED**, selected by the diode control.
- The listing describes the 1N4148 path as harder clipping and the red-LED path as softer/more overdrive-like behavior. [1]

## Construction

The 2019 build uses:
- Neutrik jacks.
- Vintage Philips, K40Y-9, Sprague Vitamin Q and Tropical Fish capacitors.
- Vintage carbon resistors.
- 22 AWG cloth wiring.
- Hand-shaped aluminum top and English Ash enclosure. [1]

## Power

- Standard Boss-type **9V center-negative** supply. [1]

## Version history

- **2019 V1:** Original/debut Fuzz Controller.
- **Retrofit identification:** The source distinguishes the original build from later retrofit work through its component selection, including the 3.3µF Philips input capacitor.
- **V2:** Fully redesigned and maintained as a separate archive record. [1][2]

## Sound

The combination of high-gain silicon fuzz, supply starve, selectable clipping and bass/treble cuts allows V1 to move from aggressive fuzz toward gated, reduced-voltage and harder/softer clipping textures. [1]
