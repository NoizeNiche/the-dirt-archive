# Bad Penny FX - The Fuzz Controller V2

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** The Fuzz Controller V2
- **Builder:** Bad Penny FX
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Fully redesigned high-gain silicon Fuzz Controller with added power filtering, boost circuitry, and starve control.

## What this pedal is
Bad Penny FX described V2 as a full redesign that retains its high-gain silicon fuzz character while adding power filtering and boost circuits to each cut plus the starve control. Internal trim pots set the maximum output of the individual stages.

## Colorways
- A documented V2 example uses a **black faceplate with a natural English-ash wood frame**.
- No complete factory colorway chronology was verified.

## Versions and factory options
### V2
- Fully redesigned circuit
- High-gain silicon fuzz
- Added power filtering
- Added boost circuits
- Starve control
- Internal trim pots for maximum stage output
- Neutrik 1/4-inch jacks
- Vintage Philips, K40Y-9 and Sprague Vitamin Q paper-in-oil capacitors
- 22 AWG cloth wiring
- True bypass
- Internal Faraday shielding

## Version changes
V2 is explicitly described by Bad Penny FX as a full redesign rather than a cosmetic refresh. The documented changes are the added power filtering, boost circuits, starve control and stage-output trim adjustment.

## Sound
V2 keeps the Fuzz Controller's aggressive, high-gain silicon foundation while expanding how each section can be driven and voiced. The added filtering, boost stages, starve control and internal output trims make the revised circuit more configurable than the earlier design.
