# Bad Penny FX - Dluxe Drive

## PRP identity

- **Builder:** Bad Penny FX
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Dluxe Drive, a limited Bad Penny FX overdrive.

## What this pedal is

Dluxe Drive is documented in Bad Penny FX's historical range as an overdrive. Surviving Reverb records show at least one six-unit collaboration run and preserve the exact model name.

## History

- Bad Penny FX was a UK builder active from 2017 until retirement.
- A documented Reverb example identifies a six-piece collaboration run with Marcus Dluxe.
- The broader Bad Penny historical range also lists Dluxe Drive among the builder's products.

## Deep research verification

This pass adds only claims supported by the historical range documentation and surviving exact-model Reverb listings.

### Verified description

The **Dluxe Drive** is a Bad Penny FX collaboration with Marcus Darby, also known as Marcus Dluxe. Guitar Pedal X lists it in Bad Penny FX's historical range and identifies Bad Penny FX as a UK builder founded in 2017 and later retired. A surviving Reverb listing identifies a **six-piece** collaboration run and describes the pedal as a drive/overdrive product.

### Verified version references

- The surviving Reverb listing explicitly states that **six pieces were ever made** in the collaboration run.
- No numbered production revision was established.
- The model is retained as its own historical catalog entry rather than being merged into another Bad Penny drive.

### Verified diode terms

### Verified sound evidence

The surviving Reverb description characterizes Dluxe Drive as a drive pedal and describes it positively as a voiced drive effect, but it does not provide technical tonal parameters suitable for a more detailed archival summary. The archive therefore retains the documented drive classification without assigning an unverified circuit lineage.
