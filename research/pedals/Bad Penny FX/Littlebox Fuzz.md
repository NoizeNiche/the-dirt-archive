## Deep research verification

This pass adds historical production and sound evidence from surviving exact-model references.

### Verified description

Littlebox Fuzz is a Bad Penny FX historical fuzz produced through a collaboration with **Funny Little Boxes**. A surviving demonstration identifies it as a custom/limited-run pedal and describes it as being based around a **Fuzz Face-style sound**.

### Verified version references

- A contemporary collector discussion states that the Bad Penny/Funny Little Boxes LittleBox run consisted of **10 pedals**.
- The model is retained as a distinct named historical product rather than collapsed into the builder's other fuzzes.
- No numbered factory revision was established in the reviewed sources.

### Verified transistor/device terms

- **Exact transistor/device:** Not established in the reviewed sources.
- The Fuzz Face-style description is treated as circuit-family context, not as proof of an exact transistor complement.

### Verified diode terms

- **Exact clipping/rectifier diode:** Not established in the reviewed sources.

### Verified sound evidence

The surviving demo describes LittleBox as a **Fuzz Face-style** fuzz with a limited-run/custom character. The collector discussion independently describes the pedal as a favorite fuzz and notes the small production quantity, but those comments are retained as community context rather than as a technical factory claim.
