## What this pedal is
Circuitbenders describes Demon Seed as a fuzz/distortion/frequency-shifting/8-bit/lo-fi pedal using the **Holtek HT8950 voice changer chip**. The HT8950 converts the analog guitar signal to 8-bit digital, applies selectable frequency-shifting or robot-arpeggio effects, then converts the signal back to analog. [1]

## Versions and factory options
Documented controls:
- Level.
- Fuzz.
- Frequency.
- Vibro switch.
- Three effect-mode switches controlling six frequency-shift/robot modes.
- Footswitch. [1]

Modes include:
- Proteus IV robot arpeggio.
- Three upward frequency-shift modes.
- Frequency Fuzz.
- Three downward frequency-shift modes. [1]

Power is standard 9V center-negative or internal 9V battery. [1]

## Transistor
The core active processor is the **Holtek HT8950 IC** with input op-amp stages. No exact discrete transistor complement was established. [1]

## Diode
No exact-model clipping diode specification was established.

## Sound
Demon Seed ranges from crushed and gated fuzz through severe digital frequency shifting and robot-arpeggio textures. The Freq control changes the HT8950 clock speed, sample rate, bandwidth, and aliasing character. [1]
