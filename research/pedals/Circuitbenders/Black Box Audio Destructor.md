## What this pedal is
Circuitbenders describes the Black Box Audio Destructor as a small standalone processor built from scratch rather than a circuit-bent commercial machine. It can move from thin crunch and gritty fuzz into severe gated, compressed, bitcrushing, and synth-like effects. [1]

## Sound
The input/drive control determines how hard the signal hits the processor. Higher input drive produces increasingly compressed, crushed, and distorted sounds; backing it down can produce gating and gritty fuzz. The frequency control can introduce pseudo-ring-modulator behavior with low-frequency source material. [1]
