## What this pedal is
Circuitbenders describes the Black Box Audio Destructor as a small standalone processor built from scratch rather than a circuit-bent commercial machine. It can move from thin crunch and gritty fuzz into severe gated, compressed, bitcrushing, and synth-like effects. [1]

## Sound
The input/drive control determines how hard the signal hits the processor. Higher input drive produces increasingly compressed, crushed, and distorted sounds; backing it down can produce gating and gritty fuzz. The frequency control can introduce pseudo-ring-modulator behavior with low-frequency source material. [1]

### Verified color/finish evidence
- ;-) This is a special very limited edition version of our popular Black Box Audio Destructor FX unit.
- Normally these units are housed in small black desktop cases but in this case the effect has been mounted in a recycled heavy duty cast metal wah case so you can control the frequency of the effect using your foot.
- In every mode the audio is distorted and ground up at a frequency selected by the rocker pedal, Selecting Crunch mode gives a spluttering crunchy distorted sound that can sound like anything from an old fuzz pedal with a nearly dead battery, to a mixing desk with every light running in the red.

### Verified sound evidence
What we don't do is disappear up our own asses with an overblown obsession with a mythical perfect guitar 'tone', and then jack the price up by 500% for no apparent reason.
The unit can create a wide range of bitcrushing, gating, distortion and throaty synthlike frequency effects with an intensity that can be anything from a gentle thin crunch to a terrifying wall of unstoppable noise.
The controls consist of a input / drive level knob on the right of the case that sets how much of the signal is sent into the effect, an output level knob, a 3 way mode selection switch and a frequency control in the form of a wah rocker pedal.
