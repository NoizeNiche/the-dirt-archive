## What this pedal is
Baa Bzz is Anarchy Audio's updated and refined interpretation of the fuzz section of the Roland Bee Baa. It is designed to cover the original's thick wool and mid-scooped buzz voices while adding more practical control. [1][2]

## Colorways
- Documented retail examples include grey and copper hammertone finishes. [2][3]

## Versions and factory options
- SHIFT blends the original mid-scooped and mid-bumped voices.
- TONE provides bass/treble shaping.
- GAIN controls sustain, VOLUME controls output, and FILTER changes the top-end character from smoother to raspier. [2]

## Sound
Baa Bzz is a high-gain fuzz with long sustain, spanning thick woolly fuzz through sharper, mid-scooped buzz textures. The SHIFT, TONE and FILTER controls make the classic Bee Baa-inspired voice substantially easier to shape than the original circuit. [1][2]
