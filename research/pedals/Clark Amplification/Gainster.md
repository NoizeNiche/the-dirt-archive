# Clark Amplification — Gainster

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Gainster
- **Builder:** Clark Amplification
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Clark Amplification Gainster, a hand-wired overdrive/boost designed to add gain while preserving the guitar's tonal character.

## What this pedal is
Clark and Effects Database describe Gainster as an overdrive/boost designed to add gain with minimal coloration, preserving the nuances of the guitar and player while producing a tweed-like growl. The original Clark version uses full-size components, hand wiring, and point-to-point construction. [1][2]

## Colorways
- The original Clark version is documented with a **gold cover and hand-painted details**. [1]
- A smaller white version was later manufactured for Clark by Barber Electronics. [1]

## Versions and factory options
The documented core controls are:
- Level
- Tone
- Gain [1]

The original large-box Clark build uses full-size components on fiberboard and hand wiring. [1]

## Version changes
Premier Guitar documents at least two distinct physical implementations: the original larger Clark build and a smaller white Barber-manufactured version. They are treated as historical build variants rather than assuming an undocumented circuit change. [2]

## Sound
Clark describes Gainster as an even-handed boost/overdrive with no intentional Tube Screamer-style mid hump or major high/low loss, and with strong preservation of pick attack. [1]
