# Bardic Audio Devices — The Beast

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** The Beast
- **Builder:** Bardic Audio Devices
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Fuzz / Overdrive
- **Identity:** Discrete-op-amp dirt circuit combined with a separate clean/boost channel and blendable octave-up, with switchable order between the dirt and boost stages. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
Delicious Audio describes The Beast as a highly tweakable dirt pedal centered around an op-amp distortion section and a separate clean boost/octave section. Increasing GAIN transforms the dirt channel from distortion into a **Muff-style gated fuzz** over the latter part of the control's rotation. [1]

## Controls
- **GAIN:** Dirt-channel gain. At roughly the last 35–40% of rotation, the dirt section moves into gated-fuzz behavior.
- **TONE:** High/low content balance.
- **SCOOP:** Variable midrange scoop, from heavily scooped to flatter response.
- **DIRT:** Distortion-channel output level.
- **CLEAN:** Clean-channel output level.
- **OCTAVE:** Amount of octave-up blended into the boost signal.
- **BOOST:** Boost-channel output level.
- **ORDER:** Reverses the order of dirt and boost sections. [1]

## Circuit architecture
- **Dirt:** Discrete op-amp distortion/fuzz.
- **Boost:** Clean boost with blendable octave-up.
- **Order switching:** Boost can hit the dirt section before it for extra saturation, or follow it and raise the combined signal into the amplifier. [1][2]
- Two footswitches provide independent dirt and boost engagement.

## Sound
The Beast moves from open/aggressive distortion into a tight gated Muff-style fuzz at higher Gain. The Tone and Scoop controls reshape the dirt section, while the clean/boost channel can add low-end, clarity or octave-up content. The Order switch changes whether the boost saturates the dirt stage or simply raises its output. [1][2]
