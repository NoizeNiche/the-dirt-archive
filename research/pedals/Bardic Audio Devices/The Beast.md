## What this pedal is
The Beast combines an op-amp-based dirt section with a separate boost channel that includes a blendable octave-up. Its gain control crosses from aggressive distortion into gated Muff-style fuzz, and an Order switch changes whether the boost hits the dirt section before or after it.

## Sound
The dirt section morphs from open, aggressive distortion into a tight gated fuzz at higher Gain settings. The Tone and Scoop controls reshape its frequency balance, while the boost channel can add clean level, octave content, or heavier gain depending on the Order setting.
