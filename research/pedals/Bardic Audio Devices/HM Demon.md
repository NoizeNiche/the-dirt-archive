# Bardic Audio Devices - HM Demon

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** HM Demon
- **Builder:** Bardic Audio Devices
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** HM-2-inspired distortion with integrated clean blend, pre/post-EQ blend placement, and flexible low/mid frequency shaping.

## What this pedal is
HM Demon is an HM-2-style distortion rebuilt around a usable integrated clean mix. The clean signal can be blended before or after the EQ stage, and the Clean control can provide up to 20 dB of boost, letting the pedal function as a preamp-like or chainsaw-style boost as well as distortion.

## Sound
The core voice follows the aggressive, mid-scooped HM-2 family, but the integrated clean path lets the player restore attack and low-frequency weight without simply changing the distortion circuit. Low Freq and Mid Freq shift the centers of the EQ bands, while Clean can add substantial level and clarity.
