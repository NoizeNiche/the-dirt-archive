# Bardic Audio Devices — HM Demon

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** HM Demon
- **Builder:** Bardic Audio Devices
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** HM-2-based high-gain distortion redesigned with an integrated clean blend, selectable pre/post-EQ blend position, variable EQ frequencies and clean boost. [1][2]

## What this pedal is

Bardic Audio Devices explicitly describes the HM Demon as an HM-2 with added functionality. The clean signal is integrated into the circuit rather than simply mixed at the output, allowing the clean path to sit before or after the EQ and to be boosted beyond unity. [1]

## Controls

- **DISTORT:** Distortion-channel volume.
- **GAIN:** Distortion-channel gain.
- **CLEAN:** Clean-signal level, with up to **20 dB** of boost.
- **LOW:** Low EQ, approximately ±15 dB.
- **MID:** Mid EQ, approximately ±15 dB.
- **HIGH:** High EQ, approximately ±15 dB.
- **LOW FREQ:** Moves low-band center frequency between **60 and 100 Hz**.
- **MID FREQ:** Moves mid-band center frequency between **250 and 500 Hz**.
- **POST / PRE:** Places the clean mix after or before the EQ stage. [1][2]

## Circuit lineage / architecture

- **Primary lineage:** Boss **HM-2**.
- Integrated clean blend.
- Clean signal can be mixed either **post-EQ or pre-EQ**.
- Three-band EQ.
- Variable low- and mid-frequency centers.
The builder's stated goal is to retain the HM-2 chainsaw territory while making it more usable for bass and high-gain applications. [1]

## Sound

The HM Demon preserves the aggressive HM-2-style “chainsaw” distortion while using the Clean, Post/Pre and frequency controls to restore low end, attack and definition. The clean channel can also be boosted to turn the pedal into a preamp-like or chainsaw-boost stage. [1][2]
A 2022 independent article corroborates the pre/post blend concept, ±15 dB EQ range and 60–100 Hz / 250–500 Hz frequency switching. [2]
