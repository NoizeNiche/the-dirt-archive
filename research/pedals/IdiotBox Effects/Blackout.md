## What this pedal is
Blackout is a parallel dual-Muff fuzz. Each side has its own Fuzz, Tone and Output controls, allowing the two fuzz sections to be blended for larger and more complex textures.

- Upper section uses a traditional Muff-style tone control.

## Sound
Blackout is intended to sound larger than a conventional single Muff by running two different fuzz sections in parallel. The differing tone networks let the player combine a classic scoop with a more mid-focused voice.
