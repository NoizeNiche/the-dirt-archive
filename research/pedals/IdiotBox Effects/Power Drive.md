## What this pedal is
The Power Drive is IdiotBox Effects' revised take on its PowerBoost, itself based on the Colorsound Power Boost concept. The current design keeps the broad boost/overdrive character while adding a charge-pump supply and modified EQ response.

## Colorways
- A documented 2026 example is **Red**.
- Other finish runs may vary.

## Versions and factory options
- The archive treats Power Drive as the current successor to the earlier PowerBoost design.
- Internal charge pump raises the circuit's operating voltage toward the original Power Boost's higher-voltage environment while using a 9V supply.
- EQ is modified to provide a broad, less-compressed response with strong low-frequency content.
- The exact production control labels are not asserted without a reliable exact-model manual.

## Sound
Power Drive ranges from loud, relatively clean boost through raw overdrive and thicker distortion. The modified EQ and internal voltage multiplier are intended to preserve punch and low-end weight while avoiding the heavily compressed feel of some similar drives.
