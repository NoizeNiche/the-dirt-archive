## What this pedal is

Cool Buzz is a bass fuzz that began as a simple fixed-voicing design and was expanded with Fuzz, Low and High controls plus a switch to engage or defeat its scoop.

## Sound

The Cool Buzz is intended for thick, aggressive bass fuzz while retaining enough low-frequency control to fit a bass rig. The scoop switch and EQ controls provide more control over the mid and low-end response.
