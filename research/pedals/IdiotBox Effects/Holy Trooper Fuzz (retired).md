## What this pedal is
IdiotBox's official retired-design documentation describes the **Holy Trooper Fuzz** as a deliberately minimal pedal: **no knobs, no internal trim pots, and no LED**. [1]

Effects Database records the model as an IdiotBox fuzz pedal and dates its database publication to October 10, 2017, with an associated 2017 IdiotBox demo entry. [2] That publication date is a database record date, not necessarily the original production date.
