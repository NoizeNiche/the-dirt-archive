## Sound
It's a tube screamer style overdrive running into two separate echo circuits.
Controls are: REFLECTION - echo 1, CHAOS - echo 2, ALCHEMY - echo signal mix, FLAME - overdrive volume, STRIKE - overdrive intensity and DARKNESS - tone.
The left switch engages echoes and the right switch engages overdrive.
