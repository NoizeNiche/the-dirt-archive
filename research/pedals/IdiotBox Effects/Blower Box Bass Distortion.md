## What this pedal is

The Blower Box began as IdiotBox's attempt to capture the bass distortion associated with **Voivod's Blacky on Nothingface**. It developed into a broader bass overdrive/distortion rather than remaining a one-record imitation.

## Colorways

- Documented finishes include **Green/Black**, **Purple/Black**, and **Pink/Black**.
- Smaller modern enclosures use the same basic circuit with the controls moved to the top.

## Versions and factory options

- Original and modern compact enclosure versions exist.
- The modern small Green/Black version uses the same circuit as the documented earlier design.
- True bypass.
- External 9V DC power.

## Sound

The Blower Box provides thick bass overdrive and distortion with enough range to move beyond its Voivod-inspired roots. IdiotBox emphasizes that it works as a broad bass dirt pedal rather than a single-purpose recreation.
