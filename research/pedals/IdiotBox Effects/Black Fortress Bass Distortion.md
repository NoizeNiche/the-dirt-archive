## What this pedal is
The Black Fortress started as an **HM-2-inspired bass distortion**. IdiotBox expanded the design with a clean blend and a low-pass filter so the distorted signal could retain a large, usable low end.

## Colorways

## Versions and factory options
- **Drive** controls the drive stage.
- **Master** controls the distortion intensity/output section.
- **Octave** controls the fuzz/octave intensity.
- **DIR** and **PASS** form a clean blend with a separate low-pass filter.
- Designed specifically around bass response.

## Sound
The Black Fortress combines aggressive HM-2-style bass distortion with octave/fuzz content and a parallel clean path. DIR and PASS let the player preserve low-end fundamentals while shaping how much filtered clean signal remains under the distortion.
