## What this pedal is
The DeathRay is IdiotBox Effects' revamped version of the original **Death Ray Frequency Mangler**. It is a square-wave fuzz designed for heavily filtered, splattery, and bit-crushed textures.

## Sound
The DeathRay ranges from high-filtered, squelchy fuzz through low, buzzed-out and bit-crushed textures. The circuit is intended to sound deliberately broken rather than behave like a conventional smooth fuzz.
