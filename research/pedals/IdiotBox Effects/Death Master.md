## What this pedal is
The Death Master is IdiotBox Effects' attempt to capture the guitar sound associated with **Celtic Frost's To Mega Therion**. The design combines distortion with a resonant filter and a parallel blend.

## Versions and factory options
- **Blend:** controls how much of the original signal passes through the resonance filter before the drive stage.
- **Resonance:** shapes the resonant filter.
- **Drive:** controls the amount of distortion.
- **Tone:** moves the midrange response from scooped toward boosted.

## Sound
The Death Master is built around a dark, aggressive distortion character with resonant filtering and a useful dry blend. The Blend control allows the filtered path to be combined with the direct signal, while Tone shapes the midrange character of the resulting distortion.
