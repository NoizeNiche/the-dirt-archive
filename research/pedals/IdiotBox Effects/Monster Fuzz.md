## Sound
Within a few hours the Monster Fuzz was born!
The Monster Fuzz is a souped up Frankensteined Super Fuzz octave style fuzz.
What's super rad about this one is that the VOLTS control sets the internal voltage anywhere from 4-18VDC and LOW and HIGH are low/high frequency controls so along with the SCOOP control you can get all kinds of wild Super Fuzz style tones!
