# BJFE / BJF Electronics - Mighty Blue Overdrive

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** BJFE / BJF Electronics
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive

## Research
A one-off two-control-set BJFE Deluxe pedal built from two early BBOD circuits. It provides two sets of Volume, Treble and Drive controls, but the reference clarifies that it is not two channels in series and was made so its owner could switch between favorite settings.
