# BJFE / BJF Electronics - Mighty Red Distortion (MRD)

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** BJFE / BJF Electronics
- **Catalog type:** Distortion

## Research
A rare BJFE distortion design whose second prototype became the circuit used for the Mad Professor Mighty Red Distortion. The BJFE historical reference says at least nine BJFE MRDs are known and describes development with improved noise cancellation and a quieter chip; the current BJFE Japan listing describes a compressed 1980s-style high-gain distortion covering low-gain through high-gain sounds.

## Sound

BJFE's current product documentation identifies Mighty Red Distortion as a compressed, high gain 1980s style distortion. The design uses a rare NPN transistor preamp and a distinctive feedback arrangement; controls are Vol, Treble, Dist and the pedal operates from 9V. BJFE documents maximum gain around 62dB, current consumption around 10.6mA, and a signal to noise ratio around 96dB.
