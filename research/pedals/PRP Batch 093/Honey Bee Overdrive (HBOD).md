# BJFE / BJF Electronics - Honey Bee Overdrive (HBOD)

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** BJFE / BJF Electronics
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive

## Research
Mild dynamic overdrive inspired by old Supro amplifiers. The historical BJFE reference dates the release to 4 December 2002 and notes that it was designed with a Flying V using light humbuckers, with BJ preferring a Les Paul for demonstrating its dynamic range.
