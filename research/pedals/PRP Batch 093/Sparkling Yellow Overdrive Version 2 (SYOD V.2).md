# BJFE / BJF Electronics - Sparkling Yellow Overdrive Version 2 (SYOD V.2)

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** BJFE / BJF Electronics
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive

## Research
A less-bright revision of the SYOD with an EQ better suited to boosting the Dyna Red Distortion. The historical reference dates its release to February 2009, says V.2 begins at serial B3, and notes that the same circuit also appeared in the limited Saffron Yellow Overdrive.
