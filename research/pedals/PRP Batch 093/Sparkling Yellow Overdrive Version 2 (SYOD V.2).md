# BJFE / BJF Electronics - Sparkling Yellow Overdrive Version 2 (SYOD V.2)

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** BJFE / BJF Electronics
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive

## Research
A less-bright revision of the SYOD with an EQ better suited to boosting the Dyna Red Distortion. The historical reference dates its release to February 2009, says V.2 begins at serial B3, and notes that the same circuit also appeared in the limited Saffron Yellow Overdrive.

## Sound

BJFE's current Woody Orange documentation confirms that Sparkling Yellow Overdrive V2 is the American voiced member of the SYOD family. Compared with V1, V2 is described as less bright and slightly more compressed, with the two versions designed to be usable together. The modern combined pedal documents V1/V2 as separate stages with independent volume, drive, bass and treble controls.
