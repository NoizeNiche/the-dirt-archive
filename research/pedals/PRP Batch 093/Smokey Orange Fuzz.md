# BJFE / BJF Electronics - Smokey Orange Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** BJFE / BJF Electronics
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz

## Research
A one-off prototype that led directly to the Arctic White Fuzz. The BJFE reference says it began with the Snow White Fuzz as its starting point, and that all subsequent Arctic White Fuzz pedals used the Smokey Orange schema.
