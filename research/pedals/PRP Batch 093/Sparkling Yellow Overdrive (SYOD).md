# BJFE / BJF Electronics - Sparkling Yellow Overdrive (SYOD)

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** BJFE / BJF Electronics
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive

## Research
A bright overdrive that began as the Mystoury Drive and was officially announced as SYOD in September 2007. The historical reference says the first batches were B1 and B2, after which the circuit was revised into SYOD V.2; an experiment called Sparkling Orange Overdrive explored adding more low mids and improving low-gain use.
