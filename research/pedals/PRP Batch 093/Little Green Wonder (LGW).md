# BJFE / BJF Electronics - Little Green Wonder (LGW)

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** BJFE / BJF Electronics
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive

## Research
An updated Tube Screamer-style BJFE design released 8 January 2003. The historical reference describes it as a smooth, relatively simple distortion useful on clean sounds or for enhancing already-crunchy tones, with early and later circuit revisions including LF353 and TLC272 op-amps.
