## PRP identity

## Research

A vintage fuzz update released 9 March 2002 and retired in October 2007, with 179 made. Its unusual Nature control can attenuate highs clockwise from noon or add low mids and bass counter-clockwise, and the reference notes that its nontraditional behavior contributed to the pedal's eventual discontinuation.
