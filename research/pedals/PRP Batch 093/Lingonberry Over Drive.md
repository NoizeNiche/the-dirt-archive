# BJFE / BJF Electronics - Lingonberry Over Drive

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** BJFE / BJF Electronics
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive

## Research
A higher-gain relative of the Strawberry Red Overdrive. Bjorn Juhl's description says the Lingonberry was developed with optimized low-gain control, altered filtering for more bite and a tighter sound, and controls for Volume, Treble and Drive plus a low-end trimmer.

## Deep research verification

One Control's LBOD-N is documented as the BJFE Lingonberry Over Drive lineage. The design is a higher-gain development of Strawberry Red Overdrive, with optimized low-gain control, altered filtering for more bite and a tighter response. The documented external controls are **Volume, Treble, Drive**, with an additional low-end trimmer. Exact original BJFE semiconductor part numbers are not established by the surviving documentation.
