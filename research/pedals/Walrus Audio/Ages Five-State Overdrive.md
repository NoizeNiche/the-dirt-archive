## What this pedal is

The Ages is a five-state overdrive with switchable clipping/gain structures, a dry blend control, and pre-gain bass shaping. Walrus Audio designed it to cover clean and sparkly gain through thicker, compressed overdrive.

## Colorways

- Standard enclosure: **olive green** with white, cream, and black artwork.
- Walrus has also produced documented special editions, including Platinum and Craftsman Series versions.
- Special finishes are treated as finish editions rather than separate circuits unless Walrus documents a hardware change.

## Versions and factory options

The five-position gain selector uses these documented states:
1. Low gain, symmetric silicon soft clipping.
2. Low gain, symmetric LED soft clipping.
3. High gain, symmetric silicon soft clipping.
4. High gain, symmetric LED soft clipping.
5. High gain, symmetric silicon hard clipping.
- **Dry** control blends the clean signal with the driven path.
- **Bass** control boosts or cuts low frequencies before the overdrive stage.
- True bypass.
- 9V DC center-negative operation.
- Walrus specifies a minimum 100mA supply.

## Sound

Ages spans several distinct gain and clipping behaviors, from smooth low-gain drive through higher-gain, harder-clipping sounds. Dry blending can preserve attack and clarity, while the pre-gain Bass control changes how the low end hits the drive circuit.
