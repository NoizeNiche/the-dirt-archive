## What this pedal is

The Janus is a dual-joystick fuzz and tremolo pedal. Walrus Audio designed the two effects so they can be used separately or together in series to create moving, heavily textured sounds.

## Colorways

## Versions and factory options

- Tremolo joystick: **Rate** and **Depth**.
- Fuzz joystick: **Fuzz amount** and **Tone**.
- Separate Volume controls for tremolo and fuzz.
- Fuzz **Blend** control mixes unaffected and fuzzed signal.
- True bypass.
- 9V DC operation.
- Current draw: 8mA.

## Sound

The Janus combines tremolo movement and fuzz texture in an unusually interactive interface. The two effects can be isolated, stacked in series, or blended to create rhythmic modulation, saturated movement, and dense fuzz textures.
