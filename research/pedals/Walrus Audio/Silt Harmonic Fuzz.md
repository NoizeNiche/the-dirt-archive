## What this pedal is

The Silt Harmonic Fuzz combines a fuzz circuit with an internal 12AU7 tube drive section and a frequency-doubler Harmonic Mode.

## Colorways

- Walrus documents red and black enclosure options.
- Special artwork editions are treated as finish variants unless an electrical change is documented.

## Versions and factory options

- **12AU7** tube drive.
- **Harmonic Mode** frequency doubler.
- **Contour** control with high-pass, flat, and low-pass curves in the pre-tube EQ stage.
- 9V to 12V operation.
- True bypass.
- 9VDC, 300mA minimum.

## Sound

Silt combines thick fuzz with harmonically rich tube coloration. Harmonic Mode adds octave-like upper harmonics before the tube section, while Contour controls the pre-tube frequency shape.
