## What this pedal is
The 385 Overdrive MKII is Walrus Audio's expanded version of the original 385 Overdrive, inspired by the tube-powered audio section of the Bell & Howell Filmosound 385 projector. The MKII adds an A/B selector with separate Gain and Volume controls plus a 385+ gain boost.

## Colorways
- Walrus documents a textured black version and a flat black version with different artwork.
- Special finishes such as the Floral Series are treated as finish editions rather than circuit revisions.

## Versions and factory options
- **A/B** selector toggles between two Gain/Volume control sets.
- Shared **Bass** and **Treble** controls.
- **385+** switch increases front-end gain and saturation.
- Internally operates at 18V from a 9V supply.
- True bypass.
- 9VDC, 100mA minimum.

## Version changes
- The MKII expands the original 385 by adding the A/B control architecture and 385+ high-gain option.
- Walrus dates the original 385 to 2016.

## Sound
The 385 MKII is designed for responsive, tube-like overdrive. Lower gain settings emphasize dynamic, amp-like breakup, while higher settings and 385+ push the circuit into thicker distortion.
