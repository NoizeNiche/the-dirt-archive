## What this pedal is

Eras is a high-gain distortion pedal with five selectable distortion modes and a Blend control. Walrus Audio designed it for tight response, palm-muted playing, and a wide range of gain structures.

## Colorways

- Standard documented versions include **Transparent Red** and **Black** presentations.

## Versions and factory options

- Five selectable distortion modes.
- **Blend** control for parallel mixing and stacking.
- Boost/Cut EQ section.
- True bypass.
- 9V DC center-negative operation.
- Walrus specifies a minimum 100mA supply.

## Sound

Eras is voiced for high-gain distortion with several distinct clipping structures. The Blend control allows the driven signal to be combined with the dry/parallel path, which can preserve attack and clarity while adding saturation.
