## What this pedal is

Eons is a five-state fuzz with a variable Voltage control that can raise or starve the circuit's internal operating voltage. Walrus Audio designed the pedal to range from large, open fuzz sounds to gated, starved textures.

## Colorways

- Standard documented enclosure: **sandy tan** with red, cream, and black artwork.
- Black variants and other limited finishes are treated as editions unless Walrus documents a circuit change.

## Versions and factory options

- Five-position clipping selector.
- **Voltage** control varies the circuit from approximately 3V to 18V.
- **Bass** and **Treble** boost/cut EQ.
- True bypass.
- 9V DC center-negative operation.
- Walrus specifies a minimum 100mA supply.
- Documented clipping states include combinations of silicon, germanium, LED, and hard-clipping structures.

## Version changes

## Transistor

- One documented high-gain mode uses hard-clipping silicon transistors, but the complete production transistor complement is not published.
- **Exact production transistor part number:** Unknown.

## Diode

- Walrus documents silicon, germanium, and LED clipping configurations across the five modes.
- **Exact production diode part numbers:** Unknown.

## Sound

Eons ranges from compressed, smooth fuzz to crunchy germanium textures and more extreme hard-clipping sounds. Starving the circuit with the Voltage control can create spitty, gated behavior, while higher voltage settings open up the response and headroom.
