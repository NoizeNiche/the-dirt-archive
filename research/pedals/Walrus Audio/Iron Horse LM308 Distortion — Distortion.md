## What this pedal is
The Iron Horse is Walrus Audio's high-gain take on a classic LM308-era distortion architecture. The later V3 replaces the earlier multi-position clipping switch with a continuously variable Si/LED clipping control.

## Colorways
- Walrus documents a Gold finish with black ink for V3.
- Limited editions and other finishes are treated as finish variants unless a circuit change is documented.

## Versions and factory options
- **V3:** top-mounted jacks.
- Soft-relay true bypass.
- **Si/LED** blend control replaces the earlier three-position clipping switch.
- Distortion control provides a broad high-gain range.
- V3 is specified at 9VDC, 10mA.

## Version changes
- Earlier Iron Horse versions used a three-position clipping switch.
- V3 replaces that switch with a blend control between silicon and LED clipping, and addresses the earlier minimum-distortion volume drop.

## Sound
The Iron Horse is designed for thick, punchy, high-gain distortion. The Si/LED control changes both clipping character and compression, moving from tighter, more compressed silicon clipping toward a more open LED response.
