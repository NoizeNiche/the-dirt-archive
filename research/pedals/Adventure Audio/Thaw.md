# Adventure Audio — Thaw

## PRP identity

- **Identity:** Two-knob Adventure Audio fuzz released around 2019. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
Thaw is a compact two-control fuzz designed to move from smoother fuzz into gated, sputtering and octave-like textures. Surviving retailer documentation identifies the controls as Volume and Push. [1]

## Colorways

## Sound
Thaw is described as moving from smooth fuzz through gated and octave-ish territory. The two-control format keeps it deliberately direct, while the Push control provides the main change in gain and texture. [1]
