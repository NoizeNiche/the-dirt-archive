## What this pedal is
Dream Reaper combines fuzz, feedback/oscillation, filtering, and voltage-starve behavior. Dream mode introduces self-oscillation, while the Force/Sense system emulates the interaction of a guitar pickup and tone control with the feedback circuit. [1][2]

## Sound
Dream Reaper ranges from gated, blown-out and glitchy fuzz to screaming feedback and self-oscillation. The Filter, Bias, Sense and Reap controls interact strongly, so small adjustments can move the pedal between thick fuzz, sputtering textures, and sustained electronic squeal. [1][2]
