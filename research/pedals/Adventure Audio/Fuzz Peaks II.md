# Adventure Audio — Fuzz Peaks II

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Fuzz Peaks II
- **Builder:** Adventure Audio
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Revised Fuzz Peaks design based on a Russian Big Muff concept and aimed particularly at bass. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
Fuzz Peaks II retains the core fuzz concept while revising the controls, I/O and voicing. It uses a four-transistor fuzz stage, clean and fuzz volume controls, improved bias control, a three-position midrange selector, and top-mounted jacks. [1]

## Version changes
Documented Rev. 2 changes include top-mounted jacks, a three-position mid selector, separate Clean and Fuzz Volume controls replacing Master Volume/Clean Blend, improved Bias control, and removal of the Clean Out jack. [1]

## Sound
Fuzz Peaks II is Adventure Audio's Russian Big Muff-inspired bass fuzz, retaining low-end content through its clean/fuzz architecture while adding midrange shaping. The Bias control can alter the high-frequency behavior and the Peak switch adds glitchy, sputtering textures. [1][2]
