# Adventure Audio — Fuzz Peaks

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Fuzz Peaks
- **Builder:** Adventure Audio
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Adventure Audio's Black Russian Big Muff-inspired fuzz for bass and guitar. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
Fuzz Peaks modifies the Black Russian Muff concept with a buffered clean blend, parallel clean output, momentary feedback control, a Behave control, clipping selection, and a less aggressively scooped tone stack. [1]

## Colorways
- Bare aluminum and printed production finishes are documented.
- A purple sparkle finish is also documented in a surviving 2026 marketplace listing. [3]

## Versions and factory options
### Fuzz Peaks
- Fuzz
- Spectrum tone control
- Behave control
- momentary feedback switch
- clipping selection toggle
- buffered clean blend
- parallel clean output [1][2]

### Fuzz Peaks V2 / Fuzz Peaks II
See separate catalog record for the revised model.

## Version changes
The original Fuzz Peaks is documented as the earlier version of the design. The later Fuzz Peaks II/V2 changes the control and I/O arrangement substantially. [4]

## Transistor
- The original circuit is described as a Black Russian Muff-derived design, but exact production transistor part numbers were not verified.
- **Exact transistor/device:** Not publicly documented.

## Diode
- Clipping selection is documented, but the exact production diode types are not publicly documented.

## Sound
Fuzz Peaks keeps the thick, sustaining character of a Russian Big Muff while preserving more bass, attack and clarity through the clean blend. Its feedback and clipping controls can push the sound toward aggressive, squealing and more compressed fuzz textures. [1][2]
