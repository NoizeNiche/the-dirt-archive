- **Identity:** Adventure Audio Demogorgon, a fuzz combined with tremolo and reverb. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
Demogorgon is a multi-effect dirt pedal in which the fuzz section is always available while the tremolo/reverb block can be engaged separately. The documented design uses Gain and Filter for the fuzz, with Deep and Rate for tremolo, Decay for reverb, and routing controls for the order of the blocks. [1]

## Versions and factory options
### Demogorgon
- Fuzz section: Gain, Filter
- Tremolo: Deep, Rate
- Reverb: Decay
- Order switch
- X switch for expression control assignment
- Upside Down switch
- Volume control
- 9 V DC, center negative; 100 mA documented by ModularGrid [2]

### Demogorgon V2
The V2 is documented as a later Adventure Audio/The Tone Mob version with the same core fuzz/tremolo/reverb architecture. [1][3]

## Sound
The fuzz is described as a constant fuzz stage with a Gain control and frequency-sweep Filter. The tremolo/reverb block adds deep modulation, decay, and routing options, allowing the pedal to move from conventional fuzz into highly animated, ambient textures. [1][2]
