# Claybridge Sound Systems / Claybridge Audio — Fuzzmaster MKIV

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Fuzzmaster MKIV
- **Builder:** Claybridge Sound Systems / Claybridge Audio
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Claybridge Fuzzmaster MKIV, a late-1960s Australian fuzz model documented as a distinct later member of the Fuzzmaster family.

## What this pedal is
Tone Machines Blog documents the MKIV as a later Claybridge Fuzzmaster variant and notes that surviving examples are extremely rare. The same research distinguishes it from the earlier green Fuzzmaster and from the later company-name shift to Claybridge Audio. [1][2]

A 2024 Freestompboxes thread documents a traced MKIV schematic and identifies the pedal as an Australian-made fuzz from the late 1960s. [3]

## Colorways
- Surviving MKIV examples are documented primarily in **silver hammerite**.
- A rare gold-looking example is discussed by Tone Machines as probably having been repainted, so it is not treated as a verified original factory finish. [2]

## Versions and factory options
The MKIV is documented as a distinct Fuzzmaster variant with its own enclosure and circuit. A schematic traced in 2024 provides a stronger technical reference, but the archive does not infer unverified factory options from a modern clone or redrawn layout. [3]

## Version changes
The MKIV follows earlier Fuzzmaster forms within the Claybridge family. The available historical sources establish the family progression but do not provide a complete factory engineering change log. [1][2]

## Transistor
The checked sources confirm a distinct MKIV circuit but do not securely establish universal transistor part numbers for all surviving examples.

## Diode
No exact-model diode specification was established.

## Sound
The MKIV belongs to the raw, gate-capable Australian Fuzzmaster family. Tone Machines' surviving-example comparisons describe it as having a distinctive gating character and a different attack/sustain balance from the early Fuzzmaster. [2]
