# Chase Bliss — Brothers AM

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Brothers AM
- **Builder:** Chase Bliss
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive
- **Identity:** Chase Bliss Brothers AM, a collaboration with Analog Man expanding the King of Tone circuit family with two multi-mode channels and a bonus treble booster.

## What this pedal is
Chase Bliss describes Brothers AM as a collaboration with Analog Man that expands the King of Tone overdrive concept. The pedal has two identical gain channels, each switchable between Boost, Overdrive, and Distortion, plus a bonus treble booster inspired by Analog Man's Beano Boost. [1][2]

## Colorways
- The current product documentation confirms a black example, but a complete factory colorway sequence was not established.

## Versions and factory options
Each channel has:
- Gain
- Volume
- Tone
- Mode: Boost / Overdrive / Distortion [2]

The pedal also includes:
- Analog Man-inspired treble booster.
- Four internal presets.
- MIDI, CV, and expression control.
- Presence adjustment.
- Momentary or latching bypass behavior. [1][2]

## Transistor
The checked sources do not establish exact transistor part numbers for the core King of Tone-derived channels.

The bonus treble booster is described as a **Rangemaster-style** circuit, but the manufacturer documentation does not publish a specific transistor part number. [2]

## Diode
The pedal provides selectable boost/overdrive/distortion modes derived from the Analog Man architecture, but exact diode part numbers were not established in the checked sources.

## Sound
The Boost mode provides a clear, lower-gain push; Overdrive supplies a softer, open King of Tone-style response; Distortion supplies a higher-gain mode. The treble booster adds a cutting upper-mid/treble stage ahead of the gain channels. [1][2]
