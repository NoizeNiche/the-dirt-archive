# BSQ Effects — MS-2 Mean Street - Distortion + Booster

## What this pedal is
Mean Street MS2 was BSQ Effects' first high-gain distortion pedal, introduced in 2013, with an integrated booster.

## Versions and factory options
- High-gain distortion
- Integrated booster
- Analog circuit
- No numbered MS2 revision verified

## Version changes
BSQ later redesigned the platform as MS3, adding a three-band active EQ and a switch for effect order. Those changes are not assigned to MS2.

## Transistor
Exact device not publicly documented.

## Diode

## Sound
MS2 is the original high-gain Mean Street design, centered on strong distortion with an additional booster section.

BSQ Effects's MS-2 Mean Street - Distortion + Booster is cataloged as a distortion pedal.

### Verified sound evidence
Based on that same powerful and acclaimed distortion, we developed the Mean Street MS3, and made it more flexible and versatile.
Taking into account the different scenarios in which the Mean Street could be used, we thought about what we could improve to get the ideal sound in any situation, and after countless hours of work redesigning the circuit, we got the perfect distortion.
By using a 3-band active EQ instead of a simple tone control, we made the MS3 perfectly suited to any style, from a heavy, hollowed-out heavy, to the most mid-rangey, harmonically rich rock.
