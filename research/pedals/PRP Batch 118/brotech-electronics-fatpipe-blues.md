# Brotech Electronics — Fatpipe Blues

## PRP identity

- **Catalog type:** Distortion

## What this pedal is
Brotech's Fatpipe Blues is a three-band distortion/sustain effects box with Gain and Level controls and a separate boost. The manufacturer manual says it can cover sounds from warm, soft sustain to hard, powerful distortion. [1]
