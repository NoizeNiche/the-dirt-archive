# BSQ Effects — SB-1 Scuttle Buttin' - Overdrive

## What this pedal is
Scuttle Buttin' SB1 is an analog overdrive designed for smooth saturation while preserving guitar dynamics. BSQ describes increased gain and bass versus the earlier design.

## Versions and factory options
- Volume
- Tone
- Drive
- On/Off switch
- 9V alkaline battery
- Approx. 9.5 mA current draw when on

## Sound
The SB1 balances clean and saturated signal to retain attack and articulation. Its three controls cover output, drive amount and high-frequency shaping.
