# Brotech Electronics — Fatpipe Pro

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Brotech Electronics

- **Identity:** Professional Fatpipe distortion/boost effects box.

## What this pedal is
The Fatpipe Pro is documented by Brotech as a distortion and sustain effects box with a three-band equalizer, gain and level controls, and separate boost and distortion footswitches. [1]

## Sound
The manual describes the Fatpipe Pro as capable of modern metal-crunch, smoother low-gain drive and broad distortion/sustain sounds. Its separate boost and three-band EQ make it more flexible than a fixed-voicing distortion. [1]
