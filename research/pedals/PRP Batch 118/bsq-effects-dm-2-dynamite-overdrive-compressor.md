# BSQ Effects — DM-2 Dynamite - Overdrive/Compressor

## What this pedal is

Dynamite DM2 combines a compressor and transparent overdrive. BSQ describes a warm, tight compressor with attack and input-gain controls, plus an overdrive ranging from booster to stronger saturated drive.

## Versions and factory options

- Compressor attack and input-gain controls
- Overdrive gain and volume
- Active bass and treble EQ, ±18 dB
- 9V DC, center-negative
- Approx. 5.8 mA current draw
- Anodized aluminum enclosure documented

## Sound

Dynamite can work as a booster, light overdrive or more saturated drive. The compressor adds sustain and control, while the active bass/treble EQ expands the shaping range.

### Verified description
BSQ Effects's DM-2 Dynamite - Overdrive/Compressor is cataloged as an overdrive pedal.

### Verified sound evidence
Unlike common guitar compressor effects with only 2 controls, the Dynamite adds attack and input gain controls, which are essential for shaping the character of the instrument.
The overdrive, at the same time, has a transparent, dynamic, and super-sensitive nature that responds to the most subtle nuances of your playing.
It's equipped with an active bass and treble EQ with a range of ±18dB, and gain and volume controls, allowing you to use the Dynamite as a full Booster, a light overdrive with just the right bite, or even dive into the realm of distortion.
