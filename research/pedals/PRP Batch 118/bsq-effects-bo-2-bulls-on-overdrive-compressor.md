# BSQ Effects — BO-2 Bulls On - Overdrive/Compressor

## What this pedal is
Bulls On BO2 is a bass-focused analog overdrive and compressor. BSQ describes a wide saturation range from rounded, subtle drive to sharper aggressive distortion, plus a blend control that balances direct and processed signal.

## Versions and factory options
- Overdrive/saturation section
- Compressor section
- Blend control
- 9V DC, center-negative
- Approx. 5.5 mA current draw

## Sound
The pedal is designed around preserving bass dynamics while adding compression and saturation. Its blend control is intended to retain direct low-end information while adding the processed path.
