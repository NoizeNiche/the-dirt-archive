## What this pedal is
The Dover Drive was designed by Hermida Audio as a tube-free overdrive inspired by Tube Driver-style response. It spans mild overdrive to Fuzz Face-like saturation and is designed to remain touch-sensitive and dynamic.

## Sound
Dover Drive ranges from mild, smooth overdrive into more saturated and Fuzz Face-like attack. The internal Bias control can move the response toward starved, spitty textures or a more open, sustaining response.
