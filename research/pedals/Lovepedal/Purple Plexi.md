## What this pedal is

The Purple Plexi is Lovepedal's British amp-in-a-box overdrive, spanning JTM45-style breakup through higher-gain JCM800-style roar. Lovepedal's current Old School Build explicitly documents a redesign of the layout, parts and gain-control taper.

## Versions and factory options

- **Old School Build** is a documented later presentation.
- **Drive** and **Tone** controls.
- 9V battery or 9VDC negative-center adapter.
- Approximately 10mA current draw in the documented Old School Build.
- Smaller enclosure than earlier Purple Plexi versions.

## Version changes

For the Old School Build, Lovepedal documents:
- Redesigned layout and parts.
- Large old-school film capacitors.
- Carbon resistors.
- A changed gain-control taper.
- An input-signal shunt that changes decay and guitar-volume cleanup behavior.

## Diode

- Historical build documentation references **BAT46** terminology, but a complete production clipping network was not established.

## Sound

Purple Plexi moves from JTM45-like breakup at lower Drive settings through late-1960s Plexi tones and into JCM800-style high-gain saturation. Lovepedal emphasizes strong dynamic response and cleanup from the guitar volume control.
