## What this pedal is
Lovepedal’s Purple Plexi is designed to sound like an old Marshall guitar amp from the 60’s, 70’s, with a little bit of 80’s thrown in there for good measure.

## Sound
The biggest difference you would notice right away is the taper in the gain control.
The new ones are spread out and have a even progression to the max gain setting.
It also has a better note decay to my ears because of the way we configured the new gain control.
