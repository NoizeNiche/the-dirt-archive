## What this pedal is
The Super Six is a blackface Fender-inspired buffer/boost and tone-shaping pedal. Lovepedal describes it as a recreation of the sound and feel of a mid-1960s Blackface Super amplifier.

## Versions and factory options
- The later **Super Six Stevie Mod** adds:
  - **Level**
  - **Drive**
  - **Bass**
  - **Treble**
- Bass is an active pre-clipping bass section.
- Treble is a passive post-clipping tone control.
- +9VDC to +22VDC operation, center-negative, or 9V battery.
- Documented current draw: 18mA.
- True bypass.

## Version changes
- The archive distinguishes the simpler original Super Six concept from the expanded **Stevie Mod** presentation.
- The manufacturer describes the Stevie Mod as adding Master Volume, Drive, Bass and Treble controls.

## Sound
Super Six is designed for blackface-era American amp tone, including soft vintage compression, boosted response and a vintage-style tone-stack character. The Stevie Mod expands the control range while retaining that core blackface voicing.
