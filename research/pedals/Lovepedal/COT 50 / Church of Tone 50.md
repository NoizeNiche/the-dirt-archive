## What this pedal is

The original COT 50, or Church of Tone 50, is a one-knob bias-controlled boost/overdrive designed around the response of a late-1960s British plexi amplifier. Lovepedal emphasizes its ability to clean up from the guitar volume control.

## Colorways

- Historical production includes multiple finish and artwork variants.
- Finish changes are not treated as circuit revisions.

## Versions and factory options

- Single **Bias** control acting as the gain/drive control.
- True bypass.
- Original units can be powered by a 9V battery or external supply; Lovepedal notes the circuit is very low draw.
- No 9V battery socket or additional tone controls on the original one-knob form.

## Version changes

- Later COT/Church of Tone variants added controls or different housings. Those are not merged into the original one-knob record.

## Transistor

- Exact production transistor/device information was not established in the checked sources.

## Diode

- The LED indicator is documented; exact clipping device is not established.

## Sound

The COT 50 provides anything from a small amount of grit to substantial overdrive as Bias is increased. It responds strongly to pick dynamics and guitar-volume changes and was designed as an always-on amp-enhancing drive/boost.
