# Lovepedal — COT 50 Boost / Overdrive

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** COT 50 Boost / Overdrive

- **Identity:** Lovepedal's COT 50 Boost / Overdrive, also known as Church of Tone.

## What this pedal is
The COT 50 Boost / Overdrive is the simple Church of Tone design centered on a single bias/gain control. Lovepedal describes it as a preamp-style boost/overdrive with strong dynamics and guitar-volume cleanup.

## Versions and factory options
- One **Bias** control.
- True bypass.
- Low-current circuit.
- Original versions rely heavily on the guitar's own volume and tone controls.

## Sound
The pedal spans low-level grit through hot overdrive and works especially well as an amp-enhancing boost. Rolling back the guitar volume can clean the sound rapidly.
