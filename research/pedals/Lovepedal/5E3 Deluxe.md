## What this pedal is

The 5E3 Deluxe is Lovepedal's pedal interpretation of the Fender Tweed Deluxe 5E3 amplifier. Lovepedal describes it as touch-sensitive and designed around the rounded, compressed breakup associated with a driven low-power tweed amp.

## Sound

The 5E3 Deluxe aims for touch-sensitive tweed breakup. Lower-gain settings stay rounded and dynamic, while higher settings move toward the compressed, harmonically dense distortion associated with a pushed Tweed Deluxe.
