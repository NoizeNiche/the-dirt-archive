## What this pedal is
Limetone Audio describes **funther** as a low-gain to mid-gain overdrive intended to preserve the sense of the original instrument and the nuance of the player's picking while producing a polished, dimensional drive sound. The manufacturer identifies it as a discontinued product. [1]

The product page states that funther was developed from the sound of Limetone's **irodori** and was also usable as a booster. [1]

## Sound
Limetone characterizes funther as a low-gain to mid-gain overdrive with a glossy, dimensional drive sound that retains string articulation. The manufacturer says the Tone control can move from a warmer, mellow response toward more high-frequency edge. [1]

An independent 2026 review also documents the three-control layout and treats funther as a low/mid-gain overdrive, providing a current secondary reference. [3]
