## Sound

Limetone Audio describes **funther** as a low-gain to mid-gain overdrive intended to preserve the sense of the original instrument and the nuance of the player's picking while producing a polished, dimensional drive sound.
The product page states that funther was developed from the sound of Limetone's **irodori** and was also usable as a booster.
**Volume** - **Gain** - **Tone** Limetone documents all three controls on the discontinued product page.
