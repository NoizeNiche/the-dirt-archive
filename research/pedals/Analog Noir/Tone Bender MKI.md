## What this pedal is
Analog Noir's Tone Bender MKI is a hand-wired recreation of the 1965 Sola Sound Tone Bender MKI using a three-transistor germanium topology. [1]

## Sound
The MKI has the dense, raw and sustaining character associated with the earliest Tone Bender topology. Its added gain control broadens the range from gated, sputtering textures toward fuller sustained fuzz. [1]
