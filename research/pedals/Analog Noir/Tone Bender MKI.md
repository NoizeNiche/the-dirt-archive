# Analog Noir — Tone Bender MKI

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Tone Bender MKI

## What this pedal is
Analog Noir's Tone Bender MKI is a hand-wired recreation of the 1965 Sola Sound Tone Bender MKI using a three-transistor germanium topology. [1]

## Colorways
- Current and historical builds include small-batch enclosure finishes; no complete production chronology was established.

## Versions and factory options
- Level and Attack controls are documented.
- A gain control is used on current builds to extend the range beyond the original configuration. [1][2]

## Version changes

## Transistor
- **Technology:** Germanium.
- Current listings document three selected vintage germanium transistors. [1]

## Diode

## Sound
The MKI has the dense, raw and sustaining character associated with the earliest Tone Bender topology. Its added gain control broadens the range from gated, sputtering textures toward fuller sustained fuzz. [1]
