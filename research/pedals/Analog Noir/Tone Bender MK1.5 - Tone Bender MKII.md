# Analog Noir — Tone Bender MK1.5 / Tone Bender MKII

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Tone Bender MK1.5 / Tone Bender MKII
- **Builder:** Analog Noir
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Dual-circuit germanium fuzz pairing the two-transistor MK1.5 and three-transistor MKII.

## What this pedal is
Analog Noir combines recreations of the Sola Sound MK1.5 and MKII in one enclosure. The MK1.5 adds an external bias control, while the MKII adds gain and a three-position voice switch. [1][2]

## Colorways
- Documented builds include finishes such as hammered silver and hammered copper. [1][2]

## Versions and factory options
- Volume, Fuzz and Bias are shared.
- MKII adds Gain and Voice switch.
- A footswitch selects MKII / MK1.5. [1]

## Version changes
- The two circuits represent different historical Tone Bender topologies rather than a numbered production V2.

## Transistor
- **Technology:** Germanium.
- Analog Noir documents selected 1960s Texas Instruments and Mullard devices for this model. [1]

## Diode
- Exact diode type was not established.

## Sound
The MK1.5 side is brighter, lower-gain and highly responsive to guitar-volume cleanup. The MKII side adds more gain and saturation with selectable voicing, producing a thicker and more aggressive vintage fuzz character. [2]
