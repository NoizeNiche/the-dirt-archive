# Analog Noir — Tone Bender MK1.5 / Tone Bender MKII

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Tone Bender MK1.5 / Tone Bender MKII
- **Builder:** Analog Noir
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Dual-circuit germanium fuzz pairing the two-transistor MK1.5 and three-transistor MKII.

## What this pedal is
Analog Noir's Dual Tone Bender MKII / MK1.5 combines recreations of the Sola Sound MK1.5 and MKII circuits in one enclosure. The builder describes the MK1.5 as a two-transistor fuzz with lower gain, pronounced cleanup from the guitar volume control, and an external bias control. The MKII is a three-transistor development with additional input gain, adjusted bias/EQ, more gain and sustain, plus a three-way voice switch. [1][2]

## Colorways
- Documented builds include **Hammered Silver** and **Hammered Copper** finishes. [1][2]

## Versions and factory options
- Shared controls include **Volume, Fuzz, and Bias**.
- The MKII side adds **Gain** and a **Voice** switch on current/related documented builds.
- The circuit selection is footswitchable between **MKII and MK1.5**.
- Some documented builds add a bypassable **MKIII tone section**. [1][2]

## Version changes
The archive record represents a dual-circuit product rather than a numbered production V2. The two sides reproduce historically distinct Tone Bender topologies:
- **MK1.5:** two-transistor design, lower maximum gain, smoother cleanup and a sweeter fuzz response.
- **MKII:** three-transistor design with an added input gain stage, altered bias/EQ, and higher gain/sustain. [1][2]

## Transistor
- **Technology:** Germanium.
- Analog Noir documents a mixed set of vintage germanium devices in its MKII/MK1.5 builds, including **1960s Mullard GET**, **1974 Texas Instruments CV**, and **1960s New Market NKT** examples. The documented units use vintage PNP germanium transistors and require an isolated supply from standard 9V DC power. [1][2]

## Sound
The **MK1.5** side is described as brighter and less saturated, with strong response to guitar-volume changes and useful cleanup. The **MKII** side pushes harder into gain and sustain, with three selectable voice/EQ positions for fuller, mid-forward, or upper-mid emphasis. [2]
