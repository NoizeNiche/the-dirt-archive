# Analog Noir — Tone Bender MKI / Rangemaster Treble Booster

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Tone Bender MKI / Rangemaster Treble Booster
- **Builder:** Analog Noir
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Dual-circuit pedal pairing a 1965 Sola Sound Tone Bender MKI recreation with a 1965 Dallas Arbiter Rangemaster treble booster.

## What this pedal is
Analog Noir combines two classic early-1960s British circuits in one hand-built enclosure. The MKI side is a component-accurate recreation of the 1965 Sola Sound Tone Bender MKI, while the second side recreates the 1965 Dallas Arbiter Rangemaster Treble Booster. [1][2]

## Colorways
- A documented 2025 build uses a **Pastel Green enclosure with Neon Orange knobs**. [1]
- Earlier/sold examples also document other enclosure finishes, so the archive should treat finish as a build variation rather than a different pedal identity. [2]

## Versions and factory options
- **MKI controls:** Level, Attack, Gain.
- **Rangemaster controls:** Boost and three-position EQ switch with high / mid / low voicings.
- The MKI gain control expands the range beyond the stock Tone Bender setting; the noon position is described as restoring the original setting.
- The Rangemaster high position retains the traditional vintage voicing. [1]

## Version changes
No numbered production revision was established in the exact-model sources reviewed. Documented builds differ by enclosure finish and component selection, while the core dual-circuit identity remains the same.

## Transistor
- **Technology:** Germanium.
- Analog Noir documents vintage germanium devices including **mil-spec Mullard CV**, **1960s New Market NKT277**, and **early-1960s Texas Instruments CV7042** examples.
- The documented builds use **vintage PNP transistors** and require an isolated supply when powered from the standard 9V DC jack. [1][2]

## Sound
The MKI side is described as raw, saturated and harmonically dense, with the added gain control extending its range from more gated/sputtering textures toward full sustain. The Rangemaster side provides the characteristic sharp attack, harmonic lift and amplifier push of the classic treble booster. Its three-position EQ switch broadens the response from focused upper-mid bite toward thicker, fuller voicings. [1]
