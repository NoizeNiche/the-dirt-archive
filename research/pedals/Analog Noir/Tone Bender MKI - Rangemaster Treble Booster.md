# Analog Noir — Tone Bender MKI / Rangemaster Treble Booster

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Tone Bender MKI / Rangemaster Treble Booster
- **Builder:** Analog Noir
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Dual-circuit pedal pairing a Tone Bender MKI recreation with a Dallas Arbiter Rangemaster treble booster.
- **Current builder product name:** **Tarasque**. The current Analog Noir product page uses that name for the dual fuzz/boost while describing the Tone Bender MKI and Rangemaster circuits explicitly. [1]

## What this pedal is
Analog Noir combines a true-to-spec recreation of the Tone Bender MKI fuzz with a reproduction of the Dallas Arbiter Rangemaster. The current builder page describes the fuzz side as a hand-wired eyelet-board MKI circuit using new-old-stock components and curated vintage germanium transistors, while the boost side uses a 1960s Texas Instruments CV7042 and adds a three-position EQ switch. [1][2]

## Colorways
- Current builder options shown for **Tarasque** include **Seafoam green**, **Olive drab**, and **Ivory**. [1]
- A 2025 documented build used **Pastel Green with Neon Orange knobs**, demonstrating that finish can vary between builds without changing the underlying dual-circuit identity. [2]
- The archive should treat finish changes as color/build variations rather than separate canonical pedal identities.

## Versions and factory options
- **MKI controls:** Level, Attack, Gain.
- **Rangemaster controls:** Boost and three-position EQ switch with high / mid / low voicings.
- The MKI gain control extends gain above or below the stock setting; the noon position is the stock circuit setting.
- The Rangemaster “high” position is described by the builder as true to the original circuit. [1][2]
- The current builder page shows a standard 9VDC jack, vintage PNP transistors, isolated-supply requirement, and **<15mA** current draw. [1]
- The current official page presents Tarasque as the live product name; older exact-model listings use the descriptive dual-circuit Tone Bender/Rangemaster name. [1][2]

## Version changes
No numbered production revision was established in the exact-model sources reviewed. Builds can also differ in enclosure finish and transistor selection. [1][2]

## Transistor
- **Technology:** Germanium.
- The current builder page explicitly identifies a **1960s Texas Instruments CV7042** in the Rangemaster boost channel and describes the fuzz channel as using curated vintage germanium transistors. [1]
- Exact-model Reverb documentation additionally records examples including **mil-spec Mullard CV**, **1960s New Market NKT277**, and **early-1960s Texas Instruments CV7042** devices, and identifies the documented builds as using vintage **PNP** transistors. [2]
- The builder states that the vintage PNP transistor implementation requires an isolated supply from the standard 9VDC jack. [1]

## Sound
The MKI side is described as aggressive, with the added gain control extending its range around the stock setting. The Rangemaster side provides the characteristic treble-boost function and can be used alone or stacked with overdrive/fuzz; its three-position EQ expands the response beyond the traditional high-frequency voicing. [1][2]
