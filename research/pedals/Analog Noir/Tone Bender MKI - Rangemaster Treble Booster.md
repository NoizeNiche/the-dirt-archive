# Analog Noir — Tone Bender MKI / Rangemaster Treble Booster

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Tone Bender MKI / Rangemaster Treble Booster
- **Builder:** Analog Noir
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz

## What this pedal is
This dual pedal combines a 1965 Sola Sound Tone Bender MKI recreation with a 1965 Dallas Arbiter Rangemaster treble booster. [1][2]

## Colorways
- Documented small-batch finishes include pastel and metallic enclosure options.

## Versions and factory options
- MKI controls include Level, Attack and Gain.
- Rangemaster section includes Boost and a three-position EQ switch. [1]

## Version changes
- No named production revision established.

## Transistor
- **Technology:** Germanium.
- Analog Noir documents selected early-1960s Texas Instruments, New Market NKT and Mullard devices for this design. [1]

## Sound
The MKI side supplies raw, saturated early Tone Bender fuzz, while the Rangemaster side boosts upper mids and treble to push an amplifier or another drive stage. The EQ switch expands the booster beyond the original narrow high-frequency emphasis. [1][2]
