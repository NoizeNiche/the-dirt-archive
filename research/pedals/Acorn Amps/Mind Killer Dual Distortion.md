# Acorn Amps — Mind Killer Dual Distortion

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Mind Killer Dual Distortion
- **Builder:** Acorn Amps
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** Dual, stackable distortion pedal using two independently switchable op-amp distortion circuits.

## What this pedal is
Mind Killer contains two separate distortion circuits wired in series, each independently switchable and each equipped with its own bass-boost and clipping-mode toggle switches. Acorn identifies both sides as single-op-amp distortion circuits. [1]

## Colorways
- The current official presentation uses a light enclosure with black/gold artwork and blue SMD LEDs.
- No separately verified factory colorway list was established.

## Versions and factory options
### Current production
- Two independently switchable distortion circuits
- Series stacking
- Separate Life and Spice controls for each side
- Independent Bass and Clip toggles
- SMD LEDs
- True bypass
- Standard 9V pedal power
- Less than 70 mA current draw [1]

## Diode
- **Function:** Each distortion side has a clipping-diode mode toggle.
- **Exact diode type/part:** Not publicly documented. [1]

## Sound
Each side can be used alone or stacked with the other, moving from individual crunchy distortion voices into much denser, higher-gain combinations. The bass and clipping options let each side change character before the two are combined. [1]
