## What this pedal is
The Epstein Isn't Dead Op-Amp Boost/Distortion is a single-op-amp drive circuit built to cover clean boost through heavy distortion with two main controls. A separate Relocation Services toggle controls the illuminated N'T section of the front-panel artwork. [1]

## Sound
The boost side is intended to add a warm, touch-sensitive lift, while the drive control takes the circuit into heavier distortion. The result is a straightforward boost-to-distortion pedal with a deliberately simple control layout. [1]
