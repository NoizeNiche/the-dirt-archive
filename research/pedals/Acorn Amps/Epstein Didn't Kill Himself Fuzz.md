## What this pedal is
The Epstein Didn't Kill Himself Fuzz is a three-transistor silicon fuzz designed around a simple volume and fuzz control scheme. Acorn's current product page describes a range from gritty boost to heavy fuzz crunch, with a separate toggle controlling the illuminated “N'T” section of the front-panel lettering. [1]

## Versions and factory options
### Current production
- Three-transistor silicon fuzz
- Erase Footage volume control
- Fuzzy Recollection fuzz control
- Security Camera toggle for the illuminated N'T letters
- True bypass
- 9VDC operation
- Less than 30 mA current draw [1]

## Sound
The pedal moves from lightly dirty boost into heavier, fuzzier crunch as the Fuzzy Recollection control is advanced. Acorn says its voicing is sonically similar to the discontinued Bweep Fuzz, keeping the effect in a direct, aggressive fuzz family. [1]
