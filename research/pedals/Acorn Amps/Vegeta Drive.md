## What this pedal is
The Vegeta Drive is a hard-clipping single-op-amp distortion pedal with a boost toggle. Acorn also gives it an illuminated “Scouter” indicator and a simple Level/Drive control layout. [1]

## Sound
The core voice is harder-edged than a soft-clipping overdrive, with the boost switch adding another level of push. The simple control set makes it easy to move from a direct drive sound into more forceful gain textures. [1]
