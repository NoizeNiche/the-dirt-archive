# Acorn Amps — Solid State Amp/Preamp

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Solid State Amp/Preamp
- **Builder:** Acorn Amps
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** Full solid-state amplifier and preamp recreation in a 1590BB pedal enclosure.

## What this pedal is
The Solid State Amp/Preamp is a full 10-watt solid-state guitar amp circuit built into a pedal enclosure, with both preamp and power-amp sections. Acorn's design was presented as a recreation of the Peavey Decade practice amp and can feed a normal pedal chain, a speaker cabinet, or both. [1][2]

## Colorways
- The current official stock presentation is a dark enclosure with silver controls and white labeling.
- Acorn's product page also exposes a Daniel Danger artwork option, but the reviewed material does not establish a separate circuit version. [1]

## Versions and factory options
### Current production
- Full 10-watt solid-state amp circuitry
- Low, Mid and High controls
- Preamp and power-amp sections
- Separate speaker and normal output jacks
- Normal and Saturation footswitchable modes
- Output-point selection and mute control
- True bypass
- 18VDC pedal power, with published 9V operating figures as well [1]

## Version changes
No separate named circuit revision was documented. A later Solid State Preamplifier exists as a distinct, smaller preamp-only product, so it is not treated as a revision of this Amp/Preamp pedal. [3]

## Transistor
- **Exact transistor/device:** Not publicly documented.
- The power section uses a TDA-series class-AB power-amplifier device, matching the architecture Acorn described from the original amp. [1][2]

## Diode
- **Exact production clipping diode/device:** Not publicly documented.

## Sound
The Normal mode is designed for bright, wiry clean tones, while Saturation pushes the circuit into stronger breakup and gain. Because the pedal contains the power-amp section as well as the preamp, it can operate as a pedalboard preamp or as a small direct-to-speaker amplifier. [1]
