# Acorn Amps — Kakarot Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Kakarot Fuzz
- **Builder:** Acorn Amps
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Hybrid two-transistor fuzz using a hand-picked NOS germanium transistor, a silicon transistor and an added LED clipping section.

## What this pedal is
Kakarot Fuzz uses one germanium transistor feeding one silicon transistor, followed by a pair of LEDs for an additional clipping section. Acorn describes the Fuzz control as sweeping the pedal from gritty boost territory into fuzz crunch. [1]

## Colorways
- **Original:** Red/orange anime-artwork finish.
- **Blue:** Blue alternate finish.
Acorn's current product page explicitly lists Original and Blue as selectable color options. [1]

## Versions and factory options
### Current production
- One hand-picked NOS germanium transistor
- One silicon transistor
- Additional pair of LED clipping devices
- Level control
- Fuzz control
- True bypass
- 9VDC operation
- Less than 30 mA current draw [1]

## Version changes
No separate electronic revision was documented. The current official page presents Original and Blue as color options of the same Kakarot Fuzz circuit. [1]

## Transistor
- **Type:** One germanium transistor + one silicon transistor.
- **Exact part numbers:** Not publicly documented. [1]

## Diode
- **Type:** LED.
- **Count:** Pair of LEDs in the additional clipping section. [1]

## Sound
The Kakarot moves from gritty boost into increasingly fuzzy and compressed sounds as the Fuzz control is advanced. The extra LED clipping section adds another clipping stage beyond the two-transistor core, helping the pedal cover more aggressive textures. [1]
