# Acorn Amps — TMA-1 Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** TMA-1 Fuzz
- **Builder:** Acorn Amps
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Four-stage transistor fuzz with independently selectable clipping approaches in two stages.

## What this pedal is
The TMA-1 is a four-stage transistor fuzz with selectable clipping options in two separate stages. Acorn provides three states for those clipping sections: germanium diodes, silicon diodes, or bypass of the clipping diodes. [1]

## Colorways
- The documented production presentation is a black face with a silver outer enclosure and a large red-lit lens.
- No separately verified factory colorway list was established.

## Versions and factory options
### Current production
- Four-stage transistor fuzz
- Volume, Gain and Tone controls
- Two independent clipping-mode switches
- Germanium, silicon or diode-bypass clipping options
- Large red lens indicator
- True bypass
- 9V tip-negative pedal power [1]

No separate named production revision was documented in the official material reviewed.

## Transistor
- **Type:** Transistor-based four-stage fuzz.
- **Exact transistor count per stage and exact part numbers:** Not publicly documented. [1]

## Diode
- **Type:** Germanium and silicon clipping options, plus clipping-diode bypass.
- **Stage relationship:** The selectable clipping options are provided in two separate stages. [1]

## Sound
The multiple clipping states let the TMA-1 move between softer germanium-style clipping, harder silicon clipping and a no-diode mode. Combined with four gain stages, that gives it a broad range from more open fuzz textures to denser, more compressed distortion-like sounds. [1]
