## Sound
It’s a low to mid gain overdrive handmade in Leipzig, Germany, and it sounds like your amp on a very good day: clear, chimey, dynamically alive.
Four global knobs shape both channels at once, each channel has its own Drive and Level, and two mini toggles dial in sensitivity and midrange character.
Controls The top row runs left to right: DYN, FEED, BODY, TONE.
