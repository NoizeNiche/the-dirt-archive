## What this pedal is

The TwinSonic MkII is a dual-channel analog overdrive and preamp. The **Blue** channel is the original TwinSonic-style discrete overdrive, while the **Red** channel is a smoother, more transparent overdrive designed to stack with the Blue side or operate independently.

## Colorways

- Standard production uses separate **Blue** and **Red** channel identities on the enclosure.
- Finish variants are not treated as circuit revisions.

## Versions and factory options

Each channel has:
- **Gain**
- **Level**
- **Voice**
- **Hi-Cut**
- **Mode** toggle for bass response.

Additional features:
- Two independent footswitches.
- Two true-bypass channels.
- Internal charge-pump supply raises the circuit's working voltage to 18V.
- 9V battery or 9V DC center-negative power.
- Made in Germany.

## Sound

The TwinSonic MkII can be used as two separate overdrives or stacked. The Blue side provides a more open, grainy, discrete drive character; the Red side is smoother and more transparent. The Voice, Hi-Cut, and Mode controls let each channel be tailored independently.
