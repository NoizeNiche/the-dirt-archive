# Caline - CP-515 Carmilla - Hi Gain Distortion Machine

- **Builder:** Caline
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Primary research sources:** Official Caline Technology demonstration; exact-model retail imagery; current Caline clone-list documentation.
- **Identity:** CP-515 Carmilla is a high-gain British-style distortion in the CP-500 series.
- **Colorways:** Black enclosure with yellow bats and red gothic artwork is documented in exact-model imagery.
- **Versions:** No numbered factory version verified.
- **Version changes:** Caline's own 2023 demonstration explicitly states that Carmilla uses the **same circuit as the older Sandstorm**, presented with a new paint job.
- **Controls:** Volume, Gain, Presence, Tone.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented by the checked model-level sources.
- **Diode type:** Not documented by the checked model-level sources.
- **Sound description:** Caline describes classic British high-gain distortion with a mid-focused voice suited to rock, metal and blues. The Presence and Tone controls give the user broad upper-frequency shaping around the core high-gain circuit.
- **Circuit lineage:** Official Caline material documents Carmilla as the same circuit as Sandstorm. Secondary catalog research identifies Sandstorm as an MI Audio Crunch Box-style / JCM800-in-a-box design; the archive records the official same-circuit statement and the secondary donor attribution separately.
- **Power / bypass:** True bypass is documented by Caline; exact current draw was not established from the strongest exact-model source reviewed.
- **Photo status:** Exact-model source imagery located; no local image promoted in this batch.
- **Research confidence:** High for the Carmilla/Sandstorm same-circuit statement; medium for the Crunch Box attribution because that step relies on secondary comparison; low for internal components.

## Sound

This "Hi Gain Distortion Machine" is a great looking and incredibly responsive distortion pedal.
Using the Presence and Distortion control it's possible to go from a light boost, through to a classic rock crunch.
