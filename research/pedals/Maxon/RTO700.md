## What this pedal is
The RTO700 is a real-tube overdrive from Maxon's Real Tube family. The pedal uses a small dual-triode preamp tube in place of the conventional diode-focused clipping architecture and provides three gain stages with EQ and noise-control functions.

## Sound
The RTO700 is designed around a tube-based overdrive response with enough gain range to move from lower-gain drive into more saturated tones. The Real Tube architecture, three gain stages and active EQ provide a broader shaping range than a simple one-stage overdrive.
