## What this pedal is

Maxon describes the OSD-9 as a reissue of its first overdrive, the OD880, originally released circa 1977. The manufacturer describes it as a low-gain, natural overdrive with a tube-amp-like response and booster use. [1]

## Versions and history

- Original reference design: **OD880 Overdrive/Soft Distortion**, circa 1977. [1]
- Limited early-1990s OD880 reissue is noted by the manufacturer. [1]
- The current OSD-9 is the later 9-Series resurrection of that design. [1]
- No separate factory colorway chronology was established.

## Op amps and diodes

- **Input buffer:** 2SK246 JFET. [1]
- **Gain stage:** UA741CP op amp. [1]
- **Post-gain stage:** second UA741CP. [1]
- **Output stages:** 4558 op amps. [1]
- **Clipping:** pair of diodes to ground after the first UA741CP. [1]

## Controls

- **DRIVE:** controls gain. [1]
- **LEVEL:** controls output. [1]

## Circuit / architecture

Maxon documents a 2SK246 JFET buffer feeding a UA741CP gain stage. The next stage uses a pair of diodes to ground for clipping, followed by a second UA741CP, then 4558 unity-gain/output stages. A NJM2374A voltage converter stabilizes the internal 9V supply. Mechanical true-bypass switching uses a Fujisoku 4PDT arrangement. [1]

## Power and physical specification

- **Operating voltage:** 9V. [1]
- **Dimensions:** 74 × 124 × 54 mm. [1]
- **Weight:** 580 g including battery. [1]
- **Battery:** 9V. [1]
- **Maximum gain:** 41.5 dB at 700 Hz. [1]

## Sound

Maxon describes the OSD-9 as warm, open-sounding, dynamically responsive, and suitable both as a standalone dirt box and as an amp booster. The manufacturer also describes a mild high-end roll-off. [1]
