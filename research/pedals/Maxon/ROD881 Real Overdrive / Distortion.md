## What this pedal is
The ROD881 is a tube-based overdrive/distortion with switchable operating modes, a footswitchable boost, and a dedicated noise-reduction section. The manufacturer user guide identifies a built-in real tube and separate overdrive/distortion modes. [1]

## Controls and functions
- **GAIN:** distortion level. [1]
- **BASS:** low-frequency emphasis. [1]
- **MIDDLE:** midrange emphasis. [1]
- **TREBLE:** high-frequency emphasis. [1]
- **MASTER:** effected output level. [1]
- **MODE:** switches between overdrive and distortion operation. [1]
- **BOOST:** footswitchable boost for level changes. [1]
- **NOISE REDUCTION:** dedicated switch/threshold function. [1]

## Tube
- The ROD881 uses a built-in real tube to generate its overdrive/distortion character. [1]
- **Exact production tube designation:** The archived user guide describes a selected dual-triode tube but does not establish a production tube part number in the material checked here. [1]

## Circuit / architecture
The verified guide establishes a real-tube gain architecture with selectable overdrive/distortion operation, boost, and noise reduction, but does not provide a complete component-level schematic. [1]

## Power and physical specification
- The archived user guide specifies dedicated Maxon AC-adaptor operation. [1]
- **Dimensions:** approximately 110 × 160 × 72 mm. [1]
- **Weight:** approximately 700 g. [1]

## Sound
A Maxon-hosted 2006 Guitar Player review describes the ROD881 as offering a tube-like overdrive mode, a higher-gain distortion mode, broad 3-band EQ control, and usable boost/clean-boost behavior. Those are review observations rather than independent measurements by the archive. [2]
