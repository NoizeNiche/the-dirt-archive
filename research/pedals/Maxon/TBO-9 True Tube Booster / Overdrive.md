## What this pedal is
Maxon describes the TBO-9 as a clean booster/overdrive with a real tube gain stage, intended to push clean or semi-clean tube amplifiers while retaining the amplifier's character. [1]

## Tube and circuit
- **Tube:** mil-spec 5703WB JAN Raytheon sub-miniature vacuum tube. [1]
- The tube stage sits at the front of the audio path. [1]
- The following gain section uses dual-stage clipping: an 808-style feedback-loop clipping stage plus a pair of diodes to ground similar to the SD-9 and RAT approach. [1]
- Internal voltage conversion raises 9V input to **30VDC** for the tube circuit. [1]
- Maxon also documents an inrush-current-control system for warming the tube at low voltage during startup. [1]
- Mechanical true bypass uses a Fujisoku 4PDT switch. [1]

## Controls
- **BOOST:** controls tube-gain-stage level. [1]
- **DRIVE:** blends clean and distorted signals. [1]
- **TONE:** tonal response. [1]
- **LEVEL:** output level. [1]

## Power and physical specification
- **Input power:** 9VDC from the Maxon AC2009 supply; the manufacturer lists no battery operation. [1]
- **Internal circuit voltage:** 30VDC. [1]
- **Current consumption:** 270 mA at 9VDC. [1]
- **Dimensions:** 74 × 124 × 54 mm. [1]
- **Weight:** 560 g excluding adaptor. [1]
- **Maximum gain:** 52 dB at 1.6 kHz. [1]

## Sound
Maxon describes the TBO-9 around warm tube gain, clean blending, transparency, increased headroom, and a slightly chewy overdrive response created by its two clipping stages. [1]
