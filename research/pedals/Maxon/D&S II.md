## What this pedal is
Maxon describes the D&S II as a distortion/sustainer using a dual op-amp design related to the OD808, with increased low end, output, and gain compared with that reference. [1]

## Controls
- **Distortion:** distortion amount. [1]
- **Tone:** tonal response. [1]
- **Balance:** clean/effected balance. [1]
- **Normal/Effect:** effect switching. [1]

## Circuit / architecture
The manufacturer describes a dual op-amp design similar to the OD808. [1] The verified product page does not disclose a complete component-level schematic or production transistor/diode part numbers.

## Power and physical specification
- **Power:** 9V manganese battery or special AC adaptor. [1]
- **Input impedance:** 500k ohms. [1]
- **Output impedance:** 10k ohms. [1]
- **Current consumption:** 6 mA at 9V / 7 mA at 10V. [1]
- **Dimensions:** 70 × 112 × 55 mm whole unit. [1]
- **Weight:** 260 g. [1]

## Sound
Maxon describes the D&S II as offering a broader low-end/output/gain envelope than the OD808-style reference, from subtle crunch through heavier saturation. [1]
