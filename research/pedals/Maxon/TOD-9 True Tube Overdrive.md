## Colorways
- For reference, this BB+ has 2 boosts- the red yields tight low end response and is more aggressive, the blue is a bit fatter and yields a more natural tone, retaining more low end.
- I usually hang on Red for most stuff, I like Blue for some leads First I set it up the Maxon with the tube off and slight drive, as I normally would to juice a hi gain head.
- First thing I noticed is that I need to crank that volume all the way up and it still is a bit less than the red at 1oclock.
