## What this pedal is
Maxon/Godlyke describes the SSD-9 as a modified version of the standard SD-9 Sonic Distortion. The documented changes include increased output, an expanded Tone-control range emphasizing lows and lower mids, and optional 18V operation for additional headroom and reduced compression. [1]

## Versions and relationship
- **Base design:** modified SD-9. [1]
- The SSD-9 is kept as a separate catalog identity because Maxon/Godlyke documents it as a specific modified product rather than simply relabeling the standard SD-9. [1]
- The product page also identifies its later relationship to the TWA SH9 Scott Henderson signature distortion. [1]

## Sound
Maxon/Godlyke describes the SSD-9 around a rich, thick, amp-like distortion voice, massive sustain, increased output, stronger low/lower-mid response, and greater headroom at 18V. [1]
