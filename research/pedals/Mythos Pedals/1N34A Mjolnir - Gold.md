## What this pedal is
The 1N34A Mjolnir is Mythos Pedals' Mjolnir variant built around a matched pair of 1N34A germanium diodes. Mythos describes the circuit as a tonal recreation of an early Klon Centaur example, using the same overall boost/overdrive concept while giving the pedal a distinct finish and component treatment.
