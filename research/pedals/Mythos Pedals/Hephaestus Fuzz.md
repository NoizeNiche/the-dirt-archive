## Sound
In the spirit of an icon Inspired by the Bosstone circuit, the Hephaestus started life as an homage to that classic "Spirit in the Sky" fuzz box.
Combining a NPN and PNP transistor pair give you some familiar gain sounds but it wasn't enough.
All while retaining that classic fuzz clean up we all want from these sort of pedals.
