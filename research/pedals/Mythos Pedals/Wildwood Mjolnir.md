## Sound
The Wildwood Edition Mjolnir Overdrive is our attempt to perfectly replicate Zach's personal silver "Centaur".
It has a beautiful low gain overdrive tone that compresses perfectly and makes your guitar signal just sound bigger.
The latest 2023 iteration has a slight reduction in low mids, a tuned Treble knob, and Gain & Output taper so that it matches the original units feel.
