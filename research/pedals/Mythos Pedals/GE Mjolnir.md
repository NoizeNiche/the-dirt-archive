## What this pedal is

The GE Mjolnir is a germanium-diode overdrive designed around the feel of a classic low-to-medium gain drive. Mythos describes its clipping response as smooth and compressed and intended for stacking.

## Versions and factory options

- **1N34A** germanium diode terminology is documented.
- Buffered bypass.
- Approx. 25mA current draw in the checked product data.
- Enclosure approximately 4.82 × 2.62 × 1.43 inches.
