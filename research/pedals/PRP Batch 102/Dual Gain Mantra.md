# BlackOutEffectors - Dual Gain Mantra

## PRP identity

- **Builder:** BlackOutEffectors
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive

## Research

The Limited Edition Dual-Gain Mantra is a modified Mantra overdrive with an added low-gain mode. The added toggle extends the range downward while retaining the original higher-gain Mantra setting. The core uses cascaded MOSFET and JFET amplifier stages for touch-sensitive response and volume-control cleanup, while a Baxandall-style EQ makes the pedal adaptable to both scooped and mid-present amplifiers.

## Features

- High- and low-gain modes
- Cascaded MOSFET/JFET gain stages
- Baxandall-style bass/treble EQ
- Responsive to guitar-volume cleanup
- Limited-edition cosmetic treatment
- Handmade in Asheville, North Carolina
