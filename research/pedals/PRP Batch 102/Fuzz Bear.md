# BlackOutEffectors - Fuzz Bear

## PRP identity

- **Builder:** BlackOutEffectors
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz

## Research

Fuzz Bear is a silicon fuzz rooted in the Tone Bender MkII family and was developed as a wide-range, highly interactive fuzz/noise instrument. Its Range, Bias and Sag controls alter the signal level, transistor bias and voltage reaching the circuit. Those controls can move from conventional thick fuzz and lower-gain drive toward gated, splatty and oscillating sounds, including noisemaker-style behavior.

## Controls

- Range
- Bias
- Sag
- Fuzz
- Level

The control interaction is especially important: Range shapes frequency emphasis and input drive, Bias adjusts the fuzz transistor operating point, and Sag reduces the voltage available to the circuit.
