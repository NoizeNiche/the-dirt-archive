# BlackOutEffectors - Crystal Dagger

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** BlackOutEffectors
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz

## Research
Crystal Dagger combines an octave-up fuzz with a ring-modulator/phaser section. The fuzz side is independently controllable and adds its upper-octave effect with a mini-toggle. The modulation side uses an LFO-swept filter in parallel with the input signal. Premier Guitar documents dedicated analog circuit boards for the two sections and a control arrangement that allows the effects to be engaged separately and blended.

## Controls and architecture
- Fuzz side filter and volume
- Switchable octave-up
- Ring-modulator/phaser carrier-frequency control
- Modulation-side volume
- Blend between the two sections
- Separate analog circuit boards
- True bypass
