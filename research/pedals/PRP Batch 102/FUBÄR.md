# BlackOutEffectors - FUBÄR

## PRP identity

- **Builder:** BlackOutEffectors
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz

## Research

FUBÄR is a heavily modifiable silicon fuzz descended from Blackout Effectors' Fuzz Bear. The documented circuit uses a Tone Bender MkII foundation with added control over range, bias and voltage sag. Those controls can move the pedal from conventional fuzz into gated, oscillating, compressed and synth-like textures. An expression connection can control the sag behavior in real time.

## Controls and architecture

- Range
- Bias
- Sag
- Fuzz
- EXP
- Level
- Dedicated SAG/EXP footswitch
- Expression input
- Internal EXP configuration DIP switch
- True bypass
- 9V center-negative power
