# BlackOutEffectors - Fix'd Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** BlackOutEffectors
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz

## Research
Fix'd Fuzz is the original multi-stage Blackout Effectors fuzz platform. The V3 documentation describes two contrasting fuzz voices plus a full-bodied boost/overdrive stage and a broad tone-sculpting stage. Each of the four circuit stages can be bypassed with its own toggle, allowing the pedal to move from boost and lighter drive sounds into denser stacked fuzz. The documented Poppy and Ant graphics are cosmetic variants of the same circuit.

## Features
- Two contrasting fuzz stages
- Boost through light overdrive
- Wide tone-shaping stage
- Four independently bypassable circuit stages
- True bypass
- Handmade construction
