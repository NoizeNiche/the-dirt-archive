# BlackOutEffectors - Mantra Overdrive

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** BlackOutEffectors
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive

## Research
Mantra is an all-discrete overdrive built around multiple cascaded MOSFET and JFET gain stages. Blackout's documentation emphasizes touch sensitivity and the ability to clean up from the guitar's volume control. The pedal uses a Baxandall-style bass/treble section and has enough gain range to move from lower-output vintage-style overdrive into stronger crunch and drive.

## Controls
- Bass
- Treble
- Gain
- Volume

Blackout documents the circuit as using discrete transistors/components with no diode clipping.
