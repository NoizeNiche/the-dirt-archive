# BlackOutEffectors - Fix'd Fuzz Deluxe

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** BlackOutEffectors
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz / Overdrive

## Research
Fix'd Fuzz Deluxe expands the original Fix'd Fuzz with more external control. Effects Database describes two contrasting fuzz tones, a full-range clean boost through overdrive, and a broad tone-sculpting stage in a compact enclosure. Compared with the stock Fix'd, the Deluxe brings additional tweaking capability, including the externalized controls used on the Twosome versions.

## Features
- Two contrasting fuzz voices
- Clean boost through overdrive
- Wide tone-sculpting stage
- External controls for the fuzz stages
- Multiple switchable stages
- True bypass
- Handmade construction
