# BlackOutEffectors - A Very Special Twosome

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** BlackOutEffectors
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz

## Research
A Very Special Twosome is a limited-edition dual-fuzz version of Blackout Effectors' Twosome, combining the Musket Fuzz and Fix'd Fuzz Deluxe in one enclosure. The special run allowed the two circuits to operate independently or together, with a switch selecting the stacking order. The Fix'd side covers boost, overdrive and multiple fuzz textures, while the Musket side expands the Big Muff family with Pre, Mids and Focus controls.

## Features and architecture
- Fix'd Fuzz Deluxe section
- Musket Fuzz section
- Independent or simultaneous operation
- Selectable signal order when stacking
- True bypass
- 9V DC operation
- ProGuitarShop-exclusive limited run, including a documented 100-unit run
