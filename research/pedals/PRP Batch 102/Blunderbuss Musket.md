## PRP identity

## Research
Blunderbuss Musket is the alternate Musket-derived version that became the basis for the standalone Blunderbuss and the Blunderbuss-equipped Special Twosome family. Blackout's documented description calls it an alternate take on the Musket circuit rather than a later V3 Musket. The design retains the Musket's heavy four-stage fuzz foundation while shifting the character toward a louder, more open version.

## Architecture
- Musket-family four-stage fuzz foundation
- Alternate Blunderbuss revision rather than the standard V3 Musket
- Closely related to the hybrid germanium/silicon Blunderbuss circuit
- Used in Blackout's limited Special Twosome variants
