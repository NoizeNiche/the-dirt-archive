# Cordovox - FZ-7 Fuzz

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Cordovox
- **Catalog identity:** FZ-7 Fuzz
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Exact Cordovox FZ-7 Fuzz, also marketed by Guitar Villa as the CP-FZ-7 Multimode Fuzz.

## Controls and modes
- **LEVEL:** output volume.
- **TONE:** tonal balance.
- **FUZZ:** fuzz amount.
- **Mode switch:** three voices:
  - **MUFF:** classic fuzz character.
  - **BRIGHT:** clearer, sharper, higher-output response.
  - **WARM:** fuller, higher-gain fuzz response.

## Construction
- True bypass footswitch with LED indicator.
- Stainless-steel enclosure with mirror surface finish.
- German WIMA capacitors.
- Sealed metal potentiometers.
- Input impedance: 1 MΩ.
- Output impedance: 100 kΩ.

## Power
- 9V battery.
- 9V DC adapter, 200 mA, center negative.

## Dimensions
Approximately 9.5 x 12.5 x 4.5 cm / 3.7 x 4.9 x 1.8 in.

## Research notes
The Cordovox FZ-7 is part of the same modern Cordovox family that includes the DS-8, DS-9, OD-7, and OD-8. Effects Database identifies the pedal as a three-mode fuzz and documents the switching and component-family details above.
