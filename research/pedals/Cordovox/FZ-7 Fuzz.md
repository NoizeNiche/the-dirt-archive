## What this pedal is

The Cordovox FZ-7 is a three-mode fuzz that combines vintage- and modern-voiced distortion textures in one pedal.

## Controls and modes

- **LEVEL:** output volume.
- **TONE:** tonal balance.
- **FUZZ:** fuzz amount.
- Mode toggle:
  - **MUFF:** classic fuzz character.
  - **BRIGHT:** clearer, sharper, higher-output response.
  - **WARM:** fuller, higher-gain fuzz response.

## Construction

- True bypass footswitch with LED indicator.
- Stainless-steel enclosure with mirror surface finish.
- German WIMA audio capacitors.
- Sealed metal potentiometers.
- Input impedance: 1MΩ.
- Output impedance: 100kΩ.

## Power

- 9V battery.
- 9V DC, 200mA center-negative adapter.

## Sound

MUFF provides the most traditional fuzz response, BRIGHT emphasizes clarity and edge, and WARM produces a fuller and more saturated voice. The three-position design gives the FZ-7 a broader range than a fixed-voicing fuzz.
