# Cordovox - OD-8 X-Drive

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Cordovox
- **Catalog identity:** OD-8 X-Drive
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Exact Cordovox OD-8 X-Drive, also sold as CP-OD-8.

## What this pedal is
The OD-8 X-Drive is an overdrive pedal with a swappable-chip architecture and three selectable operating modes. The installed JRC4558 can be replaced without soldering using the supplied TL072 or NE5532 options.

## Controls and modes
- **TONE:** tonal balance.
- **VOLUME:** output level.
- **DRIVE:** gain amount.
- **Mode switch:** TS, BRIGHT, or WARM.
  - **TS:** saturated variation of the classic TS-9-type response.
  - **BRIGHT:** brighter, smoother-saturated, higher-output response.
  - **WARM:** warmer, higher-gain overdrive.

## Circuit and components
- Installed chipset: JRC4558.
- Included interchangeable chipsets: TL072 and NE5532.
- German WIMA audio capacitors.
- True bypass footswitch with LED indicator.
- Stainless-steel / metal mirror-finish enclosure.

## Power and dimensions
- 9V battery or 9V DC adapter, center negative.
- DC adapter specification: 200 mA.
- Approximately 9.5 x 12.5 x 4.5 cm / 3.7 x 4.9 x 1.8 in.
- Approximate weight: 480 g / 16.9 oz.

## OEM / design-family note
Effects Database identifies the OD-8 as an OEM design manufactured by Biyang and distributed under other brand names. The site also groups the Cordovox OD-8 with the Biyang OD-8 X-Drive family.

## Sound

The circuit provides three selectable overdrive modes via a toggle switch: TS mode delivers a saturated variation of the classic TS 9 sound, BRIGHT mode emphasizes brightness, smooth saturation, and higher output, while WARM mode provides a warm, high gain overdrive tone.
The pedal is equipped with TONE, VOLUME, and DRIVE controls, providing flexible shaping of the overdrive response.
Housed in a durable metal chassis with a mirror finished surface, the OD 8 X Drive combines rugged construction with a polished appearance.
