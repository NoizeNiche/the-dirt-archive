# Cordovox - DS-9 Distortion

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Cordovox
- **Catalog identity:** DS-9 Distortion
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** Exact Cordovox DS-9 Distortion.

## What this pedal is
The DS-9 is a straightforward analog distortion with two primary controls, LEVEL and DISTORTION. It is aimed at a simple progression from lower-gain grit to heavier saturation.

## Controls
- **LEVEL:** output volume.
- **DISTORTION:** gain amount.
- **Footswitch:** true bypass with LED indicator.

## Construction and power
- Analog distortion circuit.
- German WIMA audio capacitors.
- Stainless-steel enclosure with mirror finish.
- Sealed metal potentiometers are part of the Cordovox construction family.
- Input and output are 1/4-inch jacks.
- 9V battery or 9V DC adapter, center negative.
- Current consumption: 4 mA.
- Dimensions: approximately 12.7 x 9.5 x 6.3 cm.
