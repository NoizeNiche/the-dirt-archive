# Cordovox - OD-7 Overdrive

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Cordovox
- **Catalog identity:** OD-7 Overdrive
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Exact Cordovox OD-7 Overdrive, also sold as CP-OD-7.

## What this pedal is
The OD-7 is a three-mode overdrive built around the JRC4558 chipset and inspired by classic Ibanez TS-series circuits. Its three voices cover a TS-style saturated setting plus brighter and warmer alternatives.

## Controls and modes
- **TONE:** tonal balance.
- **VOLUME:** output level.
- **DRIVE:** gain amount.
- **Mode switch:** TS, BRIGHT, or WARM.

## Circuit and construction
- JRC4558 chipset.
- German WIMA audio capacitors.
- Sealed metal potentiometers.
- Stainless-steel enclosure with mirror finish.
- True bypass footswitch with LED indicator.
- Input impedance: 1 MΩ.
- Output impedance: 100 kΩ.

## Power
- 9V battery.
- 9V DC adapter, 200 mA, center negative.

## Dimensions and weight
Approximately 9.5 x 12.5 x 4.5 cm / 3.7 x 4.9 x 1.8 in.
Effects Database lists approximate weight at 465 g.

## Related-design note
The Effects Database related-design tree places the Cordovox OD-7 in the same broader family as Biyang OD-7 and several other rebranded three-mode pedals. Treat that as family/OEM provenance rather than as a blanket claim that every implementation is component-identical.
