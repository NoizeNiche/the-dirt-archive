# Cordovox - DS-8 Mouse

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Cordovox
- **Catalog identity:** DS-8 Mouse
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** Exact Cordovox DS-8 Mouse, also sold as CP-DS-8.

## What this pedal is
The DS-8 Mouse is a RAT-inspired distortion pedal using a high-quality American chipset and classic distortion circuitry. It provides three selectable clipping modes with different diode stages.

## Controls and modes
- **Volume:** output level.
- **Filter:** overall tone shaping.
- **Distortion:** gain/saturation.
- **Mode switch:** Normal, Turbo, or Max.
  - **Normal:** smoother, more natural distortion.
  - **Turbo:** higher output and a thicker presentation.
  - **Max:** denser, higher-gain distortion.

## Construction and signal path
- True bypass footswitch with LED indicator.
- German WIMA capacitors.
- Sealed metal potentiometers.
- Stainless-steel enclosure with mirror-surface finish.
- Input impedance: 1 MΩ.
- Output impedance: 100 kΩ.

## Power
- 9V battery or 9V DC adapter.
- Effects Database documents center-negative DC power.

## OEM / related-design note
Effects Database lists the DS-8 alongside related Biyang/Tonefancier designs. That relationship should be treated as an OEM/design-family note rather than assuming every internal component is identical to another brand's version.
