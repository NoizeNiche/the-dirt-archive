# Champion City Effects — Cosmic Owl

## PRP identity

- **Identity:** Champion City Effects Cosmic Owl, an analog two-control IC distortion based on a classic tan-and-black 1970s-style distortion circuit with substantial modifications.

## What this pedal is
RockBoard PedalPedia documents the Cosmic Owl as Champion City Effects' take on a classic two-knob IC-based distortion used by guitarists since the 1970s, with substantial changes to the original design. It identifies the circuit as analog and the power requirement as 9V DC center negative. [1]

Champion City Effects describes its pedals as hand-built one at a time, using stripboard construction and in-house-finished enclosures. [2]
