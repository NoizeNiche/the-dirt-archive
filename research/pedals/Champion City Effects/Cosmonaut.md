# Champion City Effects — Cosmonaut

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Cosmonaut
- **Builder:** Champion City Effects
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** Champion City Effects Cosmonaut, a Green Tall Font Russian Big Muff-inspired distortion/fuzz design using matched Russian transistors and diodes.

## What this pedal is
Champion City Effects' PedalPedia entry describes the Cosmonaut as based on the builder's favorite Green Tall Font Russian Big Muff, emphasizing smoothness, massive low end, and clarity. The builder says its version uses original Russian KT3102E/KT3102EM transistors and Russian KD521 diodes, with matched transistor gain. [1]

## Transistor
Champion City Effects explicitly identifies **KT3102E and KT3102EM** Russian transistors and states that the KT3102E devices are selected and gain matched. [1]

## Diode
Champion City Effects explicitly identifies **KD521** Russian diodes and says they are matched. [1]

## Sound
The Cosmonaut is voiced around the thick, smooth low-end-heavy character associated with the Green Russian Big Muff family, with the builder emphasizing clarity and matched components. [1]
