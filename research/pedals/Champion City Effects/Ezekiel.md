# Champion City Effects — Ezekiel

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Ezekiel
- **Builder:** Champion City Effects
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz / Overdrive / Distortion
- **Identity:** Champion City Effects Ezekiel 25:17, a bass-oriented high-output dirt pedal with blend and selectable clipping.

## What this pedal is
Champion City Effects describes the Ezekiel 25:17 as a bass overdrive/distortion design with high output, substantial gain, tone control, blend, and multiple clipping options. The builder modified the clipping arrangement of the original concept and added Germanium and Silicon options. [1]

## Versions and factory options
The builder describes the clipping arrangement as moving from medium/low-gain overdrive through smoother germanium clipping to more aggressive silicon and LED clipping. [1]

The pedal also features a **blend control** alongside the gain/tone/output controls described by the builder. [1]

No formal numbered factory V1/V2 sequence was established.

## Version changes
Champion City Effects states that it changed the clipping arrangement of the original circuit and added two options, Germanium and Silicon. [1]

## Sound
The builder positions the Ezekiel from medium/low-gain overdrive through smoother germanium clipping and increasingly aggressive silicon/LED clipping. Its blend control and high output are specifically presented as useful in a bass-oriented dirt context. [1]
