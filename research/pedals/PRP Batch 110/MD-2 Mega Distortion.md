# BOSS - MD-2 Mega Distortion

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** BOSS
- **Catalog type:** Distortion

## Research
The MD-2 Mega Distortion is designed around extreme, bottom-heavy distortion for modern metal and hard rock. BOSS describes a dual-stage distortion circuit with a separate gain-boost section to create high levels of distortion and sustain.

Its Bottom control shapes low-frequency weight, while the Tone control balances high and low frequencies. The front panel provides Dist, Bottom, Tone, Level, and Gain Boost controls.

## Colorways

## Versions and factory options
- Gain Boost provides additional drive and sustain.
- Bottom control emphasizes low-frequency weight.
- Tone control balances high and low frequencies.

## Version changes
BOSS documents the MD-2 as a single production model; the factory options are its gain-boost and tone-shaping controls rather than separate versions.

## Sound
The MD-2 is voiced for huge low-end distortion and sustained modern high-gain sounds. Bottom can reinforce the low-frequency weight, while the separate gain boost can take the dual-stage distortion into a more saturated range.
