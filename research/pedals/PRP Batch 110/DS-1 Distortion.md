# BOSS - DS-1 Distortion

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** BOSS
- **Catalog type:** Distortion

## Research
The DS-1 Distortion was introduced in 1978 as BOSS's first distortion pedal. BOSS continues to describe the standard DS-1 as the original design, with hard-edged attack, smooth sustain, and a wide-ranging Tone control.

Its gain section uses a two-stage circuit with transistor and op-amp gain stages. BOSS specifies the three-control layout as Dist, Tone, and Level.

## Colorways
- Classic orange BOSS enclosure.
- A separate limited DS-1-WH pearlescent-white edition was introduced in 2024.

## Versions and factory options
- Standard production DS-1.
- DS-1-WH limited white finish, introduced in 2024.

## Version changes
The standard DS-1 is described by BOSS as retaining its original design. The 2024 DS-1-WH changes the exterior finish rather than establishing a new circuit version.

## Transistor
BOSS documents transistor and op-amp gain stages in the DS-1 circuit, but does not publicly specify the individual production transistor part numbers on the current product page.

## Diode
BOSS does not publicly specify the individual production diode part numbers on the current product page.

## Sound
The DS-1 produces a tight, hard-edged distortion with smooth sustain and strong harmonic presence. Its Tone circuit can move the response from brighter, cutting sounds toward fuller and smoother voices, while lower Dist settings can be used as a boost.

## Controls and specifications
- Dist
- Tone
- Level
- 9V battery or PSA-series AC adapter
- 10 mA current draw
- 1M ohm input impedance
- 1k ohm output impedance
- 73 x 129 x 59 mm
