# BOSS - DS-1X Distortion

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** BOSS
- **Catalog type:** Distortion

## Research
The DS-1X is a distinct digital-era member of the DS-1 family. BOSS describes it as using Multi-Dimensional Processing (MDP) technology to produce a tight, responsive distortion with controlled lows and strong definition across complex playing.

Unlike the original DS-1 family, the DS-1X uses BOSS digital signal-processing technology rather than a traditional analog distortion circuit. Its controls are Level, Low, High, and Dist.

## Colorways
- Silver/chrome-finished BOSS compact enclosure with the DS-1X control and graphics treatment.

## Versions and factory options
- Standard DS-1X production model.

## Version changes
The DS-1X is not a Waza Craft version of the DS-1. It is a separate MDP-based design.

## Transistor

Individual production diode part numbers are not publicly documented by BOSS for the DS-1X.

## Sound
The DS-1X is voiced for a tight low end, pronounced note definition, and a responsive distortion character that stays articulate with chords and fast playing. Its Low and High controls let the player shape the spectrum directly rather than using the original DS-1's single Tone control.
