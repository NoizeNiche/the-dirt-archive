# BOSS - FZ-1W Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** BOSS
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz

## Research
The FZ-1W is a Waza Craft fuzz introduced in 2021 as a new all-analog design inspired by classic 1960s fuzz sounds. BOSS states that it uses high-quality silicon transistors and provides separate Vintage and Modern modes.

Vintage mode offers a wider gain range and a raspy, assertive character that responds strongly to guitar-volume changes. Modern mode raises the gain and emphasizes a fatter, more singing fuzz voice.

## Colorways
- White Waza Craft enclosure with dark control graphics.

## Versions and factory options
- **Vintage mode:** broader gain range and vintage-style response.
- **Modern mode:** more gain, focused mids, and fuller sustain.

## Version changes
The two modes are factory voicing options within one FZ-1W model. BOSS presents the FZ-1W as a new Waza Craft circuit inspired by 1960s fuzz rather than as an exact recreation of one historic circuit.

## Transistor
BOSS explicitly states that the FZ-1W uses high-quality silicon transistors. Individual transistor part numbers are not publicly specified.

## Diode
BOSS does not publicly specify the individual production diode part numbers.

## Sound
Vintage mode is raspy, dynamic, and particularly responsive to guitar-volume changes. Modern mode increases gain and sustain while concentrating the mids, producing a thicker and more singing fuzz character.

## Controls and specifications
- Fuzz
- Tone
- Level
- Vintage/Modern mode switch
- All-analog circuit
- Silicon-transistor design
- 9V operation
- 16 mA current draw
