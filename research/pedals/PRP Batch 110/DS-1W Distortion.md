# BOSS - DS-1W Distortion

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** BOSS
- **Catalog type:** Distortion

## Research
The DS-1W is the Waza Craft version of the classic DS-1. BOSS states that it keeps the original character in Standard mode while using a newly revised discrete all-analog circuit for expanded range and versatility.

Custom mode provides a fatter distortion with more focused mids and increased touch response. The Waza Craft circuit is built around discrete analog components rather than the standard DS-1 implementation.

## Colorways
- Black Waza Craft enclosure with the DS-1 graphic identity retained in the panel design.

## Versions and factory options
- **Standard mode:** classic DS-1 character.
- **Custom mode:** fatter, more mid-focused distortion with greater touch response.

## Version changes
The DS-1W is a materially revised Waza Craft version of the DS-1. Its major factory change is the revised discrete all-analog circuitry plus the switchable Custom mode.

## Transistor
The production DS-1W uses discrete analog circuitry, but BOSS does not publicly specify the individual transistor part numbers.

## Diode
BOSS does not publicly specify the individual production diode part numbers.

## Sound
Standard mode stays close to the familiar DS-1 voice, including its hard-edged attack and smooth sustain. Custom mode is thicker and more mid-focused, with a more responsive feel when digging into the strings.
