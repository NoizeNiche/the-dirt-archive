# BOSS - FZ-5 Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** BOSS
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz

## Research
The FZ-5 is a COSM-based BOSS fuzz designed to recreate several classic fuzz flavors through digital modeling. BOSS specifically cites the Maestro FZ-1A, Fuzz Face, and Octavia as inspirations for the modeled sounds.

Its front panel uses Fuzz, Mode, and Level controls. BOSS also documents a Boost function for pushing the modeled fuzz character into a more aggressive range.

## Colorways
- Classic silver BOSS compact enclosure.

## Versions and factory options
- Mode control for selecting the modeled fuzz character.
- Boost control for adding a more aggressive character.

## Version changes
BOSS presents the FZ-5 as a single COSM-based model rather than documenting separate hardware revisions on its current product page.

## Sound
The FZ-5 covers several vintage-inspired fuzz families, from a classic Fuzz Face-style response to more aggressive octave-flavored textures. The Boost function can increase attack and intensity without changing the pedal's basic modeled architecture.
