# BOSS - JB-2 Angry Driver

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** BOSS
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive

## Research
The JB-2 Angry Driver was introduced in 2017 through a collaboration between BOSS and JHS Pedals. It combines the Blues Driver voice with the JHS Angry Charlie voice in one compact pedal.

Three dual-concentric control sets provide separate Drive, Tone, and Level controls for the two drive sides. A six-position mode selector permits independent operation, series stacking, or parallel operation.

## Colorways
- Classic BOSS compact enclosure with BOSS and JHS branding.

## Versions and factory options
- BOSS/Blues Driver side.
- JHS/Angry Charlie side.
- Individual operation, two series configurations, and parallel operation through the six-position selector.
- Built-in footswitch toggling between the two drive types.

## Version changes
The JB-2 is a combined-circuit collaboration model, not a version of the BD-2. It was introduced as a new product for the 40th anniversary of the BOSS compact pedal line.

## Transistor
BOSS does not publicly specify individual production transistor part numbers for the JB-2.

## Diode
BOSS does not publicly specify individual production diode part numbers for the JB-2.

## Sound
The Blues Driver side covers lower-gain boost and overdrive, while the Angry Charlie side reaches medium-high overdrive and distortion. Series and parallel modes turn the two voices into stacking and blend-oriented combinations with substantially different gain and response.

## Controls and specifications
- Dual-concentric Drive, Tone, and Level controls
- Six-position mode selector
- Built-in mode toggle
- Remote switch jack
- 9V operation
- 55 mA current draw
