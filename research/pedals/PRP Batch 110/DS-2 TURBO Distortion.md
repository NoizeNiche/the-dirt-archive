# BOSS - DS-2 TURBO Distortion

## PRP identity

- **Builder:** BOSS
- **Catalog type:** Distortion

## Research

The DS-2 was released in 1987 as a two-mode evolution of the BOSS compact distortion format. Its Turbo I and Turbo II modes provide different emphasis and response, with the first covering dynamic mid-focused drive and distortion and the second providing a stronger resonant midrange boost.

BOSS identifies the DS-2 as a discrete analog circuit with low noise and an active Tone control that shapes both high and low frequencies. The pedal also has a Remote jack for external Turbo switching.

## Colorways

- Classic orange BOSS enclosure.

## Versions and factory options

- **Turbo I:** mid-focused sound with enhanced low end.
- **Turbo II:** stronger midrange boost and a more harmonically rich high-gain voice.
- External footswitch control is supported through the Remote jack.

## Version changes

The two Turbo modes are factory voicing options within the DS-2 rather than separate catalog versions.

## Sound

Turbo I ranges from gritty drive to thick distortion and responds to picking intensity at lower gain settings. Turbo II pushes the midrange forward for a more forceful, harmonically dense distortion character.
