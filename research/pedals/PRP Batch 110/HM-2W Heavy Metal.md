# BOSS - HM-2W Heavy Metal

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** BOSS
- **Catalog type:** Distortion

## Research
The HM-2W is the Waza Craft reissue of the HM-2 Heavy Metal, the orange-and-black BOSS pedal originally produced from 1983 to 1991. BOSS describes the HM-2W as using an authentic analog implementation of the classic circuit with lower noise and a higher maximum output level.

Standard mode is intended to deliver the original HM-2 character, while Custom mode applies a more aggressive Waza Craft tuning. The pedal also adds a premium buffer while preserving the recognizable orange-and-black appearance.

## Colorways
- Orange-and-black enclosure matching the classic HM-2 appearance.

## Versions and factory options
- **Standard mode:** original HM-2 voicing with lower noise and increased level.
- **Custom mode:** more aggressive Waza Craft variation.
- Mode switch relocated to the top to preserve the classic control layout.

## Version changes
The HM-2W is a Waza Craft redesign/reissue rather than a simple cosmetic reissue. BOSS documents refined analog circuitry, lower noise, a higher maximum level, and a premium bypass buffer.

## Transistor
BOSS publicly documents the HM-2W as an authentic analog implementation but does not specify the individual production transistor part numbers.

## Diode
BOSS does not publicly specify the individual production diode part numbers.

## Sound
Standard mode retains the focused, aggressive character associated with the original HM-2, while Custom mode pushes the attack and fullness further. The familiar mid-heavy character can be used for heavily saturated distortion, including the classic chainsaw-style approach associated with the original pedal.
