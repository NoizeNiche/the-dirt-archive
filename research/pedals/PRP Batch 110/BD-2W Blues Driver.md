# BOSS - BD-2W Blues Driver

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** BOSS
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive

## Research
The BD-2W is the Waza Craft version of the BOSS Blues Driver. BOSS describes it as an all-discrete analog redesign of the classic BD-2, with Standard mode preserving the familiar Blues Driver character and Custom mode adding more body and sustain.

The pedal is made in Japan and is designed to remain highly responsive to picking dynamics and guitar-volume changes. It uses Gain, Tone, and Level controls plus a Standard/Custom mode switch.

## Colorways
- Standard Waza Craft black enclosure with BOSS/Waza Craft branding.

## Versions and factory options
- **Standard mode:** classic BD-2 voicing.
- **Custom mode:** increased body and sustain.
- The model is a Waza Craft redesign rather than a cosmetic-only reissue.

## Version changes
Compared with the standard BD-2, the BD-2W uses a newly revised all-analog discrete amplifier circuit and adds the Custom mode.

## Transistor
BOSS documents an all-discrete analog amplifier circuit, but does not publicly specify the individual transistor part numbers for the production BD-2W.

## Diode
BOSS does not publicly specify the individual production diode part numbers for the BD-2W.

## Sound
Standard mode delivers the familiar Blues Driver response, while Custom mode adds more body and sustain. The circuit remains highly touch-sensitive, so picking attack and guitar-volume changes have a noticeable effect on the amount of breakup.

## Controls and specifications
- Gain
- Tone
- Level
- Standard/Custom mode switch
- 9V battery or PSA-series AC adapter
- 18 mA current draw
- 1M ohm input impedance
- 1k ohm output impedance
- 73 x 129 x 59 mm
- 430 g including battery
