# Acid Fuzz — Mk1.5

## PRP identity
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Hand-built recreation of the 1965 Sola Sound Tone Bender Mk1.5.

## What this pedal is
Acid Fuzz describes the Mk1.5 as a faithful clone of the 1965 Sola Sound Mk1.5 Tone Bender. It is extremely close in design to an Arbiter Fuzz Face, with the key documented distinction being its use of OC75 germanium transistors. [1]

## Colorways
- Multiple custom enclosure presentations existed across Acid Fuzz builds.
- No single finish should be treated as universal.

## Versions / options
- Hand-selected germanium transistor pair.
- Built-in on/off power isolation switch.
- Earlier production format documented as a rugged, pedalboard-friendly build. [1]

## Transistor
- **Type:** Germanium.
- **Documented part:** OC75 pair, selected for gain and leakage. [1]

## Diode
- Separate clipping diode: **Not publicly documented.**

## Sound
The Mk1.5 occupies the two-transistor Tone Bender/Fuzz Face family, with a strong vintage fuzz character and cleanup associated with that topology. The OC75 pair is selected to reproduce the classic 1960s response. [1]
