# Acid Fuzz — Professional MkII

## PRP identity
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Hand-built recreation of the 1960s Sola Sound/VOX Professional MkII Tone Bender.

## What this pedal is
The Professional MkII is a recreation of the 1960s Professional MkII Tone Bender. Acid Fuzz documents hand-selected germanium transistors, adjustable bias, a tone switch, and a built-in power isolation arrangement. Documented transistor options include Mullard OC75, OC81D and AC125 devices depending on build. [1]

## Colorways
- Documented hammered-silver finish on a surviving Professional MkII listing. [2]
- Other Acid Fuzz production finishes may vary.

## Versions / options
- Standard Professional MkII: selected Mullard OC75 germanium set.
- Alternate transistor builds: OC81D or AC125.
- Adjustable bias.
- Tone switch / bass boost.
- Built-in on/off power switch.
- Later MKII Mini: 125B enclosure, NOS germanium set, optional OC75/OC81D, LED, external bias, FAT switch and 9V DC jack. [1][3]

## Version changes
The MKII Mini is a smaller, pedalboard-friendly version with optional external bias and additional build options. The original Professional MkII format is the larger vintage-style recreation with its built-in controls and component options. [1][3]

## Transistor
- **Type:** Germanium.
- **Documented parts:** Mullard OC75; optional OC81D and AC125. [1]
- Individual production units can therefore differ by selected transistor set.

## Diode
- Separate clipping diode: **Not publicly documented.**

## Sound
The Professional MkII is voiced for thick, smooth, highly sustaining Tone Bender fuzz with a characteristic germanium edge. The bias control can move it from the centered vintage voice toward sputtery, dying-battery and chainsaw-like textures, while the tone switch can thin the low/mid content for more cutting lead and rhythm placement. [1]
