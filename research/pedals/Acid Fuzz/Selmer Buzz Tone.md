# Acid Fuzz — Selmer Buzz Tone

## PRP identity
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Acid Fuzz's recreation of the late-1960s Selmer Buzz Tone, built from the original circuit concept and using a trio of RCA 2N2613 germanium transistors.

## What this pedal is
The original Selmer Buzz Tone is a British late-1960s fuzz with two controls, Depth and Volume, and an unusual low-voltage power arrangement. Acid Fuzz describes its version as an exact replica and specifically says it uses the original trio of three RCA 2N2613 germanium transistors. Acid Fuzz also describes the circuit as a dirty full-range booster rather than a conventional fuzz, despite cataloging it in the fuzz section. [1][2]

## Colorways
- Acid Fuzz's current product page does not document a definitive production colorway list.
- The original Selmer pedal used an angled metal enclosure with a hammered finish. [3]

## Versions / options
- **Selmer Buzz Tone:** Acid Fuzz's main limited-edition recreation, with Depth and Volume controls.
- **Selmer Buzz Tone Re-build:** separate Acid Fuzz rebuild using the original eyelet-board layout, NOS germanium transistors, LED status light, true bypass, and 3V power from two AA batteries. [4]

## Version changes
The Re-build is a separate construction variant rather than simply a cosmetic revision. It retains the original eyelet-board layout but adds modern true-bypass switching and an LED, while the main Buzz Tone recreation is wired in a more original-style format with a fixed input cable and integrated on/off switch. [1][4]

## Transistor
- **Type:** Germanium according to Acid Fuzz.
- **Documented parts:** three RCA 2N2613 germanium transistors. [1]
- **Important historical note:** Effects Database lists the original Selmer circuit as three 2N2613 devices but labels them silicon, which conflicts with Acid Fuzz's direct builder documentation. The archive follows the builder's explicit component identification for the Acid Fuzz recreation and records the discrepancy rather than silently resolving it. [2]

## Diode
- Separate clipping diode: **Not publicly documented.**

## Sound
This is a loud, wide-range, amp-pushing dirt box with a strong vintage transistor character rather than a conventional woolly fuzz. Acid Fuzz describes it as adding amp-like coloration and enough output to push an amplifier hard, while the Depth control changes the intensity and character of the dirt. [1]
