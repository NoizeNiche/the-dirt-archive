# Acid Fuzz — Sonic Boom

## PRP identity
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Acid Fuzz's multi-effect recreation of the effects built into Vox Starstream/Ultrasonic/Delta instruments, combining booster, fuzz/distortion, and repeater/tremolo functions.

## What this pedal is
Sonic Boom is a three-effect analog unit based on the Vox onboard effects circuit. It combines a frequency-selective booster, a germanium or silicon fuzz/distortion section depending on build, and a highly modified Vox Repeat Percussion-style repeater. Acid Fuzz produced both point-to-point and PCB construction, with later versions adding more routing, voicing, and control options. [1][2]

## Colorways
Documented surviving finishes include:
- Black silicon version.
- Blue germanium version.
- Silver hammered germanium version.
- Custom Brian Jonestown Massacre-logo examples also exist. [3][4][5]

These finish examples document known builds, not a guaranteed complete factory colorway list.

## Versions / options
- **Original point-to-point (p2p):** hand-wired construction; same core circuit and sound as the PCB version. [1]
- **PCB Sonic Boom:** more compact production construction.
- **Silicon version:** documented with silicon transistors and used by Brian Jonestown Massacre members. [3]
- **Germanium version:** documented NOS germanium builds, including OC141 examples. [4][5]
- Routing switch for booster-first or fuzz-first.
- Repeater tone-voicing and speed-mode switching.
- Effects loop, expression input, remote Hi/Lo repeater switching, LED indicators, and 9V power on the later design. [1][2]

## Version changes
The major changes are construction and transistor flavor. The p2p version is the original hand-wired format, while the PCB version moved the same basic circuit into a more production-friendly build. Germanium and silicon versions change the fuzz/boost transistor character, while later PCB revisions expanded routing and repeater controls. [1][2][3]

## Transistor
- **Germanium builds:** documented NOS OC141 examples; other historical Germanium component sets may vary by build. [4][5]
- **Silicon/PCB design:** Acid Fuzz's Effects Database documentation lists NOS BC108 transistors for the fuzz and booster, low-noise MPSA18 devices elsewhere in the circuit, and the original 2N2646 unijunction transistor for the repeater. [2]
- Individual Sonic Boom units can therefore differ substantially by version.

## Diode
- Separate clipping diode: **Not publicly documented.**

## Sound
Sonic Boom is less a single fuzz sound than a small psychedelic effects rig in a box. The fuzz can get aggressive and saturated, the booster can either slam the fuzz for more gain or precede it for a filtered, parked-wah-like character, and the repeater ranges from choppy tremolo into more extreme ring-mod-like territory. [2]
