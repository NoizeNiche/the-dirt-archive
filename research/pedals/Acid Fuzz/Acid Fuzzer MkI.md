# Acid Fuzz — Acid Fuzzer MkI

## PRP identity
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Hand-built Tone Bender MkI-derived germanium fuzz.

## What this pedal is
Acid Fuzz's Acid Fuzzer MkI is a recreation of the 1965 Tone Bender MkI family, with period-style point-to-point construction and selected germanium transistor sets. The builder produced multiple enclosure formats and limited Vintage Series runs. [1][2]

## Colorways
- Multiple custom and Vintage Series finishes exist.
- A documented gold Acid Fuzzer MkI Mini is shown in the Reverb archive. [2]

## Versions / options
### 1965 MKI
- Limited run of five 125B builds.
- Mullard OC75 plus Texas Instruments 2G381-family germanium devices. [2]

### MKI Mini
- Mullard OC75 first stage.
- Two AC128 or similar germanium devices in later stages. [1][2]

### Vintage Series
- Custom-painted larger enclosure.
- Period-style OC75/2G381-family germanium sets. [1]

## Version changes
The documented differences are mainly enclosure format and selected germanium transistor sets. The builder keeps the MkI circuit identity while changing physical format and transistor sourcing across runs. [1][2]

## Transistor
- **Type:** Germanium.
- Documented parts include Mullard OC75, Texas Instruments 2G381-family devices, and AC128-family devices on Mini builds. [1][2]

## Diode
- Separate clipping diode: **Not publicly documented.**

## Sound
The MkI voice is gated, raspy and cutting, with strong attack response and a distinctive sputtering character as the gain/bias is manipulated. The documented builds are designed for aggressive early-Tone-Bender-style fuzz rather than smooth modern saturation. [1][2]
