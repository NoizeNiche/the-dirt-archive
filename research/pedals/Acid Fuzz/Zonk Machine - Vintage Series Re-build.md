# Acid Fuzz — Zonk Machine - Vintage Series Re-build

## PRP identity
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** A strictly limited Acid Fuzz rebuild of the approximately 1966 John Hornby Skewes & Co. Zonk Machine.

## What this pedal is
This is the larger vintage-style Acid Fuzz Zonk Machine rebuild, designed to reproduce the aggressive and dynamic character of an original mid-1960s Zonk Machine while retaining sensitivity to playing dynamics. Acid Fuzz documents a hand-selected trio of NOS germanium transistors and vintage-style capacitors. [1][2]

## Colorways
- The documented Vintage Series build is a vintage-style limited edition.
- No complete factory colorway list was found in the sources checked.

## Versions / options
- **Vintage Series Re-build:** strictly limited to 8 units.
- NOS hand-selected germanium transistor trio:
  - Texas Instruments 2G374
  - Mullard OC75
  - Mullard OC44/CV7003
- Vintage Mullard tropical-fish and/or HUNTS capacitors.
- 9V battery only.
- Original-style on/off switching integrated into the Swell control. [1][2]

## Version changes
This is the limited Vintage Series reconstruction rather than the smaller Zoink Machine. It keeps the larger original-style format and a specific transistor trio, while the Zoink was developed later as a more compact pedalboard version with additional tone shaping and tuning options. [1][3]

## Transistor
- **Type:** Germanium.
- **Documented exact trio:** one Texas Instruments 2G374, one Mullard OC75, and one Mullard OC44/CV7003. [1][2]

## Diode
- Separate clipping diode: **Not publicly documented.**

## Sound
The Vintage Series Zonk is designed to be aggressive, dynamic and highly responsive to picking strength. It retains the raw attack and expressive cleanup associated with the original Zonk Machine rather than smoothing the circuit into a modern high-gain fuzz. [1]
