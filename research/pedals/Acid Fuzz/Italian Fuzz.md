# Acid Fuzz — Italian Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Hand-built recreation of the 1966 Italian Vox Tone Bender.

## What this pedal is
The Acid Fuzz 1966 Italian is a faithful clone of the 1966 production Vox Tone Bender. Effects Database documents a hand-built circuit loaded with a matched Mullard OC76 germanium transistor pair and an adjustable bias control. The current Acid Fuzz catalog also offers an Italian Fuzz Compact and a limited, numbered Vintage Series. [1][2]

## Colorways
- Vintage Series: documented as a limited/numbered edition.
- Current Compact: enclosure/finish varies by production listing.

## Versions / options
- Italian Fuzz Compact: Attack, Volume, external bias, and tone-voicing control. [2]
- Italian Fuzz Vintage Series: limited and numbered edition. [3]
- Earlier 1966 Italian build documented with built-in on/off battery isolation and adjustable bias. [1]

## Version changes
The current Compact adds a smaller pedalboard-oriented format and explicit tone-voicing control compared with the earlier documented 1966 Italian format. The Vintage Series is the limited/numbered presentation. [1][2][3]

## Transistor
- **Type:** Germanium.
- **Documented parts:** Mullard OC76 matched pair. [1]
- A historical builder demonstration also states that original-style 1966 Italian examples were tested against a genuine Vox unit using SFT337 and OC76 devices. [4]

## Diode
- Separate clipping diode: **Not publicly documented.**

## Sound
The circuit is intended to deliver the thick, vocal, mid-forward character of the 1966 Italian Vox Tone Bender. The adjustable bias can move the fuzz from stable vintage saturation toward sputtery, dying-battery and chainsaw-like textures. [1][4]
