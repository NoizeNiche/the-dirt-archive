# Acid Fuzz — NKT275 Acid Fuzz Face

## PRP identity
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Limited-run recreation of the Dallas-Arbiter NKT275 germanium Fuzz Face.

## What this pedal is
The original limited Acid Fuzz NKT275 Fuzz Face used a Newmarket NKT275 germanium transistor pair, an internal bias trimmer, and a volume control that also functions as the power switch. The current limited “teardrop” edition continues the original-style enclosure and circuit concept with NOS Newmarket NKT275 devices. [1][2]

## Colorways
- Limited “teardrop” edition.
- Original-style Fuzz Face enclosure presentation.

## Versions / options
- Original limited run: internal bias trimmer, fixed bias architecture, battery power and volume/power control. [1]
- Current limited teardrop edition: original-style enclosure and guts, NOS NKT275 pair, vintage capacitor set. [2]

## Version changes
The current teardrop edition is a later limited presentation. The original listing documents an older limited run with a wacky old film input capacitor and Philips capacitors, while the current edition specifies NOS Philips “chicklet” and “mustard” capacitors and NOS Newmarket NKT275s. [1][2]

## Transistor
- **Type:** Germanium.
- **Documented part:** New Old Stock Newmarket NKT275 pair. [1][2]

## Diode
- Separate clipping diode: **Not publicly documented.**

## Sound
This is the classic two-transistor Fuzz Face family: thick, blooming fuzz with a strong cleanup response as the guitar volume is reduced. Bias adjustment on the original production can push the circuit toward more unstable or sputtery textures. [1]
