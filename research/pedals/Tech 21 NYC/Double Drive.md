## Sound
The Tech 21 Double Drive captures the lush, muscular power amp distortion of both Class A and Class A/B tube amps.
Instead of just blending these tones, it feeds one into the other for massive ‘cascading’ distortion and limitless tonal variation.
CONTROLS INCLUDE DRIVE A controls the amount of Class A power amp output stage distortion (like Vox AC30®-style amp), rich in even harmonics.
