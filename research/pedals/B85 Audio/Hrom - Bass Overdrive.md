# B85 Audio - Hrom - Bass Overdrive

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Hrom - Bass Overdrive
- **Builder:** B85 Audio
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Bass-focused CMOS overdrive derived from the B85 Audio Bass Overdrive with an added tone control and related changes.

## What this pedal is
B85 Audio's Hrom is built around the **CD4049UBE CMOS chip** and derives from the company's Bass Overdrive. The documented changes include an added Tone control and other circuit adjustments intended to keep notes clear and the full frequency range readable even at high distortion settings.

## Sound
Hrom is designed to preserve note definition and frequency separation while moving from subtle warmth and grit into stronger overdrive. The three-position Attack and Grunt controls independently shape high- and low-frequency saturation, making the pedal especially suited to bass-focused voicing.
