## Deep research verification

### Verified description

B85 Audio describes Oktadrive as a floor octave effect producing **one octave up** with subtle distortion. The builder specifies that the unit has **no controls**, with its behavior fixed and operated only by switching it on or off. The effect is handmade, including its printed circuit board. Effects Database independently lists it as an octave fuzz and dates its publication record to January 25, 2025.

### Verified version references

- The manufacturer page identifies the product code as **OKTADRIVE** and categorizes it as an octave effect.
- The manufacturer states that **six units total were produced**, with the last one made on **February 4, 2024**.
