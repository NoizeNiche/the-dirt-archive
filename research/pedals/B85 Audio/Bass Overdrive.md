## What this pedal is
B85 Audio states that the Bass Overdrive is a handcrafted floor pedal built around the **CD4049UBE CMOS chip**. Controls are LEVEL, DRIVE, BLEND, ATTACK (3-position high-frequency saturation), and GRUNT (3-position low-frequency saturation).

- GRUNT, 3 positions

- 9V DC center-negative

## Sound
B85 Audio describes strong clarity, dynamics, and full frequency response even at higher distortion. BLEND retains clean signal while ATTACK and GRUNT separately shape high- and low-frequency saturation.
