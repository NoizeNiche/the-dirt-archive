# CostaLab - Custom Muff Classic

## Research identity

## What this pedal is
Custom Muff Classic is CostaLab's Big Muff-inspired silicon-clipping fuzz/distortion. CostaLab explicitly describes it as a re-engineered interpretation rather than a simple clone, with more midrange presence and improved definition.

## Controls
- **Gain:** increases distortion.
- **Tone:** balances brightness/frequency response.
- **Master:** overall output volume.

## Switching and power
- True mechanical bypass with an anti-pop filter.
- 9V, 12V or 18V DC center-negative external supply, 2.1 mm connector.
- Current draw under 20 mA.
- A 9V battery is also supported on the documented manual.
- Approximate enclosure size: 60 x 111 x 30 mm on the current manufacturer page, excluding knobs/jacks.

## Sound
CostaLab describes a high-gain Muff-style sound with increased midrange intelligibility, a controlled low end, strong sustain and clear, cutting distortion. It is intended to remain articulate with both humbuckers and single coils and to cut through a dense mix.
