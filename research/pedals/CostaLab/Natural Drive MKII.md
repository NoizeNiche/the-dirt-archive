# CostaLab - Natural Drive MKII

## Research identity
- **Builder:** CostaLab
- **Catalog identity:** Natural Drive MKII
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Exact CostaLab Natural Drive MKII.

## What this pedal is
Natural Drive MKII is the buffered evolution of CostaLab's Natural Drive. External documentation describes the same dynamics/expressiveness concept with additional headroom and a more modern buffered implementation.

## Controls
- **Drive/Sustain:** controls distortion amount.
- **Tone:** shapes brightness/frequency balance.
- **Level:** output volume.
- The exact external sources describe the functional control set but do not establish every physical panel label/value for every production unit.

## Circuit and switching
- Analog overdrive.
- Symmetrical clipping network.
- **Germanium diodes** are explicitly documented.
- Always-active **Class A buffer** is described in the product lineage.
- The MKII is the buffered version of the original Natural Drive.

## Versions and factory options
- **Natural Drive (2011)** is the documented earlier version.
- MKII is a separate catalog model and should not be treated as merely a cosmetic variation.

- Exact diode part number: **not established**.

## Sound
The MKII is designed for dynamic, expressive overdrive with increased headroom and a clear, harmonically rich response. External documentation describes a tube-amplifier-like feel under strong playing dynamics while preserving clean-up at lighter attack.
