## What this pedal is
Dual Drive is an analog overdrive designed around the familiar 808-style overdrive territory. CostaLab's product description presents it as a modern interpretation of that gain family with a second gain stage for additional drive.

## Controls and switching
- **MORE DRIVE switch:** activates a second gain circuit that reaches higher overdrive levels.
- The checked evidence does not preserve a reliable complete knob-label/value transcription, so the archive does not guess additional controls.

## Versions and factory options

## Circuit
- **Analog** circuit, according to Sugaree Licks.
- First gain stage is described as a light overdrive intended to preserve dynamics and guitar expressiveness.
- A second gain circuit is engaged by MORE DRIVE.

## Sound
Dual Drive is intended to cover a wide overdrive range, from lighter dynamic enhancement to significantly higher-gain overdrive. The first stage is described as working well with humbuckers while remaining responsive to playing dynamics.
