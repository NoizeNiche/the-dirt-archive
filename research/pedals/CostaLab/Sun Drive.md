## What this pedal is
Sun Drive is a silicon-clipping Muff-inspired fuzz/distortion. External documentation identifies it as a four-stage gain design with two stages containing silicon clipping circuits.

## Controls
The verified source set establishes a Muff-family distortion/fuzz design but does not provide a complete trustworthy control transcription for the exact production version. The archive therefore records only confirmed functions rather than guessing labels.

## Circuit
- Analog.
- Four gain stages.
- Two gain stages contain **silicon clipping circuits**.

## Versions and factory options
- External documentation identifies a **Sun Drive V1** and a later **Sun Drive V2**.
- The checked external record is specifically the V2 model.
- Do not merge V1 and V2 into a single revision.

## Sound
The documented Sun Drive character is massive and harmonically rich, with powerful but controlled low frequencies, strong midrange presence, aggressive gain and a response intended to remain focused rather than muddy.
