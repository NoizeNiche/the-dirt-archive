# CostaLab - Natural Drive

## What this pedal is
Natural Drive is an analog overdrive built around dynamics and expressiveness. External documentation describes a classic distortion network with symmetrical clipping and germanium diodes.

## Controls
- **Gain/Sustain:** controls the amount of drive and sustain.
- **Tone:** shapes the frequency balance.

## Sound
Natural Drive is designed to retain the guitar's character while responding strongly to playing dynamics. The external model record describes a round, harmonically rich vintage tone that can clean up with lighter playing or lower instrument volume and become more incisive under stronger attack.
