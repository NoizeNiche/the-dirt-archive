## What this pedal is
Moon Drive is a germanium fuzz inspired by the Big Muff, built around four gain stages, with two stages containing dedicated clipping circuits.

## Controls
- **Drive:** increases distortion.
- **Volume:** output level.
- **Tone:** dynamic tone control.

## Circuit and switching
- Germanium fuzz architecture.
- Four gain stages, two with clipping circuits.
- Always-on **Class A buffer** for signal integrity and note definition.
- Ground-loop circuit for noise reduction.
- Buffered bypass switching with anti-pop filter.

## Power
- 9V DC center-negative, 2.1 mm connector.
- Current draw under 20 mA.
- Manufacturer recommends an isolated or Boss-style supply.

## Sound
CostaLab describes Moon Drive as a harmonically rich, strongly characterful Muff-family fuzz. The tone control and gain interact to balance darker low-gain settings against increased high-frequency content as sustain rises.
