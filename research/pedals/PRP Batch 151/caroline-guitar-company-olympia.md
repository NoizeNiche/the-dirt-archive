# Caroline Guitar Company Olympia

- **Builder:** Caroline Guitar Company
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Fuzz / Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** Olympia
- **What this pedal is:** Caroline's early fuzz pedal, launched from the company's 2011 Kickstarter period and built around a transistor-based fuzz design.
- **Colorways:** The standard Olympia was documented in blue; limited finishes included the White Russian cream version and later custom/artist editions.
- **Versions:** Standard Olympia and documented special editions, including White Russian.
- **Version changes:** The White Russian edition changed the first clipping stage with intentionally mismatched germanium and silicon diodes and adjusted biasing and emitter resistance, giving it a fuller, lower-gain response.
- **Transistor type:** Exact transistor part numbers are not documented in the reviewed primary material.
- **Diode type:** Standard type not documented; White Russian specifically used mismatched germanium and silicon diodes in the first clipping stage.
- **Controls / switching:** Two main controls, Gain and Volume, with the documented Olympia enclosure using Caroline's symbolic control labels. Early units used mechanical true bypass.
- **Power:** 9V battery operation is documented for the original Olympia.
- **Sound:** Olympia is voiced for a dynamic fuzz range that can stay articulate and relatively low-noise while moving into aggressive, garage-like saturation. The White Russian variant shifts toward more low-end and a softer, lower-gain but still saturated response.
- **Research confidence:** High for the launch history, control count, and White Russian changes; medium for exact standard component identities.
