# Caroline Guitar Company Wave Cannon Zero

- **Builder:** Caroline Guitar Company
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Fuzz / Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** Wave Cannon Zero
- **What this pedal is:** Caroline's refined late-generation Wave Cannon design, presented as the final form of the original 2010 concept with an expanded HAVOC system and a voltage control for starved/broken textures.
- **Colorways:** Standard production plus the documented Silicon Zero version and limited artwork/special runs.
- **Versions:** Standard Wave Cannon Zero; Silicon Zero.
- **Version changes:** The Zero refines the Shape control for low- and medium-gain depth, adds Voltage for supply-starved response, and expands HAVOC with tuning/mix control. Silicon Zero is a documented variant rather than a separate unrelated circuit family.
- **Transistor type:** Exact transistor type not documented in the reviewed primary product material.
- **Diode type:** Exact diode type not documented in the reviewed primary product material.
- **Controls / switching:** GAIN, SHAPE, TONE, VOLUME, VOLTAGE, and HAVOC are documented across manufacturer and catalog material. HAVOC can be momentary or latching and can be placed earlier or later in the signal chain.
- **Power:** Exact standard specification not established from the reviewed primary product page.
- **Sound:** Wave Cannon Zero covers gritty overdrive through large late-70s/early-80s-style distortion while the voltage and HAVOC controls can push it into starved, broken, feedback-heavy territory. Caroline explicitly treats it as broader than a conventional distortion pedal, with the controls intended for exploratory sound design.
