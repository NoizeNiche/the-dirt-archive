# Caroline Guitar Company Shigeharu

- **Builder:** Caroline Guitar Company
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Fuzz / Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** Shigeharu
- **What this pedal is:** A cascading hybrid fuzz combining op-amp and BC184 transistor gain stages with a parallel octave-up fuzz circuit controlled by HAVOC.
- **Colorways:** Standard production finishes plus documented limited runs, including an Army Green Germanium edition.
- **Versions:** Standard Shigeharu IC Fuzz + Octave; documented limited Germanium edition; later Shigeharu family revisions are kept distinct where the catalog names them separately.
- **Version changes:** The Army Green Germanium limited run used NOS Soviet parts in the parallel octave circuit and revised transistor-stage voicing for a woodier, woolier response. These changes are treated as a special run rather than silently applied to standard production.
- **Transistor type:** BC184 in the documented standard gain stages.
- **Diode type:** Exact standard diode type not documented in the reviewed manufacturer material.
- **Controls / switching:** DRIVE, BODY, TONE, and LEVEL on the main panel; HAVOC adds the parallel octave-up voice. Internal controls provide bias/gate adjustment and a switch that can lock the octave on or turn HAVOC into a kill/mute function.
- **Power:** 9V DC center-negative.
- **Sound:** Shigeharu combines Muff-like sustain and mass with a punchier, more defined attack than a conventional Muff-style circuit. HAVOC introduces a parallel octave-up texture, while the internal bias/gate control can push the response toward broken and sputtery sounds.
- **Research confidence:** High.
