# Caroline Guitar Company Wave Cannon MKII

- **Builder:** Caroline Guitar Company
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Fuzz / Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** Wave Cannon MKII
- **What this pedal is:** The second-generation Wave Cannon, also called the Superdistorter, designed around cascaded gain stages for substantially more distortion than the original.
- **Colorways:** Standard production finishes plus documented special editions, including the 1985 Black Friday edition and other limited artwork runs.
- **Versions:** Wave Cannon version 2.0 / MKII Superdistorter; documented special editions such as 1985.
- **Version changes:** Caroline reused the quad op-amp concept from Haymaker but cascaded gain stages, kept symmetrical hard clipping, and added a recovery stage after the tone controls. Special editions changed cosmetics and selected tuning rather than turning the pedal into the original Wave Cannon.
- **Transistor type:** Exact transistor type not documented in the reviewed primary material.
- **Diode type:** Exact clipping diode type not documented in the reviewed primary material.
- **Controls / switching:** RUMBLE, GAIN, TONE, FOCUS, VOLUME, and HAVOC are documented. FOCUS shapes the midrange, RUMBLE adds low end, and HAVOC handles feedback-style effects.
- **Power:** Exact specification not established from the reviewed primary material.
- **Sound:** MKII is designed for aggressive, high-gain distortion but can clean up into chimey lower-gain textures. Its cascaded architecture gives it a distinctly nastier voice than the original Wave Cannon while preserving enough output and tone control for broader use.
- **Research confidence:** High.
