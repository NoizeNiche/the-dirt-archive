# Caroline Guitar Company The Blues

- **Builder:** Caroline Guitar Company
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Fuzz / Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** The Blues
- **What this pedal is:** Caroline's 2021 amplifier-style overdrive, intended to cover transparent light drive through a rougher, more aggressive drive mode.
- **Colorways:** Standard BLUE plus a documented WOOD-PANEL LTD variant.
- **Versions:** Standard production pedal and documented limited/special editions.
- **Version changes:** A Music Villa special Pro-Drive version used NOS Soviet KTE3102 transistor and D9E germanium diode parts, giving that limited build a hotter and burlier response. That special hardware is not assigned to the standard model.
- **Transistor type:** Standard transistor type not documented in the reviewed primary product material.
- **Diode type:** Standard diode type not documented; D9E germanium is documented for a special limited build.
- **Controls / switching:** Two operating modes are documented, moving between transparent always-on drive and rawer drive. Exact standard control labels were not sufficiently documented in the reviewed product text to state them here.
- **Power:** Exact specification not established from the reviewed primary product page.
- **Sound:** Caroline describes the circuit as predominantly drive with a smaller fuzz component, making it suitable for low-gain always-on use as well as more ragged saturation. The two modes provide the main contrast rather than turning it into a multi-effect design.
- **Research confidence:** High for the intended voicing and documented color variants; medium for special-run chronology and exact control labeling.
