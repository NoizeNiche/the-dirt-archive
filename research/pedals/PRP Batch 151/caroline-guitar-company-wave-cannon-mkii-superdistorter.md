# Caroline Guitar Company Wave Cannon MKII / Superdistorter

- **Builder:** Caroline Guitar Company
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Catalog identity:** Wave Cannon MKII / Superdistorter
- **What this pedal is:** This catalog entry is the alternate-name form of Caroline's Wave Cannon version 2.0, marketed as the MKII Superdistorter.
- **Colorways:** Standard MKII production finishes plus limited/special editions such as the 1985 Black Friday run.
- **Versions:** Same documented MKII / version-2 platform as the corresponding Wave Cannon MKII entry.
- **Version changes:** The MKII platform uses cascaded gain stages, symmetrical hard clipping, and a recovery stage after the tone controls. Limited editions primarily add artwork or documented small voicing changes.
- **Transistor type:** Exact transistor type not documented in the reviewed primary material.
- **Diode type:** Exact clipping diode type not documented in the reviewed primary material.
- **Controls / switching:** RUMBLE, GAIN, TONE, FOCUS, VOLUME, and HAVOC are documented for the MKII Superdistorter.
- **Power:** Exact specification not established from the reviewed primary material.
- **Sound:** The Superdistorter is the intentionally high-gain member of the Wave Cannon family, ranging from lightly dirty amplifier-like tones to heavy, saturated distortion. Its architecture and response are deliberately different from both the original Wave Cannon and Haymaker.
- **Research confidence:** High.
