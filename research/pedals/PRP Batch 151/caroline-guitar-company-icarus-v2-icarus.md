# Caroline Guitar Company Icarus V2 / Icarus

- **Builder:** Caroline Guitar Company
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** Icarus V2 / Icarus
- **What this pedal is:** A catalog-combined Icarus family record covering the original Icarus clean boost and the later Icarus V2. The original was a simple boost/preamp with separate boost and treble controls; V2 expanded the family into a richer buffer/booster/overdrive platform.
- **Colorways:** Multiple production and limited finishes are documented across the Icarus family.
- **Versions:** Original Icarus Boost; Icarus V2; later V2.1/limited component runs.
- **Version changes:** The original Icarus was a two-control boost/preamp. V2 added the expanded gain/treble/output architecture and internal bypass choice; later limited runs changed clipping components and finishes.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented in the reviewed manufacturer material.
- **Diode type:** V2-family Russian germanium diode variants are documented; the original Icarus is treated here as the earlier boost design rather than retroactively assigning those parts to it.
- **Controls / switching:** Original Icarus uses BOOST and TREBLE functions. V2 uses GAIN, HIGHS, and OUTPUT, with buffered/true-bypass switching documented.
- **Power:** 9V DC for the V2; exact original-unit power details were not established from the reviewed sources.
- **Sound:** The original Icarus emphasizes clean volume lift, treble shaping, and low-impedance drive. V2 moves that family concept into richer overdrive while preserving clean-boost utility, so this combined catalog entry should not be read as proof that the two circuits are identical.
- **Research confidence:** High for the family distinction and documented controls; medium for the historical mapping implied by this combined catalog name.
