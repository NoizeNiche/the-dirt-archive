# Creber Instruments - Classic Fuzz

## What this pedal is
Classic Fuzz is a handwired fuzz pedal from Creber Instruments, built around a bright, aggressive silicon-transistor fuzz circuit. The manufacturer explicitly identifies **BC108 silicon transistors** and describes the design as a modern handwired interpretation of classic 1960s fuzz behavior.

## Controls
- **Gain:** overall fuzz/gain range.
- **Volume:** output level, with additional output available for pushing an amplifier or following pedal.
- **Bias:** adjusts the voltage delivered to the fuzz circuit and changes its character and saturation.

## Switching and power
- Popless true-bypass switching.
- Standard 9V DC, negative-center supply.
- Approximate current draw under 3 mA.
- Standard 2.1 mm power socket.
- Battery clip option is documented internally.
- Reverse-polarity protection and power filtering.
- Approximate input impedance: 1 MΩ.
- Approximate output impedance: 3 kΩ or lower.

## Versions and factory options
- The manufacturer presents Classic Fuzz as an original handwired design.
- A current/alternate catalog description uses the name **Classic Fuzz - Silicon Face** for the same BC108-based concept.

## Version changes
No distinct electrical revision was established in the checked manufacturer evidence. Independent 2025 coverage describes the same pedal with the Volume/Gain/Bias control set, while the manufacturer identifies the BC108 silicon architecture.

## Sound
The manufacturer describes Classic Fuzz as bright, aggressive and capable of saturated, harmonically rich fuzz. The Bias control varies the circuit operating voltage to move between different fuzz characters, while the extra output volume can push a valve amplifier or subsequent effects.
