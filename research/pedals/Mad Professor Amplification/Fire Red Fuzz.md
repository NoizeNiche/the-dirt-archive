## What this pedal is
The Fire Red Fuzz is a three-control fuzz pedal built around a silicon fuzz circuit. Contemporary reviews describe it as a broad fuzz design that ranges from thick, compressed tones to thinner, brighter textures.

## Controls and features
- **Volume**
- **Tone**
- **Fuzz**
- The Tone control covers a wide tonal range from deep and mellow through a mid-cut area to bright, cutting sounds.

## Sound
Reviews describe thick, sustained and heavily compressed fuzz at stronger settings, alongside thinner and brighter sounds as the Tone control is advanced. Low Tone settings are described as deep, mellow or liquid-like, around the middle a slight mid cut is noted, and higher Tone settings become brighter, raspier and more cutting. The pedal is reported to cover both beefy saturated sounds and more classic 1960s-style fuzz textures.
