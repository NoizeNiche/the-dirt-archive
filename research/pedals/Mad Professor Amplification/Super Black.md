## Sound

The core design goal was to preserve the harmonic richness and compression behavior of a properly loaded 30W Class AB push-pull stage while enabling clean-to-crunch-to-saturated transitions with minimal pedal dependency.
To achieve this, Kärkkäinen abandoned traditional cascaded gain stages in favor of three independent preamp signal paths — Clean, Crunch, and Lead — each with its own dedicated tone stack, cathode follower, and master volume control.
Preamp Signal Path Breakdown The Clean channel uses a single 12AX7 dual triode (ECC83) configured in a low-gain, high-headroom topology.
