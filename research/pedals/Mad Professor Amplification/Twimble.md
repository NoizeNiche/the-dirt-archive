## Sound
It’s a tone that can be described as rich, thick and bolstered by a distinctively throaty mid-range hump.
Using the level control, you’ll be able to use this pedal to control the drive of your amp.
Moreover, it’s useful to think of the controls of this pedal in the same way you would your amp — allowing the colouration of the Twimble to accentuate your base tone with something distinct.
