# Coffin Gear / Coffin Case — BDFX-1 Blood Drive

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** BDFX-1 Blood Drive
- **Builder:** Coffin Gear / Coffin Case
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** Coffin Case BDFX-1 Blood Drive, the 2006 coffin-shaped analog distortion and warm-boost pedal.

## What this pedal is
Effects Database documents the BDFX-1 as a Coffin Gear/Coffin Case distortion introduced at Frankfurt Musikmesse and Summer NAMM in 2006, with shipping beginning September 1, 2006. [1]

The product combines a classic rich analog distortion stage with a warm boost circuit, while adding gain and tonal control for a wider modern range. [1][2]

## Colorways
- A documented original example uses a **black** coffin-shaped enclosure with red/white graphics.
- Glow-in-the-dark knob indicators are part of the original design. [2][3]

## Versions and factory options
Documented controls:
- **Inject:** level/output.
- **Bleed:** tone.
- **Filth:** distortion/gain. [3]

Other documented features:
- True bypass.
- 100% analog circuitry.
- Rugged die-cast metal coffin-shaped enclosure.
- Spiderweb non-slip bottom.
- Glow-in-the-dark knob indicators.
- 9V battery or standard 9V supply support in period documentation.
- Made in the USA on the original production version. [2][3]

## Version changes
No formal numbered factory revision sequence was established from the checked historical sources.

A current used listing identifies a later example as made in China, so country of manufacture should not be generalized across all surviving BDFX-1 units. [4]

## Sound
The BDFX-1 ranges from lower-gain breakup through denser crunch and distortion, with a warm boost character. Contemporary review material notes that rolling Filth back produces classic-rock-style breakup and that heavier settings remain responsive to guitar-volume cleanup. [1][3]

## Historical note
Effects Database records the model's 2006 trade-show debut, while period press coverage confirms the coffin-shaped construction and three-control interface. [1][2][3]
