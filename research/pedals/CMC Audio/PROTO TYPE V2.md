# CMC Audio — PROTO TYPE V2

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** PROTO TYPE V2
- **Builder:** CMC Audio
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Belgian-made low/medium-gain overdrive with parallel clean and drive paths and an internal voltage booster, revised as V2. [1]

## What this pedal is
CMC Audio describes PROTO TYPE V2 as a low-to-medium-gain overdrive designed to preserve bass and attack by splitting the signal into clean and overdriven paths before recombining them. [1]
