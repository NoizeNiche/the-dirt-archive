## What this pedal is
The Ladyland Fuzz is one of the three Cosmic Sound FX pedals documented in the brand's original Effects Database listing from February 2012. The exact model is categorized as a fuzz.

## Circuit and family
Effects Database indexes The Ladyland Fuzz as a **germanium** fuzz and places it with the Fuzz Face family. A contemporaneous guitar-forum discussion also describes the Ladyland as a Fuzz Face clone. Because the primary manufacturer documentation is sparse, the archive records this as a documented lineage rather than inferring an exact schematic.
