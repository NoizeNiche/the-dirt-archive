# Axcess by Giannini - DS-102 Distortion 2

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** DS-102 Distortion 2
- **Builder:** Axcess by Giannini
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** Second numbered distortion model in the Axcess by Giannini line, documented at Musikmesse Frankfurt in 2012.

## What this pedal is
The DS-102 uses three primary controls: Tone, Level, and Distortion. A contemporary Giannini demonstration and period pedal documentation identify the unit as a compact metal-cased true-bypass distortion with 9 VDC battery or adapter power.

## Versions and factory options
### DS-102 production
- Distortion control
- Tone control
- Level control
- True bypass switching
- On/off LED
- P10 input and output
- 9 VDC battery or center-negative adapter operation
- Metal enclosure

## Version changes
The DS-102 is presented as a distinct second model rather than a numbered revision of the DS-101. Effects Database dates its catalog entry to March 13, 2012 and associates it with Musikmesse Frankfurt 2012.
