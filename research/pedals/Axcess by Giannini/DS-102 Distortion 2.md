# Axcess by Giannini — DS-102 Distortion 2

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** DS-102 Distortion 2
- **Builder:** Axcess by Giannini
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** Giannini Axcess distortion pedal with Tone, Level and Distortion controls, true bypass, LED indication and 9V battery/adapter operation. [1][2]

## Controls

- **TONE**
- **LEVEL**
- **DISTORTION**
- True-bypass footswitch.
- LED effect indicator. [1][2]

## What this pedal is

The DS-102 is a dedicated compact distortion. Its manual describes a **multiple clipping circuit** whose tonal balance is shaped by the Tone control, with balanced bass/treble and a mid-scooped center response. [2]

## Electrical / hardware specifications

The Giannini manual specifies:
- **9VDC** stabilized external adapter, center-negative.
- **9V alkaline battery**.
- **Input impedance:** 1MΩ.
- **Current draw:** **5.5mA**.
- **Output impedance:** >4kΩ.
- **Dimensions:** **110 × 60 × 53mm**.
- **DC jack:** 2.1mm.
- **Metal enclosure**.
- Manufactured in China. [2]
