# Axcess by Giannini - MD-102 Mystic Drive

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** MD-102 Mystic Drive
- **Builder:** Axcess by Giannini
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Axcess overdrive introduced in the early 2010s with a dedicated Scoop control for alternate mid/high-frequency shaping.

## What this pedal is
Giannini documents the MD-102 with Tone, Drive, and Power Level controls plus a special Scoop switch. The manufacturer's catalog specifies true bypass switching, LED indication, 9 VDC battery or adapter operation, and a metal enclosure.

## Sound
The period Giannini leaflet, quoted by a 2011 owner, describes Power Level as sensitive enough to let the MD-102 work as a boost and says Scoop changes the Tone control into a mid-high-frequency control. The same description notes a multiple-stage clipping system that changes as Drive is adjusted.
