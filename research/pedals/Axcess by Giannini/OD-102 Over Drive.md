# Axcess by Giannini — OD-102 Over Drive

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** OD-102 Over Drive

- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Compact three-control warm overdrive from Giannini's Axcess line with Drive, Tone and Level controls, true bypass, LED indication and 9V battery/adapter power. [1][2]

## Controls

- **DRIVE**
- **TONE**
- **LEVEL**
- True-bypass footswitch.

## What this pedal is

Giannini describes OD-102 as a warm classic overdrive capable of moderate blues saturation through stronger, organic hard-rock drive. A current secondary listing identifies the model, yellow finish and 5.1mA current draw. [2]
