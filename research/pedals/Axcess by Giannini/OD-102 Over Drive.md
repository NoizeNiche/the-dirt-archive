# Axcess by Giannini - OD-102 Over Drive

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** OD-102 Over Drive
- **Builder:** Axcess by Giannini
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Compact Giannini Axcess overdrive from the 2010 drive lineup, intended to cover warm low-to-moderate drive through heavier overdriven tones.

## What this pedal is
Giannini specifies Drive, Tone, and Level controls, true bypass switching, LED indication, 9 VDC battery or adapter operation, and a metal enclosure. Contemporary retailer and manual material describes the pedal as a warm overdrive with a wide practical gain range.

## Colorways
- Multiple surviving retail examples document different enclosure finishes, including yellow and red examples.
- No reliable factory chronology was verified for those finishes, so they are recorded as cosmetic colorway evidence rather than separate versions.

## Sound
Giannini describes the OD-102 as a warm, classic overdrive that can move from light blues saturation into heavier, organic hard-rock drive. The Drive, Tone, and Level controls keep the pedal's operation direct and familiar.
