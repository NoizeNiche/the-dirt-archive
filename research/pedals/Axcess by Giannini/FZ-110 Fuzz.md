# Axcess by Giannini - FZ-110 Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** FZ-110 Fuzz
- **Builder:** Axcess by Giannini
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Compact fuzz in Giannini's Axcess drive line, cataloged by Effects Database from 2010.

## What this pedal is
Giannini's catalog identifies the FZ-110 with Fuzz, Tone, and Level controls, true-bypass switching, LED indication, 9 VDC battery/adapter operation, and a metal enclosure. Effects Database also links the model with the broader Belcat FUZ-510/FUZ-610 family, but that relationship is treated here as catalog-family evidence rather than a factory statement.

## Transistor
- **Third-party exact-model evidence:** A 2010 Freestompboxes teardown reports a Belcat FUZ-520 PCB, a J201 transistor, and a uA741 op-amp.
- **Qualification:** This is an independent teardown of an FZ-110, not Giannini factory documentation.

## Diode
- **Third-party exact-model evidence:** The same 2010 teardown reports red LEDs being used for clipping in the op-amp feedback path.
- **Qualification:** The finding is from a forum teardown and is not published as a Giannini component specification.

## Sound
Giannini's three-knob layout keeps the pedal centered on fuzz amount, tone, and output. A later owner/reviewer characterized the FZ-110 as a tight, aggressive silicon fuzz with an early-metal edge, while a separate teardown notes that it does not clean up like a classic germanium Fuzz Face when the guitar volume is rolled back.
