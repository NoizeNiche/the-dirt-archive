# Axcess by Giannini — FZ-110 Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** FZ-110 Fuzz
- **Builder:** Axcess by Giannini
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Compact three-control fuzz from Giannini's Axcess line; an independent teardown identifies the PCB as Belcat FUZ-520 and reports a J201, uA741 and red LED clipping. [1][2]

## Controls / power
- **FUZZ**
- **TONE**
- **LEVEL**
- True bypass.
- LED indicator.
- **9V battery or 9VDC adapter**, center-negative.
- Metal enclosure. [1]

## Circuit / OEM evidence
Effects Database links FZ-110 with the Belcat FUZ-510/FUZ-610 family, while a 2010 Freestompboxes teardown of a physical FZ-110 reports a **Belcat FUZ-520 PCB**, **J201** transistor, **uA741** op-amp and **red LEDs** used for clipping. [1][2]
These are third-party teardown findings, not Giannini factory specifications.

## Active devices
- **J201:** Reported by the independent teardown.
- **uA741:** Reported op-amp in the same teardown.
- The evidence does not establish whether every FZ-110 production unit used identical components. [2]

## Clipping
- **Red LEDs:** Reported as clipping devices in the teardown's op-amp feedback path. [2]
- Exact LED part number is unknown.

## Sound
The teardown author described the pedal as usable but noted that it did not clean up like a germanium Fuzz Face when the guitar volume was reduced. This is tester evidence, not a factory specification. [2]

## Version history
- No numbered Giannini electronic revision established.
- Possible Belcat OEM variants are retained as family context, not treated as revisions.

## Deep research verification
Effects Database, Giannini catalog and the physical 2010 teardown were cross-checked. The teardown is the key evidence for J201/uA741/red-LED clipping, while Giannini sources establish the exact model and control/power format. [1][2]
