# Axcess by Giannini — BD-108 Blues Drive

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** BD-108 Blues Drive
- **Builder:** Axcess by Giannini
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Compact analog overdrive designed to recreate the warm saturation of small vintage tube amplifiers.

## What this pedal is
The Giannini manual describes BD-108 as a warm overdrive intended to enrich the instrument without substantially changing its basic tone. It uses True Bypass, a metal enclosure and simple tone shaping.

### OEM relationship
Effects Database groups the BD-108 with the Belcat BLD-608 Blues Drive family and notes the same control-layout lineage. The Giannini manual itself does not identify the OEM relationship, so the archive treats that as a secondary research note rather than a factory statement.

## Sound
BD-108 is designed for smooth, moderate blues overdrive that preserves the instrument's underlying tone while adding tube-like saturation. Its Tone control adjusts the treble region, allowing the basic warm drive character to be opened up or softened.
