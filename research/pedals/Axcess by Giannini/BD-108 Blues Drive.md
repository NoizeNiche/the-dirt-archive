# Axcess by Giannini — BD-108 Blues Drive

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** BD-108 Blues Drive
- **Builder:** Axcess by Giannini
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Compact analog overdrive voiced to reproduce the warm saturation associated with small tube amplifiers. [1][2]

## Controls
- **GAIN**
- **TONE**
- **LEVEL**
- True-bypass footswitch.
- LED effect indicator. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
Giannini's manual describes BD-108 as a warm overdrive designed to add tube-like saturation without substantially altering the instrument's basic tonal character. The Tone control changes treble/harmonic content. [1]

## Electrical / hardware specifications
The Giannini manual specifies:
- **Power:** 9V alkaline battery or stabilized **9VDC** adapter.
- **Adapter polarity:** center negative.
- **DC jack:** 2.1mm.
- **Current draw:** **5.4mA**.
- **Input impedance:** **1MΩ**.
- **Output impedance:** **1kΩ**.
- **Dimensions:** **110 × 60 × 53mm**.
- **Weight:** **225g**.
- Metal enclosure. [1]

## Circuit lineage
Effects Database groups BD-108 with the Belcat BLD-608 Blues Drive family. The Giannini manual does not itself claim this relationship, so the archive records it as a secondary OEM-family lead rather than a factory statement. [2][3]
