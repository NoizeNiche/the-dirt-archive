# ChadderBox Effects — Loud/Louder

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Loud/Louder
- **Builder:** ChadderBox Effects
- **Catalog type:** Boost / Overdrive
- **Identity:** V3 dual-MOSFET booster with independent gain channels, Flashback momentary switching and a parallel “LOUDEST” combination mode. [1][2]

## Controls / architecture
- Two independent **GAIN** controls.
- Two independent footswitches.
- **LOUDEST:** Runs both channels together in parallel.
- **FLASHBACK:** Momentary switching behavior while a footswitch is held.
- Soft-touch relay true bypass. [1][2]

## Transistor
- **Technology:** Dual MOSFET gain stages.
- Exact MOSFET part numbers are not publicly documented. [1]
## Diode / clipping
- Exact clipping/protection diode details are not publicly documented.

## Power
- Exact model-specific voltage/current figures are not securely preserved in the reviewed sources.

## Version history
- **V3** is the documented current generation.
- The defining V3 additions are modern Flashback switching and LOUDEST parallel operation. [1][2]
- Color options include White, Blue, Pink, 80's, and B-stock variants. These are cosmetic/production-status options, not circuit revisions. [1]

## Sound
The left channel provides a lower-gain boost range, while the right channel provides more gain and a thicker response. Engaging both channels in LOUDEST mode creates substantially more drive and can push an amplifier further into breakup. [1][2]
