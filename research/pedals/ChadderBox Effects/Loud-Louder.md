# ChadderBox Effects — Loud/Louder

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Loud/Louder
- **Builder:** ChadderBox Effects
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** ChadderBox Effects Loud/Louder V3, a dual-MOSFET booster with two independent gain channels and Flashback switching.

## What this pedal is
ChadderBox Effects documents the Loud/Louder as a dual MOSFET booster with two independent channels. Each channel has its own gain control and footswitch, while the louder channel offers more gain and a thicker voice. The current version adds a LOUDEST mode that runs both channels together in parallel. [1]

The builder calls the current model **v3** and documents Flashback Switching, which temporarily changes a channel's state while a footswitch is held. [1][2]

## Versions and factory options
### Loud/Louder V3
The documented features include:
- Two independent gain controls.
- Two footswitches.
- Dual MOSFET gain stages.
- Flashback momentary switching.
- LOUDEST mode for running both channels in parallel.
- Soft-touch true-bypass relay switching. [1]

## Version changes
The builder identifies this model as **V3**, describing it as an updated version of the older Loud/Louder with modern switching functionality. [1][2]

## Transistor
The builder explicitly identifies the circuit as **dual MOSFET**. Exact MOSFET part numbers were not established. [1]

## Diode
No exact-model diode specification was established.

## Sound
The left channel provides a lower-gain boost voice, while the right channel offers more gain and a thicker sound. Stacking both channels can produce considerably more drive and can push an amplifier into breakup or distortion. [1][2]
