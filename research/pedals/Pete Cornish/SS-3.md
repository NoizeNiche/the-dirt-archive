# Pete Cornish - SS-3

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Pete Cornish
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** SS-3 Soft Sustain with passive top cut and bass cut.

## What this pedal is
Pete Cornish lists SS-3 as **Soft Sustain with passive top cut + bass cut**. Aion FX's tracing journal identifies it as an update of the earlier SS-2.

## Controls / version change
- Soft-sustain overdrive stage.
- Passive top-cut network.
- Added **bass-cut** function compared with SS-2.

## Circuit / lineage
Aion FX describes SS-3 as a 1970s-style overdrive related to the MXR Distortion+ family, with Cornish-specific buffering and circuit changes. The archive does not reduce it to a stock Distortion+ clone.

## Sound
Cornish positions SS-3 below G-2 in gain, with a softer sustained crunch/overdrive response and additional low-end control.

## Research confidence
- **Identity:** High.
- **SS-2 update:** High.
- **Added bass cut:** High.
- **Exact components:** Unknown.

## Deep research verification
The official Pete Cornish catalog was cross-checked against Aion FX's November 2019 tracing journal. The latter specifically discusses the SS-2/SS-3 relationship and low-cut update.
