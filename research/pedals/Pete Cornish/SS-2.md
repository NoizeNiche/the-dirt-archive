# Pete Cornish - SS-2

## PRP identity

- **Builder:** Pete Cornish
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** SS-2 Soft Sustain.

## What this pedal is

Pete Cornish lists the SS-2 as **Soft Sustain with passive top cut**. It is one of the company's core Grey/Deluxe standalone dirt pedals.

## Circuit / lineage

Aion FX's tracing research describes SS-2 as a 1970s-style overdrive with roots in the **MXR Distortion+** circuit, but notes that the floating Cornish SS-2 schematic is actually associated with the Skrydstrup ODR-2 trace plus the Cornish buffer. The archive therefore preserves the lineage as documented tracing research rather than calling SS-2 an unmodified Distortion+ clone.
