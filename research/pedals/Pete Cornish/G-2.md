# Pete Cornish - G-2

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Pete Cornish
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive
- **Identity:** G-2 four-stage Germanium-enhanced distortion.

## What this pedal is
Pete Cornish describes G-2 as a **four-stage overdriven discrete transistor Class-A circuit** with additional Germanium components and a high-impedance Class-A buffer. It is designed for higher-gain overdrive and distortion.

## Circuit / components
- Four-stage **discrete transistor Class-A** gain architecture.
- Additional **Germanium components** in the gain/distortion network.
- Input high-impedance unity-gain Class-A buffer.
- Low-impedance output.
- Exact transistor and germanium device part numbers are not published on the reviewed official page.

## Sound
Cornish positions G-2 above the SS-3 in gain, from strong rhythmic crunch through rounded dynamic lead distortion.
