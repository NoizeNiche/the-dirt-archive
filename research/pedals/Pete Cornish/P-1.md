# Pete Cornish - P-1

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Pete Cornish
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** P-1 Precision Fuzz.

## What this pedal is
Pete Cornish currently lists P-1 as **Fuzz with bass/treble single-tone control**. Cornish has also confirmed in interview material that the P-1 label on David Gilmour's board was his original **1982 Precision Fuzz**.

## Circuit / history
The Precision Fuzz is the standalone Cornish nomenclature attached to the original custom fuzz lineage used in Gilmour's 1982 board. Modern circuit analysis associates P-1 with the mid-1970s Rams Head Big Muff family plus Cornish buffering, but the archive treats that as secondary circuit analysis rather than a factory clone statement.

## Controls
- **Fuzz**
- **Bass/Treble single tone control**
- Output/level control is part of the pedal's current product architecture.

## Sound
Cornish positions P-1 as a classic fuzz voice shaped by its buffered architecture and single broad tone control.
