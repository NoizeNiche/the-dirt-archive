# CKK Electronic — Honey Jar

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** Honey Jar - Overdrive
- **Builder:** CKK Electronic
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Hand-soldered NOS-component overdrive based on the original TS808 concept, with production limited by component availability. [1][2]

## What this pedal is

CKK describes Honey Jar as its first hand-soldered pedal. The design is based on the **TS808** overdrive concept and uses NOS components accumulated over time. The source emphasizes technician-level hand assembly and limited production caused by NOS parts availability. [1]

## Sound

Honey Jar is intended to preserve the detailed, responsive feel associated with TS808-era overdrive while providing a focused boost/drive response with useful low-frequency fullness. [1][2]
