## What this pedal is
CKK's Honey Jar is documented as the builder's first hand-soldered pedal. The design is based on the original TS808 overdrive, with the builder using NOS components accumulated over years to preserve the character of the earlier circuit. [1][2]

The model is explicitly described as individually completed by senior technicians, with production limited by the availability of the NOS components. [1]

## Sound
The builder's stated goal is to restore the detailed character of the classic TS808-era overdrive using carefully selected NOS components. The pedal is intended to add articulation and retain low-frequency fullness when used as a boost. [1][2]
