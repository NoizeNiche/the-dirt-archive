# CKK Electronic — Scream Drive - Two Gain Stage Classic Overdrive

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Scream Drive - Two Gain Stage Classic Overdrive
- **Builder:** CKK Electronic
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** CKK Electronic Scream Drive, a classic overdrive with two gain settings, three clipping options, and a boost/disdrive switch.

## What this pedal is
CKK's product description and independent demo coverage describe Scream Drive as a classic overdrive offering broader control than a basic Tube Screamer-style circuit. It provides multiple clipping choices and a gain-boost/DisDrive mode that can move the pedal from boost and overdrive into stronger distortion. [1][2]

## Versions and factory options
Documented controls/features:
- Volume.
- Gain.
- Tone.
- Three-position CLIP switch.
- BO / DisDrive gain-boost switch.
- Two gain stages.
- True bypass.
- 9V DC or battery operation. [1][2]

The three-way CLIP switch changes the clipping configuration, while DisDrive raises the available distortion level. [1]

## Sound
CKK positions Scream Drive as a transparent, touch-responsive classic overdrive. The standard range covers clean boost through medium gain, while DisDrive raises the saturation level for a more aggressive distortion response. [1][2]
