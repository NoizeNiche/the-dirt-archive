# CKK Electronic — Scream Drive

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** Scream Drive - Two Gain Stage Classic Overdrive
- **Builder:** CKK Electronic
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive / Distortion
- **Identity:** Two-gain-stage overdrive with a three-position clipping selector and BO/DisDrive gain expansion. [1][2]

## Controls

- **VOLUME**
- **GAIN**
- **TONE**
- **CLIP:** Three clipping options.
- **BO / DisDrive:** Gain-boost/expanded-distortion mode.
- Two gain stages.
- True bypass. [1][2]

## Circuit / topology

- Analog two-stage overdrive.
- Three selectable clipping configurations.
- DisDrive increases the available saturation beyond the standard overdrive range. [1]

## Sound

The standard range spans clean boost through medium-gain overdrive, while DisDrive raises the saturation into more aggressive distortion. The CLIP selector changes the way the drive section breaks up. [1][2]
