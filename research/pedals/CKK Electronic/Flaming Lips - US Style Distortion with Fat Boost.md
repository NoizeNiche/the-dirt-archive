# CKK Electronic — Flaming Lips

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Flaming Lips - US Style Distortion with Fat Boost
- **Builder:** CKK Electronic
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** High-gain distortion with extended low-end control, H.MID and PRESENCE shaping, and a MUSCLE post-mid boost circuit. [1][2]

## Controls / architecture
- **H.MID:** Midrange shaping around the 1 kHz region in the documented product description.
- **PRESENCE:** Upper-frequency/presence shaping around the 3 kHz region.
- **MUSCLE:** Post-mid-frequency boost circuit.
- High-gain distortion core. [1][2]

## Circuit lineage
- Effects Database describes the pedal as **US-style** high-gain distortion.
- CKK's surviving product archive describes it as **British-style** distortion.
- Those are preserved as source descriptions, not resolved into separate hardware versions. [1][2]
