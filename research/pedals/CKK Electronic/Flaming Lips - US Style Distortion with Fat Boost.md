# CKK Electronic — Flaming Lips - US Style Distortion with Fat Boost

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Flaming Lips - US Style Distortion with Fat Boost
- **Builder:** CKK Electronic
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** CKK Electronic Flaming Lips, a high-gain distortion with extended low end, H.MID/PRESENCE shaping, and a post-mid-frequency boost switch.

## What this pedal is
Effects Database documents Flaming Lips as a US-style high-gain distortion with a large but controlled low-frequency range, full mids, rich high-frequency overtones, and a **MUSCLE** switch controlling a post-mid-frequency boost circuit. [1]

A surviving CKK product listing describes the same model as a “British style” distortion while retaining the H.MID, PRESENCE, and MUSCLE architecture. The archive records that naming difference rather than resolving it as a hardware revision. [2]

## Versions and factory options
Documented controls/features include:
- H.MID.
- PRESENCE.
- MUSCLE mid-frequency boost switch.
- High-gain distortion stage.
- True bypass.
- 9V DC or battery operation. [1][2]

The H.MID control is described as shaping roughly the 1 kHz region and PRESENCE the 3 kHz region. [2]

## Sound
Effects Database describes the pedal as a high-gain distortion with compact low end and strong midrange/high-frequency definition. The MUSCLE circuit adds a second mid-focused contour intended to increase the sense of depth and cut for solo use. [1]
