## Sound
Vertex Effects Unveils The T Drive: A Deep Technical and Musical Analysis of the Next-Generation Tape Saturation Pedal
Unlike digital plugins or hybrid circuits that approximate tape behavior through DSP modeling, the T Drive generates authentic second- and third-order harmonic distortion, hysteresis-dependent low-end compression, and variable slew-rate limiting entirely in the analog domain.
Measured total harmonic distortion (THD) ranges from 0.08% at unity gain to 7.3% at maximum drive, with spectral analysis confirming harmonic ratios closely matching those of Ampex ATR-102 playback heads running at 15 ips on Scotch 250 tape.
