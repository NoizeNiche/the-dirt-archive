## What this pedal is
Effects Database documents the Century SHM-8 as a vintage distortion pedal produced in Korea during the 1980s. It belongs to a broad PSK-derived family of Boss HM-2-style pedals and shares circuitry and layout with the Century Metal Rocker. [1]

## Sound
The SHM-8 follows the high-gain, saturated character associated with the Boss HM-2 family, with separate low- and high-frequency shaping through the Color Mix controls. Effects Database describes the pedal as delivering thick distortion suitable for hard rock and early Swedish death-metal-style sounds. [1]
