# Century — Century SDI-1 Distortion

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** Century SDI-1 Distortion
- **Builder:** Century
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** Century SDI-1/SDI-1A, documented as the originating model of a later standardized three-control OEM distortion family. [1][2]

## What this pedal is

Effects Database traces later DS-30-branded versions back to the **Century SDI-1A** design. The family appears across multiple brands and is characterized by a compact three-control distortion layout. [1][2]

Because the accessible evidence does not prove that every SDI-1 and SDI-1A build is electrically identical, the archive preserves the Century identity without inventing a universal component BOM.

## Controls

The related OEM family is consistently represented with:
- **LEVEL**
- **TONE**
- **DISTORTION / INTENSITY** [2]

Exact SDI-1 front-panel wording is not independently re-verified from a surviving manufacturer manual.
