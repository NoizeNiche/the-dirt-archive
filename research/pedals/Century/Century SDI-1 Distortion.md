# Century — Century SDI-1 Distortion

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Century SDI-1 Distortion
- **Builder:** Century
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** Century SDI-1, an early member of a standardized Taiwanese/Korean OEM distortion family later sold under multiple brand names.

## What this pedal is
Effects Database documentation for later rebranded DS-30 pedals traces their shared OEM design back to the **Century SDI-1A** distortion pedal. The later VShock and Maxx records describe the standardized family as a compact three-control distortion with Level, Tone, and Distortion/Intensity functions. [1][2]

The exact Century-branded SDI-1 unit in this archive is therefore recorded as the documented originating model for that OEM family. The checked evidence does not securely establish whether every SDI-1 and SDI-1A example used identical component values or control labeling.

The related OEM family is documented with a compact three-control layout centered on:

## Version changes

## Sound
The documented OEM family is intended as a straightforward, aggressive guitar distortion with direct control over output, tonal balance, and distortion intensity. The later rebranded examples are described as bright and cutting, with strong high-gain attack. [1][2]
