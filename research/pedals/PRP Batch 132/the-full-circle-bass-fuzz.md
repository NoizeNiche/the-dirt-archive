# Build Your Own Clone — The Full Circle Bass Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Build Your Own Clone
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Bass fuzz based on the dirty channel of the Acoustic 360 amplifier.

## What this pedal is
The Full Circle Bass Fuzz recreates the fuzz channel of the classic Acoustic 360 bass amplifier. BYOC added an input buffer for pickup compatibility while retaining true bypass.

## Controls and features
- Level
- Fuzz
- Dry Blend
- The blend is a crossfader capable of fully dry through fully wet operation.

## Active device
- NOS germanium transistor documented.

## Sound
The circuit is intended to retain bass fundamentals while moving from subtle warmth to heavy, torn-speaker-style fuzz through the Fuzz and Blend controls.
