# Build Your Own Clone — The Swede

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Build Your Own Clone
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** Boss HM-2 Heavy Metal clone.

## What this pedal is
The Swede is a proper BYOC recreation of the Japanese Boss HM-2, including the original M5218L integrated circuits documented for the Japanese version.

## Construction
- True bypass.
- Historical finished-enclosure options included unpainted and black powder-coated versions.

## Active device
- Original M5218L chips are documented.

## Sound
The HM-2 identity is the defining characteristic, including the aggressive, buzzsaw-style distortion associated with the circuit.
