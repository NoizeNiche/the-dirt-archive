# Bulinski Effect Pedals — Filth Foundry Guitar Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Bulinski Effect Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Silicon-transistor take on the Fuzz Factory circuit.

## What this pedal is
The Filth Foundry modifies the familiar Fuzz Factory architecture by using silicon transistors instead of the more typical germanium devices.

## Sound
The documented range runs from mild distortion through compressed velcro-style fuzz, octave-like textures, and oscillating fuzz.

## Construction
- Analog, hand-built boutique pedal.
- Current product dimensions are approximately 4.5 x 2.5 x 1.5 inches.
