# Bulinski Effect Pedals — Gnarly Bee

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Bulinski Effect Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Boutique clone of the Mosrite Fuzzrite.

## Version
The current product page identifies the reviewed version as the updated 2023 version.

## Controls and features
- Depth
- Volume
- High-pass filter toggle
- True bypass
- LED indicator

## Circuit
The documented Depth control mixes the two transistor signal paths. The high-pass toggle changes the second-transistor collector path.

## Sound
The design is based on the distinctive 1960s Fuzzrite circuit and retains its characteristic buzzing fuzz family.
