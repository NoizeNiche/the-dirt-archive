# Bulinski Effect Pedals — Deluxe Velociraptor Diode Bass Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Bulinski Effect Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Deluxe bass-oriented fuzz with selectable diode clipping.

## What this pedal is
The Deluxe Velociraptor expands the original Velociraptor Diode Bass Fuzz with additional Fuzz and Gain controls.

## Controls and features
- Volume
- Fuzz
- Gain
- Diode toggle
- Vintage germanium and modern silicon clipping choices.

## Construction
- Hand-wired in the U.S.
- Supports 9V battery or 9V center-negative DC power.

## Sound
The Fuzz control can reduce the amount of dirt, while Gain controls the signal entering the circuit. The design is intended for both passive and active bass pickups.
