# Build Your Own Clone — The Large Beaver

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Build Your Own Clone
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Big Muff-family kit with Triangle-era and Ram's Head-era builds documented.

## What this pedal is
The Large Beaver is a BYOC Big Muff clone. Historical documentation describes versions based on the Triangle and Ram's Head eras, allowing the builder to select the supported circuit specification.

## Controls and options
- Standard Volume, Tone, and Sustain controls.
- Historical versions include tone-bypass and alternate mid-response options.

## Active devices
- Historical Triangle documentation lists 2N5133 transistors.
- Other Large Beaver versions used different transistor selections, so the archive does not assign one universal transistor set to every revision.

## Sound
The Triangle and Ram's Head specifications produce distinct Big Muff-family responses. The archive keeps those versions separate from later BYOC Li'l Beaver variants.

### Verified sound evidence
It includes a tone bypass and a switch that allows you to select between scooped, flat, or hump mids.
The pedal uses NOS BC239C transistors and high-quality metal film resistors and capacitors to ensure a vintage tone with minimal noise.
The Large Beaver has become the go-to choice for vintage Muff Clones due to its accurate tone and versatile controls.
