# Build Your Own Clone — The Mimosa

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Build Your Own Clone
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive (catalog classification)
- **Identity:** BYOC compressor based on the Dan Armstrong Orange Squeezer.

## Classification note
The current archive catalog classifies The Mimosa as Overdrive, but the surviving product descriptions identify it as a compressor/sustain pedal. The research record preserves both facts rather than silently changing the catalog taxonomy.

## What this pedal is
The Mimosa stays close to the Orange Squeezer circuit while adding bass-friendly changes and a crossfade dry-blend control.

## Controls and features
- Volume
- Compression
- Dry blend
- Low-noise, transparent compression is the documented design goal.

## Sound
The circuit is intended for smooth compression with added bass friendliness while allowing the dry signal to be mixed back in.
