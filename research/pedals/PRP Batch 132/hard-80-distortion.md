# Bulinski Effect Pedals — Hard 80 Distortion

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Bulinski Effect Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** 1980s-style distortion based around MXR Distortion+ and DOD 250 circuit ideas.

## Circuit
The documented design uses an LM741 op amp and tantalum capacitors to retain the lo-fi character associated with the reference-era circuits.

## Controls and clipping
- Level
- Volume
- Toggle between silicon and germanium hard-clipping diodes.
- Through-hole diodes make clipping modifications practical.

## Sound
The Level control ranges from dirty boost into full distortion, while the separate Volume control sets output level.

## Power
- 9V DC, center-negative.
