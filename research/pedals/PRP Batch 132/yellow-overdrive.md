# Build Your Own Clone — Yellow Overdrive

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Build Your Own Clone
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Exact clone of the first-version BOSS OD-1 circuit.

## What this pedal is
The Yellow Overdrive reproduces the earliest OD-1 architecture with a quad op-amp circuit rather than the later dual-op-amp and transistor-buffer arrangement.

## Active device
- Early documentation identifies the Raytheon RC3403AD quad op amp.

## Documented modifications
- A capacitor/resistor change can increase gain, restore low end, and flatten the EQ response.
- Replacing the stock clipping diodes with red LEDs changes compression and clipping texture.

## Sound
The archive treats the first-production-run OD-1 circuit as the defining identity rather than merging it with later OD-1 revisions.
