# ADA Amps — MP-1 Channel

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** MP-1 Channel
- **Builder:** ADA Amps
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive
- **Identity:** Pedal-format recreation of ADA's MP-1 tube preamp.

## What this pedal is
The MP-1 Channel is a compact pedal version of the ADA MP-1 rackmount guitar preamp. ADA's documentation describes a four-stage vacuum-tube design using two 12AX7 tubes, with switchable Clean and Distortion voicings and an internal power supply that raises the pedal's 12V input to 270V for the tube circuit. [1][2]

## Colorways
- The documented production presentation is a black enclosure with blue graphics.
- No separately verified factory colorway was established.

## Versions and factory options
### MP-1 Channel production
- Two 12AX7 vacuum tubes
- Four-stage tube gain design
- Clean and Distortion voicings
- Master, Bass, Mid, Treble and Presence controls
- True bypass
- 12V power input
- Internal 270V high-voltage supply [1][2][3]

No separate factory circuit revision was verified in the reviewed sources.

## Version changes
No reliable production revision history was established. The “Channel” pedal is treated as its own pedal-format product derived from the original MP-1 architecture, not as a revision of the rackmount MP-1. [1]

## Sound
The Clean and Distortion voicings give the pedal two distinct preamp characters, from a clearer tube-preamp sound to a warm, smooth high-gain distortion. The two 12AX7 tubes and high-voltage internal supply are central to the MP-1-style gain structure described by ADA. [1][2]
