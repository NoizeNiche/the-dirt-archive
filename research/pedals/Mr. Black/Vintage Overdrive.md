## What this pedal is

Mr. Black describes the Vintage Overdrive as a smooth, round, singing overdrive with gentle compression and more gain/output than the classic circuit that inspired it. The builder identifies the design as an original Jack Deville circuit. [1]

## Power and physical specification

- **Input impedance:** approximately 470kΩ. [1]
- **Output impedance:** less than 25kΩ. [1]
- **Bypass:** mechanical true bypass. [1]
- **Power:** 9VDC, 2.1mm negative center, or internal 9V battery. [1]
- **Recommended supply:** regulated supply greater than 100mA. [1]
- **Current draw:** approximately 5mA at 9VDC. [1]
- **Footprint:** approximately 2.4 × 4.5 × 1.8 inches. [1]
- **Enclosure:** CNC-milled genuine Hammond aluminum enclosure. [1]
- **Origin:** handmade in Portland, Oregon. [1]

## Sound

Mr. Black describes a smooth attack, singing sustain, gentle compression, and an expanded gain/output envelope compared with the classic reference. RockBoard's exact-model entry also describes the 4558-based architecture as a green-screamer-family interpretation. [1][2]
