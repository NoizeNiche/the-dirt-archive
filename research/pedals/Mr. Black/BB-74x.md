## What this pedal is
Mr. Black describes the BB-74x as a pure-analog overdrive designed to emulate the response and harmonic behavior of a British 18W EL84 combo amplifier. The documented range runs from light drive through heavy saturation. [1]

## Circuit / architecture
The builder describes a responsive analog circuit intended to reproduce the sag and response of the target 18W combo. A complete production schematic and semiconductor bill of materials were not established in the verified sources.

## History
Effects Database lists the BB-74x as an original Jack Deville design and notes a limited edition available around Black Friday 2017. [2]

## Sound
The builder characterizes the BB-74x around low-wattage British EL84 breakup, with dynamic response from light drive through heavier saturation. [1][2]
