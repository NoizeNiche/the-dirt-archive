## What this pedal is

Mr. Black describes the Bass OD Mk. II as a bass-specific overdrive/preamp with a two-band active EQ tuned for bass guitar. The Mk. II adds increased transient response and a faster feel. [1][2]

## Circuit / architecture

The builder documents an internal split-rail power supply intended to provide high headroom, clarity, and dynamic response. [1]
The verified sources do not publish a complete production schematic or semiconductor bill of materials.

## Sound

Mr. Black describes the Bass OD Mk. II around touch-responsive natural bass grind that works with both active and passive instruments, with active two-band EQ and high headroom. [1][2]
