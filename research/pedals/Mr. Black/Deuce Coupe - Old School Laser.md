## Circuit / architecture
The underlying Deuce Coupe design is described by Mr. Black as a pure-analog, high-headroom overdrive with internal +/-9V split-rail operation and two footswitchable drive/boost channels. [1]

## Controls and switching

- **Ultra Boost:** external boost control. [1]
- **Drive / Ultra:** two footswitchable channels. [1]
- **Soft-touch footswitches:** documented. [1]
