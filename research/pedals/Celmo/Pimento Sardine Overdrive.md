# Celmo — Pimento Sardine Overdrive

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Pimento Sardine Overdrive
- **Builder:** Celmo
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Celmo Pimento Sardine, an analog overdrive using E.A.S.E. (Enhanced Analog Sag Emulator) and a three-position Color switch.

## What this pedal is
Premier Guitar describes the Pimento Sardine as a hand-crafted French overdrive using E.A.S.E. to emulate amplifier power-supply sag. The pedal also has a three-position Color switch intended to alter the response to suit the connected amplifier. [1]

Effects Database independently identifies the Pimento Sardine as an analog overdrive using E.A.S.E. [2]

## Sound
E.A.S.E. is intended to recreate the dynamic sag/compression associated with a tube amplifier under load. Premier Guitar reports that the Pimento can cover boost and saturated crunch, with strong response to the guitar's volume control. The Color switch changes the upper-frequency character without functioning as a conventional full EQ. [1][2]
