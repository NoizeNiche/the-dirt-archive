# Celmo — Sardine Can

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Sardine Can
- **Builder:** Celmo
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Celmo Sardine Can, an optical compressor with an integrated soft-crunch overdrive/line-driver section and tone-shaping switches.

## What this pedal is
Effects Database documents the Sardine Can as a compressor pedal that can also serve as a preamp/line driver with a soft-crunch drive function. The pedal includes a high-pass/low-cut filter and a three-position Color switch. [1]

OVNI Labs independently describes the pedal as combining compression and overdrive functions, with very high available compression ratios and tone-shaping controls. [2]

## Versions and factory options
The documented controls/functions include:
- Optical compression.
- Hi Pass / Lo Cut filter switch.
- Color switch with **Flat / Brown Sound / Violet** positions.
- Soft Crunch drive function activated through the pedal's drive section. [1]

Effects Database also documents the Sardine Can as individually tested and numbered in France. [1]
