# Basic Audio — Marq Won

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Marq Won
- **Builder:** Basic Audio
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Germanium Tone Bender MKI-style fuzz with three-position low-end shaping and high-cut tone control. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
Basic Audio's Marq Won interprets the Tone Bender MKI family and adds controls that alter how much low-frequency material reaches the later gain stage. A high-cut tone control then shapes the upper end. [1][2]

## Controls / architecture
- **VOLUME**
- **FUZZ**
- **High-cut TONE**
- **Three-position low-end-cut switch**
The low-end switch changes both bass content and the resulting stage drive/compression. [1][2]

## Circuit lineage
- **Primary lineage:** Tone Bender MKI.
- Basic Audio's circuit is a modern interpretation, not an untouched historical component recreation.

## Transistor
- **Technology:** Germanium.
- A documented Reverb example uses socketed germanium transistors, but no exact device number is established as universal for every Marq Won. [3]
## Diode / clipping
- Exact clipping-device type and part number are not publicly documented.

## Power
- **9V center-negative** with battery support. [1]

## Version history
- No numbered electronic revision established.
- Individual hand-painted/custom finishes are cosmetic variations.

## Sound
Marq Won occupies the warm, loose and somewhat spitty side of MKI-style fuzz. The low-end switch changes compression and stage drive, while the high-cut control can tame the upper end for thicker or rougher textures. [1][2][3]
