# Basic Audio — Marq Won

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Marq Won
- **Builder:** Basic Audio
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Germanium Tone Bender MKI-style fuzz with a three-position low-end-cut switch and high-cut tone shaping. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
Marq Won is Basic Audio's take on the Tone Bender MKI family. The low-end switch changes how much of the low frequencies hit the later stage, altering compression and stage drive; a high-cut control then shapes the top end. [1]

## Controls / architecture
- Tone Bender MKI-style fuzz.
- Three-position **low-end-cut** switch.
- **High-cut Tone** control.
- Volume/fuzz controls are part of the documented production layout.
- **9V center-negative** power with battery support. [1][2]

## Circuit lineage
- **Primary lineage:** Tone Bender MKI.
- Basic Audio's implementation is a modern interpretation, not a claim of untouched historical component equivalence.

## Transistor
- **Technology:** Germanium.
- A documented Reverb unit uses **socketed germanium transistors**, but the exact device number is not universalized to every Marq Won. [3]

## Diode / clipping
- Exact clipping-device type and part number are not publicly documented.

## Version history
- No numbered electronic revision established.
- Individual hand-painted/custom finishes are cosmetic variations.

## Sound
Marq Won sits in the warm, loose and spitty side of the MKI family. The low-end switch changes both compression and stage drive, while the high-cut control can tame the top end and move the response between thicker and more ragged textures. [1][2][3]
