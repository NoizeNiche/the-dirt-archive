## What this pedal is
Basic Audio’s Super Fuzz is an octave fuzz based on the Univox Super Fuzz. The builder adds Half Gain and Octave Cut switches to vary the amount of gain and the octave emphasis.

## Sound
Super Fuzz is built for the doubled, buzzy octave texture associated with the Univox Super Fuzz family. Half Gain can pull the pedal back toward a less saturated response, while Octave Cut changes how strongly the upper-octave character dominates the sound.
