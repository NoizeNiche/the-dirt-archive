# Basic Audio — Super Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Super Fuzz
- **Builder:** Basic Audio
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Basic Audio octave fuzz based on the Univox Super Fuzz, with Half Gain and Octave Cut switches. [1]

## Controls / architecture
- **HALF GAIN** switch.
- **OCTAVE CUT** switch.
- Univox Super Fuzz-derived octave-fuzz core.
- 9V center-negative operation with battery support. [1]

## Circuit lineage
- **Primary reference:** Univox **Super Fuzz** / Shin-Ei FY-6 family.
- Basic Audio adds Half Gain and Octave Cut to expand the historical operating range.

## Sound
The pedal targets the doubled, buzzy octave character associated with Super Fuzz designs. Half Gain reduces saturation, while Octave Cut reduces the prominence of the upper-octave component. [1]
