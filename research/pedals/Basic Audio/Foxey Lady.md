# Basic Audio — Foxey Lady

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Foxey Lady
- **Builder:** Basic Audio
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Silicon fuzz based on the Guild Foxey Lady / late-1960s pre-Big Muff design, expanded with Mids control and tuned for a tighter, more defined low end. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
Basic Audio's Foxey Lady draws directly from the Guild Foxey Lady family while changing the response for modern use. The builder emphasizes more defined bass, faster attack and independent Mids control. [1]

## Controls
- **VOLUME**
- **FUZZ**
- **TONE**
- **MIDS**
The extra Mids control is part of the production design rather than a numbered revision. [1]

## Circuit lineage
- **Primary reference:** Guild Foxey Lady / 1969 pre-Big Muff family.
- Basic Audio identifies the design as a modified/refined interpretation, not an untouched vintage circuit. [1][2]

## Transistor
- **Technology:** Silicon.
- Exact transistor part number is **not publicly documented**. [1][4]
## Diode / clipping
- Exact clipping-device type and part number are not published.

## Power
- **9V center-negative** with battery support. [1]

## Version history
- No numbered electronic revision established.
- Off-white, yellow and other finishes are treated as cosmetic variations.

## Sound
Basic Audio emphasizes a more defined bottom end, quicker attack and adjustable mids than the vintage Foxey Lady-style baseline. The circuit retains thick sustain while giving the player more control over where that fuzz sits in a mix. [1][2]
