# Basic Audio — Foxey Lady

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Foxey Lady
- **Builder:** Basic Audio
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Silicon fuzz based on the late-1960s Guild Foxey Lady / pre-Big Muff family, expanded with mids control and tuned for tighter low end and faster attack. [1][2]

## Controls / architecture
- **VOLUME**
- **FUZZ**
- **TONE**
- **MIDS**
- Silicon fuzz circuit. [1][4]

## Circuit lineage
- **Primary reference:** Guild Foxey Lady / 1969 pre-Big Muff design.
- Basic Audio's implementation intentionally changes the response, including a more defined bottom end and faster attack. [1][2]

## Transistor
- **Technology:** Silicon.
- Exact transistor part number is not publicly documented. [4]

## Diode / clipping
- Exact clipping-device type and part number are not published.

## Power
- **9V center-negative** operation with battery support. [1]

## Version history
- No numbered electronic revision established.

## Sound
Basic Audio emphasizes a more defined low end, quicker attack and adjustable mids compared with a conventional Foxey Lady/Muff-family presentation. That keeps the sustain and thickness of the lineage while providing greater control over presence in a mix. [1][2]
