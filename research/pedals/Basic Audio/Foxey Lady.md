# Basic Audio — Foxey Lady

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Foxey Lady
- **Builder:** Basic Audio
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** One parent pedal. Basic Audio's take on the 1969 pre-Big Muff/Guild Foxey Lady family.

## What this pedal is
Foxey Lady is based on the Guild Foxey Lady, a late-1960s pre-Big Muff design. Basic Audio adds a mids control and tunes the circuit for a more defined bottom end, faster attack, and a more cutting voice. [1][2]

## Colorways
- An **off-white enclosure with black illustrated graphics** is documented in Basic Audio's official photograph.
- A **yellow** Foxey Lady finish is documented in a Reverb listing. [3]
- Basic Audio says pedal colors are subject to change, so these examples are treated as documented finishes rather than fixed generations. [1]

## Versions and factory options
### Foxey Lady production
- silicon fuzz
- Guild Foxey Lady / 1969 pre-Big Muff basis
- Volume, Fuzz, Tone and Mids controls
- more defined low end than the original concept
- center-negative 9V operation
- battery snap [1][4]

No numbered factory revision was verified.

## Version changes
The important evolution documented by Basic Audio is the refinement of the old Foxey Lady family rather than a numbered revision. The added mids control and tighter, faster response are part of the Basic Audio design itself. [1][2]

## Sound
Foxey Lady has the sustain and thickness associated with early Muff-family fuzz, but the faster attack and adjustable mids make it easier to keep defined and audible in a mix. Lower gain settings can remain present and articulate, while the circuit can still deliver the heavier, singing side of the design. [1][2]
