# Basic Audio — Gypsy fuzz

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** Gypsy fuzz
- **Builder:** Basic Audio
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Silicon fuzz derived from the sound/behavior of a 1960s console microphone preamp, with an unusually balanced response and strong cleanup. [1][2]

## What this pedal is

Basic Audio developed Gypsy around the behavior of an old console microphone preamp rather than around a named Fuzz Face or Muff circuit. The result is intended to keep a comparatively even harmonic balance while still producing substantial fuzz. [1]

## Sound

Gypsy is described by Basic Audio as balanced and harmonically even, with a little bloom as it is pushed and strong cleanup when the pedal or guitar volume is reduced. [1][2]
