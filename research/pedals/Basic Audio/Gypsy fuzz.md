# Basic Audio — Gypsy fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Gypsy fuzz
- **Builder:** Basic Audio
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Silicon fuzz based on a 1960s console microphone-preamp concept, emphasizing balanced harmonic response and strong cleanup. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
Basic Audio developed Gypsy around the sound of an old console mic preamp rather than a Fuzz Face. The builder describes an even, balanced fuzz that blooms when pushed and cleans up strongly from the instrument or pedal controls. [1]

## Sound
Gypsy is intended to remain relatively balanced and harmonically even rather than aggressively gated. Basic Audio describes a little bloom under heavier attack and substantial cleanup when the pedal or guitar volume is reduced. [1]
