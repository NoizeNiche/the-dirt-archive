# Basic Audio — Gypsy fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Gypsy fuzz
- **Builder:** Basic Audio
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Basic Audio's silicon interpretation of the 1960s console-mic-preamp-style Gypsy fuzz design.

## What this pedal is
Gypsy fuzz is a silicon fuzz based on a 1960s console microphone preamp concept, not a Fuzz Face. Basic Audio describes it as balanced and harmonically even, with a bloom when pushed hard and strong cleanup from the guitar or the pedal itself. [1]

## Sound
Gypsy fuzz aims for an even, balanced harmonic response rather than the overtly broken character of many starved or gated fuzzes. When the circuit is pushed it develops a little bloom, while backing off the pedal or guitar volume can clean it up substantially. [1]
