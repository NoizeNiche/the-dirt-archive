## What this pedal is
DOT is Basic Audio's Germanium Fuzz Rite-based fuzz, tuned for more low end and a fuller sound than the leaner vintage concept it comes from. [1]

## Colorways
- A **pink enclosure with a silver/black patterned faceplate** is documented in the official Basic Audio photograph.

## Sound
DOT keeps the buzzy, wiry identity associated with a Fuzz Rite style circuit but pushes the low end toward a fuller voice. The result is intended to feel less thin while retaining the raw, vintage fuzz character of the source design. [1]
