# Basic Audio - Zonk

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Zonk
- **Builder:** Basic Audio
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Silicon fuzz based on the Zonk Machine, with adjustable bias for a clearer or more aggressive response.

## What this pedal is
Zonk is Basic Audio’s silicon interpretation of the Zonk Machine. Its Tone control also functions as an adjustable bias, allowing the player to move from a clearer voice toward a more aggressive sound with additional grunt and a semi-octave-up quality.

## Sound
Zonk moves from a clearer, more articulate Zonk-style fuzz toward a more aggressive voice as the bias is shifted, with extra grunt and a semi-octave-up edge available from the Tone/Bias control. The overall character stays rooted in the sputtery, upper-harmonic attack associated with Zonk Machine-derived fuzzes.
