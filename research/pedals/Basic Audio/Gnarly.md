# Basic Audio — Gnarly

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Gnarly
- **Builder:** Basic Audio
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Silicon fuzz based on the Maestro FZ-1, with a Texture control that blends the behavior of its two main transistor stages. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
Basic Audio's Gnarly adapts the early Maestro FZ-1 fuzz concept around a stage-interaction control. The pedal is intended to move between grainier lower-gain fuzz and larger, sharper textures without requiring a different circuit family. [1]

## Controls
- **LEVEL:** Output.
- **TEXTURE:** Balances the first and second transistor stages.
- **FUZZ:** Fuzz amount. [1]

## Circuit lineage
- **Primary reference:** Maestro **FZ-1**.
- This is an interpretation of the vintage circuit family, not a component-for-component clone claim.

## Transistor
- **Technology:** Silicon.
- Exact production transistor part number is **not publicly documented**. [1][2]

## Power

## Sound
Gnarly's Texture control changes the relationship between the two gain stages, producing a broad range of fuzz character. Basic Audio describes settings that move from grainier fuzz toward more cutting, octave-like upper content. [1][2]
