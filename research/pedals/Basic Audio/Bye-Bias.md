# Basic Audio — Bye-Bias

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Bye-Bias
- **Builder:** Basic Audio
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Original silicon Bye-Bias parent. The separately cataloged Germanium version is documented as a distinct pedal rather than a cosmetic revision.

## What this pedal is
Bye-Bias is a dual-bias fuzz built around independently adjustable transistor biasing. Basic Audio describes it as a fixed-gain fuzz with dual bias and a deep high-cut tone control, capable of open, gated, and very sludgy textures. [1][2]

## Colorways
- An **orange, white, and blue splatter finish** is documented in an official Basic Audio photograph.
- Basic Audio says pedal colors are subject to change, so finishes are not treated as fixed production versions. [1]

## Versions and factory options
### Bye-Bias — silicon
- Fixed gain
- two independently adjustable bias controls
- deep high-cut tone control
- center-negative 9V operation
- battery snap [1][2]

### Separate Germanium model
Basic Audio also produced **Bye-Bias (Germanium)**. It is kept as its own archive entry because the builder calls it essentially a different pedal and gives it a gain control. [3]

## Version changes
The documented change is the existence of a separate Germanium model rather than a numbered revision of the silicon pedal. The silicon Bye-Bias remains its own parent identity.

## Transistor
- **Technology:** Silicon.
- **Architecture:** Two-transistor fuzz, with independent bias controls for the two transistor stages. [2][4]
- **Exact transistor/device:** Not publicly established for factory production.

## Diode
- **Type:** Not publicly documented.
- **Exact diode/device:** Unknown.

## Sound
Bye-Bias is highly adjustable, moving from open and sustaining fuzz into soft or hard-gated sounds depending on the two bias controls. The high-cut tone control lets the player remove fizzy top end, while deliberately uneven bias settings can create thick, synth-like or broken textures. [1][2]
