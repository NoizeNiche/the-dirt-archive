# Basic Audio — Low Volt

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Low Volt
- **Builder:** Basic Audio
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Germanium low-voltage/starved fuzz based on Basic Audio's Zippy and the Shin-Ei Companion family, deliberately exploiting mis-bias and supply-starvation textures. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
Low Volt is designed around controlled malfunction: lower operating voltage and bias extremes push the germanium fuzz into grainy, ducking, sputtering and bloom-like responses. [1]

## Controls / architecture
- Germanium fuzz core.

## Sound
Low Volt is explicitly intended to produce broken-sounding fuzz: starved behavior can collapse the signal, increase grain and create ducking/sputtering textures. The bias range provides a deliberately unstable side of the Germanium fuzz family. [1][2]
