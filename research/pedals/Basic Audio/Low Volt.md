# Basic Audio — Low Volt

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Low Volt
- **Builder:** Basic Audio
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Germanium starved/low-voltage fuzz drawing on the Basic Audio Zippy and Shin-Ei Companion traditions, with intentionally unstable bias/supply behavior. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
Low Volt is designed around controlled under-voltage and mis-bias behavior. Basic Audio uses the low-voltage concept to move the germanium fuzz into grainy, ducking, sputtering and unstable textures. [1]

## Sound
Low Volt deliberately exploits the failure modes of a low-voltage germanium fuzz. Starvation can collapse the signal, increase grain and produce ducking, sputtering and bloom-like behavior. [1][2]
