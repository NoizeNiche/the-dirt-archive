## What this pedal is
Low Volt is a Germanium fuzz based on Basic Audio's Zippy alongside the Shin Ei Companion fuzz. The design leans heavily into low-voltage, broken and mis-biased behavior, with extreme settings producing grain, bloom, ducking and unstable textures. [1][2]

### Low Volt production

- Zippy / Shin Ei Companion basis

- bias/saturation-style extreme settings

## Sound
Low Volt is deliberately unstable and rude around the edges: starved-voltage settings can make the fuzz collapse, sputter and bloom, while the extreme biasing can create a large-grain, ducking character. It is a pedal for finding broken-sounding textures on purpose rather than trying to make the circuit behave conventionally. [1][2]
