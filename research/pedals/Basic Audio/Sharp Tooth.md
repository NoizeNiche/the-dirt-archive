# Basic Audio - Sharp Tooth

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Sharp Tooth
- **Builder:** Basic Audio
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Silicon Orpheum-inspired fuzz with a Texture control spanning a dirt-boost response to full ripping fuzz.

## What this pedal is
Sharp Tooth is loosely based on the 1960s silicon Orpheum, but Basic Audio describes it as a substantially reworked design in tonality and texture. Its Texture control pans between one and two transistor stages, giving it a wide range from lightly dirtied boost to aggressive fuzz.

## Sound
Sharp Tooth runs from gritty, light-boost territory into a ripping fuzz as the Texture control brings in the second transistor stage. Basic Audio describes the overall character as a cross between a Tone Bender MKI and a gritty light boost, with the Texture control filling in the shades between those extremes.
