# Basic Audio — Sharp Tooth

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Sharp Tooth
- **Builder:** Basic Audio
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Silicon Orpheum-inspired fuzz whose Texture control moves between one- and two-stage gain behavior, from gritty light boost to ripping fuzz. [1]

## Controls / architecture
- **TEXTURE:** Determines how much the circuit is using its gain stages.
- Additional level/tone functionality is part of the production design, but the exact printed control legend is not fully preserved in the current archive source.
- **9V center-negative** operation with battery support is documented. [1]

## Circuit lineage
- **Primary reference:** 1960s silicon Orpheum.
- Basic Audio describes Sharp Tooth as a substantially reworked interpretation, not an untouched historical clone. [1]

## Transistor
- **Technology:** Discrete silicon transistor stages.

## Sound
Texture changes the stage relationship from lighter gritty boost-like operation to a fuller, more aggressive fuzz. Basic Audio describes the design as spanning sounds around Tone Bender MKI and silicon Orpheum territory. [1]
