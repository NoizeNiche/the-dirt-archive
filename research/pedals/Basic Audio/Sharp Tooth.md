# Basic Audio — Sharp Tooth

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Sharp Tooth
- **Builder:** Basic Audio
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Silicon Orpheum-inspired fuzz built around a Texture control that changes the contribution of its gain stages. [1][2]

## Controls / architecture
- **TEXTURE:** Changes how much the circuit uses its gain stages.
- Additional Level/Tone functions are part of the production design, but the current archive does not force a complete printed control legend where source evidence is incomplete. [1]
- **9V center-negative** with battery support. [1]

## Circuit lineage
- **Primary reference:** 1960s silicon Orpheum.
- Basic Audio presents Sharp Tooth as a substantially reworked interpretation, not an untouched historic clone. [1]

## Transistor
- **Technology:** Discrete silicon transistor stages.
- Exact production transistor part numbers are not publicly documented.
## Diode / clipping
- Exact clipping-device type and part number are not published.

## Version history
- No numbered electronic revision established.
- Cosmetic finish differences are not treated as revisions.

## Sound
Texture changes the stage relationship from lighter gritty/boost-like response toward fuller and more aggressive fuzz. Basic Audio places the design near Tone Bender MKI and silicon Orpheum territory while giving it its own control range. [1]

## Research confidence
- **Identity:** High
- **Silicon Orpheum lineage:** High
- **Texture-stage behavior:** High
- **9V operation:** High
- **Exact transistor:** Unknown
- **Exact diode:** Unknown

## Deep research verification
Basic Audio's Sharp Tooth page was checked against its product catalog. The silicon Orpheum relationship and Texture-stage concept are supported directly; exact semiconductor part numbers remain unpublished. [1]
