# Basic Audio — Scarab Deluxe

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Scarab Deluxe
- **Builder:** Basic Audio
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Silicon Tone Bender MkII-type fuzz expanded from the earlier Scarab with external Bias and Fat controls. [1][2]

## Controls
- **LEVEL**
- **TONE**
- **FUZZ**
- **BIAS**
- **FAT** [1]
FAT changes the amount of low-frequency content feeding the saturation stages.

## Circuit lineage
- **Primary reference:** Tone Bender MkII.
- Basic Audio explicitly describes Scarab Deluxe as a silicon Tone Bender MkII-type circuit. [1]

## Transistor
- **Technology:** Silicon.
- An independent Guitar Pedal X report identifies **4 × BC549B** in its documented Scarab Deluxe reference. This is retained as attributed independent evidence, not as a universal factory BOM. [3]
- Exact gain grading is not documented.
## Diode / clipping
- Exact clipping-device type and part number are not documented in the reviewed reliable sources.

## Power
- **9V center-negative** operation with battery support. [1]

## Version history
- **Scarab:** Earlier three-knob form.
- **Scarab Deluxe:** Adds external **Bias** and **Fat** controls. [1][2]
- The archive keeps the two names distinct.

## Sound
Scarab Deluxe spans tighter, potentially gated silicon fuzz into thicker sustained sounds. Bias shifts the operating point, while Fat changes the amount of low-frequency material entering the fuzz stages. [1]
