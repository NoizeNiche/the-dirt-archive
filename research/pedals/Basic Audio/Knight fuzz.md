# Basic Audio — Knight fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Knight fuzz
- **Builder:** Basic Audio
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Hybrid Germanium/Silicon interpretation of the 1960s Knight fuzz, with Basic Audio tailoring the response across lower- and higher-gain ranges. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
Knight fuzz takes the historical Knight fuzz concept and uses a hybrid transistor arrangement to create a broader response than a fixed single-technology build. Basic Audio emphasizes texture and a wide gain range. [1]

## Sound
Knight is intentionally variable in texture, using its hybrid transistor arrangement to move from relatively open/grainy fuzz toward more saturated and aggressive behavior. [1]
