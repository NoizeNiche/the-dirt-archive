# Basic Audio — Knight fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Knight fuzz

## What this pedal is
Knight fuzz is based on the 1960s Knight fuzz and uses a hybrid Germanium/Silicon transistor arrangement. Basic Audio emphasizes texture and variation, with the circuit able to move between lower- and higher-gain behavior. [1]

## Sound
Knight fuzz is built around contrast: the hybrid transistor arrangement gives it a broad range of textures rather than one narrowly defined fuzz voice. Lower-gain settings can stay relatively open and grainy, while the upper range becomes more saturated and aggressive without losing the deliberately variable feel of the design. [1]
