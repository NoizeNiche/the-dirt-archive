# Basic Audio — Knight fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Knight fuzz
- **Builder:** Basic Audio
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Hybrid Germanium/Silicon interpretation of the 1960s Knight fuzz, designed to cover a broad range of textures and gain. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
Basic Audio's Knight fuzz takes the historical Knight fuzz concept and combines germanium and silicon devices to broaden the response. The builder emphasizes texture and a wide range of usable gain rather than a single fixed vintage setting. [1]

## Circuit lineage
- **Primary reference:** 1960s Knight fuzz.
- **Technology:** Hybrid Germanium + Silicon. [1][2]
- Basic Audio's implementation is an interpretation, not a claim of exact historical component equivalence.

## Sound
Knight is intended to vary from relatively open/grainy fuzz into more saturated and aggressive textures through the hybrid device arrangement. Basic Audio's design goal is broader texture and gain range rather than strict replication of one historical build. [1][2]
