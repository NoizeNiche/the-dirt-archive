# Basic Audio — Orpheum

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Orpheum
- **Builder:** Basic Audio
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Basic Audio's Germanium interpretation of the 1960s Orpheum Fuzz.

## What this pedal is
Orpheum is based on the 1960s Orpheum Fuzz. Basic Audio describes it as bold and springy, with a blendable Texture control and high-cut tone shaping, while the builder's own demo material documents a Germanium version with Volume, Fuzz, Texture and Tone controls plus FAT and RAW tonal functions. [1][2]

## Colorways
- A **gold/bronze metallic enclosure with a distressed black-and-white faceplate** is documented in Basic Audio's official photograph.
- Basic Audio states that colors are subject to change. [1]

Basic Audio also made a separate **Orpheum II** model, which is treated as its own pedal identity rather than a numbered revision of this archive entry. [3]

## Sound
Orpheum has a springy, elastic fuzz character with a strong low-end body and a texture control that changes how the gain stages feel under the fingers. FAT and RAW-style tonal options can push the sound toward thicker, more broken-up textures or a leaner, rougher response while the high-cut tone control keeps the top end manageable. [1][2]
