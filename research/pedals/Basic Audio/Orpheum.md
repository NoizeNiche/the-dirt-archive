# Basic Audio — Orpheum

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Orpheum
- **Builder:** Basic Audio
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Germanium interpretation of the 1960s Orpheum Fuzz with additional texture and tonal controls; the separately cataloged Orpheum II is retained as its own identity. [1][3]

## What this pedal is
Basic Audio's Orpheum starts from the historical Orpheum fuzz and expands the circuit with a Texture control plus FAT and RAW voicing functions. The builder's own demo material documents a Germanium production version. [1][2]

## Controls / architecture
- **VOLUME**
- **FUZZ**
- **TEXTURE**
- **TONE**
- **FAT** function
- **RAW** function
- 9V center-negative with battery support. [1][2]

## Sound
Basic Audio's Orpheum is intended to feel elastic and springy rather than rigid, with Texture altering the gain-stage interaction and FAT/RAW voicings moving the response between thicker and rougher sounds. [1][2]
