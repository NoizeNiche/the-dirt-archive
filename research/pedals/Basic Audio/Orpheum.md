# Basic Audio — Orpheum

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Orpheum
- **Builder:** Basic Audio
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Germanium interpretation of the 1960s Orpheum Fuzz with additional Texture and voicing controls; Orpheum II remains a separate catalog identity. [1][2][3]

## What this pedal is
Basic Audio's Orpheum starts with the historical Orpheum fuzz concept and expands it with control over texture, tone and the overall character of the signal. The builder's own material identifies the production design as Germanium. [1][2]

## Sound
Basic Audio's Orpheum is intended to feel elastic and springy rather than rigid. Texture changes the gain-stage interaction while FAT/RAW move the response between fuller and rougher textures. [1][2]
