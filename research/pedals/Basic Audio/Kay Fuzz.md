# Basic Audio — Kay Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Kay Fuzz
- **Builder:** Basic Audio
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Kay Fuzz Tone/Fuzztone-derived octave fuzz with modern Octave, Diode Lift and FAT switching. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
Basic Audio's Kay Fuzz adapts the 1968 Kay F-1 Fuzztone/Fuzz Tone family with substantially more control over octave contribution, clipping behavior and low-end weight. [1]

## Controls / switching
- **LEVEL**
- **FUZZ**
- **TONE**
- **OCTAVE ON/OFF**
- **DIODE LIFT / ENGAGE**
- **FAT ON/OFF** [1][2]

## Circuit lineage
- **Primary reference:** Kay F-1 Fuzztone / Kay Fuzz Tone family.
- The modern control system is an intentional expansion of the historical concept. [2]

## Sound
Kay Fuzz is built around an aggressive octave-up character. Octave switching controls the overtone component, Diode Lift changes the clipping character, and FAT adds additional body/low-frequency weight. [1][2]
