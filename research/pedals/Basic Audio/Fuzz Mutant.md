# Basic Audio — Fuzz Mutant

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Fuzz Mutant
- **Builder:** Basic Audio
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Basic Audio's modern take on the 1960s Os Mutantes fuzz.

## What this pedal is
Fuzz Mutant is based on the fuzz associated with the Brazilian psychedelic band Os Mutantes. Basic Audio describes the result as balanced, highly textured and "grippy," with controls that can move between sustaining fuzz and tighter, gated textures. [1][2]

## Colorways
- An official Basic Audio photograph documents **green and orange** finished examples together.
- Effects Database notes that individual enclosures have unique finishes, supporting the idea that cosmetic variation is normal for this model. [2]
- Basic Audio states that colors are subject to change. [1]

## Versions and factory options
### Fuzz Mutant production
- Level
- Fuzz
- Tweak
- Clamp
- saturation control for low-end feeding into the later stage
- center-negative 9V operation
- battery snap [1][2]

The Basic Audio description and surviving documentation do not establish a numbered factory revision.

## Sound
Fuzz Mutant has a thick, tactile fuzz character that can feel sticky or "grippy" under the fingers, with the circuit capable of both sustained walls of fuzz and abrupt gated decay. The Clamp and Saturation controls help move it from more open and clear sounds toward brighter, more broken-up and synth-like textures. [1][4]
