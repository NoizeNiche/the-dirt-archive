# Basic Audio — Fuzz Mutant

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Fuzz Mutant
- **Builder:** Basic Audio
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Modern Basic Audio interpretation of the Brazilian Os Mutantes fuzz, with four principal controls and additional texture/clamp shaping. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
Fuzz Mutant is based on the fuzz associated with **Os Mutantes**, with Basic Audio expanding the circuit into a broader, more controllable fuzz. The builder describes a thick, tactile response that can move from sustaining fuzz toward gated and more broken textures. [1]

## Controls / architecture
- **LEVEL**
- **FUZZ**
- **TWEAK**
- **CLAMP**
- A saturation control changes how much low-frequency content is fed into the later stage. [1][2]

## Circuit lineage
- **Primary reference:** Os Mutantes / 1960s Brazilian fuzz.
- DIY reverse-engineering projects exist, but those are not treated as authoritative factory BOM evidence. [3][4]

## Transistor
- Factory transistor part number is not publicly documented.
- A DIY build report uses **BC549B**, but the archive explicitly treats that as a clone/build choice, not proof of the original Basic Audio component. [3]

## Diode / clipping
- The precise factory clipping diode is not documented.
- DIY build reports discuss clipping substitutions, but those are excluded from the factory record. [4]

## Power
- **9V center-negative** operation with battery support. [1][2]

## Version history
- No numbered factory revision established.
- Finish variation is treated as cosmetic.

## Sound
Basic Audio describes Fuzz Mutant as balanced but very textured, with “grippy” response. Clamp and saturation controls can move the circuit from open fuzz toward more compressed, gated and synth-like textures. [1][2]
