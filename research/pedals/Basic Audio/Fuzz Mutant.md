# Basic Audio — Fuzz Mutant

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** Fuzz Mutant
- **Builder:** Basic Audio
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Modern interpretation of the Os Mutantes-associated 1960s Brazilian fuzz, expanded with Tweak and Clamp controls for broader texture and saturation control. [1][2]

## What this pedal is

Basic Audio describes Fuzz Mutant as a very textured fuzz based on the sound associated with the Brazilian band Os Mutantes. The design expands the original-style concept so the player can alter the gain structure and how aggressively the later stage is driven. [1]

## Controls

- **LEVEL:** Output level.
- **FUZZ:** Fuzz amount.
- **TWEAK:** Tonal/response adjustment within the fuzz circuit.
- **CLAMP:** Changes how hard the circuit's later stage is driven and can move the character toward more compressed/gated behavior. [1][2]

## Sound

Fuzz Mutant is intended to remain thick and highly tactile while allowing the player to move from more conventional sustain into broken, gated and synth-like textures. Tweak and Clamp substantially alter how the fuzz stage responds to the input and how hard the following stage is driven. [1][2]
