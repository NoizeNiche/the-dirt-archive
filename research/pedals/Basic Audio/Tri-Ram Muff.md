# Basic Audio - Tri-Ram Muff

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Tri-Ram Muff
- **Builder:** Basic Audio
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Muff-style fuzz combining Ram’s Head and Triangle Big Muff references.

## What this pedal is
Tri-Ram Muff is Basic Audio’s Muff-family fuzz based on both the Ram’s Head and Triangle Big Muff circuits. The model is intended to cover those two related vintage Muff voicings rather than reproduce only one historical circuit.

## Version changes
No numbered production revision was established in the checked sources. Independent pedal coverage documents earlier Tri/Ram versions with a broader control set, but those historical variants are kept separate from the current official specification.

## Sound
Tri-Ram Muff is voiced around the large, sustaining character of Ram’s Head and Triangle Big Muff variants. Moving between those historical reference points changes the emphasis and feel of the fuzz, making the pedal useful for both thick rhythm textures and more pronounced lead fuzz.
