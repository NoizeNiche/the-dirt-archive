## What this pedal is
Basic Audio's Tri-Ram Muff is built around the large, sustaining Muff family but intentionally bridges two classic variants: Triangle and Ram's Head. Historical coverage of earlier Tri/Ram versions shows that the design has existed in more than one control configuration, while the current product page is kept as the canonical parent identity. [1][2]

## Circuit lineage

- **Ram's Head Big Muff**
- Modern Basic Audio interpretation of those two families. [1][2]

## Sound
Tri-Ram Muff is intended to cover thick, sustaining Triangle and Ram's Head-style fuzz characteristics, giving players access to both broad rhythm sustain and more cutting lead-oriented Muff textures. [1][2]
