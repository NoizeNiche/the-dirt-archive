# Basic Audio — Futureman

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Futureman
- **Builder:** Basic Audio
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive / Distortion / Fuzz
- **Identity:** Medium-gain interpretation of the Colorsound Overdriver, with separate High and Low controls and a wide gain range from dynamic overdrive into heavy fuzz. [1][2]

## Controls
- **VOLUME**
- **GAIN**
- **HIGH**
- **LOW**
- Center-negative 9V power and battery support. [1][2]

## Circuit lineage
- **Primary reference:** Colorsound Overdriver.
- Basic Audio's version is an interpretation that changes the usable range and provides independent high/low control. [1][2]

- Exact production active-device part numbers are not publicly documented.

## Power
- **9V center-negative** operation with battery support. [1]

- No numbered factory electronic revision established.
- Color changes are treated as finish variation rather than revisions.

## Sound
Futureman covers relatively clean, dynamic overdrive at lower gain settings and can expand into thicker, heavier fuzz as gain and low-frequency content rise. The separate High and Low controls make it useful for shaping the response of the pedal itself or another drive placed in the signal chain. [1][2]
