# Basic Audio — Futureman

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Futureman
- **Builder:** Basic Audio
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive / Distortion / Fuzz
- **Identity:** Colorsound Overdriver-derived medium-gain drive with separate HIGH and LOW controls and a wide range from dynamic overdrive into heavier fuzz. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
Basic Audio describes Futureman as its take on the Colorsound Overdriver family. The design keeps the broad gain architecture of the reference while adding independent High and Low controls for additional frequency shaping. [1][2]

## Controls
- **VOLUME**
- **GAIN**
- **HIGH**
- **LOW**
These four controls define the production pedal. [1]

## Circuit lineage
- **Primary reference:** Colorsound Overdriver.
- The Basic Audio version is an interpretation/refinement, not a documented untouched vintage clone. [1][2]

## Sound
Futureman ranges from relatively clean, dynamic overdrive at lower gain through much heavier saturation and fuzz as Gain increases. Independent High/Low controls let the player emphasize or restrain different parts of the spectrum without changing the basic drive identity. [1][2]
