## What this pedal is
Futureman is Basic Audio's medium-gain interpretation of the Colorsound Overdriver. It uses separate Low and High controls to shape the frequency balance, with the gain control allowing the pedal to move from dynamic overdrive into a much heavier fuzz texture. [1][2]

## Sound
Futureman can cover relatively clean, dynamic overdrive at lower gain settings and grow into thick, heavy fuzz when the gain and low-end controls are pushed. Its separate High and Low controls make it especially useful for shaping either guitar or bass signals and for tightening or widening another dirt pedal when stacked. [1][2]
