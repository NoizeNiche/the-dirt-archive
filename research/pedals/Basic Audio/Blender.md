# Basic Audio — Blender

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** Blender
- **Builder:** Basic Audio
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Fender Blender-inspired octave fuzz with blend, tonal filtering and octave reduction controls. [1][2]

## What this pedal is

Basic Audio's Blender takes the Fender Blender concept and adds practical mixing/filtering controls. An independent review confirms the octave-up character, clean/fuzz blend and octave-cut functionality. [1][2]

## Controls

- **BLEND:** Parallel clean/fuzz balance.
- **TONE:** Three-position voicing.
- **HI-CUT:** High-frequency reduction.
- **OCTAVE-CUT:** Reduces the octave contribution. [1][2]

## Sound

The pedal is built around pronounced octave-up fuzz, with the Blend control retaining clean articulation and Hi-Cut/Octave-Cut allowing the brighter and octave-heavy extremes to be reduced. [1][2]
