# Basic Audio — Alter-Destiny

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** Alter-Destiny
- **Builder:** Basic Audio
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Hybrid/modified Big Muff-style fuzz with expanded low-end, midrange and high/low voicing control. [1][2]

## What this pedal is

Basic Audio describes Alter-Destiny as a Muff-derived design with substantially more tonal control than a conventional fixed-voicing Muff. Effects Database identifies it specifically as a **Hybrid Muff**. [1][2]

## Controls / architecture

- **VOLUME**
- **TONE**
- **FUZZ**
- **MIDS**
- **FAT / GIRTH:** Low-end shaping.
- Two-position **high/mid boost** voicing switch. [1][2]
The Mids control and FAT/Girth shaping allow the player to move from large, bass-heavy Muff tones toward leaner, more articulate settings.

## Circuit lineage

- **Primary lineage:** Big Muff / Muff family.
- Basic Audio's implementation is a modified/expanded design, not documented as an untouched copy of one historical Muff revision. [1]
- DIY reports mentioning 2N5088/BAT42 substitutions are explicitly excluded as factory evidence. [3]

## Power

- **9V center-negative** with battery support is documented. [1]

## Sound

Effects Database describes big bass, flat mids, or lean/articulate mid-forward tones as available through the expanded controls. Lower FUZZ settings retain more treble and bite, and the circuit can clean up with the guitar volume control. [2]
