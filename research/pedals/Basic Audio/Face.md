## What this pedal is
Face is Basic Audio's Germanium Fuzz Face-style pedal with front-panel control over bias, starve, and the amount of bass retained at the output. Basic Audio describes it as slightly rougher and more cutting than typical vintage Fuzz Face circuits, while still cleaning up from the guitar volume control. [1]

## Sound
Face covers the familiar woody, singing Fuzz Face territory but can be pushed into rougher, thinner, gated, or more aggressive textures through its bias and starve controls. A bass-cut control helps keep the low end from getting too heavy, and the circuit remains responsive to changes on the guitar itself. [1][2]
