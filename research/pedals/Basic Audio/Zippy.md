# Basic Audio — Zippy

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** Zippy
- **Builder:** Basic Audio
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Coarse, abrasive fuzz based on the Shin-Ei FY-2, aimed at raw garage/fuzz tones. [1][2]

## What this pedal is

Zippy is Basic Audio's interpretation of the Shin-Ei FY-2 family. The circuit is intentionally rough and reedy rather than smooth, with enough output and gain to move from sizzle toward harsher, saw-like textures. [1]

## Circuit lineage

- **Primary reference:** Shin-Ei **FY-2**.
- The archive treats this as a documented lineage relationship, not a claim of exact vintage component equivalence. [1][2]

## Sound

Zippy sits squarely in the coarse FY-2 tradition, with reedy attack, abrasive harmonics and a wide movement from thick to thin response. The design is particularly suited to raw single-note lines and noisy chords. [1][2]
