## What this pedal is
Zippy is Basic Audio’s take on the Shin-Ei FY-2 family. The builder describes it as coarse, highly textured and especially suited to single-note lines, while still capable of loud abrasive chords.

## Sound
Zippy has the reedy, coarse and abrasive character associated with FY-2-style garage fuzz, moving from lower-level sizzle toward harsher saw-blade textures as it is driven harder. The available descriptions also note a wide shift from thick to thin response, making it especially effective for raw single-note parts and noisy chord work.
