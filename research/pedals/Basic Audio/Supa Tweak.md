# Basic Audio — Supa Tweak

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Supa Tweak
- **Builder:** Basic Audio
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Expanded Supa MKI/Marshall Supa Fuzz-style fuzz with externally separated Gain and Bias, interstage low/gain cut and Sag switching. [1][2]

## Controls / architecture
- **GAIN:** Gain amount independent from the bias setting.
- **BIAS:** Operating-point control.
- **Interstage LOW/GAIN CUT:** Reduces the low-frequency/gain feed between stages and improves cleanup.
- **SAG:** Adds more apparent gain and a voltage-droop response under harder attack. [1]

## Circuit lineage
- **Primary reference:** Basic Audio Supa MKI / Marshall Supa Fuzz MKI family.
- The expanded controls are Basic Audio's own implementation.

## Power
- **9V center-negative** with battery support. [1]

## Version history
- No numbered electronic revision established.
- Supa Tweak is treated as a distinct model because its Gain/Bias/Sag control architecture is materially different from the simpler Supa MKI.

## Sound
Basic Audio describes the interstage cut as improving cleanup and the Sag switch as adding gain plus a noticeable dip under strong picking. The result can move from open vintage fuzz toward softer, collapsing and compressed textures. [1][2]
