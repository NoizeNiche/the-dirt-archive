# Basic Audio - Pirk

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Pirk
- **Builder:** Basic Audio
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Harmonic Percolator-based fuzz/drive with a three-way low-end input boost.

## What this pedal is
Pirk is Basic Audio's take on the Harmonic Percolator. The builder specifically documents a three-way input low-end boost intended to move between clearer and more oscillating overtone behavior.

## Sound
Pirk expands the Harmonic Percolator idea with substantially more output and control range than the basic circuit family. Its low-end selector shifts the response toward clearer or more overtone-heavy/oscillating textures, while Gain, Tone, and Bias provide additional control over the fuzz/drive behavior.
