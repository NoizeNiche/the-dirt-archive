# Basic Audio — Pirk

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Pirk
- **Builder:** Basic Audio
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz / Drive
- **Identity:** Harmonic Percolator-derived fuzz/drive with a three-way low-end boost and external Bias. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
Pirk is Basic Audio's expanded Harmonic Percolator interpretation. The low-end selector changes how much bass enters the circuit, while Gain, Tone and Bias shape the resulting nonlinear response. [1]

## Controls
- **LEVEL**
- **TONE**
- **GAIN**
- **BIAS**
- **3-way low-end boost**
- 9V center-negative with battery support. [1][2]

## Circuit lineage

## Sound
Pirk's low-end selector moves the circuit toward clearer or more overtone-heavy operation. Gain and Bias provide additional control over saturation and broken-up textures. [1][2]
