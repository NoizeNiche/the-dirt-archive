# Basic Audio — Solar Myth

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Solar Myth
- **Builder:** Basic Audio
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Silicon Fuzz Face-style fuzz with pre-circuit fuzz adjustment and low-end/fatness shaping. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
Solar Myth is built around a silicon Fuzz Face-style core while providing more control over how much gain and low-frequency content enters the circuit. The design is intended to move between leaner/cutting and fuller/rounder fuzz voices. [1][2]

## Controls / architecture
- Pre-circuit **FUZZ** adjustment.
- **FATNESS / low-end** shaping.
- Silicon Fuzz Face-style gain core.
- **9V center-negative** with battery support. [1][2]

## Sound
Solar Myth can be biased toward leaner and more articulate fuzz or thicker, rounder and more compressed response by changing the pre-circuit fuzz and low-end controls. [1][2]
