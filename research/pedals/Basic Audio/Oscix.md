# Basic Audio — Oscix

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Oscix
- **Builder:** Basic Audio
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Germanium oscillating fuzz derived from Fuzz Face/Fuzz Factory concepts, with extensive external control over bias, supply starvation, gating and oscillation. [1][2]

## Controls / architecture
- **LEVEL**
- **STARVE**
- **FUZZ**
- **GATE**
- **PINCH**
- **OSC**
- **VINTAGE** toggle
- **FAT** toggle [1][2]

The controls interact strongly, allowing both conventional fuzz and deliberately unstable self-oscillating behavior.

## Circuit lineage
- **Fuzz Face / Fuzz Factory-style germanium architecture.**
- The archive records this as a lineage relationship rather than claiming exact schematic identity with either historical pedal. [1][2]

## Transistor
- **Technology:** Germanium.
- Exact production transistor part number is not publicly documented.
## Diode / clipping
- Exact clipping-device type and part number are not publicly documented.

## Power
- **9V center-negative** operation with battery support is documented by the Basic Audio product system.

## Version history
- No numbered electronic revision established.
- Multiple photographs on the product page represent production examples/angles rather than separate revisions.

## Sound
Oscix is designed to cross the line between fuzz and electronic instrument: Bias/Starve/Gate/Pinch can choke or distort the signal while OSC can push it into controlled squeal and self-oscillation. The Vintage and Fat functions add further tonal shifts around the core response. [1][2]

- **Germanium technology:** High
- **Oscillation controls:** High
- **Fuzz Face/Fuzz Factory lineage:** High as builder description
- **Exact transistor:** Unknown
