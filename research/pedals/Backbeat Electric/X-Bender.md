# Backbeat Electric — X-Bender

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** X-Bender
- **Builder:** Backbeat Electric
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz / Distortion
- **Identity:** Three-knob hand-built fuzz/distortion from Backbeat Electric, documented in 2010 as a German-built pedal with a heavy aluminum case and distinctive cherry-wood side walls. [1]

## What this pedal is

Analog War Cry's March 2010 feature identifies X-Bender as a fuzz/distortion with a deliberately simple, user-friendly control set. The design emphasis is rugged construction and classic-rock-oriented fuzz/drive rather than a published boutique circuit lineage. [1]

## Controls

- **VOLUME**
- **TONE**
- **SUSTAIN**
- True bypass footswitch.
- Standard input/output jacks. [1]

## Construction / hardware

The 2010 feature documents:
- Heavy-duty aluminum case.
- Stained Fireland cherry-tree side walls.
- Anti-slide bottom plate.
- Sliding drawer **9V battery compartment**.
- **9V DC jack with battery-off switch**.
- Hand-built in Germany.
- High-quality components. [1]
