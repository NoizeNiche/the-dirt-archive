# Backbeat Electric - Blue Blender

## PRP identity

- **Builder:** Backbeat Electric
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Backbeat Electric Blue Blender, an octave-fuzz blend design from the builder's early catalog.

## What this pedal is

Effects Database documents Blue Blender as an **octave-up fuzz** released in the early Backbeat Electric catalog. The design blends an unprocessed signal with a Fender Blender-style processed path.

## Controls

- **Volume:** Output level.
- **Sustain:** Increases output boost as the input signal fades, extending notes.
- **Tone:** Sweeps tonal balance from low-frequency emphasis at 1 toward high-frequency emphasis at 10.
- **Blend:** Mixes unprocessed signal with the processed Fender Blender path.
- **Tone Boost:** Enhances the action of the Tone control.

## Circuit / clipping

- **Clipping:** Germanium diodes.
- **Signal concept:** Fender Blender-style fuzz processing with clean/processed blending.
- Exact transistor part numbers were not established.

## Switching / construction

- **True bypass.**
- Late-'60s/'70s-style F knobs.
- Dimensions documented by Effects Database as approximately **2.25 x 7.25 x 4.75 in**.

## History

Effects Database published the Blue Blender entry on **August 22, 2006**. This is retained as a historical Backbeat product even though the builder's original web page is no longer reliably reachable.

## Sound

The documented architecture combines octave-fuzz coloration with a controllable clean blend, while the Sustain, Tone and Tone Boost controls expand the usable range beyond a fixed vintage Blender-style circuit.
