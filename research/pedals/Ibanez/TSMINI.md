## What this pedal is
The Tube Screamer Mini is the compact Ibanez version of the classic Tube Screamer overdrive. It preserves the familiar mid-focused drive character in a significantly smaller enclosure.

## Colorways
- The documented production finish is **green**.
- The smaller enclosure is a packaging change, not a claim of a different Tube Screamer circuit family.

## Sound
The TSMINI provides the familiar Tube Screamer combination of mid-forward overdrive, smooth clipping, and useful boost behavior. It is intended to reproduce the recognizable Tube Screamer response in a small footprint.
