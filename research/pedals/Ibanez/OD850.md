## What this pedal is
The Ibanez OD850 is a 1970s-era fuzz pedal that carries an “Overdrive” product name but belongs sonically and historically to the fuzz family. The later limited reissue preserves that identity.

## Sound
The OD850 is associated with thick, full fuzz rather than transparent overdrive. Historical examples are prized for a large, sustaining character that sits closer to the classic fuzz family than to the low-gain overdrive family.
