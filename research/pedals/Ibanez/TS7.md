## What this pedal is
The Ibanez TS7 is the Tone-Lok version of the Tube Screamer. Ibanez's owner manual describes it as a Tube Screamer-style overdrive with the classic TS9 mode plus a higher-saturation HOT mode.

## Colorways
- The standard TS7 Tone-Lok enclosure is documented. A complete finish chronology was not established.

## Versions and factory options
- **TS9 mode:** the manual states that this uses the same circuit as the classic TS9.
- **HOT mode:** provides stronger distortion and sustain than TS9 mode.

## Controls
- **DRIVE:** controls distortion and sustain.
- **TONE:** boosts or cuts the high-frequency region.
- **LEVEL:** controls output volume.
- **MODE:** selects TS9 or HOT operation.

## Sound
In TS9 mode, the TS7 follows the classic Tube Screamer circuit description in the Ibanez manual. HOT mode increases distortion and sustain for a heavier, more saturated sound.
