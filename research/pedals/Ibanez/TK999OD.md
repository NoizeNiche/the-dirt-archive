## What this pedal is

The **TK999OD Tube King** is a high-voltage tube overdrive built around a **12AX7 vacuum tube**. Ibanez describes it as covering sounds from light breakup through smooth high-gain saturation.

## Controls

The documented control set includes:

- **Drive**
- **Level**
- **Low**
- **Middle**
- **High**
- **Presence**

The pedal also has a **Hi/Lo input switch**.

## Power

The owner's manual specifies use of the included AC adapter and warns against substituting another adapter. The model operates from a dedicated AC/AC power arrangement rather than ordinary 9V DC pedal power.
