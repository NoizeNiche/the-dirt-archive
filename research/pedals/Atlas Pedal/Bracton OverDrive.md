# Atlas Pedal — Bracton OverDrive

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Bracton OverDrive
- **Builder:** Atlas Pedal
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Medium-compression overdrive using an op-amp gain stage and symmetrical LED clipping.

## What this pedal is
Atlas Pedal describes Bracton as a non-inverting op-amp overdrive with symmetrical LED clipping. It is positioned as a Tube Screamer alternative and can move from clean through crunch into overdrive.

## Sound
The gain range is aimed at low- to medium-gain drive with noticeable compression. The Presence control changes the upper-frequency response of the gain stage.
