# Atlas Pedal — Bracton OverDrive

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** Bracton OverDrive
- **Builder:** Atlas Pedal
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Medium-compression overdrive using a non-inverting op-amp gain stage and symmetrical LED clipping. [1]

## What this pedal is

Atlas Pedal describes Bracton as an overdrive developed as an alternative to a Tube Screamer-style drive. Its gain range runs from clean through crunch into overdrive, with a deliberately non-transparent, effect-forward voice. [1]

## Controls / circuit behavior

- **GAIN:** Main overdrive amount.
- **VOLUME:** Output level.
- **PRESENCE:** A gain-linked filter rather than a conventional fixed high-cut tone circuit. Atlas says it changes brightness, saturation, distortion texture and overall character in conjunction with Gain. [1]
- **Clipping:** Symmetrical LEDs in the published circuit description. [1]

## Circuit lineage

- **Gain stage:** Non-inverting op-amp amplifier.
- **Clipping:** Symmetrical LED clipping.
- Atlas identifies Sutherland OverDrive as a variation model of Bracton. [1]

## Electrical specifications

Atlas Pedal publishes:
- **Supply:** 9V DC.

## Version history

## Sound

Atlas describes the Bracton as moderately saturated with a little fuzziness, moving from clean through crunch to overdrive. The Presence circuit is intentionally interactive with Gain, changing the feel and tonal balance rather than acting as a simple passive treble cut. [1]
