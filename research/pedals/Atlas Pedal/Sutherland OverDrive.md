## PRP identity

## What this pedal is
Atlas Pedal identifies Sutherland as a variation model of Bracton. It uses Gain, PreGain, Tone and Volume, with the Tone circuit after the gain stage.

## Sound
PreGain changes distortion texture and saturation, while the Tone control acts as a conventional post-gain high cut. The builder describes a smooth gain response ranging from moderately compressed drive toward more transparent overdrive.
