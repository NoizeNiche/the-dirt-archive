# Atlas Pedal — Sutherland OverDrive

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** Sutherland OverDrive
- **Builder:** Atlas Pedal
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Four-control variation model of Atlas Pedal's Bracton OverDrive, adding PreGain and moving the post-gain tone shaping to a conventional high-cut Tone control. [1]

## What this pedal is

Atlas Pedal identifies Sutherland as a variation of Bracton. Its additional PreGain control changes distortion texture and saturation before the main Gain control, giving the pedal more expressive range than the related Bracton. [1]

## Controls

- **GAIN:** Main gain/saturation.
- **PREGAIN:** Adjusts the character and saturation entering the gain stages.
- **TONE:** Post-gain high-cut control.
- **VOLUME:** Output level. [1]

## Circuit relationship

- **Family relationship:** Variation of Bracton.
- Bracton uses a gain-linked Presence filter; Sutherland instead uses a conventional post-gain high-cut Tone stage. [1]

## Sound

Atlas describes Sutherland as smoother and more expressive than the related Bracton, ranging from moderately compressed drive toward more transparent overdrive. PreGain adjusts the saturation texture, while Tone acts as a conventional post-gain high cut. [1]
