# BOSS - MT-2W Metal Zone

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** BOSS
- **Catalog type:** Distortion

## Research
The MT-2W is the Waza Craft version of the Metal Zone, with a redesigned analog gain section using discrete components and two operating modes. Standard mode refines the familiar MT-2 character, while Custom mode expands the voice with a wider response, tighter low end, and greater dynamic range.

## Colorways
- Black Waza Craft enclosure with orange Metal Zone lettering and Waza Craft branding.

## Versions and factory options
- **Standard mode:** refined MT-2-style voicing.
- **Custom mode:** wider, tighter high-gain voicing.
- Made in Japan.

## Version changes
Compared with the standard MT-2, the MT-2W adds Waza Craft refinements, a discrete dual-stage gain design, revised EQ behavior, improved clarity/noise performance, and the Standard/Custom mode switch.

## Transistor
- BOSS describes a discrete analog gain circuit but does not publicly specify the individual production transistor part numbers.

## Diode
- BOSS does not publicly specify the individual production diode part numbers for the MT-2W.

## Sound
Standard mode keeps the Metal Zone's dense, mid-driven distortion while improving attack definition and noise performance. Custom mode opens the sound up, adds lower-mid body and tighter low end, and is designed to stay articulate at very high gain.
