# BOSS - OD-3 OverDrive

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** BOSS
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive

## Research
The OD-3 is a BOSS overdrive built around a dual-stage circuit designed for increased sustain, compression, and dynamic range. BOSS describes it as a smoother, larger-sounding evolution of its earlier overdrive line, with a three-control interface.

## Sound
The OD-3 has a smooth, full overdrive character with more sustain and compression than the earlier BOSS compact overdrives. Its response stays dynamic and comparatively open, with a sound BOSS associates with the feel of classic overdriven stack amplifiers.
