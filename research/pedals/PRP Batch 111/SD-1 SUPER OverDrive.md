# BOSS - SD-1 SUPER OverDrive

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** BOSS
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive

## Research
The SD-1 Super OverDrive was introduced in 1981 and developed from the OD-1's asymmetrical clipping concept. BOSS added an active tone control and subtle low-end changes, creating a focused midrange overdrive that became a long-running standard in the compact pedal range.

## Colorways
- Standard bright yellow BOSS compact enclosure.

## Versions and factory options
- The cataloged model is the **SD-1 Super OverDrive**.
- BOSS service documentation shows hardware component changes across early and later units, but the catalog treats these as the same public model rather than separate versions.

## Version changes
- The 1981 service notes document later-unit changes to some hardware parts such as the AC-adapter jack. These are service-level production details, not separate catalog identities.

## Transistor
The 1981 BOSS service notes list **2SC945-P, 2SC732TM-GR, and 2SK30ATM-Y** semiconductor devices among the SD-1's transistors/FETs.

## Diode
The 1981 service notes list **RD11EB-3, RD5.1EB-3, and 1S2473** diode devices.

## Sound
The SD-1 produces mild-to-moderate overdrive with a focused midrange and tight low end. Its asymmetrical clipping gives the response a soft, tube-like compression, while the guitar's volume control and playing dynamics can move it from relatively clean rhythm drive toward thicker lead tones.

## Controls and specifications
- LEVEL
- TONE
- DRIVE
- Buffered bypass
- 9V battery or PSA-series AC adapter
- 4 mA current draw in the original 1981 service notes
- 470k ohm input impedance in the original service notes
- 70 x 125 x 55 mm in the original service notes
