# BOSS - ST-2 Power Stack

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** BOSS
- **Catalog type:** Distortion

## Research
The ST-2 Power Stack is a BOSS compact distortion designed to reproduce the response and tonal range associated with driven tube-stack amplifiers. Its single Sound control sweeps from cleaner response through fat crunch and into high-gain distortion, with separate Bass and Treble controls.

## Sound
The ST-2 responds to playing intensity and guitar-volume changes, moving from relatively clean sounds into thick crunch and then extreme gain. At the high end it emphasizes strong low-end weight and a sharp edge intended to retain presence in dense rock and metal arrangements.
