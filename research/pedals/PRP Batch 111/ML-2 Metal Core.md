# BOSS - ML-2 Metal Core

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** BOSS
- **Catalog type:** Distortion

## Research
The ML-2 Metal Core is a high-gain BOSS distortion pedal built around an especially aggressive, heavy voice. BOSS positions it for metal styles including nu metal, grindcore, industrial, and hardcore, with dedicated Low and High controls for extending the tonal range.

## Sound
The ML-2 is voiced for dense, extremely saturated distortion with a sharp attack and extended low end. The Low control is intended to support heavy low-tuned or seven-string-style riffing, while the High control adds a biting metallic edge.
