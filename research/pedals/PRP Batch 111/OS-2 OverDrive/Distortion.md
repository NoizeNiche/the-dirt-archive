# BOSS - OS-2 OverDrive/Distortion

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** BOSS
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive

## Research
The OS-2 combines separate overdrive and distortion circuits in one compact pedal. A Color control selects either side or blends the two paths in parallel, giving the pedal a broader range than a conventional single-drive stompbox.

## Sound
The overdrive side is voiced around asymmetrical clipping and a rich, singing midrange, while the distortion side supplies harder-edged attack and punch. Blending the two with Color can soften the distortion, add clarity to overdrive, or create intermediate sounds that do not behave like simply stacking two pedals in series.
