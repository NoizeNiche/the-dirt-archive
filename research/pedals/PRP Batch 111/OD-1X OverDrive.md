# BOSS - OD-1X OverDrive

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** BOSS
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive

## Research
The OD-1X is a digitally assisted BOSS overdrive built around the company's Multi-Dimensional Processing (MDP) technology and high-speed DSP. BOSS describes MDP as analyzing characteristics such as frequency, overtones, dynamics, pickup response, note register, chords, bends, and vibrato in real time to shape the drive response.

## Sound
The OD-1X aims for punchy, articulate overdrive with strong note definition and a relatively open response. BOSS describes the available range as extending from light edge-of-breakup and fat rhythm crunch to higher-gain, singing lead tones while retaining the character of the guitar and pickups.
