# BOSS - MT-2 Metal Zone

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** BOSS
- **Catalog type:** Distortion

## Research
The MT-2 Metal Zone is an analog high-gain distortion pedal introduced by BOSS in 1991. Its signal path uses two gain stages in series, followed by extensive filtering and a three-band active EQ with a sweepable semi-parametric midrange.

## Colorways
- Standard dark gray/black BOSS enclosure with orange Metal Zone graphics.

## Versions and factory options
- The standard production model is the **MT-2 Metal Zone**.
- The later **MT-2W Metal Zone** is treated as a separate public model/version in the catalog and is not folded into the standard MT-2 record.

## Version changes
- The MT-2 itself remains the standard Metal Zone model; MT-2W is documented separately as a Waza Craft redesign.

## Transistor
The 1991 BOSS MT-2 service notes list these semiconductor parts among the replacement components: **2SC3378GR, 2SC2240GR, 2SC2458GR, 2SK184GR, 2SK117GR, and 2SK118Y**. The service document is a parts/service reference rather than a claim that every production run used every listed part.

## Diode
The same 1991 service notes list **S5500G, 1SS-133, and RD5.6EB3** among the diode parts.

## Sound
The MT-2 produces thick, saturated high-gain distortion with a tight, aggressive character and smooth sustain. Its active EQ can move from scooped heavy rhythms to more cutting, mid-forward sounds, with the sweepable mid control covering roughly 200 Hz to 5 kHz.

## Controls and specifications
- DIST
- LOW / HIGH
- MIDDLE LEVEL / MID FREQ
- LEVEL
- Buffered bypass
- 9V battery or PSA-series AC adapter
- 30 mA current draw
- 1M ohm input impedance
- 1k ohm output impedance
- 73 x 129 x 59 mm
- 385 g including battery
