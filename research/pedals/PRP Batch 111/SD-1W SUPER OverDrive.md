# BOSS - SD-1W SUPER OverDrive

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** BOSS
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive

## Research
The SD-1W is the Waza Craft version of the SD-1 Super OverDrive, built in Japan with a revised all-analog discrete amplifier circuit. Standard mode keeps the familiar SD-1 response, while Custom mode broadens the gain and tonal range.

## Colorways
- Yellow Waza Craft enclosure with black footpad, silver/chrome control hardware, and Waza Craft branding.

## Versions and factory options
- **Standard mode:** classic SD-1-based response.
- **Custom mode:** increased gain and broader tonal range.
- Made in Japan.

## Version changes
Compared with the standard SD-1, the SD-1W uses a revised all-analog discrete amplifier circuit and adds the Standard/Custom mode switch.

## Transistor
- BOSS describes the amplifier as all-discrete analog but does not publicly specify the individual production transistor part numbers.

## Diode
- BOSS does not publicly specify the individual production diode part numbers for the SD-1W.

## Sound
Standard mode preserves the familiar SD-1 mid-focused overdrive character, while Custom mode pushes the circuit toward more gain and a wider tonal response. The pedal remains sensitive to picking attack and guitar-volume changes, so it can move between restrained rhythm drive and thicker lead textures without changing the basic controls.

## Controls and specifications
- LEVEL
- TONE
- DRIVE
- Standard/Custom mode switch
- Buffered bypass
- 9V battery or PSA-series AC adapter
- 15 mA current draw
- 1M ohm input impedance
- 1k ohm output impedance
- 73 x 129 x 59 mm
- 430 g
