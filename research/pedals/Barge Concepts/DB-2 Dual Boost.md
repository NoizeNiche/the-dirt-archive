# Barge Concepts - DB-2 Dual Boost

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Barge Concepts
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive / Distortion
- **Identity:** Barge Concepts DB-2 Dual Boost, revised two-channel boost/preamp with cascadeable drive.

## What this pedal is
DB-2 provides two independent boost channels, each with up to 27 dB of gain, with Channel 2 feeding a master volume. The channels can be cascaded to create overdrive/distortion without forcing the output level to track the internal gain.

## Controls / architecture
- Channel 1 Gain: up to 27 dB.
- Channel 2 Gain: up to 27 dB.
- Master Volume on Channel 2.
- Effects loop between stages.
- Independent channel operation or cascaded operation.

## Circuit / construction
- Four-layer circuit board.
- Sealed tantalum capacitors.
- Burr Brown low-noise op-amps.
- Improved bias circuit intended to preserve headroom.
- Exact op-amp part numbers are not established in the checked source.
- Documented power range: **6V-18V**.

## Sound
Barge Concepts describes the DB-2 as a transparent, wide-band boost platform. Cascading both channels can produce overdrive or distortion at an independently controlled output level.
