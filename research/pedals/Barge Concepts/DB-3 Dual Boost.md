# Barge Concepts - DB-3 Dual Boost

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Barge Concepts
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive / Distortion
- **Identity:** Barge Concepts DB-3 Dual Boost, two-stage boost/preamp with documented overdrive/distortion operation.

## What this pedal is
DB-3 uses two gain channels with an effects loop between them. When the channels are cascaded, the builder documents the result as an overdrive/distortion platform while preserving a wide-band, instrument-forward response.

## Controls / architecture
- Channel 1 gain.
- Channel 2 gain.
- Master/output level.
- Effects loop between the stages.
- Send output remains active for a tuner or second amplifier.

## Circuit / behavior
The checked Effects Database record states that cascaded gain stages clip sharply, cleanly and symmetrically, with no phase inversion. Exact component part numbers were not verified here.

## Sound
The primary design goal is broad-band signal preservation, while the cascaded stages can be used for controlled overdrive/distortion.
