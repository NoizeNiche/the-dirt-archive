# Barge Concepts - DB-1 Dual Boost

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Barge Concepts
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive / Distortion
- **Identity:** Barge Concepts DB-1 Dual Boost, a two-channel boost/preamp that can be cascaded into overdrive/distortion.

## What this pedal is
The DB-1 contains two independent boost channels with an effects loop between them. Barge Concepts documents the cascaded combination as capable of producing overdrive/distortion while retaining separate channel volume states.

## Controls / architecture
- Channel 1 Gain: 0-27 dB.
- Channel 2 Gain: 0-27 dB.
- Master Volume on Channel 2.
- Effects loop between the two channels.
- Four practical level states: bypass, Channel 1, Channel 2, both combined.

## Circuit / power
The checked Effects Database record documents a wide-band boost/preamp design. Exact op-amp/transistor part numbers and a complete schematic were not verified here.
- Power specification remains unresolved in this archive record.

## Sound
With the two stages cascaded, the unit can be driven into overdrive/distortion at a controlled output level. As a standalone boost, the design is intended to preserve instrument bandwidth.
