# Big Tone Music Brewery - Germanium FuzzBender

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Big Tone Music Brewery
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz

## Research
Hand-built Tone Bender recreation using three germanium transistors and a germanium diode. Effects Database describes more gain, deeper clipping and more sustain than Fuzz Face-type circuits. Both MkII two-knob and MkIII three-knob versions were offered, with NOS Mullard OC75 or OC81M transistor options. Published February 20, 2006.
