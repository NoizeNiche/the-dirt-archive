# Big Tone Music Brewery - Parametric Overdrive

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Big Tone Music Brewery
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive

## Research
Parametric overdrive built around three tonal parameters: Gain, Timbre and Symmetry. Gain sets the available drive range, Timbre controls the frequency where the tone circuit's midrange roll-off occurs, and Symmetry blends symmetrical and asymmetrical clipping. The boost channel has a Pre/Post switch, allowing the boost before the overdrive for interaction or after it for extra volume. Effects Database also categorizes it as a parametric equalizer / overdrive.
