# Big Tone Music Brewery - Germanium Fuzz Master

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Big Tone Music Brewery
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz

## Research
Vintage Fuzz Face-based fuzz using two NOS Mullard OC75 germanium transistors. Four top controls are Volume, Tone, Fuzz and Bias, with a side-mounted five-position input-capacitor selector. A hybrid switch can replace one germanium device with a high-gain BC214 silicon transistor for extra sustain, punch and fizz. An accessible internal trimmer provides further setup control. The Effects Database record dates the model to 2006.
