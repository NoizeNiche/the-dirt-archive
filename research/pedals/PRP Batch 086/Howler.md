# Big White Monkey Amps - Howler

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Big White Monkey Amps
- **Catalog type:** Distortion

## Research
Analog distortion designed around the sound and modifications associated with Keeley-modded RAT pedals. Effects Database documents a three-way tone switch producing three distinct distortion palettes. Construction is 100% analog and hand-made, with a military-grade epoxy-glass circuit board, steel enclosure, heavy-duty switch, LED and true-bypass operation. Source: Effects Database.
