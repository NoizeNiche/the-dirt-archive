# Big Tone Music Brewery - MOSFET Overdrive & Boost

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Big Tone Music Brewery
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive

## Research
MOSFET-based overdrive paired with an independent MOSFET boost, intended for guitar and bass. The design includes a three-band EQ with true parametric mids, wet/dry blend, Class A/Class AB switching, and Bright/Normal switching. The midrange is sweepable from 300 Hz to 3 kHz. Source: Big Tone Music official product page.
