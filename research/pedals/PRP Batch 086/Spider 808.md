# Big White Monkey Amps - Spider 808

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Big White Monkey Amps
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive

## Research
100% analog, hand-made overdrive with true bypass, military-grade epoxy-glass circuit board, steel enclosure and heavy-duty switch. Controls are documented as Volume, Tone and Drive. Includes LED status indication, a 9V battery compartment and 2.1mm DC adapter socket. Published in the Effects Database in 2011.
