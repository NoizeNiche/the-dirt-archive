# Bigfoot Engineering - Bigfoot 15th Anniversary Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Bigfoot Engineering
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz

## Research
One-of-a-kind 15th anniversary fuzz based on the King Fuzz circuit and re-biased around a gold-pin NOS germanium transistor. Controls are Gain and Volume. The builder states it can be placed anywhere in the signal chain, including after buffers, active pickups and wireless systems. Power is 9VDC with no battery option. The enclosure is a custom retro Goldtop nitrocellulose finish with 2009-2024 anniversary graphics.
