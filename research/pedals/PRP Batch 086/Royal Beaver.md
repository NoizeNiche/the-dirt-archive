# Big Tone Music Brewery - Royal Beaver

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Big Tone Music Brewery
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Fuzz / Overdrive

## Research
Expanded Big Muff-inspired design based on the BYOC Large Beaver concept. Documentation describes coverage of Triangle, Ram's Head, NYC and Green Russian-style Muff families, with extensive transistor-control options beyond the basic Muff core. A surviving product description documents a four-stage EQ and transistor controls including V+, Gain and Bias. Source: Effects Database and archived dealer/product material.
