# Bigfoot Engineering - Bigfoot Germanium EQ Drive

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Bigfoot Engineering
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive

## Research
Hybrid amplifier-style overdrive using a JFET input circuit, a studio-quality transformer, a gold-pin germanium transistor and a three-band EQ. The builder provides Bass, Middle, Treble and Volume controls, with no separate Gain control. Overdrive amount is strongly dependent on player dynamics and guitar controls. It accepts buffers, active pickups and wireless systems. Power is 9VDC, no battery; true bypass.
