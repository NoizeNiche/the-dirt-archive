# Big Tone Music Brewery - Six Of Swords

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Big Tone Music Brewery
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz / Overdrive

## Research
Wide-range fuzz-to-overdrive design centered on highly interactive controls. V is the output volume; I (Intensity) uses the guitar volume as a major part of the drive response and changes attack, compression, sustain and saturation; X (Texture) changes hair and intermodulation character; B and T provide a powerful two-band EQ that can reshape the voicing. Source: Effects Database.

## Deep research verification
Effects Database documents the Six Of Swords as a continuous fuzz-to-overdrive design. **V** is volume, **I** changes attack, compression, sustain and saturation while using the guitar volume as a major part of drive behavior, **X** alters hair and intermodulation, and **B/T** form a two-band EQ capable of shifting the voice from loose fuzz toward tighter distortion.
