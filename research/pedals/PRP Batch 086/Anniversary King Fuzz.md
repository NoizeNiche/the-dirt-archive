# Bigfoot Engineering - Anniversary King Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Bigfoot Engineering
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz

## Research
Special-reserve Anniversary King Fuzz from Bigfoot Engineering's limited-edition line. The model is based on the King Fuzz circuit and is documented as an all-analogue, hand-built English fuzz with gain and volume controls. A selectable output arrangement provides true-bypass or buffered-bypass operation, and the builder documents 9VDC operation without a battery. Source: Bigfoot Engineering limited-edition catalog.
