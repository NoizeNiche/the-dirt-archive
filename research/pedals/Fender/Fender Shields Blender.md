## What this pedal is
The Fender Shields Blender is a Kevin Shields signature fuzz inspired by the original Fender Blender pedals that influenced his sound. Fender designed it as an expanded, highly interactive fuzz system that can combine multiple fuzz paths for dynamic, splattery, and heavily saturated textures.

## Colorways
- The documented production enclosure is **silver**.
- The Kevin Shields signature branding is part of the model identity.

## Versions and factory options
- Multiple fuzz paths can be blended for combined textures.
- Independent control of the fuzz stages allows the player to move from relatively conventional fuzz into more extreme layered sounds.
- Octave and tone-shaping functions are part of the expanded design.
- Fender uses top-mounted jacks and an aluminum enclosure.
- True bypass switching.
- 9V DC power.

## Sound
The Shields Blender is designed for dynamic, unstable, high-impact fuzz rather than a single fixed vintage voice. Its blended fuzz architecture allows dense sustain, octave coloration, gated behavior, and heavily distorted splatter to be combined and shaped.
