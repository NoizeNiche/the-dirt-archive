## What this pedal is
The Trapper Dual Fuzz is a two-voice fuzz pedal from Fender that combines distinct fuzz characters in one enclosure. Fender designed it to provide contrasting fuzz textures and let the player switch between the two voices as part of a live rig.

## Sound
The Trapper provides two contrasting fuzz characters in a single pedal, allowing a player to move between voices without changing pedals. The two sections are intended to cover complementary vintage and modern fuzz textures rather than one fixed fuzz sound.
