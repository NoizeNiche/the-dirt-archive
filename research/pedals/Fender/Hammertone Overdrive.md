## What this pedal is
The Hammertone Overdrive is a Fender-designed analog overdrive intended to add warmth, grit, and amp-pushing gain. Fender describes a pre-gain mid boost as the defining feature, with an internal tone control for trimming very high frequencies.

## Colorways
- Fender documents a **Gray with Red Accents** finish.
- Aluminum enclosure.

## Versions and factory options
- Fender-designed analog overdrive circuit.
- **Pre-gain Mid Boost** adds mids before the gain stage for extra punch and bite.
- Top-mounted input/output jacks.
- True bypass.
- 9V battery or center-negative AC adapter.
- Internal tone trim can reduce high frequencies.
- Input impedance: 450kΩ.
- Output impedance: 5kΩ maximum.
- Power consumption: 80mA.

## Sound
The Hammertone Overdrive covers warm low-gain drive through harder amp-pushing overdrive. The pre-gain mid boost tightens the response and adds bite, while the internal tone control helps tame bright pickups and amplifiers.
