# Fender — The Pelt Fuzz

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** The Pelt Fuzz
- **Builder:** Fender
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Fender's The Pelt Fuzz.

## What this pedal is

The Pelt Fuzz is a transistor-based Fender fuzz with an unusually broad set of controls for shaping gain, low end, mids, and attack. Fender later introduced **The Pelt V2** as a distinct revision with expanded octave/Chaos and bypass features.

## Colorways

- Historic production is associated with a **purple** enclosure.
- The archive treats the later Pelt V2 as a separate revision rather than merging its features into this original record.

## Versions and factory options

- Original Pelt fuzz design with dedicated fuzz shaping controls.
- Transistor-based gain circuit.
- Fender pedal enclosure with top-mounted jacks.
- 9V center-negative DC power.
- The original record does not inherit V2-only features such as the V2 footswitchable Chaos mode or rear-panel buffered/true bypass selector.

## Version changes

- The current Fender product is **The Pelt V2 Fuzz**, a distinct revision.
- V2 adds switchable Octave/Chaos behavior, Bloom and Sag controls, Blend, rear-panel multiswitch functionality, and selectable buffered/true bypass modes.
- This archive record remains the original Pelt identity.

## Sound

The original Pelt is a broad-range fuzz with a wooly character that can be shaped toward different levels of compression, bass response, and mid presence. The later V2 extends the concept considerably, but its new features are not retroactively assigned to the original record.
