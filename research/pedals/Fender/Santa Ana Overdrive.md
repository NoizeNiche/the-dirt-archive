## What this pedal is
The Santa Ana Overdrive is a JFET-based Class A overdrive with an internal 18V rail and two voicings. Its independently switchable three-band EQ can be positioned before or after the distortion circuit.

## Sound
Santa Ana provides dynamic, medium-to-high gain overdrive with two primary voicings. The switchable three-band EQ can be placed before or after the drive stage, making the pedal unusually flexible as both a standalone drive and a front-end shaping tool.
