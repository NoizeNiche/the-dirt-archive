## What this pedal is
The Smolder Acoustic Overdrive is an all-analog Fender overdrive designed specifically for acoustic instruments and piezo pickups. Fender developed it to add musical distortion while controlling the bright attack and feedback problems that can arise with acoustic amplification.

## Colorways
- Fender documents a **Brown** anodized-aluminum enclosure.

## Versions and factory options
- **Pickup Compensation:** combination high/low-pass filtering for optimizing piezo pickup response.
- **Drive:** distortion amount.
- Three-band **Bass, Middle, Treble** EQ.
- **Filter** control for additional sound shaping.
- Onboard cabinet simulation for full-range amps, PA systems, and direct use.
- 9V DC center-negative power.
- Power consumption: 105mA.

## Sound
The Smolder is voiced for acoustic-guitar distortion while retaining control over piezo harshness and feedback. Pickup Compensation softens overly bright pickups, the three-band EQ provides broad shaping, and the cabinet simulation helps the pedal work into full-range acoustic systems.
