## What this pedal is

The Yngwie Malmsteen Overdrive is Fender's signature overdrive for Yngwie Malmsteen, voiced for aggressive lead playing and high-output amplifier setups. It is a compact analog overdrive rather than a generic distortion pedal.

## Versions and factory options
