## What this pedal is
The Compugilist combines an analog compressor with an analog distortion circuit in one enclosure. Fender designed the two effects to be used independently or together.

## Versions and factory options
- Independently switchable compression and distortion sections.
- Bass boost.
- LED-backlit controls.
- True bypass.
- 9V operation.
- All-analog circuitry.

## Sound
Compugilist can provide compression on its own, distortion on its own, or a combined compressed-and-distorted sound. The bass-boost function adds weight when the distortion stage is engaged.
