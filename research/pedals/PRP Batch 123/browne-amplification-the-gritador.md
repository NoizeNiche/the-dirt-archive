# Browne Amplification — The Gritador

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Browne Amplification
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Current naming for Browne's Gritador overdrive.

## What this pedal is
The Gritador is Browne's modified version of a classic Tube Screamer-style overdrive. Browne says it refines the midrange, makes clipping less symmetrical, and adds low-end fullness while keeping the response tight and punchy. [1]

## Colorways
The current manufacturer page shows green and black finishes. [1]

## Versions and factory options
- Modified Screamer-style circuit
- Refined midrange
- Less-symmetrical clipping
- Fuller, tighter low end
- Blemished cosmetic examples are finish variants rather than circuit revisions [1]

## Diode
The manufacturer documents less-symmetrical clipping but does not publish exact diode parts. [1]

## Sound
Gritador keeps the familiar mid-forward character of the Screamer family while reducing nasal coloration and restoring low-end fullness. Browne positions it as a vocal, articulate overdrive that can cover blues through modern-rock drive. [1]
