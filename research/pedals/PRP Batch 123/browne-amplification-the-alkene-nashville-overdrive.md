# Browne Amplification — The ALKENE Nashville Overdrive

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Browne Amplification
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Current product-page naming for the ALKENE Nashville Overdrive.

## What this pedal is
The ALKENE combines Nashville-style low-to-medium-gain drive with gentle amp-like compression, tightened bass and restored mids. Browne describes it as especially suited to clear crunchy rhythm tones and expressive lead drive. [1]

## Colorways
The manufacturer exposes selectable color options but does not define one permanent finish in the accessible text.

## Versions and factory options
- External symmetrical/asymmetrical clipping switch
- Internal More Clip switch using an alternate hard-clipping diode set
- Internal Low Cut switch
- Level, Gain and Tone controls [1]

## Version changes
The current ALKENE expands the older ATOM/Nashville-drive concept with additional clipping and low-cut options. [1]

## Transistor
Exact active devices not publicly documented.

## Diode
Browne explicitly documents alternate hard-clipping diodes in More Clip mode, but not the exact part numbers. [1]

## Sound
The ALKENE is voiced for full, clear Nashville-style overdrive with controlled lows and gentle compression. Asymmetrical clipping opens the response and slightly reduces compression, while More Clip increases aggression and gain. [1]
