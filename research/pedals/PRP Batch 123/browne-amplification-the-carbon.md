# Browne Amplification — The Carbon

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Browne Amplification
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Standalone Carbon, derived from the blue side of the Protein.

## What this pedal is
The Carbon is the standalone blue side of Browne's Protein. Browne describes it as a heavily modified Bluesbreaker-style pedal designed with a simpler control approach; the current product page identifies V2 as adding a three-position hi-cut. [1]

## Colorways
Blue and black finishes are shown on the current manufacturer page. [1]

## Versions and factory options
- Standalone blue-side Protein-derived circuit
- V2 three-position hi-cut: Subtle Cut / OG Carbon / More Cut
- Soft switching
- Adjustable startup state [1]

## Version changes
V2 adds the three-position hi-cut to the original Carbon setting. [1]

## Sound
Carbon is designed as a clear, high-headroom Bluesbreaker-style overdrive. The V2 hi-cut gives additional control over treble content while retaining the core Carbon voice. [1]
