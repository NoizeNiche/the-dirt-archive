## What this pedal is
The Rodeo Drive is a modified Bluesbreaker-style overdrive with a more advanced EQ section and an internal mid-control trimmer. Effects Database and RockBoard both identify it as an analog overdrive, while contemporary 6 Degrees coverage describes it as a hand-wired pedal built around the Bluesbreaker platform. [1][2][3]

- Internal mid-control trimmer on the documented version. [1][3]

## Sound
The documented design target is a cranked-Marshall-style response with the dynamics and harmonic emphasis associated with a modified Bluesbreaker platform. Effects Database describes it as a straightforward overdrive that works with the controls around noon, while other period coverage describes a fat, full sound with retained high-frequency definition and a range from lighter crunch to more saturated drive. [1][3][4]
