## What this pedal is
The Sally Drive is a boutique overdrive based on the original Ibanez Tube Screamer 808 design, built with a hand-wired construction and intended to retain the guitar and amplifier character while adding a more full-bodied, dynamic overdrive response. Effects Database identifies the standard Sally Drive as a TS808-derived design and documents the pedal's 9V DC adapter-only power arrangement. [1]

## Controls and options
- Volume / Level
- Tone
- Drive / Gain
- The **Sally Drive Advanced** adds a three-way Shift control for additional tonal voicings and uses internal trimmers for low-mid adjustment and dynamic bias. [2]

## Circuit / architecture
- TS808-derived overdrive architecture. [1][2]
- The Advanced version's Tone control splits bass and treble response for broader shaping than the standard three-knob version. [2]
- The Advanced version's Shift switch selects three documented voicings: a clear treble-boost setting, a stock-style 808 mid voice, and a fuller/tighter rhythm voice. [2]
- 9V DC adapter power is documented for the standard Sally Drive; the original compact enclosure leaves no room for a battery. [1]
- Exact production transistor/op-amp part numbers are not fully established in the reviewed sources.

## Versions and factory options
- **Sally Drive / Classic:** original three-control presentation documented by Effects Database. [1]
- **Sally Drive Advanced:** later expanded version with three-way Shift control and internal trimmers. [2]
- Effects Database also records V2 references for the Sally Drive family. [1]
- The checked sources do not establish a complete numbered factory-revision history.

## Version changes
The Advanced version adds substantial tonal control compared with the standard three-knob Sally Drive, including the three-way Shift control, a more flexible Tone section, and internal low-mid/dynamic-bias trimmers. The evidence checked here does not support a complete component-level change log for every production revision. [1][2]

## Sound
Effects Database describes the Sally Drive as warm, full-bodied, natural and rounded, with a dynamic response that stays relatively transparent to the guitar and amplifier. The same documentation says it is not a high-gain overdrive and emphasizes touch sensitivity, tighter bass, clarity and a tube-like response. [1] A detailed review describes smooth, rounded and harmonically rich overdrive that works from clean drive through classic-rock territory, with stronger results at louder amplifier settings. [3]
