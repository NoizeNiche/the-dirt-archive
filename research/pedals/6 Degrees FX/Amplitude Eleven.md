## What this pedal is
The Amplitude ELEVEN is a two-channel Class A overdrive with separate rhythm/lead channels and an active two-band preamp EQ. The Blue channel uses two gain stages for lower-gain crunch, while the Red channel uses four gain stages for higher-gain saturation.

## Sound
The Blue channel is intended for lower-gain, crunchy overdrive, while the Red channel provides more saturated high-gain drive. The active preamp EQ and G.A.S. clipping selector expand the range from relatively transparent breakup through heavier, more saturated tones.

## Construction
6 Degrees FX describes the pedal as hand-wired in Vancouver, B.C., with point-to-point construction and a 2-channel Class A architecture.
