# 6 Degrees FX - Hyper Catalyst

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** 6 Degrees FX
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive / Preamp
- **Identity:** Hyper Catalyst, a 3-stage Class A JFET preamp/overdrive.

## What this pedal is
Effects Database documents Hyper Catalyst as a **3-stage Class A JFET preamp/overdrive** designed both as a standalone low-gain overdrive and as a stacking device for other drives or overdriven amplifiers. It was published in 2019.

## Controls
- **Focus:** Treble clarity / high-mid emphasis.
- **Tight:** Body and low-mid fatness.
- **Edge:** Attenuates high frequencies and reduces fizz.
- **Bias:** Overall gain/saturation.
- **Output:** Output volume.
- **Headroom switch:** Three positions, High / Medium / Low, changing clipping and compression.

## Switching / power
A surviving exact-model Reverb listing documents **true-bypass switching**, top-mounted input/output connections and operation from a **9V battery or 9V to 18V center-negative power supply**.

## Circuit / architecture
- **3-stage Class A JFET** preamp/overdrive.
- The headroom control changes the clipping/compression behavior rather than selecting a separate named circuit family.
- Exact JFET part numbers were not established in the reviewed sources.

## Sound
The manufacturer/product documentation characterizes the High-headroom mode as the most dynamic and least compressed, Medium as crunchier, and Low as more clipped and saturated. The stacking-oriented controls are intended to tighten bass, add high-mid clarity, trim fizz and push the following gain stage.

## History
- Product coverage dates to **2019**.
- Effects Database treats Hyper Catalyst as a distinct product, separate from Amplitude Eleven.
