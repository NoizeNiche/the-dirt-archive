# BSM — Spectrum Fuzz Booster

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** BSM
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Tommy Bolin-inspired fuzz/treble-booster based on the modified Sam Ash Fuzzz.

## What this pedal is
BSM describes Spectrum as a reproduction of the modified Sam Ash Fuzzz associated with Tommy Bolin. Its unusual Attack control moves continuously from treble boost toward fuzz, while the Spectrum control shifts the tonal range across almost six octaves. [1][2]

## Sound
Spectrum is designed to move smoothly between treble-boost and fuzz territory rather than behaving like a single fixed fuzz circuit. The Attack control can produce progressively dirtier textures, while the broad Spectrum control reshapes the frequency emphasis and can help produce fat, biting lead tones, crunch with guitar-volume cleanup, and harmonic-feedback textures. [1][3]
