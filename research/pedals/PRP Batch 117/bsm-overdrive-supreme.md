# BSM — Overdrive Supreme

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** BSM
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Boutique overdrive designed around Ritchie Blackmore-style tone.

## What this pedal is
BSM's Supreme was designed as a more modern overdrive approach to the company's Blackmore-oriented sound, complementing its traditional booster line. Effects Database describes it as combining the medium output behavior of an overdrive with a characteristic Blackmore-style voice. [1]

## Sound
The Supreme emphasizes a beefy low end, punchy mids and relatively uncolored treble while providing a medium-output overdrive response. Its Drive control is intended to cover different shades of Blackmore-style gain rather than extreme modern saturation. [1][2]
