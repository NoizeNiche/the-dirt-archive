# Barber Electronics — Deep Fryer

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Deep Fryer
- **Builder:** Barber Electronics
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Limited-production Barber bass overdrive with clearly documented V1 and V2 generations. V1 is single-channel; V2 adds a second independently adjustable channel. [1]

## What this pedal is
Effects Database describes Deep Fryer as a bass-oriented unit designed to cover tones from mildly compressed thickening through a full “onslaught” of sustain and drive. It was designed for cold solid-state amplifiers but also found use among guitarists. [1]

## Version changes
The V1-to-V2 change is substantive:
- V1 has one drive channel.
- V2 adds a second Volume/Fry channel and a Channel selector.
- The shared Dynamics and Tone controls remain. [1]
The archive keeps this as a genuine model generation difference, not merely a cosmetic variation.

## Sound
Barber described Deep Fryer as ranging from natural compression and fattening through very heavy sustain and drive. The bass-oriented design aims to preserve useful low-frequency body while allowing substantial saturation. [1]
A contemporary owner source also describes the early unit as smooth and amp-like, with internal low-frequency shaping. [2]
