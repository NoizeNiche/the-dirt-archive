# Barber Electronics — Deep Fryer

## What this pedal is
Deep Fryer is a bass-oriented Barber overdrive/distortion built to keep useful low-end body while moving from mild compression into heavier saturation. The original V1 is a single-channel design, while V2 adds a second channel with independent controls. [1][2]

## Colorways
- Documented examples are black.
- Some used units have been rehoused, but post-sale rehousing is not a factory variant.
- Cosmetic differences do not create versions.

## Versions

### Deep Fryer V1
- Single channel
- Volume
- Fry
- Dynamics
- Tone
- internal low-end/bass adjustment
- true bypass
- limited production [1][2]

### Deep Fryer V2
Builder/product-designated revision
- Two channels
- Volume 1 / Fry 1
- Volume 2 / Fry 2
- shared Dynamics
- shared Tone
- channel selector
- internal trim allowing low-frequency adjustment
- limited production [1]

## Factory modifications
- V2's second channel and revised controls are factory production changes.
- User rehousings, clones and aftermarket changes are excluded.

## Version changes
The V1-to-V2 change is substantive: V1 is one drive channel, while V2 adds a second independently adjustable drive channel and channel selection. V2 also documents a more explicit internal low-frequency adjustment. [1][2]

## Sound
Deep Fryer was aimed at bassists wanting anything from mild thickening to heavier sustain and drive. Historical players describe it as smooth and amp-like, while the internal bass adjustment can retain or trim low-frequency weight. [1][3]
