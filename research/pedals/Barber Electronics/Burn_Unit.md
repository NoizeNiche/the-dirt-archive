# Barber Electronics — Burn Unit

## PRP identity
- Archive parent: Burn Unit
- Builder: Barber Electronics
- Catalog types: Overdrive / Distortion
- Introduction: 2001
- Identity: One parent representing the original Burn Unit design. Burn Unit 2, Burn Unit EQ and Compact Burn Unit remain separate named products.

## What this pedal is
The original Burn Unit is a highly adjustable Barber overdrive/distortion designed around dynamics, sustain and amp-style voicing. Historical documentation describes it as capable of clean-ish boost through smooth, harmonically rich distortion, with Dynamics and Burn interacting to change compression and gain response. [1][2]

### Later full-size Burn Unit production
The design evolved into a more configurable production format with additional internal/external controls and precision-matched clipping. This remains inside Burn Unit and is not merged with separately named Burn Unit 2 or Burn Unit EQ. [1]

### Factory modifications
- Builder-authorized internal trim settings are part of the design.

## Version changes
The clearest documented historical change is the move from the original four-knob Burn Unit into later, more configurable Burn Unit-family production. The separately named Burn Unit 2 and Burn Unit EQ are intentionally kept as separate archive parents because Barber cataloged them independently. [1]

## Sound
Burn Unit moves from subtle warm overdrive and boost into smooth sustaining distortion while retaining chord definition. Dynamics changes the feel from compressed and singing toward a more open response, while Burn adds sustain and distortion for a wide usable range. [1][2]
