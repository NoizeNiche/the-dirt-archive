# Barber Electronics — Burn Unit

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Burn Unit
- **Builder:** Barber Electronics
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive / Distortion
- **Identity:** Original Barber Burn Unit family, introduced in 2001 and later evolved through additional internal/external voicing refinements; Burn Unit 2 and Burn Unit EQ remain separate archive parents. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
The original Burn Unit is a highly adjustable Barber overdrive/distortion built around control of gain, dynamics and tonal shaping. Historical documentation describes a range from relatively clean boost through smooth sustaining distortion. [1][2]

## Controls / early generation
The early four-knob generation documents:
- **VOLUME**
- **TONE**
- **DYNAMICS**
- **BURN**
- Internal bass adjustment. [1][2]

## Sound
The Burn Unit moves from warm low-gain drive toward smoother, more sustaining distortion. DYNAMICS changes compression/open response, while BURN increases gain and sustain. These control interactions define the model's core character. [1][2]
