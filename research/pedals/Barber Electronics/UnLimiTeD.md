# Barber Electronics - UnLimiTeD

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** UnLimiTeD
- **Builder:** Barber Electronics
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive
- **Identity:** Discontinued two-channel higher-gain extension of the Barber LTD family.

## What this pedal is
UnLimiTeD is a dual-channel Barber overdrive designed to provide two independently set gain and volume stages. It adds global Tone and Midrange controls plus internal Presence, Bass, and Note Shape trims, allowing the two channels to cover contrasting gain levels through one pedal.

## Sound
Each channel can be set independently from lighter breakup to higher-gain saturated tones, effectively providing two drive voices in one enclosure. Midrange sweeps from relatively flat LTD-style voicing toward thicker, more forward mids, with internal trims providing additional response shaping.
