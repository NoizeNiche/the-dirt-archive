# Barber Electronics — UnLimiTeD

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** UnLimiTeD
- **Builder:** Barber Electronics
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Higher-gain two-channel Barber overdrive developed from the LTD SR, with independent gain/volume presets and a shared Midrange control. [1][2]

## What this pedal is

Barber described the unLimiTeD as a higher-gain version of the LTD SR's relatively flat response. The design adds enough drive to reach toward Direct Drive territory while retaining the large/open response of the LTD SR Tone control. It uses two switchable channels so different gain/volume settings can be preset. [1][2]

## Controls

- **DRIVE 1 / DRIVE 2:** Independent gain settings.
- **VOLUME 1 / VOLUME 2:** Independent channel levels.
- **TONE:** Shared tonal balance.
- **MIDRANGE:** Shared midrange control.
- **Two channel footswitches:** Select the independent drive presets.
- Internal voicing trims are documented in the broader Barber family material. [2]

## Circuit lineage

- **Primary lineage:** Barber **LTD SR**.
- Barber's own description explicitly calls it a higher-gain version of the LTD SR.
- The design adds nearly as much drive as the Direct Drive while preserving the unusually wide LTD SR tone response. [1]

## Sound

The unLimiTeD is designed to cover warm edge-of-breakup through harmonically complex higher-gain crunch. The shared Midrange control lets the player sweep through different LTD-family mid voices, while the two channel presets make it practical as a two-level gain platform. [1][2]
