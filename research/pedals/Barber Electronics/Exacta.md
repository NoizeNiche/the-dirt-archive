# Barber Electronics — Exacta

## PRP identity
Builder: Barber Electronics. Type: Fuzz.

## Colorways
Purple standard production. A RAM's Head edition is documented as a circuit variation.

## Versions and version changes
Triangle / Jumbo Tonebender family voices, EQ-family switching and three-way Sludge low-end shaping. No numbered factory revision verified.

## Transistor
Exact factory transistor part number: Unknown.

## Diode
Exact factory diode part number: Unknown.

## Sound
Thick sustaining Triangle/Muff fuzz can move toward a more pronounced Tone Bender character. Sludge changes the low-end from tighter to deeper/woollier.

## Research confidence
Identity/features and colorway: High. RAM variation: Moderate. Components: Unknown.
