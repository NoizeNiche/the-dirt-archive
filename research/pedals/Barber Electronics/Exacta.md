# Barber Electronics — Exacta

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Exacta
- **Builder:** Barber Electronics
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Hand-built fuzz combining Triangle-fuzz and Jumbo Tonebender-derived voices, with a flattened-mids Triangle option and a Sludge low-end switch. [1]

## What this pedal is
Barber describes the Exacta as a placement-friendly vintage fuzz designed to work with both humbuckers and single-coils. It uses two toggle switches to access multiple classic fuzz voices and an additional Sludge switch to tailor low-end girth. [1]

## Circuit / voices
- **Triangle fuzz** voice.
- **Jumbo Tonebender** voice.
- Additional **flattened-mids Triangle** voice.
- **Sludge** switch with low-end shaping for different pickup types.
- High sustain and enhanced EQ are core design goals. [1]

## Sound
Barber describes the Exacta as a thick, sustaining fuzz whose multiple classic voices cover Triangle-style saturation and Jumbo Tonebender character. The Sludge switch adjusts the low-end “girth” for different pickup configurations. [1]
