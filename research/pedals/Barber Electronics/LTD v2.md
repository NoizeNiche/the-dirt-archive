# Barber Electronics — LTD v2

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** LTD v2
- **Builder:** Barber Electronics
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Second-generation Barber LTD overdrive with the thicker/fatter EQ character that later became one of the Gain Changer SR's selectable voices. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
LTD v2 is the thicker, more mid-present LTD generation. Barber's later Gain Changer documentation explicitly uses the LTD v2 as the reference for its thick-mid EQ position. [1]

## Controls
- **VOLUME**
- **TONE**
- **DRIVE**
- Internal voicing trims are documented in surviving LTD-family material.
- The Gain Changer manual identifies the LTD v2 response as the center position of its EQ toggle. [1][2]

## Circuit lineage
- Barber **LTD** family.
- **LTD v2** is the “fat”/thick-mid side of the LTD response that Barber later combined with LTD SR and unLimiTeD voicings. [1][2]

## Version / build-history notes
- **v2** is a genuine Barber model-generation designation.
- Surviving market documentation places production in approximately the late-2000s/early-2010s, but the reviewed sources do not establish exact production start/end dates.
- LTD SR remains a separate, flatter Special Recipe model.

## Power
- Historical LTD-family documentation specifies conventional 9V pedal power.
- Exact current draw for the specific LTD v2 is not reliably established.

## Sound
Barber describes the LTD v2 voice as a vintage, snarling/fat EQ response. Relative to LTD SR, the v2 position emphasizes more midrange/fundamental content, which later became a selectable Gain Changer SR EQ voice. [1][2]
