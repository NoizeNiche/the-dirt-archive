# Barber Electronics — Midnight Standard

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Midnight Standard
- **Builder:** Barber Electronics
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Modern Barber Bluesbreaker-style overdrive with a two-stage signal path, wide Gain range, and three-position Body and Compression controls. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
Barber describes Midnight Standard as an expanded Bluesbreaker-style overdrive. The core two-stage overdrive architecture remains intact, while the Gain, Body and Compression controls broaden the usable response for different pickups, amplifiers and gain levels. [1]

## Controls
- **VOLUME:** Overall output level.
- **TONE:** High-frequency shaping.
- **GAIN:** Drive/sustain amount.
- **BODY:** Three positions, **Deep / Stock / Thick**, changing low-end and low-mid emphasis.
- **COMPRESSION:** Three positions, **Toasty / Stock / Push**, changing clipping feel and compression response. [1][2]

## Circuit lineage
- **Bluesbreaker-style** overdrive.
- Barber states that the basic two-stage signal path is retained.
- The design deliberately avoids adding active bass/output stages, keeping the response tied to the underlying two-stage architecture. [1]

- Current draw is not published in the reviewed current product page.

## Version / build-history notes
- No numbered electronic revision is established.
- Body and Compression switching are production design features, not user modifications.

## Sound
Barber describes Gain as moving from open and articulate low gain through thicker sustaining overdrive. Body shifts the low-frequency foundation from classic/tighter through deeper to thicker low-mid emphasis, while Compression changes the feel from open Stock response toward Toasty or Push behavior. [1][2]
