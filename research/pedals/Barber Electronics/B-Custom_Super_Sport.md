# Barber Electronics — B-Custom Super Sport

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** B-Custom Super Sport
- **Builder:** Barber Electronics
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Barber's B-Custom realization of the Direct Drive Super Sport concept, with three external controls and five internal voicing trims. [1][2]

## What this pedal is

The B-Custom Super Sport is a medium/high-gain overdrive built around Barber's Super Sport voice. The manual documents external **Volume, Tone and Drive**, with five internal trimmers for **Note Shape, Harmonics, Bass, Midrange and Presence**. [1]

## Controls

### External
- **VOLUME**
- **TONE**
- **DRIVE**

### Internal
- **NOTE SHAPE**
- **HARMONICS**
- **BASS**
- **MIDRANGE**
- **PRESENCE** [1]

The manual recommends adjusting the internal trims with a very small screwdriver and describes Note Shape as a pickup-matching control. [1]

## Circuit lineage

- **Primary lineage:** Barber Direct Drive Super Sport.
- The B-Custom implementation is a Super Sport-oriented British-voiced overdrive with additional internal voicing range. [2]

## Sound

The Super Sport voice is designed around British-style amplifier breakup and can move from relatively clean drive into medium/high gain. The internal Note Shape, Harmonics, Bass, Midrange and Presence controls allow substantial changes in feel, low-end fullness and upper-frequency emphasis. [1][2]
