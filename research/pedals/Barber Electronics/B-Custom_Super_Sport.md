## What this pedal is
B-Custom Super Sport is a medium/high-gain, British-voiced overdrive derived from Barber's Direct Drive Super Sport concept. It places Volume, Tone and Drive on the outside and gives the player five internal controls for note shape, harmonics, bass, midrange and presence. [1][2]

## Version changes
Barber's documentation treats Super Sport as the B-Custom realization of the earlier Direct Drive SS concept. The B-Custom production model expanded the hidden voicing controls rather than simply reproducing the older three-knob Direct Drive SS. [1][3]

## Sound
Super Sport is voiced toward British-style amp breakup, from clean-ish pushed tones into medium/high gain. The internal note-shape control changes the amount of bass entering the overdrive stage, while harmonics, midrange and presence can move the sound from tight and focused toward rounder, thicker and more forward. [1][2]
