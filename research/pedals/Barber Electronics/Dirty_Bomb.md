## What this pedal is
Dirty Bomb is a high-gain Barber distortion with a two-band external EQ, a three-position midrange character switch and an internal bass boost adjustment. Historical Barber material describes it as a wide-range distortion that can move from smoother overdrive-like settings through scooped and thick high-gain sounds. [1][2]

## Sound
Dirty Bomb is built for large, aggressive distortion while still allowing substantial EQ control. The three-position mid switch moves the pedal between neutral, mid-forward and scooped responses, while the internal bass boost can add the low-frequency weight needed for heavier settings. [1][2]
