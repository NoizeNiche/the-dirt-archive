# Barber Electronics — Dirty Bomb

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** Dirty Bomb
- **Builder:** Barber Electronics
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** High-gain Barber distortion with Volume, Treble, Bass and Gain, a three-position midrange toggle and an internal Bass Boost adjustment. [1][2]

## What this pedal is

Dirty Bomb is designed as a wide-range high-gain distortion. The controls allow substantial tonal shaping, including a dedicated bass circuit and a three-position midrange balance switch. [1][2]

## Controls

- **VOLUME:** Overall output.
- **TREBLE:** High-frequency content.
- **BASS:** Low-frequency content.
- **GAIN:** Distortion/sustain/harmonic amount.
- **Midrange toggle:** Neutral, mid-boosted or mid-scooped positions.
- **Internal Bass Boost:** Additional factory voicing adjustment. [1][2]

## Sound

The manual describes settings ranging from classic 1970s rock through dynamic rock, 1990s-style tones, large 1980s sounds, Drop D, fat lead and very heavy/tight applications. The practical range comes from the strong Bass/Treble controls, midrange toggle and Gain control. [1]
