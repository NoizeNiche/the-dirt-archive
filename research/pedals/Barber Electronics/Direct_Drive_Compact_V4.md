# Barber Electronics — Direct Drive Compact / Direct Drive v4

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** Direct Drive Compact / Direct Drive v4
- **Builder:** Barber Electronics
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive / Distortion
- **Identity:** Compact Direct Drive incorporating voices from the Direct Drive, Direct Drive LG and Super Sport families; Barber explicitly identifies the 2020 update as **v4**. [1]

## What this pedal is

Barber says the Compact Direct Drive is 2.3 inches wide and combines the desirable sounds of the Direct Drive, Direct Drive Low Gain and Super Sport. The 2020 update added a new look, three selectable midrange levels and a third, hyper-dynamic gain voice. [1]

## Controls / switching

- Three main knobs.
- **Gain** 3-position switch in v4.
- **Harmonics** switch.
- v4 adds **three selectable midrange levels** through a three-way switch. [1]

## Circuit / construction

- Hand-built construction.
- Solder-lug connection for jacks, switches and pots rather than PCB-mounted panel parts.
- True bypass footswitch.
- The circuit covers several British amplifier-style gain voices, from elastic/saggy lower-gain response to tighter EL34-oriented distortion. [1]

## Transistor

- Exact transistor/device part numbers are not established from the reviewed factory documentation.

## Diode

- Barber's service schematic explicitly shows **1N4148 silicon switching diodes**.
- This is pedal-specific service documentation, not an inference from the circuit lineage. [2]

## Electrical specifications

Barber currently specifies:
- **9–18 VDC**
- **Negative-tip / center-negative**
- Approximately **12 mA current draw**
- **No internal battery**. [1]

## Version history

- Earlier Direct Drive family: established the parent concept.
- **v4, 2020:** new cosmetic presentation, three midrange levels, expanded gain switch with third hyper-dynamic “4 input” style sound. [1]
- The archive keeps v4 as a genuine factory generation inside this parent.

## Sound

Barber positions the Compact Direct Drive as a broad British-amp-style palette, including KT66-oriented early-UK-combo response, 4-input-head wall-of-sound response and a modified-master-volume style voice. The third v4 gain position is described as hyper-dynamic. [1]
