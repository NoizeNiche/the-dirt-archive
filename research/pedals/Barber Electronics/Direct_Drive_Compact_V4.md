## What this pedal is
The Compact Direct Drive combines core voices from the Direct Drive, Direct Drive Low Gain and Super Sport into a smaller enclosure. Barber states that the 2020 update introduced the v4 presentation, a three-level midrange switch and an expanded three-way gain switch with a third hyper-dynamic sound. [1]

## Colorways
- v4 examples include black and deep-blue production finishes.
- Color differences do not create versions.

## Versions

### Earlier Direct Drive family
The full-size Direct Drive family established Barber's British-voiced, dynamically responsive overdrive concept and later spawned Low Gain and Super Sport variants.

### Compact Direct Drive v4
Builder-designated revision: v4, updated 2020
- compact 2.3-inch-wide enclosure
- combines Direct Drive, Direct Drive LG and Super Sport voices
- three-position gain control
- three-position harmonics control
- three selectable midrange levels in v4
- third hyper-dynamic / four-input-head style gain voice
- solder-lug construction for jacks, toggles and pots
- true bypass
- 9–18V DC negative tip
- no internal battery [1]

### Service-schematic component evidence
Barber's service schematic for Compact Direct Drive v4 explicitly shows 1N4148 diodes. This is pedal-specific factory/service documentation. [2]

## Factory modifications
- The v4 changes are factory design revisions.
- Higher-voltage operation and routine internal adjustments are not user modifications.

## Version changes
Barber explicitly identifies the 2020 update as v4 and describes the changes in the gain and midrange switching. The compact model is therefore a genuine later generation within the Direct Drive parent. [1]

## Sound
Compact Direct Drive v4 is designed as a broad British-amp-style palette, moving from elastic low-gain breakup toward tighter EL34-style distortion and a higher-gain four-input-head voice. Gain and harmonics switches alter saturation and feel, while the v4 midrange switch makes the same circuit easier to fit around different amps and pickups. [1]
