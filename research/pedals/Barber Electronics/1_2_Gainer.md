# Barber Electronics — 1/2 Gainer

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** 1/2 Gainer
- **Builder:** Barber Electronics
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Dual-channel Barber low-gain overdrive derived from the LTD family, with independent Gain/Volume per channel and shared tonal controls. [1][2]

## What this pedal is

The 1/2 Gainer packages two independently adjustable low-gain Barber overdrive stages in one enclosure. Each side has its own gain and level, while the shared Tone and Midrange Character controls shape both channels. Internal trims provide additional bass, presence and note-shape adjustment. [1][2]

## Controls

- **GAIN 1 / GAIN 2:** Independent gain amount for each channel.
- **VOLUME 1 / VOLUME 2:** Independent output level for each channel.
- **TONE:** Shared high-frequency shaping.
- **MIDRANGE CHARACTER:** Shared tonal character control.
- Internal trims for note shape, bass and presence. [1][2]

## Circuit lineage

- **Primary lineage:** Barber LTD-style low-gain overdrive.
- Barber/period documentation treats the 1/2 Gainer as a dual-channel realization of this low-gain architecture rather than a separate high-gain distortion family. [1]

## Sound

The 1/2 Gainer is intended for low-gain applications from edge-of-breakup through fuller overdrive. The separate channel controls make it practical to keep two gain levels available, while the shared Midrange Character control changes the tonal emphasis of both channels. [1][2]
