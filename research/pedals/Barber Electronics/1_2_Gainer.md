## What this pedal is
The 1/2 Gainer puts two independently adjustable Barber low-gain overdrive channels into one enclosure. Each channel has its own gain and volume, while Tone and Midrange Character are shared, with internal trims for bass and presence. [1][2]

No numbered factory revision was established. Period documentation treats the 1/2 Gainer as a dual version of Barber's LTD-style low-gain architecture, with the shared Midrange Character control spanning the LTD/LTD SR tonal range. [1][2]

## Sound
The 1/2 Gainer covers subtle edge-of-breakup through authoritative low-gain grind, with the shared Midrange Character control moving from open chordal response toward a thicker single-note voice. Separate gain and volume controls give the two channels distinct drive levels for stacking or quick level changes. [1][2]
