# Barber Electronics — LTD SR

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** LTD SR
- **Builder:** Barber Electronics
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Barber's very-low-gain LTD Special Recipe with a nearly flat response and internal Bass/Presence trims for rig matching. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
The LTD SR is an extremely clear low-gain overdrive. Barber's documentation describes it as flatter than the Silver LTD when the Tone control is centered, with a strong two-way tone action and internal trims for bass and presence. [1][2]

## Controls
- **VOLUME:** Overall level.
- **TONE:** Simultaneously cuts bass/adds treble as it is turned up, and cuts treble/adds bass as it is turned down.
- **DRIVE:** Sustain and drive.
- **Internal BASS trim:** Adds body/low-end.
- **Internal PRESENCE trim:** Adds bite/clarity. [2]

## Circuit lineage
- Barber **LTD** family.
- The LTD SR is the flatter Special Recipe branch and serves as one of the source voices incorporated into the Gain Changer SR. [1][2]

## Sound
The LTD SR is intended for transparent, low-gain drive. The strong Tone control makes it possible to shift from brighter/leaner to warmer/full response, while Drive moves from light push into vintage cranked-amp-style sustain. [1][2]
