# Barber Electronics — Small Fry Burn Unit

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Small Fry Burn Unit
- **Builder:** Barber Electronics
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive / Distortion
- **Identity:** Barber Small Fry / Small Fry Burn Unit, a compact evolution of the Burn Unit with Dynamics and Burn controls, three clipping/dynamics positions and four internal voicing trims. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
The Small Fry is the later compact Burn Unit-family design. Its external controls are Volume, Tone, Dynamics and Burn, with the Dynamics/Burn relationship determining compression, sustain and clipping behavior. A three-position toggle selects stock Burn Unit behavior, a lower-gain vintage-style mode, or an asymmetrical “power tubes” style response. [1][2]

## Controls
- **VOLUME:** Overall level.
- **TONE:** Brightness.
- **DYNAMICS:** Compression and dynamic response.
- **BURN:** Sustain, drive and harmonic ratio.
- **Three-position mini-toggle:** Stock Burn Unit / vintage lower-gain / asymmetrical “power tubes” behavior. [1][2]

### Internal
- **NOTE SHAPE:** Bass fed into the overdrive stage.
- **BASS**
- **MIDRANGE**
- **PRESENCE**
These trims are used to match the circuit to the player's guitar and amplifier. [1][2]

## Circuit lineage
- Later generation of the **Barber Burn Unit** family.
- Barber's own documentation treats the Small Fry as a refined compact Burn Unit architecture rather than a completely unrelated design. [1]

## Sound
The Small Fry spans warm low-gain drive, smooth singing overdrive and more aggressive distortion. The Dynamics control changes compression/attack behavior, while Burn increases sustain and drive. The three-position toggle lets the same control set move between the stock Burn Unit character, lower-gain vintage response and asymmetrical “power tubes” clipping. [1][2]
