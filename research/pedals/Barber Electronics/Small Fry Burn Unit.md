## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Small Fry Burn Unit

## What this pedal is
The Small Fry is a Barber overdrive/distortion designed around a proprietary clipping and recovery approach. It combines a continuously variable Dynamics control with three clipping-symmetry choices and internal Note Shape, Bass, Midrange, and Presence trims.

## Sound
Small Fry is designed to retain strong note definition through increasing gain, with settings ranging from semi-clean boost into smooth singing overdrive and deeper, raspier distortion. The Dynamics control changes the transition and feel of the distortion, while Note Shape and the internal EQ trims move the response from tight and focused toward bigger, rounder tones.
