# Barber Electronics — FWW BUSS

## PRP identity
Builder: Barber Electronics / Five Watt World. Type: Distortion / Overdrive.

## Colorways
White, with black also documented.

## Versions and version changes
Burn Unit / Direct Drive Super Sport dual voice. Volume, Tone, Drive, Super Sport/Burn Unit toggle and three-way harmonics/dynamics. No numbered revision verified.

## Transistor
Exact device: Unknown.

## Diode
Exact type: Unknown.

## Sound
Super Sport gives British/tweed-style drive; Burn Unit is thicker and Dumble-like. The harmonics/dynamics switch changes feel and harmonic density.

High for identity/features and colorways. Components remain unknown.
