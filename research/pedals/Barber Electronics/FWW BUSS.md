# Barber Electronics — FWW BUSS

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** FWW BUSS
- **Builder:** Barber Electronics / Five Watt World
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Dual-voice overdrive combining Barber Super Sport and Burn Unit circuits, developed with Five Watt World around two historic amp-tone references. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
The B.U.S.S is a compact dual-tone overdrive. Barber says it derives from the Direct Drive Super Sport and Burn Unit circuits; Five Watt World describes the two positions as its preferred classic tones, associated with a JTM-45 “Beano” sound and Dumble Overdrive Special #39. [1][2]

## Controls / switching
- Main **Volume, Tone and Drive** controls.
- **Left toggle:** Super Sport / Burn Unit mode.
- **Right three-way switch:** Harmonics / Dynamics range.
- White production finish documented by Barber. [1]

## Circuit lineage
- **Super Sport** circuit on one side.
- **Burn Unit** circuit on the other.
- Historic tone references are part of the collaborative product description, not a claim that the pedal is a schematic duplicate of those amplifiers. [1][2]

## Version / build-history notes

- **FWW BUSS HG** is a separate higher-gain model and is not treated as a cosmetic revision of BUSS.
- No numbered electronic revision established for the original BUSS.

## Sound
The Super Sport position is described as classic British/high-powered tweed oriented; the Burn Unit position is louder and punchier with thicker Dumble-like response. The Harmonics/Dynamics switch allows the player to adapt the response to guitar output and amplifier. [1][2]
