# Barber Electronics — Gain Changer SR

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** Gain Changer SR
- **Builder:** Barber Electronics
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Compact Barber overdrive combining the low-gain LTD/LTD SR family with the higher-gain unLimiTeD voice, plus three EQ settings. [1][2]

## What this pedal is

The Gain-Changer SR was designed as a compact “many Barber drives in one” platform. Barber explicitly says it incorporates sounds from the **LTD, LTD SR and unLimiTeD** families. [1][2]

## Controls / switching

- **VOLUME:** Output level.
- **TONE:** Equalization.
- **GAIN:** Sustain/drive amount.
- **EQ toggle:** LTD SR flat EQ / LTD v2 thick-mid EQ / “flat+fat” EQ.
- **Gain toggle:** LTD-style low gain / unLimiTeD medium-high gain. [1][2]

The manual notes that the “flat+fat” position stays nearly flat while adding fundamentals intended to keep the guitar in the mix. It also notes that the Gain control behaves differently depending on the gain-toggle position. [2]

## Circuit lineage

- **LTD SR**
- **LTD v2**
- **unLimiTeD**
The pedal combines Barber's own established voicings rather than being a clone of an unrelated commercial circuit. [1][2]

## Construction / hardware

- Compact **2.3-inch-wide** enclosure.
- Hand-built construction.
- Solder-lug connections for jacks, toggles and pots.
- True bypass.
- Barber states that the construction is intended to keep serviceable panel components off the PCB. [1]

## Power

Barber specifies:
- **9–18 VDC**
- **Negative tip / center-negative**
- Approximately **12 mA current draw**
- **No internal battery**. [1]

## Version / build-history notes

- No numbered revision beyond the SR model designation is established.
- The defining design feature is the combination of LTD/LTD SR/unLimiTeD voices in the compact enclosure.

## Sound

Barber describes the low-gain side as sweet and responsive and the unLimiTeD side as harmonically charged with singing sustain. The EQ switch moves between flatter LTD SR response, thicker LTD v2 voicing and a flat-plus-fat hybrid. [1][2]
