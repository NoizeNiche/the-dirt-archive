# Barber Electronics — Gain Changer SR

## PRP identity
Builder: Barber Electronics. Type: Distortion / Overdrive.

## Colorways
Olive documented.

## Versions and version changes
Compact model combining LTD, LTD SR and unLimiTeD-derived voices. Volume, Tone, Gain, EQ flat/fat/flat+fat and LTD/unLimiTeD gain switching. No numbered revision verified.

## Transistor
Exact active device: Unknown.

## Diode
Exact type: Unknown.

## Sound
Low-gain mode is sweet and responsive; higher gain adds harmonic density and sustain. EQ switching moves from flat to fat or flat-plus-fat.

High for identity/features and colorway. Components remain unknown.
