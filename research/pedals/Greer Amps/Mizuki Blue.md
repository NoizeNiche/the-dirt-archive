## What this pedal is
Mizuki Blue is a dual-JFET overdrive designed by Greer Amps around the feel and dynamics of two cascading tube gain stages. Greer describes it as a responsive drive that can move from a clear boost into richer overdrive and can stack with other gain pedals.

## Versions and factory options
- Three-knob layout.
- Dual-JFET gain stages.
- 9-18V negative-center operation or 9V battery.
- Current draw: 5mA at 9V.
- Internal trim pots set the JFET bias and are not intended for casual adjustment.

## Sound
Mizuki Blue is designed for touch-sensitive, tube-stage-like overdrive behavior. It can provide a clean-ish boost, fat low-gain grunt, or richer saturated drive, and Greer specifically notes its usefulness for stacking.
