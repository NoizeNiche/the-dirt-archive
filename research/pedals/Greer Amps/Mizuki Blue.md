## Sound
Mizuki (noun, Japanese origin) —A term meaning “beautiful moon.” Mizuki Blue is a dual-JFET overdrive pedal designed to replicate the feel and dynamics of two cascading tube gain stages.
JFET transistors were created to emulate the warm, responsive character of tubes in a solid-state format, and Mizuki Blue puts them to work—one stage driving the other into rich, chewy overdrive.
From fat vintage grunt to a clear, musical boost, this circuit covers a wide tonal spectrum.
