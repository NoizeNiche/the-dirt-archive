## What this pedal is

The Sucker Punch is a discontinued Greer high-gain distortion designed around a JFET preamp driving a 386D chip amplifier section. Greer positioned it as a high-gain pedal capable of early JCM-style amplifier distortion.

## Sound

The Sucker Punch is a thick, high-gain distortion with a wide Tone sweep from fat to cutting. It is associated with early Marshall/JCM-style gain while retaining enough range for lower-gain breakup.
