## What this pedal is

The Supa Cobra is an analog op-amp overdrive with switchable clipping and a dedicated Body control. Greer describes it as touch-sensitive, with natural amp-like breakup and a range from medium-gain overdrive into crunch.

## Sound

FAT mode produces a softer, thicker clipping response. LED mode is stiffer and crunchier, while the center Open position exposes the op-amp's natural drive. The Body control changes low-end presence and also increases gain as it is turned up.
