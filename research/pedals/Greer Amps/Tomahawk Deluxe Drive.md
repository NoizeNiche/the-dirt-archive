## What this pedal is

The Tomahawk Deluxe Drive is a medium-gain, second-stage overdrive with a deliberate EQ shift toward the tonal range of large tweed amplifiers.

## Versions and factory options

- 9-18V negative-center operation.
- Current draw: 9mA at 9V.
- Greer describes a purposeful EQ shift toward tweed-style response.
- At 9V the circuit compresses somewhat; at 18V it provides increased headroom and a stiffer response.
