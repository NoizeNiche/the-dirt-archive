## What this pedal is
The Tarpit is Greer's integrated-circuit Muff-style fuzz. Greer based it on a vintage Big Muff variant that uses two ICs rather than the standard four-transistor topology.

## Sound
With lower Sustain settings, the Tarpit can move toward darker, spitty, lower-gain textures. Higher settings provide the sustained, aggressive character associated with Greer's IC-based Muff design.
