## What this pedal is

The Southland Harmonic Overdrive is a higher-gain sibling to the Lightspeed Organic Overdrive. Greer says it uses the Lightspeed platform as a starting point, then increases gain, shifts the voicing toward the mids and presence range, and uses two clipping stages with different diode types.

## Versions and factory options

- True bypass.
- 2.1 mm negative-center power connection.

## Sound

The Southland provides mild-to-medium overdrive and crunch with more gain than the Lightspeed, a focused midrange, extended presence, thick harmonics, and strong touch sensitivity.
