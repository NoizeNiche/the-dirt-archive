## What this pedal is
The Ghetto Stomp is a low-to-medium gain overdrive built around transistor gain stages and designed to feel like natural amplifier breakup.

## Sound
The Ghetto Stomp emphasizes natural-feeling breakup and touch-sensitive response, moving from relatively clean edge-of-breakup tones into thicker overdrive as the drive control increases.
