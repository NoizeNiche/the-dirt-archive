## What this pedal is
The Black Fuzz is a simple, one-control fuzz designed around an overdriven amplifier character with additional fuzz texture. Greer reissued the design for its **18th Anniversary** as a limited edition, describing it as the classic circuit that started the company.

## Sound
Greer describes the Black Fuzz as combining the character of a pushed Fender Bassman-style amplifier with a small amount of fuzz. The single volume control leaves the guitar's volume and playing dynamics as major parts of the usable range.
