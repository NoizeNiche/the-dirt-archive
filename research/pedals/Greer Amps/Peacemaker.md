## Sound
What happens when you run one old school transistor based gain stage into another one, and use them as a drive-meets-distortion!?
These are fun pedals that can do a slight "vintage feeling and sounding" eq shift with boost capabilities, or they can push into drive meets distortion territory.
The sound is very much a transistor drive-distortion sound, and has a very unique and fun character to it.
