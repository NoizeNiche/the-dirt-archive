## What this pedal is
The Super Hornet is a silicon fuzz based on a vintage Big Muff variant that uses two integrated circuits rather than the classic four-transistor arrangement. Greer added additional tone-shaping options.

## Colorways
- Documented examples include a blue metallic enclosure.
- No complete factory colorway chronology was established.

## Versions and factory options
- Deep tone control for boosted bass.
- Tone-shaping options for different midrange responses.
- Two-IC Muff-style fuzz architecture.
- True bypass.
- 9V battery or standard 9V center-negative supply.
- Hand-wired production in Athens, Georgia.
