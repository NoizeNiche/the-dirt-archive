# 1981 Inventions — DRV2

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** DRV2
- **Builder:** 1981 Inventions
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** V2 circuit and standard DRV2 enclosure used for the later production generation.

## What this pedal is
DRV2 is the redesigned DRV circuit that 1981 Inventions describes as its new standard enclosure and circuit. The builder says it was redesigned by John Snyder of Electronic Audio Experiments after earlier engineering by Jon Ashley, with the goals of lower noise, greater efficiency and 9V/18V operation. [1]

## Colorways
- Official examples include **white**, **blue**, **indigo**, and Hyperfade-style finishes.
- Finish changes are not treated as circuit revisions unless the builder identifies one.

## Versions and factory options
### DRV2 V2 circuit
- redesigned V2 circuit
- lower noise than earlier DRV
- more efficient operation
- 9V or 18V operation
- internal true-bypass selection
- current standard enclosure [1]

### DRV2 No3
The current No3 product presentation is a production finish/version within the DRV2 family, not a new circuit generation. [1]

## Version changes
The important change is the move from the original DRV to the redesigned V2 circuit. Later No3 and finish-specific listings are retained under the DRV2 parent.

## Sound
DRV2 is designed to be quieter and clearer while retaining the DRV's broad range. 1981 Inventions specifically highlights barely-clipping clean sounds at the low-gain end, conventional rock drive in the middle, and very heavy settings approaching fuzz. [1]
