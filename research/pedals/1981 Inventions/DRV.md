## What this pedal is
The DRV is a low-to-medium gain overdrive/distortion pedal with a fixed preamp section and a RAT-inspired design direction. 1981 Inventions describes the DRV as its mainstay distortion pedal, while historical reporting identifies a 1985 Whiteface ProCo RAT as an important starting point for the design.

## Colorways
- 1981 Inventions has released the DRV in numerous small-batch finishes and special editions.
- A complete chronological colorway list is not asserted here.

## Versions and factory options
- **DRV:** original model.
- Separate modified editions such as **DRV2** and **DRV MOD** are treated as distinct records where documented.
- Original DRV controls: **DRV**, **CUT**, and **VOL**, plus an on/off footswitch and LED.

## Version changes
- The archive does not merge DRV2 or DRV MOD component changes into the original DRV record.
- No complete production revision chronology was established for the original run.

## Transistor
- The checked sources describe an op-amp-based distortion architecture rather than a single-transistor fuzz topology.
- **Exact production transistor/device:** Unknown.
## Diode
- LED indicator is documented, but the exact clipping-device part for the original DRV was not established in the checked sources.
- **Exact clipping part:** Unknown.

## Sound
The DRV covers clean boost, low-gain overdrive, and more aggressive distortion. The DRV and CUT controls interact closely, with the CUT control shaping the high-frequency content and the DRV control increasing gain and perceived low end.
