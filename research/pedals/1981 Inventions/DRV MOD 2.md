# 1981 Inventions — DRV MOD 2

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** DRV MOD 2
- **Builder:** 1981 Inventions
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** MOD 2 DRV circuit with the larger input capacitor and hybrid clipping section documented by 1981 Inventions.

## What this pedal is
MOD 2 keeps the DRV concept but deliberately changes the low end and clipping section. 1981 Inventions documents a considerably larger input capacitor for more low end and a hybrid clipping section using BAT41 and LED clipping together. [1]

## Colorways
- A **black enclosure with bright teal printing** is officially documented.
- Other MOD 2 finishes may exist, but color is treated as cosmetic unless the builder identifies a circuit change.

## Versions and factory options
### DRV MOD 2
- larger input capacitor for more low end
- hybrid BAT41 Schottky + LED clipping
- internal true-bypass selection switch
- 9–18V operation
- hand assembled in Nashville [1]

## Version changes
MOD 2 is a genuine circuit change from the original DRV: more low end and a different clipping section. The hybrid BAT41 + LED combination is specifically identified by the builder.

## Transistor
- **Technology:** Op-amp based distortion circuit; exact active devices are not fully documented.
- **Exact transistor/device:** Not publicly documented.

## Diode
- **Type:** **BAT41 Schottky + LED**, confirmed by 1981 Inventions. [1]
- **Exact LED part:** Unknown.

## Sound
MOD 2 is bigger and more aggressive than the standard DRV, with noticeably more low-end punch and a louder, warmer feel. The BAT41 plus LED clipping combination adds punch and extra midrange grit while the open CUT setting allows the added low end to come through strongly. [1]
