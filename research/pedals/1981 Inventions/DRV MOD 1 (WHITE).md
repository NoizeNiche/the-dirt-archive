# 1981 Inventions — DRV MOD 1 (WHITE)

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** DRV MOD 1 (WHITE)
- **Builder:** 1981 Inventions
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** White-finish catalog entry for the DRV MOD 1 V1 circuit.

## What this pedal is
This is the white production finish of the DRV MOD 1. 1981 Inventions describes the current white version as the original V1 through-hole circuit, with a Burr Brown OPA2134 op amp, socketed components, zero-gain mode and the original circuit board. [1]

## Colorways
- **White** finish documented by the builder.
- This entry is a finish-specific catalog record, not a separate circuit generation.

## Versions and factory options
- V1 through-hole circuit
- Burr Brown OPA2134 op amp
- original op amp supplied for swapping
- zero-gain mode
- aluminum enclosure
- standard 9V power [1]

## Version changes
No additional circuit change is documented for the white finish. It is retained as the white catalog presentation of MOD 1.

## Sound
The white MOD 1 has the warmer, thicker and clearer V1 character described by 1981 Inventions, with the zero-gain setting providing a particularly thick, dark preamp/boost sound. Turning the DRV control up restores the normal distortion path while keeping the V1 voice. [1]
