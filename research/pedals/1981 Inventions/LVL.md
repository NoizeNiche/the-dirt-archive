# 1981 Inventions — LVL

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** LVL
- **Builder:** 1981 Inventions
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Standard LVL full-range low-gain overdrive.

## What this pedal is
LVL is 1981 Inventions' full-range overdrive for guitar, bass and other electronic instruments. The builder describes it as a subtle gain device built around light clipping and op-amp push, designed to stack well and to cover a broad range without turning into a conventional high-gain drive. [1][2]

## Colorways
- The builder describes a **black satin** standard finish.
- Limited editions such as Forget and Not Slow Down LVL are treated as separate cataloged finishes/editions.

## Versions and factory options
### Standard LVL
- LVL and VOL controls
- full-range operation
- low-gain overdrive
- light-clipping + op-amp push design
- standard 9V operation
- suitable for guitar, bass and electronic instruments [1][2]

## Version changes
The core LVL design is retained across standard and commemorative versions. Cosmetic editions are kept separate in the catalog when they were separately listed.

## Sound
LVL is voiced for light, controlled breakup and works especially well as a stage-setting overdrive or stacking pedal. It can add gain without heavily changing the character of a rig and stays full-range enough to be useful with bass and other instruments. [1][2]
