# Coolpedals - Strutter

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Coolpedals
- **Catalog identity:** Strutter
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Coolpedals Strutter, a German-made overdrive pedal documented by Effects Database and surviving Reverb listings.

## What this pedal is
Effects Database catalogs the Strutter as an overdrive pedal and published the model record July 15, 2009. [1]
Surviving Reverb listings describe it as a Coolpedals pedal aimed particularly at blues sounds. [2]

## Controls
The documented external control set is:
- **Volume**
- **Gain**
- **Tone** [2]

A separate internal gain-boost adjustment is documented inside the pedal. [2]

## Colorways
- A surviving Reverb example is identified as yellow. [2]
