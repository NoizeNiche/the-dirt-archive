## What this pedal is
Effects Database lists the Coolpedals Steam Machine as a distortion/overdrive pedal, published July 15, 2009. [1]
The same exact-model page includes a later video entry titled “Steam Machine - germanium overdrive,” providing model-linked evidence for a germanium overdrive implementation. [1]
