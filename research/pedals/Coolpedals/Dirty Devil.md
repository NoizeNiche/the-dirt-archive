# Coolpedals - Dirty Devil

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Coolpedals
- **Catalog identity:** Dirty Devil
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive
- **Identity:** Handmade German Coolpedals boost/overdrive/distortion pedal documented in a 2008 contemporary review and later catalog sources.

## What this pedal is
A contemporary GuitarWorld.de review identifies Andreas Ziegler of Coolpedals as the maker and describes the Dirty Devil as a boost plus overdrive/distortion pedal, with three selectable distortion modes and three voicings. [1]
Audiofanzine independently catalogs the Dirty Devil as a Coolpedals saturation effect and notes the 2008 introduction as a versatile overdrive/distortion pedal. [2]

## Controls
The documented external controls are:
- **Boost**
- **Volume**
- **EQ**
- **Gain** [1]

Two three-position toggle switches are documented:
- **Mode:** TS / NT / DS
- **Voicing:** Classic / Turbo / Devil [1]

Two footswitches are documented:
- **Boost**
- **On/Off** [1]

## Colorways
- A contemporary review describes a red enclosure with a small devil graphic. [1]
- Finish differences are treated as cosmetic unless direct evidence establishes a revision.

## Versions and factory options
- Handmade in Germany by Andreas Ziegler. [1]
- True bypass when the effect sections are bypassed. [1]
- A second-gain-stage version without the booster was available on request according to the contemporary review. [1]
- 9V battery or power supply operation is documented in a surviving market listing. [3]

The pedal was handmade by Coolpedals in Germany. The checked sources do not provide a complete component-level schematic, so the archive does not infer transistor or clipping-device specifics. [1][2]

## Sound
The three Mode positions provide TS, NT (No Teeth), and DS (Distortion) voicings, with the additional Voicing selector providing Classic, Turbo, and Devil settings. The contemporary review describes the range as extending from clean-ish boost through crunch and into heavier distortion. [1]
