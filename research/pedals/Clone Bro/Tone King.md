# Clone Bro — Tone King

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** Tone King
- **Builder:** Clone Bro
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Clone Bro Tone King, a dual-channel overdrive with independent controls and selectable clipping.

## What this pedal is

Effects Database describes Tone King as two independent overdrive circuits in one enclosure. Channel 1 has a higher-gain drive sweep, while Channel 2 covers clean through overdrive. The two channels can be combined for a larger gain structure. [1]

## Colorways

- The documented product used separate **bright blue and white LEDs** for the two switching functions.
- No complete enclosure colorway sequence was established. [1]

## Versions and factory options

Documented controls/features:
- Independent Drive, Tone, and Level controls for each channel.
- Independent tone/“phat” switch for each channel.
- Internal DIP switches with three clipping options.
- True bypass.
- 9V battery clip.
- Neutrik jacks.
- JRC4580 op-amp.
- 1S188 and MA856 clipping diodes. [1]

## Version changes

No formal numbered factory revision sequence was established.

## Transistor

The gain stages are documented around a **JRC4580** op-amp. [1]

## Diode

The exact model-specific source names **1S188** and **MA856** clipping diodes. [1]

## Sound

Channel 1 is voiced for a grittier clean-to-medium-drive range; Channel 2 covers clean through overdrive. Running both channels together increases the overall drive range. The independent phat switches and internal clipping selections provide further voicing changes. [1]

## Historical note

Effects Database lists Tone King alongside Taurus as one of Clone Bro's first cataloged pedals in 2011. [2]
