# Brunetti — Mercury Box 2

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Brunetti
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** Three-channel evolution of the Mercury Box.

## What this pedal is
Mercury Box 2 is the 2014 evolution of the Mercury Box. It adds a dedicated solo channel with its own Gain and Volume controls and a Mid EQ section, creating a three-channel system while retaining the Mercury family's clear-to-liquid high-gain range. [1]

- 9V DC maximum
- 500 kOhm input impedance
- 25 kOhm output impedance
- 30mA maximum current absorption
- WIMA capacitors and sealed relay switching [1]

## Version changes
The defining change from Mercury Box is the added dedicated solo channel with independent Gain/Volume and Mid controls. [1]

## Sound
Mercury Box 2 retains the Mercury family's defined crunch and smooth hard-rock distortion while adding a dedicated solo voice that can be shaped independently. Brunetti describes the higher-gain range as smooth and penetrating while remaining respectful of the instrument's timbre. [1]
