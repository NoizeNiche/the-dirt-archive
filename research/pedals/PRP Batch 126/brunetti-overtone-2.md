# Brunetti — Overtone 2

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Brunetti
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Expanded all-tube Overtone preamp pedal.

## What this pedal is
Overtone 2 is an all-tube pedal with 2+1 channels. Brunetti describes it as capable of warm and fat tones through aggressive metal sounds and as usable either as a preamp into a power amp, as a speaker-emulated direct recording source, or with both functions simultaneously. [1]

## Sound
The Overtone 2 is designed to cover a broad preamp range, from warm/fat clean or driven colors through aggressive metal tones. Its ability to feed a power amp or go directly to recording gear makes it more of a complete preamp/effect unit than a simple overdrive stompbox. [1]
