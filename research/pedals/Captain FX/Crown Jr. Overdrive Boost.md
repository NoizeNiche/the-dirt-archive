## What this pedal is

Captain FX's surviving product listing identifies the Crown Jr. as an **upgraded single channel of the Analogman King of Tone**. The same listing describes the King of Tone lineage as a modified Marshall Bluesbreaker and says the Crown Jr. is intended to work both as a transparent stand-alone overdrive and as a boost into an already overdriven amplifier.

The manufacturer-era listing also describes the pedal as completely handmade and specifies boutique-grade hardware components including Alpha potentiometers and stomp switch, Panasonic/Nichicon/JB capacitors, and Neutrik/Lumberg/Rean jacks. These details are product-page claims and are retained as such.

## Controls / circuit

- **External Presence:** The Captain FX listing explicitly documents an external Presence control, added to widen the usable range of the tone control.
- **Circuit basis:** The listing explicitly identifies the Crown Jr. as a single channel of the Analogman King of Tone, itself described there as a modified Marshall Bluesbreaker.
- The surviving source does not disclose a complete schematic, exact op-amp/transistor/diode part numbers, or a complete control list.
- **Exact transistor/device:** Unknown.
- **Exact clipping/diode device:** Unknown.

Analog Man's own King of Tone documentation confirms that the King of Tone is a Bluesbreaker-derived overdrive and documents its internal Presence adjustment and selectable clean/overdrive/distortion modes. Those King of Tone details are circuit-family context, not proof that every internal feature is present in the Crown Jr.

## Sound / use

The Captain FX listing describes the Crown Jr. as **really transparent**, suitable either as a stand-alone unit or for boosting the front end of an overdriven amplifier, with substantial boost available. The manufacturer also says the external Presence control broadens the tone-control range.

The archive does not add a more specific tonal description beyond those model-specific claims because the surviving exact-model evidence does not support one.
