# Arcane Analog - TONE BENDERS (MK1 // MK1.5 // MK2 // MK3 // MK4)

## What this pedal is
This Arcane Analog catalog entry covers a family of old-school Tone Bender builds spanning MK1, MK1.5, MK2, MK3, and MK4 configurations. The builder documents custom tuning, bias options, and selected germanium transistor sets, with detailed public descriptions for the MK1, MK1.5, and MK2 variants. [1]

## Colorways
- Custom powder-coated enclosures with one-of-a-kind etched plates are documented. [1]
- Individual builds can use different finishes.

## Versions and factory options
- MK1
- MK1.5
- MK2
- MK3
- MK4
- Optional external Bias
- Selected OC75 or AC125 germanium transistors
- OC76 option for a Vox-spec MK1.5
- Tagboard or veroboard construction
- True bypass
- Custom component choices by request [1]

## Version changes
This is a family listing rather than one fixed revision. Arcane Analog differentiates MK1, MK1.5, and MK2 behavior and component choices, while MK3 and MK4 are included in the family listing without separate detailed production histories on the reviewed page. [1]

## Transistor
- NOS trio of germanium transistors at family level.
- Carefully selected **OC75** or **AC125** options are documented.
- **OC76** is documented for a Vox-spec MK1.5 option.
- Other alternatives are available by request. [1]

## Diode
- Exact clipping diode/device specifications are not publicly documented on the reviewed family page.

## Sound
Arcane Analog describes MK1 as configurable from quirky vintage gating to higher-sustain tuning, MK1.5 as chunky and woolly with added bite, and MK2 as a high-sustain classic fuzz. The reviewed public description does not provide enough model-specific detail to characterize MK3 or MK4 beyond their inclusion in the family. [1]
