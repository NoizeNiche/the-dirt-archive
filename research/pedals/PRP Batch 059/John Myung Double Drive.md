# Ashdown Engineering - John Myung Double Drive

## What this pedal is
The John Myung Double Drive is a dual-channel bass distortion pedal developed by Ashdown Engineering with John Myung. Drive 1 provides harmonic distortion with Gain, Loud, and Tone controls, while Drive 2 is a vintage-style fuzz/distortion stage with Grit and Burn controls; the two channels can also be stacked. [1][2]

## Colorways
- Black enclosure with dual VU meters and John Myung signature graphics is documented in Ashdown's product photography. [1]

## Versions and factory options
- Drive 1: Gain, Loud, Tone
- Drive 2: Grit, Burn
- Two independent drive footswitches
- Ground Lift
- Transformer-isolated balanced Pro DI output
- Dual VU meters for input/output monitoring
- 9-18V center-negative external power
- 60 x 180 x 120 mm; 1.2 kg [1]

## Version changes
No separate numbered production revision was established in the reviewed current product documentation. The pedal is the John Myung signature dual-drive design introduced through Ashdown's collaboration with him. [1][2]

## Sound
Drive 1 is voiced for harmonic, tube-like breakup that can move from subtle warmth to a stronger edge. Drive 2 supplies a vintage-style fuzz voice with longer sustain and higher gain, and stacking the two channels expands the available distortion textures. [1][2]
