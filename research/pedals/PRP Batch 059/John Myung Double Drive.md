# Ashdown Engineering — John Myung Double Drive

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** John Myung Double Drive
- **Builder:** Ashdown Engineering
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Fuzz
- **Identity:** A dual-channel bass drive pedal developed with John Myung, with independently switchable Drive 1 and Drive 2 sections that can also be stacked. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
The John Myung Double Drive combines two distinct dirt circuits in a single bass-focused pedal. Drive 1 is the harmonic distortion section with Gain, Loud and Tone controls. Drive 2 is a vintage-style fuzz/distortion section with Grit and Burn controls. Both sections can operate independently or together. [1][2]

Ashdown's documentation also makes the pedal a practical stage/studio device through dual VU metering, balanced transformer-isolated DI, and a ground-lift switch. [1]

## Controls / operation
### Drive 1
- **GAIN:** Amount of distortion.
- **LOUD:** Output level. At minimum/off, Drive 1 produces no audible output.
- **TONE:** More gives a treble-forward sound; less gives a bassier response. [1]

### Drive 2
- **GRIT:** Amount of distortion.
- **BURN:** Output level. At minimum/off, Drive 2 produces no audible output. [1]

### Switching / monitoring
- **DRIVE 1 footswitch**
- **DRIVE 2 footswitch**
- Both may be engaged simultaneously to stack the two drive circuits.
- **Dual VU meters** monitor input and output levels. [1]

### Ground lift
- A **GROUND LIFT** switch is provided to address unwanted hum/buzz from noisy mains environments. [1]

## Circuit / signal-path character
Ashdown describes Drive 1 as a harmonic distortion stage and Drive 2 as a vintage-style fuzz/distortion stage. A Guitar World review describes Drive 1 as delivering substantial sustain with the low end preserved, while lowering Loud brought forward prominent mids; these are reviewer observations rather than circuit-level component claims. [2]

## Connectivity / hardware
Ashdown documentation specifies:
- 1/4-inch instrument input and output.
- **Balanced transformer-isolated XLR DI** output.
- Ground lift.
- Dual VU meters.
- The user guide specifies physical dimensions of approximately **180 × 120 × 60 mm** and a weight around **1.28 kg**. [1]
- Power supply: **9–18 V external, center-negative**. [2]

## Version / build-history notes
- No separate numbered production revision was established in the reviewed material.
- This is a John Myung signature design developed with Ashdown and presented as a dual-drive architecture rather than a single distortion circuit. [2][3]

## Sound
Ashdown positions the two channels as complementary dirt sections. Drive 1 moves from warmth/grit into stronger harmonic distortion; Drive 2 supplies fuzzier, vintage-oriented saturation. Stacking them increases the total gain and lets the user combine the two voicings. [1][2]
