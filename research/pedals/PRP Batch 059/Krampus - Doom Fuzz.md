# Astral ToneWorx - Krampus - Doom Fuzz

## What this pedal is
Krampus is a handmade doom-oriented fuzz from Astral ToneWorx, inspired by the darker side of Colorsound-style fuzz. The current documented version uses a single large Level control plus a voice toggle, with a Bass setting intended to extend the pedal's usefulness for bass as well as guitar. [1]

## Colorways
- White enclosure with black/red graphics is documented in current builder photography. [1][2]

## Versions and factory options
- Level
- Voice toggle
- Guitar / Bass voice selection
- True bypass
- Standard Boss-type center-negative supply
- 9V battery support
- Handmade Canadian construction [1]

## Sound
Krampus is aimed at heavy, down-tuned, floor-shaking fuzz with a deliberately darker and less fizzy top end than many conventional fuzz boxes. The builder describes the Bass voice as preserving the pedal's heavy character while adapting it for lower-frequency instruments. [1]
