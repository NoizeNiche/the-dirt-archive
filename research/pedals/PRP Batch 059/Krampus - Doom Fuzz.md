# Astral ToneWorx — Krampus - Doom Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Krampus - Doom Fuzz
- **Builder:** Astral ToneWorx
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Handmade doom-oriented fuzz with a deliberately simple current version: one large control and a voice toggle, including a Bass position. The builder cites the Colorsound fuzz tradition as an inspiration. [1]

## What this pedal is
Astral ToneWorx describes the current Krampus as a no-frills, high-impact fuzz designed for down-tuned, heavy playing. The builder characterizes it as darker and heavier than many conventional fuzz boxes because it reduces the amount of fizz associated with fuzz. [1]

## Controls
- **Big knob:** Primary level/drive control for the current simplified version.
- **Voice toggle:** Selects the alternative voice.
- **Bass position:** Intended for bass and emphasizes the pedal's low-frequency character. [1]

## Sound
The builder describes the Krampus as a heavy, down-tuned fuzz with substantial low-frequency impact. The Bass voice is designed to extend that character to bass, while the overall circuit intentionally reduces the fizzy top-end quality associated with some fuzz designs. [1]
A verified 2026 user review on the builder's current page describes the pedal as very large and heavy in output while remaining tight; this is retained only as a user report, not as a measured engineering claim. [1]
