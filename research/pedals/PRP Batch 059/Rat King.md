# Asheville Guitar Pedals — Rat King

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** Rat King
- **Builder:** Asheville Guitar Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** RAT-family distortion/fuzz reinterpretation using slow-slew op-amps and germanium clipping diodes to produce a smoother and somewhat fuzzier response than a conventional RAT 2. [1][2]

## What this pedal is

The Rat King is Asheville Guitar Pedals' take on the Pro Co RAT circuit. The builder states that it uses slow-slew op-amps and germanium diodes, with the slower op-amp response intended to evoke tube-amp-like processing and the germanium clipping giving a fuller, smoother and fuzzier result. [1][2]

## Sound

The builder describes the Rat King as a smoother, fuller, more fuzz-oriented interpretation of a RAT-style circuit. The slow-slew op-amp is intended to produce slower signal processing more reminiscent of tube behavior, while the germanium diodes increase fullness and fuzziness compared with a regular RAT 2. [1][2]
