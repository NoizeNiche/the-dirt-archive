# Asheville Guitar Pedals - Rat King

## What this pedal is
Rat King is Asheville Guitar Pedals' take on the Pro Co RAT-style circuit. The builder describes using a slow-slew op-amp approach for a more tube-like signal response and germanium diodes for a fuller and smoother, more fuzzy character than a standard RAT 2. [1]

## Colorways
- White enclosure is documented on a 2025 exact-model example.
- Black and gold finishes are also documented on recent Rat King examples. [2][3]

## Versions and factory options
- Volume
- Gain
- Filter
- Slow-slew op-amp implementation
- Germanium clipping diodes
- Standard 9V DC operation
- Custom / limited-run enclosure finishes [1][2]

## Version changes
No numbered circuit revision was established in the reviewed material. Recent 2025 and 2026 examples show cosmetic/limited-run finish variations while retaining the same Rat King identity. [2][3]

## Transistor
- Op-amp based circuit; exact op-amp part number is not publicly documented in the reviewed builder material.

## Diode
- **Germanium diodes** are explicitly documented by the builder. [1]

## Sound
Rat King is designed to bridge RAT-style distortion and fuzz, with the slow-slew op-amp contributing a smoother, less abrupt response. The germanium clipping arrangement is intended to add fullness and a fuzzier edge than a conventional RAT-style circuit. [1]
