# Arcane Analog - Overdrive Overload

## What this pedal is
Overdrive Overload is a cataloged family of Arcane Analog one-off overdrive builds rather than one fixed production circuit. The builder lists GLS (Greer Lightspeed), TSX (Ibanez TS10), TS808 (Ibanez TS808), Gold (Morning Glory), and Magenta (KOT 1/2), with additional custom builds. [1]

## Colorways
- Silver and olive-green one-off enclosures are documented in the builder's product photography. [1]
- Custom powder-coated colors, etched plates, and inked finishes are part of the one-off format.

## Versions and factory options
- GLS
- TSX
- TS808
- Gold
- Magenta
- Additional DLS, OCD, Honey Bee OD, and Harmonic Overdrive builds are listed by the builder
- Custom plates / inked finishes
- Hand-wired construction
- PCB construction
- True bypass [1]

## Version changes
There is no single numbered revision history for this catalog family. Each named option is a distinct one-off circuit/build, so controls and internal components depend on the selected variant. [1]

## Sound
The sound is variant-dependent because the Overdrive Overload page groups several distinct overdrive circuits under one catalog identity. The named GLS, TSX, TS808, Morning Glory, and KOT variants therefore cover different gain, EQ, compression, and clipping responses rather than one fixed voice. [1]
