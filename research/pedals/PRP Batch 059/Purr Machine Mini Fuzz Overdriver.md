# Asheville Guitar Pedals - Purr Machine Mini Fuzz Overdriver

## What this pedal is
Purr Machine Mini is a compact fuzz/distortion pedal based on the Acapulco Gold concept. It uses audio power amplifiers normally associated with driving speakers rather than a conventional pedal gain stage, giving the small pedal an unusually forceful, speaker-amp-like clipping character. [1][2]

## Colorways
- Pink mini enclosure is documented in a 2024/2025 builder listing.
- White and other small-run color variants are also documented in the builder's resale catalog. [2]

## Versions and factory options
- Volume
- Gain
- Compact Mini enclosure
- 9V DC power
- Hand-built production
- Uses two hand-picked audio amplifiers [1][2]

## Version changes
Purr Machine Mini is the compact Mini version of the Purr Machine family. A 2024 Reverb listing documents a limited “1 of 10” Mini run, while later listings show additional finishes; no separate numbered circuit revision is established in the reviewed material. [2]

## Sound
Purr Machine Mini produces a loud, aggressive fuzz/distortion response with a direct, power-amp-like character. The unusual audio-amplifier stage allows very saturated textures and can behave like a tiny preamp when driven hard, although the builder does not recommend using it that way. [1][2]
