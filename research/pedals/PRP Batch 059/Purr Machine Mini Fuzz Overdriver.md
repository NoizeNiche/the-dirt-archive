# Asheville Guitar Pedals — Purr Machine Mini Fuzz Overdriver

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Purr Machine Mini Fuzz Overdriver
- **Builder:** Asheville Guitar Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Fuzz
- **Identity:** Compact Mini version of the Purr Machine concept, loosely based on the EarthQuaker Devices Acapulco Gold and using two hand-picked audio amplifiers as the active gain devices. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
The Purr Machine Mini is a compact, high-output fuzz/distortion design. The exact-model Reverb listing says it uses **two hand-picked audio amplifiers** to create a lush and aggressive fuzz and that, when cranked, it can technically operate like a tiny preamp because of the way the circuit achieves fuzz. [1]

A separate 2025 Mini listing describes the unit as being based on the Acapulco Gold and capable of moving from amp-like distortion to fuzzed-out textures. [2]

## Controls / version notes
- The accessible exact-model listings do not expose a complete stable control specification for every Mini variant.
- A compact Mini form factor is consistent across the documented 2024/2025 examples.

## Circuit lineage
- **Primary inspiration:** EarthQuaker Devices **Acapulco Gold**.
- **Active devices:** Two hand-picked audio amplifiers. [1][2]
- This is an inspiration/lineage statement, not a claim that the Mini is an identical schematic reproduction of the original Acapulco Gold.

## Sound
The Mini is described as lush, aggressive and capable of strong fuzz saturation. One exact-model listing notes that its power-amplifier-based clipping can be driven hard enough to function as a tiny preamp into a speaker, although the builder does not recommend using it that way. [1]
Another 2025 listing describes the Mini as an Acapulco Gold-based distortion/fuzz machine able to move from amp-like distortion to fuzz-heavy textures. [2]
