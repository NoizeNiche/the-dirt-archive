# Astral ToneWorx — 666 The Beast

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** 666 The Beast
- **Builder:** Astral ToneWorx
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** Fully handmade high-gain distortion from the Canadian Rockies, voiced around 1980s classic-metal/NWOBHM-era tones with a dedicated EQ control that emphasizes mids and can become increasingly gated at higher settings. [1]

## What this pedal is
Astral ToneWorx describes 666 The Beast as a high-gain distortion aimed particularly at early Iron Maiden and broader NWOBHM-era metal tones, while also citing Judas Priest, Saxon and Helloween as part of the sonic reference set. [1]

## Controls
- **VOLUME**
- **GAIN**
- **TONE**
- **EQ:** Specialized EQ circuit controlling midrange boost.
Astral ToneWorx notes that with EQ at zero the signal is unaffected by the EQ circuit, while increasing EQ first adds substantial mids and then produces a cocked-wah-like emphasis and stronger gating past noon. [1]

## Sound
Astral ToneWorx describes EQ at minimum as suited to saturated riffs and chugging rhythms. Turning the EQ clockwise adds midrange, reaching a cocked-wah-like lead emphasis around noon. Beyond noon the effect becomes more aggressive and noticeably gated, which the builder associates with high-gain shred leads and heavily gated riffs. [1]
