# Astral ToneWorx - 666 The Beast

## What this pedal is
666 The Beast is a fully handmade high-gain distortion from Astral ToneWorx, voiced around classic 1980s heavy-metal and NWOBHM-era tones. Its specialized EQ control boosts the mids and can become increasingly aggressive and gated as it moves past noon. [1]

## Sound
With EQ at zero, Astral ToneWorx describes the pedal as suited to saturated riffing and chugging rhythms. Increasing EQ pushes the mids forward, then adds a more cocked-wah-like emphasis and stronger gating for lead or tightly gated high-gain parts. [1]
