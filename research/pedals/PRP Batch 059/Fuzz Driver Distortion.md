# Asheville Guitar Pedals - Fuzz Driver Distortion

## What this pedal is
Fuzz Driver Distortion is a compact fuzz/distortion pedal designed to emulate the warm, organic response of tube fuzz. Effects Database documents a pair of NOS Motorola JFETs and describes the circuit as dynamic and capable of moving from smoother fuzz into more aggressive distortion. [1][2]

## Colorways
- Gray/silver enclosure with black graphics is documented in Effects Database photography.
- Other finishes may exist, but a complete production colorway history was not established.

## Versions and factory options
- Dual NOS Motorola JFETs
- Two-knob presentation is documented in the original Effects Database description
- Current used examples are also cataloged with Fuzz, Tone, and Level controls, indicating later/alternate control presentation in the market record. [1][2]

## Version changes
The reviewed sources establish the pedal and its JFET design but do not provide a reliable numbered revision chronology. Control labeling varies across surviving references, so no unverified revision change is asserted here.

## Transistor
- **Dual NOS Motorola JFETs** are explicitly documented by Effects Database. [1]

## Diode

## Sound
Fuzz Driver is intended to give a warm, amp-like fuzz response while retaining enough attack to move into harder distortion. The available descriptions emphasize saturated sustain, dynamic response, and cleanup from the guitar volume control rather than a heavily compressed fuzz texture. [1][2]
