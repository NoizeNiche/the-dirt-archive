# Asheville Guitar Pedals — Fuzz Driver Distortion

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Fuzz Driver Distortion
- **Builder:** Asheville Guitar Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz / Distortion
- **Identity:** Compact fuzz/distortion designed around NOS JFETs to emulate the warm, organic response of tube-driven fuzz, with a two-control layout documented by an independent 2024 demo. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
Pedal of the Day describes the Fuzz Driver as a powerful distortion/fuzz unit using NOS JFET transistors to produce an amp-like, dynamic response. The published review describes the available range as moving from smoother, creamy fuzz to more aggressive, biting distortion. [1]

Effects Database documents the pedal and identifies a pair of NOS Motorola JFETs in the circuit. [2]

## Controls
- A **two-knob layout** is documented by Pedal of the Day and Effects Database.
