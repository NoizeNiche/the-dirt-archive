# Arcane Analog - BC108 Fuzz Face

## What this pedal is
Arcane Analog's BC108 Fuzz Face is a hand-wired silicon Fuzz Face build using BC108 transistors, standard Fuzz and Volume controls, DC power, true bypass, and a custom etched top plate. The builder identifies the photographed unit as a one-off exact build. [1]

## Colorways
- White powder-coated enclosure with a one-of-a-kind etched top plate is documented for the reviewed unit. [1]
- The archived example is a single custom build rather than a standardized production colorway.

## Versions and factory options
- Fuzz
- Volume
- BC108 silicon transistors
- DC power
- True bypass
- Custom etched top plate [1]

## Version changes
The reviewed example is a one-off/custom build rather than a numbered production revision. Arcane Analog's page specifically identifies the BC108 transistor set and unique enclosure treatment for this unit. [1]

## Transistor
- **BC108 silicon transistors** are explicitly documented by the builder. [1]

## Diode
- Exact clipping diode/device is not publicly documented.

## Sound
A BC108 Fuzz Face typically gives the classic Fuzz Face combination of singing sustain, dynamic attack, and cleanup from the guitar volume control, with silicon devices tending toward a firmer and brighter response than many germanium examples. The exact Arcane Analog build will also reflect the particular BC108 devices and the builder's biasing. [1][2]
