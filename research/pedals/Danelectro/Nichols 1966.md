### Verified sound evidence
It was in the garage of that home on Nichols Canyon Place that Steve created his first effects pedal, which he named “The Liverpool Fuzz Tone”.
Steve had no money to buy a fuzz and no access to any schematics.
Although he called his box a “fuzz”, it was in fact half way between a distortion and a fuzz.
