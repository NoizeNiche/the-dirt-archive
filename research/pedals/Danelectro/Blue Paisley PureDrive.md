## What this pedal is

The Blue Paisley Pure Drive is a full-size Danelectro dirt pedal from the discontinued Paisley line. Wikimedia Commons' Danelectro model list identifies **Blue Paisley PureDrive** among the Paisley effects and marks the Paisley line as discontinued. Multiple surviving Reverb listings identify the exact model, and one explicitly gives the model as **PD-01 Blue Paisley Pure Drive**.

## History / versions

- Reverb documents the exact model as **PD-01 Blue Paisley Pure Drive**, with a blue Paisley finish and Made in China.
- A used listing describes a unit as having been bought new in 2003 with original packaging and paperwork, which supports early-2000s circulation but is not treated as a factory production date for all units.
- The surviving sources reviewed do not establish a complete production span or numbered circuit revisions.
