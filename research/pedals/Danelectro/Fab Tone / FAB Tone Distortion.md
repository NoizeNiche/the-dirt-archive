# Danelectro — Fab Tone / FAB Tone Distortion

## PRP identity

- **Builder:** Danelectro
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** Danelectro DD-1 Fab Tone, also documented as Fab Tone / FAB Tone Distortion.

## What this pedal is

Effects Database identifies the pedal specifically as the **Danelectro DD-1 Fab Tone**, a distortion pedal from Danelectro's Original series. Its documented control set is **Vol, Bass, Treb, and Fab**.

## Controls / circuit

- **Volume:** documented as Vol.
- **Bass:** documented as Bass.
- **Treble:** documented as Treb.
- **Fab:** documented as Fab.
- **Power:** 9V battery or Danelectro DA-1 power supply.
- A Freestompboxes discussion documents the presence of clipping diodes in the circuit and discusses user modifications to them, but it does not establish the complete stock component values or an authoritative factory schematic.
- **Exact transistor/device:** Unknown from the surviving sources reviewed.
- **Exact stock clipping/diode part numbers:** Unknown.

## Sound

Contemporary and later user reports consistently describe the Fab Tone as a high-gain, aggressive distortion with substantial output and a strongly voiced EQ section. The archive treats those descriptions as listening/report evidence rather than electrical measurements.

A 2026 review describes it as a high-gain distortion with Level, three EQ bands, and a distortion control, but the older Effects Database source is treated as the primary control-layout reference because it explicitly names the four controls.

## History / use

- Effects Database places the model in Danelectro's Original series and dates its published record to 2004.
- Guitarcloud's Prince Equipment Archive documents the Fab Tone as a distortion pedal used by Prince in the late 1990s and early 2000s.
- A 2024 video review identifies it as the 1990s Danelectro FAB TONE distortion and discusses its very high-gain character.
- The current archive retains it as a historical dirt product regardless of current production status.
