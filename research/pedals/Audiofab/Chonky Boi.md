# Audiofab — Chonky Boi

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Chonky Boi
- **Builder:** Audiofab
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** Op-amp distortion inspired by the ProCo RAT and Ibanez FC10 Fat Cat, with a three-way clipping selector and deliberately preserved low-end “chonk.” [1]

## What this pedal is
Audiofab describes Chonky Boi as a late-1980s-inspired op-amp distortion covering light drive through very high-gain distortion. The design specifically aims to keep the low end fat even at high Chonk and Scratch settings. [1]

## Controls
- **CHONK:** Gain/drive.
- **HEFT:** Output level. It has enough level to push an amplifier input harder, especially at higher Scratch settings.
- **PURR:** High-frequency cut, from brighter to darker.
- **SCRATCH:** Three-way clipping selection:
  1. Germanium diodes
  2. Silicon diodes
  3. LEDs [1]

## Circuit lineage
- **Op-amp distortion family.**
- Explicit inspirations: **ProCo RAT** and **Ibanez FC10 Fat Cat**.
- Audiofab says the Fat Cat was produced from 1987 to 1990 and uses that historical family as a reference for Chonky Boi's full-frequency response. [1]

## Sound
Audiofab describes Chonky Boi as retaining substantial thickness from light purr through “MEGACHONK” gain. The Scratch setting changes the clipping character and can also alter output level, while Purr trims treble to fit different guitars and amplifiers. [1]
