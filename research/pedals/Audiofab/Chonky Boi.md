## PRP identity

- **Builder:** Audiofab
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** Op-amp distortion inspired by late-1980s RAT-style circuits, specifically the ProCo RAT and Ibanez FC10 Fat Cat, with selectable clipping types and a deliberately fat low-end response.

## What this pedal is
Audiofab describes Chonky Boi as an op-amp-based distortion that moves from light drive to very high-gain "MEGACHONK" while retaining a full low-end character. The circuit is explicitly inspired by the ProCo RAT and the 1987–1990 Ibanez FC10 Fat Cat.

## Sound
Chonky Boi is voiced to stay thick as the gain rises rather than becoming thin or nasal, making it suitable for both guitar and bass. The Scratch selector changes the clipping behavior while Purr trims the top end, so the pedal can move from open, light breakup through compressed high-gain distortion.
