# Audio Kitchen — The Big Trees

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** The Big Trees
- **Builder:** Audio Kitchen
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** 2.5-watt all-valve Class A amplifier/preamp in a pedal-sized enclosure.

## What this pedal is
The Big Trees is both a small tube amplifier and a line-level preamp. Audio Kitchen built it around ECC82/ECC83 preamp valves and a single EL84 output valve, with a six-position Dirt control that reduces output headroom and increases breakup.

No numbered factory revision was verified. The major documented operating distinction is Clean versus Amp/dirty operation and the six-position Dirt control.

## Transistor
- **Technology:** Valve, not transistor-based.
- **Active devices:** ECC82, ECC83 and EL84 tubes.

## Diode
- **Type:** No clipping diode component was publicly documented as the defining gain element.

## Sound
The Clean path provides a clean gain/buffer/line-driver function, while the Amp path engages the valve gain stages and tone stack. Turning Dirt toward heavier settings reduces headroom and increases breakup, compression and eventually fuzz-like saturation.
