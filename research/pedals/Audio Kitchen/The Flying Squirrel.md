# Audio Kitchen — The Flying Squirrel

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** The Flying Squirrel
- **Builder:** Audio Kitchen
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Large-format all-valve Class A drive/fuzz pedal built around Audio Kitchen's high-headroom tube architecture.

## What this pedal is
Audio Kitchen designed The Flying Squirrel (TFS) to produce an unusually large, harmonically dense combination of drive and fuzz. Contemporary product descriptions identify it as an all-valve Class A preamp/fuzz with an EL84 output stage.

## Sound
Audio Kitchen's stated target is an extremely large, rich and harmonically dense drive/fuzz sound. The pedal is capable of clean-to-crunch operation as well as very heavy fuzz and is designed to respond strongly to playing dynamics.
