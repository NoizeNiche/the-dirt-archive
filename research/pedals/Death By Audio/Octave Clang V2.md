## What this pedal is
The Octave Clang V2 is Death By Audio's revised octave-fuzz/distortion design. The V2 revisits the original Clang circuit with extended control range, a footswitchable octave, and updated circuitry while retaining the deliberately unstable, clangorous character of the original.

## Versions and factory options
Outer controls:
- **Volume:** output level.
- **Intensity:** gain of the circuit, up to +39 dB according to Death By Audio.
- **Shape:** pre-gain tilt EQ that changes the balance of highs and lows entering the circuit.

Additional features:
- Footswitchable **Octave** mode.
- Internal **Bias** control for intentionally misbiasing the distortion stage.
- Special octave transformer.
- Precision-matched diodes.
- Standard 2.1mm 9V DC center-negative supply.
- 9V battery supported.
- Current draw: 10mA.
- Approximate dimensions: 4.8 x 3.7 x 2.1 inches.

## Version changes
- Death By Audio identifies the V2 as a revised version of the original Octave Clang.
- V2 adds footswitchable octave and under-the-hood circuit refinements while retaining the original control concept.

## Sound
The Octave Clang V2 combines octave-up fuzz with pseudo-ring/noise behavior and heavy distortion. The Intensity and Shape controls strongly affect the harmonic structure, while the internal Bias control can push the response toward rougher and more unstable textures.
