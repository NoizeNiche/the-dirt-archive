## What this pedal is

The Germanium Filter combines a console-style drive circuit using germanium transistors with a highly flexible filter section. Death By Audio describes it as a vintage-style distortion/drive design with a very wide tonal range.

## Versions and factory options

- **Filter:** shapes the frequency response from very large low end through a mid scoop and into sharp, high-frequency sounds.
- **Gain:** ranges from mild overdrive to blown-out fuzz and responds strongly to the guitar's volume control.
- **Volume:** internal control for overall output level.
- Standard 9VDC, 2.1 mm negative-center power.

## Sound

The Germanium Filter can move from relatively mild drive into saturated fuzz while the filter sweeps from deep lows through scooped mids to piercing highs. The Gain control is notably interactive with the guitar's volume control.
