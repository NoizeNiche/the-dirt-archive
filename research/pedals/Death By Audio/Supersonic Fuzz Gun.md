## What this pedal is
The Supersonic Fuzz Gun is a fuzz pedal built around highly interactive controls and a Gate/Oscillation mode. Death By Audio describes it as a circuit capable of gated fuzz, oscillation, and extreme shifts in texture.

## Sound
The five main controls interact strongly. Bias changes the output transistor's operating point, Density affects the signal entering the fuzz and the oscillation behavior, Fuzz controls intensity, Filter removes high frequencies, and Level controls output. Gate mode produces a more conventional gated fuzz response, while Oscillation mode lets the circuit self-oscillate and generate sustained electronic tones.
