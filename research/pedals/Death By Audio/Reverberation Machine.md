## What this pedal is

The Reverberation Machine combines heavily saturated reverb with a distorted reverb path for large, experimental ambience.

## Versions and factory options

- **Altitude** control increases the intensity of the reverb drive.
- **Verb** controls the reverb level/character.
- Standard 9VDC, 2.1mm negative-center power.
- Current draw: approximately 66mA.
- Approx. 4.86 × 3.67 × 2.06 inches including hardware.

## Safety note

Extreme Altitude and Verb settings can produce very loud output, white noise and other artifacts. Users should reduce amplifier level before exploring those settings.
