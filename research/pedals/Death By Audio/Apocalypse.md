## What this pedal is
The Apocalypse is a five-voice fuzz and distortion pedal. After the input is heavily driven, the signal is routed through one of five selectable post-amplifier paths, each producing a different response. A sweepable frequency equalizer provides additional tonal shaping.

## Versions and factory options
- **Volume:** output level.
- **Drive:** controls the initial preamp gain.
- **Sweepable Frequency Equalizer:** moves through bass boost, mid scoop, mid boost, and treble-boosting regions.
- Five selectable signal paths:
  - **Twin-T Scoop**
  - **War Fuzz**
  - **Dual J-FET**
  - **Octave Rect**
  - **Gain x1000**

## Sound
The five signal paths cover different forms of boost, distortion, fuzz, octave-oriented saturation, and high-gain processing. Death By Audio also documents the Apocalypse as suitable for use as a boost, amp-simulated recording DI, fuzz, or bass-oriented gain stage.
