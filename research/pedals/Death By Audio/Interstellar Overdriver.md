## What this pedal is
The Interstellar Overdriver is a two-control, discrete overdrive designed to reproduce the response of an amplifier being pushed hard. Effects Database dates the model record to December 8, 2005.

## Versions and factory options
- **Overdriver:** input gain, from subtle boost into heavy overdrive.
- **Master:** output volume.
- Standard 9V operation.

## Sound
The Overdriver control moves from a relatively clean boost into a thick, harmonically rich overdrive. The Master control lets the player reduce output volume while retaining the drive character. Death By Audio describes the circuit as amp-like and designed to respond progressively as the drive control is increased.
