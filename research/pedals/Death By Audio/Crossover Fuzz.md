## What this pedal is
The Crossover Fuzz is a filter-fuzz design that splits the signal into high- and low-frequency bands and processes those bands separately. It can also be configured for mono, stereo, or split-routing applications.

## Colorways
- Death By Audio documents **Sky Light** and **Cyber Light** variants.
- Sky Light uses yellow low-pass and cyan high-pass indicators.
- Cyber Light uses red low-pass and blue high-pass indicators.

## Versions and factory options
- Independent high and low frequency paths.
- **Freq** control sweeps two analog resonant filters in tandem.
- High and low levels can be adjusted independently.
- Dual inputs and outputs support several routing configurations.
- Standard 9VDC, 2.1 mm negative-center power.
- Current draw: 120mA.

## Sound
The Crossover Fuzz produces dense filter-fuzz textures by independently shaping the high and low portions of the signal. The filter section can move from focused tone carving into resonant and oscillating effects.
