# Black Cat Pedals - Super Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Black Cat Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz

## Research
Black Cat Super Fuzz is a modern recreation of the Univox Super Fuzz / Shin-ei design. Its two-stage octave-doubling circuit combines heavy fuzz with a noticeable upper-octave component, while Balance and Expander controls reproduce the core control concept of the original. Effects Database documents multiple versions, including the Fred Bonte version and a 2009 version, with later enclosure/layout changes.

## Versions
- **Fred Bonte version:** documented by Effects Database as an earlier production version.
- **2009 version:** documented as a later production version with revised presentation.
- **Later layout:** top-mounted jacks were introduced while retaining the fundamental circuit/control approach.

## Colorways
- Holographic Sparkle powder-coat finishes are documented among Black Cat Super Fuzz examples.

## Diode
- **Documented:** NOS Germanium diodes.

## Transistor
- **Not reliably documented in the reviewed sources**

## Sound
Super Fuzz produces a dense, aggressive fuzz with a subtle upper-octave layer rather than the more isolated octave emphasis of an Octavia. The Balance control changes how hard the second stage is driven, which in turn affects fuzz saturation and the apparent octave intensity.
