# BigNoise Amplification - Dark Distortion

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** BigNoise Amplification
- **Catalog type:** Distortion

## Research
Dark Distortion is a high-gain distortion pedal from BigNoise Amplification aimed at heavy metal, heavy rock and aggressive blues. The current manufacturer description centers on a three-knob active tone stack plus three three-position toggles: Fat adds or tightens low end, Crush changes gain intensity from lean to more saturated settings, and Bright shifts the high-frequency emphasis. The builder also lists black or silver Marshall-style knobs as stock options.

## Versions

## Sound
Dark Distortion is designed for brutal, saturated metal and heavy-rock sounds but can be tightened for chunky rhythms or pulled back toward heavier blues. The three-way Fat, Crush and Bright controls make the voicing unusually adjustable for a high-gain distortion.
