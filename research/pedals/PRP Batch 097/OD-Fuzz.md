# Black Cat Pedals - OD-Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Black Cat Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz / Overdrive

## Research
OD-Fuzz combines the Black Cat OD-1 with a separate Fuzz Face-style fuzz channel in one enclosure. The OD and Fuzz sections each have independent controls and footswitching, and they can be used separately or stacked for a much wider range of dirt textures. Effects Database documents standard silicon fuzz plus Germanium and Ge/Si Hybrid variations.

## Versions
- **Standard:** silicon fuzz configuration.
- **Germanium:** germanium fuzz variation.
- **Ge/Si Hybrid:** hybrid transistor configuration in the fuzz circuit.
- **CME Edition:** later CME version with a specially tapered wide-range Bias control.

## Transistor
- **Documented variant:** Ge/Si Hybrid uses a combination of Germanium and Silicon transistors in the fuzz circuit.

## Diode
- **Not reliably documented in the reviewed sources**

## Sound
The OD side covers cleaner breakup through saturated drive, while the fuzz side supplies a more traditional Fuzz Face-style texture. Running both together creates dense, interactive gain that can move from thick overdrive into full fuzz.
