# Black Cat Pedals - OD-1 Freddy Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Black Cat Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz / Overdrive

## Research
The Black Cat OD-1, also known as Freddie Fuzz, is an original design based around the OP275 dual operational amplifier. Its Drive and Volume controls cover clean, slightly crunchy and saturated distortion/fuzz territory, while the circuit is highly responsive to picking and guitar-volume changes. Effects Database also identifies a Holographic Sparkle powder-coat finish.

## Colorways

## Sound
The OD-1 is unusually touch-sensitive, moving from clean or lightly crunchy tones into saturated fuzz-like distortion as Drive rises. Rolling back the guitar volume can clean the sound up much like a responsive vintage fuzz circuit.

## Deep research verification

Effects Database and Analog Man support the exact Black Cat OD-1 / Freddie Fuzz identity as an original design using an **OP275 dual operational amplifier**. Verified controls are **Drive** and **Volume**. The pedal is highly responsive to picking and guitar-volume changes, ranging from clean/crunchy drive toward saturated fuzz-like distortion. Holographic Sparkle powder-coat is documented as a production finish.
