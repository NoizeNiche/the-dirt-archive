# Black Cat Pedals - OD-Boost

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Black Cat Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive

## Research
OD-Boost combines the Black Cat OD-1 overdrive section with an independent clean boost. The two channels can run separately or together, and a toggle changes their order between Boost into OD and OD into Boost. The OD side uses Drive and Volume controls while the boost has a dedicated level control, with no tone or EQ stage in the signal path.

## Sound
OD-Boost is built around raw gain rather than tone shaping. Stacking the channels and reversing their order lets it move from a louder clean push to gritty overdrive and further into aggressive distortion.
