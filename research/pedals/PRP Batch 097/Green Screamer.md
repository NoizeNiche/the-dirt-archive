# BBE - Green Screamer

## PRP identity

- **Builder:** BBE
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** BBE's Green Screamer, a vintage-style overdrive with documented V1, V2 and V3 production variants.

## What this pedal is

The Green Screamer is an overdrive designed around smooth, warm, dynamic drive associated with vintage tube-amp behavior. Effects Database dates its listing to March 6, 2006 and records V1, V2 and V3 variants. BBE documentation and retailer records identify the pedal as a compact three-control overdrive centered on Level, Tone and Gain.

## Controls

- **LEVEL:** Output level.
- **TONE:** Tone shaping.
- **GAIN:** Overdrive amount.

## Versions

- **V1 / V2 / V3:** Effects Database records three production versions.
- **V2:** BBE's later redesign adds a switchable input buffer and a 3-position Deep switch while retaining the Level, Tone and Gain control set. Sweetwater identifies the V2 as a complete redesign of the previous Green Screamer.
- The checked sources do not provide a reliable factory change log for every transition between V1, V2 and V3, so no additional differences are inferred.

## Circuit / components

- **Original Green Screamer:** Exact production op-amp, transistor and diode part numbers were not verified from authoritative model documentation.
- **V2:** BBE/Sweetwater product documentation specifies a **4558 op-amp** and through-hole PCB construction.
- Exact clipping diode part numbers remain undocumented in the checked sources.

## Bypass / switching

- Original model: **Hardwire bypass**.
- V2: **True hardwire bypass**, switchable input buffer, 3-position Deep switch.

## Power / hardware

- Original model documentation specifies an easy-access **9V battery compartment** and included external power supply.
- Exact current draw was not reliably established from the primary sources reviewed.
- V2 has top-mounted input, output and DC connections and a compact pedalboard-oriented enclosure.

## Sound

BBE and Effects Database characterize the Green Screamer as smooth, warm and dynamic, intended to add harmonic overdrive while preserving the instrument's basic character. The V2 is presented as a more extensively redesigned version of the same broad green-overdrive concept.

## Deep research verification

Effects Database was cross-checked against current BBE/Sweetwater product documentation. The combined evidence establishes the product's 2006-era catalog presence, three-version history, original hardwire/battery architecture, and the later V2 redesign with 4558 op-amp, Deep switch and switchable buffer.
