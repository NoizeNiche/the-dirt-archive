# BBE - Green Screamer

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** BBE
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive

## Research
The Green Screamer is BBE's tube-amp-style overdrive, designed around smooth, warm, dynamic overdrive rather than extreme saturation. Effects Database records multiple production versions, including V1, V2 and V3; documented features include hardwire bypass, LED indication, a 9V battery compartment and an external power supply. The pedal is intended to push an amplifier with a responsive, vintage-leaning overdrive character.

## Sound
The Green Screamer is voiced for smooth, warm overdrive with a dynamic response. Its basic role is to add a tube-amp-like push and harmonic drive rather than produce a heavily compressed high-gain distortion.
