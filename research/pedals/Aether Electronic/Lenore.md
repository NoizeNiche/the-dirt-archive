# Aether Electronic — Lenore

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Lenore
- **Builder:** Aether Electronic
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz / Overdrive
- **Identity:** Aether Electronic's three-knob fuzz/overdrive based on an overlooked German circuit from the late 1960s. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
Lenore is a modernized interpretation of a late-1960s German dirt circuit. Aether Electronic changed the component set for modern availability and replaced some of the original controls with more interactive options. [1]

## Versions and factory options
- Gain
- Starve
- Volume

  - Mode 2: Fuzz
- 3PDT switching
- top-mounted Switchcraft jacks
- point-to-point tagboard construction
- 9 V center-negative adapter power [1]

## Sound
Lenore is designed to move from edge-of-breakup boost and classic grit into thick overdrive, raspy textures, ripped-speaker tones and splatty decay. The Starve control and two-position Mode switch are the main tools for moving between overdrive behavior and more unstable fuzz textures. [1][2]
