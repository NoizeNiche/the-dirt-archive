# BMF Effects - Marc F'N Ford Overdrive/Boost

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** BMF Effects
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive

## Research
The Marc F'N Ford Overdrive/Boost is a signature dual-gain pedal built around two existing BMF circuits. The boost side is BMF's GB Boost, a germanium treble-booster design related to the Dallas Rangemaster concept. The drive side is the Purple Nurple Overdrive.

Each side can operate independently or be stacked. The published design is based on the setup used by Marc Ford, with the boost feeding the Purple Nurple in the intended stacked configuration.

## Features
- GB germanium boost
- Purple Nurple overdrive
- Independent switching
- Stackable boost into drive
- True bypass
- 9V operation
- 13mA current draw
- Signature design
