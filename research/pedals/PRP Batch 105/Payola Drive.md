# BLAMMO! Electronics - Payola Drive

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** BLAMMO! Electronics
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive

## Research
Payola Drive is a natural-sounding overdrive voiced to cover transparent boost through heavier driven tones. Its LESS/MORE switch changes the amount of drive, while the GAIN control has enough range to push the circuit into a stronger overdrive and provide output boost.

BLAMMO! describes the circuit as a distant cousin of the Tube Screamer with a fixed bass/treble response and a passive TONE filter. The clipping network combines soft and hard clipping rather than relying on one type alone.

## Features
- LESS / MORE drive switching
- Wide gain range
- Passive tone filter
- Soft and hard clipping
- True bypass
- Relay-based no-click switching
- 125B enclosure
- 9V center-negative
- 35mA current draw
