# BMF Effects - Purple Nurple Overdrive

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** BMF Effects
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive

## Research
The Purple Nurple Overdrive is a low- to medium-gain BMF design that moves from gritty clean boost through tweed-like breakup into mild fuzz. BMF emphasizes its response to picking dynamics and the guitar's volume control rather than requiring frequent pedal adjustments.

The pedal uses true bypass switching, a DC jack and can operate from 9V to 18V. It is also the drive circuit used in the Marc F'N Ford signature pedal.

## Features
- Gritty clean boost to mild fuzz range
- Touch-sensitive response
- Strong interaction with guitar volume
- True bypass
- 9V to 18V operation
- 11mA current draw
- 2.9 x 4.5 inch enclosure
