# BLAMMO! Electronics - Fuzz Detonator

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** BLAMMO! Electronics
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz

## Research
The Fuzz Detonator is a vintage-inspired fuzz with a second feedback/noise section controlled from its own footswitch. The main fuzz is designed to clean up from the guitar volume control, while the feedback section can move into oscillating, gated and highly unstable textures.

BLAMMO! uses a relay-based true-bypass system, NOS Tropical Fish tone capacitors, and a momentary/latching feedback footswitch. The enclosure is a larger 1590BB format with top-mounted jacks.

## Features
- Main vintage-style fuzz section
- Feedback/oscillation section
- Guitar-volume cleanup
- NOS Tropical Fish tone capacitors
- Relay true bypass
- Momentary or latching feedback switch
- 1590BB enclosure
- 9V center-negative
