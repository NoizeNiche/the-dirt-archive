# BMF Effects - Rocket 88 Overdrive

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** BMF Effects
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive

## Research
The Rocket 88 is BMF Effects' refined version of the classic green overdrive family. BMF describes it as keeping the recognizable response while adding more low end, more clarity and a wider, more controllable gain range. Contemporary listings consistently describe it as a Tube Screamer-family or “green style” drive.

The pedal uses a straightforward tone control and a smooth gain sweep, with true bypass switching and a DC jack.

## Features
- Classic green-drive foundation
- Increased low-end response
- Increased clarity
- Expanded gain range
- Smooth gain taper
- Tone control
- True bypass
- DC jack
