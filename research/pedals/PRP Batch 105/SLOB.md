# BLAMMO! Electronics - SLOB

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** BLAMMO! Electronics
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz

## Research
The SLOB is BLAMMO!'s version of the Prescription Electronics COB, or Clean Octave Blend. It is based on the Octavia-style analog octave-up circuit and combines the fuzz with a clean blend, letting the player retain dry attack while adding octave-up saturation.

BLAMMO! updates the original concept with relay-based true bypass, compact top-mounted-jack construction, matched diode forward voltage in the signal path, stronger power filtering and polarity protection.

## Features
- Analog octave-up fuzz
- Clean blend
- Relay true bypass
- No-click footswitch
- Matched signal-path diodes
- Beefed-up power filtering
- 9V center-negative
- 32mA current draw
