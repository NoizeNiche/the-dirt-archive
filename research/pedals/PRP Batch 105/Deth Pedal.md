# BLAMMO! Electronics - Deth Pedal

## PRP identity

- **Builder:** BLAMMO! Electronics
- **Catalog type:** Distortion

## Research

The Deth Pedal is a limited collaboration between BLAMMO! Electronics and Dirty Haggard. The circuit combines a RAT-style distortion with a Green Ringer-style analog octave-up circuit on a blend control, plus a FET clean boost on its own footswitch.

BLAMMO! changes the clipping control to a continuous potentiometer that sweeps between symmetrical and asymmetrical clipping. The RAT-derived section is voiced with more bass and low mids, and an internal noise gate reduces idle hiss while allowing notes to decay naturally.

## Features

- RAT-derived distortion
- Analog octave-up blend
- FET clean boost
- Continuous symmetrical/asymmetrical clipping control
- Internal noise gate
- Relay soft switching / true bypass
- OP07 op-amp
- Top-mounted jacks
- 9V center-negative
- Limited to 50 units
