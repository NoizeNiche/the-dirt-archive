# BLAMMO! Electronics - L.A. Square Face

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** BLAMMO! Electronics
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz

## Research
The L.A. Square Face is BLAMMO!'s interpretation of the Cesar Diaz Texas Square Face, itself derived from the Dallas-Arbiter Fuzz Face family. BLAMMO! states that its version retains the character of the original concept while updating the switching, power filtering and enclosure.

Documented versions include germanium and silicon transistor builds. The silicon version uses higher-gain BC108B transistors and is described as retaining familiar Fuzz Face response while adding more crunch at maximum settings.

## Features
- Fuzz Face-family circuit
- Germanium and silicon versions documented
- Relay-based soft switching
- True bypass
- Beefed-up power filtering
- Top-mounted jacks
- 125B enclosure
