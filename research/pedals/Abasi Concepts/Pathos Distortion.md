# Abasi Concepts — Pathos Distortion

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Pathos Distortion
- **Builder:** Abasi Concepts
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** Abasi Pathos distortion pedal, developed by Tosin Abasi with Brian Wampler.

## What this pedal is
Pathos is an electric-guitar distortion designed to retain the feel of an **American-style tube-amp channel**. Abasi's official product page describes two voicings, **Smooth** and **Edge**, plus gain, level, EQ shelving and mid control. [1]

Effects Database identifies the model as a Tosin Abasi signature pedal, developed with Brian Wampler, made in the USA. It documents a limited run of 1,000 pieces worldwide and a 9V battery or external-power option. [2]

## Controls
- **Gain**
- **Level / Volume**
- **Bass**
- **Mid**
- **Treble**
- **Smooth / Edge** voicing switch. [1][2]

## Voicings
Abasi describes Smooth and Edge as alternate voicings spanning crunch through controlled high-gain distortion and sustain. The archive treats these as the documented factory voicings, not separate model identities. [1]

## Construction and switching
The official specification states:
- crafted in the USA by Tosin Abasi with Brian Wampler,
- top-mounted jacks,
- relay true-bypass switching,
- soft-touch footswitch,
- 9V battery connection and 9V power jack,
- limited 5-year warranty. [1]

## Components
The builder states that high-grade components were selected for sound and response, but neither the official product page nor the Effects Database entry establishes exact production transistor or diode part numbers. [1][2]

- **Technology:** Solid-state analog distortion.
- **Transistor:** Exact device unknown.

## Sound
Pathos is intended to move from an amp-push/crunch role into a full distortion voice without losing the feel associated with an American-style tube channel. Smooth and Edge provide the main voicing contrast, with the EQ and Mid controls providing additional frequency shaping. [1]

## Version history
No numbered factory hardware revision was verified in the checked sources. The documented 1,000-piece limitation is production quantity information, not a separate revision. [2]
