# Abasi Concepts — Pathos Distortion

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Pathos Distortion
- **Builder:** Abasi Concepts
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** Original Pathos distortion pedal developed by Tosin Abasi with Brian Wampler.

## What this pedal is
Pathos is designed to retain the feel of an American-style tube-amp channel. It has two voicings, **Smooth** and **Edge**, with gain, level, three-band-style EQ shelving and a dedicated mid control for moving from crunch into controlled high-gain distortion. [1][2]

## Sound
Pathos is designed to cover a wide range without leaving the amp-like feel behind. Smooth supplies softer crunch and sustain, while Edge pushes toward a more immediate, aggressive distortion; the EQ and mid controls let the player keep the sound tight or broaden it as gain increases. [1][2]
