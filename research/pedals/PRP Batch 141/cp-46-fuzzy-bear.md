# Caline - CP-46 Fuzzy Bear

- **Builder:** Caline
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Primary research sources:** Effects Database exact-model record; Free Stompboxes trace/discussion; Effects Database forum report.
- **Identity:** The CP-46 Fuzzy Bear is a compact Fuzz Face-influenced fuzz.
- **Colorways:** Exact-model imagery exists; no authoritative production colorway list verified.
- **Versions:** No numbered factory version verified.
- **Version changes:** No documented production revision verified.
- **Controls:** Volume and Fuzz.
- **Transistor type:** A documented trace identifies **BC549 NPN silicon transistors** in the traced unit.
- **Diode type:** The trace documents an **SS14 Schottky diode** in the power-supply/reverse-polarity section, not as a clipping diode.
- **Sound description:** Effects Database describes the pedal as punchy, raunchy and capable of moving toward a roar, while the trace identifies it as a standard silicon Fuzz Face topology. A documented owner report says it cleans up well from the guitar volume control.
- **Marketing discrepancy:** A 2018 report challenged the product's “Germanium” advertising after a gut-shot inspection found BC549 silicon transistors; the archive records the silicon evidence rather than repeating the older Germanium claim.
- **Power:** The trace reports roughly 8.3V after the protection resistor from a 9V supply and about 2mA pedal consumption in that unit; these are trace/owner observations rather than universal factory specifications.
- **Photo status:** Exact-model source imagery located; no local image promoted in this batch.
- **Research confidence:** High for the Fuzz Face/silicon classification in the traced unit; medium for production-wide component consistency; high that the cited Germanium marketing should not be treated as proof of germanium transistors.
