- **Catalog type:** Overdrive

- **Colorways:** Exact-model imagery exists; no authoritative full colorway catalog verified.

- **Sound description:** Caline describes a warm, organic drive that reacts strongly to pick attack, from glistening grit under lighter playing to dynamic overdrive when struck harder.
