## What this pedal is
DeadastronautFX's surviving builder catalog explicitly lists **Skinwalker Hi Gain Distortion** among its guitar and music effects. The builder describes its catalog as custom-designed circuits made available as PCB projects.

A separate builder video published December 29, 2017 is titled **SKINWALKER hi Gain distortion** and states that the designer developed it as a high-gain metal distortion, with PCB projects available.

## Technical evidence
- The builder video describes a **bass cut** intended to avoid flabby bass when detuning.
- It describes **3-way tone settings** to accommodate amps that are brighter or darker.
- It also states that the designer added these controls after experimenting with multiple high-gain distortion designs.
- **Exact control labels:** Not fully established by the surviving text.
- **Exact circuit topology:** Unknown.
- **Exact transistor/device:** Unknown.
- **Exact clipping device:** Unknown.

A separate effects reference describes the Skinwalker as high-gain distortion with an additional pre-gain boost control, but the archive treats that secondary description as corroboration rather than a substitute for the builder source.

## Sound / use
The builder's own demo description frames Skinwalker as a **high-gain metal distortion** intended to provide tonal scope for tweaking with the amplifier EQ and the pedal's own EQ. The described bass cut is specifically intended for lower-tuned guitars.
