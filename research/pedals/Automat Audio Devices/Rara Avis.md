## PRP identity

## What this pedal is
Rara Avis is the more compact of Automat Audio Devices' initial two fuzz designs. Published coverage documents Gain, Bias, Treble, Bass, Filter, Output and a Big/Lean mode, with Lean engaging a tighter high-pass-filtered presentation while Big provides the fuller fuzz setting.

## Sound
Rara Avis moves from thick, raunchy fuzz to a tighter distortion-leaning response through the Big/Lean switch and Filter controls. External reviews also note that the Bias control can move the texture toward more gated or Velcro-like behavior, giving the pedal a wider working range than a fixed-voicing fuzz.
