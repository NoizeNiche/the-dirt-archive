# Automat Audio Devices — Triplegänger

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Triplegänger
- **Builder:** Automat Audio Devices
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Parallel triple-fuzz system combining independent Fuzz 1 and Fuzz 2 circuits with a switchable sub-octave section.

## What this pedal is
Triplegänger places three fuzz paths in parallel: Fuzz 1, Fuzz 2 and a Sub circuit. Fuzz 2 offers Splatter, Octave or Double voicings, while the Sub section can be assigned to Fuzz 1, Fuzz 2, or both.

## Sound
Triplegänger can layer a conventional fuzz, an octave/splatter fuzz and sub-octave content in parallel, making the result range from articulate upper-octave textures to very heavy low-end blends. Each section has its own level and the sub circuit can be routed between channels, so the player's mix of fuzz layers becomes part of the sound design.
