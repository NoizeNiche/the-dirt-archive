# Big Knob Pedals - Octavius

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Big Knob Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz

## Research
Recreation of the classic Octavia circuit associated with Hendrix, producing an octave-up plus ring-modulation effect. Controls: Level and Fuzz. Fully hand-wired analog, compact MXR-sized enclosure, true bypass 3PDT, bright LED, standard 9V adaptor and 9V battery clip. Source: Big Knob product page (https://bigknobpedals.com/product/octavius-octave-fuzz/).
