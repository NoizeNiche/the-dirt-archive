# Big Knob Pedals - Pink Freud

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Big Knob Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz / Overdrive

## Research
Hybrid fuzz/overdrive combining a classic germanium Fuzz Face-style fuzz with an overdrive stage. Controls: Level, Tone and Attack. Uses two matched NOS Russian military germanium transistors. Fully hand-wired analog construction, compact MXR-sized enclosure, true bypass 3PDT, bright LED, standard 9V adaptor and 9V battery clip. Source: Big Knob product page (https://bigknobpedals.com/product/pink-freud/); Effects Database (https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/bigknob/pinkfreud).

### Verified sound evidence
The best of both worlds – fuzz and overdrive – in a single pedal!
Freud provides the perfect cure for fuzz sounding thin and stark by adding a built-in overdrive at the end of a classic FF fuzz circuit.
A versatile pedal, Pink Freud goes from screaming fuzz to shimmering clean, all with a simple roll-off of your guitar’s volume knob.
