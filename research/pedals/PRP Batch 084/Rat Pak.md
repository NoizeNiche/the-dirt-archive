# Big Knob Pedals - Rat Pak

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Big Knob Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Distortion

## Research
Circuit and component recreation of the 1985 Whiteface RAT. Controls: Distortion, Filter and Volume, plus a micro-switch for classic versus newer RAT2/Turbo-style clipping modes. Uses an original LM308N op-amp. Fully hand-wired analog, compact MXR-sized enclosure, true bypass 3PDT, bright LED, standard 9V adaptor and 9V battery clip. Sources: Big Knob product page (https://bigknobpedals.com/product/rat-pak/); Effects Database (https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/bigknob/ratpak).
