# Big Knob Pedals - Tone Blender

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Big Knob Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz

## Research
Reproduction of the classic Tone Bender family fuzz. Controls: Level and Attack. Uses three matched NOS 1960s-era Russian military germanium transistors. Fully hand-wired analog, compact MXR-sized enclosure, true bypass 3PDT, bright LED and 9V battery operation. Sources: Effects Database Tone Blender MkI '65 (https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/bigknob/toneblender/mk1); Big Knob Pedals official catalog (https://www.bigknobpedals.com/); Reverb archival listing (https://reverb.com/item/54382008-big-knob-tone-blender-fuzz-pedal).
