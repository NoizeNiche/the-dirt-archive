# Big Knob Pedals - Rock-Ola

## PRP identity

- **Catalog type:** Distortion

## Research
An original Big Knob overdrive designed to maximize playing dynamics and interaction with the guitar's volume and tone controls. Controls: Volume, Gain and Tone. The design focus is single-coil Strat-style guitars, while Big Knob also notes humbucker compatibility. Fully hand-wired analog, compact MXR-sized enclosure, true bypass 3PDT, bright LED, standard 9V adaptor and 9V battery clip. Source: Big Knob product page (https://bigknobpedals.com/product/rock-ola/).
