# Big Knob Pedals - Tone-Nation

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Big Knob Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Distortion

## Research
Klon-style overdrive/boost interpretation that Big Knob describes as the affordable version with a little more bite and gain. Controls: Volume, Gain and Tone. Hand-wired analog construction with no printed circuit board, compact MXR-sized enclosure, true bypass 3PDT, bright LED, standard 9V adaptor and 9V battery clip. Sources: Big Knob Pedals official catalog (https://www.bigknobpedals.com/); Reverb listing (https://reverb.com/item/99860405-big-knob-pedals-tone-nation-overdrive-boost-klon-centaur-variant).
