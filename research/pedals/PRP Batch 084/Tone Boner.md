# Big Knob Pedals - Tone Boner

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Big Knob Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive

## Research
Transparent MOSFET boost based on the Super Hard-On style circuit family. Big Knob describes it as a very clean boost intended to restore clarity and level lost through the guitar signal chain. The accessible sources did not expose enough model-specific front-panel control labeling to document knob names safely. Sources: Big Knob Pedals official catalog (https://www.bigknobpedals.com/); Gearmanndude demo (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=SMPLQYVnnww).
