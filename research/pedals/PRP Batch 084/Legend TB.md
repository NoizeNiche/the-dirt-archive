# Big Knob Pedals - Legend TB

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Big Knob Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz

## Research
A limited-run Big Knob prototype based on a modified MKII Tone Bender fuzz. Controls are Level and Attack. Surviving Big Knob-era documentation describes a positive-ground PNP germanium design, fully hand-wired analog construction, true-bypass 3PDT switching, a compact MXR-sized enclosure, bright LED, and 9V battery operation. Sources: Big Knob Pedals official catalog (https://www.bigknobpedals.com/); archival Reverb listing for the Big Knob Yardbirds '68 / Jimmy Page MkII Fuzz (https://reverb.com/item/75678384-big-knob-pedals-yardbirds-68-jimmy-page-mkii-fuzz-2021-steel).
