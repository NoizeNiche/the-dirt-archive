# Big Knob Pedals - Range Blaster

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Big Knob Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive

## Research
Recreation of a Dallas Rangemaster-style treble boost. Controls: Range and Boost. Uses a single NOS Russian germanium transistor. The builder documents positive-ground PNP operation and internal 9V battery-only power to preserve the original approach. Fully hand-wired analog, compact MXR-sized enclosure, true bypass 3PDT and bright LED. Sources: Big Knob Pedals official catalog (https://www.bigknobpedals.com/); Reverb listing (https://reverb.com/item/32824451-big-knob-pedals-range-blaster).
