# Big Knob Pedals - NYC Version 6

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Big Knob Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz

## Research
Recreation of the Big Muff Version 6 / Circuit 3034 variant manufactured around 1981-82. Controls are Volume, Tone and Fuzz. Big Knob describes it as unusually raw compared with modern NYC Muff sounds, aimed at garage and alt-blues fuzz. Construction: fully hand-wired analog, compact MXR-sized enclosure, true bypass 3PDT, bright LED, standard 9V adaptor and 9V battery clip. Sources: Big Knob product page (https://bigknobpedals.com/product/nyc-version-6/); Reverb archival listing (https://reverb.com/item/77896992-big-knob-pedals-nyc-version-6-big-muff-pedal-circuit-3034-2020-s-silver).
