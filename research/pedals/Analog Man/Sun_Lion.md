# Analog Man — Sun Lion

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Sun Lion
- **Builder:** Analog Man
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Introduction:** 2005
- **Identity:** One parent combining a Sun Face fuzz and Beano Boost in one enclosure. The two sections remain distinct internal effects, but the product is one pedal.

## What this pedal is
Sun Lion was introduced in the early 2000s by combining Analog Man's Sun Face fuzz with the Beano Boost. Analog Man specifies the internal signal order as **SUNFACE -> BEANO BOOST**, allowing the boost section to drive the fuzz or provide a separate output lift depending on use. [1][2]

## Colorways and hardware
- Early units used simpler hand-stamped/hand-labeled graphics.
- A **4-jack version** was offered to provide additional routing flexibility.
- Later color-printed graphics replaced the early hand-labeled presentation because the older labels did not hold up well.
- These are hardware/graphics production details, not separate parent pedals.

## Versions and factory options
### Sun Lion — standard production
- Sun Face fuzz section
- Beano Boost section
- separate true-bypass switching for each side
- two 3PDT footswitches
- separate LEDs
- battery operation
- optional power jack depending on build
- optional 4-jack version [1][2]

### Factory transistor options
Analog Man selects NOS germanium transistors for the Sun Face and Beano sections according to the requested build and available stock. Specific transistor choices are factory configuration details, not aftermarket modifications. [1]

## Version changes
The important documented evolution is from early hand-stamped graphics and 2-jack configurations to later color-printed and optional 4-jack builds. The underlying Sun Face -> Beano Boost concept remains the defining identity, so the archive does not invent V1/V2 labels where the builder does not use them. [1][2]

## Transistor
- **Technology:** Germanium transistors are used in the Sun Face and Beano Boost sections, with Analog Man selecting NOS parts for tone and low noise.
- Exact part depends on the ordered/build configuration and historical availability. [1]

## Diode
- **Type:** Unknown.
- No Sun Lion-specific diode type or production-wide part number established.

## Sound
Sun Lion combines a responsive vintage-style fuzz with the more forward, amplifier-pushing character of Beano Boost in one pedal, with the internal order placing fuzz before boost. This makes it capable of thick fuzz that can be tightened and pushed from the Beano side, as well as cleaner boost textures when the two sections are used independently. [1][2]
