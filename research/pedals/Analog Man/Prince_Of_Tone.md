# Analog Man — Prince Of Tone

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Prince Of Tone
- **Builder:** Analog Man
- **Catalog types:** Overdrive / Distortion
- **Introduction:** 2009-era production
- **Identity:** Separate parent from King Of Tone. It is derived from one half of the King Of Tone architecture but has its own enclosure, control set, mode switch and production identity.

## What this pedal is
Prince Of Tone is essentially one King Of Tone side placed into a single enclosure, with changes to the mode architecture and controls. The external three-way switch selects Overdrive, Boost and Distortion, while internal DIP switches provide low-mid lift and higher-gain/Turbo behavior. [1]

## Sound
Prince Of Tone covers a broad range from low-gain boost and transparent overdrive through a louder, stronger distortion voice, with the mode switch changing the gain/output relationship rather than simply adding more volume. The internal Turbo and low-mid options can make the sound more muscular and forward while keeping the basic King Of Tone-derived response. [1]
