# Audible Disease — FR-1 Fracture

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** FR-1 Fracture
- **Builder:** Audible Disease
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** High-gain oscillating distortion and LFO-controlled tremolo unit documented in 2008.

## What this pedal is
Effects Database documents the FR-1 as a combined distortion and tremolo unit. SPEED controls tremolo rate and STAGGER controls oscillation of the distorted sound.

## Versions and factory options
### FR-1 production
- SPEED tremolo control
- STAGGER oscillation control
- LED indicates tremolo pulse rate
- Tremolo range about 60ms to 3.4s
- 9V battery or standard 9V negative-tip adapter

## Sound
FR-1 is designed around unstable, oscillating distortion rather than a conventional static dirt sound. Its tremolo can move from extremely fast to very slow while STAGGER adds a separate oscillating component.
