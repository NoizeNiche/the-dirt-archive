# Color Audio - Cassette Preamp

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Color Audio
- **Catalog identity:** Cassette Preamp
- **Catalog type:** Boost / Preamp
- **Identity:** Finnish pedal recreating the channel strip of a vintage Japanese cassette recorder, with added filtering and modern reliability features.

## What this pedal is
Color Audio describes the Cassette Preamp as the channel strip of a vintage Japanese cassette recorder in pedal form. Although the original circuit concept was intended for microphone/line preamplification, the pedal is explicitly designed to work with guitar, bass, and direct recording setups. Its range runs from compressed clean sounds through saturated and blown-out fuzz textures. [1][2]

## Controls
The documented control set includes:
- **Gain:** moves from clean through saturation to heavily blown-out tones.
- **LPF:** high-frequency roll-off, with fully clockwise as filter-off.
- **Level:** output volume.
- **Bass:** ±10dB low-frequency control in a passive two-band tone stack.
- **HPF:** three-way low-frequency filter before the gain stage.
- **Treble:** ±10dB high-frequency control in the passive two-band tone stack. [1]

The HPF settings let the lowest frequencies hit the gain stage for bassier/fuzzier behavior or filter them for a more forward distortion response. [1]

## Colorways
- The documented production enclosure is black with red "cassette" lettering.
- A current European retailer identifies the item as reference CA-CP-BK-V1, providing evidence of a black production variant. [3]

## Versions and factory options
- **9V-18V DC operation** is documented, allowing headroom to be varied by supply voltage. [3]
- Relay-switched true bypass with automatic bypass on power loss is documented. [3]
- Top-mounted Neutrik jacks, Alpha potentiometers, reverse/over-voltage protection, and a rugged aluminum enclosure are specified. [1][3]

## Version changes
No separate hardware revision chronology beyond the documented current production reference CA-CP-BK-V1 was established in the checked sources.

## Construction
Color Audio states that the pedal uses a modernized part-for-part reproduction of the source circuit while adding modern manufacturing refinements and lowering the noise floor. The documented active devices include **NJM2068** and **M5218** op-amps. [1]

## Transistor

- The published active-device information centers on NJM2068 and M5218 op-amps. [1]

## Sound
Color Audio emphasizes a raw, compressed DI character that remains usable with guitar and bass directly into an interface or as a preamp in front of an amplifier. The variable HPF/LPF stages expand the range from tighter distortion to bassier, fuzzier saturation, while the passive Bass/Treble stack provides broad tonal shaping. [1][2]
