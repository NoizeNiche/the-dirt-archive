# Biyang - OD-12 X-Drive

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Biyang
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive

## Research
The OD-12 X-Drive is a Biyang Classic-series overdrive with three switchable voices: TS, BRIGHT, and WARM. Effects Database documents a JRC4558-based analog circuit, true bypass, interchangeable op-amp access, WIMA capacitors, and 9V battery/DC operation. The pedal was positioned as a flexible, amp-like overdrive spanning classic, brighter, and warmer voicings.
