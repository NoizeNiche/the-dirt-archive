# Biyang - FZ-12 Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Biyang
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz

## Research
The FZ-12 is a Biyang Classic-series fuzz introduced as a newer iteration of the company's earlier FZ-7 fuzz. The documented control set is Level, Tone, and Fuzz, with a three-position MUFF, BRIGHT, and WARM mode switch. Available product documentation identifies it as a Chinese-made, true-bypass pedal.
