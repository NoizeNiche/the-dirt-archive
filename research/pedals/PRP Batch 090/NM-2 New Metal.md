# Biyang - NM-2 New Metal

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Biyang
- **Catalog type:** Distortion

## Research
The NM-2 New Metal is one of Biyang's early signature metal-oriented distortion pedals. Contemporary Chinese coverage identifies it as a flagship metal effect that had already been used for recording and live performance by multiple Chinese rock and metal musicians by the mid-2000s. The documented history supports its role as a high-gain metal distortion, but the checked sources do not provide a sufficiently complete control-by-control technical specification for the archive.
