# Biyang - FZ-7 Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Biyang
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz

## Research
The FZ-7 is a Biyang Tonefancier fuzz pedal introduced in 2007. Effects Database documents three available fuzz tones, while RockBoard describes it as an analog fuzz with a three-way voicing concept and notes its relationship to the smaller FZ-10 Fuzz Star. Contemporary coverage identifies the three-position character as MUFF, BRIGHT, and WARM.
