# Biyang - OD-8 X-Drive

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Biyang
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive

## Research
The OD-8 X-Drive is a Biyang Tonefancier overdrive designed around a changeable op-amp/chip concept. Effects Database documents three tonal modes and the ability to change the chip without soldering, while other contemporary references describe its TS-style foundation and wider tonal range. It uses a Volume, Tone, Drive control layout with TS, BRIGHT, and WARM modes.

## Deep research verification
The cited Effects Database and Effects-Pedals sources support OD-8 X-Drive as a Tonefancier overdrive with **Volume, Tone, Drive** and **TS, BRIGHT, WARM** modes. Its defining feature is a changeable plug-in op-amp/chip arrangement that permits chip changes without soldering. Exact stock chip supplied with each production unit is not established by the reviewed sources.
