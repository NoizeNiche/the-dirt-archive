# Biyang - DS-9 Distortion

## PRP identity

- **Builder:** Biyang
- **Catalog type:** Distortion

## Research

The DS-9 is a Biyang Tonefancier distortion pedal from the later part of the original Tonefancier range. Effects Pedals describes a simple Level and Distortion control layout and true bypass operation. Contemporary testing described its character as similar to a modified Boss DS-1, with a broad distortion range from vintage-leaning blues textures through a harder punk-oriented voice.
