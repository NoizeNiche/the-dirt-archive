# Biyang - Junky Drive

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Biyang
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive

## Research
Junky Drive is a Biyang Tonefancier drive pedal covering both overdrive and distortion territory. A documented three-position mode switch provides three related voices: an edge-focused British sound, a broader and louder mode, and a +1 mode derived from the first with added midrange and reduced edge. The documented circuit description cites a Burr-Brown OPA2134PA op-amp, WIMA capacitors, true bypass, and 9V operation.
