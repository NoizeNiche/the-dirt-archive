# Biyang - Metal-End

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Biyang
- **Catalog type:** Distortion

## Research
Metal-End is a Biyang Tonefancier distortion family documented with ME-29 Metal-End and ME-36 Metal-End King variants. Effects Database describes it as a high-quality-parts Tonefancier design aimed at a tube-like distortion character. RockBoard lists it as an analog distortion with extensive tone-shaping controls and a 9V center-negative supply.
