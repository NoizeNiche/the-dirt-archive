# Biyang - DS-8 Mouse

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Biyang
- **Catalog type:** Distortion

## Research
The DS-8 Mouse is a Biyang Tonefancier distortion pedal introduced in 2008. Effects Database describes three clipping modes, NORMAL, TURBO, and MAX, with the modes moving from a smoother, more natural distortion toward thicker output and greater sustain. Contemporary coverage identifies it as a RAT-family style distortion while noting its own voicing and a three-control Volume, Filter, Distortion layout.
