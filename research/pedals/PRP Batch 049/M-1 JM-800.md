# AMT Electronics — M-1 JM-800

## What this pedal is
A Legend Amp series amp-simulation/preamp pedal based on Marshall JCM-800-style gain and response. The M-1 was introduced in the 2008 Legend Amp series. [1]

## Sound
The M-1 targets the classic JCM-800-style rock distortion range, from dynamic crunch through higher-gain lead sounds. AMT emphasizes amp-like dynamics and response to guitar-volume changes. [1]
