# Amsterdam Cream — Plain Drive

## What this pedal is
A compact Korean-made low-gain overdrive. Contemporary descriptions identify it as a transparent/cleanly voiced drive that can also become a coarse vintage-fuzz-like sound as the Drive control is advanced. [1][2]

## Colorways
- Green finish documented in period retail listings. [1]
- Hand-painted variants also existed. A later listing documents a hand-painted version with stronger mids, a changed clipping diode, and increased drive. [3]

## Versions and factory options
- Compact enclosure
- Drive control
- Low-gain boost/overdrive character
- Green production finish documented [1]

## Version changes
A documented hand-painted version added midrange emphasis, changed the clipping diode, and increased available drive compared with the earlier Plain Drive. [3]

## Transistor
- Exact transistor/device not documented.

## Diode
- A version change involving a different clipping diode is documented, but the exact diode types are not identified. [3]

## Sound
Plain Drive can function as a low-gain boost/overdrive with relatively open EQ behavior. Reports describe a marked transition toward coarse, vintage-fuzz-like texture when Drive is pushed beyond roughly the upper-middle range. [2]
