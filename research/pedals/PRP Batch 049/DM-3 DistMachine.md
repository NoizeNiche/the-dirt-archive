# AMT Electronics — DM-3 DistMachine

## What this pedal is
A programmable multi-mode distortion unit with editable patches, multiple banks, amp/mic simulation, and digital-style preset management in a pedal format. [1][2]

## Versions and factory options
- Level, Low, High, Drive
- Four banks of patches
- Patch navigation footswitches
- Save, Copy, Undo functions
- Combo, Mic, Mod, and amp-simulation parameters
- Balanced output for mixer/line use [1][2]

## Sound
DM-3 is designed as a broad distortion workstation rather than a single fixed distortion voice. Its programmable architecture covers screaming lead, rock, metal, grunge/crunch, and amp/cab-oriented direct applications. [1][2]
