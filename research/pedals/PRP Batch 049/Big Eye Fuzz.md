# Amsterdam Cream — Big Eye Fuzz

## What this pedal is
A compact Korean-made fuzz using a silicone transistor circuit. Amsterdam Cream describes it as a wild, vintage-oriented fuzz with rich harmonics, strong low/mid body, high output, and long sustain. [1]

## Colorways
- Original compact Amsterdam Cream enclosure documented.
- Later “Bigbox” versions are documented in Effects Database media, but a complete factory colorway/version history was not established. [2]

## Versions and factory options
- Compact mini-pedal format
- True bypass
- IN/OUT jacks
- Hand made in Korea
- Published dimensions: 91 × 38 × 30 mm [1]

## Version changes
- A later Bigbox version is visible in manufacturer media, but exact circuit changes were not established. [2]

## Transistor
- Silicone transistor documented by the manufacturer. [1]
- Exact part number not documented.

## Diode
- Exact clipping diode not documented.

## Sound
Big Eye Fuzz is voiced for a high-output, vintage-flavored fuzz with long sustain and rich harmonics. The manufacturer specifically emphasizes a large overall volume and a “weeping” sustained character. [1]
