# AMT Electronics — BS British Sound

## What this pedal is

## Sound
The BS is voiced toward cranked British stack tones, with separate low/high shaping and a dedicated Drive control. The output-emulation circuitry is intended to make the pedal usable into an amp, combo, mixer, or recorder input. [1]
