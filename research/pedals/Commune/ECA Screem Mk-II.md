# Commune/ECA - Screem Mk-II

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Commune/ECA (Empire Custom Amplification)
- **Catalog identity:** Screem Mk-II
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** Rare Japanese Commune/ECA distortion model documented as Screem Mk-II in early-2000s touring records.

## What this pedal is
Screem Mk-II is documented in the equipment records for DIR EN GREY bassist Toshiya, who used a **Commune/ECA Screem Mk-II** during the 2004 "KEEN UNDER THE SUN" tour period. The same source notes that available information is scarce and describes it as a distortion pedal. [1]
