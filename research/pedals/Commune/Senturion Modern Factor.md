# Commune - Senturion Modern Factor

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Commune
- **Catalog identity:** Senturion Modern Factor
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive / Distortion
- **Identity:** Japanese bass-focused overdrive/distortion with blend, strong boost capability, three-stage gain selection, and three-band EQ.

## What this pedal is
Senturion Modern Factor was designed as a bass-specific overdrive/distortion. Contemporary user and retailer descriptions emphasize retaining bass fundamentals by mixing dry and driven signals, while providing enough boost and gain range to cover overdrive through distortion. [1][2]

## Controls
The documented front panel uses:
- **ROAR BOOST:** dry/original-signal level.
- **MODERN:** drive/effected-signal amount.
- **HI**
- **MID**
- **LOW:** three-band EQ.
- **OPERATE:** overall operation/level control. [1][2]

A source description also identifies three selectable distortion stages/gain settings. [1]

## Sound
The Modern Factor is described as producing a full, forward bass drive while retaining the core of the instrument through dry/effected mixing. The gain range spans moderate overdrive to more aggressive distortion, and the three-band EQ provides broad frequency shaping. [1][2]
