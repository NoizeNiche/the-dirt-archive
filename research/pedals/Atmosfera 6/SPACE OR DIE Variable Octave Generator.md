# Atmosfera 6 — SPACE OR DIE Variable Octave Generator

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** SPACE OR DIE Variable Octave Generator
- **Builder:** Atmosfera 6
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Analog octave-up, booster and overdrive with SPACE and DIE modes.

## What this pedal is
Effects Database documents SPACE as a Green-Ringer-based octave-up circuit and DIE as an Elektra-based gritty overdrive. The pedal is intended to precede other distortion or fuzz.

## Colorways
- No reliable production colorway history was established.

## Versions and factory options
### SPACE mode
- Green-Ringer-based octave up
- LEVEL controls octave intensity
- LEVEL at zero functions as a clean booster

### DIE mode
- Elektra-based overdrive
- LEVEL blends octave content
- LEVEL at zero functions as boosted overdrive

## Version changes
SPACE/DIE is the documented functional variation; no numbered hardware revision was verified.

## Sound
SPACE adds a pronounced octave-up component that can move toward octave-fuzz when placed into dirt. DIE shifts toward gritty overdrive with the octave mixed back in through LEVEL.
