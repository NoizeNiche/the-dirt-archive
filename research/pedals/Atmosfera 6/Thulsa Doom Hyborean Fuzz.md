# Atmosfera 6 — Thulsa Doom Hyborean Fuzz

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** Thulsa Doom Hyborean Fuzz
- **Builder:** Atmosfera 6
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Fuzz
- **Identity:** Heavy fuzz derived from a Colorsound/Meathead-style circuit, with separate bass and gain-voice toggles. The builder presents it as a crushing, low-end-focused fuzz rather than a strict single-model clone. [1][2]

## What this pedal is

The Thulsa Doom is designed around very heavy fuzz and enhanced bass response. Atmosfera 6's exact-model listings describe two toggles that independently alter bass response and gain voice, giving four functional combinations. [1][2]

## Controls

- **FLESH:** Master/output level.
- **Bass / standard voice toggle:** Chooses the bass-emphasis mode.
- **Gain voice toggle:** Selects the alternate gain character.
- The documented alternate gain setting modifies transistor feedback. [1]

## Sound

Atmosfera 6 positions the Thulsa Doom as a crushing, bass-heavy fuzz. One toggle extends the low-frequency response, while the alternate gain voice uses modified transistor feedback to push the circuit further toward aggressive distortion. The two toggles provide four combinations of bass and gain character. [1][2]
