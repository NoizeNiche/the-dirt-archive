# Atmosfera 6 — Thulsa Doom Hyborean Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Thulsa Doom Hyborean Fuzz
- **Builder:** Atmosfera 6
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Fuzz
- **Identity:** High-gain fuzz based on a Colorsound/Meathead-style circuit with four selectable voicing combinations.

## What this pedal is
The builder's listing describes a Colorsound/Meathead-derived design with enhanced bass response. One toggle selects bass response and the other selects the gain voice.

## Colorways
- Oldschool black with bone-white print
- Blood Red edition with layered print

## Versions and factory options
### Thulsa Doom production
- FLESH master volume
- Standard / enhanced bass toggle
- Classic / alternate gain voice toggle
- Alternate voice uses modified transistor feedback
- Hand-soldered construction
- Made in Spain

## Version changes
The documented differences are voicing modes and graphic finishes, not a numbered revision sequence.

## Transistor
- **Technology:** Transistor gain stage in the documented Colorsound/Meathead-style topology.
- **Exact device:** Unknown.

## Diode

## Sound
It is voiced for crushing, bass-heavy fuzz and can push further into distortion through the alternate feedback voice. The two toggles independently change bass response and gain character.
