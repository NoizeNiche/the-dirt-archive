# Atmosfera 6 — TERROR COSMICO

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** TERROR COSMICO
- **Builder:** Atmosfera 6
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Fuzz
- **Identity:** Full-germanium distortion fuzz based on a modified Tone Bender MK.III circuit.

## What this pedal is
Effects Database documents TERROR COSMICO as a modified Tone Bender MK.III design using hand-selected NOS Soviet germanium transistors and diodes.

## Colorways
- Satin black / relic-metal presentation
- Stone or sand-style presentation
- Listings document different indicator-light treatments

## Versions and factory options
### TERROR COSMICO production
- Volume
- Distortion
- INVASION / ABDUCCION voicing toggle
- Positive-ground 9V DC
- Isolated power supply required
- Hand-wired and hand-painted in Spain

The alternate voice is documented as a circuit/output-capacitor variation.

## Sound
The design covers crunchy drive into aggressive high-gain fuzz. The vintage Tone Bender basis, germanium clipping and voicing control are central to the documented character.
