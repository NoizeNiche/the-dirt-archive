# Atmosfera 6 — TERROR COSMICO

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** TERROR COSMICO
- **Builder:** Atmosfera 6
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Fuzz
- **Identity:** Germanium distortion/fuzz based on a modified Tone Bender MKIII concept, using hand-selected NOS Soviet germanium transistors and a germanium diode. The builder does not present it as a literal vintage MKIII clone. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
TERROR COSMICO is a high-gain germanium distortion/fuzz designed around the aggressive, wide-band behavior associated with vintage high-gain fuzz. Effects Database identifies a modified Tone Bender MKIII circuit, while an independent 2023 demonstration emphasizes its dynamic response and its ability to move between distortion and traditional fuzz. [1][2]

## Controls / voicing
- **VOLUME:** Output level.
- **DISTORTION:** Amount of drive/distortion.
- **INVASION / ABDUCCION:** Voicing switch affecting the circuit/output-capacitor behavior. [1]
The pedal's two-knob core is intentionally simple, with the mode switch supplying the primary alternate voice.

## Circuit lineage
- **Modified Tone Bender MKIII-inspired architecture.**
- Atmosfera 6's own/product evidence should be treated as the authority for this exact pedal.
- Independent demonstration material explicitly notes that it is **not intended as a literal MKIII replica**. [2]

## Transistor
- **Technology:** Germanium.

- Exact transistor numbers are not consistently published in the reviewed exact-model sources. [1][2]
## Diode / clipping
- **Technology:** Germanium diode.
- An independent 2023 review/demo explicitly describes **one NOS germanium diode** in the build.
- Exact diode part number is not documented. [2]

## Construction / hardware
- Hand-wired / hand-built in Spain is documented.
- True bypass is documented by independent demo material.
- The product appears in multiple cosmetic versions, including Sand/Black and Gold Edition examples. [1][2]

## Version history
- **INVA**/alternate voicing is a built-in functional variation rather than a numbered revision.
- Effects Database documents multiple finishes/editions.
- No sequential numbered hardware revision was established.

## Power
- The reviewed sources indicate external pedal power and no internal battery operation for the demonstrated build.
- A fixed universal voltage/current specification was not established with sufficient confidence from the reviewed evidence.

## Sound
Independent demonstration material describes TERROR COSMICO as highly dynamic, with strong attack, substantial bandwidth and useful cleanup from pick dynamics and the guitar volume knob. It can cover dynamic distortion as well as more traditional fuzz, and the alternate mode is especially useful for lower-frequency instruments. These are source-described observations, not measured frequency-response data. [2]
