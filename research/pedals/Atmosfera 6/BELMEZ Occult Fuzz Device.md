# Atmosfera 6 — BELMEZ Occult Fuzz Device

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** BELMEZ Occult Fuzz Device
- **Builder:** Atmosfera 6
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Experimental heavy fuzz developed from a Roland AD-50 starting point with Orange/Matamp-style tone shaping.

## What this pedal is
Atmosfera 6 describes BELMEZ as an experimental heavy fuzz refined after two years of development, with two operating modes and a Shape control spanning multiple EQ balances.
