## What this pedal is

BELMEZ is described as a heavy, experimental fuzz developed over an extended design period. The exact-model listing emphasizes a large-grain, powerful fuzz with deep low end that can also suit bass. [1]

## Controls / modes

- **VOLUME:** Output level.
- **SHAPE:** Internal double potentiometer balancing two EQ stages, providing a broad range from scooped to mid-forward and low-heavy tones.
- **GAIN:** Overall gain.
- **STD:** Original BELMEZ circuit.
- **EXP:** Alternate BELMEZ version affecting gain and EQ and expanding the available tonal range. [1]
