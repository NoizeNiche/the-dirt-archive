## Sound

Red Snapper Overdrive Review: Pretty much everything but metal.
The pedal has two controls for gain (both the potmeter for 'Gain' and 'Bite' control the gain of the pedal) and a single control for the output volume ('Vol').
As you can spot both the 'Gain' and 'Bite' knob changes the gain of the pedal in their own way.
