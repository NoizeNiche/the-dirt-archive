## Sound

Based on the same circuit but with the added 'PORK' control for more gain.
The PORK control turns the first gain stage from a unity gain buffer, to a 12db exrtra boost stage similar to a MKii fuzz.
The Law Bender goes from light overdrive to full blown nastiness.
