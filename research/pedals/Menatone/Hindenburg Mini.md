## What this pedal is
The Hindenburg Mini is Menatone's compact version of the Hindenburg, based on the Supro Thunderbolt amplifier associated with early Led Zeppelin-era sounds.

## Versions and factory options
- Same parts and circuit as the original Hindenburg.
- Full-size through-hole components.
- Handmade in the USA.
- 9V power supply only, no battery.
- Approx. 1.5 × 3.66 inches.

## Version changes
- The Mini is a compact-format derivative rather than a separate circuit.

## Sound
Hindenburg is voiced for large, fat British-style drive with a grinding midrange and plenty of bass. Gain can move from clean boost into full rock distortion.
