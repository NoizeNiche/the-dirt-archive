## What this pedal is
The Blue Collar Mini is the compact version of Menatone's Blue Collar overdrive. Menatone describes the original as a fat, midrange-heavy drive created in response to demand for more gain and compression than the Red Snapper.

## Versions and factory options
- 2025 Mini adds a **Presence** control.
- Same basic Blue Collar voicing.
- Handmade in the USA.
- 9V power supply only, no battery.
- Approx. 1.5 × 3.66 inches.

## Version changes
- The 2025 Mini adds Presence while retaining the core Blue Collar circuit.

## Sound
Blue Collar has a fat, midrange-forward voice with a gain range from just above clean to smooth, sustaining grind. Menatone specifically notes its affinity for single coils.
