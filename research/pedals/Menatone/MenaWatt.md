## What this pedal is

MenaWatt is Menatone's interpretation of the 1960s and 1970s British amplifier family, using internally jumpered Normal and Brilliant gain sections.

## Versions and factory options

- **V.III / 2025 version** is documented.
- Normal and Brilliant first-stage gain controls.
- Passive EQ based on the HiWatt tone stack.
- Volume.
- Internal trim for additional gain.
- V.III adds **Power Gain** and **Presence**.
- Top-mounted jacks.
- Handmade in the USA.

## Version changes

- V.III adds Power Gain and Presence while retaining the internally jumpered Normal/Brilliant architecture.

## Sound

MenaWatt is brash and articulate with a tight bass response and substantial midrange. The internally jumpered gain sections let the Normal and Brilliant sides be blended like the inputs of a vintage amplifier.
