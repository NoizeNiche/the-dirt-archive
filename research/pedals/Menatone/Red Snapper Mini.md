## What this pedal is
The Red Snapper Mini is the compact version of Menatone's classic three-knob Red Snapper overdrive.

## Versions and factory options
- Same circuit as the original three-knob Red Snapper when the internal treble-cut switches are off.
- Internal treble-cut switches.
- Through-hole construction.
- Handmade in the USA.
- 9V power supply only, no battery.
- Approx. 1.5 × 3.66 inches.
- V3 is referenced in the archive evidence.

## Version changes
- The Mini adds internal treble-cut options without changing the core three-knob circuit when those switches are off.
