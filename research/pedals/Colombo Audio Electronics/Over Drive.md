# Colombo Audio Electronics - Over Drive

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Colombo Audio Electronics
- **Catalog identity:** Over Drive
- **Catalog type:** Booster / Overdrive
- **Identity:** Boutique recreation of the earliest Boss OD-1 production concept, specifically the 1977-1981 quad-op-amp era.

## What this pedal is
Colombo Audio Electronics describes Over Drive as a recreation of the sonic character of early OD-1 units from 1977-1981, centered on the quad-op-amp architecture associated with those earliest examples. The pedal is intended for smooth, progressive saturation rather than extreme distortion and can also serve as a clean boost. [1][2]

## Controls
- **Level:** overall output volume.
- **Over Drive:** gain/saturation amount. [1][2]

## Bypass options
Colombo offers two bypass configurations:
- **Original Buffered Bypass:** intended to retain the signal-conditioning behavior of the historical circuit.
- **True Bypass:** removes the pedal from the signal path when off. [1][2]

## Colorways
- The manufacturer offers graphic customization.
- Custom artwork is therefore treated as a finish/customization state rather than an automatic separate model. [1]

## Versions and factory options
The current documented production option is the selectable bypass configuration above. Colombo specifically targets the early 1977-1981 circuit era, but no numbered Colombo V1/V2 production sequence was established. [1]

## Version changes
No reliable Colombo-specific dated hardware revision chronology was established from the checked sources.

## Construction
Colombo describes Over Drive as hand-built with selected components and individual testing. [1] The broader Colombo construction practice is described as handmade point-to-point construction without printed circuit boards. [3]

## Transistor
- **Exact transistor/device:** Not publicly documented in the checked sources.

## Sound
The target response is warm, dynamic, progressively compressive, and sensitive to picking attack and guitar-volume changes. With low Over Drive and high Level it can be used to push an amplifier; higher settings provide a vintage-style crunch. [1][2]
