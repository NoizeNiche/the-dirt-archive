# Colombo Audio Electronics - Plexi Breed

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Colombo Audio Electronics
- **Catalog identity:** Plexi Breed
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive / Distortion / Preamp
- **Identity:** Three-voice analog British amp-style pedal covering JTM45, 1959 Plexi, and 1987 50W Plexi references.

## What this pedal is
Plexi Breed is designed around three early Marshall-style voices:
- **JTM:** JTM45-inspired response, including the perceived sag behavior associated with its rectifier supply.
- **1959:** 100W Plexi-inspired high-headroom voice.
- **1987:** 50W Plexi-inspired voice with somewhat more pronounced distortion and faster attack. [1]

## Controls
The shared controls are:
- **Normal:** gain of the darker/low-end-oriented channel.
- **High Treble:** gain of the brighter, mid/high-focused channel.
- **Tone:** overall high/low tonal correction.
- **Volume:** output level. [1]

The pedal can blend the two channel controls, or either channel can effectively be excluded by turning that channel down. [1]

## Colorways and orientation
- Colombo provides an orientation choice for the pedal.
- Graphic customization is also offered. [1]
- Orientation and artwork are kept as factory/customization options, not separate circuits.

## Versions and factory options
- **9V or 18V operation:** the pedal automatically configures its bias for the selected supply.
- Colombo states that 18V provides more headroom and a less lo-fi footprint. [1]
- No numbered production revision was established.

## Sound
Plexi Breed is aimed at the attack, channel interaction, and gain behavior of early Marshall Plexi-family amplifiers. The manufacturer records its demonstrations into an extremely clean Marshall JCM900 Dual Reverb and 4x12 cabinet. [1]
