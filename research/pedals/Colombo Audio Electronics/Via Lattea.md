# Colombo Audio Electronics - Via Lattea

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Colombo Audio Electronics
- **Catalog identity:** Via Lattea
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive / Booster
- **Identity:** High-gain Tube Screamer-derived overdrive with a parallel clean blend/boost path.

## What this pedal is
Via Lattea was developed around the question of what an overdrive should provide, using the TS808 family as a starting point while extending gain, output, and clean-signal preservation. Colombo describes it as a universal overdrive with substantially more available gain than a standard Tube Screamer and a dedicated Clean control for mixing unchanged guitar signal in parallel with the overdriven path. [1]

## Controls
The published controls include:
- **Overdrive:** saturation amount.
- **Tone:** tonal balance.
- **Clean:** parallel unaltered clean-signal amount and clean boost contribution.
- **Volume:** overall output. [1]

Colombo states that the Clean path can provide up to +20dB and can be used by itself as a clean boost with the overdrive controls at zero. [1]

## Sound
The three published examples include low-gain funky overdrive, a higher-gain rock setting, and a 60s surf/rock setting. Colombo emphasizes preserved picking dynamics from the clean parallel path, while still allowing substantial overdrive gain and output. [1]
