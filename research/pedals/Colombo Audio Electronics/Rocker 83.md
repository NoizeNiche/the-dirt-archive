# Colombo Audio Electronics - Rocker 83

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Colombo Audio Electronics
- **Catalog identity:** Rocker 83
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive / Distortion
- **Identity:** Analog pedal developed to evoke the guitar tone of the Rockman X100 pocket amp and the 1983-1989 80s recording aesthetic.

## What this pedal is
Colombo developed Rocker 83 as a conventional stompbox version of the tonal concept associated with the Rockman X100. The manufacturer explicitly says it is not the original X100 and is designed to work with an ordinary guitar amplifier rather than requiring the original unit's specialized power arrangements. [1]

## Controls and switching
The documented layout uses three primary knobs:
- **Volume**
- **Treble**
- **Gain**

A front-panel **Edge / Dist** function changes the distortion character. [1][2]

## Colorways
- A blue/black Rocker 83 finish is documented in marketplace photography. [3]
- Colombo's custom-pedal line also includes Rocker 83 Stellar, a separate custom combination of Rocker 83 and stereo chorus. That functional addition makes Stellar a distinct catalog product rather than a cosmetic Rocker 83 variation. [4]

## Versions and factory options

- Approximate current draw: 10mA. [1]
