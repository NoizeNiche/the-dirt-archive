# Colombo Audio Electronics - Southern Stalker

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Colombo Audio Electronics
- **Catalog identity:** Southern Stalker
- **Catalog type:** Booster / Treble Booster / Overdrive / Preamp
- **Identity:** Colombo's American-style boost design aimed at Stevie Ray Vaughan-style and classic Southern-blues guitar applications.

## What this pedal is
Colombo identifies Southern Stalker as an American-style treble booster, and its catalog describes the model across booster, treble-booster, overdrive, and preamp roles. [1][2]

## Controls
The surviving exact-model product photograph and retailer description show:
- **Boost**
- **Twang**
- **Blackface Output**
- a mode switch selecting **Boost** or **Boost + Blackface**. [2]

## Colorways
- A silver/metallic finish with black Southern Stalker artwork is documented in current retailer photography. [2]
- Cosmetic changes are treated as finish/customization unless circuit changes are established.

Colombo describes its pedals as handmade in Italy. Its construction pages describe point-to-point assembly without printed circuit boards. [3]

## Sound
Colombo positions Southern Stalker within its treble-booster family and specifically describes it as an American/SRV-oriented design. The additional Blackface mode extends the boost concept toward a fuller Fender-style preamp character. [1][2]
