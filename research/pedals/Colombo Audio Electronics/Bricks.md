# Colombo Audio Electronics - Bricks

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Bricks
- **Builder:** Colombo Audio Electronics
- **Catalog identity:** Bricks
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Fuzz / Boost / EQ
- **Identity:** Colombo Audio Electronics Bricks, a dual-block overdrive/distortion and boost pedal designed around David Gilmour-associated gain and EQ approaches.

## What this pedal is
Colombo Audio Electronics describes Bricks as a double pedal with separate **Distortion** and **Boost** blocks that can be used individually or together in series. The circuits are original designs influenced by several gain and EQ approaches associated with David Gilmour's rigs. [1]

## Distortion block
The Distortion side combines a pre-boost, distortion stage, tone control, and output volume. Colombo describes the pre-boost as similar in purpose to a Tube Screamer-style push used to drive a Big Muff-type distortion stage, while the distortion section draws on characteristics associated with Ram's Head, Civil War, and Cornish P-2-style circuits. [1]

Controls:
- Pre-Boost.
- Distortion.
- Tone.
- Volume. [1]

## Boost block
The Boost side is intended to cover boost/EQ, overdrive, and fuzz-like operation through the interaction of its gain stage and controls.

Controls:
- Gain.
- Treble.
- Bass.
- Output. [1]

Colombo's recommended ranges include boost/EQ operation at lower-to-moderate gain, Gilmour-style overdrive at higher gain with reduced treble, and a high-gain setting for a fuzz-like character. [1]

## Circuit / construction
The manufacturer states that Colombo pedals use fully hand-wired point-to-point construction with no printed circuit boards. Bricks uses original circuits rather than a literal copy of the reference units, despite those influences. The accessible documentation does not establish exact transistor or diode part numbers. [1][2]

## Routing
The Distortion and Boost blocks can be used independently or simultaneously in series, allowing the player to combine pre-boost, distortion, and boost/EQ stages. [1]

## Sound
Bricks is designed to cover multiple Gilmour-associated gain roles in one enclosure, from a Big Muff-oriented saturated distortion through boost/EQ and warmer overdrive textures. The dual-block architecture permits different combinations rather than a single fixed voicing. [1]

## Versions and finish
Current resale photography shows Bricks in a silver/white enclosure styled with a brick-wall graphic and red BRICKS lettering. Colombo also documents broad enclosure and graphic customization across its product line, so finish should not be treated as a circuit revision without separate evidence. [2][3]

## Power
RockBoard's PedalPedia documents Bricks as an analog 9V DC, center-negative pedal with approximately 58 mA current draw. [4]
