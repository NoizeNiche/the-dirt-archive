# Colombo Audio Electronics - Muff Blaster

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Colombo Audio Electronics
- **Catalog identity:** Muff Blaster
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz / Distortion
- **Identity:** Four-control, three-mode fuzz/distortion design drawing directly from three historically distinct Big Muff families.

## What this pedal is
Muff Blaster is Colombo's multi-mode Muff-oriented fuzz/distortion pedal. The manufacturer documents three modes inspired by the **Ram's Head**, **Civil War**, and **Green Russian** Big Muff families. [1]

## Controls and modes
Four potentiometers are documented:
- **Sustain:** distortion and sustain amount.
- **Tone:** frequency balance.
- **Volume:** output level.
- **Gate:** reduces the gate effect as it is turned toward maximum; lower settings introduce increasingly degraded/lo-fi behavior. [1]

The three-position mode switch is:
- **RAM:** Ram's Head-inspired.
- **WAR:** Civil War-inspired, described by Colombo as somewhat more defined and less aggressive than the other modes.
- **SOV:** Green Russian-inspired, described as more scratchy and wild. [1]

## Sound
The pedal is designed to cover three major Muff tonal families while adding a dedicated gate/lo-fi control. Colombo's examples use clean-set Marshall and Supro amplifiers and demonstrate the RAM, WAR, and SOV voices at different sustain, tone, gate, and volume settings. [1]
