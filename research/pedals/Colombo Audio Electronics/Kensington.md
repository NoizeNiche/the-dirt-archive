# Colombo Audio Electronics - Kensington

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Colombo Audio Electronics
- **Catalog identity:** Kensington
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive / Preamp
- **Identity:** Vox AC30-oriented preamp/overdrive pedal developed around the Brian May-style treble-booster-to-AC30 signal concept.

## What this pedal is
Colombo Audio Electronics describes Kensington as a Vox-inspired preamp/overdrive intended to reproduce the character of a Vox AC30, with the pedal usable at lower volume than a real high-powered amplifier. The manufacturer documents Gain, Cut, and Volume controls. [1]

## Colorways
- Colombo offers customization of the pedal graphics. [1]
- Surviving marketplace examples show multiple finishes, including a dark blue/black patterned face and a white enclosure with the same general Kensington identity. [2][3]
- These examples are treated as finish/customization evidence, not automatically as separate revisions.

## Versions and factory options
No numbered factory revision was verified.
- **Gain** controls distortion amount.
- **Cut** filters the high frequencies.
- **Volume** controls output level. [1]
- Colombo presents Kensington separately from Kensington Star, which adds cabinet-simulation functionality and is therefore retained as a distinct catalog identity rather than a cosmetic Kensington variation. [4]

## Construction
Colombo's general construction documentation states that its pedals are handmade in Italy using point-to-point construction without printed circuit boards. [5]

## Sound
The manufacturer demonstrates Kensington in Vox AC30-style settings and with Colombo treble boosters placed before it. The documented example setting is Gain 8/10, Cut 9/10, Volume 3/10. [1]
