# Colombo Audio Electronics - Lead Trilogy

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Colombo Audio Electronics
- **Catalog identity:** Lead Trilogy
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** Fully analog British-voiced distortion covering three Marshall high-gain reference voices from the 1980s and 1990s.

## What this pedal is
Lead Trilogy is a three-voice distortion pedal built to condense the characters of three Marshall amplifier references into a compact pedal. Colombo and independent demonstrations identify the voices as **800**, **900**, and **Jubilee**. [1][2]

## Versions and factory options
Controls:
- **Gain**
- **Tone**
- **Volume** [1]

The three-way voice section covers:
- **800:** mid-rich, direct JCM800-oriented response.
- **900:** more compressed distortion and sustain.
- **Jubilee:** asymmetric-clipping character associated with the Silver Jubilee. [1]

## Colorways
- Colombo's pedal line is customizable in graphics and finish. [3]
- A Reverb example documents the standard Lead Trilogy identity and Italian manufacture. [4]
- Finish differences are not treated as separate model revisions without supporting hardware evidence.

## Version changes
No numbered production revision sequence was established from the checked sources.

## Construction
Colombo describes Lead Trilogy as a fully analog circuit and its broader pedal line as handmade point-to-point without PCBs. [1][3]

## Transistor

## Diode
- **Exact clipping diode/device:** Not publicly documented in the checked sources.
- Colombo specifically describes the Jubilee mode as recreating the character of an asymmetric clipping circuit, but does not publish the individual diode part numbers in the sources reviewed. [1]

## Sound
The three voices move from the more mid-forward 800 character through the more compressed 900 voice to the upper-harmonic, asymmetric-clipping emphasis of the Jubilee setting. Brett Kingman's 2021 demo confirms the three-mode concept and identifies Lead Trilogy as a Colombo Audio Electronics workshop design by Luca Colombo. [1][2]
