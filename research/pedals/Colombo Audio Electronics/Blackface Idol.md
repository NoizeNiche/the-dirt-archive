# Colombo Audio Electronics - Blackface Idol

- **Identity:** Colombo Audio Electronics Blackface Idol, a Fender Blackface-inspired overdrive/preamp with switchable Super, Twin, and Deluxe voicings.

## What this pedal is
Colombo Audio Electronics designed Blackface Idol around three representative Fender Blackface amplifier voices: **Super**, **Twin**, and **Deluxe**. The manufacturer describes it as usable either as a conventional overdrive or as a preamp-like device for adding Blackface characteristics to another amplifier. [1]

## Controls and voicing
- Gain: distortion amount, intended to behave more like the volume control of the reference amplifiers.
- Bass: low-frequency response.
- Treble: high-frequency response.
- Volume: main output level.
- Three-position mode switch: Super / Twin / Deluxe. [1]

The manufacturer documents the three modes as having distinct characteristics:
- **Super:** richer low end and more natural compression.
- **Twin:** similar low-frequency foundation with less compression and much higher headroom.
- **Deluxe:** narrower frequency range, pronounced mids, and a spongier compression character. [1]

## Circuit / construction
Colombo Audio Electronics says Blackface Idol combines **MOSFET and JFET** technologies to recreate the interaction and sonic behavior of the reference tube amplifiers electronically rather than merely presenting a generic distortion simulation. The broader Colombo pedal line is built point-to-point without printed circuit boards. Exact transistor part numbers and diode part numbers are not published in the accessible documentation. [1][2]

## Sound
Blackface Idol is intended to preserve a transparent, amp-like response while shifting the frequency balance, compression, and headroom characteristics between its Super, Twin, and Deluxe settings. The manufacturer demonstrates the pedal with both single-coil and humbucker guitars. [1]

## Versions and finish
A current Reverb listing identifies a black finish, while other retail images show the same Blackface Idol design in the black enclosure with the distinctive grille graphic. Colombo also offers custom enclosure graphics and colors elsewhere in its product range, so finish should not be treated as a circuit revision without evidence. [2][3]
