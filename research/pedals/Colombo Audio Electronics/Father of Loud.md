# Colombo Audio Electronics - Father of Loud

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Father of Loud
- **Builder:** Colombo Audio Electronics
- **Catalog identity:** Father of Loud
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive / Distortion
- **Identity:** Colombo Audio Electronics Father of Loud, a dual-section Marshall-style amp-in-a-box combining the Plexi Breed and Lead Trilogy circuits in one enclosure.

## What this pedal is
Colombo Audio Electronics describes Father of Loud as a custom pedal that combines its **Plexi Breed** and **Lead Trilogy** designs, providing six Marshall-oriented voices in a single enclosure: JTM45, 1959 Super Lead, 1987 50W Plexi, Silver Jubilee, JCM800, and JCM900. [1]

## Architecture and voices
The first three voices come from the Plexi Breed side and cover the non-master-volume Marshall family. The latter three come from Lead Trilogy and cover later high-gain Marshall voices. The pedal uses a footswitch to move between the two blocks and a three-position selector within each block to select its voice. [1][2]

### Plexi Breed voices
- **JTM / JTM45**
- **1987 / 50W Plexi**
- **1959 Super Lead** [1]

The Plexi Breed section provides controls for Normal, High Treble, Tone, and Volume. [1]

### Lead Trilogy voices
- **800 / JCM800**
- **900 / JCM900**
- **Jubilee / Silver Jubilee** [1][2]

The Lead Trilogy section uses Gain, Tone, and Volume. [1]

## Circuit / construction
The manufacturer describes the underlying Plexi Breed design as a fully analog circuit capable of 9V or 18V operation with automatic bias configuration; 18V provides more headroom. Lead Trilogy is described as a fully analog Marshall-oriented distortion circuit with three distinct amplifier voices. Father of Loud packages the two designs together rather than presenting a new single circuit architecture. [1]

Colombo Audio Electronics states that its pedals are hand-wired point-to-point without printed circuit boards. Exact transistor and diode part numbers are not published in the accessible Father of Loud documentation. [1][3]

## Power
The manufacturer documents the underlying Plexi Breed section as supporting 9V or 18V operation. Brett Kingman's 2025 Father of Loud demo notes the combined pedal can likewise be run from 9V to 18V, with 18V providing greater headroom. [2]

## Sound
The six voices span classic non-master-volume British crunch through later higher-gain Marshall distortion. The Plexi voices emphasize the Normal/High Treble input behavior of the earlier amplifiers, while the 800, 900, and Jubilee voices provide progressively more compressed and saturated high-gain textures. [1][2]

## Versions and customization
Father of Loud is listed by Colombo as a **custom pedal**, and the manufacturer offers an orientation choice for the Plexi Breed portion plus broader graphic customization across its custom-pedal line. [1][4]

## Photo provenance
The Colombo Audio Electronics Father of Loud product page provides model-specific imagery of the combined pedal. Current Reverb documentation also contains an exact-model product photograph and identifies Father of Loud as the Colombo Audio Electronics model. [1][5]
