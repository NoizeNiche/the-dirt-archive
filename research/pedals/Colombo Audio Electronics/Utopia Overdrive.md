# Colombo Audio Electronics - Utopia Overdrive

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Colombo Audio Electronics
- **Catalog identity:** Utopia Overdrive
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive / Distortion / Booster
- **Identity:** Three-voice analog overdrive combining Tube Screamer-, Klon-, and internally saturated lead-style reference responses.

## What this pedal is
Utopia is a handmade Italian overdrive with three operating voices selected by a three-position switch. The modes are documented as:
- **Vintage:** inspired by the Ibanez TS808.
- **Hot:** inspired by the Klon Centaur.
- **Ultra:** internally reconfigures the second gain stage to emulate the response of an overdrive driving an already distorted amplifier. [1][2]

## Controls
Three controls are used across the modes:
- **Volume:** output level, with higher output available in Hot mode.
- **Tone:** high-frequency shaping.
- **Overdrive:** saturation amount.
The three-position switch selects Vintage / Hot / Ultra. [1]

The controls are described as interactive and self-configuring according to the selected mode. [1]

Colombo describes Utopia as completely handmade in Italy; its broader construction documentation describes point-to-point assembly without printed circuit boards. [1][3]

## Sound
Utopia moves from warm, compressed TS-style Vintage, through the creamier/higher-output Klon-inspired Hot voice, to the denser internally saturated Ultra mode. [1][2]
