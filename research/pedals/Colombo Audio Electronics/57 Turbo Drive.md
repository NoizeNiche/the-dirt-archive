## What this pedal is
Colombo Audio Electronics describes the 57 Turbo Drive as a modern reinterpretation of the dynamic sound of late-1950s Fender amplifiers. It is designed to work as a transparent overdrive and boost, with a separate Turbo mode that adds gain and a rougher, more aggressive character while retaining the pedal's basic dynamic response. [1]

## Controls and functions
- **Engine:** gain, from a clean boost through vintage-style overdrive.
- **Power:** output volume.
- **Turbo:** engages the more aggressive, higher-gain operating mode. [1]

The manufacturer demonstrates the pedal in standard and Turbo settings with different guitar/pickup combinations, including sparkling drive, 1950s-style tones, California vintage, modern alternative, Chicago blues, dynamic boost/drive, and Turbo pop. [1]

## Sound
The 57 Turbo Drive is intended to react strongly to picking intensity and the guitar volume control. Lower Engine settings retain clean, open dynamics and can act as a boost; increasing Engine produces warmer breakup and crunch. Turbo mode increases gain for a more saturated and rougher response. [1][2]

## Construction
Colombo Audio Electronics states that its pedals and circuits are built entirely point-to-point, with no printed circuit boards, and that its enclosures can be customized in color, graphics, connector positions, and control layouts. The accessible 57 Turbo Drive documentation does not establish exact transistor or diode part numbers. [3]

## Versions and finish
The manufacturer's current product page documents the 57 Turbo Drive as a Fender-inspired overdrive/boost pedal. A current Reverb listing identifies a white-and-red finish and confirms Colombo Audio Electronics as the maker in Italy. Because Colombo offers enclosure customization, finish should not be treated as a circuit revision without separate documentation. [1][2]

## Power
The current Reverb listing documents standard 9V DC power and true bypass. [2]
