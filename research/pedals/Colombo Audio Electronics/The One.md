# Colombo Audio Electronics - The One

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Colombo Audio Electronics
- **Catalog identity:** The One
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive / Distortion
- **Identity:** True-bypass pedal recreating the tonal concept of the Marshall JMP-1 rack preamp in a conventional stompbox format.

## What this pedal is
Colombo developed The One to put the sound associated with the Marshall JMP-1 into a standard pedalboard-friendly format. The manufacturer explicitly describes it as a recreation of the reference sound rather than a literal clone or replica. [1][2]

The JMP-1 reference was a tube preamp introduced in 1992, with Clean 1, Clean 2, OD1 and OD2 modes and MIDI control. Colombo's pedal focuses on translating the overdrive/distortion side of that reference into a simple stompbox. [1]

## Controls and voice
The documented design uses three core controls plus a mode/shape section. Brett Kingman's 2025 demonstration describes **Tone Shape** and **Mid** controls as key to moving the response between smoother and more aggressive voices. [2]
Surviving pedal imagery should be used for exact front-panel labeling rather than inferred control names from other Colombo models.

## Sound
The intended result is a mid-focused 90s British rack-preamp flavor with adjustable tonal shaping from smoother to more aggressive distortion. Brett Kingman's 2025 demo specifically notes the JMP-1 OD1/OD2 reference and the use of Tone Shape and Mid adjustments. [2]
