## What this pedal is
November Child is a Colombo Audio Electronics distortion pedal inspired by Slash's 1980s and 1990s Marshall tones. Colombo documents three modes: **#36**, **2555**, and **#34**, corresponding to three different reference amplifier states. [1]

## Modes
- **#36:** warmer, vocal distortion associated by Colombo with the *Appetite for Destruction* recording tone.
- **2555:** Silver Jubilee-inspired live sound, with pronounced attack and harmonic content.
- **#34:** more aggressive attack/presence, based on the modified JCM800 2203 state Colombo associates with later studio work. [1]

Colombo's own explanation acknowledges conflicting historical reports about Slash's exact amplifiers and presents the three modes as reference-inspired pedal voices rather than proof that one physical amplifier story is universally settled. [1]

## Controls
The manufacturer documents:
- **Gain**
- **Tone**
- **Volume** [1]

Published demonstration settings include Gain 7-8/10, Tone 4-5/10, and Volume 5-6/10 across the three modes. [1]
