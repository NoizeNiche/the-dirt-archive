# Colombo Audio Electronics - 22 Overdrive/Distortion

## PRP identity

- **Identity:** Colombo Audio Electronics 22, a handcrafted dual-circuit overdrive/distortion pedal combining an open overdrive section with a more powerful modern distortion section.

## What this pedal is
Colombo Audio Electronics describes the 22 as a handcrafted dual-circuit gain platform. The two matched sections are a warm, open overdrive and a powerful, articulate distortion. They can be used separately or together. [1]

## Controls and documented modes
The manufacturer's current demo material documents:
- Distortion Gain.
- Distortion Tone.
- Distortion Middle.
- Distortion Presence.
- Distortion Volume.
- Overdrive Drive.
- Overdrive Tone.
- Overdrive Volume.
- Combined Overdrive + Distortion operation. [1]

The available manufacturer material demonstrates the pedal across dynamic distortion, crystal overdrive, pop lead, doom, pop/rock rhythm distortion, fat/heavy rock, Santana-style distortion, and mellow overdrive settings. [1]

## Circuit / construction
Colombo Audio Electronics states that its pedals and circuits are built entirely point-to-point, without printed circuit boards. The company emphasizes extensive customization of enclosure colors, graphics, connector positions, and potentiometer layouts across its pedal range. The accessible 22 documentation does not publish exact transistor or diode part numbers. [2]

## Sound
The 22 is designed to preserve the character of the guitar and amplifier while responding strongly to playing dynamics and guitar-volume changes. The overdrive section is described as warm and open, while the distortion section is described as powerful, articulate, modern, and capable of tight low end and controlled highs. The two sections together form a broad gain platform from barely broken-up cleans through saturated distortion. [1]

## Versions and finish
The current Reverb listing identifies a silver finish and Colombo Audio Electronics as the maker in Italy. Because the manufacturer explicitly offers customizable graphics and colors, resale finish should not be treated as a fixed circuit revision without separate documentation. [1][3]
