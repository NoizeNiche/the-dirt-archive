# Colombo Audio Electronics - Kensington Star

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Colombo Audio Electronics
- **Catalog identity:** Kensington Star
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive / Preamp / Cabinet Simulator
- **Identity:** Vox AC30-oriented preamp/overdrive derived from Kensington with a full-analog cabinet-simulation section.

## What this pedal is
Kensington Star is the expanded Kensington design with an integrated 2x12 cabinet simulation. Colombo positions it as a full-analog way to reproduce the preamp and power-amp/cabinet character associated with a Vox AC30 Normal channel, including a Mic Position control. [1][2]

## Colorways
- Colombo explicitly offers graphic customization for Kensington Star. [1]
- Marketed finish variants should be treated as color/custom-art states unless a separate circuit revision is documented.

## Versions and factory options
The documented control set includes:
- **Volume**
- **Cut**
- **Output**
- **Mic Position** [1]

The cabinet simulation can be used directly into an audio interface. Colombo's demonstrations also show the pedal with its cabinet simulation active and with treble boosters placed before it. [1]

## Version changes
No reliable numbered factory revision sequence was established.
Kensington Star is retained as a separate model from standard Kensington because the integrated cabinet simulation is a functional design distinction, not merely a finish change. [1][3]

## Construction
Colombo's broader construction documentation states that its pedals are handmade in Italy and point-to-point, without printed circuit boards. [3]

## Sound
Colombo's published settings for its Star demonstrations are Volume 10/10, Cut 0/10, Output 5/10, and Mic Position 7/10, with the pedal connected to an audio interface and cabinet simulation active. [1]
