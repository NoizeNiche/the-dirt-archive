- **Identity:** Colombo Audio Electronics Eruption, an original distortion design built around a four-control gain, EQ, and electronic Variac-style response.

## What this pedal is
Colombo Audio Electronics describes Eruption as a distortion pedal developed to evoke the guitar sound associated with the first six Van Halen albums and the so-called “brown sound.” The manufacturer emphasizes that the circuit is original rather than a clone of another commercial effect. [1]

## Controls
- **Gain:** amount of distortion.
- **Middle:** boost or cut of midrange.
- **Tone:** overall EQ focus, moving from darker/fatter settings toward brighter, more biting response.
- **Volume:** overall output level.
- **Variac:** additional control that changes the virtual voltage-drop behavior and harmonic response of the circuit. [1][2]

## Sound and operation
With the Variac control kept low, Colombo describes Eruption as capable of a classic Plexi-style distortion response. Increasing the virtual voltage-drop effect changes the harmonic behavior toward the compressed, sagging character associated with the historical brown-sound concept. The pedal can also cover lower-gain classic-rock overdrive through higher-gain distortion. [1][2]

## Circuit / construction
The manufacturer states that Eruption uses an original electronic circuit developed specifically for the pedal rather than cloning a particular device. Colombo's pedals are hand-built and hand-wired using point-to-point construction without printed circuit boards. Exact transistor and diode part numbers are not published in the accessible Eruption documentation. [1][3]

## Versions and artwork
The manufacturer's current product page documents selectable artwork including **EVH Lava** and **EVH Stripes**. Contemporary documentation and resale listings also show a white enclosure with black geometric striping and red Eruption lettering. The manufacturer explicitly notes that it is not affiliated with Eddie Van Halen or related trademarks. [1]

## Power
The visible manufacturer enclosure artwork specifies 9V DC operation. Exact current draw was not established from the accessible manufacturer product text.
