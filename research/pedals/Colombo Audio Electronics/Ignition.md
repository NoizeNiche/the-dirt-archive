# Colombo Audio Electronics - Ignition

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Colombo Audio Electronics
- **Catalog identity:** Ignition
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive / Booster
- **Identity:** Handbuilt Italian dynamic drive pedal with a fixed, pre-calibrated control set and no external knobs.

## What this pedal is
Ignition is a Colombo Audio Electronics drive pedal designed around touch sensitivity, guitar-volume cleanup, and a broad range from boost through low-gain drive, crunch, and fuller drive. The manufacturer describes it as fully analog and intended to work into clean or driven amplifiers and in stacked combinations with other Colombo amp-in-a-box designs. [1]

## Controls and operating concept
Ignition deliberately has **no external controls**. Colombo states that the gain structure, EQ balance, and dynamic response are pre-calibrated, with the player's picking attack and guitar volume used as the main means of shaping the response. [1]

## Construction
Colombo describes Ignition as hand-assembled in Italy using selected components, a low-noise optimized layout, true bypass, and individual testing. The manufacturer's broader construction policy states that its pedals are built point-to-point without printed circuit boards. [1][2]

## Sound
The manufacturer's demonstrations cover Plexi, JCM800, Mesa/Boogie Rectifier, and Vox-style amp contexts. Colombo describes the response as dynamic, harmonically rich, and capable of moving from boost through punchy crunch and structured drive. [1]
