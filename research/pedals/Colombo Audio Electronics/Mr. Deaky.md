# Colombo Audio Electronics - Mr. Deaky

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Colombo Audio Electronics
- **Catalog identity:** Mr. Deaky
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Preamp
- **Identity:** Deacy Amp-inspired pedal designed to reproduce the recording-oriented concept and tonal behavior of the small Queen-associated amplifier.

## What this pedal is
Mr. Deaky is Colombo Audio Electronics' pedal interpretation of the Deacy Amp. Colombo describes it as a distortion/preamp unit intended to reproduce the core concept and tone of the tiny amplifier associated with John Deacon and Brian May, while using modern components and pedal-standard operation rather than duplicating the original hardware wholesale. [1]

## Controls
The manufacturer documents two controls:
- **Level:** amount of distorted signal/output level.
- **Dampen:** a low-pass filtering control intended to recreate the muffled studio character Brian May achieved by placing books in front of the Deacy Amp. [1]

Colombo's demonstrations run the pedal directly into an audio interface rather than a conventional guitar amplifier. A QueenMaster Treble Booster is placed before it in the manufacturer's demos. [1]

## Original Deacy reference
Colombo's account states that the original Deacy Amp used two germanium driver transistors and was built from repurposed radio electronics. The manufacturer then explicitly distinguishes Mr. Deaky as a modern pedal implementation with changed componentry and current pedal standards. [1]

## Sound
Mr. Deaky is intended as a direct/recording-friendly full-range Deacy-style distortion, with Dampen controlling the high-frequency roll-off and Level controlling output/distorted-signal level. The manufacturer demonstrates it directly into an audio interface, reinforcing that intended use case. [1]
