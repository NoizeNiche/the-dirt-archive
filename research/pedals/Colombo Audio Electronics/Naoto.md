# Colombo Audio Electronics - Naoto

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Colombo Audio Electronics
- **Catalog identity:** Naoto
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive / Distortion / Booster
- **Identity:** 1980s-inspired dynamic drive/distortion design drawing its tonal concept from the intersection of Marshall JCM800 and ADA MP1-style sounds.

## What this pedal is
Colombo describes Naoto as a distortion pedal aimed at the solid, sharp, thick, and scratchy character associated with 1980s distorted guitar. The manufacturer explicitly places it between overdrive and distortion and compares the concept to a midpoint between a Marshall JCM800 and an ADA MP1 preamp. [1]

The design is credited by Colombo to Antonio Marsano with collaboration from Kazuya Yamaguchi. [1]

## Controls
The manufacturer demonstrations use:
- **Gain**
- **Tone**
- **Volume** [1]

The catalog page also classifies Naoto across booster, overdrive, and distortion categories. [1]

## Colorways
- Colombo offers graphic customization for Naoto. [1]
- A documented example uses a white enclosure with Tiger Mask artwork and red NAOTO branding. [2]
- A separate Reverb listing identifies an orange factory-version example dated 2020-2024. [3]
These examples are retained as finish/version evidence without assuming a complete production chronology.

## Versions and factory options
A Reverb listing documents a **Factory Version 2020-2024** example in orange. [3] The available evidence is not sufficient to define this as a complete V1/V2 sequence, so the archive records it as a dated marketplace-described production state rather than a universal revision boundary.

## Sound
Colombo demonstrates Naoto from low-gain settings through higher-saturation applications. Published examples range from Gain 2/10 through 10/10, with rock, heavy-metal, and clean-input contexts. [1]
