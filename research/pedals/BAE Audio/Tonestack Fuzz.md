## Deep research verification

This pass verifies the historical 2018 product announcement and exact architecture from Effects Database's preserved BAE documentation.

### Verified description

Effects Database records the **BAE Audio Tonestack Fuzz** announcement for NAMM 2018. The pedal combines a **silicon fuzz circuit based on a classic 1970s fuzz** with a separate **three-band EQ section**, each with its own true-bypass stomp switch.

### Verified controls and architecture

- Fuzz section: **Attack** controls the amount of distortion; **Level** controls output volume.
- EQ section: three-band equalization for broader tonal shaping than a traditional single-tone fuzz.
- The fuzz and EQ can be engaged independently.
- Effects Database describes the fuzz as a silicon-based circuit and characterizes its output as a spitting, buzzy grit with Attack ranging from subtle bite to raging fuzz.
- The original announcement describes the EQ section as capable of substantial low/mid/high shaping, including bass boost with a midrange scoop for riffing and midrange boost for lead use.

### Verified version references

- Effects Database dates the public announcement to **January 23, 2018**, with the database entry published February 5, 2018.
- No numbered production revision was established in the reviewed sources.
