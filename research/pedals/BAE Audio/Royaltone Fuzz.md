# BAE Audio - Royaltone Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Royaltone Fuzz
- **Builder:** BAE Audio
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Dual-section English-fuzz-inspired pedal combining a 3-band JFET tone stack/boost with a Tone Bender-derived fuzz section.

## What this pedal is
BAE describes the Royaltone as a dual-stomp design exploring vintage English fuzz circuits. One footswitch controls a three-band high-frequency boost/tone section, while the second engages the fuzz section with additional Attack and Level controls.

## Colorways
- Documented production examples use a **yellow painted metal enclosure** with black/red labeling.
- No complete factory colorway chronology was verified.

## Versions and factory options
### Royaltone Fuzz
- MID
- BASS
- TREBLE
- ATTACK

- Two true-bypass footswitches
- 3-band active/high-frequency boost section
- Tone Bender-derived fuzz section
- Hand-built in the USA
- 9 V battery or external center-negative supply

## Version changes
No numbered factory revision was verified in the official BAE documentation reviewed. The current official product page notes four bipolar transistors in its feature summary, while the detailed topology specifies 3 JFETs in the tone-stack section plus 1 JFET and 2 bipolar transistors in the fuzz section.

## Transistor
- **Technology:** Solid-state.
- **Documented topology:** The official description contains a four-bipolar-transistor summary and separately specifies **3 JFETs** in the tone stack and **1 JFET + 2 bipolar transistors** in the Tone Bender section. The documentation is internally inconsistent on the aggregate count, so the detailed stage breakdown is preserved rather than reconciled by assumption.

## Diode
- **Exact clipping/protection arrangement:** Not publicly documented in the checked official sources.

## Sound
BAE characterizes the Royaltone as a spitting, buzzy vintage-English-style fuzz that cuts through dense mixes. The separate boost/tone section and fuzz section can be used independently, with the three-band controls shaping the frequency emphasis before the Tone Bender-derived stage.
