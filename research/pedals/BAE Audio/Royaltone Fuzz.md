# BAE Audio — Royaltone Fuzz

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** Royaltone Fuzz
- **Builder:** BAE Audio
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Dual-stomp vintage-English-style fuzz and tone/boost unit combining a three-band active JFET section with a Tone Bender-derived fuzz section. [1]

## What this pedal is

BAE designed Royaltone as a two-section pedal for shaping and fuzzing the guitar signal. The left section is an active three-band tone/gain stage; the right section is a Tone Bender-derived fuzz. Each has its own footswitch, allowing independent or combined operation. [1]

## Controls / sections

### High-frequency boost / tone section
- **MID:** Up to 12.5dB gain.
- **BASS:** Up to 13.6dB gain.
- **TREBLE:** Up to 16.1dB gain.
- Individual section gain controls and green status LED. [1]

### Tone Bender-derived fuzz section
- **ATTACK:** Up to 20dB gain.
- **LEVEL:** Up to 20dB gain.
- Red status LED.
- Independent true-bypass footswitch. [1]

## Circuit topology

BAE's current product page explicitly documents:
- **Tone Stack:** 3 JFET transistors used as buffers and gain.
- **Tone Bender:** 1 JFET + 2 bipolar transistors.
- **Power supply:** +9V DC.
This is more authoritative than the older marketing summary that simply described four bipolar transistors, so the detailed stage topology is retained and the aggregate-count discrepancy is noted rather than “corrected” by assumption. [1]

## Transistor

- **Tone section:** 3 JFETs.
- **Fuzz section:** 1 JFET + 2 bipolar transistors.
- Individual part numbers are not published. [1]

## Diode / clipping

- Exact clipping/protection diode arrangement is not published on the current product page.
- No diode material is inferred from the transistor topology.

## Power

BAE specifies:
- Single **9V battery** or external supply.
- External jack is center-negative, approximately 2.0mm.
- The current product page gives **idle 7mA** and **maximum 13mA** with both sections active. [1]
- Pedal remains powered whenever an input plug is connected, even if LEDs are off, so BAE advises removing the input cable to conserve the battery. [1]

## Construction / hardware

- Hand-built in the USA.
- Dimensions from BAE's current page: approximately **3.625 × 6.25 × 3.125 inches**.
- Individual gain pots for the sections.
- Sonically selected film, ceramic, mica and electrolytic capacitors. [1]

## Sound

BAE characterizes Royaltone as a spitting, buzzy English-style fuzz that cuts through dense mixes. The active tone/boost section can operate independently or feed the Tone Bender-derived fuzz for a more shaped and forceful result. [1]
