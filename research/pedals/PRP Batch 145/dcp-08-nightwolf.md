# Caline DCP-08 Nightwolf

- **Builder:** Caline
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz / Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** DCP-08 Nightwolf
- **Colorways:** Black enclosure with purple moon/wolf artwork is documented in exact-model Caline imagery.
- **Versions:** Dual fuzz/overdrive design combining the CP-46 Fuzzy Bear fuzz with the CP-12 Pure Sky overdrive.
- **Version changes:** No confirmed production hardware revision was found in the reviewed sources.
- **Transistor type:** The reviewed exact-model documentation does not identify a transistor part number or package for the DCP-08 as a whole.
- **Diode type:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.
- **Controls / switching:** Fuzz side uses Volume and Fuzz; Pure Sky side uses Treble, Bass, Volume, and Gain. Two footswitches independently engage the fuzz and overdrive.
- **Sound:** The fuzz side is described as a smooth, vintage-leaning fuzz, while the Pure Sky side emphasizes transparency and high-end clarity and can serve as overdrive or tone shaping. The two circuits together can cover soft vintage fuzz, more open drive, or combinations where one stage pushes the other.
