# Caline DCP-07 Brigade

- **Builder:** Caline
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** DCP-07 Brigade
- **Colorways:** Red enclosure with black skull graphics is documented in exact-model listings.
- **Versions:** Dual overdrive combining the Caline Midlander/Tube Screamer-style side with the Pure Sky transparent overdrive side. A Voice switch changes circuit order.
- **Version changes:** No confirmed production hardware revision was found in the reviewed sources.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.
- **Diode type:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.
- **Controls / switching:** Midlander side: Tone, Gain, Volume. Pure Sky side: Treble, Bass, Gain, Volume. Two footswitches select the drives and a Voice switch changes their order.
- **Sound:** One side supplies a mid-focused, smoother Tube Screamer-style drive while the Pure Sky side is more open and transparent, with broader bass/treble shaping. Running them separately or changing their order gives noticeably different stacked responses, from focused overdrive to a wider, more open pushed tone.
- **Research confidence:** High for the dual architecture, controls, and Voice function; medium for the Tube Screamer/Timmy-style lineage because it is a documented design comparison rather than a schematic identity.
