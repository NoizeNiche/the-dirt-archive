# Caline DCP-09 Tiger Shark

- **Builder:** Caline
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Catalog identity:** DCP-09 Tiger Shark
- **Colorways:** White enclosure with a blue/red tiger-shark graphic is documented in exact-model listings.
- **Versions:** Dual distortion/noise-gate pedal combining the CP-77 Bounty Hunter-style high-gain distortion with a dedicated gate section.
- **Version changes:** No confirmed production hardware revision was found in the reviewed sources.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.
- **Diode type:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.
- **Controls / switching:** Distortion section uses Volume, Mid, Distortion, and an Extreme/Classic selector. Gate section uses Noise/Sensitivity-type control and a two-position response selector; two footswitches independently engage the sections.
- **Sound:** The distortion side is aimed at thick, aggressive high-gain/metal tones, while the gate is there to tighten the pauses and reduce the noise floor that can become prominent with heavy saturation. The Extreme/Classic switch changes the distortion character, while the gate's alternate response modes change how abruptly it clamps.
- **Research confidence:** High for the dual-effect architecture, controls, and purpose; medium for exact CP-77/CP-39 circuit equivalence because that relationship is documented by product/database sources rather than a schematic.
