# Caline DCP-11 Andes

- **Builder:** Caline
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** DCP-11 Andes
- **Colorways:** Blue enclosure with dark mountain-themed graphics is documented in exact-model listings.
- **Versions:** Dual overdrive/boost design combining a Pure Sky-style transparent overdrive with a boost placed in front of it.
- **Version changes:** No confirmed production hardware revision was found in the reviewed sources.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.
- **Diode type:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.
- **Controls / switching:** Overdrive side uses Treble, Bass, Volume, and Gain; boost side uses a stepped boost level control. Two footswitches independently engage the stages.
- **Sound:** The Pure Sky side is the open, transparent stage of the pair, suitable for light breakup or broader EQ shaping, while the front-end boost increases the signal feeding it and can add headroom or push the overdrive into more saturation. Secondary documentation describes the boost in stepped level increments, with the two stages intended to work independently or together.
- **Research confidence:** High for the dual architecture, Pure Sky relationship, and documented control scheme; medium for the exact boost lineage because retailer/database descriptions use differing predecessor names.
