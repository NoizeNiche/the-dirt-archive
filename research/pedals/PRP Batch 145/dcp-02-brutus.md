# Caline DCP-02 Brutus

- **Builder:** Caline
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** DCP-02 Brutus
- **Colorways:** Metallic magenta/purple enclosure with dark wolf/Brutus artwork is documented in exact-model listings.
- **Versions:** Dual-dirt design combining an Action Replay-style distortion side and a Midlander-style overdrive side. A Voice switch changes the order of the two circuits.
- **Version changes:** No confirmed production hardware revision was found in the reviewed sources.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.
- **Diode type:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.
- **Controls / switching:** Distortion side Volume, Drive, Treble, Low-mid; overdrive side Treble, Gain, Volume; two independent footswitches plus Voice order switch. The overdrive section is documented around a 4558 IC.
- **Sound:** The distortion side is voiced toward a British/plexi-style crunch that can move from just-above-overdrive into thicker classic-rock distortion. The overdrive side is a smoother, mid-focused Tube Screamer-style voice, and changing the Voice order lets the two stages interact in either sequence or be stacked.
- **Research confidence:** High for the dual architecture, controls, Voice function, and 4558 statement; medium for the named predecessor/lineage descriptions because those are product/database descriptions rather than a published schematic.
