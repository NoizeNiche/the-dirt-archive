# Caline DCP-05 Key West

- **Builder:** Caline
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** DCP-05 Key West
- **Colorways:** White enclosure with tropical/palm graphics and orange-blue striping is documented in exact-model listings.
- **Versions:** Dual compressor/overdrive design. The two effects can operate independently or together.
- **Version changes:** No confirmed production hardware revision was found in the reviewed sources.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.
- **Diode type:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.
- **Controls / switching:** Compressor side has Volume, EQ, and Comp controls; the reviewed product documentation also identifies Hard/Soft compression behavior. Overdrive side uses the published tone/gain/volume controls; two footswitches independently engage the sections.
- **Sound:** The compressor side is intended to provide controllable squash and sustain, while the overdrive side is described as a transparent-style, bass/treble-shapable drive. Pedal of the Day describes the compressor as a Pressure Tank-style design and the overdrive as Zen Drive-inspired, but these are lineage descriptions from secondary documentation rather than a published Caline schematic.
- **Research confidence:** High for the dual-effect format and independent/tandem operation; medium for the exact circuit lineages because sources disagree on how those inspirations should be labeled.
