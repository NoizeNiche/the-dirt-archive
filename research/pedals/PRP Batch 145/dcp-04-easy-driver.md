# Caline DCP-04 Easy Driver

- **Builder:** Caline
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Catalog identity:** DCP-04 Easy Driver
- **Colorways:** Gray enclosure with black controls and motorcycle-themed artwork is documented in exact-model listings.
- **Versions:** Dual pedal combining a Mark 4-style distortion section with a six-band graphic EQ section.
- **Version changes:** No confirmed production hardware revision was found in the reviewed sources.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.
- **Diode type:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.
- **Controls / switching:** Distortion section uses Gain 1, Gain 2, and Level; EQ section uses six sliders at 100 Hz, 200 Hz, 400 Hz, 800 Hz, 1.6 kHz, and 3.2 kHz plus Level. Two footswitches allow the sections to run independently or together.
- **Sound:** The distortion stage is designed around the Mark 4/Cali-style high-gain concept, with separate gain controls shaping treble-side and bass-side saturation. The graphic EQ can scoop or push selected frequency bands after or independently from the distortion, making the combined pedal more flexible than a fixed-voicing distortion alone.
- **Research confidence:** High for the two-section design, control layout, and published frequency bands; medium for exact circuit equivalence because the Mark 4 description is a style/reference designation rather than a schematic disclosure.
