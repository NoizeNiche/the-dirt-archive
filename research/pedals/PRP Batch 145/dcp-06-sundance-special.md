# Caline DCP-06 Sundance Special

- **Builder:** Caline
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** DCP-06 Sundance Special
- **Colorways:** Black enclosure with bright yellow mountain/sun graphics is documented in exact-model listings.
- **Versions:** Dual overdrive-and-boost design combining an Orange Burst-style overdrive with a Recluse/Villain-style boost section.
- **Version changes:** No confirmed production hardware revision was found in the reviewed sources.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.
- **Diode type:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.
- **Controls / switching:** Overdrive section uses Bass, Treble, Gain, and Volume; boost section uses a dedicated stepped Boost control; two footswitches engage overdrive and boost independently.
- **Sound:** The overdrive side is described as a flexible, amp-like drive that can stay relatively tame or move into more pronounced grit. The separate boost adds substantial level/headroom and can be used either to push the drive or to raise the overall signal.
- **Research confidence:** High for the dual layout and documented controls; medium for the named internal lineage because those descriptions come from product/database documentation rather than a schematic.
