## Sound
The pedal has controls for: Overdrive; the amount of gain of the overdrive Tone; the output tone of the pedal Out level; the volume of the overdrive When the pedal is opened up, you can spot the two individual PCB’s Luckily, a schematic was already available.
(this schematic comes from this post on Freestompboxes.org) As you can spot the pedal is like a Tubescreamer pedal followed by a Big Muff-ish style tone section.
