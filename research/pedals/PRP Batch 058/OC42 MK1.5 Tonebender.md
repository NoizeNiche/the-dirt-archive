# Arcane Analog — OC42 MK1.5 Tonebender

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** OC42 MK1.5 Tonebender
- **Builder:** Arcane Analog
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** A specific Arcane Analog MK1.5 Tone Bender run built around three NOS Mullard-branded OC42 germanium transistors, using a phenolic board and documented as a limited run of three units. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
Arcane Analog's documented OC42 MK1.5 is a historically scoped Tone Bender build rather than the builder's broader generic Tone Bender family. The builder describes the circuit as an MK1.5 tuned with more bite and better ability to cut through a mix. [1]
