- **Identity:** A configurable family of Tone Bender builds covering MK1, MK1.5, MK2, MK3 and MK4 variants, with transistor and control options chosen per build. The family listing is not one fixed schematic or BOM. [1]

## What this pedal is
Arcane Analog presents this product as a classic old-school Tone Bender family with the major historical variants exposed as selectable builds. The builder emphasizes individual tuning, offering different transistor sets and optional external Bias controls rather than treating every variant as electronically identical. [1]

## Variant notes
### MK1
Arcane Analog describes the MK1 as difficult to build and tune, with the option to retain its original quirky gating or tune it toward more modern maximum sustain. An external Bias control can be added for temperature stability and tone shaping. [1]

### MK1.5
The builder describes the MK1.5 as a chunky, woolly fuzz with more bite than a Fuzz Face. The standard layout uses Level and Attack controls, with optional external Bias. Arcane Analog also offers a Vox-style MK1.5 specification using **OC76** transistors. [1]

### MK2
Arcane Analog describes the MK2 as a high-sustain classic fuzz. Level and Attack are the standard controls, with optional external Bias. [1]

### MK3 / MK4
Both are included in the current family listing, but the reviewed builder text does not provide the same model-specific circuit and control detail given for MK1, MK1.5 and MK2. The archive leaves those specifics unresolved rather than borrowing from another variant. [1]

## Transistor
- The family uses **NOS germanium transistors**.
- Arcane Analog explicitly offers carefully selected **OC75** or **AC125** transistors.
- An **OC76** option is specifically offered for a -spec MK1.5 build.
- Additional transistor alternatives are available by request.
- Exact transistor choice is therefore **variant/build-dependent**. [1]
