# Arcane Analog - TONE BENDERS (MK1 // MK1.5 // MK2 // MK3 // MK4)

## What this pedal is
A family listing covering MK1, MK1.5, MK2, MK3 and MK4 Tone Bender builds. Arcane Analog documents custom tuning, bias options and selected germanium transistor sets; the reviewed text gives its most detailed descriptions for MK1, MK1.5 and MK2.

## Colorways
- Custom powder-coated enclosures with one-of-a-kind etched plates are documented.

## Versions and factory options
- MK1, MK1.5, MK2, MK3, MK4
- Optional external Bias
- Selected OC75 or AC125 germanium transistors
- OC76 option for a Vox-spec MK1.5
- Tagboard or veroboard, true bypass

## Version changes
This is a family listing rather than one fixed revision. Arcane Analog differentiates MK1, MK1.5 and MK2 behavior and component choices; MK3 and MK4 are included but not separately detailed on the reviewed product text.

## Transistor
- NOS trio of germanium transistors at family level.
- Selected OC75 or AC125 options, with OC76 available for a Vox-spec MK1.5.
- Other transistor alternatives by request.

## Diode
- Exact clipping diode/device specifications are not publicly documented.

## Sound
The builder describes MK1 from quirky gating to high sustain, MK1.5 as chunky and woolly with added bite, and MK2 as a high-sustain classic fuzz. The reviewed product text does not provide enough model-specific detail for MK3 or MK4 beyond their inclusion.
