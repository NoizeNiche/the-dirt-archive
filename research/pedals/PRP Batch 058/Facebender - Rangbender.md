# Arcane Analog - Facebender / Rangbender

## What this pedal is
A combined Rangemaster-style treble booster and Fuzz Face or MK1.5 Tone Bender. The sections can run independently or together.

## Colorways
- Lime green exact-model retailer photograph archived.
- Custom options and finishes are available.

## Versions and factory options
- Range, Gain, Fuzz, Master, Bias
- Rangemaster + Fuzz Face or Rangemaster + MK1.5 Tone Bender
- Negative-ground configuration
- NOS hand-selected transistor sets

## Version changes
The key configuration choice is the downstream Fuzz Face versus MK1.5 Tone Bender section. Custom transistor and enclosure options are also documented.

## Transistor
- NOS trio of hand-selected transistors is documented.
- Exact part numbers are build-dependent.

## Diode
- Exact clipping diode/device is not publicly documented.

## Sound
The sections can provide boosted treble or fuzz separately, while stacking them creates a larger, more aggressive MKII-style response. Transistor selection and bias affect gain, feel and cleanup.
