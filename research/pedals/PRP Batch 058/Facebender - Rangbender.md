# Arcane Analog — Facebender / Rangbender

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** Facebender / Rangbender
- **Builder:** Arcane Analog
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz / Boost
- **Identity:** A two-stage combination of a Rangemaster-style treble booster feeding either a Fuzz Face or a MK1.5 Tonebender, with the player choosing the downstream fuzz circuit. Both sections can operate independently or together. [1][2]

## What this pedal is

Arcane Analog describes the Facebender and Rangebender as essentially a Rangemaster feeding a Fuzz Face or MK1.5 Tonebender. Used together, the builder describes the combination as capable of approximating a high-gain MKII Tonebender-style response. The sections may also be used independently. [1]

A historical Reverb listing identifies one Facebender as a **prototype**, with a two-knob Rangemaster treble booster and a three-knob Fuzz Face, loaded with germanium transistors. That particular documented example had a silver etched finish and could be powered from a Boss-style barrel supply. [2]

## Controls / architecture

The exact control set depends on the build:
- **Rangemaster section:** two controls on the documented prototype.
- **Fuzz section:** three controls on the documented Fuzz Face prototype.
- Arcane Analog's broader current family can include **Range, Gain, Fuzz, Master and Bias** controls depending on the configured circuit.
- The downstream fuzz choice is **Fuzz Face or MK1.5 Tonebender**. [1][2]

## Circuit lineage

- **Upstream:** Rangemaster-style treble booster.
- **Downstream:** Fuzz Face **or** MK1.5 Tonebender.
- The two sections may be combined for a more aggressive, high-gain Tonebender-style effect. [1]
- This is a hybrid family rather than one fixed schematic. Individual builds can differ by downstream fuzz circuit, transistor selection and optional hardware.

## Transistor

- Arcane Analog states that the standard transistors are **negative-ground** and that the build uses a **NOS trio of hand-selected transistors**.
- The builder offers multiple transistor options, including classic, low- and high-gain choices.
- Exact transistor part numbers are **build-dependent** and are not established for the generic family listing. [1]
- The historical prototype is described as loaded with germanium transistors, but its exact device part numbers are not given. [2]

## Diode / clipping

- Exact clipping diode/device part numbers are not publicly documented in the reviewed Facebender / Rangebender materials.
- No diode material is inferred from the transistor technology.

## Construction / hardware

Arcane Analog documents:
- 100% hand-crafted and hand-wired construction.
- True-bypass switching.
- NOS trio of hand-selected transistors.
- Customization including side or top-mounted jacks, DC jack, LED, additional output volume, transistor options, premium tagboard and classic/low/high gain choices. [1]
The 2014 prototype listing additionally documents a silver etched finish, compact two-section control layout and Boss-style barrel power. [2]

## Power

- Arcane Analog states the standard Facebender / Rangebender transistors are **negative ground**, so the pedals can be daisy-chained on a standard power supply.
- DC-jack installation is available as a customization. [1]
- A historical prototype listing says that specific unit could be powered with a Boss-style barrel supply. [2]
- The reviewed sources do not establish a universal voltage/current specification for every build.

## Version / build-history notes

- The current family is configurable rather than a single numbered production revision.
- Main structural variant: **Fuzz Face** versus **MK1.5 Tonebender** as the downstream fuzz stage.
- The historical silver Facebender example is explicitly identified as a prototype. [2]
- Custom enclosure, transistor and hardware options are part of the builder's documented offering. [1]

## Sound

The design is intended to let the Rangemaster section provide a treble-boost stage and the fuzz section provide the downstream clipping texture. Running them independently allows boost-only or fuzz-only operation; stacking them increases drive and upper-mid emphasis into a more aggressive Tonebender-like result. The exact response varies with the chosen fuzz circuit and transistor set. [1][2]

The current Arcane Analog product page was cross-checked with a historical Reverb listing for an exact Facebender prototype.
