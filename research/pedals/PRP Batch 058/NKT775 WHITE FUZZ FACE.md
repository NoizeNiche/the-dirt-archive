# Arcane Analog — NKT775 WHITE FUZZ FACE

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** NKT775 WHITE FUZZ FACE
- **Builder:** Arcane Analog
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Modern Fuzz Face implementation using NOS NKT775 white-dot germanium transistors, with the standard Fuzz and Gain controls expanded by an external Bias control. The documented example is the Daphne Blue, one-of-a-kind-faceplate build. [1]

## What this pedal is

Arcane Analog describes this as a modern take on the classic Fuzz Face circuit. The specific build adds Bias to the standard Fuzz/Gain layout and uses NOS NKT775 germanium transistors. The builder also documents a DC jack and LED on this individual Daphne Blue build. [1]

## Controls

- **FUZZ:** Fuzz amount.
- **GAIN:** Core Fuzz Face gain/output response control.
- **BIAS:** External bias control allowing the player to vary the circuit's operating point and fuzz character. [1]
