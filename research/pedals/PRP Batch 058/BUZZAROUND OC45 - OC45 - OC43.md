# Arcane Analog - BUZZAROUND OC45 / OC45 / OC43

## What this pedal is
A tuned Baldwin Burns Buzzaround recreation with added Master Volume. Arcane Analog specifies two OC45 and one OC43 germanium transistors, hand-selected as a trio.

## Colorways
- White/gray enclosure with etched Arcane Analog faceplate is documented.
- Individual builds may use custom finishes.

## Versions and factory options
- Tone, Bias, Fuzz, Master Volume
- DC power, true bypass, veroboard
- NOS OC45 / OC45 / OC43 germanium transistor set

## Version changes
The Master Volume adds output control to the three-control Buzzaround layout. This is the OC45/OC45/OC43 build and should not be merged with Arcane Analog's separate NKT213 Buzzaround historical build.

## Transistor
- 2 x OC45 + 1 x OC43 germanium transistors.

## Diode
- Exact clipping diode/device is not publicly documented.

## Sound
The builder describes a range from crunch through high-gain distortion and fuzz. Bias, Tone and Fuzz shape the response while Master Volume makes high-gain settings easier to level.
