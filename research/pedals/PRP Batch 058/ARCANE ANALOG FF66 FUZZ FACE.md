# Arcane Analog - ARCANE ANALOG FF66 FUZZ FACE

## What this pedal is
The FF66 is Arcane Analog's custom Fuzz Face platform. The builder describes multiple transistor flavors based on the classic Fuzz Face circuit, with carefully selected devices and optional external Bias and Tone controls; available builds can use germanium, silicon, or hybrid transistor sets. [1][2][3]

## Colorways
- Custom powder-coated enclosures with screened or etched top plates are offered.
- A Copper Top / etched-plate style is documented on an earlier FF-66 example. [2][3]

## Versions and factory options
- Fuzz
- Volume / Gain
- Optional Tone
- Optional external Bias
- NOS germanium, silicon, or hybrid transistor options
- Custom enclosure / top-plate options
- Optional DC jack, LED, negative-ground and gain-range choices are documented by the builder. [1]

## Version changes
This is a configurable FF66 family rather than a single fixed production revision. The builder documents multiple transistor and control combinations, so the exact build can vary by ordered specification. [1]

## Transistor
- Exact device is build-dependent.
- The builder documents NOS germanium, silicon, and hybrid options and says the devices are selected for gain, leakage, and sound. [1]

## Diode
- Exact clipping diode/device is not publicly documented in the reviewed FF66 material.

## Sound
The FF66 stays in classic Fuzz Face territory, with the selected transistor set shaping gain, cleanup, compression and overall response. The external Bias control expands the texture range and helps compensate for temperature-related germanium variation, while an optional Tone control broadens the usable EQ range. [1][2]
