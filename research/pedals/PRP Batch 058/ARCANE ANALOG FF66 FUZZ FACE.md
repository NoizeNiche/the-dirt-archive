# Arcane Analog — ARCANE ANALOG FF66 FUZZ FACE

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** ARCANE ANALOG FF66 FUZZ FACE
- **Builder:** Arcane Analog
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Configurable Fuzz Face family built around the classic Fuzz Face circuit, with transistor selection, optional external Bias and Tone controls, and custom enclosure options. [1]

## What this pedal is

The FF66 is not presented by Arcane Analog as one immutable BOM. The builder describes multiple flavors of Fuzz Face, with different transistor choices selected for gain, leakage and sound. This matters for the archive because component-level claims attached to one FF66 example should not automatically be generalized to every FF66 build. [1]

## Controls / options

- **FUZZ**
- **VOLUME / GAIN (Level)**
- **External BIAS:** Available as an option and described as expanding the sound range while helping with temperature fluctuations.
- **TONE:** Optional.
- Arcane Analog states that all four controls can be combined: Fuzz, Volume, Tone and Bias. [1][2]

## Circuit lineage

- **Primary lineage:** Classic **Fuzz Face** circuit.
- The Effects Database entry for a specific **FF-66 OC42** example describes the unit as a classic Fuzz Face design with modern features and identifies the typical Gain/Level and Fuzz/Attack controls plus external Bias. [2]
- The current builder page describes the broader FF66 family as having selectable NOS germanium, silicon or hybrid transistor configurations. [1]

## Transistor

- **Build-dependent.**
- Arcane Analog explicitly offers **NOS germanium, silicon or hybrid** transistor sets.
- The builder says its default FF66 devices are Russian germanium transistors, selected for gain, leakage and sound, but other transistor types can be requested. [1]
- The specific Effects Database example is identified as **FF-66 OC42**, establishing OC42 as a documented device choice for that example, not as the universal FF66 transistor. [2]

## Diode / clipping

- Exact clipping diode type or part number is **not publicly documented** in the reviewed FF66 sources.
- The archive should not infer diode material from the selected transistor technology.

## Construction / hardware

Arcane Analog documents:
- 100% hand-crafted and hand-wired construction.
- Veroboard construction, with tagboard available.
- True-bypass switching.
- Custom powder-coated enclosures with screened or etched top plates.
- Neutrik jacks.
- BC/Philips electrolytic capacitors and military-grade capacitors.
- Carbon-composition resistors available on request.
- Optional top- or side-mounted jacks, battery-kill pot switch, DC jack and LED.
- Optional negative-ground transistor arrangements and classic/low/high-gain transistor choices. [1]

## Version / build-history notes

- The FF66 should be treated as a **configurable family**, not a single numbered production revision.
- A documented **Copper Top / etched-plate** FF-66 OC42 example was published in 2016, while the current builder listing continues to offer multiple transistor and control combinations. [1][2]
- The exact feature set of any historical FF66 therefore depends on the individual build.

## Sound

The circuit stays within Fuzz Face territory, while the transistor choice can materially alter gain, leakage, brightness, compression and cleanup behavior. The external Bias option broadens the range of available textures and is specifically described as useful for temperature stability in germanium-oriented builds. [1][2]
A useful archive distinction is therefore:
- **Circuit lineage:** Fuzz Face
- **Transistor technology:** Build-dependent
- **Exact device:** Build-dependent unless tied to a documented individual example

## Power

- Arcane Analog documents optional **DC jack** and negative-ground transistor configurations.
- The reviewed FF66 materials do not establish one universal supply voltage for every build, so no fixed voltage/current specification is asserted here. [1]
