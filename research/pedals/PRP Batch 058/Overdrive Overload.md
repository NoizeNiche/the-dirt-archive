# Arcane Analog - Overdrive Overload

## What this pedal is
A family of one-off Arcane Analog overdrive builds, not one fixed production circuit. The builder lists GLS (Greer Lightspeed), TSX (Ibanez TS10), TS808 (Ibanez TS808), Gold (Morning Glory), and Magenta (KOT 1/2), plus additional custom builds.

## Colorways
- Silver and olive-green one-off builds are shown.
- Custom powder-coated finishes and plates are used.

## Versions and factory options
- GLS, TSX, TS808, Gold, Magenta
- DLS, OCD, Honey Bee OD and Harmonic Overdrive builds are also listed but not pictured
- Custom plates / inked finishes
- PCB construction, true bypass

## Version changes
There is no single revision history. Each named option is a distinct one-off build, so controls and internal components depend on the selected variant.

## Transistor
- Exact transistor/device part numbers are not publicly documented for the catalog family.

## Sound
The sound is variant-dependent because several distinct overdrive circuits are grouped under one catalog family. GLS, TSX, TS808, Morning Glory and KOT variants therefore do not represent one fixed voice.
