# Arcane Analog — Overdrive Overload

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** Overdrive Overload
- **Builder:** Arcane Analog
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** A catalog of one-off Arcane Analog overdrive builds using several different circuit families. It is not one fixed production circuit, so the selected variant must be preserved as part of the record. [1]

## What this pedal is

Arcane Analog describes Overdrive Overload as a collection of personal one-off builds made to explore different overdrive circuits. The published options include:
- **GLS:** Greer Lightspeed
- **TSX:** Ibanez TS10
- **TS808:** Ibanez TS808
- **Gold:** Morning Glory
- **Magenta:** King of Tone 1/2 (KOT 1/2)
The builder also lists unpictured DLS, OCD, Honey Bee OD and Harmonic Overdrive builds with custom plates. [1]

## Variants

The catalog identity is variant-dependent:
- **GLS:** Greer Lightspeed-derived build.
- **TSX:** Ibanez TS10-derived build.
- **TS808:** Ibanez TS808-derived build.
- **Gold:** Morning Glory-derived build.
- **Magenta:** King of Tone 1/2-derived build.
- **Additional unpictured builds:** DLS, OCD, Honey Bee OD, Harmonic Overdrive. [1]

These are separate one-off circuits grouped under one Arcane Analog page. Controls, semiconductor choices and component values therefore cannot be safely generalized across the whole Overdrive Overload family.

## Construction / hardware

Arcane Analog states that these builds are:
- 100% hand-crafted and hand-wired.
- Built on **PCB**.
- **True bypass**.
- Made with quality components.
- Housed in custom powder-coated enclosures with one-of-a-kind plates or inked finishes.
- Built as one-off personal units, with only one of each advertised option. [1]
