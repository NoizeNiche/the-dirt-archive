# Arcane Analog - BUZZSAW

## What this pedal is
A circuit-faithful Baldwin Burns Buzzaround recreation with an optional Master Volume. Arcane Analog specifies three hand-selected NOS germanium transistors.

## Colorways
- Purple exact-model retailer photograph archived.
- Custom colors are available because the pedals are made to order.

## Versions and factory options
- Timbre/Tone, Balance/Bias, Sustain/Fuzz
- Optional Master Volume, upgraded capacitors and LED
- Tagboard construction
- NOS germanium transistor trio

## Version changes
The main documented variation is the optional Master Volume. Custom colors and component options are also offered.

## Transistor
- Three hand-selected NOS germanium transistors.

## Diode
- Exact clipping diode/device is not publicly documented.

## Sound
The builder describes tight lows, fat mids, long sustain and a thick, chewy fuzz response. The optional Master Volume prevents the large level jump that can occur at higher gain.
