## Sound
Analog Man's technical overview of silicon Fuzz Face circuits provides context for the BC108 family: silicon Fuzz Faces are generally fuzzier and brighter than germanium versions, have higher transistor gain, retain sensitivity to guitar dynamics, and can clean up substantially from the guitar volume control without becoming fully clean in the same way some germanium builds do. These are **family-level silicon Fuzz Face characteristics**, not a measured response claim for the individual Arcane Analog pedal. [2]
