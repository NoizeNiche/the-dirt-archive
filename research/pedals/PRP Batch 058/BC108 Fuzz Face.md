# Arcane Analog - BC108 Fuzz Face

## What this pedal is
Arcane Analog's BC108 Fuzz Face is a hand-wired silicon Fuzz Face build using BC108 transistors, standard Fuzz and Volume controls, and DC power. The builder identifies the photographed unit as the exact pedal offered.

## Sound
A BC108 Fuzz Face is generally on the brighter, higher-gain side of the Fuzz Face family, with more pronounced fuzz and useful cleanup from the guitar volume control. The exact Arcane Analog unit's final response also depends on the individual transistor selection and biasing.
