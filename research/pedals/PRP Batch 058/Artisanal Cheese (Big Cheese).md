# Artisanal Effects — Artisanal Cheese (Big Cheese)

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Artisanal Cheese (Big Cheese)
- **Builder:** Artisanal Effects
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Independent recreation of the Lovetone Big Cheese circuit in a substantially smaller enclosure, with four selectable fuzz modes and the same circuit used as one half of the builder's Cheddar Source. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
Artisanal Effects describes the Artisanal Cheese as a faithful reproduction of the discontinued Lovetone Big Cheese circuit. The builder emphasizes four separate operating modes, including the deliberately overbiased “cheese” mode, and builds each pedal by hand. [1]

The builder explicitly describes the product as an independent recreation and not an affiliated, authorized or endorsed Lovetone product. [1]

## Controls / modes
- **Volume**
- **Fuzz**
- **Tone**
- **Four-mode selector**
The fourth mode is the characteristic **overbiased / gated “cheese”** setting. The builder also notes that this exact circuit forms one half of the Cheddar Source dual pedal. [1]

## Circuit lineage
- **Primary lineage:** Lovetone Big Cheese.
- The builder states the Artisanal Cheese is the **identical circuit** used for the fuzz half of Cheddar Source. [1]
- This should be treated as circuit heritage rather than a claim of manufacturer affiliation with Lovetone. [1]

## Construction / hardware
Artisanal Effects states:
- Hand-built, one at a time.
- Compact enclosure compared with the original Big Cheese.
- Standard Boss-style **9VDC** adapter power.
- **No battery clip**. [1]
Exact internal board layout and component values are not published in the reviewed material.

## Version / build-history notes
- No numbered production revision was established.
- The principal documented modernization is the much smaller enclosure and contemporary power arrangement.
- The four-mode architecture is retained from the Big Cheese circuit heritage. [1]

## Sound
The builder describes four modes ranging from conventional fuzz through more restricted and synthetic textures, with the overbiased “cheese” mode producing a dying-amp/gated character. A user review of the current pedal also specifically describes the gated/restricted-bias mode and its usefulness for synth-like bass textures. The review is treated as user experience rather than component evidence. [1][2]
