# Artisanal Effects - Artisanal Cheese (Big Cheese)

## What this pedal is
A hand-built four-mode fuzz based on the Lovetone Big Cheese, made as an independent recreation in a smaller enclosure. Artisanal Effects states that its circuit is identical to one half of the dual Cheddar Source.

## Colorways
- Orange enclosure with purple artwork is documented.

## Versions and factory options
- Four-mode fuzz selector
- Volume, Fuzz, Tone
- 9VDC Boss-style adapter power
- No battery clip
- Hand-built construction

## Version changes
No numbered production revision history was established. The notable change from the historical Big Cheese is the compact modern enclosure.

## Sound
The pedal has four fuzz modes, including an overbiased/gated “cheese” setting, moving from conventional fuzz into restricted and synthetic textures. The builder also notes that the same circuit is used as one half of the Cheddar Source.
