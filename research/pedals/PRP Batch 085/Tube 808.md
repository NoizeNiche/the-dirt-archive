# PRP1 - Tube 808

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Big Tone Music Brewery
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive

## Research
Exact circuit and component replica of the classic Tube Screamer-style overdrive. Uses the original JRC4558D op-amp and adds a mini-toggle fat boost modification. Controls: Drive, Tone and Volume. Fully hand-wired analog, compact MXR-sized enclosure, true bypass 3PDT, bright LED, standard 9V adaptor and 9V battery clip. Sources: Effects Database (https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/bigknob/tube808); Big Knob reviews (https://bigknobpedals.com/reviews/); Big Knob Tube 808 demo (https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=VlCCoW_HYfg).
