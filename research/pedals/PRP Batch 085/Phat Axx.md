# PRP1 - Phat Axx

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Big Lloyde
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive

## Research
Phat AXX is a Big Lloyde / IndyGuitarist overdrive based on the classic Tube Screamer design with major tonal changes. It has Volume, Gain and Tone controls, plus Cream/Crunch and Bass Boost switching; the Tone control acts more like a presence control than a conventional tone control. True bypass and a 9V Boss-style power jack are documented. Sources: Effects Database (https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/biggloyde/phataxx); Reverb archival listing (https://reverb.com/item/65448016-best-price-big-lloyde-phat-axx-2005).
