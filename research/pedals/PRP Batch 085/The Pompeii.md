# PRP1 - The Pompeii

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Big Monk Electronic Device Co.
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Fuzz

## Research
The Pompeii is a silicon Fuzz Face design with a specially engineered fuzz buffer that lets the pedal be placed anywhere in the signal chain without the usual buffer interaction. Reviews describe a lower-gain silicon Fuzz Face voice through stronger BC10xC-type saturation, with strong volume-rolloff cleanup and sustain. Sources: Tinkercity review (https://www.tinkercitymusic.com/gearheads/2024/3/18/big-monk-devices-the-pompeii-a-modern-fuzz-face); Reverb listing (https://reverb.com/item/91636779-big-monk-electronic-device-co-the-pompeii-fuzz); Big Monk official site (https://bigmonkdevices.com/).
