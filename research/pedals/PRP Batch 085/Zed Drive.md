# PRP1 - Zed Drive

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Big Tone Music Brewery
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive

## Research
Big Knob's boutique overdrive based on the Hermida Zen Drive. A surviving Reverb listing confirms the Zen Drive lineage and the same basic control layout/function set, with true bypass and 9V or battery power. Sources: Big Knob official catalog (https://www.bigknobpedals.com/); Reverb archival listing (https://reverb.com/item/60035573-new-big-knob-pedals-zed-drive); current J-Guitar listing (https://www.j-guitar.com/products/detail.php?id=1606291).
