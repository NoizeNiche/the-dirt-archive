# PRP1 - Monk Boost

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Big Monk Electronic Device Co.
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive

## Research
Monk Boost is a modern update of the 18V Colorsound Powerboost concept. The builder adds a Master volume to the original Boost Volume and EQ controls, expanding the available level and tonal range. A recent Reverb listing identifies the builder, model and overdrive/distortion categories and dates the example to 2025. Sources: Big Monk Electronic Device Co. official site (https://bigmonkdevices.com/); Reverb listing (https://reverb.com/item/92603748-big-monk-colorsound-monk-boost).
