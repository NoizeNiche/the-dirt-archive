# PRP1 - Anthology Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Big Tone Music Brewery
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz

## Research
Anthology Fuzz combines six classic fuzz families in one analog pedal: Ge Face, Si Face, Brit 2, Brit 3, SupaBrit and USA. It uses four NOS germanium transistors and four silicon transistors, switching between Fuzz Face, Tone Bender and Big Muff-derived circuits. Effects Database documents the individual circuit families and component types. Source: Effects Database (https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/bigtone/anthologyfuzz).
