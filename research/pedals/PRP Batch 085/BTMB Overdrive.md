# PRP1 - BTMB Overdrive

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Big Tone Music Brewery
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive

## Research
BTMB Overdrive was designed from the ground up rather than as simply another modified Tube Screamer. It uses three controls: Level, Tone and Drive, with a very wide gain range from sparkly boost to higher-gain overdrive. A hand-set JFET operational-amplifier bias is used to shape a more tube-like response. The pedal is hand-assembled with through-hole components, true bypass, and can run from a 9V battery or 9-18V negative-tip supply. Source: Effects Database (https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/bigtone/overdrive).

## Sound

Effects Database documents the exact model as a ground up overdrive, not simply a modified Tube Screamer. Verified controls are Level, Tone, Drive . The design uses a hand set bias on its JFET based operational amplifier to shape tube like compression and sag characteristics. It supports a 9V battery or 9V, 12V, 15V, or 18V negative tip supply, draws about 4.5 mA, and lists 470 kOhm input and 100 kOhm output impedance. Construction is hand assembled with through hole, panel mounted components, Neutrik jacks, and 3PDT true bypass. Source: Effects Database.
