# PRP1 - BTMB Overdrive

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Big Tone Music Brewery
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive

## Research
BTMB Overdrive was designed from the ground up rather than as simply another modified Tube Screamer. It uses three controls: Level, Tone and Drive, with a very wide gain range from sparkly boost to higher-gain overdrive. A hand-set JFET operational-amplifier bias is used to shape a more tube-like response. The pedal is hand-assembled with through-hole components, true bypass, and can run from a 9V battery or 9-18V negative-tip supply. Source: Effects Database (https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/bigtone/overdrive).
