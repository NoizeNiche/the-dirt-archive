# PRP1 - Classic Overdrive+

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Big Tone Music Brewery
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive

## Research
Classic Overdrive+ is part of Big Tone Music Brewery's EQ'd Vintage overdrive family and is cataloged separately from the standard Classic Overdrive. The accessible Effects Database index confirms the exact model, while later used-gear evidence confirms that physical examples exist. The broader EQ'd Vintage platform used expanded three-band EQ with parametric mids, with hand-built analog construction and premium/NOS components. Model-specific controls beyond that shared platform were not sufficiently established in the checked sources, so they are not invented. Sources: Effects Database Big Tone Music Brewery index (https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/bigtone); Dave's Guitar Shop listing (https://www.davesguitar.com/collections/all-products-latest/products/classic-overdrive-plus-recent); Guitar Pedal X EQ'd Vintage overview (https://www.guitarpedalx.com/news/in-praise-of-the-under-appreciated-big-tone-music-brewerys-eqd-vintage-series-of-classic-fuzz-overdrive-and-distortion-pedals).
