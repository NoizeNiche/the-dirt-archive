# Chaos Custom Shop / Chaos FX — C.Drive

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** C.Drive
- **Builder:** Chaos Custom Shop / Chaos FX
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Chaos Custom Shop C.Drive, a Klon Centaur-inspired overdrive with selectable EQ range, clipping symmetry, and low-end boost.

## What this pedal is
RockBoard PedalPedia describes the C.Drive as a refined Klon Centaur-style overdrive with three mini-toggle switches controlling EQ range, clipping symmetry, and added low-end fatness. It uses an analog signal path and is handcrafted in Poland. [1]

## Colorways
No complete factory colorway sequence was established in the checked sources.

## Versions and factory options
The pedal documents:
- Gain, Tone, and Level-style overdrive controls.
- **EQ** three-position frequency-range switch.
- **SYM** three-position clipping symmetry switch.
- **FAT** low-end boost switch. [1][2]

## Version changes
The current C.Drive+ naming appears in contemporary listings, but the catalog record is **C.Drive**. The checked evidence is not sufficient to collapse the C.Drive and C.Drive+ into a single revision history.

## Transistor
No exact-model transistor part number was established.

## Diode
The pedal provides selectable symmetrical and asymmetrical clipping behavior, but exact diode part numbers were not established. [1][2]

## Sound
The C.Drive is intended to retain the balanced, clean/overdrive blend associated with the Klon family while adding selectable clipping symmetry and additional low-end content. The FAT switch directly addresses the low-frequency limitation commonly associated with the classic Klon topology. [1][2]
