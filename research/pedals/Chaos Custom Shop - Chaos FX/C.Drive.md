# Chaos Custom Shop / Chaos FX — C.Drive

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** C.Drive
- **Builder:** Chaos Custom Shop / Chaos FX
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Klon Centaur-inspired analog overdrive with three independent voicing switches for EQ range, clipping symmetry and low-end fatness. [1][2]

## Controls / switching
- **GAIN**
- **TONE**
- **LEVEL**
- **EQ:** Three-position frequency-range selector.
- **SYM:** Three-position clipping-symmetry selector.
- **FAT:** Adds low-end content. [1][2]

## Circuit lineage
- **Primary reference:** Klon Centaur.
- The design is a Klon-style interpretation with additional selectable clipping/EQ behavior, not a claim of identical historical components. [1]

## Transistor
- Exact production transistor/device part number is not publicly documented.
## Diode / clipping
- **SYM** provides selectable symmetrical/asymmetrical clipping behavior.
- Exact diode part numbers are not documented. [1][2]

## Power
- Exact model-specific voltage/current figures are not securely preserved in the reviewed sources.

## Construction
- Analog signal path.
- Handcrafted in Poland. [1]

## Version history
- **C.Drive** is the archive identity.
- Contemporary listings also use **C.Drive+**, but the evidence does not establish a complete revision history, so the names are not silently merged.

## Sound
The pedal is intended to retain the balanced boost/overdrive response associated with the Klon family while allowing substantial changes to clipping symmetry and low-end content. EQ and Fat broaden the range beyond the traditional three-knob Klon interface. [1][2]
