# Big Game Pedals - Solace State

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Solace State
- **Builder:** Big Game Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Distortion

## What this pedal is
Solace State is a preamp/distortion derived from Big Game Pedals' limited CVLT Preamp and documented as a derivative of the Peavey F800B-style preamp concept. Effects Database records a simplified two-band EQ and a clean-preamp/distortion switch.

## Controls
- Volume
- 50Hz bass boost/cut
- 5KHz treble boost/cut
- Switch between Clean Boost/Preamp and Distortion
- Contemporary builder discussion also documents an internal pre-gain trim.

## Circuit / architecture
- Internal voltage conversion raises the circuit to 24V.
- Reported lineage: Peavey F800B / CVLT-style solid-state preamp.
- Exact component part numbers were not established.

## Sound
The documented design moves from clean preamp/boost territory into aggressive solid-state distortion, with substantial low-end capability.
