# Big Joe Stomp Box Company - R-402 Classic

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** R-402 Classic
- **Builder:** Big Joe Stomp Box Company
- **Catalog type:** Distortion

## What this pedal is
R-402 Classic is an analog Raw Series overdriven tube-amp-style distortion with speaker/cabinet simulation.

## Controls
- Presence
- Gain
- Output
- Two-position cabinet-simulation toggle

## Power
- 9V battery or DC supply.
- Supports 9V, 12V and 18V operation.
- True bypass.

## Sound
The manufacturer describes thick, chunky harmonic saturation and a Texas Flood-style voice, with more gain and midrange bite than the Vintage Raw-series boxes.
