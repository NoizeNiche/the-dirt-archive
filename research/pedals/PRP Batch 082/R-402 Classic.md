# Big Joe Stomp Box Company - R-402 Classic

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** R-402 Classic
- **Builder:** Big Joe Stomp Box Company
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** Big Joe R-402 Classic, an analog Raw Series tube-amp-style distortion with selectable cabinet simulation.

## What this pedal is

Big Joe describes the R-402 Classic as a higher-gain Raw Series box built around thick, chunky harmonic saturation. A two-position switch engages speaker/cabinet simulation intended to smooth the response like a speaker-loaded amplifier.

## Controls

- **Presence**
- **Gain**
- **Output**
- **2-position cabinet simulation toggle**

## Sound

Big Joe characterizes the R-402 as a Texas Flood-style gritty distortion with more gain and midrange bite than its Raw-series Vintage boxes. The cabinet-simulation position smooths the frequency response.
