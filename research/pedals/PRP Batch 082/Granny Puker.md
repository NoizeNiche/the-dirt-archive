# Big John Effects - Granny Puker

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Granny Puker
- **Builder:** Big John Effects
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive

## What this pedal is
Granny Puker combines a preamp/overdrive section with a dirty analog octave-down effect. Big John documents it for bass, guitar and synth.

## Controls
- Volume
- Mixer: blends octave-down signal
- Puke: gain/distortion amount

## Operation
- Mixer minimum: usable as a distortion/overdrive.
- Mixer maximum: emphasizes the octave-down effect.
- Intermediate settings blend the two.

## Construction
- Hand-built, point-to-point soldered.
- Designed for bass, guitar and synth.

## Sound
The builder describes the overdrive as smooth and gritty at lower gain, progressing toward chunkier gain while the Mixer introduces increasingly prominent octave-down content.

## History
Big John's official timeline places Granny Puker in 2008.
