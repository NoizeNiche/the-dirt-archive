# Big Joe Stomp Box Company - R-404 Vintage II

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** R-404 Vintage II

- **Identity:** Big Joe R-404 Vintage II, an analog Raw Series vintage tube-amp-style overdrive with four selectable amp contours.

## What this pedal is

The R-404 Vintage II is a four-voice Raw Series overdrive. Big Joe describes four selectable tube-amp profiles with different combinations of warmth, bark, bite and attack.

## Controls

- **Presence**
- **Gain**
- **Output**
- **4-position amp-contour rotary switch**

## Power / construction

- **9V-18V DC**, center negative.
- Supports **12V and 18V**.
- Manufacturer documentation also lists **9V battery compatibility**.
- 2.1 x 5.5 mm barrel connection.
- **True bypass**.
- Status LED.
- 1/4-inch mono input and output.
- Dimensions: **5.5 x 4 x 2.5 in**.
- Weight: **22 oz**.

## Sound

Big Joe describes four amp profiles ranging from smooth to sharper, more biting responses, with emphasis on pick attack and vintage tone. The design remains intended to be transparent and responsive as Gain is increased.
