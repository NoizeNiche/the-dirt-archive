# Big Joe Stomp Box Company - R-404 Vintage II

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** R-404 Vintage II
- **Builder:** Big Joe Stomp Box Company
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive

## What this pedal is
R-404 Vintage II is an analog Raw Series vintage tube-amp-style overdrive with four selectable amp contour voices.

## Controls
- Presence
- Gain
- Output
- Four-position amp-contour rotary switch

## Power / construction
- 9V-18V DC center-negative.
- 12V and 18V supported.
- True bypass.
- 5.5 x 4 x 2.5 inches; 22 oz.

## Sound
The design is documented as a multi-voicing vintage tube-amp-style overdrive with four selectable amp contours.
