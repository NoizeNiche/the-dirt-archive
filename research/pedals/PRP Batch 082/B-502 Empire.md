# Big Joe Stomp Box Company - B-502 Empire

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** B-502 Empire
- **Builder:** Big Joe Stomp Box Company
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive

## What this pedal is
B-502 Empire is a dual-preamp analog overdrive/distortion developed with Paul "TFO" Allen. The two channels can operate independently or be combined in either order.

## Controls / switching
- Channel A: Presence, Drive, Output, Body, Attack
- Channel B: Gain, Output, Tone
- Firm / Sponge mode per channel
- Separate Line Drive control with up to +15dB boost
- Buffered / True Bypass switch
- Channel-order switch

## Power
- 9V battery or 9VDC supply.
- 12V and 18V support.
- Analog.

## Sound / architecture
The two preamp sections provide multiple gain and voicing combinations, with Body and Attack controls shaping overall response and the Sponge/Firm modes changing touch response.
