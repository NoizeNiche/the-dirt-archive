# Big Joe Stomp Box Company - B-502 Empire

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** B-502 Empire
- **Builder:** Big Joe Stomp Box Company
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive
- **Identity:** Big Joe's dual-preamp EMPIRE, developed in collaboration with Paul "TFO" Allen.

## What this pedal is

The B-502 EMPIRE is a two-channel analog overdrive/distortion with independent preamp sections that can be used alone or combined in either direction, A/B or B/A. Effects Database dates the product announcement to December 1, 2014 and says it was introduced at NAMM 2015.

## Channel controls

- **Channel A:** Presence, Drive, Output, Body, Attack.
- **Channel B:** Gain, Output, Tone.
- **Sponge/Firm** toggle for each channel changes the feel/response.
- **Line Drive:** Adjustable buffer/clean boost, up to **+15 dB**.
- **Bypass:** Selectable buffered or true bypass.
- **Channel order:** Selectable A/B or B/A.

## Power / construction

- **9V battery or 9VDC supply**.
- Manufacturer supports **12V and 18V** operation.
- 1/4-inch mono input and output.
- Dimensions: **6 x 4.25 x 3 in**.
- Weight: **22 oz**.
- Analog signal path.

## Circuit / architecture

The manufacturer describes two analog preamp sections rather than a single named clone circuit. Exact semiconductor part numbers and detailed schematics were not established.

## Sound / behavior

Big Joe describes the Sponge/Firm control as altering tube-like sag, response and feel. Body and Attack further shape tonal girth and pick dynamics. The two channels and their order create multiple combined gain/voicing configurations.

## History

The original release announcement was published **December 1, 2014**, with a planned NAMM 2015 debut. The product was developed with Paul "TFO" Allen.
