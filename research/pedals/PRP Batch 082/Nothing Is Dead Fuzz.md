# Big Game Pedals - Nothing Is Dead Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Nothing Is Dead Fuzz
- **Builder:** Big Game Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz

## What this pedal is
Nothing Is Dead Fuzz is a customizable fuzz from Big Game Pedals. A 2015 review documents user-selectable modifications including Tone Bypass, a diode selector and the "Creamy Dreamer" higher-gain modification.

## Controls / options
- Volume
- Tone
- Sustain
- Mids
- Optional Tone Bypass
- Optional diode-selection switch
- Optional Creamy Dreamer gain modification

## Circuit / components
- Muff-derived design, described in contemporary Big Game discussion as originating around the Sovtek Green Muff family.
- Diode selector can choose silicon, LED or no-diode clipping.
- Earlier examples also used NOS KD521A diode options.

## Sound
The selectable clipping and gain modifications make this a broad-format fuzz, from more open silicon/LED responses to higher-gain modified settings.
