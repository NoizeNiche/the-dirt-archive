# Big Game Pedals - Nothing Is Dead Fuzz

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** Nothing Is Dead Fuzz
- **Builder:** Big Game Pedals

## What this pedal is

Nothing Is Dead Fuzz is a customizable Big Game fuzz documented in 2015 as a modified Muff-family design. The surviving product/review material records several user-selectable circuit options rather than one immutable schematic.

## Controls / options

- **Volume**
- **Tone**
- **Sustain**
- **Mids**
- **Tone Bypass** option
- **Diode selector** option
- **Creamy Dreamer** high-gain modification

## Circuit / clipping

- Contemporary Big Game discussion places the design in the **Sovtek Green Muff** family.
- Documented selectable clipping options include **silicon, LED and no-diode** settings.
- Earlier development also references **NOS KD521A** diode options.

## Sound

The selectable clipping and gain modifications allow the pedal to move among more open silicon/LED responses and higher-gain modified Muff-style sounds.

## Version / evidence note

Because Big Game documented modifications and options over the life of the pedal, the archive does not assume every surviving unit contains every option listed in development or review material.

## Research confidence
