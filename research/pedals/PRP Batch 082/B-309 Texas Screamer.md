# Big Joe Stomp Box Company - B-309 Texas Screamer

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** B-309 Texas Screamer
- **Builder:** Big Joe Stomp Box Company
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Big Joe's B-309 Texas Screamer, developed with Johnny Winter.

## What this pedal is

Big Joe states that the Texas Screamer was developed in collaboration with **Johnny Winter**. It is a compact analog, tube-style overdrive with a three-control interface aimed at vintage rock/blues use.

## Controls

- **Gain:** Drive amount.
- **Tone:** Tone shaping.
- **Output / Level:** Output volume.

## Power / construction

- **9V battery** or **9V regulated DC supply**.
- 2.1 x 5.5 mm barrel connection, **center negative**.
- **True bypass**.
- Status LED.
- 1/4-inch mono input and output.
- Dimensions: **4.80 x 2.91 x 2.12 in**.
- Weight: **19 oz**.

## Circuit / components

- **100% analog**, according to the manufacturer.
- Exact transistor, op-amp and diode part numbers are not publicly documented in the checked sources.
- No unsupported Tube Screamer circuit attribution is made from the product name alone.

## Sound

Big Joe and contemporary review coverage describe the gain range as moving from a low, rumbling boost through bluesy crunch. Review coverage specifically notes a broad Tone range and useful high-frequency presence as Gain rises.

## History

Big Joe's collaboration with Johnny Winter is central to the product identity. A 2015 review confirms the pedal was already in the market by that year.
