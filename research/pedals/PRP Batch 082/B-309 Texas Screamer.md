# Big Joe Stomp Box Company - B-309 Texas Screamer

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** B-309 Texas Screamer
- **Builder:** Big Joe Stomp Box Company
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive

## What this pedal is
B-309 Texas Screamer was developed in collaboration with Johnny Winter. It is a three-control analog overdrive intended around a vintage rock/blues voice.

## Controls
- Gain
- Tone
- Output / Level

## Power / construction
- 9V battery or 9V regulated supply.
- True bypass.
- Analog.
- 4.80 x 2.91 x 2.12 inches; 19 oz.

## Sound
Contemporary descriptions place the gain range from low boost through bluesy crunch, with a tone control emphasizing useful high-end presence.
