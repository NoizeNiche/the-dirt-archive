# Big Joe Stomp Box Company - B-301 Tube

## PRP identity

## What this pedal is
B-301 Tube is an analog tube-style overdrive designed around a responsive, transparent gain structure.

## Controls
- Gain
- Tone
- Level
- Tone is documented as mid-range sensitive.

## Power / construction
- 9V battery or 9VDC supply.
- True bypass.
- Analog.
- 4.80 x 2.91 x 2.12 inches; 19 oz.

## Sound
The manufacturer describes a screaming tube-amp-style profile with preserved dynamics and resistance to muddiness.
