# Big Joe Stomp Box Company - R-403 Vintage

## PRP identity

## What this pedal is

Big Joe describes R-403 as a responsive overdrive with a strong emphasis on pick attack and vintage amp dynamics. A two-position low-boost switch adds additional low-frequency response.

## Controls

- **Presence**
- **Gain**
- **Output**
- **2-position low-boost toggle**

- **True bypass**.

## Sound

The manufacturer describes the R-403 as warm and smooth with substantial harmonic bark, plus a responsive pick-sensitive attack. The low-boost setting is intended to add the low-end behavior of a close-miked amp.
