# Big Joe Stomp Box Company - R-403 Vintage

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** R-403 Vintage
- **Builder:** Big Joe Stomp Box Company
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive

## What this pedal is
R-403 Vintage is an analog Raw Series overdrive designed around responsive vintage tube-amp dynamics.

## Controls
- Presence
- Gain
- Output
- Two-position low-boost toggle

## Power
- 9V, 12V or 18V operation.
- True bypass.
- 9V battery compatible.

## Sound
Manufacturer and review documentation describe a warm, syrupy response with substantial pick sensitivity and additional low-end boost.
