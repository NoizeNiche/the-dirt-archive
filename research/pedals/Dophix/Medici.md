## What this pedal is
The **Dophix Medici** is a fuzz pedal in Dophix's Italian-made catalog. Dophix's product page labels it **MEDICI MORE FUZZ** and identifies the product as made in Italy. [1]

The name is tied to the **Medici family**, the historical Italian dynasty associated with Tuscany and the Mugello region. Dophix uses that name as the product's thematic inspiration. [1]

## Versions

The archive therefore records MKII as an observed version reference rather than inventing additional revisions.
