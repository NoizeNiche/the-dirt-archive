## What this pedal is

The **Dophix Michelangelo Gold** is a special collector edition of the Michelangelo overdrive. Dophix's product page describes it as a collector's product created to celebrate one of the company's best-selling models. [1]

The archive treats it as an **Overdrive** and as a distinct catalog presentation because the manufacturer gives it a named Gold edition identity. It should not be assumed to represent a completely different circuit without additional evidence.

## Finish and edition evidence

The verified evidence identifies an **antique gold color box**. A 2024 Reverb listing independently identifies the product as the **Michelangelo Gold Edition 2024**. [2]

No evidence reviewed here establishes a circuit redesign, component change, or new functional architecture unique to the Gold edition.

## Relationship to Michelangelo

Michelangelo Gold should remain linked conceptually to the base **Michelangelo Overdrive Plus**. The available evidence supports an edition relationship, not a new unrelated dirt design.
