# BearFoot FX — Arctic White Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Arctic White Fuzz
- **Builder:** BearFoot FX
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Silicon Fuzz Face/Tone Bender-family fuzz derived from the BJFE/Bjorn Juhl lineage, with documented early and increased-gain production evolution. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
Arctic White Fuzz grew from Bjorn Juhl's fuzz designs and was produced by BearFoot as a practical, volume-responsive silicon fuzz. Juhl's archived history documents an early version followed by an increased-gain version, with associated changes in filtering, bass tightness and mid focus. [1]

- **FUZZ**

## Version history
- Early Arctic White Fuzz.
- Later increased-gain version.
- Juhl's archive also records possible smaller changes to filtering, bass tightness and mid focus.
- Cosmetic white finish is the defining presentation; isolated custom finishes are not treated as standard factory revisions. [1]

## Sound
AWF is designed as a usable, volume-responsive fuzz that can move from relatively restrained fuzz into nastier saturation. The underlying Juhl architecture emphasizes cleanup from guitar-volume changes and practical mid/bass balance rather than an excessively narrow vintage voicing. [1][2]
