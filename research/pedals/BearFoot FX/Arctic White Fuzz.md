# BearFoot FX - Arctic White Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Arctic White Fuzz
- **Builder:** BearFoot FX
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Silicon Fuzz Face/Tone Bender-family fuzz evolved from the BJFE fuzz lineage, with the BearFoot version adding a broader practical control set.

## What this pedal is
The Arctic White Fuzz grew out of BJFE's earlier Mystoury/Snow White/Smokey Orange fuzz lineage. Bjorn Juhl's archived description notes that the AWF received an increased-gain version after its first few pedals, with possible smaller changes to filtering, bass tightness and mid focus.

## Colorways
- White enclosure is the defining documented finish.
- BearFoot/BJFE archives also record custom-color one-offs elsewhere in the lineage, but these are not treated as standard Arctic White factory colorways.

## Versions and factory options
### Arctic White Fuzz
- Volume and Fuzz controls
- BearFoot listings document Treble on later/current cataloged examples
- Nature/voicing control appears in the later Polar Bear presentation; it is not assumed here to be universal to every AWF example.
- Silicon Fuzz Face-style design

## Version changes
The BJFE history records an early AWF followed by an increased-gain version, plus possible minor filtering/bass/mid revisions. The archive treats these as historical circuit evolution rather than inventing numbered factory versions.

## Transistor
- **Technology:** Silicon Fuzz Face-family design.
- **Exact production transistor part numbers:** Not publicly documented in the checked sources.

## Diode
- **Exact clipping/protection arrangement:** Not publicly documented.

## Sound
The AWF is intended as a usable, volume-responsive fuzz that can move from relatively tame fuzz toward nastier textures. The Fuzz Face/Tone Bender-inspired architecture is voiced for cleanup from the guitar volume and a broad, band-friendly response.
