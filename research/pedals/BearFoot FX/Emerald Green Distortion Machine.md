# BearFoot FX - Emerald Green Distortion Machine

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Emerald Green Distortion Machine
- **Builder:** BearFoot FX
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** Voxy distortion/booster designed around the feel of a late-1960s Vox AC15/AC30 family of sounds.

## What this pedal is
The EGDM began as a BJFE design requested for Komet/VOX-style use. Bjorn Juhl's description says he developed it around a late-1960s Vox AC15 Twin and conceived it as a distortion booster that could also work with already driven amplifiers.

## Colorways
- Early BJFE examples used flat green nitro paint; later examples used brighter oil-based green.
- BearFoot examples include translucent and metallic green finishes.

## Versions and factory options
### Emerald Green Distortion Machine
- Volume
- Drive/Distortion
- Voice
- Treble
- Internal treble trimmer in documented BJFE examples
- BearFoot versions were produced in germanium and later silicon forms, including the EGDM Silver.

## Version changes
The documented evolution includes the original high-gain Germanium EGDM and later BearFoot silicon EGDM Silver. The Drive control also interacts with compression, while Voice changes more than simple treble response.

## Transistor
- **Historical versions:** Germanium and silicon implementations are documented.
- Exact device part numbers are not treated as universal without unit-specific evidence.

## Diode
- **Exact clipping/protection arrangement:** Not publicly documented.

## Sound
The EGDM moves from bright, cleanish Vox-like response toward crunchy British drive and heavier saturation as Drive and Voice are advanced. Its compression and frequency response shift with the control settings, producing the feel of turning up a Vox-style amplifier while remaining useful as a booster into a driven amp.
