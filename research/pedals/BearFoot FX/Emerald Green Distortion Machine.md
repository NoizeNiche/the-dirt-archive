# BearFoot FX — Emerald Green Distortion Machine

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Emerald Green Distortion Machine
- **Builder:** BearFoot FX
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive
- **Identity:** Dynamic Vox-style distortion/booster derived from Bjorn Juhl's EGDM design, originally conceived around late-1960s Vox AC15/AC30 behavior. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
Bjorn Juhl's history describes EGDM as a distortion booster built around the feel of a late-1960s Vox AC15 Twin. It was intended to act both as an amp-like distortion and as a booster into an already driven amplifier. [1]

## Controls
- **VOLUME**
- **DRIVE / DISTORTION**
- **VOICE**
- **TREBLE**
- Some BJFE examples also document internal trim adjustments; these are not assumed universal to every BearFoot build. [1][2]

## Version history
- Original BJFE EGDM: Germanium-focused high-gain design.
- BearFoot production: associated EGDM line.
- Later **EGDM Silver:** Silicon implementation.
- Paint changes are cosmetic unless circuit evidence establishes otherwise. [1][2]

## Sound
EGDM is intended to move from bright, articulate Vox-like response toward crunchy British drive and heavier saturation as Drive and Voice increase. The circuit was also designed to work as a booster into an already driven amp, where its EQ/compression interaction becomes part of the overall amp response. [1][2]
