# BearFoot FX - Honey Bee Overdrive

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Honey Bee Overdrive
- **Builder:** BearFoot FX
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Low-gain, touch-sensitive overdrive designed around the response of small vintage tube combos.

## What this pedal is
The Honey Bee is a BJFE/BearFoot low-gain overdrive intended to behave like a small, worn-in vintage combo. The core controls are Volume, Drive and Nature, with the Nature control shaping the EQ and interacting with the saturation.

## Colorways
- A defining BearFoot production finish is the translucent yellow/gold enclosure.
- Special one-off finishes exist elsewhere in the BJFE/BearFoot family, but no complete factory Honey Bee colorway chronology was established.

## Versions and factory options
### Honey Bee Overdrive
- Volume
- Drive
- Nature
- Low-gain vintage-style overdrive
- BearFoot examples use standard 9V DC negative supply; the BJFE design history also documents related variants.

## Version changes
BJFE's archived history dates the Honey Bee to December 2002. The family later expanded with Deluxe and other Bee variants, but those remain separate catalog identities rather than being collapsed into the base Honey Bee.

## Sound
Honey Bee responds strongly to pick attack and guitar level, moving from almost-clean warmth into increasingly saturated, compressed small-amp-style overdrive. The Nature control is central to its character, moving between a fuller, bassier response and a brighter, tighter top end while also changing how the drive stage behaves.
