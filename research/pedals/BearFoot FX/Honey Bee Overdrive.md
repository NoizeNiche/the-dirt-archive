# BearFoot FX — Honey Bee Overdrive

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** Honey Bee Overdrive
- **Builder:** BearFoot FX
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Low-gain, touch-sensitive BJFE/BearFoot overdrive designed around small vintage-combo breakup, with Volume, Drive and Nature controls. [1][2]

## What this pedal is

Bjorn Juhl's archive dates the Honey Bee to **December 2002**. The design was intended to behave like a small, worn-in vintage combo, with the Nature control changing tonal balance and the feel of the drive stage rather than simply acting as a conventional tone knob. [1]

## Controls

- **VOLUME**
- **DRIVE**
- **NATURE** [1][2]

## Version history

- **Honey Bee:** original low-gain model.
- Later Bee-family products such as Honey Bee Deluxe and Honey Beest are separate catalog identities. [1][2]
- Late-paint tint differences are cosmetic.

## Sound

Honey Bee responds strongly to picking and guitar-volume changes, moving from nearly clean warmth through progressively fuller and more compressed small-amp-style overdrive. Nature changes bass/treble balance and also interacts with how the gain stage responds. [1][2]
