# BearFoot FX - Burgundy BossHorn Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Burgundy BossHorn Fuzz
- **Builder:** BearFoot FX
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Unusual Jordan Bosstone-derived fuzz/drive designed by Bjorn Juhl around a Crumhorn-inspired tonal concept.

## What this pedal is
The Burgundy BossHorn started with the Jordan Bosstone circuit, which Juhl modified into a more unusual fuzz/drive voice. BearFoot's public demo describes a wide range from low-to-medium dynamic fuzz drive to more extreme, grotesque fuzz textures.

## Colorways
- The documented BearFoot finish is a translucent flip-flop burgundy/pink/purple enclosure.
- This finish is a defining visual characteristic of the model.

## Versions and factory options
### Burgundy BossHorn Fuzz
- Volume
- Fuzz
- Bass
- Treble
- BearFoot documentation identifies both germanium and silicon examples.

## Version changes
Source evidence shows at least an early germanium version and later silicon examples. A TalkBass owner documented a silicon example using a 2N3638A device, but that observation is retained as example-level evidence rather than a universal factory parts list.

## Transistor
- **Historical versions:** Germanium and silicon examples are documented.
- **Example silicon device:** 2N3638A, reported from an inspected pedal.
- Do not assume every BossHorn uses 2N3638A.

## Diode
- **Exact clipping/protection arrangement:** Not publicly documented.

## Sound
The BossHorn can produce lower-gain, touch-sensitive dirt and drive before moving into rougher, more aggressive fuzz. The Bass and Treble controls are highly interactive with Fuzz, so small moves can radically reshape the resonant and frequency-heavy character.
