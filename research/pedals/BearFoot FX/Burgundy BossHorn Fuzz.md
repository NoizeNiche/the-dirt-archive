# BearFoot FX — Burgundy BossHorn Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Burgundy BossHorn Fuzz
- **Builder:** BearFoot FX
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz / Overdrive
- **Identity:** Jordan Bosstone-derived fuzz/drive redesigned by Bjorn Juhl around an unusually horn-like tonal concept, with documented Germanium and Silicon builds. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
The Burgundy BossHorn began with the Jordan Bosstone circuit, which Bjorn Juhl modified into a more unusual fuzz/drive voice. The model can cover lower-gain touch-sensitive drive and much more aggressive fuzz, with Bass and Treble interacting strongly with the Fuzz control. [1][3]

## Controls
- **VOLUME**
- **FUZZ**
- **BASS**
- **TREBLE**
- BearFoot examples exist in both Germanium and Silicon implementations. [1][3]

## Circuit lineage
- **Primary lineage:** Jordan Bosstone.
- Juhl's adaptation changes the original concept substantially enough that “Bosstone-derived” is more accurate than “clone.”

## Transistor
- **Documented technologies:** Germanium and Silicon.
- A TalkBass inspection of a Silicon Burgundy BossHorn reports **2N3638A**.
- The 2N3638A identification is scoped to that inspected silicon example, not generalized to all BossHorn production. [3]

## Diode / clipping
- Exact clipping/protection device and part number are not publicly documented.

## Version history
- Early Germanium versions are documented.
- Later Silicon versions are documented.
- The technology change is a substantive production variation, but no numbered V1/V2 designation is established.

## Sound
The model covers lower-gain, touch-responsive drive before moving into rougher fuzz. Bass and Treble are highly interactive with Fuzz, allowing strong shifts in weight and upper-frequency content. [1][2]

## Research confidence
- **Identity:** High
- **Bosstone lineage:** High
- **Germanium/Silicon split:** High
- **2N3638A example:** High as unit-specific evidence
- **Universal transistor:** Not established
- **Exact diode:** Unknown

## Deep research verification
Bjorn Juhl/BJFE archived documentation, BearFoot's exact-model demo and a TalkBass inspection were cross-checked. The result supports the Bosstone-derived architecture and both transistor-technology variants while preserving the 2N3638A as example-level rather than universal evidence. [1][2][3]
