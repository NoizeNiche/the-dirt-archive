# BearFoot FX — Dyna Red Distortion

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Dyna Red Distortion
- **Builder:** BearFoot FX
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** Dynamic British/plexi-style distortion derived from Bjorn Juhl's Dyna Red concept, with Drive, Tone and Volume controls. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
Dyna Red Distortion is an early Juhl design later produced by BearFoot. Its core concept is highly dynamic British-style distortion capable of moving from lighter breakup to substantial saturation. [1][2]

## Controls
- **DRIVE**
- **TONE**
- **VOLUME**
BearFoot examples may differ in exact additional production details, so controls not consistently documented across sources are not generalized. [2]

## Circuit lineage / history
- Juhl's archive identifies the earliest DRD as **FUZZ 2**, later becoming Dyna Red Distortion.
- The design belongs to the Juhl/BJFE lineage and is associated with British/plexi-style overdrive/distortion.
- BearFoot production should not be assumed component-for-component identical to every original BJFE example. [1]

## Sound
At lower Drive settings, Dyna Red is intended to preserve strong pick sensitivity and dynamic breakup. Increasing Drive moves it toward fuller British-style distortion with greater saturation and sustain. [1][2]
