# BearFoot FX - Dyna Red Distortion

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Dyna Red Distortion
- **Builder:** BearFoot FX
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** Dynamic British/plexi-style distortion derived from BJFE's Dyna Red concept.

## What this pedal is
Dyna Red Distortion was an early BJFE design later produced by BearFoot. The core idea is dynamic British-style distortion that can move from light breakup to substantial saturation and respond strongly to picking dynamics, especially at lower Drive settings.

## Colorways
- BJFE examples moved from early flat red nitrocellulose finishes to brighter oil-based red paint.
- BearFoot examples include distinctive translucent/red patterned finishes.

## Versions and factory options
### Dyna Red Distortion
- Drive
- Tone
- Volume
- BearFoot examples may include additional/variant controls depending on production configuration
- 9V DC operation is documented in BearFoot references.

## Version changes
The BJFE archive identifies the earliest DRD as FUZZ 2, then Dyna Red Distortion. It also records early-versus-later paint changes and individual decorated examples. BearFoot is a related production line, not assumed identical in every detail to the original BJFE units.

## Sound
With Drive below noon, the Dyna Red can retain strong pick sensitivity and dynamic breakup; above noon it moves toward fuller distortion with less dependence on attack. Its tonal target is the broad British/plexi family, with enough gain to become a dedicated crunch/distortion voice.
