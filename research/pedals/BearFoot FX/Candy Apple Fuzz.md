# BearFoot FX — Candy Apple Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Candy Apple Fuzz
- **Builder:** BearFoot FX
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** BearFoot/BJFE FUZZ 3-derived fuzz with documented germanium-hybrid and silicon production variants and multiple historical circuit refinements. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
Candy Apple Fuzz descends from Bjorn Juhl's **FUZZ 3**. The archived Juhl history documents circuit changes involving transistor beta, the Fuzz control and an added trimmer, while BearFoot examples exist in Germanium-hybrid and Silicon forms. [1][2][3]

## Controls
- **VOLUME**
- **FUZZ**
- **NATURE**
- **TREBLE**
The Nature and Fuzz controls are central to the model's usable range; Treble provides additional top-end shaping. [1][2]

## Version history
Historical BJFE documentation records:
- lower-beta first-stage transistor changes;
- revised Fuzz control behavior;
- an added trimmer extending the Nature control's usable range. [1]
BearFoot also produced both Germanium-hybrid and Silicon variants. [2][3]

These are substantive production/design changes, but the reviewed sources do not establish a clean numbered V1/V2 chronology.

## Sound
Candy Apple ranges from heavy gated fuzz through more open and octave-doubling textures. Nature, Fuzz and Treble shape the balance between hard-edged aggression and lower-gain dirt, while transistor-technology variants can alter attack and upper-harmonic response. [1][2]
