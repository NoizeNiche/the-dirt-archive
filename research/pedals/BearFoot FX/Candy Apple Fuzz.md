# BearFoot FX - Candy Apple Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Candy Apple Fuzz
- **Builder:** BearFoot FX
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Versatile fuzz spanning gated fuzz through octave-doubling textures, with a documented Germanium hybrid and later silicon BearFoot versions.

## What this pedal is
The Candy Apple Fuzz descends directly from the BJFE FUZZ 3. Bjorn Juhl's archive documents several circuit changes through the Candy Apple's history, including lower-beta first-stage transistors, a revised Fuzz control, and an added trimmer for more control over the gating/playability balance.

## Colorways
- Standard production is associated with translucent red/cherry-red finishes.
- BJFE's archive documents early nitro amber examples, later brighter red/oil-based paint, and special one-off color treatments.

## Version changes
The BJFE lineage includes three documented circuit changes before/through the production evolution: transistor-beta changes, revised Fuzz control behavior, and an added trimmer beyond the Nature control's range. BearFoot examples can also differ by germanium versus silicon implementation.

## Transistor
- **Factory technology:** Germanium hybrid versions documented.
- **Silicon variant:** BearFoot produced silicon versions.

## Diode
- **Exact clipping/protection arrangement:** Not publicly documented.

## Sound
Candy Apple covers a striking range from heavy gated fuzz to octave-doubling textures. The Nature, Fuzz and Treble controls let it move from hard-edged, aggressive fuzz toward lower-gain dirt, while the hybrid germanium/silicon variants give different attack and upper-harmonic emphasis.
