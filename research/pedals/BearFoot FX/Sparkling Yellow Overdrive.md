# BearFoot FX - Sparkling Yellow Overdrive

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Sparkling Yellow Overdrive
- **Builder:** BearFoot FX
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Multi-version BearFoot overdrive family aimed at Marshall-like crunch, boost, and distortion.

## What this pedal is
The Sparkling Yellow Overdrive family includes multiple versions with related but distinct voicings. The commonly documented SYOD3 version uses five controls and a mode control, while earlier SYOD1 and SYOD2 designs share the broad ability to move from near-clean boost to full distortion.

## Colorways
- Yellow/yellow-sparkle finishes are the defining documented appearance.
- Version 3 examples are commonly seen in bright yellow finishes.

## Versions and factory options
### SYOD family
- Earlier SYOD1/SYOD2 versions
- SYOD3 with expanded control layout
- Mid and tone controls are central to the family
- 5-15V operation is listed in BearFoot power references
