# BearFoot FX — Sparkling Yellow Overdrive

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Sparkling Yellow Overdrive
- **Builder:** BearFoot FX
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Versioned BearFoot/BJFE overdrive family covering SYOD1, SYOD2 and SYOD3, with pre/post EQ and progressively expanded switching. [1][2][3]

## What this pedal is
Sparkling Yellow Overdrive is a three-generation family rather than one fixed circuit. Effects Database documents **SYOD1** and **SYOD2** as related licensed BJFE designs with four primary controls and 5–15V operation, while **SYOD3** adds a rotary mode control that switches between the SYOD1 and SYOD2 voicings. [1][2][3]

## Version 1
- **VOLUME**
- **DRIVE**
- **M / MIDS:** Pre-distortion low-mid/cabinet-speaker shaping.
- **T / TREBLE:** Post-distortion treble shaping.
- **5–15V DC**
- Handmade in the USA.
- Licensed BJFE design. [1]

## Version 2
- **VOLUME**
- **DRIVE**
- **M / MIDS:** Pre-distortion low-mid shaping.
- **T / TREBLE:** Post-distortion EQ.
- **5–15V DC**
- Effects Database describes a rounder/more compressed American-amp orientation relative to SYOD1. [2]

## Version 3
- Adds a **two-position rotary switch** as a fifth control.
- The switch moves between the original SYOD1 and SYOD2 settings. [3]
- Reverb's exact-model listing confirms the SYOD3 model designation and identifies the switch as the control that cycles between those two modes. [4]

## Circuit lineage
- BJFE licensed designs.
- SYOD1/2 are distinct voicings.
- SYOD3 contains both through the rotary selector rather than being treated as merely a cosmetic update. [1][2][3]

## Sound
Effects Database and an independent tourbox review describe the family as spanning near-clean boost through blooming overdrive, distortion and fuzz-like saturation. SYOD1 is associated with a British-amp/speaker orientation, SYOD2 with a rounder American-amp response, and SYOD3 provides access to both via its rotary mode control. The LoMid and Treble controls are especially useful for tightening bass, adding warmth or increasing clarity. [1][2][5]

Effects Database's exact SYOD1/SYOD2/SYOD3 records, Reverb's exact SYOD3 listing and DecoWaves' 2015 tourbox review were cross-checked. The evidence directly supports the three-version structure, SYOD3's two-position selector, pre/post EQ layout and 5–15V operation. [1][2][3][4][5]
