## What this pedal is
BlueBerry Bass Overdrive is the BearFoot version of Bjorn Juhl's bass overdrive design. The BJFE archive dates the original 3BOD to December 2001 and retirement in January 2010; the design is known for an interactive EQ/drive response rather than a conventional post-drive treble-cut tone control.

## Colorways
- The classic BearFoot/BJFE finish is a deep berry/maroon color commonly described as mahogany.
- The BJFE archive notes slightly different late-paint tints and some unpainted units as the original paint supply ran out.

## Sound
BlueBerry is voiced to retain bass punch while producing a touch-sensitive overdrive that changes substantially as the Nature/EQ control is moved. Users also note that the control interacts with gain, so turning it toward the bass-heavy side can increase drive and output rather than simply darkening the sound.
