# BearFoot FX — BlueBerry Bass Overdrive

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** BlueBerry Bass Overdrive
- **Builder:** BearFoot FX
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** BearFoot version of Bjorn Juhl's BlueBerry Bass Overdrive / 3BOD design, originally documented by BJFE from 2001 through retirement of the original in 2010. [1][2]

## What this pedal is

BlueBerry Bass Overdrive is a bass-oriented Juhl design built around a highly interactive Drive/Nature response rather than a conventional simple post-drive tone control. The design retains bass punch while moving from lower-gain thickening into more saturated overdrive. [1][2]

## Controls

- **VOLUME**
- **DRIVE**
- **NATURE**
The Nature control changes the tonal/gain relationship rather than acting only as a conventional treble cut. [1][2]

## Sound

BlueBerry is intended to preserve bass weight while providing touch-sensitive overdrive. The Nature control interacts with gain, so moving it toward the bass-heavy side can change drive/output rather than simply darkening the sound. [1][2]
