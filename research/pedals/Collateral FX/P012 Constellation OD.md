# Collateral FX - P012 Constellation OD

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** P012 Constellation OD
- **Builder:** Collateral FX
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Collateral FX Constellation OD, a Rat-family low/medium-gain overdrive with asymmetric hard clipping and buffer functionality.

## What this pedal is
Collateral FX describes Constellation as a low/medium-gain overdrive whose structure harks back to the ProCo RAT while retaining bass and using asymmetric hard clipping. In bypass it functions as a buffer. [1]

## Sound
Constellation covers crunch through near-distortion, with strong string separation and intact bass. Higher voltage provides more headroom and less compression. [1]
