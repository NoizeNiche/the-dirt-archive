- **Identity:** Collateral FX Touchstone, a faithful reproduction of the 1986 Ibanez TS-10 Tube Screamer, updated with modern true-bypass switching.

## What this pedal is
Collateral FX describes Touchstone as a faithful reproduction of the rare Ibanez TS-10, retaining its characteristic mid-focused Tube Screamer behavior while updating the bypass hardware for reliability. The manufacturer positions the circuit as less compressed and more open than the TS808 and TS9 families. [1]

## Colorways
The manufacturer notes that colors and material details may vary with monitor calibration and supplier batches. No complete factory colorway sequence was established. [1]

## Versions and factory options
- Faithful TS-10 topology reproduction.
- JRC4558 operational amplifier.
- Philips MKT NOS signal capacitors.
- 1% metal-film resistors.
- 3PDT true-bypass switching replacing the original electronic/fragile bypass arrangement.
- 9V DC operation.
- 11 x 6 x 4 cm enclosure. [1]

## Version changes
The defining modernization is replacement of the original TS-10 switching system with a mechanical 3PDT true-bypass implementation. No additional numbered factory revision history was established. [1]

## Transistor
The gain stage centers on the **JRC4558 operational amplifier**; the manufacturer does not publish a discrete transistor configuration. [1]

## Sound
Touchstone targets the open, articulate TS-10 response while retaining the Tube Screamer's familiar mid emphasis, controlled low end, and versatile gain range. The manufacturer specifically characterizes the TS-10-derived voicing as less compressed and more open than TS808/TS9 predecessors. [1]
