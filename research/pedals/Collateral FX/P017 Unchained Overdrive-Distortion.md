# Collateral FX - P017 Unchained

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** P017 Unchained
- **Catalog identity:** P017 Unchained Overdrive/Distortion
- **Builder:** Collateral FX
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive / Distortion
- **Identity:** Collateral FX Unchained, a JFET amp-emulation drive inspired by 1970s and 1980s Marshall Super Lead rigs.

## What this pedal is
Collateral FX describes Unchained as a JFET-based emulation of the preamp and power-stage behavior of a Marshall Super Lead. The design includes an active three-band EQ and supports higher-voltage operation for more headroom and dynamics. [1]

## Sound
Unchained is voiced around the compressed, sustaining behavior associated with heavily driven 1970s and 1980s Marshall Super Lead amplifiers. Collateral FX describes a range from hard-rock drive through higher-gain 1980s metal and early-1990s-style distortion. [1]
