# Collateral FX - P013 Black Hole Sunn

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** P013 Black Hole Sunn
- **Builder:** Collateral FX
- **Catalog type:** Preamp / Overdrive
- **Identity:** Collateral FX Black Hole Sunn, based on the preamp architecture of the Sunn Model T.

## What this pedal is
Collateral FX states that Black Hole Sunn is based on the Sunn Model T preamp, including its Normal and Bright channel concept. The pedal gives those two gain stages independent controls and adds a gain boost stage plus a three-band EQ. [1]

## Sound
The Normal and Bright stages can be used together to imitate channel-jumping on the original Model T. Collateral FX describes the pedal as capable of bright cleans through bass-heavy high-gain sustain and notes its usefulness with lower tunings and other drive/fuzz pedals. [1]
