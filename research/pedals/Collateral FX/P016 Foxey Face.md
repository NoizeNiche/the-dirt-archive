# Collateral FX - P016 Foxey Face

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** P016 Foxey Face
- **Builder:** Collateral FX
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Collateral FX Foxey Face, a Fuzz Face-family fuzz with selectable germanium or silicon transistor sets and external Bias.

## What this pedal is
Collateral FX describes Foxey Face as a Fuzz Face-derived design requiring careful transistor-pair selection. The builder offers germanium and silicon versions and documents examples of AC128, OC72, BC108, and BC109 devices among its available transistor families. [1]

## Colorways
The manufacturer notes that colors and details can vary by production batch. No complete factory colorway sequence was established. [1]

## Versions and factory options
- Input control, internal.
- Level.
- Fuzz.
- Bias.
- Selectable germanium or silicon transistor versions.
- 9V operation.
- 11 x 6 x 4 cm enclosure. [1]

The germanium version includes an onboard voltage inverter so it can use standard Boss-style power supplies. [1]

## Version changes
The primary documented variant is the transistor-material choice: germanium or silicon. The archive keeps those as factory options rather than inventing numbered revisions.

## Transistor
Documented transistor families include **AC128, OC72, BC108, and BC109**, among other Japanese and Soviet devices. The exact installed pair varies by build and selected version. [1]

## Diode
No exact diode specification was published.

## Sound
The internal Input control limits how much signal reaches the fuzz circuit. External Bias compensates for temperature changes in germanium versions and can move the response from overdrive-like saturation toward more gated, Velcro-style decay. [1]
