# Collateral FX - P010 The Year of the Rat (1985)

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** P010 The Year of the Rat (1985)
- **Catalog identity:** P010 Year of Rat 1985
- **Builder:** Collateral FX
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** Collateral FX The Year of the Rat (1985), a Whiteface ProCo RAT reproduction using the Motorola LM308.

## What this pedal is
Collateral FX describes P010 as a faithful reproduction of the 1985 ProCo RAT Whiteface version and explicitly identifies the **Motorola LM308** op-amp as a defining component. [1]

## Colorways
No complete factory colorway sequence was established.

## Versions and factory options
- Whiteface RAT 1985 circuit target.
- LM308 op-amp.
- Filter.
- Gain.
- Volume/output.
- 9V operation.
- Guitar and bass compatibility.
- 11 x 6 x 4 cm enclosure. [1]

The manufacturer notes that some original Whiteface units used tantalum capacitors instead of electrolytics. [1]

## Version changes
The archive target is specifically the 1985 Whiteface form rather than a generic modern RAT.

## Transistor
The gain stage is centered on the **Motorola LM308 op-amp**. [1]

## Diode
No exact diode part number was published.

## Sound
The manufacturer describes a range from light crunch through overdrive and distortion into fuzz-like settings. Its Filter control reduces high frequencies as it is turned clockwise. [1]
