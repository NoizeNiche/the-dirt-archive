## What this pedal is
Collateral FX describes Element 118 as a dense, abrasive silicon fuzz designed around a Fuzz Face-inspired topology but expanded for mix placement and extreme gain. The circuit uses selected rare 1960s silicon transistors, a three-range Shift control for mid frequencies, and a React control that adds gain and output. [1]

## Sound
Element 118 is voiced for very high gain, dense sustain, and an abrasive wall of fuzz. The active mid section can move from scooped tones toward focused midrange, while Shift selects among three mid-frequency ranges. React adds further gain and output for heavier, feedback-friendly settings. [1]
