# Collateral FX - P008 Golden Ratio

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** P008 Golden Ratio
- **Builder:** Collateral FX
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Catalog identity:** P008 Golden Ratio Fuzz
- **Identity:** Collateral FX Golden Ratio, a one-knob fuzz/drive based on the Alan Yee Fuzzy Nuts.

## What this pedal is

Collateral FX states that Golden Ratio uses a one-transistor circuit with selected diodes and takes its starting point from the rare Alan Yee Fuzzy Nuts, followed by Collateral FX modifications. [1]

## Colorways

No complete factory colorway sequence was established.

## Versions and factory options

- One control.
- One-transistor fuzz architecture.
- Selected diodes.
- 9V operation.
- Guitar and bass compatibility.
- 11 x 6 x 4 cm enclosure. [1]

## Version changes

No formal numbered production revision history was established.

## Transistor

The manufacturer specifies **one transistor** but does not publish its part number. [1]

## Diode

The manufacturer specifies **selected diodes** but does not publish their part numbers. [1]

## Sound

The single control moves from a moderate boost through aggressive fuzz. Lower guitar-volume settings produce a more vintage fuzzy texture according to the manufacturer. [1]
