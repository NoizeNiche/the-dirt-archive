# Collateral FX - P028 Palindrome Dual Overdrive

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** P028 Palindrome Dual Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** P028 Palindrome Dual Overdrive
- **Builder:** Collateral FX
- **Catalog type:** Boost / Overdrive
- **Identity:** Collateral FX Palindrome Dual Overdrive, two independent Bluesbreaker-inspired drive channels with selectable clipping modes and shared expanded voicing concepts.

## What this pedal is
Collateral FX describes Palindrome as two rigorously matched, independent channels based on an expanded interpretation of the Marshall Bluesbreaker V1 circuit. Each side can operate independently or be stacked with the other for additional gain and volume. [1]

## Colorways
The manufacturer states that colors and material details may vary with monitor calibration and supplier batches. No complete factory colorway sequence was established. [1]

## Versions and factory options
- Two mirrored independent overdrive channels.
- Individual Volume, Gain, and Tone controls for each channel.
- Independent clipping selection on each side: clean boost, soft clipping, hard clipping, or combined clipping stages.
- Presence control for additional high-frequency shaping.
- 9V-18V DC operation.
- True-bypass switching for each channel.
- 11 x 6 x 4 cm enclosure. [1]

## Version changes
No formal numbered factory revision history was established. The documented operating variation is the choice of clipping mode on each side and 9V versus 18V supply operation. [1]

## Transistor
The manufacturer describes a manually built circuit using operational-amplifier gain stages, precision resistors, and film capacitors, but does not publish exact op-amp or transistor part numbers. [1]

## Diode
Each channel provides selectable diode-clipping behavior: no diode clipping for clean boost, soft clipping, hard clipping, or combined clipping stages. Exact diode part numbers are not published. [1]

## Sound
Palindrome is intentionally open and transparent rather than strongly mid-humped. The soft-clipping mode targets a smoother Bluesbreaker-like response, hard clipping produces a denser and more compressed drive, and the no-diode setting functions as a clean boost. The two channels can be stacked for thicker gain and sustain. [1]
