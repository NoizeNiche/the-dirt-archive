# Collateral FX - P021 Parsec

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** P021 Parsec
- **Catalog identity:** P021 Parsec Overdrive
- **Builder:** Collateral FX
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive / Boost
- **Identity:** Collateral FX Parsec, a hi-fi low-compression overdrive/boost using a high-fidelity op-amp and symmetrical silicon clipping.

## What this pedal is
Collateral FX describes Parsec as a highly transparent overdrive/boost designed not to add a mid hump or remove bass. The active device is a hi-fi op-amp, with symmetrical clipping provided by silicon diodes. [1]

## Sound
Parsec is designed to preserve the instrument's frequency balance, with natural compression and enough output to push an amplifier or another drive. Its gain range extends from clean boost into medium-gain overdrive. [1]
