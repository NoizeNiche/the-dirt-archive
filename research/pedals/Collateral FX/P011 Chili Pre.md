## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** P011 Chili Pre

- **Identity:** Collateral FX Chili Pre, a Boss CE-1 preamp reproduction.

## What this pedal is
Collateral FX built Chili Pre around the always-active preamp section of the Boss CE-1 chorus. The pedal isolates that preamp, adds true bypass, and converts the original mains-powered scheme to standard pedal 9V with internal conversion to approximately +14V/-13V. [1]
