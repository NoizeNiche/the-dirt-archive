# Collateral FX - P024 Sirius Dual Overdrive

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** P024 Sirius Dual Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** P024 Sirius Dual Overdrive
- **Builder:** Collateral FX
- **Catalog type:** Boost / Overdrive
- **Identity:** Collateral FX Sirius Dual Overdrive, a two-channel drive combining an ODR-1-inspired green channel and a Bluesbreaker V1-inspired blue channel.

## What this pedal is
Collateral FX describes Sirius as two independent overdrive circuits in one enclosure. The green channel is based on the Nobels ODR-1, refined for firmer low end, while the blue channel is rooted in the Marshall Bluesbreaker V1 and is voiced as a transparent, lower-compression drive/boost. The two sides can be used separately or stacked. [1]

## Colorways
The manufacturer states that colors and material details can vary with monitor calibration and supplier batches. No complete factory colorway sequence was established. [1]

## Versions and factory options
- Two independent drive channels.
- Individual Volume, Gain, and Tone controls for each side.
- Green channel: modified ODR-1 architecture with richer open mids and firmer bass.
- Blue channel: refined Bluesbreaker V1 architecture with expanded output and balanced highs.
- Independent footswitching / true bypass for each channel.
- 9V DC operation.
- 6.5 x 11.8 x 9 cm enclosure. [1]

## Version changes
No formal numbered factory revision history was established. The defining functional variation is the choice of using either channel individually or combining both through gain stacking. [1]

## Sound
The green channel emphasizes open, rich mids with tighter bass and is designed to remain articulate at higher volumes. The blue channel aims for a transparent, dynamic response with low compression and ample output. Stacking the channels creates denser gain and sustain while retaining pick attack. [1]
