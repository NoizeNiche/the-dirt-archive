# Collateral FX - P005 Continuum OD

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** P005 Continuum OD

- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Collateral FX Continuum OD, a dual-channel overdrive combining TS10 and Klon-derived voices.

## What this pedal is
Collateral FX describes Continuum as the combination of characteristics from the Ibanez TS10 and Klon Centaur in one enclosure, with Collateral FX modifications to both ideas. The two channels can be used independently or stacked. [1]

## Sound
Collateral FX designed Continuum to combine TS10 mid-focused overdrive characteristics with Klon-style transparent boost behavior. Running both channels together increases the available gain and stacking range. [1]

### Verified sound evidence
The killer Strat tone is the result, among other factors, of using pedals like the Ibanez TS10 and the Klon Centaur.
With the Continuum, you can obviously achieve that Strat sound leaning towards John Mayer and Stevie Ray Vaughan, but you can go further, especially if you combine both channels and crank up the gain.
But if you’re not so keen on his sound, remember that the Continuum is an opportunity to have in one pedal the two most iconic overdrives in history.
