# Collateral FX - P004 Icarus

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** P004 Icarus
- **Builder:** Collateral FX
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Collateral FX Icarus, a Klon-derived overdrive/boost with germanium diodes and a clean-signal blend.

## What this pedal is
Collateral FX states that Icarus is based on the Klon circuit but modifies the gain behavior and treble voicing. The gain control is more linear, and the treble control is shifted toward the mid-bass region. [1]

## Sound
Icarus covers clean boost through main overdrive duty. Collateral FX emphasizes more useful low-gain range than a stock Klon-style sweep and a warmer treble placement for use with bright amplifiers. [1]
