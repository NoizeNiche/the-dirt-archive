# Collateral FX - P022 Drive for the Dead

- **Identity:** Collateral FX Drive for the Dead, a JFET amplifier-emulation overdrive/preamp voiced around classic Ampeg tube-amp behavior.

## What this pedal is
Collateral FX describes Drive for the Dead as a JFET emulation of the sonic structure of classic Ampeg tube amplifiers. The pedal uses a two-position gain/voicing switch: **Deaf** covers the lower-gain preamp/overdrive range, while **Dead** supplies substantially more gain and compression with a fuzzy high-gain character. [1]

## Colorways

## Versions and factory options
- JFET tube-amp emulation architecture.
- Two gain/voicing modes: Deaf and Dead.
- Active equalization intended to preserve low end, including for bass use.
- 9V-18V operation.
- Guitar and bass compatibility.
- Overdrive / preamp function.
- 6.5 x 13 x 9 cm enclosure. [1]

Running the pedal at 18V increases headroom, reduces compression, and increases response to picking dynamics and the instrument volume control. [1]

## Version changes
No formal numbered factory revision history was established. The primary documented operating variation is the 9V versus 18V supply range and the two factory voicing modes. [1]

## Transistor
The manufacturer specifies **JFET transistors** as the active gain technology and describes the circuit as an emulation of Ampeg tube amplifier stages, but does not publish exact JFET part numbers. [1]

## Diode
No exact diode part number or clipping-diode topology was published on the manufacturer page. [1]

## Sound
The Deaf mode is intended for lower-gain classic-rock, blues, and preamp use, while the Dead mode moves into high-gain, compressed, fuzzy territory associated by the manufacturer with the *Songs for the Deaf* era of QOTSA. The circuit is designed to retain bass weight and also works as a bass preamp/overdrive. [1]
