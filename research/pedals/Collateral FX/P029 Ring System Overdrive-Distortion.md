# Collateral FX - P029 Ring System Overdrive

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** P029 Ring System Overdrive/Distortion
- **Catalog identity:** P029 Ring System Overdrive/Distortion
- **Builder:** Collateral FX
- **Catalog type:** Boost / Overdrive / Distortion
- **Identity:** Collateral FX Ring System Overdrive, a limited reproduction of the Ibanez MT10 Mostortion using a Harris/Intersil CA3260 MOSFET op-amp and Toshiba MA165 diodes.

## What this pedal is
Collateral FX describes Ring System as a limited, numbered recreation of the Ibanez MT10 Mostortion circuit. The builder states that approximately 20 units use original NOS CA3260 MOSFET op-amps and Toshiba MA165 signal diodes, paired with active three-band equalization and true-bypass switching. [1]

## Colorways
No complete factory colorway sequence was established. The manufacturer notes that colors and material details can vary with display calibration and material batches. [1]

## Versions and factory options
- Limited numbered run of approximately 20 units.
- Harris/Intersil CA3260 MOSFET op-amp.
- Toshiba MA165 diodes.
- Active three-band Bass, Mid, Treble equalization.
- 9V DC operation.
- True-bypass 3PDT switching.
- 11 x 6 x 4 cm enclosure. [1]

## Version changes
No formal numbered production revision history was established beyond the limited numbered run. [1]

## Transistor
The gain stage is built around the rare **CA3260 MOSFET operational amplifier** associated by the manufacturer with the original MT10 circuit. The pedal page does not specify discrete transistor part numbers. [1]

## Diode
Collateral FX specifies **Toshiba MA165** signal diodes and describes the pedal as using the original NOS clipping components of the vintage design. [1]

## Sound
The Mostortion-derived circuit is described as a low-to-medium-gain overdrive with rich low frequencies rather than a high-gain distortion. Its active three-band EQ can move the response from more scooped to more focused mids, while bass and treble controls allow the pedal to add weight without losing note definition. [1]
