# Collateral FX - P026 Banana Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** P026 Banana Fuzz
- **Catalog identity:** P026 Banana Fuzz
- **Builder:** Collateral FX
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Collateral FX Banana Fuzz, a Fuzz Face-derived fuzz offered with selected germanium or silicon transistor sets and an external Bias control.

## What this pedal is
Collateral FX describes Banana Fuzz as a Fuzz Face derivative tailored for hot/tropical climates. The germanium version uses carefully selected vintage transistors tested for gain and leakage current under warmer conditions, while a silicon option is available for greater thermal stability. [1]

## Colorways
The manufacturer states that colors and material details may vary with monitor calibration and supplier batches. No complete factory colorway sequence was established. [1]

## Versions and factory options
- Fuzz Face-derived circuit.
- External Bias control.
- Germanium or silicon transistor options.
- Germanium version uses selected vintage devices from the 1960s/1970s transistor families; exact installed pair varies by build.
- 1/2W Carbon Comp resistors selected to 5% tolerance.
- NOS axial capacitors, including Philips or Siemens examples.
- 9V operation.
- Guitar and bass compatibility.
- 11 x 6 x 4 cm enclosure. [1]

The germanium version uses -9V polarity and can run from a 9V battery or isolated supply; the silicon version uses standard +9V operation. [1]

## Version changes
The documented factory variation is the choice between germanium and silicon transistor sets. The external Bias control allows adjustment of the operating point, particularly useful when the germanium version is exposed to changing temperatures. [1]

## Transistor
Collateral FX specifies **pairs of carefully selected germanium transistors** for the germanium version, with vintage examples including Toshiba, Matsushita, and Texas Instruments, or **silicon transistors** for the thermally stable version. Exact installed part numbers are build-dependent and are not fixed on the product page. [1]

## Diode
No exact diode specification was published. [1]

## Sound
The pedal is intended to provide the sustain, dynamic response, and cleanup associated with a Fuzz Face while reducing the excessive noise and gating behavior that hot temperatures can cause in germanium circuits. The manufacturer emphasizes smooth cleanup from the guitar volume control and a glassy clean response at lower volume settings. [1]
