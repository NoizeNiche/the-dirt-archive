# Collateral FX - P023 The Bendz

## PRP identity

- **Catalog identity:** P023 The Bendz Fuzz

- **Identity:** Collateral FX The Bendz, a modern Tonebender-oriented fuzz using selected NOS silicon and germanium transistors.

## What this pedal is
Collateral FX presents The Bendz as a modern reinterpretation of the classic Sola Sound Tonebender family. The design aims to retain the aggressive, mid-forward, sustaining character associated with Tonebender-style fuzz while reducing the size, power limitations, temperature sensitivity, and inconsistency associated with vintage germanium units. [1]

## Versions and factory options
- Tonebender-inspired fuzz architecture.
- Two selected NOS silicon transistors for the main fuzz circuit.
- A carefully selected germanium transistor is also used in the build to permit standard Boss-style power supply operation.
- 9V operation.
- 11 x 6 x 4 cm enclosure. [1]

## Version changes
No formal numbered factory revision history was established. The defining construction choice is the use of selected NOS silicon devices with germanium circuitry retained in the design for practical power compatibility. [1]

## Transistor
Collateral FX specifies **two NOS silicon transistors** selected for performance and stability, plus a **carefully selected germanium transistor** in the power arrangement. Exact transistor part numbers are not published on the product page. [1]

## Diode
No exact diode specification was published. [1]

## Sound
The Bendz is voiced for the aggressive, high-gain, mid-rich and sustaining response associated with Tonebender-style fuzz. Collateral FX describes the circuit as suited to 1960s and 1970s rock and garage rock, with a more balanced low-frequency response and vocal-like mid character than the original-era circuits. [1]
