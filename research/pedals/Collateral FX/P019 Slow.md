# Collateral FX - P019 Slow

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** P019 Slow
- **Catalog identity:** P019 Slow Distortion/Fuzz
- **Builder:** Collateral FX
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz / Distortion
- **Identity:** Collateral FX Slow, a silicon Big Muff-oriented fuzz with extensive internal voicing switches.

## What this pedal is
Collateral FX describes Slow as a high-gain silicon fuzz designed for guitarists and bassists who want Big Muff-style sustain with tighter low end and additional mid control. [1]

## Colorways

## Versions and factory options
Documented features include:
- Silicon fuzz/distortion architecture.
- Multiple internal DIP-switch banks.
- Modern/full-range voicing option.
- Multiple mid voicings, including traditional Muff-style scoop and mid-forward settings.
- Bass-boost settings.
- 9V operation.
- 6.5 x 13 x 9 cm enclosure. [1]

## Diode
No exact diode part number was published. The internal switches alter clipping/voicing behavior, but the manufacturer does not provide a component-level clipping chart. [1]

## Sound
The internal switch banks significantly reshape frequency balance and compression. One group broadens the voicing and increases compression, another changes the midrange from a traditional Muff scoop toward stronger mids, and a final group adds Russian-style bass weight. [1]
