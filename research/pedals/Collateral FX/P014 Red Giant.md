# Collateral FX - P014 Red Giant Distortion

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** P014 Red Giant Distortion
- **Builder:** Collateral FX
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Preamp
- **Identity:** Collateral FX Red Giant Distortion, a Revv G3-inspired high-gain distortion/preamp.

## What this pedal is
Collateral FX describes Red Giant as starting from the Revv G3 circuit and refining it with smoother highs, strong note separation, multiple clipping stages, and a three-position Mass voicing switch. [1]

## Versions and factory options
- Bass, Mid, Treble active EQ.
- Three-position Mass voicing switch.
- Multiple clipping stages.
- Preamp / distortion / overdrive functions.
- 9 to 18V operation.
- 11 x 6 x 4 cm enclosure. [1]

## Sound
The three Mass positions provide distinct gain/compression voicings, ranging from modern high gain to a more Marshall-inspired response and a less-compressed, mid-forward high-gain voice. The active three-band EQ allows broad shaping. [1]
