# AJ Peat — Dirty Buzzard - Overdrive

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Dirty Buzzard - Overdrive
- **Builder:** AJ Peat
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz & Distortion
- **Identity:** Hybrid overdrive/fuzz/distortion design using NOS germanium and silicon transistors, with a separate boost channel. [1]

## What this pedal is
Dirty Buzzard combines germanium and silicon transistor stages and is designed to move from transparent overdrive through higher-fidelity distortion into articulate high-gain fuzz. A dedicated Boost footswitch provides an additional level lift for leads or extra output. [1]

## Sound
Dirty Buzzard is intended to cover a wide dirt range, from relatively transparent overdrive through articulate distortion and into high-gain fuzz. The bias and attack controls allow the transistor stages to be pushed toward different textures, while the independent boost switch adds extra level for leads. [1]
