- **Archive parent:** Fat Peacock - Distortion w/ Boost

- **Identity:** British-voiced analog distortion/overdrive with a built-in boost channel. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
Fat Peacock is designed around British amp-style drive, ranging from 1960s Plexi-style tones toward more modern high-gain British distortion. The right footswitch engages the built-in boost, giving the pedal a separate level lift for solos. [1][2]

## Sound
Fat Peacock is voiced toward British-style crunch and high gain, with bass and treble controls providing broad tonal shaping around the core drive character. The integrated boost can raise the output for lead parts without requiring a second pedal. [1][2]
