## What this pedal is
Screaming Flamingo is a two-stage drive design intended to retain transparency, grit, gain and fidelity while offering separate overdrive and distortion controls. AJ Peat identifies the use of three stacked op-amps as a key part of the circuit's tone. [1]

## Sound
The Screaming Flamingo covers a broad drive range with a two-stage architecture that preserves clarity while adding grit and gain. Separate Drive and Distortion controls let the player move between lower-drive overdrive textures and denser distortion without reducing the pedal to a single fixed voicing. [1]
