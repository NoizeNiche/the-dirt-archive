# Colfax Recorders - Zephyr Overdrive

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Zephyr Overdrive
- **Builder:** Colfax Recorders
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Colfax Recorders Zephyr Overdrive, a four-control overdrive with independent tone and mid-hump shaping.

## What this pedal is
Pedal of the Day describes Zephyr as a versatile overdrive that can function as an always-on transparent crunch or move into stronger distortion. The design combines a conventional Level and Gain section with a two-band tone-shaping network using Tone and Hump controls. [1]

## Sound
Zephyr is designed to cover boost-to-crunch through heavier distortion. The Tone control works with the Hump control to shape high-shelf and midrange emphasis, making the pedal useful as an always-on drive or a more aggressive dirt stage. [1]
