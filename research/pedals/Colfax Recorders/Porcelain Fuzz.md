# Colfax Recorders - Porcelain Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Porcelain Fuzz

- **Identity:** Colfax Recorders Porcelain Fuzz, a three-knob fuzz with strong cleanup from the guitar volume control and high input impedance.

## What this pedal is
Pedal of the Day describes Porcelain Fuzz as a three-knob fuzz designed to cover dirty, squishy textures through gnarlier psychedelic fuzz. The Tone control can boost or scoop the fuzz bite, and the Fuzz control responds well to guitar-volume cleanup. [1]

## Sound
Pedal of the Day characterizes Porcelain as capable of squishy fuzz, stronger psychedelic saturation, and useful tonal placement via the Tone control. The pedal also cleans up with guitar-volume reduction and works naturally with the builder's Zephyr Overdrive for stacking. [1]
