## Sound

The Keeley Dark Side combines a fuzz section with a separate modulation/delay section.
A detailed product description identifies the fuzz as a Big Fuzz-style design derived from studying earlier transistor-based fuzzes, while the second side provides multi-head tape delay and four modulation families: flanger, rotary speaker, U-Vibe, and phaser.
**Older units:** TRS insert arrangement between fuzz and modulation/delay.
