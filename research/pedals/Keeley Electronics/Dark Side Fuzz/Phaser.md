## What this pedal is
The Keeley Dark Side combines a fuzz section with a separate modulation/delay section. A detailed product description identifies the fuzz as a Big Fuzz-style design derived from studying earlier transistor-based fuzzes, while the second side provides multi-head tape delay and four modulation families: flanger, rotary speaker, U-Vibe, and phaser. [1]

The archive keeps the catalog classification **Fuzz** because that is the canonical catalog type for this record, while the research record documents the pedal's broader multi-effect architecture rather than reducing it to fuzz alone. [1][2]

## Versions and factory options
- The evidence establishes **KDark / Dark Side** as the core model identity. [1][2]
- Later units use an external push-button effect-order switch; older units used a TRS insert arrangement for the internal routing point. [1]
- No separate cosmetic factory colorway chronology was established in this pass.

## Version changes
- **Older units:** TRS insert arrangement between fuzz and modulation/delay. [1]
- **Newer units:** external push-button effect-order switching. [1]
- The archive does not merge those physical control/routing changes into separate catalog records because the available evidence treats them as revisions of the Dark Side identity. [1]

## Controls and routing
The manual/product documentation establishes:
- **Fuzz:** LEVEL, FILTER, FUZZ, FLAT/SCOOP, FULL/HALF. [1][2]
- **Modulation/Delay:** LEVEL, BLEND/TAPE HEAD, DEPTH/TIME, RATE/FDBK, plus DELAY/MOD and PHASE/ROTARY/UVIBE mode selection. [1][2]
- **Expression:** TRS expression-pedal input. [1]
- **ORDER:** changes whether fuzz occurs before or after the modulation/delay section on the revised version. [1]

## Circuit / architecture
Keeley describes a hand-built board containing an **analog fuzz** section and a **24-bit DSP engine** for the modulation/delay side. [1] The fuzz section is described as a 1977-era Big Fuzz-style design with changes intended to capture characteristics of earlier transistor fuzzes. [1]

## Sound
Keeley describes the fuzz as a more transparent Big Fuzz-style voice with sustain, while the modulation/delay side covers multi-head tape delay, flanger, rotary, U-Vibe, and phaser textures. [1] These are manufacturer descriptions, not independent measurements.
