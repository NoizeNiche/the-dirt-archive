## Sound
Designed to create overwhelming textures and dreamy soundscapes, Loomer delivers soft‑focus ambience, reverse reverb rhythms, cascading octave feedback, and crushing fuzz in one inspiring pedal.
This is not a subtle effect—it’s a thrill machine built for players who want their tone huge, immersive, and unapologetically bold.
A low‑pass tone filter, inspired by the Jazzmaster rhythm circuit and centered at 156.6Hz , allows thick, dark textures that pair perfectly with fuzz.
