# Attic Audio — PUTTY Saturation Preamp

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** PUTTY Saturation Preamp
- **Builder:** Attic Audio
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz / Overdrive
- **Identity:** Multi-role saturation preamp that can operate as boost, overdrive, distortion or fuzz.

## What this pedal is
Guitar World describes the Putty as a Malta-built pedal from Luke Galea, partly inspired by the Boss FA-1 preamp. It combines saturation, active EQ and multiple clipping/EQ-position options.

## Colorways
- Review photography documents a light-colored utilitarian enclosure with hand-drawn artwork.

## Versions and factory options
### Putty production
- Gain / Saturation
- Volume
- Bass ±15dB around 350Hz
- Treble ±10dB around 1.2kHz
- Low / Off / High clipping
- Pre / Post gain EQ
- Symmetrical / Asymmetrical clipping
- Momentary or latching footswitch
- 9V pedalboard power
- Not intended for 18V input

## Sound
Putty ranges from clean boosting and light rhythm drive through saturated overdrive and fuzz. Active EQ and the clipping/symmetry controls move the response toward heavier, more aggressive textures.
