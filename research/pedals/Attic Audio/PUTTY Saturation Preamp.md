# Attic Audio — PUTTY Saturation Preamp

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** PUTTY Saturation Preamp
- **Builder:** Attic Audio
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz / Overdrive
- **Identity:** Multi-role saturation preamp from Maltese builder Luke Galea, drawing on the Boss FA-1 preamp concept and combining active EQ with selectable clipping and EQ-position modes. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
Attic Audio describes Putty as a flexible preamp that can function as clean boost, overdrive, distortion or fuzz. Its two defining design ideas are FA-1-style clear EQ and a saturation-based gain approach intended to remain musical at higher gain. [1]

## Controls / switching
- **Volume**
- **Saturation / Gain**
- **Bass:** ±15dB around 350Hz.
- **Treble:** ±10dB around 1.2kHz.
- **Clipping:** Low / Off / High.
- **EQ position:** Pre-gain / Post-gain.
- **Clipping symmetry:** Symmetrical / Asymmetrical.
- **Footswitch:** Flexible momentary or latching operation. [1][2]

## Circuit lineage / architecture
- **Inspiration:** Boss FA-1 preamp.
- **Gain concept:** Saturation rather than reliance on a conventional single clipping stage.
- The reviewed public sources do not disclose a complete schematic or active-device BOM. [1][2]

## Electrical specifications
Attic Audio publishes:
- **Input:** 1/4-inch TS, unbalanced, 2.2MΩ.
- **Output:** 1/4-inch TS, unbalanced, 470Ω.
- **Switching:** Flexible true bypass, momentary/latching.
- **Enclosure:** Standard 125B, approximately 122 × 66 × 39mm.
- **Power:** 9V DC, center-negative; isolated supply recommended.
- Guitar World's review also notes the pedal should not be fed 18V. [1][2]

## Sound
Attic Audio positions Putty from clean enhancement and boost through overdrive, distortion and fuzz. Guitar World's August 2026 review describes a broad saturation range and notes that high-gain settings retain substantial note clarity. These are source-described behavior, not measured frequency-response data. [1][2]
