## What this pedal is
The Dream Mangler combines two fuzz circuits in one enclosure. Surviving product descriptions identify a **Disaster Fuzz** on one side and a vintage fuzz circuit on the other, with a joystick controlling intensity and texture across the design.

## Sound
The Dream Mangler is built for interactive fuzz textures rather than a conventional fixed-gain fuzz. The two circuits can produce contrasting fuzz characters, while the joystick changes intensity and texture and interacts with the oscillation behavior of the Disaster Fuzz side.
