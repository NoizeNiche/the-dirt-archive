## What this pedal is
The Necromancer is a Super Fuzz-style fuzz with a switchable EQ section. Dwarfcraft described the circuit as starting from the Super Fuzz-style section of the Silver Rose V2 and adding a dedicated middle band to the EQ.

## Versions and factory options
- Super Fuzz-style fuzz circuit.
- Three-band EQ.
- **Mids** toggle creates the classic mid-scooped setting.
- **Mids** control is tuned around the deepest dip created by the Mids toggle.
- Designed for guitar and capable of use with bass and other line-level sources.

## Sound
The Necromancer retains the aggressive character of a Super Fuzz-style circuit while adding much more control over the midrange. Its Mids switch creates the classic scoop, and the Mids control lets the player adjust around that dip. The wide EQ range can push the pedal toward very large low-end and high-end responses.
