## Sound
Both ran on standard 9V DC center-negative power (2.1mm barrel), drawing 18mA (Pitch Grinder) and 22mA (Surrender Dorothy), with no battery option—a deliberate choice to preserve voltage stability and prevent tone compression under load.
I spent over three hours at the booth, demoing both units across multiple rigs: a Fender Telecaster ’52 Reissue into a 1965 Vox AC30 Top Boost, a Gibson Les Paul Standard into a 1972 Marshall JMP 50-watt head with 4×12 G12M Greenbacks, and a Roland Jazz Chorus JC-120 for clean-path testing.
The result is a warm, slightly smeared, organically decaying shift that retains harmonic integrity even at extreme intervals.
