# Boulevard Effects — Darkship

## PRP identity

- **Builder:** Boulevard Effects
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Combined delay and fuzz pedal.

## What this pedal is

Darkship combines a tap-delay section with an independently switchable fuzz circuit. The delay can run by itself, the fuzz can run by itself, or both sections can be engaged together.

## Colorways

- A black production finish is documented in a surviving Reverb listing.

## Versions and factory options

- Delay controls: Repeat, Mix, Time
- Tap tempo
- Fuzz controls: Level, Fuzz, Tone
- Fuzz modes: Destroy, Blast, Burn
- Independent engagement of delay and fuzz
- Standard 9V power
- Maximum delay time documented as 1300 ms

## Version changes

A 2021 example is documented in the surviving Reverb listing. No separate numbered factory revision was verified.

## Sound

Darkship is built around a less conventional signal relationship: the delay section feeds the fuzz, allowing repeats and sustained delay textures to be driven by the independent fuzz circuit. The three fuzz modes move between different degrees of aggressive saturation while the delay section supplies repeat-heavy ambient textures.
