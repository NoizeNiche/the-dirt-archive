# BMF Effects — The Godfather Overdrive

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** BMF Effects
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Analog low-to-medium-gain overdrive.

## What this pedal is
The Godfather is an analog overdrive designed for low-to-medium gain drive. BMF describes it as warm and fat, with a slight sag in the initial attack intended to provide an amp-like response.

## Sound
The Godfather is voiced for low-to-medium gain rather than extreme saturation. BMF characterizes the response as warm and fat with a touch of sag on the initial attack, while the internal bass-boost trim allows additional low-end shaping. [1]
