# Bowman Audio Endeavors — Odious Fuzz Octave

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Bowman Audio Endeavors
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Dual-section fuzz/octave pedal.

## What this pedal is
The Odious Fuzz Octave combines the Bowman Satan's Fingers silicon-transistor fuzz circuit with a separate octave/ring-modulation section. The octave section can be added independently, including without using the fuzz side.

## Colorways
- 125B-sized powder-coated enclosure with UV-printed graphics.

## Versions and factory options
- Fuzz section based on Satan's Fingers
- Volume and Fuzz controls on the fuzz side
- Separate footswitches for fuzz and octave sections
- Octave section adds a ring-mod / one-octave-up character
- Standard center-negative power
- 9V operation
- 100mA current requirement

## Sound
The fuzz side is the core high-saturation voice, while the separate octave section can add a ring-modulated, one-octave-up edge. Because the two sections have independent switching, the pedal can be used as fuzz alone, octave alone, or as a combined texture.
