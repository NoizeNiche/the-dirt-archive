# Bowman Audio Endeavors — The Bowman Overdrive

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Bowman Audio Endeavors
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Klon-style transparent overdrive.

## What this pedal is
The Bowman Overdrive is Bowman Audio Endeavors' interpretation of the Klon Centaur. Bowman describes the circuit as a hand-made transparent overdrive using quality components and NOS germanium diodes.

## Colorways
- Silver powder-coated enclosure.
- Oxblood Davies-style knobs.

- Hand-made construction
- NOS germanium diodes

## Sound
The Bowman is intended to occupy the transparent overdrive space associated with the Klon format, retaining the guitar and amplifier character while adding drive. A third-party demo description says the design was derived by measuring components from beneath a gooped original Silver Klon PCB, but the archive treats Bowman's own published description as the factory source of record.
