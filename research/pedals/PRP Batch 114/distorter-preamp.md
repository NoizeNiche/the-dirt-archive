# Bouteek — Distorter - Preamp

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Bouteek
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Rare 2009 preamp-style drive pedal associated with Marshall-style high-gain amp tones.

## What this pedal is
The Distorter Preamp was part of Bouteek's original 2009 catalog and is described by Aion FX as an extremely rare drive pedal designed to reproduce Marshall-style high-gain amp tones. Effects Database lists it as one of Bouteek's 2009 releases. [1][2]
