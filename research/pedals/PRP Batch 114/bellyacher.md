# Bowman Audio Endeavors — Bellyacher

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Bowman Audio Endeavors
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** Limited-edition full-range distortion/sustain pedal created with Frank Iero for the 10th-anniversary release of *Stomachaches*.

## What this pedal is
The Bellyacher is a hand-built distortion/sustain pedal with a wide range that can cover Muff-type fuzz textures as well as heavier distortion. Bowman describes it as a collaboration with Frank Iero and features custom artwork by Ash Lowe.

## Colorways
- Powder-coated enclosure with UV-printed graphics.
- The collaboration artwork is integral to the documented edition.

## Versions and factory options
- Level

## Sound
Bellyacher is designed to move from broad, sustaining fuzz-like textures into heavier distortion. The Body control changes the clipping balance, while the Texture switch can push the pedal from sputtering dying-battery-style behavior toward full-gain distortion.
