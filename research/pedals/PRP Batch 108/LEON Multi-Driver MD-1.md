## PRP identity

## Research

LEON MD-1 is a wide-range multi-driver designed to cover sounds from transparent clean drive through heavy distortion. Its defining control is CHARACTER, which changes the frequency band being emphasized by the gain structure. LEVEL, TONE and GAIN provide the main continuous shaping.

Boorocks describes the pedal as hand-wired with selected parts, including German components, and optimized for wide bandwidth and low noise. The manual documents separate setting examples for single-coil and humbucker pickups.

## Controls

- 9V 006P battery or regulated external adapter
