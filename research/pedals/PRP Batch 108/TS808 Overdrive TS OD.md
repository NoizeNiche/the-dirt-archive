# Boo Instruments - TS808 Overdrive (TS O/D)

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Boo Instruments
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive

## Research
The TS808 Overdrive is Boo Instruments' boutique recreation of the Ibanez/Maxon TS-808 overdrive. Boo retains the original-style JRC4558D op amp while upgrading the surrounding component set to metal-film resistors and Panasonic film capacitors. The pedal also uses custom audio potentiometers.

Boo presents the circuit as a classic analog Tube Screamer-style overdrive in a robust, true-bypass enclosure. The design is hand-built in England and is available in standard and illuminated-lid versions.

## Controls and architecture
- Tube Screamer-style Drive, Tone and Level controls
- JRC4558D op amp
- Panasonic film capacitors
- Metal-film resistors
- Custom audio potentiometers
- True bypass
- 9V operation
