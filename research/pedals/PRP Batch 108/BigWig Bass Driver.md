# Bookworm Effects - BigWig Bass Driver

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Bookworm Effects
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz / Overdrive

## Research
The BigWig Bass Driver combines two dirt circuits designed specifically around bass: the Frith overdrive and Hrududu fuzz. In V1 the signal order was fuzz into overdrive; V2 changes this to overdrive into fuzz for heavier, fuller stacked sounds.

The Frith side ranges from clean boost through grit into borderline-fuzz distortion and includes switchable silicon-diode or LED clipping. The Hrududu side provides a wide dark-to-bright fuzz tone. Both circuits can be used independently or stacked.

## Controls and architecture
- Frith overdrive volume
- Frith drive
- Silicon / LED clipping selector on V2
- Hrududu fuzz volume
- Hrududu tone
- Independent footswitches
- V1: Fuzz -> Overdrive
- V2: Overdrive -> Fuzz
- True bypass
- 9V center-negative
- Handmade in Morgantown, WV
