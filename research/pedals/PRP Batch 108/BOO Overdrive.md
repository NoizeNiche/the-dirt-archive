# Boo Instruments - BOO Overdrive

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Boo Instruments
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive

## Research
The BOO Overdrive is Boo Instruments' modified Tube Screamer-style overdrive. Boo retains the familiar JRC4558D op-amp foundation but changes the voicing for a flatter overall frequency response, stronger low end, a wider gain range and a somewhat different tone response.

The Drive control moves from gentle tube-like overdrive/crunch into heavier distortion. Tone rolls treble back for warmth, while Volume sets the output level. Standard versions can use a 9V battery or 9V DC supply.

## Controls and construction
- Drive
- Tone
- Volume / Level
- JRC4558D op amp
- Discrete supporting circuitry
- True bypass
- 9V DC or 9V battery on standard version
