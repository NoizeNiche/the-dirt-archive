# Boorocks - ZEAL Fuzz FZ-1

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Boorocks
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz

## Research
ZEAL is a flexible fuzz designed to cover vintage 1960s fuzz territory as well as heavier contemporary sounds. Boorocks emphasizes that the part of the circuit responsible for overall color is made variable, giving the pedal a wider range than a fixed-vintage fuzz.

The pedal uses Level, Fuzz and Gain controls and true bypass. It can also produce a distinctive high-frequency-leaning clean response when the guitar volume is rolled back.

## Controls
- Level
- Fuzz
- Gain
- True bypass
- Input / output / DC input
- 9V operation
