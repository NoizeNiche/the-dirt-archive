# Boo Instruments - KC Klon Centaur

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Boo Instruments
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive

## Research
The KC Klon Centaur is Boo Instruments' boutique Klon-style overdrive. Boo describes it as a hand-made UK pedal using a voltage pump and buffered bypass, with an analog overdrive circuit based around the characteristic clean/drive blend approach of the Klon family.

The pedal is built in a metal enclosure and uses selected Panasonic capacitors and metal-film resistors. Standard versions draw less than 20mA, while the illuminated-lid version can draw up to 40mA and requires an external supply.

## Controls and construction
- Klon-style clean/drive overdrive architecture
- Voltage pump
- Buffered bypass
- 9V DC center-negative
- Less than 20mA standard current draw
- 112 x 61 x 50 mm
- Hand-made in the UK
