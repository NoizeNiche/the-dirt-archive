# Boorocks - DUSH Distortion DS-1

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Boorocks
- **Catalog type:** Distortion

## Research
DUSH is a three-control distortion designed around Boorocks' stated priorities of fast response, wide dynamic range and clear note separation. The circuit is intended to preserve pick dynamics and respond quickly to changes in guitar volume.

The pedal uses a simple Level, Tone and Gain layout. It has true bypass and can be powered by a 9V battery or external adapter.

## Controls
- Level
- Tone
- Gain
- True bypass
- 9V 006P battery or external AC adapter
