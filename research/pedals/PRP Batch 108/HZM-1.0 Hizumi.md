# Boot-Leg Hand Made Effects - HZM-1.0 Hizumi

## PRP identity

- **Builder:** Boot-Leg Hand Made Effects
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive

## Research

HZM-1.0 Hizumi is a dual-stage distortion/overdrive designed mainly for a clean amplifier. The main drive section contains two serially connected subcircuits with different characters, and a single shared Hizumi control lets the user move from mild overdrive into heavier distortion.

The Range control shifts the peak frequency, allowing the pedal to compensate for the different resonances of guitar bodies and pickups. Boot-Leg describes the resulting sound as retaining sustain and string-to-string balance even at higher settings.

## Controls

- Dual serial drive sections
- 9V operation
