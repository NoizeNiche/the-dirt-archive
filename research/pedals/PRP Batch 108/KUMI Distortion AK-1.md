# Boorocks - KUMI Distortion AK-1

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Boorocks
- **Catalog type:** Distortion

## Research
KUMI is the signature distortion developed by Boorocks with guitarist Kumi Adachi. The design was intended to handle both single-coil and humbucking pickups while maintaining fast response, wide dynamics and low noise. A Fat switch provides additional range for different pickup outputs and tonal demands.

The circuit uses carefully selected components and a true-bypass footswitch. The documented control set is Level, Tone and Gain plus the Fat switch.

## Controls and construction
- Level
- Tone
- Gain
- Fat switch
- True bypass
- 9V 006P battery or external AC adapter
- 110 x 110 x 45 mm
- Approx. 393g
