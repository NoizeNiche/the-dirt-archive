# Bob Burt Cabinets & Pedals - GR8T Distortion

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Bob Burt Cabinets & Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Distortion

## Research
The GR8T is Bob Burt's multi-voice distortion design. Burt describes a single gain-stage architecture with a precision Texas Instruments op-amp and an eight-position rotary switch that progressively changes the amount of compression and the resulting distortion waveform.

With Gain, Tone and Level controls, the pedal can move from open, aggressive distortion through warmer and more compressed D-style overdrive. Burt emphasized that the rotary control was intended to provide easily recalled voices without relying on multiple mini toggles.

## Controls and architecture
- Gain
- Tone
- Level
- 8-position compression/voice rotary switch
- Precision TI op-amp
- True bypass
- Hand-built construction
