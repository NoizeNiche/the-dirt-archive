# Bogner Amplification - Ecstasy Blue

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Bogner Amplification
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive

## Research
The Ecstasy Blue is based on Bogner's Ecstasy amplifier blue channel and translates its gain and tone-structure changes into a pedal. It uses discrete Class A gain stages rather than an op-amp/diode clipping architecture and provides separate boost controls alongside the main drive section.

The pedal includes Plexi/Blue gain modes, a Variac setting, pre-EQ and structure switches, and a boost with independent gain and volume. The structure controls mimic different Ecstasy amplifier generations, while the boost can be used independently or with the main effect.

## Controls and architecture
- Gain
- Volume
- Treble
- Middle
- Bass
- Boost gain and boost volume
- Plexi/Blue mode
- Variac
- Pre-EQ
- Structure
- Discrete Class A gain stages
- True bypass
- 9V battery or external 9V DC
