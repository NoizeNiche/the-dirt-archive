# BMF Effects - The Godfather II Dual Overdrive

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** BMF Effects
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive

## Research
The Godfather II puts two Godfather overdrive circuits into one enclosure. Channel A is the standard Godfather voicing, while Channel B uses a modified voicing with more gain and sag. A Select footswitch chooses the active channel, a master Bypass switch handles overall engagement, and a rear/center mode control allows A/B operation or A+B stacking.

The standard Godfather itself is a low- to medium-gain overdrive with Volume, Drive and Tone controls plus an internal bass-boost trim. The Godfather series can be ordered in different gain configurations; BMF's current FAQ identifies standard gain for the original Godfather and standard/high-gain options for the Godfather II.

## Features
- Two Godfather drive channels
- Channel A standard voicing
- Channel B higher-gain / higher-sag voicing
- A/B or A+B operation
- Independent channel selection and master bypass
- True bypass
- 9V to 18V operation
- Handmade construction
