# BMF Effects - Velvet Hammer Overdrive

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** BMF Effects
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive

## Research
The Velvet Hammer is a two-stage overdrive built around a core Velvet circuit and a second Hammer boost. The Velvet side provides Volume, Tone and Gain controls and is voiced for a warm, classic overdrive range from mild to fairly driven. Engaging the Hammer adds a fixed midrange and gain bump for a stronger push without requiring a second external pedal.

BMF documents the design as true bypass and analog, with the two sides sharing an enclosure and switching system.

## Features
- Velvet side: Volume, Tone, Gain
- Hammer side: fixed midrange/gain boost
- Two footswitches
- True bypass
- Approx. 4mA current draw
- 4.2 x 4.5 inch enclosure
- Handmade in USA
