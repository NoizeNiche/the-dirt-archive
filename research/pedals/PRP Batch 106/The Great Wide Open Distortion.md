# BMF Effects - The Great Wide Open Distortion

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** BMF Effects
- **Catalog type:** Distortion

## Research
The Great Wide Open started as a one-knob distortion with only a Volume control. The design goal is an open, articulate distortion that behaves like a driven amplifier and preserves the character of the guitar and amp rather than imposing a complex EQ or multi-control voicing.

The pedal remains intentionally simple compared with most modern distortion designs. BMF documentation describes the circuit as useful across a wide range of amplifiers, with the output level used to set the amount of push.

## Controls and construction
- Volume
- True bypass
- DC jack
- Hand-built BMF construction
- Simple fixed-voiced distortion architecture
