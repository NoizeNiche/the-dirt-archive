# Bogner Amplification - Burnley

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Bogner Amplification
- **Catalog type:** Distortion

## Research
The Burnley is a compact distortion pedal using a custom transformer designed with Rupert Neve Designs. It provides a gain range from hot crunch into high-gain distortion, with the overall character shifted by the two-position Fat/Tight switch.

The core controls are Level, Gain and Tone. The Fat/Tight switch changes the entire gain structure rather than acting only as a conventional EQ toggle. The pedal uses an all-analog audio circuit, true bypass switching and can operate from a 9V battery or 9V DC supply.

## Controls
- Level
- Gain
- Tone
- Fat/Tight switch
- True bypass

## Power and construction
- 9V DC, negative tip, 50mA or greater
- Internal 9V battery
- Custom Rupert Neve Designs transformer
