# BMF Effects - Sisyphuzz Silicon Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** BMF Effects
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz

## Research
Sisyphuzz is BMF Effects' silicon Fuzz Face-style fuzz. BMF selected silicon transistors for consistency while voicing the circuit toward the response associated with germanium Fuzz Face pedals. The builder notes that different transistor and voicing options have existed depending on parts availability, including a House Blend option.

The pedal is designed to clean up from the guitar volume control and spans lighter overdrive-like sounds through thick sustaining fuzz. A slight midrange emphasis is intended to help the circuit retain presence without the temperature and bias sensitivity associated with many germanium builds.
