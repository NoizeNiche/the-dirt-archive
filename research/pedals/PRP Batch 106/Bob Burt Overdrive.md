## Research
The Bob Burt Overdrive, commonly abbreviated BBOD, is a hand-built overdrive designed around transparency, low-end retention and a wide tone range. Burt's documented design approach emphasizes increased lows, reduced mids, high output and a tone sweep that moves from a darker brown-style voice toward a brighter, more cutting character.

The standard control layout is Gain, Tone and Volume with true-bypass switching. Published variants include Low Gain and High Gain versions, plus a rare bass version with additional routing.

## Controls
- Gain
- Tone
- Volume
- True-bypass footswitch
