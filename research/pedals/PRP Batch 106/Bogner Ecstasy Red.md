# Bogner Amplification - Ecstasy Red

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Bogner Amplification
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive

## Research
The Ecstasy Red is the higher-gain counterpart in Bogner's Ecstasy pedal family. The control system mirrors the amplifier-style approach of the Blue model but is voiced for heavier gain. The pedal provides separate boost gain and volume, five main amplifier-style controls, and four structure controls that alter the gain and EQ behavior.

The Variac switch adds the characteristic dropped-voltage compression feel. Mode selects Tight, Mellow or Full gain structures, while Pre-EQ and Structure settings select different front-end and amplifier-voicing combinations. A remote jack can control the main effect and boost functions.
