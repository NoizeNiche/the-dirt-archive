# Black Arts Toneworks - FNORD

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Black Arts Toneworks
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Fuzz

## Research
FNORD is an octaver/fuzz circuit that blends a Scrambler/octaver-style fuzz with a more traditional fuzz/distortion stage. Black Arts describes sounds ranging from zipper/Velcro textures through full-throated distortion and lightly gated distortion.
