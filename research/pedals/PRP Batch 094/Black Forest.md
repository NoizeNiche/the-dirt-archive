# Black Arts Toneworks - Black Forest

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Black Arts Toneworks
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Fuzz / Overdrive

## Research
The Black Forest is Black Arts Toneworks' dirted-up take on a classic rock circuit. The builder describes increased gain and fatness, with a six-position selector for gain, low-end and voicing plus Fuzz, Bass, Treble and Volume controls.
