# Black Arts Toneworks - LSTR

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Black Arts Toneworks
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Fuzz

## Research
LSTR is the heavier counterpart to the Pharaoh, built around the same general Big Muff family but voiced for more low end and more aggressive fuzz. Its paired tone controls move between traditional Muff-style scoop and more open Pharaoh-like tones. Black Arts lists it as out of production.
