# Black Arts Toneworks - Coven

## PRP identity

- **Builder:** Black Arts Toneworks
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Fuzz / Overdrive

## Research

The Coven combines the Pharaoh fuzz and Black Forest overdrive in one enclosure. Both circuits can be used independently or together, and an order switch lets the player run Pharaoh into Black Forest or Black Forest into Pharaoh.
