# Black Arts Toneworks - Crown of Horns

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Black Arts Toneworks
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Fuzz

## Research
The Crown of Horns is an Op-Amp Muff-based fuzz with a hybrid Pharaoh/LSTR tone section. Its Horns switch bypasses the tone stack for a large flat volume boost. Black Arts lists the model as out of production.
