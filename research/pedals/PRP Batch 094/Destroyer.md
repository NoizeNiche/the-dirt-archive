# Black Arts Toneworks - Destroyer

## PRP identity

- **Builder:** Black Arts Toneworks
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Fuzz

## What this pedal is

Destroyer combines the **Ritual** and **Oath** dirt circuits in one enclosure. The documented design can use either circuit independently or both together, creating a controllable fuzz-to-high-output combination.

## Deep research verification

### Architecture
Black Arts Toneworks' exact-model material identifies the Destroyer as a unified Ritual/Oath combination. The **V2** documentation specifically identifies a V2 pairing of **Ritual V2 + Oath**.

### Sound
The documented range moves from a controllable wall of fuzz toward a much more aggressive preamp-slamming combination. The Ritual V2 side is described as having more gain, compression and low mids than the earlier Ritual behavior, with guitar-volume reduction changing bite and compression.

### Component evidence
The reviewed exact-model sources do not establish a complete transistor/diode bill of materials for Destroyer V2.
