# Black Arts Toneworks - Destroyer

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Black Arts Toneworks
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Fuzz

## Research
The Destroyer combines the Ritual and Oath circuits in one enclosure. Black Arts described it as usable either circuit separately or with both engaged, moving from controllable fuzz toward a high-output amp-slamming combination.
