# Cornerstone Music Gear - Antique V3

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Cornerstone Music Gear
- **Catalog identity:** Antique V3
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Exact Antique V3 Classic Drive, distinct from the earlier Antique.

## What this pedal is
Antique V3 is Cornerstone's updated classic overdrive platform, designed around Tube Screamer-style drive with a Normal mode and a separate Hot Mode. Cornerstone describes Hot Mode as a two-stage boost architecture that can act as a second overdrive channel.

## Controls and switching
- **Gain:** primary overdrive amount.
- **Volume:** output level.
- **Tone:** main tonal shaping.
- **Presence:** V3 tone/upper-mid shaping control.
- **Compression:** selects the compression character.
- **Hot Gain:** independently controls the pre-overdrive boost.
- **Hot Volume:** independently controls the post-overdrive volume boost.
- **Normal / Hot:** footswitchable operating modes.
- **External footswitch:** latching control can switch Normal and Hot modes remotely.

## Hot Mode
The V3 adds two independently controllable boost sections: one before the overdrive for extra gain/saturation and one after the overdrive for output level. This lets the pedal operate as a dual-channel drive without requiring a second enclosure.

## Construction and specifications
- Handmade in Italy.
- Dimensions: 120 x 94 x 42.4 mm.
- Weight: approximately 0.5 kg.
- Up to 61 combinations of color, faceplate, and knob options are offered.

## Research notes
Do not merge Antique V3 with earlier Antique or the separate Imperium circuit. Cornerstone's current product page identifies V3 as the version with Hot Mode and the dual-boost architecture.
