# Cornerstone Music Gear - Centurio

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Cornerstone Music Gear
- **Catalog identity:** Centurio
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Exact Centurio Professional Drive.

## What this pedal is
Centurio is a Klon-style dual-channel professional drive. Cornerstone keeps the core headroom-oriented architecture and adds a second footswitchable gain/volume channel, a variable clipping control, and an external Hot Mode switch input.

## Controls and switching
- **Gain and Volume:** independent controls for the main and Hot channels.
- **Clip:** continuously sweeps the clipping behavior rather than selecting only fixed clipping presets.
- **Bright:** changes the voicing of the Hot Mode channel.
- **Normal / Hot:** two footswitchable voices.
- **External footswitch input:** permits remote switching of the Hot Mode channel.

## Circuit behavior
Cornerstone describes the main circuit as retaining the voltage architecture and general feel associated with the classic Klon approach. The Hot Mode adds another gain stage, increasing saturation and compression. The Clip control spans a range from a more open, lightly asymmetric clipping behavior toward denser clipping.

## Power and specifications
- 9V DC.
- Center-negative polarity.
- Current draw: approximately 120 mA.
- Input impedance: 510 kΩ.
- Output impedance: 10 kΩ.
- Dimensions: 120 x 94 x 42.4 mm.
- Handmade in Italy.

## Research notes
Centurio is a distinct model from Cornerstone's Colosseum and Imperium. Do not substitute Colosseum's dual Klon/Bluesbreaker architecture for Centurio.
