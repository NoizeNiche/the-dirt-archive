# Cornerstone Music Gear - Sparkle - Dynamic Overdrive

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Cornerstone Music Gear
- **Catalog identity:** Sparkle - Dynamic Overdrive
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Exact early Cornerstone Sparkle Dynamic Overdrive.

## What this pedal is
Sparkle is a highly touch-sensitive transparent overdrive designed to follow picking dynamics and guitar-volume changes without requiring frequent knob adjustments.

## Controls
- **Volume:** master output level.
- **Gain:** amplification / overdrive amount.
- **Bass:** low-frequency response.
- **Treble:** high-frequency response.

With Bass and Treble at noon, published product material describes a broadly flat frequency response across the guitar range.

## Voltage operation
- External supply is standard 9V DC, center negative.
- An internal 9V/18V selector changes the circuit operating voltage.
- 18V operation increases headroom, especially useful with higher-output pickups.

## Sound and use
Low-gain settings are described as transparent boost/edge-of-breakup operation. Increasing Gain moves toward stronger overdrive while retaining the pedal's dynamic response.

## Construction and provenance
- Handmade in Italy.
- Reverb examples identify Sparkle as an early Cornerstone model, including limited numbered production runs.
- Effects Database dates its model entry to January 3, 2015.

## Research notes
Do not merge Sparkle with later Cornerstone models such as Antique V3. Keep the exact catalog identity "Sparkle - Dynamic Overdrive" even when marketplace listings shorten the name to "Sparkle".
