# Cornerstone Music Gear - Nero

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Cornerstone Music Gear
- **Catalog identity:** Nero
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz / Overdrive
- **Identity:** Exact Cornerstone Nero Dual Bias Fuzz.

## What this pedal is
Nero is a modern Fuzz Face-style fuzz/overdrive with two independent bias controls. Cornerstone presents it as a Dumble-fuzz concept, combining classic fuzz behavior with expanded control over the operating point.

## Controls
Published dealer specifications identify:
- **Volume:** output level.
- **Gain:** overall fuzz/gain amount.
- **Tone:** tonal shaping.
- **Comp:** compression/response character.
- **Voice 1 / Voice 2:** two voice/bias sections.
- **Dual bias architecture:** independent bias controls allow the two sides of the fuzz circuit to be balanced and shaped separately.

## Circuit and character
Independent bias controls are the defining feature of the design. They provide more control over the transition between cleaner, more open fuzz behavior and denser gated/compressed textures than a single fixed bias setting.

Guitar Pedal X identifies Nero as a Fuzz Face-style dual-bias fuzz with a Dumble-oriented interpretation. The exact transistor part numbers were not established in the checked sources, so they are intentionally not asserted here.

## Construction
- Handmade in Italy.
- Current retail listings identify the standard Nero as a black-finish pedal.

## Research notes
Nero is a distinct catalog identity from the older Gladio Double Preamp, Gladio SC, and from Cornerstone's separate overdrive models. Do not substitute Gladio or Imperium specifications for Nero.
