# Cornerstone Music Gear - Colosseum

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Cornerstone Music Gear
- **Catalog identity:** Colosseum
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Exact Cornerstone Colosseum dual overdrive.

## What this pedal is
Colosseum is Cornerstone's community-designed dual overdrive that combines a Klon-derived side with a modified Bluesbreaker-style side. The two circuits are independently controllable and can be routed in either order.

## Klon side
- Tone, Gain, and Volume controls.
- Clip blender moves between standard 1N34A germanium diodes and a pair of hand-selected silicon diodes.
- The clip blend provides a continuous range rather than only a fixed diode choice.

## Bluesbreaker side
- Tone, Gain, and Volume controls.
- Clipping diodes are removed from the gain path, allowing the op amp to provide the gain and output.
- **Clean** control adds dry signal to help preserve pick attack and low-end feel.

## Routing and switching
- Effect-order switch determines which side is first in the signal path.
- Four rear jacks provide independent routing of the two circuits.
- The pedal can therefore operate as two independently accessible drives or as a stacked dual overdrive.

## Construction
- Handmade in Italy.
- Dimensions documented by retailer sources are approximately 120 x 94 x 42 mm.

## Research notes
Cornerstone describes the Colosseum as a community pedal rather than a literal copy of the source circuits. Keep the Klon/Bluesbreaker references as design lineage and do not describe the unit as an exact clone.
