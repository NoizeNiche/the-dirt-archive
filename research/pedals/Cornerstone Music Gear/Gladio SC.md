## What this pedal is
Gladio SC is the compact single-channel successor to the larger Gladio Double Preamp. It is a Dumble-inspired preamp/overdrive designed to preserve pick response while blending clean and driven signals.

## Controls and switching
- **Gain:** drive amount.
- **Volume:** output level.
- **Tone:** tonal shaping.
- **Clean:** blends original guitar signal back with the driven path.
- **Compression switch:** changes the compression/response character.
- **Bypass:** footswitchable operation.

## Circuit concept
The Gladio SC uses parallel clean and overdriven paths so the original attack and low end can remain present as gain is increased. Cornerstone positions it as an amp-like preamp/overdrive suitable for always-on use.

## Version and lineage
Planet Guitar describes Gladio SC as having been developed from the larger Big Gladio platform. The original Gladio Double Preamp is a separate catalog identity and should not be merged with SC.
