# Cornerstone Music Gear - Imperium V2

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Cornerstone Music Gear
- **Catalog identity:** Imperium V2
- **Catalog type:** 2-in-1 Overdrive
- **Identity:** Exact second-generation Imperium, combining Gladio SC and Antique V3.

## What this pedal is
Imperium V2 places the Gladio SC and Antique V3 circuits in one enclosure. Each side retains its own gain and volume controls and can be used independently, stacked, or routed as separate pedals.

## Gladio SC side
- Gain and Volume.
- Tone.
- Clean blend.
- Compression character switch.
- Dumble-inspired preamp/drive behavior.

## Antique side
- Gain and Volume.
- Tone.
- Compression character control.
- Presence / mid-high shaping.
- Tube Screamer-inspired overdrive voicing.

## Routing and switching
- Independent bypass controls for both sides.
- Four-jack independent routing in V2.
- Effect-order switch allows the player to place Gladio before Antique or Antique before Gladio.
- Direct-stack and flip-flop switching modes are documented in the V2 manual.

## Power and specifications
- 9V DC, center negative.
- Dimensions: approximately 4.73 x 3.70 x 1.62 in / 120 x 94 x 42.4 mm.

## Version history
Imperium V2 is not the same product as the original Imperium. V2 combines the current Gladio SC and Antique platforms and adds the redesigned enclosure and independent four-jack routing.
