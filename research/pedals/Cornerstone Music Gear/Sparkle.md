# Cornerstone Music Gear - Sparkle

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Cornerstone Music Gear
- **Catalog identity:** Sparkle
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Exact Cornerstone Sparkle Dynamic Overdrive.

## What this pedal is
Sparkle is a touch-sensitive overdrive designed to track a guitarist's playing dynamics closely without requiring frequent control changes during a song.

## Controls
- **Volume:** master output level.
- **Gain:** overdrive amount.
- **Bass:** low-frequency response.
- **Treble:** high-frequency response.

Cornerstone's published description states that Bass and Treble at noon provide a broadly flat response across the guitar frequency range.

## Voltage operation
- External supply remains a standard 9V source.
- A **9V/18V selector** changes the circuit operating voltage.
- 18V operation provides more headroom, particularly with higher-output pickups.

## Sound
Sparkle is voiced for responsive, dynamic overdrive rather than a fixed gain texture. Lower settings can function as a transparent boost or edge-of-breakup stage, while more Gain moves into stronger overdrive without giving up the pedal's touch-sensitive behavior.
