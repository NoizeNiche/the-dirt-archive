# Cornerstone Music Gear - Gladio - Double Preamp

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Cornerstone Music Gear
- **Catalog identity:** Gladio - Double Preamp
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive / Preamp
- **Identity:** Original Gladio Double Preamp, distinct from later Gladio SC and Imperium V2.

## What this pedal is
The Gladio Double Preamp is a two-channel, Dumble-inspired preamp and overdrive. Cornerstone designed the two channels with different voices so they can be used separately or stacked for more gain and texture.

## Channels
- **Channel 1:** smoother, creamier voice.
- **Channel 2:** more aggressive / raucous voice.
- Each channel has its own tonal variation switch.
- Both channels can be stacked for higher-gain operation.

## Circuit concept
The Gladio is designed around the feel of a tube-compression preamp rather than a conventional one-voice distortion box. Cornerstone's design goal references the Dumble preamp sound and the playing dynamics associated with players such as Robben Ford and Eric Johnson.

## Version history
- The original Gladio Double Preamp is a discontinued predecessor to the smaller Gladio SC.
- In 2022, Cornerstone replaced the Double Preamp in its lineup with Imperium, combining the Gladio SC and Antique platforms.
- Do not use Imperium V2 photos or specifications as substitute data for the original Gladio Double Preamp.

## Research notes
The original Double Preamp's exact component-level specifications were not established in the checked sources. Preserve the version distinction rather than backfilling later Gladio SC specifications.
