## What this pedal is
Yeti Fuzz is Abominable's take on 1960s Germanium transistor fuzzes. The builder specifically says the circuit uses a pair of selected NOS Germanium transistors and a charge-pump voltage inverter so it can run from a normal center-negative 9V supply. [1]

## Colorways
- The documented production enclosure is **acid-roughened black** with glow-in-the-dark hand-printed graphics. [1]
- Cosmetic wear/roughness is part of the intentional enclosure treatment.

## Versions and factory options
### Yeti Fuzz production
- vintage-style Germanium fuzz
- matched/selected NOS Germanium transistor pair
- carbon-composition resistors
- NOS electrolytic capacitors
- Orange Drop capacitor
- charge-pump voltage inverter
- center-negative 9V compatibility [1]

No numbered factory revision was verified.

## Sound
Yeti Fuzz is aimed squarely at the soft, harmonically rich feel of vintage Germanium fuzzes rather than the harder edge of modern silicon circuits. The selected transistor pair and vintage-style parts support a touch-sensitive, singing fuzz response that can clean up as the guitar level is reduced. [1]
