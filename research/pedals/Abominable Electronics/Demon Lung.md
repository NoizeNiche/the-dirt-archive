# Abominable Electronics — Demon Lung

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Demon Lung
- **Builder:** Abominable Electronics
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Abominable Electronics' Boss FZ-2 Hyper Fuzz-inspired pedal.

## What this pedal is
Demon Lung is based on the **Boss FZ-2 Hyper Fuzz** and provides two switchable fuzz modes for changing the sound during performance. Abominable says the 2020 redesign changed the artwork and moved the pedal to a vertical enclosure while adding a Mids control and soft-touch relay switching for the fuzz-mode footswitch. [1]

## Colorways
- Current production uses **textured black enclosures** according to Abominable's product page.
- Purple variants and limited artwork versions have also been sold, including a 4/20 edition. These are treated as cosmetic/limited variants unless a circuit difference is documented. [1][2]

## Versions and factory options
### Earlier Demon Lung
- two switchable fuzz modes
- Boss FZ-2 Hyper Fuzz basis

### 2020 redesign
- vertical enclosure
- changed artwork
- added Mids control
- soft-touch relay for the Fuzz 1/2 switch
- clickless bypass switching
- top-mounted jacks [1]

### Later cosmetic variants
- 2025 textured black enclosure
- limited purple / special-artwork variants
- same core Demon Lung circuit unless otherwise stated by the builder [1][2]

## Version changes
The 2020 redesign is a documented hardware/control change, not just a new artwork treatment. The added Mids control and updated switching are the clearest production changes in the available record. [1]

## Sound
Demon Lung is aimed at the dense, aggressive side of fuzz, with two fuzz modes giving contrasting versions of the FZ-2-inspired voice. The added Mids control on the later version makes it easier to keep the huge, heavy fuzz audible rather than letting it disappear into a deep mid scoop. [1]
