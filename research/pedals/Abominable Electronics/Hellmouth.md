# Abominable Electronics — Hellmouth

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Hellmouth
- **Builder:** Abominable Electronics
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Abominable Electronics' heavily modified Tube Screamer-style overdrive.

## What this pedal is
Hellmouth starts with the classic green Tube Screamer/TS9-style overdrive and changes the circuit to keep more low end and offer substantially more gain. Abominable also adds a clean blend, an input-cap switch, a more-gain switch, and a second footswitch that changes the clipping from stock silicon diodes to red LEDs. [1][2]

## Colorways
- Current production uses a **textured black enclosure** with Wizard of Barge artwork. [1]
- Documented limited finishes include **translucent red sparkle**, Bouncing Souls editions, and earlier throwback artwork. [2][3]

## Versions and factory options
### Earlier Hellmouth
- Volume, Tone and Gain controls
- input-cap low-end modification
- additional gain range
- switchable clipping using the second footswitch [2]

### Current Hellmouth
- Volume
- Clean blend
- Tone
- Gain
- Input-cap low-end switch
- More-gain switch
- Bypass footswitch
- Silicon / red-LED clipping footswitch
- top-mounted jacks
- clickless switching on current production [1]

## Version changes
The model evolved from the earlier Hellmouth into the current four-knob version with a Clean control, updated switching and top-mounted jacks. The main circuit concept remained the same: a modified TS-style overdrive with more low end and more gain. [1][2]

## Transistor
- **Technology:** Solid-state overdrive.
- **Exact transistor/device:** Not publicly documented.

## Diode
- **Type:** Stock **silicon diodes** with switchable **5 mm red LED** clipping. [1]
- **Exact silicon diode part:** Unknown.

## Sound
Hellmouth keeps the familiar mid-focused feel of a green Tube Screamer but adds more low end and much more gain, letting it reach from punchy overdrive into thicker distortion. The clipping switch adds a louder, rougher LED mode, while the Clean blend can retain some of the dry guitar to keep the heavier settings clearer. [1][2]
