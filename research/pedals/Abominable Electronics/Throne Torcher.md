# Abominable Electronics — Throne Torcher

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Throne Torcher
- **Builder:** Abominable Electronics
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** Abominable Electronics' expanded Boss HM-2-style chainsaw distortion.

## What this pedal is
Throne Torcher began as a request for an HM-2 clone that could lose some of the original pedal's harshness. Abominable added a Mids control, switchable LED clipping and an additional Germanium-diode clipping network, making the pedal capable of both dense HM-2-style grind and thicker boosted textures. [1][2]

## Colorways
- Documented finishes include **translucent sparkle red** and a limited **black-splatter** chase finish. [2]
- Effects Database also records a **Translucent Slime Green** version and a no-knob limited edition. [1]

## Version changes
The later version added Clean Blend and modernized the switching while preserving the HM-2-based core. The clipping footswitch remains an important part of the design because it provides both a tonal change and a volume/thickness boost. [2]

## Transistor
- **Technology:** Solid-state HM-2-style distortion.
- **Exact transistor/device:** Not publicly documented.

## Diode
- **Type:** Additional Germanium-diode clipping plus switchable LED clipping are documented. [1]
- **Exact parts:** Unknown.

## Sound
Throne Torcher delivers the familiar dense, saturated HM-2-style grind but adds more control over the mids and a clipping switch that can fatten the normally thin chainsaw tone. The later Clean Blend makes it easier to mix the dry signal back in for punch and clarity. [1][2]
