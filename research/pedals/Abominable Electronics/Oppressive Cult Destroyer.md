# Abominable Electronics — Oppressive Cult Destroyer

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Oppressive Cult Destroyer
- **Builder:** Abominable Electronics
- **Catalog types:** Overdrive / Distortion
- **Identity:** Bass-oriented overdrive/distortion built from an OCD-style foundation with additional EQ and clipping options.

## What this pedal is
Oppressive Cult Destroyer is tuned for bass as well as guitar and is designed to move from subtle overdrive through crunch into heavier distortion. The circuit uses a charge pump to run internally at a higher voltage and adds boost, bass and switchable clipping features around the core drive circuit. [1][2]

## Colorways
- Surviving examples include **blue/green psychedelic artwork** and other limited artwork treatments.
- The builder's later production used new artwork while keeping the same core pedal identity. [2]

## Versions and factory options
### Earlier production
- Volume
- Tone
- Gain
- Boost toggle
- Bass toggle
- Destroy footswitch
- higher-voltage internal charge pump
- stock MOSFET clipping with added Germanium diode
- alternate LED clipping [1]

### Later production
- Volume
- Clean
- Tone
- Gain
- Volume Boost toggle
- Bypass / Bass Boost footswitch functions
- clickless switching and updated artwork from 2021 [2]

## Version changes
The pedal gained a Clean blend and updated switching/artwork in later production, while its bass-friendly high-headroom design remained intact. Earlier documentation specifically identifies MOSFET clipping with an added Klon-style Germanium diode and an alternate LED clipping mode. [1][2]

## Transistor
- **Technology:** Solid-state overdrive/distortion; MOSFET clipping is documented in the original version. [1]
- **Exact transistor/device:** Unknown.

## Diode
- **Type:** Original documentation describes a **Germanium diode added to the MOSFET clipping network**, with a separate **LED clipping** mode. [1]
- **Exact parts:** Unknown.

## Sound
Oppressive Cult Destroyer is built to stay thick and controlled on bass while still working on guitar, with enough headroom to keep low frequencies from turning to mush. The boost, bass and clipping controls can move it from comparatively restrained drive into a much louder, harder-edged distortion voice. [1][2]
