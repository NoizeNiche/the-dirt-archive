# Abominable Electronics — Hail Satan Deluxe

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Hail Satan Deluxe
- **Builder:** Abominable Electronics
- **Catalog types:** Distortion / Fuzz
- **Identity:** Expanded Deluxe version of the Hail Satan circuit.

## What this pedal is
Hail Satan Deluxe keeps the original Hail Satan concept and adds much more control. Abominable documents Highs, a Clean Blend, a switch between the original and Russian versions, and a dry-signal buffer switch, while retaining the Mids, Tone, Sustain and Dirt controls plus the red-LED clipping footswitch. [1]

## Colorways
- A current documented finish is a **textured white enclosure with Bakerz Dozen artwork**.
- Earlier Deluxe examples use other graphic finishes; cosmetic changes are not treated as circuit revisions. [1]

## Versions and factory options
### Hail Satan Deluxe
- Dirt
- Clean
- Tone
- Sustain
- Mids
- Highs
- Normal / Russian voice switch
- buffer switch
- red-LED clipping footswitch
- true bypass
- clickless switching on current production [1][2]

### Production changes
- **June 2020:** Abominable added clickless switching to the clipping footswitch and later made the bypass switch clickless as well. [1]
- **2025:** textured white enclosures with Bakerz Dozen artwork documented on the current product page. [1]

## Version changes
The major Deluxe evolution is documented through switching improvements and cosmetic updates, while the basic expanded control set remains the identity of the model. The Russian/Normal switch and Clean Blend are part of the Deluxe design rather than separate versions. [1]

## Transistor
- **Technology:** Big Muff-derived solid-state fuzz/distortion.
- **Exact transistor/device:** Not publicly documented.

## Diode
- **Type:** Red LED clipping is documented for the secondary footswitch; the base clipping network is part of the Muff-derived circuit but exact standard diode parts are not fully published. [1][2]
- **Exact diode/device:** Unknown.

## Sound
The Deluxe is designed to make the Muff-style foundation considerably more adjustable, from deep and scooped to mid-forward and cutting. The Clean Blend helps preserve the dry signal while the Highs and Mids controls shape the upper and middle frequencies, and the Normal/Russian switch gives two broad voicing families from the same box. [1][2]
