# Tube Works - 911 Tube Driver

## PRP identity
- Builder: Tube Works
- Catalog type: Overdrive
- Identity: Tube Works 911 Tube Driver, later four-control production generation.

## What this pedal is
The 911 is a later Tube Works Tube Driver generation associated with B.K. Butler's four-control Tube Driver format and 12AX7 tube architecture.

## Controls / circuit
- Four-control Tube Driver layout.
- 12AX7 tube-driven overdrive.
- Exact control assignments and component values are deferred to exact-model documentation.

## History
The 911 belongs to the later Tube Works Tube Driver era before some units carried B.K. Butler branding. It is therefore retained separately from the generic B.K. Butler Tube Driver catalog row.
