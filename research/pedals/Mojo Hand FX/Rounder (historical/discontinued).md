## What this pedal is
Mojo Hand FX describes the Rounder as a fuzz inspired by the rare **Burns Buzzaround**. The design adds external control over tone, transistor bias, gain, and output compared with the original reference. [1][2]

## Controls
- **TIMBRE:** overall tone. [1]
- **BALANCE:** transistor bias; on earlier units without a master volume it also served as volume. [1]
- **SUSTAIN:** overall gain. [1]
- **VOLUME:** master output. [1]

## Circuit / architecture
The manufacturer states that the Rounder uses real germanium transistors. [1]
The design is therefore documented as a germanium fuzz inspired by the Buzzaround family, but the verified sources do not establish the exact production circuit topology or transistor part numbers.

## Transistor
- **Documented device class:** germanium transistors. [1][2]
- **Exact production transistor part numbers:** Unknown.

## Power and physical specification
- **Power:** independent 9V center-negative supply or 9V battery. [1]
- **Current draw:** approximately 2mA. [1]
- **Daisy chaining:** not permitted; the builder specifically warns that the pedal requires its own isolated supply. [1]
- **Dimensions:** 4.8 × 2.62 × 1.55 inches. [1]
- **Switching:** true bypass. [1]

## Status
- **Discontinued:** Mojo Hand FX currently places Rounder on its discontinued-products page. [1]

## Sound
The builder characterizes Rounder as a flexible germanium fuzz with a vintage-inspired base and substantial external control over bias and response. [1][2]
