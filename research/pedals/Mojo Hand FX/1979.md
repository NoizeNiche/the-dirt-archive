## What this pedal is
Mojo Hand FX describes the 1979 as an evolution of the discontinued 1978, based on high-gain Big Muff models from the late 1970s, but using op amps instead of the traditional transistor set. [1]

## Circuit / architecture
- **Core device class:** op-amp-based Muff-style fuzz. [1]
- The manufacturer says the 1979 uses an IC-based gain structure rather than the traditional Big Muff transistor arrangement. [1]
- A complete production schematic was not established in the verified sources.

## Controls and switching
- **GAIN**
- **TONE**
- **LEVEL**
- **TONE BYPASS LEVEL** [1]
- Left footswitch bypasses or engages the tone circuit.
- Right footswitch bypasses or engages the fuzz circuit. [1]

## Transistor
- **Exact production transistor/device:** Not established as a core gain-stage device because the documented design uses op amps. [1]
## Diode
- **Exact production clipping diode:** Unknown.

## Version history
- **1978:** predecessor, discontinued. [1]
- **1979:** successor, with revised gain-pot taper and a separate level control for the tone-bypassed path. [1]
- The packet references V2, but a complete factory revision history is not established.

## Power and physical specification
- **Power:** center-negative 9VDC. [1]
- **Current draw:** approximately 8mA. [1]
- **Bypass:** true bypass. [1]
- **Dimensions:** approximately 4.7 × 3.69 × 1.37 inches. [1]
- **Enclosure:** die-cast aluminum. [1]

## Sound
Mojo Hand FX describes the 1979 as raw, aggressive, very high gain, and intentionally unsubtle, with the tone-bypass path adding extra bass, treble, mids, and volume/boost. [1]
