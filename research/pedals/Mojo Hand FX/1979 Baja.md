## Sound
An evolution of the now discontinued Mojo Hand FX 1978, the 1979 is based on the notoriously high gain Big Muffs that were manufactured during the late 70s.
But most importantly, the tone bypass has it’s own volume knob so you can set it to unity or boost!
We also adjusted the taper of the original gain pot to have a more usable sweep.
