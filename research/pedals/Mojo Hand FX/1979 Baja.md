## What this pedal is
Mojo Hand FX explicitly describes 1979 Baja as the same op-amp fuzz design as the 1979 with a new finish. [1]

## Version / finish
- **1979 Baja:** special finish of the 1979 fuzz. [1]
- The builder's page repeats the 1979 controls and operating characteristics, so no electrical revision is inferred from the finish. [1]

## Circuit / architecture
The underlying 1979 uses op amps in a late-1970s high-gain Big Muff-inspired fuzz architecture rather than the traditional transistor set. [1]

## Controls and switching
- **GAIN**
- **TONE**
- **LEVEL**
- **TONE BYPASS LEVEL**
- Left footswitch: tone circuit bypass.
- Right footswitch: fuzz bypass. [1]
