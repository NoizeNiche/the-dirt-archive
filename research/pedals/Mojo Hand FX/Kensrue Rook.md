## Sound
Even with the Gain dimed, the Rook maintains astonishing note articulation and chord clarity, (think a highly tweakable Tube Scre@mer).
Whether stacked with various Overdrives, Fuzzes or Muffs…it can make all of your other pedals sound even better!
The magic is the 2 band Baxandall EQ circuit, which offers up a different approach to tone control.
