## What this pedal is

Mojo Hand FX describes the Colossus as a Russian/Civil War Muff-style fuzz designed around broad low-end response, sustain, and substantial tonal flexibility. [1]

## Controls and tone switching

- **TONE**
- **GAIN**
- **MIDS**
- **VOLUME**
- **3-way tone switch:** center standard, down more bass, up most bass. [1]
