## What this pedal is
The Rock City is a dual overdrive that evolved from Mojo Hand FX's discontinued Odessa concept. It uses two independent drive circuits with a specialized switching system for combining and switching the channels.

## Versions and factory options
- Two independent overdrive circuits.
- Independent bypass switching for the two sides.
- **Pozze** switching system designed for live operation.
- The pedal can be used as two separate drives or as a stacked dual-drive unit.

## Sound
The Rock City combines two interactive overdrive channels for stacking and tonal shaping. Mojo Hand's design evolved from the Odessa platform but is treated here as its own dual-drive model rather than as an Odessa revision.
