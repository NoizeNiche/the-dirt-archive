## What this pedal is
Mojo Hand FX's Crosstown Rasta is a **Germanium/Silicon hybrid Fuzz Face-style fuzz**. The current 2026 revival is sold as the Rasta edition of Crosstown, with the same four-control architecture as the revived Crosstown family. [1][2]

## Circuit / architecture
The revived Crosstown uses one germanium and one silicon transistor in a hybrid Fuzz Face-style arrangement. Earlier examples used **AC187 germanium** and **BC109 silicon** devices, while Mojo Hand FX has stated that the replacement germanium device is undisclosed. [2]
The archive records the historical device identities but does not assume that every production unit used those exact parts.

## Controls
- **GAIN**
- **TONE**
- **VOLUME**
- **BODY** [2]

BODY provides additional low-frequency shaping beyond the traditional two-control Fuzz Face layout. [2]

## Transistor
- **Historical documented devices:** AC187 germanium + BC109 silicon on earlier units. [2]
