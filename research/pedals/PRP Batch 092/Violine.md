## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Bizarre Engineering
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive

## Research
The Violine was developed in Portugal in two versions. The V1 used Gain and Volume, while the later version retained the same gain/preamp structure and added an independent clean volume plus an EQ section affecting the driven signal. Contemporary descriptions also note clean blending, top-mounted jacks, remote-switch connectivity, and a latch/momentary footswitch.
