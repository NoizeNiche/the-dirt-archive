# BJFE / BJF Electronics - Dyna Blue Distortion

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** BJFE / BJF Electronics
- **Catalog type:** Distortion

## What this pedal is
Dyna Blue Distortion is a historical BJFE distortion that began as a small joke/prototype project and developed into a serious dynamic distortion design.

## History
Björn Juhl's historical reference identifies **two early Dyna Blue Distortion units**. DBD #1 was blue; DBD #2 was translucent blue. DBD #1 had an internal control that was later brought to the top and became the basis for the later Sunset Wonder identity. The historical reference places Dyna Blue Distortion in the lineage leading toward Sunset Wonder and the later Mad Professor Sky Blue family.

## Sound
The historical description characterizes Dyna Blue as a muscular distortion for players who liked Baby Blue Overdrive but wanted more distortion. It retains a dynamic response and works particularly well with brighter amplifiers. The low-gain character was described as similar to BBOD.

## Controls / circuit
The historical reference does not provide a dependable complete external control set for the two original Dyna Blue units. Exact transistor and diode part numbers remain unknown.

## Deep research verification

The BJFE historical reference documents Dyna Blue Distortion as a **two-unit early run**, with the first unit containing an internal pot that was later exposed on top and evolved into the Sunset Wonder control arrangement. It describes the design as a dynamic, muscular distortion derived from the role occupied by Baby Blue Overdrive, but with substantially more distortion.
