# BJFE / BJF Electronics - Candy Apple Fuzz (CAF)

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** BJFE / BJF Electronics
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz

## Research
A BJFE fuzz capable of sounds ranging from heavy gated fuzz through octave-doubling textures. The circuit evolved through several revisions, including transistor changes and modifications to the Fuzz and Nature controls for a wider range of usable settings.
