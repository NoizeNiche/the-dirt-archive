# BJFE / BJF Electronics - BlueBerry Bass Overdrive (3BOD)

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** BJFE / BJF Electronics
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive

## Research
An overdrive designed specifically for bass. It was released in December 2001 and retired in January 2010. The reference notes its later transition into the Mad Professor product line and documents its distinctive blueberry-themed enclosure artwork.
