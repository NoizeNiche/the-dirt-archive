## PRP identity
- **Builder:** BJFE / BJF Electronics

## Research
A BJFE forum research fuzz derived from the Snow White/Smokey Orange lineage. The AWF uses the SOF schema and was developed with a Les Paul in mind. The documented run included revisions such as increased gain, with later units incorporating tighter bass or more focused mids.
