# BJFE / BJF Electronics - Dyna Red Distortion (DRD)

## PRP identity

- **Builder:** BJFE / BJF Electronics
- **Catalog type:** Distortion

## Research

The DRD is a dynamic distortion that produces more dynamic distortion below noon on the Drive control and fuller distortion above noon. The reference says it was designed around P-90 pickups and notes that it can work into clean amplifiers or alongside other BJFE dirt pedals.
