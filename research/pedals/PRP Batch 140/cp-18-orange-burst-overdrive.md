# Caline - CP-18 Orange Burst / Overdrive

- **Builder:** Caline
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Primary research sources:** Effects Database exact-model record; Free Stompboxes CP-18 schematic discussion; retail documentation.
- **Identity:** The CP-18 is documented as **v1 Overdrive / unbranded** and **v2 Orange Burst**.
- **Colorways:** Orange is documented for the Orange Burst version. No verified production colorway list beyond that naming state.
- **Versions:** v1 - Overdrive/unbranded; v2 - Orange Burst.
- **Version changes:** The checked sources establish the v1/v2 naming evolution but do not document a definitive production-wide circuit change.
- **Controls:** Gain, Treble, Volume, Bass.
- **Transistor type:** Free Stompboxes reports two C1815 transistors in the traced unit.
- **Diode type:** The traced unit uses two silicon diodes in the op-amp loop; exact diode part number was not established in the accessible text.
- **Sound description:** Caline describes the circuit as a 30dB+ clean boost with an active two-band EQ and a pre-gain stage that can move from pristine clean through smooth, compressed overdrive. Retail demonstrations also describe the pedal as capable of boost, bluesy drive and higher-gain sounds.
- **Circuit lineage:** The traced unit uses two TL072 op-amps and two C1815 transistors, differing from the original Xotic BB Preamp arrangement documented by the forum contributor. The archive records this as a traced implementation rather than a claim that every production unit used identical components.
- **Power:** Retail documentation specifies 9V DC center-negative, 30mA and a compact 11.2 x 6.4 x 3.4 cm enclosure.
- **Photo status:** Exact-model source imagery located; no local image promoted in this batch.
- **Research confidence:** High for v1/v2 naming, controls and broad topology; high for the traced-unit component evidence; medium for production-wide circuit consistency.
