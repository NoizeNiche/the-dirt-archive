# Caline - CP-21 Rock Face

- **Builder:** Caline
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Primary research sources:** Effects Database exact-model record; effects-pedals.info exact-model summary.
- **Identity:** The CP-21 is documented as **v1 Distortion** and **v2 Rock Face**.
- **Colorways:** Turquoise/green-blue enclosure is documented in exact-model retail imagery.
- **Versions:** v1 - Distortion; v2 - Rock Face.
- **Version changes:** The checked evidence establishes the version/name progression and that the design uses switchable clipping, but does not establish the exact circuit difference between v1 and v2.
- **Controls:** Drive, Tone, Level. Exact-model retailer documentation also describes a Special / Normal mode switch.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented by the checked model-level sources.
- **Diode type:** Exact clipping-diode part number not verified; the manufacturer description only establishes two clipping types.

- **Power / bypass:** 9V DC and true bypass are documented in the checked retail summary.
