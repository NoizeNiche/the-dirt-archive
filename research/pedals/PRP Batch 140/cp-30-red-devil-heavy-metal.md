# Caline - CP-30 Red Devil - Heavy Metal

- **Builder:** Caline
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Primary research sources:** Effects Database exact-model record; Caline manufacturer description; manufacturer demonstration video.
- **Identity:** The CP-30 Red Devil is a high-gain distortion with a Vintage/Modern toggle, three-band EQ and Gain/Volume controls.
- **Colorways:** Red enclosure is documented by the model name and exact-model imagery.
- **Versions:** Effects Database marks a **v2** entry for the CP-30; the accessible text does not document the precise change list.
- **Version changes:** v2 is documented, but the checked sources do not provide a reliable component-level delta from the earlier version.
- **Controls:** Gain, Volume, Bass, Mid, Treble, plus Vintage/Modern toggle.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented by the checked model-level sources.
- **Diode type:** Not documented by the checked model-level sources.
- **Sound description:** Caline describes the Red Devil as blending classic distortion, tube-like drive and heavy-metal character, with rich timbre and dynamic range. Its isolated bass, mid and treble controls are intended to move between classic rock and modern high-gain rhythm sounds, while Vintage/Modern changes the overall voicing.
- **Power / bypass:** True bypass is documented; current official exact electrical specs were not sufficiently surfaced in the checked sources.
- **Photo status:** Exact-model source imagery located; no local image promoted in this batch.
- **Research confidence:** High for identity, controls and v2 marker; medium for tonal description because it is manufacturer copy; low for internal components and exact v2 delta.
