# Caline - CP-20 Crazy Cacti

- **Builder:** Caline
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Primary research sources:** Effects Database exact-model record; Effects Database review; current retailer documentation.
- **Identity:** The CP-20 Crazy Cacti is a dual-mode overdrive/boost modeled on the Fulltone Fulldrive 2 Mosfet feature set.
- **Colorways:** Green enclosure documented in current retail/source imagery.
- **Versions:** No numbered factory version verified.
- **Version changes:** No documented production revision established in the checked sources.
- **Controls:** Volume, Tone, Drive, Boost.
- **Switching:** One three-position mode switch (Vintage / FM / Comp Cut) and one two-position clipping switch (MOSFET / Standard).
- **Transistor type:** Not documented by the checked exact-model sources.
- **Diode type:** Exact clipping-diode part numbers were not verified; the manufacturer documents MOSFET versus Standard clipping modes.
- **Sound description:** The CP-20 is a versatile medium-gain overdrive centered on TS/Fulltone-style mid-voiced drive but with flat-mids and boost options. Vintage mode emphasizes mid-heavy tones, FM flattens the mids for a more open response, and Comp Cut moves toward cleaner boost behavior; the second switch changes the clipping character.
- **Construction / power:** True bypass, metal enclosure, 9V DC center-negative; a retailer lists approximately 30mA and 11.9 x 9.4 x 3.8 cm.
- **Photo status:** Exact-model source imagery located; no local image promoted in this batch.
