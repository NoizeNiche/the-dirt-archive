# Caline - CP-12 Pure Sky

- **Builder:** Caline
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Primary research sources:** Effects Database exact-model record; Free Stompboxes circuit analysis; current retail/manual documentation.
- **Identity:** The CP-12 Pure Sky is a four-control Caline overdrive using Volume, Gain, Treble and Bass controls.
- **Colorways:** Turquoise/blue enclosure is documented in current retail imagery. No verified production colorway catalog found.
- **Versions:** No numbered factory version verified.
- **Version changes:** No documented factory revision found in the checked sources.
- **Controls:** Volume, Gain, Treble, Bass.
- **Transistor type:** No discrete transistor type was established by the checked circuit references; the traced circuit uses a JRC4558D dual op-amp.
- **Diode type:** Free Stompboxes identifies four 1N4148 diodes plus a 1N4001 in the CP-12/CP-25 family trace.
- **Sound description:** The Pure Sky is consistently documented as a transparent, Timmy-derived style overdrive/boost with independent bass and treble shaping. The available user documentation describes settings from clean boost through progressively stronger overdrive, while Effects Database notes its long-running place in Caline's CP-xx series.
- **Circuit lineage:** A Free Stompboxes trace states that CP-12 and CP-25 share the same basic Paul Cochrane Timmy circuit, with the CP-12 using C50K bass/treble pots in the documented unit. The archive records this as traced-family evidence rather than a factory schematic.
- **Power:** Retail/manual documentation specifies 9V DC center-negative; a current product listing reports about 9mA.
- **Photo status:** Exact-model source imagery located; no local image promoted in this batch.
- **Research confidence:** High for controls, identity and broad circuit lineage; high for the traced diode/op-amp evidence; medium for production-wide component consistency.
