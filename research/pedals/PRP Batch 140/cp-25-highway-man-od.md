# Caline - CP-25 Highway Man OD

- **Builder:** Caline
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Primary research sources:** Effects Database exact-model record; Free Stompboxes circuit trace; current product specification.
- **Identity:** The CP-25 Highway Man is a transparent boost/overdrive with Gain, Level, Bass and Treble controls.
- **Colorways:** Exact-model source imagery exists; no verified production colorway list established.
- **Versions:** No numbered factory version verified.
- **Version changes:** No documented revision found in the checked sources.
- **Controls:** Gain, Level, Bass, Treble.
- **Transistor type:** No discrete transistor type established; the traced circuit uses a JRC4558D dual op-amp.
- **Diode type:** Free Stompboxes identifies one 1N4001 and four 1N4148 diodes in the traced CP-25/CP-12 family circuit.
- **Sound description:** The Highway Man is positioned as a transparent low- to mid-gain boost/drive, moving from clean lift through light overdrive while retaining guitar/amp character. The active two-band EQ gives independent low/high shaping.
- **Circuit lineage:** Free Stompboxes states that CP-25 and CP-12 share the same basic Paul Cochrane Timmy circuit in the traced units, with different pot values. The archive records this as community-traced evidence, not a manufacturer schematic.
- **Power:** 9V DC center-negative, true bypass and approximately 30mA are documented; listed dimensions are about 11.2 x 6.4 x 3.4 cm.
- **Photo status:** Exact-model source imagery located; no local image promoted in this batch.
- **Research confidence:** High for identity, controls and broad circuit lineage; high for the traced diode/op-amp evidence; medium for production-wide component consistency.
