# Caline - CP-15 Tantrum / Heavy Metal

- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Primary research sources:** Effects Database exact-model record; Caline product documentation.
- **Identity:** The CP-15 appears in two documented naming states: v1 Heavy Metal and v2 Tantrum.
- **Colorways:** Exact-model imagery exists in the reviewed sources, but no authoritative colorway list was established.
- **Versions:** **v1 - Heavy Metal; v2 - Tantrum.**

- **Transistor type:** Not documented by the checked model-level sources.
- **Diode type:** Not documented by the checked model-level sources.
