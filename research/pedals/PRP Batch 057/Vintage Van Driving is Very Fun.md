# Animals Pedal — Vintage Van Driving is Very Fun

## What this pedal is
Vintage Van Driving is Very Fun is an analog low-gain overdrive based on the classic British "BB" / Bluesbreaker-style platform. Its OD/BOOST switch allows low-to-medium overdrive in OD mode and cleaner boost through light overdrive in BOOST mode. [1][2]

## Colorways
- Current production is documented in a white enclosure with the illustrated van artwork.
- V2 and other graphic/finish editions exist.

## Versions and factory options
- VOL
- TONE
- DRIVE
- OD / BOOST switch
- True bypass
- 9 V battery or standard center-minus DC9V adapter
- 64 x 112 x 50 mm; 381 g; 6 mA [1]

## Version changes
V2/current production retains the core controls and OD/BOOST function while moving the footswitch toward the front-right area, improving battery access and fine-tuning the circuit for broader pickup and amplifier compatibility. [1][3]

## Sound
Vintage Van Driving is designed to move from clear, attacking clean boost into warm medium-gain overdrive, with the BOOST mode useful for pushing another dirt stage or amplifier. Its response is deliberately broad enough to complement both single-coil and humbucker guitars. [1][2]
