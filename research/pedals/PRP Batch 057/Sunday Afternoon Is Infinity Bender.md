# Animals Pedal — Sunday Afternoon Is Infinity Bender

## What this pedal is
Sunday Afternoon Is Infinity Bender is a silicon-transistor fuzz designed with Wren and Cuff to reproduce the feel of a mid-1960s germanium Tone Bender while using silicon devices for greater consistency and modern usability. [1][2]

## Colorways
- White enclosure with minimalist illustrated graphics is documented for the current edition.

## Versions and factory options
- VOLUME
- FUZZ
- SMOOTH
- True bypass
- 9 V battery or center-minus DC9V adapter
- 64 x 112 x 50 mm; 378 g [1]

## Version changes
Animals Pedal describes the current version as fine-tuned and expanded for broader amplifier compatibility while retaining the same core sound and function. No separate numbered circuit revision is established in the reviewed material. [1]

## Sound
The pedal aims for the thick, warm and low-to-mid-focused response of a classic Tone Bender while using silicon for a more repeatable and stable behavior. The SMOOTH control adjusts warmth and edge, letting the player move from broad woolly fuzz toward sharper textures. [1][2]
