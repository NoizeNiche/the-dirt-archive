## What this pedal is
Sunday Afternoon Is Infinity Bender is a Tone Bender-inspired fuzz from Animals Pedal and Wren and Cuff. The design goal is not to duplicate a single vintage component set exactly, but to capture the thick, warm, responsive character associated with a mid-1960s germanium Tone Bender using silicon transistors for repeatability and easier integration into modern rigs. [1][2]

## Controls
- **VOLUME:** output level.
- **FUZZ:** amount of fuzz/sustain.
- **SMOOTH:** adjusts the tonal balance between softer/woollier and sharper/brighter fuzz behavior.
- **Footswitch:** true bypass. [1]

## Sound
The pedal is intended to deliver the thick, low/mid-focused character of a classic Tone Bender while avoiding some of the variability associated with vintage germanium devices. The **SMOOTH** control allows the player to move between broader, softer fuzz and more pronounced high-frequency edge. [1][2]
