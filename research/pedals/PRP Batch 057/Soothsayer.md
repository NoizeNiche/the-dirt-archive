# ARC Effects — Soothsayer

## What this pedal is
Soothsayer is ARC Effects' LM308-based overdrive/distortion, built as an expanded take on the King of Kalamazoo concept. It adds four clipping choices, internal high/low gain modes and a reverse-acting Filter control that behaves like a conventional tone control. [1]

## Colorways
- Black enclosure with a silver control plate is documented.

## Versions and factory options
- VOLUME
- FILTER
- GAIN
- Four clipping options: Classic / Boutique / Open / Turbo LED
- Internal HI / LOW gain switch
- Top-mounted jacks
- True bypass
- 9 V DC plus internal battery snap
- Hand-wired construction [1]

## Sound
Soothsayer ranges from low-gain overdrive through higher-gain distortion and fuzz-like textures, with the four clipping options changing compression and attack character. Its internal gain switch lets the same control set move between a more standard gain range and a lower-gain overdrive mode. [1]
