# ARC Effects — Soothsayer

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Soothsayer
- **Builder:** ARC Effects
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive
- **Identity:** LM308-equipped overdrive/distortion in the King of Kalamazoo lineage, expanded with multiple clipping choices, internal high/low gain selection, and a reverse-acting Filter control. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
ARC Effects describes the Soothsayer as an all-encompassing take on the LM308-equipped King of Kalamazoo. It is designed to cover low-gain overdrive through higher-gain distortion and fuzz-like textures without changing the basic three-knob control layout. [1]

## Controls
- **VOLUME:** Overall output level.
- **FILTER:** Tone control implemented in reverse. Counter-clockwise is described as bassier and clockwise as brighter/treblier.
- **GAIN:** Amount of gain.
- **External 3-position clipping toggle:**
  - **Left:** Uses the internal DIP-selected clipping configuration.
  - **Middle:** No clipping.
  - **Right:** Boutique asymmetrical clipping. [2]
- **Internal DIP 1:** Selects high-gain or low-gain operating mode.
- **Internal DIP 2 / 3:** Configure the clipping option presented at the left side of the external toggle. The instruction sheet documents LED clipping and stock symmetrical clipping among those internal options, with the remaining state used to leave clipping off in that branch. [2]

## Circuit lineage / architecture
- **Core device named by builder:** LM308.
- **Lineage named by builder:** King of Kalamazoo / classic RAT-style distortion family.
- **Clipping system:** Four user-accessible clipping choices are documented overall: Classic, Boutique, Open, and Turbo LED. [1]
- The instruction sheet further clarifies the external toggle and internal DIP relationship: internal switches configure the left external-toggle branch, the center position bypasses clipping, and the right position selects Boutique asymmetrical clipping. [2]
- The reviewed sources do not publish a complete schematic or exact diode part numbers.

## Diode / clipping
- **Documented clipping choices:** Classic, Boutique, Open, Turbo LED.
- **Turbo LED:** Explicitly identified as an LED clipping option.
- **Exact diode/device part numbers:** Not publicly documented in the reviewed material. [1][2]

## Construction / components
ARC Effects states:
- Hand-wired construction.
- True-bypass switching.
- Top-mounted jacks.
- No ceramic or electrolytic capacitors in the signal path. [1][2]
The reviewed sources do not provide a complete component-level bill of materials.

## Power
- **9V DC**.
- Instruction sheet specifies a **2.1 mm negative-center adapter**.
- An internal battery snap is also documented by ARC Effects. [1][2]

## Version history
- No separate numbered Soothsayer circuit revision is established in the reviewed material.
- ARC Effects' current product page still presents the Soothsayer alongside Soothsayer Solo and Soothsayer Bass as related products. [1]

## Sound
ARC Effects positions the Soothsayer across low-gain overdrive, conventional distortion and fuzz-like high-gain territory. The internal gain switch and clipping choices alter gain range and clipping character, while the reverse Filter control provides conventional bass-to-treble tone behavior despite its reversed control action. [1][2]
