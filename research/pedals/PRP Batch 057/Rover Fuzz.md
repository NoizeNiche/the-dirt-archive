# Animals Pedal — Rover Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Rover Fuzz
- **Builder:** Animals Pedal
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Designer:** Marc Ahlfs of Skreddy Pedals.
- **Identity:** Modernized classic fuzz voiced broadly around 1960s fuzz-pedal sounds, with the WOOL control intended to move among several classic fuzz tonal characters. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
Rover Fuzz is designed to make vintage-style fuzz easier to use in a modern pedalboard and band context. Animals Pedal describes it as retaining the character of classic fuzz while expanding the usable range for modern amplifiers and guitars rather than simply reproducing one historical circuit. [1][3]

## Controls
- **LEVEL:** output volume.
- **ATTACK:** adjusts the intensity/edge of the fuzz voice.
- **WOOL:** changes the overall density and frequency character, allowing the player to move among different classic-fuzz-style voices. [1][3]

## Circuit lineage
- Animals Pedal explicitly credits **Marc Ahlfs of Skreddy Pedals** with the Rover Fuzz design. [1]
- The builder describes the pedal as drawing broadly on the sound of 1960s fuzz pedals rather than documenting one exact vintage circuit as the sole source.
- Because the manufacturer does not name one specific original circuit in the reviewed product documentation, the archive records the lineage as **classic 1960s fuzz-inspired** rather than forcing a Tone Bender, Fuzz Face, or other exact family classification. [1][2]

## Versions
### Earlier Rover Fuzz
- Earlier production used the original enclosure/foot-switch arrangement.
- The pedal was designed around the same three-knob control concept and classic-fuzz voicing. [1]

### Latest documented version
Animals Pedal states that the newer version:
- moves the footswitch farther forward/right for improved playing access;
- makes battery replacement easier;
- retains the established sound and basic function;
- broadens the usable tonal range through fine circuit adjustment so the pedal works well with more guitars and amplifiers. [3]
This is a genuine manufacturer-designated update, but the builder specifically says there are **no major sound/function changes**.

## Power / electrical specifications
Animals Pedal specifies:
- **Supply:** 9V battery or standard **center-negative DC 9V** adapter.
- **Switching:** true bypass.
- **Dimensions:** approximately **64 × 112 × 50mm** including protrusions.
- **Weight:** approximately **375g**. [1]
- The current Japanese product listing confirms the same 9V center-negative/battery arrangement. [4]
- Current product documentation does not publish a reliable current-draw figure in the material reviewed for this pass.

## Sound
Animals Pedal describes Rover Fuzz as a broad classic-fuzz palette with a pronounced woolly/full range controlled by WOOL, while ATTACK can move the response toward sharper, more aggressive textures. The builder emphasizes modern usability, including better compatibility across guitars and amplifiers in the latest version. [1][3]
