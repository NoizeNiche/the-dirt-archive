# Animals Pedal — Rover Fuzz

## What this pedal is
Rover Fuzz is an analog fuzz designed by Marc Ahlfs of Skreddy Pedals around a classic Tone Bender-style fuzz concept. Animals Pedal describes it as a modernized, stable version of vintage fuzz that can move from thick fuzz and distortion into hot overdrive and cleaner, bell-like tones. [1][2]

## Colorways
- Current standard production is documented in a white enclosure with illustrated artwork.
- Custom Illustrated and limited graphic editions exist separately.

## Versions and factory options
- ATTACK
- WOOL
- LEVEL
- True bypass
- 9 V battery or center-minus 9 V adapter
- 64 x 112 x 50 mm; 375 g [1]

## Version changes
The latest documented version moves the footswitch to the front-right area, makes battery access easier, and fine-tunes the circuit for a wider range of guitars and amplifiers. Animals Pedal also documents custom illustrated Rover releases as cosmetic/special editions. [1][3]

## Sound
Rover Fuzz is voiced around warm, woolly Tone Bender-style fuzz but with tighter attack and easier cleanup than many vintage fuzzes. The WOOL control changes density and thickness, while ATTACK moves the response from smoother fuzz toward sharper, more aggressive textures. [1][2]
