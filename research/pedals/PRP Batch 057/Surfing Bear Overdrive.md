# Animals Pedal — Surfing Bear Overdrive

## What this pedal is
Surfing Bear Overdrive is an analog overdrive built around an upgraded 808-style platform. Animals Pedal describes it as covering clean boost, light crunch, warm overdrive and treble-boosted settings with a wide dynamic response. [1]

## Colorways
- Current documented enclosure: light blue with surf-bear artwork by Jonas Claesson.

## Versions and factory options
- VOL
- DRIVE
- TONE
- True bypass
- 9 V battery or standard center-minus DC9V adapter
- 64 x 112 x 50 mm; 382 g [1]

## Version changes
The latest edition moves the footswitch toward the front-right side, improves battery access and updates the artwork. Animals Pedal also describes the circuit as an upgraded and expanded version of the classic 808-style platform rather than a strict stock clone. [1]

## Sound
Surfing Bear can operate as a clean booster at low Drive, then move into light to medium overdrive with clear attack and low noise. The TONE control can emphasize treble for a more cutting response, while the upgraded circuit keeps a broad full-spectrum character. [1]
