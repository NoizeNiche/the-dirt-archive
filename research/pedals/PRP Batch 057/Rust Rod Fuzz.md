# Animals Pedal — Rust Rod Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Rust Rod Fuzz
- **Builder:** Animals Pedal
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Designer:** Marc Ahlfs of Skreddy Pedals.
- **Identity:** Modernized **Ram's Head Big Muff-style** fuzz designed to retain thick sustain and harmonic density while adding a more useful midrange for contemporary guitars and amplifiers. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
Rust Rod Fuzz is Animals Pedal's take on the early-1970s Ram's Head Big Muff sound. The builder describes the design as retaining the huge, sustained Muff character while tuning the frequency response so the pedal remains easier to place in a modern band mix. The pedal is not presented as a component-for-component historical recreation. [1][2]

## Controls
- **VOLUME:** overall output level.
- **TONE:** broad frequency balance.
- **SUSTAIN:** amount of fuzz/sustain. [1][2]
- **Switching:** true bypass.

## Circuit lineage
- **Primary lineage:** early-1970s **Ram's Head Big Muff** family.
- Animals Pedal credits **Marc Ahlfs / Skreddy Pedals** with the design. [1]
- The builder emphasizes that the circuit is tuned for broader compatibility with current guitars and amplifiers rather than simply copying one vintage unit's response. [1][2]

## Versions

## Sound
Rust Rod Fuzz emphasizes thick, long-sustaining Ram's Head-style fuzz with a stronger usable midrange than many traditional Muff settings. Animals Pedal describes the sound as suitable for classic-rock and lead use while retaining a dense harmonic texture. Higher Tone settings can be driven toward a more aggressive, squealing edge. [1][2]
