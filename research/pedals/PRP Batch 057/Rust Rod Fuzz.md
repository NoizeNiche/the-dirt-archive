# Animals Pedal — Rust Rod Fuzz

## What this pedal is
Rust Rod Fuzz is a Skreddy Pedals/Marc Ahlfs-designed fuzz based on the early-1970s Ram's Head Big Muff style. Animals Pedal describes the circuit as tuned for stronger mids, thick sustain and compatibility with a broad range of guitars and amplifiers. [1]

## Colorways
- Current production is documented in a white enclosure with illustrated artwork.
- Earlier editions and artwork variants exist.

## Versions and factory options
- VOLUME
- TONE
- SUSTAIN
- True bypass
- 9 V battery or center-minus DC9V adapter [1]

## Version changes
The current edition moves the footswitch toward the front-right side for easier use, improves battery access and fine-tunes the circuit for wider guitar/amp compatibility. The enclosure artwork has also been updated across production editions. [1]

## Sound
Rust Rod Fuzz emphasizes the thick, harmonically rich and sustaining side of the Ram's Head Muff family, with more pronounced mids than some traditional examples. Animals Pedal also notes that higher TONE settings can push the pedal toward an aggressive, squealing edge. [1]
