# Anode Effects — MOS Fuzz

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** MOS Fuzz
- **Builder:** Anode Effects
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Full-frequency fuzz based on an expansion of the Bazz Fuss concept, using MOSFETs instead of the conventional Bazz Fuss device approach. [1]

## What this pedal is

The MOS Fuzz is a two-control fuzz designed to keep a broad, full-frequency response while offering more low end and unusually strong cleanup from the guitar volume control. Anode Effects describes the circuit as an expansion of Bazz Fuss, modified to use MOSFETs for a smoother, less gritty response. [1]

## Controls

- **VOL:** Overall pedal output volume.
- **FUZZ:** Amount of fuzz.
- The builder specifically notes that lowering FUZZ adds more bass and can make the lower strings sound muddier. [1]

## Circuit lineage

- **Primary lineage:** Bazz Fuss.
- **Design change:** Anode Effects expands the concept and uses MOSFETs in place of the original Bazz Fuss device approach.
- **Intended result:** Smoother and less gritty fuzz behavior than the reference design, while retaining a strong low-frequency response. [1]

## Construction / components

Anode Effects lists:
- Japanese-made Lumberg power and audio jacks.
- Alpha pots.
- Gorva footswitch.
- 1/2-watt carbon-composition resistors.
- Philips and Sprague capacitors.
- Vintage silicon diodes.

The builder describes the pedal as using NOS components, but does not publish a complete bill of materials.

## Sound

The manufacturer describes the response as flat across the guitar's usable frequency range with unusually strong bass and low excess noise. The pedal is intended to react significantly to pickup strength and guitar-volume changes. At lower FUZZ settings, Anode Effects describes a velcro-like texture resembling a dying battery; at higher settings, the circuit stays smoother and less gritty than a traditional fuzz. [1]
