# Anode Effects — MOS Fuzz

## What this pedal is
MOS Fuzz is a full-frequency fuzz that expands on the Bazz Fuss concept but uses MOSFETs for a smoother, less gritty response. Anode Effects emphasizes unusually strong low end, broad frequency response and excellent cleanup from the guitar's volume control. [1]

## Sound
MOS Fuzz stays unusually full-range for a fuzz, with more bass than a conventional unit and strong cleanup when the guitar volume is reduced. Lower FUZZ settings can produce a velcro or dying-battery-style texture, while stronger settings remain smoother and less gritty than a traditional fuzz. [1]
