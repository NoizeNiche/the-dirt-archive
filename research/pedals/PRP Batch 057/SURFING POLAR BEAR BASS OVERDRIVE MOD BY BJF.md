# Animals Pedal — SURFING POLAR BEAR BASS OVERDRIVE MOD BY BJF

## What this pedal is
This bass-focused overdrive is a Surfing Bear Overdrive derivative developed with Björn Juhl of BJFe. Animals Pedal describes it as a light-gain solution intended to retain bass tone and root-note definition while adding an articulate edge. [1]

## Sound
The SPBBOD is tuned for bass clarity, preserving low-end and root notes while adding overdrive when the player digs in. Animals Pedal emphasizes dynamic response from clean-ish playing toward a stronger OD edge, making the distortion follow finger and pick intensity rather than flattening the bass sound. [1]
