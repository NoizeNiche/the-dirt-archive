# Animals Pedal — Tioga Road Cycling Distortion

## What this pedal is
Tioga Road Cycling Distortion is an analog distortion designed by Wren and Cuff around an early-1980s "big box" distortion sound. Its center toggle changes the clipping behavior among symmetrical, bypassed and asymmetric modes. [1][2]

## Colorways
- Current teal/green enclosure with cycling-bear artwork is documented.
- Other graphic editions exist.

## Versions and factory options
- VOLUME
- TONE
- DIST
- Three-way clipping toggle: Symmetrical / Bypass / Asymmetric
- True bypass
- 9 V battery or center-minus DC9V adapter
- 64 x 112 x 50 mm; 385 g; 1 mA [1]

## Version changes
The current edition relocates the footswitch toward the front-right, improves battery access and expands/fine-tunes the circuit for more guitars and amplifiers. The core historical target remains the early-1980s big-box distortion family. [1]

## Sound
Tioga Road spans thick nostalgic early-1980s distortion through lighter distortion and fuzz-like territory. The clipping selector changes the texture and dynamic feel, while the EQ keeps the response useful for heavy rhythm and broader modern applications. [1][2]
