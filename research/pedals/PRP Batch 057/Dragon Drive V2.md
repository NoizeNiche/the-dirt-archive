# Anode Effects — Dragon Drive V2

## What this pedal is
Dragon Drive V2 is a medium-high-gain hard-clipping overdrive with a passive three-band EQ and externally visible clipping LEDs. Anode Effects describes V2 as a modified circuit with the volume control isolated from the tone stack and flat-top reactive LEDs mounted flush with the enclosure. [1][2]

## Colorways
- V2 is documented in a light enclosure with Dragon artwork and colored LED "flame" elements.

## Versions and factory options
- VOLUME
- DRIVE
- BASS
- MID
- TREBLE
- Reactive clipping LEDs
- Passive 3-band EQ
- Top-mounted jacks
- +9 V center-negative only [1]

## Version changes
Compared with the earlier Dragon Drive, V2 adds a new look, modifies the circuit to isolate volume from the tone controls and uses flat-top LEDs flush with the face of the pedal. [1][2]

## Diode
- The clipping system uses external LED clipping diodes.
- Exact LED part numbers are not publicly documented. [1]

## Sound
Dragon Drive V2 is voiced around a cranked Marshall-style overdrive response, but the passive three-band EQ expands it from warm boost through bluesy grit into heavy distortion. The LED clipping arrangement is designed to keep pick dynamics more evident by using a relatively low-compression clipping behavior. [1]
