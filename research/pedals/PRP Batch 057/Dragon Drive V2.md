# Anode Effects — Dragon Drive V2

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Dragon Drive V2
- **Builder:** Anode Effects
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive
- **Identity:** Medium-high-gain hard-clipping overdrive with passive three-band EQ and externally visible reactive LED clipping. The V2 is explicitly presented by Anode Effects as a modified revision of the earlier Dragon Drive. [1]

## What this pedal is
The Dragon Drive V2 is an amp-inspired hard-clipping overdrive intended to move from warm boost and bluesy grit into heavy distortion while retaining clarity. Anode Effects says its reference point is the sound of a cranked Marshall-style amplifier, but the published material describes the Dragon as an inspired design rather than as a named commercial amp clone. [1]

## Controls
- **VOLUME:** Overall output level.
- **DRIVE:** Amount of overdrive/clipping.
- **BASS:** Low-frequency adjustment.
- **MID:** Midrange adjustment.
- **TREBLE:** High-frequency adjustment.
- **Clipping LEDs:** Yellow and red LEDs visibly respond to the guitar signal; their brightness/flicker changes with picking strength and drive setting. [1]

## Circuit / clipping architecture
- **Topology described by builder:** Hard-clipping overdrive with a passive three-band EQ.
- **Clipping element:** External LED clipping diodes.
- **LED behavior:** Lighter picking produces less visible clipping; harder picking produces brighter/more continuous LED activity. Increasing DRIVE also increases visible clipping activity. [1]
- **Tone-stack relationship in V2:** Anode Effects specifically says the V2 modification isolates the volume control from the tone controls. [1]
- **EQ behavior:** With the three EQ controls at noon, the builder describes a small mid scoop around approximately 1 kHz. [1]
- Exact schematic topology and diode part numbers are not publicly documented in the reviewed sources.

## Visual indicator system
Three LEDs form the Dragon flame:
- **Blue LED:** power indicator.
- **Yellow LED:** part of the reactive clipping flame.
- **Red LED:** part of the reactive clipping flame.
The blue indicator does not serve as the signal-clipping indicator described for the yellow/red pair. [1]

## Power / construction
- **Power:** +9 V DC, center-negative only. [1]
- **Jacks:** Top-mounted Lumberg jacks.
- **Footswitch:** Heavy-duty soft-click type.
- **Components called out by builder:** 1% metal-film resistors; Silver Mica and Wima capacitors.
- **Warranty:** Lifetime warranty stated by Anode Effects. [1]

## Version history
- **Earlier Dragon Drive:** Original Dragon design.
- **Dragon Drive V2:** New look, modified circuit that isolates volume from the tone controls, and flat-top reactive LEDs mounted flush with the face of the pedal. [1][2]
- No additional numbered electronic revision is established by the reviewed sources.

## Sound
The builder characterizes the Dragon as relatively low-compression LED clipping intended to preserve picking dynamics. The passive three-band EQ gives the circuit a broad usable range rather than fixing it to one narrow voicing. Anode Effects also states that the pedal retains low end and works well for bass, producing a growly full-range distortion. [1]
