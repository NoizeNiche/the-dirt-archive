# Bogner Amplification - La Grange

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Bogner Amplification
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive

## Research
La Grange is Bogner's Plexi-style overdrive, designed to reproduce the interaction of the bright and normal channels of a late-1960s four-input Plexi amplifier. Its Channel Blend control crossfades those two modeled inputs, while the gain, structure, presence and Variac switches reshape the feel and frequency response.

A separate footswitchable boost sits after the main overdrive and can be used alone or stacked. The audio path is analog, with an expression input that can control gain.

## Controls and architecture
- Volume
- Tone
- Channel Blend
- Gain
- Independent Boost Level
- 3-position Gain switch
- 3-position Structure switch
- 2-position Presence switch
- Variac on/off
- Boost footswitch
- Main bypass footswitch
- Expression input for gain
- True bypass
- 9V DC or internal 9V battery
