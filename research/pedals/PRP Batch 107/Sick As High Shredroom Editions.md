# Bondi Effects - Sick As High Shredroom Editions

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Bondi Effects
- **Catalog type:** Distortion

## Research
The Sick As High Shredroom Editions entry represents the documented High Shredroom production/finish variants of Bondi's high-gain Sick As platform. Bondi's collaboration with Earthling Designs created the High Shredroom circuit, while later editions were released in multiple finishes including Blackout, Graphite and Neon Pink.

The circuit identity stays with the High Shredroom design: higher gain than the standard Sick As, a narrower mid focus, more open top and bottom, internal 18V operation and an active two-band EQ.

## Documented editions
- Blackout
- Graphite
- Neon Pink
- Earlier limited High Shredroom runs
