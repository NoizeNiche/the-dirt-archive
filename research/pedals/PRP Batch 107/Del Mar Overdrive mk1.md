# Bondi Effects - Del Mar Overdrive mk1

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Bondi Effects
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive

## Research
The original Del Mar Overdrive is a hybrid of several classic clipping approaches. Bondi describes its development as an attempt to combine the clean half of a Klon-style circuit with a Tube Screamer-style clipping stage, while the resulting circuit also incorporates Bluesbreaker-like openness.

The pedal provides clean boost through overdrive by blending clean and driven signal with the Drive control. Its active Bass and Treble controls provide broad tonal adjustment, and a toggle switches between a more compressed, mid-focused response and a more open voice with more bass extension.

The Mk1 internally boosts the 9V supply to 18V.

## Controls and architecture
- Output
- Drive
- Bass
- Treble
- Compression/headroom toggle
- Clean/drive blend behavior
- Soft-touch true bypass
- Internal 18V operation from 9V input
- 500k input impedance
- 20mA documented current draw on Mk1
