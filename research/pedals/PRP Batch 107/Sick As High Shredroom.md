# Bondi Effects - Sick As High Shredroom

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Bondi Effects
- **Catalog type:** Distortion

## Research
The Sick As High Shredroom is a higher-gain development of Bondi's Sick As overdrive, created with Earthling Designs. Bondi narrowed the usable gain sweep and narrowed the mid focus, opening more top and bottom end for a more articulate and aggressive response.

The circuit retains the Sick As architecture of internal voltage boosting and active two-band EQ. Bondi specifies internal 18V operation, an active tone stack capable of 15dB boost or cut, a headroom/character toggle and soft-touch true bypass.

## Controls and architecture
- Level
- Gain
- Bass
- Treble
- Headroom/character toggle
- Internal 18V operation
- Active ±15dB bass/treble EQ
- Soft-touch true bypass
- Approx. 65mA current draw
