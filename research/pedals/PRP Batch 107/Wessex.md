# Bogner Amplification - Wessex

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Bogner Amplification
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive

## Research
The Wessex is a low- to moderate-gain Bogner overdrive built around a Rupert Neve Designs audio transformer. It ranges from clean boost through driven amp-like overdrive, with a two-band active EQ and an Enhance/Neutral switch that changes the gain character.

Bogner specifies a 100% analog signal path and true bypass. The interactive jewel indicator responds to playing dynamics rather than serving only as a static on/off lamp.

## Controls and architecture
- Level
- Gain
- Treble
- Bass
- Enhance / Neutral switch
- Rupert Neve Designs audio transformer
- True bypass
- 9V DC, 50mA or greater
