# Bondi Effects - Del Mar Overdrive mk2

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Bondi Effects
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive

## Research
The Del Mar mk2 keeps the core Del Mar voice while revising the power and switching sections. Bondi states that the Mk2 reduces the noise floor substantially compared with the original, adds a more robust power supply, raises the internal operating voltage to 24V and uses quieter true-bypass switching.

The Drive control continues to blend clean and overdriven signal, while Bass and Treble are active boost/cut controls. The toggle changes between a more compressed, mid-focused setting and a more open, transparent setting.

## Controls and architecture
- Level
- Gain/Drive
- Bass
- Treble
- Headroom/character toggle
- Internal 24V operation from a 9V supply
- Soft-touch true bypass
- 500k input impedance
- Output impedance below 10k
- 9V DC, 100mA specified
