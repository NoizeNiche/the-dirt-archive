# Bogner Amplification - Oxford

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Bogner Amplification
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz

## Research
The Oxford is Bogner's ferrosilicon fuzz, developed with a Rupert Neve-designed transformer. It covers three broad fuzz characters and two gain ranges rather than presenting a single fixed fuzz voice. The Gain switch changes the gain structure, while the three-position Fuzz switch moves between darker and brighter voicings.

The main Level, Tone and Gain controls provide the continuous part of the range. Bogner's design uses a temperature-resistant ferrosilicon device and a 100% analog signal path with true-bypass switching.

## Controls and architecture
- Level
- Tone
- Gain
- 2-position Gain switch
- 3-position Fuzz switch
- Rupert Neve-designed transformer
- Temperature-resistant ferrosilicon transistor/device
- True bypass
- 9V operation
