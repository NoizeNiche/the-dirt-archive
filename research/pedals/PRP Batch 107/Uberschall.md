# Bogner Amplification - Uberschall

## PRP identity

- **Builder:** Bogner Amplification
- **Catalog type:** Distortion

## Research

The Uberschall pedal translates Bogner's high-gain Uberschall amplifier concept into a compact analog distortion. The control set uses five amplifier-style controls: Volume, Treble, Middle, Bass and Gain. The Middle control covers a particularly wide range, from heavily scooped sounds toward fuller, focused midrange tones.

A dedicated boost provides an adjustable volume increase and a preset amount of gain lift. The pedal has a fully analog audio path, true bypass and an internal low-battery monitoring circuit that does not carry audio.

## Controls and architecture

- 9V DC or internal 9V battery
- Fully analog audio path
- True bypass
