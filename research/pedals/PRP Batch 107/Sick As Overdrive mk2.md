# Bondi Effects - Sick As Overdrive mk2

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Bondi Effects
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive

## Research
The Sick As mk2 is the second major revision of Bondi's Sick As platform. It retains the core clean-blend overdrive architecture, dual active EQ and two-mode voicing while changing the physical implementation and component layout from the earliest Sick As builds.

Documented Mk2 examples use the familiar Level, Gain, Bass and Treble controls plus the voicing toggle. Published comparisons identify the Mk2 as a distinct hardware generation from the original and note later changes to enclosure, component selection and board layout before the Mk3.

## Controls and architecture
- Level
- Gain
- Bass
- Treble
- Voicing toggle
- Clean/drive blend behavior
- Internal 18V operation
- True bypass
- Analog circuit
