# Baltimore Sonic Research Institute - Oriole Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Oriole Fuzz
- **Builder:** Baltimore Sonic Research Institute
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Rare germanium fuzz using a NOS military-grade Philips CV7112 device with matched silicon gain devices.

## What this pedal is
Oriole Fuzz combines a rare NOS Philips CV7112 germanium transistor with a pair of matched old-stock silicon transistors. BSRI voiced the design to combine Fuzz Face-like texture with the tighter response of cascaded gain stages.

## Sound
BSRI describes the Oriole as a smooth and punchy fuzz that cleans up from the guitar volume control and becomes more aggressive when pushed. Its tilt-EQ filtering moves between low-pass and high-pass emphasis, while the cascaded gain structure tightens the response compared with a basic Fuzz Face.
