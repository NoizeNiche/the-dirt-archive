# Baltimore Sonic Research Institute — Oriole Fuzz

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** Oriole Fuzz
- **Builder:** Baltimore Sonic Research Institute
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Hybrid germanium/silicon fuzz using a NOS Philips CV7112 germanium transistor with matched old-stock silicon gain devices. [1][2]

## What this pedal is

Effects Database documents the Oriole as a fuzz with a rare **Philips CV7112** germanium transistor paired with matched old-stock silicon transistors. BSRI's design combines the warmth/texture of germanium with the comparatively firm response of cascaded silicon gain stages. [1]

## Controls / architecture

- **VOLUME**
- **FUZZ**
- Tilt-style EQ section moving between low-pass and high-pass emphasis.
- Hand-built Baltimore production. [1][2]

## Circuit architecture

- Hybrid **germanium + silicon** gain structure.
- A rare military-grade Philips **CV7112** germanium device is documented.
- Two matched old-stock silicon transistors are also documented. [1]

## Sound

BSRI/Effects Database describe the Oriole as smooth but punchy, capable of cleaning up from the guitar volume and becoming more aggressive when driven. The tilt-EQ section moves the tonal emphasis across the spectrum, while the hybrid transistor architecture is intended to combine different response characteristics. [1][2]
