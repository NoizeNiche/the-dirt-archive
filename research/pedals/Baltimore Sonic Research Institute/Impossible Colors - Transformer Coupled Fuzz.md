# Baltimore Sonic Research Institute — Impossible Colors - Transformer Coupled Fuzz

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** Impossible Colors - Transformer Coupled Fuzz
- **Builder:** Baltimore Sonic Research Institute
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Original Impossible Colors, a transformer-coupled fuzz/drive with vintage selected transistors, three clipping modes, Range and Depth switching, and highly interactive Attack/Texture controls. [1]

## What this pedal is

BSRI designed Impossible Colors to cover a very broad continuum from overdrive through distortion and compressed splattery fuzz, including unusual octave, ghost-tone and horn-like textures. The transformer-coupled input stage is designed to emulate the inductance/electrical behavior of a guitar pickup so the pedal can remain responsive when placed after buffers. [1][2]

## Controls

- **VOLUME:** Output level with boost above unity.
- **ATTACK:** Input gain, from lighter boost to heavy clipping.
- **TEXTURE:** Changes the voltage delivered to part of the circuit, altering clipping character and dynamic range.
- **VOICE:** Tilt-style EQ using low-pass and high-pass behavior.
- **RANGE:** Changes the Texture control's range.
- **DEPTH:** Selects between two frequency ranges entering the clipping circuit.
- **MODE:** Three clipping configurations, from more dynamic overdrive through heavier distortion and stranger compressed textures. [1][2]

## Circuit architecture

- **Transformer-coupled input stage** that simulates pickup inductance/electrical behavior.
- Selected vintage transistors are used specifically in this circuit; BSRI states it spent months selecting devices for the design. [1]
- Exact transistor part numbers are not published in the reviewed official material.

## Transistor

- **Technology:** Discrete vintage transistor design.
- **Selection:** Tested/selected vintage transistors for each unit.
- **Exact part numbers:** Unknown. [1]

## Diode / clipping

- The product provides **three clipping configurations** through the MODE switch.
- Exact clipping diode/device part numbers are **not publicly documented**.

## Version history

- **Original Impossible Colors:** Volume, Attack, Texture, Voice plus Range, Depth and three-position Mode.
- **Impossible Colors II / V2:** separate archive record. V2 replaces Voice with Bass/Treble and adds a Boost stage plus revised switching. [1][2]
These are maintained as distinct records.

## Sound

BSRI describes the original as moving from dynamic analog overdrive into distortion and compressed fuzz, with extreme settings producing octave-like, ghost, suboctave and horn-like effects. The **Texture** and **Attack** controls are deliberately interactive because Texture changes both clipping voltage and dynamic range. [1]
