# Baltimore Sonic Research Institute - Impossible Colors - Transformer Coupled Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Impossible Colors - Transformer Coupled Fuzz
- **Builder:** Baltimore Sonic Research Institute
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Transformer-coupled fuzz covering boost, overdrive, distortion, compressed fuzz, and unusual octave/horn textures.

## What this pedal is
Impossible Colors uses a transformer-coupled input stage to emulate the electrical behavior of a guitar pickup, allowing the pedal to respond naturally even after buffered devices. The original version combines selected vintage transistors with extensive controls for gain, texture, filtering, and clipping behavior.

## Colorways
- A documented production example uses a **dark textured enclosure with black, silver, and white illustrated graphics**.
- No complete factory colorway chronology was verified.

## Versions and factory options
### Original Impossible Colors
- Volume
- Attack
- Texture
- Voice
- Range switch
- Depth switch
- Mode switch
- Transformer-coupled input
- Selected vintage transistors

## Version changes
The original uses Voice, Range, Depth, and a three-position Mode control. BSRI later released Impossible Colors V2 / II, replacing Voice with separate Bass and Treble controls and adding a Boost stage plus updated switching, so V2 is maintained as a distinct archive target.

## Transistor
- **Technology:** Discrete transistor fuzz.
- **Documented selection:** BSRI states that the original uses selected vintage transistors, but the checked sources do not publish a stable part-number list.

## Diode
- **Exact clipping/protection arrangement:** Not publicly documented in the checked sources.

## Sound
Impossible Colors spans light boost, dynamic overdrive, distortion, and heavily compressed fuzz, with extreme settings producing octave-like, ghost, suboctave, and horn-like tones. Attack controls the input gain while Texture changes the voltage applied to part of the clipping circuit, making the two controls highly interactive.
