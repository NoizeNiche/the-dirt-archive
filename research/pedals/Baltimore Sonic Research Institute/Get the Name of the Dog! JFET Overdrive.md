# Baltimore Sonic Research Institute - Get the Name of the Dog! JFET Overdrive

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Get the Name of the Dog! JFET Overdrive

- **Identity:** Discrete JFET overdrive with multiple voicing controls for low- and mid-gain tones.

## What this pedal is
Get the Name of the Dog! is a completely discrete transistor overdrive built around JFET gain stages. BSRI describes it as a detailed, amp-like alternative to soft-clipping op-amp drives, with enough range to boost and tighten an amp or provide chunky low- and mid-gain tones.

## Sound
BSRI describes the pedal as covering chunky low- and mid-gain sounds, with enough flexibility to boost and tighten an amplifier for harder rock tones. The discrete gain design is intended to retain broad dynamics and an amp-like response rather than the compressed feel of traditional soft-clipping op-amp drives.
