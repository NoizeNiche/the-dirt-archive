## What this pedal is

BSRI's Ashmaker is designed as a flexible high-gain distortion for stage and studio use. The builder's retail documentation describes broad preamp gain, a three-band EQ and a gain boost for heavier riffs, explicitly noting suitability for drop tunings and multi-string guitars. [1][2]

## Controls

- **VOLUME:** Output level.
- **GRIND:** Distortion amount.
- **BASS:** Low-frequency EQ.
- **CONTOUR:** Midrange EQ.
- **TREBLE:** High-frequency EQ.
- **BOOST:** Additional gain boost.
- Two-switch/two-channel-style operation is documented in retailer descriptions. [1][2]

## Sound

The Ashmaker is designed for aggressive high-gain distortion with substantial EQ control. The three-band section provides bass, mids and treble shaping, while BOOST adds additional gain for heavier parts. The builder/retailer positioning specifically emphasizes low-tuned and multi-string use. [1][2]
