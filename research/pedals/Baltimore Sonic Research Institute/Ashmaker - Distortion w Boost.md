# Baltimore Sonic Research Institute - Ashmaker - Distortion w/ Boost

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Ashmaker - Distortion w/ Boost
- **Builder:** Baltimore Sonic Research Institute
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** Two-channel high-gain distortion with a footswitchable gain boost and active three-band EQ.

## What this pedal is
Ashmaker is a high-gain distortion designed around broad preamp-style gain and an active three-band EQ. The boost footswitch adds another gain layer for heavier passages, and the pedal is explicitly intended to work with drop tunings and extended-range guitars.

## Sound
Ashmaker is voiced for broad, high-gain distortion with enough low-frequency control for drop tunings and multi-string instruments. The separate boost and grind controls let the pedal move from substantial base distortion into heavier, more forceful riffing.
