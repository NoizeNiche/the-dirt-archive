## What this pedal is
Radical Conversion is BSRI's discrete-opamp reinterpretation of the Metal Zone concept. Instead of integrated op-amps, the circuit uses multiple discrete transistor op-amp stages, with BSRI documenting 27 transistors across the design and three preamp voicings ahead of the main gain stage.

## Sound
Preamp I preserves the classic Metal Zone-style midrange snarl, Preamp II tightens the low end for low-tuned playing, and Preamp III feeds more low end into the gain stage for a fatter, more compressed response. The active Bass, Treble, Mid Freq, and Mid Gain controls then provide broad shaping around the discrete multi-stage distortion.

### Verified sound evidence
The design originated from a project in which the developer analyzed and rebuilt multiple Boss analog distortion pedals that used discrete opamp circuits.
This approach intentionally substitutes lower performance integrated amplifiers with discrete stages that introduce additional harmonic complexity when driven into distortion.
The overall concept is a discrete reinterpretation of the Metal Zone topology, including its active semi-parametric midrange equalizer and multi-stage gain structure.
