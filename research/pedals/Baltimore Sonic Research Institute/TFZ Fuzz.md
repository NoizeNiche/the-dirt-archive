# Baltimore Sonic Research Institute - TFZ Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** TFZ Fuzz
- **Builder:** Baltimore Sonic Research Institute
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Analog multi-mode silicon fuzz with voltage-starve texture control and multiple clipping/frequency modes.

## What this pedal is
TFZ is a silicon fuzz built around three metal-can silicon transistors and three clipping diodes. Its Texture control varies the voltage feeding part of the circuit, while Voice, frequency, and clipping controls reshape the response from conventional fuzz toward starved, gated, octave-like, and more extreme textures.

## Sound
The Texture control functions as a voltage-starve control, moving the fuzz toward more compressed and unstable textures as the supply voltage is reduced. Voice is a tilt-style EQ, while the Tuba/Megaphone and three-position Clipping controls extend the pedal from tighter guitar-like fuzz into unusual octave, horn-like, and gated responses.
