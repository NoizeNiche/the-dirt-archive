# Baltimore Sonic Research Institute — Impossible Colors II Transformer Coupled Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Impossible Colors II Transformer Coupled Fuzz
- **Builder:** Baltimore Sonic Research Institute
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Second-generation Impossible Colors retaining the transformer-coupled input while adding active Bass/Treble EQ, a switchable preamp Boost, Low Cut/Low Boost switching and two clipping modes. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
Impossible Colors II expands the original platform rather than simply changing the graphics. BSRI adds a dedicated active EQ, a switchable boost stage and revised low-frequency/clipping controls while retaining the distinctive Attack/Texture interaction of the original. [1]

## Controls
- **VOLUME:** Output level, with substantial boost available.
- **BASS:** Boost/cut below 100 Hz by 12 dB.
- **TREBLE:** Boost/cut above 4 kHz by 12 dB.
- **TEXTURE:** Changes clipping voltage/dynamic range.
- **ATTACK:** Input gain from light boost through heavy clipping.
- **BOOST:** Preamp boost feeding the transformer stage.
- **LOW CUT / LOW BOOST:** Selects between two frequency ranges feeding the clipping stage.
- **MODE I / II:** Selects between two clipping configurations. [1]

## Circuit architecture
- Transformer-coupled input stage simulating guitar-pickup electrical behavior.
- Active Bass/Treble EQ.
- Switchable preamp Boost.
- Revised clipping and low-frequency switching. [1]

## Transistor
- BSRI describes the Impossible Colors family as using selected vintage transistors.
- Exact transistor part numbers are **not publicly documented** in the reviewed sources.
## Diode / clipping
- Two user-selectable clipping configurations are provided by MODE I/II.
- Exact diode/device part numbers are not published.

## Version changes from original
Compared with the original Impossible Colors:
- **Voice** becomes separate **Bass** and **Treble** controls.
- A **Boost** stage is added.
- **Depth** becomes the two-position **Low Cut / Low Boost** switch.
- The three-position original Mode control becomes **Mode I / II**.
- The transformer-coupled input and Attack/Texture concept remain. [1][2]

## Sound
BSRI describes V2 as covering saggy tube-amp overdrive, amp-on-fire distortion and highly unusual suboctave/horn-like fuzz. Low Cut/Low Boost, Mode, Attack and Texture interact strongly, allowing tighter guitar-like clipping or stranger parallel horn/suboctave textures. [1]
