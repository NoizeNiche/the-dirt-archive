# Baltimore Sonic Research Institute - Impossible Colors II Transformer Coupled Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Impossible Colors II Transformer Coupled Fuzz
- **Builder:** Baltimore Sonic Research Institute
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Second-generation Impossible Colors with active EQ, preamp boost, and revised clipping/low-frequency switching.

## What this pedal is
Impossible Colors II builds directly on the original transformer-coupled fuzz platform. The redesign adds active Bass and Treble controls, a switchable preamp Boost, and revised Low Cut/Low Boost and Mode I/II switching while retaining the Attack and Texture interaction.

## Version changes
V2 replaces the original Voice control with separate Bass and Treble controls and adds a Boost stage. It also revises the switching to a two-position Low Cut/Low Boost selector and a two-position clipping Mode selector.

## Sound
V2 retains the original's range from dynamic overdrive and distortion through compressed fuzz while giving the player more direct low/high-frequency control. The Boost stage can be used for additional fuzz and gain-staging flexibility, while the Low Cut/Boost and Mode switches strongly alter the clipping response.
