## What this pedal is
**MÅNE** is a Fjord Fuzz fuzz-and-filter pedal. The manufacturer’s own demo describes it as a dual fuzz / dual filter pedal with expression control. Independent coverage identifies the fuzz section and filter section as separate controls within the same pedal. [1][2][3]

## Controls and operation
The documented design includes a two-voice fuzz section, two filter modes, level and cutoff controls, and an expression-pedal input for filter cutoff. [2][3]
