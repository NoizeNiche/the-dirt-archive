## Sound
As a guitarist I personally prefer maximum dynamic range from my rig and HEDDA is designed specifically for this purpose.
The way this is done is by using a very low input impedance and very high gain silicon transistors as well as built-in asymmetrical clipping.
Low input impedance means don’t stick it after a buffer or an unbuffered wah and please don’t expect the output or gain you’ll find in ODIN or BERSERK.
