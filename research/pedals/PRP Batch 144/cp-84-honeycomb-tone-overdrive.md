# Caline CP-84 Honeycomb Tone - Overdrive

- **Builder:** Caline
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** CP-84 Honeycomb Tone - Overdrive
- **Colorways:** Yellow/gold enclosure with honeycomb artwork and black knobs is the documented standard appearance.
- **Versions:** No distinct production versions were confirmed; the core design uses three controls.
- **Version changes:** No confirmed hardware revision or circuit change was found.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.
- **Diode type:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.
- **Controls / switching:** Volume, Tone, Honey; true bypass.
- **Sound:** The CP-84 is a low-gain overdrive intended to hit the familiar “sweet spot” where an amp begins to break up, and it can also be used to push or shape an already dirty signal. Pedal of the Day identifies the design as based around the Keeley Sweet Spot concept, while the RockBoard documentation describes a broad clean-to-warm-overdrive range and a softer distorted edge.
- **Research confidence:** High for controls, use case, and documented voicing; medium for the specific Sweet Spot lineage because that attribution comes from independent pedal review rather than a Caline schematic.
