# Caline CP-79 Wolf Pack / King Of Ga-Ga - Boost/Overdrive

- **Builder:** Caline
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** CP-79 Wolf Pack / King Of Ga-Ga - Boost/Overdrive
- **Colorways:** Blue enclosure with white controls and white wolf graphic is documented in exact-model listings.
- **Versions:** The reviewed sources treat Wolf Pack and King Of Ga-Ga as names for the same CP-79 design, with an overdrive channel and a separate boost.
- **Version changes:** No confirmed hardware revision was found. Caline-facing secondary documentation identifies the CP-79 as an elaboration of the CP-49 Midlander with an added boost function.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.
- **Diode type:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.
- **Controls / switching:** Volume, Treble, Gain, Boost, with separate Overdrive and Boost footswitches; true bypass.
- **Sound:** The CP-79 is a low-to-medium gain overdrive designed around the familiar Tube Screamer-style mid-focused response, with the extra boost providing more level and a cleaner, tighter push. The reviewed product documentation describes it as useful both for smooth overdrive and as a clean boost, with the boost switch also helping cut excess low end.
- **Research confidence:** High for controls, operating layout, and general voicing; medium for the exact relationship to CP-49 because that linkage appears in retailer/product documentation rather than a schematic.
