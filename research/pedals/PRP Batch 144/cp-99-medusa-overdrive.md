# Caline CP-99 Medusa - Overdrive

- **Builder:** Caline
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** CP-99 Medusa - Overdrive
- **Colorways:** Gold enclosure with black knobs and red Medusa/archer artwork is documented across multiple exact-model listings.
- **Versions:** No distinct production versions were confirmed. The model is a compact, higher-gain development of Caline’s CP-43 Pegasus concept.
- **Version changes:** No confirmed hardware revision or circuit change was found in the reviewed sources.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.
- **Diode type:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.
- **Controls / switching:** Volume, Tone, Gain; true bypass.
- **Sound:** The CP-99 is a transparent-style overdrive designed to remain clean and articulate at lower gain while producing smoother, more saturated overdrive as gain is increased. Caline’s documentation ties it to the original Centaur concept and describes it as a higher-gain compact version of the CP-43 Pegasus, while independent reviews note that it is inspired by the Klon rather than being an exact traditional Klon circuit.
- **Research confidence:** High for controls, general gain behavior, and the CP-43 relationship; medium for exact Klon circuit equivalence because the available independent evidence explicitly notes design differences.
