## What this pedal is
The ST-9 is documented as a **1984-only** Super Tube Screamer variation of the TS9 family. It retains the mid-focused overdrive architecture and adds a dedicated **MID BOOST** control. [1][2]

## Controls
- **DRIVE**
- **TONE**
- **LEVEL**
- **MID BOOST** [1][2]

## Circuit / architecture
Effects Database documents the ST-9 as an analog Maxon 9-Series overdrive in the Tube Screamer family, while RockBoard describes the added Mid Boost as the feature separating it from the standard TS9 control set. [1][2]
