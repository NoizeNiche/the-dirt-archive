## What this pedal is
The OD-9 is Maxon's 9-Series overdrive. Current product documentation describes it as a faithful reissue of the classic 1980s OD-9/TS-9 family circuit, with more drive and output than the OD808 reference and mechanical true-bypass switching. [1][2]

## Controls
- **DRIVE**
- **TONE**
- **LEVEL** [1][2]

## Circuit / architecture
Dealer and manufacturer documentation identify the OD-9 as an analog overdrive built around the **JRC4558** family op-amp, with the classic Tube Screamer-style feedback-loop clipping architecture. [2]
The current reissue also uses a true-bypass switching arrangement unlike some earlier buffered implementations. [1][2]

## History
- The OD-9 belongs to Maxon's modern 9-Series and reproduces the 1980s OD-9 circuit family. [1][2]
- The archive keeps OD-9 distinct from OD-9 Pro+ and from Maxon/Nisshin Onpa OD-9 variants only where the catalog identity explicitly distinguishes the later product.

## Sound
Maxon/dealer documentation characterizes the OD-9 around mid-focused overdrive, natural compression, higher drive/output than the OD808, and responsive amp boosting. [1][2]
