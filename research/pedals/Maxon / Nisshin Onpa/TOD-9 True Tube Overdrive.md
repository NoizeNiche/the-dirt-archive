## What this pedal is

The TOD-9 combines Maxon's classic OD-9 circuit with a front-end tube gain stage. The manufacturer identifies a **mil-spec 5703WB JAN Raytheon sub-miniature vacuum tube** and a dedicated Tube control. [1][2]

## Tube/circuit architecture

The tube stage sits at the front of the signal path and drives the OD-9 clipping section. The manufacturer documents internal voltage conversion from 9V input to **30VDC** for the tube circuit and a startup inrush-current-control system that warms the tube at low voltage. [1]

## Controls

- **TUBE**
- **DRIVE**
- **TONE**
- **LEVEL** [1][2]

With Tube at zero, Maxon states that the unit behaves as an OD-9; increasing Tube adds low/low-mid warmth and additional saturation as the stage drives the OD-9 clipping circuit. [1]

## Power and physical specification

- **Internal circuit voltage:** 30VDC via DC-DC converter. [1]
- **Current consumption:** 260mA at 9VDC. [1]
- **Power:** Maxon AC2009 supply; no battery operation. [1]
- **Input impedance:** 500kΩ. [1]
- **Output impedance:** 10kΩ or less. [1]
- **Maximum gain:** 48.5dB at 1.6kHz. [1]
- **Dimensions:** 74 × 124 × 54mm. [1]
- **Weight:** 560g excluding adaptor. [1]
- **Switching:** mechanical true bypass via Fujisoku 4PDT. [1]

## Sound

Maxon describes the TOD-9 around OD-9 clarity plus added tube warmth, increased girth, and tube-associated sag as Tube gain is introduced. These are manufacturer characterizations rather than independent measurements. [1][2]
