## What this pedal is
Maxon describes the OOD-9 as an updated version of its OD880/OSD-9-derived organic overdrive concept. The manufacturer documents current-production NJM/JRC 4558 op amps in place of the NOS 741 devices used in the OSD-9 and positions the OOD-9 as a low-gain, dynamic overdrive. [1]

## Circuit / architecture
Maxon documents a **2SK246 JFET input buffer** and current-production **NJM/JRC 4558** op-amp gain stages. The OOD-9 is explicitly presented as an evolution of the OSD-9 circuit rather than a wholly unrelated design. [1]
The verified manufacturer documentation does not disclose a complete production schematic or full diode bill of materials.

## Sound
Maxon characterizes the OOD-9 as a warm, open, low-gain and dynamic overdrive, with a somewhat more pronounced high-frequency response than the OSD-9. These are manufacturer characterizations rather than independent measurements. [1]
