## What this pedal is

The SM-9 is documented as an early-1980s/1984 Maxon Super Metal. Contemporary and surviving-unit references identify a five-control format and Made-in-Japan production. An independent dealer archive describes the Maxon unit as the same underlying pedal as the Ibanez SM-9, made by the same manufacturer in the same period. [1][2]

Independent surviving-unit coverage ties the Maxon-branded SM-9 to the better-known Ibanez SM-9 platform, indicating a shared production lineage. [2]
