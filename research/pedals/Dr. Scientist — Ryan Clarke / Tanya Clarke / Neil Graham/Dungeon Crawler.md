## What this pedal is
A 2026 Guitar Pedal X review describes the **Dungeon Crawler** as an extreme-metal, high-gain distortion with active gyrator EQ, a separate **Grind** rectifier function, and a **Boost** stage. A contemporary maker video is also explicitly titled **DUNGEON CRAWLER by Dr. Scientist Sounds**.

## Circuit / technical evidence
The Guitar Pedal X technical summary reports:
- **Three very-high-gain 2N7000 MOSFET clipping stages** at the heart of the distortion circuit.
- **TL074 JFET-input op-amp** gyrator EQ stages.
- Post-gain EQ points at approximately **90 Hz (Lows)**, **420 Hz (Lower Mids)**, **1 kHz (Upper Mids)**, and **3 kHz (Highs)**.
- A **Thick** control centered around approximately **130 Hz** before the gain stages.
- A **Presence** control.
- **Grind** using a silicon NPN rectifier to add a coarser character.
- A comparator-based **Gate** with an internal sensitivity trimmer.
- A **Boost** function that adds volume and gain.
- Two status LEDs whose brightness changes with input gain.

The exact factory schematic, complete component values, and the manufacturer’s full production bill of materials were not recovered in the sources reviewed, so those details remain unknown.

## Sound / use
The documented design target is **extreme-metal high-gain distortion**, with very high saturation and sustain. Guitar Pedal X characterizes it as tight and modern in delivery rather than vintage-saggy, while noting that the EQ system can move the sound substantially. The archive treats those descriptions as reviewer observations, not universal sonic measurements.
