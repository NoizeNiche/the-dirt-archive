## What this pedal is
Bass Musician Magazine's exact-model review describes the **Frazz Dazzler** as a fuzz-oriented dirt pedal designed to work well with bass, including active basses. The defining feature is a **blend/mix control** that lets the dry and dirt signals be combined while retaining low end.

## Controls / operation
The exact-model review documents:
- **Volume**
- **Mix**
- **Sizzle**, which controls high frequencies
- **Gain**, which controls overall gain
- A mode switch selecting between two gain stages:
  - an **overdrive** stage
  - a heavily **gated fuzz** stage
- An **expression jack** that can control the Mix control.

The review notes that the Mix control can retain low-end information even at 100% wet, and that the gated fuzz side can produce pronounced digital-like artifacts as Gain is increased.

## Sound / use
The first gain stage is described as a usable overdrive that can work for heavy rock and edge-of-breakup applications. The second is a large gated fuzz stage whose character changes considerably with Sizzle and Gain. The reviewer specifically notes that the pedal can preserve low-end content while blending wet and dry signals, which is particularly relevant to bass use.

These are reviewer observations from an exact-model test, not a quantified frequency response.
