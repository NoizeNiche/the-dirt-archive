## What this pedal is

The Pickle Pie B is Wren and Cuff's bass-oriented evolution of the Pickle circuit. It retains the basic fuzz architecture while adding features intended to preserve low-frequency clarity and blend naturally with clean bass signal.

## Versions and factory options

- FET-buffered active clean boost.
- Changed FET-hybrid clipping sections.
- Bass-focused tone-stack range.
- Wider **Shape** range with a less pronounced mid scoop.
- Altered **Saturation/Fuzz** control for more usable lower-gain settings.
- True hard-wire bypass.
- 9V battery or 2.1mm adapter.
- 5mA current draw.

## Version changes

- Pickle Pie B incorporates substantial circuit changes from the earlier Pickle Pie, including the active clean boost and altered clipping/tone sections.

## Sound

Pickle Pie B is designed for thick bass fuzz that retains clarity when blended with the dry signal. The Shape and Saturation ranges were specifically altered for bass use and lower-gain settings.
