# Wren and Cuff — Super Russian

## PRP identity

## What this pedal is
The Super Russian combines a Tall Font Russian-style Muff with a separate germanium buffered boost. The internal signal order is boost into Muff, allowing the boost to be used alone or to push the fuzz harder.
