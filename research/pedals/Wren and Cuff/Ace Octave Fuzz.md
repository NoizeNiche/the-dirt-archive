## Sound
This is a bold, rich, fuzz with a very clear octave-up, especially once the player goes beyond the 12th fret.
A much different pedal than the Tycobrahe Octavia or Univox Superfuzz, this pedal has a tone that is unique among octave fuzzers.
The “Tone” toggle switch provides the option of a more mid-range focused sound, or a fat girthy sound with tons of extra lows.
