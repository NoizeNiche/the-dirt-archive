## Research
The Pi π Bass Fuzz is Blackhawk's bass-focused Pi π fuzz design and was developed as a replacement for the older Åsgard Bass Fuzz. The MKII/Russian version takes influence from the Green Russian Pi π circuit but adds a parallel clean-bass path with active bass boost and low-pass filtering. Internal switches select diode or MOSFET clipping and a high/full-range clipping frequency mode. Blackhawk specifies KT3102E transistors for the green version and BC549C for the black version, with individual transistor selection and measurement for gain/HFE matching.
