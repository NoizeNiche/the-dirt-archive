## Research

Valhalla is Blackhawk's sludgy, heavy fuzz platform. Early handwired versions used a passive-pickup simulator so the circuit could tolerate buffers, followed by a fuzz stage and a parametric/notch-style tone section. Documented versions add a bypass for the notch section, a bass-boost/output tone switch, and later models provide parallel clean blending and additional gain control. The circuit is intended to cover thick vintage fuzz through darker, heavier sounds.

## Controls and architecture

Version-dependent controls include:
- Volume/effect volume
- Tone or notch-frequency control
- Notch/tone bypass
- Bass-boost or output-frequency switch
- Gain on later deluxe versions
- Mid control and clean blend on later versions
- Impedance-correction transformer on later versions
