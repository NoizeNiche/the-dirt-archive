# Blackhawk Amplifiers - Windhand LBM Fuzz

## PRP identity

- **Builder:** Blackhawk Amplifiers
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Blackhawk Amplifiers Windhand LBM Fuzz.

## What this pedal is

The Windhand LBM Fuzz was designed for Garrett from WINDHAND as a live replacement for his vintage LBM fuzz/distortion pedal. Blackhawk added a gain control, indicator light, true bypass switching, upgraded WIMA capacitors, and additional filtering on the 9V supply.

## Colorways

- Blackhawk currently offers the Windhand LBM Fuzz in **Krackle Blackened Black** and related made-to-order finishes.
- Finish availability can change between production batches.

## Versions and factory options

- **Gain** control added to the LBM-based design.
- Internal tone selection between **treble** and **bass** modes in the earlier documented version.
- Later update replaced the internal tone toggle with a trim pot.
- True bypass.
- Standard 9V negative-tip, 2.1mm power.
- Handmade to order.

## Version changes

- Blackhawk's November 2023 update replaced the internal tone toggle with a trim pot.
- The darker setting corresponds to Garrett's preferred tone-pot position.
