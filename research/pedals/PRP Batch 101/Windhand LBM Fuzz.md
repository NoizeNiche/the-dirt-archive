# Blackhawk Amplifiers - Windhand LBM Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Blackhawk Amplifiers
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Fuzz

## Research
The Windhand LBM Fuzz was designed for Garrett from WINDHAND as a live replacement for his vintage LBM fuzz/distortion pedal. Blackhawk's version adds a gain control, indicator light and true bypass switching, plus upgraded WIMA capacitors, additional filtering on the 9V supply and a PCB-mounted internal tone switch. The documented update replaces that tone toggle with a trim pot and notes that the dark setting reflects Garrett's preferred tone-pot position.

## Controls and architecture
- Gain
- Tone behavior selectable between treble/bass modes in early documentation
- Later internal trim for the preferred dark/bright range
- True bypass
- Standard 9V negative-tip, 2.1mm supply
- Blackhawk notes that its fuzz pedals can be placed anywhere in the chain without buffer/active-pickup concerns
