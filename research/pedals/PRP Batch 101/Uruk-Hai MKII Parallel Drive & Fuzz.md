# Blackhawk Amplifiers - Uruk-Hai MKII {Parallel} Drive & Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Blackhawk Amplifiers
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Fuzz / Overdrive

## Research
The Uruk-Hai MKII {Parallel} Drive & Fuzz combines Blackhawk's Valkyrie Drive and Valhalla Fuzz circuits in one enclosure with a parallel clean or clean-bass path. The design allows the fuzz, drive and clean paths to be mixed, with an A/B/Y arrangement for fuzz, drive or both. The left and right control groups operate the Valkyrie and Valhalla sections independently.

## Controls and architecture
- Valkyrie Drive side: gain, effect volume, treble, bass, depth, clipping
- Valhalla Fuzz side: gain/effect volume, tone/treble, mids, depth and clipping/voicing controls depending on version
- Center clean/clean-bass control
- A/B/Y style switching for fuzz, drive, both and bypass
- Transparent impedance-correction transformer
- True bypass
- Standard 9V negative-tip, 2.1mm supply
