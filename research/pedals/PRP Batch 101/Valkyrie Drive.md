# Blackhawk Amplifiers - Valkyrie Drive

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Blackhawk Amplifiers
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive

## Research
Valkyrie Drive is an op-amp-based overdrive designed to move between tight and woolly amp-like saturation. Blackhawk's documented circuit progression used a 741 initially, then an LM308, and later an OP07. The drive section is followed by an LM386 stage intended to produce power-amp-like saturation and a large level boost. A Baxandall bass/treble section, internal depth and gain trims, and selectable LED, no-clip or MOSFET clipping provide a wide range of low- to high-gain sounds.

## Controls and architecture
- Gain
- Effect volume
- Bass
- Treble
- Internal depth/gain trims
- Power-section clipping: LED, no clipping, or MOSFET
- True bypass
- Standard 9V negative-tip, 2.1mm supply
