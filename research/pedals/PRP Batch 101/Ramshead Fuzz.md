# Blackhawk Amplifiers - Ramshead Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Blackhawk Amplifiers
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz

## Research
The Blackhawk Ramshead is a Ram's Head / Big Muff-family fuzz voiced around warm, woolly 1970s-style distortion/fuzz tones. A documented Blackhawk build uses BC183 and BC184 transistors and adds a mid boost/scoop control so the player can move from the traditional mid shape to flatter or boosted mids. Blackhawk's circuit description also emphasizes lower-gain operation that can move toward transistor-style distortion and clean up with the guitar volume control.

## Controls
- Mid
- Tone
- Volume
- Gain

The pedal uses true bypass and a standard 9V negative-tip, 2.1mm supply.
