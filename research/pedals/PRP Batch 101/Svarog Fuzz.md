## PRP identity

## Research

Svarog is a hybrid germanium/silicon fuzz derived from the Marshall SupaFuzz / MkII Tone Bender family. The MKII V3 uses germanium NPN transistors with 2N2222 silicon transistors and adds an impedance-correction transformer plus a JFET-boosted tone stack based on Blackhawk's Pi π fuzz work. Blackhawk describes it as covering tube-amp-like fuzz, from thick vintage textures through heavier modern fuzz.

## Controls and architecture

- Transparent impedance-correction transformer
