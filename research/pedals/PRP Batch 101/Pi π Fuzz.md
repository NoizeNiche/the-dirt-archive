# Blackhawk Amplifiers - Pi π Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Blackhawk Amplifiers
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz

## Research
The Pi π Fuzz is a high-gain fuzz/distortion circuit built from cascaded transistor gain stages. Blackhawk describes it as capable of moving from heavy distortion into full fuzz, with a discrete transistor boost stage in front, a dedicated mid control, bass/depth control, and selectable clipping. The MKII adds clickless relay true bypass and a hybrid SMD/through-hole construction while retaining handwired jacks and switching.

## Controls and architecture
- Volume
- Gain
- Boost volume/gain
- Tone
- Mid
- Bass/depth
- Boost footswitch
- Diode or MOSFET clipping
- Internal Russian-style or standard triangle clipping selection
- Internal output, tone-shift and threshold trims
- Impedance-correction transformer on Deluxe variants
