# BlackOutEffectors - A Seriously Special Twosome

## PRP identity

- **Builder:** BlackOutEffectors
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz

## Research

A Seriously Special Twosome is a limited-edition dual-fuzz collaboration/run built around the Fix'd Fuzz Deluxe and Blackout Effectors' Blunderbuss circuit. Effects Database documents the Blunderbuss as a variation on the Musket fuzz with hybrid germanium/silicon clipping and a brighter, less compressed character than the Musket. The two sides can be stacked and their order can be switched, making the pedal both a pair of independent fuzz voices and a series-stacking platform.

## Features and architecture

- Fix'd Fuzz Deluxe side
- Blunderbuss side
- Stack the two circuits in either order
- True bypass
- Standard 9V operation
- Limited-edition run associated with ProGuitarShop
