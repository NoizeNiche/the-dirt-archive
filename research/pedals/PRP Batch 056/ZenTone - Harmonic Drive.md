# Analog King — ZenTone - Harmonic Drive

## What this pedal is
ZenTone is a hand-soldered overdrive inspired by Dumble-type and "Zen..." overdrive sounds. Effects Database documents a mid-cut switch, true bypass, 9 V operation, and a Burr-Brown OPA2604 op-amp. [1]

## Colorways
- Polished stainless-steel top with black-steel bottom enclosure.
- Custom magenta LED. [1]
- Complete colorway history is not established.

## Versions and factory options
- Mid-cut switch
- 9 V battery or 9 V tip-negative barrel supply
- True bypass
- Neutrik gold-plated input/output jacks
- AVX box capacitors, Nichicon audio capacitors and 1% metal-film/Dale resistors [1]

## Version changes
- No distinct production revision history established.

## Transistor
- No discrete transistor part number is documented in the source checked.
- Active op-amp: Burr-Brown OPA2604. [1]

## Diode
- ST Microelectronics BAT41
- ST Microelectronics BAT46
- Vintage glass germanium IN60 [1]

## Sound
At lower gain settings ZenTone is documented as covering jazz, country and fusion-oriented overdrive, while higher gain settings move toward smoother, sustaining Dumble-associated drive with a dynamic response. The mid-cut switch lets the player adjust the spectrum for different pickup types. [1]
