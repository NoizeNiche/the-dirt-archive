# Analog Noir — Harmonic Percolator

## What this pedal is
Analog Noir's Harmonic Percolator is a hand-wired recreation of the 1972 Harmonic Percolator using a hybrid silicon/germanium transistor design and selectable clipping modes. The builder documents Balance, Harmonics and a three-position Si/Ge/Bypass clipping switch. [1]

## Transistor
- Hybrid silicon/germanium transistor design.
- Exact production transistor part numbers are not documented. [1]

## Diode
- Pair of 1960s germanium diodes for the warm/original-style setting.
- Separate silicon clipping option and diode-bypass position.
- Exact production diode part numbers are not specified. [1]

## Sound
The pedal preserves the Harmonic Percolator's asymmetric, intermodulating response, from warm compressed lower-gain textures through fuzzier and more chaotic settings. The clipping selector changes the character substantially while keeping the underlying Percolator behavior intact. [1]
