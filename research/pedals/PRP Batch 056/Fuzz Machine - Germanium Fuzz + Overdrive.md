# Analog King — Fuzz Machine - Germanium Fuzz + Overdrive

## What this pedal is
Analog King's Fuzz Machine is a germanium fuzz based on the maker's factory circuit with an added overdrive range. Effects Database documents VOL, GATE, COMP, DRIVE and STAG controls, with DRIVE moving the pedal from fuzz toward overdrive near the end of its range. [1]

## Colorways
- Polished stainless-steel top with black-steel bottom enclosure is documented. [1]
- Complete colorway history is not established.

## Versions and factory options
- VOL, GATE, COMP, DRIVE and STAG controls
- 9 V battery or 9 V power supply
- 3PDT heavy stomp switch
- Hand-soldered construction
- AVX box capacitors, Nichicon audio capacitors and 1% metal-film resistors are documented. [1]

## Transistor
- Two hand-selected genuine germanium transistors. Exact part numbers are not documented. [1]

## Diode

## Sound
The pedal is primarily a germanium fuzz with controllable gate and compression behavior, but its DRIVE control adds a transition into overdrive. STAG sweeps the frequency emphasis of a small boosted band from bass toward treble, adding another layer of shaping to the fuzz sound. [1]
