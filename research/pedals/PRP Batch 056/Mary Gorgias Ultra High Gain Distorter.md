# Analog Music Company — Mary Gorgias Ultra High Gain Distorter

## What this pedal is
Mary Gorgias is a low-production, through-hole, hand-built crossover between fuzz and distortion with a bendable octave and active EQ. The builder documents a NOS LM308N IC and glass-cased silicon and germanium clipping diodes. [1]

## Sound
Mary Gorgias is designed to cross between extreme distortion and fuzz while adding a controllable octave layer. Its active EQ provides additional low/high shaping around the high-gain core, giving it a broader sound-design range than a simple fixed distortion circuit. [1]
