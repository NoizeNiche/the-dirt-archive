# Animals Pedal — PUSH & PULL DISTORTION LIMITED EDITION

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** PUSH & PULL DISTORTION LIMITED EDITION
- **Builder:** Animals Pedal
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** Limited graphic/finish edition of the standard PUSH & PULL DISTORTION, with the same documented distortion circuit, controls, power arrangement, and electrical specifications.
- **Edition context:** The current Animals Pedal USA listing identifies this edition as a USA Official Shop exclusive and states that it is not sold individually; it must be bundled with one of a defined set of eligible Animals Pedal products. [1]

## What this pedal is
The PUSH & PULL DISTORTION LIMITED EDITION uses the same PNPD brown-sound distortion platform as the standard PUSH & PULL DISTORTION. Animals Pedal's USA product material repeats the standard pedal's Level, Drive, Tone, true-bypass, 9V-power and electrical specifications and does not document a circuit change for the limited edition. [1][2]

## Colorway / edition
- The documented limited-edition enclosure uses a **black textured finish with blue PUSH & PULL DIST. graphics**. [1]
- The manufacturer also sells other illustrated/collaborative Push & Pull editions separately; those are not automatically assigned to this exact limited-edition record without evidence of identity or circuit differences. [1]

## Versions and factory options
- **LEVEL:** overall volume.
- **DRIVE:** distortion amount.
- **TONE:** high-frequency-centered tone control.
- **Switching:** true bypass.
- **Supply:** 9V battery or standard center-negative DC 9V adapter. [1][2]
- This limited edition is a graphic/market edition rather than a documented electronic revision.

## Version changes
No circuit, control, or power change is documented between the Limited Edition and the standard PUSH & PULL DISTORTION in the current manufacturer material. The distinguishing characteristic is the limited-edition graphic treatment and the Animals Pedal USA bundle-only sales format. [1]

## Power / electrical specifications
The USA and Japanese manufacturer pages specify:
- **Supply:** 9V battery or **center-negative DC 9V** adapter.
- **Current draw:** **0.5mA**.
- **Input impedance:** **500kΩ**.
- **Output impedance:** **<2kΩ**.
- **Switching:** true bypass.
- **Dimensions:** approximately 64 × 112 × 50mm including protrusions.
- **Weight:** approximately 381g. [1][2]

## Sound
Animals Pedal describes the standard PUSH & PULL platform as a thick early-1980s “brown sound” distortion intended to work well into clean amplifiers, with enough gain range to move from distortion toward boost/preamp use. The Limited Edition carries the same published sound/control description. [1][2]
