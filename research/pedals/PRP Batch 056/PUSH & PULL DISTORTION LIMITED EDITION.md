# Animals Pedal — PUSH & PULL DISTORTION LIMITED EDITION

## What this pedal is
The Limited Edition is a graphic/enclosure variant of the PUSH & PULL DISTORTION, retaining the documented LEVEL, DRIVE and TONE controls and the same brown-sound distortion concept. Animals Pedal USA identifies it as a limited-edition product. [1]

## Colorways
- Black textured enclosure with blue PUSH & PULL DIST. graphics is documented. [1]

## Versions and factory options
- LEVEL
- DRIVE
- TONE
- True bypass
- 9 V battery or center-minus 9 V adapter
- Limited graphic treatment [1]

## Version changes
- Cosmetic limited edition; no circuit revision is documented in the product copy. [1]

## Sound
The available documentation describes the Limited Edition using the same PNPD gain and tone architecture, so its stated range remains thick brown-sound distortion through lower-gain boost/preamp operation. [1]
