# Analog Music Company — Evil Pumpkin

## What this pedal is
Evil Pumpkin is a circuit-bent Boss DS-1-derived experimental instrument/noise machine. Its switch network connects predefined circuit points, producing fuzz, distortion, drones, glitch textures and synth-like behavior rather than a fixed distortion sound. [1]

## Colorways
- Current documentation identifies a revised double-format/tabletop enclosure.
- Complete historical colorway history is not established.

## Versions and factory options
### Earlier iterations
- Circuit-bent DS-1 platform
- True-bypass and master-volume options existed on earlier iterations. [1]

### Current double-format / Evil Pumpkin Ghazala
- Tone, Vol and Gain
- Mission Control switch matrix
- THER/LFO selector
- LFO rate control and rate indicator
- Photocell/theremin interaction
- Guitar, bass, synth and drum-machine use [1]

## Version changes
The current double-format version adds an additional block that works as a synthesizer-style patchbay for circuit connections. Analog Music Company states that true bypass was removed in the current design because it limited some fuzz and drone behaviors. [1]

## Sound
Evil Pumpkin is intentionally unpredictable and works as much like a tabletop sound-design instrument as a conventional pedal. Different switch combinations can create gated fuzz, distortion, drones, pulses from the built-in LFO and photocell-controlled oscillation. [1]

### Verified sound evidence
The crew at Analog Music Company took a nice, pretty, normal Boss DS-1 Distortion , threw it on Dr.
Each switch (12 total) has three positions, and each one of those interacts with the positions of the Tone, Level and Distortion knobs.
The results are killer distortion tones, blissed-out fuzz , droning, synth effects, tremolo and more.
