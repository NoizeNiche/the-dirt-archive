# Analog Noir — Fuzz Face / Rangemaster Treble Booster

## What this pedal is
This dual hand-wired pedal combines a Dallas Arbiter Rangemaster treble booster with a late-1960s germanium Fuzz Face recreation. The boost has a three-position EQ switch, while the fuzz adds an external Bias control. [1]

## Sound
The Rangemaster side emphasizes upper-mid and treble frequencies, while the Fuzz Face side supplies touch-sensitive germanium fuzz with different gain and cleanup characteristics depending on the selected transistor pair. Bias adds another degree of control over texture and cleanup. [1]
