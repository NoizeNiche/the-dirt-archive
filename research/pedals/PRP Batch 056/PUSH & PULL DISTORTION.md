# Animals Pedal — PUSH & PULL DISTORTION

## What this pedal is
PUSH & PULL DISTORTION (PNPD) is an analog three-control distortion voiced for the late-1970s/early-1980s brown-sound and British-amp-style gain range. Animals Pedal documents LEVEL, DRIVE and TONE controls, true bypass and 9 V battery or center-minus adapter operation. [1]

## Colorways
- White enclosure with illustrated artwork is documented for the standard current edition. [1]

## Versions and factory options
- LEVEL
- DRIVE
- TONE
- True bypass
- 9 V battery or center-minus 9 V adapter
- 64 x 112 x 50 mm including projections [1]

## Version changes
- Standard current production edition.
- Animals Pedal also offers separate limited graphic editions using the same PNPD control architecture. [1][2][3]

## Sound
Animals Pedal describes the PNPD as thick and articulate, retaining chord definition under heavy gain while also working as a lower-gain booster or preamp when Drive is reduced and Level is increased. [1]
