# Animals Pedal — PUSH & PULL DISTORTION LIMITED EDITION C

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** PUSH & PULL DISTORTION LIMITED EDITION C
- **Builder:** Animals Pedal
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** Distinct limited-edition graphic release of the standard PUSH & PULL DISTORTION (PNPD), with no documented circuit change.
- **Release context:** Animals Pedal's Japan site announced the edition on **April 14, 2026**. The USA official shop lists it as a limited edition that cannot be purchased individually and must be bundled with one of the eligible standard pedals. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
Limited Edition C uses the same documented PNPD platform as the standard PUSH & PULL DISTORTION: LEVEL, DRIVE and TONE controls, true bypass, 9V battery/center-negative 9V DC power, and the same published electrical specification. The distinguishing element is the C-series graphic/enclosure treatment and bundled sales format, not a documented circuit revision. [1][3]

## Power / electrical specifications
Manufacturer documentation for the standard PNPD, repeated on the Limited Edition C page, specifies:
- **Supply:** 9V battery or standard **center-negative DC 9V** adapter.
- **Current draw:** **0.5mA**.
- **Input impedance:** **500kΩ**.
- **Output impedance:** **<2kΩ**.
- Battery guidance recommends unplugging the input jack during storage. [1][3]
