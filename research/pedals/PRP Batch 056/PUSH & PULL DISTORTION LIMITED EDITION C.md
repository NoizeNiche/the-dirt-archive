# Animals Pedal — PUSH & PULL DISTORTION LIMITED EDITION C

## What this pedal is
Limited Edition C is a distinct graphic release of the PUSH & PULL DISTORTION sold through the Animals Pedal USA shop. Its product page retains the PNPD's LEVEL, DRIVE and TONE architecture. [1]

## Colorways

## Versions and factory options
- LEVEL
- DRIVE
- TONE
- True bypass
- 9 V battery or center-minus 9 V adapter
