# Analog Noir — Si / Ge Fuzz Face

## What this pedal is
Analog Noir's Si / Ge Fuzz Face is a hand-wired Fuzz Face recreation offered in both germanium and silicon forms, with an added external Bias control. [1]

## Colorways
- Complete production finish history is not established.

## Versions and factory options
### Germanium
- Mullard GET / 1974 Texas Instruments
- New Market NKT / Mullard GET

### Silicon
- Mullard BC108
- Phillips BC109

### Controls
- Volume
- Fuzz
- Bias [1]

- Internal battery snap
- 9 V DC jack
- Vintage PNP transistor configurations require an isolated supply
- Less than 10 mA current draw [1]

## Version changes
The principal factory distinction is the germanium versus silicon transistor option. Bias is an external added control intended to tune texture and cleanup compared with a fixed Fuzz Face configuration. [1]

## Transistor
- Mullard GET / 1974 Texas Instruments
- New Market NKT / Mullard GET
- Mullard BC108
- Phillips BC109 [1]

## Diode
- Exact clipping diode/device is not publicly documented.

## Sound
The germanium options are described as warmer, more touch-sensitive and more cleanable, while the silicon choices move toward thicker saturation, brighter attack and higher gain. Bias provides additional control of texture and cleanup. [1]
