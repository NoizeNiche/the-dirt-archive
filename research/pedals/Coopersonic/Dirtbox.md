# Coopersonic - Dirtbox

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Coopersonic
- **Catalog identity:** Dirtbox
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Fuzz
- **Identity:** Hand-built Nottingham, England Coopersonic transistor overdrive/distortion pedal with strong fuzz range.

## What this pedal is
Effects Database describes the Dirtbox as a Coopersonic distortion/overdrive pedal and notes that the design extends into intense fuzz at high dirt settings. [1]
The record dates to November 20, 2009 and documents the unit as a handmade Nottingham, England pedal. [1]

## Controls
The checked public source specifically documents a **Dirt** control but does not provide a reliable complete external control list. [1]

## Versions and factory options
- **Power:** 9V battery supplied or 9V DC power supply. [1]
- **Size:** 120 × 70 × 50 mm. [1]
- **Construction:** hand-made in Nottingham, England. [1]
- The surviving description identifies a yellow enclosure. [1]

## Construction
