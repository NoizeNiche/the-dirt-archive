# Coopersonic - Valveslapper

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Coopersonic
- **Catalog identity:** Valveslapper
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive
- **Identity:** Historical name for the Coopersonic Dual Valve Distortion.

## What this pedal is
Coopersonic's Dual Valve Distortion manual identifies the model as **“previously known as the Valveslapper.”** The manual describes two high-quality tubes running at high voltage, with a wide range from subtle overdrive through extreme fuzz-like distortion. [1]

## Controls
The manufacturer manual documents a **powerful two-band EQ** but the checked text does not expose the complete control names. [1]

## Versions and factory options
- Historical/predecessor naming retained as a separate catalog identity.
- Two high-voltage valves. [1]
- True bypass. [1]
- Rugged diecast enclosure. [1]
- Neutrik jacks. [1]
- International power supply included. [1]

## Version changes
The name transitioned from Valveslapper to Dual Valve Distortion. The archive retains Valveslapper as a historical catalog identity rather than erasing the name from surviving units. [1]

## Construction
Hand-built in Nottingham, England, with two valves operating at high voltage. [1]

## Transistor
- **Principal gain devices:** vacuum valves.
- Exact supporting semiconductor device list not documented.
## Diode
- **Exact clipping/rectification diode/device:** Not publicly documented.

## Sound
The manufacturer manual describes a range from subtle bluesy crunch through paint-stripping fuzz. [1]
