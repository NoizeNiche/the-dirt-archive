# Coopersonic - Deluxe Dual Valve Overdrive

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Coopersonic
- **Catalog identity:** Deluxe Dual Valve Overdrive
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive / Distortion
- **Identity:** Historical Coopersonic deluxe dual-valve model documented by Effects Database and later superseded by the Deluxe Dual-Valve Distortion naming.

## What this pedal is
Effects Database lists **Coopersonic Deluxe Dual Valve Overdrive** as a distinct product in its 12-product Coopersonic catalog and dates its addition to the database to June 25, 2012. [1]
Coopersonic's current site explains that the later **Deluxe Dual-Valve Distortion** replaced the earlier **Deluxe Valve Overdrive**, using vintage Mullard ECC81 valves and component changes. [2]
The archive preserves the exact historical catalog identity used by Effects Database rather than merging it with the later product name.
