# Coopersonic - The Germaniac - Vintage Fuzz

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Coopersonic
- **Catalog identity:** The Germaniac - Vintage Fuzz
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Vintage-style germanium fuzz with a Mania control for positive-feedback instability and self-oscillation.

## What this pedal is
Effects Database documents the Germaniac as a vintage-style germanium fuzz using NOS germanium transistors. The page describes a basic two-knob fuzz behavior at minimum Mania, with the added Mania control increasing instability toward self-oscillation. [1]
Coopersonic's current product page confirms the same core concept, describing the Mania control as a positive-feedback system that can create unstable, self-oscillating and octave-jumping tones. [2]

## Controls
The documented controls are:
- **Level**
- **Fuzz**
- **Mania** [1][2]

## Versions and factory options
- **Transistors:** NOS germanium transistors. [1]
- **Switching:** true bypass. [1][2]
- **Power:** 9V battery or Boss-type 9V adapter, center negative. [1][2]
- **Enclosure:** diecast metal case. [2]
- **Construction:** hand-built. [2]

## Sound
At minimum Mania the pedal behaves as a vintage two-knob fuzz. Increasing Mania introduces progressively greater instability, including self-oscillation and octave-jumping behavior. [1][2]
