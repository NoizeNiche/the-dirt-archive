# Coopersonic — Dual-Valve Distortion (Deluxe)

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Coopersonic

- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** Deluxe version of Coopersonic's Dual-Valve Distortion.

## What this pedal is
The Deluxe Dual-Valve Distortion uses vintage Mullard ECC81 valves and component changes relative to the regular Dual-Valve Distortion. Coopersonic states that it replaces the former Deluxe Valve Overdrive.

## Versions and factory options
- **Valves:** vintage Mullard ECC81.
- Two-valve, high-voltage design.
- True bypass.
- Die-cast metal enclosure.
- High-brightness pink LED.
- Coopersonic's valve-pedal FAQ specifies 12V DC, 500mA, centre-positive operation.

## Version changes
- The Deluxe is a distinct version from the regular Dual-Valve Distortion.
- It also replaces the former Deluxe Valve Overdrive.

## Sound
Coopersonic describes the Deluxe as smoother and subtler than the regular version, with somewhat less gain while retaining substantial distortion capability.
