# Coopersonic - Dual-Valve Distortion (Deluxe)

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Coopersonic
- **Catalog identity:** Dual-Valve Distortion (Deluxe)
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** Deluxe version of Coopersonic's Dual-Valve Distortion, using vintage Mullard ECC81 valves and component changes relative to the regular version.

## What this pedal is
Coopersonic documents the Deluxe Dual-Valve Distortion as a distinct version of the Dual-Valve Distortion. It replaces the former Deluxe Valve Overdrive and uses vintage Mullard ECC81 valves with component changes that produce a subtler, smoother driven response with less gain than the regular version. [1]

## Controls
The checked manufacturer page confirms the two-band EQ architecture of the family but does not expose a complete control list. [1]

## Versions and factory options
- **Valves:** vintage Mullard ECC81. [1]
- **Power:** international AC adapter supplied; Coopersonic's FAQ specifies 12V DC, 500mA, centre-positive for valve pedals. [2]
- **Switching:** true bypass. [1]
- **Enclosure:** diecast metal case. [1]
- **Indicator:** high-brightness pink LED. [1]

## Version changes
The Deluxe is a separate catalog version and explicitly replaces the earlier Deluxe Valve Overdrive. [1]

## Construction
Two valves operate at high voltage in a hand-built Coopersonic pedal design. The public material does not supply a complete component-level schematic. [1][3]

## Transistor
- **Principal gain devices:** vacuum valves.
- Exact supporting semiconductor device list not documented.

## Diode
- **Exact clipping/rectification diode/device:** Not publicly documented.

## Sound
Coopersonic describes the Deluxe as subtler and smoother than the regular version, with somewhat less gain while retaining very high-gain distortion capability. [1]
