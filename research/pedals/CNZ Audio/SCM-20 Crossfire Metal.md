# CNZ Audio — SCM-20 Crossfire Metal

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** SCM-20 Crossfire Metal
- **Builder:** CNZ Audio
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** Compact analog metal distortion with Volume, Distortion, Tone and three-position Hi-Boost/Boost-Off/Lo-Boost switching. [1][2]

## Controls
- **VOLUME**
- **DISTORTION**
- **TONE**
- **HI-BOOST / BOOST-OFF / LO-BOOST** toggle. [1][2]

## Circuit / topology
- Analog high-gain distortion.
- The boost toggle shifts the emphasis around the core distortion rather than simply selecting a different pedal.
- The design is related in layout to CNZ's GDS-20 but substitutes Distortion for Gain and turns the toggle into an adjustable boost system. [1]

## Power / electrical
- **9V DC, center-negative**.
- Approximately **10 mA** listed in the product specification, while one exact-model retail record also reports **25 mA working current**. The archive preserves the discrepancy rather than choosing one value without a reason. [1][2]
- **1 MOhm input impedance**
- **470 Ohm output impedance**.
- Solid-aluminum mini enclosure. [1][2]

## Sound
CNZ markets Crossfire Metal for hard rock and metal, emphasizing long sustain, dense saturation and a full tone. The three-position boost switch provides upper, neutral or lower-frequency emphasis. [1][2]
