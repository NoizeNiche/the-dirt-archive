## What this pedal is
Featuring a new hybrid of Class-A discrete FET design, the Looking Glass’ asymmetrical clipping is so responsive that it can go from one end of the spectrum to the other with not only a flick of a switch, but with pick attack alone.The Looking Glass Overdrive is collaboration between DOD and the boutique pedal company SHOE Pedals.
