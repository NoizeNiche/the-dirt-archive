## Sound

The **DOD FX86 Death Metal Distortion** is a fixed-gain, high-gain distortion pedal built around a four-control interface: **R.I.P., GUTS, PAIN, and SCREAM**.
The DOD owner-manual material explains that the circuit has no conventional distortion/gain control.
Instead, the distortion stage is fixed at a very high setting and the front-panel controls shape output level and the three main EQ bands.
