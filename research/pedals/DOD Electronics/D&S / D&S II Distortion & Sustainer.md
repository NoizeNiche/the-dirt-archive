# DOD Electronics — D&S / D&S II Distortion & Sustainer

## Surface catalog record
- **Builder:** DOD Electronics
- **Pedal:** D&S / D&S II Distortion & Sustainer
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Research level:** Deep
- **Deep research status:** Identity conflict flagged
- **Identity basis:** The exact catalog identity currently present in The Dirt Archive could not be corroborated as a DOD Electronics model from the sources reviewed. The strongest exact-model evidence identifies **Maxon** D&S and **Maxon OD-802 D&S II** products. This record therefore documents the conflict instead of transferring Maxon specifications onto the DOD entry.

## Identity finding
The available evidence consistently associates **D&S Distortion & Sustainer** and **D&S II Distortion & Sustainer II** with **Maxon**, not DOD.

Effects Database identifies the **Maxon OD-802 D&S II** as a Maxon 808-series distortion/sustain pedal and dates the D&S II to **1974**. Maxon's own current reissue product page calls the model **DISTORTION AND SUSTAINER II (D&S II)** and identifies it as a Maxon pedal. [1][2]

A separate manual for the D&S II identifies controls for **Distortion, Tone, and Balance**, plus Normal/Effect switching and 9V battery/adapter operation. The same manual identifies the pedal as D&S II Distortion & Sustainer II. [3]

Searches for an exact DOD-branded D&S or D&S II pedal did not produce comparable first-party DOD documentation. The current DigiTech/DOD product-manual index includes many DOD products but did not surface a DOD D&S/D&S II manual in the reviewed results. [4]

## What can be documented about the Maxon identity
Because the archive record currently says DOD Electronics, the following information is **evidence about the Maxon model family only** and should not be rendered on this DOD entry as settled DOD data until the catalog identity is corrected or independently corroborated.

### Maxon D&S II
- **Manufacturer:** Maxon
- **Model:** D&S II / OD-802
- **Catalog family:** Maxon 808 series
- **Type:** Distortion / sustainer
- **Controls:** Distortion, Tone, Balance
- **Circuit description:** Maxon describes a dual-op-amp design related to the OD808, with increased low-end, output, and gain. [1][2]
- **Current draw:** Maxon's reissue specification lists 6 mA at 9V battery operation and 7 mA at 10V adapter operation. [2]
- **Power:** 9V battery or special AC adapter; Maxon's reissue specification lists DC9V battery operation and DC10V adapter operation. [2]
- **Dimensions:** 61 mm × 112 mm × 35 mm main body; 70 mm × 112 mm × 55 mm overall in the current Maxon reissue specification. [2]
- **Weight:** 260 g in Maxon's current reissue specification. [2]

Historical/technical sources further describe the D&S II as using a diode-clipping stage and a tone circuit with added low-frequency control; some secondary sources identify germanium clipping and different op-amp implementations across vintage examples. Those component claims vary by version and source, so they should not be normalized into one universal D&S II circuit without a specific unit or schematic. [1][5][6]

## Catalog correction recommendation
**Do not silently relabel this record from DOD Electronics to Maxon inside this research file.** The catalog record itself needs an identity decision because:
1. The exact name points strongly to Maxon.
2. Maxon has both D&S and D&S II products with this naming.
3. The archive currently combines them into one DOD Electronics record.

A future catalog-maintenance pass should verify the original source that created the current DOD record. If no DOD-specific evidence is found, the record should be split or relinked to the appropriate Maxon historical products rather than preserving a manufacturer/model mismatch.
