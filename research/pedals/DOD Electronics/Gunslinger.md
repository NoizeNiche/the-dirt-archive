## What this pedal is
The **DOD Gunslinger** is a MOSFET-based dirt pedal from DOD's modern/revival-era catalog. DOD's product material explicitly characterizes it as an **aggressive MOSFET distortion**. [1]

The archive retains the existing **Distortion / Overdrive** catalog classification because the product's documented operating range includes overdrive-style use while its manufacturer identity centers on distortion.

## Technical evidence boundary
The reviewed DOD material supports the MOSFET architecture at the product-description level but does not establish a complete production schematic or universal component BOM.

The archive therefore does not invent transistor part numbers, clipping-diode inventories, or revision-specific component values.

## Historical/catalog context
Gunslinger belongs to DOD's later pedal catalog and is separate from the historical FX-series models such as FX59, FX69B, and FX86. The archive keeps those as distinct model identities rather than merging them solely because they occupy overlapping high-gain territory. [2]
