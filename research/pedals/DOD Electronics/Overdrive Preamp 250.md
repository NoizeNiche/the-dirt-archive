## What this pedal is

The DOD Overdrive Preamp 250 is the classic two-control DOD overdrive/preamp design. Effects Database documents the original family and later reissues, while current DigiTech documentation describes modern 250 descendants using the same core lineage.

## Colorways

- Historic DOD 250 units are documented in multiple enclosure finishes across production and reissue periods.
- No complete factory colorway chronology is asserted for this family record.

## Versions and factory options

- **Original Overdrive Preamp 250:** the early DOD design with Gain and Level controls.
- **2013 reissue:** adds true bypass, blue LED, modern 9V DC input, and an aluminum chassis.
- **250-50th Anniversary:** limited-edition 2024 model with expanded clipping options.
- **250-X:** later production version with Silicon, Asym, and Lift clipping modes.
- Version-specific controls and component details should not be merged into every 250 record.

## Sound

The 250 is a relatively simple gain-and-level overdrive/preamp design that can provide light clipping, thicker overdrive, or a strong boost by raising Level with lower Gain settings. Effects Database documents it as closely related to the early DOD overdrive lineage and notes the different feel of historical versions.
