## What this pedal is
The **DOD FX33 Buzz Box** is a hybrid dirt/octave effect that combines a heavily distorted signal with a signal divided **two octaves down**.

## Colorways
- an **MXR Blue Box-style two-octave-down divider**.
- Reverb's exact-model reference summarizes the design as replacing the RAT stage of the original Melvins-associated rig with DOD's FX69 Grunge circuit while retaining the Blue Box-style octave function.
- Reverb's exact-model catalog identifies the FX33 as a yellow-finish DOD pedal and dates examples to 1994–1996.

## Sound
DOD's 1994 manual describes the pedal as a distortion unit with a built-in octavizer that takes the distortion signal and drops it two octaves.
**Buzz:** controls distortion/fuzz gain.
The original DOD manual identifies the pedal as inspired by the guitar sound associated with **King Buzzo of the Melvins**, specifically the combination of aggressive distortion and octave-divider effects.
