## What this pedal is
The **DOD FX58 Metal Maniac** is a historical DOD FX-series **Distortion** pedal. DOD's historical reconciliation explicitly keeps FX58 distinct from later high-gain models such as FX70 and FX86 even though those products occupied overlapping heavy-distortion territory. [1]

The manufacturer's preserved product-manual index and DOD historical catalog work support the exact FX58 / Metal Maniac identity. [1][2]

## Historical context
FX58 is part of DOD's historical FX-series dirt catalog. The archive keeps the named product identity rather than treating model numbers as interchangeable placeholders.

The reviewed evidence does not establish a complete production run, numbered revision sequence, or universal circuit BOM. Those remain open research targets.
