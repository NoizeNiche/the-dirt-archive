## Sound
The **DOD FX101 Grind Rectifying Overdrive** is a late-1990s DOD dirt pedal designed to create a modern, high-gain **rectifier-style** sound.
DOD's manual describes a special circuit that adds even harmonics to emulate an overdriven tube response and then rectifies the signal to produce what the manufacturer calls a modern “rectifier crunch” sound.
Although DOD classified it as an **overdrive**, the documented gain structure and contemporary descriptions place it toward high-gain distortion territory.
