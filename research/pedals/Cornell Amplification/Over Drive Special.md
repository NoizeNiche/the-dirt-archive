# Cornell Amplification - Over Drive Special

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Cornell Amplification
- **Catalog identity:** Over Drive Special
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Exact Cornell Over Drive Special pedal.

## What this pedal is
The Cornell Over Drive Special is a flexible overdrive designed to cover clean boost, crunch, and higher-gain distortion. Cornell's published material describes two gain channels plus an independent boost function.

## Controls
- **Gain 1:** increases guitar-signal gain and distortion.
- **Gain 2:** provides more gain and a darker character than Gain 1.
- **Output:** sets overall output level.
- **Tone:** cuts treble for a smoother high-gain sound.

## Switching
- **Channel:** selects Gain 1 or Gain 2.
- **Boost:** adds additional signal after the gain stage.
- **Bypass:** true bypass.
- LEDs indicate operation, channel position, and boost state.

## Power
- PP3 9V battery.
- Standard 9V DC supply, 300mA.
- The operation guide specifies **center negative** polarity.

## Sound
Channel 1 can be used as a clean boost, while Channel 2 gives a crunchier voice with more available gain. Turning up either gain control increases distortion, and the Boost function can be added at any setting for extra level and drive.
