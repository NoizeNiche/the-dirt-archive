# Cornell Amplification - The 1st Fuzz

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Cornell Amplification
- **Catalog identity:** The 1st Fuzz
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Exact Cornell The 1st Fuzz.

## What this pedal is
The 1st Fuzz is Cornell's germanium fuzz built around the sound of early-1960s fuzz circuits. Cornell selected germanium transistors to its own parameters and chose the NKT 275 type for its preferred fuzz character.

## Controls
- **Volume:** output level.
- **Fuzz:** fuzz intensity.
- **Bias:** internal bias control used to compensate for drift in germanium transistor behavior.

## Circuit and components
- Germanium transistors.
- Cornell-selected transistor characteristics.
- NKT 275 is the documented preferred transistor type.
- Carbon Composition resistors, following the construction approach of early germanium Fuzz Face-style pedals.

## Research notes
Cornell explicitly added the bias adjustment because germanium transistor behavior can drift with time and temperature. Do not treat the NKT 275 reference as proof that every individual unit contains an identical original-production transistor.

## History and construction
The pedal is based on early-1960s germanium fuzz practice and was documented by Effects Database in 2008. The model is discontinued in later retailer references.
