# Baroni Lab - Moon Sound

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Moon Sound
- **Builder:** Baroni Lab
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** Analog distortion designed around David Gilmour / Dark Side of the Moon-style lead and rhythm tones.

## What this pedal is
Moon Sound is a Baroni Lab distortion built to recreate the character of David Gilmour's lead tone from Pink Floyd's *The Dark Side of the Moon*. Baroni Lab's own writing describes using the pedal to target the "Money" solo and notes that pickup type changes the required settings.

## Sound
Moon Sound is voiced for smooth, singing distortion as well as stronger rhythm work, with a boost stage intended to help lead tones stand out. Baroni Lab specifically documented using it to reproduce the lead character of Gilmour's "Money" solo and noted that active pickups require different settings from passive pickups.
