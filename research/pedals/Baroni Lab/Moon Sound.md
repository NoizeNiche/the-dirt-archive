## What this pedal is

Baroni Lab describes Moon Sound as a distortion stompbox designed to reproduce David Gilmour's lead character from Pink Floyd's *The Dark Side of the Moon*. The builder specifically developed settings for the “Money” solo and documented that active and passive pickups require different setup choices. [1]

## Sound

Moon Sound is aimed at smooth, singing lead distortion and stronger rhythm use, with Boost providing additional level/drive. Baroni Lab's own tone article says the pedal was set up specifically to reproduce the “Money” solo and had to be adjusted differently for active versus passive pickups. [1][2]
This is a builder-target description, not a measurement of Gilmour's original recording chain.
