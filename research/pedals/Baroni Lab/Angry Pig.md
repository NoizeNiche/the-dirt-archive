# Baroni Lab — Angry Pig

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Angry Pig
- **Builder:** Baroni Lab
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** Warm, fuzzy 1970s-style distortion with Drive, Tone, Volume and a switchable mid boost, using a specially selected transistor to alter the low-frequency response. [1]

## What this pedal is
Baroni Lab presents Angry Pig as a warm, fuzzy distortion based around a specially chosen transistor. The builder says the selected device naturally reduces some low-frequency content, while the circuit's Tone, Drive and mid-boost controls provide additional shaping. [1]

## Controls
- **DRIVE**
- **TONE**
- **VOLUME**
- **MID BOOST** switch
- Footswitch/bypass. [1]
