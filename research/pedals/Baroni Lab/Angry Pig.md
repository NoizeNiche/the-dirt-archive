# Baroni Lab - Angry Pig

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Angry Pig
- **Builder:** Baroni Lab
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** Warm, fuzzy 1970s-style distortion using a specially selected transistor and a mid-boost switch.

## What this pedal is
Angry Pig is a lead-oriented distortion voiced around a selected transistor that naturally cuts some lower guitar frequencies. Baroni Lab pairs the resulting sharper distortion with Drive, Tone, Volume, and a switchable mid boost.

## Sound
The circuit is described as a warm, fuzzy 1970s-style distortion that becomes sharper through the selected transistor's low-frequency reduction. The responsive Tone and Drive controls, plus the mid-boost switch, cover both tighter rhythm-oriented distortion and more forward lead sounds.
