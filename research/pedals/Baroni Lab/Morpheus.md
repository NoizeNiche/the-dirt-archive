# Baroni Lab - Morpheus

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Morpheus
- **Builder:** Baroni Lab
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** Multi-control analog distortion/drive covering heavy-metal-style gain through warm 1970s overdrive.

## What this pedal is
Morpheus is a Baroni Lab distortion with a broad gain range and separate boost, bass-contour, and mid-boost functions. The builder describes it as using classic vintage analog methodology.

## Sound
The Tone and Bass Contour controls span from colder metallic distortion into a fatter, warmer overdriven sound. Boost can thicken the drive or push it toward saturation, while the Mid Boost function raises presence without requiring an overall volume increase.
