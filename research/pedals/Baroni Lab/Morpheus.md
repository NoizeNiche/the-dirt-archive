# Baroni Lab — Morpheus

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Morpheus
- **Builder:** Baroni Lab
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive
- **Identity:** Six-control analog drive/distortion with Gain, Tone, Volume, Boost, Bass Contour and Mid Boost, intended to span cold heavy-metal dirt through fat 1970s overdrive. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
Baroni Lab describes Morpheus as a large-range distortion/drive using classic vintage analog methodology. Tone and Bass Contour work together to move the response from colder metallic distortion toward thicker, warmer overdrive, while Boost and Mid Boost add gain or mid presence. [1][2]

## Controls
- **GAIN**
- **TONE**
- **VOLUME**
- **BOOST:** Adds additional drive/saturation.
- **BASS CONTOUR:** Shapes low-frequency character.
- **MID BOOST:** Adds mid presence without relying on overall volume. [1][2]
