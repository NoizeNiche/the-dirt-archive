# Baroni Lab - Rat'N Box

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Rat'N Box
- **Builder:** Baroni Lab
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** Boutique distortion inspired by the classic RAT family of crunchy drive and trebly fuzz-distortion tones.

## What this pedal is
Rat'N Box is Baroni Lab's boutique take on the RAT-style distortion sound. Baroni's description emphasizes crunchy mild drive, trebly fuzz-like distortion, and a grittier hard-rock voice.

## Sound
The Rat'N Box targets the crunchy, gritty side of RAT-style distortion, with enough gain to move into trebly fuzz-distortion territory. Baroni Lab specifically positions it for hard-rock riffs and aggressive rhythm playing.
