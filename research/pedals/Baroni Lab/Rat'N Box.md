# Baroni Lab — Rat'N Box

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Rat'N Box

- **Identity:** Boutique RAT-style distortion with Distortion, Filter and Volume controls, designed for crunchy mild drive through trebly fuzz-distortion. [1]

## What this pedal is
Baroni Lab explicitly presents the Rat'N Box as a boutique take on the classic RAT tone. Its target range includes crunchy mild drive and trebly fuzz-distortion, with enough grit for hard-rock riffing. [1]
