# Baroni Lab — GD Drive

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** GD Drive
- **Builder:** Baroni Lab
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Germanium overdrive/distortion using specially selected and matched germanium components to produce natural paired harmonics. [1][2]

## What this pedal is

Baroni Lab positions GD Drive between overdrive and distortion. The builder says the germanium components are selected and matched and placed in the circuit for a sweet, creamy response with natural paired harmonics. [1]

## Controls

- **DRIVE**
- **TONE**
- **VOLUME**
- Footswitch. [1][2]

- **Technology:** Germanium.

## Version / naming notes

- Surviving records use **GD Drive** and **GD Bender Germanium Drive** naming.

## Sound

The builder describes a sweet, creamy distortion thick with natural paired harmonics, usable with both single coils and humbuckers. The pedal is intended to cover softer drive through more saturated lead tones. [1][2]
