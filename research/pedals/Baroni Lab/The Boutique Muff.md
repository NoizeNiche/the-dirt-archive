# Baroni Lab - The Boutique Muff

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** The Boutique Muff
- **Builder:** Baroni Lab
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** Muff-style distortion centered on thick, singing saturation with a mid-boost option.

## What this pedal is
The Boutique Muff is Baroni Lab's interpretation of the classic Muff distortion family. The builder emphasizes raw, singing saturation and a broad drive range, with a mid-boost switch for extra presence.

## Sound
The Boutique Muff is voiced for thick, creamy saturation that can push a clean amp toward a hot-tube-amp character. The mid-boost switch adds a focused mid lift so the distortion can remain prominent in a mix without simply increasing output level.
