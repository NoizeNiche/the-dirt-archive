# Baroni Lab — The Boutique Muff

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** The Boutique Muff
- **Builder:** Baroni Lab
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Fuzz
- **Identity:** Muff-style distortion with Drive, Tone, Volume and a dedicated Mid Boost switch, built as a boutique analog interpretation of the Muff family. [1][2]

## What this pedal is

Baroni Lab describes The Boutique Muff as a boutique Muff-style distortion designed for thick, saturated guitar tones. The builder's description emphasizes the Muff family while adding a Mid Boost circuit to make lead sounds more forward. [1]

## Controls

- **DRIVE**
- **TONE**
- **VOLUME**
- **MID BOOST** switch
- Footswitch/bypass. [1]
