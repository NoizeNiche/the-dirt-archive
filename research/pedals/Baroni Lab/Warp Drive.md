# Baroni Lab - Warp Drive

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Warp Drive
- **Builder:** Baroni Lab
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Germanium overdrive with a warm vintage voice and reduced-noise fuzz character.

## What this pedal is
Warp Drive is a Baroni Lab overdrive built with vintage germanium components. The builder describes a range from light crunch through lead guitar, with a sweet fuzz component and reduced noise.

## Colorways
- A documented 2019 example has a **multi-color burst** finish.
- Another documented example uses the colorful Warp artwork on a black enclosure.
- No complete factory colorway chronology was verified.

## Sound
Warp Drive covers light crunch through full-on lead tones and adds a sweet fuzz edge without abandoning the overdrive identity. Baroni Lab emphasizes a warm, rich vintage response and reduced noise compared with the rawer fuzz character associated with old germanium circuits.
