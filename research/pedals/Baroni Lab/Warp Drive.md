# Baroni Lab — Warp Drive

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Warp Drive
- **Builder:** Baroni Lab
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive / Distortion
- **Identity:** Germanium overdrive/distortion using vintage germanium components, with Drive, Tone and Volume controls. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
Baroni Lab and surviving dealer records describe Warp Drive as a warm, rich vintage-style germanium drive covering light crunch through full-on lead guitar. The design is also described as delivering a sweet fuzz component with reduced noise relative to rawer germanium fuzz textures. [1][2]

## Sound
Warp Drive is positioned between mild crunch and saturated lead overdrive, with a warm and rich vintage response and a sweet fuzz edge. The builder/retailer description emphasizes usefulness in rock contexts and the ability to retain lower noise than some raw vintage-germanium designs. [1][2]
