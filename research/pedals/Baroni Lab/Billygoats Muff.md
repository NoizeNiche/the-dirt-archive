## What this pedal is
BillyGoats Muff is Baroni Lab's Muff-style distortion, designed around broad drive and tone control and a dedicated mid-boost circuit. The company specifies a relay-controlled buffered bypass and 12V DC operation.

## Sound
Baroni Lab describes the pedal as covering tones from dark and mysterious through bright and punchy, with a versatile Tone control and broad drive range. The switchable mid boost adds a more forward voice for lead passages without replacing the underlying Muff-style character.
