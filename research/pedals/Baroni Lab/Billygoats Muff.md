# Baroni Lab — Billygoats Muff

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Billygoats Muff
- **Builder:** Baroni Lab
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Fuzz
- **Identity:** Boutique Muff-style distortion with Drive, Tone, Volume and a dedicated Mid Boost switch, using a relay-controlled buffered bypass. [1]

## What this pedal is
Baroni Lab calls the BillyGoats Muff a boutique Muff-style distortion. Its design emphasizes a broad tonal range from dark/mysterious to bright/punchy, with a mid-boost circuit intended to make lead sounds more prominent. [1]

## Controls
- **DRIVE**
- **TONE**
- **VOLUME**
- **MID BOOST** switch
- Relay-controlled bypass. [1]

## Sound
Baroni Lab describes the pedal as ranging from dark and mysterious to bright and punchy, with a precise Tone control and broad drive range. Mid Boost adds a more forward lead voice without redefining the underlying Muff-style character. [1]
