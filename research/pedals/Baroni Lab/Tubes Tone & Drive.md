# Baroni Lab - Tubes Tone & Drive

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Tubes Tone & Drive
- **Builder:** Baroni Lab
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Tube-equipped overdrive using an internal 12AX7 valve.

## What this pedal is
Tubes Tone & Drive is a tube overdrive pedal built around an internal 12AX7. Dedicated bass and treble controls shape the frequency response, while Boost Drive provides an additional gain/drive control.

## Sound
Baroni Lab describes the pedal as producing thick overdrive that can drive a tube amplifier into more natural breakup or add richer, deeper drive in front of other distortion pedals. The dedicated bass and treble controls give it a wider shaping range than a simple single-tone tube drive.
