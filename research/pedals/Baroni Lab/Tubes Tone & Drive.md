# Baroni Lab — Tubes Tone & Drive

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Tubes Tone & Drive
- **Builder:** Baroni Lab
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Tube-equipped overdrive using an internal 12AX7, with independent Boost Drive, Bass, Treble and Volume controls. [1]

## What this pedal is
Baroni Lab describes Tubes Tone & Drive as a thick tube overdrive designed around an internal **12AX7**. The Bass and Treble controls provide dedicated low/high-frequency shaping, while Boost Drive provides additional gain/drive control. [1]

## Sound
The internal 12AX7 is used as the central tube gain element for thick overdrive. Baroni Lab says the pedal can drive a tube amplifier into natural overdrive or pair with other distortion pedals for richer, deeper drive, while Bass/Treble allow wider shaping than a simple single-tone drive. [1]
