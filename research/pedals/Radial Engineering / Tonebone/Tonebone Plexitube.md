## What this pedal is
The Tonebone Plexitube is a two-channel tube distortion pedal built around a 12AX7 tube and a multi-stage gain circuit. Radial's own manual describes it as a British-voiced distortion derived from the earlier Hot British concept, with separate channels intended to cover clean, rhythm, and lead applications.

## Sound
The Plexitube is designed around a thick British amplifier-style distortion response, with multiple gain stages and a tube stage used to add harmonics and warmth. Radial documents the pedal as a two-channel design with a mix function that can retain clean low frequencies, making the circuit useful for fuller blended distortion sounds as well as conventional guitar drive.
