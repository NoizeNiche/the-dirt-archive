## What this pedal is
The Tonebone Plexitube is a two-channel tube distortion pedal built around a **12AX7** tube and a multi-stage gain circuit. Radial positions it as a British-voiced distortion design descended from the Tonebone Hot British concept.

## Sound
Plexitube is built around a tube-based gain stage intended to deliver British-style distortion, from lower-gain rhythm sounds into more saturated lead textures. Its two-channel design makes the pedal useful for storing separate drive settings rather than relying on a single fixed gain voice.
