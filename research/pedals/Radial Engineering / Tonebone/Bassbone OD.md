## What this pedal is
The Bassbone OD is a two-channel instrument preamp designed around bass and other instruments, with a built-in overdrive section and an effects loop. Radial documents it as a flexible unit for switching between two basses or pickups, combining magnetic and piezo sources, and feeding both a stage amplifier and a balanced PA output.

## Versions and factory options
- Two input channels with independent tone shaping.
- Built-in overdrive with **Drive**, **Mix**, and **Tone** controls.
- Footswitch assignment can operate the overdrive, effects loop, or both.
- **Mid** selector provides 250 Hz, 500 Hz, or 1 kHz emphasis for Channel B.
- Dedicated tuner output, headphone output, balanced XLR output, and effects loop.
- Radial specifies a **15 VDC / 400 mA** supply.

## Sound
The Bassbone OD combines clean bass preamp duties with a controllable overdrive section whose Mix control blends the original instrument signal with the distorted path. Its two-channel architecture and filtering let players keep the low-end foundation intact while using the overdrive as a separately shaped layer.
