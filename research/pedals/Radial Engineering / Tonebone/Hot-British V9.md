# Radial Engineering / Tonebone — Hot-British V9

## PRP identity

## What this pedal is
The Hot-British V9 is the solid-state successor to the earlier **Tonebone Hot British** distortion. A curated historical equipment record dates the V9 to **2017** and identifies the earlier Hot British V1 as spanning **2008-2016**.

The V9 is described as a **Plexi-style distortion** with three gain stages, a Class A input buffer, and extensive post-distortion EQ controls.

## Controls / circuit behavior
The exact-model documentation reviewed establishes:
- **Drive** for the distortion amount.
- **Contour** for changing the distortion character from warmer/fatter to brighter/airier textures.
- **2-band EQ**.
- **Top End** switch.
- **Mid Boost** switch.
- A **Class A buffer** for lower impedance, noise rejection, and driving longer cable or pedal chains.

A historical product description calls the V9 a solid-state update to the earlier Tonebone Hot British tube distortion pedal.

The evidence reviewed does not expose a complete schematic or exact semiconductor part numbers.
- **Exact active devices:** Unknown.
- **Exact clipping/diode devices:** Unknown.
- **Complete schematic:** Unknown.

## Sound / use
The cited equipment record describes the V9 as delivering Plexi-oriented harmonics, sustain, and high-gain distortion while allowing substantial shaping through its contour and EQ system. Those statements are treated as product-description language and curated reviewer characterization rather than laboratory measurement.
