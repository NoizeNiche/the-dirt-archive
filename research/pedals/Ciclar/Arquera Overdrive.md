# Ciclar — Arquera Overdrive

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Arquera Overdrive
- **Builder:** Ciclar
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Ciclar Arquera Overdrive, a hand-built vintage-oriented overdrive from Argentina.

## What this pedal is
Ciclar is documented as an Argentine pedal maker building its effects by hand while using period-inspired component choices and adding selected modern improvements. Contemporary Argentine coverage documents the Arquera Overdrive as one of its early pedals. [1][2]

## Sound
Community discussion comparing Arquera with the TS-9 describes it as occupying a similar overdrive territory while providing more control over the tonal response and avoiding as pronounced a nasal character. This is treated as user commentary rather than factory specification. [3]
