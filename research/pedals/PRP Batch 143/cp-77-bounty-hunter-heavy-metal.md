# Caline CP-77 Bounty Hunter - Heavy Metal

- **Builder:** Caline
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Catalog identity:** CP-77 Bounty Hunter - Heavy Metal
- **Colorways:** Black enclosure with yellow graphic is the documented standard appearance.
- **Versions:** Classic and Extreme selector positions are built into the pedal.
- **Version changes:** No confirmed production hardware revision was found.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.
- **Diode type:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.
- **Controls / switching:** Volume, Mid, Distortion, plus Classic/Extreme selector and true bypass.
- **Sound:** The CP-77 is a high-gain metal distortion designed to cover thick, punchy rhythm sounds through aggressive lead settings, with the Mid control shaping the core voice. Classic is described as the more compressed setting, while Extreme opens the response toward a more modern high-gain character.
- **Research confidence:** High for controls, selector behavior, power, and enclosure; medium for circuit lineage because third-party clone lists identify the Micro Metal Muff as a close match without a Caline schematic.
