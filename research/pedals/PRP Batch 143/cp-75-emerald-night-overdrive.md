# Caline CP-75 Emerald Night - Overdrive

- **Builder:** Caline
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** CP-75 Emerald Night - Overdrive
- **Colorways:** Green enclosure with white knobs is the documented standard appearance.
- **Versions:** Three voicing modes are documented: Fat, Normal, and Super.
- **Version changes:** No confirmed production hardware revision was found; the three-position selector is part of the documented CP-75 design.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.
- **Diode type:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.
- **Controls / switching:** Tone, Drive/Gain, and Level, plus a Fat/Normal/Super selector and true bypass.
- **Sound:** The CP-75 is explicitly inspired by the Ibanez TS808 family and is described as moving from clean-to-soft warm overdrive into softer distortion while retaining fullness. The selector broadens the basic drive character, with the documented Fat/Normal/Super positions providing additional tonal and gain emphasis.
- **Research confidence:** High for the design, controls, modes, and published specifications; medium for direct circuit equivalence because the sources say inspired by the TS808 rather than documenting a schematic match.
