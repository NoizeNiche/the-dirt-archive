# Caline CP-78 Red Thorn Distortion

- **Builder:** Caline
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Catalog identity:** CP-78 Red Thorn Distortion
- **Colorways:** Red enclosure is documented in the reviewed catalog imagery.
- **Versions:** No distinct production versions were confirmed in the reviewed sources.
- **Version changes:** No confirmed hardware revision or circuit change was found.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.

- **Controls / switching:** Tone, Level, and Gain; true bypass.
- **Sound:** The CP-78 is described by Caline catalog/database material as covering warm distortion with the ability to move from a more subtle overdrive-like response into fuller distortion.
