# Caline CP-68 Distortion + Delay

- **Builder:** Caline
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Catalog identity:** CP-68 Distortion + Delay
- **Colorways:** Red enclosure is the standard documented finish in the reviewed exact-model listings.
- **Versions:** One documented CP-68 design in the reviewed sources; it combines delay and distortion in one enclosure.
- **Version changes:** No confirmed hardware revision or circuit change was found in the reviewed sources.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.
- **Diode type:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.
- **Controls / switching:** Mix, Repeat, Gain, Tone, Time, Level, plus a Natural/Tight/Classic mode selector and separate Delay/Distortion footswitches.
- **Sound:** The delay section is described as a digital delay voiced to resemble the characteristics of a bucket-brigade-style analog delay, with up to 600 ms of delay time and feedback capable of building into oscillation. The distortion side is a high-gain voice described as dynamic and defined, with three modes intended to shift its response and character.
- **Research confidence:** High for the documented functions, controls, and published specifications; low for component-level details because no verified schematic or gut-shot source was located.
