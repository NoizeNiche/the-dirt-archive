# Caline CP-76 Captain Silver Overdrive

- **Builder:** Caline
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** CP-76 Captain Silver Overdrive
- **Colorways:** Silver/white enclosure is documented in the reviewed exact-model listings.
- **Versions:** Two selectable voicings are built into the pedal: TS808 and TS9.
- **Version changes:** The reviewed sources document the selector as part of the model rather than as separate production revisions; no other confirmed hardware change was found.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.
- **Diode type:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.
- **Controls / switching:** Drive, Tone, Volume, and TS808/TS9 selector; true bypass.
- **Sound:** The CP-76 is a Tube Screamer-inspired overdrive that provides a more open/brighter TS808-style position and a tighter, more compressed TS9-style position. This gives the pedal a compact way to move between two familiar mid-focused overdrive responses rather than relying on a single fixed voicing.
- **Research confidence:** High for the two-mode design and published specifications; medium for exact circuit equivalence because the reviewed sources describe it as inspired by Tube Screamer designs.

## Sound

It produces the iconic blues and rock overdrive sound but with the choice of TS808 or TS9 settings.
The TS808 is a more open and brighter sound, switch to the TS9 Settings and serve up a tighter more compressed tone.
