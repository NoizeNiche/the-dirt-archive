- **Identity:** CircuitFX Soldano SuperCharger, a tube overdrive/preamp with added clean channel and standby switching.

## What this pedal is
CircuitFX documents the Soldano SuperCharger as a tube overdrive design built around a Soldano-style gain circuit. The builder added a separate clean channel, a standby switch intended to protect tube life, and support for both AC and DC 12V power. [1]

## Versions and factory options
Documented features:
- Overdrive channel.
- Clean channel.
- Standby switch.
- 12V AC/DC operation.
- Tube-based gain section. [1]

The builder notes that an early version received a small circuit modification because the original midrange response was considered too swollen. [1]

## Version changes
The checked builder article explicitly documents a small circuit modification during development, particularly around the midrange response. [1]

## Transistor
No discrete transistor complement was established; the core gain architecture is tube based.

## Sound
The builder describes the pedal as producing harmonic, full midrange tube drive and notes that AC and DC powering can change the resulting sound. Later testing showed increased sustain and tighter low-mid response after the circuit settled. These are builder observations rather than a general factory specification. [1]
