- **Identity:** CircuitFX Cruncher, a handmade overdrive/boost design documented as a circuit the builder originally released in the mid-2000s.

## What this pedal is
CircuitFX's own archive describes Cruncher as a legendary effect first produced around 2005-2007. The builder states that it became a strong-selling design and can function either as the main overdrive or as a boost in front of another circuit. [1]

The builder's article also describes a deliberately selected uncommon IC rather than a standard 4558/2134/072 type, chosen for its dynamic behavior and tone. [1]
