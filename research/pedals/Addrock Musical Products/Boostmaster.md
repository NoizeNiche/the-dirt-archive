## What this pedal is
The Addrock Boostmaster is documented as a germanium-powered boost pedal that switches between full-range boost and treble boost, with a Drive control that adds overdrive. Effects Database lists Output, Range (Full/Treble), and Drive as the controls. [1]

## Sound
Boostmaster is designed to move between a full-range boost and a sweeter treble-boost response, with added overdrive available from the Drive control. Effects Database notes that into an already overdriven amp the treble-boost setting emphasizes harmonics strongly. [1]
