# Addrock Musical Products — Hism Scism

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Hism Scism
- **Builder:** Addrock Musical Products
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Silicon-transistor fuzz designed as Addrock's 1970s-style counterpart to the Geranium Fuzz. [1]

## What this pedal is
Hism Scism is a fuzz pedal built around a pair of carefully selected silicon transistors. Effects Database describes it as a warmer, smoother silicon fuzz with a slightly scooped response and a stronger, more elastic bottom end than the Geranium Fuzz. [1]

## Sound
Hism Scism is voiced as a smoother, less spitty silicon fuzz with a somewhat scooped midrange and a firmer, more flexible low end. The Input control changes how hard the signal hits the fuzz circuit, while the Hi-Cut switch was intended to help match the fuzz to different amplifiers. [1]
