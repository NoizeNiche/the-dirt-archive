# Addrock Musical Products — Geranium Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Geranium Fuzz
- **Builder:** Addrock Musical Products
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Germanium transistor fuzz designed around late-1960s-style fuzz tones. [1]

## What this pedal is
The Addrock Geranium Fuzz uses carefully selected germanium transistors and provides Output, Hi-Cut, Fuzz, and Input controls. Effects Database describes the circuit as designed to recreate the character associated with classic late-1960s germanium fuzz. [1]

## Sound
Addrock positions the Geranium Fuzz around the warm, vintage character associated with late-1960s germanium fuzz. The Hi-Cut control provides additional high-frequency shaping, while Input changes the drive entering the fuzz stage. [1]
