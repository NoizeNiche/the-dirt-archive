# Addrock Musical Products — Ol' Yeller

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Ol' Yeller
- **Builder:** Addrock Musical Products
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Addrock's three-control overdrive, with a documented V2 change set. [1]

## What this pedal is
Ol' Yeller is a three-control overdrive with Output, Tone, and Drive. Effects Database documents a V2 revision that retained the basic tone while eliminating clean signal leakage, increasing output, adding a small amount of bass, and improving dynamics. [1]

## Colorways
- The documented production finish is **yellow**. Reverb examples from 2003 and later show the same yellow enclosure. [2]
- No color-specific circuit revision was verified.

## Versions and factory options
### Ol' Yeller
- Output
- Tone
- Drive
- true-bypass Addrock format [1]

### Ol' Yeller V2
- same basic tone
- reduced/eliminated clean leak
- increased output
- slight bass increase
- improved dynamics [1]

## Version changes
The Effects Database entry explicitly documents the V2 changes above. The archive treats V2 as a meaningful factory revision of the Ol' Yeller rather than inventing additional changes. [1]

## Sound
Ol' Yeller is a flexible overdrive with a flatter-mid presentation than a typical Tube Screamer-style circuit, according to Addrock's own description. The V2 keeps the basic voice but gives it more output, slightly more low end, and improved dynamic response. [1][2]
