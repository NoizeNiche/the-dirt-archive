# Addrock Musical Products — Not So Ol' Yeller

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Not So Ol' Yeller
- **Builder:** Addrock Musical Products
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Two-channel Addrock overdrive that extends the Ol' Yeller concept with more gain, more output, and separate gain structures. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
The Not So Ol' Yeller is described by the original dealer material as basically an Ol' Yeller V2 concept with a second channel and greater available gain. The documented design adds two internal high-end trim pots, one for each channel, while retaining the broad, highly adjustable tone range of the Ol' Yeller family. [1]

## Colorways
- A **yellow** 2006 example is documented by Reverb. [2]
- No separate circuit generation tied to finish was verified.

## Versions and factory options
### Not So Ol' Yeller
- Two gain structures / channels
- master tone control with unusually wide range
- two internal high-end trim pots
- increased output versus the earlier Ol' Yeller concept
- true bypass
- LED and DC jack
- +9 V circuit according to Addrock dealer documentation [1][2]

## Version changes
The dealer history documents a last-minute production modification adding two internal trim pots, one per channel, for high-frequency adjustment. This is preserved as a documented factory change rather than treated as an informal mod. [1]

## Sound
Not So Ol' Yeller is described as an overdrive with flatter mids and substantially more available gain than a conventional Tube Screamer-style pedal. Its wide tone control can move from dark and thick to very bright, and the internal high-end trims allow additional shaping while the pedal remains responsive to guitar-volume cleanup. [1][2]
