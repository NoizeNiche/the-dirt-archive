# Bad Pixel Pedals — Germanium Gold Face Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Germanium Gold Face Fuzz
- **Builder:** Bad Pixel Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Point-to-point germanium Fuzz Face-style fuzz using two Russian NOS GT402 germanium transistors and an external Bias control, with a gold-leaf finish. [1][2]

## Controls / architecture
- **Fuzz**
- **Volume**
- **External Bias**
- True bypass.
- Positive-ground configuration. [1]

## Transistor
- **2 × Russian NOS GT402 germanium transistors.**
- Individual gain/leakage measurements are not published. [1]

## Diode / clipping
- Exact clipping/protection diode arrangement is not documented.

## Construction / hardware
Bad Pixel specifies:
- No PCB, all point-to-point.
- Push-back cloth wire.
- NOS USSR military-spec capacitors.
- 1/2-watt carbon-composition resistors.
- Alpha pots.
- Neutrik jacks.
- Handmade in Greece.
- Gold-leaf finish over the enclosure. [1][2]

## Power
- **9V battery**.
- Standard negative-tip external supply is documented.
- **Positive ground**, so the pedal is not intended for conventional daisy chaining with negative-ground pedals. [1]

## Version history
- No numbered electronic revision established.
- Gold-leaf/relic patterns are individual-finish details, not circuit versions.

## Sound
Bad Pixel describes the Gold Face as sustaining strongly while cleaning up from the guitar volume control. External Bias provides additional control over saturation and operating point. [1]
