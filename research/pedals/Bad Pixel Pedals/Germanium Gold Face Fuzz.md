# Bad Pixel Pedals - Germanium Gold Face Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Germanium Gold Face Fuzz
- **Builder:** Bad Pixel Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Point-to-point Germanium Fuzz Face-style pedal using Russian GT402 transistors and a gold-leaf finish.

## What this pedal is
The Gold Face is a hand-built Fuzz Face-style germanium fuzz with no PCB, using point-to-point wiring and an external bias control. Bad Pixel documents Russian NOS GT402 germanium transistors, Soviet-spec capacitors, carbon composition resistors, and a decorative gold-leaf exterior.

## Colorways
- **Gold leaf / relic-distressed finish** is the documented production treatment.
- The builder also describes decorative patterns derived from family hand-knit designs.
- No complete factory colorway chronology was verified.

## Sound
The builder describes strong sustain and substantial cleanup from the guitar volume control, with the external bias providing additional range. The overall response is intended to cover classic Fuzz Face-style germanium fuzz from more open settings into heavier sustained tones.
