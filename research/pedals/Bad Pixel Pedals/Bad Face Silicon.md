# Bad Pixel Pedals - Bad Face Silicon

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Bad Face Silicon
- **Builder:** Bad Pixel Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Fuzz Face-style silicon fuzz built around an accurate Fuzz Face PCB with an external bias control.

## What this pedal is
Bad Pixel's Bad Face Silicon is a silicon Fuzz Face-style circuit in a large Hammond 1590P1 enclosure. The builder documents an external bias control, true bypass, and a deliberately selected NOS metal-can silicon transistor in the exact builder listing.

## Colorways
- A documented build uses a **brushed silver aluminum enclosure with a red circular Fuzz Face graphic**.
- No complete factory colorway chronology was verified.

## Versions and factory options
### Bad Face Silicon
- External bias control
- 1590P1 Hammond aluminum enclosure
- Accurate Fuzz Face PCB
- Carbon composition resistors
- NOS USSR capacitors
- Alpha pots
- True bypass
- 9 V battery or 9 V adapter

## Version changes
The official Silicon Face page links to an “older version,” but the checked sources do not establish a documented circuit or production change between the versions.

## Transistor
- **Technology:** Silicon fuzz.
- **Documented device:** The builder's exact Reverb listing specifies a **NOS metal-can 2N2222A silicon transistor**.

## Diode
- **Exact clipping/protection diode:** Not publicly documented in the checked sources.

## Sound
The builder describes a fat, full silicon fuzz that cleans up strongly from the guitar volume control. The external bias control broadens the response from tighter settings toward more splatty textures, while the silicon circuit is described as having a strong presence in a mix.
