# Bad Pixel Pedals — Bad Face Silicon

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Bad Face Silicon
- **Builder:** Bad Pixel Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Fuzz Face-style silicon fuzz using an accurate Fuzz Face PCB, an external Bias control, and a documented NOS metal-can **2N2222A** transistor. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
Bad Pixel's Bad Face Silicon is a modern silicon Fuzz Face-style build. The builder emphasizes the ability to bias the transistor externally, giving the player more control over the fuzz response than a fixed-bias two-knob circuit. [1]

## Controls / architecture
- **Fuzz**
- **Volume**
- **External Bias**
- Accurate Fuzz Face PCB.
- True bypass. [1][2]

## Transistor
- **Technology:** Silicon.
- **Documented exact device:** **NOS metal-can 2N2222A silicon transistor** for the documented build. [2]
- The exact device should be treated as build-specific unless the builder documents it as universal for the entire Bad Face Silicon production.

## Diode / clipping
- Exact clipping-diode device is not published in the reviewed builder/retailer material.
- Do not infer the clipping components from the 2N2222A transistor.

## Construction
Bad Pixel documents:
- Hammond **1590P1** aluminum enclosure.
- Carbon-composition resistors.
- NOS USSR capacitors.
- Alpha potentiometers.
- True bypass.
- Handmade construction in Greece. [1][2]

## Power
- **9V battery**.
- Standard **9V negative-tip adapter** support is documented.
- The external Bias makes the pedal particularly sensitive to operating-point adjustment. [1][2]

## Version history
- The builder links an older version, but the reviewed sources do not establish a complete circuit-change chronology.
- Cosmetic differences are not treated as electronic revisions.

## Sound
Bad Pixel describes the silicon circuit as fat and full, with strong cleanup from the guitar volume control. External Bias broadens the sound from more controlled fuzz toward splattier and more saturated operating points. [1][2]
