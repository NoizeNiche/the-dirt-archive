# Bad Pixel Pedals - Germanium Pink Face Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Germanium Pink Face Fuzz
- **Builder:** Bad Pixel Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Fuzz Face-style germanium fuzz with an external transistor selector and bias control.

## What this pedal is
The Pink Face uses an accurate Fuzz Face PCB, NOS Soviet parts, and Russian germanium transistors. A selector lets the player choose between a GT402 plus MP20 combination or two MP20 devices, changing the available gain and response.

## Colorways
- The documented finish is **pink with a deep-etched silver aluminum chassis**.
- The builder also links an older-version page, but no complete colorway chronology was verified.

## Versions and factory options
### Germanium Pink Face
- Accurate Fuzz Face PCB
- External bias control
- Transistor selector
- GT402 for Q1 + MP20 for Q2
- 2x MP20 option
- Alpha pots
- Neutrik jacks
- 1590P1 Hammond aluminum enclosure
- Positive-ground circuit
- Battery or standard negative-tip adapter

## Transistor
- **Technology:** Germanium Fuzz Face-style fuzz.
- **Documented devices:** Russian NOS **GT402 and MP20** germanium transistors.
- **Selectable combinations:** GT402 + MP20 or 2x MP20.

## Diode
- **Exact clipping/protection arrangement:** Not publicly documented in the checked sources.

## Sound
The transistor selector is intended to provide a slightly edgier/higher-gain option or a lower-gain, woodier response. The builder also emphasizes dynamic cleanup with the guitar volume, so the pedal can cover both fuzz and cleaner textures without switching it off.
