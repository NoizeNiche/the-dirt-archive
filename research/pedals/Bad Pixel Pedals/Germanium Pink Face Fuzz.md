# Bad Pixel Pedals — Germanium Pink Face Fuzz

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** Germanium Pink Face Fuzz
- **Builder:** Bad Pixel Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Germanium Fuzz Face-style fuzz with external Bias and a transistor selector choosing between GT402 + MP20 or a dual-MP20 configuration. [1][2]

## Controls / architecture

- **Fuzz**
- **Volume**
- **External Bias**
- **Transistor selector**
- True bypass.
- Positive-ground circuit. [1]

## Transistor

- **Technology:** Germanium.
- **Documented choices:** Russian NOS **GT402 + MP20**, or **2 × MP20**. [1]
- The exact gain/leakage characteristics of each selected device are not published.

## Diode / clipping

- Exact clipping/protection diode arrangement is not publicly documented.

## Construction

- Accurate Fuzz Face PCB.
- NOS Soviet components.
- Alpha pots.
- Neutrik jacks.
- Hammond **1590P1** enclosure.
- Deep-etched silver aluminum chassis.
- Handmade in Greece. [1]

## Power

- **9V battery**.
- Standard negative-tip adapter support.
- Positive ground means ordinary mixed-pedal daisy chaining is not recommended. [1]

## Sound

The transistor selector is intended to change gain and response, while external Bias expands the available operating range. Bad Pixel emphasizes dynamic cleanup from the guitar volume. [1][2]
