# Bad Pixel Pedals - Fuck_Fuck Twin Tube Boost Overdrive

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Fuck_Fuck Twin Tube Boost Overdrive
- **Builder:** Bad Pixel Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Discontinued dual-tube boost/overdrive pedal using two 12AU7 valve stages.

## What this pedal is
The Fuck_Fuck is a discontinued two-stage tube pedal that combines two independently switchable boost stages into a larger overdrive when both are engaged. Bad Pixel documents point-to-point wiring, a Hammond enclosure, and individual Volume, Tone, and Gain controls for each stage.

## Colorways
- A documented build uses a **silver aluminum enclosure with blue chemically etched graphics and two exposed tube sockets**.
- No complete factory colorway chronology was verified.

## Versions and factory options
### Fuck_Fuck / Twin Tube Boost Overdrive
- Two tube stages
- 12AU7 tubes
- Individual Volume, Tone, and Gain controls per stage
- Individual on/off footswitches per stage
- Point-to-point wiring
- Hammond aluminum enclosure
- Neutrik jacks
- Alpha pots
- True bypass
- 9 V standard Boss-type negative-tip supply
- Operation up to 12 V is documented by the builder

## Version changes
The checked official page identifies the pedal as discontinued. No separate numbered factory revision was documented.

## Transistor
- **Technology:** Vacuum-tube gain stages.
- **Transistor:** Not applicable to the documented gain stages.

## Diode
- **Exact clipping/protection arrangement:** Not publicly documented in the checked sources.

## Sound
Each tube stage can operate as an individual boost and the two stages can be combined for overdrive. The builder describes the circuit as a lower-gain, open tube drive that can saturate heavily when pushed beyond its intended range.
