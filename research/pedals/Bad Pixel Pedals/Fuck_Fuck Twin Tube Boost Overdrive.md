# Bad Pixel Pedals — Fuck_Fuck Twin Tube Boost Overdrive

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** Fuck_Fuck Twin Tube Boost Overdrive
- **Builder:** Bad Pixel Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive / Boost
- **Identity:** Discontinued two-stage all-tube boost/overdrive using two 12AU7 tubes and point-to-point wiring. The two stages can be used separately or combined for heavier overdrive. [1][2]

## What this pedal is

Bad Pixel describes the Fuck_Fuck as essentially two boost/drive stages in one enclosure. Each footswitch activates its own tube stage; using both together produces a larger, more saturated overdrive. The builder explicitly calls the design all-tube and identifies **12AU7** valves. [1]

## Controls / architecture

- Two independent channels/stages.
- Each stage has its own **Volume, Tone and Gain** controls.
- Each stage has its own footswitch.
- Both stages can be engaged simultaneously for stacked overdrive. [1]

## Active devices

- **Vacuum tubes:** two **12AU7** valves.
- The builder says NOS or as-new vintage tubes may be supplied, with brand/source varying.
- **Transistor:** Not applicable to the documented gain stages. [1]

## Diode / clipping

- Exact clipping/protection diode implementation is **not publicly documented**.
- The builder's description centers on tube-stage saturation rather than a published diode-clipping network.

## Construction / hardware

Bad Pixel documents:
- **Point-to-point wiring**.
- Hammond aluminum enclosure.
- High-grade military/NOS capacitors.
- 1/2-watt carbon-composition resistors.
- NOS/vintage European, American or Japanese 12AU7 tubes depending on availability.
- The unit is explicitly marked **discontinued**. [1]

## Power

- The original builder documentation specifies a standard **Boss-type negative-tip supply** and notes operation up to **12V**. [1]
- Exact current draw is not established.

## Version / build-history notes

- Discontinued model.
- No numbered electronic revision was established in the reviewed sources.
- Tube brands and exact NOS examples can vary between builds, so the archive does not treat one tube brand as universal. [1]

## Sound

Bad Pixel describes each stage as an independent boost that becomes a more substantial overdrive when both are combined. The all-tube design is intended to produce an open tube-driven response, with the builder emphasizing the amount of punch available from the stacked stages. [1][2]
These are builder/reviewer descriptions, not measurements.
