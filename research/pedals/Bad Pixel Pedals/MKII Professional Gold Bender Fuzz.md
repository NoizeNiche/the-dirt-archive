# Bad Pixel Pedals — MKII Professional Gold Bender Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** MKII Professional Gold Bender Fuzz
- **Builder:** Bad Pixel Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Handmade Professional MkII Tone Bender-style germanium fuzz with three Russian NOS MP20/26 transistors, external Bias, and point-to-point Bakelite strip-board construction. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
Bad Pixel describes the Gold Bender MkII as an accurate **Tone Bender MkII** recreation with an added external Bias control. The builder uses a three-transistor Soviet germanium set and deliberately vintage-oriented construction. [1]

## Controls
- **ATTACK**
- **VOLUME**
- **GOLD BIAS**
- True bypass. [1][2]

## Transistor
- **3 × Russian NOS MP20/26 germanium transistors.**
- Individual gain/leakage measurements are not published. [1][2]
## Diode / clipping
- Exact clipping/protection diode arrangement is not publicly documented.

## Construction / hardware
- NOS USSR Bakelite strip-board construction.
- **Point-to-point / no PCB** implementation.
- NOS USSR military-spec capacitors.
- 1/2-watt carbon-composition resistors.
- Alpha pots.
- Neutrik jacks.
- Hammond **1590C** die-cast aluminum enclosure.
- Gold-leaf finish.
- Handmade in Greece. [1][2]

## Power
- **9V battery**.
- Standard negative-tip 9V adapter support.
- **Positive-ground** and unbuffered operation are documented, so the pedal is intended to be placed early in the signal chain and is not suited to arbitrary daisy-chain combinations. [2]

## Version history
- Professional MkII is the documented model designation.
- No later numbered electronic revision established.
- Handmade gold-leaf finishes vary from unit to unit and are not circuit revisions. [1][2]

## Sound
Bad Pixel describes the Bias range as moving from highly saturated and velcro-like settings through thick, woolly Tone Bender territory toward splatty/open fuzz. The builder also emphasizes strong cleanup from the guitar volume control. [1][2]
