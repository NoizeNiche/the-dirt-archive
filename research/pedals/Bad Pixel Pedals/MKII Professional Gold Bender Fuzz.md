# Bad Pixel Pedals - MKII Professional Gold Bender Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** MKII Professional Gold Bender Fuzz
- **Builder:** Bad Pixel Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Handmade Professional MkII Tone Bender-style germanium fuzz with an added external bias control and gold-leaf finish.

## What this pedal is
Bad Pixel's Gold Bender MkII Professional is built around a Tone Bender MkII-style circuit with three Russian NOS germanium transistors and point-to-point construction on Soviet Bakelite strip boards. The pedal adds a dedicated bias control for changing the fuzz response across a broad range.

## Colorways
- The documented finish is **imitation gold leaf over a Hammond 1590C die-cast aluminum enclosure**.
- The builder notes that handmade gold-leaf finishes vary from pedal to pedal.

## Sound
The bias control spans from heavily saturated germanium fuzz toward woolly, fuller Tone Bender textures and more splatty/open settings. The builder also emphasizes cleanup from the guitar volume control, allowing the same circuit to move from cleaner tones into full fuzz.
