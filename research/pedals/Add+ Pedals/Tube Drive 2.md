# Add+ Pedals — Tube Drive 2

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Tube Drive 2
- **Builder:** Add+ Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Historical Add+ overdrive model documented as a separate product in the 2011 Effects Database catalog. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
Effects Database lists Add+ Tube Drive 2 as an overdrive pedal and dates the database entry to March 31, 2011. The historical Add+ catalog also distinguishes Tube Drive, Tube Drive 2, and Tube Drive Silver Edition as separate named products. [1][2]

A July 2023 TurkRock sale listing identifies the exact model as an “Overdrive Boost” pedal. That wording is useful secondary-market evidence for the pedal's intended category, but it is not treated as a factory specification. [3]

The current Add+ website describes the company as a maker of handmade analog signal-processing pedals since 2005. Its present overdrive menu includes Tube Drive but does not list Tube Drive 2, so the current site does not provide a maintained Tube Drive 2 product page. [4]

## Version changes
The “2” suffix establishes Tube Drive 2 as a separately cataloged model rather than merely a cosmetic presentation of Tube Drive. The surviving sources do not document the factory changes made between Tube Drive and Tube Drive 2, so no circuit, control, or component difference is inferred. [1][2]

## Sound
The strongest surviving model-specific evidence classifies Tube Drive 2 as an overdrive, while the 2023 secondary-market listing describes it as an overdrive/boost pedal. No reliable source located during this pass provides enough pedal-specific tonal detail to make a more precise description without speculation. [1][3]

## Historical documentation notes
- Effects Database preserves a link to an older first-party Add+ Tube Drive 2 page. The linked URL now resolves to the Add+ site's 404 page, limiting direct verification of the original factory specifications. [1][6]
- The current Add+ TubeDrive page documents the standard TubeDrive as a 9 V center-negative, true-bypass overdrive with Volume, Tone, and Drive controls, but those specifications are explicitly not assigned to Tube Drive 2. [5]
