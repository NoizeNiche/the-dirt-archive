## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Ratortion 3 v2
- **Builder:** Add+ Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** Small-batch analog overdrive/distortion version of the Ratortion 3 platform. Effects Database documents the v2 as a distinct named revision. [1]

## What this pedal is
Ratortion 3 v2 is an analog overdrive/distortion pedal with separate OverDrive and Distortion gain controls, a dedicated footswitch for switching between those gain modes, a four-position voicing selector, and a three-way clipping switch. The documented voicings are Germanium, Turbo, Classic, and MOSFET, while the clipping switch selects hard, soft, or no clipping. [1]

## Sound
Ratortion 3 v2 spans lower-gain overdrive through heavier distortion and can move between very different clipping/voicing responses with its switches. Effects Database describes the Germanium setting as fuzz-like, Turbo and Classic as mid-focused rock voices, and MOSFET as a more modern distortion character. [1]
