# Add+ Pedals — Blues Player

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Blues Player
- **Builder:** Add+ Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Original Add+ analog overdrive in the Blues Player line.

## What this pedal is
The Blues Player is an analog overdrive from Add+ Pedals, cataloged by Effects Database as an overdrive pedal. A surviving Reverb listing describes it as a vintage-sounding overdrive built around an op-amp approach, capable of clean drive and usable as a boost. [1][2]

## Colorways
- The documented original example is a compact dark enclosure with blue-and-white Blues Player graphics.
- Later Blues Player Pro versions use a larger dual-section enclosure and are cataloged separately, so they are not treated as the same physical model. [3]

## Versions and factory options
### Blues Player
- Analog overdrive
- Drive, Tone and Level controls are documented on surviving product material
- True bypass
- 9V operation [1][2]

No separate numbered circuit revision was verified.

## Version changes
The later Blues Player Pro is a separate named product that combines two Blues Player 2 sections in series. That later dual-channel product is not treated as a revision of the original single Blues Player. [3]

## Sound
The available description points to a vintage-voiced overdrive with a relatively clear, open response that can also work as a boost. The surviving description specifically notes that individual notes remain audible under rhythm playing rather than collapsing into a harsh, smeared distortion. [2]
