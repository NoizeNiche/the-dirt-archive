## What this pedal is
The Add+ Distortion is a vintage-character distortion pedal with Volume, Tone and Drive controls. Surviving product documentation describes a MOSFET setting and a vintage setting that combines MOSFET and diode clipping, plus a Low Pass / High Pass switch that changes the tonal emphasis. [1][2]

## Colorways
- The documented example is a brushed-metal enclosure with dark blue/black face graphics and white labeling.
- No separate factory colorway was verified.

## Versions and factory options
### Distortion production
- Volume, Tone and Drive controls
- Clipping select: MOSFET or Vintage
- Vintage mode combines MOSFET and diode clipping
- Low Pass / High Pass switch
- True bypass
- 9V DC center-negative [1][2]

No separate numbered revision was verified.

## Diode
- **Vintage mode:** Uses a MOSFET and diode combination.
- **Exact diode type/part:** Not publicly documented. [1]

## Sound
The pedal is voiced around vintage-style distortion but can move toward a more modern character in MOSFET mode. The Low Pass / High Pass switch changes the brightness and frequency emphasis, giving the basic drive circuit two additional tonal directions. [1][2]
