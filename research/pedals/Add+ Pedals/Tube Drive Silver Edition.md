## What this pedal is
Tube Drive Silver Edition is separately named in Effects Database's Add+ catalog census. A contemporary owner discussion also identifies the Tube Drive Silver Edition as an Add+ overdrive model, but the discussion is not treated as factory technical documentation. [1][2]
