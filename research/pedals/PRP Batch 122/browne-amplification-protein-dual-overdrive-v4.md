# Browne Amplification — Protein Dual Overdrive V4

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Browne Amplification
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Current fourth-generation Protein dual overdrive.

## What this pedal is
Protein V4 is a dual overdrive whose blue side is based on Browne's refined Carbon circuit and whose green side is a revised Nashville-style drive. The two sides can be stacked for a combined third drive voice. [1]

## Colorways
The manufacturer offers selectable finishes; no single permanent colorway is assumed.

## Versions and factory options
- 2-in-1 dual overdrive
- 3-way hi-cut on the blue side
- Green-side Low Cut
- Green-side heavier clipping option
- Green-side asymmetrical clipping option
- 9V, 130mA
- Optional MIDI upgrade [1]

## Version changes
V4 adds the 3-way hi-cut, low-cut, heavier-clipping and asymmetrical-clipping options documented by Browne. These are functional circuit/voicing changes from earlier Protein versions rather than cosmetic editions. [1]

## Transistor
Exact active devices not publicly documented.

## Diode
The heavier-clipping option uses a distinct clipping configuration, but exact production diode part numbers are not published. [1]

## Sound
Protein V4 is designed to cover clear low-gain overdrive, fuller Nashville-style drive and stacked lead tones. Browne emphasizes improved adaptability, with the blue side's high-cut shaping the top end and the green side's added clipping/low-cut options changing aggression and low-end tightness. [1]
