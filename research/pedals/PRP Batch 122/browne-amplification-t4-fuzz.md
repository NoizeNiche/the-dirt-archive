# Browne Amplification — T4 Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Browne Amplification
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Four-transistor fuzz inspired by classic four-transistor fuzz circuits.

## What this pedal is
The T4 Fuzz is a four-transistor fuzz that Browne says was highly modified to retain midrange and clarity. The company compares the architecture to classic four-transistor fuzz circuits such as Big Muff-style designs. [1]

## Sound
Browne describes the T4 as smoother and less gain-heavy than many versions of the four-transistor fuzz format, with a flatter EQ curve that restores mids and improves mix clarity. It can still reach aggressive, ferocious fuzz settings. [1]
