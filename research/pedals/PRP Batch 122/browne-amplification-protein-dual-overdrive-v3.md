# Browne Amplification — Protein Dual Overdrive V3

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Browne Amplification
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Third major Protein dual-overdrive version.

## What this pedal is
Protein V3 combines the low-gain/high-headroom Carbon side with the Nashville-inspired ATOM side. Browne documents top-mounted jacks and soft switching for this version. [1]

## Colorways
Multiple production finishes are documented; no single permanent colorway is assumed.

## Versions and factory options
- Carbon side
- Nashville-inspired ATOM side
- Top-mounted jacks
- Soft switching
- Optional split/loop configuration through TRS connections and an internal switch [1]

## Version changes
V3 changes the mechanical layout to top-mounted jacks and labels the green channel as ATOM/Nashville-inspired compared with the earlier V2.2 architecture. Browne later introduced V4 with additional high-cut and clipping controls. [1][2]

## Transistor
Exact active devices not publicly documented.

## Diode
Exact factory clipping devices not publicly documented.

## Sound
The Carbon side emphasizes headroom and transparent low-gain drive, while the Nashville-inspired side supplies more singing mids and gain. Cascading the green side into blue creates additional gain and fullness without giving up note definition. [1]
