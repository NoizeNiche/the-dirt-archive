# Browne Amplification — Protein Dual Overdrive V2.2

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Browne Amplification
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Version 2.2 of the Protein dual overdrive.

## What this pedal is
The Protein V2.2 combines a blue-side low-gain/high-headroom overdrive with a green-side Nashville-inspired overdrive. The two sides can be cascaded for a third combined drive texture. [1]

## Colorways
Multiple finishes are documented across Protein production; the current archive does not assume one permanent colorway for the V2.2 identity.

## Versions and factory options
- Dual overdrive
- Blue and Green channels
- Green side cascades into Blue
- V2 side-mounted jacks
- Internal split-mode switch for loop-switcher use [1]

## Version changes
Browne's current store documents V2.2 separately from V3 and V4. V2.2 retains side-mounted jacks and the split-mode architecture, while V3 changes to top-mounted jacks and V4 adds further tonal controls and clipping options. [1][2][3]

## Sound
The blue side supplies a lower-gain, high-headroom base, while the green side is voiced toward Nashville-style drive with added mids and tighter low end. Cascading green into blue adds gain and fullness while retaining note clarity. [1]
