## What this pedal is
Venus is a tube overdrive/preamp inspired by the Chandler Tube Driver family. Unlike a purely solid-state emulation, the documented design uses a real dual-triode tube and adds broader tone shaping for modern pedalboard use.

## Controls
Verified coverage establishes:
- **Volume**
- **Drive**
- Three-band EQ
- **Bias** control

The bias control allows the tube operating character to move from smoother overdrive toward more unstable, sputtery textures.

## Sound
Venus produces thick, harmonically rich tube overdrive with substantial sustain. Independent review coverage notes that it cleans up well from the guitar volume and that the bias control can move the response toward spitty, Velcro-like fuzz textures. The three-band EQ expands the tonal range beyond the original Tube Driver-style format.
