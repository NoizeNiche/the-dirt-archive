## What this pedal is
Falcon is an all-analog JFET amp-in-a-box overdrive designed from a custom amplifier Crazy Tube Circuits built in 2016 to move between a **5E3 Tweed Deluxe** and a **6G2 Brownface Princeton**. The builder states that the pedal reproduces the behavior, sound and feel of the preamp, phase inverter and power amp of those two designs, including their compression and tube-rectifier sag.

## Controls and switching
- **Volume:** output level.
- **Output:** additional output-level control documented in GuitarPlayer's specification.
- **Tone:** overall tone control.
- **’55 / ’61 toggle:** selects the two amp voicings.
  - **’55:** Tweed Deluxe-style response.
  - **’61:** Brownface Princeton-style response.

The checked sources do not expose a complete additional control/value transcription beyond these documented functions.

## Circuit
- All-analog signal path.
- JFET technology used to reproduce the overdriven and lower-gain/clean behavior of the reference amplifier circuits.
- The design specifically aims to reproduce power-amp compression and tube-rectifier sag for a responsive, dynamic feel.

## Sound
The ’55 position produces raw, loose low-end Tweed Deluxe-style overdrive with ragged high end and strong touch/guitar-volume clean-up. GuitarPlayer describes the ’61 position as more focused in the low end and smoother than the Tweed voice, sitting between the rougher Tweed years and later Blackface response. The pedal is especially responsive to picking attack and guitar-volume changes.

## Construction and provenance
