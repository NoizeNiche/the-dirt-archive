# Crazy Tube Circuits - Hi-Power

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Crazy Tube Circuits
- **Catalog identity:** Hi-Power
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive / Amp-in-a-Box
- **Identity:** Exact Crazy Tube Circuits Hi-Power.

## What this pedal is
Hi-Power is a dual-section analog drive/amp-in-a-box pedal combining a Colorsound Power Boost/Overdriver recreation with a Hiwatt-inspired amplifier section. The concept is explicitly associated with the Gilmour-era combination of Power Boost and Hiwatt amplification.

## Boost / Overdriver section
- Faithful recreation of the Colorsound Power Boost / later Overdriver family.
- **Volume:** gain/boost amount on the Power Boost side.
- **Master:** output level.
- **Bass / Treble:** two-band Baxandall EQ.
- **PWR/ODR:** selects Power Boost-style or Overdriver-style response.
- The boost section uses an internal voltage multiplier for higher-headroom operation.
- The builder describes the original Power Boost recreation as using **BC184 transistors** and an internal 18V supply arrangement.

## Hiwatt amp section
- **Master:** output level.
- **Presence:** negative-feedback/high-frequency emphasis control.
- **Volume:** preamp/gain level.
- **Bass / Middle / Treble:** full three-band EQ.
- **103/504:** switches between 100W and 50W Hiwatt-style power-section response.
- **B/L/N:** selects normal, bright or linked-input behavior.
- A separate bypass/engage footswitch controls the amp section.

## Routing and switching
- Two sections can operate independently.
- Passive series effects loop sits between the boost and amp sections.
- Two footswitches provide independent bypass of the sections.
- Power-up bypass/engage state can be preset for the footswitches.
- True bypass.

## Power
- 9V DC external supply.
- The boost section internally generates the higher operating voltage used for the Power Boost-style circuit.
- Published current draw is approximately 84 mA on the documented retail specification.

## Versions and factory options
The Power Boost side intentionally incorporates both the earlier 18V Power Boost and later 9V Overdriver voicings through the PWR/ODR switch. This is a functional mode selection within Hi-Power, not a separate catalog model.

No separate factory circuit revision was established in the checked evidence.

## Transistor
- BC184 transistors are explicitly documented by Crazy Tube Circuits' published description of the Power Boost recreation.
- Exact semiconductor inventory for the Hiwatt-emulation side is not established.

## Diode
- Exact diode type or part number: **not established**.

## Sound
Hi-Power is designed to move from high-headroom clean and boost sounds through overdrive and fuzz-like saturation on the Power Boost side, while the Hiwatt section provides high-headroom clean through compressed, crunchy drive. The two sections can be combined to reproduce the interaction of a Power Boost feeding a Hiwatt-style amp circuit, or used separately.

## Construction and provenance
- Handmade in Greece.
- Compact dual-footswitch enclosure, approximately 122 x 95 x 53 mm in published specifications.
