# Crazy Tube Circuits - Constellation of Fuzz

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Crazy Tube Circuits
- **Catalog identity:** Constellation of Fuzz
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz / Booster
- **Identity:** Exact Crazy Tube Circuits Constellation of Fuzz catalog identity.

## What this pedal is
Constellation of Fuzz is the original product identity for Crazy Tube Circuits' six-mode all-analog germanium fuzz/booster. Effects Database introduced it in January 2018 as a multi-mode fuzz/boost pedal using three NOS germanium transistors.

## Controls and modes
- **Volume**
- **Gain**
- **Mode**
- Six documented settings:
  - Fuzz Face
  - Vox Tone Bender
  - MKI.V
  - MKII
  - RangeMaster
  - RangeMaster into Fuzz Face

## Versions and factory options
The documented family includes OC45, OC41 and a later CV7003 limited edition. The original OC45-era units are the earliest production presentation; the OC41 transition is marked by a transistor change and updated graphics.

## Version changes
- Original production: selected Valvo Black Glass OC45 devices.
- From summer 2019: Philips Black Glass OC41 devices with cyan graphic treatment.
- Later limited edition: Texas Instruments CV7003, 200 units.

## Transistor
- Three selected NOS germanium transistors.
- Exact production variants are documented separately under OC45, OC41 and CV7003 designations.
- Exact matching values are not established.

## Diode
- Exact diode type or part number: **not established**.

## Sound
The six-mode design provides a compact set of classic Fuzz Face, Tone Bender and RangeMaster-style voices. The product family is consistently described as smooth, harmonically rich and capable of substantial clean-up through the guitar volume control.
