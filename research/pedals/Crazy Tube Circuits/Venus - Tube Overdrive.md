## What this pedal is
Venus is Crazy Tube Circuits' compact real-tube overdrive inspired by the **BK Butler Tube Driver** family. It uses a dual-triode tube in a modern pedalboard-sized enclosure and adds a three-band EQ plus a bias control for a wider range of drive textures.

## Controls
- **Gain / Drive:** amount of tube overdrive.
- **Bias:** changes the tube operating point and can move the sound from smoother saturation toward more starved, sputtery textures.
- **Bass:** low-frequency EQ.
- **Middle:** midrange EQ.
- **Treble:** high-frequency EQ.
- **Volume:** output level.

## Circuit and operation
- Real **ECC823** dual-triode tube in the documented production configuration.
- Crazy Tube Circuits chose ECC823 as a middle ground between higher-gain and lower-gain Tube Driver-style dual-triode choices; compatible dual-triode substitutions are possible.
- Internal high-voltage generation supplies the tube from a standard pedalboard power connection.
- True bypass.

## Power
- External **9V DC** center-negative supply.
- Published current draw is approximately **400 mA**.

## Sound
Venus is designed for thick, harmonically rich tube overdrive that can clean up with guitar volume and playing dynamics. The bias control can push the tube toward more compressed, spitty and fuzz-like textures, while the three-band EQ provides substantially more tonal control than the original Tube Driver format.
