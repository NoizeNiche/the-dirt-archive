## What this pedal is

Black Magic is an analog high-gain distortion/fuzz pedal built around cascaded JFET gain stages and a tube-amp-style signal path. The documented design was intended to cover American and British high-gain characters while retaining guitar-volume clean-up and strong note definition.

## Controls

- **Volume:** output level.
- **Presence:** high-frequency/presence shaping.
- **Gain:** overall gain.
- **Treble / Mid / Bass:** three-band passive EQ.
- **Boost toggle:** post-gain upper-mid boost.

## Versions and factory options

- The original **Black Magic** is the predecessor to the later Black Magic MkII.
- The MkII is a separate catalog model because it adds an assignable boost footswitch and further circuit refinement.
- Finish is cosmetic unless a documented circuit revision exists.

## Version changes

Compared with the original, the MkII adds a footswitchable boost system that can be assigned to gain, volume or both. The underlying JFET/cascaded-gain concept remains the documented platform.

## Sound

The documented design ranges from lower-gain crunch to heavily saturated, sustained distortion/fuzz. The three-band EQ, presence control and upper-mid boost make the pedal adaptable to different amplifier voicings, while the guitar-volume response allows clean-up from the instrument.
