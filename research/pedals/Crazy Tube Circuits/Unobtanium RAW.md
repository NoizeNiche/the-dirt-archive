# Crazy Tube Circuits - Unobtanium RAW

## Research identity

- **Builder:** Crazy Tube Circuits
- **Catalog identity:** Unobtanium RAW
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive / Amp-in-a-Box
- **Identity:** Exact Crazy Tube Circuits Unobtanium Raw hardware represented by the catalog's uppercase RAW label.

## Catalog-label note

The manufacturer consistently styles the product as **Unobtanium Raw**, while the archive also contains the uppercase **Unobtanium RAW** label. No separate hardware revision was established for the capitalization difference. This record preserves the catalog label without inventing a second circuit.

## Core architecture

Unobtanium Raw retains the standard Klon-style overdrive plus D-style ODS/SSS amp-in-a-box structure, passive series effects loop, relay true bypass and external XT amp-voicing control.

## RAW changes

- Stock setting uses the standard germanium-diode clipping.
- **OC45 setting** uses NOS Mullard/Valvo OC45 germanium transistors as the alternate K-side clipping element.
- The D-side is voiced glassier and punchier than the standard Unobtanium.
- Power-up bypass/engage presets are documented.

## Controls

- K side: Gain, Treble, Volume, Stock/OC45, Buffer/True.
- D side: Gain, Emphasis, Tone, Volume, ODS/SSS.
- Passive effects loop between sections.
- XT external amp-voicing control.

## Power

- 9V DC center-negative.
- Approximately 83 mA.
- Internally boosted operating voltage for added headroom.

## Transistor

- NOS Mullard/Valvo **OC45 germanium transistors** in the RAW clipping mode.
- Exact individual device numbers/batch matching details: not established.

## Diode

- Stock mode retains germanium-diode clipping.
- Exact diode part number: not established.

## Sound

RAW preserves the recognizable Klon/D-style dual-drive foundation but extends it toward more compressed, higher-gain K-side behavior and a glassier, punchier amp-side response.
