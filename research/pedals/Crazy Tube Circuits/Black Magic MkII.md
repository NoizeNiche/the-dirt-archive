## What this pedal is
Black Magic MkII is the refined successor to the original Black Magic. It uses cascaded JFET gain stages to emulate the response and dynamics of high-gain tube amplification, covering open crunch through fully saturated distortion/fuzz.

## Controls and switching
- Three-band passive EQ.
- **Presence** control.
- **Gain, Volume** and the core drive controls.
- **Assignable Boost footswitch:** can be configured for gain, volume, or both.

The exact physical panel layout varies from the original Black Magic, so the archive keeps the documented MkII feature set rather than copying the original control layout wholesale.

## Versions and factory options
- **Black Magic MkI:** predecessor.
- **Black Magic MkII:** upgraded model introduced in 2014.
- Used-market B-stock and finish differences are treated as condition/cosmetic variants unless a circuit change is documented.

## Version changes
The MkII introduced the assignable boost footswitch and refined the original JFET/cascaded-gain architecture. It retains the broad American/British high-gain concept of the original.

## Sound
The MkII spans low overdrive and crunchy sounds through very high gain, large sustain and heavy distortion/fuzz. Sources emphasize clarity, touch sensitivity and the ability to clean up with the guitar volume.
