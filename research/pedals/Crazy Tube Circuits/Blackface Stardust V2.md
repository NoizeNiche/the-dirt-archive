## What this pedal is
Blackface Stardust V2 is Crazy Tube Circuits' Blackface Fender-style overdrive. The exact-model documentation says it is designed to reproduce the harmonic response and dynamics of a driven American Blackface amp, from sparkly clean through crunchy overdrive.

## Sound
The manufacturer describes the V2 as a juicy, dynamic Blackface-style overdrive that can move from sparkly clean into crunchy drive and can be controlled substantially from the guitar. An independent V2 demo notes a bright response and a high-gain range that can become wild and fuzz-like at maximum settings.
