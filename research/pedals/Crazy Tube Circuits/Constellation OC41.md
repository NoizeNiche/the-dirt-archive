# Crazy Tube Circuits - Constellation OC41

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Crazy Tube Circuits
- **Catalog identity:** Constellation OC41
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz / Booster
- **Identity:** Exact Crazy Tube Circuits Constellation OC41 variant.

## What this pedal is
Constellation OC41 is the later germanium-transistor version of Crazy Tube Circuits' six-mode Constellation fuzz/booster. The documented production change moved from the original OC45 devices to Philips Black Glass OC41 transistors in summer 2019.

## Controls and modes
- **Volume**
- **Gain**
- **Mode**
- Six documented voices:
  - Fuzz Face
  - Vox Tone Bender
  - MKI.V
  - MKII
  - RangeMaster
  - RangeMaster into Fuzz Face

## Versions and factory options
- **OC45:** earlier production with Valvo Black Glass OC45 transistors.
- **OC41:** later production with Philips Black Glass OC41 transistors.
- Early visual examples used all-white graphics on navy; later OC41-marked examples use cyan tube-icon graphics.
- The archive keeps OC41 as its own catalog identity because the component era is explicitly documented.

## Version changes
The OC41 era begins with the documented summer 2019 transistor change. The controls and six-mode architecture remain the same documented platform.

The Constellation OC41 retains the broad six-voice vintage fuzz/boost format. The documented OC45-to-OC41 transition is treated as a real component-era change; exact unit-to-unit tonal differences beyond the documented family behavior are not inferred.
