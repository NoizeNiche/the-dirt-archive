## Catalog-label note
The checked manufacturer, Effects Database and retailer sources use **Space Charged V2** and **Space Charged V2 - Tube Overdrive/Distortion** for the same V2 hardware. No separate circuit was established for the longer catalog label.

## What this pedal is
Space Charged V2 is the updated version of the original real-tube Space Charged. It retains the core 12AY7 overdrive while adding a switchable gain/volume boost, a wider Tone range and a Tight/Fat low-frequency control.

## Controls
- **Gain:** main overdrive amount.
- **Tone:** brightness/frequency balance with a wider range than V1.
- **Volume:** main output level.
- **Boost Gain:** gain added by the boost section.
- **Boost Volume:** boost output level.
- **Tight/Fat:** low-frequency response shaping.
- **Boost footswitch:** engages the boost section.

## Circuit and power
- Real 12AY7 tube overdrive.
- Internal voltage multiplier supplies the elevated tube operating voltage from the external supply.
- True bypass.
- External 9V DC center-negative supply.
- Published current draw is approximately 425-430 mA depending on source specification.

## Version changes
Compared with V1, V2 adds:
- gain boost
- volume boost
- wider Tone range
- Tight/Fat low-frequency shaping

The core 12AY7 overdrive circuit is documented as retained from V1.

## Tube substitutions
Compatible dual-triode substitutions such as 12AX7, 12AT7, 12AU7 and 5751 are documented as user tube options for different gain/character. They do not constitute separate factory circuit versions.

## Sound
The V2 preserves the warm, touch-sensitive tube breakup of the original while extending the available gain and adding a separate boost path. The wider Tone control allows brighter settings, and Tight/Fat changes low-frequency attack and fullness.
