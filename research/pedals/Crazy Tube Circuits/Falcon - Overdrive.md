## What this pedal is
Falcon is a JFET-based amp-in-a-box overdrive designed to recreate the behavior of two classic Fender amplifier circuits: the **1955 Tweed Deluxe / 5E3** and **1961 Brownface Princeton / 6G2**. Crazy Tube Circuits describes the design as reproducing the preamp, phase inverter and power-amp behavior, including the compression and sag associated with the original tube power sections and rectifiers.

## Controls
- **Volume:** controls the amount of gain/drive.
- **Tone:** shapes the high-frequency response; lower settings smooth the sound while higher settings add upper-frequency emphasis and additional gain.
- **Output:** controls the final output level and can be set to match bypass level or provide additional boost.
- **'55 / '61 toggle:** selects between the Tweed Deluxe and Brownface Princeton voicings.

## Switching and power
- True bypass.
- 9V DC center-negative external supply.
- Published current-draw figures vary by source and production listing, so an exact factory current value is not asserted here.

## Versions and factory options
- **'55 Tweed mode:** higher gain, more low-end response, and greater power-amp compression/sag.
- **'61 Brownface mode:** lower gain with a tighter, more focused low end.
- These are the two documented amplifier voicings inside the same Falcon model, not separate catalog pedals.

## Version changes
No distinct production circuit revision was established in the checked sources. The documented '55 and '61 modes are the core design feature.

## Sound
The Falcon is designed to move from dynamic low-gain breakup toward expressive tweed-style overdrive. The '55 side has more gain, low end and compression, while the '61 side is cleaner, tighter and more focused. Independent GuitarPlayer coverage notes strong touch dynamics, responsive cleanup with guitar volume, and distinct tweed/brownface behavior between the two modes.
