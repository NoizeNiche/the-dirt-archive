## What this pedal is
Pin Up Fuzz is an analog MOSFET fuzz from Crazy Tube Circuits with a gain range that begins around overdrive and extends into high-gain fuzz. Its interactive tone section can move the sound from smoother to harsher/raspier textures.

## Controls and switching
- Two interactive **tone controls** shape the fuzz response.
- **Gain range:** moves from overdrive into high-gain fuzz.
- **Upper octave / ring-mod switch:** adds the pedal's more percussive octave/ring-mod voice.
- **True bypass**.

The checked exact-model sources do not preserve a complete trustworthy physical panel transcription, so unverified control labels and values are not guessed.

## Sound
The documented gain range moves from overdrive to high-gain fuzz, with the interactive tone controls determining whether the response is smooth, nasty, raspy or spitty. The upper-octave/ring-mod function adds a more percussive effect with strong sustain and pick attack.
