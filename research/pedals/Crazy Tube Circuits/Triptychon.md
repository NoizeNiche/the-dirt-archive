# Crazy Tube Circuits - Triptychon

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Crazy Tube Circuits
- **Catalog identity:** Triptychon
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz / Overdrive / Octave / Boost
- **Identity:** Exact Crazy Tube Circuits Triptychon.

## What this pedal is
Triptychon is a fully analog three-part gain system combining a multi-voice fuzz, a selectable upper-octave/boost section, and an assignable amp-style drive section in one enclosure.

## Fuzz section
- Four selectable fuzz voices inspired by:
  - Fuzz Face
  - Vox Tone Bender
  - Sola Sound Tone Bender Mk1.5
  - Tone Bender MkII
- **Fuzz:** fuzz gain/intensity.
- **Volume:** fuzz output level.
- **Voice selector:** selects the four fuzz families.
- Switchable anti-buffer circuit restores impedance interaction when used after buffered equipment or active pickups.

## Octave / Boost section
- **Mode:** switches between upper-octave fuzz and boost operation.
- **Gain:** output level of this section.
- **MOD:** selects alternate voicings:
  - boost mode: treble or mid boost
  - octave mode: cleaner or more harmonically rich octave fuzz
- Switchable anti-buffer circuit.

## Drive section
- **Drive:** gain of the amp-style drive circuit.
- **Tone:** high-frequency response.
- **Master:** drive-section output level.
- **Assign:** routes the drive section with the fuzz section, the octave/boost section, or an external controller.

## Routing and switching
- Fuzz + drive and octave/boost + drive combinations can be activated independently.
- Optional external footswitch can control the drive section through the XT connection.
- Relay-based true bypass.
- Two onboard footswitches.
- Standard input and output plus XT connection.
- The architecture allows the three sections to act independently or as a stacked gain chain.

## Power
- 9V DC center-negative external supply.
- Published current consumption is approximately **80 mA**.

## Sound
Triptychon is designed to recreate the interaction of classic fuzz, octave/boost and driven-amplifier stages. The four fuzz voices span familiar vintage gain families, the right-hand section adds octave or treble/mid boost behavior, and the assignable drive lets those stages be combined in different orders and pairings.
