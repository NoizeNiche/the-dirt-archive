## What this pedal is
Orama is an all-analog dual-drive pedal combining a British amplifier-inspired JFET amp section with a boutique fuzz section. Crazy Tube Circuits describes it as two complete voices that can be used independently or stacked, with a passive series effects loop between them.

## AMP section
The AMP side is inspired by the high-headroom Orange/Matamp family of late-1960s and early-1970s British amplifiers.

- **Gain:** amplifier-style gain control.
- **F.A.T. (Fatness Adjustment Tweak):** shifts how bass and lower mids feed the gain stages.
- **H.F.D. (High Frequency Dynamics):** adds upper-harmonic gain, definition and presence.
- Additional frequency controls documented on current retail specifications provide fine low/high frequency shaping.
- JFET circuitry operates from an internally boosted **34 V DC** rail generated from the external 9V supply.
- The high-voltage JFET architecture is intended to provide substantial headroom and dynamic range.

## FUZZ section
- Takes cues from a landmark 1990s boutique fuzz design.
- The manufacturer describes the core as using power-amplifier ICs rather than traditional transistor/diode clipping.
- **RAW / BURN** voicing switch:
  - **RAW:** tighter low end, open response and clearer mids.
  - **BURN:** deeper bass, longer sustain and heavier saturation.
- Dedicated fuzz controls are provided on the right-hand section.

## Routing and switching
- Two sections can be used independently or stacked.
- Passive serial effects loop between the AMP and FUZZ sections.
- Clickless relay-based true bypass switching.
- Two independent footswitches.
- Top-mounted input/output connections.

## Power
- External **9V DC center-negative** supply.
- Published current draw is approximately **82 mA** on current retail specifications.
- Internal voltage boosting provides the 34 V operating rail for the JFET amp section.

## Sound
Orama covers articulate British-style crunch through thick, saturated drive on the AMP side, while the FUZZ side moves from open RAW textures to deeper, longer-sustain BURN sounds. The sections can be combined for heavily saturated rock/stoner/doom textures, and the passive loop allows additional pedals to be inserted between them.
