## What this pedal is
Ziggy v2 is a dual-channel British-flavored overdrive/distortion built with discrete MOSFET gain stages. The two channels cover different eras of British amplifier gain.

## Controls and channels
### Low-gain channel
- Dedicated **Volume**
- **Gain**
- **Tone**, functioning as a presence-style control

Voiced after a late-1960s Super Bass.

### High-gain channel
- Dedicated **Volume**
- **Gain**
- **Tone**, functioning as a presence-style control

Voiced after a late-1970s Master Volume British amplifier.

## Switching and power
- Silent relay true bypass switching.
- Two independent overdrive/distortion channels can be selected.
- Gain controls also act as voicing controls, adding more bass as they are turned clockwise.
- Internal voltage conversion raises standard 9V DC to 18V DC for headroom and tube-like response.

## Sound
The low-gain side moves from jangly, sparkling breakup toward warm crunch, while the high-gain side reaches tighter, saturated British distortion. The gain/voicing interaction and presence-style tone controls are central to the channel character.
