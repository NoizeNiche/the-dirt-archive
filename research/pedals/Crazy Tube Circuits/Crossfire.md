## What this pedal is
Crossfire is a two-sided all-analog pedal built around one of the most recognizable pedal-and-amplifier pairings in guitar history: a Fender Blackface-style amp voice on one side and the classic green mid-hump overdrive associated with the Ibanez Tube Screamer on the other.

The amp side follows the Blackface family from its bold lows, sparkly highs and slightly scooped mids through two selectable power-section responses. The overdrive side recreates the classic green overdrive circuit with common historical modifications.

## Controls and switching

### Blackface amp side
- **Volume:** output level.
- **Gain:** amount of amp-side drive.
- **Treble:** high-frequency EQ.
- **Mid:** midrange EQ.
- **Bass:** low-frequency EQ.
- **Headroom toggle:** selects between the character of a solid-state-rectified 4 x 6L6GC power section and a tube-rectified 2 x 6L6GC power section.
- **Bright toggle:** boosts high-frequency response.

### Overdrive side
- **Volume:** output level.
- **Gain:** amount of overdrive.
- **Tone:** tone/frequency balance.
- **Clipping toggle:** selects symmetrical/original or asymmetrical clipping.
- **Gain toggle:** adds more gain and changes the low-frequency response, producing a response similar to stacking two of the same overdrive pedals.

### Additional routing
- Passive effects loop between the overdrive side and amp side.
- Send is the output of the overdrive side; return is the input of the amp side.
- The two sides can be used independently.
- When both Crossfire sections are bypassed, the loop itself is true bypass.

## Power and construction
- The pedal is designed for **9V DC input only**.
- An internal switch can provide **18V DC to the overdrive circuit** for additional overdrive headroom.
- The manufacturer explicitly warns not to feed the pedal a higher-voltage external power supply.
- All-analog circuitry.
- Built in Greece.
- Dimensions documented by GuitarPlayer: approximately 4.5 x 3.5 x 1.25 inches.

## Sound
The Blackface side is designed to provide bright, spacious American clean-to-driven tones, including selectable solid-state-rectified and tube-rectified power-section behavior. The overdrive side adds the familiar green mid-hump response and can move from slight drive through aggressive gain toward near-fuzz textures.

Independent GuitarPlayer testing describes the pedal as sensitive to touch and guitar-volume changes and notes that it can cover Stevie Ray Vaughan-associated sounds while also offering a much broader range of American tube-like grit.
