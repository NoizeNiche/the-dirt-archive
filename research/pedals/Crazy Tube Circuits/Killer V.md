## What this pedal is
Killer V recreates the character and control behavior of the pitch-shifting vibrato found in vintage Magnatone-style amplifiers, combining an amp-like preamp with a true pitch-modulation section.

## Circuit and operation
- All-analog LFO drives two discrete phase-shift modulation stages.
- The modulation produces true pitch vibrato rather than a simple volume tremolo.
- A parallel dry-signal option can blend unmodulated signal with the modulated path for phaser/vibe-style textures.
- The preamp uses JFET technology and an internal voltage multiplier for increased headroom and amp-like response.

## Controls and switching
The documented pedal provides:
- preamp/level controls for the amp-style front end
- **Speed** for vibrato rate
- **Depth** for modulation intensity
- **Dry/Wet** control for parallel dry-signal blend
- **B / N / M** voicing switch for Bright, Normal and Mellow global response
- Separate bypass behavior for the preamp and vibrato sections, with the vibrato section dependent on the preamp section being active.

The checked independent review confirms the B/N/M voicing switch and the dry/wet operation; the exact panel labels should be preserved against the physical example where possible rather than inferred from family models.

## Sound
Killer V is voiced for the three-dimensional, pitch-moving vibrato behavior associated with Magnatone amplifiers. With dry signal mixed back in, the modulation can move toward phaser/vibe-style textures. The preamp section can also supply lower-gain clean/boost and driven amp-like sounds.
