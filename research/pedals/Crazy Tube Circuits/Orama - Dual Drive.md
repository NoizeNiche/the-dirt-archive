# Crazy Tube Circuits - Orama - Dual Drive

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Crazy Tube Circuits
- **Catalog identity:** Orama - Dual Drive
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz / Overdrive
- **Identity:** Exact Crazy Tube Circuits Orama hardware represented by the catalog's alternate “Orama - Dual Drive” label.

## Catalog-label note
The checked manufacturer and independent sources use **Orama** and **Orama - Dual Drive** for the same current two-section pedal. No separate hardware revision or second circuit was established for the “Dual Drive” wording. This record therefore preserves the canonical catalog label without inventing a second design.

## What this pedal is
Orama is an all-analog dual-drive pedal combining a British amplifier-inspired JFET amp section with a boutique fuzz section. The two sections can run independently or be stacked, with a passive series effects loop between them.

## AMP section
- JFET amplifier-style gain circuit.
- Internal voltage boost raises the 9V input to approximately **34 V DC** for high headroom.
- **Gain:** drive amount.
- **F.A.T.:** controls low-frequency/lower-mid feed into the gain stages and changes the drive voicing.
- **H.F.D.:** adds high-frequency dynamics, definition and presence.
- Current retail documentation also lists additional frequency-shaping controls on the amp section.

## FUZZ section
- Inspired by a 1990s boutique fuzz design.
- Current manufacturer documentation describes a power-amplifier-IC-based fuzz topology rather than traditional transistor/diode clipping.
- **RAW / BURN:** two documented voicings.
  - RAW emphasizes open response, tighter low end and clearer mids.
  - BURN emphasizes deeper bass and longer sustain.
- Dedicated fuzz gain/tone/volume controls are documented on current retail material.

## Routing and switching
- Independent or stacked operation of the two sections.
- Passive serial effects loop between sections.
- Clickless relay-based true bypass.
- Two footswitches.
- Top-mounted input/output connections.

## Power
- External 9V DC center-negative supply.
- Published current draw is approximately 82 mA.
- Internal voltage conversion produces the higher operating rail for the amp section.

## Versions and factory options
No distinct circuit revision was established for the “Orama - Dual Drive” catalog label. Current evidence treats it as the same Orama dual-drive production model.

## Transistor
- JFET circuitry is explicitly documented in the AMP section.
- Exact JFET part numbers were not established from reliable sources.

## Diode
- Exact diode type or part number: **not established**.

## Sound
The amp section ranges from clean/edge-of-breakup through thick saturated British-style drive, while the fuzz section moves from open RAW textures to deeper, more sustained BURN tones. Stacking the sections can produce very dense rock, stoner and doom textures.

## Construction and provenance
- Handmade in Athens, Greece.
- Current retail documentation lists a burnt-orange die-cast enclosure, approximately 120 x 97 x 49 mm and 423 g.
