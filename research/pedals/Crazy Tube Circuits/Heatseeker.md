## What this pedal is
Heatseeker is a dual-section overdrive and preamp/enhancer designed around classic Marshall-style tones plus the tonal enhancement associated with vintage Schaffer-Vega wireless systems.

## Controls and switching
### Overdrive section
- Three-way amp-voice switch: **JTM45 / Super Lead / Master Volume**
- Three-band tone stack
- **Presence**
- **Preamp / Gain**
- **Output Level**
- **Wall of Sound** switch

### Boost / enhancer section
- **Boost Level**, documented up to +15 dB
- **Enhance**, intended to alter dynamic response and harmonic content

The pedal has two footswitchable sections that can be used independently or together, with an effects loop between them.

## Circuit
- JFET-based design.
- Internal voltage boosting is documented for increased headroom and an amp-like response.

## Sound
The three Marshall-style voices range from rich, smooth JTM45-type drive to more saturated Super Lead and Master Volume textures. The Wall of Sound switch increases perceived soundstage, while the boost section adds a bright, dynamic lift suited to solos. Independent review coverage notes that the Enhance control is comparatively subtle.
