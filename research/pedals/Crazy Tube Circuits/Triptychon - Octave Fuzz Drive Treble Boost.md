## Catalog-label note
The manufacturer uses **Triptychon** as the product name while Effects Database uses the expanded description “Triptychon - Octave Fuzz / Drive / Treble Boost.” No separate hardware or circuit revision was established for the expanded catalog label.

## What this pedal is
Triptychon is a fully analog three-part gain system combining a four-voice fuzz, upper-octave/boost section, and assignable amplifier-style drive.

## Controls and routing
- Fuzz: Fuzz, Volume, four-way Voice selector, switchable anti-buffer.
- Octave/Boost: Gain, OCT/BST mode, MOD voicing, switchable anti-buffer.
- Drive: Drive, Tone, Master, and Assign routing.
- Two onboard footswitches manage fuzz+drive and octave/boost+drive combinations.
- XT jack allows external control of the drive section.
- Relay-based true bypass.

## Power
- 9V DC center-negative.
- Approximately 80 mA current draw.

## Sound
The expanded catalog description reflects the three core functions: vintage-family fuzz, upper-octave or treble/mid boost, and amp-style drive. The assignable routing lets the player combine these voices as a flexible analog gain system.
