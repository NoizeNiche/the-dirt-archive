## What this pedal is
Stardust V3 is a low-gain overdrive and amp-in-a-box design from Crazy Tube Circuits. Independent coverage describes it as a revoiced Blackface-style platform with additional Fender-family amp voicings.

## Versions and factory options
- **Stardust V2 / Blackface Stardust V2:** earlier generation.
- **Stardust V3:** later generation documented by Crazy Tube Circuits and Premier Guitar.
- V2 and V3 remain separate catalog identities.

## Version changes
Premier Guitar documents the V3 as adding **Bassman, Vibrolux Reverb, and Deluxe Reverb** voicings to the black-panel-style platform. These are documented tonal/voicing changes, not merely cosmetic differences.

## Sound
The V3 is designed for low-gain Fender-style overdrive and revoicing, with the additional amp voices expanding the response beyond the earlier Blackface/Stardust presentation. Independent coverage emphasizes dynamic response and a useful low-to-medium gain range.
