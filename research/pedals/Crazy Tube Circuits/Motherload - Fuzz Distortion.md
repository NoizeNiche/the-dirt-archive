## What this pedal is
Motherload is a one-channel hybrid dirt pedal combining two independent all-analog circuits inspired by the **Big Muff** and **RAT** families. It is not a conventional two-channel pedal: the R and M circuits are alternate voices within the same dirtbox, with additional routing through the LINK EQ function.

## Controls and operating modes
- **R / M toggle:** selects the RAT-inspired distortion side or Muff-inspired fuzz side.
- **Filter:** RAT-side EQ control.
- **Tone:** Muff-side EQ control.
- **Voice:** cycles through Muff-style voicings.
- **LINK EQ:** allows the EQ controls to interact across both modes, greatly expanding the available combinations.
- The exact panel layout should be checked against the local physical example when photographed.

## Circuit and switching
- Two distinct **all-analog** circuits inspired by Big Muff and RAT designs.
- R circuit ranges from touch-sensitive overdrive into heavier distortion.
- M circuit provides Muff-style fuzz with substantial sustain and string separation.
- The two EQ systems operate independently until LINK EQ is engaged.

## Sound
The RAT-inspired side covers lower-gain overdrive through aggressive distortion with a Filter control for shaping high frequencies. The Muff-inspired side provides thick fuzz, rich harmonics and long sustain. LINK EQ can activate both EQ systems in either mode, creating combinations beyond the normal voice selections.
