# Crazy Tube Circuits - Space Charged - Tube Overdrive/Distortion

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Crazy Tube Circuits
- **Catalog identity:** Space Charged - Tube Overdrive/Distortion
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive / Distortion
- **Identity:** Exact original Crazy Tube Circuits Space Charged.

## What this pedal is
Space Charged was Crazy Tube Circuits' first real-tube overdrive. It uses a **12AY7 preamp tube** and an internal voltage multiplier to generate the higher operating voltage needed from a standard 9V DC input.

## Controls
The original V1 documentation supports a conventional tube-overdrive control set centered on:
- **Gain:** amount of tube drive.
- **Tone:** brightness/frequency balance.
- **Volume:** output level.

## Circuit and power
- Real **12AY7** preamp tube.
- Internal voltage multiplication.
- External 9V DC supply.
- Published early specifications call for approximately **400 mA** current draw.
- True bypass.

## Tube options
Crazy Tube Circuits recommends the 12AY7 for the intended response, while its original documentation also states that other compatible dual-triode types such as **12AX7, 12AT7, 12AU7 and 5751** can be tried for different gain and overdrive characteristics.
These are tube-substitution options, not separate factory circuit versions.

## Versions and factory options
- The V1 design is the original Space Charged released in **2017**.
- V2 is documented separately as a later development with the same core 12AY7 real-tube overdrive circuit plus additional gain/volume boost functionality and expanded tone shaping.
- Do not merge V1 and V2.

## Version changes
V2 retains the core V1 real-tube circuit while adding a boost section, widening the Tone control range and adding a Tight/Fat low-frequency switch.

## Transistor

## Sound
The original Space Charged is designed to cover warm, organic tube tone from thick clean boost through classic-rock overdrive/distortion, with strong touch sensitivity and dynamic breakup.
