# Crazy Tube Circuits - Constellation

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Crazy Tube Circuits
- **Catalog identity:** Constellation
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz / Booster
- **Identity:** Exact Crazy Tube Circuits Constellation family.

## What this pedal is
Constellation is a multi-mode all-analog germanium fuzz/booster that combines six classic-style fuzz and boost voices in one pedal. The documented design uses three selected NOS germanium transistors and a smart switching system.

## Controls and modes
- **Volume:** output level.
- **Gain:** overall gain.
- **Mode:** selects among six documented voices:
  - Fuzz Face
  - Vox Tone Bender
  - Tone Bender MKI.V
  - Tone Bender MKII
  - RangeMaster
  - RangeMaster into Fuzz Face

## Versions and factory options
- **OC45:** original transistor-era version, using selected Valvo Black Glass OC45 devices.
- **OC41:** later production version after the documented 2019 transistor change, using Philips Black Glass OC41 devices.
- **CV7003:** separate limited 200-unit edition using Texas Instruments CV7003 devices, historically related to OC44.
- Early graphics were all-white on navy; later graphics changed to cyan around the OC41 transition.
- The archive keeps the transistor-marked versions separate where the canonical catalog does.

## Version changes
The documented production change occurred in summer 2019, when the original Valvo OC45 transistors were replaced with Philips Black Glass OC41 devices and the tube-icon graphics changed from the earliest all-white presentation to cyan. A later limited CV7003 run is documented separately.

## Transistor
- Three selected **NOS germanium transistors** are used.
- Version-specific documented devices:
  - **OC45:** Valvo Black Glass OC45.
  - **OC41:** Philips Black Glass OC41.
  - **CV7003 edition:** Texas Instruments CV7003, military-spec OC44 family.
- Exact individual transistor matching values are not established by the checked sources.

## Diode
- Exact diode type or part number: **not established**.

## Sound
The six-position architecture covers Fuzz Face, Tone Bender and RangeMaster-style gain/boost voices, with the exact germanium transistor set influencing the feel and voicing of each version. The original OC45 version is described as slightly more open-pored and reedy, while later CV7003 examples are denser and fuller with somewhat more low end.
