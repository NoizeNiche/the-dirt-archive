# Crazy Tube Circuits - Unobtanium - Klon + Dumble

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Crazy Tube Circuits
- **Catalog identity:** Unobtanium - Klon + Dumble
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive / Amp-in-a-Box
- **Identity:** Exact Crazy Tube Circuits Unobtanium.

## What this pedal is
Unobtanium is a dual overdrive combining a Klon-style high-headroom overdrive with a D-style amp-in-a-box section based on two distinct Dumble amplifier voices. The sections can run independently, be stacked, or have other pedals inserted between them through a passive series effects loop.

## OVERDRIVE section
- **Gain:** transparent boost through high-headroom overdrive.
- **Treble:** active high-frequency control.
- **Volume:** output level, with substantial boost available.
- **Stock / Mod toggle:** selects the original-style germanium-diode clipping/compression or a higher-gain, higher-headroom mode.
- **Buffer / True toggle:** selects buffered bypass or relay true bypass.

Crazy Tube Circuits explicitly describes the stock clipping as using **germanium diodes**.

## AMP section
- **Gain:** compression/drive.
- **Emphasis:** pre-drive EQ for presence and low-end response.
- **Tone:** post-drive high-frequency shaping.
- **Volume:** output level.
- **ODS / SSS toggle:**
  - **ODS:** higher-gain overdrive with enhanced mids and blooming harmonics.
  - **SSS:** cleaner, more transparent drive with natural compression.
- External XT footswitch can select amp voicing.
- Two internal trimmers provide additional voicing EQ and boost adjustment.

## Routing and switching
- Passive series effects loop sits between the Overdrive and Amp sections.
- Sections can be used independently or stacked.
- Relay-based clickless true bypass.
- The buffer/true-bypass selector is part of the Overdrive section.
- XT input accepts the dedicated passive/latching external footswitch for amp-voicing control.

## Power
- 9V DC center-negative external supply only.
- Crazy Tube Circuits specifies an internally boosted **18V DC** operating rail.
- Maximum current consumption is approximately **83 mA**.
- Do not power the pedal from a higher external voltage.

## Versions and factory options
- The standard Unobtanium is the original dual Klon/D-style design.
- Internal voicing trimmers are part of the production design, not separate models.
- The separately cataloged **Unobtanium Raw** is a later refinement with a different clipping mode and D-side voicing; it remains a distinct edition.

## Version changes
The standard model's core architecture is the Klon-inspired Overdrive section plus D-style ODS/SSS Amp section. The later Raw edition changes the K-side clipping implementation and modifies the D-side voice.

## Transistor
- Exact transistor part numbers: **not established** from the checked standard-model sources.
- The standard K-side's clipping is documented around germanium diodes rather than an identified germanium transistor clipping stage.

## Diode
- **Germanium diodes:** explicitly documented in the standard K-side stock clipping.
- Exact diode part number: **not established**.
- The Mod setting adds silicon diodes in combination with the stock germanium clipping according to Crazy Tube Circuits documentation.

## Sound
The standard Unobtanium combines transparent, high-headroom Klon-style drive with D-style amp tones ranging from clean, compressed SSS behavior to thicker, more mid-forward ODS overdrive. Running both sides together creates a stacked, highly sustaining lead sound, while the effects loop allows additional pedals to be inserted between the two sections.
