# Crazy Tube Circuits - Space Charged V2

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Crazy Tube Circuits
- **Catalog identity:** Space Charged V2
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive / Distortion
- **Identity:** Exact Crazy Tube Circuits Space Charged V2.

## What this pedal is
Space Charged V2 is the updated version of the original real-tube Space Charged overdrive. It retains the core **12AY7** tube circuit while adding a switchable gain/volume boost, a wider Tone control range, and a Tight/Fat switch for shaping low-frequency response.

## Controls
- **Gain:** main overdrive amount.
- **Tone:** expanded range compared with V1, including brighter settings.
- **Volume:** main output level.
- **Boost Gain:** gain amount for the boost section.
- **Boost Volume:** output level of the boost section.
- **Boost footswitch:** activates the additional boost.
- **Tight/Fat:** changes low-frequency response/attack.

## Circuit and power
- Real **12AY7** tube overdrive.
- Internal voltage multiplier supplies the higher tube operating voltage.
- True bypass.
- External **9V DC center-negative** supply.
- Published current draw is approximately **425-430 mA** depending on source/specification.
- The V2 retains the same core real-tube overdrive circuit as V1.

## Versions and factory options
- V2 was presented as the next version of Space Charged around **2020**.
- The core 12AY7 overdrive circuit is explicitly described as unchanged from V1.
- Added boost functionality and expanded tone shaping are the defining V2 changes.

## Version changes
Compared with V1:
- added gain boost
- added volume boost
- widened Tone range for brighter sounds
- added Tight/Fat low-frequency shaping

## Tube substitutions
Crazy Tube Circuits' documentation allows compatible 9-pin dual-triode substitutions such as 12AX7, 12AT7, 12AU7 and 5751 for tonal/gain variation. These are user tube substitutions, not alternate factory circuits.

## Sound
The V2 retains the warm, dynamic tube character of the original while extending it with more gain and a footswitchable boost. The Tight/Fat control lets the player tailor low-frequency attack, while the wider Tone control reaches brighter sounds.
