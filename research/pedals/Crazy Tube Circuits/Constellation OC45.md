# Crazy Tube Circuits - Constellation OC45

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Crazy Tube Circuits
- **Catalog identity:** Constellation OC45
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz / Booster
- **Identity:** Exact Crazy Tube Circuits Constellation OC45 variant.

## What this pedal is
Constellation OC45 is the original production transistor-era form of the six-mode Constellation germanium fuzz/booster. It uses three selected Valvo Black Glass OC45 germanium transistors.

## Controls and modes
- **Volume**
- **Gain**
- **Mode**
- Six documented voices:
  - Fuzz Face
  - Vox Tone Bender
  - MKI.V
  - MKII
  - RangeMaster
  - RangeMaster into Fuzz Face

## Versions and factory options
- Earliest documented units have all-white graphics on a navy enclosure.
- Later graphic treatment changed to cyan around the OC41 transition.
- OC41 is the later standard transistor era.
- A separate CV7003 limited run followed later and is not merged with OC45.

## Version changes
The OC45 represents the earlier production era before the documented summer 2019 move to Philips Black Glass OC41 devices. The underlying six-mode circuit concept remained the same documented platform.

## Transistor
- **Valvo Black Glass OC45**, three selected NOS germanium transistors.
- Exact matching values are not established.

## Diode
- Exact diode type or part number: **not established**.

## Sound
Independent comparison describes the original OC45 version as slightly more open-pored and reedy with a dry edge, while retaining a full-frequency, harmonically rich response and strong clean-up from the guitar volume control.
