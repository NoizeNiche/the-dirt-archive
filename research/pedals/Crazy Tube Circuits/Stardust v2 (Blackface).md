## What this pedal is
Stardust v2 (Blackface) is a distinct historical version of Crazy Tube Circuits' Blackface-inspired overdrive. Effects Database identifies the exact model as a V2 released in **2011**, with the pedal designed to respond like a pushed American Blackface amplifier.

## Controls
The surviving exact-model source confirms the core **Gain** and **Tone** control concept, with the player expected to use the guitar's volume control as part of the response. A complete panel-value transcription is not established from the checked evidence.

## Versions and factory options
- **V2 / Blackface:** distinct historical version documented in 2011.
- Crazy Tube Circuits currently lists **Blackface Stardust V2** as discontinued/old-stock and separately lists Stardust V3 as the current production version.
- B-stock/old-stock listings are treated as availability/condition variants, not new circuits.

## Version changes
V2 is the Blackface-specific version preceding the V3 three-voice design. The V3 later expanded the platform to Bassman, Vibrolux and Deluxe-style voicings.

## Sound
The documented V2 is intended to reproduce the dynamic response of a driven Blackface-style amplifier, ranging from sparkly clean through crunchy overdrive. The design emphasizes interaction with the guitar's volume and playing dynamics.
