## What this pedal is
Starlight is a smooth, liquid fuzz-distortion designed for sustaining lead sounds. The documented design combines JFET and MOSFET gain stages and spans from edgy lower-gain breakup to thick, high-gain fuzz.

## Controls
- **Gain:** drive amount.
- **Tone:** frequency response.
- **Volume:** output level.
- **Gain/boost toggle:** adds extra gain and high-mid presence for more aggressive harmonics.

## Power and switching

## Sound
The verified sources consistently describe Starlight as smooth and sustaining, with fat mids and creamy highs. The gain range can move from low-gain edge and breakup into thick fuzz, while the upper-gain toggle adds more high-mid presence and harmonic intensity.
