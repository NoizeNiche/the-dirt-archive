# Ashdown Engineering - Triple Shot - Blended Drive

## PRP identity

- **Builder:** Ashdown Engineering
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Ashdown Triple Shot, a three-band blendable bass overdrive.

## What this pedal is

Ashdown launched the Triple Shot in **September 2023** as a high-gain bass overdrive that splits the incoming signal into three frequency bands and processes each band through its own distortion generator before recombining them.

## Controls / architecture

- **Dark:** Low-frequency band drive level.
- **Medium:** Midrange band drive level.
- **Light:** High-frequency band drive level.
- Each band has its own **Roast** level control.
- Built-in **VU meter** provides visual signal-level feedback.
- The three bands can be blended independently to preserve low end while adding targeted mid/high distortion.

## Power / construction

- **9V DC**, center negative.
- **45mA** current draw.
- Dimensions: **145.5 x 116.2 x 65 mm**.
- Net weight reported by Ashdown: **1.15 kg**.
- UK-designed/manufactured product, according to current product documentation.

## Circuit / signal path

The published architecture is explicitly three parallel frequency-band distortion paths:
1. Signal split into Dark / Medium / Light bands.
2. Each band runs through its own dedicated distortion generator.
3. Band levels are adjusted independently via the Roast controls.
4. Processed bands are recombined into the output signal.

Exact transistor, op-amp and diode part numbers are not disclosed.

## History / versions

Effects Database preserves the Triple Shot as a 2019-announced product identity and marks a later **V2**. Ashdown's current product page describes the present Triple Shot as launched in September 2023. The archive keeps these records together as one product family but does not assume that the 2019 listing and 2023 production unit are electronically identical without a documented change list.

## Sound

Ashdown positions Triple Shot as a high-gain bass overdrive able to move from subtle midrange lift and chunky low-end grind to high-frequency distortion by adjusting the three Roast controls.
