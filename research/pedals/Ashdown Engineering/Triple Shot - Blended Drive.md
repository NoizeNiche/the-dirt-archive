# Ashdown Engineering - Triple Shot - Blended Drive

## PRP identity
- Builder: Ashdown Engineering
- Catalog type: Overdrive
- Identity: Ashdown Triple Shot, blended bass overdrive.

## What this pedal is
Triple Shot is a high-gain bass overdrive designed around blended drive and dry-signal retention. Effects Database dates the original pedal to January 4, 2019 and notes a later V2.

## Controls / architecture
- Blended dry/wet drive architecture.
- High-gain bass overdrive.
- Exact complete control legend not reconstructed.

## History
Triple Shot is the parent product for later related PRO-FX drive designs including Double Shot.
