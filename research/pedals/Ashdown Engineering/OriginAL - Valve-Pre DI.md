# Ashdown Engineering - OriginAL - Valve-Pre DI

## PRP identity
- Builder: Ashdown Engineering
- Catalog type: Overdrive
- Identity: Ashdown OriginAL Valve-Pre DI bass preamp with footswitchable valve overdrive.

## What this pedal is
OriginAL combines a bass preamp, five-band EQ, DI and footswitchable valve overdrive. A 12AX7 provides the valve-drive section.

## Controls / architecture
- 5-band EQ.
- Drive Gain and Drive Level.
- Output control.
- -3dB input pad.
- Showtime footswitch.
- Ground lift and DI output.
- Headphone and line input on the 18V format.

## Power / history
Effects Database dates the model to January 29, 2017 and documents 18V operation. The drive is footswitchable and powered by a 12AX7.
