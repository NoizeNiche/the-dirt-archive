## What this pedal is
Pedal of the Day describes the Cedar Drive as a low-mid-gain original-design overdrive from Cedar Custom Effects. The review identifies three controls and an in/out connection layout, and reports an OPA2134 op-amp in the design. [1]

The same source describes the pedal as intended to preserve the guitar's basic character while adding a warm, thick overdrive response. [1]

## Sound
Pedal of the Day characterizes the Cedar Drive as low-to-mid gain with a warm, thick overdrive voice. The Tone control behaves more like a treble-oriented boost than a conventional broad-spectrum tone control, allowing the player to move from a more natural response toward additional high-frequency presence.
