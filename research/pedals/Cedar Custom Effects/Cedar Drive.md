## What this pedal is

Pedal of the Day describes the Cedar Drive as a low-mid-gain original-design overdrive from Cedar Custom Effects. The review identifies three controls and an in/out connection layout, and reports an OPA2134 op-amp in the design. [1]

The same source describes the pedal as intended to preserve the guitar's basic character while adding a warm, thick overdrive response. [1]

## Sound

Pedal of the Day characterizes the Cedar Drive as low-to-mid gain with a warm, thick overdrive voice. The Tone control behaves more like a treble-oriented boost than a conventional broad-spectrum tone control, allowing the player to move from a more natural response toward additional high-frequency presence. ### Verified sound evidence
The Cedar Drive from Cedar Custom Effects is the overdrive on display today, There’s not a whole lot to figure out on this low-mid gain original design overdrive pedal, as it just consists of 3 knobs and In/Outputs.
Utilizing the OPA2134 chip, which is known for having loads of clarity at high gain, the Cedar Drive maintains the original character of your guitar’s tone, while pushing your sound with a warm and thick overdrive sound.
The Tone control acts more like a treble booster, however, which helps keep the mids and low-end in the mix until you really crank it up, at which point the high end takes over the sound.
