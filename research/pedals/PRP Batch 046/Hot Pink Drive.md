# Alexander Pedals — Hot Pink Drive

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Alexander Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Limited 2018 overdrive with British-voiced two-band EQ and active mid contour.

## What this pedal is
Hot Pink Drive was a limited 2018 Alexander Pedals release described as hand-assembled. It combines Level and Drive with passive Bass/Treble EQ and an active Contour midrange filter.

## Colorways
- The model is explicitly documented as a very pink limited edition for 2018.

## Versions and factory options
- Level
- Drive
- Bass
- Treble
- Contour
- true-bypass relay switching
- soft-touch bypass
- settings memory
- operation up to 18V for increased headroom

## Sound
Hot Pink combines a British-voiced passive two-band tone stack with an active mid filter that can boost or scoop the midrange substantially. Drive handles gain and distortion, while higher supply voltage can provide additional headroom.
