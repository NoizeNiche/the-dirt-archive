# Alen Geere — Preface Drive

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Alen Geere
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Overdrive/boost pedal documented in early Alen Geere demonstrations.

## What this pedal is
Preface Drive is documented in a 2011 demonstration using a Fender Blues Jr. and Stratocaster. The surviving report specifically highlights a very strong boost function and notes that the boost can be increased without forcing the drive control upward.

## Sound
A contemporary demonstration describes the boost as exceptionally strong while remaining clear, with tone shaping available without requiring additional drive. The report also describes enough output to push the associated amplifier very hard, making the pedal useful as both a clean-ish level lift and an amp-driving device.
