# Alen Geere — Loverdrive

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Alen Geere
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Three-stage analog overdrive with internal trim controls for transistor bias.

## What this pedal is
Loverdrive uses three gain stages and was designed for medium-to-high gain applications. External controls are Level and Gain, while three internal trimmers set the operating levels and tonal behavior of the transistor stages.

## Colorways
- White finish documented in the Reverb product listing.

## Versions and factory options
- Level
- Gain
- three internal transistor-stage trimmers
- operation from 9V, with documented use up to 18V for increased headroom

## Sound
Loverdrive is designed around a warm, fluent medium-to-high gain sound, with the internal bias controls allowing a wide range of responses. Lower gain settings can clean up from the guitar volume, while higher-voltage operation provides greater headroom.
