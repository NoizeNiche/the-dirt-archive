## What this pedal is
Crown Centaur is Alen Geere's remake of the Klon Centaur concept, using a similar topology while adding additional clipping and EQ options. The builder describes a gain control that blends clean and dirty signal, a three-position clipping switch, extra bass control, and a three-position mid switch.

## Sound
The Crown Centaur is intended to cover clean boost through overdrive while retaining substantial headroom. Its selectable clipping modes allow a fuller unclipped drive, germanium-style clipping, or a smoother silicon/LED response, while the added bass and mid controls broaden the tonal range beyond the original Centaur-style layout.
