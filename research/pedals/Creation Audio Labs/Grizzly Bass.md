# Creation Audio Labs - Grizzly Bass

## What this pedal is
Grizzly Bass is a bass-oriented overdrive/distortion and tone-shaping pedal that adapts Creation Audio Labs' Holy Fire analog wave-shaping technology for bass while adding the company's tone-shaping and buffered-output approach.

## Controls
- **Gain:** master output/boost level.
- **Overdrive:** analog wave-shaping drive amount.
- **Distortion:** hard-clipping distortion amount.
- **Mid-Scoop:** removes/reshapes midrange while preserving overall level behavior.
- **Hi-Cut:** high-frequency roll-off for a more speaker-like/vintage response.

## Switching and power
- Buffered bypass is the stock configuration.
- True bypass is available as an option/special request.
- Can function as a DI into a line input.
- 9V DC, 250 mA, center-negative supply.
- The manufacturer recommends a high-current power output.

## Circuit
- Proprietary analog wave-shaping technology derived from the Holy Fire.
- Internal power boosting raises the operating voltage for increased headroom.
- Manufacturer describes usable response down to approximately 20 Hz.

## Sound
Grizzly Bass covers clean boost, subtle tube-like warmth, overdrive and heavier distortion while preserving bass fundamentals. Independent Bass Musician coverage notes the unusual control interaction and emphasizes the pedal's response to playing dynamics.
