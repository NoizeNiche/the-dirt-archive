## What this pedal is
Holy Fire Classic is the original 48V version of Creation Audio Labs' Holy Fire, using the company's proprietary analog wave-shaping approach to emulate tube-like overdrive and distortion without a physical tube.

## Controls
- **Gain:** clean boost/gain.
- **Overdrive:** analog wave-shaping overdrive.
- **Distortion:** hard-edge clipping.
- **Hi-Cut (~):** high-frequency attenuation for smoothing harder sounds.

## Switching and power
- True bypass only in the documented classic configuration.
- Dedicated **48V DC** power supply.
- Manufacturer specifies a universal 90-260VAC input supply for the included 48V adapter.
- Compact footprint approximately 3.1 x 4.25 inches.

## Circuit and version history
- Original Holy Fire platform.
- The later Holy Fire 9 retains the wave-shaping concept but moves to a 9V external supply with internal voltage boosting.
- The classic 48V unit is a distinct production model, not a colorway of Holy Fire 9.

## Sound
The Holy Fire Classic ranges from clean boost and smooth overdrive into very heavy distortion/fuzz-like saturation. Premier Guitar's original review documents thick, bold gain with strong low-frequency retention, dynamic cleanup from the guitar volume control, and unusually low noise.
