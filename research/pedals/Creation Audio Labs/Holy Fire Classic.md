# Creation Audio Labs — Holy Fire Classic

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Creation Audio Labs
- **Catalog identity:** Holy Fire Classic
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive / Distortion / Boost
- **Identity:** Creation Audio Labs Holy Fire Classic, the original 48V version.

## What this pedal is
Holy Fire Classic is the original 48V version of Creation Audio Labs' Holy Fire, using analog wave-shaping to produce tube-like overdrive and distortion without a physical preamp tube.

## Controls
- Gain
- Overdrive
- Distortion
- Hi-Cut

## Switching and power
- True bypass in the documented classic configuration.
- Dedicated 48V DC supply.
- Compact enclosure.
- Heavy stainless-steel construction is documented.

## Version changes
- The later Holy Fire 9 uses a 9V external supply and internal voltage boosting.
- The 48V Classic is kept separate from Holy Fire 9.

## Sound
The Holy Fire Classic covers clean boost, smooth overdrive and very heavy distortion. Published reviews also describe strong low-end retention and low noise.
