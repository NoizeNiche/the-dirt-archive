# Creation Audio Labs - Holy Fire 9

## What this pedal is
Holy Fire 9 is the 9V successor to Creation Audio Labs' 48V Holy Fire, using the same analog wave-shaping concept in a pedalboard-compatible format.

## Controls
- **Gain:** clean boost range from off through approximately +12 dB.
- **Overdrive:** analog wave-shaping tube-simulator drive.
- **Distortion:** hard-edge clipping.
- **Hi-Cut (~):** progressively removes high frequencies for smoother, vintage-speaker-style response.

## Switching and power
- True bypass is the standard configuration; buffered bypass can be requested.
- 9V DC, approximately 250 mA, center-negative 5.5 x 2.1 mm connector.
- Manufacturer recommends using a high-current pedal-power output.
- Internal power boosting supplies elevated bipolar rails for the analog wave-shaping circuit.

## Circuit and version history
Holy Fire 9 combines the wave-shaping core of the original Holy Fire with the internal 9V power-boosting technology developed for the later Creation Audio Labs designs.

Compared with the original Holy Fire Classic:
- external supply changes from dedicated 48V operation to standard 9V pedal power
- circuit is retuned for the 9V-based high-headroom implementation
- the 9V version is a distinct model, not merely a cosmetic edition.

## Sound
Holy Fire 9 spans clean boost through smooth overdrive and thicker distortion. The manufacturer emphasizes touch sensitivity and preservation of guitar tone, while the Hi-Cut control can smooth harder distortion toward a creamier response.
