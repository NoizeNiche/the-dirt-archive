## Sound
The Lumen DESCRIPTION The Lumen is a State Variable FET-Based Overdrive.
It marks a return to the overdrive world for Hungry Robot Pedals.
The filter is actually made up of 3 individual filters (low pass, band pass, high pass) that split the incoming signal into 3 frequency ranges and amplifies those ranges without tone loss.
