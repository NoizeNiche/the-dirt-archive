## Sound
Whether you’re chasing gritty fuzz, lo-fi textures, or blown-out chaos, this pedal delivers a sound that feels alive and unpredictable.
The Amp Volume control dials in the amount of fuzz, while Woofer/Horn lets you blend between full-range and high-passed signals for different tonal textures.
Speaker Trouble introduces unpredictable breakup, simulating the sound of an amp on its last legs.
