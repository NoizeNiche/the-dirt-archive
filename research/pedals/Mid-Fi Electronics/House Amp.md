## What this pedal is
The House Amp is a lo-fi fuzz/noise pedal designed around unstable, broken-speaker-style textures. Mid-Fi describes it for raw garage, lo-fi and intentionally damaged amp sounds.

## Versions and factory options
- 100% true bypass.
- 9V DC operation.
- **Amp Volume** controls the fuzz amount.
- **Woofer/Horn** blends the frequency response between fuller-range and high-passed behavior.
- **Speaker Trouble** adds unpredictable breakup.

## Sound
House Amp is intentionally rough and unstable. The controls can move it from gritty fuzz into blown-speaker-like breakup, with Woofer/Horn shaping the tonal bandwidth.
