## What this pedal is
Cocoon is a low-to-mid gain fuzz designed by Doug Tuttle at Mid-Fi Electronics. Mid-Fi describes it as a spongy, gritty fuzz with an almost tube-amp-like attack and breakup.

## Sound
The Cocoon is a relatively low-to-mid gain fuzz with a spongy, gritty response. Mid-Fi describes an attack and breakup that feel closer to an overdriven tube amp than to a stiff, heavily compressed fuzz.
