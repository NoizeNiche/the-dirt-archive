## Sound
Home Effect Types Fuzz Big Muff variants BC239 Fuzz BC239 Fuzz Extra edgy & cutting sustainy fuzz A throwback to the late "rams head" era.
The BC239 transistor will tend to render less bass, resulting in a more biting and articulate muff tone.
Add to that the fact that the later rams heads employed plastic film capacitors instead of the softer, grittier tone of ceramic discs used in the triangle knobs and earlier rams heads, and you get a clear, cutting top end.
