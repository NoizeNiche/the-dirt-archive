Home Effect Types Fuzz Big Muff variants Juicy Pig Juicy Pig Powerful, dynamic, transparent, sustaining fuzz Another modification of the circa 1976 "rams head" Big Muff Pi*.
It is designed to enhance the clarity of the signal to provide all the gain and sustain of the original but with less buzz-saw grind and more amp-like transparency, especially in the juicy low end.
