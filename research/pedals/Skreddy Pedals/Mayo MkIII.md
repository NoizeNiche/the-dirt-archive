## Sound
Manual Skreddy Pedals™ Mayo MkIII: Reissue using better quality/higher-gain/lower-noise transistors than the 2006 version.
Heavy fuzz that does a great job at sounding like the tone in its namesake song "Mayonaise." High Gain, Creamy, Vintage-Style Fuzz/Distortion A vintage, 4-transistor distortion, made with a tasteful blend of NOS and new parts, resulting in a creamy, fat, circa 1973 fuzz tone ( but with some extra kick ).
Modern upgrades include SMALL SIZE , mid-boost switch, standard DC power, LED on/off indicator, and true bypass switching.
