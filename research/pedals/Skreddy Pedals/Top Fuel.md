## What this pedal is
Top Fuel is a Big Muff-derived high-gain distortion/fuzz from Skreddy Pedals. Skreddy describes it as a Big Muff design with a MOSFET input stage, MOSFET clipping devices, and a tone stack with prominent midrange boost.
