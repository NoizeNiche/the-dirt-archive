## What this pedal is

Pig Mine is a Skreddy Pedals fuzz designed for smooth, articulate, sustaining distortion. Skreddy describes it as a balanced four-transistor fuzz with a strong singing sustain rather than an intentionally extreme, uncontrollable fuzz.

## Sound

Pig Mine is voiced for smooth, intense sustain with enough articulation to keep chords and single notes defined. Skreddy describes the pedal as walking the line between aggressive and laid-back rather than simply maximizing gain.
