## Sound
Manual Reissue using better quality/higher-gain/lower-noise transistors than the 2004 version.
The tone of these transistors is nearly identical to the NOS units the original Mayonaise used (they look the same, too).
Made of the same types of parts as used in our first product ever, which was a sonic clone of the circa 1971 "triangle knob" version fuzz box.
