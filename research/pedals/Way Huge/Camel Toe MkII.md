## What this pedal is

The Camel Toe Mk II is a **dual overdrive pedal** that combines Way Huge's **Green Rhino** and **Red Llama** circuit concepts. The Llama side adds a high-cut control not present on the original standalone Red Llama.

## Sound

The pedal provides two distinct overdrive voices based on the Green Rhino and Red Llama circuits. The Llama side's added high-cut expands its treble control relative to the original Red Llama.
