## What this pedal is

The Smalls Pork & Pickle is the compact version of Way Huge's combined **Pork Loin overdrive and Russian-Pickle fuzz** concept. It keeps the two dirt voices in one pedalboard-friendly enclosure.

## Versions and factory options

The Smalls model is a separate compact product from the original full-size **Pork & Pickle WHE214**. The archive keeps those identities distinct.

## Sound

The Smalls Pork & Pickle combines the warm overdrive character of the Pork Loin circuit with the thicker fuzz character of the Russian-Pickle circuit. It is designed as a dual-purpose dirt pedal rather than a blend of two independent pedals.
