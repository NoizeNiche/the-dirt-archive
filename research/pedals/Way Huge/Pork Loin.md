## Sound
Together they sound three-dimensional, in a way that a single overdrive pedal into a single amp almost never does.
He called it the Pork Loin Soft Clip Injection Overdrive , gave it a model number of WHE201 , painted it purple, and — with a straight face — printed the phrase “the masses of pork power that exude from its space age circuitry” in the official product copy.
Underneath it is one of the most quietly intelligent overdrive designs of the modern era, and one of the very few that is genuinely useful to guitarists and bassists.
