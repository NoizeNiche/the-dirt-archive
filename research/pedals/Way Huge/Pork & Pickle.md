## What this pedal is
The original Way Huge Pork & Pickle, introduced in 2018 as WHE214, combines two existing Way Huge circuits in one bass-oriented pedal: the Pork Loin overdrive and Russian-Pickle fuzz. It is a switchable OD/fuzz design rather than a blend of both dirt circuits at once.

## Colorways
- The documented original WHE214 is the larger original enclosure. This record is separate from the later **Smalls Pork & Pickle** model.

## Sound
In **OD** mode, the pedal provides the Pork Loin-derived overdrive character. In **Fuzz** mode, it provides the Russian-Pickle-derived fuzz character. Clean Blend mixes the built-in clean path with the selected dirt circuit, while Clean Tone adjusts the tone of that clean signal.
