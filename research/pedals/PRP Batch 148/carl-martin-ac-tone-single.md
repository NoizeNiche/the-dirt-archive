# Carl Martin AC-Tone Single

- **Builder:** Carl Martin
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** AC-Tone Single
- **Colorways:** Brown finish with cream knobs is documented in current product photography and retail listings.
- **Versions:** The current AC-Tone Single is a compact one-channel version of the Pro Series AC-Tone platform.
- **Version changes:** Compared with the larger original AC-Tone platform, the Single uses one overdrive channel, a more compact enclosure, a slightly reinforced gain section, and a DC-DC converter that allows regulated 9V operation while the circuit operates internally at ±12V.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.
- **Diode type:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.
- **Controls / switching:** Level, Drive, Cut, and Bypass. The Cut control works as a high-frequency low-pass filter. The bypass is an active buffered circuit.
- **Construction / power:** Analog circuit; regulated 9V DC input, maximum 62 mA; no battery support. Internal operation is at ±12V.
- **Sound:** AC-Tone Single is voiced after the classic Vox AC30/Top Boost family, moving from chiming low-gain breakup to fuller British-style overdrive. The Cut control rolls back high-frequency content without functioning as a conventional full-range tone control, while the compact single-channel layout keeps the core AC-Tone character under straightforward foot-control.
- **Research confidence:** High for the current product layout, power architecture, control behavior, and stated AC30-oriented voicing.
