# Carella Guitars Overdistover

- **Builder:** Carella Guitars
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** Overdistover
- **Colorways:** The reviewed historical example uses a black-and-green metal enclosure; no formal colorway catalog was confirmed.
- **Versions:** The documented design combines three gain stages: overdrive, distortion, and a second overdrive usable as a boost. No numbered factory version was established.
- **Version changes:** No confirmed hardware revision was established from the reviewed historical material.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.
- **Diode type:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.
- **Controls / switching:** Three sections, each documented with Gain, Volume, and Tone; three footswitches; true bypass. The unit runs from a 9V power supply and does not use a battery.
- **Signal path / function:** The historical description places the three sections as overdrive -> distortion -> overdrive, with the second overdrive also usable as a boost.
- **Sound:** Overdistover is designed as a compact multi-stage dirt rig rather than one fixed voice. The documented configuration offers two overdrive flavors around a central distortion stage, allowing low-gain breakup, harder distortion, stacked gain, or a boost-like final stage while retaining a deliberately touch-sensitive response.
- **Research confidence:** High for the three-stage architecture, controls, switching, and 9V/true-bypass details because the historical account explicitly documents them; low for component-level claims.
