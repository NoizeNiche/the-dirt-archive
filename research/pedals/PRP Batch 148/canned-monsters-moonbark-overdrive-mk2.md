# Canned Monsters Moonbark Overdrive MK2

- **Builder:** Canned Monsters
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** Moonbark Overdrive MK2
- **Colorways:** The reviewed manufacturer page shows the leather-covered pedal; no formal factory colorway list was confirmed.
- **Versions:** The documented product identity is Moonbark Overdrive MK2. No separate numbered factory revision beyond the MK2 designation was established in the reviewed sources.
- **Version changes:** The reviewed manufacturer description does not provide a V1-to-MK2 change log. Do not infer one from the MK2 name alone.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented. The pedal is documented as using two LM386 integrated circuits.
- **Diode type:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.
- **Controls / switching:** Gain and Volume only; true bypass using a GORVA 3PDT footswitch.
- **Construction:** Two LM386 chips are connected together for high gain. The builder describes point-to-point construction and a leather-covered enclosure.
- **Sound:** Moonbark MK2 is designed primarily for bass and aims at the openness, clarity, and crunch of a cranked tube amp. Its two-knob interface is intentionally simple, while the LM386 high-gain arrangement gives it a large, loud dirt voice that can also be used with guitar.
- **Research confidence:** High for the current product identity, two-knob layout, LM386 construction, true bypass, and bass-first voicing.
