# Captain FX Yeti Fuzz

- **Builder:** Captain FX
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Catalog identity:** Yeti Fuzz
- **Colorways:** The reviewed production listing uses a fur-covered enclosure. No formal alternate-color catalog was confirmed.
- **Versions:** A 2018 Yeti Fuzz example is documented; the reviewed source does not establish numbered production revisions.
- **Version changes:** No confirmed factory circuit revision was established.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.
- **Diode type:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.
- **Controls / switching:** Volume, Fuzz, Tone, Starve, and Blend. True bypass. Runs from a 9V battery or power adapter.
- **Sound:** Yeti is a classic-fuzz-derived circuit modified with bass players in mind, and the lower settings can function as bass overdrive. The builder also positions it for guitar, particularly lower tunings and stoner, doom, and sludge styles, with Blend providing dry-signal preservation.
- **Research confidence:** High for controls, power, bypass, bass-oriented voicing, and the documented 2018 example; medium for the unspecified classic-fuzz lineage.
