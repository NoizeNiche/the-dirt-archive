# Carella Guitars Real Tube

- **Builder:** Carella Guitars
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Catalog identity:** Real Tube
- **Colorways:** No formal factory colorway list was confirmed in the reviewed sources.
- **Versions:** No numbered factory version was confirmed.
- **Version changes:** The reviewed historical material does not establish a numbered revision.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.
- **Diode type:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.
- **Controls / switching:** The historical Overdistover documentation identifies the Real Tube section as using the same Gain, Volume, and Tone control arrangement used by the three constituent stages.
- **Sound:** Real Tube is the distortion section used in Carella's Overdistover concept. Historical user documentation describes it as highly dynamic and sensitive to playing, with a large, defined distortion character that responds noticeably to picking and guitar level.
- **Research confidence:** Medium for the standalone Real Tube record because much of the surviving documentation describes it inside the Overdistover rather than as a separate standalone product.
