# Carella Guitars Kung Fuzz

- **Builder:** Carella Guitars
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Catalog identity:** Kung Fuzz
- **Colorways:** No formal factory colorway list was confirmed in the reviewed sources.
- **Versions:** The reviewed historical interview describes Kung Fuzz as a revised version of a classic Fuzz Face design. No numbered factory version was confirmed.
- **Version changes:** Carella describes revisions to parts of the design to make it more manageable and dynamic than a traditional Fuzz Face. No further numbered revision was established.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.
- **Diode type:** Not documented in the reviewed sources.
- **Controls / switching:** Full control labeling was not confirmed from the reviewed text sources.
- **Sound:** Kung Fuzz is described by Carella as a revised Fuzz Face intended to clean up more controllably with playing dynamics. The builder says it can cover booster, light-overdrive, and fuzz duties, making the traditional Fuzz Face concept more flexible.
- **Research confidence:** High for the Fuzz Face lineage and intended dynamic behavior; low for exact component details.
