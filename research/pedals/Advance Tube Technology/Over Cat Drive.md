## What this pedal is
Over Cat Drive is a discrete overdrive housed in a heavy metal enclosure. The documented design uses Gain, Tone and Output controls, true bypass, and a 9 V supply option. It is intended to add moderate saturation while retaining dynamics and note definition. [1][2]

## Colorways
- A black enclosure with orange graphic treatment is documented for an early production example. [2]
- An orange/red finish is also documented for the Over Cat Drive Red variant. [3]

## Versions and factory options
### Over Cat Drive

- 9 V battery or standard external DC supply [2]

## Sound
The standard Over Cat Drive is described as a moderate-gain overdrive with a persistent upper-mid emphasis and a slightly roughened texture even at low gain. Its Tone control works primarily through the upper-midrange while retaining substantial low-end and high-frequency definition. [1][2]
