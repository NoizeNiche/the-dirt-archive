## What this pedal is
Virus Drive is a dedicated distortion pedal in Advance Tube Technology's Cat Drive family. The manufacturer's surviving description emphasizes a 1970s-style, Zeppelin-associated character, with thick harmonic density and strong response to picking dynamics. [1]

## Sound
Advance Tube Technology describes Virus Drive as a thick, harmonically dense distortion with a distinctly 1970s, Zeppelin-associated character. The builder also emphasizes unusually strong touch sensitivity across the control range, while the Gain/Volume pairing covers a broad range of saturation levels. [1]
