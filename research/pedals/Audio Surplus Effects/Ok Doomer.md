# Audio Surplus Effects — Ok Doomer

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Ok Doomer
- **Builder:** Audio Surplus Effects
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Fuzz
- **Identity:** High-gain fuzz/distortion built from multiple cascading gain stages for an intentionally huge, collapsing wall-of-sound response.

## What this pedal is
Ok Doomer is a limited-run Audio Surplus Effects design centered on extreme gain. Secondary documentation describes later production as adding internal Input control and Filter control, while an alternate Slider Edition uses slider-style controls.

## Colorways
- Secondary-market examples document at least a **Black** finish.
- No reliable factory colorway family or named cosmetic revision list was verified.

## Versions and factory options
### Earlier documented production
- One large external control with additional controls hidden internally
- Heavy, cascading gain-stage architecture
- Limited-batch U.K. build

### V4.0+
- Internal **Input** control to reduce the amount of signal entering the circuit and therefore reduce overall gain
- Internal **Filter** control acting as a variable low-pass filter

### Slider Edition
- Slider-style controls are documented as a factory presentation variant.

## Version changes
The clearest documented production change is the V4.0+ addition of internal Input and Filter controls. A Slider Edition is also documented, but the available sources do not establish it as a separate circuit revision.

## Transistor
- **Technology:** Solid-state gain stages.
- **Exact device:** Not publicly documented in the sources checked.

## Diode
- **Type:** Exact clipping diode arrangement is not publicly documented.

## Sound
Ok Doomer is designed for a nearly overloaded, wall-of-sound distortion/fuzz character with enormous gain and sustain. Reviews and demos place its sound in the broad territory of stoner/doom fuzz and high-gain distortion, while the internal Input and Filter controls let the player pull the pedal back from maximum saturation.
