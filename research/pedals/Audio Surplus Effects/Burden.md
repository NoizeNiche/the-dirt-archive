# Audio Surplus Effects — Burden

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Burden
- **Builder:** Audio Surplus Effects
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Fuzz
- **Identity:** High-gain fuzz/distortion designed for dark, sustained doom, sludge, drone and noise applications, with independent controls for output, gain, treble filtering and low-end density. [1]

## What this pedal is
Burden is described as a high-volume, amp-bothering fuzz/distortion built around a variable low-pass filter and a dedicated Density control for the low end. The design is explicitly aimed at maximum sustain and dark, gloomy tones. [1]

## Controls
- **LEVEL:** Overall volume.
- **GAIN:** Overall gain.
- **FILTER:** Variable low-pass filter. Turning counter-clockwise cuts treble.
- **DENSITY:** Low-frequency amount. Turning counter-clockwise cuts bass. [1]

## Circuit / functional architecture
- High-gain solid-state fuzz/distortion.
- Variable low-pass filtering.
- Independent low-end Density control.
- Designed to retain a thick, heavy response while allowing the low end to be tightened. [1]
