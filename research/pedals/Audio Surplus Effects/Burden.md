## What this pedal is
Burden is a high-volume, high-gain amp-bothering fuzz/distortion built around a small control set: Level, Gain, Filter and Density. The documented design emphasizes a variable low-pass filter plus a low-end Density control, with the builder describing it as a pedal for maximum sustain and dark, gloomy tones.

## Sound
Burden is designed around large amounts of gain and sustain, with the Filter able to darken the treble and the Density control shaping how loose or tight the low end feels. The result is intended to stay thick and menacing while moving from murky, bass-heavy textures toward tighter heavy riffing.
