## What this pedal is

MI Audio describes Version 2 as the latest iteration of the original Crunch Box, first released in 2005. The V2 adds a new lower-gain mode, a fully active three-band EQ, higher-headroom operation, and a redesigned output section. [1]

## Controls

- **GAIN:** adjusts overdrive amount and interacts with the CLIP and MODE switches. [1]
- **VOL:** output level. [1]
- **CLIP:** three-position clipping selector: Standard, Open, Compressed. [1]
- **MODE:** three gain/voicing modes: Low Gain, High Gain, Mid Gain. [1]
- **PRES.:** high-cut/presence control, renamed from the earlier Tone control. [1]
- **LOW / MID / HI:** active three-band EQ. [1]

## Circuit / architecture

The CLIP switch selects different clipping elements in the final gain stage. Standard reproduces the original Crunch Box clipping character, Open is less distorted with greater punch/output, and Compressed is the highest-gain, smoother setting. [1]

The MODE switch changes not just gain but frequency shaping before and during distortion generation. The new Low Gain mode emphasizes the upper spectrum to preserve pickup character and string detail, while High Gain retains the original Crunch Box voicing and Mid Gain sits between the two. [1]

MI Audio documents a three-stage active EQ voiced around Plexi/JCM800 frequency ranges, with approximately ±12 dB of adjustment per band and relatively isolated bands. [1]

## Power and physical specification

- **Power:** 9V battery or 9VDC negative-center supply only. [1]
- **Current draw:** approximately 20mA. [1]
- The manufacturer explicitly warns that higher voltage can damage the V2 circuit. [1]
- **Switching:** The verified manual establishes the pedal's operating controls but does not provide a complete component-level switching specification in the material checked.

## Version history

- **Crunch Box:** original production design released in 2005. [1]
- **Super Crunch Box:** expanded the Crunch Box concept with additional controls and internal higher-voltage circuitry in the development history. [2]
- **Super Crunch Box V2:** latest documented production iteration with the new Low Gain mode, active three-band EQ, revised clipping selector, and external presence control. [1][2]

## Sound

MI Audio describes V2 as retaining the original Crunch Box high-gain voice while expanding it with lower-gain response, more pickup-sensitive voicing, three clipping characters, and precise active EQ. [1]
