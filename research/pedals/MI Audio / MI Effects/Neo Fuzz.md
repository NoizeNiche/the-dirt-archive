## What this pedal is
MI Audio describes the Neo Fuzz as a configurable vintage-style fuzz intended to expose parameters that traditional fuzz circuits often leave fixed. The design allows the player to change pickup loading, transistor bias, gain range, midrange, and low/high balance. [1][2]

## Circuit / architecture
The Neo Fuzz uses **two hand-selected germanium transistors** in the documented v2 form. [3] The Load control changes input impedance, the Bias control changes the transistor operating point, and an internal gain trimmer limits the maximum gain. [1][3]

## Controls
- **FUZZ:** amount of fuzz. [1][3]
- **LOAD:** variable input impedance/pickup loading. [1][3]
- **BIAS:** transistor operating point; settings span symmetrical, asymmetrical, and gated-like behavior. [1][3]
- **BODY:** dedicated midrange control. [1][3]
- **TONE:** balances low and high frequency content. [1][3]
- **VOLUME:** output level. [1][3]
- **TAME:** three-position high-frequency control. [1][3]
- **Internal GAIN trimmer:** limits the gain range. [3]

## Sound
Independent testing describes the Neo Fuzz as unusually dynamic for a fuzz, spanning colored boost, vintage fuzz, and more extreme biased/gated textures while offering extensive control over pickup interaction and frequency shaping. [1][2]
