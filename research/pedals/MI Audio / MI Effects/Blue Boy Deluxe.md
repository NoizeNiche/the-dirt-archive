## What this pedal is
MI Audio describes the Blue Boy Deluxe as a low- to medium-gain overdrive designed around a broad gain range and multiple tone-shaping controls. The documented version expands the simpler Blue Boy design with separate brightness, midrange, and character controls. [1][2]

## Controls
- **GAIN:** wide overdrive gain range. [1]
- **VOLUME:** output level. [1]
- **TONE:** a low/high balancing control rather than a simple treble cut. [1][2]
- **MID:** dedicated midrange control. [1][2]
- **BRIGHTNESS:** high-frequency trim for matching different amps. [1][2]
- **CHARACTER:** changes the overdrive feel/low-end response from tighter to fuller, softer behavior. [1][2]

## Circuit / architecture
MI Audio documents the Blue Boy Deluxe as using a Tube Zone-derived tone-control approach. The Tone control balances low and high frequencies in opposite directions, while the separate Brightness and Mid controls further shape the response. The Character control modifies the response of the overdrive itself rather than operating only as a post-fuzz EQ. [1][2]

## Sound
MI Audio characterizes the Blue Boy Deluxe around smooth, singing low/mid-gain overdrive with reduced compression and a wide tonal range. Independent coverage describes the control set as capable of spanning transparent overdrive through softer fuzz-like textures. [1][2]
