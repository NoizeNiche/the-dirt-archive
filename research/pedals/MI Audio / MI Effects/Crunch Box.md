## What this pedal is

MI Audio describes the Crunch Box as a compact distortion designed to deliver a large, British amp-style crunch. The v3 manual documents a simple three-control interface of Gain, Tone, and Volume plus an internal Presence control. [1]

## Circuit / architecture

The manufacturer describes the Crunch Box as a multiple-clipping design in which the signal is clipped three times. MI Audio says its broad relationship to the Tube Zone comes from this progressive clipping concept, while the Crunch Box is voiced with a stronger mid presence. [1]

## Controls

- **GAIN:** distortion amount. [1]
- **TONE:** overall tone shaping. [1]
- **VOLUME:** output level. [1]
- **Internal PRESENCE:** high-frequency adjustment for matching the pedal to an amplifier. [1]

## Transistor

- **Exact production transistor/device:** Unknown.
- The verified manufacturer manual does not establish a production transistor part number.

## Diode

- **Documented clipping approach:** three clipping stages. [1]
- Independent references describe the Crunch Box as using LED clipping, but the exact production LED part specification is not established in the cited manufacturer manual. [2]

## Version history

- **Original Crunch Box:** released in 2005 according to MI Audio's Super Crunch Box V2 manual. [3]
- **v3:** documented by the MI Effects owner manual, with Gain, Tone, Volume, and internal Presence. [1]
- **Super Crunch Box:** later expanded the Crunch Box concept with more controls, additional gain modes, and selectable clipping structures. [3]
- The archive keeps Crunch Box and Super Crunch Box as separate catalog identities because the manufacturer treats them as distinct products/iterations. [3]

## Power and switching

- **Power:** 9V battery or 9VDC external supply. [1]
- **Current draw:** external independent pedal documentation lists less than 5mA for the Crunch Box; this is treated as secondary rather than manufacturer-primary evidence. [2]
- **Switching:** true bypass is documented for the later Crunch Box/Super Crunch Box family materials. [1][3]

## Sound

MI Audio characterizes the Crunch Box around a large, tight, British-style distortion voice with strong mid presence. The v3 manual describes the lower-gain range as punchy and throaty, while the overall design is intended to capture a high-gain Marshall-style preamp feel. [1]
