## What this pedal is

MI Audio describes the Super Blues Pro as an expanded version of its Blues Pro overdrive, designed around low- to mid-gain overdrive with additional clipping, EQ, and gain-shaping options. [1][2]

## Version development

The manufacturer identifies the Super Blues Pro as a newer revision of the Blues Pro, with added **BODY, TRIM, DETAIL**, and clipping-structure controls. [1][2]

## Circuit / architecture

The design is rooted in MI Audio's interpretation of the Tube Screamer-style overdrive family. Internal voltage multiplication raises a 9V input to approximately 18V for increased headroom. [1][2]

The two clipping switches independently select clipping structures using **SILICON, MOSFET, or NONE**, allowing different clipping devices on the two halves of the waveform. MI Audio also states that the two silicon diode paths use different diode types to introduce slight asymmetry. [1]

## Controls

- **Volume**
- **Gain**
- **Detail**
- **Body**
- **Trim**
- **Two clipping switches:** Silicon / MOSFET / None [1]

Gain and Trim affect different gain portions of the circuit. Detail shapes high/low content, Body adjusts bass/low-mid emphasis, and the clipping switches change the compression, attack, and symmetry of the drive signal. [1]

## Power and physical specification

- **Input power:** 9V battery or 9VDC center-negative supply. [1]
- **Internal voltage supply:** 18VDC. [1]
- **Current draw:** 10mA. [1]
- **Input impedance:** 500kΩ. [1]
- **Output impedance:** below 25kΩ. [1]
- **Size:** 55.9 × 109.2 mm. [1]
- **Switching:** heavy-duty 3PDT true bypass. [1]

## Sound

MI Audio positions the Super Blues Pro from low-gain boost and blues overdrive through compressed, fuzz-like tones, with the clipping switches and Body/Trim controls altering feel, frequency balance, and compression. [1][2]
