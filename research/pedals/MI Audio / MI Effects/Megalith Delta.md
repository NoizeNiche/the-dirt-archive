## What this pedal is

MI Audio describes the Megalith Delta as a solid-state pedal model of the Megalith Beta amplifier preamp, using JFETs to emulate the gain stages of the amplifier's valves. [1]

## Circuit / architecture

MI Audio says the design was developed around the operating points, dynamic range, clipping thresholds, output impedance, and intrinsic capacitances needed to reproduce the Beta preamp response rather than simply substituting FETs into a tube schematic. The pedal contains approximately 110 components and uses a four-layer board with ground planes for shielding. [1]

The V2 design includes internal voltage doubling to 18VDC and a speaker-simulation output on the output jack's ring contact. [2]

## Controls

- **Volume**
- **Gain**
- **Contour**
- **Treble**
- **Middle**
- **Bass**
- **EQ Shift** switch
- **Boost** footswitch [2]

The Boost footswitch moves the pedal from its base mid-gain range to higher gain with tighter response and greater upper-mid emphasis. Contour is implemented through a phase-inverter-like stage intended to reproduce the amplifier's response. [1][2]

## Power and physical specification

- **Input power:** 9V battery or 9VDC supply. [2]
- **Internal voltage supply:** 18VDC. [2]
- **Current draw:** 8mA. [2]
- **Input impedance:** 500kΩ. [2]
- **Output impedance:** below 25kΩ. [2]
- **Dimensions:** 117 × 92 mm. [2]
- **Switching:** heavy-duty 3PDT true bypass. [2]

## Sound

MI Audio positions the Megalith Delta as a high-gain distortion design covering rock, crunch, and extreme high-gain tones, with the stock voice described as thick in the low mids. The Boost mode tightens the response for higher-gain sounds. [1][2]
