## What this pedal is

MI Audio identifies the Tube Zone as its first commercial pedal, with the first unit built in 1995. The design was intended to reproduce the progressive clipping of multiple gain stages in a high-gain tube preamp rather than concentrating the distortion in a single clipping stage. [1][2]

## Circuit / architecture

The Tube Zone uses **four clipping stages**, reflecting the multiple gain stages of a high-gain tube preamp. [2] The Drive control simultaneously affects the gain of these stages, allowing the pedal to cover low-gain and high-gain operation. [2]

## Controls, v4

- **VOLUME:** output level. [2][3]
- **DRIVE:** amount of gain across the clipping stages. [2]
- **BRIGHT:** high-frequency shaping; external by v4. [3]
- **TONE:** low/high balancing control. [2][3]
- **MIDS:** continuous midrange control on later versions. [3]
- **CHARACTER:** pre-overdrive low-frequency/gain-character control. [1][3]
- **Internal PRESENCE trim:** additional high-frequency EQ. [3]

## Version history

- **v1:** initial three-knob generation. [1][2]
- **v2:** added three-position mid switching; Character became an internal control. [1][3]
- **v4:** Bright, Tone, Mids, Character, Volume, and Drive external controls, with internal Presence trim. [3]
- Later prototype/v5 material is documented, but not treated as a standard production revision without stronger factory evidence. [4]

## Power and switching

- **Power:** 9V battery or DC power supply, with up to 25V headroom documented for v4-era units. [2][5]
- **Switching:** true bypass. [3][5]
- **Status:** discontinued. [4][5]

## Sound

MI Audio describes the Tube Zone around valve-amp-like response, multiple progressive clipping stages, touch sensitivity, and a broad gain range. Independent version documentation describes v4 as capable of moving from smoother/clearer overdrive into thicker, more compressed and fuzzy high-gain textures. [1][3]
