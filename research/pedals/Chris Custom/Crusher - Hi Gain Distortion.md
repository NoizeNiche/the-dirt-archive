## What this pedal is
Chris Custom's official product page describes Crusher as a high-gain distortion designed for powerful, dense sounds suited to hard rock and extreme styles. The builder also says it can serve as a booster. [1]

## Sound
The builder characterizes Crusher as a dense, aggressive high-gain distortion with clear note definition and broad tonal adjustment. The five-band-style control layout lets the player shape low, mid, and high response around the high-gain core. [1]
