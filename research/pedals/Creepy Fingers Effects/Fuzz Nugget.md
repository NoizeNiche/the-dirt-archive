## What this pedal is
Fuzz Nugget is a modernized take on 1960s 1.5V fuzz boxes, explicitly descended from the **Maestro FZ-1A** concept but redesigned for modern pedalboard use.

## Controls
- **Attack / Fuzz:** reshaped from the original-style control to provide a broader gain range, from low gain through full fuzz.
- **Bottom:** tone control that ranges from the thin buzz of the original-style sound toward a much heavier low-end response.
- The exact surviving sources do not establish additional control labels reliably.

## Circuit and components
- The fuzz circuit itself runs at approximately **1.5V** internally to retain the characteristic squishy, sagging response.
- Production versions use **NOS silicon transistors**, replacing the original germanium approach for improved temperature stability.
- The transistors are re-biased for additional sustain and gentle gating.

## Switching and power
- Modern improvements include true bypass switching, 9V power input and LED indication.
- A battery eliminator/power jack allows pedalboard operation while the fuzz circuit retains its 1.5V internal operating behavior.
- Battery operation is supported.

## Versions and factory options
- The current version is a modernized redesign of the earlier Maestro-inspired circuit.
- Exact circuit revision dates beyond the documented modernization were not established.

## Sound
Fuzz Nugget covers thin, raspy 1960s-style fuzz through heavier, lower-end-rich textures, while the 1.5V internal circuit preserves a soft, sagging response. The re-biased silicon stages provide more stable operation and additional sustain/gating.

The Fuzz Nugget is just that: a tasty little morsel of fuzz wrapped in a big old gold box, Eatin’ Dust and just waiting to be unleashed on the world.
Well, let the unleashing begin… The Fuzz Nugget began its life as a straight up one-to-one Clone (of the Universe) of the Maestro Fz-1a, using all germanium transistors and running on an actual AA battery.
This new (ish) version remedies those issues and aims to create a better catch-all 60’s fuzz.
