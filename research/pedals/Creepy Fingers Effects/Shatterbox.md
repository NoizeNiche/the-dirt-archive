# Creepy Fingers Effects — Shatterbox

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Shatterbox
- **Builder:** Creepy Fingers Effects
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Creepy Fingers' Bartell Shatterbox-inspired fuzz.

## What this pedal is
Shatterbox is based on the obscure Bartell Shatterbox fuzz. Creepy Fingers states that Brad Davis studied three Bartell examples and found circuit variations among them, then combined the biasing of a one-knob/two-transistor example with the three-transistor circuit to create a more aggressive interpretation. [1]

## Colorways
- A documented 2024 run used a black enclosure with special white Dakaware knobs and a matching red circuit board. [1]
- The builder states that this color scheme was limited to 27 pieces. [1]
- Other used examples are documented in black finishes. [2][3]

## Versions and factory options
### Shatterbox 2024 variation
- Three-transistor fuzz circuit
- Pair of NOS Japanese 2SB54 transistors
- Custom white Dakaware knobs
- Matching red circuit board
- 9V battery operation
- More aggressive sustain than the original-style response described by the builder
- Less gating and fewer sonic artifacts than the original-style response described by the builder [1]

Other used examples are described as three-transistor germanium circuits, including a 2024 unit reported with the same RCA transistor type as an original. [3]

## Version changes
The builder explicitly describes the 2024 model as a color scheme and circuit variation. The archive keeps these documented production variations under the Shatterbox parent rather than splitting each run into a separate top-level pedal.

## Transistor
- **Technology:** Germanium. [1][3]
- **Exact transistor/device:** The documented 2024 variation uses a pair of NOS Japanese 2SB54 transistors. Other examples are reported with different transistor choices, so the 2SB54 detail is not generalized to every Shatterbox. [1][3]
## Diode
- **Type:** Exact production clipping/rectifier diode(s) were not publicly established in the reliable sources checked.
- **Exact part:** Unknown.

## Sound
The builder describes the 2024 variation as having aggressive sustain with less gating and fewer original-style artifacts, spanning a range from the rough, cutting character associated with the Maestro FZ-1 to the smoother crunch of a MkI Tone Bender. Used-market descriptions also emphasize very loud, gated and sputtery fuzz behavior. [1][2]
