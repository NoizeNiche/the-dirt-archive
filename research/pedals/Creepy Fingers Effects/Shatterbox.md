## What this pedal is
Shatterbox is Creepy Fingers Effects' interpretation of the vintage Bartell Shatterbox concept, using a compact high-gain fuzz design.

## Circuit and components
- Three-transistor fuzz design.
- A documented run uses NOS **RCA transistors** associated with the original-style design.

## Sound
Shatterbox is voiced toward aggressive, gated fuzz with a very strong output and a vintage character. Contemporary examples are described as sitting between Maestro FZ-1-style rasp and Tone Bender MkI territory, with pronounced sputter and velcro-like attack.
