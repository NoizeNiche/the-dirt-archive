## What this pedal is

Brummie is Creepy Fingers' pedalized tribute to the British heavy-music tradition, designed around the sound of a germanium booster driving a fully cranked tube amplifier.

## Sound

Creepy Fingers describes Brummie as touch-sensitive dynamic crunch with throaty British mids and smooth treble, covering classic rock and early heavy-metal territory.
