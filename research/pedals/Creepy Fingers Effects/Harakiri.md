# Creepy Fingers Effects - Harakiri

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Creepy Fingers Effects
- **Catalog identity:** Harakiri
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Exact Creepy Fingers Effects Harakiri.

## What this pedal is
Harakiri is Creepy Fingers Effects' take on the classic Univox / Shin-ei Superfuzz family.

## Controls
- **Volume:** overall output.
- **Fuzz:** overall fuzz intensity.
- **Scoop:** blends or adjusts the midrange character in place of the original Superfuzz-style switch.
- Later examples may also document internal adjustment of the octave response; exact production extent is not established for every specimen.

## Circuit and components
- Superfuzz-derived fuzz architecture.
- Brad Davis has described Harakiri as one of the more time-consuming Creepy Fingers builds because of its component count.
- Historical examples are documented with NOS transistors; a contemporary specialist source identifies Matsushita 2SC828 transistors in the circuit.
- Exact diode type is not established by the checked sources.

## Switching and power
- The checked historical listings document 9V battery or standard power-adapter operation on some examples.
- Exact power implementation can vary by production run, so no universal internal power architecture is asserted.

## Versions and factory options
- Production examples exist in multiple finishes, including sparkle red and white.
- A later example is documented with an adjustable octave-balance trimmer.
- These are retained as production/specimen differences rather than silently collapsed into one exact internal layout.

## Version changes
Available evidence shows control and adjustment differences among examples, but does not establish a complete chronological circuit-revision history.

## Transistor
- NOS transistor use is documented.
- A specialist historical source identifies **Matsushita 2SC828** devices in Harakiri examples.
- Exact transistor population for every production run is not established.

## Diode
- Exact diode type or part number: **not established**.

## Sound
Harakiri is a Superfuzz-family fuzz with strong volume and fuzz output and an adjustable scoop control for shaping the midrange character. The design retains the ragged, aggressive Superfuzz attack while allowing more continuous tone adjustment than the original switching arrangement.

## Construction and provenance
- Built by Brad Davis / Creepy Fingers Effects.
- Standard Creepy Fingers pedals were handwired; the builder has also described standard models as PCB builds with some point-to-point work.
