# Creepy Fingers Effects — Harakiri

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Harakiri
- **Builder:** Creepy Fingers Effects
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Creepy Fingers Effects' Superfuzz-family fuzz, named in reference to the Univox Superfuzz's Japanese roots.

## What this pedal is
Harakiri is Brad Davis's take on the Univox/Shin-ei Superfuzz family. The builder's own interview explains that the name refers to the circuit's Japanese roots, and surviving descriptions identify the pedal as a three-control Superfuzz-style fuzz with Volume, Scoop and Fuzz controls.

## Colorways
- Surviving examples include a white enclosure with red/orange graphics and a documented green-finish example.
- Color/finish differences are treated as cosmetic variants unless a circuit change is explicitly documented.

## Version changes
Used-market references span multiple years and finishes, but the available evidence does not establish a clean numbered hardware revision. A Reverb listing identifies a 2019 example, while the builder interview discusses the Harakiri as one of Creepy Fingers' earlier time-intensive builds. These are treated as production-era evidence, not separate catalog identities.

## Transistor
- **Technology:** The pedal follows the Superfuzz-family design. A secondary reference identifies original Matsushita 2SC828 transistors in the Harakiri circuit, but this is not independently confirmed by builder documentation. [2]
- **Exact transistor/device:** Matsushita **2SC828** reported by secondary reference; treat as externally sourced rather than builder-confirmed. [2]

## Sound
Harakiri is built for the large, searing and octave-rich character associated with the Univox/Shin-ei Superfuzz family, while the Scoop control lets the player shape the prominent midrange/filter behavior rather than relying only on the original-style fixed filter arrangement. Contemporary user references describe it as a very aggressive Superfuzz voice with substantial volume and fuzz range.
