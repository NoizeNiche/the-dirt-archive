## What this pedal is
Creepyface is Creepy Fingers Effects' second production model, released in **2008**, and built around a germanium Fuzz Face-style circuit.

## Controls and operation
- **Bottom control:** tunes the low end from tighter response to a larger/sludgier low-frequency voice.
- The exact surviving source does not provide a trustworthy complete transcription of the remaining front-panel labels, so no additional control names are guessed.

## Circuit and components
- **2 x RCA 2N404** germanium transistors are explicitly documented as hand-selected military parts.
- NOS capacitors and carbon-composition resistors are documented.
- An internal charge-pump circuit permits external modern negative-ground pedal power while preserving the ability to interact directly with a battery when the charge-pump bypass switch is used.

## Power and switching
- Externally powerable through the charge-pump circuit.
- Compatible with modern negative-ground pedal power supplies.
- Internal switch can remove the charge pump for direct battery operation.
- True-bypass style external operation is not explicitly stated in the checked manufacturer text, so the archive does not infer it.

## Sound
Creepy Fingers describes the Creepyface as a clear, lush Fuzz Face-style fuzz with crystalline cleanup from the guitar volume control. The low-end control can move from tighter fuzz toward very large/sludgy response.
