## What this pedal is
Lonely Heart is a hand-wired fuzz based on the solid-state fuzz circuits found in the Vox UL730 and Vox 7120 amplifiers.

## Controls
- **Volume:** overall output level.
- **Midrange:** shapes the midrange filter.
- **Sustain:** controls fuzz gain and sustain.
- **Internal trim:** adjusts gating/decay/sputter behavior.

## Sound
Lonely Heart produces a high-gain, spitty fuzz character derived from the Vox UL730/7120 amplifier circuits. The midrange filter lets the player move between more mid-forward vintage voicing and more scooped tones, while the internal trim controls gating, decay and sputtering behavior.
