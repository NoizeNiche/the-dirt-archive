# Creepy Fingers Effects — Lonely Heart

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Lonely Heart
- **Builder:** Creepy Fingers Effects
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Creepy Fingers' hand-wired fuzz based on the solid-state fuzz circuitry used in the Vox UL730 and 7120 amplifiers.

## What this pedal is
Lonely Heart recreates the character of the fuzz circuit found in the Vox UL730 and 7120 amplifiers. Effects Database and dealer/used-market descriptions identify it as a high-gain, spitty fuzz with an adjustable midrange filter and an internal control for gating. [1][2][3]

## Colorways

## Sound
Lonely Heart is a high-gain, spitty fuzz with a midrange-shaping control that can push the voice toward a more focused or more scooped character. The internal gate adjustment affects decay and sputter, giving the pedal a broad range from sustained vintage-style fuzz to sharper gated textures. [1][2]
