## What this pedal is
Blue Mood is a fuzz based on Creepy Fingers' preferred iteration of the Tone Bender MkII family, specifically the Marshall Supafuzz. The design adds a **Decay** control for bias/sustain behavior and a **Bottom** control that blends between a bass-heavy, high-output voice and the original treble-forward character.

## Sound
Blue Mood is voiced around the aggressive, sustaining and sputtering character associated with the Marshall Supafuzz, with enough control range to move from more treble-forward fuzz toward a thicker, bass-heavier presentation. The Decay control changes the bias and therefore the length and character of sustain/sputter, while Bottom changes the low-end balance and output feel. [1][2]
