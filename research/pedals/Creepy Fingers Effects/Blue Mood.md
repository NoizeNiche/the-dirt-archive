## What this pedal is
Blue Mood is Creepy Fingers Effects' take on the Marshall Supafuzz / favored Tone Bender MkII family, using a modern hybrid silicon/germanium design.

## Controls
- **Fuzz:** controls the amount of fuzz.
- **Decay:** controls circuit bias and fine-tunes sustain and sputter length.
- **Bottom control:** blends the bass-heavy, higher-output voicing with the original treble-forward character.

## Circuit and components
- Tone Bender MkII / Marshall Supafuzz-inspired circuit.
- Blend of silicon and germanium transistors.
- Charge-pump circuitry establishes the appropriate ground reference for the germanium devices while allowing modern power-supply use.
- Wima and Nichicon capacitors are documented.

## Sound
Blue Mood is voiced around the sputtering, psychedelic character of the Marshall Supafuzz/Tone Bender MkII family, with a treble-forward core and a more forceful bass-heavy blend available from the bottom control. The decay control lets the player move the bias response toward different sustain and sputter behavior.
