## What this pedal is
Blacklight Fuzz combines the character of the Baldwin Burns Buzzaround and Elka Dizzytone families in a three-transistor cascading fuzz design.

## Controls
- **Timbre:** balances bright/stinging treble against fuller low end.
- **Balance:** output/tone interaction control documented by the builder.
- **Sustain:** ranges from subtle crunch through maximum fuzz.
- The surviving manufacturer page does not provide a complete numerical control range, so no values are guessed.

## Circuit and components
- Three cascaded transistor stages.
- First two positions use **silicon transistors** for lower noise and greater temperature stability.
- Third position uses an **NOS SFT-type germanium transistor**.
- A selected **germanium diode** is paired with the germanium transistor stage.
- WIMA film, Styroflex and Nichicon audio-grade capacitors are documented in the signal path.

## Switching and power
- True bypass.
- Common negative-tip 9V power.
- LED indication.
- Internal circuit is designed for modern negative-ground pedalboard power.

## Versions and factory options
- Limited artwork/finish presentation is part of the model's identity.
- No separate circuit revision was established from the checked sources.

## Sound
The builder describes Blacklight as offering huge sustain and broad fuzz texture, with the Timbre control moving from stinging treble toward fat low end. The mixed silicon/germanium architecture is intended to retain stability and noise performance while preserving rich sustain and cleanup from the guitar volume.
