# Creepy Fingers Effects — Modern Sound

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Modern Sound
- **Builder:** Creepy Fingers Effects
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Creepy Fingers' silicon Fuzz Face-family design with a selectable alternate voicing.

## What this pedal is
Modern Sound is Brad Davis's tribute to the move from germanium Fuzz Face circuits to silicon versions. The builder describes a classic silicon Fuzz Face core with a scooped, bright voice plus a Mod switch that adds midrange, bass, and output. [1]

## Colorways
- The builder uses a three-color silkscreen scheme inspired by vintage finishes. [1]
- A 2024 limited run is documented with a black textured enclosure and a specific graphic layout. [2]
- Cosmetic differences are treated as colorways unless a circuit change is documented.

## Versions and factory options
### Standard Modern Sound
- Volume
- Fuzz
- Mod switch
- Silicon transistor circuit
- Internal input pre-gain trimmer
- Internal bias trimmer
- True bypass switching
- 9V power input
- Switchcraft jacks
- Three-color silkscreen on custom steel enclosure [1]

### Modern Sound NOS BC183 (2024)
A limited 2024 run is documented with two NOS BC183 transistors. The Reverb listing describes this run as warmer through the midrange than a BC108C-based example. [2]

## Version changes
The Mod switch is part of the core Modern Sound design. It changes the frequency balance and output while the builder states that transistor bias remains the same in both settings. The 2024 NOS BC183 run is treated as a documented production variation under the Modern Sound parent rather than a separate pedal identity. [1][2]

## Sound
Modern Sound provides the bright, scooped and aggressive character associated with early silicon Fuzz Face designs, then adds a Mod position with more bass, midrange and output for higher-output pickups and heavier mixes. The internal pre-gain and bias trims let the player tighten the response and choose between smoother volume-control cleanup and more gated behavior. [1]
