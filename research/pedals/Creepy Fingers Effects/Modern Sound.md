## What this pedal is
Modern Sound is Creepy Fingers Effects' silicon Fuzz Face-inspired take on the transition from early germanium Fuzz Faces to later silicon versions.

## Controls
- **Fuzz:** reverse-audio-taper control changes fuzz amount with a smoother response.
- **Mod switch:** raises bass and midrange content and increases output for heavier music and higher-output pickups.
- **Internal pre-gain trim:** adjusts input pre-gain.
- **Internal bias trim:** sets the balance between linear guitar-volume cleanup and gated texture.

## Circuit and components
- Silicon Fuzz Face-style architecture.
- Transistor bias remains the same between the two external voicings.
- Circuit refinements address sweet-spot range and treble oscillation.
- Exact baseline transistor part numbers and diode type are not established for the entire model.

## Sound
Modern Sound delivers the bright, aggressive and somewhat scooped character associated with silicon Fuzz Face circuits. The Mod switch adds bass, mids and output, while the internal trims let the player tighten the response or introduce more gated character.
