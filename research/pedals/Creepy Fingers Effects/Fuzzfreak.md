## What this pedal is
Fuzzfreak is a high-gain fuzz built around a Schmitt-trigger approach. The builder describes it as delivering maximum fuzz with a gated attack, with the **Freak** control changing the intensity of the effect's bite.

## Sound
Fuzzfreak is intentionally extreme: maximum fuzz, strong gating and a sharp, ripping attack are central to the design. The Freak control changes the intensity of that bite, making the pedal useful for abrasive, compressed and highly saturated riff textures rather than restrained overdrive-style drive. [1]
