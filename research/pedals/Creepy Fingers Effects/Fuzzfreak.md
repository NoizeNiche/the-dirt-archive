## What this pedal is
Fuzzfreak is a Creepy Fingers Effects collaboration with artist Jesse California, built around a high-gain Schmitt-trigger fuzz circuit.

## Sound
Fuzzfreak is designed for maximum, high-gain fuzz with a strongly gated attack. The Freak control changes the intensity of that biting, ripping response rather than turning the pedal into a low-gain drive.
