## What this pedal is
Hold Tight is Creepy Fingers Effects' version of the Tone Bender MkI, built as a blend of silicon and germanium transistor stages.

## Controls
- **Filter:** tames the spit and crackle of the fuzz.
- **Attack:** controls the squish and bloom of the response.
- **Volume:** provides the output level.
- The checked sources do not establish a full model-wide control transcription beyond these documented functions.

## Sound
Hold Tight is voiced around the reckless, unruly response of a Tone Bender MkI while giving the player more control over spit and crackle. The filter can smooth the attack while the attack control changes the sense of squish and bloom, with substantial volume available.
