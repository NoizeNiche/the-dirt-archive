## What this pedal is
Crazy Creepy is Creepy Fingers Effects' controlled take on the 1970s Guyatone Crazy Face/Fuzzie/Crazy Tone family, designed around the wild bias and gating behavior found in those budget Japanese fuzz variants.

## Controls
- **Fuzz:** smooth-taper control for different amounts of fuzz.
- **Crazy:** sweeps from more Fuzz Face-like response toward increasingly overbiased, gated behavior.
- **Internal DIP switch:** selects the external-power charge-pump arrangement or vintage-style unfiltered 9V battery operation.

## Circuit and components
- Pair of NOS **Matsushita 2SB54 germanium transistors**.
- Internal charge-pump circuitry supports modern external supplies while allowing the vintage battery arrangement.

## Sound
With the Crazy control turned toward the Fuzz Face side, Crazy Creepy produces a rich, smoky germanium texture with guitar-volume cleanup. Turning it toward the overbiased side brings ragged, gated and psychedelic sputter, with the control allowing the two behaviors to be blended.
