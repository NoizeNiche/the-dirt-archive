# Creepy Fingers Effects — Crazy Creepy

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Crazy Creepy
- **Builder:** Creepy Fingers Effects
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Creepy Fingers' modernized reproduction of the 1970s Guyatone Crazy Face/Fuzzie/Crazy Tone family.

## What this pedal is
Crazy Creepy is based on the 1970s Guyatone Crazy Face, a budget-oriented relative of the Fuzz Face family whose production variations could produce heavily overbiased, gated fuzz. Creepy Fingers' version uses a pair of NOS Matsushita 2SB54 germanium transistors and adds controls intended to move between smoother Fuzz Face territory and more gated, overbiased vintage behavior.

## Sound
The Crazy control lets the pedal move from a smoother, richer Fuzz Face-like response into deliberately overbiased, gated and ragged psychedelic fuzz. The Fuzz control covers multiple shades of saturation, while the germanium transistor pair is intended to retain the smoky texture and guitar-volume cleanup associated with the vintage inspiration.
