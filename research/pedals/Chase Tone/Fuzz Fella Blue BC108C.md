## What this pedal is
Chase Tone describes the Fuzz Fella Blue BC108C as a limited-edition, pedalboard-friendly BC108C high-gain fuzz that can move from thick, sustaining fuzz to overdrive and glassy clean tones through its controls and the guitar's volume knob. [1]

The builder emphasizes a temperature-stable, low-noise PCB design and a full-width set of external mini controls for rig matching. [1]

## Sound
The builder documents a high-gain silicon Fuzz Face-style response with thick sustain, strong harmonic density, and unusually effective cleanup through the guitar volume control. The Mids, Feel, and Bass controls expand it beyond the usual two-knob Fuzz Face format and help adapt it to different guitars, amplifiers, and wah placement. [1]
