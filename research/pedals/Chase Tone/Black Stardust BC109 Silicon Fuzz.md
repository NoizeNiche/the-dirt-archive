# Chase Tone — Black Stardust BC109 Silicon Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Black Stardust BC109 Silicon Fuzz
- **Builder:** Chase Tone
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Chase Tone Black Stardust BC109C Silicon Fuzz, a temperature-stable silicon Fuzz Face-style fuzz with extended bias and headroom controls.

## What this pedal is
Chase Tone describes the Black Stardust as a clear, complex BC109 silicon fuzz intended to cover clean, overdrive, crunchy fuzz, and sustaining high-gain sounds. [1]

## Colorways
- **Black Stardust** translucent star-dust powder coat is the defining documented finish. [1]
- No complete factory colorway sequence was established.

## Versions and factory options
Controls:
- Volume
- Fuzz
- Bias
- Headroom [1]

Chase Tone also documents rear-facing input/output jacks, standard 9V center-negative power, and a pedalboard-friendly trapezoidal enclosure. [1]

No formal numbered factory revision sequence was established.

## Transistor
The builder explicitly specifies **NOS cherry-picked BC109 silicon transistors**. [1]

## Diode
The builder documents a red LED indicator, but no exact clipping-diode specification was established.

## Sound
The Black Stardust is voiced toward clear, dynamic silicon fuzz with the Bias control ranging from warm/gated textures to more open, punchy responses. Headroom reduces low end and can improve guitar-volume cleanup and compatibility with wah and humbuckers. [1]
