## Sound

Chase Tone describes Black Stardust as clear and harmonically complex, covering clean-ish gain, overdrive, crunchy fuzz and sustaining higher-gain fuzz. Bias changes operating feel, while Headroom can reduce low end and improve cleanup and wah compatibility. [1]
