# Chase Tone — Candy Apple Red Glazed Germanium Fuzz Face

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Candy Apple Red Glazed Germanium Fuzz Face
- **Builder:** Chase Tone
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Chase Tone Candy Apple Red Germanium Fuzz Face, an individually tuned germanium Dallas-Arbiter-style Fuzz Face replica.

## What this pedal is
Chase Tone describes the Candy Apple Red Germanium Fuzz Face as an individually custom-built and tuned Dallas-Arbiter-style germanium Fuzz Face replica. The builder emphasizes a pure vintage-oriented organic fuzz response with added range for sustain and feedback. [1]

## Colorways
- **Candy Apple Red Glazed** finish is explicitly documented. [1][2]
- No complete factory colorway sequence was established.

## Versions and factory options
The documented controls include:
- Output
- Fuzz
- Temperature / Temper adjustment [1]

The current Fuzz Fella Candy Apple Red Germanium page also documents:
- Fuzz
- Mids
- Feel
- Bass
- Reverse polarity or battery operation [2]

Because the builder explicitly describes that page as the original Candy Apple Red circuit and sound, the archive treats those as the documented later pedalboard-friendly implementation rather than automatically rewriting the vintage replica's control set. [2]

## Version changes
Chase Tone explicitly describes the current Candy Apple Red Fuzz Fella as the original Candy Apple Red Germanium circuit and sound, with expanded practical controls. [2]

## Transistor
The exact-model builder documentation establishes a germanium Fuzz Face-style circuit but does not publish an exact universal transistor part number for every unit. [1][2]

## Diode
No exact clipping-diode specification was established.

## Sound
The Candy Apple Red is aimed at organic, thick germanium Fuzz Face response, with guitar-volume cleanup and bias/temperature adjustment to compensate for germanium operating conditions. [1][2]
