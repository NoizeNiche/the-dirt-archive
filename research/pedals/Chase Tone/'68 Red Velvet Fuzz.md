# Chase Tone — '68 Red Velvet Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** '68 Red Velvet Fuzz
- **Builder:** Chase Tone
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Pedalboard-friendly silicon recreation of the 1968 Dallas-Arbiter BC183 Red Fuzz Face, using hand-picked BC183C transistors. [1][2]

## Controls
- **VOLUME**
- **FUZZ**
- **MIDS**
- **FEEL / BIAS**
- **BASS** [1]

The expanded controls adapt the classic two-knob Fuzz Face concept for modern pedalboard rigs.

## Circuit lineage
- **Primary reference:** 1968 Dallas-Arbiter Red Fuzz Face.
- Chase Tone states that the later Fuzz Fella Red BC183C uses the same circuit and components as the '68 Red Velvet, with cosmetic changes only. [1][2]

## Transistor
- **Hand-picked BC183C silicon transistors.** [1][3]
- Exact gain matching values are not published.

## Diode / clipping
- Exact clipping/protection diode part number is not publicly documented.

## Power
- **9V center-negative** or **9V battery**. [1]

## Version history
- Limited '68 Red Velvet edition.
- Fuzz Fella Red BC183C is documented by Chase Tone as the same electronic circuit/components with different cosmetics. [2]
- No additional numbered electronic revision established.

## Construction / finish
- Sweet Red Velvet powder-coat finish.
- Pedalboard-friendly enclosure. [1]

## Sound
The builder targets thick, harmonically complex 1960s silicon Fuzz Face response with substantial guitar-volume cleanup. Feel/Bias and the expanded EQ controls increase the usable range beyond a stock vintage Fuzz Face. [1]
