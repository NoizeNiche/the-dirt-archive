# Chase Tone — '68 Red Velvet Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** '68 Red Velvet Fuzz
- **Builder:** Chase Tone
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Chase Tone '68 Red Velvet Fuzz, a pedalboard-friendly silicon recreation of the 1968 Dallas-Arbiter red Fuzz Face.

## What this pedal is
Chase Tone describes the '68 Red Velvet Fuzz as a limited-edition, pedalboard-friendly version of its 1968 Dallas Arbiter BC183 Red Fuzz Face replica. The builder states that the later Fuzz Fella Red BC183C uses the exact same circuit and components, with cosmetic changes only. [1][2]

## Colorways
- **Sweet Red Velvet** powder-coat finish is documented for the '68 Red Velvet edition. [1]
- A separate Fuzz Fella Red BC183C cosmetic edition is documented as the same circuit with a different enclosure appearance. [2]

## Versions and factory options
Documented controls include:
- Volume
- Fuzz
- Mids
- Feel / Bias
- Bass [1]

The pedal is designed to operate on standard 9V center-negative supply or a 9V battery. [1]

## Version changes
The builder explicitly states that the Fuzz Fella Red BC183C is electronically identical to the '68 Red Velvet Fuzz, with cosmetic changes only. [2]

## Transistor
Chase Tone specifies **hand-picked BC183C silicon transistors**. [1][3]

## Diode
The checked builder documentation does not establish a specific clipping-diode part number.

## Sound
Chase Tone positions the pedal as a thick, harmonically complex 1960s Fuzz Face-style fuzz that can clean up dramatically from the guitar volume control. The Feel/Bias and EQ controls expand the practical range beyond a stock vintage Fuzz Face. [1]
