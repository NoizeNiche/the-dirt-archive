# Chase Tone — Silver Stardust BC183/2L Silicon Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Silver Stardust BC183/2L Silicon Fuzz
- **Builder:** Chase Tone
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Chase Tone Silver Stardust BC183/2L Silicon Fuzz, a silicon Fuzz Face/Tone Bender hybrid with expanded bias and headroom control.

## What this pedal is
Chase Tone describes the Silver Stardust as a silicon fuzz combining elements of Fuzz Face and Tone Bender-style response, with an external Bias control spanning several vintage-inspired textures. [1]

## Colorways
- **Silver Stardust** finish is the defining documented enclosure treatment. [1]
- No complete factory colorway sequence was established.

## Versions and factory options
Controls:
- Volume / EQ
- Fuzz
- Bias
- Headroom [1]

The builder maps Bias settings to MKI Tone Bender, Eric Johnson-style, late-1960s Fuzz Face, and MKII Tone Bender-style ranges. [1]

## Version changes
No formal numbered factory revision sequence was established.

## Transistor
The builder explicitly identifies the design as silicon and names the **BC183/2L** family in the model itself. [1]

## Diode
No exact-model clipping-diode specification was established.

## Sound
The Silver Stardust is designed to move from thick, gated fuzz into smoother Fuzz Face-like tones and more aggressive Tone Bender-like crunch. Headroom reduces low end and can improve guitar-volume cleanup and wah compatibility. [1]
