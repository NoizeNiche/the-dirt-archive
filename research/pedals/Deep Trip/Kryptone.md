## What this pedal is

Kryptone is a modern Deep Trip interpretation of the first-generation Vox Tone Bender family. The design is intended to cover classic British fuzz while also reaching overdrive and boost territory.

## Sound

Kryptone targets the tighter, brighter and more textured side of early Tone Bender and Fuzz Face-style fuzz. It can move from fuzzy overdrive into stronger saturation and can also be used as a boost.
