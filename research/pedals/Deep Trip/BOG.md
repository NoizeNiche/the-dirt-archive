## What this pedal is

The BOG is Deep Trip's Fuzz Face-inspired fuzz designed around the tonal character associated with Band of Gypsys-era Jimi Hendrix sounds, with additional high-end clarity.

## Sound

BOG is designed for thick, full fuzz with substantial low end and rounded highs. Deep Trip emphasizes a broad range from controlled fuzz into more aggressive saturation and boost-like behavior.
