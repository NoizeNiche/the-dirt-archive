## What this pedal is

Effects Database documents the Acetone Fuzz as a hand-built Ace-Tone Fuzzmaster FM-2 clone with a **Dry Blend** control and a smaller enclosure. [1]

The same source documents three identified pedal versions, **V1, V2, and V3**, indicating that the model had multiple production revisions. [1]

## Versions and factory options

Documented controls/features include:
- Volume.
- Dry Blend.
- High-pass / low-pass filter footswitch.
- True bypass.
- 9V operation. [1]

The high-pass setting emphasizes treble and upper harmonics; low-pass retains more bass and is described as useful for bass applications. [1]

## Sound

The Acetone Fuzz has strong high-frequency bite in high-pass mode and much greater low-frequency retention in low-pass mode. The Dry Blend mixes the original guitar signal back into the distorted path, allowing the user to retain attack and low-end definition under heavy fuzz. [1]
