## What this pedal is
Reverb has an exact product page for **Devi Ever : FX US Fuzz**. Its product details identify the model as a red US Fuzz from the 2010s, made in the United States, and classify it under distortion and fuzz effects.

## Design / sound evidence
The Reverb product description states that Devi Ever began producing boutique effects from a Portland workshop in 2009 and stopped production in 2013, after which Dwarfcraft Devices manufactured Devi Ever pedals.

For the exact US Fuzz, the same source states that the circuit uses the same power arrangement as the Torn's Peaker but has a **distinctive gate at the end of the tone**.

A separate layout-analysis source documents a community recreation of the US Fuzz and discusses an Intensity control affecting how much signal reaches the fuzz stage. Because that evidence concerns a layout rather than a verified factory specimen, the archive does not use it to assert an original production control list.
