# Devi Ever FX — Hyperion 2

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Hyperion 2

- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Devi Ever FX Hyperion 2.

## What this pedal is
Effects Database's exact-model entry identifies the **Hyperion 2** as a fuzz pedal and states that it is like the original Hyperion with an added control for oscillation.

## Controls
- **Volume:** controls the output and has substantial gain available.
- **Oscillation:** engages and changes the pitch and texture of oscillation.
- **Intensity:** changes fuzz intensity and character.

## Technical evidence
The exact-model Effects Database record documents:
- **True bypass**.
- **Silicon transistor based** construction.
- **9V operation** through a 2.1 mm negative-tip power jack.
- An internal **9V battery snap**.
- Approximate enclosure dimensions of **4.7 x 3.7 x 1.18 inches**.
- Boutique-quality components.
- The source characterizes current draw as very low but does not provide a precise milliamp figure.

A PedalPCB technical discussion independently identifies the Hyperion 2 design and describes an oscillation control added around the power/ground section. Because this is an aftermarket circuit-analysis/build discussion rather than manufacturer documentation, the archive does not generalize its component observations into factory specifications.

## Sound / operation
The model's defining functional addition over the original Hyperion is the oscillation control, which changes the pitch and texture of the oscillation. A 2012 exact-model demo also exists in Effects Database's media record.
