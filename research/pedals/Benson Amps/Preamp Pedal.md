# Benson Amps — Preamp Pedal

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Preamp Pedal
- **Builder:** Benson Amps
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive / Fuzz / Preamp
- **Identity:** FET-based preamp/drive pedal derived from the Benson Chimera amplifier, with Gain, Volume, Treble and Bass controls. [1][2]

## Controls
- **GAIN**
- **VOLUME**
- **TREBLE**
- **BASS** [1]

## Circuit lineage / architecture
- Based on the **Benson Chimera** amplifier concept.
- FET transistors substitute for the amplifier's tube gain stages.
- Designed as an amp-like preamp capable of boost, overdrive and fuzz. [1]

## Transistor
- **Technology:** FET gain stages.
- Exact FET part numbers are not publicly documented.

## Diode / clipping
- Exact clipping/protection device and part number are not publicly documented.

## Power
- **9V DC center-negative or 9V battery**.
- Approximately **50 mA** current draw. [1]

## Version history
- No numbered electronic revision established.
- Multiple finishes, including CME-exclusive examples, are cosmetic variants unless a circuit change is documented.

## Sound
The Preamp is intended to behave more like a gain stage in an amplifier than a fixed-voice distortion pedal. Gain can move from clean boost into overdrive and fuzz, while Bass and Treble provide broad shaping around the core FET response. [1][2]
