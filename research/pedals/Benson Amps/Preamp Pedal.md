# Benson Amps - Preamp Pedal

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Preamp Pedal
- **Builder:** Benson Amps
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz / Overdrive
- **Identity:** Chimera amplifier-inspired FET preamp/drive pedal.

## What this pedal is
The Benson Preamp is based on the Benson Chimera 30-watt guitar amplifier, with FET transistors substituted for the amplifier's vacuum tubes. Benson positions it as an amp-like clean boost, overdrive, and fuzz with rich harmonics and musical EQ.

## Sound
The Preamp is designed to feel more like an amplifier stage than a conventional fixed-voice dirt box. Its four controls cover clean boost, overdrive, and fuzz territory while the Bass and Treble controls provide broad tone shaping.
