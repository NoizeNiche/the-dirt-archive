# Benson Amps — Germanium Preamp Pedal

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Germanium Preamp Pedal
- **Builder:** Benson Amps
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive / Fuzz / Preamp
- **Identity:** Benson Preamp platform with its first FET gain stage replaced by a Germanium stage derived from the Germanium Boost design. [1][2]

## Controls
- **GAIN**
- **TREBLE**
- **BASS**
- **VOLUME** [1]

## Circuit architecture
- Retains the basic Benson Preamp control/EQ platform.
- First FET gain stage is replaced by a **Germanium** gain stage.
- The result is slightly lower gain but a richer/harmonic response than the standard FET Preamp. [1]

## Transistor
- **Technology:** Germanium first gain stage.
- Exact Germanium transistor part number is not publicly documented.

## Diode / clipping
- Exact clipping/protection diode type and part number are not publicly documented.

## Power
- **9V DC center-negative or 9V battery**.
- Approximately **50 mA** draw. [1]

## Version history
- Distinct Germanium Preamp model, not merely a finish change to the standard Preamp.
- No numbered electronic revision established.

## Sound
Benson positions the Germanium Preamp as a slightly lower-gain but richer-feeling alternative to the standard Preamp. The Bass/Treble controls preserve the broad amp-like shaping of the platform while the Germanium first stage changes the gain texture. [1][2]
