# Benson Amps - Germanium Preamp Pedal

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Germanium Preamp Pedal
- **Builder:** Benson Amps
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz / Overdrive
- **Identity:** Benson Preamp platform with the first FET gain stage replaced by a germanium gain stage.

## What this pedal is
The Germanium Preamp is based on Benson's standard Preamp pedal but replaces its first FET transistor with a germanium gain stage derived from the Germanium Boost design. Benson describes it as a slightly lower-gain but richer-feeling version capable of overdrive, boost, fuzz, and related gain textures.

## Sound
Compared with the regular Preamp, the Germanium version is described as slightly lower gain but richer in feel and harmonic character. Its four controls retain the broad amp-like EQ and gain range of the original Preamp platform.
