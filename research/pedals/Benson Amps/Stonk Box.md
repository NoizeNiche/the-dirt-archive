# Benson Amps - Stonk Box

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Stonk Box
- **Builder:** Benson Amps
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Temperature-controlled germanium fuzz based on a modified Tone Bender MK1 concept.

## What this pedal is
Stonk Box takes a classic 1960s MK1 fuzz direction and adds Benson's automatic thermal-bias system. Benson retains the original Filter and Volume controls and adds Tone and Trim for a wider range of fuzz and distortion textures.

## Colorways
- Standard textured gray/silver production finish is documented.
- **Cast Iron Edition** is documented as a named limited finish/edition and is kept as a factory presentation variant.

## Versions and factory options
### Stonk Box
- Filter
- Volume
- Tone
- Trim
- Automatic thermal bias
- 9V DC center-negative only
- No battery
- Less than 100mA while heater is engaged

### Cast Iron Edition
- Named limited-edition finish/presentation.
- No separate circuit revision was established in the checked sources.

## Version changes
The Cast Iron Edition is a named finish edition. The electrical design remains the Stonk Box architecture documented by Benson.

## Transistor
- **Factory devices:** Two germanium transistors, **2N404 or 2N527**, automatically thermally biased.
- Trim controls the gain immediately ahead of the two amplifying germanium stages.

## Diode
- **Exact diode part numbers:** Not publicly documented.

## Sound
Filter changes both gain and the bias point of the first stage, reducing bass and gain as it is turned down and introducing a dynamic-expander-like response. Tone acts as a low-pass/tilt-style control, while the high output level can push an amplifier hard; Benson explicitly notes that some noise is part of the primitive fuzz character.
