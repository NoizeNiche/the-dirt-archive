# Benson Amps — Stonk Box

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Stonk Box
- **Builder:** Benson Amps
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Thermally stabilized Germanium fuzz derived from an early-1960s MK1/Tone Bender-family concept, using two 2N404/2N527-class devices and Benson's automatic thermal-bias system. [1][2]

## Controls
- **FILTER:** Alters both tonal filtering and first-stage gain/bias behavior.
- **VOLUME:** Output level.
- **TONE:** High-frequency/tilt-style shaping.
- **TRIM:** Factory/user-adjustable gain feeding the Germanium stages.
- Automatic thermal bias. [1]

## Circuit architecture
- Two matched Germanium gain devices.
- Automatic thermal-bias/heater system stabilizes transistor operating conditions.
- The design keeps the primitive interaction of an early MK1 fuzz while reducing the instability associated with temperature-sensitive Germanium devices. [1][2]

## Transistor
- **Factory devices:** Benson documents **2N404 or 2N527** Germanium devices.
- Devices are selected/matched and thermally stabilized. [1]
## Diode / clipping
- Exact diode part numbers are not publicly documented.

## Power
- **9V DC center-negative only**.
- **No battery.**
- Less than **100 mA** while the heater is engaged. [1]

## Version history
- Standard Stonk Box.
- **Cast Iron Edition:** named finish/edition, kept as a factory presentation variant with no independently verified circuit change.
- No numbered electrical revision established.

## Sound
The Filter control is unusually interactive, changing low-frequency content and the gain/bias behavior of the first stage. Tone adds further high-frequency shaping. Benson describes the result as early-60s-style Germanium fuzz with intentionally primitive/variable texture, and the thermal-bias system is intended to make that response more consistent. [1][2]
