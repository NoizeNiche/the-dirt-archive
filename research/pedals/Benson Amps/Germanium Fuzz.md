# Benson Amps - Germanium Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Germanium Fuzz
- **Builder:** Benson Amps
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Temperature-controlled germanium fuzz with input-buffer/pickup-simulation circuitry and an impedance control.

## What this pedal is
Benson's Germanium Fuzz is a 1960s-style germanium fuzz redesigned to reduce temperature drift and the classic low-input-impedance problems of the topology. Benson adds a class-A input buffer, pickup-simulator network, and Impedance control while automatically thermal-biasing the germanium transistors.

## Colorways
- **Studio Black** is documented for indoor use.
- **Solar White** is documented as the sunlight-oriented alternative.
- The two finishes are factory color variants of the same model.

## Versions and factory options
### Germanium Fuzz
- Volume
- Gain
- Impedance
- Automatic thermal bias
- Internal factory trimmer
- 9V DC center-negative only
- No battery
- Approximately 70mA while heater is engaged

## Version changes
Studio Black and Solar White are documented finish variants. No numbered electrical revision was established in the checked sources.

## Transistor
- **Factory devices:** Two germanium transistors, either **2N527** or **2N404**, graded for gain/leakage and matched.
- Automatic thermal-bias circuitry warms the devices to a target operating point.

## Diode
- **Exact diode part numbers:** Not publicly documented.

## Sound
The circuit is tuned to span clean boost, overdrive, and aggressive fuzz, with some octave overtones at higher gain. Impedance changes the effective loading to recreate the tighter, sparkly sound normally obtained by rolling down a guitar volume control.
