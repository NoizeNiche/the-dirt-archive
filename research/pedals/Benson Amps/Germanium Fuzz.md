# Benson Amps — Germanium Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Germanium Fuzz
- **Builder:** Benson Amps
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Temperature-controlled Germanium fuzz with two matched 2N527/2N404-class devices, an input buffer/pickup simulator and external Impedance control. [1][2]

## Controls / architecture
- **VOLUME**
- **GAIN**
- **IMPEDANCE**
- Automatic thermal-bias system.
- Internal factory trim. [1]

## Circuit architecture
- Two Germanium gain transistors are automatically thermally biased.
- A Class-A input buffer and pickup-simulator network address the loading behavior normally associated with classic Germanium fuzz.
- Impedance control changes the apparent source loading/response. [1]

## Transistor
- **Factory devices:** Two matched Germanium transistors identified by Benson/independent product documentation as **2N527 or 2N404**.
- Devices are selected for gain/leakage and thermally biased to reduce temperature-related drift. [1][2]

## Diode / clipping
- Exact clipping/protection diode part numbers are not publicly documented.

## Power
- **9V DC center-negative only.**
- **No battery.**
- Approximately **70 mA** while the heater is engaged. [1]

## Version history / finishes
- **Studio Black** and **Solar White** are factory finish variants of the same thermal-bias design.
- No numbered electrical revision established.

## Sound
The design spans clean boost, overdrive and aggressive fuzz. Impedance recreates part of the tighter/sparkling behavior associated with changing guitar-volume loading without requiring the player to roll the instrument volume down. Higher gain can produce pronounced upper-octave overtones. [1][2]
