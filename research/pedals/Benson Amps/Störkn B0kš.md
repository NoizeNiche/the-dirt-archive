# Benson Amps — Störkn B0kš

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** Störkn B0kš
- **Builder:** Benson Amps
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** David Torn collaboration derived from Stonk Box, with expanded Bass control and a factory positive-feedback oscillation path. [1][2]

## Controls

- **TONE**
- **FILTER**
- **BASS**
- **VOLUME**
- **TRIM**
- Oscillation footswitch.
- Bypass footswitch. [1]

## Circuit architecture

- Retains the Stonk Box's automatically thermally biased Germanium fuzz concept.
- Germanium devices are documented as **2N404 or 2N527**.
- A positive-feedback path and diode network create the oscillation effects.
- Bass control expands the low-frequency response beyond the original Stonk Box concept. [1][2]

## Transistor

- **Factory devices:** Germanium **2N404 or 2N527**, automatically thermally biased. [1]

## Diode / clipping

- Benson documents a **diode network in the oscillation path**.
- Exact diode part numbers are not published. [1]

## Power

- **9V DC** operation.
- Exact current draw is not established in the reviewed exact-model sources.

## Version history

- Störkn B0kš is the David Torn collaboration/augmented model, not a cosmetic revision of Stonk Box.
- Textured gray/silver is the documented standard presentation.
- No numbered electrical revision established.

## Sound

The added Bass control moves from a leaner, Zonk-like response toward a wider MK1-style low end. Engaging the oscillation path can create feedback tones, sub-bass, synth-like filtering and unstable self-oscillation textures. [1][2]
