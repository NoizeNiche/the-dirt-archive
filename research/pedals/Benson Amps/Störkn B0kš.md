# Benson Amps - Störkn B0kš

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Störkn B0kš
- **Builder:** Benson Amps
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** David Torn collaboration built from the Stonk Box concept with expanded bass and a positive-feedback oscillation path.

## What this pedal is
Störkn B0kš is Benson's modified/augmented Stonk Box, created with David Torn. It adds more bass for down-tuned instruments and a positive-feedback switch with a diode network that creates oscillation, sub-bass, synth-like filter effects, and unstable textures.

## Version changes
Störkn B0kš is itself the augmented collaboration version of Stonk Box rather than a cosmetic revision. Its defining changes are the variable bass range and positive-feedback/oscillation circuit.

## Transistor
- **Factory devices:** Germanium transistors, 2N404 or 2N527, automatically thermally biased.

## Diode
- **Factory function:** Benson documents a diode network in the oscillation path.
- **Exact diode part numbers:** Not publicly documented.

## Sound
The added Bass control moves between the leaner Zonk-like low end of the stock Stonk concept and the wider bass response of an MK1-style fuzz. The positive-feedback switch can introduce oscillation, sub tones, synth-like filtering, and deliberately unstable feedback behavior.
