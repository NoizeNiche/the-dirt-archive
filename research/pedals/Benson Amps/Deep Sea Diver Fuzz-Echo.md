# Benson Amps — Deep Sea Diver Fuzz-Echo

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** Deep Sea Diver Fuzz-Echo
- **Builder:** Benson Amps
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz / Echo
- **Identity:** Jessica Dobson collaboration combining a three-transistor silicon fuzz with a PT2399-based echo, with selectable fuzz/echo order. [1][2]

## Controls / architecture

- **BIAS:** Fuzz-stage operating/bias character.
- **GATE:** Gate/compression behavior.
- **ECHO:** Echo mix/level.
- **VOLUME:** Overall output.
- **FEEDBACK:** Echo regeneration.
- **RATE:** Echo timing/rate.
- Fuzz-before-echo or echo-before-fuzz can be selected at power-up.
- Momentary footswitch function can drive echo feedback. [1]

## Circuit architecture

### Fuzz
- Three-transistor **silicon** fuzz.
- Benson describes the fuzz as drawing from Mk1.5 and Mk2-style ideas. [1]

### Echo
- **PT2399-based** digital echo section.
- Echo placement can be switched before or after the fuzz stage. [1][2]

## Transistor

- **Technology:** Silicon, three-transistor fuzz.
- Exact transistor part numbers are not publicly documented.

## Diode / clipping

- Exact clipping/protection diode type and part number are not publicly documented.

## Power

- **9V DC center-negative only.**
- **No battery.**
- Benson documents operation under 100 mA in the product specifications. [1]

## Version history

- No numbered electronic revision established.
- Fuzz/echo order selection and momentary feedback are core production functions.

## Sound

The fuzz covers treble-boost-like aggression, thick wooly textures and gated/skronky sounds. The echo section adds controllable repeats and feedback, while the selectable signal order changes whether the echo feeds the fuzz or the fuzz feeds the echo. [1][2]
