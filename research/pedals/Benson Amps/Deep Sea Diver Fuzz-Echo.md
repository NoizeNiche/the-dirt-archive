# Benson Amps - Deep Sea Diver Fuzz-Echo

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Deep Sea Diver Fuzz-Echo
- **Builder:** Benson Amps
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Jessica Dobson signature fuzz/echo combining a silicon three-transistor fuzz with a PT2399-based echo.

## What this pedal is
Deep Sea Diver Fuzz-Echo is a signature pedal developed with Jessica Dobson of Deep Sea Diver. Benson describes the fuzz as a silicon three-transistor variant drawing from Mk1.5 and Mk2 ideas, paired with a tuned PT2399 echo section.

## Sound
The fuzz can move from aggressive treble-boost territory through thick dark texture and gated skronk, while the echo adds short or long feedback-driven repeats. Benson specifically describes the fuzz as capable of wooly, straightforward, gated, and highly textural sounds, with the fuzz/echo order selectable for different behavior.
