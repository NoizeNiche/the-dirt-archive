# Benson Amps — Portable Distortion 424 MKII

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** Portable Distortion 424 MKII
- **Builder:** Benson Amps
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Preamp
- **Identity:** Pedal recreation of the gritty preamp/distortion section of a cassette 4-track **Tascam Porta 424 MKII**-style recorder, with a bypassable input buffer. [1][2]

## Controls / architecture

- **TREBLE**
- **FADER**
- **BASS**
- **TRIM**
- **LO Z** switching.
- Bypassable input buffer. [1]

## Circuit lineage

- **Primary reference:** cassette 4-track 424 MKII recorder preamp.
- The design aims to reproduce the non-linear preamp/distortion behavior of the recorder rather than a conventional guitar amp distortion circuit. [1]

## Version history

- **MKII** is the current documented model generation.

- No numbered sub-revision established.

## Sound

The 424 MKII can span cleaner boost, treble-focused bite, warm/dark drive and intentionally chaotic cassette-preamp grind. The buffer switch changes the relationship between the source and the recorder-style input stage, allowing different pickup/source responses. [1][2]
