# Catalinbread Effects Dirty Little Secret Deluxe

- **Builder:** Catalinbread Effects
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** Dirty Little Secret Deluxe
- **What this pedal is:** The Deluxe expansion of Catalinbread's Dirty Little Secret Marshall-style amp-in-a-box. The circuit goes beyond the earlier DLS preamp by adding a phase inverter, power-amp simulation, output transformer, and a built-in boost.
- **Colorways:** Standard production finish. Current Catalinbread also lists a limited purple edition, but that is a cosmetic edition of the same named product and is not a separate main-catalog model here.
- **Versions:** Dirty Little Secret Deluxe is a distinct Deluxe development of the Dirty Little Secret family. Catalinbread's manual describes it as what began as a fourth version of the flagship DLS circuit.
- **Version changes:** Compared with the earlier DLS family, the Deluxe adds a full amp-style signal path, external Super Lead/Super Bass selection, external Presence, a footswitchable boost, selectable boost order, a Tightness control, and a dedicated Line Out.
- **Transistor type:** The circuit is described as all-discrete, with a MOSFET-driven phase inverter and power-amp simulation. Exact transistor part numbers are not specified in the reviewed documentation.
- **Diode type:** Not documented in the reviewed manufacturer documentation.
- **Controls / switching:** Master, Preamp, Presence, Treble, Middle, Bass, Boost Vol, Boost footswitch, Bypass footswitch, external Super Lead/Super Bass toggle, internal effect-order switch, and internal Tightness control.
- **Power:** 9V DC, center-negative, at least 7mA.
- **Sound:** Catalinbread designed the Deluxe around the feel of a cranked Marshall Plexi, including the compression and sag associated with the power section rather than stopping at a preamp imitation. Super Lead emphasizes a cutting upper-midrange character, while Super Bass is smoother with more lower-mid emphasis; the boost can add another gain stage for more saturated sounds.
- **Research confidence:** High for the architecture, controls, and stated Marshall target; moderate for undocumented internal component details.
