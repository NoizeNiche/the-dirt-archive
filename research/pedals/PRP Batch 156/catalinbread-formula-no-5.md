# Catalinbread Effects Formula No. 5

- **Builder:** Catalinbread Effects
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** Formula No. 5
- **What this pedal is:** Catalinbread's early Tweed-oriented Formula overdrive, designed around the reedy, touch-sensitive character of 1950s tweed amplifiers. The current product is sold as the Formula No. 5 Reissue.
- **Colorways:** Standard current reissue finish. Catalinbread also lists a Spaceship Art edition as a cosmetic edition of the same Formula No. 5 family.
- **Versions:** Original Formula No. 5; current Formula No. 5 Reissue; documented Spaceship Art cosmetic edition.
- **Version changes:** The current reissue is presented as a revival of the original Formula 5 concept and keeps the simple three-control layout. No unsupported internal component change was inferred between the historical and current versions.
- **Transistor type:** Catalinbread describes the circuit as using tube-simulation methods but does not publish an exact transistor family or part number in the reviewed product material.
- **Diode type:** Not documented.
- **Controls / switching:** Volume, Tone, Gain.
- **Power:** 9-18V DC in the current reissue documentation.
- **Sound:** The Formula No. 5 is centered on low-to-medium-gain tweed breakup, with a loose, reedy character that responds strongly to the guitar's volume and picking attack. Catalinbread describes Tone and Gain as interactive, creating multiple useful sweet spots rather than a fixed EQ curve.
- **Research confidence:** High for the Formula 5 identity and current reissue relationship; moderate for undocumented original-production circuit details.
