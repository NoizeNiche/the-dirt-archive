# Catalinbread Effects Fuzzrite

- **Builder:** Catalinbread Effects
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Catalog identity:** Fuzzrite
- **What this pedal is:** Catalinbread's recreation of the classic Mosrite Fuzzrite silicon circuit, presented as a compact modern production pedal while retaining the distinctive ragged garage-fuzz voice.
- **Colorways:** Standard current silver finish. Cosmetic editions are not folded into this record when they are separately named products.
- **Versions:** Current Fuzzrite silicon recreation. It is separate from Fuzzrite Germanium, Fuzzrite Germanium (White), and Fuzzrite Mini in the catalog.
- **Version changes:** The important family distinction is transistor type and circuit generation. Catalinbread explicitly identifies the current standard Fuzzrite as the silicon version; the Germanium models are separate identities.
- **Transistor type:** Silicon.
- **Diode type:** Not documented.
- **Controls / switching:** Volume and Depth.
- **Power:** 9V DC; approximately 5mA. A 9V battery is also listed by Catalinbread.
- **Sound:** The Fuzzrite is deliberately raw and splattery rather than smooth, covering the kind of ragged, cutting garage fuzz associated with early Mosrite users and classic psychedelic recordings. The Depth control changes how the gain stages combine, moving from leaner/fatter textures toward more aggressive and saturated sounds.
- **Research confidence:** High for the silicon identity and current control/power specification.
