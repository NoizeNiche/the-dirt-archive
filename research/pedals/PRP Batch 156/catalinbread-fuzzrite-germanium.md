# Catalinbread Effects Fuzzrite Germanium

- **Builder:** Catalinbread Effects
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Catalog identity:** Fuzzrite Germanium
- **What this pedal is:** A Catalinbread recreation of the rare germanium Mosrite Fuzzrite circuit from the 1966-1968 period, using two NOS PNP germanium semiconductors plus a polarity-inverter IC.
- **Colorways:** Standard raw-metal enclosure used for the main production Germanium model. The separately named white edition is cataloged as its own row.
- **Versions:** Fuzzrite Germanium; Fuzzrite Germanium (White) is a limited cosmetic edition rather than a different circuit identity.
- **Version changes:** Catalinbread adds a Modern/Vintage mode that is not present on the original. Vintage uses the lower-output-capacitance voicing, while Modern increases low-end content.
- **Transistor type:** Two NOS PNP germanium semiconductors.
- **Diode type:** Not documented as a discrete diode stage; the reviewed documentation specifically mentions a polarity-inverter IC.
- **Controls / switching:** Volume, Depth, and Modern/Vintage toggle.
- **Power:** 9V battery or filtered, regulated 9V DC center-negative adapter.
- **Sound:** The Germanium Fuzzrite is the more temperature- and character-sensitive member of the Fuzzrite family, with the two-stage blend producing everything from fat and relatively open sounds to gated, upper-harmonic, and saturated textures. Modern mode restores more low-end content than the original-style Vintage setting.
- **Research confidence:** High for the germanium semiconductor description, family history, and Modern/Vintage implementation.
