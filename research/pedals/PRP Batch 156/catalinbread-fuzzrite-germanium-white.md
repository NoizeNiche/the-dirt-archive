# Catalinbread Effects Fuzzrite Germanium White

- **Builder:** Catalinbread Effects
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Catalog identity:** Fuzzrite Germanium White
- **What this pedal is:** A white-enclosure limited edition of Catalinbread's Fuzzrite Germanium. The underlying circuit and controls remain those of the standard Germanium model.
- **Colorways:** White enclosure, explicitly produced from a batch of 50 white enclosures that had originally been intended for the Germanium release.
- **Versions:** Fuzzrite Germanium White is a cosmetic limited edition of Fuzzrite Germanium.
- **Version changes:** Catalinbread's product page describes the difference as enclosure finish rather than a new circuit. The Germanium design, Modern/Vintage behavior, and control set remain the reference configuration.
- **Transistor type:** Two NOS PNP germanium semiconductors, matching the standard Fuzzrite Germanium circuit.
- **Diode type:** Not documented.
- **Controls / switching:** Volume, Depth, Modern/Vintage toggle.
- **Power:** 9V battery or filtered, regulated 9V DC center-negative adapter.
- **Sound:** Because the white edition retains the Germanium circuit, its expected sonic behavior follows the main model: a responsive two-stage fuzz that can move from fat and open to gated and saturated, with Modern mode adding more low-end content than Vintage.
- **Research confidence:** High for the cosmetic-edition relationship and component description because Catalinbread documents both directly.
