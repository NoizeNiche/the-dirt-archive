# Catalinbread Effects Formula 5F6

- **Builder:** Catalinbread Effects
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** Formula 5F6
- **What this pedal is:** A Foundation overdrive based on the bright channel of the Fender tweed Bassman 5F6. Catalinbread recreated the key preamp structure with JFETs and added a three-band tone stack, aiming to capture both tone and response.
- **Colorways:** Standard production graphics, with historical and retailer examples showing cosmetic changes in finish/art. No evidence reviewed here supports treating those as separate circuit versions.
- **Versions:** Formula 5F6 is the Bassman Foundation model. The reviewed material also references an internal Secret Lead mode on some documented production examples.
- **Version changes:** The basic 5F6 topology remains the same across the reviewed documentation. Secret Lead increases the available gain; cosmetic changes are not treated as technical revisions without stronger evidence.
- **Transistor type:** JFET-based signal path.
- **Diode type:** Not documented.
- **Controls / switching:** Treble, Middle, Bass, Volume, Gain; documented examples include an internal Secret Lead/high-gain mode.
- **Power:** 9-18V DC operation is supported in the documented product family.
- **Sound:** The Formula 5F6 covers sparkling low-gain Bassman-like clean and edge-of-breakup sounds through thicker, creamy crunch when driven harder. The three-band EQ follows the Bassman's approach, while the JFET topology is intended to preserve picking dynamics and the interactive feel of the original circuit.
- **Research confidence:** High for the Bassman 5F6 identity and JFET signal path; moderate for production-specific Secret Lead implementation.
