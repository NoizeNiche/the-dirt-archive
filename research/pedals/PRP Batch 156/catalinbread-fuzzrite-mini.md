# Catalinbread Effects Fuzzrite Mini

- **Builder:** Catalinbread Effects
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Catalog identity:** Fuzzrite Mini
- **What this pedal is:** A compact version of the classic Fuzzrite developed in collaboration with the estate of Mosrite founder Semie Moseley. Catalinbread states that the Mini distills the exact circuit of the original.
- **Colorways:** Standard silver Mini enclosure.
- **Versions:** Fuzzrite Mini is its own compact product and is separate from the full-size Fuzzrite and Fuzzrite Germanium models.
- **Version changes:** The defining change is physical format rather than a newly voiced circuit. Catalinbread describes the Mini as a direct compact implementation of the original Fuzzrite circuit.
- **Transistor type:** Not separately specified on the reviewed Mini product documentation. The page identifies the circuit as the original Fuzzrite circuit but does not publish exact transistor part details.
- **Diode type:** Not documented.
- **Controls / switching:** Volume and Depth.
- **Power:** 9V DC, center-negative, filtered and regulated.
- **Sound:** The Mini retains the Fuzzrite's ragged, vintage fuzz character while using the Depth control as a blend of its two gain stages. Low settings are fatter and relatively clean, while higher settings can become gated, upper-harmonic, and aggressively saturated.
- **Research confidence:** High for the exact-circuit statement, controls, and form factor; moderate for undocumented internal component details.
