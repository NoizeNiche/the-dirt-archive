# Catalinbread Effects Galileo

- **Builder:** Catalinbread Effects
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** Galileo
- **What this pedal is:** A Rangemaster-style treble-booster into a cranked Vox AC30 concept, voiced around the way Brian May used a treble booster with his AC30-based rig.
- **Colorways:** Standard black-and-gold Galileo finish. Galileo Red Special is a separately named limited edition and remains separate in the catalog.
- **Versions:** Galileo is the original named model in this family. Galileo 4K is a later, separately cataloged version with a revised EQ section.
- **Version changes:** The later Galileo 4K expands the EQ from the original Tone approach to a two-band Baxandall section. The separately named Red Special is a cosmetic limited edition of the Galileo family.
- **Transistor type:** Exact semiconductor type is not specified in the reviewed official Galileo documentation.
- **Diode type:** Not documented.
- **Controls / switching:** Volume, Tone, Gain.
- **Power:** 9-18V DC, center-negative, approximately 5mA.
- **Sound:** The Galileo is built around the focused upper-mid push and harmonic response of a treble booster driving a loud AC30. Catalinbread describes the design as capable of jangly cleaner tones when the guitar volume is rolled back and searing leads or aggressive focused rhythm tones when pushed.
- **Research confidence:** High for the Vox/Rangemaster design target, controls, power, and family relationship to Galileo 4K; moderate for undocumented internal components.
