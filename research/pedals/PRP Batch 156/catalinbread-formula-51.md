# Catalinbread Effects Formula 51

- **Builder:** Catalinbread Effects
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** Formula 51
- **What this pedal is:** A Tweed Champ 5F1-inspired Foundation overdrive. Catalinbread reproduces the amp concept with a preamp, discrete power-amp simulator, and output transformer rather than only imitating the preamp.
- **Colorways:** Standard production finish. No separately documented factory colorway was found in the reviewed official material.
- **Versions:** The cataloged Formula 51 is the 5F1/Tweed Champ Foundation model. The reviewed sources do not establish a separate materially different public version that should be merged into this identity.
- **Version changes:** No major production revision was established from the reviewed official product documentation. Later visual changes, if any, should be treated separately from circuit claims unless documented.
- **Transistor type:** The official product page describes a discrete power-amp simulator but does not specify the semiconductor family or exact part numbers.
- **Diode type:** Not documented.
- **Controls / switching:** Volume, Gain, Tone, and Sag.
- **Power:** 9-18V DC, center-negative, at least 2mA.
- **Sound:** The Formula 51 is voiced around the Tweed Champ's small-amp progression from lightly gritty cleans into thick, mid-forward distortion. Its Sag control deliberately loads the modeled output transformer, increasing the soft, compressed character associated with a pushed tweed amp; Catalinbread also notes that higher supply voltage can increase available headroom.
- **Research confidence:** High for the 5F1 identity, controls, and power requirements; moderate for undocumented circuit-level component details.
