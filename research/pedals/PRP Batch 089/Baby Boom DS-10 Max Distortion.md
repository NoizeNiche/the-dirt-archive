# Biyang - Baby Boom DS-10 Max Distortion

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Biyang
- **Catalog type:** Distortion

## Research
Published October 29, 2009 in the Biyang Baby Boom series. The pedal is designed around three Marshall-style amp voices: Power, Open and Comp. Power is described as a fat four-input Plexi voice; Open as a crunchier hot-modded Plexi; Comp as a more compressed, mid-scooped hot-rodded JCM-800 style. The source also documents true bypass, German-made Wima capacitors, metal-film potentiometers, a heavy-duty footswitch, 9V battery or Boss-style 9V adapter operation, 7mA current draw, 1MΩ input impedance and an 11.2 x 6.3 x 6.5cm enclosure.
