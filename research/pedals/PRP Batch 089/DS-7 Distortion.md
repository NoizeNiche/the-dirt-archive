# Biyang - DS-7 Distortion

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Biyang
- **Catalog type:** Distortion

## Research
Published October 10, 2007 in the Tonefancier series. The DS-7 provides three distortion voicings: NORMAL, BRIGHT and WARM. Effects Database describes NORMAL as classic/saturated/moderate, BRIGHT as a broader smoother tube-like voice, and WARM as a higher-gain setting with longer delay.
