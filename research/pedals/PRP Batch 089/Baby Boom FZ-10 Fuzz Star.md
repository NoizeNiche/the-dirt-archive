# Biyang - Baby Boom FZ-10 Fuzz Star

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Biyang
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz

## Research
Published October 29, 2009 in the Baby Boom series. Effects Database identifies it as a fuzz pedal and describes it as inheriting the FZ-7 prototype approach, with three kinds of fuzz available. The source notes a smooth fuzz range that can also become aggressive. The same Baby Boom platform documentation covers true bypass and 9V battery/Boss-style adapter power; model-specific electrical figures are not sufficiently exposed in the accessible record.
