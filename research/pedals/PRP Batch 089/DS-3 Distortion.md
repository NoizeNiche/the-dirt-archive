# Biyang - DS-3 Distortion

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Biyang
- **Catalog type:** Distortion

## Research
Published November 15, 2006. This is a three-function pedal integrating distortion, speaker simulation and time delay. Effects Database documents six controls corresponding to distortion level/factor, tone, delay interval, delay feedback/degree, delay blend/position and overall volume. The source describes a longer delay range than the basic integrated design and positions it for rhythm and solo use.
