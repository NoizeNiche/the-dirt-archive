# Biyang - BL-8 Blue

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Biyang
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive

## Research
Published June 30, 2008 in the Tonefancier series. Controls are Level and Drive. Biyang describes the sound as creamy yet clear, modeled around old 1970s tube amplifiers, with an emphasis on blues and country applications.
