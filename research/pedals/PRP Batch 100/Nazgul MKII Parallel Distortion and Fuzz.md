## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Blackhawk Amplifiers
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Fuzz

## Research
The Nazgûl MKII combines the Balrog Distortion MKII and Fellbeast Fuzz MKII as two parallel dirt circuits in one enclosure, with a separate clean-bass volume path. The Balrog side supplies high-gain distortion/overdrive, while the Fellbeast side provides high-output fuzz with Baxandall EQ and mid shaping. The clean-bass section can be boosted above unity and includes low-pass and low-frequency shaping, making the combined pedal particularly suited to bass and baritone while remaining usable for standard guitar. Blackhawk lists separate controls for gain, volume, treble, bass, mids, depth, gate and clean.
