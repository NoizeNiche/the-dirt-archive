# Blackhawk Amplifiers - Green / Black Russian Pi π Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Blackhawk Amplifiers
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz

## Research
The Green / Black Russian Pi π Fuzz is Blackhawk's take on the Russian-era Big Muff family, offered in Green Russian and Black Russian versions. The Green version emphasizes extended low end and a more transistor-distortion-like response, while the Black version offers substantially more gain and moves further into fuzz. Blackhawk uses selected transistors for each stage and adds a mid control alongside Tone, Volume and Gain. The circuit is designed to work with bass and baritone tunings as well as guitar, and Blackhawk states that its fuzz pedals can be placed anywhere in the chain.
