## Research

The Heimdall-3 MKII is the stripped-down companion to the Heimdall-2, retaining the core low-tuned distortion architecture without the larger model's extra clean/EQ features. Blackhawk describes it as a versatile circuit inspired by the desire for Godflesh-like clarity and tight low end rather than a one-purpose HM-2 copy. The MKII uses an OP07 gain stage and offers Bass, High Mid, Treble, Gain, Depth and Volume controls. A three-position clipping switch selects MOSFET hard clipping, no clipping for dirty boost/drive, or LED soft clipping, while an internal Presence trim controls the upper highs.
