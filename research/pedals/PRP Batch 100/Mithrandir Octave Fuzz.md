## Research
The Mithrandir Octave Fuzz is a loud octave fuzz with a footswitchable octave section and adjustable octave and clean blends. Blackhawk describes an op-amp driving a transformer and germanium diodes to generate an octave-up/ring-mod component. Controls include volume, gain, Baxandall bass and treble, octave blend and clean blend, with a footswitch for octave on/off. Turning the octave off also allows the circuit to function as a dirty boost. Later Blackhawk listings identify the current version as the Mithrandir Octave Fuzz MKII for baritone and bass use.
