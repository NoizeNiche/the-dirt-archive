# Blackhawk Amplifiers - Fellbeast Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Blackhawk Amplifiers
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz

## Research
The Fellbeast Fuzz is a very high-output fuzz built around a Baxandall tone stack and a notch-filter midrange section. Blackhawk positions it from sludge through shoegaze, with enough boosted output to drive an amplifier hard. The pedal uses an impedance-correction transformer so it can be placed anywhere in the signal chain, including after buffers or with active pickups. The V2 revision adds a Gate/Bias control that changes negative feedback to move between gated, sputtery textures and thicker, more articulate fuzz.
