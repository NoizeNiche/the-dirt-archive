# Blackhawk Amplifiers - Basilisk Fuzz

## PRP identity

- **Builder:** Blackhawk Amplifiers
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz

## Research

The Basilisk is a warm, mid-gain fuzz based on the Roland Bee Baa concept, expanded with a full tone section. Available controls include gain, volume, bass, mids and tone, giving the circuit more control over its frequency balance than the original-style concept. Blackhawk's Basilisk is intended to work well as a gain stage into another drive, and contemporary descriptions place it in a mid-gain, palm-muting-friendly role. The circuit is designed to retain low-end weight while providing a strong mid voice.
