# Blackhawk Amplifiers - Balrog Distortion MKII

## PRP identity

- **Builder:** Blackhawk Amplifiers
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive

## Research

The Balrog Distortion MKII is a high-gain distortion/overdrive built around three RC4558 opamps. Blackhawk describes it as a combined preamp, distortion and overdrive, with a range from vintage-style overdrive through saturated high-gain tones. The controls provide volume, mids, gain, treble, bass and depth, with Depth tightening the low end. The MKII adds clickless relay true bypass and a hybrid SMD/through-hole construction while retaining handwired jacks and switching.
