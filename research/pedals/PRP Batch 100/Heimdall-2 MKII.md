## Research
The Heimdall-2 MKII is a distortion, overdrive and powerful EQ designed especially for low baritone tunings and bass, while remaining usable with standard-tuned guitar. It has a parallel clean path, sweepable mid frequency control, bass, high-mid, treble, gain and volume, plus clipping options for MOSFET hard clipping, no clipping, or LED soft clipping. The March 2026 Rev-B update adds dual power rails for increased headroom, allowing the pedal to function more like a preamp as well as a drive. Internal controls provide presence, clean and dirt volume, clean low-pass/bass shaping, bass frequency and depth.
