# Blackhawk Amplifiers - Morgul Drive MKII

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Blackhawk Amplifiers
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive

## Research
The Morgul Drive V2/MKII is a warm, moderate-to-high-gain overdrive that can also function as a clean boost. Reported controls are Treble, Volume, Gain and Depth, with a three-position clipping selection for soft clipping, no clipping and hard clipping. Blackhawk's Depth control changes the character between clearer and dirtier gain, and the circuit retains substantial low-end content compared with a typical Tube Screamer-style drive. The MKII version uses Blackhawk's clickless relay bypass and updated hybrid construction.
