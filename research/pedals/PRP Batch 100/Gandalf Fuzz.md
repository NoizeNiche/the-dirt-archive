# Blackhawk Amplifiers - Gandalf Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Blackhawk Amplifiers
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz

## Research
The Gandalf Fuzz is a compact one-knob octave fuzz. Blackhawk describes an op-amp driving a transformer and germanium diodes to create an octave-up/ring-mod character, with a simple volume control on the outside. The V2 design adds internal trim pots for octave blend and tone. With its single external control, it is intended as a straightforward high-impact fuzz that can stack before or after other dirt pedals.
