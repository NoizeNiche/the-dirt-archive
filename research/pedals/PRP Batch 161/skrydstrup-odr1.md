# Caveman Audio / Skrydstrup ODR1

- **Builder:** Caveman Audio / Skrydstrup
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz / Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** ODR1
- **What this pedal is:** The Skrydstrup R&D ODR1 was originally designed to add a Plexi feeling to the company's OD50 amplifier, then developed as a standalone stompbox.
- **Colorways:** Period Skrydstrup R&D enclosure; cosmetic differences are not treated as separate identities.
- **Versions:** ODR1; later documentation identifies Caveman Audio as the successor/current brand to Skrydstrup R&D.
- **Version changes:** No dated internal revision boundary established by the sources checked.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented.
- **Diode type:** Old germanium diodes are specifically documented.
- **Controls / switching:** A Tone control functions as high-frequency damping rather than a conventional tone control; at maximum it becomes inactive. A complete control list was not established from the source checked.
- **Power:** Not documented by the sources checked.
- **Factory modifications:** None documented.
- **Sound:** Effects Database describes the ODR1 as tube-like, highly dynamic overdrive with a touch of fuzz. The Plexi-oriented voicing and germanium clipping are intended to produce responsive, amp-like breakup rather than a flat distortion texture.
- **Research confidence:** High for the Plexi intent, dynamics, germanium diode use and Tone behavior; moderate for the remaining hardware details.
