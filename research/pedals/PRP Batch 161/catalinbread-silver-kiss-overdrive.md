# Catalinbread Silver Kiss Overdrive

- **Builder:** Catalinbread Effects
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Catalog identity:** Silver Kiss Overdrive
- **What this pedal is:** A low-to-medium gain dynamic overdrive combined with clean boost and active three-band EQ.
- **Colorways:** Original Silver Kiss presentation; cosmetic differences are not separate identities.
- **Versions:** Silver Kiss / Mk II family; the 2007 Reissue is cataloged separately.
- **Version changes:** The Mk II architecture combines boost, overdrive and active EQ; exact original-to-Mk II chronology is not asserted beyond the documented family relationship.
- **Transistor type:** Not documented.
- **Diode type:** Not documented.
- **Controls / switching:** Gain, Bass, Mid, Treble, Volume.
- **Power:** 9–18V DC, center-negative.
- **Factory modifications:** None documented.
