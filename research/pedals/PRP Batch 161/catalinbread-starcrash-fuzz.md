# Catalinbread StarCrash Fuzz

- **Builder:** Catalinbread Effects
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Catalog identity:** StarCrash Fuzz
- **What this pedal is:** A silicon Fuzz Face evolution with fixed full fuzz, adjustable bias/starvation and a low-cut filter.
- **Colorways:** '70s Collection presentation; cosmetic differences are not separate identities.
- **Versions:** StarCrash Fuzz.
- **Version changes:** No materially distinct production hardware revision established.
- **Transistor type:** Silicon, two-transistor Fuzz Face topology.
- **Diode type:** Not documented.
- **Controls / switching:** Volume, Bias, Low-Cut.
- **Power:** 9V DC, center-negative.
- **Factory modifications:** None documented.
- **Sound:** Full, saturated fuzz with Bias capable of moving from smoother vintage textures toward gated, sputtery dying-battery effects; Low-Cut tightens the bass response.
- **Research confidence:** High.
- **Sources checked:** https://pedalfilter.com/catalinbread/starcrash-fuzz ; https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/catalinbread/starcrash
