## What this pedal is
Effects Database identifies the GA-1040 Gain as an overdrive pedal in Alber's steel series, listed as the **AA-X00** enclosure family. The exact-model record was published November 16, 2009. [1]

The same source explicitly distinguishes three related Alber models by enclosure family: **GA-104 Gain** in a zinc enclosure, **GA-1040 Gain** in a steel enclosure, and **GA-1040P Gain** in a plastic enclosure. The archive therefore treats them as separate catalog identities rather than assuming that their housings are the only difference. [1]
