# Alber — OD-600P Over Drive

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** OD-600P Over Drive

- **Identity:** Plastic-enclosure version of the Alber OD-600 analog overdrive platform. [1]

## What this pedal is
The OD-600P is documented as an analog, mid-gain overdrive with warm, smooth symmetrical clipping. The surviving record gives Overdrive, Tone, and Level controls, electronic switching, 9V operation, a plastic enclosure, and a 4558-type dual op-amp with diode clipping. [1]
