# Alber — OD-600 Over Drive

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** OD-600 Over Drive
- **Builder:** Alber
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Alber OD-600 Over Drive from the steel AA-X00 series. [1]

## What this pedal is
Effects Database identifies the OD-600 as an analog overdrive in Alber's steel AA-X00 family. Its closely related plastic OD-600P is documented as the plastic version of this platform and provides the clearest surviving technical information for the shared analog design family. [1][2]

## Colorways
- **Steel / metallic enclosure** is documented by the AA-X00 series designation. [1]
- Exact painted production variants were not reliably documented.

## Versions and factory options
### OD-600
- Steel AA-X00 enclosure
- Overdrive
- Related OD-600P plastic version exists as a separate cataloged model. [1][2]

## Version changes
OD-600P is cataloged as the plastic version of the OD-600 platform. A numbered circuit revision of the steel OD-600 itself was not verified. [2]

## Transistor
- **Exact transistor/device:** Not publicly documented.

## Diode
- **Exact clipping diode/device:** Not publicly documented for the steel OD-600.
- The related OD-600P is documented with a 4558-type dual op-amp and diode clipping, but that is not treated as a verified device list for the steel model. [2]

## Sound
The surviving OD-600 documentation establishes the pedal as an analog overdrive, but its individual control behavior and detailed voicing are not preserved in the accessible record. The archive therefore avoids assigning a specific clipping texture or component set beyond the documented platform relationship. [1][2]
