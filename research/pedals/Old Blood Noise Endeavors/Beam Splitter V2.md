## What this pedal is
Beam Splitter V2 has: Buffered trails or true bypass switching Presets Expression control over every knob MIDI control over everything in the pedal Mono or stereo input (stereo will be collapsed to mono when the pedal is on), and mono, stereo, or trereo output Controls include: Gain , Volume , and Tone to shape the amount of distortion, overall volume, and brightness of each drive section Time and Feedback controls for each delay to set the timing (up to 211mS) and the amount of resonance or decay Deviate to randomly vary the delay time, giving a more natural feel and chorus effect Spread to set the amount of stereo spread Delay Select to choose which drives are delayed For more information, see the Beam Splitter V2 manual available here .
