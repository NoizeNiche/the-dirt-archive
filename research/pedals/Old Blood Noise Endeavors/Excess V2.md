## What this pedal is

Excess V2 combines distortion with a selectable modulation section that can operate as delay, chorus or harmonized fifths. OBNE designed it for complex, 1980s-inspired distorted textures.

## Versions and factory options

- Distortion controls: **Gain, Tone, Dist Vol**.
- Modulation controls: **Time, Depth, Mod Vol**.
- Modulation modes: **Delay, Chorus, Fifth**.
- Effect-order switch: distortion into modulation, modulation into distortion, or parallel.
- Expression input for Rate/Depth control.
- True bypass switching.
- **V2** is the documented revision.

## Version changes

## Sound

Excess V2 can combine high-gain distortion with delay, chorus or fifth-harmony modulation. The routing switch makes the order of the two sections part of the sound rather than merely a wiring detail.
