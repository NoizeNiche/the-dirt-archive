bitoun fuzz is a double-fuzz pedal combining the Feed Me circuit with a Super Fuzz circuit. Anasounds lists it in the Origins range. [1]

## Sound
The pedal combines the fat, articulate character of Anasounds' Feed Me with a Super Fuzz-style voice, giving two substantially different fuzz textures in one unit. The Feed Me side is known for strong sustain and useful high-frequency definition. [1][2]
