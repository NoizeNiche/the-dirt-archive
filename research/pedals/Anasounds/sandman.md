## What this pedal is
Sandman is a limited Anasounds dual-overdrive combining the original Savage MkI circuit with the Ego Driver. Anasounds describes the Savage section as an unchanged MkI circuit paired with the Ego Driver. [1][2]

## Colorways
- Limited-edition production; no complete colorway chronology established.

## Versions and factory options
- Savage MkI section: Out, Tone, Gain and Ge/Si switch.
- Ego Driver section: Out, Tone and Gain.
- Each side has its own footswitch. [1]

## Version changes
- Sandman is explicitly a new format combining two established circuits rather than a modified Savage MkI. [1]

## Sound
The Savage section can move from warm clean coloration to thick saturation and can be used as a preamp, while the Ego Driver adds a mid-focused gain stage and presence. Together they provide complementary transparent-to-mid-forward overdrive textures. [1]
