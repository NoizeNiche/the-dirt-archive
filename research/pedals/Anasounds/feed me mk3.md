## What this pedal is
Feed Me MK3 is Anasounds' customizable take on the classic late-1960s Fuzz Face family. The MK3 moves the design into the FX Teacher format with front-panel controls and internal adjustment/customization points. [1]

## Colorways
- No reliable production colorway chronology established.

## Versions and factory options
- Feed, Out and Smash controls.
- Bass and Crack switches.
- Internal Bias and Fuzz trimmers.
- Terminal blocks allow capacitor changes. [1]

## Version changes
- MK1 used Feed, Fuzz and Out controls around a BC108 silicon circuit.
- MK2 removed external controls for a plug-and-play format while retaining internal trimmers.
- MK3 added the FX Teacher customization system, including bass and supply-voltage manipulation. [1]

## Transistor
- The original Feed Me is explicitly a BC108 silicon design. MK3 documentation does not establish a different transistor type. [1][2]

## Diode
- Exact diode type not established.

## Sound
Feed Me MK3 can cover the classic bright, sustaining Fuzz Face-like sound, thicker modern fuzz, and gated/velcro textures. The Crack and Smash controls deliberately reduce supply voltage to create compressed, sputtering behavior, while the Bass switch can restore a much heavier low end. [1]
