# Anasounds — high voltage

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** high voltage
- **Builder:** Anasounds
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Plexi-style distortion/overdrive pedal developed around AC/DC rhythm tones, with a stated design goal of reproducing the character of “It’s a Long Way to the Top (If You Wanna Rock ’n’ Roll).”

## What this pedal is
High Voltage is Anasounds' Plexi-oriented overdrive/distortion. The builder says the design began as an attempt to approach a pushed Marshall Plexi, then used frequency analysis of the intro to AC/DC's “It’s a Long Way to the Top” from the album High Voltage to refine the voicing. [1][2]

## Versions and factory options
- Front-panel controls: **Gain, Tone, Out**.
- **Bass Cut** switch on the exterior reduces low frequencies for a tighter, more mix-forward response.
- Internal **Voicing** control changes the overall frequency balance.
- Internal **Treble Cut** switch can cut frequencies above approximately **4kHz**.
- Internal **Light** control adjusts the intensity of the illuminated logo. [1][2][3]

## Version changes
No numbered production revision was established in the reviewed sources.
- Anasounds also produced a **Savage + High Voltage limited-edition dual overdrive**, placing both circuits in one enclosure with a switch to reverse their order in the internal signal chain. The power-management section isolates and filters the supply for the two circuits. This is a separate combined-format product rather than a revision of the standalone High Voltage. [4]

## Circuit / technology
- The builder describes a **high-performance OPA** stage intended to reduce the noise floor.
- The published material establishes the pedal as an analog overdrive/distortion with relay true bypass, but does not specify a discrete transistor family or exact op-amp part number. [1][3]

## Controls and voicing
### External
- **Gain:** from transparent overdrive at minimum to stronger distortion at maximum.
- **Tone:** ranges from bassier/warmer distortion toward higher-mid/high-frequency emphasis.
- **Out:** output level capable of driving an amplifier harder.
- **Bass Cut:** retains the massive low end in the lower position or reduces bass in the upper position for more separation in a mix. [1][3]

### Internal
- **Voicing:** the builder describes a warm, bassy vintage end of the control through a more modern, heavy response with greater high/mid rolloff.
- **Treble Cut:** cuts frequencies above approximately 4kHz when engaged.
- **Light:** controls the brightness of the pedal's illuminated logo. [1][2]

## Power / electrical specifications
The original High Voltage manual specifies:
- **Power supply:** 9V DC
- **Current:** 55mA
- **Input impedance:** 1.4MΩ
- **Output impedance:** 7.6kΩ
- **Switching:** relay true bypass
- **Dimensions:** approximately 11.8 × 6.4 × 5.9cm
- **Weight:** approximately 290g. [3]

The current builder documentation advertises **40V of dynamics/headroom** as a design feature and continues to identify relay true bypass and a high-performance OPA noise-reduction stage. [1][2]

## Sound
High Voltage is voiced for defined, dynamic Plexi-style drive. The builder's Bass Cut switch is intended to keep rhythm chords from becoming muddy while helping lead parts stand out. Gain can move from transparent overdrive toward stronger distortion, while the internal Voicing and Treble Cut controls broaden the available spectrum from warmer/vintage-oriented tones to tighter, more modern responses. [1][2][3]
