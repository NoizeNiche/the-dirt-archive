# Anasounds — ego driver

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** ego driver
- **Builder:** Anasounds
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** 100% analog mid-boost overdrive developed from Anasounds' analysis of more than one hundred 808-inspired schematics.
- **U.S. market name:** **FX Driver**. Anasounds states that the FX Driver is the same pedal as the Ego Driver under a different name for the U.S. market. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
The Ego Driver is Anasounds' take on the classic 808-style mid-boost overdrive. The builder describes it as a pedal that can act as a clean-ish boost, a warm organic overdrive, or a more saturated drive, with an emphasis on exposing and experimenting with the saturation-stage circuit rather than presenting it as a fixed clone. [1][3]

## Versions and factory options
- Front-panel controls are **Volume/Out, Tone, Gain**, plus voicing and clipping controls.
- **Voicing:** a switch selects capacitor networks for different frequency emphasis. The builder documents the stock 47nF value plus 22nF for stronger high mids, 68nF for wider mids, 220nF for more low mids, and 470nF for more bass. [1]
- **Clipping:** the stock position uses two **1N914** diodes; the builder documents interchangeable alternatives including two 1N4001 diodes, two 3mm red LEDs, and a mixed 2N7000/1N34 option. The middle switch position removes the clipping diodes for a clean-boost mode. [1]
- Internal trimpots provide additional voicing control for bass and custom-frequency behavior; clockwise adjustment adds gain to the selected band and moves the response toward a more saturated/modern character. [1]
- The pedal uses a gold-plated **3PDT true-bypass** footswitch and Lumberg DC jack. [1]

## Version changes
Anasounds later updated the Ego Driver to fit the FX Teacher product identity. The builder states that the **circuit remained the same**, with the primary change being the voicing terminal-block capacitor selection. The updated configuration places 22nF in the upper voicing position for stronger high mids and adds three more capacitor choices for a wider low-mid/bass range. [3]
- For the U.S. market, the assembled version is sold as **FX Driver** rather than Ego Driver. Anasounds explicitly states the pedals are the same product under different names. [2]

## Transistor
- **Technology:** Not established.
- The official Ego Driver documentation focuses on the saturation/clipping stage and does not specify a discrete transistor type in the reviewed material.

## Diode
- **Technology:** Silicon / mixed documented clipping devices.
- Stock clipping: **2 × 1N914** silicon switching diodes.
- Documented alternatives: **2 × 1N4001**, **2 × red 3mm LEDs**, and a mixed **2N7000 + 1N34** clipping arrangement. [1]
- The archive records these as documented clipping options rather than treating the pedal as having a single fixed diode type across all configurations.

## Sound
The builder positions the Ego Driver as a mid-boost overdrive capable of pushing an already-crunching amplifier as well as functioning as a standalone overdrive. Its voicing network can emphasize upper mids, wider mids, lower mids, or bass, while the interchangeable clipping network changes compression, saturation, and harmonic behavior. [1] Independent 2019 user documentation also identifies it as a boutique Tube Screamer-style overdrive with selectable voicing and clipping modes. [4]

## Technical specifications
- **Headroom:** 18V with a 9V supply. [1][3]
- **Off-load consumption:** 11.2mA. [1]
- **Under-load current draw:** 37.7mA. [1]
- **Input impedance:** 1.3MΩ. [1]
- **Output impedance:** 146Ω. [1]
- **Processing:** 100% analog. [1]
- **Bypass:** true bypass. [1]
