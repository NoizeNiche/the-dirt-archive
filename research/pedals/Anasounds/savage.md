## What this pedal is
Savage was Anasounds' first overdrive, launched in 2015. The builder describes it as a transparent overdrive inspired by a famous US transparent overdrive design. [1][2]

## Colorways
- No reliable complete production colorway chronology established.

## Versions and factory options
- Savage MkI uses Out, Tone, Gain and clipping selection.
- The builder later returned to the MkI circuit in the Sandman format. [2]

## Version changes
- The Savage MkI is the first-generation design. Sandman uses the original MkI circuit unchanged as one half of a dual pedal. [2]

## Transistor
- The Sandman manual documents a Ge/Si clipping selector for the Savage MkI section, but exact transistor part numbers were not established.

## Diode
- Exact diode type not established.

## Sound
Savage can function as an always-on preamp or transparent overdrive, moving from warm clean coloration at low gain to thicker saturation at higher gain. The Ge/Si selection changes the compression and dynamics of the clipping character. [2]
