# Anasounds — savage

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** savage
- **Builder:** Anasounds
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Anasounds' transparent overdrive platform inspired by the Klon Centaur, redesigned rather than presented as a direct clone. The current product is the Savage MkII. [1][2]

## What this pedal is
Anasounds describes the Savage as a warm, powerful transparent overdrive that preserves the general electronic structure of the historical circuit it references while rearranging the clipping, tone, voicing, buffer, and output stages. The pedal can blend clean and overdriven signal and function as an enhancer, boost, or overdrive. [1]

## Versions and factory options
### Savage MkI
- Original first-generation Savage, launched in **2015**.
- The later Sandman uses the **original Savage MkI circuit unchanged** rather than a modified reissue. [3]
- The Savage MkI uses the three main controls **Out, Tone, Gain** and a Vintage/Modern clipping selection in the original format; Sandman documents the equivalent selection as **Ge/Si**. [3]

### Savage MkII
- External controls: **Out, Tone, Gain**.
- Internal controls: **Voicing, Light, Treble, OD-Bass, Clipping**.
- The internal Clipping control changes between vintage/germanium and modern/silicon clipping arrangements.
- OD-Bass adds low-frequency content to the overdrive stage.
- Treble changes the voicing of the tone section; Anasounds describes it as a tone control rather than a simple treble booster.
- Voicing sets the character of the overdrive stage. [1][2]

### Savage Extended
- A limited edition based on the Savage MkII circuit.
- Moved the **Voicing** control and **Clipping** selector from the internal trim configuration to the front panel.
- The Clipping selector switched between germanium and silicon clipping arrangements.
- Used a plexiglass/engraved faceplate rather than the standard bamboo presentation. [4]

### Sandman-format Savage MkI
The later Sandman combined the unchanged Savage MkI circuit with an Ego Driver in one enclosure. Its Savage side uses the **Ge/Si** clipping switch and retains the high-volume/preamp behavior of the original circuit. [3]

## Version changes
The core evolution was from the original Savage MkI toward the more extensively adjustable MkII:
- **MkI:** original production format and external controls.
- **MkII:** internal voicing, clipping, bass, and treble customization while keeping a simple three-knob external interface.
- **Savage Extended:** brought selected MkII customization back to the front panel as a limited edition.
- **Sandman:** reused the unchanged MkI circuit alongside an Ego Driver as a separate dual-overdrive product. [1][3][4]

## Transistor / integrated technology
- **Integrated circuit:** current Anasounds documentation specifies **OPA Texas Instruments**.
- **Discrete transistor technology:** not established in the reviewed sources.
- The archive should therefore not label Savage as a discrete germanium or silicon transistor design simply because its clipping choices use those technologies. [2]

## Diode / clipping
- **Technology:** Germanium / Silicon.
- Anasounds' documentation identifies the Savage's clipping control as a choice between vintage germanium and modern silicon clipping.
- The Savage MkI's germanium clipping component is described by Anasounds as the hard-to-find diode responsible for part of the original circuit's character; this scarcity also influenced the limited production of the Sandman. [3]
- No exact diode part number was established for the standard Savage MkII in the reviewed official sources.

## Controls and documented behavior
- **Out:** high output reserve, capable of more than **25dB** of output according to Anasounds' current description.
- **Tone:** a true tone control rather than a simple treble booster.
- **Gain:** mixes clean and saturated signal, allowing lower-gain enhancement through thicker overdrive. [1]
- **Voicing:** shapes the overdrive stage.
- **OD-Bass:** adds bass to the overdrive stage.
- **Treble:** changes the treble/tone response.
- **Clipping:** vintage/germanium or modern/silicon arrangement.
- **Light:** controls logo light intensity. [1]

## Power / electrical specifications
Current Anasounds documentation lists:
- **Supply:** external **9V DC**, negative polarity.
- **Power consumption:** **22.9mA**.
- **Input impedance:** **260kΩ**.
- **Output impedance:** **1.8kΩ**.
- **Integrated circuit:** Texas Instruments OPA.
- **OPA dynamic:** **40V effective**.
- **Switching:** gold true bypass.
- **Construction:** handmade in France. [2]

## Sound
The Savage is designed as a transparent-to-overdrive platform that preserves the guitar/amp relationship while adding harmonics, thickness, volume, and controlled midrange. The Gain control's clean/drive blend allows it to remain comparatively open at lower settings while moving toward thicker saturation as gain rises. The internal voice, OD-Bass, treble, and clipping controls make the MkII substantially more configurable than the original external interface suggests. [1][2]

## Deep research verification
