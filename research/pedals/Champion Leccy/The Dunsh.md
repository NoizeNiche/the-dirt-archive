# Champion Leccy — The Dunsh

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** The Dunsh
- **Builder:** Champion Leccy
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Fuzz / Boost
- **Identity:** Original Champion Leccy design combining a modified single-transistor gain stage with op-amp filtering/EQ, plus a separately footswitchable Dunsh stage. [1][2]

## Controls / architecture
- **GAIN**
- **BIAS**
- **GATE**
- **DUNSH VOLUME**
- **TOP EQ**
- **BOTTOM EQ**
- Three-way Gate switch.
- Second Dunsh footswitch.
- Internal DIP switches for low-end roll-off, high-end roll-off and Dunsh intensity. [1][3]

## Circuit architecture
- Modified **single-transistor** gain stage derived from the Gett/Canny Fettle family.
- Surrounding op-amp stages provide filtering, additional gain and EQ.
- Separate footswitch activates the namesake Dunsh stage. [1]

## Transistor / active devices
- Gain stage: modified single transistor.
- Surrounding circuit: op-amp stages.

## Sound
The Dunsh spans cleaner boost through saturated distortion and very aggressive fuzz-like sounds. Gate and Bias strongly reshape the operating point, while the second footswitch adds a louder, heavier low-end stage. [1][2]
