# Champion Leccy — The Dunsh

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** The Dunsh
- **Builder:** Champion Leccy
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Fuzz
- **Identity:** Champion Leccy The Dunsh, a wide-range gain pedal combining a modified transistor gain stage with op-amp filtering, gain, and EQ stages.

## What this pedal is
Champion Leccy describes The Dunsh as an original design built around a modified transistor gain stage derived from the Gett/Canny Fettle Boost lineage, surrounded by op-amp stages for filtering, gain, and EQ. The builder describes it as covering clean boost through distortion with substantial output and two gating controls. [1]

The pedal includes a second footswitch that activates the namesake Dunsh stage. [1][2]

## Colorways

Champion Leccy notes that knob shape and color can change with supply-chain availability. [2]

## Versions and factory options
The builder describes:
- Gain control.
- Bias control.
- Gate control with a three-way switch.
- Dunsh volume control.
- Top and bottom EQ controls.
- Second Dunsh footswitch.
- Internal DIP switches for low-end roll-off, high-end roll-off, and Dunsh intensity. [1][3]

No numbered V1/V2 factory sequence was established.

## Transistor
The Dunsh contains a modified **single-transistor gain stage** derived from the transistor stage used in the Gett/Canny Fettle Boost family. [1]

The surrounding gain and EQ architecture also uses op-amp stages. Exact transistor and op-amp part numbers were not established.

No exact-model diode specification was established.

## Sound
The builder positions The Dunsh from clean boost through distortion and very aggressive fuzz-like saturation. The gate and pre-gain EQ controls strongly shape the texture, while the second footswitch adds the loud, bottom-heavy Dunsh stage. [1][2]
