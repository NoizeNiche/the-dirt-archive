# Champion Leccy — Canny Fettle Boost

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Canny Fettle Boost
- **Builder:** Champion Leccy
- **Catalog type:** Boost / Overdrive / Distortion
- **Identity:** Two-stage Champion Leccy design pairing the original JFET Fettle boost with a substantially expanded Clart/Dunsh-style second stage. [1][2]

## Architecture
### Fettle side
- **JFET** gain stage.
- Warm, articulate boost/preamp behavior.
- Internal volume trim. [1]

### Clart side
- **BJT** gain stage derived from the builder's LPB-1-style lineage.
- Three-position **Dunsh** switch.
- Low-pass and high-pass filter switches.
- Separate footswitch. [1][2]

The two stages can be used separately or together, creating three practical combinations. [1]

## Sound
The Fettle side functions as boost/preamp while the Clart side extends into stronger distortion and fuzz. The Dunsh switch changes gain/aggression, and the two sections can be stacked for substantially heavier sounds. [1][2]
