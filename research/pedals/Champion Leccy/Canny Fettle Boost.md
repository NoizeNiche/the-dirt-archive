# Champion Leccy — Canny Fettle Boost

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Canny Fettle Boost
- **Builder:** Champion Leccy
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive
- **Identity:** Champion Leccy Canny Fettle Boost, an expanded two-stage gain pedal combining a JFET boost stage with a more aggressive second gain stage derived from the builder's Dunsh concept.

## What this pedal is
Champion Leccy describes the Canny Fettle Boost as an expanded version of the original Fettle Boost. The Fettle side retains the original JFET gain stage, while the Clart side evolved far beyond the original LPB-1-style boost and can move from clean boost through major distortion. [1]

The builder documents the pedal as two stacked gain stages with separate footswitches, providing three usable combinations. [1]

## Versions and factory options
The Canny Fettle Boost adds:
- Fettle gain/boost side.
- Clart gain/distortion side.
- Separate footswitches.
- Low-pass and high-pass filter switches.
- Three-position Dunsh switch on the Clart side.
- Internal volume trim for the Fettle side. [1][2]

## Version changes
The builder explicitly describes the Canny Fettle Boost as an expanded version of the earlier Fettle Boost. Its major circuit change is the evolved Clart side, incorporating the Dunsh concept. [1]

No formal numbered V1/V2 factory sequence was established.

## Transistor
The Fettle side uses a **JFET** as its always-on gain stage. The Clart side uses a **BJT**-based gain stage that evolved from an LPB-1-style circuit and later incorporated the Dunsh modification. [1]

Exact transistor part numbers were not established.

## Diode
No exact-model diode specification was established in the checked sources.

## Sound
The Fettle side works as a warm, articulate preamp/boost and can also function as a treble or solo boost. The Clart side broadens the pedal into heavier distortion and fuzzy textures, with the Dunsh switch adding increasingly loud and aggressive low-end fuzz. [1][2]
