# Champion Leccy — The Divvy

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** The Divvy
- **Builder:** Champion Leccy
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz / Overdrive
- **Identity:** Champion Leccy The Divvy V3, a Harmonic Percolator-derived fuzz with selectable voicing.

## What this pedal is
Champion Leccy documents the Divvy as a fuzz derived from the Harmonic Percolator concept. The V3 version has two external controls, Fuzz and Volume, and a three-way voicing switch. [1][2]

The builder has described the V3 as the returning version of the pedal, after earlier versions used different control arrangements. [1]

## Colorways

## Versions and factory options
The version history is documented as:
- **V1:** one-knob fuzz.
- **V2:** added a three-way voicing switch, previously under the hood.
- **V3:** added master volume, initially through an internal trim and later as an external control. [2]

The V3 therefore has **Fuzz, Volume**, and the three-way voicing switch. [1][2]

## Version changes
Champion Leccy's documentation explicitly distinguishes V1, V2, and V3 changes as above. [2]

## Sound
The Divvy is described as a Percolator-inspired fuzz that emphasizes high mids and upper frequencies while remaining relatively open rather than becoming a heavily compressed square-wave fuzz. The voicing switch adds pseudo-octave or crunch-oriented responses depending on position. [1][2]
