# Circus Freak Music — The Pickled Punk

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** The Pickled Punk
- **Builder:** Circus Freak Music
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** Circus Freak Music The Pickled Punk, an original analog high-gain distortion with a buffered bypass path and 9–18V operation.

## What this pedal is
Premier Guitar describes Pickled Punk as an original analog distortion designed for higher-gain 1980s and 1990s tones. The circuit is hand-built, through-hole, and completely analog. [1]

## Colorways

## Versions and factory options
The documented control set is:
- Volume.
- Tone.
- Distortion. [1][2]

Additional documented features include:
- Buffered full-bypass circuitry.
- Extruded die-cast aluminum enclosure.
- 9–18V operation.
- Custom pedalboard rail-mount geometry. [1]

## Sound
Premier Guitar describes Pickled Punk as muscular, open, and punchy rather than extremely compressed. At lower gain it can act as a thick overdrive, while higher settings produce substantial distortion with strong mids and low-end body. [1]
