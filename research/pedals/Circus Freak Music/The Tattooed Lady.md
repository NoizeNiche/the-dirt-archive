## What this pedal is

Guitar Gear documents The Tattooed Lady as an open, dynamic overdrive designed to work both as a reactive overdrive and an amp-in-a-box. The pedal uses active Bass and Treble EQ, a Voice toggle, and substantial gain/output range. [1]

Circus Freak's original launch documentation lists the Tattooed Lady as the overdrive member of its first pedal line. [2]

## Versions and factory options

Documented features:
- Volume.
- Gain.
- Independent active Bass and Treble EQ.
- Voice toggle: flat response or slight treble boost.
- True bypass.
- Up to 18V input capability. [1][3]

## Sound

The Tattooed Lady has an open, lightly compressed distortion/overdrive response and unusually strong volume/gain headroom. Guitar Gear describes it as highly touch-sensitive, maintaining note separation on chords while responding strongly to attack and guitar-volume changes. [1]
