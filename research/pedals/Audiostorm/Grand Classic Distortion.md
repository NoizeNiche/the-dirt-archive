# Audiostorm — Grand Classic Distortion

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Grand Classic Distortion
- **Builder:** Audiostorm
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** High-gain distortion with cascaded FET-input op-amp gain stages, active tone shaping and an analog pre-distortion "Amp" stage.

## What this pedal is
Audiostorm describes Grand Classic as a dynamic high-gain distortion that can move from nuanced overdrive to extreme metal. Its cascaded gain circuit uses FET-input op-amps, followed by active tone-shaping sections and an Amp control intended to alter the harmonic character before distortion.

### Development / production changes
- **2022:** Removable modular effects board added for changing the function of the expression jack.
- **November 2022:** True relay bypass and silent switching added.
- **2023 final prototype:** Main audio circuit layout was reworked to solve stability/noise problems before final PCB production.

### Documented controls/features
- Gain/distortion control
- Amp pre-distortion harmonic-shaping control
- Mid and High active EQ
- Lomids mid-frequency shift
- Growl low-end enhancement
- Crunch broad mid boost
- Modular expression-jack system

## Version changes
The builder's development history documents functional revisions during the final design cycle rather than simple cosmetic changes. The notable changes are the modular expression system, relay bypass, and a re-laid-out BETA 2 audio board used to resolve noise/stability issues.

## Sound
Grand Classic is designed to span articulate overdrive through dense high-gain distortion while retaining control over the harmonic profile. The Amp, Mid, High, Lomids, Growl and Crunch functions provide broad shaping before and around the distortion stages, including tighter, fuller, honkier and more aggressive voicings.
