# Audiostorm — F258 Drive

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** F258 Drive
- **Builder:** Audiostorm
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Small-run British germanium overdrive loosely based on the DOD 250, with unusually elaborate hand-wired construction. [1]

## What this pedal is

Audiostorm's retired-product history identifies F258 Drive as a germanium overdrive loosely based on the DOD 250. The builder notes that the construction involved substantial internal wiring and was particularly tedious to assemble, which helps explain the limited production. [1]
