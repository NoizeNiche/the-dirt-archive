# Audiostorm — ADNA

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** ADNA

- **Identity:** Extremely small-run experimental overdrive whose design intentionally allowed user-accessible component customization. [1]

## What this pedal is
Audiostorm's retired-product history describes ADNA as a highly hand-wired experimental pedal. Only a few units were made, and the concept included provisions for owners to alter components rather than locking the circuit to one published production recipe. [1]
