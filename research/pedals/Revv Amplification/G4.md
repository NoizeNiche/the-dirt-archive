## What this pedal is
The G4 is Revv Amplification's high-gain distortion pedal designed around the company's red-channel amplifier voicing. Revv's current G4 V2 documentation treats the V2 as a later production version while retaining the core G4 concept.

## Sound
The G4 is a high-gain distortion voice intended to deliver tight, sustaining amplifier-style saturation rather than a low-gain overdrive response. Revv's current V2 documentation keeps the model in its aggressive red-channel family.
