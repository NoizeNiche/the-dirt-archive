# Chase Bliss Audio — Preamp MKII

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Preamp MKII
- **Builder:** Chase Bliss Audio / Benson Amps
- **Catalog type:** Boost / Overdrive / Fuzz / Preamp
- **Identity:** Automatone Preamp MKII, a motorized-fader analog preamp platform developed with Benson Amps, adding a two-mode fuzz section ahead of the preamp/EQ. [1][2][3]

## What this pedal is
Preamp MKII combines a Benson Chimera-derived preamp concept with a dedicated fuzz section and extensive analog EQ under digitally controlled motorized faders. The fuzz can operate in **wide-open** or **gated** modes and is placed before the preamp and midrange controls. [3]

## Circuit / architecture
- Analog signal path.
- Motorized faders and presets under digital control.
- Benson-inspired preamp/drive section.
- Fuzz section with **wide-open** and **gated** modes.
- Fuzz precedes preamp/midrange circuitry. [2][3]

## Sound
Preamp MKII covers clean boost, amp-like overdrive and fuzz, with the motorized analog controls allowing highly repeatable EQ and gain settings. The fuzz modes can shift from open saturation to strongly gated textures before the preamp/EQ section. [2][3]
