- **Identity:** Chase Bliss Audio Automatone Preamp MKII, a motorized-fader analog preamp/drive platform designed with Benson Amps.

## What this pedal is
Preamp MKII is a collaboration between Chase Bliss Audio and Benson Amps. Contemporary coverage describes it as spanning boost, overdrive, and fuzz with extensive EQ and clipping options, while retaining a fully analog audio path under digital control. [1][2]

The design combines the character of the Benson Chimera preamp with a fuzz section and resonant parametric mid controls associated with the Condor concept. [2]

## Versions and factory options
The pedal uses motorized faders and presets for its analog controls. The fuzz section has two modes: **wide-open fuzz** and **gated fuzz**, and it is inserted before the preamp and midrange controls. [3]

The broader platform supports expression/CV assignment and preset control. [2][3]

## Version changes
The checked sources identify Preamp MKII as the second-generation Automatone preamp design. No later numbered hardware revision was established.

## Sound
Preamp MKII can span clean boost, overdrive, and fuzz. Its motorized EQ/fader system allows extensive shaping, and the two-mode fuzz section can supply either open saturation or gated textures before the preamp/EQ stages. [1][3]
