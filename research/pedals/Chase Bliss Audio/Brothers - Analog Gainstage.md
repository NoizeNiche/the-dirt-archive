# Chase Bliss Audio — Brothers - Analog Gainstage

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Brothers - Analog Gainstage
- **Builder:** Chase Bliss Audio
- **Catalog type:** Boost / Overdrive / Fuzz
- **Identity:** All-analog dual gain platform developed with Resonant Electronic Design, with one IC-based side and one JFET side, each offering Boost/Drive/Fuzz modes. [1][2]

## Circuit architecture
- **Channel A:** Three gain modes, **Boost / Drive / Fuzz**.
- **Channel B:** Three gain modes, **Boost / Drive / Fuzz**.
- One side uses **JFET** gain circuitry.
- The other uses an **IC-based** gain circuit.
- Channels can run in series or parallel and can be ordered in either direction. Chase Bliss documents **33 routing combinations**. [1]

## Controls / digital layer
- Independent Gain/Tone/Mode control for each side.
- Global **Master** volume.
- Routing/order controls.
- Presets plus MIDI/expression/CV and internal DIP switching.
The audio path remains analog while digital control manages presets and external assignment. [1][2]

## Transistor / active devices
- **JFET** gain circuitry on one channel.
- **IC-based** gain circuitry on the other.
- Exact JFET/IC part numbers are not established.

## Sound
Brothers spans clean boost, overdrive and fuzz on both sides. Series routing can make one side push the other, while parallel routing preserves separate textures for mixed gain layers. [1][2]
