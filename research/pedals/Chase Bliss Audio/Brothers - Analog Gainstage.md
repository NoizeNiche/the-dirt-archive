# Chase Bliss Audio — Brothers - Analog Gainstage

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Brothers - Analog Gainstage
- **Builder:** Chase Bliss Audio
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Fuzz
- **Identity:** Chase Bliss Audio Brothers Analog Gainstage, a digitally controlled all-analog dual gain-stage pedal designed with Resonant Electronic Design.

## What this pedal is
The Brothers uses two independent analog channels, one IC-based and one JFET-based, with three circuit modes on each side: Boost, Drive, and Fuzz. Together they provide six gain circuits. [1][2]

The channels can be routed in parallel or series and in either order, producing 33 distinct routing combinations. The digital control layer stores presets and handles MIDI/expression/CV while the audio path remains analog. [1]

## Versions and factory options
Each channel has:
- Gain
- Tone
- Boost / Drive / Fuzz mode

The pedal also has a global Master volume and a routing control for parallel or series operation. Expression/MIDI/CV and DIP-switch functions provide additional control. [1][2]

## Transistor
One channel uses **discrete JFETs**; the other uses an **IC-based** gain circuit. Exact JFET and IC part numbers were not established in the checked sources. [1][2]

## Sound
Brothers spans clean boost, overdrive, and fuzz in both channels. The channels can remain independent, stack into each other, or run in parallel, allowing the player to move from transparent gain to more saturated and fuzzy combinations. [1][2]
