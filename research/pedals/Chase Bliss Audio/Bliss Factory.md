# Chase Bliss Audio — Bliss Factory

## PRP identity

- **Archive parent:** Bliss Factory
- **Builder:** Chase Bliss Audio / ZVEX Effects
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Collaboration combining a ZVEX Fuzz Factory-style analog fuzz with Chase Bliss digital control, presets, MIDI/expression/CV and a resonant low-pass filter. [1][2]

## What this pedal is

The Bliss Factory keeps an **analog fuzz audio path** but adds Chase Bliss's digital control architecture. Its six main parameters include the familiar Fuzz Factory-style Drive/Gate/Comp/Stab controls plus a resonant LPF. [1][3]

## Controls / architecture

- **VOLUME**
- **DRIVE**
- **GATE**
- **COMP**
- **STAB**
- **LPF**
- Rear-panel DIP/switch functions alter filter behavior, sub-frequency response and external-control assignments.
- Presets and MIDI/expression/CV are handled by the digital control system while the audio signal path remains analog. [1][3]

## Sound

Bliss Factory combines the unstable, highly interactive Fuzz Factory response with resonant low-pass filtering and preset recall. The result can move from conventional fuzz through gating, oscillation and extreme filter-resonant textures. [1][3]
