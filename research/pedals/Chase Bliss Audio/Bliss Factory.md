# Chase Bliss Audio — Bliss Factory

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Bliss Factory
- **Builder:** Chase Bliss Audio
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Chase Bliss Audio x ZVEX Bliss Factory, a digitally controlled analog fuzz based on the Fuzz Factory with a resonant low-pass filter.

## What this pedal is
The Bliss Factory is a collaboration between Chase Bliss Audio and ZVEX Effects. It combines the Fuzz Factory concept with Chase Bliss digital control, presets, MIDI/expression/CV functionality, and a resonant low-pass filter. [1][2]

## Colorways
- Reverb's product guide documents **Anodized Black** and **Silver** finishes for the 2020–2021 Pedal Movie edition. [2]
- A complete colorway history was not established.

## Versions and factory options
The documented main controls are:
- Volume
- Drive
- Gate
- Comp
- Stab
- LPF [1][3]

Additional rear-panel switches/DIP controls provide filter behavior, sub-frequency response, AUX control behavior, bypass/ramping, and external-control functions. [1][3]

## Version changes
The pedal was originally introduced as a Pedal Movie collaboration and later expanded beyond the first limited run. The checked sources do not establish a separate numbered hardware revision sequence. [2]

## Transistor
The pedal uses **two germanium transistors** in the fuzz circuit. Exact transistor part numbers were not established in the checked sources. [1][3]

## Diode
No exact-model diode specification was established.

## Sound
The Bliss Factory retains the unstable, highly interactive character of the Fuzz Factory while adding the ability to recall presets and shape the signal through the resonant low-pass filter. The additional controls can push it from conventional fuzz toward oscillation and extreme texture. [1][3]
