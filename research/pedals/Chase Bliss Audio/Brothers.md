# Chase Bliss Audio — Brothers

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Brothers
- **Builder:** Chase Bliss Audio
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz / Overdrive
- **Identity:** Chase Bliss Audio Brothers Analog Gainstage, an all-analog dual gain-stage pedal designed with Resonant Electronic Design.

## What this pedal is
Effects Database and Chase Bliss documentation describe Brothers as two independently controllable analog gain channels. Each side offers Boost, Drive, and Fuzz modes, giving six gain circuits, while the routing can be changed between series and parallel operation. [1][2]

## Colorways
No complete factory colorway sequence was established.

## Versions and factory options
Each channel provides:
- Gain.
- Tone.
- Mode: Boost / Drive / Fuzz.

The pedal also includes master volume, channel routing, presets, MIDI, expression/CV control, and internal DIP-switch customization. [1][2]

## Version changes
The checked sources do not establish a numbered factory revision sequence.

## Transistor
One gain channel is based on **JFET** circuitry and the other on an **IC-based** gain circuit. Exact component part numbers were not established. [1][2]

## Diode
No exact-model diode specification was established.

## Sound
Brothers spans boost, overdrive, and fuzz across two channels. The channels can be stacked or run in parallel, giving broad combinations from cleaner gain to more saturated and fuzzy textures. [1][2]
