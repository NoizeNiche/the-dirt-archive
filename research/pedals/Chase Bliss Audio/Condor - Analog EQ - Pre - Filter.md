# Chase Bliss Audio — Condor - Analog EQ / Pre / Filter

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Condor - Analog EQ / Pre / Filter
- **Builder:** Chase Bliss Audio
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Chase Bliss Audio Condor, an all-analog EQ/preamp/filter pedal with switchable clean/overdrive operation and digital control.

## What this pedal is
Effects Database documents the Condor as a 100% analog signal path with digital control, preset storage, MIDI, and a wide-ranging EQ/filter section. [1]

## Colorways
No complete factory colorway sequence was established in the checked sources.

## Versions and factory options
The documented features include:
- Switchable clean or overdrive.
- Parametric mid boost/cut from 150 Hz to 5 kHz with three Q settings.
- Bass boost/cut with three intensity settings.
- Low-pass filter with normal, slightly resonant, and very resonant settings.
- Ramping/hold functionality for filter-tremolo effects.
- Selectable true or buffered bypass. [1][2]

## Version changes
The original Condor predates the later Condor HiFi, which was redesigned around a higher-headroom internal architecture. The checked sources do not establish a numbered V1/V2 sequence for the original Condor itself. [1][3]

## Sound
Condor is designed as an all-in-one tone-shaping tool that can move from EQ and preamp duties into resonant filtering and overdrive. The sweepable midrange and resonant low-pass filter allow extreme frequency shaping as well as conventional gain use. [1][2]
