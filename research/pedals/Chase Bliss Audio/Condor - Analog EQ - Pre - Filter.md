# Chase Bliss Audio — Condor - Analog EQ / Pre / Filter

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Condor - Analog EQ / Pre / Filter
- **Builder:** Chase Bliss Audio
- **Catalog type:** EQ / Filter / Overdrive
- **Identity:** All-analog EQ/preamp/filter pedal with digital control, switchable clean/overdrive operation and resonant filtering. [1][2]

## Architecture
- Switchable **clean / overdrive** operation.
- Parametric mid control from approximately **150 Hz to 5 kHz** with selectable Q.
- Bass boost/cut.
- Resonant **low-pass filter** with multiple resonance settings.
- Ramping/hold capability for filter-tremolo-style effects.
- True or buffered bypass selection. [1][2]

## Circuit / control system
- Audio path is analog.
- Presets, MIDI, expression/CV and ramping are digitally controlled. [1]

## Sound
Condor can act as EQ, preamp, overdrive and resonant filter. The sweepable mids and resonant low-pass filter permit both subtle tone shaping and extreme frequency-focused effects. [1][2]
