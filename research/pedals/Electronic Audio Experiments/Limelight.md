## Sound

In preparation for the recording of Lament , we worked with Nick and Clayton to create a new op amp overdrive with equal parts aggression and chime.
While loosely based in the “breaker” lineage, we made significant changes to the circuit for superior articulation and a much wider range of gain.
An independent front-end boost rounds out the package, perfect for adding power to clean tones or pushing the overdrive side into heavy distortion.
