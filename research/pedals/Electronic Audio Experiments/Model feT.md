## Sound
In fact, on its own it is a fantastic mid-gain amplifier with a tone that is dark, yet clear and present—making it an exceptional pedal platform.
Most famous Model T tones come from putting a distortion or fuzz in front of the amp.
This broadens the range of gain and—in the true spirit of the original amp—allows for massive headroom and volume output.
