## What this pedal is

The Citadel is a preamp-style overdrive inspired by British tube amplifiers and the sound of the Sunn Model T lineage. EAE describes it as combining preamp and power-amp behavior to provide a full-bodied drive that responds naturally to boosts, drives, and fuzzes placed before it.

## Colorways

- Documented standard finishes include **Acid Etch** and **Fawn**.
- Special runs may use additional finishes, but finish is not treated as a circuit revision.

## Versions and factory options

- JFET-based amplifier-style gain stages.
- Relay switching with true bypass.
- Input impedance: 500kΩ.
- Output impedance: 1kΩ.
- 9V DC, 2.1mm center-negative power.
- The Citadel is designed to function as both a drive pedal and an amplifier-style preamp.

## Sound

The Citadel covers warm clean-ish drive, bright overdrive, and sizzling higher-gain tones. Its low-frequency response is deliberately large, reflecting the Model T/Super Bass lineage that influenced the design.
