## Sound

When the Halberd is disengaged, your signal passes through a high-headroom, class A JFET buffer to pre- serve its integrity over long cable runs.
There is a gentle midrange emphasis and a moderate amount of gain.
The up position (Clang) adds a significant high mid boost, to achieve more aggressive sounds than what the Depth control offers on its own.
