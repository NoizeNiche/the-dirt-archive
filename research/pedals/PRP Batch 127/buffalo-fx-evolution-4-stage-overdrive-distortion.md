# Buffalo FX — Evolution - 4 Stage Overdrive/Distortion

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Buffalo FX
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive
- **Identity:** Four-stage, Muff-derived drive/distortion with contour control.

## What this pedal is
The Evolution is a heavily reworked distortion/overdrive derived from the 1970s Big Muff family and a British builder's early-1990s interpretation of that circuit. Buffalo FX describes it as a four-stage design that combines silicon and germanium components and reaches from light drive through British-style tube-amp distortion. [1]

## Sound
Evolution is designed to retain articulate mids while moving from moderate drive into heavier British-style saturation. The Contour control changes the midrange balance, while the combined silicon/germanium implementation is intended to keep the response dynamic and touch-sensitive. [1]
