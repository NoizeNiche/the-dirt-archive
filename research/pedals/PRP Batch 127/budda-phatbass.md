# Budda — Phatbass

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Budda
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive
- **Identity:** Two-tube bass overdrive/preamp pedal.

## What this pedal is
Phatbass is a large-format tube overdrive pedal for bass, using two 12AX7 tubes. A surviving firsthand review documents Gain, Bass, Treble and Volume controls and a dedicated 12V supply, while Budda documentation groups Phatbass with its tube pedal family. [1][2]

## Sound
Phatbass is designed around a thick tube-overdrive response rather than a compact transistor bass drive. The independent Bass and Treble controls provide broad low/high shaping around the tube gain stage, with the large enclosure accommodating the two-valve preamp. [1]
