# Budda — Phatman

## PRP identity

- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Dual-12AX7 tube overdrive/preamp pedal.

## What this pedal is
The Phatman is a dual-12AX7 pure vacuum-tube overdrive pedal. Budda describes it as covering rhythm crunch through blistering lead gain, with Gain, Bass, Treble and Volume controls and easy access to the preamp tubes. [1]

## Sound
Phatman is designed to deliver tube-style crunch and high-gain lead saturation rather than a restrained low-gain overdrive. Budda notes that the Bass and Treble controls emphasize the midrange where the pedal develops its characteristic girth. [1]
