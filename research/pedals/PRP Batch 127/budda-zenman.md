# Budda — Zenman

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Budda
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Overdrive

## What this pedal is
The Zenman Overdrive/Boost was introduced by Budda in 2013. It provides a broad range from smooth overdrive to crunch and grind, adds a Phat/Vintage EQ switch, and includes an independent boost that can operate with or without the overdrive. [1][2]

## Sound
Zenman is designed as a flexible drive-and-boost unit, moving from smoother overdrive into harder crunch and grind. The Phat/Vintage switch changes the EQ character, while the footswitchable boost can be used alone for a clean level lift or alongside the drive section. [1][2]
