# Buffalo FX — CVIII - BC108 Silicon Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Buffalo FX
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Hand-built silicon Fuzz Face-style fuzz using selected BC108 transistors.

## What this pedal is
CVIII is Buffalo FX's silicon fuzz based on the classic Hendrix-era silicon Fuzz Face sound, with additional control over the fuzz range and low end. Effects Database and contemporary Japanese product coverage document selected BC108 transistors, Shape, Fuzz and Level controls, true bypass and hand construction. [1][2]

## Sound
CVIII starts from the classic silicon Fuzz Face sound but is designed to stay clearer as the fuzz is reduced. Shape trims low end, making the pedal useful for thicker fuzz sounds as well as tighter lead and band-mix applications. [1][2]
