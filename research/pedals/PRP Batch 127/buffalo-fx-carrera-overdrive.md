# Buffalo FX — Carrera Overdrive

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Buffalo FX
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Low-gain, amp-enhancing overdrive and boost.

## What this pedal is
Carrera is a low-to-mid-gain Buffalo FX overdrive designed to fill in low and upper mids while preserving the character of the guitar and amplifier. Surviving product coverage documents Gain, Level, Bass and Treble controls and describes the pedal as especially useful for clean and edge-of-breakup tones. [1][2][3]

## Sound
Carrera is intentionally low gain and works as both a foundation overdrive and an amp-enhancing boost. Buffalo's voicing emphasizes lower and upper mids for stronger note definition and presence without obscuring the amplifier's basic character. [2][3]

### Verified sound evidence
After getting the chance to review and demo the M-1 Ram’s Head Fuzz from Buffalo FX, we were looking forward to something a little milder from them, as the M-1 was a real powerhouse (in only the best of ways, mind you).
They responded with the Carrera Overdrive, a low-to-mid gain boostable dirt pedal that was designed for clean and edge-of-breakup types of tones.
The Carrera has a unique feature , in that it automatically adds in a boost to the low and upper mids, something that tends to be lacking with just a straight signal, or in a a number of drive pedals as well.
