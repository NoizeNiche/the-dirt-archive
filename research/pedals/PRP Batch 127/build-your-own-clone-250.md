# Build Your Own Clone — 250+

## PRP identity

## What this pedal is
BYOC 250+ is a DIY effects kit designed around the very closely related DOD 250 overdrive and MXR Distortion+ circuits. The kit includes the parts needed to build either configuration and extra diodes for experimenting with the clipping section. [1]

The 250+ can be built toward the classic DOD 250 overdrive/preamp sound or the closely related MXR Distortion+ response.
