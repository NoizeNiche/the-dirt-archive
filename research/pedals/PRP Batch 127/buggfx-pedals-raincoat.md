# BuGGFX Pedals — Raincoat

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** BuGGFX Pedals
- **Catalog type:** Distortion / Fuzz / Overdrive
- **Identity:** Wide-range dirt pedal that moves between overdrive, distortion and octave fuzz.

## What this pedal is
Raincoat is a BuGGFX pedal designed to span from dirty, fat overdrive through distortion and into octave-tinged fuzz. Effects Database describes it as a deliberately broad dirt design whose character changes substantially with the guitar's volume control. [1]

## Sound
Raincoat is unusually responsive to guitar volume, moving from a darker clean boost into fat overdrive and more aggressive, octave-tinged fuzz as the signal level rises. The available descriptions consistently place it near the border between overdrive, distortion and fuzz rather than in one narrow category. [1][3]
