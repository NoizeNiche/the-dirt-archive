# Budda — Om Overdrive

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** Budda
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** Focused-EQ overdrive with enough gain to reach fuzz-like saturation.

## What this pedal is
Budda introduced the Om Overdrive as part of its 2013 pedal line. Budda describes it as a solo-oriented overdrive whose gain moves from vintage overdrive toward the beginning of fuzz territory, with a focused EQ intended to help the guitar cut through a dense mix. [1][2]

## Sound
The Om is voiced for a focused, forward drive sound that helps solos remain articulate in a busy mix. Its gain control covers vintage-style overdrive and can be pushed toward fuzzy saturation, while the Tone control ranges from dark to bright. [1][2]
