# A.Y.A — Bass Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Bass Fuzz
- **Builder:** A.Y.A
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** A.Y.A Tokyo Japan Bass Fuzz, a bass-focused two-control fuzz distinct from the later A.Y.A FrontGate BassDrive and from the later-labeled Bass Fuzz II listings.

## What this pedal is
A.Y.A's Bass Fuzz is documented by Effects Database as a fuzz pedal with only two user controls: **Fuzz** and **Volume**. Effects Database published the exact-model entry on **October 3, 2007**. That publication date should not be treated as the original manufacturing/release date. [1]

The builder is A.Y.A Tokyo Japan, associated with designer/builder **Makoto Kawai**. Effects Database identifies Kawai as A.Y.A's owner and builder; A.Y.A's own history describes the development of its compact pedal line after the earlier wah work and names later products including BM-Fuzz, Drivesta, Warm Chorus and FrontGate BassDrive. [2][3]

## Controls
- **Fuzz:** controls the amount of fuzz. [1]
- **Volume:** controls the effect output level. [1]

No additional tone, blend, bias, gate, mode, or internal-control specification was found for the base Bass Fuzz in the checked exact-model sources.

## Production history
A first-person owner reference from 2011 lists the **A.Y.A tokyo japan BASS FUZZ** as a **limited 30-unit fuzz** and says the owner had sold his example. A separate version of the same owner's equipment index identifies the pedal as a limited 30-unit run. The owner later described his own example as number 20 of that 30-unit run. This is valuable historical evidence, but the production count remains **owner-reported rather than independently confirmed by surviving A.Y.A factory documentation**. [4][5]

A current Japanese used-equipment/buyback reference separately lists **A.Y.A Tokyo Japan / BASS FUZZ**, supporting the continued recognition of the exact base model in the used market. [6]

## Version separation
The base catalog identity is **Bass Fuzz**, not **Bass Fuzz II**.

Current used-market listings explicitly labeled **BASS FUZZ II** are treated as a separate model/version and are not used as evidence for the base model's controls, artwork, circuitry, or photo. [7]

No reliable numbered factory revision history for the original Bass Fuzz was established beyond this later Bass Fuzz II identity.

## Colorways and enclosure
A surviving exact-model image indexed from a 2024 Mercari listing shows a compact enclosure with:
- blue/sparkle finish,
- orange floral graphic,
- A.Y.A tokyo japan branding,
- **Fuzz** and **Vol** cream-colored knobs,
- **BASS FUZZ** lettering,
- central footswitch.

This is strong exact-model visual evidence because the listing is explicitly titled **A.Y.A tokyo japan BASS FUZZ** and the photographed control layout matches the two-control documentation. The image is retained as research evidence, not as a local archive asset, because the underlying marketplace page is no longer reliably retrievable for direct image extraction. [8]

The archive should not treat later BM-FUZZ imagery as a substitute. A.Y.A's current product page documents BM-FUZZ as a separate three-control design with Fuzz, Tone and Level, visually unlike the two-control Bass Fuzz. [9]

## Sound
The strongest firsthand sonic evidence comes from the original owner reference. He characterizes the Bass Fuzz as a strongly distorted, gritty bass fuzz that still preserves the bass's core rather than becoming completely smeared. He reports strong forward presence in a band mix and relatively little sustain, making the effect particularly useful for aggressive, active bass playing rather than long held notes. He associated its character with John Wetton's live bass tone on **USA**. [4]

These are **user-reported observations**, not manufacturer frequency-response or circuit measurements.

## Current manufacturer status
The current A.Y.A product page lists newer/current models including **BM-FUZZ, FrontGate BassDrive, Drivesta, R-COMP and SuperAtomicDistortion**, but does not list the historical Bass Fuzz. This establishes the current page's product lineup, not a documented discontinuation date. [9]
