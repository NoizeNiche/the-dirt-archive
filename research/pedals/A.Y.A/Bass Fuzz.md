# A.Y.A — Bass Fuzz

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Bass Fuzz
- **Builder:** A.Y.A
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** A.Y.A Tokyo Japan bass-oriented fuzz, separately cataloged from the later Frontgate Bass Drive.

## What this pedal is
Bass Fuzz is a simple two-control fuzz for bass, built around **Fuzz** and **Volume**. Effects Database lists it as an A.Y.A fuzz pedal, while surviving used-market references identify it as a limited Japanese-made model. [1][2]

## Colorways
- Surviving examples show hand-finished, sparkly/custom artwork rather than a single standardized finish.
- Color differences are treated as cosmetic unless a circuit change is documented.

## Versions and factory options
### Bass Fuzz production
- Fuzz control
- Volume control
- bass-focused fuzz voice
- compact A.Y.A handmade enclosure

An older owner reference describes a **30-unit limited run**, but this production quantity is not independently confirmed by A.Y.A documentation. [2]

## Sound
Bass Fuzz is a straightforward, compact fuzz intended to add aggressive harmonic grit without a complicated control set. The small control layout suggests a direct approach: set the amount of fuzz, set the output level, and let the character of the bass and amplifier provide much of the final tone. [1]
