# A.Y.A — Frontgate Bass Drive

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Frontgate Bass Drive
- **Builder:** A.Y.A
- **Catalog type:** Overdrive
- **Identity:** A.Y.A Tokyo Japan FrontGate BassDrive, a bass-specific overdrive/preamp with internal gain adjustment.

## What this pedal is
Effects Database describes Frontgate Bass Drive as a boutique overdrive designed specifically for bass. A.Y.A's official documentation says the pedal keeps low frequencies from collapsing as the Drive control is raised and can move naturally from a near-clean sound into drive. [1][2]

Effects Database published the exact-model entry on **September 21, 2011**. That is a database publication date, not a guaranteed factory release date. [1]

## Controls
A.Y.A specifies four front-panel controls:
- **Drive**
- **Treble**
- **Bass**
- **Vol** [2]

Effects Database describes these as Drive, Bass, Treble and Volume and explains their basic functions. [1]

## Internal gain switch
The pedal contains a mini-switch on the circuit board that provides **two gain settings** for the drive circuit. A.Y.A explicitly states that this is **not** a clipping-diode switching system. [2]

Factory/default operation is set for typical passive pickups. For higher-output active or passive pickups, the alternate setting reduces gain and can make the pedal useful in a preamp-like role with less unwanted distortion. [2]

## Power and bypass
A.Y.A documents:
- DC input
- mono input/output
- true bypass
- **battery not supported** [2]

Effects Database specifies **9V DC center-negative** power. [1]

## Circuit / components
The pedal is an analog overdrive, but no exact transistor or diode part number is established in the checked sources.

- **Transistor:** Exact device unknown.
- **Diode:** Exact device unknown.
- **Schematic/component values:** Not verified.
- **Important distinction:** The internal gain switch is not a diode-clipping selector. [2]

## Sound
A.Y.A characterizes the pedal as bass-specific overdrive that retains the bass core as gain increases. Effects Database describes a range from clean boost through gritty distortion while retaining low-end clarity. [1][2]

In a 2011 interview, A.Y.A builder Makoto Kawai said FrontGate Bass Drive was the pedal he was most proud of, specifically citing its artwork, circuit design and ability to keep a fat sound while crunching. That statement is builder commentary, not a laboratory measurement. [3]

## Naming / artwork
A.Y.A explains that “FrontGate” refers to Tokyo's Otemon gate and the flowering quince blossoms near the Imperial Palace gate that inspired the artwork and name. The company uses Japanese seasonal imagery throughout the A.Y.A pedal line. [2][4]

## Manufacturer history
A.Y.A's history identifies FrontGate BassDrive as part of the compact pedal line that followed R-COMP, BM-Fuzz and Drivesta. A.Y.A credits Makoto Kawai as the builder and describes the pedals as hand-built with circuit boards plus hand wiring. [3][4]
