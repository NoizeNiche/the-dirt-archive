# A.Y.A — Frontgate Bass Drive

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** Frontgate Bass Drive

## What this pedal is
Frontgate Bass Drive is a bass overdrive designed to **retain low-end weight as gain rises**. It uses Drive, Treble, Bass and Volume controls plus an internal gain switch that adjusts the drive circuit for different pickup outputs. [1][2]

## Colorways
- The documented finish is a **silver enclosure with pink, green and orange floral artwork**.
- A.Y.A examples commonly use hand-finished graphics, so cosmetic differences are not automatically treated as circuit versions. [2]

## Versions and factory options

A Korean retailer also documents a 100-unit version and an internal active/passive-related gain setting, but that production quantity is treated as retailer documentation rather than a universally verified factory count. [2]

## Sound
Frontgate is designed to keep the low end intact while moving from mostly clean signal reinforcement into gritty overdrive. The internal gain selector helps the pedal behave sensibly with different bass pickup output levels, and the separate bass/treble controls make it practical as both a drive and preamp-style shaping stage. [1][2]
