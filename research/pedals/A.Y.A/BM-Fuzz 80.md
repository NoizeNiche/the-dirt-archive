# A.Y.A — BM-Fuzz 80

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** BM-Fuzz 80
- **Builder:** A.Y.A
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** A.Y.A's 1980s third-generation Big Muff-inspired BM-Fuzz.

## What this pedal is
BM-Fuzz 80 keeps the BM-Fuzz format but targets the **1980s, third-generation Big Muff/discrete** character rather than the earlier 1970s sound. The same basic Fuzz, Tone and Level controls are used, while A.Y.A's published descriptions identify different transistor gain and internal wiring choices from the BM-Fuzz 70. [1][2]

## Colorways
- A **dark blue/purple face in a silver enclosure** is documented in surviving examples.
- The artwork and knob color differ from BM-Fuzz 70, but those cosmetic changes are not treated as separate revisions.

## Version changes
The meaningful change is the voicing and gain-stage arrangement relative to BM-Fuzz 70. Contemporary descriptions specifically call out changes to transistor gain and internal wiring while keeping the same basic control layout. [2]

## Sound
BM-Fuzz 80 is tighter and more assertive than the 1970s-oriented BM-Fuzz 70, with the later Muff influence giving it a stronger, more direct attack. It still ranges from conventional distortion into crushed fuzz, but its emphasis is the more focused, late-period Muff character. [1][2]
