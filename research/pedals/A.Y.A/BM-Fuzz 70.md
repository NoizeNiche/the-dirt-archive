# A.Y.A — BM-Fuzz 70

## PRP identity
- **Archive parent:** BM-Fuzz 70
- **Builder:** A.Y.A
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** A.Y.A's 1970s Big Muff-inspired BM-Fuzz.

## What this pedal is
BM-Fuzz 70 is a Japanese-made fuzz based on the **1970s Big Muff / Ram's Head family**. It uses a simple three-control layout and was marketed as capable of moving from distortion-box territory into crushed fuzz. [1][2]

## Colorways
- A documented example uses a **black faceplate in a silver enclosure**, with the A.Y.A artwork and large cream knobs.
- Cosmetic variations are treated separately from the circuit identity.

## Version changes
The BM-Fuzz 70 is the 1970s-flavored member of the BM-Fuzz family. A.Y.A's related BM-Fuzz 80 uses a different gain/transistor arrangement and internal wiring approach, so the two are kept as separate catalog identities. [1]

## Transistor

- **Exact transistor/device:** A Fairchild transistor is cited in surviving product descriptions, but the exact part number is not established. [1][2]

## Sound
BM-Fuzz 70 aims for thick Big Muff-style fuzz while retaining enough definition to cover more conventional distortion sounds. The Fuzz, Tone and Level controls keep the operation simple, while the 1970s voicing leans toward a smoother, more sustaining Muff family response. [1][2]
