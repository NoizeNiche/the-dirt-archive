# Copper Gear - Black Howler

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Copper Gear
- **Catalog identity:** Black Howler
- **Catalog type:** Volume / Swell
- **Identity:** Copper Gear momentary swell pedal documented by Effects Database.

## What this pedal is
Effects Database documents Black Howler as a swell pedal that sits between an automatic swell and a manual volume control. [1]
Copper Gear's description gives the unit two knobs and a momentary footswitch. [1]

## Controls
- **Start Volume:** sets the starting volume. A lower starting level creates a larger swell.
- **Time:** controls the swell rise time, from immediate response to roughly 10 seconds.
- **Momentary footswitch:** activates the swell while held; releasing it returns the signal to the starting volume. [1]
