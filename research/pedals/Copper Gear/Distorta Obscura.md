# Copper Gear - Distorta Obscura

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Copper Gear
- **Catalog identity:** Distorta Obscura
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz / Distortion
- **Identity:** Exact-model Copper Gear Distorta Obscura documented by Effects Database.

## What this pedal is
Distorta Obscura is a user-configurable analog fuzz/distortion platform built around socketed components. A ZIF (zero insertion force) socket lets the user replace the circuit's transistor and capacitor pairs instead of locking the pedal to one fixed component set.

## Controls
- **Volume:** overall pedal output.
- **Degree:** gain setting.
- **Bias:** controls the transistor operating point. Correct bias favors smoother operation; intentionally incorrect bias can push a transistor toward saturation or cutoff.

## Factory component set
- Five pairs of different NPN transistors.
- Six pairs of different-value ceramic capacitors.
- The documented design permits other NPN transistors and capacitor types to be substituted experimentally.
- A center-positive 9V battery adapter, manual, and certificate of authenticity are documented accessories.

## Sound
The pedal's sound is intentionally variable. Effects Database documents the component interchangeability as producing anything from thick low-end distortion to lighter high-frequency breakup, with transistor gain/operating differences directly affecting the resulting fuzz or distortion.

## Construction
