# Copper Gear - Distorta Azura

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Copper Gear
- **Catalog identity:** Distorta Azura
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Copper Gear analog fuzz with transistor-bias control derived from the Distorta Obscura concept.

## What this pedal is
Effects Database describes Distorta Azura as Copper Gear's fuzz design for players seeking very thick fuzz, using the same analog purity and transistor-bias control concept as Distorta Obscura. [1]

## Controls
The documented controls are:
- **Bias**
- **Volume**
- **Gain / Degree** [1]

## Versions and factory options
- **True bypass.** [1]
- **Power:** heavy-duty center-positive 2.1mm jack and 9V battery adapter cable. [1]
- **Construction:** acid-etched 20-gauge copper, hand-cut and stained solid-wood sides, recessed input/output jacks and hardened knob rails. [1]
- No numbered hardware revision sequence was established.

## Version changes
No reliable dated hardware revision sequence was established.

## Construction
Copper Gear states its pedals are handmade in-house, including the copper, wood, leather and brass work. [2]
Distorta Azura uses an analog fuzz architecture with transistor-bias control rather than digital modeling. [1]

## Transistor
- **Architecture:** transistor-based analog fuzz with adjustable bias. [1]
- Exact transistor part number was not documented in the checked sources.

## Diode
- **Exact clipping/rectification diode/device:** Not publicly documented.

## Sound
The Bias control can starve the transistors for gated, deteriorating behavior; increased bias produces the intended thick wall of fuzz. Gain extends the range from full fuzz into deeper saturation. [1]
