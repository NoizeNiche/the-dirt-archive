## What this pedal is
The Distorta Destructo is a silicon fuzz derived from the Distorta Obscura. Its defining feature is a deliberately narrow transistor-bias range that can move the circuit from conventional fuzz into sputtering, gated, and synth-like behavior. Effects Database documents it as a highly experimental fuzz rather than a fixed-voice recreation.

## Controls
- **Volume:** overall output level.
- **Degree (Gain):** controls the amount of fuzz/gain.
- **Bias:** shifts the transistor operating point; intentionally incorrect bias can move the circuit toward saturation/cutoff behaviors.

## Construction and power
- Acid-etched 20-gauge copper enclosure.
- Hand-cut and stained solid-wood sides.
- Recessed input/output jacks.
- Hardened knob rails.
- Heavy-duty true-bypass footswitch.
- Heavy-duty center-positive 2.1 mm power jack.
- 9V battery adapter cable; the documented power guidance warns against daisy chaining.

## Sound
Copper Gear describes a very wide silicon-fuzz range, from near-clean operation through aggressive fuzz, with the Bias control enabling sputtering, gated, and near-synth textures. The builder explicitly embraces operation outside a narrowly fixed normal setting.
