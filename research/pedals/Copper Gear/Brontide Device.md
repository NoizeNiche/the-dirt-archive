# Copper Gear - Brontide Device

## Research identity
- **Builder:** Copper Gear
- **Catalog identity:** Brontide Device
- **Catalog type:** Distortion
- **Identity:** Handmade diode-customizable analog guitar distortion pedal from Copper Gear.

## What this pedal is
Effects Database describes Brontide Device as a handmade distortion pedal whose defining feature is user-selectable clipping components. [1]
Copper Gear's maker interview describes the company as fully analog and hand-built, using transistors, op-amps and sometimes tubes while avoiding digital and surface-mount construction. [2]

## Controls
- **Volume**
- **Degree**: distortion amount/gain.
- **Clean/Aberration toggle**: routes clean versus distorted signal.
- **Diode selector/socket:** allows different pairs of diodes to be installed and used as the clipping network. [1]

## Versions and factory options
- Supplied with **1N4148 silicon switching diodes**. [1]
- User can substitute silicon, germanium or LED devices, or remove the diodes entirely for raw distortion. [1]
- **Power:** 9V DC, center positive, 300mA or greater.
- External 5mm-to-9V battery adapter included. [1]
- True bypass. [1]
- Construction materials include wood, copper, brass and aluminum; internal wiring is solid-core copper. [1]

## Version changes
No numbered production revision sequence was established.

## Construction
All devices were handmade, tested and numbered before shipping according to the Effects Database record. [1]

## Transistor
- Copper Gear states its analog pedals can use transistors, op-amps and sometimes tubes; exact Brontide device part numbers were not established. [2]

## Diode
- **Stock clipping devices:** 1N4148 silicon switching diodes. [1]
- **User-adjustable:** diode pairs may be replaced with other diode types, including germanium and LEDs, or omitted. [1]

## Sound
The diode configuration directly changes the distortion character. The documented pedal can cover buzz, fuzz, crunch and other distortion textures depending on the clipping arrangement. [1]
