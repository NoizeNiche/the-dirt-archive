## What this pedal is
How to Get the Tone ↓ The short version The Myriad Fuzz is a hybrid germanium/silicon fuzz, hand-built by Vemuram in Tokyo in a solid brass enclosure, developed with American blues guitarist and producer Josh Smith.

## Colorways
- At sixteen he toured the United States with his band the Rhino Cats, who released Born Under a Blue Sign and Woodsheddin’ ; at eighteen he formed the trio Josh Smith and the Frost, whose Too Damn Cold was produced by Jim Gaines — the man behind Stevie Ray Vaughan ‘s In Step .
- But the name belongs first to a much larger device — the Myriad, sometimes catalogued as the Myriad “Big Box” Wet/Dry Fuzz — which Josh Smith demonstrated at the Vemuram booth at Winter NAMM on 17 January 2020, and which Equipboard dates to 2020 in a brass finish.
