# BreakFuzz — Surly Fuzz Germanium

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** BreakFuzz
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Two-transistor germanium fuzz based on the classic Fuzz Face circuit.

## What this pedal is
Surly Fuzz Germanium uses a classic Fuzz Face-style topology with two NOS Russian GT402 germanium transistors. It is point-to-point wired and handmade in Thailand. [1]

## Sound
The circuit is intended to cover the familiar Fuzz Face range from lighter fuzz into heavy saturation. The separate pre-gain and bias controls give the player more control over input drive and transistor operating point than a basic two-control Fuzz Face format. [1]
