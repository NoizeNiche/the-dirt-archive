# BreakFuzz — Surly Fuzz Silicon BC183

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** BreakFuzz
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Two-transistor silicon Fuzz Face-style fuzz using BC183 transistors.

## What this pedal is
The Silicon BC183 is a point-to-point, handmade Fuzz Face-based circuit using NOS silicon BC183 transistors. It adds Pre-gain and Bias controls to the standard Volume/Fuzz arrangement. [1]

## Sound
The silicon version follows the Fuzz Face family but uses silicon transistors for a firmer, more direct response than the germanium versions. Pre-gain and bias provide additional control over the amount and character of saturation. [1]
