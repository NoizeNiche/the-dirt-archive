# BreakFuzz — Surly Fuzz Germanium Low Gain

## PRP identity
- **Builder:** BreakFuzz
- **Catalog type:** Fuzz
- **Identity:** Lower-gain variant of the Surly Fuzz Germanium.

## What this pedal is
The Low Gain version keeps the classic Fuzz Face-style germanium circuit concept but specifies a lower-gain Q2 stage. BreakFuzz uses NOS Russian GT402 germanium transistors and point-to-point construction. [1]

## Version changes
The defining change is the lower-gain Q2 configuration. [1]

## Transistor
- **Technology:** Germanium.
- **Device:** NOS Russian GT402, with low gain selected for Q2. [1]

## Diode
Exact clipping diode/device not documented.

## Sound
Compared with the standard germanium Surly Fuzz, the Low Gain version is deliberately biased toward a less saturated response. It retains the Fuzz Face-derived character while making the fuzz easier to keep articulate at lower drive settings. [1]
