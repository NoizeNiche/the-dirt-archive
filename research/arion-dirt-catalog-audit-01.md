# Arion historical dirt catalog audit 01

## Scope
Builder-level catalog pass for Arion, concentrating on overdrive, distortion, fuzz and adjacent octave/saturation products. This is a catalog record, not a circuit-analysis document.

## Identity
Effects Database documents Arion as a Japanese effects brand first made by Prince Tsushinkogyo, with later production by Ueno Kaihatsu Center Ltd. Preserve Arion as the marketed manufacturer/brand node, with production-location changes retained as separate historical states.

## Dirt catalog signals
The indexed Arion universe contains at least the following dirt or dirt-adjacent products:
- MDI-1 Tube Mania
- MDI-2 Bass Distortion
- MDS-1 Distortion
- MMP-1 Metal Plus
- MOC-1 Octave
- SOD-1 Stereo Overdrive
- SDI-1 Stereo Distortion
- SMM-1 Stereo Metal Master
- MTE-1 Tubulator

Effects Database's Arion category indexes 35 products overall, so the dirt layer above should be treated as a strong indexed baseline rather than a lifetime-complete catalog.

## Relationship / OEM signals
Effects Database links Arion MDI-1 to Monarch MDI-1 Distortion and Arion SDI-1 to Sakura SDI-1, indicating that marketed-name relationships must be retained instead of automatically canonicalizing every label as a separate factory. A separate series-level relationship also connects the Arion Tube Driver family with Axtron, Columbus, Vision and Profile+ branding.

## Chronology
The surviving public evidence strongly supports an original Japanese Arion manufacturing phase followed by later production in Sri Lanka. Individual web-database dates are inconsistent or incomplete, so this audit does not assign unsupported exact production years to every model.

## Evidence disposition
- VERIFIED_BASELINE: Arion manufacturer identity and 35-product indexed category.
- INDEXED_DIRT: MDI-1, MDI-2, MDS-1, MMP-1, MOC-1, SOD-1, SDI-1, SMM-1, MTE-1.
- RELATIONSHIP_EVIDENCE: Monarch MDI-1, Sakura SDI-1, and later multi-brand Tube Driver family.
- UNRESOLVED: exact transition dates between Japanese and Sri Lankan production for each dirt model; complete lifetime catalog outside indexed sources.
