## Required work for every record
1. Establish builder/brand/OEM/distributor/designer identity separately where evidence allows.
2. Establish product family and manufacturing family separately where useful.
3. Record introduction, revision, generation, production period and edition dates independently.
4. Capture external identification clues: enclosure, artwork, knobs, control count/layout, jacks, switches, labels, country markings and distinctive finishes.
5. Record functional differences and documented electronics identity only to the level needed for historical identification.
6. Preserve source evidence and uncertainty claim-by-claim.
7. Connect research findings to the actual public pedal record instead of leaving them stranded in discovery files.
8. Backfill previously cataloged pedals so every one receives a variant/generation review and identification assessment, even when the result is "no confirmed variant located".

## Editorial boundary
Document the object, its history, lineage, production identity and identification evidence. Do not publish schematics, PCB layouts, complete component recipes or cloning instructions.
