## Evidence anchors
Effects Database indexes Teisco, ELK, Aria, Moollon, Korg, Kingston and Sekova histories/products. Key records include Teisco TF-1 and Wau Wau Fuzz, ELK 11-product universe, Moollon's 2002-2003 timeline, Korg Series 1's DST/OVD models, and Sekova Model 59 attribution notes.

## Research policy applied
Retail brand, physical manufacturer, OEM and relationship roles remain separate. Current/revival-era products are not merged into vintage families merely because names match. Ambiguous attribution is retained as discovery/evidence-qualified status.
