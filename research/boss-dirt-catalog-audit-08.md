# BOSS Dirt Catalog Audit 08

## Scope
Focused catalog reconciliation of BOSS compact dirt products using current BOSS historical service/anniversary records and official product documentation. This pass adds or strengthens models that were previously underrepresented in the master BOSS ledger, while preserving retail-model identities and production corridors.

## Confirmed chronological anchors from official BOSS records

The official BOSS anniversary chronology confirms a continuous dirt timeline that includes HM-2 (1983-1992), DF-2 (1984-1993), OD-2 (1985-era), DS-2 (1987-present lineage), MZ-2 (1987-1992), OS-2 (1990-present), MT-2 (1991-present), FZ-2 (1993-1997), FZ-3 (1997-1999), XT-2 (1996-1998), OD-3 (1997-present), MD-2 (2001-present), and later dirt models. This provides a reliable backbone for the 1980s-2000s catalog layer.

The official service-history table is especially useful because it distinguishes discontinued products from continuing lineages. It records OD-1 as 1977-1985, OD-2 as 1985-1995 in one current service view, OD-2R as 1994-1999, OD-20 as 2002-2010, and OD-3 as beginning in 1997. Where BOSS sources disagree on a boundary year, the archive marks that disagreement rather than silently choosing one value.

## Archive rules reinforced by this pass

1. A BOSS product appearing inside a digital multi-effect or modeling platform can be a dirt-relevant catalog record without being treated as equivalent to a compact analog pedal.
2. Anniversary and Waza products remain separate editions/states even when the marketing references an older model.
3. Hybrid pedals such as DF-2 remain in scope because distortion is a core marketed function, while their secondary effect function is documented as a hybrid attribute.
4. Short-lived products such as XT-2 are catalog records in their own right even when later secondary sources focus mostly on better-known BOSS models.
5. Production dates are evidence fields, not assumptions. Conflicting official date tables remain explicitly unresolved.
