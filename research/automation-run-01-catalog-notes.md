# Catalog Automation Run 01

## Research completed

This run cross-checked three builder-level catalogs in Effects Database for complete-lineup preservation:

### BJF Electronics (BJFE)
Effects Database currently lists 30 products and identifies Björn Juhl as the Stockholm-based builder. The documented timeline says the company was founded July 1, 1999 and the first Baby Blue Overdrives were delivered December 13, 2000. The catalog spans fuzz, overdrive, distortion, boost, compression, EQ, modulation and filter products. Notable preservation priorities include Bone Bender MkI/MkII, Dyna Red Distortion, Face 109 Fuzz, Fuzz 68, Honey Bee Overdrive, Little Green Wonder, Model-series products, Pine Green Compressor, Sparkle Face and Snow White Fuzz.

### Fuhrmann
Effects Database currently lists 39 products. The catalog is unusually valuable because it captures a broad Brazilian effects manufacturer lineup rather than only dirt pedals. It includes Big Fuzz, Classic Fuzz, Bass Fuzz, Battle Power De Ros signature Hyper Gain, Classic 800, Dual High Gain, High Gain, Hot Rod, Lead Drive, Metal Drive, Power Drive, Punch Box, Rocking Tube, Super Metal Drive, Tube Drive, plus modulation, utility and delay products. Current 2026 multimedia shows an active production effort, including new mini-pedal production in January 2026, so current catalog changes should be treated as living data rather than a closed historical snapshot.

### Stomp Under Foot
Effects Database currently lists 30 products and identifies Matt Pasquerella in Haverhill, Massachusetts as the builder. Timeline material places organized pedal building in summer 2006. The lineup is especially important for Big Muff, Tone Bender, Japanese Big Muff Sustainar and related historical recreation work. High-priority variation records include Dirty Rooster, Red Menace, The Shag MkII/MkIII, Silver Foxx, Vintage Pumpkin Pi, Mud Creek, Mountain Goat, Mean Green Machine, Rhinosaur and Elk Big Mag Sustainar. Product pages document meaningful high-level distinctions such as germanium/silicon/hybrid approaches, limited editions, special enclosure formats and NOS transistor sourcing without publishing reconstruction-level circuit details.

## Editorial handling

- Preserve complete lineup discovery records even where product-level history is not yet researched.
- Treat EDB as a discovery backbone, not as a sole authority.
- Preserve source URLs and dates for later verification.
- Product-level follow-up should prioritize production periods, enclosure/control changes, OEM relationships, high-level component distinctions, community evidence and image/rights leads.
- Do not publish gutshots, schematics, PCB layouts, full BOMs or cloning instructions.
