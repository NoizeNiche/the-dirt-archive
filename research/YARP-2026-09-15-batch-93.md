# YARP 2026-09-15 Batch 93: ACA Fuzz King / Fuzz Sound Label Reconciliation

## Scope
Builder-first reconciliation of the Amplifier Corporation of America Fuzz King and related Fuzz Sound marketed states.

## Findings
- Amplifier Corporation of America (ACA) is retained as the physical manufacturer/product entity for the Fuzz King.
- Effects Database dates the Fuzz King to summer 1967 and identifies Merson/Unicord as distributor.
- A fall 1967 Lafayette Radio Electronics catalog advertised the same product as the Fuzz Sound. Lafayette remains a retail/private-label/export identity, not a separate factory.
- The archive therefore keeps ACA -> Fuzz King as the product/manufacturer relationship and Lafayette -> Fuzz Sound as a marketed-label relationship.
- No evidence in this pass is sufficient to merge ACA with LRE, UMI, Applied Audio Products, or the Japanese wedge-fuzz builders merely from visual or functional resemblance.
