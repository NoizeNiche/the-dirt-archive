## Evidence / boundary notes
- Indian FX's first-party site identifies the operation in Santo André, São Paulo and currently presents JUMA Drive, SABUIÁ Distortion, KAMPA Drive and ARUAK Fuzz v2. JUMA is explicitly based on the Zen Drive concept, SABUIÁ is presented as a RAT-inspired distortion, and ARUAK v2 is a handwired silicon Fuzz Face-based fuzz.
- RAD Custom Shop's current storefront exposes a larger overall catalog spanning compressor, modulation, overdrive, fuzz, distortion and preamp categories. The batch maps recoverable dirt products while keeping the current storefront snapshot separate from older catalog names such as Haze.
- Cachalote Audio's current and archived storefronts document Ushuaia Drive, Formiga B. Drive, Formiga Drive Special Edition and 214 Fuzz MKII. Specialist coverage dates a 2024 catalog overview and identifies 214 as evolved from the Sam Ash Fuzz Stainer, while Formiga and Ushuaia provide distinct gain architectures.
- Ed's Mod Shop is documented as a São Paulo handmade builder that also modifies commercial pedals. Its surviving catalog evidence includes eight products; six dirt/bass-dirt records are mapped here, while additional text-only model names remain queued for later reconciliation.
- MG Music's historical timeline dates its first four pedals to early 2004. The builder interview documents in-house construction and names a dirt-heavy catalog that includes Creamy, Crunsh, MgMuffFeliz, Lexotone and Tony Tender. The archive records historical model names without assuming every later variant is a new product family.
- Luxury Tone FX's current pages document the handcrafted Redbender, Zonk Project and Overdrive Sustainer 2 concepts, plus the El Amir signature high-gain distortion. Tribute/interpretation language is retained as a relationship note rather than rewritten as authorship of the historical originals.

New builder records therefore represent only entities not already present in the main-branch census search results at the time of this pass.
