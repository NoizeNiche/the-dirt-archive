# Archive Collection Rules

## Current mission

Build the inventory first.

**Builder → Products → Photos → Light pedal information**

Every research task should directly strengthen one of these four layers.

## Builder collection

Record builder, manufacturer, or marketed-brand names connected to dirt pedals. Keep relationship details only to the degree needed to avoid obvious duplicate or incorrect attribution.

## Product collection

Inventory overdrive, distortion, and fuzz products. Capture the product/model name and enough category information to place it correctly.

Do not pause inventory because variants, generations, OEM history, or exact production dates remain unresolved.

## Photo collection

Collect exterior pedal photographs. Prefer manufacturer or builder photographs, permission-cleared/owned photographs, and appropriately licensed reference images. Keep source and credit information.

Internal views, gutshots, PCBs, schematics, and reconstruction material are outside the collection target.

## Light information collection

Capture only enough information to describe what the pedal is and its intended character or use.

Source order:

1. Manufacturer description.
2. Reliable contemporary or archival source describing the product.
3. Neutral manufacturer-style description written from established evidence when no useful manufacturer description is available.

Do not fabricate specifications, dates, circuit details, historical events, or variants merely to make a record look complete.

## Later deep-dive phase

Once the inventory is broad, work back through builders A–Z. Then investigate variants, generations, production changes, OEM relationships, serial/date clues, historical disagreements, and specialist/forum evidence.

Deep research is important, but it follows the inventory. It must not replace the inventory during the current phase.

## Stop test

Before doing any task, ask:

**Will this add or improve a builder, a dirt product, a pedal photo, or light pedal information?**

If the answer is no, defer it.
