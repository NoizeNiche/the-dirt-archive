# YARP Breadcrumb 113

## Purpose
Builder census expansion pass focused on overlooked OEM manufacturers and later independent builders, with explicit deduplication against existing Guild, Electro-Harmonix, Ace Tone and Australian research.

## Cross-reference / dedupe decisions

- **AUL Instruments** is promoted as a distinct historical physical manufacturer/OEM node. It must not be merged into Guild, Mike Matthews, or Electro-Harmonix. Its most important current archive relationship is the transitional Guild Foxey Lady / early Matthews fuzz production corridor.
- **Chunk Systems** is added as a distinct Australian builder lineage. FZ002 and Brown Dog are kept as successive product states under one manufacturer rather than separate builders.
- **Ace Tone** remains separate from Roland/BOSS despite Ikutaro Kakehashi founding both lineages. Recent research strengthens FM-2 chronology and reinforces the FM-1/FM-2/FM-3 family model.
- **Lyle Distortion Sustainer** remains an Electro-Harmonix OEM/marketed identity, not a new manufacturer.
- **Gary Hurst / Electronic Sounds** remains a designer/brand lineage with the Italian factory unresolved. No duplicate Italian manufacturer is asserted from the product branding alone.

## Evidence notes

Kit Rae's OEM history identifies AUL Instruments as the contract manufacturer for the transitional Foxey Lady production state associated with Mike Matthews and Guild. Fuzzboxes provides an independent historical account of AUL-built fuzzes and the complicated chronology among Mosrite, AUL, Guild and early Electro-Harmonix production.

Chunk Systems' own documentation identifies the FZ002 as a handmade Australian bass fuzz and states that it was superseded by the Brown Dog. Contemporary 2005 discussion describes the Brown Dog as a reengineered FZ002, supporting a successor relationship.

Recent Ace Tone research places FM-2 availability as early as March 1968 and documents long catalog persistence, so the archive should avoid the narrow 1969-1971 window sometimes repeated in secondary sources.
