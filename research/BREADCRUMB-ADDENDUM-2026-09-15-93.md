- Banshee Fuzz remains Applied-built at medium confidence because the builder evidence is strong but the production chronology is still unresolved.
- Rogue Fuzz is retained as a high-confidence Applied-built product, with Nomad treated as a marketed state rather than a factory identity.
- Conrad Bass Hawk is retained as an Applied OEM state at research-only confidence based on the 1969-1970 Conrad catalog evidence reported by Tone Machines.
- Goya Boom Box, Fury Box, and Power Box remain research-only Applied-linked states. Boom Box has additional Effects Database model documentation, but enclosure/family similarity is not being used as sole factory proof.
- The Applied Big Bass record is deliberately not promoted because current secondary sources use inconsistent maker naming and the model-level factory chain is not yet closed to primary documentation.
