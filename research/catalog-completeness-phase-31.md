# The Dirt Archive — Catalog Completeness Phase 31

## Objective
Breadth-first enumeration of six builders/operations, with image discovery captured alongside product discovery.

## Builders
- AnaSounds
- Leila Vintage Electronics
- Jamés Pedals
- Loophole Pedals
- Orion Effekte
- G.S. Wyllie

## Discovery snapshots and sources
### AnaSounds
Effects Database snapshot: 20 products. Current official site also exposes a smaller active catalog and DIY kits, including current saturation products such as Blues Believer, Bitoun Fuzz, Ego Driver, Feed Me Mk3, Full Story, Savage and Tape Preamp. The historical database list is retained for breadth-first completeness rather than replacing it with the modern roster.
Source: https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/anasounds
Official current catalog: https://anasounds.com/products/
Historical product-family source: https://anasounds.com/products/origins-pedals/

### Leila Vintage Electronics
Effects Database snapshot: 5 products. Morgan Hargrave's timeline records amp repair beginning in 2002, first pedal build in 2004, Leila Vintage Electronics sales beginning in 2005, and SoftDelay/PulseTrem dual-channel pedals sold in 2008–2010.
Source: https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/leila

### Jamés Pedals
Effects Database snapshot: 7 products. James Bennett began making pedals in 2004, registered a company in 2005 for parts/repairs/modifications, the pedal side mostly took over by 2007, and a later hiatus ended when he moved to permanent premises in 2012.
Source: https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/james
Interview: https://www.effectsdatabase.com/interviews/brands/james

### Loophole Pedals
Effects Database snapshot: 1 cataloged retail pedal, Grey Matter. Current official site documents additional finished products including Signal Blender, Nine Lives Boost, Chromium 2 Knob Clean Boost and a Maestro MPF-1 Custom Shop reissue, plus Grey Matter editions/versions and utility hardware.
Sources: https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/loophole
Official store: https://loopholepedals.com/collections/all
Grey Matter: https://loopholepedals.com/products/grey-matter
Grey Matter v2: https://loopholepedals.com/products/grey-matter-v2-blackout-edition
Signal Blender: https://loopholepedals.com/products/signal-blender
Nine Lives Boost: https://loopholepedals.com/products/nine-lives-boost
Chromium: https://loopholepedals.com/products/chromium-2-knob-boost
Maestro MPF-1 Custom Shop: https://loopholepedals.com/products/maestro-parametric-filter-mpf-1-custom-shop

Important editorial note: the current Loophole collection contains many modification services, rehousing services and repair offerings. Those are not silently treated as original pedal models in this phase. They should be preserved later as service/modification history and manufacturer relationships.

### Orion Effekte
Effects Database snapshot: 30 products. Jan van Triest describes Orion as a one-person German manufacturer. Official current site lists active fuzz, drive, gain and time products including Wildkraut Fuzz, de Luxe Motor Fuzz, Ramlon Fuzz, Russlon Fuzz, Schwarzweiß, Western Drive 2, Distortion General, Silver Drive, Twangtone Dakota Red, Twangtone Dakota Drive, Elektro Booster, Minus Booster, Minus Booster Pro, Null Booster/Buffer and Kafka Reverb.
Sources: https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/orion
Official site: https://www.orion-fx.com/
History interview: https://reverb.com/news/pedals-und-ihre-geschichte-orion-effekte-082418

### G.S. Wyllie
Effects Database snapshot: 10 products. Glenn S. Wyllie's interview describes a largely one-person, made-to-order operation, self-cast or foundry-sourced enclosures, and a catalog mixing original designs with modified historical derivatives. Rising Sun was a modified Shin-ei Superfuzz design with expanded controls; Wylo was a modified Tone Bender; later R.U.X. was an octave fuzz/expander in a rectangular enclosure.
Sources: https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/gswyllie
Interview: https://www.effectsdatabase.com/interviews/brands/gswyllie
