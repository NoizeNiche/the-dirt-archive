## Discovery notes
### Klinger Custom Pedals
Effects Database records vintage-oriented fuzz, distortion and octave-fuzz products from the Melbourne, Australia operation. Documented examples include MkI Fuzz, Bender MkII, Chroma Zone Fuzz, Buzz Fuzz, Jimi Fuzz, Psychedelic Fuzz Machine and Brown Sound. Klinger pages expose manufacturer and collector video/image references that are useful visual-identification leads. Klinger MkI documentation also explicitly identifies its Tone Bender MkI relationship; Psychedelic Fuzz Machine is described as a Foxx Tone Machine clone; Chroma Zone has documented germanium and silicon versions. These are discovery observations for later family/variation research, not canonical generation claims.

### KMA Audio Machines
Effects Database currently lists 24 products, spanning fuzz, distortion, overdrive, boost, octaver, modulation, filters and utility products. The catalog includes distinct related products that deserve later lineage work, including Wurm, Wurhm limited edition and Guardian of the Wurm; Queequeg and Queequeg 2; Strokkur and Sweet Sinner; and Fuzzly Bear, Minos and Chief Disrupter. KMA also has manufacturer video/image records on product pages.
Source: https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/kma

### Kittycaster FX
Current/historical Effects Database entries include Double Date, Mohair Fuzztortion, Meerkat Wide Range Boost and Kittyface BC183L Silicon. Manufacturer copy identifies the FXCORE series as modern reimaginings of classic pedal archetypes, and specifically identifies Kittyface as the concept/design of former kcFX alum Howard Gee. Later research should keep brand lineage and individual design attribution distinct.
Sources:
- https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/kittycaster/mohair
- https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/kittycaster/meerkat
- https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/kittycaster/kittyface
