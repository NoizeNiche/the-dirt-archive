## Editorial rule for these images

Use image sources as identification and historical reference material first. Do not automatically convert third-party or reference-only photographs into cleared Archive media. Record the exact source page, creator/photographer when exposed, license/rights state, and the production/variant identity being illustrated.

For variant galleries, favor images where the physical differences are actually visible: enclosure shape, finish, control layout, graphics, branding, jack placement, footswitch style, label treatment, and documented production-period markings. Avoid presenting an unrelated clone or reissue as though it were the historical variant.

## Important finding

Effects Database is particularly valuable here because individual pages frequently retain multiple user/specimen photographs and dated image/video references, while also linking related variants and derivative models. Its variant graph can be used as a discovery index, with individual photographs still checked at the source-page level before public use.
