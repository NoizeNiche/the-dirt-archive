# Barber Electronics: 1999-2000 Catalog Audit 01

Research status: DISCOVERY_TO_BASELINE
Scope: late-1990s founding/catalog anchors and early dirt products. No circuit reconstruction.

## Identity
Barber Electronics was established by David Barber in the late 1990s. Current Barber history describes its products as primarily unique designs rather than direct copies. Secondary boutique-maker indexes date the company to 1999.

## Early dirt anchors
- Burn Unit: surviving market/specimen evidence places the original version in the 1999-2000 corridor. It is a core early Barber overdrive family.
- Direct Drive: later Barber flagship overdrive family. Current Barber documentation distinguishes later Direct Drive generations and the 2020 v4 update; an early V2 example is separately documented as a 2000 product.

## Catalog expansion candidates
Effects Database currently indexes a 32-product Barber universe, including Burn Unit, Burn Unit 2, Burn Unit EQ, Tone Pump, Tone Pump II, Direct Drive, Direct Drive LG, Direct Drive SS, Dirty Bomb, L.T.D., Trifecta, Exacta, Gain-Changer, and other families. This is a discovery universe, not proof that every listed product existed in the first 1999-2000 production window.

## Chronology cautions
The 1999 date is strongest for the original Burn Unit through surviving period listings/specimens. The Direct Drive is demonstrably a later documented Barber family, with a 2000 V2 specimen/listing available. Do not backdate every later Barber model into the founding year.

## Evidence disposition
- Company identity/catalog: current Barber Electronics site and historical maker indexes.
- Early product dating: period/collector listings for original Burn Unit and Direct Drive V2.
- Broader model universe: Effects Database.

## Archive rule
Keep Barber Electronics as a canonical builder entity. Separate original-era models, revisions, and modern compact/reissue states. Do not merge Burn Unit and Direct Drive despite their later shared Barber ecosystem.
