# Hermida Audio Technology — 2003–2005 Dirt Catalog Audit

## Status
**CANONICAL LINEAGE / EARLY BOUTIQUE VERIFIED** for the initial Hermida Audio dirt products.

## Core chronology
- **Mosferatu** — first Hermida pedal, 2003. Alfonso Hermida told Premier Guitar that this was his first pedal and that he built it with more gain/sustain than the tone he was ultimately chasing.
- **Zendrive** — developed before/around the Mosferatu effort and commercially released in 2004. The first Zendrive sent to Robben Ford had three controls; a later four-control version added Voice.
- **Zendrive 2** — subsequent tube overdrive/distortion family branch. Keep separate from the original Zendrive rather than treating it as a minor revision.

## Important historical relationship
Hermida described the Zendrive as a response to the sonic target that first produced the higher-gain Mosferatu. Robben Ford received early Zendrive examples, but the archive should describe this as a musician/designer interaction and inspiration story, not as a formal endorsement or co-design claim.

## Product taxonomy
Classify Mosferatu as overdrive/distortion. Classify Zendrive as overdrive. Zendrive 2 remains a separate family/generation record. Do not fold later Lovepedal manufacturing into the original Hermida production entity without an explicit manufacturing transition record.

## Archive caution
The 2003 date is strongest for the Mosferatu. The Zendrive commercial release is 2004, while development and early examples preceded that release. Exact production counts, serial chronology, and all enclosure variants remain specimen-layer research.
