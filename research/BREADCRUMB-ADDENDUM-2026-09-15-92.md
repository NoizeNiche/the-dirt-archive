### Findings

- Tone Machines gives the Nomad Banshee Fuzz a working date of likely 1968, but its later September 2025 research explicitly says the manufacturing dates remain unknown. The ledger therefore records 1968 as an estimate rather than a verified production date.
- The Nomad Banshee is consistently associated with the Applied product family. Effects Database catalogs it as Nomad by Applied and separately lists an Applied Banshee.
- The Banshee and Rogue share a distinct enclosure family and are documented as Applied-associated products. This supports a related Applied product family but does not establish identical dates for every specimen.
- The early Nomad Super Fuzz remains a separate unresolved production state. It must not inherit the Applied attribution from the later Banshee/Rogue products.
- No new public lineage edge is justified by this batch.
