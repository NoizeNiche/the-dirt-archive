## Evidence notes
BECOS FX was founded in Romania and developed the TS8-MS there. Guitar.com documented the company and its handmade pedal operation in Constanta in 2019. The current BECOS site is based in Vienna and still carries the TS8-MS as a retired product while listing the later Ziffer Overdrive. The Ziffer is explicitly a Jordan Ziff signature collaboration derived from the TS8-MS platform. Preserve this as a builder-lineage and relocation relationship, not duplicate builders.

Stomping Stones' current site lists Haxan, The Virus, Sygil, 2X2L and discontinued Iron Giant/AT Action Perk states. A long-running Reverb shop record places the maker in Abilene, Texas, and contemporary community documentation identifies the Virus as a small-builder fuzz product. One-off products such as the Valnott Distortus Maximus clone were not promoted into the central retail catalog because the evidence identifies them as individual clone work rather than a named Stomping Stones production family.
