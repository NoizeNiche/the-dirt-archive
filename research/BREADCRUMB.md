Fuzz:
- PDF-2
- Kliptonite — historical / discontinued
- Fig Fumb — current/legacy lineage
- Rise & Shine — current/legacy lineage

Key restraint: Stone Deaf explicitly describes PDF-2 as offering overdrive, distortion, and fuzz in one pedal. PDF-1X Ghost is explicitly Overdrive / Distortion. Historical specialist coverage documents Kliptonite as Overdrive + Fuzz and Trashy Blonde as Overdrive + Distortion. Warp Drive is a dedicated high-gain Distortion; Fig Fumb and Rise & Shine are explicitly Fuzz products. PDF-1 is retained historically under Distortion rather than being inflated from its broad operating range. Boost, filter, octave, tremolo, and expression-only products remain outside the target census.
