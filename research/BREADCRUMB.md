## Block 104 — Old Blood Noise Endeavors
Old Blood Noise Endeavors (OBNE).

Overdrive:
- Fault V2
- Beam Splitter V2

Distortion:
- Fault V2
- Beam Splitter V2
- Excess V2

Fuzz:
- Haunt Fuzz
- Alpha Haunt
- Pardner

Key restraint: OBNE explicitly presents Fault V2 as Overdrive + Distortion. Beam Splitter V2 uses three overdrive voices while OBNE also presents it as trereo distortion, so it is retained in both categories as one named pedal. Excess V2 is a distortion paired with modulation/delay. Haunt, Alpha Haunt, and Pardner are explicitly fuzz products. Screen Violence, Dark Star, Black Bobbin Boost, and other non-target or boost-first products remain excluded.
