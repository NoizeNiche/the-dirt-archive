- Olympia — historical / discontinued

Key restraint: Caroline itself describes CROM as a “fuzzstortion,” so it is retained in both Fuzz and Distortion. Caroline's historical Wave Cannon/Cannonball material explicitly describes overdrive and distortion tones plus a fuzzy Havoc mode, so that identity is retained across all three target categories. Hawaiian Pizza and Shigeharu remain Fuzz identities despite drive-like ranges; Icarus remains Overdrive because its official page explicitly identifies rich overdrive. Limited colorways and special variants are consolidated.
