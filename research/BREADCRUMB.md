## Block 086 details
MOOER Audio is treated as an unrepresented builder/brand identity in the active census. MOOER's current product pages explicitly categorize Blues Crab, Blues Mood, Green Mile, Black Secret, Hustle Drive, Cruncher, Blade, Solo, Rage Machine, and Ultra Drive MKII within the target dirt taxonomy; historical Micro Series catalog coverage adds Rumble Drive, Ultra Drive, Greenwood, Thunderball, Eight Hands, Triangle Buff, MooerFog/Fog, Blue Faze, and Grey Faze. Thunderball is recorded as Fuzz + Distortion because authoritative MOOER catalog coverage identifies it as Bass Fuzz/Distortion. Ultra Drive and Ultra Drive MKII remain separate named identities because the historical catalog distinguishes the original Ultra Drive Overdrive from the later MKII Distortion. Hustle Drive remains Distortion only because MOOER explicitly labels it as a Distortion Pedal despite its lower-gain drive behavior. Micro Preamp digital amp-model products, Red Truck/Black Truck multi-effects, Pure Boost/Flex Boost, and non-dirt products remain excluded.

## Block 087 details
NUX Audio is treated as an unrepresented builder/brand identity in the active census. NUX's current product catalog documents Queen of Tone, Horseman, Morning Star, Tube Man MKII, Drive Core Deluxe MKII, Fireman, and the Reissue Series dirt pedals; authoritative historical catalog coverage documents earlier NUX die-cast, Core, Mini, Mini Core, and Verdugo dirt products including OD-2, OD-3, BC-2, Drive Core, Scream Bass, Mini Supa Drive, DS-3, HG-5, HG-6, MX-6, Mini Distortion, and FZ-2 Vintage Fuzz. NUX's current catalog also places Metal Core Deluxe MKII in the preamp category, so it is excluded from the target Distortion set. Scream Bass is retained as Overdrive despite its published overdrive-to-fuzz range, and Horseman is retained as Overdrive despite its natural-distortion operation because the builder's product category is Overdrive. Drive Core Deluxe/MKII and Tube Man/MKII are handled as product families rather than unnecessary variant duplicates. NUX MG/Mighty/Cerberus/modeling products and internal modeled effects remain outside the standalone pedal census.
