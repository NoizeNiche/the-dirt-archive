# Fulltone Dirt Catalog Audit 02

## Scope
Fulltone's overdrive, distortion, and fuzz product universe, with emphasis on separating original production-era identities from later variants, Custom Shop runs, and the current Fulltone USA continuation.

## Builder identity and chronology

Fulltone was founded by Michael Fuller in California in 1991. The archive treats Fulltone as a canonical builder entity while keeping later ownership, licensing, revival, or continuation entities separate when the documentary record requires it.

## Current manufacturer/catalog baseline

The current Fulltone USA catalog lists a dirt-related group including OCDv2, OCD-Germanium, Limited Edition OCD, Custom Shop OCD v1.4, Full-Drive1, Full-Drive2 v2, Full-Drive3, PlimSoul mkII, 69 Pedal mkII, 70 Pedal-BC, Queen Bee, Soul-Bender v2, Octafuzz 2, Ultimate Octave, Mas Malo, and Hi-Mu. The current manuals page independently confirms product identities for 69-MKII, 70 BC-108C, FD1, FD2v2, FD3, Hi-Mu, Mas Malo, CS-OCD 1.4, CS-OCD Ge, OCDv2, Octafuzz, PlimSoul, Queen Bee, Soul-Bender and related current products.

## OCD chronology correction

Fulltone's current OCD product documentation explicitly says the OCD was born in 2004 and has undergone many slight tweaks. Fulltone states that the commonly used labels "version 2," "version 3," and "version 4" are misleading for much of the early sequence, which the company describes as V1.0 through V1.8. The archive therefore records OCD generation and serial-range claims as evidence-qualified production states rather than creating a separate builder-model identity for every informal version label.

A specialist historical source gives a commonly circulated serial chronology for V1.1, V1.2, V1.3 and V1.4, but this is retained as secondary evidence only. The public archive should privilege Fulltone's own explanation when reconciling version nomenclature.

Fulltone also states that OCDs with serial numbers 205811 and higher are the less-compressed state often called "v2.01." This is useful specimen-level evidence, but should remain a production-state note, not a claim that every later nickname represents a new retail product.

## Full-Drive family treatment

The Full-Drive family is one of Fulltone's principal overdrive lineages and should be represented as a family with separate retail states. Current Fulltone USA lists Full-Drive1, Full-Drive2 v2 and Full-Drive3. Historical research must next reconcile the original Full-Drive, Full-Drive II, Full-Drive II MOSFET, Full-Drive 2 variants and Full-Drive 3 chronology using dated Fulltone manuals, catalogs, dealer sheets and advertisements.

## Fuzz family treatment

Fulltone's fuzz universe should not be collapsed into a single "Tone Bender clone" bucket. The archive keeps 69, 70, Soul-Bender, Octafuzz and Ultimate Octave as distinct retail families/products, then links their historical inspirations or relationships separately. Current Fulltone USA documentation confirms 69 Pedal mkII, 70 Pedal-BC, Soul-Bender v2, Octafuzz 2 and Ultimate Octave as separate listed products.

## Evidence boundaries

No schematics, PCB layouts, complete BOMs, gutshot libraries, cloning instructions, or circuit-reconstruction material are included. Component-level claims from secondary sources are not reproduced unless needed only to distinguish a documented production state at the product-history level.

## Research gaps

2. Reconcile every Full-Drive suffix/variant and production corridor.

4. Reconcile 69, 70, Soul-Bender and Octafuzz generations, limited runs, and current reissues.
5. Identify whether Mas Malo, Queen Bee, PlimSoul and related later products have discontinued predecessors or region-specific editions.
6. Separate Fulltone Musical Products-era production from the current Fulltone USA continuation where documentation supports a distinction.
