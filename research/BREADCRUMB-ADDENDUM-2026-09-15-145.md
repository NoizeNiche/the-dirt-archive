# YARP Breadcrumb 145

## Focus
Continue the hard dirt-maker census into regional manufacturers and small domestic pedal builders, with particular attention to New Zealand.

## Promoted
- Jansen: verified New Zealand dirt maker. Effects Database identifies Jansen as a New Zealand music-equipment manufacturer and explicitly documents the Fuzzman as produced in New Zealand, designed by Bruce Eady and built in limited numbers from the mid-1960s into the early 1970s.

## Cross-pass findings
- Jansen joins the already-verified New Zealand regional layer alongside Bernie Bisphan / Action Fuzz Box and the documented Rawnsley custom lineage.
- Bruce Eady is preserved as designer rather than incorrectly promoted as the manufacturing entity.
- The Jansen Fuzzman chronology remains deliberately broad. Production totals, complete enclosure chronology and exact factory workflow are not asserted without stronger evidence.

## Held / exclusions
- The usual Japanese wedge-fuzz names remain relationship/OEM research nodes until the physical maker can be established product-by-product.
- Australian and New Zealand one-off builders remain custom/micro-builder records when the evidence does not support a commercial catalog.

## Editorial consequence
The builder census now captures another national manufacturing branch that is routinely omitted from broad pedal histories. The archive should continue identifying the physical builder first, then map designers, labels, retailers and OEM customers around that node.

## Next queue
Continue the regional sweep, with Canada, New Zealand and continental Europe next, while reopening the late-1960s U.S. mystery-label families only where new manufacturing evidence appears.
