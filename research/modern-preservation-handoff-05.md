# Modern Preservation Track Handoff 05

## Current position
Continue newest-to-oldest modern preservation work. The active window is 2026 into 2024, moving next through 2023.

## New work in this tranche
- Batch 05: Walrus Audio 385 Overdrive MKII, documented as a 2024-present revision.
- The public catalog now has a variant-evidence bridge for this record, including a structured MKII generation, an identification distinguisher, a source record and a claim record.
- Added `research/variant-evidence-model.md` to formalize the rule that research findings must eventually flow into the actual pedal record.

## Required backfill direction
Every previously added pedal is now considered incomplete until its documented generations/variants and meaningful identifying differences have been evaluated. Backfill should prioritize existing modern records first, then proceed backward through the historical catalog.

## Variant recording priorities
For every pedal, preserve supported differences across appearance, controls, connectivity, function, production identity, electronics identity, edition status and collector-facing identification clues. Keep evidence and uncertainty attached to the individual claim rather than collapsing all confidence into one score.

## Current next target
Continue through 2024, then 2023, while also building the retroactive backfill queue for existing public pedal records. Prefer official manufacturer pages/manuals and dated primary evidence where available.
