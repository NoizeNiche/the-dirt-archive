## Important normalization decisions
1. Red Brick and Yellow Brick are separate retail pedal models despite sharing a discrete circuit platform, because the official pages give them distinct names, voicing and product identities.
2. Black Brick Bass Overdrive and Silver Brick Bass Overdrive are separate bass overdrive models. Their common lineage to Red Brick is preserved as a relationship, not collapsed into one entry.
3. Newport Muff '71Δ, '73Λ and '76η are retained as separate named product records. The first-party catalog treats them as distinct historical Big Muff target variants.
4. Midnight Ace Fuzz is central because the builder explicitly presents it as an ACE TONE FM-3 recreation and sells it as its own pedal model.
5. BOOSTAR stays relationship-only because its documented purpose is clean volume boost without changing tone/distortion amount.
6. No internal images, schematics, PCB diagrams, BOMs or cloning instructions are being archived.
