## Builder covered

### KITAZAWA EFFECTOR

- Identity retained as **CANONICAL_CANDIDATE** rather than promoted to an unqualified physical-manufacturer entity.
- First-party story says the project grew from a Shimokitazawa rehearsal/recording-studio operation and was developed with cooperation from nine established local live houses.
- Launch documentation dated 2021-01-18 states that nine models were released and that Nasu of Blackberry JAM assisted with board design and repeated prototyping.
- Mixwave currently describes Kitazawa Effector as a Made-in-Japan effects maker and sales partner, but the exact division of design, manufacture, and project ownership remains worth preserving separately.

## Information layer

Only lightweight product-level descriptions were promoted in this batch: category, model identity, launch/project context, and basic controls where clearly documented. No schematics, PCB diagrams, gutshots, BOMs, or cloning/reconstruction instructions were added.
