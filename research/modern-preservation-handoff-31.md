## Important research notes
- AC Noises URLA is a highly identifiable experimental hybrid: CMOS fuzz feeding a dual resonant filter, with a jFET dry blend and MS-20-derived filter concept. Preserve it as a distinct AC Noises product rather than reducing it to either fuzz or filter lineage.
- Bardic 2 Stroke Beaver and Shelob's Lair are the same product identity under different artwork/name states. Preserve the small-batch artwork variation as a specimen or edition layer instead of duplicating the catalog object.
- Black Mass Kill Chain demonstrates a deliberately simple fuzz architecture with fixed filtering and a three-position low-frequency voice. Its October 2022 release evidence gives a clean launch anchor.
- KMA Chief Disruptor is a feature-dense Muff-style fuzz/distortion with three gain structures, pre/post clean blend, parametric mid section, top boost and expression. KMA's December 2022 quick-start guide is a valuable dated primary artifact.
- EarthQuaker Special Cranker is the successor-inspired expansion of Speaker Cranker, not simply a renamed version. The new Level/More/Tone control set and Germanium/Silicon selector establish a distinct product identity.
- Fjord Kvasir D882 grew out of transistor experiments during Berserk development and acquired its own collaboration/product identity around D882 power transistors, Bias and Thickness controls. Shared development history should not merge it with Berserk.
- SolidGoldFX 76 MKII is a genuine production update to the earlier 76 concept, with a JFET input, Toshiba transistor set, dual tone controls and octave bypass. Preserve the MKII boundary separately.
- ThorpyFX Boneyard is a compact germanium multi-Bender designed to cover major Tone Bender families without treating each voicing as a separate pedal. Its transistor-count Mode and Feed/Attack interaction are strong external identifiers.
- Wampler Triumph and Phenom belong to the 2022 Collective Series. Triumph draws from Bad Monkey and SD-1 influences with a five-control EQ-heavy interface, while Phenom is a ground-up JFET high-gain design. Preserve both separately from older Wampler Plextortion-family products.

## Data rule reinforced
Modern preservation remains explicitly chronological while resisting false precision. A dated source can establish existence or launch without proving every production boundary. Product identity, design lineage, generation, edition, enclosure state and specimen clues remain separate dimensions.
