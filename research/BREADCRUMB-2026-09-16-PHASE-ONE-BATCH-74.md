## Evidence notes
Sinvertek has a substantial historical dirt/preamp record. Effects Database documents No. 5 Distortion, Drive N5, Drive N5+, Dark Fire and Tiger Attack, while Guitar Pedal X describes the N5+ as handmade in Hong Kong and identifies Wu Nan as the operator associated with both Sinvertek and CKK Electronic. This makes the brand relationship historically meaningful. Keep Sinvertek as a Hong Kong public-facing brand and preserve the later CKK identity separately.

CKK Electronic is documented as the later Chinese-facing Wu Nan brand, with Beijing provenance and an analog-focused stompbox catalog. The available catalog evidence supports Q Cat Overdrive, Scream Drive, Gambler and Lunar OD Special as named gain products. Do not merge those product identities into Sinvertek just because the builder lineage is connected.

MOEN has a separate Chinese company history. Its own manufacturer history says the MOEN brand was established in 2005 and that early handmade effects carried the CFX label before CFX joined MOEN's production line. Effects Database preserves early dirt products including MO-OD Over Drive and MO-DB Deep Blue from the 2006 vintage series. The archive should keep the CFX ancestry as a brand/history relationship and avoid using later factory production as evidence of handmade construction.

Ravenstone is a current Taiwan boutique record with New Taipei storefront provenance. Current marketplace records document APOLLO V2 and 1959 Distortion as named products made in Taiwan. The 1959 product provides especially useful object-level evidence through its JTM45/Bluesbreaker, Plexi and #34 mode structure. Keep the builder canonical but mark chronology/provenance as partial until a stronger first-party company-history source is captured.

## Boundary / scope notes
- Sinvertek and CKK Electronic are connected public-facing identities under one builder lineage; do not double-count Wu Nan as two unrelated individual builders.
- CKK's later Chinese production context should not be projected backward onto every Sinvertek unit; retain regional and production-state distinctions.
- MOEN's CFX ancestry is historical brand/process evidence, not proof that every MOEN pedal was handmade.
- Ravenstone's current Reverb storefront is useful product evidence but remains secondary provenance for chronology until a stronger direct builder-history source is found.
- South Korea discoveries that only expose generic rebrands, retailer listings or insufficiently attributable products remain in the research queue rather than entering the canonical census.
- No schematics, PCB layouts, gutshots, cloning instructions, or complete BOM data are published.
