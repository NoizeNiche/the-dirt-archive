## Important normalization decisions
1. SABBATH generations and named variants are kept distinct from color-only records until evidence shows a substantive product identity.
2. SABBATH MK2 is a separate generation, documented as adding a Tone control and a Sabbath/Sabbath Fog switching relationship compared with the original one-knob Sabbath.
3. SABBATH Bliss is retained as a separate named fuzz model because surviving documentation describes it as a later Sabbath-family product with a different design target.
4. MTR is central because it is sold and documented as a distinct Phantom fx pedal derived from the MOTHER concept with alternative tuning and a smaller enclosure.
5. BELLs is not collapsed into the Phantom fx core catalog because LEP International explicitly identifies it as a Phantom fx × Shun Nokina collaboration and credits Toddy/Phantom FX with product design/build and Shun Nokina with circuit design.
6. HACK and DAY&AGE remain outside central dirt because their primary function is boosting.
7. No gutshots, schematics, PCB diagrams, complete BOMs, or cloning instructions are included in the public archive layer.

## Photo layer

Surviving current/recent secondary records describe Phantom fx as a Japanese boutique brand associated with guitarist/builder Masafumi Todaka. XTONES describes the revived catalog around MOTHER, Sabbath and MTR, while dealer archives preserve earlier Sabbath variants and the Phantom fx/Shun Nokina BELLs collaboration.
