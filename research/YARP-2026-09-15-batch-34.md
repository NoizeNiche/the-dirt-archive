# YARP 2026-09-15 Batch 34

## Focus
Czechoslovakian historical dirt, centered on the Tesla AYZ-025 family and its status as a regional manufacturer record.

## Findings

### Tesla AYZ-025 Wah-Wah Booster / fuzz-wah boundary
Surviving specialist documentation identifies the Tesla AYZ-025 as a 1970s Czechoslovakian pedal with separate booster and wah controls, originally using Soviet-standard DIN connections. Marketplace documentation labels the unit "Wah-Wah Booster" while collector descriptions commonly classify it as a fuzz-wah or booster/fuzz hybrid. The archive therefore records the product as a historical dirt/hybrid boundary item, not as a pure fuzz without qualification.

### Attribution status
Tesla is retained as the marketed/manufacturing entity because the surviving pedal is explicitly branded Tesla and identified as Czechoslovakian-made. The present evidence does not establish a more granular internal factory, plant, or OEM supplier, so no deeper manufacturing attribution is invented.

### Dating
Accessible surviving listings consistently place AYZ-025 in the 1970s, with one specimen dated 1978. The exact first-sale year is not established and remains a broad period corridor.

### Catalog significance
AYZ-025 demonstrates that the Eastern-European dirt census cannot be reduced to Böhm/Klingenthal and Soviet Kazan records. Tesla adds a separate Czechoslovakian production node and should remain searchable under both booster and fuzz-wah terminology.

## Evidence boundary
Marketplace listings are retained as specimen/date corroboration rather than treated as complete corporate histories. No schematics, PCB layouts, BOMs, gutshots, cloning instructions or circuit-reconstruction material added.
