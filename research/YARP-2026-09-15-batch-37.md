# YARP 2026-09-15 Batch 37

## Focus
BJFE / Björn Juhl as a Scandinavian designer-builder lineage, separating small Swedish hand-built production from later authorized production and retail relationships.

## Findings

### BJFE / BJF Electronics
Surviving historical material identifies Björn Juhl as the designer behind BJFE effects, with the Honey Bee Overdrive released on 4 December 2002. Other historical BJFE material documents named overdrive, distortion and fuzz products and treats the artwork/serial lineage as part of the BJFE identity.

### Honey Bee manufacturing evolution
Aion FX's historical research states that original Honey Bee pedals were handmade in Sweden and that an agreement was made in 2011 for BearFoot FX to produce them in the USA. This is retained as a production-state transition, not as a disappearance of Björn Juhl's design identity.

### Designer vs brand vs producer
The archive treats Björn Juhl as designer, BJFE/BJF Electronics as the original small-production brand, and BearFoot FX as a later authorized producer/brand relationship. This prevents the common mistake of counting BearFoot as an unrelated circuit lineage.

### One Control relationship
Research on the Honey Bee history documents a later collaboration with One Control in Japan, where Juhl served as an official analogue engineer and One Control produced the BJFe Series Honey Bee OD. The archive retains One Control as a later authorized production/marketing relationship rather than merging it with the original BJFE manufacturing state.

## Catalog signal
High-priority dirt families for future BJFE completion include Honey Bee Overdrive, Blueberry Bass Overdrive, Model H, Model G, Honey Beest, Uber Bee, Sparkling Yellow Overdrive and other documented overdrive/distortion/fuzz designs. Individual variants should be separated only where surviving evidence supports a real model or production-state distinction.

## Evidence boundary
Secondary sources that reproduce circuit information are not used for circuit reconstruction. The archive records identity, chronology and relationships only. No schematics, PCB layouts, BOMs, gutshots, cloning instructions or circuit-reconstruction material added.
