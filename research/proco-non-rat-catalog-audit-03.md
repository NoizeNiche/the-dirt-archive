# ProCo Non-RAT Catalog Audit 03

## Scope
ProCo Sound dirt products outside the core RAT retail family. Goal: determine whether ProCo had additional overdrive, distortion, or fuzz products that should be counted independently, while preserving RAT-family relationships and excluding circuit reconstruction material.

## Findings

### Solo
- **Product:** ProCo Solo
- **Category:** Distortion / overdrive hybrid
- **Period:** modern ProCo era, documented by surviving current and historical references
- **Status:** VERIFIED_ANCHOR
- **Treatment:** Count as its own ProCo retail model, not as another RAT-branded model. Product identity is RAT-family-adjacent but the marketed name is distinct.
- **Evidence trail:** ProCo product references; historical RAT-family references; current dealer documentation.

### Juggernaut / Bass RAT relationship
- **Product:** ProCo Juggernaut
- **Category:** Bass distortion / extended-range dirt
- **Period:** original late-1970s/early-1980s corridor remains date-qualified; modern reissue is separate
- **Status:** VERIFIED_ANCHOR with chronology caveat
- **Treatment:** Keep Juggernaut as a distinct retail family member. Some historical sources also describe it as a Bass RAT, but this should be represented as a relationship/alias note rather than collapsing it into standard RAT records.
- **Evidence trail:** JHS RAT history; Guitar Center ProCo RAT guide; Griffin Effects; other specialist historical references.

### RATZO / development prototype
- **Product:** RATZO
- **Category:** Distortion
- **Period:** 2006 prototype corridor
- **Status:** CANDIDATE / DEVELOPMENT_RECORD
- **Treatment:** Preserve as a prototype/development node associated with the later Solo, not as a normal production-model count unless independent production evidence emerges.
- **Evidence trail:** historical RAT-family documentation.

### Preamp / non-central experimental records
- **Product:** ProCo preamp prototype / experimental development references
- **Category:** BOUNDARY_RELATIONSHIP
- **Period:** early-2000s corridor
- **Status:** CANDIDATE
- **Treatment:** Keep outside the central dirt catalog unless stronger evidence demonstrates a retail overdrive/distortion/fuzz product. Do not inflate the ProCo model count from prototype references alone.

## Negative-evidence conclusion

The surviving public historical record continues to point to the **RAT family as ProCo's dominant dirt catalog**, with **Solo** as the clearest independently named non-RAT distortion model and **Juggernaut** as a distinct RAT-family retail branch with bass-oriented identity in historical sources. No strong evidence was found for a large hidden ProCo catalog of unrelated fuzz/overdrive models comparable in scale to BOSS, DOD, EHX, or MXR.

This is negative evidence, not a claim of absolute completeness. Dealer catalogs, regional catalogs, and pre-Internet trade literature may still surface additional short-lived ProCo products.

## Archive treatment rules

- Preserve ProCo as the canonical builder/manufacturer entity.
- Keep RAT, Solo, Juggernaut, BRAT, Roadkill, Deucetone, Turbo RAT, You Dirty RAT, FATRAT, RAT 2, Lil' RAT, and other marketed identities separately searchable.
- Do not duplicate-count BRAT and Roadkill as unrelated physical lineages when evidence shows a shared economy-market relationship.
- Do not promote prototypes to ordinary retail products without production evidence.
- Do not publish schematics, PCB layouts, BOMs, gutshots, cloning instructions, or circuit reconstruction material.
