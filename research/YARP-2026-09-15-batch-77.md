# YARP 2026-09-15 Batch 77: Sperrin Fuzz Box

David Sperrin of Southport represents a genuinely independent British micro-builder rather than an OEM label. The Sperrin Fuzz Box was advertised repeatedly in the music press during 1966. Family testimony and surviving historical research indicate that David assembled the pedals at the family home on Matlock Road, with Beryl Sperrin handling customer postings. The surviving documentary trail describes a foot-operated fuzz with two controls.

The Archive records Sperrin as a canonical micro-builder candidate with direct home-workshop production evidence. No surviving original example is currently known, so the production chronology remains advertisement-led.
