# Tycobrahe Historical Catalog Audit 01

## Scope
Builder/entity census and public-product chronology for Tycobrahe Sound Company / Tycobrahe Engineering Company. This audit intentionally excludes schematics, component inventories, PCB information, and circuit-reconstruction material.

## Entity / production anchors

- Tycobrahe Sound Company is documented as a Los Angeles-area sound company before and during its effects period.
- A first-person account by former line technician Mark Barton states that in **1975** Tycobrahe entered the guitar-pedal business and manufactured three pedal products: **Octavia, Parapedal, and Pedalflanger**.
- A 1976 Tycobrahe advertisement names the corporate entity **Tycobrahe Engineering Company, Inc.**, gives a Hermosa Beach, California address, and explicitly states that the Octavia, Parapedal and Pedalflanger were designed and manufactured by Tycobrahe.

## Dirt / dirt-adjacent product universe

### Octavia
- Retail identity: **Tycobrahe Octavia**.
- Category: octave/fuzz / octave-up distortion effect.
- Period anchor: production in the **mid-1970s**, with a 1975 museum record and a 1976 period advertisement providing strong contemporaneous evidence.
- Later collector literature commonly dates original production to approximately 1976-1977; treat this as a secondary range rather than a hard factory start/stop until stronger primary documentation is found.
- Historical relationship: strongly associated with the Roger Mayer Octavia lineage, but the Archive should record **relationship/derivation claims separately from manufacturer identity**. Roger Mayer's own historical page identifies Tycobrahe as a later Octavia-derived clone.
- Identification caution: surviving specimens and later reproductions should not be collapsed into one generation.

### Parapedal
- Retail identity: **Tycobrahe Parapedal**.
- Category: wah / resonant-filter effect, not a core OD/fuzz product but important to the Tycobrahe dirt-adjacent catalog.
- 1976 primary advertisement describes it as a parametric equalizer-based wah with a bass-wah behavior at the heel end of the travel.
- Secondary vintage literature places original examples around 1976-1977.

### Pedalflanger
- Retail identity: **Tycobrahe Pedalflanger**.
- Category: flanger / modulation, catalog boundary item rather than dirt.
- 1976 primary advertisement explicitly lists manual and automatic sweeping behavior.
- Former Tycobrahe technician Mark Barton states the three pedals manufactured in the 1975 entry period were Octavia, Parapedal and Pedalflanger.

## Evidence hierarchy

1. **1976 period Tycobrahe advertisement**: strongest surviving period catalog/marketing evidence found in this pass. It identifies all three products and states Tycobrahe design/manufacture.
2. **First-person Mark Barton account**: strong production-history evidence for the 1975 entry into guitar pedals and the three-product initial lineup.
3. **Roger Mayer's own historical Octavia page**: strong evidence for the broader Octavia lineage and Tycobrahe-derived status, but not a Tycobrahe catalog itself.
4. **Museum / collector sources**: useful for dating surviving examples, but retained as secondary evidence where they conflict with primary-period material.

## Important chronology cautions

- Do not assign a single exact launch year from collector databases alone. The evidence supports a Tycobrahe effects launch in **1975**, a contemporaneous **1976 advertisement**, and secondary dating around 1976-1977 for surviving original pedals.
- Do not treat the Tycobrahe Octavia as identical to Roger Mayer's original Octavia. The Archive should preserve a relationship node such as `derived_from_claim / inspired_by / clone_of_claim`, with provenance and confidence recorded separately.
- Do not treat Chicago Iron's later Tycobrahe-branded products as original 1970s production. They belong in a later revival/license/trademark relationship layer.

## Revival / trademark boundary

Secondary sources report that the Tycobrahe trademark was later acquired by Chicago Iron, which subsequently produced reproductions of the Octavia, Parapedal and Pedalflanger. These must remain a separate later production entity in the Archive rather than being merged with the original Tycobrahe builder record.

## Current catalog conclusion

For the original Tycobrahe effects business, the strongest defensible public catalog baseline is:

**Core product set:**
- Octavia
- Parapedal
- Pedalflanger

**Dirt relevance:**
- Octavia = direct fuzz/octave dirt entry.
- Parapedal and Pedalflanger = dirt-adjacent catalog/context entries.

No additional original Tycobrahe stompbox model should be promoted from rumor/catalog aggregators until primary or unusually strong secondary evidence is found.
