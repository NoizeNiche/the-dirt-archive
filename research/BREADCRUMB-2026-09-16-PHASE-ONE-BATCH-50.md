# Phase One Breadcrumb — Batch 50

Date: 2026-09-16

## Mission state
Continued Phase One builder-first census with mandatory duplicate/OEM gate. This batch focused on Hungary, adding two builders with substantial documented dirt/gain catalogs.

## Duplicate gate
Candidate names were cross-referenced against the active master census context and prior Phase One batch history before canonical addition. Pause&Effects and S.L.Amps were not represented as canonical builders in the reviewed active records. Existing builders and referenced circuit lineages were not duplicated: the fact that Pause&Effects products are based on historic Fuzz Face, Tone Bender, Colorsound, MXR, DOD and related circuits does not make those vintage manufacturers aliases of Pause&Effects. S.L.Amps was kept separate from amplifier brands referenced by its products.

- Pause&Effects — Hungary
- S.L.Amps — Hungary (Budapest)

- Pause&Effects: Aeras; Camping de Luxe; Camping Record; Dahlia; Farmako; Faros; Fengari; Feta; Filos; Fotia; Graetz Page; Helium; Ichos; Micro; Orionton (Terta version); Orionton (Videoton version); Psychi; Rio; Strand; Thalassa; Yefira.
- S.L.Amps: NewBlues Overdrive; PHIL.

## Evidence notes
Pause&Effects currently exposes 23 products in its live catalog. Product pages state that the pedals are hand built in Hungary. Its catalog is unusually useful for the archive because it spans original named product families while explicitly documenting historical circuit inspirations and alternative vintage-construction options. Those references are retained as relationship metadata only.

S.L.Amps states that it is a Budapest manufacture for building and repairing tube amps and effect pedals, launched in 2010. Its current pedal page lists NewBlues Overdrive and PHIL; VintageClean is treated as a preamp/gain-adjacent boundary rather than central dirt.

## Photo workflow
Every inventory row contains a product or catalog photo-source breadcrumb. These are source trails for later specimen/rights review and do not constitute permission to republish images.

## Scope discipline
Boost-only models remain gain-adjacent. Vintage radio products are retained where their internal selectable options include dirt or boost circuits. Non-dirt amplification products and preamp-only products remain outside the central dirt count. No schematics, PCB layouts, gutshots, cloning instructions, or complete BOM data are published.
