# YARP 2026-09-15 Batch 122: Katayama Electric Instruments / Mirano / Rozz

## Builder identity
- **Katayama Denshi Gakki Co., Ltd. / Katayama Electric Instruments Co. (片山電子楽器)** appears in recent Japanese historical research as the manufacturer behind the Mirano effects brand.
- The same research associates Katayama with the later **Rozz** effects line.
- A 1970s Japanese trade directory independently shows **Katayama Shokai** in Fukuoka as a distributor, but this should not automatically be equated with Katayama Denshi Gakki without corporate-name evidence.

## Boundary / non-dirt signal
- Rozz is also documented with a **Super Gas** model in a closely related enclosure, identified as a flanger rather than dirt. Retain it as a builder-catalog boundary record if a later catalog sweep confirms the line.

## Mirano chronology correction
- Older collector lore sometimes placed the EF-1 in the late 1960s or very early 1970s.
- Newer documentary work found the EF-1 still offered in a Japanese 1976 gear buyer's guide, and argues that this strongly favors a **mid-1970s** release window rather than 1968-70.
- The 1976 evidence is especially useful because it is period retail/catalog documentation rather than a modern seller's estimated year.

## Attribution boundary
- Treat **Katayama -> Mirano/Rozz** as a strong builder relationship, but keep the exact corporate history, factory location, staffing, and production quantities unresolved until primary Japanese company documents are located.
- Do not infer that every Katayama-branded or visually similar Japanese fuzz was made at the same facility.
- Do not conflate **Katayama Shokai** (documented as a distributor in Billboard directories) with **Katayama Denshi Gakki** without a direct identity bridge.
