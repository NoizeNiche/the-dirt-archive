## Evidence notes
Ground Pedals' official site identifies the operation as a boutique guitar-pedal builder in Slovenia and currently exposes Obraz Fuzz as a new take on classic silicon fuzz.

Overkill Effects' official site identifies a hand-wired Croatian workshop and its current catalog contains Flowdrive, La Crème, Objekt 808 and V7. Marketplace evidence documents the earlier Revolution Fuzz. The V7 page documents a Civil War-era Big Muff-inspired design; La Crème and Objekt 808 are documented through marketplace material as Jan Ray and TSV808-inspired builds respectively. Preserve these as product-level reference relationships.

FOBS Audio, based in Pécs, Hungary, was not promoted into the product inventory. The available Reverb shop profile establishes a handmade Hungarian guitar-pedal project and identifies the builder as Sándor Barcsuk, but the accessible product evidence did not expose a sufficiently clear model catalog for a defensible Phase One inventory record.

## Photo workflow

## Scope discipline
Only dirt/gain products with clear evidence were included in the inventory. Non-dirt products are not counted. No schematics, PCB layouts, gutshots, cloning instructions, or complete BOM data are published.
