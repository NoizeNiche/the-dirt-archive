# YARP 2026-09-15 Batch 41

## Focus
Lafayette Radio Electronics / LRE catalog and Japanese OEM relationship reconciliation.

## Findings
The Lafayette Radio Electronics 1970/71 catalog documents a Lafayette-branded Fuzz Sound and a separate Super-Drive foot controller. The Fuzz Sound is retained as a marketed/private-label product sold through LRE rather than assumed to be a Lafayette manufacturing facility.

LRE also carried Super Fuzz products. Period-focused research documents an LRE Super Fuzz beginning in 1968 and identifies the early gray version with the Honey Baby Crying / Super Fuzz family. Later LRE catalogs show additional Super Fuzz states and an LRE-branded Super Drive related to the Uni-Drive corridor.

The evidence strongly supports LRE as a retailer/distributor/private-label identity with imported products, while specific physical manufacturers remain model-specific. The archive therefore links LRE to Japanese OEM families without promoting LRE into a physical builder merely from branding.

## Evidence boundary
The Lafayette catalog is strong evidence for retail availability and catalog existence. It is not by itself proof of factory attribution.
