## Major 2018 research findings
- Guitar Pedal X's 2018 annual article identifies a strong innovation year and names RevivalDRIVE Custom, Plasma Distortion and other products among the year's standout field. It specifically records Royal Jelly, Bonsai, RevivalDRIVE, REVV G3, Spaceman Mercury IV and Wampler Pantheon among notable 2018 products.
- Origin Effects provides unusually strong primary chronology for RevivalDRIVE: announcement March 1, 2018; official release expected at the end of April; initial pre-orders limited to 200 units. RevivalDRIVE CUSTOM adds the separate front-panel amp-matching/filter controls and should remain a distinct product state.
- Fender's September 6, 2018 announcement explicitly identifies Engager Boost, Pelt Fuzz and Full Moon Distortion as new pedals with all-original circuits designed by Fender's in-house experts and released globally that day.
- BOSS announced MT-2W on October 4, 2018 as a Waza Craft addition. The MT-2W must remain a separate Waza Craft edition of the long-running MT-2 rather than a replacement or new vintage generation. Standard and Custom modes plus the revised analog/EQ implementation are key identification fields.
- Lunastone's 2018 NAMM announcement documents four numbered dirt models for Spring availability: Red Fuzz 1, Distortion 1, Blue Drive 1 and Smooth Drive 1. The numbered model names are preserved exactly because they provide useful chronology and identification anchors.
- Wampler's own October 2018 article documents the Pantheon as Brian Wampler's BluesBreaker-based release and provides a dated release context. The archive keeps BluesBreaker as lineage and Pantheon as the Wampler product identity.
- ThorpyFX The Dane is preserved as an artist-associated boost/overdrive developed around Peter Honoré's preferences. GPX notes that the concept began with the Peacekeeper but evolved into a substantially different signature product.
- Gamechanger Plasma Distortion is retained as a distinct high-voltage/plasma product identity because the 2018 coverage characterizes it as an innovative gated-fuzz-like distortion rather than a conventional lineage clone.

## Data rule reinforced
2018 demonstrates why the archive needs separate fields for product identity, edition, lineage, launch chronology and technical identity. A Waza Craft edition can preserve the original product lineage while remaining its own production object; a numbered Lunastone model establishes a model identity; and RevivalDRIVE CUSTOM is a distinct externally identifiable state within the same family.
