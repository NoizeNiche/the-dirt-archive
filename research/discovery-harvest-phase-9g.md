# The Dirt Archive — Discovery Harvest Phase 9G

## Focus

This pass deepens the Italian EF-EL / Caldironi / Cosmosound cluster. It is a good example of why The Dirt Archive needs manufacturer, brand and model entities kept separate: the same underlying production ecosystem appears under a large number of badges and product families.

## 1. Cosmosound / EF-EL manufacturing cluster

ToneHome's current Cosmosound research describes a 1970s Italian effects ecosystem in which similar or identical products appeared under Cosmosound, Silversound, Goldsound, G.I.S., GUN, EF-EL, JEI and VOX names. ToneHome treats Caldironi Musica as the probable factory attribution and explicitly notes that little is known about the company. citeturn899700search5turn899700search9

Effects Database independently exposes a substantial Cosmosound model family and links Cosmosound, Silversound, Goldsound, G.I.S., EUR and Vox records together. citeturn308868search1turn308868search0turn308868search3

### Archive rule
Do not collapse this into a single "Cosmosound pedal" family. Preserve:
- manufacturer attribution,
- brand/badge,
- model,
- housing/version,
- relationship evidence,
- and uncertainty.

A brand-level page can then show all known models carrying that badge while the manufacturer lineage page explains the shared production environment.

## 6. EUR Distortion Whau-Whau

**Brand:** EUR
**Model:** Distortion Whau-Whau
**Category:** fuzz-wah
**Country:** Italy
**Approximate period:** late 1960s–early 1970s according to EDB

This is a hybrid dirt record and an excellent example of the archive's category policy. It belongs in the dirt archive because it combines a fuzz/distortion section with a wah function. The correct public-facing category can remain `fuzz-wah`, with a dirt classification of fuzz/distortion when filtering expands beyond the three founding categories.

EDB relates the EUR version to sister models under Meazzi, Goldsound, G.I.S., Mac and Cosmosound names and attributes the family to Caldironi Musica in Milan. citeturn308868search3

### Architectural note
This suggests the eventual category taxonomy should support **compound effects** without forcing them into mutually exclusive buckets. `primary_category = Fuzz-Wah` plus `dirt_family = Fuzz` is a cleaner long-term model than deleting the wah context.

## 7. Goldsound Distortion Whau-Whau / Repeat family

Effects Database preserves Goldsound Distortion Whau-Whau and Goldsound Distortion Whau-Whau Repeat records, and relates the latter to Cosmosound and Silversound equivalents. These names should remain separate models because the Repeat version adds an additional time/modulation function and represents a distinct product configuration. citeturn308868search6turn308868search8

### Archive treatment
- Goldsound Distortion Whau-Whau: separate model
- Goldsound Distortion Whau-Whau Repeat: separate model
- Related models: Cosmosound / EUR / G.I.S. / Gun / Mac / Meazzi / Silversound variants as individually evidenced

Do not infer that cosmetically similar housings always mean identical circuits. Preserve the relationship as an evidence-backed family connection rather than a cloning recipe.

---

## 8. Structural lesson

The Italian cluster is one of the strongest arguments for building the archive around **relationships instead of names alone**.

A single historical path can look like:

`Manufacturer → Brand → Model → Variant / Housing → Related badge → Regional distributor`

and a model can simultaneously participate in:

`Model → Effect family → Dirt family → Historical lineage`

That structure will become increasingly important once the public catalog reaches thousands of discovery records.
