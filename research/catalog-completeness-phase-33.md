# The Dirt Archive — Catalog Completeness Phase 33

## Objective
Expand the builder universe through historical/obscure effects companies while simultaneously capturing visual-reference leads.

## Builder scope
- Klinger Custom Pedals
- KMA Audio Machines
- Kittycaster FX
- Tim/Timmy-era historical boutique builders discovered through builder-index cross-reference

## Research method
Pass A: enumerate the documented product lineup.
Pass B later: reconcile model families, generations, OEM/rebrands, production periods, and derived designs.
Media is captured during Pass A as evidence/reference, but image discovery does not imply republication rights.

## Important identification rule
Product thumbnail selection should prefer, in order: permissioned/manufacturer-owned images; compatible Creative Commons/public-domain images; Archive-owned photographs; then reference-only collector/marketplace images for research. A reference-only image should never be silently presented as licensed Archive artwork.

## Initial discoveries
Klinger has a compact historical catalog containing Tone Bender-, Buzzaround-, Foxx-, and other vintage-derived products. KMA has a broader catalog with several explicit family/version relationships such as Wurm/Wurhm/Guardian of the Wurm. Kittycaster contributes smaller-batch boutique fuzz and related products.

## Status
Discovery/Enumerated only. Do not promote to Canonical without product-level source reconciliation.
