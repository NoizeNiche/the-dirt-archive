# Modern Preservation Track — Batch 12

## Position

## High-value 2023 dirt candidates surfaced
- Haggtronix Mothman Dual Drive — Premier Guitar documented release July 24, 2023. Nashville builder; dual fuzz/boost circuits; two independent volume controls and footswitches; PCB designed by Paul Haggard; 1590BBS enclosure; 9–18V operation. Treat as a verified 2023 product candidate.
- Electro-Harmonix Lizard Queen — 2023 production model derived from Josh Scott/JHS prototype concept, with Daniel Danger artwork. Important lineage case: designer/concept relationship and EHX manufacturing/brand identity should remain distinct.
- Electro-Harmonix Satisfaction Plus — 2023 evolution of EHX's Satisfaction Fuzz, with NORM/FAT modes, Bias, Tone, Attack and Volume. Requires primary-source follow-up before promotion.
- Benson Amps Stonk Box Fuzz — 2023 Tone Bender MkI variant with temperature-management technology and expanded controls. Requires primary-source follow-up.
- EAE 0xEAE Fuzz — 2023 op-amp fuzz with Texture and Weight controls; strong candidate for product-family/variant mapping.
- Fender Shields Blender — 2023 Kevin Shields collaboration/reinterpretation of Fender Blender lineage; Sag and Octave Blender functions make it an important collaboration/lineage case.
- Crazy Tube Circuits Motherload — 2023 dual fuzz/distortion candidate; needs primary-source verification.
- KMA Machines Mandrake — 2023 octave/fuzz candidate; needs primary-source verification.
- Great Eastern FX Focus Fuzz — 2023 Ge/Si fuzz candidate already identified in earlier research; retain for deeper variant work.
- Haggtronix Rotten Sorcerer and Grassman Fuzz — mentioned in the Mothman release as existing recent products; discovery leads only until individually verified.

## Editorial/identity notes
- Lizard Queen should not be flattened into either a simple JHS product or a simple EHX product. Preserve concept/design collaboration, final EHX production identity, and artwork attribution separately.
- Shields Blender should be treated as a collaboration/reinterpretation within Fender Blender lineage, not automatically as a generic new Fender Blender generation.
- Haggtronix Mothman has unusually strong 2023 evidence from a contemporary trade publication and should be a priority for public-catalog promotion after repository schema review.
