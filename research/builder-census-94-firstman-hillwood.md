# YARP Batch 94 — Firstman / Hillwood

## Canonical identity
- **Canonical builder/lineage:** Firstman / Hillwood
- **Entity state:** `CANONICAL_LINEAGE`
- **Region:** Japan
- **Era:** late 1960s-1980s
- **Role:** historical manufacturer / brand / OEM lineage
- **Catalog state:** `INDEXED_BASELINE_MAPPED`

## Why this batch matters

Firstman / Hillwood is an important example of why the Archive cannot treat the logo on a pedal as equivalent to the physical manufacturer. The surviving evidence points to a Japanese manufacturer/brand lineage with products appearing under Firstman, Hillwood and customer brands such as Multivox. The Dirt Archive should preserve those roles separately.

## Dirt findings

### 1. Firstman-marketed FUZZrite, 1969

Tym Guitars reports that Firstman showed a FUZZrite in its 1969 catalogue. The same source explains that Firstman primarily used Teisco-Gen-Gakki manufacturing after Kazuo Morioka left Teisco. This means the archive can establish a Firstman catalog/market relationship, but should **not** currently assign physical manufacture of that FUZZrite to Firstman/Hillwood itself.

Status: `RELATIONSHIP_RECORD`

### 2. Multivox Big Jam SE-8 Distortion

The SE-8 is a strong OEM relationship record. Effects Database identifies it as a late-1970s/early-1980s Japanese-made distortion produced by Firstman for Nihon-Hammond and distributed under the Multivox brand. Guitar Pedal X likewise identifies Firstman Electronics/Hillwood as the OEM manufacturer for Multivox's late-1970s Big Jam series. Surviving sales records provide specimen-level evidence including serial-numbered examples and the distinctive red enclosure, slider controls and soft-touch switching.

Status: `INDEXED_BASELINE_MAPPED`

### 3. Multivox Big Jam Space Driver SE-13

The SE-13 is documented as a Big Jam boost/overdrive product. Current evidence is insufficient to promote Firstman/Hillwood from candidate OEM relationship to confirmed manufacturer for this particular model. It remains a boundary relationship record until period catalog/manual evidence is located.

Status: `RELATIONSHIP_RECORD`

## Identity discipline

Do not merge:

- Firstman the marketed brand
- Hillwood the manufacturing entity/lineage
- Multivox the U.S. marketed brand/distributor
- Nihon-Hammond the customer/company behind the Big Jam line
- Teisco-Gen-Gakki, which appears in the earlier Firstman manufacturing ecosystem

The Archive should represent these as typed relationships rather than as aliases for a single company.

## Evidence notes

The strongest current dirt-specific evidence is:

- period/catalog evidence for a Firstman-marketed FUZZrite
- specialist historical evidence connecting Firstman/Hillwood to Multivox Big Jam OEM production
- surviving SE-8 specimen listings with serial numbers and physical identification details

The next pass should prioritize period Firstman catalog scans, Japanese advertisements, Big Jam manuals/catalogs, surviving serial-numbered specimens and Japanese-language historical sources that can clarify the Firstman/Hillwood corporate transition and exact production responsibility.
