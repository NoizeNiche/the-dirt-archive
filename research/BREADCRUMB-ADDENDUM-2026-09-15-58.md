## Historical findings

- Clark SS-600 / Clark Fuzz is now treated as a high-confidence Clark Music Industries / Clark Electronics production attribution for the Clark-branded state. The strongest evidence combines the Clark enclosure instruction label, which states Manufactured and Distributed by Clark Music Industries, with former employee Rich Excellente's account of wiring fuzz boxes in Clark's small operation.
- An October 5, 1968 Syracuse Herald Journal classified establishes that Clark Fuzz was already circulating by that date.
- The Clark evidence is not being generalized to Manny's, Orpheum, Andre, MGI, AAT, Gracin's, or Gilsonite without model-specific proof. These remain family states with unresolved physical builders.
- The January 18, 1968 Manny's retail advertisement remains an important commercial date anchor, showing domestic distribution well outside New York.
- The September 13, 1968 Orpheum classified similarly establishes broad circulation but does not identify a factory.
- The East Coast Fuzz family list continues to include Manny's, Orpheum, Andre, Clark, E.U. Wurlitzer, Gracin's, Gilsonite, AAT, MGI and other variants; labels and builder identities are being kept distinct.

## Validation posture

Clark is now the strongest identified physical-builder lead in the East Coast Fuzz family, but the archive does not yet assert that Clark built every East Coast Fuzz-branded pedal. Enclosure production, assembly, and third-party populated circuit sourcing remain separate possibilities where the surviving evidence does not resolve them.
