# EarthQuaker Devices — 2004–2007 Origin Audit

## Status
**Builder entity:** CANONICAL
**Period:** 2004–2007 origin and first-production period
**Confidence:** HIGH for founding window and Hoof production milestone; exact earliest named-product sequence remains specimen-sensitive.

## Historical spine
EarthQuaker Devices was founded by **Jamie Stillman in 2004** in Akron, Ohio. The official company history says Stillman's pedal-building began after he successfully repaired a broken overdrive, followed by a period of obsessive experimentation and one-off sales.

Stillman's own interview history gives the first commercially meaningful activity a wider window: he began selling a few pedals on eBay and Harmony Central in **late 2005**. Another interview identifies the first pedal sold under the EarthQuaker Devices name as **Fantasy Fuzz**, with only about 2–3 examples made.

The major production milestone is **Hoof fuzz in 2007**, identified by EarthQuaker as its first full-production-run pedal. The early family also included Grand Orbiter, Disaster Transport, Hummingbird, and Ghost Echo, but their exact first-release years should not be assumed solely from today's historical page.

## Dirt catalog spine
- Fantasy Fuzz — tiny pre-production/name-anchor run, late 2005 or nearby.
- Hoof — first full-production run, 2007.
- Tusk Fuzz — early production-era dirt model; date needs specimen/catalog narrowing.
- OD/drive experiments and prototypes — retain as historical development material, not automatically as public retail products.

## Historical significance
EQD is an important case study in the transition from DIY repair/mod culture to a full boutique company. Stillman's work also demonstrates how online forums and word-of-mouth accelerated early pedal adoption.

## Archive cautions
Do not replace the 2004 founding date with 2007 simply because that is the first full-production year. Conversely, do not backdate every early EQD model to 2004. Preserve founding, experimental builds, named micro-run products, and full-production releases as distinct events.

No circuit schematics, PCB layouts, BOMs, or cloning instructions are included in this audit.
