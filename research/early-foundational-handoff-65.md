# Early Foundational Handoff 65

## Pass 16: 1994-2004 RAT descendants, late compact generations, and digital-platform context

This pass continues the archive into the mid-1990s through early-2000s boundary. It consolidates the late compact generations already surfaced in research and keeps digital-platform records separate from dedicated stompbox dirt.

### Newly documented / reinforced

- **BOSS OD-2R TURBO OverDrive**: official 1994-1999 production window retained as a distinct generation from OD-2.
- **BOSS OS-2 OverDrive/Distortion**: preserved as a hybrid model rather than being merged into OD or DS families.
- **BOSS MT-2 Metal Zone**: official 1991 introduction remains the chronology anchor.
- **BOSS DS-2 TURBO Distortion**: Japan-to-Taiwan corridor retained as specimen-led regional production history.
- **BOSS FZ-2 HYPER Fuzz**: official 1993-1997 production window retained separately from earlier FZ models.
- **Ibanez TS5**: Soundtank-era molded-plastic Tube Screamer generation reinforced.
- **Ibanez TS7**: Tone-Lok-era Tube Screamer generation reinforced.
- **ProCo Deucetone RAT**: dual-RAT-format early-2000s branch added as a separate retail object.
- **ProCo RAT 2**: 2003 sloped-enclosure state retained as a strong external identification marker.
- **DOD FX66 Flashback Fuzz**: 1999 final FX-series introduction retained with U.S./China regional distinction as specimen evidence.
- **Roland GP-16**: 1989 rack-era programmable digital platform context retained.
- **DigiTech DSP-128**: early digital DSP context retained without treating it as a dedicated dirt pedal.

### Key research findings

1. **1990s BOSS history is best modeled by product-specific states.** OD-2R, OS-2, MT-2, DS-2 and FZ-2 each have their own chronology rather than sharing one generic company transition.
2. **The RAT family is now an ecosystem.** RAT 2, Vintage RAT, BRAT, Deucetone and You Dirty RAT should remain linked as descendants while retaining their distinct retail identities and production windows.
3. **Late Tube Screamer history is series-driven.** TS5 and TS7 should remain visibly separate from TS808/TS9/TS10 generations.
4. **Digital belongs in the same historical map, but not the same object class.** GP-16 and DSP-128 are useful for understanding guitar-signal processing history, while dedicated dirt models remain distinguishable from multi-effect/rack platforms.
5. **Regional manufacturing is an identification layer.** Japan, Taiwan, U.S. and later China states can be meaningful specimen clues, but the archive should avoid turning an approximate transition into a universal date.
6. **Reissue status matters.** Later TS9 and Vintage RAT products should never be silently treated as original-era production merely because the model name matches.

### Evidence discipline

- **VERIFIED** is reserved for manufacturer or contemporary documentary support.
- **RESEARCH** remains appropriate for specialist chronology, regional transitions, and disputed dating.
- Product format, enclosure, country marking, logo, controls, dated advertisements, catalog appearance, and model-specific chronology may be public identification evidence.
- Internal circuit reconstruction, schematics, PCB layouts, complete BOMs, gutshot libraries, and cloning instructions remain outside the archive.

### Current lineage position

The research corridor now runs from early Japanese/OEM fuzz networks through 1980s DOD/BOSS/Ibanez compact families, the RAT ecosystem, and into late-1990s/early-2000s digital and boutique-era crossover.
