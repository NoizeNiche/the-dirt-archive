# Early Foundational Handoff 65

## Pass 15: 1990s Metal Zone, TS9 reissues, late DOD fuzz, and digital-platform context

This pass pushes the archive deeper into the 1990s, where high-gain compact pedals, reissued classics, late FX-series products, and programmable/digital platforms increasingly coexist. Ten reconciliation records were added, with context-only digital records kept distinct from dedicated dirt models.

### Newly documented

- **BOSS MT-2 Metal Zone**: officially anchored to 1991 and continuous production.
- **BOSS DS-1**: 1994 redesign/service-document state retained as a generation marker rather than a universal specimen rule.
- **BOSS MZ-2 Digital Metalizer**: 1987-1992 digital compact-era state documented separately from analog metal pedals.
- **Ibanez TS9**: 1993 reissue and later 1996 CE-marked state preserved separately.
- **Ibanez TS9**: 2002+ reissue generation documented as a later external-identification state.
- **DOD FX56 American Metal**: early box-style graphic transition identified as an important visual-generation clue.
- **DOD FX66 Flashback Fuzz**: final FX-series fuzz, with early U.S. and later China-made examples kept specimen-level.
- **DigiTech DSP-128**: early digital DSP platform context added to the archive.
- **Roland GP-16**: 1989 programmable digital guitar processor added as the next rack-era link after GP-8.
- **DigiTech PDS 8000**: retained as programmable/digital context rather than counted as a dedicated dirt model.

### Evidence discipline

- **VERIFIED** is reserved for manufacturer or contemporary documentary support.
- **RESEARCH** remains appropriate for specialist chronology, regional manufacturing transitions, service-manual interpretations, and specimen-level production claims.
- Reissues and regional manufacturing states remain distinct retail/generation objects when their external identifiers materially differ.
- Context-only digital records do not inflate the dedicated dirt count.
- No schematics, PCB layouts, complete BOMs, gutshot libraries, or cloning instructions are added.
