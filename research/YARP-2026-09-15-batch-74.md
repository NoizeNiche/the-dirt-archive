# YARP 2026-09-15 Batch 74: Univox UD-50 Uni-Drive

## Builder / brand identity
Univox is retained as the marketed U.S. brand associated with Merson Musical Products and later Unicord, while Shin-Ei remains the stronger Japanese manufacturing relationship for the UD-50. The surviving documentation identifies the pedal as Made in Japan and dates examples to the early 1970s.

## Catalog finding
- UD-50 Uni-Drive / Volume Harmonic Booster: a treadle-style gain/drive device that works as a boost and overdrive and can reach fuzz-like saturation. Effects Database and surviving retail specimens support late-1960s/early-1970s availability, with a 1970 date common on surviving examples.
- The UD-50 is distinct from the Shin-Ei FY-6 Super Fuzz and Companion families already mapped. Similar Japanese manufacture does not make the products one family.

## Evidence posture
Manufacturer attribution to Shin-Ei is evidence-qualified from surviving retail documentation, while the Univox/Merson/Unicord marketed-brand lineage is separately represented. Exact first-sale date and full production endpoint remain unresolved.

## Sources

- TC Gakki historical listing describing the Univox trademark lineage and Japanese Shin-Ei production relationship.
- Surviving Reverb specimens dated 1970 and identified as Made in Japan.
