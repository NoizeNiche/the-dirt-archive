# YARP 2026-09-15 Batch 83: Music-Son Spanish Fuzz Builder

## Builder identity
Music-Son is added as a Spanish amplifier/electronics brand with an evidence-qualified dirt catalog. The surviving documentation is strongest for the 1970 catalog and later surviving pedals, while the exact corporate manufacturing history remains incomplete.

## Dirt catalog
- Distorsion Booster: documented in a 1970 Music-Son catalog. Specialist research has identified multiple surviving production states, beginning with a germanium-equipped early example and continuing through later silver-hammerite silicon examples.
- Distorsion Sustain: separate Music-Son fuzz product, reported as a very aggressive Fender Blender-style design and sharing construction characteristics with later Distorsion Booster states. Keep as its own model rather than treating it as a revision.

## Chronology posture
The 1970 catalog establishes a strong documentary floor. Specialist specimen research suggests the Distorsion Booster existed across multiple production evolutions from the late 1960s through the mid-1970s, but exact first-sale and end dates remain unresolved.

## Attribution posture
Do not infer a foreign OEM factory from enclosure similarity. Music-Son is retained as the marketed Spanish builder/brand, with physical factory identity left open pending stronger primary evidence.
