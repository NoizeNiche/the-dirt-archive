# Machine Head Pedals (MHP) Historical Catalog Audit 01

## Research status
CANONICAL_BASELINE

## Identity
Machine Head Pedals is associated with Keith Aviles in Maryland. His documented chronology distinguishes decades of private electronics experimentation and modification work from the point when MHP became a public production pedal business.

## Chronology anchors
- 2001: first scratchbuilt pedals made in larger numbers, not yet a public production line.
- Spring 2008: MHP becomes a commercial venture with pedals, modifications, and replicas sold online.
- Late 2008 / early 2009: 72 Degrees Overdrive becomes available through Atomic Music in College Park, Maryland.

## Indexed dirt catalog
Effects Database indexes seven MHP products:

- 72 Degrees Overdrive — overdrive.
- Code Green — fuzz/gain-family dirt.
- Evelyn - Dual Drive — drive/distortion family.
- Evelyn MkII — distortion.
- Lightning — unusual dirt/effect design; retain as a historical catalog member.
- Nitrous Boost — boost boundary.
- RAW Preamp — preamp/gain boundary.

The archive should not treat Aviles's earlier modifications of mass-produced pedals as separate MHP retail models. Those activities are a distinct service/modification lineage.

## Archive interpretation
MHP is useful evidence for the transitional nature of the 2000s boutique scene: a builder could spend years modifying existing commercial products, then move into original scratchbuilt production only after a sufficiently mature design and demand. The record therefore separates modification history, private prototypes, first public sale, and later catalog models.

The exact first production dates of Code Green, Evelyn, Lightning and other MHP models remain unresolved where dated primary/period evidence is unavailable.
