# Modern Preservation Track Handoff 32

## Mission command: YARP
YARP means resume the full Dirt Archive mission: continue researching builders and their overdrive, distortion and fuzz products; collect trustworthy historical, product, production, variant, edition, lineage and identification evidence; update the public catalog; preserve uncertainty; and keep this breadcrumb current.

## Current position
The preservation track has moved into **2021** after the first structured 2022 batch. This pass prioritizes products with especially clean primary documentation, recognizable physical control layouts, reissue boundaries, signature relationships and strong manufacturing/design attribution.

## Important research notes
- BOSS HM-2W is a Waza Craft reissue identity, not a vintage HM-2 generation. BOSS explicitly documents its 2021 introduction, Standard and Custom modes, lower noise, increased level and preserved orange/black layout.
- Benson Germanium Boost is important because its servo/error-correction bias system directly addresses germanium temperature and drift problems. Preserve it separately from the later Germanium Preamp, despite shared technology.
- Redbeard Angry Rhubarb MKII is the builder's first overdrive family, refined around two independent drive stages and an interactive parametric EQ. Keep the MKII boundary distinct from earlier development states.
- Decibelics Golden Royale is a handcrafted dual Klon-style product derived from two Golden Horse channels. Preserve Golden Royale as its own dual-channel product rather than treating it as two ordinary Golden Horses.
- Walrus Eras belongs to the same modern five-state dirt family as Ages but is a separate distortion product. Its five clipping modes and clean blend are core identification markers.
- Wampler Gearbox is an Andy Wood collaboration combining modified Tumnus and Pinnacle-derived channels, with selectable order, dual I/O and a Channel 2 gate. Preserve both artist collaboration and embedded family lineage.
- Catalinbread Dreamcoat R2R is a nonstandard gain product based on the Aiwa TP-1011 reel-to-reel preamp. Its Frequency, Blend, Saturation, Mic Volume and Master controls give it a distinctive identification footprint.
- Blackhawk Sauron is a large high-gain distortion with three-way mid focus and an additive HM-2 mode. Preserve the relationship to the HM-2 aesthetic without collapsing product identity into BOSS lineage.
- Pete Cornish GC-1 is a multi-stage gain product with calibrated EQ and anniversary positioning. Treat its SS-3/G-2/P-2 relationships as lineage, not as interchangeable product identities.

## Data rule reinforced
Reissue, signature, collaboration and derivative-family relationships remain separate from generation. A modern product can recreate an older object while still having its own production identity, artwork, switching, control layout and chronology.

## Evidence note
The 2021 GPX annual fuzz/OD/distortion indexes were used as census and discovery layers. Primary builder and manufacturer material was preferred for promoted records, especially BOSS, Benson, Redbeard, Decibelics, Walrus and Wampler.

## Next target
Continue downward through 2020, then 2019, 2018 and the remaining 2010s. The further back we move, the more the method should shift from annual release tracking toward builder catalogs, archived product pages, manuals, distributor catalogs, production photographs and contemporary interviews so that introduction, revision and manufacturing dates do not get conflated.
