# YARP 2026-09-15 Batch 107: Italian Distortion/Fuzz Catalog Cross-Check

## Scope
Cross-check the Italian manufacturer layer around JEN, Meazzi/Caldironi, EKO, Montarbo, and Moreschi for additional dirt-family evidence.

## Findings
- The existing Italian network is not one factory. JEN Elettronica, Caldironi Musica/Meazzi, EKO, Montarbo, and Moreschi remain separate builder or manufacturer nodes.
- JEN retains the strongest model-level OEM evidence for the Vox V828/JEN FZ-III corridor.
- Caldironi/Meazzi remains the principal candidate for several Whau-Whau and branded export states, but model-specific factory certainty varies.
- EKO's Multitone is retained as a dirt/wah/boost hybrid and should not be collapsed into JEN or Caldironi merely because the products appeared in overlapping Italian export channels.
- Montarbo Sinphoton remains a separate Italian product line; the exact commercial introduction date remains less certain than its 1970s existence.
- Moreschi Octave Fuzz and Wah-Fuzz remain distinct Italian products with their own manufacturer node.
