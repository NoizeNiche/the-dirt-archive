# The Dirt Archive · Breadcrumb · Batch 121

## Completed

Phase One Batch 121 continued the Japanese builder-first sweep under the governing **Builder → Pedals → Photos → Information on the pedal** priority.

## Builder

This pass promoted two builder identities from the census/discovery layer into a documented working record:

- **ARION** — treated as the canonical builder/brand record for the historical Japanese pedal line, while preserving the later Sri Lankan production phase. Effects Database identifies the early Japanese production as Prince Tsushinkogyo and the later Sri Lankan production as Ueno Kaihatsu Center Ltd.
- **CAT'SFACTORY** — treated as a current Japanese builder with a substantial Akira Takasaki signature catalog and documented electronics-industry history. The official catalog distinguishes drive, distortion and fuzz products from unrelated boosters, chorus and wah products.

## Pedals

Central records added:

- CAT'SFACTORY — AT-222D 8I-II0 Distortion; AT-222Fz GermaniumTransistor FUZZ 2024 Limited; AT-222OD Over Drive; AT-222ODD OverDriveDistortion; AT-222D2 Distortion

## Photos

The batch records exterior/specimen photo trails from surviving manufacturer and specialist sources. The archive continues to treat those URLs as evidence and credit trails rather than automatic republication rights.

## Information on the pedal

- ARION's surviving category contains 35 products. The nine listed above are the dirt subset established in this pass, excluding chorus, delay, reverb, EQ, tuner, compressor, power and other non-dirt products.
- Arion's historical production must be split by production phase where evidence supports it: early units were made in Japan, while later units were made in Sri Lanka. The archive should not flatten those into one undifferentiated manufacturing period.
- CAT'SFACTORY's own site says its effects are built by a builder with more than 40 years in the electronics industry and that professional and general-sale units use the same specifications, process, parts and builder. Its current Takasaki collection lists 19 products, including the central overdrive, distortion and fuzz models retained here.
- The 2024 AT-222Fz is retained as a limited fuzz product because the official catalog explicitly identifies it as a germanium-transistor fuzz. The 2026 AT-222D2 is a new current distortion product announced in July 2026.

## Normalization decisions

- Sakura SDI-1 remains an OEM-branded relationship to Arion SDI-1 and is not promoted to a separate physical builder.
- CAT'SFACTORY anniversary/presentation states are not counted as new conceptual families without stronger evidence.
- Clean boosters, wah, chorus and other non-dirt Cat'sFACTORY products remain outside the central dirt inventory.
- No schematics, PCB layouts, gutshots, complete BOMs or cloning instructions are collected.
