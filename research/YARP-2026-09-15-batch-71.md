# YARP 2026-09-15 Batch 71: Guyatone FS-Series Audit

## Scope
Builder-first chronology and catalog refinement for the early Guyatone FS fuzz family.

## Catalog findings
- FS-1 Bazz/Buzz Box: specialist historical references place an early model around 1967. Keep as an early family candidate rather than backdating later FS models by assumption.
- FS-2 Buzz Box: documentary/catalog references place it in the late 1960s, with specialist chronology commonly centered around 1969. Preserve earlier conflicting dates as source disagreement.
- FS-3 Fuzz: Effects Database places it in 1972-1973; contemporary Japanese catalog research places it in the early 1970s.
- FS-5 Wah-Fuzz: retain as a distinct fuzz-wah family member rather than treating the wah section as a later modification of FS-3.
- FS-6 Fuzz Sustainer: catalog evidence places it by 1974-1975 as a later FS-series dirt product.

## Evidence posture
This batch does not force a single year for FS-1 or FS-2 because surviving specialist sources disagree. The builder-level fact that Guyatone maintained a sequence of distinct FS products is substantially stronger than any one collector dating claim.
