# The Dirt Archive — Discovery Harvest Phase 6B

## Purpose

Expand the discovery universe using current Effects Database builder catalogs. These entries are preserved as discovery candidates first. They are not automatically publication-ready historical records. The archive should normalize aliases, collaborations, reissues, editions and dirt categories before public promotion.

## Current catalog-scale targets

Effects Database currently exposes large builder catalogs that are useful for bulk discovery. Current observed counts include JHS Pedals 98 products, Catalinbread 124, Keeley Electronics 125, EarthQuaker Devices 88, Wren and Cuff 45, Crazy Tube Circuits 54, Fuzzrocious 43, Death By Audio 45, Stomp Under Foot 30, Basic Audio 27 and Analog Man 20. These counts are discovery snapshots and can change as the source changes.

## Research handling rules

1. EDB product counts are snapshots, not fixed census totals.
2. Product pages can mix manufacturer-owned products, collaborations and historically related products, so every record needs normalization.
3. Prototype, limited-edition, signature, artist and collaboration records should be preserved when they represent real products, while keeping the relationship type explicit.
4. A modern recreation of a vintage circuit is a modern record. Do not rewrite it as the historical product it references.
5. Small builders and one-person workshops are first-class archive material even when their catalogs contain only a handful of products.
6. Keep the discovery layer broad. Deep evidence/claim modeling can happen later, exactly as planned.
