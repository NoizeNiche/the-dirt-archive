## Evidence notes
Tonewave is documented by PALF as a recent Portuguese boutique brand from the Porto area, with five indexed products: Borealis, Caramel, Emery, Orchard and Preset. A 2026 Guitar Pedal X report additionally documents Bright alongside Caramel and Preset and identifies the brand as Portuguese. The current searchable product universe therefore contains at least five relevant dirt/gain models when Bright is included, with Orchard outside the central dirt scope.

Borealis is documented as a Portuguese-made overdrive/amp-in-a-box with Drive, Volume, Bite and Filter controls plus an internal clipping selector. Audiofanzine places Borealis in the 2024 Portuguese Tonewave announcement corridor, while older Portuguese-language coverage documents the pedal as an earlier Tonewave product. This creates a later chronology question for Protocol B rather than a reason to collapse dates prematurely.

Emery is Tonewave's first fuzz model according to external product coverage and is built in Portugal. PALF documents Grain and Coarse controls that substantially alter fuzz texture and low-frequency response.

Preset is retained as gain-adjacent preamp/overdrive rather than pure boost because current product documentation explicitly places drive functionality alongside the tone-shaping/preamp role.

## Photo workflow

Reverb provides specimen photography for Borealis; PALF, Guitar Pedal X and PedalFilter provide catalog-level visual/product trails for the wider Tonewave universe. ## Scope discipline
Tonewave is kept distinct from T.X Pedals / Tone.electroniX despite both being Portuguese boutique pedal brands. This prevents a false alias merge based only on country and overlapping product categories. No schematics, PCB layouts, gutshots, cloning instructions, or complete BOM data are published.
