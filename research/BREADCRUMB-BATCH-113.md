## Information on the pedal

- PPSE'79: TS-family mid character with open treble and dynamic response; founder's manual explains the Pike Place Smoky Emerald name and 1979 reference.
- PPSE Classic: symmetrical clipping variant intended as a cleaner PPSE-family voice while preserving responsiveness and separation.
- UFO'84: germanium fuzz with Fuzz Face-family character, adjustable gain and a special tone control that spans vintage to more modern sounds.
- Snake Head: designed specifically for full-hollow guitar, aiming for 1940s-50s tube-amp drive while preserving the instrument's thick core; two external controls plus internal tone/voltage configuration points are documented by TAURUS.

## Normalization decisions

- PPSE'79 Ver.2 and Ver.3 are revisions, not separate conceptual models.
- UFO'84 Ver.2 is retained as a revision relationship.
- Noblesse remains outside central dirt because its primary identity is booster.
- Snake Tail remains outside central dirt because it is a low-cut utility pedal, even though it can be used ahead of an amp or drive.
- 8/10 Pedal remains relationship-only until its product identity and chronology can be better established.
- No schematics, PCB layouts, gutshots or complete component inventories are collected.
