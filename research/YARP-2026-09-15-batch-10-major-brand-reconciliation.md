# YARP Batch 10 — Major Brand Reconciliation

## Purpose
Continue breadth-first catalog completion for major builders while distinguishing current catalog visibility, historical model existence, and variant/edition identity.

## BOSS
The BOSS dirt layer now has a stronger evidence-reviewed baseline covering OD-1, DS-1, SD-1, DS-2, HM-2, OS-2, MT-2, MD-2, OD-2/OD-2R pending chronology, BD-2, OD-3, FZ-2, FZ-3, FZ-5, FZ-1W, DS-1W, SD-1W, BD-2W, JB-2, ODB-3, PW-2 and DN-2.

Important corrections:
- BOSS's own historical article states the compact series began in 1977 with OD-1, PH-1 and SP-1; DS-1 followed in 1978.
- BOSS's fuzz history states FZ-2 was released in 1993, FZ-3 superseded it in 1997, and FZ-5 followed in 2007.
- Current BOSS catalog pages must not be mistaken for lifetime catalogs. A model disappearing from the current storefront is not evidence that it never existed or that it had only one production state.
- Waza Craft models are separate production/edition records, not replacements that erase the original retail identity.
- JB-2 is explicitly a BOSS/JHS collaboration and therefore belongs in the relationship graph rather than a BOSS-only bucket.

## DOD
The official DigiTech manual index independently confirms FX51, FX55C, FX59, FX66, FX69B, FX70, FX86, FX91, FX92, FX101, FX102 and YJM 308. The index also contains non-dirt FX models such as FX75C, reinforcing the rule that FX-series numbering cannot itself define the dirt catalog.

Historical FX50/52/53/54/58 records remain in the archive but are marked for period-catalog reconciliation because current official manual availability is incomplete relative to the historical catalog universe.

## Research boundary
No schematics, PCB layouts, cloning instructions, complete BOMs or circuit reconstruction material were added. This batch records identity, catalog status, chronology corridors, relationships and evidence only.
