# Block 077 — Bogner Amplification

## Builder
**Bogner Amplification**

## Verification basis
Primary and authoritative sources reviewed:
- Bogner-authored pedal manuals and product documentation where preserved in manufacturer-branded documents
- Bogner product descriptions reproduced in authoritative product documentation
- established specialist product catalogs and retailer records for model/category confirmation
- surviving Bogner pedal family references covering the Ecstasy, Uberschall, Transformer, and La Grange families

The active repository contained no prior Bogner match before this block was added. Bogner Amplification is treated as the builder identity for the Bogner-branded dirt pedals documented below.

The reviewed material supports seven clearly documented core dirt identities: Ecstasy Blue, Ecstasy Red, Uberschall, Wessex, Burnley, La Grange, and Oxford. Dirt categories are assigned from explicit product/category descriptions. Generic references to gain range, breakup, or aggressive tone are not used to manufacture additional categories.

## Overdrive
Current / manufacturer-supported identities:
- production status not established from reviewed current manufacturer material

Historical / manufacturer-supported identities:
- Ecstasy Blue
- Ecstasy Red
- Wessex
- La Grange

Verification notes:
- **Ecstasy Blue** is documented as an overdrive pedal based on the Ecstasy amplifier's Blue channel. Product descriptions emphasize classic-rock crunch, Plexi-style tones, and overdrive operation.
- **Ecstasy Red** is explicitly documented in surviving product material as an Overdrive/Distortion pedal and spans vintage overdrive through high-gain operation. It therefore receives both Overdrive and Distortion below.
- **Wessex** is explicitly presented as an overdrive pedal, with a range from clean boost to moderate overdrive and a selectable higher-gain voicing.
- **La Grange** is consistently documented as an overdrive pedal recreating jumpered 1960s Plexi-style operation. Its wide gain range is retained within the single Overdrive identity rather than creating a separate Distortion identity.

## Distortion
Current / manufacturer-supported identities:
- production status not established from reviewed current manufacturer material

Historical / manufacturer-supported identities:
- Ecstasy Red
- Uberschall
- Burnley

Verification notes:
- **Ecstasy Red** is explicitly described as an Overdrive/Distortion pedal and is retained in both target categories.
- **Uberschall** is explicitly presented as a high-gain distortion pedal derived from the Bogner Uberschall amplifier. The preserved Bogner manual describes its savage high-gain character while also noting lower and medium gain ranges. It remains Distortion only because the named product identity is a distortion pedal.
- **Burnley** is documented as a distortion pedal. High-gain and harmonic-range language does not create an additional Overdrive identity because the product-level category is Distortion.

## Fuzz
Current / manufacturer-supported identities:
- production status not established from reviewed current manufacturer material

Historical / manufacturer-supported identities:
- Oxford

Verification notes:
- **Oxford** is explicitly documented as a fuzz pedal. Its Gain switch spans amp-like breakup through extreme fuzz, but the named product identity and product description are centered on fuzz. It is therefore recorded as Fuzz only.

## Category-overlap decisions
- **Ecstasy Blue**: Overdrive only. Product-level references identify it as an overdrive pedal.
- **Ecstasy Red**: Overdrive + Distortion. Surviving product specifications explicitly call it an Overdrive/Distortion pedal and describe its range from vintage overdrive to high gain.
- **Uberschall**: Distortion only. Manufacturer-authored material identifies it as a high-gain distortion pedal; lower gain range is treated as operating range rather than a second product category.
- **Wessex**: Overdrive only. Product-level descriptions identify it as an overdrive pedal.
- **Burnley**: Distortion only. Product-level descriptions identify it as a distortion pedal.
- **La Grange**: Overdrive only. Product-level descriptions identify it as an overdrive pedal; wide gain range does not by itself create a separate Distortion identity.
- **Oxford**: Fuzz only. Product-level descriptions identify it as a fuzz pedal; its low-gain breakup range does not create a separate Overdrive identity.

## Historical / variant handling
- The Bogner pedal lineup represented here spans multiple product generations. The archive records the functional named identities rather than treating every finish, production revision, or enclosure change as a new dirt product.
- **Ecstasy Blue Mini** and **Ecstasy Red Mini** are consolidated with their corresponding Ecstasy Blue and Ecstasy Red functional identities. The minis are reduced-size versions of the same named Ecstasy channel concepts rather than separate builder/product identities for this initial census.
- **Burnley V2** and **Wessex V2** are treated as revisions of the same named products rather than separate dirt identities.
- Current availability is not used as the identity boundary. Historical/discontinued dirt products remain in scope because the archive covers historical builders and products.
- Booster functions on Ecstasy pedals and the independent boost portions of La Grange are treated as secondary functions rather than standalone target products.

## Exclusions
- **Harlow**: boost/compressor-oriented product, excluded from the target dirt taxonomy.
- **Lyndhurst**: compressor, excluded.
- Generic gain, saturation, breakup, or high-gain references without explicit product-level category evidence: excluded as secondary categories.
- Amp models, preamp modules, and non-pedal Bogner products: outside this builder-to-pedal dirt census.
- Cloning, schematics, internal construction, component details, and reverse-engineering material: outside scope.
