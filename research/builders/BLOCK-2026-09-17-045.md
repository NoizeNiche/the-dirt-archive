# Builder → Pedals Research Block 045

## Builder
**Animal Factory Amplification — Aditya Nandwana**

## Verification basis
Animal Factory Amplification's own About/Team material identifies **Aditya Nandwana as founder**, and the company's current material identifies the operation as originating in Bombay/Mumbai, India. The official catalog and manufacturer-authored product/history pages were used to establish pedal identities and dirt categories. The census is limited to physical guitar/bass pedal products, not the company's separate Eurorack modules, DIY module kits, or unrelated electronics.

The manufacturer explicitly documents the relationship between several pedals and their later/revised forms. Cosmetic revisions and ordinary version changes are not split into separate census identities unless the manufacturer presents a materially distinct product identity. Where a pedal was discontinued but its identity is preserved in the official catalog/history, it remains in the historical census.

## Product notes
- **Pit Viper:** Animal Factory's official product page identifies the pedal as a guitar overdrive/drive design and says the 2019 revision used hard/soft clipping controls to produce varied diode-clipping and compression textures. The company's 2023 product announcement describes the returning Pit Viper as an overdrive with expanded controls. It is retained under Overdrive and not separately promoted to Distortion merely because its clipping range can become aggressive.
- **Godeater:** Animal Factory's official historical material calls the original product a bass fuzz/bass distortion pedal and describes its low-end compression and bass-distortion circuit. Because the manufacturer itself uses both fuzz and distortion terminology for the pedal across its historical materials, it is retained in both Fuzz and Distortion.
- **Godeater+:** the current official page identifies it as a Dynamic Bass Distortion Pedal and describes a signature distortion circuit ranging from mild grunt to extreme saturation. It is retained under Distortion. The plus model is kept as a distinct current product identity because Animal Factory presents it as an expanded successor with new routing, sidechain, crossover, MIDI/CV and 24V internal operation rather than merely a cosmetic revision.
- **Ozymandias:** the official product page explicitly names it a Dual Overdrive/Distortion Pedal and describes two independent overdrive circuits with hard/soft clipping options. It is therefore retained in both Overdrive and Distortion.
- **Chemical Burn:** Animal Factory's own history identifies Chemical Burn as the company's first product and describes it as a modified Shin-Ei Superfuzz FY-6 variant. The 2023 company announcement calls the returning Chemical Burn a "Face-Melting Wall of Fuzz." It is retained under Fuzz. The v1/v2/v3 iterations are not split into separate census entries because the manufacturer treats them as successive versions of the Chemical Burn product identity.
- **Baron Samedi:** Animal Factory's official history and product-development material identify Baron Samedi as a fuzz pedal. The manufacturer later described the returning Baron as a chaotic fuzz and separately developed the Baron Samedi II Eurorack module. The pedal itself is retained under Fuzz; the Eurorack module is excluded from the pedal census.
- **Dirty Mirror:** the official 2025 product announcement identifies the pedal as a dual-parallel fuzz design. Its BURN channel is based on Chemical Burn and its CHURN channel is based on a heavily modified Big Muff Pi, with an integrated delay and parallel mixing. It is retained under Fuzz only; the internal delay does not create another target-category identity.

## Explicit exclusions / restraint
- **Eurorack modules:** Godeater Eurorack, Baron Samedi II, Pit Viper V2 module, Orobas I/II, Coma Reactor, Bonesaw, Vivisect, and Tannhäuser Gates are excluded from the physical pedal census. Animal Factory's own manuals and catalog separate these from the pedal products.
- **DIY module kits:** excluded from the pedal census.
- **Orobas:** although the Eurorack product provides tube saturation/distortion, it is a module rather than a pedal and is therefore excluded.
- **Chemical Burn versions:** v1/v2/v3 are treated as one product identity, not three pedals.
- **Baron Samedi versions:** pedal revisions are treated as one Baron Samedi pedal identity; Baron Samedi II is a separate Eurorack module, not a second pedal.
- **Pit Viper revisions:** the 2015/2019 pedal versions are retained as one Pit Viper identity. The later Ozymandias is a separate product because Animal Factory explicitly describes it as the successor and as a dual-channel design.
- **Godeater revisions:** the historical Godeater pedal remains one identity; Godeater+ is separately listed because the manufacturer presents it as an expanded successor with materially expanded functionality.
- **Boost functions:** not counted as a separate target category. Chemical Burn's switchable level boost does not create a boost identity.
