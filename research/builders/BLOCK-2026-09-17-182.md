## Scope

Continued the C-section Builder -> Pedals census after Block 181 with dirt-only inclusion: overdrive, distortion, fuzz. Existing builders were checked against the canonical index before creating new identities. Utility, modulation, delay, routing, compressor-only, boost-only, generic/unresolved brands, and non-dirt products were excluded.

## Evidence / classification notes

- Conrad was a historical house brand associated with David Wexler & Co. Its Effects Database catalog contains four products; three are explicitly dirt products. The Conrad Sky Hawk is cataloged as fuzz plus treble boost, and the Fuzzy Fury and Buzz Box are cataloged as fuzz. Bass Hawk is not added because its dirt classification was not sufficiently clear in the reviewed evidence. Sources: Effects Database Conrad manufacturer page and product pages.
- Coolmusic's Effects Database manufacturer page lists C-DI01 Insane Distortion, C-DI02 Classic Distortion, C-FC1 Fuzzster, C-OV01 Crunchy Overdrive, HG-7 High Gain, MT-6 Distortion, and Multiple Distortion alongside non-dirt products. The seven listed here are retained as the dirt subset.
- Coolpedals' Effects Database catalog contains Dirty Devil, Steam Machine, and Strutter. Dirty Devil is classified as distortion/overdrive, Steam Machine as distortion/overdrive, and Strutter as overdrive. The builder therefore contributes five company/pedal/type rows.
- Cordovox is an older accordion/electronic brand that now also appears on guitar pedals. Effects Database lists DS-8 Mouse, DS-9 Distortion, FZ-7 Fuzz, Metal-End, OD-7 Overdrive, and OD-8 X-Drive among its products. Metal-End is excluded from this block because its dirt subtype was not sufficiently explicit in the reviewed source.
- Coron is represented in Effects Database by multiple Japanese-era dirt models spanning distortion, heavy distortion/heavy metal, and overdrive. Coron Octaver 2 is retained as Fuzz because the reviewed catalog evidence describes it as a downward octave fuzz; other non-dirt Coron equipment is excluded.
- Cosmic Sound FX was a French micro-business active around 2010-2012, making pedals based on older 1960s-70s designs. Effects Database identifies three dirt products: The Fuzz Machine, The Ladyland Fuzz, and The MkII. A biographical source for Maxime Crozon independently documents Cosmic Sound FX as a 2010-2012 micro-enterprise making guitar effects pedals based on older models.
- Cosmodio Instruments states that it launched in 2024 and hand-builds pedals in Massachusetts. Its current product line includes the Glitch Witch fuzz and Pet Yeti distortion/fuzz; modulation products such as Gravity Well and Splinter Twin are excluded.
- Cosmosound was an Italian 1970s effects brand associated with Sandro Marchetti and EF-EL. Effects Database lists the dirt-bearing CSE and E-series models. A later interview with Marchetti describes the E-6 and E-8 as distortion products and E-7 as a tremolo distortion; Effects Database separately catalogs E-8 Wild Sound as fuzz. Non-dirt Cosmosound products remain excluded.

## Exclusions / non-additions

- Concert: Effects Database lists only the SS-100 Echo Chamber, so no dirt pedal.
- Console Pedals: Effects Database currently lists zero products.
- Cool: insufficient evidence to establish a distinct dirt builder identity from the reviewed sources.
- Cool Bear Effects: insufficient evidence to establish a distinct dirt builder identity from the reviewed sources.
- Correct Sound: reviewed catalog evidence is centered on EQ/preamp products rather than dirt.
- Cort and Cortez: no sufficiently established standalone dirt builder catalog found in this pass.
- Countryman Associates: no dirt product retained in this pass.
- Cordovox Metal-End: held back pending a clearer primary dirt classification.
