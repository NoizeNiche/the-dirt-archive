# Builder → Pedals Research Block 037

## Builder
**OKKO FX — Heiko Lauenroth**

## Scope
Builder → Pedals only. This block records OKKO FX's documented overdrive, distortion, and fuzz identities from the current official catalog. Non-dirt effects and boost-only products are excluded unless they are part of a qualifying dirt unit.

## Builder identity
OKKO FX is based in Leipzig, Germany. The official site identifies the seller as Heiko Lauenroth / OKKO FX and states that the company officially launched in 2003 and handbuilds its pedals in Leipzig.

## Current / currently listed dirt pedals

### Overdrive
- **DIABLO DUAL** — official product page classifies it as a 2-channel overdrive / distortion.
- **DIABLO GH Signature** — official catalog classifies it as a super-dynamic overdrive and clean boost.
- **TWINSONIC MKII** — official catalog classifies it as a dual-stacking overdrive + preamp.
- **HOLY GRIT** — official product page classifies it as overdrive / fuzz / preamp; it is retained under both overdrive and fuzz because the builder explicitly uses both terms.

### Distortion
- **DIABLO DUAL** — 2-channel overdrive / distortion.
- **MOTÖRBASS** — official catalog classifies it as aggressive punchy bass distortion.
- **DIRTY DIABLO** — official catalog describes the current Dirty Diablo versions as variations of the classic Diablo; retained as a dirt identity but not split into separate cosmetic variants.

### Fuzz
- **HOLY GRIT** — official product page classifies it as overdrive / fuzz / preamp and describes its circuit as a heavily modified Fuzz Face / Tone Bender-derived design.
- **BFGRMWB — Big Fucking Green Russian Muff With Benefits** — official catalog currently lists the fuzz product in big-box and white editions. Cosmetic editions are treated as one pedal identity.

## Verification notes
1. OKKO's official homepage currently lists DIABLO DUAL, DIABLO GH Signature, TWINSONIC MKII, MOTÖRBASS, HOLY GRIT, DIRTY DIABLO, and BFGRMWB among its products.
2. The official DIABLO DUAL page explicitly calls the pedal a "2-channel overdrive / distortion."
3. The official HOLY GRIT page explicitly calls it "Overdrive Fuzz Preamp" and describes the circuit as based on the classic Fuzz Face / Tone Bender family but heavily modified.
4. The official homepage explicitly classifies MOTÖRBASS as bass distortion and TWINSONIC MKII as dual-stacking overdrive + preamp.
5. The official homepage lists BFGRMWB as "Big Fucking Green Russian Muff With Benefits," making its fuzz identity explicit through the Muff designation and product context. It is recorded as one identity despite separate big-box/white listings.
6. DIRTY DIABLO is retained as a current dirt identity because OKKO explicitly describes it as a variation of the classic Diablo. Cosmetic black/white editions are not split into separate archive products.
7. COCAINE and COCA COMP MKII are excluded because the current official catalog classifies them as compressor / EQ / preamp / boost and optical compressor respectively, not overdrive, distortion, or fuzz.
8. No unsupported historical names were promoted into this block. The current official catalog was sufficient to establish a multi-pedal dirt footprint.

**OKKO FX — Heiko Lauenroth** is added to the Builder → Pedals census as a German builder with a documented current catalog spanning overdrive, distortion, and fuzz, supported directly by OKKO's official catalog and product pages.
