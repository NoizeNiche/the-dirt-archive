# The Dirt Archive — Builder → Pedals Research Block 034

## Builder
**Himmelstrutz Elektro Art — Joakim Hedeby (Sweden)**

## Scope
Builder → Pedals only. This block records identifiable Himmelstrutz overdrive, distortion, and fuzz products. Amp products, modulation, utility effects, and non-dirt designs are excluded. Closely related named variants are retained when the manufacturer documents them as distinct pedal models, but the block does not attempt to enumerate cosmetic changes or every prototype.

## Overdrive
- **FITZO — Overdrive Avant-garde / Amp Enhancer** — Official Himmelstrutz manual identifies FITZO explicitly as an overdrive and describes its Drive control as the overdrive control. The same manual states that every pedal is handmade, tested, and adjusted by the original designer in Sweden.
- **FITZO+** — Overdrive/distortion variant of FITZO. The official owner manual identifies the pedal's drive/volume behavior and documents its additional gain configuration. Retained as a distinct documented model because Himmelstrutz provides a separate manual and model identity.
- **Under Over** — Overdrive / pre-EQ drive design. Himmelstrutz's official download index identifies Under Over and Under Overdriver as separate product identities in its pedal documentation archive. Retained as documented Himmelstrutz dirt products, with exact generation differences not expanded here.
- **FETTO Standard** — Overdrive/distortion. Himmelstrutz's official FETTO manual describes FETTO as an overdrive/distortion stomp box and the official download archive lists FETTO Standard as a model.
- **FETTO 9.0 / 11.1** — Overdrive/distortion. Official FETTO documentation describes the FETTO family as an overdrive/distortion stomp box and the Himmelstrutz download archive separately identifies the 9.0 to 11.1 documentation lineage.
- **FETTO Custom** — Great Wide Distortion / overdrive-distortion. The official manual explicitly describes it as an overdrive/distortion effect and identifies it as FETTO Custom.
- **FETTO Jr.** — FETTO-family drive. Himmelstrutz's official download index identifies FETTO Jr. as a distinct pedal model. Kept in the builder census without inferring a narrower category beyond the documented FETTO drive family.
- **FETTO Nord+** — FETTO-family overdrive/distortion. Official Himmelstrutz documentation lists FETTO Nord+ as a distinct model.
- **FETTO Nord 70+** — FETTO-family overdrive/distortion. Official Himmelstrutz documentation lists FETTO Nord 70+ as a distinct model.
- **FETTO Maximus** — FETTO-family high-gain drive. Official Himmelstrutz documentation lists FETTO Maximus as a distinct model.
- **FETTO Apache** — FETTO-family drive/distortion. Official Himmelstrutz documentation lists FETTO Apache as a distinct model.
- **FETTO C-Luxe** — FETTO-family drive/distortion. Official Himmelstrutz documentation lists FETTO C-Luxe as a distinct model.
- **FETTO SC** — FETTO-family drive/distortion. Official Himmelstrutz documentation lists FETTO SC as a distinct model.
- **TÔNKer** — Dirt/drive prototype-to-product identity. Himmelstrutz's official download archive lists TÔNKer among its pedal documentation. The builder's own historical interview also describes TÔNKer as a developing pedal. Retained as a documented Himmelstrutz dirt identity without over-specifying its final category.

## Distortion
- **FETTO Standard** — Overdrive/distortion. Official FETTO documentation explicitly uses both terms for the pedal.
- **FETTO 9.0** — Overdrive/distortion. The official manual repeatedly describes the unit as an overdrive/distortion stomp box and documents its gain/distortion behavior.
- **FETTO Custom** — Overdrive/distortion. Official manual identifies Drive as the drive/distortion control and calls the design a Great Wide Distortion effect.
- **GRAMPS** — Fuzztortion/overdrive-distortion family. Himmelstrutz's official download archive lists GRAMPS, and the builder's interview/documentation describes the GRAMPS family as capable of oversaturated/distorted behavior at reduced operating voltage. Retained as a documented dirt model.
- **GRAMPS+ — Fuzztortion Grande** — Fuzz/distortion. The official owner manual is titled “GRAMPS+ — Fuzztortion Grande,” documents Gain and Temp/Bias controls, and describes the pedal as producing increasingly fuzzy behavior across its bias range.
- **FUGAZI** — Dirt/drive model documented in Himmelstrutz's official pedal archive. Retained as a documented dirt identity, but no narrower category is asserted here because the available official index does not provide a sufficiently precise category description.
- **FUGAZI Jr.** — Dirt/drive model documented in Himmelstrutz's official pedal archive. Retained as a documented dirt identity without inferring a more specific category.

## Fuzz
- **GRAMPS+ — Fuzztortion Grande** — Fuzz/distortion. The official manual explicitly calls the design Fuzztortion Grande and provides a Temp/Bias control with the middle position described as most fuzzy.
- **GRAMPS** — Fuzz/distortion family identity. Himmelstrutz's own historical documentation discusses the GRAMPS family as becoming over-saturated and extreme at lower supply voltage, placing it in the builder's fuzz/distortion dirt family.

## Verification notes
- Himmelstrutz's official download archive is unusually important for this builder because it preserves manuals for current and historical pedal generations, including FETTO variants, FITZO, FITZO+, GRAMPS, GRAMPS+, FUGAZI, Mr. Nutcracker, Under Over, TÔNKer, and related models.
- The official FETTO 9.0 manual explicitly calls FETTO an “overdrive/distortion stomp box,” so the archive does not force the FETTO family into a single category.
- The official GRAMPS+ manual explicitly identifies it as “Fuzztortion Grande,” making fuzz/distortion classification direct rather than inferred.
- The Himmelstrutz archive lists many product names separately. They are retained where the manufacturer gives them separate model identities, but this block does not attempt to treat every revision number, artwork change, or prototype as a separate census entry.
- **Mr. Nutcracker** is intentionally not categorized here because the available official index confirms the product identity but the available evidence in this pass was not sufficient to confidently assign it to overdrive, distortion, or fuzz. It remains a future verification lead rather than a forced classification.
