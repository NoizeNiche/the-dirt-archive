# Builder → Pedals Research Block 099

## Builder
**Caroline Guitar Company — Philippe Herndon / Caroline Guitar Company**

## Verification basis
Caroline Guitar Company's official site describes the company as making pedals in Columbia, South Carolina. Its current product catalog, Legacy Products section, official product pages, manuals, and company articles were used to establish the dirt catalog. Older products are retained only where Caroline's own material directly identifies the product and its dirt function.

The archive keeps product identity separate from a pedal merely being capable of reaching another gain range. A named fuzz that can be used as an overdrive remains Fuzz unless Caroline explicitly presents it as an Overdrive product too. Clearly hybrid names such as “fuzzstortion” are retained in both relevant categories when the builder itself uses that identity.

## Product notes
- **Aaron Graves Overdrive:** Caroline explicitly calls this an Overdrive and describes it as a preamp/power-amp drive pedal. It is retained under Overdrive only.
- **The Blues:** Caroline describes this as a straightforward circuit that moves from transparent light drive to gnarly raw drive and identifies the product as predominantly drive. It is retained under Overdrive; the small amount of fuzz-like character mentioned in the description is not treated as a separate Fuzz identity.
- **Haymaker:** Caroline's own material explicitly calls Haymaker an overdrive and documents it as a discontinued pedal. It is retained as a historical Overdrive identity.
- **Icarus V2 / Icarus:** Caroline's official product page describes Icarus V2 as including rich overdrive alongside its buffer/boost functions. Because the product's standalone identity includes overdrive, it is retained under Overdrive; preamp and boost functions remain outside scope.
- **Wave Cannon / Cannonball:** Caroline's official comparison material identifies the original Wave Cannon/Cannonball as the company's 2010 starter pedal and describes its DOD OD-250/ProCo Rat-style frame. Caroline's own historical material also documents low-gain overdrive, true distortion, and a Havoc fuzz mode. It is therefore retained in all three target categories rather than forcing it into one box.
- **Wave Cannon MKII / Superdistorter:** Caroline explicitly describes the MKII as an unrepentant distortion pedal and calls it the Superdistorter. It remains Distortion only.
- **Wave Cannon Zero:** Caroline's current product page explicitly calls it a Distorter with Havoc and describes late-1970s/early-1980s big-amp distortion. It is retained under Distortion.
- **CROM:** Caroline calls CROM a Supa Tone Bender-style “fuzzstortion” and identifies its relationship to the earlier Olympia fuzz. The product is therefore retained in both Fuzz and Distortion.
- **Hawaiian Pizza:** Caroline's current product page explicitly identifies it as a fuzz. Older Caroline material calls it a versatile fuzz-drive, but the separate drive language is treated as behavior around the fuzz identity rather than a second Overdrive category.
- **Shigeharu:** Caroline explicitly labels Shigeharu “IC Fuzz + Octave” and describes its Muff-style fuzz character with a parallel octave-up circuit. It remains Fuzz only.
- **Olympia:** Caroline's own site documents the original Olympia as a fuzz pedal launched through Kickstarter. It is retained as a historical Fuzz identity.

## Explicit exclusions / restraint
- **Météore, Kilobyte, Parabola, and related multi-effect/preamp products:** excluded where the primary product identity is reverb, delay, or modulation even though Caroline describes internal overdrive/preamp stages.
- **Megabyte / Kilobyte delays:** excluded because their primary identities are delay products; their drive/preamp stages are not standalone dirt identities.
- **Arigato, Somersault, Parabola, and other modulation pedals:** excluded.
- **Special colorways and limited finish variants:** consolidated into the underlying pedal identity.
- **Caroline Shigeharu Limited:** treated as a limited variant of Shigeharu rather than a separate pedal identity.
- **Soviet Pizza:** treated as a limited Hawaiian Pizza variant rather than a separate pedal identity.
- **Alpha Gain:** not promoted as a separate core identity because Caroline presents it as a special repurposing/voicing of Icarus clipping stages rather than a necessary new regular product identity.
- **Gain-range inflation:** Hawaiian Pizza, Shigeharu, The Blues, and similar products are not given additional target categories solely because Caroline describes them as capable of overlapping neighboring gain textures.

## Status
**Verified research block.** Caroline Guitar Company is a previously uncovered builder in the fresh census. The catalog establishes a mixed dirt history spanning Overdrive, Distortion, and Fuzz. Wave Cannon/Cannonball is the clearest three-category historical hybrid; CROM is explicitly a fuzzstortion and is retained in both Fuzz and Distortion. Hawaiian Pizza and Shigeharu remain Fuzz-only, while Aaron Graves, The Blues, Haymaker, and Icarus are Overdrive identities. Non-dirt modulation, delay, reverb, and preamp functions were deliberately kept outside the target catalog.
