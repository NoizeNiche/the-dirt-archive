# Builder -> Pedals Research Block 200

## Scope

Continued the C-section Builder -> Pedals census with one new canonical builder. Inclusion remains dirt-only: overdrive, distortion, and fuzz.

## New canonical builder

### 475 — Circuit Rider Effects
- SiClops — Overdrive

## Evidence / classification notes

- Circuit Rider Effects maintains an official site for boutique guitar and synth pedals and custom pedal designs.
- The official site currently presents the SiClops as a pedal and explicitly describes it as a faithful recreation of a mythical gold overdrive.
- Effects Database has a current Circuit Rider Effects brand record and links to the builder's official site, although the Effects Database brand page currently reports zero cataloged products.
- Because the builder's own current site directly identifies SiClops as an overdrive, it qualifies for the archive as Overdrive.
- The current official site also shows Analog Phaser and Resonate Phaser products, but these are modulation effects and are outside the archive dirt taxonomy.

## Exclusions / non-additions

- No additional Circuit Rider Effects dirt pedal was identified on the current official site.
- Analog Phaser and Resonate Phaser remain excluded as modulation.
- The Effects Database zero-product count is retained as a provenance note rather than used to erase the builder, because the builder's own live site provides direct current product evidence.
