Scope: Builder → Pedals only

This block records builder/product relationships only. Product names are taken from current manufacturer catalogs and official product pages. This is a research checkpoint, not an exhaustive lifetime catalog.

Builder / brand: Suhr

### Distortion

- Riot Mini
- Eclipse

### Overdrive
- Shiba Drive
- Shiba Mini

### Fuzz
- Rufus Fuzz
- Rufus Reloaded Fuzz

Notes:
- Suhr's official pedal catalog currently separates pedals into Distortion, Overdrive, Boost, Fuzz, Compression, Modulation, and Time categories.
- The official distortion page identifies Riot, Riot Reloaded, and Eclipse as distortion products; the current product page notes that Riot Reloaded is discontinued while the Riot replaces it in the current line.
- The official overdrive page identifies Shiba Drive, Shiba Drive Reloaded, and the Kiko Loureiro Signature Shiba Drive Reloaded as overdrive products. The current top-level catalog also lists Shiba Drive and Shiba Mini.
- The official fuzz page identifies Rufus Fuzz and Rufus Reloaded Fuzz; the top-level catalog also lists Thornicus Fuzz.
- Riot Reloaded is retained as a discontinued/historical product identity only where Suhr's official current product page explicitly identifies it as discontinued. No additional historical product identities are invented.

## 2. Jackson Audio

Builder / brand: Jackson Audio

### Current dirt products verified from the official Jackson Audio catalog
- FUZZ - Modular Fuzz — modular fuzz
- Golden Boy Mini Overdrive — overdrive
- The Optimist Dual Overdrive & EQ — dual overdrive
- Asabi Overdrive/Distortion — overdrive / distortion
- Golden Boy Transparent Overdrive — overdrive
- BROKEN ARROW v2 - MIDI — overdrive / distortion
- Belle Starr Overdrive — overdrive
- 1484 - Twin Twelve Pedal — amp-style gain/preamp dirt product

### Current modular fuzz identities documented by the manufacturer
- Modern Fuzz
- Modern Fuzz Deluxe
- Classic Vintage Fuzz
- Page MK II
- Goat Head

Notes:
- Jackson Audio's official pedals collection currently lists FUZZ - Modular Fuzz, Golden Boy Mini Overdrive, The Optimist, Asabi Overdrive/Distortion, Golden Boy Transparent Overdrive, BROKEN ARROW v2, Belle Starr Overdrive, and 1484 - Twin Twelve Pedal among its products.
- The official FUZZ product page identifies six interchangeable analog fuzz modules: Modern, Deluxe, Vintage, Classic, Page MK II, and Goat Head. These are recorded here as manufacturer-documented module identities, not as separate enclosure products.
- Jackson's official manuals page identifies product documentation for Prism, Bloom, Broken Arrow V2, Belle Starr, Asabi, Golden Boy, and Modular Fuzz. Only the dirt-relevant products are retained in this block.
- Bloom, Blossom, Prism, and Amp Mode are excluded where their primary identity is compression/EQ, clean boost, or other non-dirt operation rather than core overdrive, distortion, or fuzz.
- No cosmetic Custom Art Series finish is treated as a new pedal identity.
