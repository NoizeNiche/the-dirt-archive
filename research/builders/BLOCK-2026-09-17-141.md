# Block 141 — ZVEX Effects

## Scope
Builder → Pedals only. Target categories: Overdrive, Distortion, Fuzz. Non-target functions are not counted as separate dirt categories.

## Explicit overlaps
- Channel 2 = Overdrive. ZVEX's official 2019 catalog explicitly describes it as ranging from a unity-gain line buffer to a light transparent overdrive.
- Box of Rock = Distortion. ZVEX's current catalog places it in Distortion; its built-in high-headroom boost is excluded as a non-target function.
- Double Rock = Distortion. The official product page describes configurations including two Distortrons and a Box of Rock, while boost functions remain outside the census.
- '59 Sound = Distortion. ZVEX's official page describes it as a distortion pedal modeled on a 1959 Tweed Bassman.
- Basstortion = Distortion. The official page describes it as capturing tube-amp distortion for bass.
- Fuzz Factory family = Fuzz. Fuzz Factory 7, Vexter, Vertical and artwork editions are treated as the same core fuzz lineage where they are presentation or edition variants.
- Woolly Mammoth family = Fuzz. Woolly Mammoth 7 and Vexter versions remain within the same fuzz lineage.
- Machine = Fuzz. ZVEX explicitly calls it a unique fuzz; its unusual oscillator behavior does not create a separate target category.

## Variant / historical restraint
- Vexter, Vertical, hand-painted, custom-art, anniversary, engraved, and similar presentation variants are not multiplied into separate builder relationships when the underlying pedal remains the same lineage.
- Fuzz Factory 7 is retained as a named family generation, but its Vexter/custom-art presentations are consolidated.
- Woolly Mammoth 7 is retained with the Woolly Mammoth family rather than multiplying every artwork or Vexter version.
- Custom one-offs and Mod Bench listings are excluded unless they represent an established standard pedal relationship.
- The Sylvia Massy Woolly Mammoth special release remains within the Woolly Mammoth fuzz lineage.
- Super Hard On and Super Duper products are excluded because their official identity is boost; Channel 2 is included because ZVEX explicitly describes it as light overdrive in the 2019 catalog.
- Modulation, octave, sampler, tremolo, phaser, filter, meter and other non-target effects are excluded.
- No components, schematics, PCB, BOM, gutshots, cloning, or build instructions are included.

## Verification note
ZVEX's current official guitar-pedal catalog separates its products into categories including Distortion and Fuzz. The current catalog lists Box of Rock, Distortron, Double Rock, '59 Sound and Basstortion under Distortion, while listing Fuzz Factory, Fat Fuzz Factory, Woolly Mammoth, Fuzzolo, Mastotron, Octane 3, Machine and Fuzz Probe under Fuzz. ZVEX's 2019 official catalog explicitly describes Channel 2 as capable of light transparent overdrive and describes Box of Metal as a distortion pedal. These relationships form the basis of this census block.
