# The Dirt Archive — Builder Research Block 056

## Builder
**McGregor Pedals — Garth McGregor Heslop**

## Verification basis
Primary manufacturer sources used:
- McGregor Pedals official product catalog
- McGregor Pedals official individual product pages
- McGregor Pedals official press page

McGregor Pedals' current catalog identifies Crunch Transparent Overdrive, Double Crunch Transparent Overdrive, Crunch Plus Overdrive/Distortion, The Classic Fuzz, The Cozmic Fuzzball, and Octave Fuzz with Stank as dirt products. The official product pages explicitly classify the drive and fuzz identities, while the official press page corroborates the Cozmic Fuzzball as combining distortion and fuzz sounds. The manufacturer identifies Garth McGregor Heslop as the founder/designer behind the line.

## Product-level verification notes
- **Crunch Transparent Overdrive**: McGregor's official product page explicitly identifies it as a low-gain transparent overdrive for guitar and bass. It is retained under Overdrive only.
- **Double Crunch Transparent Overdrive**: The official catalog identifies this as a Double Crunch Transparent Overdrive, and the product page describes the dual-channel/three-gain-stage Crunch design. It is retained under Overdrive only.
- **Crunch Plus Overdrive/Distortion**: McGregor's official catalog explicitly labels the product "Overdrive/Distortion." It is therefore recorded in both Overdrive and Distortion.
- **The Classic Fuzz**: McGregor's official product page explicitly identifies it as a fuzz pedal and describes a modern recreation of early two-transistor fuzz behavior. It is recorded under Fuzz only, despite descriptions of distortion-like tones at some settings.
- **The Cozmic Fuzzball**: McGregor's official press page quotes Vintage Guitar describing the pedal as bringing together distortion and fuzz sounds. It is therefore recorded under both Distortion and Fuzz.
- **Octave Fuzz with Stank**: McGregor's official catalog explicitly names this as an Octave Fuzz. Because Fuzz is an explicit part of the product identity, it is recorded under Fuzz. The octave function is not treated as a separate target category.

## Scope decisions / exclusions
- **MP Reverb** is excluded because it is a reverb, not a target dirt effect.
- The archive does not promote "distortion-like" behavior into Distortion unless the manufacturer explicitly identifies the product that way. This is why The Classic Fuzz remains Fuzz only.
- Product revisions/versions are not split into duplicate identities when McGregor presents them as the same named pedal family.
- No unsupported dirt classifications are inferred from third-party descriptions when manufacturer material is available.
