# Block 069 — Lovepedal

## Builder
**Lovepedal / Love Pedal**

## Verification basis
Primary manufacturer sources reviewed:
- Lovepedal official home page and Pedals index
- Lovepedal official New Products index
- Lovepedal official History page
- individual Lovepedal product pages
- official Lovepedal press archive

The manufacturer presents Love Pedal / Lovepedal as the builder brand and currently exposes a mix of current and small-batch products alongside historical product information. This block records the Lovepedal builder relationship only. Hermida Audio products that the Lovepedal site now sells or documents are retained as Lovepedal product identities only where the Lovepedal manufacturer page itself presents them within the Lovepedal catalog; this does not create a separate Hermida Audio builder block here.

Dirt categories are based on explicit manufacturer product titles or descriptions. References to clipping, gain, saturation, or distortion as a sonic range do not automatically create an additional target category unless the manufacturer clearly identifies the product as overdrive, distortion, or fuzz.

## Overdrive
Current / manufacturer-supported identities:
- 200lbs of Gold
- Purple Plexi
- Eternity Burst
- Zendrive
- Dover Drive
- Amp Eleven
- Kalamazoo
- Super Six
- 5E3 Deluxe
- Tchula

Historical / manufacturer-supported identities:
- COT 50 / Church of Tone 50
- Eternity Overdrive
- Amp 50
- Mosferatu
- Zendrive 2
- Tiki Drive

Verification notes:
- **200lbs of Gold** is explicitly described as a modified vintage Fuzz Face feeding a TS808-style overdrive, so it is recorded as Overdrive and Fuzz.
- **Purple Plexi** is explicitly described as balanced, medium-gain overdrive through full JCM800-style roar.
- **Eternity Burst** is explicitly presented as a hand-wired Eternity Overdrive build.
- **Zendrive** is explicitly described as a dynamic overdrive and low-distortion pedal.
- **Dover Drive** is explicitly described as a wide-ranging overdrive, even though its upper range can become Fuzz Face-like.
- **Amp Eleven** is explicitly described as low-to-medium-gain overdrive with a separate boost channel.
- **Kalamazoo** is explicitly titled and described as an overdrive.
- **Super Six** exposes a Drive control and feedback-loop clipping, but the manufacturer does not explicitly label it as an Overdrive pedal on the current page; it is retained conservatively here because the product is presented as an amp-tone dirt device with dedicated Drive clipping.
- **5E3 Deluxe** explicitly says the GAIN/BIAS control selects the amount of overdrive.
- **Tchula** is explicitly described by Lovepedal as a 2-stage COT50-based “Dirt Box”; because the builder identifies its underlying COT50 circuit as Boost / Overdrive, it is retained as an Overdrive identity without adding Distortion or Fuzz.
- **COT 50 / Church of Tone 50** is explicitly called Boost / Overdrive by the Lovepedal history page.
- **Eternity Overdrive** is explicitly identified as an overdrive in the manufacturer history and press material.
- **Amp 50** is explicitly identified as Overdrive in Lovepedal's account of its 2009 Mini Line.
- **Mosferatu** is identified by the manufacturer as one of Alfonso Hermida's early overdrive pedals and is retained as an Overdrive identity.
- **Zendrive 2** is explicitly described as low-to-medium-gain tube overdrive.
- **Tiki Drive** is described as a multi-gain-stage pedal developed to reproduce overdriven and distorted sounds from clean amplifiers. It is retained in Overdrive and Distortion below.

## Distortion
Current / manufacturer-supported identities:
- Tiki Drive

Historical / manufacturer-supported identities:
- none separately promoted beyond Tiki Drive

Verification notes:
- **Tiki Drive** is the only Lovepedal identity promoted into the Distortion category in this block because the manufacturer explicitly describes its design as intended to produce overdriven/distorted sounds and discusses its multiple gain stages in those terms.
- Other Lovepedal products use phrases such as “low distortion,” “distortion amount,” or describe a transition into aggressive gain, but the manufacturer presents them as Overdrive products rather than as a separate Distortion category. They remain Overdrive only.

## Fuzz
Current / manufacturer-supported identities:
- 200lbs of Gold

Historical / manufacturer-supported identities:
- FAB 50

Verification notes:
- **200lbs of Gold** is explicitly described as a modified vintage Fuzz Face feeding a TS808-style overdrive, so it is retained as Fuzz and Overdrive.
- **FAB 50** is identified by Lovepedal's official history as a one-knob design that simulated a cranked Marshall at lower gain settings and produced a “nasty fuzz tone” at the upper end. Because the manufacturer itself explicitly describes the product's high-gain result as fuzz, it is retained as a historical Fuzz identity.
- **Dover Drive** is not added to Fuzz despite its Fuzz Face-like upper range because the manufacturer explicitly presents it as an Overdrive product.

## Category-overlap decisions
- **200lbs of Gold**: Overdrive + Fuzz. Manufacturer description explicitly combines vintage fuzz-face circuitry with TS808-style overdrive.
- **Tiki Drive**: Overdrive + Distortion. Manufacturer description explicitly discusses both overdrive and distortion as target sounds of the multi-stage design.
- **FAB 50**: Fuzz only. Manufacturer explicitly describes the upper range as fuzz but does not identify a separate Overdrive category for the product.
- **Dover Drive**: Overdrive only. Manufacturer explicitly calls it an overdrive even though it reaches Fuzz Face-like textures.
- **Zendrive**: Overdrive only. “Low distortion” is treated as a range descriptor, not a second category.
- **Eternity Burst / Eternity Overdrive**: Overdrive only. Product identity is explicitly Overdrive.
- **Amp Eleven / Amp 50**: Overdrive only. Boost functionality is outside the target taxonomy and does not create an additional category.
- **COT 50**: Overdrive only. Boost/Overdrive is retained under the target Overdrive category.
- **Tchula**: Overdrive only. Lovepedal identifies it as a COT50-derived 2-stage dirt box, but does not explicitly classify it as Distortion or Fuzz.
- **Super Six**: Overdrive only for this census. The current page presents dedicated Drive clipping in an amp-tone pedal, but does not provide a separate Distortion or Fuzz identity.

## Historical / variant handling
- Lovepedal maintains a mixture of current products, custom small-batch builds, old-school builds, and historical references. This block consolidates cosmetic, hand-wired, NOS-component, and small-batch versions when the underlying product identity is clearly the same.
- **Eternity Burst** is retained as a separate current named build because the manufacturer gives it its own product identity, while it still belongs to the broader Eternity overdrive family.
- **Zendrive 2** remains separate from Zendrive because Lovepedal gives it a distinct product name and construction.
- **COT50 Eternity Stack / COT50 Eternity Stack Dual Unit** is not promoted as a new dirt identity. It is a two-product stack of COT50 and Eternity Overdrive, and the underlying identities are already represented.
- **Hermida Audio Zendrive / Dover Drive** are retained here only because the Lovepedal manufacturer site currently presents these named products within its own catalog and product pages. No separate Hermida builder block is created from this evidence alone.
- Cosmetic finishes, old-school versus current cosmetic editions, and limited small-run batches are consolidated unless Lovepedal presents a clearly distinct functional product name.

## Exclusions
- Rubber Chicken and Hermida EPH3: non-target filter/delay products excluded.
- COT50 Eternity Stack: no new identity; constituent COT50 and Eternity are already represented.
- Boost-only functionality and clean-boost sections: excluded from target categories.
- General amp-emulation language without explicit target dirt wording is not used to force a category.
- Generic “distortion amount,” “low distortion,” gain, clipping, saturation, or fuzz-adjacent wording does not add categories unless the manufacturer explicitly identifies the product as such.
