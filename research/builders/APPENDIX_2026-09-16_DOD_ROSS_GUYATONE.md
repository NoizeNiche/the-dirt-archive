# Builder Census Appendix — DOD / Ross / Guyatone

Research-stage note. This appendix does not constitute canonical public catalog data.

## DOD

A service-manual index documents a broad historical DOD dirt-pedal roster: FX52 Classic Fuzz, FX53 Classic Tube Distortion, FX54 Attacker, FX55 Supra Distortion, FX57 Hard Rock Distortion, FX59 Thrash Master, FX66 FlashBack Fuzz, FX69 Grunge, FX70 Corrosion Distortion, FX76 Punkifier, FX91 Bass OverDrive, FX102 Mystic Blues OverDrive, FX33 Buzz Box and additional related models. The source is useful for discovery, but model-level public publication should later be cross-checked against period manufacturer catalogs or stronger first-party documentation.

Source: https://www.synthxl.com/service-manual/dod/

## Ross Electronics

Ross's current relaunch site states that the brand is rebuilding its classic line around Compressor, Distortion, Chorus, Phaser and Fuzz. For the builder census this supports Ross Distortion and Ross Fuzz as distinct dirt products. Avoid treating the current relaunch as proof of every historical Ross model until older catalogs are checked.

Source: https://rosselectronics.com/

## Guyatone

Guyatone's PS-03x family contains multiple dirt products including PS-030 Fuzz Distortion, PS-031 X-I Distortion Pro, PS-032 Z-II Overdrive Pro, PS-033 B-III Distortion for Bass, PS-040 X-II Distortion Performer and PS-041 B-V Bass Performer. A separate Guyatone index documents HD-2 Harmonic Distortion, HDm5 Hot Drive, OD-2 Overdrive, OD-X Overdrive, ODm5 Overdrive, TZ-2 The Fuzz and TZm5 Torrid Fuzz. Guyatone should remain a single builder in the census while individual family/generation relationships are deferred.

Sources:
- https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/guyatone/ps03x
- https://www.pedalboard.org/strom-liste/guyatone

## Handling Notes

- Keep different recognized builder/product identities separate.
- Do not split one builder's product merely for revisions, colorways, enclosure changes or components.
- Keep uncertain historical attribution in research until stronger evidence is available.
- No internal photos, schematics, PCB imagery or component research are required for this pass.
