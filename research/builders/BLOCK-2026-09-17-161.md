## Brimstone Audio
- BL-1 Basilisk — Distortion
- XD-1 Crossover Distortion — Distortion

The Basilisk is documented as a distortion pedal, while the XD-1 is documented as a crossover distortion design with overdrive-oriented preamp sections. The archive retains the XD-1 under its primary distortion classification. Sources: https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/brimstone and product references.

Broughton's current and archived catalogs document a large group of qualifying drive products. Locust Star spans full-range overdrive and Rat-style distortion; Sentinel combines overdrive and distortion behavior; Silver Deluxe is a JFET design capable of mild overdrive; SV-Pre provides an overdrive-capable preamp stage. The archive records only the documented dirt functions and omits boost-only, filter-only, DI, and modulation products. ## Deliberate exclusions / identity checks

- Brady Tone Electronics: reviewed catalog material did not produce a qualifying standalone dirt pedal.
- Braindead Audio Designs: reviewed material did not establish a qualifying dirt-pedal catalog.
- Bravo Audio: reviewed catalog centered on tube/utility products rather than a clearly documented dirt pedal.
- Broadcast Pro Audio: product references reviewed were utility/DI oriented, not standalone dirt pedals.
- Boredbrain Music: routing and patchbay products; no qualifying standalone dirt pedal established.
- Breg Music, Breña Effects, Brian Farmer's Farmland Effects, Briar Electrics, BrickTone, Bridge City Sound, Brockway Amplification, Broken Audio Devices, Bros, Browner Sound, Brownsville, Browntone Electronics, Bruce Bennett Music Labs, Bruno, Bryce Amps, BST, Buchla, Bucktone Audio, Buffalo Audio, Buffalo FX, BugBrand, BuGGFX Pedals, Bugio Audio, Build Your Own Clone, Built By Abel, Built By Ryan, Bulinski Pedals, Bulldog Amplification, Bullseye Pedals (by GMF), Bulwa, Bümbox Custom Audio, Bumper Crop Studio, Burford Electronics, Burkey, Burner, Burns, Burnstine Engineering, Burriss Engineering, Butcher Pedals, Butterfly Effect Pedals, Butterfly Effects, Butterworth Electronics, Buzz Electronics, Buzzing Bugs Audio Devices, Buzztone Audio Effects, BWE, Byron Amplification, BYW Audio, and Bzzt Electronics: no sufficiently clear qualifying dirt catalog was established in the material reviewed for this pass, so they are not forced into the census.

## Research notes

- Braking Train Pedals, Brantone Electronics, Breakfast Audio, BreakFuzz, Brière Pedals, Brimstone Audio, British Pedal Company, Brotech Electronics, and Broughton Audio are new canonical builder identities in this continuation.
- The pass deliberately leaves uncertain or non-dirt brands out of the census rather than padding the catalog with weak classifications.
- Broughton Audio is recorded as one builder with current and archived products unified under the same canonical identity.
