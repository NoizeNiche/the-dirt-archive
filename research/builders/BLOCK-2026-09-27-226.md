# Builder -> Pedals Research Block 226

## Scope

Historical completeness sweep for Pete Cornish's standalone dirt-pedal catalog. Dirt inclusion covers documented overdrive, distortion, crunch, fuzz, fuzz-plus, and bass-drive products. Compressors, line drivers, switching/routing utilities, chorus, echo, and other non-dirt products are excluded.

## New canonical builder

### 588 — Pete Cornish

The Pete Cornish Grey, Deluxe, and Battery-Free catalogs document the following dirt-bearing standalone products:

- CC-1 — Cornish Crunch / Overdrive
- SS-2 — Soft Sustain / Overdrive
- SS-3 — Soft Sustain / Overdrive
- G-2 — Distortion / Overdrive
- GC-1 — High Gain Crunch
- BD-1 — Bass Driver / Overdrive
- P-1 — Precision Fuzz
- P-2 — Fuzz
- NG-2 — Fuzz+
- NG-3 — Fuzz+ with Bias

## Evidence notes

- Cornish's official Grey Series and Battery-Free pages identify all ten models and their core functions.
- P-1 is explicitly connected by Pete Cornish to the original **1982 Precision Fuzz** used on David Gilmour's board.
- G-2 is documented by Cornish as a four-stage discrete transistor Class-A distortion with additional Germanium components and a high-impedance Class-A buffer.
- Aion FX's tracing journal identifies SS-3 as an update of SS-2 with an added bass-cut function and discusses the SS-2's relationship to the MXR Distortion+ family plus Cornish-specific buffering.
- Current Cornish catalog descriptions distinguish P-1, P-2, NG-2 and NG-3 as separate fuzz architectures/features rather than cosmetic editions.

## Exclusions

The same Cornish catalog also contains OC-1 compressor, LD-1/LD-3/DUAL LD-3 line-driver utilities, ST-2/NB-3/NBM-2 boost-led products, A/B/Y routing, acoustic preamp/DI, and other non-dirt equipment. These are excluded from this dirt-specific census unless a verified dirt-primary function warrants a separate record.
