## Bulinski Effect Pedals
- Gnarly Bee — Fuzz
- Filth Foundry Guitar Fuzz — Fuzz
- Hard 80 Distortion — Distortion
- Velociraptor Diode Bass Fuzz — Fuzz
- Deluxe Velociraptor Diode Bass Fuzz — Fuzz
- RC Bass Fuzz — Fuzz

Bulinski's current shop documents Gnarly Bee, Filth Foundry Guitar Fuzz, Velociraptor Diode Bass Fuzz, and Hard 80 Distortion as dirt products. The historical Deluxe Velociraptor and RC Bass Fuzz are also documented as fuzz products in the bass-effects catalog and/or Reverb records. Product revisions are not split into separate builder identities. ## Bruno
- BOD-1 — Overdrive

Bruno Guitars' official BOD-1 page identifies the pedal as an overdrive. Source: https://www.brunoguitars.com/

## Buzzing Bugs Audio Devices
- BB01 Fuzz Pre-Amp — Fuzz
- BB02 Percolator Fuzz — Fuzz
- BB04 Full Range Drive — Overdrive
- Mortal Joy - Los Campesinos! Collaboration — Overdrive
- Bolster — Fuzz

The builder's current and archived product pages document BB01 as a fuzz pre-amp, BB02 as a Percolator fuzz, BB04 as an overdrive, and Mortal Joy as a dirt/modulation pedal whose drive section is a transistor overdrive. The 2026 Bolster is explicitly a dual-channel fuzz. BB03 Lo-Fi Chorus and Stargazy are excluded as non-dirt effects. Sources: https://buzzingbugsfx.com/store/p/bb01-fuzz-pre-amp ; https://buzzingbugsfx.com/store/p/bolster ; https://buzzingbugsfx.com/store/p/mortaljoy ; https://buzzingbugsfx.com/store/p/bb04-full-range-drive ; https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/buzzingbugs/bolster

## BYW Audio
- Blacky' Blower — Distortion
- Gros Buzz — Fuzz

BYW Audio's product pages explicitly identify Blacky' Blower as a distortion pedal and Gros Buzz as a high-gain fuzz with vintage distortion characteristics. The archive records the primary named dirt families and does not treat the built-in compressor or boost function as separate dirt products. Sources: https://bywaudio.com/index.php/product/blacky-blower/ ; https://bywaudio.com/index.php/product/gros-buzz/

## BZZT Electronics
- SELEKTOR 16 — Fuzz
- SELEKTOR 16 — Overdrive

The SELEKTOR 16 is documented as a multi-transistor Fuzz Overdrive with four transistor pairs and multiple clipping/bias configurations. Sources: https://delicious-audio.com/new-pedals-bzzt-electronics-selector-16/ ; https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=i_VGuWwTwvI
