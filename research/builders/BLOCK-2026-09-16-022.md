## 1. 1981 Inventions

Builder / brand: 1981 Inventions

### Current / catalog dirt products verified from the official 1981 Inventions catalog
- DRV2 — distortion
- DRV MOD 1 — modified DRV distortion
- DRV MOD 2 — modified DRV distortion
- LVL — full-range overdrive
- Forget and Not Slow Down LVL — named LVL edition / overdrive product

Notes:
- 1981 Inventions' official About page identifies the DRV as its distortion pedal and the LVL as a later product.
- The official DRV2 page explicitly describes DRV2 as a distortion pedal and distinguishes it from the original DRV circuit.
- The official DRV MOD 1 page describes the pedal as the original DRV configuration with modifications and identifies the DRV as a distortion pedal.
- The official catalog currently lists DRV2 and DRV MOD 2, while the current site also exposes DRV MOD 1 and the LVL family.
- Forget and Not Slow Down LVL is retained as an explicitly named LVL product identity because the manufacturer presents it as a separate product listing; no deeper edition normalization is attempted here.
- No component or circuit-analysis information is being carried into the archive during this phase.

## 2. Bondi Effects

Builder / brand: Bondi Effects

### Current dirt products verified from the official Bondi catalog
- Sick As Overdrive — overdrive / clean boost
- Del Mar Overdrive mk2 — overdrive / clean boost
- Sick As High Shredroom — all-day distortion / higher-gain drive variant

### Named catalog identities and earlier versions referenced by the manufacturer
- Sick As Overdrive mk2 — overdrive
- Del Mar Overdrive mk1 — overdrive
- Sick As High Shredroom Editions — distortion-oriented named product editions

Notes:
- Bondi's official catalog currently lists Sick As Overdrive, Del Mar Overdrive mk2, and Sick As High Shredroom among its pedal products.
- The official Sick As page identifies the product as clean boost, overdrive, or a blend of the two.
- The official Del Mar mk2 page identifies clean boost, overdrive, or a blend of the two.
- The official High Shredroom page describes the product as distortion and presents it as a higher-gain development of the Sick As platform.
- Named product versions are retained only where Bondi itself presents them as named catalog products. Cosmetic B-stock listings are not treated as new pedal identities.
