# Block 135 — Death By Audio

## Scope
Builder → Pedals only. Target categories are Overdrive, Distortion, and Fuzz. Non-target effects and secondary functions are excluded. Ordinary revisions, editions, and cosmetic variants are consolidated unless the builder presents them as distinct dirt products.

## Explicit overlap handling
- **Fuzz War**: Fuzz + Distortion + Overdrive. The official product description explicitly describes its sweep from boost/overdrive through thick distortion to full fuzz.
- **Germanium Filter**: Distortion + Fuzz + Overdrive range. Official description says the gain moves from dirty boost through saturated drive and distortion to fuzz.
- **Apocalypse**: Fuzz + Distortion + Overdrive range. The official description presents multiple post-amplifier paths and explicitly describes distortion/fuzz plus cleaner overdrive/boost settings.
- **Bass War**: Fuzz + Distortion + Overdrive range. Official description explicitly describes drive, distortion, and fuzz settings.
- **Absolute Destruction**: Distortion + Fuzz. Its official description explicitly covers crushing distortion, thick fuzz, and octave-like destructive behavior. Octave behavior is not separately counted.
- **Thee Treble Overload**: Overdrive/Distortion treatment is retained as Overdrive because the official product is presented as a treble-overdrive/overload device. Its high-gain distortion capability does not create a separate distortion product entry.
- **Crossover Fuzz**: Fuzz only. Its filter/crossover/stereo/CV functions are excluded from the dirt census.

## Exclusions / restraint
- Delay, reverb, modulation, filter, vocal, and experimental pedals without a clear standalone OD/distortion/fuzz identity are excluded.
- Secondary gain behavior alone does not create an additional category unless the official builder description clearly presents the product as functioning in that dirt family.
- Do not multiply cosmetic editions or ordinary revisions into separate builder relationships.
- No photos, biographies, component inventories, schematics, PCB/BOM information, gutshots, cloning information, or later archive phases.

## Verification note
Death By Audio's current official site explicitly separates its pedal catalog into Overdrive, Distortion, and Fuzz collections, and individual product descriptions provide the basis for overlap decisions. The Fuzz War, Germanium Filter, Apocalypse, Bass War, and Absolute Destruction pages were especially useful for distinguishing primary dirt identity from secondary gain behavior.
