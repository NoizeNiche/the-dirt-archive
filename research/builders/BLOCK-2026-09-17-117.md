# The Dirt Archive — Builder Census Block 117

## Builder
**JHS Pedals**

JHS Pedals is a previously uncovered builder with a broad dirt catalog spanning Overdrive, Distortion, and Fuzz. The current official catalog separates Overdrive, Fuzz/Distortion, Preamp, and other effect families, while the official history preserves older product lineages and the Legends of Fuzz series.

## Scope result
JHS has one of the broadest dirt catalogs encountered in this fresh census. The archive records current products plus clearly documented historical/legacy lineages, while consolidating routine revisions, ordinary graphics, hand-painted editions, and other finish-level variants.

JHS's own product pages are used as the primary classification source. A pedal is not promoted into another dirt category merely because its gain control can reach a sound associated with that category. Where JHS explicitly names multiple target dirt identities, all of those identities are retained.

## Explicit category overlaps
- **Kilt V2 / Kilt 10:** Overdrive + Distortion + Fuzz. JHS explicitly describes the Kilt as a dirt box covering overdrive, distortion, and fuzz, and the Kilt 10 page gives distinct settings that move between those three target identities.
- **424 Gain Stage:** Overdrive + Distortion + Fuzz. JHS's current page titles it Preamp / Distortion / Overdrive and explicitly describes smashed-out fuzz in its gain range.
- **PackRat:** Overdrive + Distortion + Fuzz. JHS describes the RAT family as spanning overdrive through distortion and all the way to fuzz, and the PackRat is built around multiple RAT evolutions.
- **Colour Box V2 / Colour Box 10:** Overdrive + Distortion + Fuzz. JHS explicitly titles the product Preamp / EQ / Overdrive / Distortion / Fuzz / DI Box.
- **Crayon:** Distortion + Fuzz. JHS explicitly titles the current product Preamp / Distortion / Fuzz and describes compressed overdrive and fuzz as the central sounds. The archive does not separately add Overdrive because JHS does not use it as a distinct category on the product page.
- **Cheese Ball:** Distortion + Fuzz. JHS explicitly titles it Distortion / Fuzz and describes both gated fuzz and distortion sounds.
- **3 Series Distortion:** Distortion only. JHS notes that it can reach heavy overdrive and fuzz, but the product's named identity is Distortion, so those operating ranges are not promoted to separate categories.
- **3 Series Fuzz:** Fuzz only. JHS explicitly calls it a fuzz while mentioning distortion and pushed-amp overdrive as possible sounds. Those are treated as behavior inside the fuzz design.
- **All American Distortion:** Overdrive + Distortion + Fuzz historically. JHS explicitly describes the RAT-style pedal as spanning clean/grit, heavy overdrive, and compressed sustaining fuzz while naming it All American Distortion.
- **Steak and Eggs:** Overdrive within target scope. The product is an Overdrive/Compressor collaboration; the compressor half is outside the archive's dirt taxonomy.

## Historical/variant restraint
- **Morning Glory** revisions are consolidated into the Morning Glory lineage. JHS's history documents the family and rare higher-gain experiments without treating every small revision or paint variant as a separate pedal.
- **Kilt V1, Kilt V2, and Kilt 10** are consolidated as one Kilt lineage for archive identity purposes, while the current Kilt 10 and Kilt V2 are both retained as named products where the official catalog still distinguishes them.
- **PackRat White** is consolidated into the PackRat lineage rather than counted as a separate circuit identity.
- **Colour Box V2 and Colour Box 10** are consolidated as one Colour Box lineage unless later research establishes a materially different named circuit identity.
- **Muffuletta** modes are not split into multiple pedals. The product is one JHS pedal containing several analog Muff-family voices.
- **Bonsai** is retained as one product despite its multiple Tube Screamer-style modes.
- **Legends of Fuzz** products such as Bender, Smiley, Crimson, Firefly, and 4 Wheeler are retained as named fuzz products because JHS presents the series as a collection of distinct historic fuzz circuits.
- **Coyote** is retained as Fuzz only. Although it includes octave behavior, its primary identity is an octave fuzz, not a separate octave-up effect entry.
- **Double Dragon** is excluded from the dirt census because JHS presents it primarily as an analog octave device. Its octave-up section can produce distortion, but distortion is not promoted to a separate target identity.
- **DIY kits, buffers, utilities, modulation, delay, reverb, and other non-target products** remain outside scope.
- No components, schematics, cloning, PCB, BOM, or internal-build information is recorded in this block.

## Research note
JHS is particularly valuable to the archive because it documents both original designs and historically oriented reproductions. The cleanest multi-category cases are Kilt, 424 Gain Stage, PackRat, Colour Box, and All American. The archive keeps those overlaps explicit while refusing to turn ordinary high-gain behavior into duplicate dirt categories.
