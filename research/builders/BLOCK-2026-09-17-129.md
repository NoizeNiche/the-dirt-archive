# The Dirt Archive - Builder Census Block 129

## Builder
**EarthQuaker Devices (EQD)**

EarthQuaker Devices is a previously uncovered builder with a substantial documented dirt catalog spanning Overdrive, Distortion, and Fuzz. EQD's current site explicitly separates Distortion & Overdrive from Fuzz, while its Legacy catalog preserves a large historical catalog of discontinued dirt pedals. The census below follows EQD's own product/category language and consolidates ordinary reissues and variants into lineages where appropriate. citeturn0search0turn0search6turn0search1

## Scope result
This block records named EQD dirt pedals whose official current catalog, Legacy catalog, or official product/manual documentation clearly establishes Overdrive, Distortion, or Fuzz identities. Gain behavior alone is not used to create extra categories. Hybrid entries are retained where EQD explicitly describes the product as spanning multiple target dirt types.

## Research note
EQD's catalog is unusually useful for the builder census because the current site explicitly separates Distortion & Overdrive and Fuzz, while the Legacy catalog preserves the historical product identities. The conservative classification therefore follows EQD's own named categories instead of inferring new categories from clipping behavior. The clearest hybrids are Gary and Black Ash; Palisades remains Overdrive despite its fuzzier clipping voices, while Life Pedal and HalfLife remain Distortion with their boost functions excluded.
