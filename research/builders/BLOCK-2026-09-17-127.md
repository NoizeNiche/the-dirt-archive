# The Dirt Archive - Builder Census Block 127

## Builder
**Amptweaker**

Amptweaker is a previously uncovered builder with a dirt catalog spanning Overdrive, Distortion, and Fuzz. The official current catalog lists Tight Drive, Tight Rock, Tight Metal, Big Rock Pro II, and Fat Metal Pro II; historical Amptweaker documentation establishes the broader TightDrive, TightMetal, TightRock, BigRock, FatRock, TightFuzz, and BluesFuzz families. citeturn717005search0turn717005search1turn388482search6

## Scope result
This block records named Amptweaker pedals whose documented identity clearly establishes Overdrive, Distortion, or Fuzz. ## Historical / variant restraint
- **TightDrive family** remains Overdrive only. Amptweaker explicitly calls TightDrive an Overdrive pedal even though its gain control reaches heavy overdrive/distortion territory. Generic gain descriptions do not create a separate Distortion identity. citeturn804911search0turn717005search3
- **Bass TightDrive** remains part of the TightDrive Overdrive lineage despite the ability to produce thicker distortion at higher settings; the product is explicitly identified as an overdrive. citeturn717005search6turn804911search4
- **TightRock** remains Distortion only because its named product identity and manual explicitly call it a distortion pedal, even though lower-gain settings can overdrive an amp. citeturn717005search5turn117972search7
- **TightMetal** remains Distortion only. Its original product description explicitly identifies it as a distortion pedal, and the current model retains the same distortion circuitry. citeturn717005search4turn804911search1
- **FatMetal** remains Distortion only even though a review notes a broad range from medium overdrive to saturated distortion. Amptweaker's product family is explicitly a distortion design. citeturn717005search9turn770909search8

- **TightRock Jr and Performance Series Tight Rock** are consolidated into the TightRock distortion lineage. citeturn717005search1turn717005search8

## Research note
Amptweaker's catalog is especially useful because James Brown's Tight-series naming makes the builder's intended dirt boundaries unusually explicit. TightDrive is the core Overdrive family, TightMetal and TightRock are named Distortion families, BigRock is the clearest documented Overdrive + Distortion hybrid, and TightFuzz and BluesFuzz are dedicated Fuzz families. The archive does not promote generic references to lower gain, higher saturation, or distortion-like behavior into additional categories when the named product identity remains clear.
