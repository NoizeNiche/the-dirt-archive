## Research notes

- C.I.C, C14 Devices, C.Q.O., Cactus, Cajita Stompboxes, Calangary Pedals, California (by Eleca), California Valveworks, Caline, and Camuro are new canonical builder identities from this pass.
- Several brands in this opening C range are OEM/rebrand-heavy. The archive keeps the displayed brand identity when the catalog documents it as a distinct builder listing, while obvious pedal-function duplicates remain product records rather than new builders.
- Cactus Ampworks Stagecoach Audio Brake is excluded because it is an attenuator/boost utility, not a standalone dirt pedal. CajeFX's current catalog is primarily wah/restoration work and is not entered without a qualifying dirt pedal.
- Existing C builders are preserved under their canonical identities rather than duplicated: Caroline Guitar Company, Catalinbread, Chase Bliss Audio, and Crazy Tube Circuits.
- The block deliberately keeps uncertain models out rather than padding the census with speculative type assignments.
