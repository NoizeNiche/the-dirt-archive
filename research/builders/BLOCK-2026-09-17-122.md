# The Dirt Archive — Builder Census Block 122

## Builder
**Crazy Tube Circuits**

Crazy Tube Circuits is a previously uncovered builder with a broad dirt catalog spanning Overdrive, Distortion, and Fuzz. Its official pedal catalog currently groups the dirt range together, while individual product pages explicitly identify the relevant effect identities. The current catalog also includes time-based, modulation, and boost products that remain outside this dirt census.

## Scope result
This block records Crazy Tube Circuits products that the builder itself clearly identifies as Overdrive, Distortion, or Fuzz, plus historical products where authoritative documentation establishes a distinct dirt identity. The taxonomy stays conservative: a high-gain pedal is not automatically promoted into every neighboring category unless the builder explicitly describes that category or a named operating range reaches it in authoritative product documentation.

## Overdrive
- **Stardust V3** — current; explicitly described as an overdrive.
- **Crossfire** — current; includes an explicitly recreated classic overdrive section.
- **Space Charged V2** — current/legacy; tube overdrive/distortion product, so both target identities are retained.
- **Falcon** — current; explicitly designed to recreate overdriven amplifier sounds.
- **Ziggy V2** — current; explicitly described as dual overdrive/distortion.
- **Unobtanium** — current; dual overdrive product with two D-style amplifier overdrive voices.
- **Unobtanium RAW** — limited/current lineage; same dual overdrive identity as Unobtanium with altered clipping implementation.
- **Hi-Power** — current; pedal + amp-in-a-box overdrive, with the British boost side including an Overdriver high-gain setting.
- **Venus** — current; explicitly described as a tube overdrive with a range into distortion.
- **Heatseeker** — current; dual drive / amp-style product with explicit driven amp-gain function; retained as Overdrive.
- **Starlight** — historical/discontinued; builder/designer describes the gain progression as overdrive into distortion and then fuzz.
- **Triptychon** — current; its separate drive section is described as an amplifier-foundation drive rather than a typical overdrive, so it is not promoted to Overdrive.

## Distortion
- **Space Charged V2** — current/legacy; explicitly documented as overdrive/distortion.
- **Ziggy V2** — current; explicitly described as dual overdrive/distortion.
- **Motherload** — current; R side runs from overdrive through distortion.
- **Venus** — current; explicitly described as a tube overdrive with high-gain distortion in its range.
- **Starlight** — historical/discontinued; designer describes its gain progression from overdrive through distortion to fuzz.
- **Limelight** — historical/current lineage; builder describes it as a fuzz/distortion unit.

## Fuzz
- **Orama** — current; dedicated fuzz section paired with an amp-style drive section.
- **Motherload** — current; dedicated Muff-style fuzz side paired with RAT-style distortion.
- **Limelight** — historical/current lineage; explicitly described as a fuzz/distortion unit.
- **Constellation** — historical/limited; multi-mode Germanium fuzz/booster product.
- **Starlight** — historical/discontinued; builder/designer describes the gain progression all the way into fuzz.
- **Triptychon** — current; dedicated four-voice fuzz engine plus upper-octave fuzz mode.

## Explicit category overlaps
- **Space Charged V2:** Overdrive + Distortion. Contemporary authoritative cataloging describes it as a tube overdrive/distortion pedal and its original announcement says it runs from clean boost to classic-rock distortion.
- **Ziggy V2:** Overdrive + Distortion. Crazy Tube Circuits explicitly calls it a dual overdrive/distortion with separate low- and high-gain channels.
- **Motherload:** Overdrive + Distortion + Fuzz. The R circuit runs from a touch of overdrive to roaring distortion, while the M circuit is a dedicated fuzz circuit.
- **Venus:** Overdrive + Distortion. Crazy Tube Circuits explicitly presents it as a tube overdrive that reaches high-gain nasty distortion. The archive does not add Fuzz merely because the builder says the top end is within the realm of fuzz.
- **Starlight:** Overdrive + Distortion + Fuzz. In a builder interview, Christos Ntaifotis explicitly describes the pedal as starting as overdrive, moving into distortion, and then reaching fuzz.
- **Limelight:** Distortion + Fuzz. Crazy Tube Circuits explicitly calls it a fuzz/distortion unit and describes the Beam control as moving from gated fuzz into fuzz-distortion.
- **Orama:** Fuzz + amplifier drive. The fuzz section is an explicit target identity; the paired amp section is treated as amplifier drive rather than promoted to Overdrive because the builder distinguishes it from a typical overdrive.
- **Triptychon:** Fuzz plus a separate drive/boost/octave system. The dedicated fuzz identity is retained; the drive section is not promoted to Overdrive because the builder explicitly says it is not a typical overdrive.

## Historical / variant restraint
- **Stardust V2 / Stardust V3** are treated as one Stardust lineage, with the current V3 retained as the named current product. The archive does not multiply the family by routine revisions.
- **Space Charged / Space Charged V2** are treated as one named lineage, while the V2 remains identifiable as the current/legacy named version.
- **Unobtanium / Unobtanium RAW** are retained as a named standard and limited RAW lineage because Crazy Tube Circuits explicitly distinguishes the RAW edition as a separate limited product with altered clipping and voicing.
- **Constellation OC41 / OC45 / CV7003** are consolidated into the Constellation fuzz lineage rather than counted as separate circuit identities. The builder presents them as limited Germanium editions of the same multi-mode product.
- **Limelight** is retained as one fuzz/distortion identity despite its boost setting; clean boost does not become a separate target entry.
- **Motherload** remains one pedal with two dirt circuits. Its internal M and R voices are not split into separate product identities.
- **Orama** remains one product with an amp-style drive section and a distinct fuzz section. Only Fuzz is promoted from the fuzz side; the amp side remains amplifier drive.
- **Triptychon** remains one product despite its four fuzz voices and upper-octave fuzz options. Those are modes inside one named product, not separate pedals.
- **Killer V, Memphis, Cyclone, TI:ME, Sidekick, White Whale, Mirage, Mirage JR, Splash, and other modulation/time-based products** remain outside the dirt census.
- **Super Conductor** remains outside scope as a boost-first product.
- **Crossfire** is retained for its explicit overdrive side; its blackface amp side is treated as amplifier emulation rather than a second target category.
- **Unobtanium** and **Hi-Power** are not promoted to Distortion merely because their high-gain settings become more aggressive; their documented primary identity is overdrive.
- No components, schematics, cloning, PCB, BOM, or internal-build information is recorded in this block.

## Research note
Crazy Tube Circuits is especially useful to the archive because the builder has repeatedly built pedals around interacting gain stages rather than rigid single-effect boxes. Starlight provides the clearest explicit all-three dirt progression; Motherload provides a distinct Overdrive + Distortion + Fuzz architecture; and the newer Orama and Triptychon designs show the builder separating dedicated fuzz voices from amplifier-style drive.
