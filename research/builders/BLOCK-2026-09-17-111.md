# Builder → Pedals Research Block 111

## Builder
**Chase Bliss — Joel Korte / Chase Bliss team**

## Verification basis
Chase Bliss's official About page identifies Joel Korte as the founder and says the company began in 2013. The current official pedal catalog lists Brothers AM as a current product, while the legacy/current catalog pages and official manuals document older dirt-focused products including Brothers, Bliss Factory, Condor, and Preamp MKII. Official Chase Bliss product pages and manuals are the primary sources used here.

The archive follows named product identity and explicit dirt functions. Boost-only behavior, incidental clipping, and gain range are not enough by themselves to create an Overdrive, Distortion, or Fuzz entry.

## Product notes
- **Brothers AM:** Chase Bliss explicitly describes the two gain channels as selectable between boost, overdrive, or distortion. It therefore receives both Overdrive and Distortion. The added treble-booster circuit is outside the archive target categories.
- **Brothers:** the official Brothers manual describes two independent analog channels containing six circuits across boost, drive, and fuzz, with each channel able to select its own effect type. It is therefore retained historically as Overdrive/Drive + Fuzz. The builder does not document a separate Distortion identity for Brothers, so it is not promoted into Distortion.
- **Condor HiFi:** Chase Bliss explicitly describes Condor HiFi as part EQ, part filter, and part overdrive, with a dedicated drive section ranging from sparkly boost to full crunch. It remains Overdrive only. Its filter and internal modulation are outside scope.
- **Preamp MKII:** Chase Bliss describes Preamp MKII as a do-it-all pedal for drive, filtering, and fuzz, with a preamp section providing responsive breakup and a separate fuzz section with Open and Gated modes. It is retained as Overdrive + Fuzz. The official page does not establish a separate Distortion identity, so it is not listed there.
- **Bliss Factory:** the official manual identifies Bliss Factory as a two-germanium-transistor fuzz derived from the Fuzz Factory concept. The DRIVE control increases distortion within the fuzz circuit, but the named identity is Fuzz, so it remains Fuzz only.

## Explicit exclusions / restraint
- **Clean:** excluded because Chase Bliss presents it primarily as a creative compressor/limiter, even though it can be pushed toward overdrive and fuzz-like behavior. Those are operating modes of a compressor rather than the primary dirt identity.
- **Warped Vinyl, Wombtone, Gravitas, Thermae, Tonal Recall, MOOD, blooper, Habit, Dark World, CXM 1978, Generation Loss, Lossy, Onward, Big Time, and other non-target products:** excluded because their primary identities are vibrato, phaser, modulation, delay, reverb, looping, sampling, or other effects.
- **Preamp MKII distortion:** not promoted into Distortion merely from broad breakup/gain language. Chase Bliss explicitly separates the pedal's preamp/drive section from its fuzz section but does not name it as a dedicated distortion product.
- **Bliss Factory distortion wording:** not promoted into Distortion because the manual uses distortion language to describe how the fuzz DRIVE control behaves inside the fuzz circuit.
- **Brothers boost modes:** excluded from the archive's target categories because Boost is outside scope.
- **Brothers AM treble booster:** excluded as Boost rather than Overdrive/Distortion/Fuzz.
- **Condor filter/modulation sections:** excluded because they are EQ/filter/modulation functions rather than additional dirt categories.
- **Normal revisions and current/legacy handling:** current Brothers AM is kept as its own named product; older Brothers, Bliss Factory, Condor HiFi, and Preamp MKII are retained historically because Chase Bliss still documents them in its official manuals/catalog.
