# Builder -> Pedals Research Block 207

## Scope

Performed a historical product-line backfill within the existing Colorsound / Sola Sound canonical builder identity. The goal was to capture missing branded variants of the same fuzz circuit that were sold under distributor or trade names. Inclusion remains dirt-only: overdrive, distortion, and fuzz.

## Existing canonical builder expansion

### 348 — Colorsound / Sola Sound
- B&M Fuzz Unit — Fuzz
- G.B. Fuzz — Fuzz
- G.B. Fuzz Unit — Fuzz
- Pro Traffic Fuzz Unit — Fuzz
- Eurotec Black Box Fuzz Module — Fuzz
- Sola Sound Bum Fuzz Unit — Fuzz
- Colorsound Supa Wah-Fuzz-Swell — Fuzz

## Evidence / classification notes

- Historical fuzz documentation identifies the Colorsound/Sola Sound Jumbo Tone Bender circuit as being sold in multiple enclosures and under multiple distributor or trade names, including B&M Fuzz Unit, G.B. Fuzz, G.B. Fuzz Unit, Pro Traffic Fuzz Unit, and Eurotec Black Box Fuzz Module.
- The same source identifies the Sola Sound Bum Fuzz Unit as a related branded name and notes that the circuit was also used in the Colorsound Supa Wah-Fuzz-Swell.
- Effects Database's Champion page identifies Champion effects as being made by Colorsound for Barnes & Mullins and lists the B&M Champion Fuzz Unit and B&M Champion Wah Fuzz. This supports keeping the newly recovered B&M variants under the existing Colorsound / Sola Sound canonical builder rather than creating separate builder identities.
- The archive already contains B&M Champion Fuzz Unit, B&M Champion Wah Fuzz, and Sola Sound Tone Bender records, so only the missing names above are appended.

## Exclusions / non-additions

- CMI Fuzz Unit is not duplicated here because CMI already has canonical builder identity 450 and the active C census already contains CMI Fuzz Unit.
- Existing Colorsound / Sola Sound Tone Bender and Tone Bender variants were not duplicated.
- No new canonical builder identity was created in this block.
