# The Dirt Archive — Builder Census Block 118

## Builder
**Analog Man**

Analog Man / Mike Piera is a previously uncovered builder with a focused but historically significant dirt catalog. The builder's own site documents handmade Sun Face, Astro Tone, Peppermint Fuzz, Sun Lion, King Of Tone, and Prince Of Tone products, with the King Of Tone and Prince Of Tone explicitly providing both overdrive and distortion modes.

## Scope result
Analog Man has a smaller dirt lineup than some recent blocks, but it clearly spans all three target categories. This block keeps the census conservative: named dirt products and explicitly documented modes are retained, while modifications to other companies' pedals, boost-only products, and routine transistor/finish options are not counted as separate Analog Man pedal identities.

Analog Man's own product pages and manuals are the primary classification sources. A product is not promoted into another category merely because it can become heavier or fuzzier at extreme settings. The archive uses the builder's explicit product identity or explicit named operating mode.

## Explicit category overlaps
- **King Of Tone:** Overdrive + Distortion. Analog Man's current page and V4 manual explicitly provide separate OD and DISTORTION modes for each side of the pedal.
- **Prince Of Tone:** Overdrive + Distortion. Analog Man's manual explicitly lists Normal Overdrive and Distortion modes, with Boost as a third mode outside the target taxonomy.

## Historical / variant restraint
- **King Of Tone Ver. 2 / Ver. 3 / Ver. 4** are treated as one King Of Tone lineage rather than separate dirt identities. Analog Man documents the revisions as versions of the same pedal and retains the same core OD/DIST concept.
- **Prince Of Tone** remains one product despite its internal mode choices. Boost is outside the target taxonomy; OD and Distortion are retained because Analog Man explicitly names both.
- **Sun Face** transistor options such as germanium and silicon are not split into separate pedals. They are documented options within the Sun Face product family.
- **Peppermint Fuzz / Dark Peppermint Fuzz** are consolidated as one Peppermint Fuzz identity; the builder describes Dark as a related version using a different transistor source rather than a separate dirt design.
- **Sun Lion** is recorded as Fuzz because Analog Man explicitly describes it as the combination of Sun Face fuzz and Beano Boost. The Beano Boost side is outside the target taxonomy.
- **Astro Tone** remains Fuzz only. Analog Man describes it as a fuzz and says it sits somewhere between fuzz and distortion; that broader operating range is not enough to create a separate Distortion entry.
- **Sun Bender / early three-transistor Tonebender work** is not added as a separate current product because Analog Man's own Sun Lion history says those versions were stopped rather than presenting a clean, independently maintained named product listing.
- **Modified Boss, DOD, MXR, Ibanez, and other third-party pedals** are not counted as Analog Man-built product identities. Analog Man clearly performs modifications, but the archive's builder census is centered on pedals the builder makes or made as its own named products.
- **Beano Boost, Bad Bob, and other boost-first products** remain outside the dirt taxonomy.
- **Comprossor, Juicer, Chorus, delay, buffers, switchboxes, and other non-target products** remain outside scope.
- No components, schematics, cloning, PCB, BOM, or internal-build information is recorded in this block.

## Research note
Analog Man is a useful multi-category builder for the archive because the dirt lineup crosses classic fuzz families and the King Of Tone / Prince Of Tone drive family. The cleanest overlap evidence comes directly from Analog Man's own manuals: OD and DISTORTION are named modes, while Sun Face, Astro Tone, Peppermint Fuzz, and Sun Lion are explicitly presented as fuzz products.
