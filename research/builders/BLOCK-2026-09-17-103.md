# Builder → Pedals Research Block 103

## Builder
**Spaceman Effects — Spaceman Effects / Zak Martin**

## Verification basis
Spaceman Effects' official site maintains separate current and legacy equipment catalogs and directly labels individual products as overdrive or fuzz. Current and legacy product pages were used as the primary evidence. The archive keeps the builder's product identity separate from mere operating range: a fuzz that can reach overdrive or distortion is not automatically placed into those extra categories unless Spaceman presents that function as part of the product identity.

## Product notes
- **Apollo VIIb:** Spaceman explicitly presents Apollo VIIb as a Preamp / Overdrive. The current handmade PDX Custom version and Cadet version use the same named product identity and are consolidated rather than counted as separate pedals.
- **Polaris:** Spaceman explicitly identifies Polaris as a Resonant Overdrive. The current Cadet and legacy/NOS versions are treated as one Polaris identity.
- **Aphelion:** the official page explicitly calls Aphelion a Harmonic Overdrive and describes it as an overdrive pedal. It remains Overdrive only.
- **Saturn V:** the official page describes Saturn V as a tone enhancer, harmonic boost, and low-gain overdrive. It is retained under Overdrive because Spaceman explicitly identifies the pedal as an overdrive as well as a boost. The boost function itself is outside scope.
- **Sputnik I:** the current product page explicitly labels Sputnik I Silicon Fuzz. It remains Fuzz only.
- **Sputnik IIb:** the current catalog identifies Sputnik IIb as a Deluxe Germanium Fuzz. It remains Fuzz only.
- **Sputnik III:** the official catalog identifies Sputnik III as Germanium Fuzz. The legacy and Cadet presentations are consolidated into the Sputnik III identity.
- **Gemini IV:** Spaceman explicitly calls Gemini IV a Dual Fuzz Generator with two parallel fuzz circuits. It remains Fuzz only.
- **Gemini III:** the official page identifies Gemini III as a Dual Fuzz Generator. It is retained historically even though Gemini IV is its later redesigned successor.
- **Spacerocket II:** Spaceman explicitly labels this an Octave Fuzz. It remains Fuzz only.
- **Spacerocket:** the official catalog identifies the earlier Spacerocket as an Intermodulation Fuzz. It is retained as a historical Fuzz identity.
- **Rumblefuzz II:** Spaceman's own page calls it a Rumblefuzz and describes the pedal as moving from overdrive into a wall of fuzz, with overdrive, distortion, and fuzz levels of gain available. The named product identity is Fuzz, so it remains Fuzz only. Rumblefuzz II is retained separately from the original Rumblefuzz because Spaceman presents it as a revisited and expanded product with substantial control and circuit changes.
- **Rumblefuzz:** the original pedal is explicitly identified as a warm, fat fuzz and a “fuzz-stortion.” It remains Fuzz only despite its light-overdrive range.
- **Charon:** Spaceman explicitly labels Charon a Germanium-Silicon Hybrid Fuzz. The wide range from crunchy overdrive through distortion to fuzz is treated as operating range inside the fuzz identity.
- **Charon v2 Mod:** Spaceman presents Charon v2 Mod as a new take on the original circuit and explicitly labels it Ge+Si Fuzz. It is retained as a separate named product from the original Charon.
- **Titan II:** Spaceman explicitly labels Titan II a Fuzz Machine. Its page describes fuzz, distortion, and overdrive across its gain range, but these are operating ranges within a product whose named identity is fuzz. It remains Fuzz only.

## Explicit exclusions / restraint
- **Atlas III / Atlas IV:** excluded because Spaceman presents Atlas primarily as a Preamp Booster / clean boost. Although it can push an amplifier into overdrive and distortion, those are effects on the amp rather than the product's named target dirt identity.
- **Saturn VI:** excluded because the product is presented primarily as a Harmonic Booster and only crosses into overdrive when the Drive control is used. It does not receive a separate Overdrive identity in this block.
- **Voyager II:** excluded because its primary identity is Dynamic Tremolo, even though it contains built-in overdrive.
- **Mercury and other harmonic boost products:** excluded as boost-first designs.
- **Charon / Charon v2 Mod:** retained as Fuzz rather than duplicating them into Overdrive or Distortion from their gain-range descriptions.
- **Rumblefuzz / Rumblefuzz II:** retained as Fuzz only despite explicit references to overdrive and distortion levels of gain, because Spaceman's named product identity is Fuzz.
- **Sputnik, Gemini, and Spacerocket revisions:** current and historical named generations are consolidated only where Spaceman presents them as the same named product or a normal version family. Gemini III and Gemini IV remain separate named identities because Gemini IV is presented as a redesigned and expanded successor, not simply a version-number revision.
- **No standalone distortion identity:** the reviewed official current and legacy catalogs did not establish a sufficiently direct Spaceman pedal whose primary product identity is Distortion.

## Status
**Verified research block.** Spaceman Effects is a previously uncovered builder in the fresh census. Its official current and legacy material establishes a compact overdrive catalog and a broader fuzz catalog. Apollo VIIb, Polaris, Aphelion, and Saturn V are retained as Overdrive identities because Spaceman explicitly identifies them that way. Sputnik, Gemini, Spacerocket, Rumblefuzz, Charon, and Titan products are retained as Fuzz identities. No standalone Distortion pedal was established strongly enough to promote. Boost-first and modulation products remain excluded, and gain-range descriptions are not used to manufacture extra categories.
