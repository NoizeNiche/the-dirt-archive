# Builder -> Pedals Research Block 206

## Scope

Performed a multi-builder C-section pull rather than a single-builder pass. Added one new canonical builder and expanded two existing canonical builders. Inclusion remains dirt-only: overdrive, distortion, and fuzz.

## New canonical builder

### 487 — Cherry Music
- Mighty Sound M1 Distortion — Distortion
- Mighty Sound M2 Overdrive — Overdrive
- Mighty Sound M3 Fuzz — Fuzz

## Existing canonical builder expansions

### 024 — Chase Bliss Audio
- Brothers - Analog Gainstage — Distortion
- Brothers - Analog Gainstage — Fuzz
- Brothers AM — Distortion
- Brothers AM — Overdrive
- Dirt Bird — Distortion
- Bliss Factory — Fuzz
- Condor - Analog EQ / Pre / Filter — Overdrive
- Condor Hifi - Analog EQ / Pre / Filter — Overdrive

### 433 — Cave Passive Pedals
- B-Drive Grunt — Overdrive

## Evidence / classification notes

- Effects Database's Mighty Sound record links the brand directly to Cherry Music and lists M1 Distortion, M2 Overdrive, and M3 Fuzz. The archive therefore treats Cherry Music as the builder identity behind the Mighty Sound mini pedal line rather than creating a separate builder for the product-facing name.
- Chase Bliss Brothers is explicitly tagged distortion and fuzz and described as six analog boost/drive/fuzz circuits. Both dirt rows are retained.
- Chase Bliss Brothers AM is explicitly tagged distortion and overdrive and is described as a tribute to the King of Tone overdrive. Both dirt rows are retained.
- Chase Bliss Dirt Bird is explicitly tagged distortion.
- Chase Bliss Bliss Factory is explicitly tagged fuzz and described as a germanium transistor fuzz.
- Chase Bliss Condor and Condor HiFi are explicitly switchable / described as overdrive and are retained as Overdrive.
- Cave Passive Pedals' B-Drive Grunt is documented as an overdrive. The builder already has canonical identity 433, so this is an expansion rather than a duplicate.

## Exclusions / non-additions

- Cherry Music's M4 Chorus, M5 Delay, M6 Phaser, M7 Compressor, M8 Flanger, and PT-06 tuner remain outside the dirt taxonomy.
- Chase Bliss Clean remains excluded because it is a compressor despite its ability to imitate some dirt behavior.
- Chase Bliss Onward and Generation Loss remain excluded as sampler/multi-effect and tape-degradation products rather than discrete dirt pedals.
- Cave Passive Pedals' Octa-Bass and Bass Ming-Rodulator remain excluded as octave/ring-modulation effects.
- Cloak Audio Jackdaw remains deferred for Fredric Effects identity reconciliation.
- No duplicate canonical builder identities were created for Chase Bliss Audio or Cave Passive Pedals.
