# Block 081 — Marshall Amplification

## Builder
**Marshall Amplification**

## Verification basis
Primary and authoritative sources reviewed:
- Marshall official current Pedals catalog
- Marshall official individual product pages for the current Overdrive Pedal series
- Marshall official individual pages for Bluesbreaker, Guv'nor, Drivemaster, and Shredmaster Vintage Reissue pedals
- Marshall manufacturer-authored pedal-history material and official Marshall pedal-history video
- Marshall-authored / preserved Jackhammer JH-1 manual
- manufacturer-authored / preserved Bluesbreaker II BB-2 manual
- authoritative product catalog/database material for historical Guv'nor Plus GV-2
- active-repository search for “Marshall” before addition

The active repository contained no prior Marshall match before this block was added. Marshall is treated as the builder identity for Marshall-branded dirt pedals documented below. Park-branded products and other separate brand identities are not merged into Marshall merely because Marshall has historical or corporate relationships with them.

The reviewed material supports current and historical Marshall dirt identities across Overdrive, Distortion, and Fuzz. Cosmetic editions, reissue presentation, and hardware revisions are consolidated under the functional product identity unless the product is a separately named model.

## Overdrive
Current / manufacturer-supported identities:
- 1959 Overdrive Pedal
- JCM800 Overdrive Pedal
- JCM900 Overdrive Pedal
- DSL Overdrive Pedal
- JVM Overdrive Pedal
- Bluesbreaker Vintage Reissue Pedal
- Guv'nor Vintage Reissue Pedal
- Drivemaster Vintage Reissue Pedal

Historical / manufacturer-supported identities:
- Bluesbreaker II BB-2
- Guv'nor Plus GV-2
- Jackhammer JH-1

Verification notes:
- **1959 Overdrive Pedal** is explicitly titled and described by Marshall as delivering classic Plexi crunchy overdrive.
- **JCM800 Overdrive Pedal**, **JCM900 Overdrive Pedal**, **DSL Overdrive Pedal**, and **JVM Overdrive Pedal** are explicitly named Overdrive products in Marshall's current catalog. Descriptions of saturated or aggressive distortion at higher settings are treated as operating range rather than automatic secondary categories unless the product is explicitly presented as a separate target identity.
- **Bluesbreaker Vintage Reissue Pedal** is explicitly described by Marshall as a classic low-gain drive / overdrive pedal.
- **Guv'nor Vintage Reissue Pedal** is explicitly described by Marshall as a smooth overdrive / overdrive effect pedal.
- **Drivemaster Vintage Reissue Pedal** is explicitly described by Marshall as a guitar overdrive pedal with a range extending from subtle crunch upward.
- **Bluesbreaker II BB-2** is retained as Overdrive from the manufacturer-authored manual's explicit Blues-mode overdrive description. Its Boost mode is a clean boost and is not separately counted.
- **Guv'nor Plus GV-2** is retained as Overdrive from authoritative catalog/database classification and its documented expanded gain structure.
- **Jackhammer JH-1** is Overdrive + Distortion because its manufacturer-authored manual explicitly documents two operating modes named Overdrive and Distortion.

## Distortion
Current / manufacturer-supported identities:
- Drivemaster Vintage Reissue Pedal
- Shredmaster Vintage Reissue Pedal

Historical / manufacturer-supported identities:
- Guv'nor Plus GV-2
- Jackhammer JH-1

Verification notes:
- **Drivemaster Vintage Reissue Pedal** is retained as Distortion as well as Overdrive because Marshall's current pedal catalog describes it as offering “vintage overdrive and distortion,” while its product page identifies the same named pedal as an overdrive pedal spanning substantial gain.
- **Shredmaster Vintage Reissue Pedal** is explicitly presented by Marshall as a high-gain distortion pedal.
- **Guv'nor Plus GV-2** is retained as Overdrive + Distortion because authoritative historical product classification identifies it under both categories and its documented gain range extends from vintage breakup through super-saturated distortion.
- **Jackhammer JH-1** is retained as Overdrive + Distortion because the manufacturer-authored manual explicitly states that the pedal has separate Overdrive and Distortion modes.
- **Guv'nor Vintage Reissue Pedal** remains Overdrive only in this census. Marshall's current product presentation emphasizes its overdrive identity, while generic historical descriptions of “distortion” are not enough to create an additional category here.

## Fuzz
Current / manufacturer-supported identities:
- none identified

Historical / manufacturer-supported identities:
- Supa Fuzz

Verification notes:
- **Supa Fuzz** is explicitly documented by Marshall as an early Marshall fuzz pedal. Marshall's official pedal-history material identifies the Supa Fuzz as the company's first pedal and describes it as a fuzz developed in the 1960s, with production continuing into the early 1970s.
- No current Marshall-branded standalone Fuzz pedal was identified in the reviewed current catalog.

## Category-overlap decisions
- **Drivemaster Vintage Reissue Pedal**: Overdrive + Distortion. Marshall's current catalog uses both terms for this named pedal.
- **Jackhammer JH-1**: Overdrive + Distortion. The manufacturer manual explicitly gives it two modes with those names.
- **Guv'nor Plus GV-2**: Overdrive + Distortion. Historical product classification supports both categories; the product's broad gain structure does not require splitting it into multiple identities.
- **Bluesbreaker II BB-2**: Overdrive only. Boost mode is a clean boost and is excluded from the target taxonomy.
- **Guv'nor Vintage Reissue Pedal**: Overdrive only. Current Marshall product wording is explicitly centered on overdrive.
- **Shredmaster Vintage Reissue Pedal**: Distortion only. Marshall explicitly describes it as a high-gain distortion pedal.
- **Bluesbreaker Vintage Reissue Pedal**: Overdrive only. Marshall explicitly calls it a low-gain drive / overdrive pedal.
- **1959, JCM800, JCM900, DSL, and JVM Overdrive Pedals**: Overdrive only from their explicit product naming and current catalog placement. Saturated distortion language in descriptive copy is treated as tonal range rather than a second catalog identity.
- **Supa Fuzz**: Fuzz only. Historical product identity is unambiguous.

## Historical / variant handling
- Marshall's 2023 Vintage Reissue versions of the Guv'nor, Bluesbreaker, Drivemaster, and Shredmaster are consolidated with their named functional identities rather than counted as separate products from their originals.
- **Drivemaster** remains a separate product identity from **Guv'nor** even though authoritative historical sources describe the Drivemaster as the later rebranded/re-housed evolution of the Guv'nor design. They are separately named Marshall products and therefore remain separate identities in the census.
- **Guv'nor Plus GV-2**, **Bluesbreaker II BB-2**, and **Jackhammer JH-1** are kept distinct from the original black-box reissue models because they are separately named products with distinct product identities.
- Hardware revisions, finish changes, and authentic-reissue presentation do not create extra dirt identities.

## Exclusions
- Marshall Supa Wah and other wah/modulation effects: outside target taxonomy.
- Marshall clean boosts and boost-only modes: outside target taxonomy unless the named pedal also has an explicitly documented target dirt mode.
- DRP-1 Direct Recording Preamp and similar recording/preamp utilities: excluded because the reviewed evidence does not establish them as dedicated Marshall overdrive, distortion, or fuzz pedal identities.
- Park-branded effects and products from other explicitly separate brands: not merged into Marshall.
- Mark I/Mark II pedal-series names and other historical references without sufficiently clear product-level target-category evidence: not promoted into the categorized census.
- Cloning, schematics, components, internal construction, and reverse-engineering material: outside scope.
