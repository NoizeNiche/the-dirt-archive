# Block 092 — VFE Pedals

## Builder
**VFE Pedals / Peter Rutter**

## Verification basis
Primary and authoritative sources reviewed:
- VFE Pedals official current shop and product pages
- VFE Pedals official catalog/design-file index identifying the standalone pedal designs and their product-level categories
- VFE Pedals official pages for Tiamat, RUP mkII, and Killer Rabbit
- authoritative Effects Database / pedal catalog coverage for historical VFE dirt products
- active-repository search for “VFE Pedals” before addition

The active repository contained no prior VFE match before this block was added. VFE Pedals is treated as the builder identity, with Peter Rutter as the associated founder/designer. The census remains focused on standalone physical Overdrive, Distortion, and Fuzz products and does not convert modulation, delay, EQ, utility, or other non-dirt products into target identities.

VFE's own catalog explicitly separates Overdrive, Distortion, and Fuzz designs. The builder also documents several multi-function dirt pedals with more than one target category. Product-level category wording is treated as authoritative where explicit, while generic descriptions of gain or saturation do not automatically create additional categories.

## Overdrive
Current / current-catalog-supported identities:
- Tiamat

Historical / catalog-supported identities:
- Blues King
- Dragon
- Dragon Hound
- Merman
- Pale Horse
- The Scream
- Distortion3

Historical predecessor identity:
- Proto OD

Verification notes:
- **Tiamat** is explicitly described by VFE as its final/definitive overdrive design and is currently offered as a limited pre-order product.
- **Blues King** is explicitly identified in VFE's catalog as a low-gain overdrive.
- **Dragon** is explicitly identified as a dynamic overdrive.
- **Dragon Hound** is explicitly identified as a parallel OD/dist design and therefore belongs in both target categories.
- **Merman** is explicitly identified as a transparent overdrive.
- **Pale Horse** is explicitly identified as a dynamic overdrive.
- **The Scream** is explicitly identified as a classic overdrive.
- **Distortion3** is explicitly cataloged as boost/OD/dist and therefore belongs in both Overdrive and Distortion.
- **Proto OD** is retained as a historical named predecessor only because VFE documentation identifies it as the earliest form of the Pale Horse lineage. It is not treated as a cosmetic or version duplicate.

## Distortion
Current / current-catalog-supported identities:
- RUP mkII
- Killer Rabbit

Historical / catalog-supported identities:
- Alpha Dog
- Dark Horse
- Distortion3
- Dragon Hound
- Fiery Red Horse
- The Triplet
- Triumvirate

Verification notes:
- **RUP mkII** is explicitly presented by VFE as a multiband distortion pedal. Its individual bands use clipping sections derived from VFE overdrive and distortion designs, but the product-level identity is Distortion.
- **Killer Rabbit** is currently offered by VFE as a stereo distortion machine containing separate overdrive, distortion, and fuzz circuits. It therefore belongs in all three target categories.
- **Alpha Dog** is explicitly identified by VFE as a vintage distortion.
- **Dark Horse** is explicitly identified as a hard-edge distortion.
- **Distortion3** is explicitly cataloged as boost/OD/dist and therefore belongs in both Overdrive and Distortion.
- **Dragon Hound** is explicitly cataloged as parallel OD/dist and belongs in both categories.
- **Fiery Red Horse** is explicitly described by VFE as a fuzzstortion pedal with large dynamic fuzz/distortion territory. It belongs in both Fuzz and Distortion.
- **The Triplet** is a standalone physical pedal containing a dedicated Blue Clipper distortion section plus compressor and octave functions. It is recorded as Distortion only because the target taxonomy tracks the dirt section rather than secondary effects.
- **Triumvirate** is explicitly described as a multiband distortion pedal and is recorded as Distortion only.

## Fuzz
Current / current-catalog-supported identity:
- Killer Rabbit

Historical / catalog-supported identities:
- Fiery Red Horse
- Fuzz Duo
- Woodchipper

Verification notes:
- **Killer Rabbit** contains a dedicated fuzz circuit derived from VFE's latest Fiery Red Horse and is therefore a Fuzz identity as well as Distortion and Overdrive.
- **Fiery Red Horse** is explicitly described by VFE as a versatile fuzz pedal and fuzzstortion design.
- **Fuzz Duo** is explicitly identified by VFE as a germanium-silicon fuzz.
- **Woodchipper** is explicitly identified as a vintage bass octave fuzz.

## Category-overlap decisions
- **Killer Rabbit**: Overdrive + Distortion + Fuzz. VFE explicitly documents two overdrive/boost circuits, one distortion circuit, and one fuzz circuit inside the standalone pedal.
- **Distortion3**: Overdrive + Distortion. VFE's own catalog identifies it as boost/OD/dist; the boost function is secondary and no separate boost identity is created.
- **Dragon Hound**: Overdrive + Distortion. The builder explicitly identifies the design as parallel OD/dist.
- **Fiery Red Horse**: Fuzz + Distortion. VFE describes it as fuzzstortion with both saturated fuzz and dynamic fuzz/distortion tones.
- **Tiamat**: Overdrive only. VFE identifies it as an overdrive and its gain/compression controls do not justify adding a separate Distortion category.
- **RUP mkII**: Distortion only. Its gain stages include soft clipping, but the builder's product identity is multiband distortion rather than a separate OD identity.
- **The Triplet**: Distortion only. Compressor and octave sections are outside the target taxonomy.
- **Woodchipper**: Fuzz only. The octave function is secondary to the named octave-fuzz identity.

## Historical / variant handling
- **Pale Horse** versions are consolidated into one Pale Horse product identity. VFE's current catalog notes multiple versions of the design.
- **Dragon** versions are consolidated into one Dragon identity; the later Tiamat is retained as a distinct named design because VFE presents it as the final overdrive product.
- **Triumvirate** versions are consolidated into one product identity.
- **Fiery Red Horse** versions are consolidated into one product identity while preserving the named design itself.
- **Killer Rabbit** is retained as a distinct named current product rather than being reduced to its component circuits.
- No enclosure artwork, color, normal production revision, or series-format variation creates a new underlying dirt identity.

## Unpromoted / cautious leads
- **Fuzz Cocktail** appears in VFE's current “ideas I'm tinkering with” section and is not promoted because it is explicitly an idea rather than an established standalone product.
- Other VFE design-file experiments and non-pedal educational/Eurorack projects are not promoted into the dirt census.
- The broad use of “drive,” “saturation,” or “distortion” in descriptions is not enough by itself to create a second target category when VFE gives the product a specific primary taxonomy.

## Exclusions
- Blueprint, Choral Reef, Bumblebee, Mini Mu, Mobius Strip, Old School, Pinball, Rocket EQ, Springboard, Standout/Focus, Tractor Beam, White Horse, Yodeler, Klein Bottle and other non-dirt products remain outside target scope.
- Multi-band or multi-function dirt pedals remain included when they are standalone physical VFE pedal products with explicit target-category circuits.
- DIY documentation itself is not treated as a separate product identity from the assembled pedal design.
- Cloning, schematics, components, internal construction, and reverse-engineering material are outside scope.
