# Block 064 — Walrus Audio

## Builder
**Walrus Audio**

## Verification basis
Primary manufacturer sources reviewed:
- Walrus Audio About page
- current Overdrive, Fuzz, & Distortion collection
- current All Effect Pedals catalog
- current Fuzz collection
- official Product Manuals archive
- official Discontinued Pedals collection
- individual manufacturer product pages and manufacturer-authored release/demo pages for current and historical dirt identities

The manufacturer describes Walrus Audio as a guitar-effects company and organizes its catalog into an explicit “Overdrive, Fuzz, & Distortion” family. Current catalog material exposes named overdrive, distortion, and fuzz products, while the official Discontinued collection and manufacturer-authored product pages preserve older dirt identities. The manufacturer also publishes official manuals for current and discontinued dirt products.

This block records builder/product relationships only. Current products are taken from the manufacturer’s current catalog and product pages. Historical identities are retained only when manufacturer-originated pages explicitly identify the product as overdrive, distortion, or fuzz. Cosmetic editions, B-stock pages, and limited floral/Onyx/other finish variants are consolidated under the underlying product identity.

## Overdrive
Current manufacturer-supported identities:
- Voyager Preamp/Overdrive MKII
- 385 Overdrive MKII
- Ages Five-State Overdrive

Historical / discontinued manufacturer-supported identities:
- 385 Overdrive
- Warhorn Mid-Range Overdrive
- Voyager Preamp/Overdrive
- Mayflower Mid-Range Overdrive
- Messner Stage-One Overdrive
- Fundamental Series Drive
- Badwater Bass Pre-amp and D.I.

Verification notes:
- Voyager Preamp/Overdrive MKII is explicitly identified by the manufacturer as a mid-focused overdrive/preamp.
- 385 Overdrive MKII is explicitly identified as an Overdrive. The manufacturer says the MKII was a reworked version of the original 385 Overdrive, adding an A/B control scheme and a 385+ high-gain switch.
- Ages Five-State Overdrive is explicitly identified as an overdrive.
- 385 Overdrive is a distinct historical named identity documented by Walrus as the original pedal released in 2016 before the later MKII.
- Warhorn is explicitly titled Mid-Range Overdrive and is retained as Overdrive.
- Voyager is explicitly described as a gain/preamp overdrive and is retained as Overdrive.
- Mayflower is explicitly described by the manufacturer as a transparent, midrange overdrive.
- Messner is explicitly described as a low-gain transparent overdrive.
- Fundamental Series Drive is explicitly described as a versatile overdrive.
- Badwater is primarily a bass preamp/DI but the manufacturer explicitly documents an overdrive/drive section, so it is retained as an Overdrive identity rather than discarded as a non-dirt preamp.

## Distortion
Current manufacturer-supported identities:
- Iron Horse LM308 Distortion
- Eras Five-State Distortion
- Melee: Wall of Noise

Historical / discontinued manufacturer-supported identities:
- Fundamental Series Distortion

Verification notes:
- Iron Horse is explicitly titled Distortion by the manufacturer; its product description says it can be dialed toward clear overdriven sounds at lower settings, but the named product identity remains Distortion.
- Eras is explicitly identified as a high-gain distortion pedal.
- Melee: Wall of Noise is explicitly a distortion + reverb combination, with the joystick controlling the distortion amount.
- Fundamental Series Distortion is explicitly identified as a three-mode distortion pedal in the manufacturer’s discontinued archive.

## Fuzz
Current manufacturer-supported identities:
- Silt Harmonic Fuzz
- Eons Five-State Fuzz
- Janus Fuzz/Tremolo with Joystick Control
- Kangra Filter Fuzz

Historical / discontinued manufacturer-supported identities:
- Jupiter Multi-Clip Fuzz V2
- Fundamental Series Fuzz
- Contraband Fuzz

Verification notes:
- Silt Harmonic Fuzz is explicitly identified as a fuzz pedal with a tube-driven fuzz section and harmonic mode.
- Eons Five-State Fuzz is explicitly identified as a five-state fuzz.
- Janus is explicitly a Fuzz/Tremolo pedal and is retained as Fuzz; the tremolo portion does not create another target dirt identity.
- Kangra Filter Fuzz is explicitly identified by the manufacturer as an octave fuzz plus filter pedal. The underlying Kangra identity is retained despite later limited-edition finishes.
- Jupiter Multi-Clip Fuzz V2 is explicitly described as a true-bypass fuzz pedal with three clipping arrangements; the original Jupiter and V2 are consolidated under the underlying Jupiter identity rather than split into unnecessary duplicate models.
- Fundamental Series Fuzz is explicitly identified as a three-mode aggressive fuzz pedal.
- Contraband Fuzz is explicitly identified by the manufacturer as a single-knob fuzz machine.

## Category-overlap decisions
- **385 Overdrive MKII**: Overdrive only. The manufacturer says cranking the gain produces a “distortion-esque” drive, but the named product identity is Overdrive and no separate manufacturer Distortion categorization is used for the product.
- **Iron Horse LM308 Distortion**: Distortion only. The manufacturer notes that it can be tuned for clearer overdriven sounds, but its product identity is Distortion.
- **Melee: Wall of Noise**: Distortion only within the target dirt taxonomy. Reverb is an additional non-target effect, but the manufacturer explicitly identifies the dirt section as distortion.
- **Silt Harmonic Fuzz**: Fuzz only. The manufacturer describes its tube section as the drive section feeding the fuzz; generic “drive” language is not promoted into a separate Overdrive identity.
- **Janus Fuzz/Tremolo**: Fuzz only. Tremolo is excluded from the dirt taxonomy.
- **Kangra Filter Fuzz**: Fuzz only. The filter section is a secondary effect and does not create an additional target-category identity.
- **Badwater Bass Pre-amp and D.I.**: Overdrive only within the target dirt taxonomy. Compression and EQ/preamp functions are not added as dirt categories.

## Historical / variant handling
- **385 Overdrive → 385 Overdrive MKII**: retained as two distinct named product identities because Walrus explicitly describes the MKII as a later reworked version of the original 385 released in 2016.
- **Jupiter Multi-Clip Fuzz / Jupiter Multi-Clip Fuzz V2**: consolidated under the V2/currently documented Jupiter identity rather than splitting ordinary revision changes into separate base products.
- **Mayflower limited editions**: consolidated under Mayflower Mid-Range Overdrive.
- **Messner limited editions**: consolidated under Messner Stage-One Overdrive / Messner Overdrive.
- **Contraband limited editions**: consolidated under Contraband Fuzz.
- **385 floral/limited editions**: consolidated under the underlying 385 Overdrive identity or 385 Overdrive MKII identity as applicable.
- **Melee floral, Onyx, and blemished listings**: consolidated under Melee: Wall of Noise.
- **Fundamental Series B-stock listings**: not separate product identities.
- **Jupiter limited editions**: not separate product identities.

## Exclusions
- Boost-only products such as Emissary Parallel Boost and Plainsman Clean Boost: not target dirt identities.
- Compressor/EQ products such as Mira and Deep Six: not target dirt identities unless an explicit dirt identity is present.
- Delay/reverb products such as Bellwether, Fathom, Descent, Slö, and R-series units: excluded.
- Modulation products such as Julia, Lillian, Monument, and related units: excluded unless paired with an explicit target dirt identity.
- Amp/cab simulation and D.I. platforms without explicit target dirt identity: excluded.
- Cosmetic-only limited editions, B-stock listings, and artwork/finish variants: not separate builder/product identities.
