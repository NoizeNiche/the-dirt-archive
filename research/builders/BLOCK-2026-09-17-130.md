## Historical / variant restraint
- Routine version changes are consolidated into lineages where the underlying JHS product identity remains the same.
- **Morning Glory V4** is not treated as a separate core dirt design from Morning Glory.
- **Kilt V1 / V2** remain one Kilt lineage, with the current V2 carrying the explicit three-way dirt classification.
- **Notaklön** special color/splatter editions remain one Notaklön lineage rather than separate pedal identities.
- **PackRat White** remains part of the PackRat lineage rather than a separate dirt design.
- **Colour Box V1 / V2 / 10** are not multiplied here. Current Colour Box V2 is the verified three-way target-dirt entry; historical variants can be reconciled in a later historical pass if needed.
- JHS DIY kits, JHS Mods applied to other manufacturers' pedals, hand-painted versions, and non-dirt utility/modulation/time-based products are excluded from the builder-to-pedal dirt census.
- **Double Dragon** is excluded from the target dirt categories because JHS identifies it primarily as a Lo-Fi Octave Device; its upper octave can create distortion, but that does not make the product itself a standalone Distortion/Fuzz identity under the conservative builder-label rule. citeturn7search3
- No components, schematics, cloning, PCB, BOM, or internal-build information is recorded in this block.

## Research note
JHS's current catalog is unusually clean for the census because the builder maintains separate Overdrive and Fuzz / Distortion collections and gives explicit dirt labels on individual product pages. The conservative rule remains: use the builder's own product identity, preserve explicit hybrids, consolidate ordinary versions, and do not manufacture new categories from tonal capability alone.
