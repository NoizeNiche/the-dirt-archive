# Builder -> Pedals Research Block 190

## Scope

Continued the C-section Builder -> Pedals census with high-confidence alphabetic backfills and three new canonical builders. Inclusion remains dirt-only: overdrive, distortion, and fuzz. Existing builders were checked before assigning new identities. Non-dirt functions and uncertain classifications remain excluded.

## Evidence / classification notes

- Cornerstone's current product catalog lists Gladio as a double preamp, but its technical description explicitly identifies it as a dual-channel overdrive. Sparkle is explicitly a dynamic overdrive. These are therefore added as Overdrive under the existing Cornerstone Music Gear identity. Antique, Centurio, Colosseum, Imperium, Gladio SC, and Nero were already present in the census, so they are not duplicated. FlameXS is documented as a clean boost and remains excluded; Aquarium Vibe and Nucleo are non-dirt.
- Caswell Modern Electronics (CME) Shark Mouth is explicitly named and indexed as a Bass Synth Fuzz. The archive records the fuzz classification while retaining the full branded model name.
- Cyclone has a catalog set that includes DI-10 Distortion, OD-30 Overdrive, and TS-5 Tube Stack alongside chorus, compressor, delay, and flanger models. The three retained models are the dirt-bearing entries. The source places these Cyclone models in a broader OEM/rebrand family, so the archive treats Cyclone as a historical branded catalog identity and does not infer independent circuit authorship.
- Craftros Pedals Musica Rev.D is documented as an overdrive model in the reviewed record set.
- Crazybox Pedals' 2014 Effects Database update lists The Box as a Massive High Gain Distortion and The Tone Box as a Vargas Blus Band Dual Overdrive. These explicit product descriptions are sufficient for dirt classification. Smokin' Boost, Loco Boost, FlexSplitter, and Loop & Push remain outside the dirt census. Loco Drive was not added without a sufficiently explicit product classification in the reviewed evidence.

## Exclusions / non-additions

- Cornerstone FlameXS is excluded as clean boost; Aquarium Vibe and Nucleo are excluded as non-dirt.
- Cyclone chorus, compressor, delay, and flanger models are excluded.
- Crazybox boost and utility products are excluded. Loco Drive remains pending because its exact classification was not established in this pass.
- Chaser HM-3 Heavy Metal was not added because the available C-section record established the product name but did not provide enough manufacturer-level evidence to avoid confusing it with unrelated HM-3 records.
- No duplicate identities were created for Cornerstone Music Gear, Caswell Modern Electronics (CME), Collision Devices, or other existing builders.
