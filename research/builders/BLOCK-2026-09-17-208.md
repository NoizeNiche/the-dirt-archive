# Builder -> Pedals Research Block 208

## Scope

Performed a multi-builder C-section expansion across four existing canonical builders. The pull prioritized product inventories with additional dirt models not yet present in the live Scrape C census. Inclusion remains dirt-only: overdrive, distortion, and fuzz.

## Evidence / classification notes

- The Effects Database Cranetortoise catalog contains 26 products and explicitly lists FB-1 Fuzz Drive Booster, SW-1 Switching Distortion, and SW-1B Switching Distortion. The remaining unrecorded Cranetortoise products in that inventory are boosters, preamps, modulation, compressors, loop boxes, or switching utilities and remain outside the dirt taxonomy.
- The Effects Database Crews Maniac Sound catalog contains 18 products and explicitly lists Addict OD, DIS-01 Distortion, SVD-001 Classic Overdrive, and VD-38 Real Valve Drive. Products identified as preamps, mixers, buffers, modulation, or utility hardware remain excluded. Texas Tornado is retained for later classification rather than guessed.
- Effects Database's 2013 weekly overview explicitly records ColdCraft Effects Black Lime IC Fuzz, making it a direct fuzz addition rather than an inference from the builder name alone.
- Effects Database identifies Cactus MTL-5 Ultra Metal as a distortion pedal. The existing Cactus builder identity receives this row rather than a duplicate builder.

## Exclusions / non-additions

- Cranetortoise GS-1 Germanium Sound is deferred because the product name alone does not establish whether it belongs in Overdrive, Distortion, or Fuzz under the archive's current taxonomy.
- Cranetortoise booster, preamp, compressor, modulation, loop, A/B, and utility products remain excluded.
- Crews Maniac Sound Texas Tornado is deferred pending a firmer dirt classification.
- No new canonical builder identities were created in this block.
