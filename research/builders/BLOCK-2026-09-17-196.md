# Builder -> Pedals Research Block 196

## Scope

Continued the C-section Builder -> Pedals census with one new canonical builder. Inclusion remains dirt-only: overdrive, distortion, and fuzz.

## New canonical builder

### 470 — Crushsound
- Farmer's Mill — Distortion
- Farmer's Mill — Fuzz
- Farmer's Mill — Overdrive

## Evidence / classification notes

- Effects Database lists Crushsound Farmer's Mill under distortion/fuzz/overdrive and modulation, making it a dirt-bearing product under the archive taxonomy.
- The individual product record describes Farmer's Mill as a handmade analog guitar effects pedal developed in 2012 to create controlled "broken sound" textures through signal degradation, interruption, crushing, stuttering, and fragmentation.
- Because the source explicitly associates the product with all three archive dirt classes, Farmer's Mill receives one row each for Distortion, Fuzz, and Overdrive.
- Crushsound is described in a separate Effects Database interview as an original builder whose Farmer's Mill was developed from scratch, so the archive treats Crushsound as a builder identity rather than as a simple rebrand.

## Exclusions / non-additions

- No additional Crushsound dirt model is identified in the reviewed manufacturer/interview record. Farmer's Mill is treated as the sole verified catalog pedal for this block.
- Modulation aspects of Farmer's Mill are not separately cataloged because modulation is outside the archive's dirt taxonomy.
- No duplicate identity was found in the canonical builder index.
