# The Dirt Archive — Builder Census Appendix 01

Research-stage appendix. This file holds newly verified builder/product discoveries before they are folded into the main census.

## Major current/historical builders

### Electro-Harmonix
First-party EHX pages identify the company as operating since 1968 and currently list dirt products including Deluxe Big Muff Pi 2 and EHX Percolator. The archive should later expand this to the broader Big Muff, fuzz, overdrive, and distortion catalog from first-party product/archive pages.

Primary source: https://www.ehx.com/

### BOSS
BOSS's current official Distortion/Overdrive/Fuzz catalog exposes a substantial dirt family including SD-1 Super OverDrive, SD-1W, OD-3, OD-1X, OS-2 OverDrive/Distortion, BD-2 Blues Driver, BD-2W, DS-1 Distortion, DS-1W, DS-1X, JB-2 Angry Driver, MD-2 Mega Distortion, FZ-1W Fuzz and FZ-5 Fuzz. The official SD-1 page states the SD-1 debuted in 1981 and traces it to the 1977 OD-1.

Primary sources: https://www.boss.info/global/categories/effects_pedals/distortion_overdrive_fuzz/overdrive/ and https://www.boss.info/global/categories/effects_pedals/distortion_overdrive_fuzz/distortion/

## Historical builders

### Maestro
The official Maestro timeline confirms the FZ-1 Fuzz-Tone as a 1962 introduction. Effects Database's Maestro index currently includes FZ-1, FZ-1S, FZ-2 and multiple Fuzz Phazzer models. These are retained as distinct recognized model names for later catalog work, while serial-number/cosmetic variants remain out of first-pass scope.

Sources: https://www.maestroelectronics.com/en-US/Page/maestro-timeline and https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/maestro

### Sola Sound / Colorsound
Historical research confirms a large Tone Bender/Colorsound dirt family, including Tone Bender MkI, Mk1.5, MkII, MkIII, MkIV, Supa Tone Bender, Colorsound Bass Fuzz and Colorsound Tone Bender Jumbo. The first-pass archive treats Sola Sound/Colorsound as one builder identity for this catalog layer; detailed branding/manufacturing distinctions are deferred.

Sources: https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/solasound/tonebender/mk3 and https://sugareelicks.com/pedal/sola-sound-colorsound-supa-tone-bender/

### Shin-Ei
Shin-Ei's current official site documents several fuzz-oriented products including Companion FY-2, FY-6 Super Fuzz and Special Fuzz 260, plus the Psychedelic Machine combination of vibe, fuzz and octave. This is sufficient to keep Shin-Ei in the high-priority historical builder queue.

Source: https://www.shin-ei.com/

### Ace Tone
A preserved 1969 US catalog reference identifies the FM-2 Fuzz Master as an American-market Ace Tone product. Historical product references also document Fuzz Master FM-1, FM-2, FM-3 and the Twin Ace FW-1 fuzz/wah. Ace Tone therefore remains a high-priority historical builder for catalog verification.

Sources: https://www.freestompboxes.org/viewtopic.php?p=288942 and https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ace_Tone

### Rotosound
Rotosound's official history page states that its fuzz pedal was originally manufactured to James How's specifications in 1967, with a later reissue based on that historical pedal. This currently verifies at least a significant historical fuzz identity; broader Rotosound dirt catalog expansion should be checked separately rather than assumed.

Source: https://www.rotosound.com/blog/cool_timeline/a-fuzz-reprisal/

### Carlsbro
A Carlsbro pedal catalog PDF confirms the builder's effects line includes fuzz, overdrive and distortion products. Model-level extraction should be performed from the catalog before publication rather than relying on category labels alone.

Source: https://carlsbro.com/wp-content/uploads/2018/01/Carlsbro-Pedals.pdf

## Naming and verification notes

- Dallas-Arbiter Fuzz Face remains a separate builder/product identity from Jim Dunlop Fuzz Face.
- Same-builder cosmetic, enclosure and minor naming variants should not become separate first-pass products.
- Separate recognized builder/product identities should remain separate even when the product name is shared.
- Internal photos, schematics, component inventories and deep manufacturing genealogy are not part of this census pass.
- Any secondary-source discovery should be promoted to a stronger first-party or period-catalog source before being treated as final public catalog data.
