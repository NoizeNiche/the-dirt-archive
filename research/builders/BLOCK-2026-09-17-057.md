## Product-level verification notes
- **Serenity Overdrive**: documented by the historical Stomp Under Foot catalog/product record as an overdrive identity.
- **Overdrive 8-Fifty**: current Stomp Under Foot catalog presents this as an Overdrive product. It is retained under Overdrive only.
- **Full Powered Halo**: Premier Guitar's manufacturer announcement explicitly describes one side as Overdrive and the other as Distortion. It is therefore recorded in both categories.
- **Motor Honey Superdrive**: current Stomp Under Foot catalog explicitly names the product Superdrive and places it in the drive lineup. It is retained under Overdrive rather than inferred into Distortion.
- **Halo Bender**: historical product documentation identifies it as a distortion pedal. It is retained under Distortion.
- **Skinner Box**: historical product documentation identifies it as a distortion pedal based on the original RAT. It is retained under Distortion; its ability to produce lower-gain overdrive is not used to inflate the Overdrive category.
- **Guv'nor** and **Brit Box**: current Stomp Under Foot catalog identities are retained as distortion products based on their Marshall-style drive/distortion lineage and historical product documentation; they are not duplicated into Overdrive solely because their gain controls cover lower-gain sounds.
- **Divided Drive**: current catalog identity is retained under Distortion as a dedicated drive product; no additional category is inferred without explicit manufacturer wording.
- **Hellephant**: Premier Guitar explicitly identifies the Hellephant as a silicon Fuzz Face-style fuzz. It is retained under Fuzz only.
- **Dirt Preacher**: historical product coverage identifies it as a fuzz pedal, with lower-gain overdrive available at low gain. The archive keeps it under Fuzz only to avoid category inflation.
- **Pink Hellephant** is not promoted into the core census because it was a retailer-exclusive custom collaboration for Mass Street Music rather than a normal Stomp Under Foot catalog identity.
- **Tri-Dirty Booster** is excluded because the target effect is a booster rather than a standalone Overdrive/Distortion/Fuzz identity.
- Ordinary finish variants, graphic variants, vintage-mini presentations, and retailer stock listings are not split into duplicate census identities.

## Scope decisions / exclusions
- The archive follows the builder/product relationship rather than treating every retailer listing as a separate product.
- Fuzz-family products are retained under Fuzz even when their gain controls can produce overdrive or distortion-like tones.
- The archive does not infer Overdrive from the word "drive" alone when the manufacturer does not explicitly support that category.
- No unsupported historical product names are promoted merely because they appear in third-party collections.
- Non-target effects are excluded.
