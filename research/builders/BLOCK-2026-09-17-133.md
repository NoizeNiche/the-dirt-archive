## Explicit overlaps
- **Oxblood Overdrive** — Overdrive + Distortion + Fuzz capability. Keeley explicitly describes clean, distortion, and fuzz range, so the dirt census keeps it in all three target categories.
- **Mk3 Driver** — Overdrive + Fuzz. Keeley describes the pedal as an overdrive workstation ranging from clean boost through overdrive to square-wave/germanium fuzz. Distortion is present in the technical description, but the product identity is primarily full-range overdrive with a fuzz mode; keep it in Overdrive + Fuzz rather than multiplying it solely because the word distortion appears in the description.
- **Super Rodent** — Overdrive + Distortion. Officially presented as a 4-in-1 overdrive/distortion pedal combining two drive families and hybrid modes.
- **SUPER BASS** — Overdrive + Distortion. Official product page explicitly identifies both pedal types.
- **D&M Drive** — Overdrive + Distortion. Treat as one dual-drive lineage rather than separate channel products.
- **Dark Side** — Fuzz + Distortion. The fuzz section is the qualifying dirt component; delay/modulation are excluded.
- **Loomer** — Fuzz. Reverb is excluded. Despite the product being a multi-effect, its qualifying dirt identity is the wall-of-fuzz section.
- **OCTA PSI** — Fuzz + Distortion. The official page explicitly lists analog transistor fuzz and distortion alongside octave/pitch functions; pitch functions are excluded.
- **Germanium Super Phat Mod** — Overdrive + Fuzz. Keeley explicitly describes it as a combination of JFET overdrive and germanium fuzz.
- **Aria Compressor Drive** — Overdrive only. Compression is excluded even though it is an integral second half of the pedal.

## Historical / variant restraint
- Treat product revisions and normal enclosure/color changes as one pedal lineage.
- Do not create separate census entries for Custom Shop artwork, anniversary finishes, artist editions, Sweetwater colorways, or one-off cosmetic editions when the underlying pedal is the same.
- Keep Loomer as one fuzz lineage despite anniversary and artist editions.
- Keep OCTA PSI variants as one lineage unless a future source demonstrates a genuinely distinct dirt product identity rather than a modification/finish.
- Keep Oxblood and Oxblood Germanium within the same builder lineage, while noting the Germanium version only where its distinct fuzz behavior is explicitly documented.
- Do not count compressor, boost, modulation, delay/reverb, pitch/octave, or switching features as separate target categories.
- Do not infer a category from gain range alone. Use explicit builder/product descriptions.

## Verification notes
Keeley’s official dirt category currently includes products such as Tube Drive Twin Triode, Red Nectar Compressor and Overdrive, Manis Overdrive, SUPER BASS Overdrive and Distortion, and SUNS Ultravoid Fuzz. Individual official product pages were used to resolve ambiguous multi-effect and multi-mode pedals. The official descriptions for Oxblood, Mk3 Driver, Super Rodent, Fuzz Bender, Dark Side, Loomer, SUNS, OCTA PSI, and SUPER BASS provide direct evidence for the category assignments above.
