## Evidence / notes
- Caveman Audio's official site identifies the company with Skrydstrup and its long-running guitar/bass effects work; Effects Database documents the Skrydstrup R&D ODR1 as both overdrive and fuzz. ODR2 is documented as a Skrydstrup/Caveman overdrive product.
- CBC Pedals has independent catalog evidence documenting its cult fuzz, octave-fuzz, and overdrive reproductions, including The Drive, Superfuzz, FY-2 Companion, Foxx Tone Machine, Harmonic Percolator, and Ampeg Scrambler.
- Effects Database documents Cat's Eye ESP's Mista' Fuzz, Mutha' Fuzz, Hop Hed Fuzztone, and Hybrid Fuzz.
- ClinchFX's official Blue Classic page explicitly describes its drive mode as rock/blues overdrive distortion.
- Analog Man's Colorsound archive documents Colorsound vintage reissues including Tone Bender variants and Overdriver; the Overdriver is independently cataloged as overdrive.
- Concrete Sound Lab's official SLAB page describes a gain range from clean-ish through overdrive and fuzz.
- Creepy Fingers' official store documents its fuzz/distortion lineup, including Blacklight Fuzz, Crazy Creepy, The Balch, Blue Mood, Modern Sound, Shatterbox, Fuzzfreak, Creepyface, Brummie, and Hold Tight. Its official product pages document The Balch, Fuzzfreak, Creepyface, and Blacklight Fuzz; Pedal of the Day documents Fuzz Nugget; Effects Database documents Lonely Heart; independent retailer evidence documents Harakiri as fuzz.
- Critter Electronics' official site documents Model III Overdrive, CFR Klone with distortion modes, 1978R distortion, 1972 octave fuzz, and American Fuzz.

## Exclusions reviewed
- Caveman Audio boost, buffer, compressor, and DI products excluded.
- ClinchFX EP/boost-only products excluded; Blue Classic retained because its own documentation explicitly describes drive/overdrive distortion.
- Caustic FX Snowstorm was reviewed but is a noise/ring-modulation distortion hybrid rather than a conventional dirt pedal, so it is deferred rather than forced into the dirt census.
