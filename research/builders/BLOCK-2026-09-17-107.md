# Builder → Pedals Research Block 107

## Builder
**IdiotBox Effects — Atarimatt**

## Verification basis
IdiotBox Effects' official About page identifies the builder as Atarimatt and says he has made effects and noiseboxes full-time under the IdiotBox name since 2011, designing the circuits and handling the build and graphics work himself. The current official catalog has a dedicated **FUZZ/DISTORTION/OVERDRIVE** category containing a broad range of dirt products. Product-level official pages were used to determine the specific dirt identity of each included pedal.

The archive follows the builder's named product identity and distinct dirt functions. Gain range alone does not create extra categories. A product is listed in multiple target categories only when the official product description gives it distinct overdrive, distortion, or fuzz functions.

## Product notes
- **Han-Taun Overdrive:** IdiotBox explicitly calls Han-Taun an overdrive and describes it as the builder's take on the Klon Centaur. It remains Overdrive only.
- **Power Drive:** IdiotBox explicitly describes Power Drive as a boost and overdrive derived from the Colorsound Power Boost. The builder also notes that lowering its voltage can produce fuzz, but the named product identity is Overdrive, so the archive does not promote it into Fuzz from operating range alone.
- **Trench Cannon:** IdiotBox describes this as the stand-alone version of the Screamer section from No Moon and gives it DRIVE, TONE, and LEVEL controls. The builder also presents it as a Tube Screamer replacement in the product testimonials. It is retained as Overdrive.
- **Necrofier Echo Drive:** IdiotBox explicitly calls this a Tube Screamer-style overdrive running into two echo circuits. The echo section is outside scope, while the overdrive section is retained.
- **repo man:** IdiotBox explicitly describes this as an echo running into an overdrive and another echo. It is retained as Overdrive; the echo circuits are not separately counted.
- **No Moon:** IdiotBox explicitly describes No Moon as a Big Muff, Tube Screamer, and RAT in one pedal, running in parallel. It therefore has distinct Fuzz, Overdrive, and Distortion functions and is retained in all three target categories.
- **No Moon 2:** IdiotBox explicitly says No Moon 2 contains the same separate Muff/Screamer/Rat-style circuits as the original and adds flexible routing. It therefore receives the same three target categories and is kept separate because it is a distinct named product.
- **Blower Box Bass Distortion / Blower Box lineage:** IdiotBox describes the Blower Box as a bass overdrive/distortion beast, with a dedicated distortion control and a current product identity centered on bass distortion. The archive retains it under Distortion rather than creating a separate Overdrive entry from broad gain wording.
- **Blower Box Deluxe:** IdiotBox explicitly describes the Deluxe as carrying the original Blower Box sound with a tighter, mid-focused voicing and controls named for the distortion section. It remains Distortion.
- **Dungeon Master:** IdiotBox explicitly describes Dungeon Master as part Muff, part SuperFuzz, and part HM-2, with a large gain/sustain range. Because the product description gives it both fuzz and HM-2-style distortion functions, it is retained in both Fuzz and Distortion.
- **Heavy Weapon:** IdiotBox describes Heavy Weapon as two of its heaviest pedals in one enclosure: the Blower Box and Dungeon Master, each providing separate high-gain dirt sections. It is retained under Distortion. The combined design does not create a new third dirt category.
- **Death Master:** The official product page describes a filter feeding a DRIVE circuit aimed at the Celtic Frost "To Mega Therion" sound, and its product testimonials identify it as a distortion pedal that can reach lower-gain overdrive behavior. The archive keeps it under Distortion and does not promote it to Overdrive based only on the lower-gain description.
- **Trash Compactor:** IdiotBox explicitly identifies this as the stand-alone RAT section of No Moon and gives it a dedicated DISTORTION control. It is retained under Distortion.
- **Black Fortress Bass Distortion:** IdiotBox explicitly describes a main HM-2-style distortion section plus a parallel analog octave fuzz section. It therefore receives both Distortion and Fuzz.
- **-D4 Distortion Reissue:** IdiotBox explicitly calls the -D4 a straight-up distortion pedal. Its description notes that lower gain can act as overdrive, but this is treated as gain range inside the named Distortion identity, so it remains Distortion only.
- **Landphil Bass Distortion:** IdiotBox identifies this as Landphil's signature bass distortion and describes it as a modified, more tweakable Blower Box Bass Distortion. It remains Distortion.
- **Wild Kyle:** IdiotBox explicitly calls this the signature distortion for Kyle Shutt of The Sword and describes its high-gain three-band EQ. It remains Distortion only.
- **REDRUM V2:** IdiotBox explicitly describes the original Redrum as a RAT-style distortion into an HM-2-style tone stack and the V2 as a revised version with expanded controls. It remains Distortion.
- **Nihilizer:** IdiotBox identifies the NIHILIZATION side as a unique distortion with volume, tone, and an internal distortion control. The other side is a noise synth. The distortion side is sufficient for inclusion under Distortion; the product is marked as a limited historical collaboration because only 60 were made.
- **Ron Fucking Swanson Super Fuzz:** IdiotBox explicitly describes this as its take on the classic Super Fuzz circuit. It remains Fuzz only.
- **Tractor Beam:** IdiotBox explicitly describes Tractor Beam as the stand-alone Muff circuit from No Moon, with SUSTAIN, TONE, and VOLUME controls. It is retained under Fuzz.
- **Monster Fuzz:** IdiotBox explicitly calls this a souped-up Super Fuzz octave-style fuzz. The internal voltage control changes behavior, but the product remains Fuzz only.
- **Blackout:** IdiotBox explicitly describes Blackout as two Muff circuits mixed in parallel, each with its own fuzz, tone, and output controls. It remains Fuzz only.
- **DeathRay:** IdiotBox explicitly calls DeathRay a square-wave fuzz and describes it as the revamped version of its earlier Death Ray Frequency Mangler. It remains Fuzz only.
- **Static Fuzz:** IdiotBox explicitly identifies Static Fuzz as a fuzz that can move from crunchy overdriven sounds to heavily compressed textures. Those are behaviors within the fuzz identity, so it remains Fuzz only.
- **WHORES. Fuzz Freq V.2:** IdiotBox explicitly describes this as a Russian germanium transistor fuzz combined with a wah-style filter. The limited metallic-black color version is the same product identity and is not counted separately.
- **Blasteroid Fuzz:** IdiotBox explicitly calls Blasteroid a vintage fuzz based on the Earth Sounds Research Graphic Fuzz. It remains Fuzz only.
- **Cool Buzz Bass Fuzz:** IdiotBox explicitly describes Cool Buzz as a bass fuzz with fuzz, low, high, volume, and mid controls. It remains Fuzz only.
- **Lost Ark:** IdiotBox explicitly calls Lost Ark a glitchy octave fuzz using a PLL-based design. It remains Fuzz only.
- **Hobo Santa Fuzz:** IdiotBox explicitly calls this a Muff-style fuzz using old Russian germanium transistors. It remains Fuzz only.

## Explicit exclusions / restraint
- **Aquabox:** excluded because IdiotBox presents it as a Blue Box / Green Ringer octave-effects combination rather than a dedicated Overdrive, Distortion, or Fuzz product.
- **Dead Space Invader:** excluded because it is a noise/LFO effect for making non-musical sounds between songs, not a dirt pedal.
- **Orange Cycle:** excluded because it is an 8-step square-wave sequencer/noisebox, not a dirt product.
- **Mad Doctor Deluxe:** excluded because it is a tremolo/stutter effect.
- **Energizer:** excluded because IdiotBox explicitly identifies it as a clean boost.
- **Dragon Sound Tone Booster:** excluded because it is a boost product.
- **TK-421 Modification:** excluded because it is a tone/level maximizer derived from the Landphil control stage, not a named dirt product.
- **End Of Line:** excluded because it is a kill-switch/signal-control utility.
- **Bad Dreams, Bat Licker, Crypto Keeper, and other current products without clear target dirt identity:** excluded rather than guessed into the census.
- **LIMITED Mystery Effect #1 Reissue and other mystery effects:** excluded because the product identity is intentionally unspecified.
- **WHORES. Fuzz Freq Gate metallic-black version:** consolidated into WHORES. Fuzz Freq V.2 because the official page presents it as a limited colorway/run of the same Fuzz Freq product rather than a separate circuit identity.
- **Power Drive fuzz behavior:** not promoted into Fuzz because IdiotBox names it a boost/overdrive and the fuzz result comes from voltage-starving that same circuit.
- **-D4 lower-gain behavior:** not promoted into Overdrive because IdiotBox names it a distortion and describes overdrive behavior as part of its gain range.
- **Static Fuzz overdriven tone:** not promoted into Overdrive because IdiotBox names it a fuzz and describes the overdriven sound as one part of its range.
- **Version and color handling:** ordinary enclosure changes and colorway runs are consolidated unless the builder presents a distinct named product or materially distinct circuit/function.

## Status
**Verified research block.** IdiotBox Effects is a previously uncovered builder in the fresh census. The official catalog shows a substantial dirt lineup, including dedicated overdrive, distortion, and fuzz products plus several explicitly multi-function designs. The strongest hybrid case is **No Moon / No Moon 2**, which explicitly combine Muff, Screamer, and RAT-style circuits in one pedal. Dungeon Master combines Muff/SuperFuzz and HM-2-style distortion functions, while Black Fortress combines HM-2-style distortion with a parallel octave fuzz. Power Drive and other fuzz-capable gain stages are not inflated into extra categories when their named identity remains Overdrive or Distortion.
