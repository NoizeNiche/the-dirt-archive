# Builder → Pedals Research Block 042

## Builder
**IdiotBox Effects — Atarimatt**

## Verification basis
IdiotBox Effects' official About page identifies the builder as **Atarimatt**, who says he has made effects and noiseboxes under the IdiotBox name full time since 2011 and designs his own circuits and graphic layouts. The official IdiotBox catalog, individual product pages, and the manufacturer's Retired Designs page were used for the Builder → Pedals census. The classification is deliberately conservative: products are included only where IdiotBox itself identifies an overdrive, distortion, or fuzz function/circuit. Cosmetic revisions and reissues that preserve the same product identity are not split unless the manufacturer presents them as distinct products.

## Product notes
- **Dungeon Master:** official page describes it as part Muff, part SuperFuzz, and part HM-2, with substantial gain and sustain. It is retained under Fuzz.
- **Blower Box Bass Distortion:** official page calls it a bass overdrive/distortion beast and provides a dedicated Distortion control. It is retained under Distortion rather than adding a separate Overdrive identity.
- **Blower Box Deluxe:** official page describes the distortion circuit and a dedicated DIST control. It is retained under Distortion.
- **Heavy Weapon:** official page combines the Blower Box and Dungeon Master in one enclosure, with separate distortion controls and the Dungeon Master's Muff/SuperFuzz/HM-2 character. It is retained under both Distortion and Fuzz as a distinct multi-dirt pedal identity.
- **Ron Fucking Swanson Super Fuzz:** official page explicitly identifies it as IdiotBox's take on the classic Super Fuzz circuit. It is retained under Fuzz.
- **Han-Taun Overdrive:** official page explicitly calls it an overdrive and describes it as IdiotBox's take on the Klon Centaur. It is retained under Overdrive.
- **No Moon:** official page identifies a Big Muff, Tube Screamer, and RAT in one enclosure. Those correspond to fuzz, overdrive, and distortion functions respectively, so the pedal is retained in all three categories.
- **No Moon 2:** official page identifies the same basic Muff/Screamer/Rat circuit family as the original No Moon, now with individually accessible inputs/outputs and series/parallel routing. It is therefore retained in all three categories. It is a distinct product identity because IdiotBox presents it as a new product rather than merely a cosmetic revision.
- **Tractor Beam:** official page calls it the standalone version of the Muff circuit from the No Moon and explicitly describes its Muff tones. It is retained under Fuzz.
- **Trench Cannon:** official page calls it the standalone version of the Screamer section from the No Moon and gives it DRIVE, TONE, and LEVEL controls. It is retained under Overdrive.
- **Trash Compactor:** official page calls it the standalone version of the RAT section from the No Moon and explicitly provides a DISTORTION control. It is retained under Distortion.
- **Death Master:** official product page describes the high-gain drive circuit, while the product's own customer material calls it a distortion pedal. The manufacturer-facing identity is retained under Distortion; the lower-gain ability to function as overdrive is not promoted into a second category solely from testimonial language.
- **Black Fortress Bass Distortion:** official page identifies an HM-2-inspired bass distortion section plus a parallel analog octave control whose intensity is described as OCT fuzz. It is therefore retained under Distortion and Fuzz.
- **Monster Fuzz:** official page explicitly calls it a souped-up Super Fuzz octave-style fuzz. It is retained under Fuzz.
- **REDRUM V2:** official page identifies the original Redrum as a RAT-style distortion into an HM-2-style tone stack and describes the V2 as the revamped version. It is retained under Distortion.
- **Blackout:** official page identifies it as two Muff circuits running in parallel, each with its own FUZZ control. It is retained under Fuzz.
- **DeathRay:** official page explicitly calls it a square-wave fuzz and identifies it as the revamped version of the original Death Ray Frequency Mangler. It is retained under Fuzz.
- **Power Drive:** official page calls it a boost/overdrive and describes a voltage control that can produce a sputtery gated sound at low voltage. Because the manufacturer explicitly identifies the core product as overdrive, it is retained under Overdrive rather than adding Fuzz from descriptive behavior alone.
- **Aquabox:** excluded from the categorized dirt census because the official page identifies it as a Blue Box/Green Ringer octave-effect design rather than explicitly naming it as overdrive, distortion, or fuzz.
- **-D4 Distortion Reissue:** official page explicitly calls it a distortion pedal, while noting that lower-gain settings can function as overdrive. It is retained under Distortion and Overdrive because both functions are directly described by the manufacturer.
- **Landphil Bass Distortion:** official page explicitly identifies it as a signature bass distortion pedal and as a modified, more tweakable version of the Blower Box Bass Distortion. It is retained under Distortion.
- **Wild Kyle:** official page identifies it as Kyle Shutt's signature distortion pedal. It is retained under Distortion.
- **Necrofier Echo Drive:** official page identifies it as a collaboration built around a Tube Screamer-style overdrive feeding two echo circuits. It is retained under Overdrive; the echo section is not counted as dirt.
- **WHORES. Fuzz Freq V.2:** official page explicitly identifies a Russian-germanium fuzz plus a wah-style filter. It is retained under Fuzz; the clean boost control is not treated as a separate dirt identity.
- **Static Fuzz:** official page explicitly calls it a fuzz and describes its range from crunchy overdriven tube-like fuzz to compressed fuzz. It is retained under Fuzz only.
- **Blasteroid Fuzz:** official page explicitly calls it a vintage fuzz and identifies it as IdiotBox's version of the Earth Sounds Research Graphic Fuzz circuit. It is retained under Fuzz.
- **Cool Buzz Bass Fuzz:** official page explicitly calls it a bass fuzz and describes its fuzz control and tone controls. It is retained under Fuzz.
- **Hobo Santa Fuzz:** official page explicitly identifies it as a Muff-style fuzz using old Russian germanium transistors. It is retained under Fuzz.
- **Nihilizer:** official page describes the NIHILIZATION side as a unique distortion with volume, tone, and internal distortion control. It is retained under Distortion. The separate noise-synth side is not treated as a dirt identity.
- **PowerBoost (retired):** IdiotBox's Retired Designs page identifies it as its take on the Colorsound Power Boost and explicitly calls it a boost and overdrive. It is retained under Overdrive.
- **Holy Trooper Fuzz (retired):** IdiotBox's Retired Designs page identifies it as a fuzz pedal. It is retained under Fuzz.
- **Lazer Phazer (retired):** IdiotBox's Retired Designs page explicitly calls it an oscillating square-wave fuzz. It is retained under Fuzz.
- **Lazer Fuzz (retired):** IdiotBox's Retired Designs page explicitly calls it a unique square-wave filtered fuzz. It is retained under Fuzz.

## Explicit exclusions / restraint
- **Aquabox:** excluded from the three dirt categories because IdiotBox describes it as a Blue Box/Green Ringer octave-effect device without explicitly classifying it as OD, distortion, or fuzz.
- **TK421:** excluded from the categorized census because IdiotBox describes it as a maximizer circuit/boosting device rather than explicitly as overdrive, distortion, or fuzz.
- **Dragon Sound Tone Booster:** excluded as a boost-only identity.
- **Energizer:** excluded because the available catalog identity does not establish it as one of the three target dirt categories.
- **Limited Mystery Effect #1 Reissue / 2025 Mystery Effect #2:** excluded because the public product listings do not establish a stable dirt classification or enough identity information for a durable census entry.
- **Noiseboxes, modulation, delay, filter, tremolo/stutter, and other non-dirt products:** excluded from the Builder → Pedals dirt census.
- **Cosmetic/color versions:** not split into separate pedal identities unless IdiotBox presents a materially distinct circuit/product.
