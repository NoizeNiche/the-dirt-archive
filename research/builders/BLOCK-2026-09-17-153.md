## Research notes

- Automat Audio Devices currently has two documented products, Rara Avis and Triplegänger, both identified as fuzz pedals.
- AVIA SD-2 is a Soviet-era small-run Super Over Drive pedal; Effects Database identifies it as an overdrive.
- Award-Session's catalog includes JD10, JD10 MkII, JD20, and SP75 dirt-capable pedal products. The JD20 and SP75 are documented as overdrive/distortion units. JD10 and JD10 MkII are amp-emulator/direct-recording pedals with documented overdrive/distortion operation.
- Awestruck Amplification currently lists Highland and Metro as its two overdrive pedals. Highland is documented as a discrete overdrive and Metro as an organic overdrive with a hard-clipping distortion mode; the catalog identity remains Overdrive rather than creating a separate product for the mode.
- Boost-only and non-dirt products were excluded.
