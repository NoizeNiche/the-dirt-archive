## 1. KHDK Electronics

Builder / brand: KHDK Electronics

### Overdrive / Distortion
- No.1 — tube-like overdrive
- Ghoul Screamer — overdrive
- Ghoul JR — overdrive
- Dark Blood — distortion
- Totality — dual-path overdrive for guitar and bass
- Mad Monarch — high-gain dirt product
- Gojira Drive — harmonic overdrive
- Paranormal — parametric EQ overdrive
- Paranormal II — parametric EQ overdrive
- Abyss — bass overdrive
- Fallout Bass Overdrive — bass overdrive
- Night of the Living Shred — high-gain preamp / dirt product
- In Waves — Trivium metal tone shaper / dirt product
- LCFR — Nergal signature dirt product
- Deathscream — Kreator signature dirt product
- Shred II — high-gain dirt product

### Fuzz
- Scuzz Box — germanium-voiced fuzz
- Unicorn Blood — octave fuzz
- Unicorn Blood II — octave fuzz
- Ascendancy — dual dirt pedal with overdrive sections

Notes:
- KHDK's official products page currently lists multiple overdrive, distortion, fuzz, bass-drive, signature, and limited-edition products.
- The official No.1 page identifies No.1 as an overdrive covering clean boost through aggressive dirty rhythm and lead tones.
- The official Ghoul Screamer and Dark Blood product descriptions classify them as overdrive and distortion respectively; the Dark Blood manual describes lower gain as overdrive and higher gain as heavily overdriven/distorted.
- The official Totality product listing identifies it as a gritty, dual-path overdrive for guitar and bass.
- The official Gojira Drive listing identifies it as harmonic overdrive, and Paranormal/Paranormal II are explicitly described as parametric EQ overdrives.
- The official Scuzz Box listing describes it as germanium-voiced fuzz; Unicorn Blood and Unicorn Blood II are explicitly described as limited-edition octave fuzz products.
- Signature and limited-edition dirt products are retained as named catalog identities when KHDK presents them as distinct products. Non-dirt products such as compressors and coffee are excluded.
- Some preamp/amp-model products are retained where KHDK's own catalog presents them as gain-producing dirt products. No circuit or component analysis is included.

## 2. Stone Deaf FX

Builder / brand: Stone Deaf FX

### Current dirt products verified from the official Stone Deaf catalog
- PDF-2 — distortion
- PDF-1X GHOST — parametric clean boost / overdrive
- Warp Drive — classic metal / rock amp-in-a-box distortion
- Warp Drive LTD — limited-edition high-gain distortion
- Fig Fumb — Muff-style fuzz
- Fig Fumb LTD — limited-edition Muff-style fuzz
- Rise & Shine — analog octa-fuzz / tremolo / sequencer

### Additional dirt identity explicitly referenced by the manufacturer's current site
- Trashy Blonde — overdrive/distortion pedal identity referenced by Stone Deaf's expression-pedal compatibility documentation
- Kliptonite — fuzz pedal identity referenced by Stone Deaf's expression-pedal compatibility documentation

Notes:
- Stone Deaf's official current home page explicitly labels PDF-2 as “Swiss Army Knife Distortion,” PDF-1X GHOST as “Parametric Clean Boost & Overdrive,” Warp Drive as “Classic Metal / Rock Amp in a box,” Fig Fumb as “50 Shades of Muff Style fuzz,” and Rise & Shine as “3 in 1 Fuzz, Analog Octave up & Tremolo Effect.”
- The official Stone Deaf expression-pedal page names PDF-2, PDF-1X, Fig Fumb, Warp Drive, Trashy Blonde, and Kliptonite as the manufacturer's overdrives, distortions, and fuzzes. Trashy Blonde and Kliptonite are therefore preserved as builder/product relationships even though their current standalone product pages are not surfaced in the current catalog.
- Limited editions remain separate named catalog identities where Stone Deaf explicitly lists them as such; no cosmetic analysis is performed.
- Non-dirt products such as Noise Reaper and EP-1 Expression are excluded.
