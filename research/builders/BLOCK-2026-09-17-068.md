# Block 068 — Foxgear

## Builder
**Foxgear**

## Verification basis
Primary manufacturer/distributor sources reviewed:
- Fox Gear Distribution official home page
- Fox Gear Product List
- Fox Gear Manuals archive
- individual Foxgear product pages
- manufacturer-authored Foxgear product manuals

The official Fox Gear Distribution site presents Foxgear as one of its product brands and separately identifies Gurus and Baroni-Lab as other brands. The Foxgear product list explicitly names several dirt products, and the manuals archive provides manufacturer-authored documentation for the relevant models. This block therefore records the **Foxgear** builder/brand only and does not merge the separately presented Gurus or Baroni-Lab brands.

This block records builder/product relationships only. Dirt categories are based on explicit manufacturer product names, descriptions, or manuals. General references to gain, saturation, clipping, or technical THD are not used to create additional target categories unless the manufacturer clearly identifies the product as overdrive, distortion, or fuzz.

## Overdrive
Current / manufacturer-supported identities:
- Fenix
- Cream

Verification notes:
- Fenix is explicitly described by Foxgear as one of its most versatile **overdrive/distortion** pedals, covering tones from early-'70s overdrive through modern hard-rock sounds. It is therefore retained in both Overdrive and Distortion below.
- Cream is explicitly titled **Screaming Overdrive** in the Foxgear product catalog, and its manufacturer manual says its gain control sets the level of overdrive and describes the pedal as a stand-alone overdrive or amp booster.

## Distortion
Current / manufacturer-supported identities:
- Fenix
- Ryder
- Muffin
- Bass Muffin
- Sinister
- Rats

Verification notes:
- Fenix is explicitly described as an overdrive/distortion pedal by Foxgear and is retained in both target categories.
- Ryder is explicitly described in the manufacturer manual as Doug Aldrich's signature **distortion pedal** and as Foxgear's latest distortion pedal at the time of the manual.
- Muffin is explicitly titled **Gilmourish Russian Muff Distortion** in the Foxgear product list/product page.
- Bass Muffin is explicitly titled **Bass Distortion with Blend** in the Foxgear product list/product page, and its manual describes an aggressive bass-oriented muff/distortion with a distortion control.
- Sinister is explicitly titled **FET Metal Distortion** and its manufacturer manual identifies it as a powerful metal distortion pedal.
- Rats is explicitly titled **Classic Distortion** in the Foxgear product list, and the manufacturer manual archive includes RATSUSERMANUAL documentation for the product.

## Fuzz
Current / manufacturer-supported identities:
- Manic

Verification notes:
- Manic is explicitly titled **Vintage Fuzz tone** in the Foxgear product list and is described on its manufacturer page as a Vintage Fuzz Face design. It is therefore recorded as Fuzz only.

## Category-overlap decisions
- **Fenix**: Overdrive + Distortion. Manufacturer wording explicitly uses both target categories in the product description.
- **Cream**: Overdrive only. The manufacturer explicitly identifies it as an overdrive and amp booster; boost functionality does not create a second target dirt category.
- **Ryder**: Distortion only. The manufacturer explicitly calls it a distortion pedal; its Germanium clipping section and RAT foundation do not create a separate Overdrive or Fuzz identity.
- **Muffin**: Distortion only. The manufacturer names it as Russian Muff Distortion; generic saturation/fuzz-adjacent terminology is not used to add Fuzz without explicit manufacturer classification.
- **Bass Muffin**: Distortion only. The manufacturer identifies it as Bass Distortion with Blend; the blend control is a secondary clean-mix feature.
- **Sinister**: Distortion only. The manufacturer explicitly identifies it as FET Metal Distortion.
- **Rats**: Distortion only. The manufacturer product list explicitly categorizes it as Classic Distortion.
- **Manic**: Fuzz only. The manufacturer explicitly identifies it as Vintage Fuzz tone and a Vintage Fuzz Face design.

## Historical / variant handling
- The reviewed Foxgear product list and manuals archive contain the relevant Foxgear dirt identities, but they do not provide enough product-level evidence in the reviewed material to construct a reliable, separate historical/discontinued dirt catalog. This block therefore records the explicitly supported Foxgear identities presently exposed by the manufacturer sources reviewed.
- **Ryder / Rats**: retained as separate named functional products. Ryder is presented as a newer signature distortion based on the Foxgear Rats platform but with different filtering and Germanium clipping; it is not collapsed into Rats because the manufacturer gives it a separate product identity.
- **Bass Muffin / Muffin**: retained separately because Bass Muffin is a dedicated bass product with an independent product name and blend architecture.
- **Foxgear vs. Gurus vs. Baroni-Lab**: kept separate because the official site presents Gurus and Baroni-Lab under separate brand sections. Gurus products such as Sexy Drive MK II and Ninja are not merged into this Foxgear block.

## Exclusions
- Gurus products such as Sexy Drive MK II and Ninja: excluded from the Foxgear builder block because the official site presents Gurus as a separate brand.
- Baroni-Lab amplifiers and related products: excluded because Baroni-Lab is presented as a separate brand.
- QBoost and other boost-only products: excluded.
- Squeeze and other compressors: excluded.
- Chorus, flanger, tremolo, delay, reverb, echo, modulation, multi-effect, power-supply, interface, amp, and other non-target products: excluded.
- Generic references to gain, saturation, clipping, or THD are not used to expand a product into another target dirt category without explicit manufacturer support.
