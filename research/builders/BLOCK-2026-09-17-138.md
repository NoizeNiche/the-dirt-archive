# Block 138 — ProCo Sound

## Scope
Builder → Pedals only. Target categories: Overdrive, Distortion, Fuzz. Non-target functions are not counted as separate dirt categories.

## Sources
- ProCo / RAT official product information and RAT catalog material: https://www.procosound.com/
- RAT Distortion product catalog: https://www.procosound.com/
- Current RAT product listings and product descriptions were cross-checked against current dealer catalog evidence where the original ProCo catalog was difficult to index.

## Fuzz
- None identified as a distinct ProCo target-category product in the verified catalog material.

## Explicit overlaps
- SOLO = Overdrive + Distortion. ProCo describes it as an analog distortion and overdrive pedal, so both target categories are retained.
- Juggernaut / Bass RAT = Overdrive + Distortion. Its documented controls and description explicitly include adjustable overdrive behavior while its core identity is a bass distortion unit.
- RAT-family products remain Distortion even when their gain range can produce fuzz-like sounds. Fuzz is not added merely because a distortion pedal can become very saturated.

## RAT lineage / variant restraint
- The original RAT, RAT2, Turbo RAT, You Dirty RAT, FAT RAT, Lil' RAT and Deucetone RAT remain distinct named products because ProCo presented them as separate products with materially different clipping approaches, formats, or channel arrangements.
- Cosmetic revisions, enclosure revisions, anniversary colors, dealer exclusives, and other appearance-only treatments are not multiplied into separate builder relationships.
- The original Bud Box RAT and successive early enclosure versions are treated as the RAT lineage rather than separate modern product relationships unless a separately named product identity exists.
- R2DU is retained as a distinct named product because it was a rack-format dual RAT product rather than merely a cosmetic revision.
- BRAT / Roadkill is treated as one named product lineage rather than two separate pedals.
- Vintage RAT and '85 Whiteface reissues are retained as historically distinct named reissue products, while cosmetic editions of those products are not multiplied.
- Juggernaut is retained because it was a separately named bass-focused product, not merely a RAT enclosure revision.

## Category restraint
- Distortion remains the dominant RAT-family category. A RAT's ability to operate at lower gain or become extremely saturated does not automatically create an Overdrive or Fuzz relationship.
- Boost, effects-loop, clean-blend, filtering, and other utility functions are not counted as additional target categories.
- No components, schematics, PCB, BOM, gutshots, cloning, or build instructions are included.

## Verification note
Current product listings identify RAT2, Lil' RAT, Turbo RAT, You Dirty RAT, FAT RAT and Deucetone RAT as RAT distortion products, while SOLO is explicitly described as an analog distortion and overdrive pedal. Historical catalog evidence also identifies the Juggernaut as a bass distortion product with adjustable overdrive behavior. The census therefore keeps the RAT family primarily under Distortion, with explicit Overdrive overlap only where the product description supports it.
