## 1. Friedman Amplification

Builder / brand: Friedman Amplification

### Overdrive / Distortion
- BE-OD
- BE-OD Deluxe
- Dirty Shirley Pedal
- Golden Pearl
- Smallbox Overdrive Pedal
- Motor City Drive

### Fuzz
- Fuzz Fiend

Notes:
- Friedman’s current pedal catalog explicitly lists BE-OD, BE-OD Deluxe, Buxom Boost, Dirty Shirley, Golden Pearl, and Smallbox Overdrive. The official product pages identify the drive functions.
- Fuzz Fiend and Motor City Drive are retained as historical/legacy Friedman dirt identities based on manufacturer-origin documentation and contemporary reporting. Fuzz Fiend is explicitly a tube fuzz; Motor City Drive is explicitly a tube overdrive.
- Buxom Boost is excluded from the core overdrive/distortion/fuzz census because Friedman identifies it as a boost rather than one of the three target dirt classes.

## 2. Pigtronix

Builder / brand: Pigtronix

### Overdrive / Distortion
- Gamma Drive
- Philosopher’s Gold
- Disnortion

### Fuzz
- Gas Giant
- Star Eater
- Octava

Notes:
- Pigtronix’s current pedal catalog explicitly identifies Gamma Drive as an overdrive, Gas Giant as an analog jumbo fuzz, Star Eater as an analog dual-stage super jumbo fuzz, and Octava as an analog octave fuzz.
- Disnortion is retained because Pigtronix explicitly identifies it as analog fuzz + overdrive.
- Philosopher’s Gold is retained because Pigtronix identifies it as compressor + germanium distortion, making distortion an explicit part of the product identity.
- Legacy products are included only when Pigtronix’s own support/catalog material identifies them as products of the builder.
