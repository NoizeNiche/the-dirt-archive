## Evidence / classification notes

- Champion Leccy's The Fat Gett is explicitly classified as fuzz and octave fuzz. The product description identifies its fuzz section as a modified Black Russian Big Muff with additional clipping and tone-shaping changes. It was missing from the active census even though The Gett and other Champion Leccy dirt models were already present.
- CopperSound Pedals has a 12-product database catalog. Foxcatcher is explicitly overdrive plus boost, so only Overdrive is counted. Mustard Gas, Ion Warped, and Renegade are explicitly fuzz-bearing models. The current CopperSound shop also identifies Mystique as Germanium Fuzz & Boost, so the fuzz function is retained while boost is not separately cataloged.
- CKK Electronic's product page explicitly identifies Destruction Drive as high-gain distortion, Flaming Lips as US-style distortion, Honey Jar and Lunar Drive as overdrive, and Scream Drive as classic overdrive. The remaining CKK products are reverb, delay, compressor, boost, filter, phaser, chorus, or other non-dirt functions.
- Circular Time Hardware's Acetone Fuzz is explicitly tagged fuzz. The record describes it as a hand-built Fuzzmaster FM-2-based pedal with a dry blend control, and notes that the low-pass setting retains low end useful for bass while the effect itself remains a fuzz/distortion device.
- Clayton's catalog contains eight X-Treme pedals. The source explicitly names DS-160 Distortion, MT-170 Metal Driver, and OD-160 Overdrive; the other five are chorus, compressor, delay, flanger, and phaser.
- Crest Audio's sole cataloged pedal is the Fuzz Face, so it is retained as a historical branded catalog identity. The source does not provide enough evidence in this pass to assign original circuit authorship beyond the branded model record.

## Exclusions / non-additions

- Champion Leccy The Kilter is modulation and Wee Fettle Boost is boost-only; The Gett and Evil Twin dirt records were already present.
- CopperSound Gravity Bomb and Strategy Boost are excluded as boost products. Broadway Treble Booster is boost-first. Captain Hook, Casino, Daedalus, Polaris, Telegraph, and Triplegraph are non-dirt functions.
- CKK ASRC was not added because its reviewed catalog entry did not establish an explicit Fuzz/Overdrive/Distortion classification. CKK Omni Boost is boost-only; other CKK models are modulation, delay, reverb, compressor, filter, or other non-dirt functions.
- Clayton CH-170 Chorus, CM-160 Compressor, DL-180 Delay, FL-170 Flanger, and PH-170 Phaser are excluded.
- Crest Audio receives a historical branded identity only; no separate OEM maker is asserted.
