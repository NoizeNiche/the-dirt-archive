# Builder -> Pedals Research Block 178

## Scope

Continued the C-section Builder -> Pedals census with dirt-only inclusion: overdrive, distortion, fuzz. Existing builders were checked against the canonical index before creating new identities. OEM-only labels and non-dirt utility products were excluded.

## Evidence / classification notes

- Classic Audio Effects was a boutique pedal builder founded by Curt Middleton; the Stumphouse Drive is documented as a medium-gain overdrive with a roller-wheel gain control.
- Comet Effects was a UK boutique pedal company; MusicRadar documented the Hyperdrive as an overdrive.
- Comodoro Pedales is an active Spanish pedal builder. Botfly is a hand-assembled fuzz with an auxiliary booster; Percolator Fuzz is a germanium Harmonic Percolator-derived fuzz; RAT BASS is a modified Rat-style distortion for bass and guitar.
- Crucial Audio's Das Götterdämmerung is a dedicated Germanium Fuzz / Ring Modulator. Its manual documents a Germanium Fuzz section and a saturation control that sets distortion, supporting both Fuzz and Distortion records.
- Custom Analog Pedals' Purple Fuzzy Drive is documented as an aggressive mid-gain drive with fuzz-like clipping and an overdrive range, supporting both Fuzz and Overdrive records.

## Exclusions / non-additions

- Crysta Professional DS-1 / OD-1 was not created as a separate canonical builder because the available evidence links the models to the Rocktek OEM platform; the same circuit family is already represented through its underlying manufacturer lineage.
- Cyclone DI-10 was not created as a separate canonical builder because Effects Database identifies it as one of several rebrands of the same OEM DI-10 distortion platform.
- CSL Super Fuzz, CSR Metal Rocker, and related labels were not split from Colorsound / Sola Sound where the historical evidence identifies them as rebranded Colorsound-family products.
- Clouds Hill FX LAUT was excluded as boost-first despite its ability to induce valve harmonics and distortion.
- Crucial Audio's WahVelope was excluded because it is fundamentally an auto-wah/envelope-filter product with overdrive available at extreme gain settings, rather than a dirt-first pedal.
