# Builder → Pedals Research Block 098

## Builder
**EarthQuaker Devices — Jamie Stillman / EarthQuaker Devices**

## Verification basis
EarthQuaker Devices' official site separates its pedal catalog into Distortion & Overdrive, Fuzz, and other effect families, and identifies the company as a hand-built pedal maker in Akron, Ohio. The current catalog and official Legacy pages were used as the main source for the dirt census. EarthQuaker's own product pages and articles were also used to verify older products that are no longer in production or no longer prominently listed in the main catalog.

The archive keeps product identity separate from mere operating range. A fuzz that can reach overdrive territory is not automatically counted as Overdrive, and a distortion pedal that contains an octave-fuzz stage is not automatically counted as Fuzz. A second category is retained where EarthQuaker itself explicitly describes the product as having that target dirt identity.

## Product notes
- **Gary:** EarthQuaker explicitly names Gary as a pulse-width-modulated fuzz paired with a natural overdrive. It is therefore retained in both Fuzz and Overdrive. The overdrive side can move from clean boost through distortion, but the separate fuzz and overdrive identities are the manufacturer's own description.
- **Black Ash:** EarthQuaker explicitly describes Black Ash as a fuzz/overdrive. It is retained in both categories despite its overall fuzz-oriented identity.
- **Bellows:** the manufacturer calls it a Fuzz Driver and describes a range from amp-like grit through fuzzy saturation and distorted sounds. It remains Fuzz only because the named product identity is a fuzz/driver rather than a separately identified overdrive product.
- **Chelsea:** EarthQuaker labels Chelsea as a Low End Fuzz Driver and explicitly calls it the chosen bass fuzz for several LCD Soundsystem recordings. Its light-overdrive-to-distortion range is treated as behavior inside the fuzz identity, not a second category.
- **Hoof:** EarthQuaker describes the Hoof as a Muff-style fuzz that can produce warm, gritty overdrive at lower gain. It remains Fuzz only because the product identity is explicitly fuzz.
- **Hoof Reaper:** EarthQuaker describes the pedal as combining the Hoof and Tone Reaper fuzzes with an octave-up section. It remains Fuzz only.
- **Tone Reaper:** EarthQuaker's official Hoof Reaper page explicitly identifies the Tone Reaper as a vintage-style fuzz device. It is retained as a historical standalone fuzz identity.
- **Dream Crusher:** EarthQuaker's official Spires page explicitly refers to the discontinued Dream Crusher as a two-transistor fuzz. It is retained as a historical fuzz identity.
- **Spires:** EarthQuaker explicitly describes both channels as fuzz, one based on a Rosac Nu-Fuzz and the other on the discontinued Dream Crusher. It remains Fuzz only.
- **Fuzz Master General:** EarthQuaker's Legacy catalog identifies it as an Octave Fuzz Blaster. The Legacy Reissue is consolidated with the underlying Fuzz Master General identity.
- **Dirt Transmitter:** EarthQuaker identifies it as a Fuzz Driver. The Legacy Reissue is consolidated with the same underlying pedal identity.
- **Rancho de la Luna Dirt Transmitter:** the official Legacy catalog lists this as a Fuzz Driver and treats it as its own named collaboration/product identity, so it is retained separately from the standard Dirt Transmitter.
- **Cloven Hoof:** the official Legacy catalog identifies it as a Fuzz Grinder. It remains Fuzz only.
- **Erupter:** EarthQuaker's Legacy catalog identifies it as Ultimate Fuzz Tone. It remains Fuzz only.
- **Park Fuzz Sound:** the Legacy catalog identifies it as Vintage Germanium Fuzz Tone. It remains Fuzz only.
- **Sound Shank:** the Legacy catalog identifies it as Germanium Fuzz. It remains Fuzz only.
- **Terminal:** EarthQuaker explicitly calls Terminal a Destructive Fuzz Device and describes its fuzz control and fuzz range. It remains Fuzz only.
- **Acapulco Gold:** EarthQuaker classifies it as Power Amp Distortion. Its ability to become very aggressive does not create a separate Fuzz identity.
- **Sunn O))) Life Pedal:** EarthQuaker's current product page describes the product identity as a distortion with a blendable octave-up and booster. The internal octave-fuzz-inspired stage is not counted separately as Fuzz.
- **Sunn O))) HalfLife:** EarthQuaker explicitly identifies it as an Octave Distortion + Booster. It remains Distortion only.
- **Gray Channel:** EarthQuaker describes it as two channels of classic overdrive. Clipping settings can reach biting distortion, but the named product identity remains Overdrive.
- **Palisades:** EarthQuaker explicitly calls it an 808-style overdrive and a Mega Ultimate Overdrive. Its clipping options can become more distorted, but it remains Overdrive only.
- **Dunes:** EarthQuaker describes Dunes as a condensed take on the Palisades 808-style overdrive and calls it a transparent overdrive. It remains Overdrive only.
- **White Light:** EarthQuaker's Legacy Reissue page explicitly labels it Overdrive. It remains Overdrive only.
- **Monarch:** EarthQuaker's own published material identifies the discontinued Monarch as an overdrive. It is retained as a historical Overdrive identity.
- **Speaker Cranker:** the official Legacy catalog explicitly labels it Overdrive.
- **Talons:** the official Legacy catalog explicitly labels it High Gain Overdrive.
- **Westwood:** EarthQuaker's official material describes Westwood as a light-to-medium gain drive and the product identity as the Translucent Drive Manipulator. It remains Overdrive only.

## Explicit exclusions / restraint
- **Life Pedal octave fuzz stage:** not separately counted as Fuzz because EarthQuaker's product identity is explicitly a distortion with octave and boost functions.
- **Tentacle:** excluded because the standalone product identity is analog octave up, not one of the three target dirt categories.
- **Arrows, Bows, Tone Job, ZE​QD-Pre, and other boost/preamp/EQ products:** excluded because their primary identities are outside Overdrive, Distortion, and Fuzz even where they can push an amplifier harder.
- **Bit Commander, Data Corrupter, Pitch Bay, Rainbow Machine, The Wave Transformer, and other pitch/synth products:** excluded because their primary identities are octave, synth, pitch, or related effects rather than standalone dirt.
- **Modulation, delay, reverb, compressor, routing, and utility products:** excluded.
- **Version handling:** normal revisions and legacy reissues are consolidated when EarthQuaker presents them as the same underlying product identity. Life Pedal V2 and the original Life Pedal are treated as one product identity for the census.
- **Gain-range inflation:** products described as reaching overdrive or distortion territory remain in their primary EarthQuaker category unless the manufacturer explicitly gives them a second standalone target identity.

## Status
**Verified research block.** EarthQuaker Devices is a previously uncovered builder in the fresh census. The current and official Legacy catalogs establish a broad dirt history across Overdrive, Distortion, and Fuzz, with explicit two-category overlaps for Gary and Black Ash. The census deliberately keeps fuzz products from becoming extra overdrive/distortion entries simply because their gain controls reach those sounds, while the Life Pedal remains Distortion despite its octave-fuzz-inspired internal stage. Historical identities such as Monarch, Tone Reaper, Dream Crusher, and older EarthQuaker fuzz/drive products are retained where official EarthQuaker material directly establishes their product identity.
