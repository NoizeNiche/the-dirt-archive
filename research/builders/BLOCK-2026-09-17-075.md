# Block 075 — Voodoo Lab

## Builder
**Voodoo Lab**

## Verification basis
Primary and authoritative sources reviewed:
- Voodoo Lab official Effects catalog
- Voodoo Lab official product pages
- Voodoo Lab official manuals/download archive
- Voodoo Lab official Legacy product index
- Voodoo Lab official FAQ and manufacturer-authored manuals

The active repository contained no prior Voodoo Lab match before this block was added. Voodoo Lab is treated as the builder identity for the pedal products listed under the Voodoo Lab brand.

The reviewed manufacturer material supports five clearly documented dirt identities: GIGGITY, Sparkle Drive, Sparkle Drive MOD, Superfuzz, and Proctavia. Dirt categories are assigned from explicit manufacturer product descriptions or manufacturer category wording. Generic references to grit, saturation, or distortion-like behavior are not used to create unsupported secondary identities.

## Overdrive
Current / manufacturer-supported identities:
- GIGGITY
- Sparkle Drive MOD

Historical / manufacturer-supported identities:
- Sparkle Drive

Verification notes:
- **GIGGITY** is explicitly presented by Voodoo Lab as an Analog Overdrive + Preamp and is retained as Overdrive.
- **Sparkle Drive MOD** is explicitly described as carrying all the original Sparkle Drive overdrive tones and is retained as Overdrive. Voodoo Lab also explicitly calls its four selectable circuits overdrive/distortion circuits, so it receives both Overdrive and Distortion below.
- **Sparkle Drive** is explicitly described as an overdrive pedal with a vintage-808-style overdrive circuit and is retained as historical Overdrive.

## Distortion
Current / manufacturer-supported identities:
- Sparkle Drive MOD

Historical / manufacturer-supported identities:
- none

Verification notes:
- **Sparkle Drive MOD** receives the Distortion category because Voodoo Lab's official product page explicitly describes its four selectable modes as "overdrive/distortion circuits." This is product-level category evidence rather than an inference from high-gain behavior alone.
- **GIGGITY** reaches gritty and compressed tones as its Loudness control is increased, but Voodoo Lab identifies the product as Overdrive + Preamp rather than a separate Distortion pedal. It therefore remains Overdrive only for this census.
- **Sparkle Drive** is retained as Overdrive only because the manufacturer identifies its effect as an overdrive circuit; its ability to increase saturation does not create a separate Distortion identity.

## Fuzz
Current / manufacturer-supported identities:
- Superfuzz

Historical / manufacturer-supported identities:
- Proctavia

Verification notes:
- **Superfuzz** is explicitly documented by Voodoo Lab as an analog circuit for classic 1960s fuzz sounds and is retained as Fuzz.
- **Proctavia** is explicitly documented as a fuzz/octave pedal and as an exact replica of the Tycobrahe Octavia. It is retained as Fuzz only. The manufacturer's references to outrageous distortion describe its sound, but the named product identity is the octave-fuzz effect rather than a separate Distortion product.

## Category-overlap decisions
- **GIGGITY**: Overdrive only. Manufacturer taxonomy explicitly identifies Analog Overdrive + Preamp.
- **Sparkle Drive MOD**: Overdrive + Distortion. Manufacturer documentation explicitly uses both terms for its selectable circuits and its inherited overdrive tones.
- **Sparkle Drive**: Overdrive only. Manufacturer identifies it as an overdrive pedal with a vintage-808 overdrive circuit.
- **Superfuzz**: Fuzz only. Manufacturer explicitly identifies its circuit as fuzz.
- **Proctavia**: Fuzz only. Manufacturer explicitly identifies it as a fuzz/octave pedal.

## Historical / variant handling
- **Sparkle Drive** is retained as a historical identity because Voodoo Lab's current Legacy index lists it as a legacy/out-of-production product while the current catalog carries Sparkle Drive MOD.
- **Proctavia** is treated as historical/legacy for the census because it appears in Voodoo Lab's Legacy product index, while the current Effects catalog highlights GIGGITY, Sparkle Drive MOD, and Superfuzz.
- **Sparkle Drive MOD** is not merged into Sparkle Drive. The manufacturer presents MOD as a distinct named product with additional selectable circuits, so it remains a separate functional product identity.
- Product versions, finishes, stock status, and presentation variants do not create additional dirt identities.
- Preamp functionality on GIGGITY is secondary to its explicit Overdrive identity and does not create another target category.
- Octave functionality on Proctavia is secondary to its explicit Fuzz identity and does not create another target category.

## Exclusions
- Non-dirt Voodoo Lab products such as Tremolo, Micro Vibe, Analog Chorus, Wahzoo, switching, power, and pedalboard products: outside the target taxonomy.
- The Voodoo Lab Guitar Preamp is not promoted into the dirt census because the reviewed manufacturer material does not provide sufficiently clear target-category wording to establish it as an Overdrive, Distortion, or Fuzz pedal identity.
- Generic grit, compression, saturation, or distortion language without explicit product-category evidence: insufficient for secondary categorization.
- Cloning, schematics, internal construction, component details, and other technical reverse-engineering material: outside scope.
