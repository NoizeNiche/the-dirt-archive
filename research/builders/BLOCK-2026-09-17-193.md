# Builder -> Pedals Research Block 193

## Scope

Continued the C-section Builder -> Pedals census with one existing-builder backfill and seven new canonical identities. Inclusion remains dirt-only: overdrive, distortion, and fuzz. The reviewed catalogs were filtered to exclude compressors, delays, modulation, tremolo, phaser, wah-only, boost-only, octave-only, utility, and other non-dirt products.

## Evidence / classification notes

- Collision Devices FZ is explicitly presented as a gated fuzz/distortion product. Because the source assigns both dirt classes, the archive preserves both type rows under existing builder ID 314.
- Clarenzio's manufacturer page contains eight products. Six are explicitly dirt: one distortion, two tube-distortion models, one tube overdrive, one overdrive, and one fuzz. The remaining products are reverb and tremolo and are excluded.
- Crossfire's 14-product catalog separates utility, chorus, compressor, delay, flanger, tuner, phaser, power, and tremolo models from four dirt-bearing products. CF-DO2 appears in the distortion category and belongs to the same dual-drive family as the Belcat platform; DST-301 is explicit distortion, FUZ-510 is explicit fuzz, and OVD-302 is explicit overdrive.
- Crysta Professional's DS-1 and OD-1 are explicitly classified as distortion and overdrive.
- Crestwood's catalog contains three fuzz-bearing models and one wah-only model. The 8-Tr Fuzz Wah is identified as a 1970s Shin-Ei OEM design; the archive records Crestwood as the historical branded catalog identity and does not infer independent circuit authorship.
- CSL's product page lists Super Fuzz and Wah Fuzz among a five-product catalog. The remaining models are phaser, power boost, and wah. The page identifies Charles Summerfield Ltd as the brand manager and shows "CSL by Colorsound" branding, so CSL is retained as a historical brand identity rather than merged outright into Colorsound / Sola Sound.
- CS.Cathey is a Japanese mid-1970s brand produced by Cathey Co., Ltd. Its four-product catalog includes AB-03 Distortion Booster and AD-02 Distortion Box, both explicitly dirt-bearing; the Upper Octave and Sustainer are excluded.
- CSR's Metal Rocker is explicitly listed as distortion. The associated CB Wah-Wah and Compression Sustainer are excluded.

## Exclusions / non-additions

- Clarenzio Campanoun - Reverb and Sugaman - Tremolo are excluded.
- Crossfire utility, chorus, compressor, delay, flanger, tuner, phaser, power, and tremolo models are excluded.
- Crestwood Wah Wah is excluded as wah-only.
- CSL Mini Phaser, Power Boost, and Wah Wah are excluded.
- CS.Cathey AO-05 Upper Octave and AS-01 Sustainer are excluded.
- CSR CB Wah-Wah and Compression Sustainer are excluded.
- No new canonical identity is created for Colorsound from the CSL page; CSL remains a historical brand-level identity with its Colorsound connection documented.
- No duplicate Collision Devices identity is created for the FZ expansion.
