# Builder -> Pedals Research Block 191

## Scope

Continued the C-section Builder -> Pedals census with five new canonical builders whose reviewed product records contain explicit dirt classifications. Inclusion remains dirt-only: overdrive, distortion, and fuzz. Compressors, delays, flangers, tremolos, oscillators, wah-only, boost-first, and other non-dirt functions were excluded.

## New canonical builders

### 449 — Chaser
- HM-3 Heavy Metal — Distortion

### 450 — CMI
- CMI Fuzz Unit — Fuzz
- Wah Fuzz Swell — Fuzz

### 451 — Cobrahawk
- B12 Fuzz — Fuzz
- B12 Go Fuzz Yourself (Paul) — Fuzz
- JRC4558 OD-8 Overdrive — Overdrive

### 452 — Code
- CDO2 Stereo Distortion O.D. — Overdrive
- CDO2 Stereo Distortion O.D. — Distortion

- Frank 'n' Beans — Fuzz

## Evidence / classification notes

- Chaser's HM-3 Heavy Metal is explicitly classified as distortion in the current Effects Database record. The other Chaser products are compression, delay, flanger, and tremolo models, so they are excluded.
- CMI has a Fuzz Unit explicitly tagged fuzz and a Wah Fuzz Swell whose function includes fuzz-wah/volume. The archive records the latter as Fuzz because fuzz is its dirt-bearing function. Effects Database identifies CMI as Cleartone Musical Instruments, a brand name owned by Marshall, so CMI is retained as a historical branded catalog identity rather than treated as an independent statement of manufacturing authorship.
- Cobrahawk has two explicit fuzz products and the JRC4558 OD-8 Overdrive. The LM556 Astable Oscillator is a synth/oscillator device and TIR212 Dirty Germ Boost is boost-first, so neither is included.
- Code's CDO2 Stereo Distortion O.D. is explicitly tagged as both distortion and overdrive, so it receives one row for each dirt type under the archive's multi-type row rule. CMM2 Stereo Metal Mate is explicitly distortion. The Stereo Analog Delay, Compressor, Flanger, and Phaser models are excluded.
- Codtone's Ben Drive is explicitly overdrive; Frank 'n' Beans and Fuzz for Bass are explicitly fuzz; Russian Bikini Ranger is cataloged with boost/preamp plus fuzz and is retained for its fuzz classification. The Codtone models that are not clearly dirt-primary remain outside the scrape.

## Exclusions / non-additions

- Chaser CS-3 Compression/Sustainer, DL-4 Super Delay, HF-3 Hi-Flanger, and TR-2 Tremolo are excluded.
- CMI Mini Fazer and Wah Wah are excluded as modulation/wah products.
- Cobrahawk LM556 Astable Oscillator is excluded as a synth/oscillator; TIR212 Dirty Germ Boost is excluded as boost-first.
- Code CAD2 Stereo Analog Delay, CCO2 Stereo Compressor, CFS2 Stereo Flanger, and CSP2 Stereo Phaser are excluded.
- Codtone Cod Wallop is boost/preamp-first and excluded. Wobulator remains outside the scrape pending sufficiently explicit classification.
- No duplicate identity is created for Marshall Amplification from the CMI records. CMI is preserved as the historical catalog brand while its Marshall ownership is documented.
