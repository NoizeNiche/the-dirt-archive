## Explicit overlaps
- The Barbershop = Overdrive. Fairfield explicitly labels the current version "Millenium Overdrive".
- Modèle B = Overdrive lineage of The Barbershop. Fairfield describes it as the same circuit as the Barbershop and an "Always On Barbershop"; it is therefore retained as an identifiable product but not treated as a different dirt lineage.
- ~900 = Fuzz. Fairfield explicitly labels it "Four Knob Fuzz" and says it traces its lineage to the discontinued Four Eyes Fuzz.
- Four Eyes Fuzz = Fuzz. It is retained as a historical named Fairfield pedal because Fairfield explicitly identifies it as the predecessor lineage of the ~900.
- The Unpleasant Surprise = Fuzz. Fairfield explicitly labels it "Experimental Fuzz/Gate"; the gate function is non-target and not counted separately.
- 20% More = Distortion. Fairfield explicitly labels it "Slew Limiting Distortion"; its filter/oscillation/envelope behaviors are not separate dirt categories.

## Variant / historical restraint
- Barbershop V1, Barbershop Millennium Edition, and later calibration revisions are one Barbershop overdrive lineage. Fairfield's FAQ states that the circuit topology was not changed between the original and Millennium versions.
- Modèle B is a Barbershop-derived product and is retained as a named product relationship, but not split into a new underlying circuit lineage.
- ~900 and Four Eyes Fuzz remain separate named products because Fairfield explicitly describes ~900 as tracing its lineage to discontinued Four Eyes Fuzz; this is a historical predecessor relationship rather than a cosmetic revision.
- Utility, modulation, delay, reverb, compressor, EQ, feedback-loop, CV, and patching products are excluded.
- Boost/pad settings are treated as supporting functions, not separate dirt categories.
- No components, schematics, PCB, BOM, gutshots, cloning, or build instructions are included.

## Verification note
Fairfield's current catalog identifies The Barbershop as a Millennium Overdrive, Modèle B as an Always On Barbershop, 20% More as Slew Limiting Distortion, ~900 as Four Knob Fuzz, and The Unpleasant Surprise as Experimental Fuzz/Gate. Fairfield's FAQ also documents the Barbershop lineage and identifies Four Eyes Fuzz as the predecessor of ~900. These relationships form the basis of this census block.
