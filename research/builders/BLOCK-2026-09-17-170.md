# The Dirt Archive - Builder -> Pedals Research Block 170

## Scope
Continue Scrape C. This block backfills verified C-section builders that were missed while the earlier pass advanced toward CUVAVE / M-VAVE. Dirt only: overdrive, distortion, fuzz. Existing canonical builders are not duplicated.

## Verified builders and pedal records

### New builder — Carcharias Effects
- Uncle 125 Bass Overdrive — Overdrive

Evidence: Effects Database documents the Uncle 125 as a bass overdrive with crunchy distortion through subtle overdrive and explicitly describes its distorted signal and gain-stacking use.

### New builder — Chaos Custom Shop / Chaos FX
- C.Drive — Overdrive
- Fuzz Machine — Fuzz
- Lugola Fuzz — Fuzz
- K.O. Drive — Overdrive
- X.Drive Distortion — Distortion

Evidence: Guitar Pedal X documents the Chaos FX / Chaos Custom Shop drive and fuzz range, including C.Drive, Fuzz Machine, K.O. Drive and X.Drive. PedalPedia independently documents C.Drive as an overdrive. One Thousand Pedals documents Lugola Fuzz as a Fuzz Face-style fuzz and Fuzz Machine as a Fuzz Factory-style fuzz. The builder is treated as one canonical identity because the sources use Chaos FX / Chaos Custom Shop for the same Polish shop.

### New builder — CroyTone Audio Technologies
- Hae Fuzz — Fuzz
- Suzumebachi Fuzz — Fuzz
- Camper Fuzz — Fuzz

Evidence: CroyTone's current catalog lists these three fuzz pedals. The Hae Fuzz is also documented by Effects Database as an octave-oriented fuzz. The current CroyTone catalog identifies the company as operating from Sapporo, Japan.

### New builder — CTech / CB Labs
- PRX-901 Amp Emulator — Overdrive
- PRX-902 Amp Emulator — Overdrive

Evidence: Effects Database identifies both PRX-901 and PRX-902 as CTech / CB Labs overdrive pedals. Contemporary user discussion also describes CTech as the successor branding to CB Labs.

### New builder — Cultco
- 001 Medium Overdrive — Overdrive

Evidence: Pedal of the Day documents the Cultco 001 Medium Overdrive as a collaboration involving Joywave and Adventure Audio and describes its gain range as an overdrive capable of more aggressive drive tones.

### New builder — Cunningham Amps
- MK 1.6 Fuzz — Fuzz
- Dual Range Fuzz — Fuzz
- Octave Fuzz — Fuzz
- Zonk/MK1 Fuzz — Fuzz
- MKII Professional Fuzz — Fuzz
- Gypsy Eyes Fuzz — Fuzz
- Supa Fuzz — Fuzz
- Nick Perri Signature MK1 Fuzz — Fuzz

Evidence: Cunningham Amps' current pedal catalog lists these fuzz families. The builder's product pages document the MK 1.6, Dual Range, MKII and related fuzz designs as handmade pedals, with lower-gain settings capable of overdrive but fuzz as the primary effect.

### New builder — Custom 77
- Push Me Pull Me — Overdrive

Evidence: Guitar World and French guitar publications document the Custom 77 Push Me Pull Me as a three-mode overdrive with Vintage, Modern and Wild gain modes. The separate King for a Day product is a booster and is excluded from the dirt census.

### New builder — Cutec
- OD-01 Over Drive — Overdrive

Evidence: Effects Database identifies the Cutec OD-01 as an overdrive pedal and classifies it under dirt/gain effects.

### New builder — Critically Overdriven
- E.O.D. — Overdrive

Evidence: Effects Database documents the E.O.D. as a two-level natural overdrive with a dedicated Aggressor mode that adds gain and volume.

## Exclusions / boundary notes
- Culler Amplification was reviewed but not entered because the available Effects Database history establishes the brand but did not provide enough clearly identified dirt-pedal product evidence in the current search pass.
- Cubist Effects was reviewed as a pedal-modification shop. Its documented work is modification of other manufacturers' pedals rather than a distinct finished-pedal catalog, so it is excluded as a builder identity here.
- Curetronic.de was reviewed and appears primarily associated with modular/synth equipment rather than a clear finished guitar dirt-pedal catalog, so it is excluded.
- Custom 77's King for a Day Booster is excluded because it is a boost-first product.
- Product finishes, transistor variants, custom artwork editions, and ordinary revisions are not separate pedal records.
