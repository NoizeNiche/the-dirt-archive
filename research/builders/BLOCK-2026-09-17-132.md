## Explicit overlaps / category handling

- Katzenkönig = Fuzz + Distortion. Official Catalinbread material describes it as combining Tone Bender MkII fuzz characteristics with Rat-style distortion.
- SFT = Overdrive + Distortion + Fuzz. Official documentation explicitly describes clean/overdriven operation, high-gain distortion, and huge fuzz sounds in Stoner mode.
- Formula 51 = Overdrive + Distortion. Its official page describes the range from gritty cleans through cranked Tweed tones to heavy distortion.
- Formula 55 / Formula 5F6 / Sabbra Cadabra are retained as overdrive/amp-in-a-box dirt products; gain-heavy descriptions do not automatically create extra categories unless the product is explicitly presented as another dirt type.
- Crooner Preamp = Overdrive + Distortion + Fuzz. Official documentation explicitly describes clean boost, overdrive, distortion, and full-throated fuzz.
- Perseus DIO is not counted as a standalone fuzz/distortion product solely because its dry path has distortion. Its primary identity is an analog octave-down device, so only the explicitly dirt-relevant dry distortion capability is noted and the pedal is not used as a separate fuzz entry.
- Naga Viper is counted as Overdrive, despite its aggressive/high-gain range.
- Silver Kiss is Overdrive; its clean-boost and EQ functions are not separate census categories.
- Airstrip Console Pre is counted as Overdrive because the current official product family describes preamp saturation and dirt operation. It is not split into a separate preamp category.

## Historical / variant restraint

- Current official catalog entries, retired-pedal entries, and official manuals were used to capture builder-to-pedal relationships without multiplying cosmetic editions, B-stock entries, artwork variants, or minor reissues.
- Fuzzrite / Fuzzrite Germanium / Fuzzrite Mini are retained as named pedal families because the official catalog distinguishes them, but cosmetic editions are not separately counted.
- Formula No. 5 reissue / Spaceship Art are treated as one Formula No. 5 lineage.
- SFT Blackout and Fireworks editions are not separate builder relationships.
- Sabbra Cadabra anniversary/art editions are not separate relationships.
- Naga Viper limited/art variants are not separate relationships.
- Retired products remain eligible because the census is builder-to-pedal, not current-stock-only.
- DIY kits are excluded from the pedal census when they are kit-only listings; the built Knight School Overdrive is included as a pedal.
- Non-dirt effects such as delays, reverbs, modulation, filters, EQ-only devices, tap accessories, apparel, cables, and gift cards are excluded.
- No photos, biographies, components, schematics, PCB/BOM information, gutshots, cloning information, or deep historical research were added.

## Verification notes

Catalinbread's official Foundation Overdrive page lists Dirty Little Secret, SFT, RAH, Sabbra Cadabra, Formula 55, Formula 5F6, Galileo, Galileo 4K, Dirty Little Secret Deluxe, and Clean Little Secret. The official Boost/Fuzz/Distortion page lists Fuzzrite, Fuzzrite Germanium, Katzenkönig, Giygas 2K Fuzz, Fuzzrite Mini, and related gain products. The full catalog and manuals were checked to catch older named dirt pedals such as Formula 51, Merkin Fuzz, Manx Loaghtan Fuzz, Silver Kiss, SideArm, and historical variants.

Official product descriptions independently verify the important overlaps: SFT covers overdrive/distortion/fuzz territory; Crooner explicitly covers boost/overdrive/distortion/fuzz; Katzenkönig combines fuzz and distortion; Formula 51 spans gritty clean through heavy distortion; Manx Loaghtan is explicitly a fuzz; Merkin is explicitly a fuzz; and Carbide is explicitly a distortion.
