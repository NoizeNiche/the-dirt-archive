# Builder -> Pedals Research Block 189

## Scope

Continued the C-section Builder -> Pedals census with a new builder and an existing-builder product backfill. Inclusion remains dirt-only: overdrive, distortion, and fuzz. Existing canonical identities were checked before creating the new identity. Non-dirt products and uncertain classifications remain excluded.

## Evidence / classification notes

- Carella Guitars has eight cataloged products in the reviewed C-section source. Chorus is excluded as modulation. The remaining six named dirt models above are explicitly cataloged as fuzz, overdrive, or distortion in their individual records.
- Carella's Overdistover is explicitly classified as both overdrive and distortion, so the same pedal receives one row for each dirt type under the archive's multi-type row rule.
- Chase Tone was already canonical as ID 307. The reviewed product taxonomy contains several Stardust and vintage-style Chase Tone fuzz models that were absent from the active C census. The six models added here are explicitly named as fuzz products; they are backfilled under the existing Chase Tone identity rather than creating a duplicate builder.
- No circuit authorship is inferred from Chase Tone's use of vintage transistor names or model lineage. The census records the branded pedal identity and its documented dirt classification.

## Exclusions / non-additions

- Carella Guitars Chorus is excluded as modulation.
- Carella Guitars Dynaros and GCM800 were not added because the reviewed record set did not establish them as a primary Fuzz, Overdrive, or Distortion product at the same confidence level used for the six retained models.
- Carella Guitars models without a dirt-primary classification remain outside the scrape.
- Chase Tone Round Red Replica was not added in this block because the reviewed source did not provide enough classification detail to safely assign it a dirt type from the model name alone.
- No duplicate Chase Tone identity was created.
