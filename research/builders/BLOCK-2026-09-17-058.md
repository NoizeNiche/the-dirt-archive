# The Dirt Archive — Builder Research Block 058

## Builder
**Dirty Boy Pedals / Dirty Boy Co. — Alex Saraceno; revived under Danny Gomez**

## Verification basis
Primary manufacturer sources:
- Dirty Boy Co. official effects catalog
- Dirty Boy Co. official product pages
- Warm Audio official Dirty Boy Jr. product page / brand materials

Authoritative corroboration:
- Premier Guitar
- Sound On Sound

Dirty Boy's official catalog currently lists the PREAMP, AFRO FUZZ, BALL BUSTER, GERMANIUM BOY, several custom pedals, and the Dirty Boy Jr. line. The official product material explicitly identifies the target dirt functions for the PREAMP, AFRO FUZZ, and Magical Mystery Fuzz; the Warm Audio collaboration material explicitly identifies the Jr. Overdrive and Jr. Fuzz as adaptations of Dirty Boy circuits. The official Dirty Boy material identifies the original work as associated with Alex Saraceno and the revived line as developed under Danny Gomez.

- None promoted in this block

## Product-level verification notes
- **PREAMP**: Dirty Boy's official product page explicitly describes it as an amplifier simulator, preamp, and "tube-sounding booster/overdrive" for an amp input. It is therefore recorded under Overdrive.
- **Dirty Boy Jr. Overdrive**: Warm Audio's official product page explicitly calls it an amp-like overdrive pedal and identifies it as a recreation of the original Dirty Boy Preamp circuit. It is recorded under Overdrive. The archive keeps the Dirty Boy/Junior relationship explicit rather than treating the collaboration as an unrelated builder.
- **AFRO FUZZ**: Dirty Boy's official catalog identifies AFRO FUZZ as a core product, and the Warm Audio collaboration documentation explicitly identifies the original Afro Fuzz circuit as the basis of the Dirty Boy Jr. Fuzz. It is recorded under Fuzz.
- **The Magical MYSTERY FUZZ**: Dirty Boy's official product page explicitly describes it as a germanium fuzz. It is recorded under Fuzz only.
- **Dirty Boy Jr. Fuzz**: Warm Audio's official collaboration materials explicitly identify it as a vintage-style fuzz built around the original Dirty Boy Afro Fuzz circuit. It is recorded under Fuzz.

## Scope decisions / exclusions
- **BALL BUSTER** is excluded because Dirty Boy describes it as a booster circuit; boost-only products are outside the target Overdrive/Distortion/Fuzz census.
- **GERMANIUM BOY** is excluded because its official product page describes it as a multi-frequency booster with a gain function rather than explicitly identifying it as Overdrive, Distortion, or Fuzz. Germanium clipping alone is not used to inflate the taxonomy.
- **GERMANIUM SL#T** is excluded because Dirty Boy's official page presents it as the original raw germanium booster design and does not explicitly classify it as one of the target categories.
- **SilverBOY** is excluded because the official page presents it primarily as an analog amplifier simulator/preamp and drive/boost device rather than explicitly as an Overdrive, Distortion, or Fuzz pedal.
- **DIRTY DOUBLE** is excluded because the official page identifies it as a dual preamp platform. Its crunch capability is not enough to infer an Overdrive category.
- Other custom product names visible in the official catalog are not promoted into the categorized census without explicit manufacturer dirt classification.
- No standalone Distortion identity is promoted in this block because the reviewed manufacturer material does not explicitly support one.
