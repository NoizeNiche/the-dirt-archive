# The Dirt Archive — Builder → Pedals Research Block 024

Date: September 16, 2026
Scope: Builder → Pedals only

This block records builder/product relationships only. Product names are taken from current manufacturer catalogs and official manufacturer documentation, with authoritative secondary sources used only where the manufacturer no longer exposes a legacy product page. This is a research checkpoint, not an exhaustive lifetime catalog.

## 1. Seymour Duncan

Builder / brand: Seymour Duncan

### Current dirt products verified from the official Seymour Duncan catalog
- 805 Overdrive — overdrive
- Diamondhead — multistage distortion + boost

### Historical / discontinued dirt products verified from official Seymour Duncan material
- Dirty Deed — distortion / wide-range dirt product combining overdrive, distortion, and fuzz characteristics
- Palladium Gain Stage — high-gain preamp / distortion product; discontinued
- FOOZ — analog fuzz synthesizer / fuzz effect

Notes:
- Seymour Duncan's current Overdrive & Distortion category currently shows 805 and Diamondhead as its two dirt catalog results.
- The official Seymour Duncan forum and product material identify Dirty Deed as a distortion pedal with a wide range from overdrive through distortion and fuzz.
- The official Palladium product page explicitly states that Palladium is discontinued and describes it as a high-gain preamp and distortion effects pedal.
- The official FOOZ manual identifies FOOZ as a fuzz and filter effect with a square-wave fuzz section capable of anything from light drive to massive fuzz.
- Other current Seymour Duncan categories such as pedal amps, pickups, compression, modulation, and delay are excluded from this phase.

## 2. J. Rockett Audio Designs

- Blue Note Select
- HRM V2

- Dude ODS V2

### Additional named dirt identities verified from current official Rockett product listings and authoritative catalog references
- GTO
- iKon
- Rockaway Archer
- Dude V2
- El Hombre
- Monkey Man
- Majestic Overdrive
- Animal Overdrive
- Hooligan Fuzz
- WTF Fuzz
- Flex Drive

Notes:
- J. Rockett's official site explicitly maintains an Overdrives shop category and currently lists six products there.
- The official Rockett home page also shows GTO and multiple B-stock forms of current/legacy drive products, establishing the named product identities even when a model is not in the current main Overdrives category.
- Guitar Pedal X's documented current/retired JRAD list is used to preserve legacy named overdrive identities including Animal, Majestic, Monkey Man, and related TOUR Series products where the current manufacturer navigation no longer exposes each legacy page.
- Hooligan Fuzz is retained as a J. Rockett fuzz identity based on manufacturer-linked documentation and authoritative retail/archive material. WTF Fuzz is retained as the related named fuzz product identity.
- Flex Drive is retained as a dirt product because the J. Rockett manual explicitly describes it as producing clean/dirty sounds and fuzz-type tones at higher gain.
- Product versions and collaborations remain catalog identities unless the source clearly treats them as the same product; no deeper variant normalization is attempted here.
