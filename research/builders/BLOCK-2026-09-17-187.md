# Builder -> Pedals Research Block 187

## Scope

Continued the C-section Builder -> Pedals census with remaining high-confidence alphabetic backfills. Inclusion remains dirt-only: overdrive, distortion, and fuzz. Existing builder identities were checked before assigning new canonical numbers. Products whose primary function is compressor, wah, tremolo, modulation, delay, amp simulation, boost-only, or other non-dirt use were excluded.

## Evidence / classification notes

- Case Study Effects Co. is documented as the successor to Neutron Pedals. The Aspen One is explicitly an overdrive plus boost, so only its overdrive function is counted. Aspen, Germanium Aspen, and Lastlight are explicitly overdrive products. The Studio Compressor is excluded despite its hidden drive mode because its primary catalog identity is compressor.
- Chellee Guitars has six clearly cataloged overdrive pedals in the C-section source: Odelya, the original Odie, Odie Classic, Odie Modified, Ponyboy, and Sir Robin. Their product records identify them as overdrive models; clean-boost settings within those designs do not change their primary dirt classification.
- Chemistry Design Werks Gigawatt is explicitly cataloged as Overdrive & Delay. The overdrive component is included while the delay function is outside the scrape.
- Clone Bro has the Tone King and Taurus in the historical Effects Database record set. Tone King is explicitly an overdrive, and Taurus is cataloged as a Klon-style overdrive.
- Custom Audio Electronics lists Freddy Fuzz as its dirt-bearing product. The archive records the CAE-branded pedal while preserving the source lineage connection to the Black Cat / Freddy Fuzz family rather than treating it as an independently originated circuit.
- Custom Guitar Innovations has five cataloged products. Four are dirt-bearing: 2 In 1 Fuzz Pedal, Experience Fuzz, Fuzz Boost, and Octavio. Range Blaster is a boost product and is excluded. Octavio is an octave-fuzz design and is therefore recorded under Fuzz.
- Custom Tones Ethos is explicitly cataloged as an overdrive pedal.
- The Champion page states that its effects were made by Colorsound for Barnes & Mullins LTD. Champion therefore expands the existing Colorsound / Sola Sound identity (ID 348) instead of creating a duplicate manufacturer identity. The Champion Fuzz Unit and Champion Wah Fuzz are retained as branded catalog records under that existing maker lineage.

## Exclusions / non-additions

- CDB Pedals, CCFX, and CDM Custom Guitar Pedals remain at zero cataloged products in the reviewed source.
- CGM Custom Shop remains a zero-product source entry.
- Chamber Of Sounds is excluded because its catalog centers on synthesis, modulation, and delay devices rather than dirt-primary pedals.
- Cherry Music's reviewed catalog is tuner-focused and contributes no dirt-primary model.
- Chris Mac Gregor's catalog is wah, filter, compressor, talk box, and synth-oriented, so no dirt row is added.
- Custom Guitar Innovations Range Blaster is boost and is excluded.
- Custom Guitar Tone is excluded because its reviewed products are compressor/boost rather than primary dirt.
- Custom Audio Electronics Super Tremolo, Twin Wah Box, V-Comp Tube Compressor, and Japanese utility pedals are excluded.
- No new canonical identity is created for Champion because the source explicitly assigns its effects to Colorsound-made products for Barnes & Mullins.
