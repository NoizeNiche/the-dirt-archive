## Research notes

- Effects Database documents the Axcess by Giannini line with BD-108 Blues Drive, DS-101 Distortion, DS-102 Distortion 2, FZ-110 Fuzz, MD-102 Mystic Drive, and OD-102 Over Drive. Non-dirt Axcess products and the dedicated Hot Boost were excluded.
- Axess Electronics currently lists OBViouS Boost/OD as a boost/overdrive pedal. Buffer and routing products, including UNZ1 Fuzz Un-Buffer, are excluded because they do not generate dirt themselves.
- Axewerx's Gain Chain is documented as an overdrive pedal, while Glass Drive is explicitly described as an octave, overdrive, distortion, and fuzz pedal. The archive records Glass Drive under all three target dirt categories.
