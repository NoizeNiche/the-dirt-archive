# Builder → Pedals Research Block 104

## Builder
**Old Blood Noise Endeavors (OBNE)**

## Verification basis
Old Blood Noise Endeavors' official current pedal catalog and individual product pages were used as the primary source for this block. The current catalog directly identifies Fault V2 as Overdrive + Distortion, Excess V2 as Distortion, and Haunt, Alpha Haunt, and Pardner as fuzz products. The archive follows OBNE's own product identity instead of turning every gain range or secondary circuit into another category.

## Product notes
- **Fault V2:** OBNE explicitly presents Fault as an overdrive pedal that ranges from boost to distortion, and the product title itself is “Fault V2 Overdrive + Distortion.” It is retained in both Overdrive and Distortion.
- **Beam Splitter V2:** OBNE describes Beam Splitter as using three different overdrive voices, while the current home page presents Beam Splitter V2 as “Trereo Distortion” and the product controls describe the gain as shaping distortion in each drive section. It is therefore retained in both Overdrive and Distortion as one named pedal, not split into separate internal drives.
- **Excess V2:** OBNE explicitly calls Excess a distortion paired with delay, chorus, or harmonized fifths. The distortion side is a core standalone dirt section, so it is retained under Distortion; the modulation/delay functions remain outside scope.
- **Haunt Fuzz:** OBNE explicitly identifies Haunt as a high-gain gated fuzz. Its smoother tones can approach overdrive, but that is treated as operating range inside the fuzz identity.
- **Alpha Haunt:** OBNE calls Alpha Haunt the expanded version of the Haunt silicon high-gain gated fuzz and explicitly identifies the core as fuzz. Its softer range and distortion control are not promoted into separate categories because the named product identity is Fuzz.
- **Pardner:** OBNE explicitly describes Pardner as a fuzz with a synthy texture and an additional overdriven effects loop called Lasso. The pedal is retained under Fuzz only because the overdrive is a secondary loop rather than a separate named dirt identity.

## Explicit exclusions / restraint
- **Screen Violence:** excluded because OBNE presents it primarily as a saturated modulated reverb with an overdrive side called Violence. The reverb/modulation system is the principal product identity rather than a standalone dirt pedal.
- **Dark Star:** excluded because OBNE identifies it as a reverb with an optional overdrive/crush function, not a standalone dirt product.
- **Black Bobbin Boost:** excluded because OBNE presents it as a dual clean boost. Its ability to become lightly overdriven does not establish an Overdrive identity.
- **MAW and other signal/utility products:** excluded where the primary product identity is microphone processing, routing, blending, or other non-target functionality.
- **Retired BL-series, reverb, delay, modulation, pitch, and utility products:** excluded because their primary identities are outside Overdrive, Distortion, and Fuzz.
- **Gain-range inflation:** Haunt, Alpha Haunt, Fault, and other products are not duplicated into extra categories solely because their gain controls reach neighboring sounds. Categories follow explicit OBNE product identity.
- **Colorways and ordinary revisions:** consolidated with the underlying product unless OBNE presents a materially distinct named dirt product. Pardner colorways are not separate identities.
