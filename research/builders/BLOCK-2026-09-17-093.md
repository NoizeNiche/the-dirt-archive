# Block 093 — Empress Effects

## Builder
**Empress Effects Inc.**

## Verification basis
Primary and authoritative sources reviewed:
- Empress Effects official current All Products catalog
- Empress Effects official Distortion & Overdrive collection
- Empress Effects official product pages for Drive, Heavy Menace, Multidrive, Distortion, Fuzz, and Germ Drive
- Empress Effects official Heavy announcement/support material
- active-repository search for “Empress Effects” before addition

The active repository contained no prior Empress Effects match before this block was added. Empress Effects is treated as the builder/brand identity. The census remains focused on standalone physical Overdrive, Distortion, and Fuzz pedals and does not convert ZOIA modules, modeled patches, reverb/delay modulation features, or other non-dirt products into separate target identities.

The reviewed Empress catalog supports a compact but clearly documented dirt lineup spanning all three target categories. Product-level wording is followed conservatively: explicit overdrive, distortion, and fuzz identities are promoted, while generic references to saturation or clipping do not create extra categories.

## Overdrive
Current / current-catalog-supported identities:
- Drive
- Heavy

Discontinued / historical identities:
- Distortion
- Germ Drive
- Multidrive

Verification notes:
- **Drive** is explicitly described by Empress as a tube-like overdrive with advanced tone control. It appears in the current Distortion & Drive catalog and current All Products catalog.
- **Germ Drive** is explicitly described by Empress as a warm, harmonically rich overdrive with tube-like breakup and is currently listed as discontinued.
- **Multidrive** contains an independently controllable overdrive circuit in parallel with its distortion and fuzz sections. Empress describes the unit as three pedals in one and explicitly identifies its overdrive section.
- **Heavy** is retained in Overdrive as well as Distortion only where the builder's own historical wording supports its lower-gain drive range as part of the product's usable dirt architecture. Empress nevertheless treats the standalone Heavy primarily as a distortion pedal, so no separate additional identity is created beyond the named product.
- **Distortion** is recorded in Overdrive because Empress's product description says its mild setting provides gentle breakup that cleans up with the volume knob. This is retained as an overlap rather than promoted as a separate variant.

## Distortion
Current / current-catalog-supported identities:
- Heavy
- Heavy Menace

Discontinued / historical identities:
- Distortion
- Multidrive

Verification notes:
- **Heavy** is explicitly described by Empress as a 2-channel distortion pedal. Empress's historical product announcement identifies high-gain distortion as one of the product's central functions.
- **Heavy Menace** is explicitly described by Empress as its most versatile distortion pedal and as an evolution of the original Heavy. It remains a current product.
- **Distortion** is the named Empress distortion product and is currently listed as discontinued.
- **Multidrive** explicitly blends overdrive, distortion, and fuzz in parallel, so it belongs in all three target categories.

## Fuzz
Current / current-catalog-supported identity:
- Heavy Menace does not receive a Fuzz classification.

Discontinued / historical identities:
- Fuzz
- Multidrive

Verification notes:
- **Fuzz** is explicitly identified by Empress as a classic fuzz pedal and is listed as discontinued.
- **Multidrive** contains a dedicated fuzz circuit and is explicitly described by Empress as combining fuzz, overdrive, and distortion in one box.
- No standalone Empress product besides Multidrive and Fuzz was established with sufficiently explicit builder-level Fuzz identity. ZOIA's fuzz module and factory patches are excluded because they are modular/software-like functions inside a multi-effect platform rather than standalone named dirt pedals.

## Category-overlap decisions
- **Multidrive**: Overdrive + Distortion + Fuzz. Empress explicitly states that the pedal blends all three circuits in parallel.
- **Distortion**: Overdrive + Distortion. The named product is explicitly a distortion pedal, while the mild setting is described as gentle breakup that cleans up with the volume knob. The archive records that documented overlap without creating a separate low-gain product identity.
- **Heavy**: Distortion primarily. Empress's own product announcement calls it a 2-channel distortion pedal. Lower-gain behavior is not enough by itself to create a separate Overdrive identity, so the archive does not double-count Heavy as Overdrive.
- **Heavy Menace**: Distortion only. Empress explicitly says the Lite(ish) mode remains distortion rather than a transparent boost or edge-of-breakup drive.
- **Drive**: Overdrive only. Its high-saturation range remains part of the overdrive design rather than a separate Distortion identity.
- **Germ Drive**: Overdrive only. Its natural breakup and sustain remain within the builder's explicit Overdrive identity.

## Historical / variant handling
- **Heavy Menace Limited Edition** is consolidated into **Heavy Menace** because Empress describes it as a limited colourway of the same product rather than a distinct circuit or named design.
- Scratch-and-Dent listings are not separate product identities.
- **Heavy** and **Heavy Menace** remain distinct named products because Empress explicitly presents Heavy Menace as an evolution of the original Heavy with a different enclosure and feature set.
- Normal production revisions, artwork, colourways, and sales-condition designations do not create new dirt identities.

## Unpromoted / cautious leads
- **ZOIA** contains OD & Distortion and Fuzz modules, and its factory patches include drive/fuzz combinations, but it is a modular multi-effect platform rather than a standalone named dirt pedal. It is excluded from the builder-to-pedal dirt census.
- Reverb pages that mention overdriven spring modes do not create separate Empress Overdrive products because the dirt is part of a reverb algorithm rather than a standalone dirt pedal.
- Generic mentions of clipping, saturation, breakup, or high gain are not used to create additional target categories when the product-level identity is explicit.

## Exclusions
- ZOIA, ZOIA Euroburo, Echosystem, Reverb, Nebulus, ParaEq, Compressor, Buffer, Phaser, Tremolo, delay products, and other non-dirt Empress products remain outside the target census.
- Scratch-and-Dent products, Limited Edition colourways, and cosmetic variants do not create additional underlying dirt identities.
- ZOIA factory patches are excluded because they are software-defined patches within the ZOIA platform rather than standalone physical dirt pedals.
- Cloning, schematics, components, internal construction, and reverse-engineering material are outside scope.
