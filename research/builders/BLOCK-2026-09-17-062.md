# Block 062 — Keeley Electronics — Robert Keeley

## Builder
**Keeley Electronics — Robert Keeley**

## Verification basis
Primary manufacturer sources reviewed:
- Keeley Electronics About page
- current Keeley Electronics shop/product index
- current manufacturer Distortion, Drive, Boost & Fuzz category
- individual manufacturer product pages for selected current and legacy/archived dirt identities
- manufacturer archive pages where needed to distinguish a named historical product from a cosmetic/technical variation

Keeley Electronics' own About page identifies Robert Keeley and says the company began with him and grew into its current Oklahoma City operation. The current manufacturer shop explicitly separates the target dirt bucket as “Distortion, Drive, Boost & Fuzz” and exposes multiple named drive/fuzz products.

This block records builder/product relationships only. Current products are taken from the manufacturer's current shop and product pages; historical identities are included only when a named Keeley product is explicitly documented by the manufacturer. Cosmetic editions, numbered finishes, B-stock pages, and substantially identical circuit variations are consolidated rather than split into duplicate pedal identities.

Verification notes:
- Tube Drive is explicitly a dual overdrive on the current manufacturer site; the 12AT7WA RKFX.com edition is treated as a Tube Drive variant, not a separate pedal identity.
- Red Nectar, Manis, Mk3 Driver, Noble Screamer, Blues Disorder, Super Rodent, SUPER BASS, D&M Drive, DDR, DCR, Aria, El Rey Dorado, Oxblood, Red Dirt, Red Dirt Germanium, 1962, 1962X, Sweet Spot, Katana Blues Drive, and Drive For a Cure are explicitly described by the manufacturer as overdrive/drive products or as products containing an overdrive/drive section.
- The manufacturer describes the DDR drive section as two overdrive tones and describes DCR as a saturated tube-gain drive with crunch/lead profiles.
- The manufacturer explicitly describes Aria as compressor + overdrive and the D&M as Drive + Boost.

## Distortion
Explicit manufacturer-supported identities:
- SUPER BASS Overdrive and Distortion
- Blues Disorder Overdrive and Distortion
- Super Rodent Overdrive and Distortion
- Angry Orange Distortion and Fuzz
- OCTA PSI Transfigurating Fuzz
- Filaments High Gain Distortion
- Noble Screamer Overdrive and Boost
- Synth-1 Reverse Attack Fuzz Wave Generator

Verification notes:
- Blues Disorder explicitly contains both hard-clipping distortion and soft-clipping overdrive.
- Super Rodent explicitly combines an overdrive circuit and a distortion circuit, plus two hybrid combinations.
- SUPER BASS is explicitly labeled Overdrive and Distortion by the manufacturer.
- Angry Orange is identified by the manufacturer as Distortion and Fuzz in the current shop/product catalog.
- OCTA PSI's manufacturer specification explicitly lists Fuzz and Distortion among its effects.
- Filaments is explicitly a high-gain distortion pedal.
- Noble Screamer includes two amp-like distortion tones in addition to its overdrive/boost functions. It is retained in both categories because the manufacturer explicitly describes both behaviors.
- Synth-1 is a fuzz/synth product whose manufacturer title and product description explicitly use both distortion and fuzz language; it remains a conservative multi-effect dirt inclusion rather than a standalone conventional distortion design.

Conservative exclusions:
- Oxblood is not separately promoted into Distortion solely because its product page says the drive control can produce distortion; the named product identity remains Overdrive.
- DCR, DDR, Aria, El Rey Dorado, Red Dirt, 1962, and Sweet Spot remain Overdrive rather than being promoted into Distortion merely from generic saturation/crunch language.

## Fuzz
Explicit manufacturer-supported identities:
- OCTA PSI Transfigurating Fuzz
- Angry Orange Distortion and Fuzz
- SUNS Ultravoid Fuzz
- Fuzz Bender
- Moon Op Amp Fuzz
- Loomer
- Dark Side
- Monterey Rotary Fuzz Vibe
- Synth-1 Reverse Attack Fuzz Wave Generator
- Mk3 Driver — Andy Timmons Full Range Overdrive
- Germanium Super Phat Mod
- Fuzz Head

Verification notes:
- OCTA PSI is explicitly an analog transistor fuzz with octave/pitch functions and is also specified as including distortion.
- Angry Orange is explicitly identified by the manufacturer as Distortion and Fuzz.
- SUNS is explicitly identified as a fuzz product in the current manufacturer shop/catalog.
- Fuzz Bender is a three-transistor hybrid fuzz with silicon and germanium fuzz stages.
- Moon Op Amp Fuzz is explicitly described as a fuzz and as four gain/fuzz stages.
- Loomer is explicitly a “Wall-of-Fuzz” plus reverb workstation; the manufacturer repeatedly identifies its dirt section as fuzz.
- Dark Side explicitly includes a fuzz section and is categorized by the manufacturer in the dirt family.
- Monterey is explicitly a Rotary Fuzz Vibe with a dedicated vintage fuzz channel.
- Synth-1 is explicitly described as creating fuzzed-out synth waves.
- Mk3 Driver remains primarily an Overdrive identity, but the manufacturer explicitly documents a clean-boost-to-square-wave-fuzz range and a germanium fuzz generator, so it is retained as an overlap identity rather than inferred from generic gain language.
- Germanium Super Phat Mod is retained as a named dirt identity because the manufacturer explicitly documents a Germanium Fuzz added to the JFET overdrive design. This is a functional product identity, not a cosmetic finish.
- Fuzz Head is explicitly described by Keeley as a fuzz/overdrive design and is retained as its own underlying pedal identity; color-specific Red/Blue pages are not split.

## Category-overlap decisions
- **Noble Screamer**: Overdrive + Distortion. Manufacturer describes it as a 4-in-1 overdrive/boost with two new amp-like distortion tones.
- **Blues Disorder**: Overdrive + Distortion. Manufacturer explicitly describes soft-clipping overdrive and hard-clipping distortion.
- **Super Rodent**: Overdrive + Distortion. Manufacturer explicitly combines classic overdrive and distortion circuits plus hybrid modes.
- **SUPER BASS**: Overdrive + Distortion. Manufacturer product identity explicitly uses both.
- **Angry Orange**: Distortion + Fuzz. Current manufacturer shop/product identity explicitly uses both.
- **OCTA PSI**: Fuzz + Distortion. Manufacturer explicitly specifies both fuzz and distortion effects.
- **Mk3 Driver**: Overdrive + Fuzz. Manufacturer's primary product identity is Full Range Overdrive, but its own feature text explicitly reaches square-wave fuzz and calls the circuit a germanium fuzz generator.
- **Synth-1**: Fuzz + Distortion. Manufacturer's title and description explicitly support both terms, while the product is fundamentally a synth/fuzz multi-effect.
- **Germanium Super Phat Mod**: Overdrive + Fuzz. Manufacturer explicitly describes the original JFET overdrive plus an added Germanium Fuzz stage.

The following are not split into additional catalog identities:

- Custom Shop colorways/limited finishes → consolidated under the underlying pedal
- Fuzz Bender Army, Ghost Prism, Tuxedo, Bubblegum/Surf Green, and 20 for 20 editions → consolidated under Fuzz Bender
- Moon Op Amp Fuzz ‘Lunatrip’ edition → consolidated under Moon Op Amp Fuzz
- Red Dirt Professional, KE-808, and GE-808 limited variants are treated as Red Dirt-family variants rather than independent base identities unless a future archive pass establishes them as materially distinct product models
- Brad’s Batch and similar Custom Shop drops are not treated as new pedal identities when they merely re-finish existing Keeley designs

## Exclusions
- Katana Clean Boost / Mini Katana / Time Machine Boost / K201 JFET Booster: boost/preamp identities; the manufacturer explicitly distinguishes the Time Machine Boost from overdrive/distortion.
- Compressor Plus / Compressor Mini / Compressor Pro / GC-2 / Bassist: compressor/limiter products, not target dirt identities.
- Halo, ECCOS, Caverns, Hydra, Nocturne, Magnetic Echo, Memphis Sun, and other delay/reverb products: non-dirt effects.
- Seafoam Plus, DynaTrem, Bubble Tron, Rotary, Vibe-O-Verb, and other modulation-first products: excluded unless an explicit dirt identity is present.
- Repair/restoration services, cables, apparel, gift cards, and accessories: excluded.
- Cosmetic Custom Shop editions and B-stock listings: not separate builder/product identities.
