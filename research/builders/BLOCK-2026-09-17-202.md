# Builder -> Pedals Research Block 202

## Scope

Continued the C-section Builder -> Pedals census with three new canonical builders. Inclusion remains dirt-only: overdrive, distortion, and fuzz.

## New canonical builders

### 478 — CDB Pedals
- Darkwater — Distortion
- Darkwater — Overdrive

### 479 — CMC Audio
- PROTO TYPE V2 — Overdrive
- Bi Filter Fuzz — Fuzz

### 480 — Circuitbenders
- Demon Seed — Distortion
- Demon Seed — Fuzz
- Black Box Audio Destructor — Distortion

## Evidence / classification notes

- Guitar Pedal X identifies CDB Pedals and its Darkwater as an Overdrive / Distortion pedal. Both dirt classes are retained in the archive.
- CMC Audio's official material says the brand builds pedals in Liège, Belgium and describes the PROTO TYPE as a bass overdrive with a slightly fuzzy character. Its current product catalog shows the PROTO TYPE V2 family. The archive records one PROTO TYPE V2 dirt product rather than splitting enclosure/color variants into separate builder identities.
- CMC Audio's official Bi Filter Fuzz product page explicitly describes the unit as a fuzz. It is retained as Fuzz.
- Circuitbenders' official site is an active custom electronics shop and currently advertises Demon Seed pedals as fuzz / distortion devices. Both dirt classes are retained.
- Circuitbenders' current product material also documents the Black Box Audio Destructor as a circuit-bent distortion noise effects box, so it is retained as Distortion.
- None of the three builders appears in the existing canonical builder index, so they receive the next unused identities 478-480.

## Exclusions / non-additions

- CMC Audio's individual PROTO TYPE V2 graphic/enclosure variants are not split into separate canonical builder identities or duplicate dirt records.
- CMC Audio's other current products that are not overdrive/fuzz/distortion are excluded from this dirt census.
- Cloak Audio Jackdaw remains deferred for future Fredric Effects identity reconciliation because Cloak Audio is documented as an offshoot line rather than an independently resolved C-section builder.
- CDM Custom Guitar Pedals remains excluded until its boost/overdrive classification and builder identity can be established more cleanly from a first-party source.
