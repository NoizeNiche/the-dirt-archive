# Builder → Pedals Research Block 038

## Builder
**SolidGoldFX — Greg Djerrahian**

## Scope
Builder → Pedals only. This block records SolidGoldFX's documented overdrive, distortion, and fuzz identities from the current official catalog and product documentation. Non-dirt effects are excluded. Dirt-bearing multi-effects are retained only when the builder explicitly identifies the dirt circuit/function.

## Builder identity
SolidGoldFX is a Canadian pedal company founded by Greg Djerrahian. Its official About page states that the company has made Canadian pedals since 2006, identifies Djerrahian as CEO & Founder, and says the pedals are made by hand in the company's Montreal shop. The official catalog currently carries SolidGoldFX and Diamond Pedals products.

## Current / currently listed dirt pedals

### Overdrive
- **Commodore Overdrive** — official product page identifies it as an overdrive pedal and describes a low- to mid-gain asymmetric-clipping drive circuit derived from a tuned BluesBreaker-style section and the Imperial output section.
- **BETA-V Bass Preamp** — official product page identifies it as a bass preamp and overdrive effects pedal, with a Drive control ranging from mild grit to warm overdrive.

### Distortion
- **No standalone current SolidGoldFX product is forced into the distortion category in this block.** The current official dirt catalog is explicit about its principal overdrive and fuzz classifications, while fuzz products are not relabeled as distortion merely because their descriptions discuss saturation or distortion.

### Fuzz
- **Fuzzbox — Bender Fuzz** — official product page identifies it as a refined low-gain silicon fuzz inspired by the Tone Bender Mk II.
- **If 6 Was 9 BC183 MKII — Silicon Fuzz Face** — official product page explicitly identifies it as a silicon fuzz pedal based on the Dallas-Arbiter Fuzz Face circuit.
- **Imperial MKII — Fuzz** — official product page identifies it as a fuzz pedal inspired by the 1970s JEN Jumbo Fuzz.
- **76 MKII — Octave-Up Fuzz** — official product page identifies it as a multi-voiced fuzz based on the Standard Fuzz family, with octave-up switching and dedicated tone-shaping controls.
- **Lysis MKII — Polyphonic Octave Fuzz Modulator** — official product page explicitly describes an analog square-wave fuzz circuit feeding the pedal's digital polyphonic modulation/pitch section. It is retained as a fuzz-bearing multi-effect identity rather than treated as a conventional standalone fuzz-only pedal.

## Verification notes
1. SolidGoldFX's official About page identifies Greg Djerrahian as CEO & Founder, dates the company to 2006, and states that its pedals are made by hand in Montreal.
2. The current official collection and dirt pages list Commodore Overdrive, BETA-V, Fuzzbox, If 6 Was 9 BC183 MKII, Imperial MKII, 76 MKII, and Lysis MKII among the company's current/active product identities.
3. Commodore's official page explicitly calls it an overdrive pedal and documents its low- to mid-gain asymmetric-clipping drive circuit.
4. BETA-V's official page explicitly calls it a bass preamp and overdrive effects pedal and documents its Drive control and warm-overdrive range.
5. Fuzzbox's official page explicitly identifies it as a fuzz and describes its Tone Bender Mk II lineage without requiring a separate historical identity to be created.
6. If 6 Was 9 BC183 MKII's official page explicitly identifies it as a silicon fuzz pedal based on the Dallas-Arbiter Fuzz Face circuit.
7. Imperial MKII's official page explicitly identifies it as a fuzz pedal inspired by the JEN Jumbo Fuzz.
8. 76 MKII's official page explicitly identifies it as an octave-up fuzz and describes its multi-voiced fuzz architecture.
9. Lysis MKII's official page explicitly identifies an analog square-wave fuzz circuit inside a broader polyphonic octave/modulation effect. It is retained as a dirt-bearing multi-effect, not promoted to a standalone fuzz-only identity.
10. The current official catalog does not provide a sufficiently explicit standalone SolidGoldFX distortion classification beyond the documented overdrive/fuzz products, so no distortion entry is invented for this block.
11. Cosmetic editions and revisions are not split into separate base identities unless the builder presents them as distinct named products. The archive therefore records the named pedal identities rather than every finish or stock-state difference.

## Exclusions
- Modulation, delay, reverb, compressor, and utility products are excluded.
- **Aurras** is excluded as a vibraphase/modulation product despite its product text mentioning distortion in the context of the phase-shifter signal path.
- **Beta-V** is retained because the builder explicitly classifies it as an overdrive effects pedal, even though its primary identity is a bass preamp.
- Lysis MKII is retained only as a fuzz-bearing multi-effect because the official page explicitly identifies its analog fuzz circuit.

## Block conclusion
**SolidGoldFX — Greg Djerrahian** is added to the Builder → Pedals census with a primary-source-verified dirt footprint spanning overdrive and fuzz, plus a documented fuzz-bearing multi-effect. The current official catalog does not justify forcing a separate standalone distortion entry, so the block remains conservative rather than inferring categories from generic saturation language.
