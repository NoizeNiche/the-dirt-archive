# Block 091 — Mid-Fi Electronics

## Builder
**Mid-Fi Electronics / Doug Tuttle**

## Verification basis
Primary and authoritative sources reviewed:
- Mid-Fi Electronics official pedal catalog and individual product pages
- Mid-Fi Electronics official purchase catalog
- Mid-Fi Electronics official pages for Magick "i", Full Frequency Overdrive, Hieracium, Cocoon, House Amp, Yard Sale, Demo Tape Fuzz, Psych Byke, Peace Gun, Glitch Computer, and Random Number Generator
- authoritative Effects Database catalog coverage for Mid-Fi Electronics dirt products
- contemporary retailer/catalog documentation for Clari(not) Fuzz and Country Fuzz
- contemporary product documentation for Another/An-other Fuzz Version and Rise/OverRun
- active-repository search for “Mid-Fi” and “Mid Fi” before addition

The active repository contained no prior Mid-Fi match before this block was added. Mid-Fi Electronics is treated as the builder/brand identity, with **Doug Tuttle** used as the associated designer/builder name where useful for identification. The census stays focused on standalone physical Overdrive, Distortion, and Fuzz pedals and does not convert modulation, pitch, delay, or other non-dirt functions into target identities.

The reviewed material supports a primarily fuzz-oriented Mid-Fi catalog with several explicit Overdrive products and no separately established standalone Distortion product identity beyond pedals already documented as fuzz or overdrive hybrids.

## Overdrive
Current / manufacturer-supported identities:
- Magick "i"
- Hieracium
- Full Frequency Overdrive
- Yard Sale

Historical / manufacturer-supported identity:
- Overdrive

Verification notes:
- **Magick "i"** is explicitly described by Mid-Fi Electronics as an envelope-controlled overdrive. It is the developed successor to the earlier **Overdrive** pedal described in the builder interview material.
- **Hieracium** is explicitly described by Mid-Fi Electronics as envelope-controlled overdrive with a small tweed-amp character.
- **Full Frequency Overdrive** is explicitly described by Mid-Fi Electronics as an uncompressed, transparent overdrive.
- **Yard Sale** is documented by the builder as a low-impedance, blown-out consumer-electronics-inspired gain pedal; contemporary product listings classify it as Overdrive and Fuzz. The archive records it in both categories because the fuzz/overdrive boundary is part of the product's documented identity.
- Historical **Overdrive** is retained as a separate dated predecessor identity only where needed to document Mid-Fi's early product lineage. Mid-Fi's own interview material states that Overdrive later evolved into Magick "i".

## Distortion
- No standalone Mid-Fi Electronics product was established with sufficiently explicit builder-level product-category wording for Distortion as its primary identity.

Verification notes:
- Mid-Fi products often reach aggressive, blown-out, or highly saturated sounds, but generic use of “distortion” in descriptions does not automatically create a separate Distortion category.
- **Demo Tape Fuzz**, **House Amp**, **Glitch Computer**, **Peace Gun**, **Psych Byke**, and related products remain under Fuzz where the builder or authoritative catalog explicitly identifies them as fuzz products.

## Fuzz
Current / manufacturer-supported identities:
- Rise/OverRun
- Cocoon
- House Amp
- Faulty Channel
- Demo Tape Fuzz
- Another/An-other (Fuzz Version)
- Country Fuzz
- Peace Gun
- Psych Byke
- Glitch Computer
- Random Number Generator

Current / historical / catalog-supported identities:
- Fuzz Wall
- What?
- Clari(not) Fuzz
- Yard Sale

Verification notes:
- **Rise/OverRun** is a current Mid-Fi pedal whose documented core is a gritty fuzz foundation feeding reactive filtering; it is retained as Fuzz rather than treated as a separate modulation product.
- **Cocoon** is explicitly described by Mid-Fi as low-to-mid gain spongy/gritty fuzz.
- **House Amp** uses a dedicated fuzz-amount control and is documented as a busted-amp fuzz pedal.
- **Faulty Channel** is explicitly sold/documented as Fuzz.
- **Demo Tape Fuzz** is explicitly identified by Mid-Fi as a fuzz pedal designed around the sound of guitar driven directly into a 4-track recorder.
- **Another/An-other (Fuzz Version)** is a separately named fuzz version of the Another/An-other design and is retained because the builder's purchase catalog explicitly lists the Fuzz Version.
- **Country Fuzz** is documented by Mid-Fi-related retail material as a fuzz inspired by 1960s country recordings.
- **Peace Gun** is documented as an intermodulating fuzz with a resonant filter.
- **Psych Byke** is explicitly described by Mid-Fi as a buzzy 1960s fuzz.
- **Glitch Computer** is explicitly described as high-gain fuzz with poorly tracking two-octave-down harmony.
- **Random Number Generator** is explicitly described by Mid-Fi as fuzz with random harmonically related low-frequency arpeggiation.
- **Fuzz Wall**, **What?**, and **Clari(not) Fuzz** are supported by authoritative catalog/retail coverage as Mid-Fi fuzz products.
- **Yard Sale** is retained as Fuzz as well as Overdrive because contemporary product documentation classifies it as Overdrive and Fuzz.

## Category-overlap decisions
- **Yard Sale**: Overdrive + Fuzz. The builder describes a blown-out, consumer-electronics-inspired gain circuit that cleans up strongly from the instrument volume, while contemporary product listings classify it under both Overdrive/Boost and Fuzz.
- **Rise/OverRun**: Fuzz only for the target taxonomy. Its pitch-reactive filters and modulation are secondary to the fuzz foundation and do not create a separate target identity.
- **House Amp**: Fuzz only. The broken-amp character includes aggressive drive tones, but Mid-Fi's own control description identifies the core gain control as fuzz amount.
- **Demo Tape Fuzz**: Fuzz only. Its saturation can move toward overdrive-like textures, but the builder's named product category is Fuzz.
- **Cocoon**: Fuzz only. Its relatively low gain does not justify a second Overdrive category when the builder explicitly identifies it as Fuzz.
- **Faulty Channel**: Fuzz only. Milder grit settings do not create a second category.
- **Magick "i" / historical Overdrive**: Overdrive only. Generic references to gain or clipping intensity do not create a separate Distortion identity.

## Historical / variant handling
- **Overdrive** and **Magick "i"** are documented as a builder lineage, with Magick "i" representing the later developed form of the earlier Overdrive design. The archive records both only when chronology/product identity requires it, not as cosmetic variants.
- **Clari(not) Clean** and **Clari(not) Fuzz** are treated as functionally distinct named versions because Mid-Fi's catalog explicitly offers separate clean and fuzz versions. The clean version is excluded; the Fuzz version is retained.
- **Another/An-other Clean Version** is excluded from the dirt census; **Another/An-other (Fuzz Version)** is retained as the explicitly dirt-oriented named version.
- Hand-painted artwork, enclosure colors, and normal production changes do not create additional dirt identities.
- Contemporary variants and limited colorways are consolidated unless the builder explicitly names a functionally distinct product version.

## Unpromoted / cautious leads
- **Wasteoid** and **Yard Sale** have dirt-adjacent descriptions in current or secondary material, but Yard Sale has enough explicit dual-category evidence to promote while Wasteoid does not have sufficient product-level category wording in the reviewed sources.
- **Faulty Channel**, **House Amp**, and **Rise/OverRun** can produce sounds commonly described as distortion or overdrive, but those descriptors are not used to create extra categories when the product's named identity is Fuzz.
- The historical Mid-Fi catalog contains one-off/custom combinations and crossover designs. They are not promoted into the main census unless a distinct named product identity is documented.

## Exclusions
- Wave Folder, Electric Yggdrasil, Standard Vibrato, Thought Form, Scrape Flutter, Random Phase & Vibrato, Random Vibrato, Deluxe Pitch Pirate, Clari(not) Clean, Audio Frequency Sample Rate Converter, Pitch Pirate, and other modulation, pitch, delay, or processing products are outside target scope.
- LSD1820 Propaganda Module and other synth/processing-oriented devices are excluded unless the builder presents them as standalone dirt pedals.
- One-off custom combinations, Franken-pedals, and customer-specific builds are not converted into new catalog identities.
- Purely descriptive references to clipping, saturation, broken electronics, or “distortion” do not create a separate category without product-level support.
- Cloning, schematics, components, internal construction, and reverse-engineering material are outside scope.
