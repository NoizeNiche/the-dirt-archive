# Builder → Pedals Research Block 105

## Builder
**Fulltone — Mike Fuller / Fulltone USA**

## Verification basis
Fulltone's current official catalog separates Overdrive, Distortion, and Fuzz collections and currently lists the OCD family, Full-Drive family, PlimSoul, Hi-Mu, Mas Malo, Queen Bee, 69 Pedal mkII, 70 Pedal-BC, Soul-Bender v2, Ultimate Octave, and Octafuzz 2. Current Fulltone product pages were used as the primary source. Historical identities were added only where product-level evidence was sufficiently direct from Fulltone manuals or established specialist references.

The archive distinguishes product identity from mere gain range. A pedal that can move from overdrive into distortion is not automatically duplicated into both categories unless Fulltone itself presents it as both or gives the product a distinct distortion function. Fuzz products with distortion or overdrive range remain Fuzz-only unless the builder clearly establishes the second dirt identity.

## Product notes
- **OCD:** Fulltone's current site places the OCD family in both Overdrive and Distortion. The OCD product page describes its characteristic amp-like drive and distortion behavior; the archive retains the named OCD identity across its normal V1.x/V2-era lineage rather than creating a separate entry for every serial-number-era variation.
- **OCD-Germanium:** Fulltone sells this as part of the OCD family and also places it in the current Distortion collection. It is retained in both Overdrive and Distortion rather than treated as a separate unrelated family.
- **Full-Drive1:** Fulltone explicitly describes this as the Full-Drive overdrive sound without the boost channel. It remains Overdrive only.
- **Full-Drive2:** the historical Full-Drive 2 lineage is fundamentally an overdrive pedal with a boost/distortion channel. Fulltone documentation describes the Boost Mode as increasing distortion, while specialist product references classify the pedal primarily as Overdrive. The archive retains the family as Overdrive rather than creating a generic Distortion category from the boost channel alone.
- **Full-Drive2 v2:** Fulltone's current product page explicitly labels the DRIVE 2 section as adding distortion and the official catalog also places Full-Drive2 v2 in the Distortion collection. It is therefore retained in both Overdrive and Distortion.
- **Full-Drive3:** Fulltone describes this as having independent OVERDRIVE and BOOST channels. It remains Overdrive; the boost is outside the target categories.
- **PlimSoul mkII:** Fulltone explicitly presents PlimSoul as a dual-stage clipping design combining soft-clipping overdrive and hard-clipping distortion and calls it an Overdrive/Distortion pedal. It is retained in both categories.
- **Hi-Mu:** Fulltone's current Distortion collection includes Hi-Mu and its product page says the Sustain control makes the sound more distorted. Because the official collection itself treats it as Distortion, it is retained there. The current page does not give it a separate explicit Overdrive identity, so it is not promoted into Overdrive from lower-gain behavior.
- **Mas Malo:** Fulltone explicitly describes this as Distortion/Fuzz, with massive fuzz and distortion and the ability to back down to more restrained tones. It is retained in both Distortion and Fuzz.
- **GT-500:** historical Fulltone documentation and period references identify the GT-500 as a combined overdrive and distortion pedal with separate circuits. It is retained in both Overdrive and Distortion as one named pedal.
- **Distortion Pro:** historical product documentation identifies the DP-1 as a dedicated Fulltone distortion pedal. It is retained under Distortion only.
- **Bass-Drive:** historical product references identify Bass-Drive as the bass-oriented member of the Full-Drive family and an overdrive pedal. It remains Overdrive only.
- **69 Pedal mkII:** Fulltone explicitly places this in the Fuzz collection and describes it as a FuzzFace-style germanium fuzz. Its bias control can produce overdrive/distortion sounds, but those are operating range within the fuzz identity.
- **70 Pedal-BC:** Fulltone explicitly identifies this as a 1970-style fuzz and describes much more fuzz than the 69 Pedal. It remains Fuzz only.
- **Queen Bee:** Fulltone explicitly describes Queen Bee as a fuzz using three germanium transistors. The page discusses added sustain and distortion but the named product identity is Fuzz, so it remains Fuzz only.
- **Soul-Bender v2:** Fulltone explicitly identifies Soul-Bender as a Tone Bender-based germanium fuzz. It remains Fuzz only.
- **Ultimate Octave:** Fulltone explicitly identifies the current Ultimate Octave as a transistor fuzz with a separate octave-up switch. It remains Fuzz only.
- **Octafuzz 2:** Fulltone explicitly identifies Octafuzz OF-2 as the Tycobrahe Octavia-type product and notes a Fuzz setting. It is retained historically as Fuzz even though the page mentions its unusual distortion character.

## Explicit exclusions / restraint
- **Ranger, 2B Booster, and other boost-first products:** excluded because their named identity is Boost rather than Overdrive, Distortion, or Fuzz.
- **Deja'Vibe, tremolo, wah, switching, and power-supply products:** excluded.
- **Fuzz gain-range inflation:** 69 Pedal mkII, Queen Bee, Soul-Bender, Ultimate Octave, and Octafuzz 2 are not duplicated into Overdrive or Distortion merely because their controls can produce harder or lower-gain textures.
- **OCD serial/V1.x/V2 naming:** consolidated as one OCD family identity where Fulltone itself treats the changes as small revisions, while OCD-Germanium remains a distinct current named product.
- **Full-Drive family:** normal revisions and MOSFET editions are consolidated where they represent the same underlying named Full-Drive identity. Full-Drive2 v2 remains separate because Fulltone presents it as a distinct current redesign.
- **Historical products:** GT-500, Distortion Pro, Bass-Drive, and the original Full-Drive are retained only because direct product-level evidence exists in period manuals or established product references. Weakly supported one-offs are not added.

**Verified research block.** Fulltone is a previously uncovered builder in the fresh census.
