# Block 074 — Ramble FX

## Builder
**Ramble FX**

## Verification basis
Primary and authoritative sources reviewed:
- archived Ramble FX website references and product identity references
- manufacturer/product documentation where available in archived dealer and manual records
- Effects Database Ramble FX brand catalog
- Guitar Pedal X archival profile of Ramble FX and Tim Krueger
- authoritative pedal documentation/manual reference for Twin Bender V3
- specialist retailer/product records for named Ramble FX models

Ramble FX is treated as a distinct builder identity. The active repository contained no prior Ramble FX match before this block was added.

The surviving documentation indicates a compact Ramble FX catalog centered on three clearly documented dirt pedals: Kismet, Marvel Drive, and Twin Bender. Dirt categories are assigned from explicit product descriptions or strong product-category documentation. Generic references to gain, clipping, harmonics, or fuzz-like behavior are not used to create unsupported secondary categories.

## Overdrive
Current / manufacturer-supported identities:
- none currently produced

Historical / manufacturer-supported identities:
- Kismet
- Marvel Drive

Verification notes:
- **Kismet** is documented as an analog Overdrive/Distortion pedal with digital control. Its wide gain range runs from dirty-boost/overdrive territory into substantial distortion. It is therefore retained in both Overdrive and Distortion below.
- **Marvel Drive** is documented as a Plexi-style Overdrive / amp-in-a-box design recreating the blended four-input Marshall gain-stage behavior. It is retained as Overdrive only for this census because its named product identity and strongest product-category references identify it as an overdrive/drive pedal rather than a separate Distortion product.

## Distortion
Current / manufacturer-supported identities:
- none currently produced

Historical / manufacturer-supported identities:
- Kismet

Verification notes:
- **Kismet** is explicitly documented as Analog Overdrive/Distortion and as a wide-ranging distortion pedal. It therefore receives both target categories.
- **Marvel Drive** can reach aggressive distortion textures, but the surviving product references identify it as a Plexi-style overdrive/drive product. No separate Distortion identity is created from range language alone.

## Fuzz
Current / manufacturer-supported identities:
- none currently produced

Historical / manufacturer-supported identities:
- Twin Bender

Verification notes:
- **Twin Bender** is explicitly documented as a Tone Bender-based Fuzz. The V3 manual describes MKII Pro and MKI.V modes, three-transistor/two-transistor fuzz stages, fuzz control, and germanium operation. It is therefore recorded as Fuzz only.

## Category-overlap decisions
- **Kismet**: Overdrive + Distortion. Product descriptions explicitly use both categories.
- **Marvel Drive**: Overdrive only. The product is consistently presented as a Plexi-style overdrive/drive device; aggressive gain is treated as range rather than a separate Distortion identity.
- **Twin Bender**: Fuzz only. The product is explicitly a Tone Bender-style fuzz; amplifier-pushing or gain language does not create an Overdrive/Distortion identity.

## Historical / variant handling
- Ramble FX was a small catalog, and surviving documentation centers on three core dirt products: Kismet, Marvel Drive, and Twin Bender.
- **Marvel Drive V1 / V2 / V3** are treated as revisions of the same functional product identity unless the builder presents a distinct named model. The later **Marvel Drive 3** remains the named identity in the census rather than generating separate entries for every version.
- **Twin Bender MK I.V / II / V3** are retained as the Twin Bender functional identity rather than separate dirt products. Distinct mode names inside the pedal do not create new builder/product identities.
- **Kismet** remains a single functional identity despite its programmable presets and digital control layer; those are control features, not separate dirt models.
- Cosmetic colors and limited presentation variants are consolidated.
- Ramble FX is treated as historical/discontinued in this census. The builder's product lineage is still retained because the archive's scope includes historical dirt pedals.

## Exclusions
- Non-dirt utility functions and digital preset/MIDI control features: excluded as secondary functionality.
- Generic distortion/gain language on Marvel Drive: insufficient for a separate Distortion identity.
- Any unverified fourth model or ambiguous “Rainier” listing: excluded. The reviewed specialist references conflict on that name, and no sufficiently strong Ramble FX product-level evidence was found to promote it into the census.
- Cloning, schematics, internal construction, component details, and other technical reverse-engineering material: outside scope.
