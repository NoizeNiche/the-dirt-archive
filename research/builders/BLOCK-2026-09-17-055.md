# The Dirt Archive — Builder Research Block 055

## Builder
**BIXONIC — Bright International Corp. / BIXONIC Inc.; relaunched under Kikusui Electronics Corp.**

## Verification basis
Primary manufacturer sources used:
- BIXONIC official Story / history
- BIXONIC official New Arrival page
- BIXONIC official product page for EXP2000DR
- BIXONIC official Axentrix II announcement
- BIXONIC official 2025/2026 product announcements

The official BIXONIC history identifies Bright International Corp. as the origin of the BIXONIC brand, records the original EXPANDORA production beginning in 1994, the EXPANDORA II in 1999, the EXPANDORA REISSUE EXP-2000R in 2004, and the 2020 relaunch under Kikusui Electronics Corp. The same manufacturer material identifies the newer AXENTRIX A1 as a successor/new-generation product and explicitly describes its four drive modes as Crunch, Overdrive, Distortion, and Fuzz.

## Product-level verification notes
- **EXPANDORA family**: BIXONIC's official history states that the original product produced three distortion tones and also had a Fuzz/Forbidden mode. BIXONIC's current New Arrival page explicitly describes the prior Bixonic Expandora as having Crunch, Overdrive, Distortion, and Fuzz modes. The archive keeps the historical EXP-2000, EXP-2001, and EXP-2000R as one named Expandora family identity rather than multiplying the census with revision-only duplicates.
- **AXENTRIX A1**: BIXONIC's official New Arrival page explicitly identifies Crunch, Overdrive, Distortion, and Fuzz modes. It is therefore recorded in all three target dirt categories.
- **EXP2000DR**: BIXONIC's official product page describes Clean plus three Drive modes: Crunch, Distortion, and Forbidden. BIXONIC's official history identifies the Forbidden mode as the fuzz mode of the Expandora lineage. It is therefore recorded under Distortion and Fuzz. No separate Overdrive entry is promoted because the current EXP2000DR page does not explicitly label one of its three modes as Overdrive.
- **AXENTRIX II**: BIXONIC's official announcement identifies Clean, Crunch, Distortion, and Forbidden (Fuzz) modes. It is therefore recorded under Distortion and Fuzz. Crunch is not independently promoted into Overdrive because BIXONIC does not explicitly label that mode Overdrive on the verified product announcement.

## Scope decisions / exclusions
- **Sugar Blues** is excluded because BIXONIC explicitly identifies it as an auto-wah, not a target dirt pedal.
- The archive does not count Crunch as Overdrive unless the manufacturer explicitly identifies it as Overdrive.
- The original EXPANDORA, EXPANDORA II, and EXPANDORA REISSUE are consolidated as one historical named family identity for this builder census. Their model numbers remain documented so future research can distinguish them if the later archive phase requires it.
- BIXONIC's current product announcements and historical material are treated as authoritative for the builder-to-pedal relationship.
- No additional dirt products are promoted from third-party listings without manufacturer support.
