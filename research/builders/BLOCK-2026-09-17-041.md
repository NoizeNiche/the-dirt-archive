# Builder → Pedals Research Block 041

## Builder
**Analog Alien — Jack & Joe Napoli**

## Verification basis
Analog Alien's official site identifies brothers **Joe and Jack Napoli** as the founders who continue to run the company and assemble its pedals. Official Analog Alien product pages, manuals, and the company's own historical/current catalog material were used to establish dirt identities. The census remains conservative: a pedal is categorized only when Analog Alien itself explicitly describes an overdrive, distortion, or fuzz circuit/function.

## Product notes
- **Rumble Seat:** official product page identifies it as a multi-effects pedal containing an Overdrive, Delay, and Reverb circuit. The Overdrive section ranges from tone enhancement through full overdrive. The newer 2026 New-Look Rumble Seat is the redesigned presentation of the same Rumble Seat product identity, so it is not counted separately.
- **Bucket Seat:** official product page calls it an overdrive pedal and identifies it as using the Rumble Drive circuit from the Rumble Seat. It is retained as its own pedal identity because it is a standalone product.
- **Joe Walsh Double Classic:** official page identifies the pedal as a compressor/overdrive product. The Classic Amp circuit introduces distortion as gain is increased, but the manufacturer labels the product's dirt function as overdrive, so it is not promoted into the separate Distortion category solely from that operating description.
- **FuzzBubble-45:** official product page explicitly identifies separate Overdrive and Fuzz sides. Analog Alien's own historical material also describes the FuzzBubble-45 as a fuzz-face/distortion pedal, so it is retained in Overdrive, Fuzz, and Distortion.
- **Alien Tone Dragon (ATD):** official product page explicitly identifies the Dragon Fire section as an Overdrive/Fuzz circuit, ranging from Rock/Blues overdrive to a wall of fuzz. It is retained in both categories. The clean boost and active EQ sections are not counted as dirt identities.
- **Alien Twister:** official product page explicitly calls it a fuzz, distortion, and overdrive pedal. It is therefore retained in all three categories.
- **Alien Bass Station (ABS):** official product page identifies a dedicated Gamma Fuzz section and an Amp Generator with an explicit Overdrive setting. The manufacturer's manual also describes the Gamma Fuzz as becoming distortion-like at higher frequencies. It is therefore retained in Overdrive, Distortion, and Fuzz, while the compressor and amp-generator functions are not treated as separate pedal identities.

## Explicit exclusions / restraint
- **Double Classic Comp / Alien Comp:** excluded from the categorized census because Analog Alien defines it as a compressor/clean boost. The ability to push an amplifier into overdrive is not treated as a standalone overdrive circuit.
- **EPi Effects Pedal Interface:** excluded as a routing/recording interface.
- **Power Pack:** excluded as a clean boost.
- **New-Look Rumble Seat:** not split from Rumble Seat because Analog Alien explicitly describes it as the same Rumble Seat with a redesigned presentation.
- **Cosmetic changes:** not split into separate identities unless Analog Alien presents a materially distinct pedal.
