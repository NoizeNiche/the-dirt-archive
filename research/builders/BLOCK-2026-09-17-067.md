# Block 067 — Pigtronix

## Builder
**Pigtronix**

## Verification basis
Primary manufacturer sources reviewed:
- Pigtronix About page
- current All Pedals catalog
- current Micro Pedals catalog
- current Product Manuals archive
- current Legacy Products archive
- individual manufacturer product pages
- manufacturer-authored product manuals
- manufacturer FAQ for historical product relationships

Pigtronix identifies itself as a boutique pedal maker and maintains a current pedal catalog plus separate current/legacy support indexes. The reviewed manufacturer pages explicitly identify Overdrive, Distortion, and Fuzz functions for multiple products. This block records only builder/product relationships and uses manufacturer wording and product taxonomy rather than inferring additional dirt categories from general saturation language.

## Overdrive
Current manufacturer-supported identities:
- Gamma Drive
- Philosopher's Tone 2
- Philosopher's Gold
- Disnortion

Historical / verification-lead identities not promoted into the core census:
- FAT Drive
- Bass FAT Drive
- XL FAT Drive
- PolySaturator

Verification notes:
- Gamma Drive is explicitly identified by Pigtronix as an overdrive with 2-band EQ. Its manufacturer product data lists Overdrive as Category 1 and Distortion as Category 2, so both target categories are recorded below.
- Philosopher's Tone 2 is primarily a compressor/sustainer, but the manufacturer explicitly describes a footswitchable Grit circuit using an overdrive stage and says the engaged circuit can range from subtle overdrive to overflowing distortion. It is therefore retained in both target dirt categories while its compression function remains outside scope.
- Philosopher's Gold is explicitly described as a compressor plus germanium-enhanced distortion, and its manufacturer product data identifies the function as Compressor Sustainer Overdrive. It is retained in both Overdrive and Distortion because the product-level language explicitly supports both.
- Disnortion is explicitly identified by Pigtronix as an Analog Fuzz + Overdrive / Parallel Fuzz and Overdrive pedal. It is retained in Overdrive and Fuzz; the manual's generic use of “Distortion” to describe gain in the fuzz and overdrive stages is not treated as a separate Distortion category.
- Pigtronix's FAQ confirms the existence of the historical FAT Drive and Bass FAT Drive relationship, but the reviewed manufacturer material does not provide sufficiently explicit product-level target-category classification to promote them into the categorized census.

## Distortion
Current manufacturer-supported identities:
- Gamma Drive
- Philosopher's Tone 2
- Philosopher's Gold
- Octava
- Star Eater

Historical / verification-lead identities not promoted into the core census:
- FAT Drive
- XL FAT Drive
- PolySaturator

Verification notes:
- Gamma Drive's manufacturer model data explicitly lists Distortion as Category 2 alongside Overdrive.
- Philosopher's Tone 2 explicitly describes its Grit circuit as germanium/silicon distortion and says the nonlinear diode clipping ranges from subtle overdrive to overflowing distortion.
- Philosopher's Gold is explicitly described by Pigtronix as compressor + germanium distortion, with a germanium overdrive stage blended by its Grit control.
- Octava's manufacturer model data lists Distortion as Category 1 even though its function is Analog Octave Fuzz; the product description also explicitly describes full-wave rectified distortion. It is therefore recorded in both Distortion and Fuzz.
- Star Eater's manufacturer model data lists Distortion as Category 1 while the function is Super-Jumbo Fuzz with Filter. The manufacturer description repeatedly identifies the effect as fuzz, so it is retained in both Distortion and Fuzz based on explicit manufacturer taxonomy/function fields.
- FAT Drive and related historical drive products are retained only as verification leads because the reviewed current Pigtronix archive does not expose sufficiently strong manufacturer category data to classify them without relying on secondary sources.

## Fuzz
Current manufacturer-supported identities:
- Gas Giant
- Star Eater
- Octava
- Disnortion

Verification notes:
- Gas Giant is explicitly identified as an Analog Jumbo Fuzz and is current in the Pigtronix pedal catalog.
- Star Eater is explicitly identified as an Analog Dual Stage Super Jumbo Fuzz and its manufacturer manual describes it as a super-jumbo fuzz.
- Octava is explicitly identified as an Analog Octave Fuzz. The manufacturer describes its front-end drive plus fuzz circuit and identifies Distortion in its model taxonomy.
- Disnortion is explicitly identified as Analog Fuzz + Overdrive and as a Parallel Fuzz and Overdrive design. It is retained in both target categories without adding Distortion from generic gain terminology.

## Category-overlap decisions
- **Gamma Drive**: Overdrive + Distortion. Pigtronix explicitly lists both categories in its product model data.
- **Philosopher's Tone 2**: Overdrive + Distortion. Manufacturer text explicitly documents an overdrive stage and distortion output range when Grit is engaged.
- **Philosopher's Gold**: Overdrive + Distortion. Manufacturer product data identifies an Overdrive function and the product description explicitly identifies germanium-enhanced distortion.
- **Disnortion**: Overdrive + Fuzz. Manufacturer function/category wording explicitly identifies both; the manual's use of “distortion” as a control quantity is not promoted into a separate Distortion identity.
- **Octava**: Fuzz + Distortion. Manufacturer function is Analog Octave Fuzz while model taxonomy explicitly lists Distortion.
- **Star Eater**: Fuzz + Distortion. Manufacturer function is Super-Jumbo Fuzz with Filter while model taxonomy explicitly lists Distortion.
- **Gas Giant**: Fuzz only. Its built-in noise gate and voicing switches are secondary features around the explicitly named fuzz circuit.

## Historical / variant handling
- Pigtronix current and legacy support pages can expose both current product pages and discontinued families. A product appearing in the current manual archive is not assumed to be currently manufactured if the Legacy Products page explicitly marks it discontinued; status is recorded conservatively in the relevant notes.
- **Disnortion / Disnortion Micro**: consolidated under the current Disnortion identity because Pigtronix presents the Micro product as the compact current expression of the Disnortion design rather than a separate dirt family.
- **Philosopher's Tone / Philosopher's Tone 2**: retained as distinct named products because the Tone 2 adds a footswitchable Grit circuit and is independently named by the manufacturer.
- **Philosopher's Gold**: retained as a distinct named product because it is a separately named compressor + germanium distortion/overdrive variant.
- FAT Drive, Bass FAT Drive, XL FAT Drive, and PolySaturator are not split into the categorized census without sufficiently explicit manufacturer product-category evidence in the reviewed current/legacy archive. They remain verification leads only.

## Exclusions
- Compressor-only Philosopher's Tone is excluded from the dirt census despite being historically related to the dirt-enabled Gold/Tone 2 variants.
- Class A Boost is excluded because the manufacturer classifies it as a clean boost/Utility, even though the page mentions saturation and pushing distortion pedals.
- Gatekeeper and other noise-gate/utility products are excluded.
- Delay, reverb, modulation, synth, looper, ring-modulator, and other non-dirt products are excluded.
- Generic references to gain, saturation, crunch, or distortion-like behavior do not create a new category unless Pigtronix explicitly supplies target-category support.
