# The Dirt Archive — Builder Census Block 112

## Builder
**Hudson Electronics UK**

Builder identity is tied directly to the Hudson Electronics product pages. Current Hudson pages identify the pedals as individually hand-built and repeatedly name **Michael Hudson** as the builder. The surviving catalog contains both current pedals and an official archived section.

## Scope result
Hudson has a strong, unusually broad dirt footprint across the three target categories. Current products are concentrated around the Broadcast family and Sidecar, while the archived catalog preserves a substantial group of fuzz and overdrive designs.

Normal Broadcast family editions and color/retail variants are consolidated rather than counted as separate pedals. Limited collaborations are retained only when they are materially distinct named products.

## Explicit category overlaps
- **Broadcast:** Overdrive + Distortion + Fuzz. Hudson's official Broadcast description covers transparent overdrive at lower gain and heavier distorted sounds at high gain; the Broadcast family documentation explicitly reaches fuzz.
- **Sidecar:** Overdrive + Distortion + Fuzz. Hudson describes it as an 808-derived overdrive that can cover classic overdrive and distortion and, at extreme settings, fuzz.
- **Broadcast-AP:** Overdrive only. Hudson explicitly says it covers clean boost through medium-gain overdrive and can become overloaded/broken/fuzzy at maximum gain, but does not present a separate distortion identity.
- **Motorcycle:** Overdrive only. Hudson describes it as a modified Sidecar overdrive and a revisit of the historical Overdrive 999.
- **Pretty Flamingo:** Fuzz only. Hudson calls it a 1967-style fuzz; its access to lighter overdrive/distortion tones is treated as behavior inside the fuzz design, not a second target identity.
- **Buzzard:** Fuzz only. Hudson describes it as a fuzz design with a dark boost side and notes dense, chewy distortion as a sound, but does not present a separate distortion product identity.
- **Full Range Bomb:** Fuzz only. Hudson describes a footswitchable light fuzz circuit alongside parallel boosts; boost remains outside the archive's target categories.
- **Soul Bomb:** Fuzz only. Hudson explicitly describes the Soul side as a germanium fuzz cascaded into a boost; boost remains outside scope.

## Historical/variant restraint
- The official Hudson product catalog currently shows the current pedal group as Broadcast, Broadcast dual-foot-switch, Sidecar, Broadcast AP-II, Broadcast-AP, and limited Broadcast editions. These are not treated as a dozen separate Broadcast products. The underlying Broadcast identity is consolidated.
- The official archived category lists 15 historical products. Only the dirt-relevant entries are pulled into the target census; **Gospel Bomb** is excluded as boost-first. Other non-target or ambiguous items are not promoted merely because they can become dirty.
- Broadcast AP-II remains outside the categorized dirt list for now. Hudson presents it as a dual preamplifier with large gain ranges that can reach heavily distorted sounds, but the surviving official description does not establish a separate named Overdrive, Distortion, or Fuzz identity strongly enough for conservative classification.
- The surviving official Overdrive Eight Fifty page is not reliably available in the current crawl. It is included only because the product is confirmed in Hudson's archived product catalog and an established effects database classifies it as fuzz and identifies its old-Ibanez-OD850/BMP lineage. This is a lower-confidence historical classification than the directly documented Hudson pages.
- No component, schematic, cloning, or internal-build information is being recorded in this block.
