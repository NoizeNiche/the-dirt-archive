# Block 083 — HardWire

## Builder
**HardWire**

## Verification basis
Primary and authoritative sources reviewed:
- DigiTech official Product Manuals archive under the HARDWIRE heading
- DigiTech official FAQ/power-supply documentation listing HardWire models
- manufacturer-era HardWire product material preserved through authoritative pedal references
- Premier Guitar's historical review identifying the HardWire CM-2, SC-2, and line context
- active-repository search for “HardWire” before addition

The active repository contained no prior HardWire match before this block was added. HardWire is treated as a distinct builder/product-line identity for the HardWire-branded dirt pedals documented below, even though DigiTech manufacturer documentation now hosts the manuals. The repository's earlier DigiTech block deliberately kept HardWire separate, and this block preserves that distinction.

The reviewed material supports three clearly documented HardWire dirt identities: one Overdrive and two Distortion products. No HardWire-branded Fuzz identity was established in the reviewed manufacturer material.

## Overdrive

Verification notes:
- **CM-2 Tube Overdrive** is explicitly named and documented by DigiTech's HardWire manual archive and historical manufacturer-era material as an overdrive pedal. Its Classic and Modified voicings remain internal modes of one named product and do not create additional identities.

## Category-overlap decisions
- **CM-2 Tube Overdrive**: Overdrive only from explicit product naming and manufacturer documentation.
- **SC-2 Valve Distortion**: Distortion only. Historical descriptions of lower-gain Crunch tones do not create a separate Overdrive identity.
- **TL-2 Metal Distortion**: Distortion only. Tight/Loose controls affect EQ/response and do not create another dirt category.

## Historical / variant handling
- HardWire product-line documentation is now hosted within DigiTech's support ecosystem, but HardWire remains a separate builder/product-line identity in this census because the pedals were explicitly branded and marketed as HardWire products.
- Product voicings, control modes, hardware revisions, and cosmetic presentation do not create additional dirt identities.
- The HardWire line's non-dirt pedals are not included.

## Exclusions
- CR-7 Stereo Chorus, DL-8 Delay/Looper, HT-2 Chromatic Tuner, HT-6 Polyphonic Tuner, RV-7 Stereo Reverb, SP-7 Stereo Phaser, TR-7 Tremolo/Rotary, and Supernatural Ambient Reverb: outside target dirt taxonomy.
- DigiTech-branded and DOD-branded products remain separate builder/brand identities and are not absorbed into HardWire.
- Generic gain, saturation, or “amp-like” wording without explicit product-level target-category naming is not used to manufacture extra Overdrive, Distortion, or Fuzz identities.
- Cloning, schematics, components, internal construction, and reverse-engineering material: outside scope.
