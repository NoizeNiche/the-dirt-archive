# Builder → Pedals Research Block 035

## Builder
**Daredevil Pedals — Johnny Wator**

## Scope
Builder → Pedals only. This block records Daredevil's documented overdrive, distortion, and fuzz identities, including current products and clearly documented historical/discontinued identities. Non-dirt effects are excluded.

## Builder identity
Daredevil's official biography identifies Johnny Wator as the founder and describes the company as hand-building pedals in Chicago since 2012. The official site says the early work grew from vintage 1960s/70s circuits into predominantly original-design pedals. 

Primary source:
- https://www.daredevilpedals.com/bio

## Current / recently listed dirt pedals

### Overdrive
- **HYPE FET Boost V.2** — listed by Daredevil as a gain boost; retained here because the builder places it in the gain/dirt family, while its primary classification is boost rather than conventional overdrive.
- **the Chicago Rat** — builder describes selectable high-gain distortion and over-clipped fuzz modes; not counted as a standalone overdrive-only product.

### Distortion
- **Deep Dish-tortion** — current 2026 Daredevil listing.
- **Necros Fuzz / Distortion** — current limited-edition listing; builder explicitly identifies both fuzz and distortion.
- **DRIVE-Bi — Dual Gain Distortion** — official product page identifies a dual-channel op-amp gain circuit with low and high distortion channels.
- **COCKED & FEARLESS** — combines Daredevil's Atomic Cock fixed-wah circuit with the Fearless distortion circuit; builder explicitly describes the Fearless section as original-design op-amp distortion.
- **the Chicago Rat** — selectable high-gain distortion mode.

### Fuzz
- **REAL COOL FUZZ** — official page identifies it as a vintage-style silicon fuzz.
- **NORTHERN CREEPER Fuzz** — current Daredevil listing; builder identifies it as fuzz.
- **Ten Tone Anniversary Fuzz** — official page identifies it as silicon fuzz and dates the project to Daredevil's tenth anniversary.
- **DAISHO — Earl Slick Fuzz Octave** — official page identifies an original-design fuzz section with an independent octave-up circuit.
- **Shrineburner Fuzz — Ltd Ed. Green** — current limited-edition listing.
- **Ron Asheton Real Cool Fuzz — Ltd. Edition Wedge** — current limited-edition listing tied to the Real Cool Fuzz identity.
- **Daredevil Cry Baby Fuzz Wah** — collaboration product with a fuzz circuit, retained because Daredevil lists it among its products and it contains a dirt/fuzz function.
- **the Chicago Rat** — selectable over-clipped fuzz mode.

## Historical / discontinued dirt identities

### Overdrive
- **Premium Overdrive** — official discontinued page describes a hand-wired transistor overdrive inspired by early-1970s Colorsound Power Boost designs.
- **Chicago Way — Fuzz & OD** — official discontinued page documents a two-in-one pedal with independent overdrive and fuzz circuits.
- **The Chicago Way** is retained as one builder identity, not split into separate fake pedals for its internal circuits.

### Distortion
- **Fearless Distortion** — official Daredevil material identifies the Fearless as an original-design op-amp distortion. Its circuit is also documented inside COCKED & FEARLESS.
- **Red Light District** — historical Daredevil identity; contemporary coverage describes it as built around the Fearless Distortion with additional fixed-wah-style filtering and gain switching.
- **British Black Belt** — appears in Daredevil's discontinued catalog. Because the current accessible manufacturer page exposes the name but not enough classification detail, it is retained as a verification lead rather than forced into the categorized distortion list.

### Fuzz
- **Supernova Fuzz** — historical Daredevil fuzz identity documented in distributor material.
- **OP Amp Fuzz V5** — historical Daredevil fuzz identity documented in distributor/artist references.
- **Real Cool Fuzz** — historical/current identity; official page provides the current classification.
- **Wolf Deluxe Fuzz** — historical Daredevil fuzz identity documented in distributor material.
- **Northern Creeper** — historical/current identity; official current listing provides the current classification.
- **Logan Square Destroyer** — historical Daredevil fuzz identity documented in distributor material.
- **Nova** — historical Daredevil fuzz identity documented in contemporary builder coverage.
- **L.S.D.** — historical Daredevil fuzz identity documented in contemporary builder coverage.
- **Almighty Bass** — official discontinued page explicitly identifies it as an original-design bass fuzz, capable of tones ranging from driven tube-amp gain to aggressive fuzz.
- **Chicago Way — Fuzz & OD** — official discontinued page documents its fuzz circuit.

## Verification notes
1. The official Daredevil homepage currently lists a 2026 product set including Deep Dish-tortion, Necros Fuzz / Distortion, Double Cocked, Silver Solo Wedge, the Chicago Rat, Ten Tone Anniversary Fuzz, HYPE FET Boost V.2, DRIVE-Bi, Real Cool Fuzz, COCKED & FEARLESS, Northern Creeper, Shrineburner Fuzz, and the Daredevil Cry Baby Fuzz Wah. Only the dirt-bearing identities relevant to this archive are included above.
2. Daredevil's official product page for the Chicago Rat explicitly states that it is not a RAT circuit and documents both high-gain distortion and over-clipped fuzz modes.
3. Daredevil's official pages explicitly classify Ten Tone as silicon fuzz, Real Cool Fuzz as vintage-style silicon fuzz, DRIVE-Bi as dual gain distortion, Premium Overdrive as overdrive, and Almighty Bass as fuzz.
4. Distributor documentation independently identifies Daredevil as founded by Johnny Wator in Chicago in 2012 and lists historical dirt identities including Supernova Fuzz, Chicago Way, OP Amp Fuzz V5, Real Cool Fuzz, Wolf Deluxe Fuzz, Northern Creeper, Logan Square Destroyer, Fearless Distortion, Red Light District, and Premium OD.
5. **British Black Belt** remains a verification lead because the accessible official discontinued index exposes the product name without enough classification detail. It is deliberately not promoted into a dirt category.
6. Boost-only identities such as Silver Solo and HYPE are not treated as overdrive/distortion/fuzz unless a source explicitly gives them a dirt classification. HYPE is mentioned as a gain-family product but remains a boost identity.
7. Variant/cosmetic editions are not split into separate base products where the builder clearly presents them as editions of an established dirt identity.

## Exclusions
- Atomic Cock / Atomic Cocked fixed-wah products are excluded because they are filter/wah effects, not dirt pedals by themselves.
- Bootleg Dirty Delay and other delay/reverb/filter products are excluded.
- Silver Solo and HYPE are retained only as notes where relevant because their primary builder classification is boost rather than overdrive, distortion, or fuzz.
- Placebo is excluded because it is a non-effect novelty product.

## Block conclusion
**Daredevil Pedals — Johnny Wator** is added to the Builder → Pedals census as a builder with a substantial documented dirt catalog spanning overdrive, distortion, and fuzz, with both current and historical identities supported by primary Daredevil pages and authoritative distributor/catalog references.
