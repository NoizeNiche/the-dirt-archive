## Research notes

- Auxiliary Workshop is documented with the current AWOD35 overdrive and the discontinued The Devils Handshake fuzz/overdrive product.
- AXL is documented as an effects-pedal maker. Its catalog includes DEQ-2 Distortion EQ, DP-1 Distortion, and TOD-9 Tube Overdrive. The FX-RAP-5 is a multi-effects pedal that explicitly includes distortion and tube overdrive sections, so those dirt functions are recorded under the same product name.
- Avora FY-6 Super Fuzz is documented as a branded version of the Japanese FY-6 Super Fuzz circuit.
- Azor's product material and retail catalog listings identify dedicated overdrive, distortion, and fuzz pedals. Current product pages also identify the AP-302 and AP-507 as distortion products.
- Azur retail listings identify dedicated Overdrive, Fuzz, and LEON DRIVE pedals. The archive records the named dirt identities without inferring technical circuitry beyond the product category.
