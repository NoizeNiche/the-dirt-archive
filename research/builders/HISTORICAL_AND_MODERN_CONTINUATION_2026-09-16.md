### Rotosound
**State:** builder-confirmed / catalog in progress

Rotosound's official history documents its dirt-pedal activity beginning in the 1960s. The official timeline identifies the Rotosound Fuzz and Growler as historical fuzz products, and the company has also produced later limited pedal runs. Rotosound's current/recent official material confirms the RFB1 as a 1967 Fuzz Pedal Reissue and describes a broader seven-pedal boutique effects range, of which the RFB1 is the dirt product relevant to this archive.

**Initial dirt products to retain:**
- Rotosound Fuzz
- Growler
- RFB1 1967 Fuzz Pedal Reissue

**Sources:**
- https://www.rotosound.com/our-story/
- https://www.rotosound.com/blog/cool_timeline/2660/

- https://www.rotosound.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/02/RFB1-Fuzz-box-user-guide.pdf

**Identity note:** The existence of historical Rotosound-branded pedals manufactured by other parties is useful background, but that relationship is deferred. Rotosound remains its own recognizable builder/product family in the census.

## Research Notes

1. The current pass continues to favor builders with multiple dirt products.
2. Separate recognized builder/product identities remain separate even when a product name or family is related to another builder.
3. Same-builder generations, colorways, enclosure changes, and minor revisions should generally be grouped for the first pass.
4. Current Teisco is intentionally not merged with historical Teisco-branded Japanese effects until identity is established.
5. Rotosound deserves a separate builder entry even where manufacturing relationships with Sola Sound or Jennings appear in historical sources; those relationships are future deep-dive material.
6. All catalog entries remain research-stage until the public canonical catalog is built and validated.
