# Builder → Pedals Research Block 059

## Builder
**Mojo Hand FX — founded by Brad Fee; acquired by Cusack Music in 2015 and continued under Cusack Music**

## Verification posture
This block was researched as a builder-to-pedal census, not as a history or biography entry. Product names were retained only where the manufacturer currently categorizes them as dirt or where manufacturer-originated historical material / authoritative historical documentation supports the dirt identity.

Primary manufacturer sources were prioritized. Mojo Hand FX's official About page states that the brand began in 2004 as the pedal custom shop of Tone Factor, that Brad Fee evolved it into Mojo Hand FX around 2008, and that Brad sold Mojo Hand to Cusack Music in 2015. The same page says Mojo Hand pedals are designed and built in Holland, Michigan under the current operation.

  - https://mojohandfx.com/pedals/
  - https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/mojohand/rook
- Rook Royale: current manufacturer catalog explicitly labels it “Overdrive and Boost.”
  - https://mojohandfx.com/pedals/
- DMBL: current manufacturer catalog explicitly labels it “Holy Grail Amp Overdrive.”
  - https://mojohandfx.com/pedals/
- Extra Special: official product page describes it as the higher-gain development of the DMBL and places it in the medium-to-higher-gain drive family. It is retained as an Overdrive identity, not promoted to Distortion solely because of its gain range.
  - https://mojohandfx.com/extra-special/
- Magpie: official product page explicitly calls it a classic British-style overdrive and labels its low-gain setting “Overdrive.”
  - https://mojohandfx.com/magpie/
- Sacred Cow: official catalog labels it “Professional Gold Overdrive”; manufacturer-originated product copy explicitly describes overdrive behavior.
  - https://mojohandfx.com/pedals/
  - https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/mojohand/sacredcow
- Odessa: official product page explicitly labels it “Dual Overdrive” and states that it contains Rook and Magpie overdrive circuits.
  - https://mojohandfx.com/odessa/
- Rock City: official product page states it began as an improved Odessa dual overdrive and describes both internal circuits as overdrive circuits.
  - https://mojohandfx.com/rock-city/
- Sericon: manufacturer-originated release material calls it an overdrive and describes multiple gain stages from clean boost through edge-of-breakup to high-gain drive.
  - https://www.premierguitar.com/news/mojo-hand-fx-sericonoverdrive
  - https://fulleffectdistro.com/sericon/

  - https://mojohandfx.com/kensrue-rook/

  - https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/mojohand/bluebonnet
- Superlative: Mojo Hand FX's official discontinued-products index identifies Superlative as “Vintage Overdrive.”
  - https://mojohandfx.com/discontinued/

## Distortion

- **Socrates**
- **Magistrate**

### Distortion verification
- Socrates: manufacturer-originated historical product copy explicitly calls it “Socrates Classic Distortion” and describes it as an LM308-style distortion with symmetrical/asymmetrical clipping. Effects Database preserves the original product description and categorizes it as distortion.
  - https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/mojohand/socrates
  - https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=r0fsSYqvbDE
- Magistrate: authoritative historical product documentation identifies the Magistrate as a high-gain distortion with a three-band EQ. Barefoot Buttons also records the product specifically as “Mojo Hand FX Magistrate Distortion.”
  - https://reverb.com/p/mojo-hand-fx-magistrate
  - https://barefootbuttons.com/pedal-compatibility-list/

### Distortion taxonomy note
Do **not** promote every Mojo Hand pedal that can produce aggressive saturation or “almost distortion” into the Distortion column. In particular, Rook remains Overdrive despite its manufacturer describing one setting as “almost distortion type drive,” and El Guapo remains Fuzz despite its ability to be dialed down toward almost overdrive. The archive records the manufacturer's named/category identity rather than inferring a new category from extreme settings.

## Explicit exclusions / non-promotions

- **Speakeasy** — preamp/boost; excluded from the dirt census because the manufacturer does not explicitly categorize it as Overdrive, Distortion, or Fuzz.
- **Balloon / booster-type products and utility effects** — excluded where they are not explicitly target dirt categories.
- **Little Wonder, Mr O, Bayou, RVT, Swim Team, Dream Mender, Octaverse, SMPLoFi, Clarity, etc.** — non-target effects; excluded.
- **Rook** is not duplicated into Distortion just because the manufacturer describes one clipping mode as almost distortion.
- **El Guapo** is not duplicated into Overdrive just because the manufacturer says it can be dialed to “almost” overdrive.
- **1979** is retained as Fuzz because the current manufacturer page explicitly identifies the product as an OpAmp Fuzz, even though the description discusses grungy distortion.
- **Variant/finish-only versions** such as B-stock, Silver Modded, Goodbye Blue, and Limited Edition Colors are not split into separate builder/product identities unless the product is a distinct named circuit identity.

## Builder relationship conclusion
Mojo Hand FX is a confirmed builder/brand relationship for the archive. The official company history establishes Brad Fee as the founder/operator who evolved the Tone Factor custom shop into Mojo Hand FX, and establishes Cusack Music's acquisition of the brand in 2015. Current Mojo Hand FX material states that the brand continues under Cusack Music in Holland, Michigan.

The archive therefore records the builder as:

> **Mojo Hand FX — founded by Brad Fee; acquired by Cusack Music in 2015 and continued under Cusack Music**
