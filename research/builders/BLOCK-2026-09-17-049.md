## Verification basis
Primary manufacturer sources used:
- Big Knob Pedals official About page
- Big Knob Pedals official current pedal catalog
- Individual official product pages where available

Authoritative historical corroboration:
- Vintage Guitar review/profile documenting Gary Kibler as the builder behind Big Knob Pedals and describing the company's hand-built catalog.

The official About page identifies Gary Kibler as President / General Manager and states that Big Knob Pedals has made handmade guitar effects since 2008. The official catalog currently exposes 32 pedal identities across Fuzz, Muff, Overdrive, Distortion, and other effects categories. The current catalog was used as the primary product-name and category census, with individual product pages used where available.

## Product-level verification notes
- **Pink Freud** is listed by the manufacturer in the Fuzz section and is explicitly described as combining a classic FF fuzz circuit with an overdrive stage. It is therefore retained under both Fuzz and Overdrive.
- **Tube 808** is explicitly categorized by Big Knob as Overdrive and described as a Tube Screamer-style overdrive.
- **Tone Boner** is categorized by the manufacturer as Overdrive and described as a MOSFET boost. Because the manufacturer places the finished pedal in the Overdrive catalog, it is retained in the census rather than reclassifying it from description alone.
- **D OverDrive 250** is categorized as Overdrive and described as a drive/boost pedal intended to push an amplifier into distortion.
- **Range Blaster** is categorized as Overdrive by the manufacturer, although its product description identifies it as a treble booster. It is retained only in Overdrive because that is the builder's catalog classification.
- **Zed Drive**, **Blues Buster**, **Mad Donkey**, and **Game of Tones** are explicitly categorized as Overdrive in the manufacturer catalog.
- **Tone-Nation**, **Rock-Ola**, **Appetite for Distortion**, **Rat Pak**, **Black Metallic**, and **Guv'nator** are explicitly categorized by Big Knob as Distortion.
- **Fuzz Space**, **Tone Blender**, **Fuzz Barn**, **Bushy Bison**, **Octavius**, **Colour Jumbo**, and **Legend TB** are explicitly presented by the manufacturer as fuzz designs. Octavius is retained as Fuzz because the manufacturer places it in the Fuzz catalog despite its octave-up function.
- **Green Russian**, **Creamy Pi**, **Civil Warp**, **I.C.B.M.**, **NYC Version 6**, and **Pig Stomp** are explicitly presented in the manufacturer's Muff/fuzz catalog. The manufacturer describes each as a fuzz-oriented Muff variant, so they are counted under Fuzz rather than creating a separate Muff category.

## Scope decisions / exclusions
- Non-dirt categories such as Echo Flex, Vibe Tone, Dyna Tone, and Wah Fix are excluded from the Builder → Pedals dirt census.
- The manufacturer exposes a separate Muff category, but Muff is treated as a fuzz-family category for this archive's required taxonomy of Overdrive / Distortion / Fuzz. Muff products are therefore recorded under Fuzz rather than as a fourth dirt class.
- Boost behavior mentioned in descriptions is not separately counted as a target category unless the manufacturer itself places the finished product in the Overdrive category, as with Range Blaster and Tone Boner.
- No cosmetic variants, enclosure finishes, or duplicate circuit descriptions were promoted into separate identities.
- No additional historical pedal names were inferred from third-party lists when the official current catalog did not provide enough evidence to establish a distinct product identity.
