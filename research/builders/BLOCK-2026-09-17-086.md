# Block 086 — MOOER Audio

## Builder
**MOOER Audio / MOOER**

## Verification basis
Primary and authoritative sources reviewed:
- MOOER official product catalog and product pages
- MOOER official product pages/manuals for Green Mile, Blues Crab, Blues Mood, Black Secret, Hustle Drive, Cruncher, Blade, Solo, Rage Machine, and Ultra Drive MKII
- MOOER official/current product-index pages identifying dirt products by category
- MOOER manufacturer promotional material for Blue Faze and core Micro Series pedals
- authoritative Effects Database catalog coverage for the broader Micro Series, including Blue Faze, Grey Faze, Triangle Buff, Fog, Thunderball, Greenwood, Eight Hands, and original Ultra Drive
- 2020 MOOER retail catalog material for the established Micro Series dirt lineup
- active-repository search for “Mooer” and “MOOER” before addition

The active repository contained no prior MOOER match before this block was added. MOOER is treated as the builder/brand identity for MOOER-branded dirt pedals documented below. The census stays focused on standalone physical dirt pedals and does not convert modeled effects inside MOOER multi-effects into separate product identities.

The reviewed material supports a substantial MOOER Overdrive, Distortion, and Fuzz catalog, especially within the Micro Series. Product names and manufacturer category labels are treated as authoritative where explicit; broader historical catalog entries are retained when the builder relationship and dirt function are well documented.

## Overdrive
Current / manufacturer-supported identities:
- Blues Crab
- Blues Mood
- Green Mile
- Rumble Drive

Historical / manufacturer-supported identities:
- Ultra Drive
- Greenwood

Verification notes:
- **Blues Crab** is explicitly labeled by MOOER as an Overdrive Pedal.
- **Blues Mood** is explicitly labeled by MOOER as Overdrive and described as a blues overdrive.
- **Green Mile** is explicitly labeled by MOOER as Overdrive.
- **Rumble Drive** is documented by MOOER as a Dumble-style overdrive with round, smooth overdrive response.
- **Ultra Drive** is retained as the original Micro Series Overdrive identity in authoritative catalog coverage. The later **Ultra Drive MKII** is kept as a separate named product and is classified as Distortion because MOOER explicitly labels the MKII that way.
- **Greenwood** is retained as the historical Micro Series Mix Overdrive identity documented in authoritative MOOER catalog coverage.

## Distortion
Current / manufacturer-supported identities:
- Black Secret
- Hustle Drive
- Cruncher
- Blade
- Solo
- Rage Machine
- Ultra Drive MKII
- Thunderball

Historical / manufacturer-supported identities:
- Eight Hands

Verification notes:
- **Black Secret** is explicitly labeled by MOOER as a Distortion Pedal.
- **Hustle Drive** is explicitly labeled by MOOER as a Distortion Pedal despite its open, tube-like drive character and lower-gain LP setting.
- **Cruncher** is explicitly labeled by MOOER as a Distortion Pedal.
- **Blade** is explicitly presented by MOOER as Distortion.
- **Solo** is explicitly presented by MOOER as high-gain Distortion.
- **Rage Machine** is explicitly labeled by MOOER as distortion and is described as a heavy-metal distortion pedal.
- **Ultra Drive MKII** is explicitly labeled by MOOER as a Distortion Pedal. Its Ultra mode is described as a fatter drive tone, but the product remains Distortion rather than gaining an additional Overdrive identity.
- **Thunderball** is retained as Distortion because authoritative MOOER Micro Series catalog coverage identifies it as Bass Fuzz/Distortion.
- **Eight Hands** is retained as a historical MOOER signature distortion identity from authoritative product catalog coverage. It remains distinct from later Eight Hands digital preamp products.

## Fuzz
Current / manufacturer-supported identities:
- Triangle Buff
- MooerFog / Fog
- Thunderball

Historical / manufacturer-supported identities:
- Blue Faze
- Grey Faze

Verification notes:
- **Triangle Buff** is explicitly identified by MOOER as a Fuzz Pedal and is part of the established Micro Series catalog.
- **MooerFog / Fog** is documented as a bass fuzz pedal in authoritative MOOER catalog coverage. The archive uses the product's catalog identity rather than treating the bass orientation as a separate builder relationship.
- **Thunderball** is retained as Fuzz as well as Distortion because authoritative catalog coverage describes it as Bass Fuzz/Distortion.
- **Blue Faze** is documented by MOOER manufacturer promotional material as a vintage fuzz pedal and by authoritative catalog coverage as the Micro Series MFZ1 Blue Faze.
- **Grey Faze** is documented in authoritative MOOER catalog coverage as MFZ4 Grey Faze, a vintage-style fuzz pedal.

## Category-overlap decisions
- **Thunderball**: Fuzz + Distortion. Authoritative MOOER catalog coverage explicitly identifies it as Bass Fuzz/Distortion.
- **Ultra Drive** vs. **Ultra Drive MKII**: kept as separate named identities because MOOER's historical catalog distinguishes the original Ultra Drive Overdrive from the later Ultra Drive MKII Distortion.
- **Hustle Drive**: Distortion only. MOOER explicitly categorizes it as Distortion even though it has a lower-gain clean-boost/drive mode.
- **Blues Mood**: Overdrive only. MOOER explicitly categorizes it as Overdrive.
- **Green Mile**: Overdrive only. MOOER explicitly categorizes it as Overdrive.
- **Blue Faze / Grey Faze / Triangle Buff / MooerFog**: Fuzz only. Their lower-output, cleanup, or bass-oriented behavior does not create additional target categories.

## Historical / variant handling
- **Ultra Drive** and **Ultra Drive MKII** remain distinct named products because MOOER's catalog presents them as separate generations with different product-level classifications.
- Cosmetic presentation, catalog-language changes, and ordinary production revisions do not create additional dirt identities.
- Product naming such as MFZ1, MFZ2, MFZ4, MDS1, MDS2, and related SKU/model codes are supporting identifiers rather than separate products.
- Blue Faze, Grey Faze, Triangle Buff, Black Secret, Hustle Drive, Cruncher, Solo, and other Micro Series products remain individual physical pedal identities even when their historical marketing describes the inspiration for the circuit.

## Exclusions
- MOOER Micro Preamp products are excluded from the dirt census as digital amplifier/preamp modeling products rather than standalone named Overdrive, Distortion, or Fuzz effects. Their modeled amplifier voices are not mined into additional pedal identities.
- GE/Prime/Black Truck/Red Truck and other MOOER multi-effects products are excluded from the individual dirt-pedal census even where their internal modules contain drive, distortion, or fuzz effects.
- Pure Boost and Flex Boost are excluded as boost-focused products.
- Tone Capture, cab simulations, EQ, modulation, delay, reverb, tuner, octave, looper, wireless, and other non-dirt products are outside target scope.
- Generic references to “drive,” “gain,” saturation, or amp-like response do not create a second category when MOOER's named product category is explicit.
- Cloning, schematics, components, internal construction, and reverse-engineering material are outside scope.
