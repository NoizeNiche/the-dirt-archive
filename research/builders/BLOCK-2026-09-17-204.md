## Evidence / classification notes

- Chancho Electronics is documented as a small hand-built pedal maker, and the Plumbus Fuzz product listing explicitly identifies the model as a fuzz pedal while describing its distortion/sustain character.
- Correct Sound was added to the Effects Database as a distinct Russian pedal brand/builder in 2011. The archive records only products with explicit dirt evidence.
- Mad Hatter is described as a high-precision distortion pedal using Sunn-style and Muff-style gain sections; the reviewed product description explicitly identifies both distortion and fuzz characteristics.
- M1 tube preamp listings identify the second channel as a distortion channel, while product specifications categorize the unit as Distortion/Fuzz.
- Tube Beta Hybrid Mk-II is categorized as Overdrive and Distortion in a contemporaneous product listing, so both dirt classes are retained.
- Black Sunn is categorized as Distortion and contains a built-in RAT-based distortion stage before the Sunn Model T preamp.
- Eclipse is documented as a Sunn Concert Lead-inspired preamp/distortion device whose reviewed sound description explicitly spans soft overdrive through distortion/fuzz.
- Cornerstone Music Gear's Sparkle is explicitly documented as a dynamic overdrive. It is an expansion of existing canonical builder 315, not a new builder.

## Exclusions / non-additions

- Correct Sound's Parametric EQ, BETA Bass Preamp, and MT Tube Preamp are not independently added where the reviewed records identify them primarily as preamp/EQ units without a clean explicit dirt classification.
- Correct Sound Scrambler is held because the available record is principally an Ampeg Scrambler clone and its dirt behavior overlaps octave/ring-modulated functions; it can be revisited in a dedicated backfill pass.
- Chancho Electronics' other non-dirt or insufficiently documented models are not added.
- Cloak Audio Jackdaw remains deferred for Fredric Effects identity reconciliation.
- Chaosound Anti-Effect remains deferred because the product is explicitly described as not being a conventional distortion despite the site's broad distortion/fuzz/overdrive tagging.
