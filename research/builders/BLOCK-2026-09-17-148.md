## Research notes

- Audile's TOAD and Bass TOAD are explicitly presented as overdrive/distortion products.
- Audio Kitchen's The Big Trees is described by the manufacturer and Effects Database as a drive/preamp pedal, while Fake Plastic Trees is an overdrive-oriented preamp pedal and The Flying Squirrel is a drive/fuzz pedal recorded here under Fuzz.
- Audio Matrix's Mini Boogee is cataloged as an overdrive; the related Mini Matrix name is treated as the same pedal renamed rather than as a second independent design.
- Audio Monk's Fuzzbeard and Fuzzpotion are documented as fuzz products; Rubie Drive is described as a dual-stage overdrive/distortion.
- Aul Instruments made a 1967 fuzz product before Mike Matthews founded Electro-Harmonix.
- Aural Dream has distinct Bold Distortion, Compelling Drive, Intense Distortion, Purely Fuzz, and Warm Drive products.
- Auralux Clear Gain is a transparent gain pedal that can be used to overdrive tube amp stages; it is retained under Overdrive for this archive's dirt taxonomy.
- Austone's Fuzz Nutz and Textone Fuzz Nutz are documented discrete fuzz products.
- Audible Disease FR-1 Fracture is a high-gain oscillating distortion/tremolo pedal; only the dirt function is recorded here.
- AWOL Pedals currently lists Virus Fuzz among its pedal products.
- AXiom Effects explicitly lists the OP-1, DP-1, and FP-1 as its overdrive, distortion, and fuzz pedal products respectively.
- Axis Guitar Effects is documented with the Skrambler octave fuzz and Axis Face MkII fuzz.
