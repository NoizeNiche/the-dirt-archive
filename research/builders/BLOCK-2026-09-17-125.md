# The Dirt Archive — Builder Census Block 125

## Builder
**Barber Electronics**

Barber Electronics is a previously uncovered builder with a large historical/current catalog centered on Overdrive, plus clear Distortion and Fuzz identities. Barber's current site emphasizes hand-built pedals and currently lists the Gain Changer, Direct Drive, Midnight Standard, Exacta and other products; historical catalog references document the broader lineage. citeturn266783search6turn266783search0turn266783search7

## Scope result
This block records named Barber pedals whose product identity or authoritative historical documentation clearly establishes Overdrive, Distortion, or Fuzz. Routine revisions, enclosure changes, and limited variants are consolidated into product families where they represent the same named design. Custom one-off B-Custom combinations are not counted as separate dirt identities.

## Overdrive
- **Midnight Standard** — current; explicitly described as a Bluesbreaker-style Overdrive.
- **Gain Changer / Gain Changer SR** — current lineage; explicitly described as an overdrive with a low-gain overdrive side and a higher-gain unLimiTeD-derived setting.
- **Compact Direct Drive / Direct Drive lineage** — current/historical; Barber explicitly presents the Direct Drive as a broad British-voiced overdrive family, with LG and SS variants consolidated into the lineage.
- **LTD / Silver LTD lineage** — historical/discontinued; flat-response overdrive family, with the Gain Changer taking over the lineage.
- **1/2 Gainer** — historical/discontinued; dual-channel version of the LTD overdrive.
- **unLimiTeD / unLTD 2-Channel Overdrive** — historical/discontinued; higher-gain dual-channel overdrive lineage.
- **Burn Unit / Burn Unit 2 / Burn Unit EQ / Compact Burn Unit** — historical/current lineage; overdrive family with a documented range from edge-of-breakup through more saturated drive.
- **Small Fry Burn Unit** — historical/discontinued; explicitly cataloged as an overdrive, with a range from warm boost through searing drive.
- **Tone Pump / Tone Pump EQ / Tone Pump II** — historical lineage; dual-channel overdrive family.
- **Deep Fryer** — historical/limited; bass-oriented overdrive with broad drive range.
- **B-Custom Cool** — historical/custom-shop; explicitly described as a low-gain overdrive.
- **B-Custom Super Sport** — historical/custom-shop; explicitly described as a medium/high-gain British-flavored overdrive.
- **B-Custom Dual Discrete** — historical/custom-shop; custom dual-overdrive format built from selected Barber overdrive circuits.
- **FWW BUSS** — current; explicitly described as a dual-tone overdrive using Burn Unit and Direct Drive Super Sport-derived voices.
- **FWW BUSS HG** — current; explicitly described as a higher-gain dual-tone overdrive.
- **FWW Plus** — current; explicitly described as an overdrive/boost-oriented pedal, with standalone overdrive as a primary documented use.

## Distortion
- **Dirty Bomb** — historical/discontinued; explicitly high-gain Distortion.
- **Burn Unit lineage** — historical/current; authoritative historical cataloging places the Burn Unit family in both Distortion and Overdrive because it deliberately spans the boundary.
- **Gain Changer lineage** — current; Barber explicitly describes the higher-gain switch position as its unLimiTeD distortion voice, so Distortion is retained alongside Overdrive.

## Fuzz
- **Exacta** — current; explicitly described as a vintage Fuzz combining Triangle and Jumbo Tonebender-style voices.
- **Trifecta** — historical/discontinued; explicitly described as a three-voice Fuzz pedal.

## Explicit category overlaps
- **Burn Unit family:** Overdrive + Distortion. Historical Barber documentation says the design blurs the lines between overdrive and distortion; the archive therefore retains both target identities for the lineage.
- **Gain Changer family:** Overdrive + Distortion. Barber explicitly identifies the low-gain side as overdrive and the higher-gain setting as an unLimiTeD distortion voice.

## Historical / variant restraint
- **Direct Drive, Direct Drive LG, Direct Drive SS, Compact Direct Drive** are consolidated into the Direct Drive overdrive lineage rather than counted as separate effect identities. The LG and SS versions are explicitly described as Direct Drive variants. citeturn959229search7turn959229search4turn803976search4
- **LTD and Silver LTD** are consolidated into one LTD overdrive lineage, with the later Gain Changer treated as the successor rather than a second copy of the same product family. citeturn305156search0turn305156search4
- **Burn Unit, Burn Unit 2, Burn Unit EQ, Small Fry, and Compact Burn Unit** are retained as the Burn Unit family rather than multiplying each revision into a new category. Barber's historical catalog consistently identifies the family as overdrive-oriented while some references explicitly place it in distortion as well. citeturn208101search2turn208101search4turn343954search8
- **Tone Pump / Tone Pump EQ / Tone Pump II** are consolidated as one dual-overdrive lineage. Historical cataloging identifies the original Tone Pump and Tone Pump EQ as overdrive products. citeturn343954search0turn343954search1
- **B-Custom Dual Discrete** is retained only as a custom dual-overdrive format, not as a unique circuit family, because Barber described it as a customer-selected combination of existing Barber overdrive pedals. citeturn343954search3
- **Exacta and Trifecta** remain Fuzz only. Their multiple classic-fuzz voices do not become separate products or additional dirt categories. citeturn803976search1turn820190search0
- **Dirty Bomb** remains Distortion only because Barber's documented identity is explicitly high-gain distortion. citeturn820190search9turn820190youtube88
- **Deep Fryer** remains Overdrive only. Historical Barber cataloging describes it as an overdrive for bass, even though some users reported it could reach heavier distortion. citeturn208101search0turn266783search13
- **FWW BUSS / BUSS HG** remain Overdrive entries; their descriptions emphasize dual-tone overdrive voices and higher-gain overdrive rather than establishing a distinct named distortion identity. citeturn803976search0turn803976search2
- **FWW Plus** remains an Overdrive entry because Barber explicitly presents standalone overdrive as a primary function alongside clean boost and tone conditioning. citeturn266783search2
- **Barb EQ, Linden EQ, Launch Pad, Tone Press, and other non-dirt Barber products** remain outside the census.
- No components, schematics, cloning, PCB, BOM, or internal-build information is recorded in this block.

## Research note
Barber Electronics is a useful archive entry because its catalog shows a consistent progression from transparent low-gain overdrive through British-voiced drive and into explicitly named high-gain distortion, while Exacta and Trifecta provide dedicated fuzz identities. The archive deliberately does not treat every high-gain setting as a new effect category; instead it follows Barber's own named product identities and only retains Distortion where the documentation explicitly establishes that identity.
