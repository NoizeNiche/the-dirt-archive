# The Dirt Archive — Builder Census Block 116

## Builder
**Catalinbread Effects**

Catalinbread's official About page says the company began in 2003 on Nic Harris's kitchen table, moved to Portland, Oregon, in 2006, and currently manufactures more than 30 unique pedals by hand. The current catalog is much larger as a storefront because it also contains variants, accessories, B-stock, and non-dirt effects.

## Scope result
Catalinbread is a major multi-category dirt builder with a broad current and historical catalog. The official site explicitly separates a Foundation Overdrive Series from a Boost/Fuzz/Distortion Series, and individual product pages provide unusually clear functional language for several hybrid designs.

This block consolidates ordinary colorways, B-stock listings, anniversary graphics, and routine reissues where they represent the same underlying pedal lineage. Distinct named collaborations or materially different products are retained.

## Explicit category overlaps
- **SFT:** Overdrive + Distortion + Fuzz. Catalinbread's current SFT description explicitly says the pedal moves from clean to slightly overdriven tones, into high-gain distortion, and can produce huge fuzz sounds. The company also describes the Stoner mode as an overdrive-meets-fuzz approach.
- **Katzenkönig:** Distortion + Fuzz. Catalinbread explicitly says it combines a Tone Bender MkII fuzz with a RAT-style distortion, producing a pedal that sings like fuzz while staying tight like distortion.
- **Crooner Preamp:** Overdrive + Distortion + Fuzz. Catalinbread explicitly states that its gain range covers clean boost, overdrive, distortion, and full-throated fuzz. Clean boost remains outside scope.
- **Dirty Little Secret Deluxe:** Overdrive only. It includes a separate built-in boost, but the primary dirt section is the Marshall-style amp/preamp circuit. Boost is outside the target taxonomy.
- **Silver Kiss:** Overdrive only. Catalinbread explicitly describes medium-gain OD plus clean boost and EQ; the boost function is not a second dirt category.
- **Formula 51:** Overdrive only. The page describes everything from gritty cleans to heavy distortion, but the product is a Foundation Overdrive and no separate named distortion identity is established.
- **Manx Loaghtan:** Fuzz only. Catalinbread identifies it as a Big Muff-derived fuzz, even though the design can reach some low-gain OD sounds.
- **Giygas 2K:** Fuzz only. The builder explicitly calls it a fuzz; the lower end of its Fuzz control can act as gritty boost, but that does not create a second target dirt category.
- **Antichthon:** Fuzz only. Catalinbread describes it as an other-worldly fuzz tremolo. Tremolo remains outside scope.
- **Perseus DIO:** Fuzz lineage is retained only at the product-family level where Catalinbread describes the device as fuzz mixed with analog octave down. Its octave-down function is not a separate target dirt category.

## Historical/variant restraint
- **Dirty Little Secret** generations and ordinary edition/color variants are consolidated into the core Dirty Little Secret lineage unless Catalinbread gives them a materially distinct named product, such as **Dirty Little Secret Deluxe**.
- **Formula No. 55 / Formula 55** and **Formula 51 / Formula No. 5** reissue forms are treated as underlying pedal lineages rather than multiplied by artwork or storefront naming.
- **Galileo** and **Galileo 4K** are retained as distinct named generations because Catalinbread presents the 4K as a new chapter in the Galileo lineage with a materially expanded EQ section.
- **Fuzzrite**, **Fuzzrite Germanium**, and **Fuzzrite Mini** are retained separately because they are distinct named products with different transistor/packaging identities rather than mere cosmetic variants.
- **Karma Suture GE/SI** remain separate named versions because Catalinbread's own manuals distinguish the two builds; both remain within Fuzz rather than being promoted to Distortion merely because the circuit is aggressive.
- **SFT Blackout** and **SFT Fireworks** are graphics/collection editions of the SFT and are consolidated into SFT.
- **Katzenkönig Fireworks** is likewise consolidated into the base Katzenkönig.
- **Blood Donor** is not separately counted in this block because Catalinbread's own 2020 release notice describes it as a revamped and fine-tuned Katzenkönig rather than establishing a cleanly separate target dirt identity.
- **Catalinbread Bitters** is excluded even though it has a digital distortion section because its primary identity is a multi-effects processor with distortion, phaser, and digital sound-mangling functions.
- **Naga Viper, Epoch Boost, VariOboost, and similar boost-first products** are excluded because Boost is outside the archive's target taxonomy.
- **Perseus DIO** is not split into a separate distortion entry merely because its dry path can produce boosted distortion. Its primary product identity is the octave-down/fuzz design.
- No components, schematics, cloning, PCB, BOM, or internal-build information is recorded in this block.

## Research note
Catalinbread is a particularly valuable builder for the archive because its current site openly separates Foundation Overdrive products from a broader dirt series and maintains both current and retired references. The strongest hybrid is SFT, where the builder itself documents the full progression from overdrive through distortion and into fuzz. Katzenkönig is a clear Fuzz + Distortion hybrid, and Crooner is an unusually explicit all-three dirt product.

The block intentionally does not turn every mention of saturation, clipping, or amplifier breakup into another category. Products remain in their builder-stated identities unless Catalinbread explicitly establishes a distinct target dirt function.
