# Builder -> Pedals Research Block 188

## Scope

Continued the C-section Builder -> Pedals census with high-confidence remaining alphabetic records. Inclusion remains dirt-only: overdrive, distortion, and fuzz. Existing builder identities were checked before assigning new canonical numbers. Amp simulation, tremolo, flanger, delay-only, wah-only, boost-only, and other non-dirt products were excluded.

- OD50 Overdrive/Distortion — Overdrive
- OD50 Overdrive/Distortion — Distortion

### 443 — Clear Sound

### 444 — Cloud 9
- DS-50 Distortion + Sustain — Distortion
- TS-30 Tube Sound Overdrive — Overdrive

## Evidence / classification notes

- Chapter 53 Boutique Effects has a cataloged Fuzzmaster explicitly identified as a fuzz pedal. No separate identity is created for non-dirt products because the reviewed source did not establish another dirt model.
- Chord's OD50 Overdrive/Distortion is explicitly tagged as both overdrive and distortion. The source says it was developed in collaboration with Tom's Line Engineering, and identifies the same core design as the Tom's Line AOD-1. The archive therefore keeps the Chord-branded catalog identity while preserving both dirt classifications.
- Clear Sound's Super Fuzz is explicitly categorized as fuzz. The source relates the model to the long-running Shin-Ei/Companion Super Fuzz family, so the archive treats Clear Sound as a historical branded catalog identity rather than making an independent circuit-origin claim.
- Cloud 9's catalog contains five products. DS-50 is explicitly a distortion + sustain pedal and TS-30 is explicitly an overdrive. The TA-40 Tube Amp Simulator, FL-10 Flanger, and TR-20 Tremolo are excluded because their primary functions are not dirt.

## Exclusions / non-additions

- Cloud 9 TA-40 Tube Amp Simulator is amp simulation and is excluded.
- Cloud 9 FL-10 Flanger and TR-20 Tremolo are excluded as modulation effects.
- No duplicate builder identity is created for Tom's Line Engineering based on the Chord OD50 record because Tom's Line Engineering is documented as a collaborating/manufacturing lineage on the Chord-branded model, not as a separate Chord-label catalog entry.
- Clear Sound is retained as a historical branded entry; no underlying OEM manufacturer is inferred beyond the source's related-product lineage.
- Zero-product C-section entries and candidates without a clear dirt-primary record remain pending/excluded.
