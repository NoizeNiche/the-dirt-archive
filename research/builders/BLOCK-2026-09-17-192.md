# Builder -> Pedals Research Block 192

## Scope

Continued the C-section Builder -> Pedals census with historical backfills and six new canonical builder identities. Inclusion remains dirt-only: overdrive, distortion, and fuzz. Existing builder identities were checked before creating new ones. Non-dirt, boost-only, modulation, delay, and utility products were excluded.

## Evidence / classification notes

- Carlsbro Fuzz is a distinct Carlsbro-branded fuzz record from the existing Carlsbro Fuzz-Tone. Effects Database identifies it as "Carlsbro by Colorsound", preserving the historical brand lineage while keeping the Carlsbro catalog identity.
- Carlson E20MT Heavy Metal is explicitly classified as distortion. Its related-product tree connects it to the Daphon E20MT/F15MT family, so the archive keeps Carlson as a historical branded/OEM catalog identity rather than claiming independent circuit authorship.
- Carruthers Dyna-Soar and Dyna-Soar Lite are documented in Effects Database as the two Carruthers products. The manufacturer page characterizes Carruthers as an alleged maker, so this canonical identity is retained with that attribution caveat rather than treating maker status as fully settled.
- Chucktone Effects Overdrive 809 is explicitly cataloged as overdrive.
- Clay Jones CJOD Overdrive is explicitly cataloged as overdrive. It is also linked by Effects Database to the Landgraff Dynamic Overdrive lineage, but no independent manufacturing inference is made beyond the Clay Jones catalog identity.
- Chord (by Daphon) is kept separate from Chord (by Tom's Line), which is already canonical as ID 442. The Daphon/AVSL catalog contains 19 products, with six dirt-bearing models retained here and chorus, compressor, delay, EQ, flanger, noise gate, octave, phaser, reverb, tremolo, and tuner models excluded.
- Chiu Luen's CL-series contains four explicit distortion-bearing models among a five-model series; CL-2 is delay and therefore excluded. The broader source links Chiu Luen DongGuan Electronics to the Alber OEM family, so the archive preserves Chiu Luen as the historical branded/OEM identity without attributing every related Alber model to the Chiu Luen name.

## Exclusions / non-additions

- Carlin Compressor/Fuzz and Carlsbro Fuzz-Tone were already present in the census and were not duplicated.
- Chaser HM-3 was already added in Block 191.
- Chord (by Tom's Line) remains ID 442 and is not merged with Chord (by Daphon).
- Chiu Luen CL-2 DeLay is excluded as delay.
- Chord (by Daphon) BCH-50, BEQ-50, CH-50, CM-50, DL-50, FL-50, GE-50, NG-50, OC-50, PH-50, RV-50, TR-50, and TU-50 are excluded as non-dirt.
- Carlson, Carruthers, and other historical branded/OEM records are not presented as definitive original circuit authorship.
