# Block 080 — Strymon

## Builder
**Strymon**

## Verification basis
Primary and authoritative sources reviewed:
- Strymon official current Drive product category
- Strymon official individual product pages
- Strymon manufacturer-authored Sunset user manual
- Strymon manufacturer-authored Fairfax quick-start/manual material
- Strymon manufacturer-authored product/support material for Deco V2 and Canoga
- active-repository search for “Strymon” before addition

The active repository contained no prior Strymon match before this block was added. Strymon is treated as the builder identity for Strymon-branded dirt pedals documented below.

The reviewed material supports five clearly documented core dirt identities: Sunset Dual Overdrive, Riverside Drive & Distortion, Fairfax Class A Output Stage Drive, Canoga Vintage Silicon Fuzz, and Deco / Deco V2 Tape Saturation & Doubletracker. Dirt categories are assigned from explicit product/category descriptions or direct manufacturer documentation. Generic saturation, gain, or sound-descriptor language is not used to manufacture additional categories.

## Overdrive
Current / manufacturer-supported identities:
- Sunset Dual Overdrive
- Riverside Drive & Distortion
- Fairfax Class A Output Stage Drive
- Deco Tape Saturation & Doubletracker

Historical / manufacturer-supported identities:
- none identified

Verification notes:
- **Sunset Dual Overdrive** is explicitly presented by Strymon as a dual overdrive pedal with six classic overdrive/boost circuits. It remains Overdrive only despite one circuit approaching fuzz territory at maximum Drive.
- **Riverside Drive & Distortion** is explicitly presented by Strymon as a multistage drive pedal producing tones from clean overdrive through colossal distortion. It therefore receives both Overdrive and Distortion below.
- **Fairfax Class A Output Stage Drive** is explicitly presented as a drive pedal and Strymon's current page documents overdrive sounds from gentle push through higher-gain saturation. It remains Overdrive only because Strymon does not present it as a separate Distortion product.
- **Deco Tape Saturation & Doubletracker / Deco V2** is included under Overdrive because Strymon's current Drive category includes Deco V2 and the manufacturer explicitly describes the tape-saturation side as transparent overdrive and overdriven tape distortion. The tape/doubletracking functionality does not create a separate target product identity.

## Distortion
Current / manufacturer-supported identities:
- Riverside Drive & Distortion
- Canoga Vintage Silicon Fuzz

Historical / manufacturer-supported identities:
- none identified

Verification notes:
- **Riverside Drive & Distortion** receives Distortion from both its product title and manufacturer description of its clean-overdrive through colossal-distortion range.
- **Canoga Vintage Silicon Fuzz** receives Distortion because Strymon explicitly states that, when turned up, it works as a fuzz or distortion. This is product-level manufacturer wording, so the Distortion category is retained alongside Fuzz.

## Fuzz
Current / manufacturer-supported identities:
- Canoga Vintage Silicon Fuzz

Historical / manufacturer-supported identities:
- none identified

Verification notes:
- **Canoga Vintage Silicon Fuzz** is explicitly presented by Strymon as a vintage-silicon fuzz pedal and as a tweaked vintage Silicon Fuzz Face-style design. It remains Fuzz and also receives Distortion because the manufacturer explicitly states that it works as fuzz or distortion.

## Category-overlap decisions
- **Sunset Dual Overdrive**: Overdrive only. The product is explicitly a dual-overdrive pedal; approaching fuzz at maximum gain does not create a second category.
- **Riverside Drive & Distortion**: Overdrive + Distortion. Strymon explicitly describes its range from clean overdrive to colossal distortion and uses both terms in the product identity.
- **Fairfax Class A Output Stage Drive**: Overdrive only. Strymon documents softer overdrive through saturated amp-like gain but does not identify a separate Distortion category for the product.
- **Canoga Vintage Silicon Fuzz**: Fuzz + Distortion. Strymon explicitly identifies it as a vintage-silicon fuzz and states it can work as fuzz or distortion at higher settings.
- **Deco Tape Saturation & Doubletracker / Deco V2**: Overdrive only. Strymon explicitly describes transparent tape overdrive and includes Deco V2 in its Drive catalog. Tape saturation, compression, modulation, and delay functions remain secondary to the target dirt identity.

## Historical / variant handling
- **Deco V2** is consolidated with the Deco functional product identity rather than treated as a separate dirt family; Strymon states that V2 preserves the original sound and functions while expanding the interface.
- Cosmetic revisions, hardware revisions, firmware versions, and presentation variants do not create additional dirt identities.
- Sunset's individual circuit selections and stacking modes are internal functions of one named pedal and do not create additional products.
- Riverside's gain-stage architecture is internal product functionality and does not create separate identities.

## Exclusions
- Boost-only products such as Compadre Compressor & Boost and related clean-boost utilities: outside the target taxonomy.
- Strymon modulation, delay, reverb, tuner, interface, amp-modeling, and utility products without an explicit target dirt identity: outside scope.
- Product presets, MIDI patches, firmware variations, and internal circuit modes are not separate builder/product identities.
- Generic saturation, clipping, fuzz-like, or distortion-like sound descriptions without product-level classification are not used to create secondary identities.
- Cloning, schematics, internal construction, component details, and reverse-engineering material are outside scope.
