## Research Handling Rules Applied

- Same builder + same underlying product: group minor naming/cosmetic variations for the first pass.
- Different recognized builders + same product name: keep separate.
- Do not chase component differences, internal photography, schematics, PCB evidence, or exhaustive production generations during this pass.
- Keep uncertain model classifications in research until model-level evidence is sufficient.
- Exterior photos will be researched only after builder/product census work is mature.
