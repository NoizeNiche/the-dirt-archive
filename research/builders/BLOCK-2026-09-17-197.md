## Evidence / classification notes

- Effects Database identifies Crazy Tube Circuits as a pedal builder based in Athens, Greece and currently catalogs 54 products for the brand.
- Black Magic is explicitly described as a high-gain distortion/fuzz pedal with a gain range extending into low overdrive; the archive retains the explicitly cataloged distortion and fuzz classes.
- Black Magic MkII is documented as a high-gain distortion and overdrive design, so both dirt classes are retained.
- Constellation of Fuzz is explicitly a multi-mode germanium fuzz/booster; only fuzz is counted because boost is outside the dirt taxonomy.
- Motherload is explicitly described as a fuzz/distortion design whose R circuit ranges from overdrive into distortion; all three dirt classes are retained.
- Orama is cataloged as fuzz/overdrive and its description covers the drive section from clean through saturated gain; the archive records the explicit fuzz and overdrive classes.
- Pin Up Fuzz is cataloged as fuzz, with its gain range moving from overdrive into high-gain fuzz; the archive retains the explicit fuzz classification.
- Space Charged is explicitly a tube overdrive/distortion design; both classes are retained for the original and V2 listings.
- Starlight is explicitly cataloged as distortion and fuzz.
- Triptychon is explicitly built around fuzz and amplifier drive, and its catalog title identifies octave fuzz / drive; the archive records fuzz and overdrive.
- Venus and Unobtanium are explicitly overdrive designs. Falcon and Locomotive are cataloged as overdrive models.
- Crowella's Animated is independently documented as a high-gain overdrive and, in a contemporaneous TalkBass discussion with the builder, as a high-gain distortion. Because the two sources use different dirt labels for the same pedal and the archive permits multiple dirt-type rows, both labels are retained.
- Crowella's Defector is documented as overdrive, while Voskhod is documented as distortion/fuzz-leaning but is categorized as distortion in the product database/listing; the archive keeps the explicit distortion class.
- Crowella Effects is documented as an actual pedal builder, not merely a reseller; its own public material and third-party cataloging identify multiple pedal models.

## Exclusions / non-additions

- Crab Guitar Effects was reviewed as a possible new C-section builder, but Effects Database currently shows 0 cataloged products for the brand. A current Reverb listing confirms a Crab Guitar Effects SATAN preamp exists, but it is listed as an amp-simulator/preamp rather than as a cataloged dirt pedal in the preferred builder index, so it is not promoted to a canonical builder identity in this block.
- Non-dirt Crazy Tube Circuits models such as delays, reverbs, modulation, and boost-only units remain outside the census.
- No duplicate canonical identity was created for Crazy Tube Circuits; its existing ID 025 was expanded.
