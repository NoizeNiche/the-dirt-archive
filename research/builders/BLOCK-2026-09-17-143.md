## 1. Aclam Guitars

Builder / brand: Aclam Guitars

### Dirt products
- Dr. Robert — distortion
- Go Rocky Go — distortion / overdrive / fuzz
- Go Rocky Go - White Album Edition — distortion / overdrive / fuzz edition
- The Mocker — fuzz
- Cinnamon Drive — overdrive / distortion
- Cinnamon Drive - Dreamer Edition — overdrive / distortion edition
- The Woman Tone — overdrive
- The Windmiller Preamp — overdrive / preamp

Notes:
- Aclam's current effects store lists eight effect-pedal products and describes its pedals as designed and built in Barcelona.
- Dr. Robert is built around the VOX UL730 character and is presented with raw and crunchy distortion.
- Go Rocky Go is the Vox Conqueror-derived pedal with a built-in distortion effect and a separate crunch mode that adds overdrive.
- The Mocker is the fuzz counterpart in Aclam's UL730 family.
- Cinnamon Drive is an original dual-stage overdrive design that reaches highly saturated distortion.
- The Woman Tone is a Plexi-style overdrive product.
- The Windmiller Preamp is a dedicated preamp product with drive/saturation use and is retained as an overdrive-oriented dirt identity.
- The White Album Edition is recorded as a named manufacturer listing, while its core pedal identity remains Go Rocky Go.
