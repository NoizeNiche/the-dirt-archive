# The Dirt Archive — Builder Census Block 114

## Builder
**Mythos Pedals**

The official Mythos Pedals About page identifies **Zach Broyles** as the person who started Mythos in 2010 and describes the company as a small team operating outside Nashville, Tennessee. The current official catalog separates products into Overdrive, Fuzz, Boost, Octave, Modulation, Utility, and Limited & Exclusive collections, with a separate Retired Models collection.

## Scope result
Mythos has a substantial dirt catalog spanning all three target categories. Its official catalog is especially useful because it explicitly labels most products by category, while individual product pages also make several hybrid cases clear.

This block consolidates normal colorways, blemished listings, and routine Mjolnir editions rather than treating them as separate pedal identities. Distinct named collaborations or materially different product identities are retained when they function as recognizable named products.

## Explicit category overlaps
- **Golden Fleece Fuzz:** Fuzz + Distortion + Overdrive. Mythos directly calls it a dirt box that blends all three target categories, so all three are retained.
- **210 Double Drive Deluxe:** Overdrive + Fuzz. Mythos describes two drive circuits covering slight boost, sweet overdrive crunch, and gnarly fuzz tones. Boost itself remains outside the archive's target categories.
- **AU-79 Fuzz:** Fuzz + Overdrive. Mythos's FAQ says the AU-79 is lower gain and smoother, functioning as an "overdrive meets fuzz" design and intended to stack with other drives.
- **Argo Boost Deluxe:** Fuzz only within the target taxonomy. Mythos explicitly describes the unit as containing the Argo octave-fuzz circuit plus a standalone boost mode. Boost is not a target category, so only Fuzz is recorded.
- **SusMaryOsep:** Overdrive only within the target taxonomy. It contains a Silicon Treble Booster, Herculean-based Overdrive, and an echo section. The booster and echo remain outside scope.
- **Herculean Deluxe:** Overdrive only. Mythos presents both internal sides as overdrive circuits, including a D-style amp-like overdrive and a Blues Breaker-based overdrive.
- **Extra Hot Dual Overdrive:** Overdrive only. Mythos explicitly presents both sides as overdrive circuits, even though one side can approach fuzz-like dirt at extreme settings.
- **Chupacabra CMOS Overdrive:** Overdrive only. Mythos describes pseudo-fuzz-like tones at the top of the gain range, but the builder's named identity remains Overdrive.
- **Big Puft:** Fuzz only. Mythos describes mellow distortion through annihilating fuzz, but names and categorizes the product as a fuzz rather than a separate distortion design.
- **Positron:** Fuzz only. Mythos describes the LOUD setting as producing fuller distortion, but the product remains explicitly a Ram's Head/Supa Tonebender-derived fuzz identity.

## Historical/variant restraint
- **Mjolnir** variants such as 1N34A Gold, 1N34A Silver, GE Mjolnir, Joey Landreth Mjolnir, and Wildwood Mjolnir are consolidated into the **Mjolnir Overdrive** lineage rather than counted as separate pedals merely because the enclosure, component set, or collaboration changes.
- **Golden Fleece** versions, including standard, High Road, and related retailer/limited forms, are not inflated into separate products unless Mythos gives them a distinct named identity. The **AU-79** is retained separately because Mythos presents it as a distinct named exclusive product with a different low-gain overdrive-meets-fuzz character.
- **Big Puft** colorways, including Inverse and retailer-exclusive finishes, are consolidated into one Big Puft lineage.
- **Positron Collider** is treated as the historical predecessor to the current **Positron Fuzz**, not as a second unrelated pedal family.
- **D-Style Herculean Overdrive - Blem** is treated as a sales/finish listing for the Herculean lineage rather than a separate product.
- **Extra Hot Dual Overdrive - Blem** is treated as a sales/finish listing, not a new pedal.
- Boost-first products such as **Luxury Drive Boost**, **Cestus Treble Booster**, and the booster portion of SusMaryOsep or Argo Boost Deluxe are not separately categorized because Boost is outside the archive's target taxonomy.
- **Argonaut Octave Up** is excluded because its primary identity is octave-up rather than a dirt pedal. Other modulation, delay, utility, and parts/accessory products are likewise excluded.
- No component, schematic, cloning, PCB, BOM, or internal-build information is recorded in this block.

## Research note
Mythos is a particularly clean fit for the archive because the builder's own storefront openly separates Overdrive and Fuzz collections and now includes a named Distortion release. The most important hybrid is Golden Fleece, where Mythos itself explicitly names all three target dirt identities. The archive does not turn every high-gain or fuzzy setting into another category when the builder continues to name the product as an Overdrive or Fuzz.
