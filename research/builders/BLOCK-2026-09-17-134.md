# Block 134 — Black Arts Toneworks

## Scope
Builder → pedals only. Target categories are Overdrive, Distortion, and Fuzz. Non-dirt functions are excluded from the census. Cosmetic editions, custom-shop one-offs, and ordinary revisions are not counted as separate pedal identities unless they represent a distinct product.

## Primary source
- Black Arts Toneworks official pedal archive: https://www.blackartstoneworks.com/pedal/
- Black Arts Toneworks official site: https://www.blackartstoneworks.com/

## Overdrive
- BAT — official description says it can act as a boost or driver and become a standalone distortion.
- Raw Heart OD — distortion/overdrive design, explicitly built around distortion use.
- Black Forest — official archive describes it as a dirt/overdrive design with added fuzz range.
- Tres Diablos Ruidosos — ranges from boost and edgy OD through distortion and into oversaturated fuzz.
- Witch Burner MkII — overdrive.
- Pharaoh — primarily fuzz, but official description explicitly covers light OD; counted as Fuzz + Overdrive.
- Revelation — amp-pusher/drive; distortion gain section is central to its operation, counted as Overdrive + Distortion.

## Distortion
- BAT — standalone distortion capability explicitly stated by builder.
- Raw Heart OD — builder describes it as a distortion pedal; despite the OD name, counted as Overdrive + Distortion.
- Quantum Mystic — distortion.
- Priestess — fuzz/distortion design; counted as Fuzz + Distortion.
- Black Forest — overdrive/fuzz design with higher-gain dirt; counted as Overdrive + Fuzz rather than inventing a separate distortion identity.
- Tres Diablos Ruidosos — explicitly reaches classic 70s rock distortion and proto-metal crunch; counted as Overdrive + Distortion + Fuzz.
- Revelation — explicit distortion gain function; counted as Overdrive + Distortion.
- FNORD — official description combines octaver/fuzz with traditional fuzz/distortion; counted as Fuzz + Distortion.
- Destroyer — combined Ritual/Oath design described as fuzz and amp-slammer; counted as Fuzz + Distortion only where the builder's description supports the distortion/preamp role.

## Fuzz
- Pharaoh — classic fuzz roots and saturated fuzz range.
- LSTR — official archive describes it as delivering more fuzz than its Pharaoh relative.
- Black Sheep — Fuzz.
- Black Forest — Overdrive/Fuzz.
- Priestess — Fuzz + Distortion.
- FNORD — Octaver/Fuzz with traditional fuzz/distortion.
- Rabid Mammal — fuzz section based on a modified Supreme/Pharaoh-family design.
- Destroyer — controllable wall of fuzz combined with a second amp-slamming section.
- Sarcophagus — dual Pharaoh/LSTR; counted as Fuzz, with the order-switch function excluded.
- Coven — dual Pharaoh/Black Forest; counted as Fuzz + Overdrive because those are the underlying dirt identities.
- Son of Pharaoh — Pharaoh-family fuzz.
- Pharaoh Supreme — Pharaoh-family fuzz.
- Witch Burner MkII — not counted as fuzz merely because it can become fuzzy; primary identity is overdrive.

## Explicit overlaps
- Pharaoh = Overdrive + Fuzz
- Raw Heart OD = Overdrive + Distortion
- Revelation = Overdrive + Distortion
- Black Forest = Overdrive + Fuzz
- Tres Diablos Ruidosos = Overdrive + Distortion + Fuzz
- Priestess = Distortion + Fuzz
- FNORD = Distortion + Fuzz
- Destroyer = Distortion + Fuzz
- Coven = Overdrive + Fuzz
- Sarcophagus = Fuzz; its two underlying circuits do not create a separate new category beyond the dirt identities already represented.

## Historical / variant restraint
- Pharaoh revisions and cosmetic/Blackout editions are consolidated into the Pharaoh lineage.
- Rabid Mammal V2 and earlier Rabid Mammal/custom-shop form are one lineage.
- Witch Burner revisions are consolidated unless a clearly distinct product is documented.
- Custom Shop one-offs, B-stock, special enclosure colors, and artwork variants are excluded.
- Boost, octave, preamp, and switching functions are not counted as separate target categories unless the builder explicitly describes the same pedal as an Overdrive, Distortion, or Fuzz device.
- No components, schematics, PCB information, BOMs, gutshots, cloning instructions, or construction details are included.

## Verification note
The official Black Arts Toneworks archive currently lists BAT, Black Forest, Black Sheep, Coven, Crown of Horns, Destroyer, FNORD, LSTR, Pharaoh, Pharaoh Supreme, Priestess, Quantum Mystic, Rabid Mammal, Raw Heart OD, Revelation, Sarcophagus, Skyboost, Son of Pharaoh, Tres Diablos Ruidosos, and Witch Burner MkII. Individual official pages were used where needed to resolve category overlap and historical status.
