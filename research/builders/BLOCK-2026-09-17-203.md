## Evidence / classification notes

- Collateral FX's current official catalog lists a large set of dirt products spanning fuzz, overdrive, and distortion. The official catalog and category pages explicitly identify the products above by those dirt functions.
- P029 Ring System is explicitly presented as an overdrive/distortion unit, so both dirt rows are retained.
- P019 Slow is explicitly presented as a distortion/fuzz design, so both rows are retained.
- P017 Unchained is explicitly presented as overdrive/distortion, so both rows are retained.
- P015 White Russian is explicitly presented as distortion/fuzz, so both rows are retained.
- P010 Year of Rat 1985 is explicitly presented as distortion/overdrive, so both rows are retained.
- Boost-only or mixed-function products are not separately added when the reviewed catalog does not explicitly identify an archive dirt class.
- Charlie Pedals is a currently documented UK pedal builder. Recent marketplace listings document the Troubadour MKI.5, Troubadour 2023, and Theophane as fuzz pedals. A February 2026 Effects Database weekly overview also records Charlie Pedals as a newly added brand.
- Charlie Pedals is treated as distinct from the already-canonical Charlie Paolo Custom Effects (#395), because the sources identify different builder names and product lines.

## Exclusions / non-additions

- Collateral FX P025 and similar preamp/booster-only products are excluded unless an explicit overdrive, distortion, or fuzz classification is given.
- Charlie Pedals variants are kept as distinct product listings where the source explicitly identifies separate models/years, but they do not create separate builder identities.
- Cloak Audio Jackdaw remains deferred for Fredric Effects identity reconciliation.
- Covington Guitar Effects remains excluded because the reviewed dirt-adjacent models are primarily boost/preamp/phaser products rather than clearly cataloged overdrive, distortion, or fuzz models.
