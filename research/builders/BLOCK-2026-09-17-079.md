# Block 079 — TC Electronic

## Builder
**TC Electronic**

## Verification basis
Primary and authoritative sources reviewed:
- TC Electronic official current product catalog
- TC Electronic official individual product pages
- TC Electronic manufacturer-authored Vintage Pedals manual
- TC Electronic manufacturer-authored VPD1 manual
- TC Electronic manufacturer-authored NOVA Drive manual
- authoritative product documentation for older TC Electronic dirt models where current product pages are archival or incomplete

The active repository contained no prior TC Electronic match before this block was added. TC Electronic is treated as the builder identity for TC-branded guitar dirt pedals documented below.

The reviewed material supports a broad dirt catalog spanning current/recent products and historical designs. Dirt categories are assigned from explicit product names, manufacturer category descriptions, or direct section/function descriptions in manufacturer manuals. Generic gain or distortion-like language is not used to create unsupported secondary categories.

## Overdrive
Current / manufacturer-supported identities:
- MojoMojo Overdrive
- MojoMojo Paul Gilbert Signature Overdrive
- Zeus Drive
- El Cambo Overdrive
- Cinders Overdrive
- Tube Pilot Overdrive

Historical / manufacturer-supported identities:
- Vintage Overdrive
- VPD1 Vintage Pre-Drive
- Nova Drive

Verification notes:
- **MojoMojo Overdrive** is explicitly identified by TC Electronic as Overdrive.
- **MojoMojo Paul Gilbert Signature Overdrive** is a distinct named MojoMojo-family overdrive product and remains separate from the standard MojoMojo identity.
- **Zeus Drive** is presented as a drive/overdrive product in TC Electronic's current guitar-effects catalog.
- **El Cambo Overdrive** is explicitly described as a vintage all-analog overdrive.
- **Cinders Overdrive** is explicitly named and cataloged as Overdrive.
- **Tube Pilot Overdrive** is explicitly named and cataloged as Overdrive.
- **Vintage Overdrive** is explicitly named in TC Electronic's manufacturer-authored Vintage Pedals manual.
- **VPD1 Vintage Pre-Drive** is explicitly described as a vintage pre-drive whose basic drive section delivers distinguished vintage overdrive.
- **Nova Drive** contains a dedicated analog overdrive section and is documented by TC Electronic as an overdrive/distortion pedal. It therefore receives both categories below.

Historical / manufacturer-supported identities:

Verification notes:

- **Eyemaster Metal Distortion** is explicitly named and cataloged as Distortion.
- **Grand Magus Distortion** is explicitly presented by TC Electronic as a distortion pedal. Its broad gain range is retained within the Distortion identity rather than creating a secondary Overdrive identity.
- **Magus Pro** is a named Magus-family distortion product and remains under Distortion for the initial census.
- **Fangs Metal Distortion** is explicitly named and cataloged as a metal distortion pedal.
- **Röttweiler Distortion** is explicitly described by TC Electronic as an all-analog distortion circuit.
- **Vintage Distortion** and **Vintage Dual Distortion** are explicitly identified in TC Electronic's manufacturer-authored Vintage Pedals manual.
- **Nova Drive** has a separate distortion section in the manufacturer manual and is explicitly documented as an overdrive/distortion pedal, so it receives both categories.

## Fuzz
Current / manufacturer-supported identities:
- Honey Pot Fuzz
- Rusty Fuzz

Historical / manufacturer-supported identities:
- none identified

Verification notes:
- **Honey Pot Fuzz** is explicitly described by TC Electronic as a vintage-flavored fuzz pedal. Its wide gain range reaches overdrive-like grind and thick distortion textures, but its product identity remains Fuzz only for this census.
- **Rusty Fuzz** is explicitly described as a silicon-based transistor fuzz pedal with vintage gated and velcro-like fuzz tones. It remains Fuzz only.

## Category-overlap decisions
- **MojoMojo Overdrive**: Overdrive only.
- **MojoMojo Paul Gilbert Signature Overdrive**: Overdrive only.
- **Zeus Drive**: Overdrive only based on its drive/overdrive product positioning.
- **El Cambo Overdrive**: Overdrive only.
- **Cinders Overdrive**: Overdrive only.
- **Tube Pilot Overdrive**: Overdrive only.
- **Vintage Overdrive**: Overdrive only.
- **VPD1 Vintage Pre-Drive**: Overdrive only. The boost section and speaker-simulation function do not create additional target identities.
- **Nova Drive**: Overdrive + Distortion. Manufacturer documentation explicitly provides separate overdrive and distortion sections and identifies the product as overdrive/distortion.
- **Dark Matter Distortion**: Distortion only.
- **Eyemaster Metal Distortion**: Distortion only.
- **Grand Magus Distortion**: Distortion only. Its Gain control may move through overdrive territory, but product identity and catalog wording are Distortion.
- **Magus Pro**: Distortion only.
- **Fangs Metal Distortion**: Distortion only.
- **Röttweiler Distortion**: Distortion only.
- **Vintage Distortion**: Distortion only.
- **Vintage Dual Distortion**: Distortion only.
- **Honey Pot Fuzz**: Fuzz only. Its overdrive-like lower-gain range does not create a separate Overdrive identity.
- **Rusty Fuzz**: Fuzz only.

## Historical / variant handling
- TC Electronic current/recent product names are retained as distinct identities when the manufacturer presents them as distinct named products.
- MojoMojo Paul Gilbert Signature Overdrive remains separate from standard MojoMojo Overdrive because it is a distinct named signature product.
- Nova Drive remains a single named identity despite its separately switchable overdrive and distortion sections; those sections determine category overlap but do not create duplicate products.
- Vintage Overdrive, Vintage Distortion, and Vintage Dual Distortion remain separate historical identities because TC Electronic's manufacturer-authored manual names them separately.
- VPD1 remains separate from Vintage Overdrive because the manufacturer presents it as a distinct vintage pre-drive product with a different control and boost architecture.
- Cosmetic editions, presentation variants, firmware/TonePrint variations, and stock status do not create additional functional dirt identities.

## Exclusions
- Boost-only and utility products such as Spark Booster, Mini Spark, Bonafide Buffer, and similar non-target products: outside the target taxonomy.
- Modulation, delay, tuner, reverb, chorus, and other non-dirt TC Electronic effects: outside scope.
- TonePrint artist presets and downloadable variations are control/preset content, not separate builder/product identities.
- Generic gain, saturation, clipping, or distortion language without product-level category evidence is not used to create secondary identities.
- Multi-effects/modeling platforms and their internal modeled dirt algorithms are not promoted into the standalone pedal census.
- Cloning, schematics, internal construction, component details, and reverse-engineering material are outside scope.
