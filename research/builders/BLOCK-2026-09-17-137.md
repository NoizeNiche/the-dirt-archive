# Block 137 — ThorpyFX

## Scope
Builder → Pedals only. Target categories: Overdrive, Distortion, Fuzz. Non-target functions are not counted as separate dirt categories.

## Sources
- ThorpyFX official pedal catalog: https://thorpyfx.com/en-us/collections/thorpyfx-pedals

- ThorpyFX official fuzz catalog: https://thorpyfx.com/collections/overdrive-pedals-copy
- ThorpyFX official all-products catalog: https://thorpyfx.com/en-us/collections/all

## Explicit overlaps
- Scarlet Tunic = Overdrive + Distortion. Its primary identity is amp-emulation/drive, so no additional non-target category is created.
- The Old Timer = Overdrive + Distortion + Fuzz. It is a three-section dirt pedal, so all three target categories are retained.
- WOPR = Fuzz. Boost is excluded as a non-target function.
- Six Zero = Fuzz. Tremolo is excluded as a non-target function.

## Variant / historical restraint
- MKI/MKII and special-edition treatments are consolidated where they represent the same pedal lineage rather than separate builder relationships.
- Birthday/heritage/artwork editions are not counted as new pedals.
- Bundles are excluded.
- Boost, compressor, modulation, and other non-target functions are excluded from the dirt census.
- No components, schematics, PCB, BOM, gutshots, cloning, or build instructions are included.

## Verification note
The official ThorpyFX catalog currently identifies pedals such as KUDU, Bunker, Electric Lightning, Peacekeeper and Gunshot as overdrive products; Warthog as distortion; and Fallout Cloud, Field Marshal and WOPR as fuzz products. The catalog also identifies Scarlet Tunic and The Old Timer as multi-function drive products. These relationships were used as the basis for this census block.
