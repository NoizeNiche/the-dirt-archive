# Builder -> Pedals Research Block 166

## Scope

Scrape C continuation after Century. Only verified dirt records are included: overdrive, distortion, and fuzz. Boost-only, modulation, delay, and utility products are excluded.

## Century

- **Century SHM-8 Heavy Metal** — Distortion
  - Effects Database documents it as a vintage Korean analog heavy-metal distortion pedal, part of the Century SXX-0 family.
  - Source: Effects Database, Century SHM-8 Heavy Metal.
- **Century SDI-1 Distortion** — Distortion
  - Effects Database identifies the SDI-1 as the original Century distortion in the shared Taiwanese OEM family later rebranded under multiple names.
  - Source: Effects Database, related-product documentation for the Century SDI-1 / SDI-1A family.

## César Diaz

- **Diaz Texas Square Face** — Fuzz
  - Effects Database identifies the pedal as a fuzz designed as a more reliable replacement for Stevie Ray Vaughan's Fuzz Face.
  - Source: Effects Database, Diaz Texas Square Face.

## ChadderBox Effects

- **Loud/Louder** — Overdrive
- **Loud/Louder** — Distortion
  - The builder describes two independent MOSFET gain channels with enough gain to drive an amp into natural overdrive; stacked operation can generate distortion. Reverb also categorizes the pedal under Overdrive and Boost and Distortion.
  - Source: ChadderBox Effects official product page; Reverb listing.

## Champion City Effects

- **Cosmic Owl** — Distortion
- **Cosmonaut** — Distortion
- **El Gato Malo** — Distortion
- **Ezekiel** — Fuzz
- **Ezekiel** — Overdrive
- **Ezekiel** — Distortion
- **Mjolnir Heavy Distortion** — Distortion
  - RockBoard's Champion City catalog identifies Cosmic Owl and Cosmonaut as distortion, Ezekiel as fuzz/overdrive/distortion, El Gato Malo as distortion, and Mjolnir as heavy distortion.
  - Sources: RockBoard PedalPedia entries for Champion City Effects; Champion City Effects Reverb shop/about page.

## Champion Leccy

- **The Dunsh** — Fuzz
- **The Dunsh** — Distortion
- **The Gett** — Fuzz
- **Evil Twin** — Distortion
- **Evil Twin** — Fuzz
- **Canny Fettle Boost** — Overdrive
- **Canny Fettle Boost** — Distortion
- **The Divvy** — Fuzz
- **The Divvy** — Overdrive
  - The Dunsh is documented by Champion Leccy as an amp-smashing fuzz-stortion/gain pedal. The Gett is a Black Russian Muff-derived fuzz. The Evil Twin has gain ranges reaching distortion and fuzz. Canny Fettle's Clart channel is an overdrive with a distortion toggle. The Divvy is documented as a fuzz/crunch pedal with overdrive-like crunchy settings.
  - Sources: Champion Leccy official product/design pages; Effects Database; RockBoard; Delicious Audio; Guitar Pedal X; Long & McQuade.

## Exclusions / notes

- Century delay products were excluded as time-based effects.
- Champion Leccy's Skitzy and Swan Hunter were excluded as reverb/echo products despite the Skitzy's ability to introduce clipping at extreme internal settings.
- No new records were added for products whose available evidence did not clearly establish a dirt function.

## Builder identity result

This block establishes five new canonical builder identities for the archive: Century, César Diaz, ChadderBox Effects, Champion City Effects, and Champion Leccy.
