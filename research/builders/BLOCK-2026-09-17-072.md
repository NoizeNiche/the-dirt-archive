# Block 072 — DOD Electronics

## Builder
**DOD Electronics / DOD**

## Verification basis
Primary manufacturer / authoritative sources reviewed:
- DigiTech/DOD official History page
- current DOD product pages hosted by DigiTech
- official DOD/DigiTech Product Manuals archive
- official DOD shop/catalog pages
- authoritative DOD catalog archive material from AmericasPedals.net for historical FX-series identities not currently surfaced on the manufacturer site

The current manufacturer history explicitly distinguishes DOD and DigiTech as two brands under one continuing legacy. This block records **DOD** only and does not merge DigiTech-branded HardWire, X-Series, Artist Series, or other DigiTech products into the builder census.

The active repository contained no prior DOD match before this block was added. Dirt categories are based on explicit manufacturer product names, catalog labels, manuals, or strong authoritative catalog documentation. Generic references to gain, saturation, clipping, or a product reaching another sound are not used to create a second target category unless the source explicitly supports Overdrive, Distortion, or Fuzz.

## Overdrive
Current / manufacturer-supported identities:
- Overdrive Preamp 250
- Overdrive Preamp 250-X
- Looking Glass
- Badder Monkey

Historical / manufacturer-supported identities:
- FX50-B Overdrive Plus
- FX51 Juice Box Overdrive
- FX91 Bass Overdrive
- FX100 Even Harmonic Overdrive
- FX101 Grind Rectifying Overdrive
- FX102 Mystic Blues Overdrive
- YJM 308 Overdrive Preamp

Verification notes:
- **Overdrive Preamp 250** is explicitly presented as an Overdrive Preamp and is the current DOD continuation of the original 250 design. Its current product information also calls it Distortion + Boost, so it is retained in Distortion below as an explicit overlap.
- **Overdrive Preamp 250-X** is explicitly presented as the tenth evolution of the DOD 250, with three selectable overdrive modes and a gain control covering subtle grit through full distortion. It remains Overdrive only because the manufacturer frames the primary product identity as Overdrive Preamp rather than as a separate Distortion product.
- **Looking Glass** is explicitly presented as an Overdrive and describes low- to high-gain overdrive modes. Its references to overdrive/distortion in the control descriptions are treated as operating-range language, not a separate Distortion identity.
- **Badder Monkey** explicitly provides three independent overdrives and retains the DOD Bad Monkey identity, so it is recorded as Overdrive only.
- **FX50-B Overdrive Plus**, **FX51 Juice Box Overdrive**, **FX91 Bass Overdrive**, **FX100 Even Harmonic Overdrive**, **FX101 Grind Rectifying Overdrive**, **FX102 Mystic Blues Overdrive**, and **YJM 308 Overdrive Preamp** are explicitly documented by DOD's official manuals/catalog archive as Overdrive products.

Verification notes:

- **Gunslinger** is explicitly described as aggressive MOSFET distortion by DOD.
- **Boneshaker** is a DOD distortion/dirty-boost product with an explicit Distortion control; the EQ and boost functions are secondary and outside the target taxonomy.
- **FX55C**, **FX57**, **FX59**, **FX70**, and **FX86** are explicitly identified in DOD's official manuals archive as Supra Distortion, Hard Rock Distortion, Thrash Master, Metal X, and Death Metal Distortion respectively.

- **FX69B Grunge** is explicitly documented by DOD as a DOD Grunge pedal and is retained as Distortion based on DOD's current history describing the model as an extreme grind/distortion design and its manufacturer documentation identifying the product as DOD Grunge.
- **FX76 Punkifier** is historically documented in DOD-era product material as a distortion pedal capable of overdrive-to-fuzz textures. It remains Distortion only for this census because the manufacturer-era product identity is distortion rather than a separate Fuzz product.
- **D&S / D&S II** are explicit Distortion & Sustainer products; sustainer functionality is outside the target taxonomy.
- **Performer 555** is explicitly a Distortion product in the DOD Performer catalog.
- **FX33 Buzz Box** is retained in Distortion because its documented DOD signal path includes the FX69 Grunge distortion stage; it is also retained in Fuzz below because authoritative product documentation identifies it as an octave-fuzz product.

## Category-overlap decisions
- **Overdrive Preamp 250**: Overdrive + Distortion. The manufacturer simultaneously presents it as an Overdrive Preamp and as Distortion + Boost.
- **Overdrive Preamp 250-X**: Overdrive only. Its heavy-distortion capability is treated as a range descriptor while the product identity remains Overdrive Preamp.
- **Looking Glass**: Overdrive only. Manufacturer product identity and operating modes emphasize overdrive; distortion wording is descriptive of the gain range.
- **Badder Monkey**: Overdrive only. Manufacturer explicitly identifies three overdrives.
- **Gonkulator**: Distortion only. Distortion is explicit; ring modulation is outside the target taxonomy.
- **Gunslinger**: Distortion only. Manufacturer explicitly calls it MOSFET Distortion / aggressive distortion.
- **Boneshaker**: Distortion only. The product exposes a Distortion control and is presented as a distortion/dirty-boost design; boost and EQ are secondary functions.
- **FX33 Buzz Box**: Distortion + Fuzz. Its historical architecture combines the DOD Grunge distortion path with an octave-fuzz function, and authoritative documentation identifies both effects.
- **FX76 Punkifier**: Distortion only. Its range from overdrive to fuzz is treated as tonal range within a distortion product identity, not as two separate target categories.
- **Carcosa / Chthonic / FX52 / FX66**: Fuzz only. Distortion-like language does not create an additional category when the manufacturer explicitly presents the product as Fuzz.

## Historical / variant handling
- DOD has a long FX-series catalog and a current revival/reissue line. Clearly named functional products are retained even when later reissues use the same underlying design.
- **Overdrive Preamp 250 50th Anniversary** is consolidated into the 250 identity rather than treated as a new dirt model because it is an anniversary presentation of the same core product identity.
- **Overdrive Preamp 250-X** remains separate because DOD explicitly identifies it as the tenth evolution with a new three-position clipping system.
- **FX55B / FX55C Supra Distortion**: the currently documented FX55C identity is retained; minor suffix/version presentation is not inflated into multiple independent dirt identities unless the manufacturer treats the model as a distinct functional product.
- **FX56 / FX56B** and other closely related FX-series revisions are consolidated where they represent the same named functional family, while distinct named follow-ons such as FX57 and FX58 are retained separately.
- DOD's current manufacturer history identifies DOD as the analog-pedal brand and DigiTech as the separate digital/effects brand. DigiTech-branded products are therefore excluded from this builder block even when the products appear on the same manufacturer website.
- Cosmetic, regional, anniversary, and minor hardware variations are consolidated unless DOD gives the product a distinct functional identity.

## Exclusions
- DigiTech-branded HardWire, X-Series, Artist Series, D-Series, Whammy, and other DigiTech products: excluded from the DOD builder block.
- Boost-only DOD products such as BiFET Boost 410: excluded unless the manufacturer explicitly identifies the product as Overdrive, Distortion, or Fuzz.
- Compressors, envelope filters, phasers, choruses, delays, reverbs, octave-only products, loopers, mixers, power products, and other non-target effects: excluded.
- Generic references to saturation, clipping, “distortion,” or high gain do not add categories without product-level target-category support.
