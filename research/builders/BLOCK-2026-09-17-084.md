# Block 084 — VOX

## Builder
**VOX**

## Verification basis
Primary and authoritative sources reviewed:
- VOX official current Single Effects catalog
- VOX official current Valvenergy product pages and manufacturer-authored Valvenergy manual
- VOX official current VFZ-1 Fuzz product page and 2026 launch material
- VOX manufacturer-authored Tone Garage manuals
- VOX Showroom archival product histories for historical VOX pedals
- historical product/manual coverage for the Joe Satriani signature pedal line
- active-repository search for “VOX” before addition

The active repository contained no prior VOX match before this block was added. VOX is treated as the builder/brand identity for VOX-branded dirt pedals documented below. The census stays at the pedal/product level and does not turn ToneLab/StompLab modeled effects into separate physical pedal identities.

The reviewed material supports a broad VOX dirt catalog spanning Overdrive, Distortion, and Fuzz from the 1960s through the current day. Current and historical product identities are retained where the VOX relationship and target dirt function are sufficiently explicit. Internal modes, preset models, and non-dirt functions do not create additional product identities.

## Overdrive
Current / manufacturer-supported identities:
- Copperhead Drive
- Mystic Edge
- Cutting Edge
- Silk Drive
- Fuel Injector
- VFZ-1 Fuzz

Historical / manufacturer-supported identities:
- Big Ben Overdrive
- Straight 6 Drive
- V810 Valvetone
- Ice 9 Overdrive

Verification notes:
- **Copperhead Drive**, **Mystic Edge**, and **Cutting Edge** are VOX Valvenergy valve-distortion pedals whose manufacturer documentation explicitly says they provide genuine overdrive and distortion tones. They therefore receive both categories below.
- **Silk Drive** is explicitly presented by VOX as a valve overdrive pedal and remains Overdrive only.
- **Fuel Injector** is explicitly presented by VOX as an overdrive pedal. Generic references to its organic tube-like distortion remain operating range rather than a second catalog identity.
- **VFZ-1 Fuzz** is included under Overdrive because VOX explicitly documents its Crunch switch as producing lower-gain overdrive tones while the main product remains a Fuzz pedal.
- **Big Ben Overdrive**, **Straight 6 Drive**, **V810 Valvetone**, and **Ice 9 Overdrive** are explicitly identified as VOX overdrive products in VOX historical/catalog material.

## Distortion
Current / manufacturer-supported identities:
- Copperhead Drive
- Mystic Edge
- Cutting Edge

Historical / manufacturer-supported identities:
- V8 Distortion
- Bulldog Distortion
- Satchurator
- VOX 7001 Distortion

Verification notes:
- **Copperhead Drive**, **Mystic Edge**, and **Cutting Edge** receive Distortion from their manufacturer presentation as valve distortion pedals and their explicit documentation of overdrive/distortion tones.
- **V8 Distortion** is retained as Distortion only. Its ability to produce lower-gain overdriven sounds is treated as range within the named distortion product rather than a separate catalog category.
- **Bulldog Distortion** is retained as Distortion only. Its historical descriptions of fuzz-like channel voices do not create a separate Fuzz identity because the named product is explicitly a distortion pedal.
- **Satchurator** is explicitly documented as a VOX Joe Satriani signature distortion pedal.
- **VOX 7001 Distortion** is retained as a distinct historical VOX distortion pedal identity from the early-1980s 7000-series effects.

## Fuzz
Current / manufacturer-supported identities:
- VFZ-1 Fuzz

Historical / manufacturer-supported identities:
- Trike Fuzz
- V828 Tone Bender
- V829 Tone Bender
- V830 Distortion Booster

Verification notes:
- **VFZ-1 Fuzz** is explicitly presented by VOX as a current vintage-style fuzz pedal inspired by the late-1960s Tone Bender Mk 1.5 family. VOX also documents its Crunch mode as a lower-gain overdrive function, supporting the Overdrive overlap above.
- **Trike Fuzz** is a historical VOX Tone Garage octave-fuzz pedal.
- **V828 Tone Bender** is a VOX-branded historical Tone Bender fuzz product from the 1960s. The pedal was sourced through VOX's relationship with Sola Sound/JEN, but the product was marketed under the VOX identity and is therefore recorded under VOX here.
- **V829 Tone Bender** is the later Korg/VOX Tone Bender fuzz product and is kept distinct from the V828 because it is a separately named historical VOX model.
- **V830 Distortion Booster** is recorded as Fuzz because VOX archival material documents “Distortion Booster” as period VOX terminology for fuzz and describes its Drive control as adjusting fuzz intensity.

## Category-overlap decisions
- **Copperhead Drive**: Overdrive + Distortion. VOX describes it as a valve distortion pedal with genuine overdrive and distortion tones.
- **Mystic Edge**: Overdrive + Distortion. VOX describes it as a valve distortion pedal and explicitly documents genuine overdrive and distortion tones.
- **Cutting Edge**: Overdrive + Distortion. VOX describes it as a valve distortion pedal and explicitly documents genuine overdrive and distortion tones.
- **Silk Drive**: Overdrive only. Explicit VOX product identity is valve overdrive.
- **Fuel Injector**: Overdrive only. Explicit VOX product identity is overdrive.
- **VFZ-1 Fuzz**: Fuzz + Overdrive. VOX explicitly gives the Crunch switch a lower-gain overdrive function, but the named product remains Fuzz.
- **V8 Distortion**: Distortion only. Lower-gain overdrive-like sounds are treated as tonal range, not a second identity.
- **Bulldog Distortion**: Distortion only. Fuzz-like internal channel voicing does not create a separate Fuzz category.
- **V830 Distortion Booster**: Fuzz only. Period VOX terminology and product documentation identify the dirt function as fuzz.

## Historical / variant handling
- **V828 Tone Bender** and **V829 Tone Bender** remain separate named identities because they are distinct VOX-branded models from different eras.
- VOX's **Valvenergy 1st Series** products are recorded as individual pedal identities: Copperhead Drive, Cutting Edge, Mystic Edge, and Silk Drive. Their STANDARD, PREAMP, and CAB-SIM output modes are internal functions.
- **VFZ-1 Fuzz** and **VTB-1 Treble Booster** are separate 2026 VOX products. VTB-1 is excluded because it is a boost-only product even though VOX describes it as capable of pushing an amplifier into overdrive.
- Cosmetic editions, regional presentation, internal voicing modes, and hardware/presentation revisions do not create extra dirt identities.
- ToneLab and StompLab modeled effects are excluded as virtual effect models inside multi-effects products rather than standalone named VOX dirt pedals.

## Exclusions
- Flat 4 Boost and Black 9 Boost: boost-focused products; no separate named dirt identity is promoted despite available gain range.
- VTB-1 Treble Booster: boost-only product; excluded from the target taxonomy.
- Brit Boost and other boost-only VOX products: outside target taxonomy.
- VOX ToneLab, StompLab, and related modeled multi-effects products: their modeled Drive effects are not treated as separate physical pedal identities.
- Wah, delay, chorus, flanger, phaser, compressor, looper, EQ, and other non-dirt VOX products: outside scope.
- Amplifiers, cabinets, and amp models are not pedal identities for this census.
- Cloning, schematics, components, internal construction, and reverse-engineering material: outside scope.
