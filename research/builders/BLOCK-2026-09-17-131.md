# The Dirt Archive - Builder Census Block 131

## Builder
**Walrus Audio**

Walrus Audio is a previously uncovered builder with a clearly documented current and historical dirt catalog. Its official catalog groups Overdrive, Fuzz, and Distortion together, while its discontinued catalog preserves several older dirt products. This block follows Walrus Audio's own product identities and consolidates routine variants into lineages where appropriate.

## Overdrive
- **Voyager Preamp/Overdrive / Voyager Preamp/Overdrive MKII** - Overdrive lineage; the original Voyager is discontinued and the MKII is current.
- **385 Overdrive / 385 Overdrive MKII** - Overdrive lineage.
- **Ages Five-State Overdrive** - current Overdrive.
- **Warhorn Mid-Range Overdrive** - historical/discontinued Overdrive.
- **Fundamental Series Drive** - historical/discontinued Overdrive.
- **Badwater Bass Pre-amp and D.I.** - historical/discontinued product with an explicitly documented overdrive section; retained as Overdrive for the dirt census.

## Distortion
- **Iron Horse LM308 Distortion** - current Distortion.
- **Eras Five-State Distortion** - current Distortion.
- **Fundamental Series Distortion** - historical/discontinued Distortion.
- **Melee: Wall of Noise** - current Distortion + Reverb; the target dirt identity is Distortion and the reverb function is outside scope.

## Fuzz
- **Eons Five-State Fuzz** - current Fuzz.
- **Silt Harmonic Fuzz** - current Fuzz.
- **Janus Fuzz/Tremolo** - historical/current product lineage; target dirt identity is Fuzz, with Tremolo outside scope.
- **Jupiter Multi-Clip Fuzz / Jupiter Multi-Clip Fuzz V2** - historical Jupiter Fuzz lineage.
- **Fundamental Series Fuzz** - historical/discontinued Fuzz.

## Explicit category overlaps
- **Badwater Bass Pre-amp and D.I. = Overdrive + non-target preamp/compression/EQ/DI functions.** Walrus explicitly says the unit features overdrive alongside optical compression and four-band EQ. Only the overdrive identity is retained here.
- **Melee = Distortion + Reverb.** Walrus explicitly describes Melee as a distortion + reverb combination; only Distortion is part of this census.
- **Janus = Fuzz + Tremolo.** Walrus explicitly identifies Janus as Fuzz/Tremolo; only Fuzz is counted.
- **Eons = Fuzz**, despite individual clipping modes producing distortion-like tones. The builder's product identity is Fuzz, so no extra Distortion category is inferred.

## Historical / variant restraint
- **Voyager and Voyager MKII** are one Voyager Overdrive lineage rather than separate unrelated designs.
- **385 and 385 MKII** are one 385 Overdrive lineage.
- **Jupiter and Jupiter V2** are one Jupiter Fuzz lineage.
- **Fundamental Drive, Fundamental Distortion, and Fundamental Fuzz** remain separate target dirt products because Walrus explicitly named and described them as three different effects.
- Cosmetic/minimal variants are not counted as new dirt designs.
- Reverb, delay, modulation, octave, boost, EQ, compression, utility, and amp/cab products remain outside the target categories unless Walrus explicitly identifies a target dirt function as part of the product. When a combination pedal contains a target dirt section, only that dirt category is recorded.
- No components, schematics, cloning, PCB, BOM, or internal-build information is recorded in this block.

## Research note
Walrus Audio's official catalog makes the current dirt group easy to verify, while the discontinued page supplies authoritative historical identities. The conservative census follows Walrus's own labels instead of creating extra categories from tonal capability. The clearest multi-effect overlaps are Badwater (Overdrive plus non-target functions), Melee (Distortion plus Reverb), and Janus (Fuzz plus Tremolo).
