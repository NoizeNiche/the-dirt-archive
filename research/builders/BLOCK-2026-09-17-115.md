# The Dirt Archive — Builder Census Block 115

## Builder
**OKKO FX**

OKKO FX's official site identifies the company as a German pedal builder based in Leipzig and states that it has been making pedals since 2003, with current pedals handbuilt by musicians and technicians there.

## Scope result
OKKO FX has a strong current and historical dirt footprint. The present catalog includes multiple overdrive and distortion products plus the Holy Grit hybrid and a current Green Russian Muff-derived fuzz release. Historical references establish the Black Beast as a distinct fuzz/distortion product and preserve the original TwinSonic.

Normal anniversary editions and routine visual variants are consolidated into their underlying named pedal lineages where the product identity is unchanged.

## Explicit category overlaps
- **DIABLO DUAL:** Overdrive + Distortion. OKKO's official page explicitly calls it a 2-channel overdrive/distortion.
- **HOLY GRIT:** Overdrive + Fuzz. OKKO's official page explicitly labels it "Overdrive Fuzz Preamp" and describes the gain control moving from gritty overdrive into low-gain fuzz.
- **Black Beast:** Fuzz + Distortion. The surviving specialist catalog describes it as a large-sounding fuzz/distortion pedal, with its gain structure moving from harmonic distortion into nasty fuzz.
- **DIABLO GH Signature:** Overdrive only within scope. OKKO presents it as overdrive plus an independent clean boost; boost remains outside the target taxonomy.
- **TWINSONIC MKII:** Overdrive only. OKKO describes two stackable overdrive circuits, even though the combined range can approach vintage distortion or fuzz territory. Those sounds are not promoted into separate target categories.
- **The Dynamist:** Overdrive only. It is the current visual/identity successor to the Diablo Dual concept and is presented as a dual FET overdrive. High-gain behavior does not create a separate Distortion entry.
- **BFGRMWB:** Fuzz only. The official current catalog names it as a Green Russian Muff-derived fuzz product; the "with benefits" phrasing does not create a second dirt identity.

## Historical/variant restraint
- **TwinSonic / TwinSonic MKII** are treated as one product lineage with a current MKII form and a historical original rather than as unrelated products.
- **DIABLO / DIABLO DUAL / DIABLO GH** remain distinct named products where the design identity materially differs, but ordinary anniversary/color/enclosure variants are not counted separately.
- **DIABLO 20th Anniversary** is a limited edition of the Diablo and is consolidated with the Diablo lineage rather than counted as a separate pedal.
- **Dirty Diablo** is treated as a variation of the Diablo family and is not promoted to a separate builder/product lineage unless later evidence establishes a materially independent design identity.
- **The Dynamist** is retained as its own current named product because OKKO currently sells it under that name, while its relationship to the Diablo Dual is documented as an evolution/reframing of the same core concept.
- Boost-first products such as the clean-boost section of Diablo GH remain outside the target taxonomy. Compressor, buffer, and other non-target products are excluded.
- No components, schematics, cloning, PCB, BOM, or internal-build information is recorded in this block.

## Research note
OKKO is a useful addition because its catalog demonstrates exactly why the archive's conservative classification matters. DIABLO DUAL is explicitly Overdrive + Distortion, Holy Grit is explicitly Overdrive + Fuzz, and Black Beast is an historically documented Fuzz + Distortion hybrid. By contrast, TwinSonic MKII and The Dynamist can reach more saturated territory but remain Overdrive because that is how the builder presents their core identities.
