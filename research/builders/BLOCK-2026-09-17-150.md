## Research notes

- Atmosfera 6 is documented with several handmade dirt devices, including germanium distortion/fuzz units and the Space or Die variable octave generator with an Electra-based overdrive stage.
- Attic Audio's Putty is a saturation preamp that can operate as overdrive or fuzz.
- Audio Aesthetic's Midnight Drive is an overdrive pedal for bass and guitar.
- Audio Surplus Effects is a small-batch builder with a documented catalog spanning fuzz, distortion, and overdrive products. Massenpanik is kept as one product identity rather than splitting revision labels; Dornenkrone is recorded as both fuzz and distortion based on its documented classification.
- Audiofab currently lists Valve Warper as an overdrive and Chonky Boi as a distortion.
- Audiostorm's documented dirt catalog includes Quad Screamer Overdrive, Grand Classic Distortion, ADNA, F258 Drive, and the Otherworld Overdrive replacement. Booster-only Tone Lord products are excluded.
