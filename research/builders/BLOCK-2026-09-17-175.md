## Evidence / classification notes

- Citadel Electronics' own site documents Particle Drive as an overdrive with a full high-gain distortion range, Overtuber as an overdrive, and the current product catalog includes Plutonium Overdrive. Independent current documentation describes Plutonium as spanning aggressive distortion through warm fuzz.
- Copilot FX's official product catalog identifies Mantis as octave-down PWM fuzz, Orbit as gated fuzz + VCO, Terra as dual parallel fuzz, and Trek as pulse-width fuzz.
- Chunk Systems' official pages document FZ002 and Brown Dog as gated bass fuzz pedals. The later Octavius Squeezer contains the Brown Dog fuzz circuit but is primarily a multi-effect synth platform, so it is not separately entered here.
- Cornell's The 1st Fuzz is documented as a germanium fuzz pedal; contemporary documentation also identifies the Over Drive Special as a dual overdrive pedal.
- Cluster Effects' Dragon-71 is documented as a distortion/sustain pedal and appears in contemporary demos explicitly as a fuzz-oriented dirt device; the database's primary classification is retained as Distortion.
- Effects Database lists Cranetortoise by Albit with the dirt models included here, while boosters, switching, compression, filter, and utility products are excluded.
- Couch Electronics' current site lists Blue Whale Overdrive and Baker's Dozen Overdrive as its pedal products.

## Exclusions / non-additions

- Citadel Bus Driver, Dynazero, Planetary Phase, and other non-dirt functions were excluded.
- Copilot FX Caracol was deferred because its defining function is octave/drone/ring-modulation with a fuzzier mode, not a conventional standalone dirt classification.
- Chunk Systems Agent 00Funk and other filter/synth utilities were excluded from the dirt census.
- Cranetortoise boosters, compressor, switchers, effects loop, and tube booster products were excluded.
- Couch Electronics' current catalog did not surface additional dirt pedals beyond the two verified overdrives in this pass.
