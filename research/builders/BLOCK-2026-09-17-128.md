# The Dirt Archive - Builder Census Block 128

## Builder
**Wampler Pedals**

Wampler Pedals is a previously uncovered builder with a substantial documented dirt catalog spanning Overdrive, Distortion, and Fuzz. Wampler's current catalog separates Distortion / Overdrive and Fuzz, and its official downloads page preserves a dedicated list of discontinued products. The census below follows Wampler's own product/category language and consolidates ordinary revisions into product lineages where appropriate. citeturn4search3turn5search0

## Scope result
This block records named Wampler dirt pedals whose official catalog, product pages, manuals/downloads, or Wampler's own historical documentation clearly establish Overdrive, Distortion, or Fuzz identities. Gain behavior alone is not used to create extra categories. Hybrid entries are retained only where Wampler explicitly presents the pedal as containing multiple dirt types or the official site categorizes it accordingly.

## Historical / variant restraint
- **Tumnus family** is consolidated into one lineage across Tumnus, Deluxe, and Germanium versions rather than multiplying routine revisions. citeturn5search0
- **Pinnacle family** is consolidated across Standard, Deluxe, Deluxe v1, and Deluxe v2. The named family remains Distortion. citeturn2search1turn5search2
- **Plexi Drive family** is consolidated across Plexi Drive, Deluxe, and Mini. The Wampler FAQ explicitly discusses the Plexi-Drive family alongside Plextortion, SuperPlex, and Pinnacle as related dirt products. citeturn5search2
- **Paisley Drive / Paisley Deluxe** remain one product lineage with the Deluxe retained as a two-channel evolution rather than a separate unrelated design. citeturn2search9turn3view1
- **Hot Wired v1/v2** remains one Hot Wired lineage. Wampler's historical documentation explicitly names both versions. citeturn5search1
- **Fuzztration** remains Fuzz despite its ability to produce fuzzy distortion-like tones. Wampler explicitly identifies the product as fuzz. citeturn2search7
- **Leviathan Fuzz** remains Fuzz despite its high-gain range. The official discontinued catalog names it as Fuzz. citeturn5search0
- **Ratsbane** is the unusual case where the builder's current taxonomy and product description support all three target categories, so it is retained as a three-way overlap rather than forcing it into one box. citeturn4search0turn4search6
- **Golden Jubilee, Gearbox, The Fearbox, Brent Mason: ReWired, and Mofetta** retain their documented multi-channel/hybrid identities because Wampler explicitly describes multiple dirt types. citeturn4search2turn4search5turn2search2turn0search12turn0search7
