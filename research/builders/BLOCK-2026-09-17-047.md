# Builder → Pedals Research Block 047

## Builder
**Subdecay — Brian Marshall**

## Verification basis
Subdecay's official site identifies Brian Marshall as the founder of Subdecay in 2004 and as the company's effects designer. The official catalog and individual manufacturer product pages/documentation were used to establish the target dirt identities below. The census includes current and clearly documented discontinued Subdecay pedals where the manufacturer still provides authoritative product material.

## Overdrive
- Gain Theory Overdrive
- Liquid Sunshine
- Supernova Drive
- Blackstar – Ultimate Overdrive
- Variac Overdrive
- Vector – Analog Preamp

## Distortion
- Vector – Analog Preamp

## Fuzz
- Harmonic Antagonizer
- Noise Theory – Parallel Waveshaper Fuzz
- Vector – Analog Preamp

## Product notes
- **Gain Theory Overdrive:** current official product page explicitly identifies it as an overdrive and describes a high-gain design inspired by 1990s amplifier design. Retained under Overdrive only.
- **Liquid Sunshine:** official manufacturer archive identifies it as a Class A Overdrive and notes that it is out of production. Retained as a historical Subdecay overdrive identity.
- **Supernova Drive:** official manufacturer page identifies it as an overdrive and says the pedal was retired. Its description says the upper gain range can approach distortion or fuzz, but the manufacturer identity remains overdrive, so it is not promoted into additional categories.
- **Blackstar – Ultimate Overdrive:** official manufacturer archive identifies the Blackstar as a high-gain overdrive. The later With Girth version is treated as part of the same product identity rather than a separate census entry.
- **Variac Overdrive:** official manufacturer page explicitly calls it an expressive overdrive pedal and describes its amp/power-stage-inspired gain behavior. Retained under Overdrive only.
- **Vector – Analog Preamp:** official product page explicitly describes the Vector as covering overdrive, distortion, and fuzz, while the manual describes its eleven channels as a palette of overdrives, fuzz, and distortion. It is therefore retained in all three target categories as one multi-dirt pedal identity.
- **Harmonic Antagonizer:** official manufacturer page explicitly identifies the product as a fuzz/oscillator and describes its square-wave fuzz circuit. Retained under Fuzz only; its chaotic distortion language is descriptive rather than a separate manufacturer category.
- **Noise Theory – Parallel Waveshaper Fuzz:** official manufacturer page explicitly identifies it as a fuzz and documents multiple fuzz types, including wave-shaper and analog fuzz modes. Retained under Fuzz only.

## Explicit exclusions / restraint
- Synthesizers, modulation, filters, reverbs, delays, utility products, and other non-dirt Subdecay identities were excluded.
- **Prometheus 3** was not counted despite containing an overdrive parameter because the product itself is a dual filter rather than a dirt pedal.
- **M3000** was not counted despite descriptive references to fuzz/overdriven-amplifier character because it is a guitar/bass synthesizer.
- Descriptions of a product approaching distortion or fuzz at extreme gain were not used to manufacture extra category entries unless the manufacturer explicitly establishes that dirt function as part of the product identity.
- **NoiseBox**, referenced historically by Subdecay as a predecessor/related effect to the Harmonic Antagonizer, was not added as a separate census entry because the current authoritative product material does not provide enough direct product identity information to classify it beyond that historical reference.
- Version/cosmetic changes such as Blackstar With Girth were not split into duplicate product identities.
