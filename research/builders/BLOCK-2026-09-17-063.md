# Block 063 — Wampler Pedals — Brian Wampler

## Builder
**Wampler Pedals — Brian Wampler**

## Verification basis
Primary manufacturer sources reviewed:
- Wampler Pedals About page
- current Distortion / Overdrive catalog
- current Overdrive tag index
- current Distortion tag index
- current Fuzz catalog
- official Downloads / manuals archive
- individual manufacturer product pages and manuals for current, discontinued, and category-overlap identities

Wampler's manufacturer About page identifies Brian Wampler as founder, CEO, and chief engineer and says Wampler produces overdrive, distortion, and fuzz pedals. The current manufacturer site maintains separate Overdrive, Distortion, and Fuzz product groupings and an official archive of discontinued product manuals.

This block records builder/product relationships only. Current products are taken from current manufacturer category/tag pages and individual product pages. Historical identities are retained only when manufacturer-originated pages or manuals explicitly identify the product as overdrive, distortion, or fuzz. Cosmetic editions, Halloween/limited finishes, and substantially identical revisions are consolidated rather than split into duplicate pedal identities.

- Brent Mason: ReWired contains separate Overdrive and Distortion circuits and is tagged for both categories.
- Mofetta is explicitly presented as an overdrive / distortion pedal and is included in both categories.
- Pantheon Deluxe is explicitly described by the manufacturer as covering creamy gain, gritty overdrive, and heavy distortion, so it is retained in both categories.
- Ratsbane is a current Distortion / Overdrive + Fuzz identity and is retained in all three target categories because the manufacturer explicitly documents the full range from light overdrive through saturated fuzz and categorizes it in the dirt family.
- Andy Wood: Gearbox has a stand-alone overdrive channel and a Pinnacle-derived distortion channel, so it is retained in both categories.
- Plexi Drive Mini is currently exposed in the manufacturer's Overdrive and Distortion indexes and is retained in both categories.
- Brad Paisley: Paisley Deluxe is treated as Overdrive only despite a current combined-category placement because the manufacturer's product description and manual identify its two channels as overdrive tones, including a v2 UnderDog channel.
- Black '65 and Tweed '57 are retained as Overdrive because manufacturer manuals identify them as vintage OD products.
- Clarksdale is retained as Overdrive from the manufacturer's discontinued product/manual material.
- Hot Wired is retained as a historical two-channel dirt identity because Wampler's v2 documentation identifies the Hot Wired lineage as a two-in-one Overdrive / Distortion design; the archive does not split the v1/v2 family into unnecessary duplicate entries.
- Low Blow is explicitly documented by the manufacturer as Bass Overdrive / Distortion / Notch Filter and is retained in both dirt categories.
- Plexi-Drive is explicitly documented by the manufacturer as a British Overdrive.
- Thirty Something is explicitly documented by the manufacturer as Overdrive.
- UnderDog is explicitly documented by the manufacturer as Overdrive.

## Category-overlap decisions
- **Golden Jubilee**: Overdrive + Distortion. Dedicated OD and DS channels are explicitly documented.
- **Brent Mason: ReWired**: Overdrive + Distortion. The two channels are separately identified as Overdrive and Distortion.
- **Mofetta**: Overdrive + Distortion. Manufacturer explicitly presents it as overdrive / distortion.
- **Pantheon Deluxe**: Overdrive + Distortion. Manufacturer explicitly documents gritty overdrive and heavy distortion.
- **Ratsbane**: Overdrive + Distortion + Fuzz. Current manufacturer categories/tags and product description support all three.
- **Andy Wood: Gearbox**: Overdrive + Distortion. Channel 1 is stand-alone overdrive; channel 2 is Pinnacle-derived distortion.
- **Plexi Drive Mini**: Overdrive + Distortion. Current manufacturer indexes expose both categories.
- **Hot Wired**: Overdrive + Distortion. Wampler's Hot Wired lineage is documented as a two-in-one OD / Distortion design; versioning is consolidated.
- **Low Blow Bass Overdrive / Distortion / Notch Filter**: Overdrive + Distortion. Both target dirt functions are explicit in the manufacturer title.
- **Triple Wreck**: Distortion + Fuzz. Its primary identity is distortion, with explicit manufacturer fuzz behavior in Boost mode.

## Conservative exclusions / variant handling
- **The Fearbox**: manufacturer-labeled limited Halloween take on Gearbox. It is not recorded as a new base pedal identity; consolidated under Andy Wood: Gearbox because the manufacturer presents it as a special edition of that design.
- **Black '65 Limited Edition** and **Tweed '57 Limited Edition**: retained as their underlying Overdrive identities rather than split into finish/edition duplicates.
- Discontinued products such as dB+, Decibel+, Talent Booster, and other booster/utility identities are excluded from the target dirt taxonomy.
- Compression, chorus, delay, reverb, echo, tremolo, and other non-dirt products in the manufacturer's Downloads/archive are excluded.
- **SuperPlex** is retained only as a verification lead, not a categorized core identity, because the primary material reviewed did not provide sufficiently explicit target-category wording.
- **Cranked AC** and **Cranked OD** are retained only as verification leads. They are named in Wampler's historical material, but the manufacturer-originated material reviewed here did not provide enough explicit product-level category evidence to promote them into the core census.
- Generic references to gain, saturation, crunch, clipping, or distortion-like response are not sufficient to create an additional category when the named product identity is explicitly presented under another target category.

## Exclusions
- Boost-only / preamp utilities: dB+, Decibel+, Talent Booster and similar products.
- Compressor-only products and compressor sections without a named target dirt identity.
- Delay/reverb/echo products including Faux Tape Echo, Faux Spring Reverb, Reflection, and related non-dirt identities.
- Modulation and chorus products including Nirvana Chorus.
- Utility/equipment products and non-pedal items in the manufacturer archive.
- Cosmetic-only limited editions and B-stock listings are not separate builder/product identities.
