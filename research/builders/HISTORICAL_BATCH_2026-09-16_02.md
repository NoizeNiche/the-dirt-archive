## Historical Research Rule Applied

This batch intentionally distinguishes between:

- strong catalog evidence suitable for the builder master,
- products visible in secondary references that are useful discovery leads,
- and candidates that require additional verification.

No internal imagery, component details, circuit analysis, exhaustive variant research, or manufacturing genealogy was added. The goal remains Builder -> Pedals first.
