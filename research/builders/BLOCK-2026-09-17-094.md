# Block 094 — Dwarfcraft Devices

## Builder
**Dwarfcraft Devices / Benjamin Hinz and Louise Woletz Hinz**

## Verification basis
Primary / authoritative and strong historical sources reviewed:
- Effects Database Dwarfcraft Devices catalog and individual product records
- Dwarfcraft product/press-release material reproduced by Effects Database and contemporary gear publications
- Premier Guitar coverage for Gears
- contemporary retailer/catalog coverage for The Internet, Hair of the Dog, Silver Rose v2, and Dream Mangler
- active-repository search for “Dwarfcraft” before addition

The active repository contained no prior Dwarfcraft match before this block was added. Dwarfcraft Devices is treated as the builder identity. The census remains focused on standalone physical Overdrive, Distortion, and Fuzz products and excludes pure synth, oscillator, filter, delay, pitch, utility, and other non-dirt devices even when those products can produce harsh or noisy sounds.

Dwarfcraft's catalog is unusually fuzzy about the boundary between fuzz, distortion, and overdrive. Product-level category labels and builder descriptions are therefore used together, with conservative overlap decisions. Cosmetic editions and normal revisions are consolidated unless the named product identity is functionally distinct.

## Overdrive
- Gears
- The Internet
- Spectacular Aenima
- Robot Devil

Verification notes:
- **Gears** is explicitly described as an overdrive with fixed drive character feeding its sub-octave and filter sections.
- **The Internet** is cataloged as an overdrive; contemporary product documentation also identifies its third gain stage as additional distortion, so it is recorded in both Overdrive and Distortion.
- **Spectacular Aenima** is categorized by Effects Database as Overdrive and its product description repeatedly calls it a fuzz pedal while describing a feedback mode capable of metallic distortion. It is retained as Overdrive + Fuzz rather than creating a separate distortion identity from its feedback behavior.
- **Robot Devil** is cataloged as a Fuzz pedal but its documented sound range explicitly includes overdrive. It is recorded as Fuzz + Overdrive because the product description directly identifies both.

## Distortion
- Abaddon
- Baby Thundaa / Baby Thunder
- Eau Claire Thunder
- The Internet
- The Great Destroyer

Verification notes:
- **Abaddon** is explicitly categorized as Distortion by Effects Database.
- **Baby Thundaa / Baby Thunder** is explicitly described as a full-frequency fuzz/distortion pedal. The later size/name presentation is consolidated into one product identity.
- **Eau Claire Thunder** is cataloged as Fuzz and described as a classically styled distortion-and-sustain pedal with a dedicated Distortion control. It is therefore retained as Fuzz + Distortion.
- **The Internet** is an Overdrive product with a third gain stage providing additional distortion; it is retained in both categories.
- **The Great Destroyer** is cataloged as Fuzz but its builder/authoritative description also calls it a distortion pedal. It is recorded as Fuzz + Distortion because both target identities are directly documented for the named product.

Verification notes:
- **Bit Mangler** is categorized as Fuzz and described as a combination of two fuzz circuits.
- **Dream Mangler** is explicitly documented as a pair of fuzz circuits, including a Disaster Fuzz and Vintage Fuzz Machine section.
- **HAX** is cataloged as Fuzz alongside its ring-modulator function; the fuzz/noise character is part of the named product's identity.
- **Hair of the Dog** is documented and sold as a Fuzz pedal, with a clean/low blend and strong distortion available at high settings; the archive retains it as Fuzz only.
- **Necromancer** is explicitly identified as Dwarfcraft's Super Fuzz-style pedal with EQ.
- **Reese Lightning** is explicitly cataloged as Fuzz.
- **Rot Yr Brain** is documented as a Fuzz pedal in contemporary catalog coverage and is retained as Fuzz only.
- **SheFuzz** is the later name for **Shiva**; the builder documentation explicitly states that Shiva was renamed She Fuzz, so the archive consolidates them into one named lineage rather than creating duplicate dirt identities.
- **Silver Rose v2** is explicitly cataloged as Fuzz and combines Super Fuzz and Eau Claire-style fuzz sections. Its ability to dial one section back to overdrive range does not create a second Overdrive identity.
- **Surrender Dorothy** is explicitly cataloged as Fuzz.
- **The Secret Destroyer** is explicitly cataloged as Fuzz and described as a deluxe Great Destroyer-derived oscillating fuzz.
- **Zhago** is explicitly cataloged as Fuzz.

## Category-overlap decisions
- **The Internet**: Overdrive + Distortion. The product is categorized as an overdrive while its third gain stage is explicitly described as additional distortion.
- **Robot Devil**: Fuzz + Overdrive. The product is sold/documented as a fuzz pedal while its description explicitly states that it covers both fuzz and overdrive territory.
- **Spectacular Aenima**: Overdrive + Fuzz. Effects Database classifies it as Overdrive while the builder description explicitly calls it a fuzz pedal. Metallic distortion appears as a feedback-loop behavior, but no separate Distortion category is created from that secondary effect.
- **Baby Thundaa / Baby Thunder**: Fuzz + Distortion. Dwarfcraft documentation directly identifies it as full-frequency fuzz/distortion.
- **Eau Claire Thunder**: Fuzz + Distortion. It is a named fuzz pedal with an explicit distortion-and-sustain description and Distortion control.
- **The Great Destroyer**: Fuzz + Distortion. The product is categorized as Fuzz while the builder/authoritative description also calls it a distortion pedal.
- **Silver Rose v2**: Fuzz only. One internal fuzz section can be backed down into overdrive range, but the named pedal identity is Fuzz and its overall design is a dual-fuzz machine.
- **Hair of the Dog**: Fuzz only. Strong overdrive/distortion language describes operating range rather than a separate named dirt identity.
- **Necromancer**: Fuzz only. Its higher-gain range remains within the builder's explicit Super Fuzz identity.

## Historical / variant handling
- **Baby Thundaa / Baby Thunder** is consolidated as one product identity; the compact Baby version is a named smaller form of Eau Claire Thunder's design.
- **Shiva** is consolidated into **SheFuzz** because Dwarfcraft explicitly states that She Fuzz used to be called Shiva.
- **Silver Rose v2** is retained as the Dwarfcraft product identity; its relationship to the earlier Devi Ever Silver Rose is not used to create a second Dwarfcraft product entry beyond the named Dwarfcraft release.
- **Dream Mangler** remains distinct from **Bit Mangler** because Dwarfcraft presented Dream Mangler as a separate dual-fuzz product with a Disaster Fuzz section and a Vintage Fuzz Machine section.
- Normal artwork, enclosure, edition, and production changes do not create additional identities.

## Unpromoted / cautious leads
- **The Hurricane** is present in the historical Dwarfcraft catalog but the reviewed source material does not establish a target dirt category strongly enough for promotion.
- **SOMMS**, **Rot Yr Brain**, **Thumping Double Squaresnakes**, and similar experimental devices can create distortion/noise textures, but only products with sufficient explicit target-category evidence are promoted. Rot Yr Brain is retained because contemporary product coverage explicitly identifies it as Fuzz.
- **Super Wizard**, **Wizard of Pitch**, **Pitch Grinder**, **Witch Shifter**, and related pitch-focused devices are excluded even when they can become noisy because their primary identities are pitch/multi-effect products.
- **ARF**, filters, delays, reverbs, tremolos, utility products, and the pure oscillator/synth designs are outside target scope.

## Exclusions
- Afterlife of Pitch, ARF, Body Mod, Dream Mangler's non-dirt behavior, Ghost Fax, Happiness, Loop Mangler, Memento, Minivan Echo, Paraloop, Pitch Grinder, Power Struggle, SOMMS, Stomping Double Squaresnakes, Super Wizard, Synth Mangler, The Curse, The Grazer, Thumping Double Squaresnakes, Total Spack Vibes, Treeverb, Twin Stags, Witch Shifter, Wizard of Pitch, and other non-dirt products remain outside target scope unless their named product identity independently satisfies the target taxonomy.
- Fuzzrocious/Dwarfcraft collaborations are not converted into standalone Dwarfcraft identities unless the product is explicitly presented as a Dwarfcraft product in the builder lineage.
- DIY layouts, schematics, components, cloning information, and internal construction are outside scope.
