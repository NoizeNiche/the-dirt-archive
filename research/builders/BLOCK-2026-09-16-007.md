### Notes
- BOSS's current official catalog explicitly divides the dirt family into Distortion, Overdrive, and Fuzz categories.
- BOSS's 40th Anniversary Compact Pedals material documents historical models including DF-2, OD-2, DS-2, and HM-2 with production-era information. Only the product relationship is being retained here.
- The official BOSS manual archive confirms historical dirt models including DF-2, FZ-2, FZ-3, HM-2, and related models.
- OD-1 is retained as a historical BOSS overdrive based on BOSS/Roland's official documentation describing the original compact pedal lineup.
- JB-2 is retained because BOSS's official catalog identifies it as an Angry Driver created jointly by BOSS and JHS Pedals. The builder relationship here remains BOSS → JB-2, with the collaboration noted rather than splitting the product into separate identities.
- Waza Craft versions are retained as named catalog products where BOSS presents them separately. No deeper variant analysis is performed in this phase.
