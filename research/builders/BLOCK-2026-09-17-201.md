# Builder -> Pedals Research Block 201

## Scope

Continued the C-section Builder -> Pedals census with two new canonical builders. Inclusion remains dirt-only: overdrive, distortion, and fuzz.

## New canonical builders

### 476 — Cool Bear Effects
- Grizzly Bear — Distortion
- Grizzly Bear — Overdrive

### 477 — CircuitFX
- Cruncher — Overdrive
- Soldano SuperCharger — Overdrive

## Evidence / classification notes

- Effects Database introduced Cool Bear Effects as a new brand in its August 2010 weekly overview and listed the Grizzly Bear as its new effect.
- The Grizzly Bear product record is explicitly tagged both Distortion and Overdrive, so both dirt rows are retained.
- CircuitFX is documented by Effects Database as a Bangkok-based builder founded in 2005 and run by Pravudh Sirinapaphaisarn. Its Effects Database record catalogs Echo-6, a delay, but first-party CircuitFX material documents additional dirt products that are not represented in the current Effects Database product count.
- CircuitFX's Cruncher is described in its first-party product/archive material as a main overdrive or boost; the archive retains the explicit overdrive function.
- CircuitFX also documents a Soldano SuperCharger tube overdrive build, so it receives an Overdrive row.
- No duplicate canonical identity for Cool Bear Effects or CircuitFX exists in the builder master index.

## Exclusions / non-additions

- Cloak Audio's Jackdaw is a verified fuzz, but source material describes Cloak Audio as an offshoot/experimental line of Fredric Effects. It is deferred rather than assigned a standalone C-section builder identity until the future F-section can reconcile Fredric Effects and its sub-brand relationships.
- CircuitFX Echo-6 is excluded because it is a delay.
- No modulation or boost-only CircuitFX products are added unless the source explicitly identifies an overdrive/distortion/fuzz function.
