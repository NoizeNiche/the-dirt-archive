# The Dirt Archive — Builder Research Block 051

## Builder
**Blackhawk Amplifiers — Brooks Blackhawk**

## Verification basis
Primary manufacturer sources used:
- Blackhawk Amplifiers official About page
- Blackhawk Amplifiers official Products/Pedals/Fuzz pages
- Individual official product pages
- Official FAQ/news material where useful for current production context

Authoritative corroboration:
- Equipboard identifies Brooks Blackhawk as founder of Blackhawk Amplifiers.
- Rattlesnake Cable Company identifies Brooks Blackhawk as the Portland amp/pedal builder in a 2026 builder reference.

The manufacturer's About page states that Blackhawk makes amplifiers, preamplifiers and pedals by hand in Portland, Oregon. The official catalog currently exposes separate Drive, Distortion and Fuzz categories. The manufacturer itself is therefore used as the primary taxonomy wherever possible.

## Product-level verification notes
- **Valkyrie Drive** is the builder's saturated op-amp drive design. The official Uruk-Hai page describes the Valkyrie section as a drive and explicitly documents its clipping behavior, including distortion/fuzz-like settings. It is retained under Overdrive because the standalone product identity is Drive.
- **Heimdall-2 MKII** is explicitly described by Blackhawk as a distortion, overdrive and EQ pedal. It therefore belongs in both Overdrive and Distortion.
- **Heimdall-3 MKII Distortion** is explicitly titled Distortion. Its manufacturer description says it can operate as classic overdrive/distortion and reach fuzzed-out hard-clipping territory, but it is not separately promoted to Fuzz because the finished product is presented as a distortion pedal.
- **Balrog Distortion MKII** is explicitly a high-gain distortion and is also described by the manufacturer as delivering mid-gain overdrive tones and huge overdrive boost. It is retained under both Overdrive and Distortion.
- **Morgul Drive MKII** is a current Blackhawk Drive identity. It is retained under Overdrive because the manufacturer places it in the Drive category.
- **Uruk-Hai MKII** is explicitly a parallel Fuzz/Drive design. The manufacturer describes its two sections as Valhalla Fuzz and Valkyrie Drive and says the combined unit can mix fuzz and drive/distortion. It is therefore retained under Overdrive, Distortion, and Fuzz.
- **Nazgûl MKII** is explicitly a parallel Distortion and Fuzz product built from Balrog and Fellbeast sections. It is retained under Distortion and Fuzz. The Balrog section can provide overdrive, but the finished Nazgûl product is not promoted to Overdrive without explicit manufacturer category placement.
- **Valhalla** is the builder's core sludgy fuzz identity. Current and historical versions are consolidated rather than split into separate circuit identities solely because of enclosure/version changes.
- **Fellbeast Fuzz** is explicitly a fuzz pedal. The manufacturer also describes lower-gain settings as producing thicker, more articulate distortion, but it remains Fuzz only.
- **Pi π Fuzz** and **Pi π Bass Fuzz** are explicitly fuzz products. The manufacturer notes that the Green Russian version can reach high-gain distortion, but its product identity remains Fuzz.
- **Asgard Bass Fuzz**, **Gandalf**, **Mithrandir**, **Svarog**, **Ramshead**, **Triangle**, and **Basilisk** are documented as fuzz-family products in Blackhawk's catalog and/or authoritative catalog references.
- **Green / Black Russian Pi π Fuzz** is retained as one product-family identity rather than splitting the Green, Black, and Chernobyl enclosure/values variants into separate archive identities.
- **Windhand LBM Fuzz** is explicitly identified by the manufacturer as a fuzz/distortion replacement design. It is retained under Fuzz and Distortion because the manufacturer itself calls the source/product a fuzz/distortion design.

## Scope decisions / exclusions
- Boost-only products such as Zeus and Volume IV are excluded from the target dirt taxonomy unless the finished product is explicitly categorized by Blackhawk as Drive/Distortion/Fuzz.
- Preamp-only products such as HEL, Ragnarok, Odin and Mjolnir are excluded from the pedal dirt census.
- Non-dirt effects such as Lothlórien Reverb, Mirkwood Delay, Black Gate, Ouroboros and Necronomicon are excluded.
- Cosmetic finishes and ordinary circuit revisions are not counted as separate pedal identities.
- The Black Russian / Green Russian / Chernobyl variants are consolidated rather than inflated into multiple separate product records.
- Descriptions that merely say a fuzz can become more distorted are not used to create a second category unless the manufacturer explicitly identifies the finished pedal as both types.
