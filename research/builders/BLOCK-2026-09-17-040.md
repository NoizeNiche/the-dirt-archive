# Builder → Pedals Research Block 040

## Builder
**Way Huge Electronics — George Tripps**

## Verification basis
Way Huge's official/current product material identifies the brand as **Way Huge by George Tripps**. Current manufacturer/distributor catalog material and official Jim Dunlop manuals were used to establish the dirt products and their categories. The research is scoped to explicit overdrive, distortion, and fuzz identities. Multi-effect products are only retained where the official documentation explicitly identifies a dirt circuit as part of the product and the product itself is meaningfully a dirt pedal.

## Product notes
- **Red Llama 3.0:** current Way Huge catalog identifies it as an overdrive. George Tripps is explicitly credited in the Way Huge product documentation.
- **Green Rhino MkIV / MkV:** official Way Huge material identifies both as overdrive products. They remain separate documented product identities because the manufacturer treats the generations as distinct models.
- **Supa-Lead:** official catalog identifies it as an overdrive.
- **Pork Loin:** official documentation identifies it as an overdrive. It is retained as a distinct Way Huge dirt identity.
- **Pork & Pickle:** official Jim Dunlop manual identifies it as a bass overdrive and fuzz combining the Pork Loin Overdrive and Russian-Pickle Fuzz circuits. It is retained in both Overdrive and Fuzz; the manufacturer's own title supports that classification.
- **Camel Toe MkII:** official manual identifies it as a triple overdrive combining Green Rhino and Red Llama circuits. It is retained under Overdrive.
- **STO:** current Way Huge catalog identifies it as an overdrive.
- **Geisha Drive:** current Way Huge catalog identifies the Geisha Drive as an overdrive.
- **Beer Overdrive:** current catalog explicitly names and classifies it as overdrive.
- **Fat Sandwich:** current Way Huge catalog identifies it as a distortion/overdrive-oriented product; retained in both Overdrive and Distortion because the manufacturer describes the circuit as a broad high-gain drive/distortion design.
- **Overrated Special:** current catalog places it in the overdrive family; retained under Overdrive.
- **Swollen Pickle:** official Jim Dunlop manuals explicitly identify the product as a Jumbo Fuzz. The MkIIS documentation describes mild crunch through high-gain fuzz, but the core classification remains Fuzz. It is also retained under Distortion because the official product documentation uses high-gain distortion language for the product's operating range.
- **Russian-Pickle:** official Jim Dunlop manual identifies it as a fuzz.
- **Doom Hammer:** current Way Huge catalog identifies it as a fuzz.
- **Stone Burner:** current catalog identifies it as a sub-atomic fuzz combining fuzz with octave-down tones.
- **Atreides Weirding Module:** official Jim Dunlop documentation identifies a dedicated FUZZ control inside the analog weirding module. It is retained under Fuzz. Because the product also produces a dirt/drive stage and official catalog material groups it among Way Huge's dirt products, it is retained under Distortion rather than treated as a pure modulation pedal.
- **Fuzz Box / Foot Pig:** these are historical Way Huge fuzz identities documented in Way Huge historical material. They are retained as historical dirt products rather than treated as current catalog items.

## Explicit exclusions / restraint
- **Aqua-Puss, Supa-Puss, Blue Hippo, Attack Vector, and other non-dirt Way Huge products:** excluded from the categorized dirt census.
- **Octave/modulation products without an explicit standalone dirt identity:** excluded unless the manufacturer documentation clearly identifies a fuzz/distortion circuit as part of the dirt function.
- **Cosmetic/limited-edition changes:** not split unless the manufacturer presents a materially distinct pedal identity.
- **Boost-only products:** excluded.
