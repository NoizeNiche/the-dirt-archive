## Fairfield Circuitry

**Initial dirt products:**

- ~900
- The Unpleasant Surprise

**First-pass handling:** Keep Fairfield Circuitry as one builder. Do not split Millenium Barbershop or other revisions at this stage. Exact archival product naming can be cleaned during catalog verification.
