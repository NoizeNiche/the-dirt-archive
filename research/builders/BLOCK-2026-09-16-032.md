# The Dirt Archive — Builder → Pedals Research Block 032

## Builder
**BJFE / BJF Electronics — Björn Juhl (Sweden)**

## Scope
This block records BJFE/BJF Electronics dirt pedals only: overdrive, distortion, and fuzz. Non-dirt effects, customer one-offs, and unrelated One Control products are excluded. The builder identity is treated as **BJFE / BJF Electronics**, with Björn Juhl as the designer/builder. One Control pages are used where they explicitly identify a pedal as a Björn Juhl/BJF/BJFE design or reproduction; the BJFE/BearFoot forum registry and descriptions are used as the historical product index.

## Verification notes
- The BJFE/BearFoot forum's registry identifies BJFE current-production, out-of-production, limited-edition, prototype, and other pedal identities and explicitly lists the major dirt models. It is an archival community resource associated with the BJFE/BearFoot forum, not a current manufacturer storefront.
- One Control's official product pages independently identify several BJF/BJFE designs and explicitly credit Björn Juhl, providing primary manufacturer evidence for the designer/product relationship.
- This block does **not** treat every prototype or one-off in the registry as part of the core census. The goal is the durable builder → recognizable dirt-pedal relationship, not an exhaustive museum inventory of every experimental enclosure.

## Overdrive
- **Baby Blue Overdrive (BBOD)** — Overdrive / dynamic overdrive-fuzz design. The BJFE archival description identifies Baby Blue Overdrive as a BJFE pedal and traces the first FUZZ 1 into the BBOD. One Control's Baby Blue OD page identifies it as designed by Björn Juhl and based on his original FUZZ 1 design. Sources: BJFE/BearFoot registry and descriptions; One Control official product page.
- **Honey Bee Overdrive (HBOD)** — Overdrive. BJFE archival description identifies the Honey Bee Overdrive as a BJFE production pedal and describes its dynamic low-gain character. One Control's BJFE-series catalog also carries the Honey Bee design. Sources: BJFE/BearFoot registry and descriptions; One Control official catalog.
- **Little Green Wonder (LGW)** — Overdrive. The BJFE archival registry lists Little Green Wonder as a BJFE dirt pedal and its category summary places it under overdrive. Source: BJFE/BearFoot registry/description archive.
- **BlueBerry Bass Overdrive (3BOD)** — Bass overdrive. Listed in the BJFE archival registry as a BJFE pedal and categorized as an overdrive. Source: BJFE/BearFoot registry.
- **Mighty Blue Overdrive** — Overdrive. Listed in the BJFE archival category summary. Source: BJFE/BearFoot pedal-description archive.
- **Sparkling Yellow Overdrive (SYOD)** — Overdrive. Listed in the BJFE archival registry/category summary, including a Version 2. Source: BJFE/BearFoot pedal-description archive.
- **Sparkling Yellow Overdrive Version 2 (SYOD V.2)** — Overdrive. Listed explicitly in the BJFE archival registry/category summary. Source: BJFE/BearFoot pedal-description archive.
- **Saffron Yellow Overdrive (SYOD)** — Overdrive / prototype-line identity. Listed in the BJFE archival registry and category summary. Source: BJFE/BearFoot pedal-description archive.
- **Cranberry Over Drive** — Overdrive. One Control's official product page identifies it as part of the BJF Series FX and states that Björn Juhl designed it as a lower-gain extension of the Strawberry Red Overdrive family. Source: One Control official product page.
- **Lingonberry Over Drive** — Overdrive. One Control's official product page identifies it as part of the BJF Series FX and states that Björn Juhl designed it as a higher-gain extension of the Strawberry Red Overdrive family. Source: One Control official product page.

One Control's official BJFE-in-Your-Hand page explicitly describes Dyna Red Distortion 4K as an approved BJFE model and credits Björn Juhl. Sources: BJFE/BearFoot registry/description archive; One Control official product page.

- **Cliff Hanger** — Distortion / prototype identity. Listed in the BJFE archival category summary as a distortion pedal. Source: BJFE/BearFoot pedal-description archive.
- **Cliff Hanger II** — Distortion / prototype identity. Listed in the BJFE archival category summary as a distortion pedal. Source: BJFE/BearFoot pedal-description archive.

## Deliberately excluded from the core list
- Boosters, treble boosters, EQs, compressors, tremolos, phasers, delays, wahs, and other non-dirt effects.
- Customer one-offs such as custom reboxes, combined pedals, and pedals built specifically for individual customers.
- One Control products that merely use a BJFE-inspired design unless the source explicitly establishes the Björn Juhl/BJF/BJFE relationship. Those belong to the appropriate One Control/BJF documentation rather than being silently counted as separate BJFE originals.
- Mad Professor products are not duplicated here merely because the BJFE archive documents circuit ancestry or collaboration. Mad Professor remains its own builder entry.
