# The Dirt Archive — Builder → Pedals Research Block 033

## Builders
- **Foxrox Electronics — Dave Fox**
- **Pete Cornish / Pete Cornish Effects**

## Scope
Builder → Pedals only. This block records documented overdrive, distortion, and fuzz products. Non-dirt effects are excluded except where a multi-effect unit contains a clearly identified dirt section that establishes the builder's dirt work.

## Foxrox Electronics

### Overdrive
- **FR 100 Overdrive** — Dual-mode stacked overdrive. Foxrox's current product page describes a medium-gain drive mode plus a stacked high-gain lead mode. Official Foxrox documentation identifies it as a dual-mode overdrive.
- **Festival Overdrive** — Dual-mode overdrive with a separate boost/lead channel. Foxrox's official product documentation describes it as an overdrive and records it as a discontinued/classics product, last manufactured December 2020.
- **ZIM Dual Drive** — Modular dual-channel drive platform. Foxrox explicitly describes ZIM as configurable with Drive, Distortion, Fuzz, Boost, and Octave ZIMcards. Because the product is itself a dirt-capable platform rather than one fixed circuit, it is recorded as a builder → modular dirt pedal relationship.

### Distortion
- **ZIM Dual Drive** — Configurable with distortion ZIMcards. Foxrox's current ZIM page explicitly describes the pedal as supporting distortion and OD in one pedal.
- **TAR+ ZIMcard** — Distortion/overdrive ZIMcard for ZIM and Mini ZIM. Foxrox's ZIMcard catalog places ZIMcards into Overdrive, Fuzz, Distortion, Boost, and Other categories, and the TAR+ card is part of that catalog.
- **Brown Muff ZIMcard** — Distortion/fuzz ZIMcard. Foxrox's current ZIMcard catalog lists it among the available ZIMcards and identifies ZIM as a platform capable of distortion and fuzz configurations.

### Fuzz
- **Hot Silicon Fuzz** — Silicon Fuzz Face-derived fuzz. Foxrox's official product page identifies it as the same Hot Silicon previously available as a FuzzCard for Captain Coconut 2. It was last manufactured as the original Hot Silicon in June 2019.
- **Hot Silicon2 Fuzz** — Later Foxrox Hot Silicon version. Foxrox's official Classics page records it as last manufactured May 2024; the current Hot Silicon documentation describes the silicon Fuzz Face lineage.
- **CC Hybrid2** — Hybrid fuzz. Foxrox's current catalog lists CC Hybrid2, while official Foxrox documentation identifies the CC Hybrid circuit as the PNP silicon/germanium Fuzz Face derivative developed for the original Captain Coconut and the default fuzz card for Captain Coconut 2.
- **Captain Coconut / Captain Coconut2 FuzzFoot** — Integrated fuzz section. Foxrox's official Captain Coconut documentation identifies FuzzFoot as a Dallas Arbiter Fuzz Face reproduction/improvement, and Captain Coconut2 documentation identifies interchangeable fuzz cards including Hot Silicon, Vintage Germanium, and Si/Ge Hybrid. These are retained as historical Foxrox dirt identities rather than counted as separate modern standalone pedals.
- **Fat Fuzz ZIMcard** — Silicon Fuzz Face-style fuzz with a mild overdrive stage. Foxrox's official page describes the card as a medium-gain Silicon NPN Fuzz Face followed by an overdrive stage.
- **Original PNP Hybrid Fuzz Card** — Fuzz card for Captain Coconut/Captain Coconut2. Foxrox's current accessories catalog identifies it as a Fuzz Card and the Captain Coconut manual identifies the FuzzFoot as the integrated fuzz section.
- **Vintage Germanium Fuzz Card** — Fuzz card for Captain Coconut2. Foxrox's current accessories catalog lists it and the Captain Coconut2 documentation identifies Vintage Germanium as one of the three fuzz-card choices.

### Foxrox verification notes
- The Foxrox Classics page is useful for discontinued-product status and gives last-manufactured dates for Hot Silicon, Festival, ZIM, and Hot Silicon2.
- ZIMcards are not all treated as independent standalone pedals. They are recorded only when their dirt function is relevant to the builder → pedal relationship.
- Octron and Paradox TZF products are excluded because they are octave/flanger products rather than overdrive, distortion, or fuzz.

## Pete Cornish

### Overdrive
- **SS-2 Soft Sustain** — Overdrive/distortion. Pete Cornish describes SS-2 as progressing from subtle overdriven amp sounds through rhythmic crunch to blues lead and explicitly discusses it as an overdrive/distortion effect.
- **SS-3 Soft Sustain** — Overdrive / higher-output soft-sustain design. Pete Cornish's official Deluxe Series catalog lists SS-3 as Soft Sustain with passive top cut.
- **CC-1 Cornish Crunch** — Crunch/overdrive. Pete Cornish's official Deluxe Series catalog identifies CC-1 as Cornish Crunch with active bass/mid/treble tone controls.
- **GC-1 High Gain Crunch** — High-gain crunch/distortion. Pete Cornish's official Deluxe Series catalog identifies GC-1 as High Gain Crunch with active B/M/T controls.
- **BD-1 Bass Driver** — Bass overdrive/drive. Pete Cornish's official Deluxe Series catalog identifies BD-1 as Bass Driver with active bass/treble controls.

### Distortion
- **G-2** — Distortion / high-gain overdrive. Pete Cornish describes the G-2 as a distortion unit and states that it moves from rhythmic crunch into rounded dynamic lead overdrive.
- **P-2** — High-gain distortion/fuzz-oriented dirt pedal. Pete Cornish describes P-2 as a fat, smooth, harmonically rich distortion pedal spanning mellow crunch through searing high-gain sustain.
- **SS-2** — Overdrive/distortion range. The official SS-2 page describes subtle overdrive through rhythmic crunch and blues lead at maximum gain.
- **SS-3** — Soft Sustain / distortion-family pedal. Listed in Pete Cornish's official Deluxe Series catalog.
- **GC-1** — High Gain Crunch. Listed in the official Deluxe Series catalog.

### Fuzz
- **P-1** — Fuzz or distortion. Pete Cornish describes P-1 explicitly as a fuzz or distortion pedal and identifies it as a high-gain lead/rhythm unit.
- **P-2** — Distortion/fuzz-family high-gain pedal. The official Deluxe Series catalog identifies P-2 as Fuzz with passive top cut, while the product description emphasizes its distortion range.
- **NG-2** — Fuzz. Pete Cornish's official Deluxe Series catalog lists NG-2 as Fuzz+ with a bass/treble single tone control.
- **NG-3** — Fuzz. Pete Cornish's official Deluxe Series catalog lists NG-3 as Fuzz+ with bass/treble and bias controls.

### Pete Cornish verification notes
- The official Deluxe Series catalog is the authoritative source used for the current/ordered Deluxe Series product names and classifications.
- Individual official product pages are used to preserve the builder's own terminology where a pedal spans categories, particularly P-1, P-2, G-2, and SS-2.
- Custom touring systems and one-off pedalboards are excluded. This block records identifiable pedal products, not every custom Pete Cornish rig ever built.
