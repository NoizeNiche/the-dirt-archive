# Builder → Pedals Research Block 101

## Builder
**Black Arts Toneworks (BAT) — Mark Wentz / Black Arts Toneworks**

## Verification basis
Black Arts Toneworks' official site identifies the company as a pedal builder operating since 2010 and maintains a catalog of current and archived products. The official product pages and archive pages explicitly identify many of BAT's overdrive, distortion, and fuzz products. The catalog is especially heavy on fuzz and high-gain drive designs.

The archive follows BAT's own product identity instead of treating every possible gain sound as another category. Hybrid products are retained across categories when BAT itself describes separate dirt functions inside the same pedal.

## Product notes
- **Black Forest:** BAT's product material identifies Black Forest as an overdrive rooted in the Colorsound Overdriver family. It remains Overdrive only.
- **Quantum Mystic:** BAT explicitly calls Quantum Mystic an Overdrive. It remains Overdrive only.
- **Witch Burner MkII:** BAT explicitly identifies Witch Burner MkII as an overdrive with very high gain. It remains Overdrive only even though the gain is aggressive.
- **Raw Heart OD:** BAT describes Raw Heart as a dual-circuit Overdrive/Distortion combining the Quantum Mystic and low-gain Witch Burner designs. It is therefore retained in both Overdrive and Distortion.
- **Tres Diablos Ruidosos:** BAT explicitly describes the three gain stages as moving from boost to edgy OD, classic distortion, and finally oversaturated fuzz. It is retained in all three target categories.
- **Coven:** BAT's own historical material identifies Coven as Pharaoh Fuzz plus Black Forest Overdrive in one enclosure. It is retained in both Fuzz and Overdrive.
- **BAT:** BAT describes this later pedal as a driver that can also function as a standalone distortion. Because BAT explicitly gives it a distortion identity while also calling it a driver, it is retained under Distortion; the archive does not turn the generic “driver” wording into a separate Overdrive entry without clearer product-level wording.
- **Revelation:** BAT explicitly describes Revelation as an amp-pushing device whose gain control dials in distortion. It is retained under Distortion.
- **Priestess:** BAT describes Priestess as a combination of multiple Muff-family gain stages producing dynamic and compressed fuzz. It is retained under Fuzz; its ability to produce aggressive saturation does not create a separate Distortion identity merely from sound range.
- **Pharaoh:** BAT explicitly describes Pharaoh as a fuzz pedal, with its roots and DNA in classic fuzz and a range from boost through light OD to saturated fuzz. It remains Fuzz only because those neighboring ranges are operating range within the named fuzz identity.
- **Pharaoh Supreme:** BAT explicitly describes Pharaoh Supreme as the “ultimate” fuzz-stortion built from the Pharaoh family. It is retained under Fuzz; the word “fuzz-stortion” is treated as a fuzz identity with distortion behavior, not automatically a second category without a separate named distortion function.
- **Son of Pharaoh:** BAT describes it as the same Pharaoh circuit family in a smaller enclosure and says it is still the Pharaoh. It is retained as a separate named pedal identity because BAT names and sold it as the Son of Pharaoh, but its category is Fuzz only.
- **LSTR:** BAT describes LSTR as the Pharaoh's fuzz-oriented counterpart with more fuzz and a different voicing. It remains Fuzz only.
- **Black Sheep:** BAT identifies Black Sheep as an all-transistor fuzz device. It remains Fuzz only.
- **Crown of Horns:** BAT describes the core as an OpAmp Muff with Pharaoh/LSTR tone-section traits. It remains Fuzz only; its bypassable tone stack can create a large volume boost, but that does not establish a separate overdrive identity.
- **FNORD:** BAT explicitly calls FNORD an octaver/fuzz and describes a blend of fuzz/distortion behaviors. It remains Fuzz only.
- **Destroyer:** BAT's official introduction describes the pedal as going from a controllable wall of fuzz to a destructive preamp texture. It is retained under Fuzz; the preamp role is outside scope.
- **Sarcophagus:** BAT explicitly describes Sarcophagus as a dual-fuzz combining complete Pharaoh and LSTR circuits. It remains Fuzz only.
- **Rabid Mammal:** BAT explicitly identifies the pedal as Boost/Fuzz. The boost side is outside the target categories, so only Fuzz is recorded.

## Explicit exclusions / restraint
- **Skyboost:** excluded because BAT presents it primarily as a boost/treble boost, even though its gain can become fuzzy.
- **Son of Pharaoh:** retained as a named product identity, but not duplicated under other categories simply because the Pharaoh family can reach lower-gain drive sounds.
- **Special color runs and custom-shop versions:** consolidated with their underlying pedal unless BAT presents a genuinely different named circuit/product identity.
- **Coven and Sarcophagus:** retained as single named pedals with multiple internal dirt circuits rather than pretending each circuit is a separate physical pedal.
- **Rabid Mammal:** boost side excluded from the three-category census; Fuzz retained because BAT explicitly identifies it as Boost/Fuzz.
- **Black Forest / Pharaoh family gain overlap:** neighboring fuzz or overdrive behavior is not promoted into additional categories without explicit builder-level product identity.
- **Custom Shop, one-off art pieces, B-stock, and ordinary finish variants:** excluded as separate identities or consolidated into the underlying pedal where appropriate.

## Status
**Verified research block.** Black Arts Toneworks is a previously uncovered builder in the fresh census. The official catalog establishes a broad dirt history dominated by fuzz and heavy overdrive/distortion. The strongest multi-category cases are Raw Heart OD (Overdrive + Distortion), Coven (Overdrive + Fuzz), and Tres Diablos Ruidosos (Overdrive + Distortion + Fuzz). Pharaoh, LSTR, Priestess, FNORD, Destroyer, Sarcophagus, Rabid Mammal, and related products are retained as Fuzz where BAT explicitly establishes that identity. Boost-only and other non-target functions remain outside the core census.
