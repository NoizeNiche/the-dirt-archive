# Builder -> Pedals Research Block 199

## Scope

Continued the C-section Builder -> Pedals census with two new canonical builders. Inclusion remains dirt-only: overdrive, distortion, and fuzz.

## New canonical builders

- Green Genie — Fuzz

- Fratz — Overdrive

## Evidence / classification notes

- Chamber Of Sounds is run by Jamie Laboz and is documented by Effects Database as a pedal/sound-effects builder based in Tucson, Arizona. Its Green Genie is explicitly tagged as an octave fuzz and described as a handmade Foxx Tone Machine-derived fuzz with an octave footswitch.
- Chris Custom is listed by Effects Database with nine products and has dedicated manufacturer links for the brand.
- Crusher is explicitly listed as a hi-gain distortion.
- DF-1 Dr.Fuzz is explicitly listed as fuzz.
- Dr.Drive is explicitly listed as overdrive.
- Dr.Muff is explicitly listed as fuzz.
- Dr.Scream is explicitly listed as overdrive.
- Dr.Silvery is explicitly listed as overdrive.
- Fratz is explicitly listed under both distortion and overdrive, so both dirt rows are retained.
- Loud Crusher is explicitly listed as distortion.
- Wave Shaker is excluded because the source classifies it as tremolo, outside the archive dirt taxonomy.

## Exclusions / non-additions

- Chamber Of Sounds' Blip, Jupiter III, Jupiter T, ZiNG, and other reviewed products were not added because their documented primary functions are synth, theremin, octave/ring modulation, or other non-dirt effects.
- Chris Custom's Wave Shaker is excluded as tremolo.
- Chaosound Anti-Effect is held for a later classification pass because the source uses the combined distortion/fuzz/overdrive umbrella but its product description explicitly says it is not a conventional distortion effect. It is not promoted here without a cleaner taxonomy decision.
