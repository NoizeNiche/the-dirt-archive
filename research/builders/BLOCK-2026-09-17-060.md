# Builder → Pedals Research Block 060

## Builder
**Prescription Electronics — Jack Brossart / Prescription Electronics Inc.**

Prescription Electronics was founded in Portland, Oregon in 1993 by Jack Brossart. The company later closed, returned with reissues in 2019, and then disappeared again after Brossart's death that same year. This block treats Prescription Electronics as one builder identity and preserves documented historical/reissue product identities without splitting ordinary revisions into separate builders.

## Overdrive
- RX Overdriver
- Germ
- Yardbox
- Dual-Tone

### Overdrive verification
- **RX Overdriver:** Effects Database explicitly categorizes it as overdrive and describes it as a sonic reproduction of the Colorsound Overdriver, ranging from clean boost to full-bore distortion. The archive keeps the builder's primary identity as Overdrive rather than converting its maximum-gain behavior into a second category.
- **Germ:** historical catalog material describes a clean-boost side and a crunch side with "fat distortion." Contemporary user documentation also describes the overdrive side. The archive retains Germ under Overdrive and Distortion because the product explicitly spans both drive modes.
- **Yardbox:** the 2019 reissue coverage describes a Tone Bender-derived fuzz that can produce a thin fuzz/OD sound at reduced volume and saturated germanium fuzz at higher settings. Because the builder-era description explicitly uses both OD and fuzz language, it is recorded in both categories.
- **Dual-Tone:** historical Prescription Electronics material describes the pedal as an overdrive/distortion pedal with two preset gain levels. It is therefore recorded in both categories.

## Distortion
- Germ
- Dual-Tone

### Distortion verification
- **Germ:** historical catalog description explicitly calls the crunch side a fat distortion.
- **Dual-Tone:** historical manufacturer wording identifies it directly as an overdrive/distortion pedal, with a lower-gain rhythm mode and higher-gain lead mode.

The archive does **not** promote Face Lift into Distortion solely because a third-party listing uses a broader "distortion" category. Its manufacturer-linked documentation consistently identifies the core effect as fuzz plus octave.

## Fuzz
- Experience
- C.O.B. (Clean Octave Blend)
- Yardbox
- Face Lift
- Frantic Fuzz
- Supernatural
- Depth Charge

### Fuzz verification
- **Experience:** manufacturer-era/reissue coverage explicitly identifies it as an octave fuzz with fuzz, octave, and swell functions. Effects Database also categorizes it as octave fuzz.
- **C.O.B. (Clean Octave Blend):** Prescription Electronics product documentation and later manufacturer/reissue coverage identify it as an octave-fuzz pedal with clean blend. Premier Guitar explicitly calls it an octave-fuzz pedal. Aion FX documentation independently identifies the original C.O.B. as an octave fuzz.
- **Yardbox:** the 2019 reissue coverage describes saturated germanium fuzz and identifies its Tone Bender basis. Effects Database categorizes it as fuzz. It is also retained under Overdrive because the reissue description explicitly documents a fuzz/OD operating range.
- **Face Lift:** Effects Database identifies the product as an octave fuzz and documents silicon/germanium Fuzz Face-style versions. Third-party product documentation consistently describes it as fuzz plus octave. Variants are consolidated into one Face Lift identity.
- **Frantic Fuzz:** retained as a historical Prescription Electronics fuzz identity in the Effects Database catalog. No separate variant entries are created.
- **Supernatural:** historical Prescription Electronics product material identifies it as a germanium fuzz. The archive records the product as Fuzz only.
- **Depth Charge:** historical Prescription Electronics material identifies it as a bass fuzz with a clean blend and gain control; later documentation and user-facing product references consistently identify it as a bass fuzz. It is retained under Fuzz only.

## Exclusions / conservative decisions
- **Vibe-Unit:** excluded because it is a Uni-Vibe/modulation product, not a target dirt category.
- **Throb:** excluded because it is tremolo.
- **C.O.B. octave-only behavior:** not split into a separate octave category; the target taxonomy is Builder → Overdrive / Distortion / Fuzz.
- **Germ boost-only behavior:** not split into Boost; only its documented dirt relationship is retained.
- **Outbox:** excluded because the reviewed historical material describes it as a multi-effect/filtering/switching module rather than a clearly documented standalone OD, distortion, or fuzz identity.
- **Static:** excluded because the available catalog evidence did not provide sufficiently clear target-taxonomy support during this block.
- Ordinary electrical/finish/reissue revisions are consolidated under the same pedal identity.
- No pedal is promoted into a target category merely because a third party describes it as "gainy," "saturated," or "distortion-like" without adequate product-level support.

## Verification posture
This block deliberately favors manufacturer-era descriptions and authoritative product archives over marketplace terminology. The current official Prescription Electronics site could not be retrieved through the available web index in this research pass, so current/reissue claims were cross-checked against the 2019 reissue report, archived product catalogs, Effects Database, Aion FX documentation, Premier Guitar, and historical product listings. No unsupported standalone distortion identity was added for the fuzz-only products.
