### Axtar
- DIS-5 Distortion — Distortion

## Research notes

- Axtar's DIS-5 is documented as a vintage Japanese distortion pedal. Audiofanzine users describe the DIS-5 as a distortion unit with a gain range from bluesy drive to extreme saturation, and Effects Database identifies Axtar as a pedal brand.
- The Axtar OCT-5 is an octaver whose lower-octave function can introduce a blendable distortion setting, but the primary product identity is an octave pedal rather than a dedicated dirt pedal, so it is excluded from the Dirt Archive.
- Compressor-only and other non-dirt Axtar products are excluded.
