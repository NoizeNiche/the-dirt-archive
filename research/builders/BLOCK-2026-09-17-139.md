## Explicit overlaps
- SL Drive = Overdrive + Distortion. Xotic's current catalog explicitly labels it "DISTORTION / OVERDRIVE."
- BB Preamp family = Overdrive. The official catalog describes the current BB Preamp V1.5 as boost/overdrive, while legacy BB variants remain part of the same overdrive lineage.
- AC Booster family = Overdrive. The current AC Booster V2 is described by Xotic as expressive overdrive; legacy AC Booster variants remain in the same drive family.
- Soul Driven = Overdrive. The official manual explicitly includes a "Full Harmonic Overdrive" setting.
- Bass BB Preamp products = Overdrive. Xotic explicitly describes them as bass boost/overdrive products.

## Variant / historical restraint
- Current revisions and artist editions are consolidated by pedal lineage where they represent the same underlying builder relationship.
- Custom Shop variants such as BB Preamp MB, BB Preamp Comp, AC Booster Comp, and artist versions are retained as named historical relationships but are not multiplied into unrelated products.
- Boost-only products such as EP Booster, RC Booster, AH Booster, Super Sweet Booster, and Super Clean Buffer are excluded because boost is outside the target categories.
- Compression, wah, volume, filter, signal-blender, and power accessories are excluded.
- No components, schematics, PCB, BOM, gutshots, cloning, or build instructions are included.

## Verification note
Xotic's official catalog currently lists SL Drive as a "DISTORTION / OVERDRIVE" product, BB Preamp V1.5 as boost/overdrive, and Soul Driven as a mid-boost overdrive. Xotic's official manuals also describe Soul Driven as capable of full harmonic overdrive. These relationships form the basis of this census block. No official Xotic fuzz product was verified in the reviewed catalog.
