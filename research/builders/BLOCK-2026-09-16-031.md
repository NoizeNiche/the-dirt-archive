## 1. LICHTLÆRM AUDIO

Builder / brand: Lichtlaerm Audio

### Current Overdrive / Distortion / Fuzz products verified from the official manufacturer catalog
- Altar — boost / fuzz / distortion
- Medusa — grinding death metal distortion
- Ragnarøk — apocalyptic tube distortion / preamp
- Total Distortion Worship MKII — extreme metal distortion with built-in overdrive
- Thorn — signature distortion
- Gehenna — metal distortion / preamp
- The King in Yellow — parallel blending overdrive
- .Ritual. — post-metal distortion
- Aquaria — transparent overdrive / dynamic boost / EQ

Notes:
- Lichtlaerm's current official shop explicitly labels Altar as “Boost · Fuzz · Distortion,” Medusa as grinding death metal distortion, Ragnarøk as tube distortion/preamp, Total Distortion Worship MKII as distortion with built-in overdrive, Thorn as signature distortion, Gehenna as metal distortion/preamp, The King in Yellow as parallel blending overdrive, .Ritual. as post-metal distortion, and Aquaria as transparent overdrive.
- Altar is retained across fuzz and distortion because Lichtlaerm explicitly describes its gain spectrum as fuzz, overdrive, and distortion rather than forcing it into a single category.
- Total Distortion Worship MKII is retained under distortion and overdrive because the manufacturer explicitly documents both the main distortion circuit and the footswitchable overdrive circuit.
- .Ritual. is retained as distortion because its manufacturer classification is explicit, even though it also contains softer-clipping overdrive forms.
- Aquaria is retained as overdrive because the manufacturer explicitly identifies the product as transparent overdrive, clean boost, and EQ.
- Speaker products, noise gate products, power amplifiers, and other non-dirt products are excluded.
- No circuit, component, schematic, PCB, BOM, or cloning information is included.
