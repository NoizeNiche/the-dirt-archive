## Research notes

- Effects Database identifies Audio Squared's Major Fuzz as a fuzz pedal and lists Audio Squared as a distinct brand.
- Audio! Audio!'s SD-1 Superdrive is explicitly classified as both overdrive and distortion.
- Effects Database lists Audio-Phonic R4 Fuzz and Audio-Phonic Twin-Fuzz as fuzz products. Contemporary Argentine pedal-history material also documents the Twin-Fuzz as a distortion/fuzz product, while the Effects Database's 2025 update identifies the R4 Fuzz as an Audio-Phonic product.
- Booster-only or non-dirt products from these brands were not added.
