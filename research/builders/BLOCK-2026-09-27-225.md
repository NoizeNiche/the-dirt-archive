# Builder -> Pedals Research Block 225

## Scope

Historical completeness sweep for a previously unrepresented builder identity: Lovetone. Dirt-only inclusion is limited to overdrive, distortion, fuzz, and products that materially contain those functions. Cosmetic editions and non-dirt Lovetone effects are excluded from the dirt census.

## New canonical builder

### 587 — Lovetone

Lovetone was founded in 1995 by Dan Coggins and Vlad Naslas. The surviving catalog contains eight principal products; four are clear dirt-bearing products for this archive:

- Big Cheese — Fuzz
- Brown Source — Overdrive
- Cheese Source — Fuzz / Overdrive
- Ring Stinger — Fuzz / Distortion

The remaining historical Lovetone products, Meatball, Doppelganger, Wobulator, and ? Flanger, are excluded from this dirt-specific block because they are primarily filter, pitch, modulation, or flanger effects rather than dirt products.

## Evidence notes

- **Big Cheese:** Effects Database documents Curds, Tone, Off/1/2/Cheese, and Whey controls and identifies the pedal as fuzz.
- **Brown Source:** Effects Database documents Drive, Tone, Off/1/2/3, and Volume controls and identifies the pedal as overdrive.
- **Cheese Source:** Effects Database documents the complete Big Cheese + Brown Source two-in-one architecture. RockBoard documents 9V DC center-negative power.
- **Ring Stinger:** Effects Database documents the transformer-matrix ring modulator, octave-up fuzz, germanium distortion, oscillator/LFO controls, and external CV/light control. It is retained because the unit contains a documented fuzz/distortion section.

## Historical context

Effects Database's Lovetone timeline places the first four dirt-bearing products in the company's early catalog, with Ring Stinger and Cheese Source appearing in 1998. Lovetone stopped regular production after its original run, with final sales continuing into the late 2000s.

## Boundary

This block registers Lovetone as a canonical builder identity and its four verified dirt-bearing historical products. It does not create separate catalog entries for the other four Lovetone effects or for cosmetic/retailer artwork variants.
