# Block 088 — Visual Sound / Truetone

## Builder
**Visual Sound / Truetone**

## Verification basis
Primary and authoritative sources reviewed:
- Truetone official About/history material establishing Visual Sound as the original company name and Truetone as the 2015 rebrand
- Truetone official archive pages and manuals for Jekyll & Hyde, Route 66, Open Road, VS-XO, Son of Hyde, Angry Fuzz, and GarageTone Drivetrain
- Truetone official manuals for Route 808, Double Trouble, Vans Warped Distortion, and GarageTone Chainsaw
- authoritative Effects Database catalog coverage for the Visual Sound dirt catalog
- active-repository search for “Visual Sound”, “Truetone”, and related names before addition

The active repository contained no prior Visual Sound or Truetone match before this block was added. Visual Sound and Truetone are treated as one builder/company lineage because Truetone's own history states that Visual Sound became Truetone in 2015. The census records the standalone physical dirt products associated with that lineage and does not turn compressor, chorus, delay, power, or buffer products into dirt identities.

The reviewed material supports a substantial Visual Sound / Truetone Overdrive, Distortion, and Fuzz catalog. Product-level labels are treated as authoritative where available; version-number and cosmetic changes are consolidated unless the product was a genuinely distinct named design.

## Overdrive
Current / archive-supported identities:
- Jekyll & Hyde
- Route 66 American Overdrive
- Open Road
- VS-XO
- Route 808
- Double Trouble
- Drivetrain / GarageTone Drivetrain
- Custom Shop Overdrive

Historical / archive-supported identities:
- Route 66

Verification notes:
- **Jekyll & Hyde** is an explicit dual-channel Overdrive + Distortion product. The Jekyll channel is Overdrive and the Hyde channel is Distortion.
- **Route 66 American Overdrive** is explicitly documented by Truetone as a two-effect housing with an Overdrive channel; the compressor side is excluded from the dirt census.
- **Open Road** is explicitly categorized by Truetone as Overdrive.
- **VS-XO** is explicitly presented as a dual-channel Overdrive.
- **Route 808** is explicitly categorized as Overdrive and is the single-channel form of Visual Sound's overdrive circuit used in Jekyll & Hyde and Route 66.
- **Double Trouble** is explicitly categorized as dual Overdrive.
- **Drivetrain / GarageTone Drivetrain** is explicitly categorized by Truetone as Overdrive. The archive keeps the Reverend-origin design as the same product identity when recorded through Visual Sound's GarageTone line because the builder/company documentation explicitly connects the reintroduced pedal to the earlier design.
- **Custom Shop Overdrive** is documented by Truetone as a dedicated Visual Sound Overdrive product and is retained as its own named identity.
- Earlier **Route 66** and later V2/V3 versions are handled as one Route 66 product identity rather than separate cosmetic/version duplicates.

## Distortion
Current / archive-supported identities:
- Jekyll & Hyde
- Son of Hyde
- GarageTone Chainsaw Distortion
- Vans Warped Distortion

Historical / archive-supported identities:
- Jekyll & Hyde (original)

Verification notes:
- **Jekyll & Hyde** explicitly contains an independent Distortion channel.
- **Son of Hyde** is explicitly documented by Truetone as the complete Hyde distortion circuit in a standalone pedal.
- **GarageTone Chainsaw Distortion** is explicitly identified in the official manual as a Visual Sound distortion pedal.
- **Vans Warped Distortion** is explicitly identified by Visual Sound/Truetone as a limited-edition distortion pedal made in partnership with the Vans Warped Tour.

## Fuzz
Current / archive-supported identity:
- Angry Fuzz

Verification notes:
- **Angry Fuzz** is explicitly identified by Truetone as Fuzz with an integrated octave circuit. The octave feature is secondary to the named Fuzz identity and does not create another dirt product.

## Category-overlap decisions
- **Jekyll & Hyde**: Overdrive + Distortion. Both channels are independently labeled by the builder and documented in the same physical pedal.
- **Route 66**: Overdrive only for the target census. Its second channel is compression, which is outside scope.
- **Double Trouble**: Overdrive only. Its two internal channels are both overdrives.
- **VS-XO**: Overdrive only. Both channels are explicitly described as overdrives; higher gain does not create a separate Distortion identity.
- **Angry Fuzz**: Fuzz only. Its integrated octave circuit does not create a separate target category.
- **GarageTone Chainsaw Distortion**: Distortion only. Despite lower-gain use cases discussed by reviewers, the builder's product category is Distortion.
- **Vans Warped Distortion**: Distortion only. It is retained as a distinct named limited-edition product rather than merged into another Visual Sound distortion.

## Historical / variant handling
- Visual Sound became **Truetone** in 2015. The archive treats this as one continuous builder/company lineage rather than separate unrelated builders.
- V1/V2/V3 versions of **Jekyll & Hyde**, **Route 66**, and related product revisions are not multiplied into separate identities unless a substantially different named design exists.
- **Route 808** and **Double Trouble** remain separate named products because each was marketed as its own standalone pedal design, even though they use the same underlying Visual Sound overdrive lineage.
- **GarageTone Drivetrain** and **GarageTone Chainsaw Distortion** remain separate GarageTone named products.
- The limited **Vans Warped Distortion** run remains distinct because it was a separately named, explicitly limited-edition product.

## Unpromoted leads
- **Visual Blues** and **Visual Metal** are documented by Visual Sound/Truetone history as early test-market pedals, but the reviewed primary material does not provide enough product-level category evidence to confidently place them into the Overdrive or Distortion census. They are therefore not promoted into a target category in this block.

## Exclusions
- H2O / Liquid Chorus, Oil Can, Axle Grease, Dual Tap Delay, Time Bandit, Visual Volume, and other non-dirt effects remain outside the target taxonomy.
- Comp 66 and Route 66's compressor channel are excluded as compression products.
- Truetone Clean Boost, Pure Tone buffer products, 1 SPOT power products, and other boost/power/accessory products are excluded.
- No modeled multi-effects identities are created from non-standalone products.
- Cloning, schematics, components, internal construction, and reverse-engineering material are outside scope.
