# Block 087 — NUX Audio

## Builder
**NUX Audio / NUX**

## Verification basis
Primary and authoritative sources reviewed:
- NUX official current Effects catalog and product pages
- NUX official current Mini Core, Core, Reissue, and Verdugo product pages
- NUX official legacy-product pages
- NUX official product/manual material for Drive Core Deluxe MKII, Steel Singer Drive, XTC OD, REC TO Distortion, Fireman, Horseman, Morning Star, Tube Man MKII, Brownie, and 6ixty5ive OD
- authoritative Effects Database catalog coverage for earlier NUX die-cast, Core, Mini, Mini Core, Reissue, and Verdugo products
- active-repository search for “NUX” and “Cherub” before addition

The active repository contained no prior NUX or Cherub match before this block was added. NUX is treated as the builder/brand identity for NUX-branded dirt pedals documented below. The census stays focused on standalone physical dirt pedals and does not convert modeled effects inside NUX multi-effects, amp-modelers, or integrated processors into separate product identities.

The reviewed material supports a broad NUX Overdrive and Distortion catalog and one clearly documented standalone Fuzz product. Current NUX pages provide the strongest category evidence, while authoritative historical catalog coverage is used for discontinued die-cast and earlier stompbox identities.

Verification notes:
- **Queen of Tone** is explicitly presented by NUX as a dual overdrive combining Horseman and Morning Star circuits.
- **Horseman** is presented by NUX as an overdrive pedal; its distortion capability does not create a second target category because NUX's named product category is Overdrive.
- **Morning Star** and **Tube Man MKII** are explicitly categorized by NUX as Overdrive products.
- **Drive Core Deluxe MKII** is explicitly an 8-way overdrive pedal. The earlier Drive Core Deluxe is treated as the same named product family rather than spawning a variant-only duplicate identity.
- **’59 Bassguy** and **’63 Diamond** are identified by NUX's Reissue catalog as overdrive products; the English pages emphasize amp-in-a-box behavior while NUX's Chinese catalog explicitly labels both as 过载 (overdrive).
- **‘6ixty5ive OD**, **Steel Singer Drive**, and **XTC OD** are explicitly identified by NUX as Overdrive products.
- **Ace of Tone** is retained as a historical dual-overdrive identity; NUX's legacy catalog lists it as a Verdugo stompbox and the product page describes it as dual overdrive.
- **Drive Core** is documented by authoritative catalog coverage as Overdrive. **BC-2 Vintage Blues Core**, **OD-2 Vintage Overdrive**, **OD-3 Overdrive**, **Scream Bass**, and **Mini Supa Drive** are likewise documented as Overdrive stompboxes in authoritative catalog coverage.

- **Brownie** is explicitly categorized by NUX as Distortion in the Mini Core catalog and product page.
- **Metal Core** and **Metal Core Deluxe** are documented by authoritative catalog coverage as Distortion products. The newer **Metal Core Deluxe MKII** is excluded because NUX currently classifies it as a preamp pedal rather than a standalone target-category distortion identity.
- **DS-3**, **HG-5**, **HG-6**, and **MX-6** are documented as Distortion products in authoritative NUX catalog coverage; DS-3 is also supported by NUX-era manuals/product material identifying it as Classic Distortion.
- **Mini Distortion** is documented as a standalone NUX Mini Series distortion pedal and remains distinct from the later Mini Core **Brownie**.

## Fuzz
Historical / manufacturer-supported identity:
- FZ-2 Vintage Fuzz

Verification notes:
- **FZ-2 Vintage Fuzz** is documented in authoritative NUX catalog coverage as a standalone fuzz pedal from the earlier die-cast series.
- **Scream Bass** is not separately promoted into Fuzz despite its published range from overdrive to fuzz because its builder-level catalog classification is Overdrive.
- Modeled fuzz effects inside MG/Mighty/Multi-Effects products are excluded and are not treated as physical NUX fuzz-pedal identities.

## Category-overlap decisions
- **Scream Bass**: Overdrive only. Authoritative catalog wording says it can range from overdrive to fuzz, but the product itself is categorized as Bass Overdrive.
- **Horseman**: Overdrive only. NUX describes a natural distortion capability, but the product category is Overdrive.
- **Drive Core Deluxe MKII**: Overdrive only. NUX explicitly describes it as an 8-way overdrive pedal with a post boost; the internal circuit choices do not create separate physical product identities.
- **’59 Bassguy / ’63 Diamond**: Overdrive only. NUX's Reissue catalog provides the target category; amp-in-a-box framing does not create an additional Distortion identity.
- **Metal Core Deluxe MKII**: excluded from Distortion because NUX currently presents it as a preamp pedal, not as a standalone target-category distortion product.

## Historical / variant handling
- **Drive Core Deluxe** and **Drive Core Deluxe MKII** are treated as one product-family identity for the initial census, with MKII retained in the product naming because it is a substantial named revision rather than an incidental cosmetic change.
- **Tube Man** and **Tube Man MKII** are consolidated under the current Tube Man MKII identity for the same reason.
- NUX model numbers such as NRO, NDO, NOD, NDS, and earlier die-cast XX-series codes are supporting identifiers rather than separate products.
- Cosmetic revisions, packaging changes, and routine production updates do not create additional dirt identities.

## Exclusions
- NUX MG, Mighty, and other multi-effects/modeling processors are excluded even when they contain modeled overdrive, distortion, or fuzz effects.
- Cerberus is excluded as an integrated multi-effects pedal even though it contains analog overdrive and distortion channels.
- Amp Academy, Solid Studio, Metal Core Deluxe MKII, Micro/amp-modeling products, and other preamp/modeler products are excluded when their product-level identity is not a standalone target dirt category.
- Pure boost products such as Lacerate, Boost Core Deluxe, and Masamune are excluded.
- Compressors, modulation, delay, reverb, EQ, tuner, octave, loop, cab/IR, wireless, power supply, and other non-dirt products are outside target scope.
- Modeled effects inside NUX digital products are not mined into separate physical pedal identities.
- Cloning, schematics, components, internal construction, and reverse-engineering material are outside scope.
