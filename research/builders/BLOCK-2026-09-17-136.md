## Explicit overlaps
- Titan II = Overdrive + Distortion + Fuzz. Official description explicitly describes low-gain overdrive, fuzz-tinged distortion, and full fuzz.
- Rumblefuzz II = Overdrive + Distortion + Fuzz. Official description explicitly states overdrive, distortion, and fuzz levels of gain.
- Charon = Overdrive + Distortion + Fuzz. Official description moves from low-gain crunchy overdrive through saturated distortion to fuzz.
- Saturn V = Overdrive. Although primarily a booster, the builder explicitly describes it as low-gain overdrive when the Drive control is advanced.
- Nebula = Fuzz. Octave and clean blend functions are not counted as separate dirt categories.

## Variant / lineage restraint
- Sputnik family editions and enclosure/reissue changes are consolidated as one lineage where the underlying product identity is the same.
- Apollo VII / VIIb are treated as one product lineage; Cadet and PDX/custom availability are not multiplied into separate pedal identities.
- Charon original and Charon v2 are treated as one Charon lineage, while the v2 Mod is retained as a documented evolution rather than a new unrelated builder entry.
- Rumblefuzz / Rumblefuzz II are treated as one lineage with II documented as the later version.
- Gemini III / IV remain identifiable generations of the Dual Fuzz Generator lineage rather than cosmetic duplicates.
- Limited colors, signed/numbered editions, NOS listings, and enclosure treatments do not create additional builder-pedal relationships.
- Boost, preamp, tremolo, phaser, compressor, filter, EQ, reverb, and other non-target functions are excluded unless the pedal explicitly qualifies for Overdrive, Distortion, or Fuzz.
