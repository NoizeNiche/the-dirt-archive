## Evidence / classification notes

- Carlin Compressor/Fuzz is cataloged as a fuzz pedal.
- Carlsbro Fuzz-Tone is documented as a fuzz pedal; Suzz is retained as a distortion/sustain dirt product based on independent vintage references.
- Carvin catalog records the TO-1 Tube Overdrive and UD-1 Ultra Distortion; the Steve Vai Legacy Drive/VLD1 is a tube gain/preamp product with an overdrive channel.
- Casimir Effects Pedals' Entropy Fuzz is a dedicated fuzz pedal.
- Chandler's Tube Driver is documented as an overdrive pedal.
- Chandler Limited's Germanium Drive uses gain/feedback settings that span driven, distorted, and fuzzy sounds, supporting both Overdrive and Distortion classifications.
- Center Street Electronics documentation/reviews identify Druid Fuzz, Godly Red, Rasputin Fuzz, The Murphy, Drone Station Fuzz, and God Hates Fuzz. Godly Red spans overdrive/distortion/fuzz; Drone Station spans overdrive to fuzz.
- CheeseBlocks Effects' Phoenix Fuzz and THE Cheese Distortion are documented dirt pedals. sCream Cheese variants were deferred because this pass did not establish a sufficiently clear dirt classification.
- ChiralityAudio's Splinter is an overdrive, while Black Swan is explicitly a high-gain distortion/fuzz.
- Chocolate Electronics documents Fuzzy Overdrive as moving from overdrive into fuzz, with Chocolate Crunch, Melty Overdrive, and Chocolate Drive as overdrive products.
- Cleveland Music Co.'s catalog includes the listed distortion, overdrive, fuzz, and overfuzz models; Bleepcakes blends transistor overdrive and harmonic fuzz.
- CNZ Audio's catalog documents GDS-20 distortion, SOD-20 overdrive, SFZ-20 fuzz, SCM-20 metal distortion, and the listed D-style/Bluesy overdrive models.
- Chesapeake Effects' Flagship Overdrive is documented as an overdrive pedal.
- Chuck Pedals' Arcadiator and Diver are fuzz products; VTO is an overdrive product.

## Exclusions / non-additions

- Casimir utility/boost products were excluded.
- Chandler Limited studio hardware and non-dirt products were excluded.
- Center Street non-dirt products were excluded.
- CheeseBlocks sCream Cheese / Jr. were deferred because the dirt classification was not strong enough.
