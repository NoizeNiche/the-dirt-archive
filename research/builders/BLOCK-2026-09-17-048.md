# Builder → Pedals Research Block 048

## Builder
**Big John Effects — Jan Detremerie, Stijn Vandebril, and Arnoud Faber**

## Verification basis
Big John Effects' official About page identifies the company's history: founded in Belgium in 2006 by bassist Jan Detremerie, later joined by guitarist Stijn Vandebril, with Arnoud Faber running the workshop since 2013. The same official history gives a product timeline covering fuzz, overdrive, distortion, and drive designs. Current official product pages and the manufacturer's owner manual were used to establish target dirt classifications. The census includes clearly documented historical identities as well as the current catalog where the manufacturer still provides product material.

## Product notes
- **Hairy Balls:** Big John's current product page explicitly identifies the pedal as a fuzz with boost, while the official 2024 owner manual explicitly describes the unit as producing preamp, overdrive, distortion, fuzz, and synth-like sounds. It is therefore retained in all three target dirt categories. The boost and gate functions are not counted as separate categories.
- **Granny Puker:** the official product page identifies the two core effects as Preamp/Overdrive and Dirty Octave Down, explicitly states that it can be used as a distortion box, and describes its smooth gritty overdrive range. It is retained under both Overdrive and Distortion. The octave-down function is not a target dirt category.
- **Pocket Rocket:** the manufacturer explicitly calls this a vintage-style Tube Screamer take with overdrive and an additional clipping mode. It is retained under Overdrive only.
- **Dirty Sandwich:** the official product page describes a switchable boost plus adjustable drive ranging from transparent overdrive to heavy drive. It is retained under Overdrive. The effects-loop/utility functions are not counted.
- **Obama Wah:** Big John's official history identifies this as a wah/filter and distortion design with voltage gain control. Because the manufacturer explicitly places distortion in the product's identity, it is retained under Distortion despite the pedal's primary filter/wah function.
- **Lil' John series:** Big John's official history identifies the 2011 Lil' John series as Boost and Fuzz designs by Stijn Vandebril. Because the official source does not expose enough separate model-level information in the current catalog to establish distinct pedal identities, the series is retained as one historical fuzz identity rather than inventing individual names.

## Explicit exclusions / restraint
- **BassDrive:** listed in the official timeline as a 2015 pre-order design, but the accessible manufacturer material does not provide enough product-level classification to place it confidently in one of the three target categories. It remains excluded from the categorized census.
- **AmpAmp:** excluded because the manufacturer identifies it as a bass preamp rather than a target dirt pedal.
- **LoLife:** excluded because the manufacturer identifies it as a flatwound-sound/preamp-oriented design without enough explicit target-category evidence.
- **TrebleJuice:** excluded because the manufacturer identifies it as a 2024 prototype treble-oriented design and provides insufficient evidence for a target dirt classification.
- **Boost-only functions:** not counted as overdrive, distortion, or fuzz merely because they can push a following stage.
- **Octave/filter/looper functions:** not promoted into the dirt census unless the manufacturer explicitly identifies the pedal itself with a target dirt function.
- **No category inflation:** descriptive terms such as grit, gain, drive, or saturation were not used to create extra categories without explicit manufacturer support.
