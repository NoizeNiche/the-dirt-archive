# Builder -> Pedals Research Block 205

## Scope

Continued the C-section Builder -> Pedals census with two new canonical builders. Inclusion remains dirt-only: overdrive, distortion, and fuzz.

## New canonical builders

### 485 — Color Audio
- Cassette Preamp — Overdrive
- Cassette Preamp — Fuzz

### 486 — Coura
- Raizor — Distortion
- Gainor — Distortion

## Evidence / classification notes

- Color Audio is a current pedal company based in Helsinki, Finland. Its official Cassette Preamp page describes the pedal as a cassette-recorder channel strip in pedal form with gain ranging from clean through saturation to blown-out fuzz, and specifically describes low-pass/high-pass settings that produce fuzzier or more forward distortion character.
- A current independent review explicitly describes the Cassette Preamp as producing dynamic, gritty overdrive and fully blown-out fuzz and places it in both fuzz-pedal and overdrive-pedal categories. The archive therefore records both Overdrive and Fuzz.
- Coura is documented as a guitar-building workshop that collaborated with an electronics specialist on several FX devices. A contemporaneous German musician-board post identifies the Raizor as a distortion unit and describes the Gainor as the Raizor circuit with a second volume control that provides a boost when bypassed. The archive records both as Distortion.
- No duplicate canonical identity exists for Color Audio or Coura in the master builder index.

## Exclusions / non-additions

- Color Audio's Cassette Preamp is not split into multiple editions or enclosure variants.
- Coura Acoustifeier and Realizer are excluded because the reviewed descriptions concern acoustic enhancement / vibration-pickup functions rather than overdrive, distortion, or fuzz.
- Cooper FX remains excluded because the reviewed evidence concerns multi-effects hardware rather than a discrete dirt catalog.
- Cubist Effects remains deferred as a modification shop rather than a clearly resolved standalone builder identity.
- Cortez Overdrive remains deferred because Effects Database ties the name into Coron/Bias OEM lineages rather than establishing a distinct builder identity.
- Cloak Audio Jackdaw remains deferred for Fredric Effects identity reconciliation.
