# Block 066 — MXR — Dunlop Manufacturing, Inc.

## Builder
**MXR — Dunlop Manufacturing, Inc.**

## Verification basis
Primary manufacturer sources reviewed:
- Dunlop / MXR current MXR catalog
- Dunlop / MXR legacy-product catalog
- individual manufacturer product pages
- manufacturer product manuals
- manufacturer-authored MXR history and product material

The official Dunlop site carries the MXR catalog and identifies MXR as a registered trademark of Dunlop Manufacturing, Inc. The manufacturer maintains a current MXR product catalog plus a separate MXR legacy-product catalog. This block therefore records MXR as the builder identity and Dunlop Manufacturing, Inc. as the corporate attribution rather than treating every artist-series, finish, or revision listing as a new builder.

This block records builder/product relationships only. Current products are taken from the live MXR catalog and individual product pages. Historical identities are retained when the manufacturer maintains them in its legacy catalog or provides manufacturer-authored product/manual material supporting the dirt identity. Limited editions, anniversary finishes, and cosmetic variants are consolidated with their underlying products unless the manufacturer presents a genuinely distinct named functional model.

Verification notes:
- GT-OD is explicitly described by MXR as an Overdrive.
- Super Badass Dynamic O.D. is explicitly named as an O.D. product in the current MXR catalog.
- Duke of Tone is explicitly named as an Overdrive; the manufacturer manual documents selectable OD, Boost, and Distortion modes, so the pedal is also retained in Distortion below.
- Timmy Overdrive is explicitly named as an Overdrive.
- EVH 5150 Overdrive is explicitly named as Overdrive and the manufacturer describes a range of OD and distortion tones; its named core identity remains Overdrive, with the manufacturer not using a separate Distortion product identity for it.
- Raw Dawg Overdrive is explicitly named and described as an OD based on a classic overdrive circuit.
- Sugar Drive is explicitly described as a transparent overdrive, and the manual identifies its Drive control as increasing overdrive.
- Fat Sugar Drive is explicitly described as providing transparent overdrive tones; its manual identifies the Drive control as increasing overdrive.
- Yngwie Malmsteen Overdrive is explicitly named and described as an Overdrive.
- Custom Badass Modified O.D. is explicitly named as an O.D. product.
- Dookie Drive is retained as Overdrive because MXR's manufacturer page describes its purpose as a full harmonic range of overdriven tones and the product is presented as a drive rather than a separately categorized distortion identity.
- Jail Guitar Doors Overdrive is explicitly named as Overdrive.
- FOD Drive is retained as Overdrive because MXR describes its two channels as recreating overdriven amp stacks and repeatedly characterizes the resulting gain as overdrive.
- Raijin Drive is retained in both Overdrive and Distortion because MXR explicitly describes its two selectable sides as Japanese overdrive and distortion tones.
- Double-Double Overdrive, CAE Boost/Overdrive, Gran Torino Boost Overdrive, and Bass Overdrive are explicitly supported by their manufacturer product names/descriptions.

Verification notes:
- Distortion+ is explicitly named and documented as a distortion product, even though MXR also describes usable overdriven blues settings.
- Randy Rhoads Distortion+ is a separately named signature/reissue identity but is a recreation of the MXR Distortion+; it is retained as a named product identity because the manufacturer presents it as a distinct current model with its own product page.
- Distortion III is explicitly named as Distortion. MXR describes its range from singing overdrive to massive distortion, but the named manufacturer identity remains Distortion.
- Super Badass Distortion is explicitly named as Distortion. MXR describes its range from low-gain overdrive to metal distortion, but it remains in Distortion.
- Fullbore Metal Distortion is explicitly named as a metal distortion product.
- Custom Badass '78 Distortion is explicitly named as Distortion; the manufacturer manual identifies its Distortion control and distortion output behavior.
- Bass Distortion+ and Bass Distortion are explicitly named bass-distortion products. Bass Distortion is marked legacy/no longer in production on the manufacturer page.
- Duke of Tone is retained in Distortion because the official MXR manual explicitly documents a Distortion mode using hard clipping; this is a functional category overlap, not an inference from tone alone.
- Raijin Drive is retained in Distortion because MXR explicitly describes a selectable distortion side.
- Prime Distortion and Dime Distortion are manufacturer-supported historical/legacy Distortion identities.

## Category-overlap decisions
- **Duke of Tone Overdrive**: Overdrive + Distortion. The MXR manual explicitly provides OD, Boost, and Distortion clipping modes. Boost remains outside the target taxonomy, while OD and Distortion are both retained.
- **Raijin Drive**: Overdrive + Distortion. MXR explicitly describes separate Japanese overdrive and distortion tones selectable by switch.
- **Distortion+**: Distortion only. Overdriven settings are documented, but the manufacturer names and categorizes the product as Distortion+ rather than creating a separate Overdrive identity.
- **Distortion III**: Distortion only. Its overdrive range does not change the named manufacturer category.
- **Super Badass Distortion**: Distortion only. Its lower-gain overdrive range does not create a second manufacturer identity.
- **EVH 5150 Overdrive**: Overdrive only. MXR describes both OD and distortion tones, but the manufacturer product identity is explicitly Overdrive.
- **Dookie Drive**: Overdrive only in the target taxonomy. The manufacturer characterizes the pedal around overdriven amp tones rather than assigning it a separate Distortion category.
- **FOD Drive**: Overdrive only. MXR's manufacturer descriptions repeatedly call the core behavior overdrive/overdriven amp stacks.
- **Blue Box Fuzz**: Fuzz only. Its octave content does not create a separate target category.
- **Slash Octave Fuzz**: Fuzz only. Sub-octave and octave-up sections are part of the fuzz design.
- **Classic 108 Fuzz Mini**: Fuzz only. The smaller housing is treated as a functional mini model, not a separate category.

## Historical / variant handling
- **Randy Rhoads Distortion+ / Randy Rhoads Special Edition Distortion+**: consolidated where the manufacturer is presenting the latter as a special-edition finish/iteration of the same Randy Rhoads Distortion+ identity; the base RR104 identity is retained in the census.
- **Dookie Drive V2 / V3 / V4 / 30th Anniversary**: consolidated under Dookie Drive rather than treating version numbers and anniversary finishings as separate base identities.
- **Classic 108 Fuzz / Classic 108 Fuzz Mini**: retained as distinct named functional models because the Mini is an independently named smaller-footprint product, while the larger Classic 108 remains a separate legacy model.
- **Sugar Drive / Fat Sugar Drive**: retained separately because MXR presents the Fat Sugar Drive as a distinct full-size housing/product identity rather than a purely cosmetic finish.
- **Vexter-style and custom-art logic is not applicable here**; MXR limited finishes and anniversary editions are consolidated under their underlying named models unless functionality creates a distinct named model.
- **Jail Guitar Doors Drive**: kept outside the categorized core census as a verification lead because the manufacturer page reviewed names it “Drive” and describes raw/gritty tones but does not provide sufficiently explicit Overdrive, Distortion, or Fuzz categorization at product level. The explicitly named Jail Guitar Doors Overdrive is categorized.

## Exclusions
- Boost-only products such as Booster Mini, Micro Amp, Micro Amp+, CAE Boost/Line Driver, and similar utility/boost identities are excluded unless the product has an explicit target dirt identity.
- Compression, EQ, noise gate, tuner, utility, power-supply, looper, modulation, delay, reverb, talk-box, synth, and octave-only products are excluded from the target dirt census.
- Products belonging to non-MXR Dunlop brands such as Cry Baby, Way Huge, and Authentic Hendrix are not merged into this MXR builder block.
- Cosmetic-only special editions, anniversary finishes, and B-stock listings are not separate base identities.
