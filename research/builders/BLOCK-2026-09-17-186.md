# Builder -> Pedals Research Block 186

## Scope

Continued the C-section Builder -> Pedals census after Block 185, including alphabetic backfills that had been visible in the C-section source but not yet reconciled. Inclusion remains dirt-only: overdrive, distortion, and fuzz. Existing builders were cross-checked against the canonical index before assigning new identities. Boost-first, amp-simulation, modulation, delay, wah-only, EQ, octave, loop, and other non-dirt functions were excluded.

## Evidence / classification notes

- C.Giant's database entry contains two products, one delay and the OD-1 Warm Blues. The OD-1 is explicitly cataloged as an overdrive/distortion product and its record notes that the design is shared with other branded versions. The archive records the C.Giant-branded pedal without treating the brand label as proof of original circuit authorship.
- Captain FX's Yeti is explicitly tagged fuzz. Its product description calls it a fuzz pedal and notes usable bass overdrive at lower settings, but fuzz is the cataloged primary dirt class.
- CAT Sound's catalog contains six products. AmpCenter is explicitly an amp-simulation pedal and Luna Shadow is a delay, so both are excluded. Classic is tagged distortion; Dancing is the named distortion model; DriveCenter and DriveCenter Bass are both tagged overdrive. The latter two also carry boost/preamp tags, but the database explicitly classifies them as overdrive, so they remain in the dirt census.
- Cave Passive Pedals is documented as an Australian builder run by Heath and Georgina Cave, with Heath as chief designer. The 60's Break Up, Blue Juice, Grunt, Grunt MkII, and Super Grunt are explicitly overdrive records. The Wasp and Wasp MkII are explicitly fuzz records. Mini Miff carries both fuzz and overdrive classifications, so it receives one row for each dirt type in accordance with the archive's multi-type row rule.
- Cave Passive Pedals products such as AmpCenter-equivalent utility items, EQ, octaver, phaser, loop switching, volume, and other non-dirt models are excluded.

## Exclusions / non-additions

- C.M. Baker Izzy Plus and Li'l Izzy remain excluded as boost/preamp products.
- CajeFX, Candy FX, Canvas Analog Devices, Caustic FX, and other zero-product or no-dirt candidates reviewed in this pass do not receive canonical builder identities.
- Calypso FX remains excluded because its cataloged products are modulation/rotating-phase effects.
- CAT Sound AmpCenter is amp simulation, and Luna Shadow is delay.
- Cave Passive Pedals Glassy Mouth remains outside the dirt census because its catalog classification is boost/preamp rather than a primary dirt effect.
- No duplicate canonical identity is created for any existing builder.
