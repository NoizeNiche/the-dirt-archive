# The Dirt Archive — Builder Census Block 119

## Builder
**Benson Amps**

Benson Amps is a previously uncovered builder with a pedal catalog that spans Overdrive, Distortion, and Fuzz. Benson's own site identifies Chris Benson as the builder and says its amps and pedals are completely handmade in Portland, Oregon. The current pedal catalog lists Portable Distortion 424 MKII, Germanium Boost, Preamp, Germanium Preamp, Delay, Störkn B0kš, Deep Sea Diver Fuzz-Echo, and Florist. citeturn226367search0turn806913search1

## Scope result
The archive records Benson's named dirt products and explicit dirt identities. The classification stays conservative: a pedal is not promoted into another target category just because it can get dirtier at extreme settings. Where Benson explicitly describes a product as overdrive/fuzz or distortion/fuzz, those target identities are retained.

## Overdrive
- **Preamp Pedal** — current; explicitly described as clean boost/overdrive/fuzz
- **Germanium Preamp Pedal** — current; explicitly described as overdrive/boost/fuzz
- **Germanium Fuzz** — current/legacy; authoritative Benson/Mixwave guide documents clean boost, overdrive, and fuzz

- **Germanium Fuzz** is retained as a named Benson fuzz identity even though it is absent from the current retail pedal grid. It is documented by Benson's own warranty material and by the official Mixwave representation updated in 2026. citeturn806913search1turn806913search5turn160195search2

## Research note
Benson is useful to the archive because its current catalog is compact but unusually clear about its dirt language. The Preamp family explicitly crosses overdrive and fuzz, while the 424 MKII is the cleanly named distortion product. Germanium Fuzz and the Stonk family add distinct fuzz identities without turning every gain range into another category.
