# The Dirt Archive — Builder Research Block 052

## Builder
**MI Audio / MI Effects — Michael Ibrahim**

## Verification basis
Primary manufacturer sources used:
- MI Audio official About page
- MI Audio official effects catalog/store
- Individual official product pages and manufacturer manuals where available

Authoritative corroboration used where the current official catalog no longer exposes older products:
- Australian trademark record identifying MI Audio and the Neo Fuzz / Blues Pro pedal marks
- Guitar Pedal X directory/profile documenting the historical MI Audio / MI Effects catalog and Michael Ibrahim relationship

MI Audio's official About page states that the company was founded in 2002 by Michael Ibrahim and that the creation of the Tube Zone Overdrive was the impetus for the company. The same page identifies the later Super Crunch Box and Super Blues Pro as updated generations of earlier drive products.

## Product-level verification notes
- **Tube Zone Overdrive** is explicitly identified by MI Audio's own company history as the pedal whose creation drove the founding of MI Audio. It is retained as Overdrive.
- **Blue Boy Deluxe** is documented as an MI Effects overdrive identity in authoritative historical catalog material. It is retained as Overdrive rather than inferring additional categories from its gain range.
- **Cross Over Drive** is an official MI Audio product. Its manufacturer page describes it as an amp-like overdrive built around four FET stages and explicitly identifies its overdrive behavior. The page notes that extreme settings can enter loose fuzz territory, but the finished product remains categorized as Overdrive.
- **Super Blues Pro** is explicitly described by MI Audio as a low-to-mid-gain overdrive. The manufacturer also documents selectable fuzz modes, so it is retained under both Overdrive and Fuzz.
- **Crunch Box** is the original MI Audio high-gain drive identity. MI Audio's official About page states that the classic Crunch Box production was later updated as the Super Crunch Box. It is retained as the historical Distortion identity rather than duplicating it as a separate current product.
- **Super Crunch Box V2** is the later documented Crunch Box generation and is consistently identified as a high-gain distortion product in manufacturer-linked historical material. It is retained under Distortion.
- **Classic Distortion** is a documented discontinued MI Effects product identity and is retained under Distortion.
- **Megalith Delta** is the stompbox version of the MI Amplification Megalith high-gain amplifier tone. MI Audio's catalog identifies it as a high-gain distortion pedal, so it is retained under Distortion.
- **GI Fuzz** and **Neo Fuzz** are documented MI Effects fuzz identities. The Neo Fuzz is also supported by the Australian trademark record for MI Audio.

## Scope decisions / exclusions
- **Boost 'N' Buff** is excluded because the manufacturer explicitly identifies it as a booster/buffer rather than one of the target dirt categories.
- **Polyanna/Pollyanna Octave** is excluded because its core product identity is octave processing rather than a target dirt pedal.
- **Compressor** and **Lo-Fi Delay** are excluded as non-dirt effects.
- **Iron Duke** and other amplifier products are excluded because this census is for pedals.
- Descriptions that merely mention fuzz-like behavior, saturation, or gain are not used to create additional categories unless the manufacturer or authoritative catalog explicitly identifies the product as belonging there.
- V1/V2 revisions are consolidated where they represent the same named product identity; the historical Crunch Box and later Super Crunch Box are retained separately because MI Audio itself distinguishes the original and updated product names.
