# Block 078 — MESA/Boogie

## Builder
**MESA/Boogie**

## Verification basis
Primary and authoritative sources reviewed:
- MESA/Boogie's official Drive Pedals catalog
- MESA/Boogie's official individual pedal pages and manuals archive
- MESA/Boogie's manufacturer-authored product announcement for the original Drive/Distortion lineup
- MESA/Boogie's official Boost Pedals catalog and Tone-Burst product page
- specialist product documentation used only where the manufacturer page does not state a target category as directly

The active repository contained no prior MESA/Boogie match before this block was added. MESA/Boogie is treated as the builder identity for the MESA-branded guitar dirt pedals documented below.

The reviewed material supports ten clearly documented core dirt identities: Cleo, DynaPlex, Gold Mine, Grid Slammer, Flux-Drive, Flux-Five, Throttle Box, Throttle Box EQ, Tone-Burst, and Flux-Drive-family material. The functional product identity is recorded once even when the manufacturer later revised or expanded the same platform.

## Overdrive
Historical / manufacturer-supported identities:
- Cleo
- DynaPlex
- Gold Mine
- Grid Slammer
- Flux-Drive
- Flux-Five
- Throttle Box
- Throttle Box EQ
- Tone-Burst

Verification notes:
- **Cleo** is explicitly presented as a transparent boost/overdrive pedal spanning subtle boost through harmonically rich overdrive.
- **DynaPlex** is explicitly presented as a medium-gain overdrive pedal.
- **Gold Mine** is explicitly presented within MESA's overdrive line and as the higher-gain member of the Cleo/DynaPlex/Gold Mine group.
- **Grid Slammer** is explicitly presented as an overdrive pedal.
- **Flux-Drive** is explicitly presented as an overdrive pedal, and MESA's launch announcement labels it an Overdrive/Distortion pedal. It therefore receives both categories below.
- **Flux-Five** is explicitly presented as a supercharged Flux-Drive overdrive and retains the original Flux-Drive low-gain mode.
- **Throttle Box** is a high-gain distortion product, but MESA's own product material also calls the platform an overdrive and describes its lower-gain mode as classic-rock/blues territory. It receives both Overdrive and Distortion below.
- **Throttle Box EQ** is the expanded EQ version of the Throttle Box platform. Its manufacturer page explicitly describes its gain range as overdrive; the product is also directly derived from the Throttle Box distortion platform, so it receives both categories in this census.
- **Tone-Burst** is officially described by MESA as a Boost/Overdrive pedal with enough gain for subtle clipped overdrive. It is included under Overdrive despite appearing within the manufacturer's Boost Pedals section.

## Distortion
Historical / manufacturer-supported identities:
- Flux-Drive
- Throttle Box
- Throttle Box EQ

Verification notes:
- **Flux-Drive** receives Distortion because MESA's manufacturer-authored launch material explicitly labels it an Overdrive/Distortion pedal while its dedicated product page identifies it primarily as an overdrive.
- **Throttle Box** is explicitly identified by MESA as a high-gain distortion pedal. Its low-gain mode is treated as operating range, but its manufacturer material also identifies the wider platform with overdrive language, so both target categories are retained.
- **Throttle Box EQ** is an expanded version of the Throttle Box gain platform. The manufacturer explicitly describes overdrive operation and its high-gain range, while authoritative product coverage identifies it as a distortion pedal. It therefore receives both categories.

## Fuzz
Current / manufacturer-supported identities:
- none identified

Historical / manufacturer-supported identities:
- none identified

Verification notes:
- The reviewed MESA/Boogie pedal catalog, individual drive pages, boost catalog, and manuals do not establish a standalone MESA-branded Fuzz pedal identity. Generic saturated or fuzzy-sounding language is not promoted into the census without product-level category evidence.

## Category-overlap decisions
- **Cleo**: Overdrive only.
- **DynaPlex**: Overdrive only.
- **Gold Mine**: Overdrive only. Its higher-gain saturation is treated as range within an overdrive product.
- **Grid Slammer**: Overdrive only.
- **Flux-Drive**: Overdrive + Distortion. MESA explicitly uses the phrase Overdrive/Distortion in manufacturer launch material.
- **Flux-Five**: Overdrive only. It is explicitly described by MESA as an overdrive and as the enhanced Flux-Drive platform.
- **Throttle Box**: Overdrive + Distortion. MESA identifies it as a high-gain distortion pedal while describing its lower-gain mode and broader platform with overdrive language.
- **Throttle Box EQ**: Overdrive + Distortion. MESA explicitly documents stinging overdrive and positions it as an expanded Throttle Box gain platform; authoritative product coverage classifies it as distortion.
- **Tone-Burst**: Overdrive only. MESA explicitly identifies it as Boost/Overdrive; no separate Distortion identity is created.

## Historical / variant handling
- The MESA Drive Pedals catalog presents Cleo, DynaPlex, Gold Mine, Grid Slammer, Flux-Drive, Throttle Box, Flux-Five, and Throttle Box EQ as a coherent pedal family; these are retained as distinct named products because the manufacturer gives each its own model identity.
- **Flux-Five** remains separate from Flux-Drive because MESA presents it as an expanded product with a five-band graphic EQ and additional switchable gain mode.
- **Throttle Box EQ** remains separate from Throttle Box because the manufacturer presents it as an expanded five-band-EQ version with additional controls and two independently levelled gain modes.
- Product finishes, production revisions, and stock/presentation variants do not create additional dirt identities.
- Boost, EQ, and preamp functions are treated as secondary functions unless the manufacturer explicitly places the named product in one of the target dirt categories.
- Historical/discontinued status does not exclude a product because the archive includes historical dirt products.

## Exclusions
- **Tone-Burst** is not excluded as a boost-only utility because MESA explicitly names it Boost/Overdrive and describes clipped overdrive operation. It is therefore included under Overdrive.
- MESA boost, EQ, switching, utility, amplifier, cabinet, and direct-interface products without explicit target-category dirt identity are outside the target census.
- Generic gain, saturation, clipping, or distortion language without product-level category evidence is not used to create secondary identities.
- Cloning, schematics, internal construction, component details, and reverse-engineering material are outside scope.
