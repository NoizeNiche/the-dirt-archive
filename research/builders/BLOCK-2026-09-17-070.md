# Block 070 — Xotic Effects

## Builder
**Xotic Effects / Xotic California**

## Verification basis
Primary manufacturer sources reviewed:
- Xotic California official About Us page
- Xotic Effects official Effects catalog
- Xotic Effects official Manuals archive
- individual Xotic product pages
- manufacturer-authored legacy manuals

The manufacturer presents Xotic Effects as its effects-pedal brand and maintains current and legacy pedal indexes. The About Us page documents the AC Booster, RC Booster, and BB Preamp as major Xotic Effects products, while the Effects catalog separates current pedals from a Legacy Effects Pedals section. This block records the Xotic Effects builder/product relationship only.

Dirt categories are based on explicit manufacturer product titles, catalog labels, and manufacturer-authored descriptions/manuals. General references to gain, clipping, saturation, or a pedal being capable of a certain sound do not automatically create another target category unless the manufacturer explicitly identifies the pedal in overdrive, distortion, or fuzz terms.

## Overdrive
Current / manufacturer-supported identities:
- SL Drive
- AC Booster V2
- BB Preamp V1.5
- Soul Driven
- Bass BB Preamp V1.5

Historical / manufacturer-supported identities:
- BB Preamp
- AC Booster
- BB Plus
- BB Preamp MB
- BB Preamp Comp
- AC Booster Comp
- BB Preamp AT
- Soul Driven AH
- Bass BB Preamp

Verification notes:
- **SL Drive** is explicitly cataloged by Xotic as **DISTORTION / OVERDRIVE** and is therefore retained in both Overdrive and Distortion below.
- **AC Booster V2** is explicitly described as offering a wide range of overdrive with amp-like touch sensitivity.
- **BB Preamp V1.5** is explicitly described as a boost / overdrive pedal and as suitable for clean boost, overdrive, or both.
- **Soul Driven** is explicitly described as a creamy boost / overdrive and as a transparent overdrive.
- **Bass BB Preamp V1.5** is explicitly described as delivering crunchy overdrive and is documented as a bass overdrive product.
- **BB Preamp** is historically described by Xotic as ranging from clean boost to blistering overdrive.
- **AC Booster** is historically presented by Xotic as a booster with high-gain capability and in its manufacturer description as able to work as a distortion pedal at maximum gain. It is retained here as Overdrive and in Distortion below because the manufacturer explicitly identifies that use.
- **BB Plus** is manufacturer-documented as a dual-channel preamp/overdrive platform; its Channel A is derived from the BB Preamp and the manual describes smooth overdrive at higher gain.
- **BB Preamp MB**, **BB Preamp Comp**, and **BB Preamp AT** are retained as named historical Xotic products because the manufacturer lists them as distinct legacy/custom-shop products and their relationship to the BB Preamp overdrive platform is explicit.
- **AC Booster Comp** is retained as a historical overdrive identity because it is the custom-shop AC Booster with compression added; the AC Booster platform is the relevant dirt identity.
- **Soul Driven AH** is retained as a named historical Soul Driven variant because Xotic lists it separately in its legacy catalog and associated manufacturer material identifies the Soul Driven overdrive platform.
- **Bass BB Preamp** is retained historically because Xotic's manuals archive lists it as a distinct Bass BB Preamp product and the manufacturer describes it as bass boost / overdrive.

## Distortion
Current / manufacturer-supported identities:
- SL Drive

Historical / manufacturer-supported identities:
- AC Booster

Verification notes:
- **SL Drive** is the clearest current Xotic Distortion identity because the manufacturer catalog explicitly labels the pedal **DISTORTION / OVERDRIVE**.
- **AC Booster** is retained historically in Distortion because Xotic's official product page explicitly states that its gain range is enough for it to work as a distortion pedal when turned all the way up.
- Other Xotic products may produce heavily driven or distorted sounds, but the manufacturer presents them primarily as boost/overdrive products and does not provide sufficient product-level Distortion classification to force another category.

## Fuzz
Current / manufacturer-supported identities:
- none explicitly categorized by the manufacturer in the reviewed Xotic Effects catalog

Historical / manufacturer-supported identities:
- none explicitly categorized by the manufacturer in the reviewed Xotic Effects catalog

Verification notes:
- No Xotic Effects fuzz pedal was promoted into the census from the reviewed official catalog and manuals. This is intentional and follows the project rule against turning sonic descriptions into unsupported product categories.

## Category-overlap decisions
- **SL Drive**: Overdrive + Distortion. Xotic's current catalog explicitly labels it Distortion / Overdrive.
- **AC Booster**: Overdrive + Distortion historically. The manufacturer presents it as a booster/overdrive platform and explicitly says the gain range can make it work as a distortion pedal at maximum gain.
- **BB Preamp V1.5 / BB Preamp / Bass BB Preamp V1.5 / Bass BB Preamp**: Overdrive only. Xotic explicitly presents these as boost / overdrive products even though their gain controls can reach aggressive sounds.
- **BB Plus**: Overdrive only. The manufacturer manual documents an overdrive channel but does not explicitly establish a separate Distortion product identity.
- **AC Booster V2**: Overdrive only. Its current manufacturer page explicitly frames the redesign as a wide-range overdrive; the older AC Booster's separate historical distortion wording is not automatically carried over as a new V2 Distortion identity.
- **Soul Driven / Soul Driven AH**: Overdrive only. Xotic explicitly calls Soul Driven a transparent overdrive.
- **BB Preamp MB / BB Preamp Comp / BB Preamp AT / AC Booster Comp**: retain the dirt category of their underlying manufacturer platform rather than treating compression or artist/custom-shop designation as new target categories.

## Historical / variant handling
- Current versus legacy status follows Xotic's own Effects catalog, which has a separate **Legacy Effects Pedals - No Longer In Production** section.
- **BB Preamp V1.5** is kept separate from the original **BB Preamp** because Xotic gives the later model its own product identity and describes it as an enhanced version of the original.
- **AC Booster V2** is kept separate from the original **AC Booster** because Xotic presents the V2 as a newly designed current product and the original appears in the legacy section.
- **RC Booster Classic** and **RC Booster V2** are not promoted into the dirt census because Xotic identifies them as boost products rather than target Overdrive, Distortion, or Fuzz identities on the reviewed current catalog pages.
- **EP Booster**, **Super Sweet Booster**, **Super Clean Buffer**, and **AH Booster** are excluded as boost/buffer products rather than target dirt identities.
- **SP Compressor** and other compression/filter/wah products are excluded.
- Cosmetic and anniversary editions are consolidated rather than created as separate functional dirt identities.
- The manufacturer currently lists Xotic Effects products alongside Xotic guitars and accessories; only effects-pedal dirt identities are in scope here.

## Exclusions
- RC Booster Classic / RC Booster V2: boost products, not promoted to Overdrive solely because they can push an amplifier into overdrive.
- EP Booster, Super Sweet Booster, Super Clean Buffer, AH Booster: boost/buffer products outside the target taxonomy.
- SP Compressor: compressor.
- X-Blender, Robotalk, wahs, volume pedals, accessories, and guitar products: non-target products.
- No fuzz identity was inferred from tone descriptions because Xotic's reviewed manufacturer catalog does not explicitly classify a product as Fuzz.
