# The Dirt Archive — Builder Census Block 121

## Builder
**Suhr**

Suhr is a previously uncovered builder with a clearly divided pedal catalog covering Overdrive, Distortion, and Fuzz. Suhr's current pedal pages organize its dirt products into those categories and explicitly identify Eclipse as a dual-channel overdrive/distortion, Shiba Drive as overdrive, Riot as distortion, and Rufus/Thornicus as fuzz. citeturn571468search0turn571468search1turn571468search3turn571468search9

## Scope result
The archive records Suhr's named dirt products from the current and legacy catalog where the builder itself clearly identifies the product as Overdrive, Distortion, or Fuzz. The classification remains conservative: a distortion pedal that can push an amplifier into overdrive is not automatically added to Overdrive, and a fuzz pedal that reaches distorted tones is not automatically added to Distortion.

## Historical / variant restraint
- **Riot / Riot Reloaded** are retained as the Riot lineage, with the original Riot and later Reloaded version distinguished only where Suhr's catalog clearly names the product. The archive does not multiply minor hardware revisions. citeturn571468search1turn571468search12
- **Rufus / Rufus Reloaded** are treated as one fuzz lineage with the named Reloaded version retained as a historical product rather than counting enclosure or finish variants separately. citeturn571468search0turn571468search10
- **Shiba Drive / Shiba Drive Reloaded / Kiko Loureiro Signature Shiba Drive Reloaded** are retained as named products because Suhr's catalog explicitly distinguishes those product names, but ordinary revision details are not expanded beyond the named lineage. citeturn571468search3

## Research note
Suhr is useful to the archive because its current catalog makes the dirt taxonomy unusually explicit. Eclipse is the key overlap, while Shiba Drive, Riot, and the Rufus/Thornicus lineages give the archive clean examples of dedicated Overdrive, Distortion, and Fuzz identities without forcing cross-category inflation.
