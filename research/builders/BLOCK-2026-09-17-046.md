# Builder → Pedals Research Block 046

## Builder
**Sitek Guitar Electronics — Andrzej “Andy” Sitek & Hania Sitek**

## Verification basis
Sitek Guitar Electronics' official About page identifies Andy Sitek and his wife Hania as the founders, and gives the company contact as Sitek Engineering / Andrzej Sitek in Gdynia, Poland. The official pedal catalog separates pedals into Booster / Pre-amp, Overdrive, Distortion / Fuzz, Delay / Modulation, and Utility categories. The core census below is limited to products whose official product pages explicitly establish one or more of the three target dirt functions.

## Product notes
- **Freya Overdrive/Booster:** Sitek explicitly describes Freya as a dual pedal combining a low-to-mid-gain overdrive with a transparent high-headroom booster. The manufacturer categorizes it under both Booster / Pre-amp and Overdrive. Only the Overdrive identity is counted here; the clean booster is not one of the archive's three target categories.
- **Fuzzie Germanium Fuzz v2:** the official page calls it a guitar pedal offering tones from mild distortion to dynamic fuzz and specifically describes its fuzz character, germanium stages, and fuzz control. It is retained under Fuzz. The description of mild distortion is treated as a range within the fuzz product, not as a separate distortion identity.
- **Pandora Octave Fuzz:** Sitek explicitly calls Pandora a high-gain fuzz pedal. Its increased gain can saturate distortion and its upper octave becomes more prominent at higher settings. It is retained under Fuzz only because the manufacturer's product identity is explicitly fuzz rather than a separately named distortion pedal.
- **Wuffy Fuzzy Distortion:** Sitek explicitly identifies Wuffy as a high-gain distortion pedal whose range crosses the borderline of distortion and fuzz. The official catalog places it in Distortion / Fuzz and the product page carries both distortion and fuzz tags. It is therefore retained in both Distortion and Fuzz.
- **Cocoa Distortion:** the official catalog identifies Cocoa as a Distortion pedal. It is retained under Distortion. No fuzz or overdrive category is inferred merely from gain behavior.

## Explicit exclusions / restraint
- **Caffeine Dirty Booster v2:** excluded from the target census because the manufacturer presents it as a dirty booster rather than an explicitly named overdrive, distortion, or fuzz pedal.
- **Vixen Germanium Booster:** excluded as a booster.
- **Nursie Transparent Booster:** excluded as a booster.
- **Cristal Signal Buffer:** excluded as a buffer.
- **Phasia Phase Shifter:** excluded as modulation.
- **Version naming:** Fuzzie Germanium Fuzz v2 is treated as the current product identity shown by Sitek, not as a separate historical pedal from the original Fuzzie name.
- **No category inflation:** descriptive references to distortion, clipping, saturation, or drive inside a fuzz/overdrive product do not automatically create additional category entries unless the manufacturer explicitly establishes that target function as part of the product identity.
