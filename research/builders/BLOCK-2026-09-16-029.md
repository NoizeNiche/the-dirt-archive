# The Dirt Archive — Builder → Pedals Research Block 029

Date: September 16, 2026
Scope: Builder → Pedals only

This block records builder/product relationships only. Product names are taken from current manufacturer catalogs and official product pages, with official discontinued-product material used for legacy identities. This is a research checkpoint, not an exhaustive lifetime catalog.

Builder / brand: Origin Effects

- Halcyon Gold Overdrive
- Halcyon Green Overdrive
- M-EQ Driver
- DCX Boost — boost/drive product with overdrive use
- RevivalDRIVE Compact — amp-recreation overdrive / distortion
- RD COMPACT Hot Rod — amp-recreation high-gain drive
- DELUXE61 Amp Tremolo & Drive
- MAGMA57 Amp Vibrato & Drive
- Deluxe55 Tweed Recreation

### Bass / amp-recreation dirt identities
- Bassrig Fifteen
- Bassrig Super Vintage
- Bassrig '64 Black Panel

### Discontinued / legacy dirt identities verified from the official Origin Effects archive
- RevivalDRIVE — amp-recreation drive/distortion
- RevivalDRIVE Hot Rod Edition — high-gain amp-recreation drive/distortion

Notes:
- Origin Effects' current official Overdrive category explicitly lists Halcyon Blue, Gold, and Green, M-EQ Driver, DCX Boost, RevivalDRIVE Compact, RD COMPACT Hot Rod, DELUXE61, MAGMA57, and Deluxe55 as overdrive/amp-recreation products.
- The manufacturer describes its Amp Recreation pedals as all-analogue amp-modeling pedals that recreate preamp, phase inverter, rectifier, and power-amp behavior; the current 2026 lineup includes RevivalDRIVE Compact, RD COMPACT Hot Rod, DELUXE61, MAGMA57, and Deluxe55.
- Bassrig products are retained only as dirt-relevant amp-recreation identities where the manufacturer's catalog places them in its overdrive/amp-recreation product family. No attempt is made to infer exact distortion behavior beyond the manufacturer's catalog framing.
- Origin's official discontinued archive lists the full-size RevivalDRIVE and RevivalDRIVE Hot Rod Edition as discontinued products. They remain valid builder/product relationships for the archive.
- Origin's own 2023 Q&A states that the company did not have plans at that time to reissue its large-format Cali76 compressor pedals and separately discusses fuzz as outside its existing lineup. No unsupported Origin fuzz product is added here.
- Compressor products are excluded even where the manufacturer notes that extreme settings can produce distortion; the active census is for products whose catalog identity is OD/drive/distortion/fuzz.

## 2. Vertex Effects

Builder / brand: Vertex Effects

### Current / catalog dirt products verified from the official Vertex store and product pages
- Ultraphonix MKII — overdrive
- Ultra-Phonix HRM — overdrive
- Steel String Supreme (Slight Return) — overdrive / clean-drive product
- Steel String (Slight Return) — overdrive / clean-drive product identity
- Steel String Clean Drive MKI — overdrive
- Tone Secret OD — overdrive
- T Drive — overdrive / amp-style drive
- Dynamic Distortion MKI — distortion / hybrid OD-fuzz-distortion
- Dynamic Dist. MKII — distortion / hybrid OD-fuzz-distortion

Notes:
- Vertex's official store currently lists Ultraphonix MKII, Steel String Supreme (Slight Return), Ultra-Phonix HRM, Tone Secret OD, T Drive, Steel String Clean Drive MKI, and Dynamic Distortion MKI among its pedal products; the store also lists the Dynamic Dist. MKII limited edition.
- The official Dynamic Distortion MKI product page explicitly describes its gain range as clean, overdrive, and fuzz, while identifying the overall product as a hybrid of Germanium Fuzz and TS-style overdrive.
- The official Dynamic Distortion MKII page describes the same hybrid product family and identifies it as a limited 250-piece edition.
- Vertex's own catalog and product pages identify Ultraphonix and Tone Secret as overdrive products; Steel String products are cataloged as drive products.
- Cosmetic gold/gray/red listings in the store are not treated as separate identities unless the manufacturer gives them a distinct product name. Slight Return and MKI/MKII names are retained when Vertex presents them as separate named catalog products.
- Non-dirt products such as volume pedals, compressors, buffers, and board accessories are excluded.
