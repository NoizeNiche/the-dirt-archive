# Block 085 — Fender

## Builder
**Fender Musical Instruments Corporation / Fender**

## Verification basis
Primary and authoritative sources reviewed:
- Fender official current Effects Pedals support/catalog
- Fender official current individual product pages for Santa Ana V2, Pugilist V2, The Pelt V2, Full Moon Distortion, MTG Tube Distortion, Smolder Acoustic Overdrive, The Trapper Dual Fuzz, Hammertone Overdrive, Hammertone Distortion, Hammertone Metal, Hammertone Fuzz, and Fender Shields Blender
- Fender official archive of Effects Pedals
- Fender official historical effects article covering Fender effects from the 1950s–1980s
- Fender official historical/news material describing the 2013 Competition Series and the later original Fender effects line
- active-repository search for “Fender” before addition

The active repository contained no prior Fender match before this block was added. Fender is treated as the builder identity for Fender-branded dirt pedals that Fender itself documents as Fender-built/designed or as Fender's own historical effects. The block consolidates ordinary product revisions such as V2 under their underlying product identity rather than inflating the census.

The reviewed material supports a substantial Fender dirt catalog spanning historical Fuzz/Distortion/Drive products, the 2012–2019 Yngwie Malmsteen Overdrive, the 2013 Competition-series Drive and Distortion, the 2018-forward Fender-designed pedal family, the Hammertone series, and the Fender Shields Blender collaboration.

## Overdrive
Current / manufacturer-supported identities:
- Santa Ana Overdrive
- Smolder Acoustic Overdrive
- Hammertone Overdrive

Historical / manufacturer-supported identities:
- Fender Drive Pedal
- Yngwie Malmsteen Overdrive Pedal

Verification notes:
- **Santa Ana Overdrive** is explicitly identified by Fender as a JFET-based class-A overdrive pedal. The current V2 is consolidated under the underlying Santa Ana Overdrive identity.
- **Smolder Acoustic Overdrive** is explicitly named and presented by Fender as an overdrive pedal for acoustic instruments. Its Drive control's distortion range does not create a second named Distortion identity.
- **Hammertone Overdrive** is explicitly identified by Fender as an analog overdrive circuit and remains Overdrive only.
- **Fender Drive Pedal** is the historical Competition-series drive product. Fender's 2013 release identifies it as creating warm overdrive and harmonic crunch.
- **Yngwie Malmsteen Overdrive Pedal** is retained from Fender's official archive as a historical Fender overdrive identity.

## Distortion
Current / manufacturer-supported identities:
- Pugilist Distortion
- Full Moon Distortion
- MTG Tube Distortion Pedal
- MTG:LA Tube Distortion
- Compugilist Compressor/Distortion
- Hammertone Distortion
- Hammertone Metal

Historical / manufacturer-supported identities:
- Fender Distortion Pedal
- Fender Blender

Verification notes:
- **Pugilist Distortion** is explicitly described by Fender as a distortion pedal with two independent distortion engines. The current V2 is consolidated under the Pugilist Distortion identity.
- **Full Moon Distortion** is explicitly described by Fender as a high-gain distortion effect pedal. Its switchable post-gain boost is secondary and does not create another target identity.
- **MTG Tube Distortion Pedal** and **MTG:LA Tube Distortion** are explicitly identified by Fender as tube-driven distortion pedals and remain Distortion only.
- **Compugilist Compressor/Distortion** is recorded as Distortion because Fender describes the unit as a combined compressor/distortion pedal with independently switchable circuits. Compression remains secondary.
- **Hammertone Distortion** and **Hammertone Metal** are explicitly identified by Fender as distortion circuits. Their lower-gain or high-gain operating ranges do not create additional Overdrive identities.
- **Fender Distortion Pedal** is retained from Fender's historical Competition-series archive and 2013 launch material, which explicitly describes the product as a distortion pedal.
- **Fender Blender** is recorded as Distortion because Fender's historical effects article explicitly calls the original Blender a distortion unit. Fender later used the Blender circuit as the basis for the Shields Blender fuzz product, but that does not retroactively change the original named product's archived category.

## Fuzz
Current / manufacturer-supported identities:
- The Pelt Fuzz
- The Trapper Dual Fuzz
- Hammertone Fuzz
- Fender Shields Blender

Historical / manufacturer-supported identities:
- Fender Fuzz Wah Pedal

Verification notes:
- **The Pelt Fuzz** is explicitly identified by Fender as a fuzz pedal. The current V2 is consolidated under The Pelt Fuzz identity.
- **The Trapper Dual Fuzz** is explicitly identified by Fender as a dual-fuzz pedal. Its two discrete fuzz voices, octave switch, and noise gate remain internal functions.
- **Hammertone Fuzz** is explicitly identified by Fender as a fuzz circuit. Its octave-fuzz switch remains secondary.
- **Fender Shields Blender** is explicitly presented by Fender as a signature fuzz pedal and current Fender product. Fender states that it is built around the vintage Fender Blender circuit and adds blendable octave/suboctave fuzz and reactive sag functions.
- **Fender Fuzz Wah Pedal** is retained from Fender's historical archive and historical effects article, which explicitly identify its fuzz and wah functions. It is recorded under Fuzz because the target dirt function is explicit.

## Category-overlap decisions
- **Santa Ana Overdrive**: Overdrive only. Explicit Fender product identity is overdrive.
- **Smolder Acoustic Overdrive**: Overdrive only. Fender explicitly names it as an overdrive pedal; its Drive control and acoustic distortion application are operating range and context.
- **Hammertone Overdrive**: Overdrive only. Explicit Fender product identity is an overdrive circuit.
- **Pugilist Distortion**: Distortion only. Product-level Fender naming is explicit despite lead-drive tones within its range.
- **Full Moon Distortion**: Distortion only. Product-level Fender naming is explicit; onboard boost is secondary.
- **Compugilist Compressor/Distortion**: Distortion only. Fender explicitly identifies its distortion circuit; compressor remains secondary.
- **Hammertone Distortion**: Distortion only.
- **Hammertone Metal**: Distortion only.
- **Fender Blender**: Distortion only. Fender's historical article explicitly labels the original unit a distortion effect.
- **The Pelt Fuzz**: Fuzz only.
- **The Trapper Dual Fuzz**: Fuzz only.
- **Hammertone Fuzz**: Fuzz only.
- **Fender Shields Blender**: Fuzz only. Fender explicitly calls it a signature fuzz pedal; octave/suboctave and sag features remain secondary.
- **Fender Fuzz Wah Pedal**: Fuzz only for this taxonomy. Wah is a secondary non-target effect.

## Historical / variant handling
- **Santa Ana Overdrive V2**, **Pugilist Distortion V2**, and **The Pelt V2 Fuzz** are consolidated with their underlying Santa Ana, Pugilist, and Pelt identities rather than treated as separate products.
- **Fender Blender Custom** is not promoted as a separate underlying dirt identity. Fender's archive places it as a later Fender Blender product/variant, so it is consolidated under Fender Blender for the initial census.
- **Fender Shields Blender** remains a distinct named product from the historical Fender Blender because Fender presents it as a new signature pedal with a different product identity, even though it is explicitly based on the vintage Blender circuit.
- Fender's 2018/2019 original pedal line and the Hammertone series are retained as separate named product families where Fender gives them distinct product names.
- Cosmetic editions, V2 revisions, and hardware/presentation variants do not create additional dirt identities.

## Exclusions
- Fender's 1986–1987 imported effects that merely carried the Fender name are not promoted when Fender's own historical article states that they were not actually built by Fender. This keeps builder attribution strict.
- Engager Boost and other boost-only products are excluded even when they can push an amplifier into breakup.
- Compression, buffer, wah, modulation, delay, reverb, filter, tuner, and other non-dirt products are outside target scope.
- Tone Master Pro, Mustang, and other multi-effects/modeling products are not mined for virtual effect-model identities.
- Generic distortion language inside an explicitly named overdrive or fuzz product does not create a second catalog identity unless Fender provides product-level support for the additional target category.
- Cloning, schematics, components, internal construction, and reverse-engineering material are outside scope.
