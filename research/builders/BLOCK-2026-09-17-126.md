# The Dirt Archive - Builder Census Block 126

## Builder
**Friedman Amplification**

Friedman Amplification is a previously uncovered builder with a clearly documented current Overdrive catalog plus historical high-voltage tube dirt pedals. The current official pedal shop lists BE-OD, BE-OD Deluxe, Dirty Shirley, Golden Pearl, and Smallbox as pedal products, while Friedman materials also document the discontinued Motor City Drive and Fuzz Fiend. citeturn824366search4turn824366search1turn824366search5turn824366search3turn824366search6

## Scope result
This block records named Friedman pedals whose builder-facing identity clearly establishes Overdrive or Fuzz. No separate Distortion category is established in this block because Friedman's own product pages consistently present the BE-OD family and related high-gain pedals as Overdrive products, even though third-party descriptions sometimes call them distortion. The archive follows the builder's named identity rather than generic descriptions of available gain.

## Overdrive
- **BE-OD** - current; explicitly described by Friedman as an Overdrive pedal based on the BE-100 amplifier.
- **BE-OD Deluxe** - current; dual-channel Overdrive built from the original BE-OD circuit with a lower-gain second channel.
- **Dirty Shirley Pedal** - current; explicitly named and described by Friedman as an Overdrive pedal.
- **Golden Pearl** - current; explicitly described as a transparent, lower-gain Overdrive.
- **Smallbox Overdrive Pedal** - current; explicitly named and described by Friedman as an Overdrive pedal.
- **Motor City Drive** - historical/discontinued; documented as a tube-driven Overdrive pedal using a high-voltage 12AX7A preamp tube.

## Distortion
- **None established** - no distinct Friedman pedal reviewed for this block meets the archive's explicit Distortion threshold under the conservative builder-label rule.

## Fuzz
- **Fuzz Fiend** - historical/discontinued; Friedman's official demo explicitly describes it as a 12AX7 tube fuzz pedal with a range from light fuzz to extreme saturated mayhem.

## Explicit category overlaps
- **None established** - the reviewed Friedman dirt products are kept in their documented primary target categories rather than assigning additional categories from generic high-gain language.

## Historical / variant restraint
- **BE-OD / BE-OD Deluxe** remain Overdrive entries. Friedman's official descriptions call both products Overdrive and emphasize British overdrive tones, even where higher settings become highly saturated. citeturn824366search1turn824366search5
- **Dirty Shirley** remains Overdrive only. Friedman explicitly calls it the Dirty Shirley Overdrive pedal and describes its range from cleaner pushing tones through gritty drive. citeturn824366search3
- **Golden Pearl** remains Overdrive only. Its documented role is transparent lower-gain overdrive, even though its tri-mode clipping switch changes the feel and clipping response. citeturn824366search6
- **Smallbox Overdrive** remains Overdrive only. Friedman's product page explicitly calls it an Overdrive pedal and describes a range from gritty Plexi-style tones through high gain. citeturn824366search2
- **Motor City Drive** remains Overdrive only. Historical documentation identifies it as a tube-driven overdrive from light breakup through higher-gain drive; generic references to saturation and distortion do not create a second target category. citeturn288471search2turn288471search3
- **Fuzz Fiend** remains Fuzz only. Friedman's official demo explicitly identifies it as a tube fuzz pedal and describes the RAGE switch as extending the fuzz into unstable, extreme behavior. citeturn288471youtube37
- **Sir Compre** is excluded even though its title and secondary descriptions can mention overdrive, because its primary product identity is a compressor rather than a standalone dirt pedal.
- **Buxom Boost** is excluded as a boost-first product.
- **Friedman IR-D, IR-J, and IR-X** are excluded as amp/preamp/IR products rather than standalone target dirt pedals.
- **Gold-72 Wah** is excluded as a wah product.
- Routine editions, finish changes, and standard packaging differences are not multiplied into separate dirt identities.
- No components, schematics, cloning, PCB, BOM, or internal-build information is recorded in this block.

## Research note
Friedman is useful to the archive because its pedal range shows a consistent amp-derived approach to dirt while maintaining unusually explicit Overdrive naming. The BE-OD family, Dirty Shirley, Golden Pearl, Smallbox, and Motor City Drive all remain Overdrive entries under the builder-label rule. Fuzz Fiend is the clear dedicated Fuzz identity. The block intentionally leaves Distortion empty rather than converting third-party use of the word distortion into a Friedman product category.
