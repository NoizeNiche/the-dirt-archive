Notes:
- JAM's manufacturer catalog separates guitar pedals into overdrive/distortion, fuzz, boost/compression, tremolo, modulation, delay, wah/filter, and other categories.
- The official catalog identifies TubeDreamer and Lucydreamer as overdrives, Double Dreamer as a dual overdrive, Rattler as distortion, Red Muck as fuzz-distortion, Octaurus as octafuzz, Fuzz Phrase Si as fuzz, and Eureka! as fuzz.
- The manufacturer's current catalog presents Rattler ltd separately as a distortion product with a low-gain stage; it is retained as a named catalog identity without deeper normalization.
- Tiny Pond? is not retained because the current catalog does not provide enough evidence from the manufacturer that it belongs to the OD/distortion/fuzz scope.
- JAM's catalog also includes Bass variants of several dirt products. They are retained where the manufacturer presents them as distinct named pedal identities.
