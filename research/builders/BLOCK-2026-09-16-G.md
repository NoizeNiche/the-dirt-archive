## Brady Tone Electronics
**Status:** candidate

The current official site identifies Brady Tone Electronics as a one-person boutique pedal project in Northeast Ohio with dedicated fuzz/distortion and overdrive categories. The homepage supports builder identity, but the immediately surfaced product evidence was insufficient in this pass to establish a clean multi-pedal dirt catalog without further model-level checking.

Do not promote Brady Tone to fully cataloged status yet. Keep as a research candidate and return for product-level verification.

**Source:**
- https://www.bradytone.com/

## Research Notes

- Menatone is a useful bridge between the late-1990s boutique wave and current small-batch production. Its official pages make the multi-pedal relationship unusually easy to verify without deep historical research.
- Amptweaker is valuable for the 2000s–current high-gain branch of the dirt-pedal story. The archive should preserve it as a builder with multiple named dirt products rather than reducing the line to one representative model.
- Brady Tone stays provisional until the product catalog can be established from first-party pages. Category labels alone are not enough for a complete model list.
- No internal pedal imagery, component analysis, schematics, or variant rabbit holes were pursued in this block.

## Governance Check

This block stays within Builder → Pedals → Photos → Information. It records builder identity and dirt products only, keeps same-builder variants consolidated for first-pass purposes, and does not merge separate recognized builders merely because product concepts may overlap.
