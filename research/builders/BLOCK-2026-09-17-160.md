## Boorocks

- KUMI Distortion AK-1 — Distortion

- LEON Multi-Driver MD-1 — Overdrive
- LEON Multi-Driver MD-1 — Distortion

Boorocks is a distinct Japanese effects brand. - RRP-1.0 Rock'n Roll Party — Overdrive

The Boot-Leg catalog contains several clearly documented drive pedals. Source: Effects Database Boot-Leg catalog and individual product records.

## Deliberate exclusions / brand-identity checks

- Boredbrain Music: routing/patchbay products found, but no qualifying standalone dirt pedal.
- Boro: Enhance Box material did not establish a qualifying dirt product.
- Boscoe Music: documented products are preamps rather than clearly cataloged dirt pedals.
- Boston Engineering: OD-200, DS-100, and HM-100 are documented OEM products manufactured by Beta Aivin and distributed under Boston Engineering among other names. Under the archive's canonical-identity rule, this is not treated as a separate new builder; Beta Aivin remains the canonical identity.
- Boston/Boston Over Drive: available evidence ties the name to the same Japanese OEM lineage used for several distributor brands, so no new builder identity is created here.
- Boulder Sound: indexed products are filters/EQ rather than qualifying dirt effects.
- Boost Switch Pedals: boost-only scope, excluded.
- Booty-Que: Heart-Breaker was found as a product reference, but the reviewed material did not provide sufficiently explicit dirt classification for inclusion in this pass.
