# Builder -> Pedals Research Block 183

## Scope

Continued the C-section Builder -> Pedals census after Block 182 with dirt-only inclusion: overdrive, distortion, fuzz. Existing builders were checked against the canonical index before creating new identities. Utility, modulation, delay, routing, compressor-only, boost-only, and non-dirt products were excluded.

Where a cataloged name is primarily a brand or later commercial incarnation rather than the underlying manufacturing company, that relationship is explicitly documented instead of being silently treated as an unrelated builder.

## Evidence / classification notes

- Canned Monsters' current official site identifies the company as a boutique effects builder founded by Kaz, with Frenzy Moss Fuzz and Moonbark Overdrive MK2 as current featured dirt products. Effects Database independently catalogs Frenzy Moss as fuzz.
- Caswell Modern Electronics (CME) is documented in contemporary pedal coverage for the Boost 90 Clean Drive and Boost 98 Bass Drive; both are treated as overdrive records because the reviewed sources describe their drive function. The Effects Database archive also records the CME brand and its historical product entries.
- CastleRock is retained as a cataloged brand identity, but this block records the OEM relationship explicitly. Effects Database documents CastleRock Overdrive, CastleRock Distortion, Metal Driver, and Bass Overdrive; separate Effects Database research identifies CastleRock Overdrive as a Daphon-made OEM product sold under multiple brand names. It should therefore not be interpreted as proof that CastleRock designed or manufactured the underlying circuit.
- CBS-Arbiter is retained as a historical commercial brand within the Electronic Sounds/Gary Hurst lineage rather than being treated as an independent circuit-design company. Effects Database lists the 1975-1979 CBS-Arbiter series and its dirt products; historical references identify Gary Hurst as the designer and Electronic Sounds as the production source for the series. Doubler is treated here as Fuzz because the historical record identifies the corresponding Electronic Sounds/UFO Doubler as an octa-fuzz product. Fuzz Phazer and Wah-Fuzz-Swell are retained as fuzz-bearing dirt products.
- Celestial Effects is documented by its own site and Effects Database. Effects Database lists ten products and identifies the company as run by Domenic Mancini and Joe Grein in Hudson, Massachusetts. The official catalog identifies Aquarius, Aries, Cancer, Capricorn, Sagittarius, Taurus, Virgo, and Scorpio. Scorpio is excluded from this block as a boost/preamp record rather than forcing the archive to reinterpret a boost-led product; Gemini VTR and other non-dirt functions are excluded. Cancer is recorded as both Fuzz and Overdrive because its documented internal effects include octave fuzz/muscle fuzz and tube overdrive. Aries and Capricorn are recorded as both Distortion and Overdrive because their manufacturer descriptions explicitly place them across those dirt categories.
- Celebrity Pedals is a current handmade analog builder. Its official site identifies Fuzztin Bieber as fuzz and the newer Fuzzbulla as fuzz; current product/news material also documents Adam Driver as overdrive and Distorsean Paul as distortion. Guitar.com independently documents Djent Reznor as an overdrive-style BB Preamp/Tube Screamer combination. Octave, wah, filter, compressor, and bit-crusher products are excluded.
- Coffin Gear / Coffin Case is kept as one canonical identity because the documented pedal line is branded Coffin Case/Coffin Gear under the same Jonny Coffin company lineage. Premier Guitar documents the Blood Drive and Batula as Coffin Case/Coffin pedals, with the Blood Drive manufactured in the USA for the brand and the Batula as a limited hand-cast, hand-wired fuzz. Effects Database catalogs both dirt products.
- Coggins Audio / Dinosaural is a single builder lineage because Coggins Audio's current site explicitly describes itself as the later incarnation of the Dinosaural brand and identifies Dan Coggins as its builder/designer. The current site documents Cogmeister, Hypoid Drive, Dan Solo, and the discontinued Dinosaural products; Effects Database separately documents the Dinosaural catalog. Hypoid is recorded across Overdrive/Fuzz/Distortion because current and recent coverage describes its range from boost/drive through fuzz-flavoured distortion. Cogmeister is likewise recorded across the three dirt categories based on the current Coggins description of the triple gain-stager.
- Colombo Audio Electronics is a current Italian handmade builder. Its official catalog lists the dirt-bearing products recorded in this block. Effects Database independently confirms the Colombo Over Drive as an active 2026 catalog product. Products centered on chorus, compressor, wah, or other non-dirt functions are excluded.

## Exclusions / non-additions

- Canned Monsters products other than the two current featured dirt products were not asserted without stronger catalog evidence.
- Caswell's Pacific Switcher and other utility products are excluded; Tahiti Terror could not be confidently established as a pedal from the reviewed evidence.
- CastleRock is included as a branded OEM identity with the manufacturing caveat above; no separate Daphon identity is created here because that manufacturer relationship is a separate historical lineage question.
- CBS-Arbiter Power Driver is excluded as boost-only; CBS-Arbiter Sustain is a compressor/sustain product; Wah Wah and Wah-Swell are non-dirt effects.
- Celestial Scorpio is excluded as a boost/preamp-led record; Gemini VTR and the Librarian are not added without a dirt-primary classification.
- Celebrity Pedals octave/filter/wah/bit-crusher/compressor products are excluded from the dirt census.
- Coffin Case guitar cases, bags, and accessories are excluded.
- Coggins Audio's 3rd Law Treble Booster is excluded as boost-only; Thorpy collaborations are not reassigned to Coggins Audio when they belong to ThorpyFX.
- Colombo Audio Electronics modulation/compressor/chorus products are excluded.
