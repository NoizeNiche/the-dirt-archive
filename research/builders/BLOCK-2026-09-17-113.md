# The Dirt Archive — Builder Census Block 113

## Builder
**BlackOutEffectors**

The surviving BlackOutEffectors site identifies the company as a boutique guitar-effects manufacturer founded in 2008, with the company beginning in Vancouver and moving to Asheville in 2009. The historical catalog is strongly associated with founder/designer Kyle Tompkins. The current site is best treated as a surviving reference/catalog rather than evidence of a continuously operating production line.

## Scope result
BlackOutEffectors is a previously uncovered builder with a concentrated but important dirt catalog. Its strongest identity is fuzz, with a smaller overdrive footprint. The builder's own catalog explicitly labels Mantra as Overdrive and describes Fix'd Fuzz products as fuzz devices that also contain boost/overdrive stages. The surviving catalog does **not** establish a distinct BlackOutEffectors Distortion product identity strongly enough for this conservative census, so no standalone Distortion entry is created.

Normal revisions and closely related limited editions are consolidated where they represent the same underlying named pedal lineage.

## Overdrive
- **Mantra Overdrive** — historical / established catalog product
- **Fix'd Fuzz / Fix'd Fuzz V3** — fuzz-based hybrid with an explicit light-overdrive range
- **Fix'd Fuzz Deluxe** — historical / deluxe hybrid; builder explicitly describes boost + overdrive alongside fuzz

## Distortion
- **None established under the archive's conservative rule.**

BlackOutEffectors products can produce aggressive, heavily clipped tones, and outside databases sometimes place pedals such as Musket or Blunderbuss in distortion categories. That is not enough here to create a separate Distortion identity when BlackOutEffectors itself presents those products as fuzz designs.

## Fuzz
- **Fix'd Fuzz / Fix'd Fuzz V3** — historical/current catalog lineage
- **Fix'd Fuzz Deluxe** — historical / deluxe hybrid
- **Musket Fuzz / Musket Fuzz V2** — historical/current catalog lineage
- **FUBÄR Fuzz** — historical / limited
- **Blunderbuss Fuzz** — historical / special-run hybrid fuzz
- **Twosome Dual Fuzz** — historical; dual fuzz combining the Musket and Fix'd Fuzz Deluxe concepts
- **Crystal Dagger** — historical; octave-fuzz section combined with ring modulation / phasing functions
- **Fuzz Bear** — historical / limited

## Explicit category overlaps
- **Fix'd Fuzz / V3:** Fuzz + Overdrive. BlackOutEffectors calls the pedal a handmade fuzz box with boost and overdrive capability, and the surviving description says the stages can be individually engaged or bypassed.
- **Fix'd Fuzz Deluxe:** Fuzz + Overdrive. BlackOutEffectors describes it as a hydra of handmade fuzz boxes, boosts, and overdrives.
- **Mantra:** Overdrive only. BlackOutEffectors explicitly titles it Mantra Overdrive and describes a range from refined low-gain overdrive through aggressive drive.
- **Musket:** Fuzz only. BlackOutEffectors explicitly calls it a highly tweakable, fuzzed-up version of the vintage Muff family. The archive does not promote the pedal to Distortion merely because external databases sometimes use a broader distortion category.
- **Blunderbuss:** Fuzz only. BlackOutEffectors calls it a Fuzz, even though some secondary catalogs place it under distortion as well. The builder's own identity wins under the archive rule.
- **Crystal Dagger:** Fuzz only. The product combines octave fuzz with ring modulation/phasing functions, but the target dirt identity remains fuzz.

## Historical/variant restraint
- **Fix'd Fuzz** and **Fix'd Fuzz V3** are treated as one product lineage. BlackOutEffectors' surviving catalog presents V3 as the current reference form, while specialist catalog records preserve the original named pedal.
- **Musket Fuzz** and **Musket Fuzz V2** are consolidated into one Musket lineage rather than counted as separate products.
- **Twosome Dual Fuzz** and its special/limited editions are treated as one underlying Twosome lineage rather than a separate entry for every graphics or dealer run. The important functional identity is the dual-fuzz combination of the Musket and Fix'd Fuzz Deluxe concepts.
- **Crystal Dagger** remains a Fuzz entry despite also being an octave/ring-mod/phaser hybrid. Those added modulation functions do not become separate dirt categories.
- **Gravity Boost** and other boost-first products are excluded. Sibling, Whetstone, and Cadavernous are excluded because they are modulation/reverb products rather than target dirt pedals.
- No components, schematics, cloning, PCB, BOM, or internal-build information is recorded in this block.

## Research note
BlackOutEffectors is useful precisely because the catalog is compact and very fuzz-heavy. The archive preserves the builder's own naming and functional language rather than forcing every aggressive clipping sound into a separate Distortion category. That keeps the census useful later when individual pedal records are built.
