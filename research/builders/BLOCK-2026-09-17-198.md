# Builder -> Pedals Research Block 198

## Scope

Continued the C-section Builder -> Pedals census with one new canonical builder. Inclusion remains dirt-only: overdrive, distortion, and fuzz.

## New canonical builder

### 472 — Caustic FX
- Snowstorm — Distortion

## Evidence / classification notes

- Caustic FX describes itself as a one-person guitar pedal company in Toronto, Ontario, Canada, building devices by hand.
- Caustic FX's Snowstorm product page explicitly describes Snowstorm as a white-noise drone/ring-modulated distortion pedal and says the signal is processed through multiple distortion algorithms.
- Snowstorm therefore qualifies for the archive's dirt taxonomy as Distortion.
- The Caustic FX home page lists Black Box, Snowstorm, Dub Siren, and Euclid. Black Box is presented as an open-source programmable guitar-pedal platform rather than a single fixed dirt circuit, so it is not counted as a separate pedal record in this block.
- No duplicate Caustic FX identity was found in the canonical builder index.

## Exclusions / non-additions

- Culler Amplification remains excluded because the current Effects Database brand record has no cataloged products and the reviewed historical listing is an attenuator rather than a dirt pedal.
- Cubist Effects remains excluded because its current Effects Database record has no cataloged products.
- CTCK remains excluded because its documented product is a compression/sustainer, outside the dirt taxonomy.
- Curetronic.de remains excluded because its documented catalog is modular/synth hardware rather than a dirt-pedal builder.
- Cruze remains excluded because the documented DLY-2 is a delay and the historical brand is associated with PSK OEM production.
- Champion's B&M Champion fuzz products are already represented under the canonical Colorsound / Sola Sound identity (ID 348), so no duplicate builder identity is created.
