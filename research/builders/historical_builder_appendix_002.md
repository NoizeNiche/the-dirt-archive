## Research cautions

- Do not convert every generation, finish, anniversary edition, or component revision into a first-pass public product record.
- Do not merge recognized products from different builders because the names are shared or the circuits are related.
- Keep exterior-photo sourcing for the later photo phase.
- Preserve disputed or uncertain builder/product relationships as `needs-review` rather than guessing.

## Source principle

First-party manufacturer catalogs are preferred when available. Specialist historical catalogs and reference sites are useful for discontinued products and chronology, but they remain research evidence rather than automatic publication authority.
