## Bruce Bennett Music Labs
- Brown Sound — Distortion
- Brown Sound — Overdrive
- Lowball — Distortion

Brown Sound is explicitly categorized as both distortion and overdrive. Lowball is documented as a powerful octave-distortion design, so the archive retains its primary dirt classification as distortion. Boost-E-A is a booster and the Tejas Trem Clone / Trema-Dona are tremolo effects, so they are excluded. Insane Gain is retained as a catalog lead but not entered here because the reviewed material did not establish a sufficiently explicit dirt type. Sources: https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/brucebennettmusiclabs ; https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/brucebennettmusiclabs/brownsound ; https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/brucebennettmusiclabs/lowball
