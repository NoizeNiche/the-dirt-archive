## Electro-Harmonix
**Status:** builder-confirmed / catalog-in-progress

## Jordan Electronics
**Status:** builder-confirmed / catalog-in-progress

Jordan Electronics emerged in the mid-1960s from Victoreen Instruments and entered the musical market with transistorized guitar products and the plug-in Boss Tone. JHS's historical account identifies the Boss Tone/Model 1000 and the Vico Vibe/Model 2000 as separate Jordan designs, with the Boss Tone remaining in production into the early 1970s. These products are historically important and fit the archive's early-period builder coverage.

**Dirt products identified from available historical sources:**
- Boss Tone / Model 1000
- Vico Vibe / Model 2000

Jordan's product history is unusually tangled with later licensed or copied versions. Keep the Jordan Electronics builder identity separate from later reissues and from other recognized builders. Model/version detail should stay in research until individual-pedal review.

**Sources:**
- https://jhspedals.info/blogs/news/the-legacy-of-jordan-electronics
- https://www.guitarworld.com/gear/guitar-pedals/the-history-of-the-jordan-boss-tone-fuzz-unit
- https://hewittsgaragestudio.com/fuzz-timeline.php

## fOXX / Ridinger Associates
**Status:** builder-confirmed / catalog-in-progress

fOXX was a Southern California pedal line associated with Steve Ridinger and Ridinger Associates in the early 1970s. Historical sources document multiple dirt-oriented products, including the Tone Machine and Fuzz & Wah & Volume, while the Tone Machine itself became the defining fOXX product and appeared under several labels in later production relationships.

**Dirt products identified from historical sources:**
- fOXX Tone Machine
- fOXX Fuzz & Wah & Volume
- fOXX Down Machine

The archive should preserve fOXX as a distinct builder identity. Do not merge fOXX Tone Machine records into Danelectro's later recreations or into other builders' similarly branded products. Alternate labels and later relationships belong in research metadata.

**Sources:**
- https://www.tonehome.de/foxx/
- https://www.tonehome.de/foxx/tone-machine/
- https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/foxx/tonemachine

## Research Notes

- Electro-Harmonix substantially strengthens coverage beginning in 1968 and continues into the current day, with a large and well-documented dirt catalog.
- BOSS fills the major 1970s–current compact-pedal branch and should be mapped as a builder with many distinct dirt models rather than reduced to a few iconic pedals.
- Jordan Electronics provides a valuable mid-1960s historical bridge and should remain distinct from later Boss Tone copies and reissues.
- fOXX adds early-1970s California boutique/independent coverage and should remain distinct from modern recreations and other recognized builders.
- No internal pedal imagery, component analysis, schematics, PCB evidence, or exhaustive generation/variant research was pursued in this block.

## Governance Check

This block remains research/discovery only and follows Builder → Pedals → Photos → Information. It prioritizes builders with multiple dirt products, expands the 1960s–current span, keeps separate recognized builder/product identities separate, and defers exterior-photo collection and detailed variant work until the builder/product census is sufficiently mature.
