# The Dirt Archive — Builder Census Block 120

## Builder
**Greer Amps**

Greer Amps is a previously uncovered builder with a large dirt-focused catalog. Greer's own site presents its pedal line around drives, distortions, fuzzes, and boosts, and the current pedal catalog includes a mix of overdrive, distortion, and fuzz products. Greer's own description of the company says its focus is distortion broadly understood to include overdrive and fuzz, so this block uses the archive's narrower target taxonomy and only assigns a product to multiple target categories when the product identity or authoritative documentation supports it.

## Scope result
Greer has one of the more useful historical/current catalogs for this census because its documented products range from low-gain overdrive through high-gain distortion and dedicated fuzz designs. Current products are recorded alongside clearly documented historical products, while routine graphics, ordinary revisions, and boost-first devices are not multiplied into separate dirt identities.

## Explicit category overlaps
- **Peacemaker:** Overdrive + Distortion. Greer's current product description directly calls it a drive-meets-distortion design.
- **Ghetto Stomp:** Overdrive + Distortion + Fuzz. Greer's historical product documentation describes natural amp-breakup overdrive, while authoritative cataloging explicitly places it in the fuzz/distortion range.
- **No.9 Drive and Distortion:** Overdrive + Distortion. The product's own name explicitly joins drive and distortion, and Greer's catalog places it in both drive and distortions/fuzzes collections.
- **Little Samson:** Overdrive + Distortion. Greer's documentation describes chunky drive at lower gain and identifies it as a higher-gain distortion pedal.
- **Fuzzy Wooly Bear:** Overdrive + Fuzz. Authoritative cataloging explicitly calls it a fuzz/overdrive pedal.
- **Batch O' Ass:** Distortion + Fuzz. Contemporary product documentation describes it as distortion bordering on fuzz.

## Historical / variant restraint
- **Greer's current catalog and older sitemap** are both used. Current products are not assumed to have existed in exactly their present form historically, and historical products are labeled as such where the evidence is clear.
- **Ghetto Stomp** is kept as one lineage rather than splitting the older hand-painted and later powder-coated versions; Greer's own 2014 release notice describes those as versions of the same pedal.
- **Super Hornet** remains one fuzz identity; its octave function is not promoted into a separate target category.
- **Fuzzy Wooly Bear** is one product identity despite germanium/silicon ordering options.
- **Sucker Punch, Hammer, Green Giant, Little Samson, and older Greer products** are retained as historical products where authoritative documentation identifies them as distinct named pedals.
- **Moonshot Germanium Pre, Sonic Boom, Sure Shot Clipping Boost, and Special Request** remain outside the dirt census as boost/preamp-first products even when they can push an amplifier into more saturation.
- **Third-party collaborations** are retained only where Greer is explicitly the pedal builder and the product has a distinct named identity, as with Little Samson / Elliott Guitars.
- **Routine finish changes, custom colors, and ordinary enclosure revisions** are not counted as separate product identities.
- No components, schematics, cloning, PCB, BOM, or internal-build information is recorded in this block.

## Research note
Greer is especially useful to the archive because the builder's catalog makes the transition from overdrive to distortion to fuzz unusually visible. The cleanest hybrid cases are Ghetto Stomp, Peacemaker, Little Samson, Fuzzy Wooly Bear, Batch O' Ass, and No.9 Drive and Distortion. The archive still avoids turning every high-gain or boost-like operating range into another category.
