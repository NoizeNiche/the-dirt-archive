# The Dirt Archive — Historical Builder Research Batch 03

Status: research-stage appendix, not production catalog.

## Pearl

**Research state:** builder-confirmed / catalog-in-progress

**Dirt products located:**
- CF-1 Cry Fuzz
- DS-06 Distortion
- OD-05 Overdrive

**Evidence:** Effects Database and period-catalog references identify Pearl as a Japanese effects brand with a Sound Spice series containing the DS-06 Distortion and OD-05 Overdrive, while collector references document the CF-1 Cry Fuzz. The period Sound Spice documentation describes the series as an original Pearl design family.

**Sources:**
- https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/pearl/soundspice
- https://www.guitar-list.com/brands/pearl
- https://wahwah.fandom.com/wiki/Pearl

## Selmer

**Research state:** builder-confirmed / needs catalog expansion

**Dirt products located:**
- Buzz-Tone
- Fuzz-Wah

**Evidence:** The Selmer historical archive documents the mid-1960s Buzz-Tone as an early solid-state distortion unit and the 1967-71 Fuzz-Wah as a cataloged fuzz/wah pedal introduced during the late 1960s.

**Source:**
- https://www.vintagehofner.co.uk/gallery/gallery3/sel6.html

## Guyatone: stronger catalog confirmation

Guyatone already appears in the historical candidate pool. This pass provides stronger evidence that it should be treated as a substantial multi-pedal builder rather than a single-product historical entry. Catalog/archive material lists dirt across PS-series, Pro-series, Flip Tube, Tube, Micro, MightyMicro and later product families, including PS-011, PS-015, PS-016, PS-024, PS-025, PS-030, PS-031, PS-032, PS-040, OD-X, HM-X, MM-X, MM-1, PD-1, FZ-X, HTD-1, TD-1, TO-2, OD2, OD2+, HD2, HD3, HR2, TZ2, TZm5 and FZ-2000.

**Sources:**
- https://guitar-catalog.com/guitar-and-amp/guyatone/1980/en_index.html
- https://efs.jimdofree.com/2013/02/14/guyatone-effectspedal/

## Research observations

The historical record is already showing a useful pattern for the archive: some brands are represented by a compact number of clearly identifiable dirt models, while others have large catalog families spread over multiple decades. The first-pass census should preserve the builder/product pairing without attempting to reconstruct every corporate, OEM, or manufacturing connection.

Pearl is especially useful because the evidence supports more than one distinct dirt product while also showing a recognizable period catalog family. Selmer is a clean example of a historical builder with at least two independently identifiable dirt-oriented products.

No internal photographs, schematics, component details, PCB analysis, or exhaustive variant documentation was added.
