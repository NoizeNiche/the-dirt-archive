# Block 090 — Radial Engineering / Tonebone

## Builder
**Radial Engineering / Tonebone**

## Verification basis
Primary and authoritative sources reviewed:
- Radial Engineering official product pages and legacy-product archive
- Radial official pages for Tonebone Classic, Hot British, Trimode, Plexitube, Regency, Bones Texas, Bones London, Bones Hollywood, and Bassbone OD
- Radial official manuals / FAQs where product-level category language is explicit
- active-repository search for “Radial” and “Tonebone” before addition

The active repository contained no prior Radial or Tonebone match before this block was added. Radial Engineering and Tonebone are treated as one builder/product lineage because Tonebone is Radial's established guitar-pedal line and Radial's own legacy archive groups the later Bones pedals with their Tonebone predecessors.

The reviewed material supports a focused Radial overdrive/distortion catalog. No standalone Radial Fuzz identity with sufficiently strong builder-level evidence was found in the reviewed material.

## Overdrive
Current / archive-supported identities:
- Regency Overdrive and Boost
- Bones Texas Dual Mode Overdrive
- Bassbone OD
- Bones Hollywood Distortion & Overdrive

Historical / archive-supported identities:
- Tonebone Classic
- Tonebone Trimode

Verification notes:
- **Regency** is explicitly identified by Radial as an Overdrive and Boost pedal. Its pre-drive section is a dedicated overdrive circuit; the separate clean boost remains secondary and outside the target category.
- **Bones Texas** is explicitly presented by Radial as a Dual Mode Overdrive pedal with multiple overdrive voicings.
- **Bassbone OD** is explicitly identified by Radial as a Bass Preamp & Overdrive with a dedicated onboard overdrive section.
- **Bones Hollywood** is explicitly identified by Radial as a dual-mode Distortion & Overdrive pedal and therefore belongs in both target categories.
- **Tonebone Classic** is a tube distortion product whose gain range begins at slight overdrive and extends to high-gain saturation. Because Radial's primary product identity is Tube Distortion rather than Overdrive, it is not promoted into the main Overdrive category.
- **Tonebone Trimode** is likewise primarily identified by Radial as a dual-channel tube distortion pedal. Its rhythm channel provides overdrive-range operation, but the builder's primary product category is Distortion, so it is not separately promoted as Overdrive.

## Distortion
Current / archive-supported identities:
- Bones London Dual-Mode Distortion
- Bones Hollywood Distortion & Overdrive
- Hot-British V9
- Classic V9

Historical / archive-supported identities:
- Tonebone Classic
- Tonebone Hot British
- Tonebone Trimode
- Tonebone Plexitube

Verification notes:
- **Bones London** is explicitly identified by Radial as a Dual-Mode Distortion pedal and a solid-state version of the Tonebone Hot British.
- **Bones Hollywood** is explicitly identified by Radial as Distortion & Overdrive.
- **Hot British / Hot-British V9** is a high-gain Tonebone distortion lineage. Radial's original product is labeled Tube Distortion; the V9 is labeled Overdrive in the page title but described throughout as high-gain Plexi-style distortion. The archive keeps the lineage under Distortion rather than multiplying the V9 into an additional category.
- **Classic / Classic V9** belongs to the same Tonebone Classic distortion lineage. Radial identifies the original Classic as a Tube Distortion pedal, while its range extends from slight overdrive to high-gain saturation. The archive keeps the identity under Distortion.
- **Trimode** is explicitly described by Radial as a Tube Distortion pedal.
- **Plexitube** is explicitly described by Radial as a dual-channel Tube Distortion pedal with two distortion channels.

## Fuzz
- No standalone Radial/Tonebone fuzz pedal was established with sufficient product-level evidence in the reviewed authoritative material.

## Category-overlap decisions
- **Bones Hollywood**: Overdrive + Distortion. Radial explicitly names both functions in the product title and description.
- **Regency**: Overdrive only. The clean booster is a secondary utility and the builder's FAQ explicitly distinguishes the pedal as power booster + overdrive, not as a separate distortion product.
- **Bones Texas**: Overdrive only. Despite very high-gain settings, Radial explicitly classifies the product as Overdrive.
- **Bassbone OD**: Overdrive only. Its dedicated onboard dirt section is explicitly an overdrive circuit; the larger preamp/DI architecture is not treated as a separate product category.
- **Tonebone Classic / Trimode**: Distortion only. Their gain ranges enter overdrive territory, but Radial's primary product identities are tube distortion pedals.
- **Hot British / Plexitube / Bones London**: Distortion only. Their documented product identities are distortion-focused even where the controls permit lower-gain operation.

## Historical / variant handling
- **Classic V9** is consolidated into the **Tonebone Classic / Classic** product lineage rather than creating a separate underlying identity solely for the later solid-state V9 revision.
- **Hot-British V9** is consolidated into the **Tonebone Hot British** lineage rather than creating a second underlying product solely for the V9 generation.
- The later **Bones Hollywood** and **Bones London** are retained as distinct named products because Radial marketed them as separate compact designs rather than mere cosmetic revisions of the Tonebone pedals.
- Tonebone and Bones are treated as one continuous Radial pedal lineage for builder attribution.
- Product revisions, power-supply changes, and cosmetic differences are not multiplied into separate dirt identities unless Radial presented them as a distinct named design.

## Unpromoted / cautious leads
- Radial's broader product catalog contains many preamps, direct boxes, speaker/interface products, and amp-emulation devices with distortion-adjacent language. These were not promoted unless Radial explicitly identified the standalone product as an Overdrive, Distortion, or Fuzz pedal.
- **JDX Direct-Drive** is excluded because Radial describes it as an amp-emulation/direct interface product rather than a standalone target-category dirt pedal.

## Exclusions
- JDX Direct-Drive, JDX 500, and other amp-emulation/direct products are outside the target dirt taxonomy.
- PZ-Pre, PZ-Deluxe, AC-Driver, and other acoustic/instrument preamps are excluded when they are not explicitly standalone dirt products.
- DI boxes, switchers, buffers, boosters without a dedicated target-category dirt section, power products, and non-dirt effects are excluded.
- Cloning, schematics, components, internal construction, and reverse-engineering material are outside scope.
