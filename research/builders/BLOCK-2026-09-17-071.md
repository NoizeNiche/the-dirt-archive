# Block 071 — Maxon

## Builder
**Maxon / Nisshin Onpa**

## Verification basis
Primary manufacturer / authoritative sources reviewed:
- Maxon official effects catalog and product pages
- Nisshin Onpa / Maxon manufacturer documentation
- Godlyke's Maxon catalog and archived Maxon product documentation where manufacturer material was unavailable or no longer surfaced in the current Maxon site

The active repository contained no prior Maxon match before this block was added. Maxon is treated as a distinct builder/brand from Ibanez even where historically related products share designs or naming. This block records builder/product relationships only.

Dirt categories are based on explicit manufacturer or authoritative catalog titles and descriptions. Generic references to gain, clipping, saturation, or a product reaching another sound are not used to create an additional category unless the source explicitly identifies the product as overdrive, distortion, or fuzz.

Verification notes:
- **OD808** and **OD-9** are explicitly identified by Maxon as overdrive pedals.
- **OD820 Overdrive Pro+**, **OD808X**, **OD-9 Pro+**, and **OOD-9** are explicitly named as overdrive products.
- **OSD-9** is explicitly titled **Overdrive / Soft Distortion**, so it is retained in both Overdrive and Distortion below.
- **RTO700** is explicitly a Real Tube Overdrive.
- **ST9Pro+** is explicitly a Super Tube Overdrive.
- **TBO-9** is explicitly a tube booster / overdrive product.
- **TOD-9** is explicitly a True Tube Overdrive.
- **RTD800** is a dual-stage tube unit with independently described overdrive and distortion circuits; it is therefore retained in both categories.
- **ROD881** is explicitly documented as Real Overdrive / Distortion and is retained in both categories.
- Historical **ST-9**, **SD-9**, and **SM-9Pro** are retained as Maxon-named historical dirt products where the Maxon/Godlyke documentation explicitly connects them to overdrive or distortion functions. Their primary target categorization follows the explicit product naming, with overlap noted below where supported.

## Category-overlap decisions
- **OSD-9**: Overdrive + Distortion. Manufacturer title explicitly combines both.
- **RTD800**: Overdrive + Distortion. Manufacturer documentation describes independent overdrive and distortion circuits.
- **ROD881**: Overdrive + Distortion. Manufacturer naming explicitly combines the two target categories.
- **ST-9 / SD-9 / SM-9Pro**: category assignment follows explicit product naming. Historical Sonic Distortion / Super Metal products are not upgraded to Overdrive merely because they can produce driven sounds.
- **SD-9**: historical Distortion identity. The older unit's broader gain behavior does not by itself create an Overdrive identity.
- **TBO-9**: Overdrive only. Booster functionality is outside the target taxonomy, while Maxon explicitly identifies the pedal as booster / overdrive.
- **Fuzz Elements family**: Fuzz only. Distortion-like technical language does not create a second category without explicit manufacturer classification.
- **D&S / D&S II**: Distortion only in this census. Sustainer functionality does not create another target category.

## Historical / variant handling
- Maxon maintains current and legacy product families, and this block separates clearly named later revisions from original products where the manufacturer gives them distinct functional names.
- **OD-9 Pro+** remains separate from **OD-9** because the manufacturer assigns it a distinct product name and revised functionality.
- **OD808X** remains separate from **OD808** because it is a separately named product.
- **ST9Pro+** remains separate from historical **ST-9** because Maxon assigns the newer unit a distinct product identity.
- **SD-9M** remains separate from historical **SD-9** because the manufacturer gives it a distinct model name.
- **SM-9Pro+** remains separate from historical **SM-9** for the same reason.
- Cosmetic finishes, anniversary editions, and minor presentation changes are consolidated when they do not establish a distinct functional product identity.
- **Maxon vs. Ibanez**: Maxon products are recorded under Maxon here rather than merged into the earlier Ibanez block, even where historical lineage is shared. Same-name products across recognized builders remain separate identities under the archive's governance rule.
- Godlyke's Maxon pages are treated as authoritative archival references rather than evidence of a current 2026 U.S. sales relationship; Maxon's manufacturer identity remains the basis for builder attribution.

## Exclusions
- Boost-only Maxon products and clean booster functions are excluded from the target census unless the manufacturer explicitly labels the product as Overdrive, Distortion, or Fuzz.
- Modulation, delay, reverb, EQ, compressor, noise gate, interface, and other non-target products are excluded.
- Same-circuit or same-family products from Ibanez are not merged into Maxon merely because of historical lineage.
- Generic terms such as gain, saturation, clipping, or “distortion” used only as a technical or sonic description do not add another category.
