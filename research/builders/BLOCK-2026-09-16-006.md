## 1. Analog Man

Builder / brand: Analog Man

### Overdrive / Distortion
- King Of Tone
- Prince Of Tone

### Fuzz
- Sun Face
- Sun Lion — combined Sun Face fuzz + boost pedal
- Astro Tone Fuzz
- Peppermint Fuzz

Notes:
- Analog Man's official King Of Tone page identifies the King Of Tone as a hand-made USA overdrive and also references the Prince Of Tone as a related Analog Man product.
- The official Sun Face page identifies Sun Face and modified Fuzz Face products as Analog Man fuzz offerings.
- The official SunLion manual identifies the Sun Face as the right side of the combined pedal, establishing the Sun Lion's fuzz product relationship.
- The official Astro Tone page identifies the Astro Tone as a fuzz pedal and explicitly describes its sound as closer to fuzz than distortion.
- The official Peppermint Fuzz page identifies the product as a fuzz pedal and states that it is hand made in the USA.
- No transistor/component information is being carried into the archive at this stage, even when the manufacturer's pages mention it.
