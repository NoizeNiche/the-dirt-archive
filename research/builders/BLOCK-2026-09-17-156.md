## Research notes

- B.C. Rich DMD-1 is documented as a digital metal distortion pedal.
- B.K. Butler's original Tube Driver is explicitly identified as an overdrive pedal.
- B85 Audio's product list includes Bass Overdrive, Fuzz Machine, Germanium Overdrive, Germanium Overdrive Deluxe, Gut Ripper, and Hrom. The archive records the products under their documented dirt categories and excludes preamp/compressor-only products.
- Backbeat Electric X-Bender is documented as a fuzz/distortion box and is recorded as Fuzz.
- Bad Cat's 2-Tone and X-Treme Tone are tube preamp pedals with dirt functions. X-Treme Tone is recorded under both Overdrive and Distortion based on its documented two-channel operation.
- Bad Penny FX's Fuzz Controller exists in V1 and V2 forms. V1 is recorded as Fuzz and Overdrive; V2 is recorded as Fuzz. Wireless and Morse are documented fuzz pedals.
- Bad Pixel Pedals is a Greek builder whose documented ten-pedal range includes eight fuzz identities and one tube boost/overdrive. The boost-only Love Tube Boost is excluded.
- Baja Tech Custom's Bone Bender, Das Fuzz Ge, and Das Fuzz Si are fuzz products. DaMOAF spans overdrive, fuzz, and distortion, while Top Boost TB-1 is explicitly an overdrive rather than a boost-only device.
- Balaguer Guitars' BG-Drive is an overdrive/preamp, while Fission Drive v2 is documented as preamp overdrive/distortion and capable of fuzz textures; all three target categories are recorded under the same product identity.
- Baltimore Sonic Research Institute's dirt catalog includes distortion, fuzz, and overdrive products; non-dirt pedals are excluded.
- Baroni Lab's own pedal grouping places six products under Distortion and three under Overdrive; those named categories are preserved here.
- Barge Concepts BP-1 is recorded as a dirt product spanning distortion, fuzz, and overdrive. Boost-only Barge products are excluded.
- Bardic Audio Devices HM Demon is a distortion pedal. The Beast is a multi-dirt product and is recorded under Overdrive, Distortion, and Fuzz.
- BearFoot FX's dirt catalog includes the listed fuzz, distortion, and overdrive products. Boosters, EQs, compressors, and other non-dirt products are excluded.
- BECOS FX's Ziffer is the documented overdrive pedal in the current catalog.
- Beta Aivin BOD-1 and BOD-2 are bass overdrive pedals.
