# Builder -> Pedals Research Block 195

## Scope

Continued the C-section Builder -> Pedals census with an existing-builder expansion. No new canonical builder identity was required. Inclusion remains dirt-only: overdrive, distortion, and fuzz.

## Existing-builder expansion

### 311 — CMAT Mods
- Signa Comp-Drive — Overdrive
- Tube Slammer Deluxe — Overdrive

## Evidence / classification notes

- The CMAT Mods catalog contains 20 products. Seven dirt models were already present in the active census: Black Plague, Brownie, Butah, Ratified, Signa Drive, Super Signa Drive, and Tube Slammer.
- Two additional dirt models were missing and are added here: Signa Comp-Drive, explicitly classified as compressor + overdrive, and Tube Slammer Deluxe, explicitly classified as overdrive.
- Signa Comp-Drive is recorded only under Overdrive because the archive's dirt taxonomy does not include compressor as a separate category.
- The canonical builder index already contains CMAT Mods as ID 311 from Block 167, so no duplicate builder identity is created.

## Exclusions / non-additions

- Analog Chorus, Analog Phaser, Boost, Buffer, Compressor, Deluxe Compressor, Deluxe Signa Comp, Deeelay, Signa Comp, Tremoglo, and Waterbox remain outside the dirt census because their primary cataloged functions are not Fuzz, Overdrive, or Distortion.
- The seven previously cataloged CMAT Mods dirt models are not duplicated.
