## Electro-Harmonix
Status: builder-confirmed / catalog-in-progress

The current official EHX catalog exposes a broad dirt category and explicitly separates Fuzz, Overdrive, and Distortion. Current official pages show 29 fuzz, 13 overdrive, and 9 distortion products in the distortion/gain taxonomy, with products including Big Muff Pi 2, Deluxe Big Muff Pi 2, Bender Royale, Flatiron Fuzz, Satisfaction Plus, Hot Tubes, Crayon, Soul Food, OD Glove, Hell Melter, Metal Muff, and Pico Triboro Bridge. This supports EHX as a major multi-pedal dirt builder for the 1960s-current archive. Do not split every current enclosure/size/reissue variant in first pass.
Sources: https://www.ehx.com/category/pedals/gain-distortion/ ; https://shop.ehx.com/category/guitar-effects-pedals-overdrive-fuzz-distortion/

## Schaller Electronic
Status: builder-confirmed / catalog-in-progress

Vintage sources document Schaller's own Fuzz pedal in multiple production versions. ToneHome identifies the Schaller Fuzz as made in Germany, with a 1976 version, and notes the earlier/later construction changes. Effects Database identifies it as a mid-1960s-through-1970s West German fuzz. For first pass, retain the recognizable product as **Schaller Fuzz** and defer version/component distinctions.
Sources: https://www.tonehome.de/schaller-electronic/fuzz/ ; https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/schaller/fuzz

## Höfner
Status: builder-confirmed / needs catalog review

Höfner's vintage effects catalog includes multiple dirt-oriented products. Effects Database documents the **Höfner No. 539 Fuzz/Distortion** and **Höfner Z** wah-fuzz. ToneHome documents a late-1960s/early-1970s Höfner effects catalog and the German-made **Wha-Wha-Fuzz**. Because some Höfner-branded units were OEM products, keep Höfner as a distinct recognized builder/brand record where the catalog/product identity is explicitly Höfner-branded, while retaining OEM relationships for later deep dives.
Sources: https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/hofner/fuzz ; https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/hofner/z ; https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/hofner/whawhafuzz ; https://www.tonehome.de/h%C3%B6fner-hofner/

## Jen / JEN Elettronica
Status: builder-confirmed / catalog-in-progress

Vintage references identify JEN-branded fuzz products and multiple branded/export variants. Effects Database documents a JEN Fuzz identity through the Vox/JEN V828 family and a Jen Harmon Booster associated with branded variants. For the initial builder census, retain **JEN / JEN Elettronica** as a builder candidate with its own recognizable dirt products, but do not merge JEN-branded products with Vox or other separate recognized branded builder/product records. Cross-brand manufacturing relationships remain background material for deep dives.
Sources: https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/vox/thomas/tonebender ; https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/jen/harmonbooster ; https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/samash/harmonbooster

## Research distinction captured

A current official source can establish a large catalog efficiently, while vintage databases and period catalog material are useful for historical builder discovery. For cross-brand OEM situations, preserve separate recognized catalog identities in the first pass and defer manufacturing relationships. This follows the governing Builder -> Pedals -> Photos -> Information workflow.
