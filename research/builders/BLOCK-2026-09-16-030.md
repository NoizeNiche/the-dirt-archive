## 1. Source Audio

Builder / brand: Source Audio

### Current dirt products verified from the official Source Audio catalog
- Ultrawave Distortion Lab — distortion
- Ultrawave Bass Distortion Lab — bass distortion
- Aftershock Bass Distortion — bass distortion with overdrive, distortion, and fuzz engines
- Kingmaker Fuzz — fuzz / programmable overdrive-fuzz-distortion platform
- L.A. Lady Overdrive — overdrive / programmable overdrive-fuzz-distortion platform

Notes:
- Source Audio's current official Overdrive & Fuzz collection contains five dirt products: Ultrawave Distortion Lab, Ultrawave Bass Distortion Lab, Aftershock Bass Distortion, Kingmaker Fuzz, and L.A. Lady Overdrive.
- The official L.A. Lady manual states that the pedal supports overdrive, distortion, and fuzz effects through its Neuro platform and describes its core operating modes as overdrive.
- The official Kingmaker product page identifies the pedal as a fuzz platform capable of storing multiple overdrive voices, while Source Audio documentation states that the L.A. Lady, Kingmaker, and Aftershock share more than 40 overdrive, fuzz, and distortion engines.
- The official Aftershock product page explicitly describes a range from natural overdrive to full-on fuzz attack and provides three selectable distortion engines: tube, high-gain, and fuzz.
- The current Source Audio catalog lists the Ultrawave and Ultrawave Bass as distortion-lab products. They are retained under distortion because that is the manufacturer's product identity, even though their sound-design systems can access multiple distortion/fuzz approaches.
- ZIO Analog Preamp products, compressors, EQ, modulation, delay/reverb, and synth/filter products are excluded because their catalog identity is not core OD/distortion/fuzz.
- No circuit, component, schematic, or cloning information is included.
