## Important Identity Rule Reinforced by This Appendix

Several historical Japanese, British, Italian, and American pedal families contain heavily reused product names and related manufacturing histories. For the first-pass archive, that complexity must not erase recognizable builder/product identities.

Use:

**recognized builder + recognizable product = separate first-pass record**

Within that record, minor naming, cosmetic, or production variations can wait for the later pedal deep dive.
