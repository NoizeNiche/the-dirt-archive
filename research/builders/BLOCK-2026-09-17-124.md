# The Dirt Archive — Builder Census Block 124

## Builder
**Mad Professor Amplification**

Mad Professor is a previously uncovered builder with a substantial documented catalog of Overdrive products, plus dedicated Distortion and Fuzz products. The official archive explicitly covers current and discontinued pedals, making it useful for the historical builder census. citeturn921210view0turn412572search5

## Scope result
This block records named Mad Professor pedals whose product identity or authoritative documentation clearly establishes Overdrive, Distortion, or Fuzz. The taxonomy remains conservative: descriptions that merely say a pedal can become "distortion-like" or "fuzz-like" do not automatically create extra category entries when the builder's named identity remains Overdrive. Amp-in-a-box and underdrive products are not promoted into the dirt categories unless the relevant target identity is explicit.

## Overdrive
- **Albert's Overdrive** — historical/discontinued; explicitly named and described as overdrive. Higher Drive can become fuzz-like, but the product identity remains Overdrive.
- **Amber Overdrive** — historical/discontinued; explicitly named Overdrive. The builder describes it as highly saturated with a hint of fuzz, but does not establish a separate Fuzz identity.
- **Big Tweedy Drive** — historical/discontinued; tweed-amp style drive pedal with an explicit gain/drive control.
- **Blueberry Bass Overdrive** — historical/discontinued; explicitly light-to-medium overdrive for bass and other instruments.
- **Bluebird Overdrive** — historical/discontinued; compact overdrive + delay pedal. Higher settings can produce medium/heavier distortion, but the product identity remains Overdrive.
- **Golden Cello** — historical/discontinued; explicitly delivers a huge singing overdrive tone with built-in tape-echo style delay.
- **The Green Wonder** — current; explicitly controls overdrive from low gain/boost through rock overdrive.
- **Little Green Wonder** — current/handwired lineage; explicit overdrive identity.
- **Little Tweedy Drive** — historical/discontinued; tweed-style overdrive pedal with adjustable drive.
- **Loud 'n Proud** — historical/discontinued; vintage Marshall amp-in-a-box with a medium-gain overdrive circuit and added fuzz section.
- **Royal Blue Overdrive** — current/handwired lineage; explicit Overdrive identity with a range into distortion.
- **Simble / Simble Overdrive Mk2** — original historical lineage plus current Mk2; both explicitly identified as transparent/organic Overdrive.
- **Sky Blue Overdrive** — historical/handwired lineage; explicitly identified as high-quality Overdrive.
- **Supreme** — historical/limited/current-return lineage; dual-channel Overdrive, with two independent drive sides that can be stacked.
- **Sweet Honey Overdrive** — current/handwired lineage; explicit low-gain touch-sensitive Overdrive.
- **Sweet Honey Overdrive Deluxe** — historical/discontinued; explicit medium-gain amplifier-like Overdrive.
- **Twimble** — historical/discontinued; combination of Simble Overdrive and Simble Predrive, retaining an explicit Overdrive section.

## Distortion
- **Mighty Red Distortion** — historical/discontinued; explicitly high-gain Distortion.
- **Stone Grey Distortion** — historical/discontinued; explicitly high-gain Distortion for heavy applications.

## Fuzz
- **Fire Red Fuzz** — historical/discontinued; explicitly named Fuzz.
- **Loud 'n Proud** — historical/discontinued; the dedicated added fuzz circuit is explicitly described as vintage germanium-style Fuzz alongside the medium-gain overdrive circuit.

## Explicit category overlaps
- **Loud 'n Proud:** Overdrive + Fuzz. Mad Professor explicitly describes the core as a medium-gain overdrive and the added circuit as a vintage germanium fuzz.

## Historical / variant restraint
- **Simble / Simble Overdrive Mk2** remain one named Simble Overdrive lineage, with the current Mk2 retained as the present named version rather than multiplying the product by routine revision.
- **Sweet Honey Overdrive / Handwired / Deluxe** are treated as the named Sweet Honey Overdrive family and Deluxe variant, while ordinary handwired-versus-factory construction is not multiplied into separate dirt identities.
- **Royal Blue Overdrive / Handwired** is one Royal Blue Overdrive lineage. The current handwired version remains a named lineage product, not a new effect category.
- **Little Green Wonder / Handwired** remains one Little Green Wonder lineage.
- **Fire Red Fuzz / Fire Red Fuzz HW** remains one Fire Red Fuzz lineage.
- **Amber Overdrive** remains Overdrive only despite the builder's "hint of fuzz" and description of a highly saturated tone bordering on fuzz.
- **Albert's Overdrive** remains Overdrive only even though higher Drive settings are described as becoming fuzz-like.
- **Bluebird Overdrive** remains Overdrive only even though the product documentation says higher drive levels produce distortion. The product is explicitly named and described as an overdrive section paired with delay.
- **Blueberry Bass Overdrive** remains Overdrive only even though the Drive control produces increasing distortion at higher settings. Its documented primary identity is light-to-medium overdrive.
- **Royal Blue Overdrive** remains Overdrive only despite its range into distortion; Mad Professor explicitly presents it as an Overdrive product.
- **Super Black** is excluded from the target categories because its primary identity is an amp-in-a-box recreating the Blackface amp signal path. Its integrated Sweet Honey section is an explicitly separate overdrive circuit, but the archive does not turn an amp-sim product into a standalone OD entry.
- **Evolution Orange Underdrive** is excluded because its stated purpose is to reduce or reshape an already overdriven signal rather than generate a named Overdrive, Distortion, or Fuzz identity.
- **Simble Predrive** is excluded as a predrive/boost utility rather than a target dirt category.
- **Ruby Red Booster** and other boost-first, modulation, delay, reverb, compressor, wah, and time-based products remain outside the dirt census.
- No components, schematics, cloning, PCB, BOM, or internal-build information is recorded in this block.

## Research note
Mad Professor is a useful archive entry because the official catalog spans a large number of named Overdrives across both current and discontinued generations, while also containing clear dedicated Distortion and Fuzz products. The strongest hybrid is Loud 'n Proud, where the builder explicitly combines a medium-gain Marshall-style overdrive circuit with a dedicated germanium fuzz circuit. The archive does not inflate saturated overdrive products such as Amber or Albert's into separate Fuzz entries, and it keeps Super Black outside the target taxonomy as an amp-in-a-box product with a separate integrated Sweet Honey overdrive section. citeturn412572search3turn348354view0turn602644search13turn985518search2
