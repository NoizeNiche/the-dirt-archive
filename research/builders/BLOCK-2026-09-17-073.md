# Block 073 — DigiTech

## Builder
**DigiTech**

## Verification basis
Primary manufacturer / authoritative sources reviewed:
- DigiTech official History page
- DigiTech official Product Manuals archive
- DigiTech official FAQ and power-supply compatibility archive
- manufacturer-authored DigiTech product manuals and archived product documentation

The manufacturer explicitly describes DOD as the analog-pedal brand and DigiTech as the separate DSP/effects brand. This block therefore records **DigiTech-branded** dirt pedals separately from the DOD builder recorded in Block 072. The current DigiTech site continues to host manuals and legacy product records for discontinued products, while the current shop is focused primarily on active DigiTech and DOD offerings.

The active repository contained no prior DigiTech match before this block was added. Dirt categories are based on explicit manufacturer product titles, catalog labels, manuals, or authoritative manufacturer-originated documentation. Generic references to gain, clipping, saturation, or a product reaching another sound are not used to create a second target category unless the product documentation explicitly supports Overdrive, Distortion, or Fuzz.

## Overdrive
Current / manufacturer-supported identities:
- none explicitly surfaced as a current DigiTech-branded standalone dirt pedal in the reviewed current shop/catalog

Historical / manufacturer-supported identities:
- Screamin' Blues Overdrive / Distortion
- Redline Modified Overdrive
- X-Series Tone Driver
- X-Series Bass Driver

Verification notes:
- **Screamin' Blues Overdrive / Distortion** is explicitly titled as both Overdrive and Distortion in its manufacturer manual and is retained in both categories below.
- **Redline Modified Overdrive** is explicitly identified by DigiTech as an overdrive pedal. Manufacturer documentation describes its circuit as overdriving the guitar signal with extra gain and low-end emphasis.
- **X-Series Tone Driver** is explicitly documented as an overdrive stompbox whose Morph control blends three distinct overdrive types. It is retained as Overdrive only.
- **X-Series Bass Driver** is explicitly documented as a Bass Driver Overdrive/Distortion pedal and as an advanced overdrive designed specifically for bass. It is therefore retained in both Overdrive and Distortion below.

- **Distortion Factory DF-7** is explicitly a distortion pedal and its documentation calls its available circuits Distortion models.
- **Hot Rod Distortion** is explicitly described as a distortion pedal whose Morph control blends three independent distortion types.
- **Metal Master** is explicitly described as a heavy metal distortion pedal with three different metal distortion types.

## Fuzz
Current / manufacturer-supported identities:
- none explicitly surfaced as a current DigiTech-branded standalone fuzz pedal in the reviewed current shop/catalog

Historical / manufacturer-supported identities:
- none promoted as a DigiTech-branded standalone Fuzz identity in the reviewed material

Verification notes:
- DigiTech's multi-effects processors contain modeled fuzz effects, but those are not standalone DigiTech fuzz pedals and therefore are excluded from this builder block.
- The current **Wah-ocTo-Fuzz** is a DOD/Morley collaboration whose product identity is explicitly presented as a three-way Wah/Octave/Fuzz collaboration. It is not promoted into the DigiTech builder record.
- Manufacturer documentation for the Hot Rod and Bass Driver mentions fuzz-range tones, but those are presented within explicit distortion or overdrive products rather than as separate DigiTech Fuzz product identities.

## Category-overlap decisions
- **Screamin' Blues Overdrive / Distortion**: Overdrive + Distortion. The manufacturer title explicitly combines both categories.
- **X-Series Bass Driver**: Overdrive + Distortion. Manufacturer documentation explicitly describes both overdrive and distortion types in the pedal.
- **Redline Modified Overdrive**: Overdrive only. Distortion is discussed as a consequence of overdriving the circuit, but the named product identity is Overdrive.
- **X-Series Tone Driver**: Overdrive only. The manufacturer describes three overdrive voicings; gain/distortion wording is treated as operating-range language.
- **Hot Head**: Distortion only. The manufacturer/manual identifies it as Distortion; broad low-gain capability does not create an Overdrive identity.
- **DigiTech Grunge**: Distortion only. Manufacturer documentation presents it as a DigiTech distortion pedal; its aggressive gain range does not create a separate Fuzz identity.
- **DigiTech Death Metal**: Distortion only. Manufacturer documentation presents it as a distortion pedal.
- **DF-7 Distortion Factory**: Distortion only. The product itself is a distortion pedal even though its modeled library includes circuits associated with overdrive/fuzz products from other builders.
- **Hot Rod Distortion**: Distortion only. Its high-gain fuzz-like range is a distortion voicing rather than a separate Fuzz identity.
- **Metal Master**: Distortion only. Manufacturer documentation explicitly identifies the product as heavy-metal distortion.

## Historical / variant handling
- DigiTech's official manual archive distinguishes current products from a large discontinued catalog. This block retains clearly named historical standalone dirt pedals where manufacturer documentation supports the target category.
- **Screamin' Blues** remains one named functional product even though its title spans Overdrive and Distortion.
- **X-Series Tone Driver, Hot Rod, Metal Master, and Distortion Factory DF-7** remain separate named X-Series functional products.
- **X-Series Bass Driver** remains separate because it has its own bass-specific product identity and documented overdrive/distortion architecture.
- **DigiTech Grunge** is not merged into the DOD Grunge identity from Block 072. The manufacturer documentation itself explains that the DigiTech Grunge lineage originated from the DOD FX69 design, but the DigiTech-branded product is a separate named product identity under this builder block.
- Cosmetic editions, minor hardware revisions, and packaging differences are consolidated unless the manufacturer assigns a distinct functional model name.
- HardWire products such as CM-2 Tube Overdrive, SC-2 Valve Distortion, and TL-2 Metal Distortion are excluded because the manufacturer's manual archive presents **HardWire** as a distinct product brand rather than DigiTech-branded products.

## Exclusions
- DOD-branded products such as Bad Monkey, 250, Carcosa Fuzz, Chthonic Fuzz, and Gunslinger: excluded because they belong to the separate DOD builder block.
- HardWire CM-2, SC-2, TL-2, and other HardWire products: excluded as a separate brand.
- DigiTech multi-effects processors and their internal modeled distortion/overdrive/fuzz algorithms: excluded because they are not standalone dirt pedals.
- Wah-ocTo-Fuzz: excluded from DigiTech because the manufacturer presents it as a DOD/Morley collaboration rather than a DigiTech-branded product.
- Boost-only, modulation, pitch, delay, reverb, compressor, synth, looper, volume, wah, and other non-target DigiTech products: excluded.
- Generic gain, saturation, clipping, or fuzz-adjacent wording does not create another target category without explicit product-level support.
