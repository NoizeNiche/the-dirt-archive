## Explicit overlaps
- Geisha Drive = Overdrive + Distortion. Jim Dunlop describes it as moving from overdrive into distortion territory across its Drive range.
- Conquistador Fuzzstortion = Fuzz + Distortion. The official product page identifies it as an aggressive gated fuzz and names it Fuzzstortion.
- Pork & Pickle = Overdrive + Fuzz. The official product page explicitly identifies it as Overdrive & Fuzz and combines the Pork Loin overdrive with Russian-Pickle fuzz.
- Penny Saver Royale = Overdrive plus modulation. Only the overdrive function is counted here; the modulation side is excluded.
- Stone Burner = Fuzz plus sub-octave. Only Fuzz is counted.
- Camel Toe = Overdrive. Its separate Green Rhino and Red Llama sections remain part of the combined triple-overdrive product relationship rather than creating additional category entries.

## Variant / historical restraint
- Swollen Pickle XXX and Swollen Pickle MKIIS are consolidated into the Swollen Pickle lineage rather than treated as separate builder relationships.
- Green Rhino Mk IV/Mk V and other edition changes are consolidated into the Green Rhino lineage.
- Russian-Pickle original and Smalls versions are consolidated into one lineage.
- Geisha Drive and Geisha Drive Blue are consolidated into one lineage; finish/artwork changes do not create another pedal relationship.
- Conspiracy Theory and the Deep State Conspiracy Theory edition are consolidated.
- Red Llama compact/reissue treatment is consolidated with the Red Llama lineage.
- Product parts listings are used only as corroboration that the pedal existed; parts themselves are not archive entries.
- Attack Vector, Aqua-Puss, Blue Hippo, Echo-Puss, Supa-Puss, Saffron Squeeze, Atreides, and other non-target effects are excluded from the dirt census.
- No components, schematics, PCB, BOM, gutshots, cloning, or build instructions are included.

## Verification note
Jim Dunlop's official Way Huge catalog currently lists Swollen Pickle variants, Doom Hammer Fuzz, Pork & Pickle Overdrive & Fuzz, Conspiracy Theory Professional Overdrive, and Green Rhino Overdrive. Its official legacy catalog also lists Stone Burner, STO, Green Rhino Mk V, Geisha Drive, Red Llama, Penny Saver Royale, Russian Pickle, Supa-Lead, Overrated Special, Conquistador Fuzzstortion, and Camel Toe Triple Overdrive. Official product documentation also explicitly describes Fat Sandwich as a distortion product, Geisha Drive as spanning overdrive to distortion territory, Stone Burner as fuzz, and Pork & Pickle as overdrive plus fuzz.
