# Builder → Pedals Research Block 108

## Builder
**Stone Deaf FX — Luke Hilton / Stone Deaf team**

## Verification basis
Stone Deaf FX is a UK pedal builder founded by Luke Hilton. Reputable industry coverage identifies Hilton as the founder and describes Stone Deaf's work as a range of drive, distortion, and fuzz pedals. The current Stone Deaf site confirms the present dirt lineup and product identities, while older specialist coverage is used only for historical/discontinued products whose names and functions are otherwise directly documented.

The archive follows Stone Deaf's named product identity and distinct dirt functions. Gain range alone does not create extra categories. A pedal is listed in more than one target category when Stone Deaf or strong product-level documentation explicitly gives it multiple target dirt functions.

## Product notes
- **PDF-2:** Stone Deaf's current product page explicitly describes PDF-2 as offering overdrive, distortion, and fuzz in one pedal. It is therefore retained in all three target categories.
- **PDF-1X Ghost:** Stone Deaf explicitly identifies this edition as a Parametric Overdrive / Distortion and describes it as a tone-shaping pedal with enough gain to move into heavier tones. It is retained in both Overdrive and Distortion. The internal gain trim and cleaner boost setting do not create additional categories.
- **PDF-1:** the original Parametric Distortion Filter is a historical/discontinued product. Historical product descriptions identify a switchable clean/dirty circuit with distortion and overdrive/fuzz behavior. The archive conservatively retains PDF-1 under Distortion because that is its named product identity; lower-gain and fuzz behavior are treated as operating range rather than automatically creating extra categories.
- **Kliptonite:** specialist product coverage describes Kliptonite as one-part overdrive and one-part fuzz, with the two gain structures blendable using the Mirror Mix control. It is therefore retained in both Overdrive and Fuzz.
- **Trashy Blonde:** historical Stone Deaf coverage identifies the pedal as Marshall-style overdrive with a dedicated distortion mode. It is retained in both Overdrive and Distortion as one named product.
- **Warp Drive:** Stone Deaf currently identifies Warp Drive as a high-gain distortion with an integrated noise gate. The limited black edition is treated as part of the same product lineage rather than as a separate pedal entry.
- **Fig Fumb:** Stone Deaf explicitly identifies Fig Fumb as a Muff-style fuzz with a parametric filter and noise gate. The Fig Fumb LTD/Black Edition is treated as the same named product lineage rather than creating a duplicate entry.
- **Rise & Shine:** Stone Deaf explicitly identifies Rise & Shine as Fuzz + Octave Up Fuzz + Octave Tremolo. Only the fuzz function belongs in the core dirt census; the octave and tremolo portions are outside scope.

## Explicit exclusions / restraint
- **Q Boost Ltd / Q Boost:** excluded because it is a boost-first product rather than Overdrive, Distortion, or Fuzz.
- **Noise Reaper:** excluded because it is a noise-gate product.
- **EP-1 Expression:** excluded because it is an expression/utility pedal used to control other Stone Deaf effects.
- **Tremotron:** excluded because it is a tremolo product.
- **Syncopy:** excluded because it is a delay product.
- **Rise & Shine octave tremolo:** the tremolo and octave functions are not separately counted as dirt categories; only its explicitly named fuzz identity is retained.
- **Fig Fumb gain range:** although the product can move across heavy and distorted textures, Stone Deaf names it as a Muff-style fuzz, so it remains Fuzz only.
- **PDF-1 gain behavior:** its original documentation mentions overdrive/fuzz behavior, but the named product identity is the Parametric Distortion Filter and the archive keeps it under Distortion rather than inflating every operating mode into a separate category.
- **PDF-1X Ghost:** the clean boost functionality is excluded because Boost is outside the three target categories. Overdrive and Distortion remain because Stone Deaf explicitly names both.
- **Variant/finish handling:** ordinary color editions and direct reissues are consolidated unless Stone Deaf presents them as a materially different named product. Fig Fumb LTD and Warp Drive LTD are therefore kept within their main product lineages.

## Status
**Verified research block.** Stone Deaf FX is a previously uncovered builder in the fresh census. The current official catalog clearly establishes PDF-2 as a multi-category Overdrive + Distortion + Fuzz product, PDF-1X Ghost as Overdrive + Distortion, Warp Drive as Distortion, Fig Fumb as Fuzz, and Rise & Shine as Fuzz. Historical product documentation adds Kliptonite as Overdrive + Fuzz, Trashy Blonde as Overdrive + Distortion, and the original PDF-1 as a historical Distortion product. The archive deliberately avoids turning boost, filter, octave, tremolo, or mere gain-range behavior into additional dirt categories.
