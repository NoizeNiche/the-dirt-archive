# The Dirt Archive — Builder Research Block 054

## Builder
**Barbarossa — Newtool**

## Verification basis
Primary/brand sources used:
- Newtool's official Barbarossa product page and current catalog
- Newtool's official company/brand site
- Birdland Guitars, Barbarossa's listed general distributor, for the historical WIVERN entry

The current Newtool Barbarossa page identifies CHIMAERA, GARGOYLE, URANUS (New Model), and CRONUS (New Model) as the brand's current effecter models and explicitly labels them Modern Over Drive, Distortion Over Drive, Over Drive, and Distortion Over Drive respectively. The same page lists Newtool as the company contact for the Barbarossa brand.

## Product-level verification notes
- **CHIMAERA** is explicitly labeled "Modern Over Drive" by Newtool. It is retained under Overdrive only. Ikebe's product listing independently describes it as a modern overdrive.
- **GARGOYLE** is explicitly labeled "Distortion Over Drive" by Newtool. It is therefore recorded in both Overdrive and Distortion. Birdland's distributor page likewise describes it as occupying the space between distortion and overdrive.
- **URANUS** is explicitly labeled "Over Drive" by Newtool and is retained under Overdrive only. Ikebe likewise describes it as an overdrive.
- **CRONUS** is explicitly labeled "Distortion Over Drive" by Newtool. It is therefore recorded in both Overdrive and Distortion. The historical old-model page separately identifies it as a "Distortion Drive Modify Model," while the current model is presented as an original New Model. The archive keeps the named CRONUS identity consolidated rather than creating a duplicate solely for the revision.
- **WIVERN** is a historical/discontinued Barbarossa overdrive. Birdland's Barbarossa page identifies it as an end-of-sale product and describes it as an original Barbarossa effecter designed as a lower-cost model incorporating the brand's existing design concepts. It is retained as an Overdrive identity because the manufacturer/distributor material does not explicitly classify it as a standalone Distortion pedal.

## Scope decisions / exclusions
- **PROMETHEUS** is excluded because Newtool identifies it as a digital tuner modification, not a target dirt effect.
- Barbarossa cables and speaker cables are excluded.
- Old-model URANUS and CRONUS are not counted as separate pedal identities from the current named models. They are revision/history notes under the same named products.
- **WIVERN** is retained despite being discontinued because it is a clearly documented historical Barbarossa pedal identity.
- No Fuzz entry is promoted because no Barbarossa product in the verified sources is explicitly categorized as Fuzz.
