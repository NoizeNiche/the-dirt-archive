## Census note

This block records builder names and their identified overdrive, distortion, and fuzz products only. Discovery sources were used to expand the Japanese independent/boutique builder pool; first-party product pages were used where available. No photos, biographies, schematics, component research, internal construction, or variant deep dives were added.
