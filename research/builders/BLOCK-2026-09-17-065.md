# Block 065 — ZVEX Effects — Zachary Vex

## Builder
**ZVEX Effects — Zachary Vex**

## Verification basis
Primary manufacturer sources reviewed:
- ZVEX Effects official site / Guitar Pedals catalog
- individual manufacturer product pages for current dirt products
- manufacturer Terms page listing ZVEX product trademarks
- manufacturer artist/product references for historical product existence
- manufacturer-authored Box of Metal instruction manual

ZVEX's current Guitar Pedals catalog explicitly exposes **Distortion** and **Fuzz** as product filters and does not expose an Overdrive filter. Current catalog listings place Box of Rock, Distortron, Double Rock, Basstortion, and '59 Sound in Distortion, while Fuzz Factory-family products, Woolly Mammoth-family products, Mastotron, Fuzzolo, Fuzz Probe, Octane 3, and Machine are placed in Fuzz. Historical manufacturer material also identifies the Box of Metal as a distortion product. This block therefore follows ZVEX's own current category structure rather than inferring Overdrive from circuit behavior or amp-model descriptions.

This block records builder/product relationships only. Current products are taken from the manufacturer's current catalog and individual product pages. Historical identities are retained where manufacturer-originated material explicitly supports the product and target dirt category. Vexter editions, anniversary editions, custom-art versions, and cosmetic variations are consolidated under their underlying product identity when the manufacturer says the parts are identical or otherwise presents the item as the same pedal.

## Overdrive
No explicitly manufacturer-categorized Overdrive identities were found in the reviewed ZVEX catalog.

Verification note:
- The current ZVEX Guitar Pedals catalog exposes Distortion and Fuzz categories but no Overdrive category. Box of Rock, Distortron, Double Rock, and '59 Sound are therefore not promoted into Overdrive merely because their descriptions involve amp-like breakup, gain, or overdrive-adjacent language. ZVEX's current taxonomy is used as the controlling category evidence.

## Distortion
Current manufacturer-supported identities:
- Box of Rock
- Distortron
- Double Rock
- Basstortion
- '59 Sound

Historical / discontinued manufacturer-supported identity:
- Box of Metal

Verification notes:
- Box of Rock is currently placed by ZVEX in Distortion. Its product page describes it as simulating a Marshall JTM45 pushed hard and includes a separate high-headroom boost section, but no separate Overdrive categorization is assigned by the current manufacturer catalog.
- Distortron is explicitly described by ZVEX as a distortion pedal and as a modified version of the Box of Rock's Distortron engine.
- Double Rock is currently placed by ZVEX in Distortion. The manufacturer describes it as containing two Box of Rock pedals and discusses using two Distortrons, while the product identity remains a Distortion-family product.
- Basstortion is currently placed by ZVEX in Distortion and is described around tube-amp-style bass distortion.
- '59 Sound is currently placed by ZVEX in Distortion. Manufacturer catalog material describes it as a distortion design based on the classic Fender 1959 Tweed Bassman.
- Box of Metal is retained historically because the manufacturer-authored instruction manual explicitly describes the pedal as an aggressive high-gain effect and labels the DRIVE control as setting the amount of distortion. The same manual lists both Vexter and hand-painted versions, which are consolidated into one Box of Metal identity.

## Fuzz
Current manufacturer-supported identities:
- Fuzz Factory
- Fuzz Factory 7
- Fat Fuzz Factory
- Woolly Mammoth
- Woolly Mammoth 7
- Mastotron
- Fuzz Probe
- Fuzzolo
- Machine
- Octane 3

Verification notes:
- Fuzz Factory is explicitly identified by ZVEX as a fuzz product and remains a core current identity.
- Fuzz Factory 7 is a distinct named fuzz model with a nine-position rotary switch and expanded controls. It is retained separately from Fuzz Factory because this is a functional model distinction rather than a cosmetic edition.
- Fat Fuzz Factory is explicitly described as a version of the Fuzz Factory with a functional three-position low-frequency toggle, so it is retained as a distinct named fuzz identity.
- Woolly Mammoth is explicitly described by ZVEX as a fuzz; current Vexter and hand-painted versions share the underlying identity.
- Woolly Mammoth 7 is explicitly described as an expanded version of the original Woolly Mammoth with an additional Marshall-style tone stack, so it is retained as a distinct named fuzz model.
- Mastotron is explicitly described as a sub-heavy silicon fuzz.
- Fuzz Probe is explicitly described by ZVEX as a fuzz product derived from the Fuzz Factory concept and paired with its antenna/probe control.
- Fuzzolo is explicitly described as a fuzz pedal and as being derived in part from Mastotron and Woolly Mammoth.
- Machine is explicitly described by ZVEX as a unique fuzz.
- Octane 3 is currently placed in Fuzz; manufacturer material describes its fuzzy gain and high-octave character.

## Category-overlap decisions
- **Box of Rock**: Distortion only. Its amp-simulation description and high-headroom boost do not create a separate Overdrive identity because the current ZVEX catalog classifies it under Distortion.
- **Distortron**: Distortion only. The manufacturer explicitly calls it a distortion pedal.
- **Double Rock**: Distortion only. The current manufacturer catalog places it in Distortion despite its internal relationship to Box of Rock and Distortron.
- **'59 Sound**: Distortion only. Current manufacturer catalog and manufacturer-authored catalog material identify it as distortion.
- **Box of Metal**: Distortion only. The manufacturer manual explicitly identifies the DRIVE control as setting the amount of distortion.
- **Fuzz Factory 7**: Fuzz only. Its expanded control set does not create an additional dirt category.
- **Fat Fuzz Factory**: Fuzz only. Its low-frequency toggle changes the fuzz behavior without creating an Overdrive or Distortion identity.
- **Woolly Mammoth 7**: Fuzz only. The added tone stack is a shaping feature around the fuzz circuit.
- **Fuzz Probe**: Fuzz only. The probe/antenna control is an interaction mechanism within the fuzz design.
- **Machine**: Fuzz only. The manufacturer explicitly uses the fuzz classification even though its timbre can cut through high distortion elsewhere in the signal chain.

## Historical / variant handling
- **Vexter editions**: consolidated with their corresponding hand-painted product when ZVEX states that the parts inside Vexters are identical to the hand-painted model. This applies to Fuzz Factory, Fuzz Factory 7 USA Vexter, Fat Fuzz Factory, Woolly Mammoth, Fuzz Probe, Box of Rock, Double Rock, and other equivalent Vexter listings.
- **Fuzz Factory 7 USA Vexter**: consolidated under Fuzz Factory 7. ZVEX explicitly states that its parts are identical to the hand-painted model.
- **Woolly Mammoth Vexter**: consolidated under Woolly Mammoth for the same reason.
- **Fat Fuzz Factory Vexter**: consolidated under Fat Fuzz Factory for the same reason.
- **Fuzz Factory Vertical**: consolidated under Fuzz Factory as a form-factor edition rather than a new underlying dirt identity.
- **Fuzz Factory Vexter**: consolidated under Fuzz Factory as the same underlying pedal with Vexter construction/art.
- **Box of Rock Vexter / Double Rock Vexter**: consolidated under their corresponding Box of Rock / Double Rock identities.
- **Sylvia Massy Woolly Mammoth**: consolidated under Woolly Mammoth because ZVEX presents it as a special-edition release of that fuzz identity rather than a separate base circuit/product family.
- **Custom-store and Mod Bench one-offs**: excluded from the core product census. ZVEX explicitly presents these as one-of-a-kind custom/artwork or experimental variants of existing pedals.
- **Silicon Fuzz Factory, Reverse Fuzz Factory, and similar Mod Bench experiments**: excluded from the core census because the manufacturer presents them as prototypes, hybrids, or experimental/custom variants rather than standard catalog identities.

## Exclusions
- Boost products such as Super Hard On and Super Duper: excluded because the active scope is Overdrive, Distortion, and Fuzz, and ZVEX categorizes these as Boost.
- Octave-only products such as Jonny Octave: excluded because the manufacturer categorizes them as Octave rather than a target dirt identity.
- Modulation, phaser, tremolo, sampler, meter, step-sequencer, volume, and other non-target products: excluded.
- Custom-store one-offs, engraved items, artwork editions, and Mod Bench experiments: excluded as variants/prototypes rather than separate core product identities.
- Generic gain, breakup, amp-emulation, or distortion-adjacent language is not enough to add a second dirt category when ZVEX's own product taxonomy does not do so.
