# The Dirt Archive — Builder Research Block 050

## Builder
**Blackout Effectors — Kyle Tompkins**

## Verification basis
Primary/manufacturer-originated material where recoverable:
- Archived Blackout Effectors product descriptions reproduced in period documentation and manuals
- Manufacturer-originated descriptions preserved in product/manual archives

Authoritative corroboration:
- Effects Database interview with Kyle Tompkins documenting the company's founding and the Fix'd Fuzz as its first release
- Effects Database product records preserving Blackout Effectors product names and manufacturer descriptions
- Premier Guitar review of the Crystal Dagger

The Effects Database interview records Kyle Tompkins saying Blackout Effectors began in early 2008 with the release of Fix'd Fuzz. The archived product corpus documents a compact but dirt-heavy catalog spanning fuzz and overdrive designs. No standalone distortion category is forced where the manufacturer documentation does not clearly establish one.

## Product-level verification notes
- **Mantra Overdrive** is explicitly documented by Blackout Effectors as an overdrive with cascaded MOSFET/JFET gain stages and a range from low gain through heavy overdrive.
- **Dual Gain Mantra** is retained as a distinct documented product identity because the historical catalog identifies it separately from the standard Mantra.
- **Fix'd Fuzz** is explicitly described by the manufacturer as a fuzz box with a boost through light overdrive stage. It is therefore retained under both Fuzz and Overdrive.
- **Fix'd Fuzz Deluxe** is the expanded version of Fix'd Fuzz and is likewise documented as containing two fuzz voices plus clean boost through overdrive. It is retained under both Fuzz and Overdrive.
- **Musket Fuzz** is explicitly a fuzz design based on the Big Muff family. Its pre-boost and aggressive clipping are not used to create a separate distortion identity.
- **Blunderbuss Musket** is documented as an alternate Musket revision and is retained as a distinct historical product identity only because it was sold as its own named version, not merely as a cosmetic variant.
- **Blunderbuss Fuzz** is documented as a fuzz product and is retained under Fuzz.
- **Fuzz Bear** is documented as a fuzz product and is treated as the predecessor identity to FUBÄR rather than silently merging the names.
- **FUBÄR** is explicitly documented as a fuzz pedal and as an expanded development of Fuzz Bear. It remains a separate named pedal identity.
- **Crystal Dagger** combines an octave-up fuzz circuit with ring modulation/phasing. Because the manufacturer-originated description explicitly identifies the fuzz circuit, it is retained under Fuzz only.
- **Twosome Dual Fuzz** combines the Musket Fuzz and deluxe Fix'd Fuzz circuits. It is retained under Fuzz and Overdrive because its Fix'd side is explicitly capable of overdrive, while the product's primary identity is a dual-fuzz pedal.
- **Special Twosome Deluxe**, **A Seriously Special Twosome**, and **A Very Special Twosome** are retained as named historical Twosome-family identities in the fuzz census only where the archival catalog distinguishes them as separate named products. They are not expanded into additional category claims beyond the documented fuzz family.

## Scope decisions / exclusions
- **Gravity Boost** is excluded because it is a boost-only product, outside the archive's three target dirt categories.
- **Cadavernous**, **Whetstone**, and **Sibling** are excluded because they are reverb/phaser products rather than target dirt pedals.
- Cosmetic artwork and enclosure-color variants are not separate identities.
- Fuzz Bear → FUBÄR is preserved as two named historical identities because the manufacturer-era catalog distinguishes them, but no extra category is inferred from the relationship.
- No standalone Distortion entry is invented from generic use of the word “distortion” in secondary descriptions or from high-gain fuzz behavior.
- The archive does not split every Musket/Twosome revision into a separate census entry when the evidence only indicates a revision or special edition rather than a distinct marketed product identity.
