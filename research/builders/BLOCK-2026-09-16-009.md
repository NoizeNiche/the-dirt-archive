## 1. Friedman Amplification

Builder / brand: Friedman Amplification

### Overdrive / Distortion
- BE-OD
- BE-OD Deluxe
- Dirty Shirley Pedal
- Golden Pearl
- Smallbox Overdrive Pedal

### Boost / dirt-adjacent
- Buxom Boost

Notes:
- Friedman’s current pedal catalog lists BE-OD, BE-OD Deluxe, Buxom Boost, Dirty Shirley Pedal, Golden Pearl, and Smallbox Overdrive Pedal among its pedal products.
- Buxom Boost is retained only as a dirt-adjacent boost because Friedman describes it as a clean boost that can push an amplifier into harmonic overdrive. It is not being classified as an overdrive pedal.
- The Friedman IR-D, IR-J, and IR-X are amp/preamp products and are excluded from the dirt-pedal census rather than forcing them into overdrive or distortion.

## 2. Pigtronix

Builder / brand: Pigtronix

### Overdrive / Distortion
- Gamma Drive
- Philosopher’s Gold
- Philosopher’s Tone 2
- Disnortion
- Octava

### Fuzz
- Gas Giant
- Star Eater
- Octava
- Disnortion

Notes:
- Pigtronix’s current catalog lists Gas Giant, Star Eater, Gamma Drive, Octava, and Philosopher’s Gold among its current pedal offerings.
- Pigtronix explicitly describes Disnortion as an analog Fuzz + Overdrive, and Octava as an Analog Octave Fuzz with a front-end overdrive stage.
- Philosopher’s Gold is explicitly described as a compressor/sustainer with germanium distortion/overdrive, so it is retained as a compound dirt product.
- Philosopher’s Tone 2 has a footswitchable Grit circuit and is described by Pigtronix as providing overdrive through distortion tones. It is retained because the dirt function is explicit, despite the pedal also being a compressor/sustainer.
- Non-dirt Pigtronix products are excluded.
