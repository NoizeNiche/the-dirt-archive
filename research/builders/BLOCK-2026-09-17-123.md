# The Dirt Archive — Builder Census Block 123

## Builder
**Beetronics FX**

Beetronics FX is a previously uncovered pedal builder with a clearly documented Overdrive + Fuzz dirt catalog. Its current site separates Overdrive and Fuzz product families, while individual pages explicitly identify the relevant dirt functions. The current catalog also contains octave, harmonizer, modulation, and delay products that remain outside the target dirt taxonomy.

## Scope result
This block records named Beetronics dirt products that the builder itself explicitly identifies as Overdrive or Fuzz. No separate Distortion category is established because the current Beetronics material reviewed here does not present a distinct named distortion product or an explicit distortion identity strong enough to meet the archive's conservative threshold. Generic mentions of "distortion" as a possible sound are not promoted into a new category.

## Overdrive
- **Fatbee** — current; explicitly described as a JFET overdrive.
- **Overhive** — current; explicitly described as a mid-gain overdrive.
- **Wannabee / Beelateral Buzz** — current; explicitly described as a dual overdrive inspired by Bluesbreaker and Klon circuits.
- **Royal Jelly** — current; explicitly described as an overdrive/fuzz blender.
- **Nectar Tone Sweetener** — current; explicitly described as an overdrive/fuzz pedal with separate overdrive and fuzz modes.

## Distortion
- **None established** — no distinct Beetronics product reviewed for this block meets the archive's explicit Distortion threshold.

## Fuzz
- **Octahive V2** — current; explicitly described as high-octave fuzz.
- **Abelha Tropical Fuzz** — current; explicitly described as a psychedelic fuzz with three fuzz modes.
- **Vezzpa Octave Stinger** — current; explicitly described as a dual-mode fuzz pedal.
- **Swarm Fuzz Harmonizer** — current; explicitly described as an analog fuzz harmonizer and regular fuzz when harmonies are reduced.
- **Royal Jelly** — current; explicit overdrive/fuzz blender.
- **Nectar Tone Sweetener** — current; explicit overdrive/fuzz pedal with dedicated fuzz mode.
- **Tuna Fuzz** — current/pre-order; explicitly described as a fuzz and a 3-transistor fuzz product.

## Explicit category overlaps
- **Royal Jelly:** Overdrive + Fuzz. Beetronics explicitly describes it as a blend of overdrive and fuzz, with two overdrive/fuzz blend presets.
- **Nectar Tone Sweetener:** Overdrive + Fuzz. Beetronics explicitly gives it two distinct drive textures, smooth overdrive and saturated fuzz, selectable by toggle.

## Historical / variant restraint
- **Octahive V2** remains one fuzz identity despite BUZZZ and OCTAVE modes; the octave function does not become a separate category.
- **Vezzpa** remains one fuzz identity despite its two fuzz modes, including the high-octave STINGER mode.
- **Swarm** remains one fuzz identity despite its harmonizer intervals and modulation controls. The builder explicitly describes it as a fuzz harmonizer, and its harmonic processing is not treated as a separate dirt category.
- **Abelha** remains one fuzz identity despite three fuzz voices and Tropical Mode; those are modes and tone-shaping features inside one named fuzz product.
- **Wannabee** remains Overdrive only. Beetronics explicitly presents the two sides as overdrive circuits; the resulting saturation is not promoted into Distortion.
- **Fatbee** and **Overhive** remain Overdrive only despite generic references to saturation.
- **Royal Jelly** limited editions and custom-shop units are consolidated into the Royal Jelly product identity rather than multiplied by finish or artwork.
- **Nectar** custom-shop units are consolidated into one Nectar product identity.
- **Tuna Fuzz** remains Fuzz only. Its unusual tuna-can enclosure is not a separate product identity.
- **Pollinator Hazee Delay, Seabee, BeeBeeDee, Larva, Zzombee, Nectar-adjacent tone tools, and other modulation/time-based products** remain outside the dirt census.
- **Generic references to distortion** in Beetronics descriptions are treated as descriptive sound language, not proof of a separate Distortion category.
- No components, schematics, cloning, PCB, BOM, or internal-build information is recorded in this block.

## Research note
Beetronics is useful to the archive because its dirt line is unusually explicit about the Overdrive/Fuzz boundary. Royal Jelly and Nectar are clear hybrid products, while Fatbee, Overhive, and Wannabee are dedicated overdrives and Octahive, Abelha, Vezzpa, Swarm, and Tuna are dedicated fuzz identities. The archive deliberately leaves Distortion empty rather than promoting generic saturation language into a new category.
