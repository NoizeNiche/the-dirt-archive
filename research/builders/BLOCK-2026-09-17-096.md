# Builder → Pedals Research Block 096

## Builder
**Death By Audio — Oliver Ackermann**

## Verification basis
Death By Audio's official About page identifies Oliver Ackermann as the founder and describes Death By Audio as a handmade effects-pedal builder operating in Queens, NYC. The current official site maintains separate pedal collections for Overdrive, Distortion, and Fuzz, along with separate collections for delay/reverb, filters, modulation, and extreme/experimental effects. This block limits the census to physical pedals with an explicit target dirt identity in the manufacturer material.

The manufacturer frequently describes a fuzz or distortion pedal as capable of producing neighboring gain ranges. Those operating ranges are not automatically promoted into additional archive categories. A second category is retained only where the product itself is explicitly presented by Death By Audio as having that target dirt identity, rather than merely being able to reach it. Normal reissues/revivals and version naming are consolidated when the manufacturer presents them as the same underlying product identity.

## Product notes
- **Interstellar Overdriver:** Death By Audio explicitly calls this a true overdrive pedal and describes its gain control as ranging from subtle boost into harmonically rich overdrive. It is retained under Overdrive only.
- **Fuzz War:** the manufacturer explicitly presents Fuzz War as a fuzz pedal and describes a sweep from booster through overdrive and thick distortion into full fuzz. Following the archive rule, those operating ranges are treated as behavior inside the fuzz identity rather than additional OD/Distortion identities.
- **Bass War:** Death By Audio explicitly calls Bass War a bass fuzz and describes a range from grinding drive through thick distortion to extreme fuzz. It remains Fuzz only.
- **Supersonic Fuzz Gun:** the manufacturer directly identifies it as a fuzz pedal built around gated and oscillating fuzz behavior. Distorted guitar sounds are described as part of its range, not as a separate distortion identity.
- **Apocalypse:** Death By Audio explicitly calls Apocalypse a fuzz pedal and describes selectable post-amplifier voices including a War Fuzz setting described as full dynamic distortion. The core product identity remains Fuzz rather than being split into multiple dirt categories.
- **Octave Clang V2:** the current manufacturer page explicitly describes a blend of octave-up fuzz and clangorous tones. Its gain can drive the circuit into overdrive and fuzz, and the internal bias concerns the distortion circuitry, but the named product identity is retained under Fuzz only.
- **Crossover Fuzz:** Death By Audio explicitly presents this as a dual-band fuzz and describes the gain range as clean, overdrive, and towering fuzz. Because the product is explicitly a fuzz pedal, the neighboring drive range is not separately counted.
- **Germanium Filter:** Death By Audio titles this product as “TRUE VINTAGE DISTORTION” and describes a gain range from mild overdrive to blasted-out fuzz. It is retained under Distortion only because the manufacturer gives it an explicit distortion identity; its fuzz/overdrive sweep is treated as operating range rather than additional categories.
- **Absolute Destruction:** the manufacturer's product page explicitly describes both thick, gnarly fuzz and crushing distortion. It is therefore retained in both Fuzz and Distortion.

## Explicit exclusions / restraint
- **Evil Filter:** excluded from the core dirt census because the product is fundamentally a multimode filter pedal. Although it contains a dedicated Evil Fuzz section that can operate as a standalone fuzz, Death By Audio's product identity is presented as a filter rather than a target dirt pedal.
- **Echo Dream 2:** excluded because the target identity is delay/echo with a fuzz control rather than a standalone dirt product.
- **Time Shadows II:** excluded because it is a multi-delay/filter/pitch-morphing pedal with a digital-distortion component rather than a standalone dirt identity.
- **Amp Crash:** excluded from the core builder-to-dirt catalog because it is a real amplifier/speaker/microphone recording and multi-effects platform, even though its internal amplifier can produce overdrive and fuzz.
- **Thee Treble Overload:** excluded because Death By Audio presents it as a treble-boost pedal; its ability to hit distortion territory does not create a standalone distortion identity.
- **Space Bender, Moonbeam Phaser, Reverberation Machine, Rooms, vocal effects, utilities, and other non-target products:** excluded.
- **Historical leads:** older names such as Armageddon and Waveformer Destroyer were not promoted here because the reviewed current manufacturer material did not establish a sufficiently direct product-level dirt classification for the present block. They remain outside the categorized census until directly verified.

## Status
**Verified research block.** Death By Audio is a previously uncovered builder in the fresh census. The reviewed manufacturer material establishes a broad dirt footprint centered on fuzz, with a clear overdrive identity in Interstellar Overdriver and explicit distortion identities in Germanium Filter and Absolute Destruction. Fuzz War, Bass War, Supersonic Fuzz Gun, Apocalypse, Octave Clang V2, and Crossover Fuzz are retained as fuzz identities without manufacturing extra OD/Distortion entries from mere gain range descriptions. Non-dirt and multifunction products were deliberately excluded.
