# Builder -> Pedals Research Block 185

## Scope

Continued the C-section Builder -> Pedals census after Block 184 with dirt-only inclusion: overdrive, distortion, fuzz. Existing builders were checked against the canonical index before creating new identities. ## Evidence / classification notes

- Coleman is documented by Effects Database as a hand-wired builder associated with Stan Day, with four cataloged products. Angry Dragon mkII is explicitly a Germanium Fuzz and Astro Fuzz is a hybrid Germanium/Silicon fuzz. Super Boost is clean boost and The Wizard is a dirty boost, so neither is added to the dirt census.
- Crate's Effects Database catalog contains four products, with CC-2681 Distortion as the sole dirt-primary model. The other catalog entries are chorus, flanger, and phaser products. CC-2681 is retained as a Crate-branded catalog record; the archive does not infer original circuit authorship from the brand label.
- Cruzer (by Crafter) is treated as a distinct catalog identity with Crafter as the brand lineage. Effects Database documents EF-DT Distortion and EF-OD Over Drive in the Cruzer by Crafter pedal series. NAMM-era Crafter material and surviving product records identify these as Crafter-branded guitar pedals. Other Cruzer products are excluded when their primary function is not dirt.
- Crown is retained as a historical catalog identity, but its products are explicitly treated as branded/OEM records. Effects Database identifies the Crown Fuzz and Treble Booster as a late-1960s Applied Audio Products Inc. OEM example and notes multiple other brandings. The Crown FW-3 is a vintage fuzz/wah/volume multi-effect from the same broader Applied Audio lineage. Crown Super Fuzz is cataloged as a Japanese OEM fuzz, while W-Fuzz is an 8-transistor fuzz-wah. Wah-only products Crown Crier and Crown Wah Wah are excluded.
- Collins CDT-1 Distortion was identified in the Effects Database Collins Pro Series catalog and added under the existing canonical Collins identity rather than creating a duplicate builder.

## Exclusions / non-additions

- Coleman Super Boost and The Wizard are excluded because they are boost-first products.
- Crate Chorus, Flanger, and Super Phaser are excluded as non-dirt.
- Cruzer chorus, delay, tremolo, and other non-dirt products are excluded.
- Crown Crier and Crown Wah Wah are wah-only. No separate canonical identities are created for the Applied Audio, Japanese OEM, or alternate-brand manufacturing lineages in this block.
- Countryman Associates, Cooper FX, Coxx, Crowe Effects, Crowella Effects, Covington Guitar Effects, Castle Instruments, Crab Guitar Effects, and similar C-section candidates reviewed in this pass do not contribute a dirt-primary product with sufficient evidence, so they remain pending/excluded.
