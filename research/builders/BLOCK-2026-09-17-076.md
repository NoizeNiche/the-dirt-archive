# Block 076 — Blackstar Amplification

## Builder
**Blackstar Amplification**

## Verification basis
Primary and authoritative sources reviewed:
- Blackstar official HT-series pedal catalog
- Blackstar official LT-series pedal catalog
- Blackstar official Dept. 10 product pages
- Blackstar manufacturer-authored product manuals
- Blackstar official product documentation / declarations

The active repository contained no prior Blackstar match before this block was added. Blackstar Amplification is treated as the builder identity for the pedals sold under the Blackstar brand, including its HT, LT, and Dept. 10 pedal families.

The reviewed manufacturer material supports a substantial historical dirt catalog. Dirt categories are assigned from explicit product names, manufacturer category descriptions, or clearly documented functional modes. Generic references to gain, saturation, breakup, or distortion-like behavior do not by themselves create an extra category.

## Overdrive
Current / manufacturer-supported identities:
- Dept. 10 Dual Drive
- Dept. 10 Dual Distortion

Historical / manufacturer-supported identities:
- HT-DRIVE
- HT-DUAL
- HT-METAL
- LT-DRIVE
- LT-DUAL

Verification notes:
- **HT-DRIVE** is explicitly presented by Blackstar as PURE VALVE OVERDRIVE and delivers gain from warm boost to screaming valve saturation. It is retained as Overdrive only.
- **HT-DUAL** is explicitly presented as a valve distortion pedal whose Channel 1 can be used as a boost or overdrive, while Channel 2 supplies distortion. It therefore receives both Overdrive and Distortion below.
- **HT-METAL** is explicitly described as a valve distortion pedal with a Clean/Overdrive switch on Channel 1. It therefore receives both Overdrive and Distortion below.
- **LT-DRIVE** is explicitly presented by Blackstar as a drive pedal whose range runs from warm boost to screaming lead saturation. It is retained as Overdrive only.
- **LT-DUAL** is explicitly described as a two-channel distortion pedal with Channel 1 moving from clean to boost to overdrive. It therefore receives both Overdrive and Distortion below.
- **Dept. 10 Dual Drive** is explicitly presented by Blackstar as a valve overdrive pedal / preamp. Its manufacturer documentation also describes channels that move through overdrive and distortion. It therefore receives both Overdrive and Distortion below.
- **Dept. 10 Dual Distortion** is explicitly presented by Blackstar as a valve distortion pedal and also as an overdrive pedal / preamp. Its Channel 2 uses Overdrive 1 and Overdrive 2 voices. It therefore receives both Overdrive and Distortion below.

## Distortion
Current / manufacturer-supported identities:
- Dept. 10 Dual Drive
- Dept. 10 Dual Distortion

Historical / manufacturer-supported identities:
- HT-DIST
- HT-DISTX
- HT-DUAL
- HT-METAL
- LT-DIST
- LT-DUAL
- LT-METAL

Verification notes:
- **HT-DIST** is explicitly presented as PURE VALVE DISTORTION. It remains Distortion only despite manual language describing the Gain control as moving from overdrive to distortion, because the product-level manufacturer identity is a distortion pedal and the archive avoids promoting generic control-range wording to a second category.
- **HT-DISTX** is explicitly presented as PURE VALVE FILTH and as an ultra-high-gain pedal. It is retained as Distortion only.
- **HT-DUAL** is explicitly a valve distortion pedal and supplies distortion through Channel 2, while Channel 1 can operate as boost or overdrive. It receives both categories.
- **HT-METAL** is explicitly a valve distortion pedal with a Clean/Overdrive mode on Channel 1 and super-high-gain lead modes. It receives both categories.
- **LT-DIST** is explicitly described as classic high-gain distortion. It remains Distortion only.
- **LT-DUAL** is explicitly titled TWO CHANNEL DISTORTION and combines an overdrive channel with higher-gain distortion/lead operation. It receives both categories.
- **LT-METAL** is explicitly designed for aggressive high-gain tones and is presented within Blackstar's LT distortion family. It remains Distortion only.
- **Dept. 10 Dual Drive** is documented as a valve overdrive pedal with multiple clean and distortion voices and channels that move through overdrive to full distorted lead tones. It receives both categories.
- **Dept. 10 Dual Distortion** is documented as a valve distortion pedal with Clean, Crunch, and Overdrive voices and as an overdrive pedal / preamp on the current manufacturer page. It receives both categories.

## Category-overlap decisions
- **HT-DRIVE**: Overdrive only. Manufacturer category is explicit Overdrive.
- **HT-DIST**: Distortion only. Product-level identity is explicit Distortion; generic Gain-control overdrive wording is not sufficient for a second category.
- **HT-DISTX**: Distortion only. Manufacturer presents it as ultra-high-gain filth / distortion rather than as a separately named overdrive product.
- **HT-DUAL**: Overdrive + Distortion. Manufacturer explicitly says Channel 1 can be used as a boost or overdrive and Channel 2 delivers distortion.
- **HT-METAL**: Overdrive + Distortion. Manufacturer explicitly includes a Clean/Overdrive switch plus high-gain distortion operation.
- **LT-DRIVE**: Overdrive only. Manufacturer product category is Drive/overdrive.
- **LT-DIST**: Distortion only. Manufacturer presents it as classic high-gain distortion; generic overdrive control-range wording is not promoted separately.
- **LT-DUAL**: Overdrive + Distortion. Manufacturer explicitly describes Channel 1 as clean/boost/overdrive and Channel 2 as higher-gain distortion/lead.
- **LT-METAL**: Distortion only. Manufacturer presents it as aggressive high-gain and as part of the LT distortion family.
- **Dept. 10 Dual Drive**: Overdrive + Distortion. Current manufacturer material calls it a valve overdrive pedal while describing clean and distortion voices and an overdrive-to-distortion range.
- **Dept. 10 Dual Distortion**: Overdrive + Distortion. Current manufacturer material calls it a valve distortion pedal and also an overdrive pedal / preamp, with explicit Overdrive 1 / Overdrive 2 modes.

## Historical / variant handling
- HT, LT, and earlier valve-pedal generations are retained because the archive includes historical dirt products.
- Product generations and manufacturer revisions are consolidated when they represent the same named functional identity. Cosmetic finishes and stock/presentation variants do not create additional identities.
- **HT-DISTX** remains a separate identity from HT-DIST because Blackstar gave it a distinct product name and positioned it as the higher-gain "filth" model.
- **HT-DUAL** remains a separate identity because it is a distinct two-channel product rather than a revision of HT-DIST.
- **LT-DUAL** remains a separate identity because Blackstar presented it as a distinct two-channel product in the LT family.
- Dept. 10 Dual Drive and Dept. 10 Dual Distortion remain separate named products. The manufacturer currently lists both as distinct products and differentiates their voice sets.
- Internal mode selections, Cab Rig settings, effects-loop functions, USB audio, and preamp/direct-recording features remain secondary features and do not create additional dirt identities.

## Exclusions
- **HT-BOOST**: boost product, not promoted into the target Overdrive/Distortion/Fuzz census even though its Gain control can push an amplifier into overdrive and distortion.
- **Dept. 10 Boost**: boost product, excluded for the same reason.
- Amplifier models, footcontrollers, software/modeling effects, cabinet simulation, utility products, and other non-dirt Blackstar products: outside target taxonomy.
- Generic references to gain, saturation, breakup, grit, or distortion without sufficient product-level classification: excluded as secondary categories.
- Cloning, schematics, internal construction, component details, and reverse-engineering material: outside scope.
