# The Dirt Archive — Builder Research Block 053

## Builder
**Audiolithe — Christophe & Gaëtan**

## Verification basis
Primary manufacturer sources used:
- Audiolithe official site and current guitar/bass pedal catalog
- Individual official product pages

Authoritative corroboration:
- Guitar Pedal X's 2024 London Synth & Pedal Expo report identifies Audiolithe as a builder collaboration between Christophe and Gaëtan and describes its then-current five-pedal range.

The manufacturer presents Audiolithe as a French effects builder with original circuits, and its current catalog explicitly places products into Overdrive/Fuzz categories. The census uses the manufacturer's own product labels rather than inferring categories from gain descriptions.

## Overdrive

- The Empress Bass

## Distortion
- No standalone Distortion identity promoted in this block.

## Fuzz
- Doomer Fuzz

## Product-level verification notes
- **Extinction Drive** is explicitly presented by Audiolithe as an Overdrive/Fuzz product. The official product page describes it as a two-channel saturation design with overdrive, boost, fuzz and octave-up functionality. It therefore belongs in both Overdrive and Fuzz.
- **Doomer Fuzz** is explicitly listed by Audiolithe as Fuzz. It is retained under Fuzz only.
- **La Montagne** is explicitly described by Audiolithe as a two-stage Fuzz pedal. It is retained under Fuzz only.
- **The Empress Bass** is explicitly categorized by Audiolithe as Overdrive. Its product identity is a bass drive, so it is retained under Overdrive.
- **Chaosculpt Bass** is explicitly categorized by Audiolithe as Fuzz. It is retained under Fuzz only.
- Audiolithe's current catalog does not expose a separate finished pedal identity explicitly categorized as Distortion. Descriptions involving saturation or aggressive drive were not used to invent a Distortion entry.

## Scope decisions / exclusions
- **Echosmos** is excluded because it is a delay/modulation/oscillation pedal, not a target dirt pedal.
- Accessories and non-pedal merchandise are excluded.
- Bass dirt products are included because the archive's target categories are effects types, not instrument restrictions.
- Boost and octave functions are not counted as independent builder identities.
- No Distortion category was forced from descriptive language alone.
