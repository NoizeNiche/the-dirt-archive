# The Dirt Archive — Builder → Pedals Research Block 018

Date: September 16, 2026
Scope: Builder → Pedals only

This block records builder/product relationships only. Product names are taken from current manufacturer catalogs and official product pages where available, with official legacy pages used when needed. This is a research checkpoint, not an exhaustive lifetime catalog.

## 1. Electronic Audio Experiments (EAE)

Builder / brand: Electronic Audio Experiments

### Current dirt products verified from the official EAE catalog
- Model feT — preamp / drive product
- Greathammer — bass-focused overdrive
- Surveyor — distortion / saturation preamp-style dirt product
- Citadel — preamp-style overdrive
- Limelight — dual-channel overdrive
- Longsword — drive through massive distortion
- Halberd — transistor overdrive
- Glaive — octave fuzz / fuzz-distortion
- The Bard — overdrive

### Legacy / collaborative dirt identities verified from the official EAE site
- Dagger — drive / overdrive; discontinued
- Dude Incredible — combined drive and fuzz/distortion product; legacy identity
- 0xEAE Boost — boost/overdrive collaboration with Obstructures
- 0xEAE Bass Driver — bass drive collaboration with Obstructures
- 0xEAE Fuzz — fuzz collaboration with Obstructures
- Eldritch Blast — intermodulation fuzz collaboration with Mask Audio Electronics
- Kerria Lacca — collaboration with Electrofoods combining a percolator-derived channel with the Surveyor circuit

Notes:
- EAE's official pedal catalog currently lists Model feT, Greathammer, Surveyor, Citadel, Limelight, Longsword, Glaive, Halberd, and The Bard among its pedals; non-dirt effects are excluded.
- EAE's own product pages explicitly identify Halberd as overdrive, Longsword as spanning clean boost through massive distortion, Limelight as a dual-channel overdrive, Citadel as overdrive, and Glaive as an octave fuzz occupying the fuzz/distortion intersection.
- The EAE legacy page identifies Dagger and Dude Incredible as discontinued/legacy products and lists the 0xEAE and Eldritch Blast collaborations. Only dirt-relevant identities are retained here.
- Surveyor is retained because the official EAE page identifies its transformer-loaded gain stage as a distortion generator and its gain control as overdrive/saturation.
- Collaborative products remain EAE builder relationships in this census, with the collaborating builder named in notes rather than splitting one named product into separate identities.

## 3. Black Mass Electronics

Builder / brand: Black Mass Electronics

### Current dirt products verified from the official Black Mass catalog
- 1312 Distortion V3 — distortion
- Dual 1312 Distortion — dual distortion product
- The First Herald — fuzz / boost compound dirt pedal
- The First Herald (Big Box) — named catalog identity
- Ceremonial Show of Force — overdrive
- Kill Chain — fuzz / fuzz-tortion

Notes:
- Black Mass's official current pedal collection lists 1312 Distortion V3, The First Herald, Ceremonial Show of Force, Kill Chain, and Dual 1312 Distortion as pedal products.
- The official 1312 page identifies the 1312 as a distortion product.
- The official Ceremonial Show of Force page identifies it as an all-analog discrete overdrive.
- The official Kill Chain page identifies it as an aggressive fuzz and describes one setting as fuzz-tortion.
- The official First Herald page identifies an independent fuzz circuit plus a colored boost stage. It is retained as a compound fuzz product rather than split into separate catalog identities.
- Malicious Intent is not included because the manufacturer presents it primarily as a signal booster rather than an overdrive, distortion, or fuzz pedal; the page mentions grit but does not make it a core dirt-product classification.
