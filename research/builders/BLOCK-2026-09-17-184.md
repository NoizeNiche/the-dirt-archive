# Builder -> Pedals Research Block 184

## Scope

Continued the C-section Builder -> Pedals census after Block 183 with dirt-only inclusion: overdrive, distortion, fuzz. Existing builders were checked against the canonical index before creating new identities. Utility, modulation, delay, routing, compressor-only, boost-only, and non-dirt products were excluded.

Historical labels and OEM relationships are preserved where relevant rather than being presented as independent circuit manufacturers without evidence.

## Evidence / classification notes

- ColorTone Fx's Effects Database brand record contains eight products and explicitly notes that ColorTone Fx is now Pedal Tank. The dirt-bearing products are Gainer and OD-909 Overdrive Pro; the remaining catalog is switching/noise-gate/chorus/phase utility or modulation. Effects Database's taxonomy places both Gainer and OD-909 in the pedal catalog, with Gainer classified as overdrive. Preserve the ColorTone Fx -> Pedal Tank lineage rather than treating the later name as an unrelated builder.
- Colortone Pedals is a current Sydney, Australia builder whose own site describes its pedals as hand built in small batches. The Parasite is the documented overdrive in the reviewed current catalog. Roundhouse is a tremolo/vibe/reverb product with overdrive in its feature set, but it is not treated as a dirt-primary pedal here.
- Columbus has an eleven-product Effects Database catalog. The dirt subset is DST-5 Distortion, MTL-5 Ultra Metal, OVD-5 Overdrive and TDR-5 Tube Driver. The TDR-5 is cataloged as overdrive, even though the product description describes its range as overdrive into distortion. Effects Database also documents that the Columbus TDR-5 belongs to a Japanese OEM family shared with Axtron, Vision, Profile+ and Series 10/Aria, so this identity is explicitly a branded/OEM record rather than a claim that Columbus designed the underlying circuit.
- Commune's catalog contains Senturion Crazy Booster, Modern Factor, Preamp 500 and Real Leveler. Contemporary Japanese coverage describes Crazy Booster as fuzz and Modern Factor as overdrive; historical catalog/retailer material describes the Preamp 500 as an overdrive. Effects Database currently classifies the latter products in the boost/preamp area, so those classification differences are preserved in the notes rather than silently erased. Real Leveler is excluded as a clean booster.
- Commune/ECA's catalog contains Screem and Screem Mk-II; both are retained as overdrive records. ECA is retained in the builder name because the historical catalog is explicitly presented as Commune/ECA rather than an unrelated company.
- Companion is preserved as a historical brand identity with a Shin-Ei OEM/manufacturer relationship. The Shin-Ei official site identifies the Companion FY-2 as a reproduction of the original 1960s Companion fuzz; Effects Database's Companion catalog includes the FY-2 and FY-6 Super Fuzz among the historical dirt products. This avoids creating a false impression that Companion was a separate modern manufacturer while still preserving the catalog label used on the original pedals.
- Collins' XXX-1 Pro Series contains CBB-1 Pro Best Bass, COD-1 Pro Over Drive and CSD-1 Pro Super Dust. CSD-1 is directly classified as fuzz by Effects Database. COD-1 is retained as overdrive based on its model name and its documented relationship to the Audiobox/Eleca overdrive family.
- Colfax Recorders was added by Effects Database as a distinct U.S. brand. Its documented dirt lineup includes Zephyr Overdrive and Porcelain Fuzz; Apache Rose is a preamp record and is therefore excluded from this dirt census.
- Cole Music Co.'s ParaDrive was documented by Premier Guitar as a parametric overdrive released in 2019.

## Exclusions / non-additions

- Combustible Man Effects remains pending. Effects Database documents the brand as a historical entry, but the reviewed current evidence did not provide a sufficiently clear product catalog to create reliable pedal rows.
- Color Audio remains excluded because its Cassette Preamp is cataloged as boost/preamp rather than dirt-primary.
- Colortone Pedals Roundhouse remains excluded because its primary function is modulation/reverb with an overdrive section.
- Commune Real Leveler is excluded as clean boost.
- Columbus' non-dirt delay, chorus, compressor, flanger, octaver and phaser products are excluded.
- Companion wah/fuzz-wah and utility products are excluded from this block to avoid over-expanding the dirt taxonomy from multi-effect functions.
- Collins CBB-1 Pro Best Bass is held pending a clearer dirt-primary classification.
- Colfax Recorders Apache Rose Preamp is held as preamp rather than overdrive.
- Collateral FX remains excluded because its Effects Database record contains zero products.
