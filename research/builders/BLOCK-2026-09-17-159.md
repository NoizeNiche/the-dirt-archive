Date: September 17, 2026
Scope: Alphabetical Scrape B, continuation after Blacet Research.

This block records Builder → Pedal relationships only. Existing canonical builders are expanded rather than duplicated. Boost-only products and non-dirt effects are excluded.

## Black Arts Toneworks — existing canonical builder
- Ritual — Fuzz
- Oath — Fuzz

Ritual is documented as a discontinued one-knob fuzz. Oath is documented as a discontinued fuzz derived from the Revelation SuperBass circuit. Neither is an additional builder identity. Sources: https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/blackarts/ritual and https://www.effectsdatabase.com/model/blackarts/oath

## Blackbox Music Electronics
- Inferno — Fuzz

Vintage Guitar's review identifies the Blackbox Inferno as a fuzz, with a hybrid germanium/silicon circuit combining vintage fuzz character and high-gain overdrive response. The same review covers other Blackbox pedals, but their dirt classifications were not sufficiently explicit in the indexed material for this pass, so they are not forced into the census. Source: https://www.vintageguitar.com/3059/blackbox-effects-inferno-x-ray-cobalt-ultraviolet-2-oxygen/

## Research notes

- Black Arts Toneworks, Bixonic, and other previously represented builders are expanded under their existing identities rather than assigned new builder IDs.
- Blackbox Music Electronics is intentionally represented by Inferno only in this block because its other indexed products did not have sufficiently explicit dirt classifications in the material reviewed.
- Black CAT Amps, Black Cat Pedals, Black Country Customs, Blackbox Music Electronics, Blackstone Appliances, Blakemore Effects, BLAMMO! Electronics, BMF Effects, Bob Burt Cabinets & Pedals, and Boo Instruments are new canonical builder identities for the master builder index.
- The archive continues to treat a product that contains a documented overdrive/distortion/fuzz section as qualifying for the relevant dirt function, while boost-only products remain excluded.
