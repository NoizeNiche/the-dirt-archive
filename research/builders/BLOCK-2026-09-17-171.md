## Evidence summary
- Cave Pedals official site identifies FETish as an all-JFET preamp overdrive/fuzz and its Quad as a classic overdrive.
- Casey Gooby is documented by Effects Database and period gear sources for Bag Of Dicks and Organ Donor fuzzes.
- Carl Martin's current official support/products documentation identifies the listed overdrive, distortion, and fuzz models.
- Cedar Drive is documented as an original low/mid-gain overdrive by Pedal of the Day.
- Chicago Stompworks' official catalog documents Gray Speck, Greeny, Fat Bald Guy, Legitimate Beef, and Black Fudge as dirt pedals; Effects Database provides additional catalog cross-checking.
- Chowny's official pedal catalog lists Fuzzster Bass Fuzz.
- Coffin Case's Blood Drive and Batula Fuzz are documented by Effects Database, Audiofanzine, and Premier Guitar.
- ColdCraft's product history is documented in an Effects Database interview and contemporaneous builder interview, including Sweet Cream, Fiesta Fuzz, Ramrod, Sweet Kiwi, Black Shuck, Cascade, Black Dynamite, Fiesta Fuzz MkII, and Fusion Fuzz.
- Creation Audio Labs' official product pages document Holy Fire Classic, Holy Fire 9, and Grizzly Bass as overdrive/distortion products.
- Crews Maniac Sound's official site and Effects Database interview document Genius Over Drive, Quadrive, Valve Drive, and Taco Fuzz.

## Exclusions / boundary notes
- Coggins Audio was not created as a separate canonical builder because its current site identifies Dinosaural as its earlier manufacturing brand and Coggins Audio as the continuing company; this is treated as a naming/company transition rather than a separate builder identity for now.
- Chowny's Bass-Mosphere Chorus/Reverb and Pitchcraft Octave were excluded because they are not dirt pedals.
- Creation Audio Labs clean boosts, buffers, DI/reamp tools, and tone-shaping utilities were excluded.
- Carl Martin utility, modulation, switching, compressor, and power products were excluded.
- Non-dirt Cave, ColdCraft, and Crews products were excluded.
