## Research notes

- BEZfx and BFX (Bonansone FX) remain unadded in this pass because the available indexed evidence did not produce a sufficiently clear dirt-pedal catalog.
- BFFX Boutique Pedals is a Brazilian boutique builder; archived product references document Demon D, Kairen, Sublime, and Guitarlab1 as gain pedals, with Guitarlab1 spanning overdrive and distortion.
- Bias is the vintage Japanese Coron-associated Bias brand in Effects Database, not the modern Positive Grid software/hardware brand. The dirt products recorded here are BDS-1 Distortion, Bias Distortion, and Bias Over Drive.
- Big Bite is documented as a combined boost/overdrive/distortion pedal, so the archive records its Overdrive and Distortion dirt functions.
- Big Ear NYC's current catalog identifies LOAF and Slice Of Pie as fuzzes, Woodcutter as distortion, and Black Betty as fuzz. Frank is documented as a low-gain overdrive.
- Big Game Pedals products recorded here are directly categorized as distortion, overdrive, or fuzz in product/database references. The octave-focused Chaka is excluded under the archive's existing octave-only convention.
- Big Lloyde's Phat Axx is documented as both overdrive and distortion; Overdose is documented as distortion.
- Big Monk Electronic Device Co. is treated as a distinct builder from its earlier VanderScott Electronic Device Co. name. The Pompeii is a fuzz/distortion pedal; Monk Boost is recorded under overdrive and distortion based on documented gain functions.
- Big Tone Music Brewery's Effects Database catalog includes Anthology Fuzz, BTMB Overdrive, Classic Overdrive+, Germanium Fuzz Master, Germanium FuzzBender, MOSFET Overdrive & Boost, Parametric Overdrive, Royal Beaver, and Six Of Swords. Royal Beaver is explicitly capable of overdrive, distortion, and high-gain fuzz; Six Of Swords spans fuzz and overdrive. Classic Distortion is retained as a separately documented distortion model.
- Big White Monkey Amps' Howler is a distortion pedal and Spider 808 is an overdrive pedal.
- Bigfoot Engineering's current catalog documents King Fuzz variants and special Germanium/anniversary models. The dedicated Germanium Octave is excluded under the archive's octave-only convention.
- Bigfoot F.X. has five products in Effects Database; MagnaVibe is non-dirt, while Dynamic Distortion Device, Fuzz Hog, Spaghetti Western Fuzz, and The Humongous Box are dirt products. The Humongous Box appears in both distortion and fuzz listings.
- BigNoise Amplification currently documents Dark Distortion and Full Blast Overdrive, and its current overdrive catalog also documents Bulls Eye Overdrive.
- Billionaire (by Danelectro) is retained as the Effects Database product-brand identity for its Trillion Dollar Fuzz listing.
