# YARP Breadcrumb 108

## Purpose
Australian micro-builder pass with strict cross-reference and duplicate suppression. The pass revisits the existing Australian corridor and promotes only a historically distinct builder identity where the repository did not already contain it.

## Cross-reference / dedupe decisions

- **Tym Guitars** was already present as a canonical Australian builder. Its FY-2-related material is a modern homage to the Honey/Shin-Ei lineage and was not treated as evidence for a new historical OEM identity.
- **Claybridge** was already present in the research layer with multiple generation/branding flags. No new builder record was created.
- **Fi-Sonic** was not found in the repository's indexed search results or the canonical census snippets reviewed. It is therefore promoted as a new historical builder identity, but only at the builder/discovery layer.
- **Plain Fi-Sonic Fuzz-Blender vs Fuzz-Blender MkIV** are treated as product/generation states within one Fi-Sonic lineage. Unknown MkII/MkIII names remain discovery flags, not catalog records.
- **McPherson** remains outside the promoted builder set because current evidence describes an individually made Australian fuzz box associated with an amplifier shop rather than a documented commercial pedal catalog. It remains a future research candidate rather than being inflated into a manufacturer record.

## Evidence notes

Tone Machines Blog's dedicated Fi-Sonic investigation reports period advertisements for Fi-Sonic fuzz products in September and December 1965 and documents a surviving plain Fuzz-Blender plus a distinct MkIV state. Effects Database lists both Fuzz-Blender and Fuzz-Blender MkIV. OzValveAmps identifies Phil Dreoni as the creator of the Fi-Sonic range and records a surviving MkIV, with Dreoni confirming that it was one of his creations. These sources make Fi-Sonic strong enough for a historical builder record while still leaving the exact production chronology and any missing MkII/MkIII states unresolved.
