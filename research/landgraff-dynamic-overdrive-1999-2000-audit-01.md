# Landgraff Effects: 1999-2000 Audit 01

Research status: VERIFIED_CORE_MODEL_WITH_OPEN_CATALOG
Scope: founding-era boutique dirt history and surviving product evidence. No circuit reconstruction.

## Identity
John Landgraff was a Florida-based handmade pedal builder. The strongest available secondary evidence places the Dynamic Overdrive (DOD) and related handmade products in the 1999-2000 period.

## Core products identified
- Dynamic Overdrive (DOD): core Landgraff overdrive model. Surviving references place its beginning around 1999, with long-lived production continuing into the 2010s.
- MO'D (MOD): documented as a separate Landgraff distortion product in the late-1990s/2000 period.
- Distortion BOX (BOX): documented as a separate Landgraff dirt product.
- Blues Boxes: family-level reference in contemporary historical accounts, with exact model enumeration still open.
- Clean Booster products: boundary/utility family and not part of the core dirt catalog except where an individual product combines gain functionality.
- Pedalman 818 Overdrive Pro: a Landgraff-associated product documented as a 2001-made unit. Preserve it as a separate product/lineage node rather than folding it into Dynamic Overdrive.

## Production characteristics relevant to future specimen work
Historical accounts describe individually handmade and hand-painted pedals, with distinctive backplate lettering and Japanese export visibility. Reverb currently catalogs Dynamic Overdrive specimens as 1999-2015, while Japanese historical commentary places the brand's first major Japanese exposure in the early 2000s.

## Dating cautions
The 1999 start date is well-supported for the Dynamic Overdrive by multiple secondary references, but the exact month and earliest serial sequence remain unresolved. Do not infer a complete 1999 catalog from later product listings.

## Significance
Landgraff belongs in the bridge between the 1990s boutique revival and the early-2000s premium overdrive market. It should be kept distinct from later derivative or Landgraff-inspired products.
