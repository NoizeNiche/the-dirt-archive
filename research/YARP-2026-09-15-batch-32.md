# YARP 2026-09-15 Batch 32

## Focus
Firstman / Hillwood and Multivox Big Jam production relationship.

## Findings

### Firstman / Hillwood
Current specialist historical coverage identifies Firstman Electronics, also known as Hillwood, as a Japanese OEM manufacturer founded by Kazuo Morioka in the 1970s. This strengthens the existing Firstman/Hillwood canonical lineage from a discovery record toward an evidence-qualified manufacturer/OEM role.

### Multivox Big Jam
The late-1970s Multivox Big Jam line was an American-marketed product family using Japanese manufacture. The Big Jam SE-8 Distortion is documented as made in Japan by Firstman for Nihon-Hammond Ltd and distributed in the United States under Multivox. New 2026 historical coverage independently identifies Firstman/Hillwood as the OEM manufacturer behind the Big Jam line and identifies the Big Jam Distortion as an important 1978-era model.

### Catalog implication
The archive must keep at least four roles distinct: Firstman/Hillwood as physical manufacturer/OEM, Nihon-Hammond as product-company/customer relationship, Multivox as U.S. marketed/distribution brand, and Big Jam as the retail product family. The Big Jam Distortion and SE-8 are retained as separate model records until primary catalog reconciliation establishes whether the names represent successive or market-specific states.
