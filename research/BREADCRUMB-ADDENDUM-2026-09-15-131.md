# YARP Breadcrumb 131

## Purpose
Builder-discovery-only pass after the scope reset. No factory attribution work was promoted unless it identified a distinct historical builder/manufacturer entity.

## New builder record

- `BUILDER-122`: Wilsic Electronics Ltd. / Wilsic Sound, Doncaster, UK. Preserved as a distinct builder/manufacturer lineage associated with Charlie Ramskir. Historical sources document Wilsic's own-branded fuzz and its production work for Hornby Skewes, plus contemporary Wilsic Sound effects kits.

## Cross-reference / dedupe decisions

- Do not create a separate builder for Hornby Skewes from the Zonk evidence solely because Hornby Skewes marketed the products. Wilsic is the builder lead; Hornby Skewes remains a separate marketed/distribution identity until independent manufacturing evidence warrants its own builder record.
- Do not create a separate builder for the Wilsic-branded wah-wah kit merely because it appears in contemporary advertisements. Treat it as a product lead under Wilsic.
- Gary Hurst, Pepe Rush, Sola Sound, JEN and other already-known builders remain unchanged in this pass.

## Evidence notes

Fuzzboxes documents that later Zonk II pedals were built by Charlie Ramskir's Wilsic Sound and that Wilsic Sound also offered a fuzz under its own brand. Contemporary Practical Wireless advertisements from 1971-1972 identify Wilsic Electronics Ltd., Doncaster, Yorks., and advertise Wilsic Sound effects kits including a wah-wah pedal kit. This is sufficient to preserve Wilsic as a builder discovery independent of Hornby Skewes' marketed identity.
