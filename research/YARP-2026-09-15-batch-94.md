# YARP 2026-09-15 Batch 94: Kimberly / LIMMCO / Applied / UMI Manufacturing States

## Scope
Builder-first reconciliation of the Kimberly Fuzz Box and Volume Expander across its documented production states and related marketed identities.

## Findings
- Effects Database distinguishes a v1 Kimberly unit attributed to LIMMCO from a later v2 attributed to Applied Audio Products.
- Tone Machines research describes LIMMCO as the Long Island Musical Merchandise Company, a wholesale/distribution and house-brand entity rather than a single enduring pedal factory.
- LIMMCO's owner, Bob Seidman, reported that Kimberly production initially consumed warehouse resources and was later moved to third-party manufacture.
- A later redesign is attributed by specialist research to United Musical Industries (UMI), which subsequently produced the Kimberly line through the end of its run.
- Related marketed states include Apollo no. 843 and Conrad Buzz Box & Volume Expander. Effects Database connects these later states to UMI.
- Applied Audio Products remains a separate manufacturing node. The current record does not treat Applied and UMI as one company merely because both appear in the Kimberly lineage.

## Evidence posture
The chronology is strong enough to model multiple manufacturing states, but exact dates for every handoff remain unresolved. The archive should retain v1=LIMMCO, v2=Applied, later Apollo/Conrad=UMI as evidence-qualified relationships, not as proof of a single unchanged circuit or factory.
