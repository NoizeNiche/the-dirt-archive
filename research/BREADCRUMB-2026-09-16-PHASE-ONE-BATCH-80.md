## Evidence notes
TKI Technology / Lazeman has a durable Korean historical trail. Effects Database documents Lazeman Mobster as a named overdrive product published in 2006, while Korean player and dealer archives preserve Lazeman Overdrive and the later Over Tune model. A Korean catalog source identifies TKI as the manufacturer for Lazeman Over Tune, and an Equipboard summary describes the Lazeman catalog as handmade in Korea with overdrive and distortion products. The surviving primary company history remains thin, so the archive keeps the lineage provisional rather than promoting it to canonical status.

3Why FX is a documented Taiwan effects-making platform founded by Alex Lee in 2012 and based at 435 Art Studio. Historical community records show more than twenty pedals available for experimentation and explicitly identify classic-effect clones including Hotcake, Fuzz Face, Tonebender Mk II, Superfuzz/Shinei Fuzz-Wah, Dinosaur Jr Big Muff, Fender Blender and Zendrive. Because the strongest surviving dirt evidence is explicitly clone-based, these objects remain deferred from the central original-design catalog. The maker/platform identity itself is retained for future research into any distinct original 3Why designs that can be recovered.

## Boundary / scope notes
- TKI/Lazeman is one maker lineage; individual version names remain product states under the same identity.
- TKI's current/legacy relationship to signal-routing products does not automatically expand the dirt catalog beyond the named drive products evidenced here.
- 3Why FX is preserved as a real historical Taiwan maker/platform, but its documented classic-effect clones are kept in the relationship queue rather than used to inflate original builder/product counts.
- Do not infer that other Korean or Taiwan pedals carrying visually similar labels share TKI/3Why manufacture without direct evidence.
- No schematics, PCB layouts, gutshots, cloning instructions, or complete BOM data are published.
