# YARP 2026-09-15 Batch 62: Ideal Fuzz Master / Japanese Wedge OEM Corridor

## Scope
Reconcile the newly documented Ideal Fuzz Master with the existing Sekova, Greco, Apollo, Tele-Star, LRE and related Japanese wedge-fuzz records without promoting a plausible OEM theory to a proven factory attribution.

## Ideal Fuzz Master
Effects Database documents the Ideal Fuzz Master as a late-1960s Japanese OEM fuzz distributed under the Ideal brand. Its wedge enclosure, two-knob Attack/Volume layout, hardwired cables and eyelet-board construction match the eyelet-board version of the Sekova Model 59 Distortion Box.

Tone Machines Blog's 2025 investigation identifies the previously unbranded mystery Fuzz Master as Ideal and traces the Ideal guitar brand to a New York City business with a long importing history. The article considers Matsumoku, Teisco, Fujigen and other Japanese guitar factories as possible contracting/manufacturing participants, with Nisshin Onpa/Maxon suggested as a possible electronics subcontractor. These remain working hypotheses, not factory-proof conclusions.

## OEM relationship status
The archive should therefore record:
- Ideal = marketed/imported brand state.
- Ideal Fuzz Master = evidence-qualified Japanese OEM product.
- Strong physical/construction relationship = Sekova eyelet-board Model 59 family.
- Possible common factory with other Japanese wedge fuzzes = unresolved.
- Nisshin Onpa/Maxon = candidate electronics/manufacturing participant, not established as the universal maker of all wedge fuzzes.

## Broader wedge-fuzz corridor
The same research connects the wedge family to Greco No. 35, Sekova Model 59, Apollo and Tele-Star-branded states and notes that several foreign-brand effects may have been ordered through Japanese distributors as part of larger instrument/effect export arrangements. This is useful for the archive because it explains why identical or near-identical products can appear under unrelated retail names without proving a single manufacturer for every example.

## Historical significance
Ideal is important less because of its rarity alone than because it strengthens the case that the late-1960s Japanese fuzz market operated through layered OEM/export contracting. The Archive should preserve that network as a relationship graph rather than collapse every wedge fuzz into a single factory.

## Evidence state
**Evidence-qualified:** Ideal Fuzz Master existence; late-1960s Japanese OEM context; close construction relationship to Sekova Model 59; Ideal's historical New York/importer identity.

**Unresolved:** exact pedal factory; exact electronic subcontractor; chronological order of the multiple wedge-fuzz construction states; whether the same factory made every Sekova/Greco/Apollo/Tele-Star variant.
