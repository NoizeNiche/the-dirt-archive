## Historical findings

- JEN Elettronica is confirmed as the physical Italian builder for the Jen Fuzz and Jen Tone Bender family, with Elka, Luxor, Nova and Unicord retained as separate marketed/OEM states.
- Schaller Electronic is documented as the physical builder behind its own Fuzz and several marketed OEM states including Höfner No. 539 and Kent 6400; Blackfield is also recorded as a Schaller OEM state.
- Van Hall remains qualified as a Schaller-family candidate pending a more model-specific source.
- Vox V828/V8281 is retained as an Italian path distinct from the British Sola Sound route.
- New source validation identifies an earlier Italian V828 manufacturing state by EME (Elettronica Musicale Europea), followed by later JEN production. This is now recorded as a period-specific manufacturing transition rather than a single-builder attribution.
- Thomas Organ's North American V828 marketed/distribution path remains separate from the physical Italian builder record.
- Later Vox V829 is treated as a successor/revival state, not as a continuation of the original V828 manufacturing record.

## Validation posture

The European reconciliation reinforces the archive rule that shared enclosure, circuit family, brand ownership, distribution, and physical manufacturing are separate evidence dimensions. No blanket brand/factory merges were made.

The EME-to-JEN V828 transition is especially important because it corrects an overly simple "V828 = JEN" attribution for the entire production span.
