## Historical findings

- JEN Elettronica is confirmed as the physical Italian builder for the Jen Fuzz and Jen Tone Bender family, with Elka, Luxor, Nova and Unicord retained as separate marketed/OEM states.
- Schaller Electronic is documented as the physical builder behind its own Fuzz and several marketed OEM states including Höfner No. 539 and Kent 6400; Blackfield is also recorded as a Schaller OEM state.
- Van Hall remains qualified as a Schaller-family candidate pending a more model-specific source.
- Vox V828/V8281 is retained as a JEN-built Italian path, while Thomas Organ / California Vox production remains model-specific and is not inherited across the entire Vox effects catalog.

## Validation posture

The European reconciliation reinforces the archive rule that shared enclosure, circuit family, brand ownership, distribution, and physical manufacturing are separate evidence dimensions. No blanket brand/factory merges were made.
