# Batch 137 Breadcrumb

## Governing Priority
**Builder → Pedals → Photos → Information on the pedal**

## Scope
Japanese boutique and microbuilder census continuation after Batch 136.

## Builders added / strengthened

### JYUGEHM STOMPS
- Canonical Japanese small workshop.
- Official site describes a domestic small factory producing fully hand-wired analog effects.
- Current dirt catalog mapped before deeper pedal archaeology.
- Central: Par Amour; Jamais Vu; ir AVANTE NEW.
- Central-pending: Ciao Bella because it spans drive and booster functions.
- Discovery-only: Stardust Paradise because the official site marks it coming soon without enough product-category/history detail.
- Guitar Magazine 2026 article provides independent builder/profile context and identifies the Osaka-origin brand and professional-guitarist collaboration.

### BARBAROSSA
- Canonical Japanese boutique builder.
- Strong documented gain-pedal family and Birdland distribution trail.
- Central: CHIMAERA; GARGOYLE; URANUS; CRONUS; WIVERN.
- WIVERN remains central as historical/end-of-sale catalog evidence.
- Birdland and Newtool records are used for family relationships and exterior/product evidence.
- Do not split the models merely because of enclosure or catalog-status changes unless substantive model identity warrants it.

### URMA Sound
- Canonical Japanese handmade builder established in 2024.
- Official About page states all pedals are made without PCBs using full point-to-point hand wiring.
- Central: Like A ENIGMA v4.5; Japanium Fuzz 312/100; ZFZ-1A+SP; 16D-1A; Elec Dist.
- Relationship-only: RGM-2A and SHD-1A because booster is the primary identity despite fuzz-capable mode/drive behavior.
- Preserve explicit model version history such as Like A ENIGMA V1-V4.5 as a generation lineage, not automatic separate conceptual families.

## Photo rule
Use exterior/reference photographs from official builder pages and reputable Japanese dealers. Photograph existence is evidence of the physical object, not permission to republish. No gutshots, schematics, PCB diagrams, or BOM/cloning material.

## Research notes
- Keep builder identity, product identity, generation, edition, and relationship records separate.
- Prefer complete catalog enumeration over flagship-only entries.
- Keep ambiguous cross-category products in central-pending until the primary identity is clear.
- Continue Japanese builder/OEM corridor before broadening to Taiwan/Hong Kong/mainland China.
