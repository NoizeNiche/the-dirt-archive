## Evidence basis
- Providence's first-party catalog states its guitar pedals are manufactured in Japan and currently lists ROD-1, SOV-2, SDT-3 and other dirt products. Effects Database preserves the brand history, including the 1998 first Providence overdrive, SOV-1, SDR generations, Heat Blaster generations and Great Deceiver products. A Providence interview with Takeshi Okuno states the company initially made overdrive/distortion pedals before expanding into other effect categories.
- Leqtique documentation identifies Shun Nokina Design and preserves a multi-model dirt lineage. Effects Database documents MAR, MAT, CLD and 9/9; current Leqtiqueshop documentation lists Rochechouart, Redemptionist, Roger, 10/10, Beryl, EDM, 11/11, Caelum OD and 6/6 within the current two-in-one ecosystem. The 2026 Azur release explicitly traces its design development back through Rochechouart/Beryl.
- TRIAL's first-party site describes the brand as handmade in Japan. Its current catalog and Japanese retail records document Bite Distortion, SF-5, Parallel 3 and Smart.OD; Japanese dealer records also preserve Half Gain+ and F-3 Steady. SF-5 has historical documentation reaching back to the early 2010s.

## Duplicate / relationship gate
- Providence generations such as SDR-3/4/5 and HBL-2/3/4 are retained as named product states where catalogs treat them as distinct models, but later normalization should distinguish product generations from conceptual families.
- Leqtique's EVR/current two-in-one options are recorded as named models but are not interpreted as evidence of separate builders. L' remains a connected Shun Nokina-related sub-brand rather than a new canonical builder.
- TRIAL B-1/B-2 clean booster/buffer products remain outside central dirt inventory. Bite Distortion/PREAMP is retained because distortion is its defining function even though it can operate in a preamp-oriented role.
- No schematics, PCB layouts, gutshots, cloning instructions, or complete BOM/component inventories were added.
