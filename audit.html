Selected audit notes

Corrections matter.

The archive is built to be corrected. This page records notable identity conflicts, duplicated labels, contaminated records, and rule changes that affect how the census is interpreted.

Identity audits
DOD D&S / D&S IIThe exact D&S and D&S II evidence reviewed points to Maxon rather than DOD. The DOD catalog record is preserved with an identity conflict flag instead of importing Maxon specifications into the DOD entry.
DOD FX101 / GrindThe archive contains both “FX101 Grind Rectifying Overdrive” and “Grind (FX101)”. They appear to be two labels for the same FX101 product and are flagged for catalog-level consolidation rather than silently merged.
DOD FX66The archive contains both “Flashback Fuzz (FX66)” and “FX66 Flashback Fuzz”. They appear to be aliases of the same model and are flagged for catalog-level consolidation.
Drunk Beaver MykolaivContemporary coverage associates Pedal Drop #35 with “Mykolaiv Soft Blooming” and also uses “Mykolaiv Broadcast Attack”. The relationship is recorded as unresolved until the naming history can be established.
Research corrections
DOD FX70 Metal XA stale record contained text about the FX69 Grunge. That unrelated material was removed so FX70 is no longer described using FX69 controls or history.
Photo coverageThe site now distinguishes an image that is merely reachable by URL from a photo stored in the archive's local pedal asset path. Only the latter counts toward exact archive-photo coverage.
Public research boundaryPedal detail pages keep internal research administration out of the public view. Research notes, source trails, evidence grades, and photo-state internals remain part of the archive's maintenance layer rather than the public record.
How to read this page

Flags are not failures.

An identity flag means the evidence deserves another look. The archive prefers an explicit unresolved relationship over a neat but unsupported merge.

For the underlying evidence hierarchy and technical-claim rules, see the methodology page.
