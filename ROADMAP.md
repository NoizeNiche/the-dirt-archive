## Research rule
The active sequence is **builder census → canonical builder identity → complete dirt catalog → individual pedal archaeology → public promotion**. Do not let isolated interesting pedals outrun incomplete builder catalogs.

## Editorial boundary
The archive documents historical identity and meaningful distinctions. It does not publish schematics, PCB layouts, gutshot galleries, cloning instructions, or complete circuit recipes.
