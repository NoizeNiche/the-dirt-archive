# Builder Organization

The Dirt Archive treats the builder as a canonical entity rather than a display-name string.

## Browse groups

- Major Manufacturers
- Independent / Boutique Builders
- Historical / Defunct Builders
- DIY / Kit / Small-Run
- Brands, Licensing & Production Relationships

## Canonicalization rules

1. One canonical builder record per real-world organization or historically meaningful entity.
2. Alternate spellings, abbreviations, punctuation variants and common aliases belong to the canonical record rather than separate builder records.
3. A pedal may retain historical manufacturer, distributor, licensee, or production relationships without creating duplicate builder cards.
4. Builder pages are the destination for the organization's history and catalog, not a place to duplicate pedal-generation research.
5. Unknown or disputed attribution remains explicitly marked as such instead of being forced into a canonical builder.

## Future browse dimensions

The public catalog should ultimately allow independent entry through:

- Builder
- Category
- Era
- Pedal
- Generation
- Identifying characteristics

This keeps the archive useful both for researchers who know what they are looking for and for visitors trying to identify an unknown pedal.
